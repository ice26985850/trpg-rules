Shadow of the God (S11)

Season: Chapter 3 — "Expedition Chapter" Basic Info - Suggested Level: Lv.50-57 - Difficulty: Hard - Expected Length: 2-3 game sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

Kotal (the Wolf God)'s avatar projection appears in the Rift for the first time — not a complete Chief-God, but already powerful enough. The players must repel this projection.

Main Location

  • A-Rank Rift (Wolf God's Domain):

Key Scenes

  • Kotal's projection appears for the first time — the shock of the power gap / Surviving in the projection's grasp — you don't have to win; staying alive is victory

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 8000, A-Rank Material (Orange · Legendary Shard), achievement for first repelling a God's projection

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Projection Hit Points (HP) −30%, damage −20%, Elites halved
