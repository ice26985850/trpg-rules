Scenario 2: The Missing Supplier

Basic Info

Item Content
Difficulty ★★ Basic
Suggested Reputation 5-20 (Unknown Shop ~ Neighborhood Favorite)
Suggested Party 3-5 players, recommend having Social or Foraging-related classes
Length 3-5 turns
Core Themes Investigation, interpersonal networks, light wilderness exploration

Synopsis

Your main material supplier-Old Hawk-hasn't delivered on time. It's the third day.

Old Hawk is a reliable Dwarf merchant who has never missed a delivery in ten years. His delivery route is fixed: Anvilhearth → Verdantvale → your town. After he set out from Anvilhearth last week, no one has seen him since.

Your inventory is running low. If you don't find him-or an alternative-you'll have nothing to sell starting next week.

Key NPCs

NPC Role Location
Old Hawk The missing supplier Somewhere on the Foothill Trade Road
Mrs. Hawk Supplier's wife, anxious Anvilhearth
Ranger Leah Boundary guard of Verdantvale, last person to see Hawk Verdantvale entrance
"Mole" A petty thug on the Foothill Trade Road, knows something Midway on the Foothill Trade Road

Turn Design

Turn 1: Stockout

Players discover Old Hawk hasn't come → inventory check (only 1-2 weeks left). GM gives three clue directions:

  • Direction A: Go to Anvilhearth to ask Mrs. Hawk about his last itinerary (Social route)
  • Direction B: Go to Verdantvale to find Ranger Leah-the last person to see Hawk (exploration route)
  • Direction C: Ask around town-has anyone else run out of stock? Could be a larger event (investigation route)

Turn 2: The Trail

If A: Mrs. Hawk provides the delivery list and route map. Among Hawk's last batch was a "special order"-a client specified rare volcanic sulfur.

If B: Leah recalls: "Three evenings ago, Hawk's mule cart passed my post. He said he'd travel by night-'an order was urgent.' I warned him not to take the night road; he said the client paid triple the freight."

If C: Players find they're not the only ones out of stock-three shop owners are all complaining. One potion shop owner whispers: "Heard there was a landslide on the Foothill Trade Road last week."

Turn 3: Exploring the Foothill Trade Road

Players head to the Foothill Trade Road to search. Wilderness exploration segment (use Foraging/Survival):

  • Encounter Check (GM secret roll): Difficulty (DC) 10 + region modifier. Failure → encounter bandit remnants or beasts
  • Search Check (Appraisal (APP), DC 12): success → discover an overturned mule cart, scattered cargo
  • Clue: One pack of volcanic sulfur is missing from the cargo. The cart was pushed down the slope, not an accident. There are pry marks on the wheel.

Turn 4: The Truth

Following the clues leads to "Mole"-a petty foothill thief living in an abandoned hunter's cabin. He's not the mastermind, but knows everything:

The Truth: An out-of-towner calling himself "the Alchemist" bought up large amounts of volcanic sulfur in Anvilhearth. Old Hawk's special order was his. But this "Alchemist" is actually making explosives-his goal is to blow open Verdantvale's ancient seal and take what's inside. Old Hawk discovered his intent and was locked in an abandoned mineshaft in the foothills.

Turn 5: Rescue and Choices

  • Rescue Old Hawk: Sneak into the mineshaft (Stamina (STA)/Stealth, DC 13). Inside are 2-3 hired thugs. Old Hawk is lightly wounded, but most cargo is intact.
  • Stop the Alchemist: If players are fast enough, they can reach Verdantvale before the seal is broken (Stamina (STA) Check DC 14, racing the clock). Verdantvale's Elf guards will assist.
  • The Mastermind: GM's option-this "Alchemist" is an outer operative of the Myriad Guild (corporate conspiracy line), or hired by the Undercurrent Network (black-market line), or simply a madman (standalone event)

Rewards
