Part One: Spreading Shadows (Lv.1-5)

Main Campaign Act One · Demacia Border → Noxus Front

Background

The White Wall Mine incident (the intro module) revealed an abnormal resonance of the Forbidden Magic Stone — not a natural phenomenon, but someone deliberately collecting Forbidden Magic Stone powder. The player squad tracking the source discovers a smuggling network spanning the Demacia border, leading to the Noxus Empire. A secret organization called "the Obsidian Circle" is using the Forbidden Magic Stone to develop anti-magic weapons — if they succeed, the mages of Runeterra will face annihilation, and Demacia will be dragged into total war.

Boss Battle: Forge Master Viktor (Lv.5)

Attribute Value
HP 800
AD 60
AR/MR 60/40
Special Forbidden Magic Stone Armor: reduces the first skill damage taken by 50% each turn

Three-Phase Mechanics: - P1 (100-70%): Throws a Forbidden Magic Bomb (3-cell range, Ability Power (AP)×1.0 magic damage + Silence 1 turn) - P2 (70-35%): Activates Forbidden Magic Field (all mage skills Difficulty Class (DC) +3, lasts until end of P2); summons 2 Forge Golems (Health Points (HP) 200, Attack Damage (AD) 35) - P3 (35-0%): Overload Mode — attacks twice per turn, but suffers Forbidden Magic backlash each turn (Health Points (HP)×5% True Damage)

Rewards

  • EXP: Total 2,800 (level up from Lv.1 to Lv.5)
  • Gold: Total 3,000-4,500
  • Exclusive Equipment: "Forbidden Magic Stone Charm" (Magic Resistance (MR)+25, 20% chance to reflect Ability Power (AP)×0.3 magic damage when taking magic damage)
  • Scenario-Exclusive Augment: "Obsidian Forging" — can inscribe a Forbidden Magic rune onto 1 piece of armor equipment (Armor (AR)+15, 10% damage reduction against mages)
