Scenario One: "Tower Awakens"

Attribute Content
Suggested Quality ★ Apprentice-grade
Player Count 1 starting mage
Estimated Sessions 1–2
Core Output 1 common Element Shard + first Apprentice + basis for a possible NPC Companion
Primary Threats Tower's residual magic, minor environmental hazards, optional social interaction
Prerequisite None — this scenario is the starting point of the campaign
Companion Opportunity May gain a first Apprentice in this scenario (who can later be promoted to a Companion; see Solo Play Supplemental Rules Chapter 2)

Background

You have inherited a tower — or rather, a tower has chosen you. It stands at the forest's edge on the outskirts of town. Its previous occupant, Silas Duskstar (塞拉斯·暮星), vanished under mysterious circumstances three years ago, leaving behind a building that is dilapidated but still functional. The basic infrastructure is intact — Laboratory Lv1, Library Lv1, Observatory Lv1, Containment Room Lv1, and Defense System Lv1.

But the tower is more than dust and vines. Silas left in too great a hurry, and some things were left behind.

Act One: Arrival and Discovery

Scene 1-1: Name Your Tower

Narration:

The carriage jolted along the last stretch of gravel road and came to a stop. Before you stands a three-story stone tower, its outer walls smothered in ivy, the observatory dome askew like a half-closed eye. In the dusk, not a single lamp burns in the tower's windows — yet you feel it watching you.

A rusted key is still lodged in the front door. The door is unlocked. Silas must have left in a real hurry.

Things for the player to do: - Name the tower - Choose the tower's location (outskirts recommended — this scenario assumes that by default, but any location works) - Assign character Attributes, choose a research style, and create a starting spell (see [Player Book §2.1–2.6]) - Describe your first impression of the tower — the GM adds details based on your description

Scene 1-2: Silas's Research Notes

Narration (after the player enters the Library):

The library's shelves lean at crooked angles, and the air is heavy with the smell of mildew and old parchment. While clearing the innermost shelf, your hand brushes against something that does not belong there — a notebook hastily wedged into a gap between the books.

The cover reads: "Silas Duskstar (塞拉斯·暮星) — Studies on Forest Magic Ecology, Volume Three."

The ink on the last few pages is darker and more frantic than what came before:

"Confirmed. The fireflies' behavior is not random. They are drawing a kind of geometric pattern I have never seen. Wait — stranger still — these patterns are connected to that thing beneath the old oak. I should make systematic observations, but… I have a bad feeling. It's as if something is watching me."

The notes break off here. The last page is dated three years ago — the day before Silas vanished.

GM Secret: While studying the shard vein in the forest, Silas accidentally triggered the alarm of an ancient seal. The seal's guardian — a slumbering fae creature — released a surge of magic upon waking that was enough to terrify Silas. Silas did not vanish; he fled. And now the seal's surges are strengthening again — because your tower (especially once the Laboratory is activated) disturbs the magical currents flowing underground.

Scene 1-3: The Tower's First "Visitor"

Narration (after the player completes initial setup, that night or the next day):

The observatory's visitor bell rings — a hesitant, uncertain chime.

NPC: Eira (艾拉) - Identity: A young woman from a nearby village who once helped Silas tend the tower - Attributes: Perception 4, Theory 3, Craft 4, Will 5, Erudition 2 - Personality: Shy but curious, with an undeveloped talent for magic - She brings: a basket of fresh bread ("The elder said the new resident might need it") and a question ("Um… do you take apprentices?")

GM Roleplay Hints: Eira is not here to demand anything. She hands over the bread, lingers by the doorway, and only then works up the courage to ask that question. If the player hesitates, she says, "I can help with cleaning and organizing — you can observe me first," then quickly changes the subject to the weather.

Player Choices: 1. Accept Eira as a novice apprentice: Requires an apprentice dorm (starting at Lv0 — not yet built, but a spare room can serve temporarily). Eira joins with Loyalty 60. → Gain an NPC Apprentice (promotable to Companion later) 2. Decline but stay in contact: Eira leaves the bread and says, "If you change your mind, I work at the mill in the village." → Recruitable any time in the future, starting Loyalty +10 (because you refused her first — which makes her feel you are serious) 3. Refuse outright: Eira leaves disappointed. → You may have lost an apprentice with special talent (GM Secret: Eira's hidden potential — if she becomes Chief Apprentice, her special skill is "Secret Talent," a d8 that directly yields 8, the content of which the GM designs)

Act Two: The First Research Loop in the Forest

Scene 2-1: Reproducing Silas's Observations

Trigger: Whether or not the player recruits Eira, the anomaly in the forest will naturally draw attention. If the player has read Silas's notes, they gain Difficulty Class (DC) –2 from the observational records in the notes. If Eira is present, she can provide the specific times and locations of the firefly appearances (she grew up in the village and has known these things since childhood).

Phase 1: Observe (Perception DC 12)

  • Success:

    You kept watch in the forest for two nights. On the third night — just as you were about to give up and file it away as the delusions of Silas's old age — you saw it. The fireflies' light did not flicker at random. They were drawing circles — precise geometric circles, about three feet in radius. And all the circles centered on a particular old oak — its trunk bearing a vine-covered hollow.

DC –2 to the next phase. Gain a research-note record.

  • Failure:

    You fed mosquitoes for two nights and saw plenty — but all ordinary fireflies. Maybe Silas really was too old. (GM note: next Observe DC –2 — you have begun, half-consciously, to notice that something is off.)

  • Critical Success (double 10):

    You saw not only the circles — you saw their source. Beneath the old oak's roots, a faint light pulsed — one beat of brightness, one of darkness, like a heartbeat. That was not a shard vein — that was alive. Something was sleeping beneath the tree.

Phase 2: Hypothesize (Theory DC 10)

  • Success (DC –2 to Experiment):

    You drew three pages of sketches and suddenly realized: the fireflies' light spectrum contained a line that did not belong to bioluminescence. That line's wavelength matched the resonance pattern of a basic Element Shard — especially the transition band between "Light" and "Life." Beneath the old oak, a shard was forming. Extract it, and you would gain your first research output.

  • Failure (DC +2 to Experiment):

    You proposed six hypotheses, each ingenious — but they contradicted one another. After repeated revisions, you realized you no longer knew which was correct. You decide to experiment with the most intuitive one. (GM: pick a wrong hypothesis direction, revealed when the experiment fails)

Phase 3: Preparation

Cost: 5 Gold Pieces (GP) (basic reagents), a collection container, and a shovel. If Eira is present, she provides a lantern and mosquito-repelling herbs (no check required).

Phase 4: Experiment (Craft DC 12)

You kneel at the base of the old oak; the shovel strikes a hard surface — not stone. It is a fist-sized crystal, semi-transparent, with a flowing point of light inside. It quivers slightly the instant you touch it.

You carefully scrape off a small flake with a reagent knife — the crystal splits into a perfect little point of light, hovering three inches above your palm.

  • Success (within Safety Threshold): Gain a "Light" Element Shard (common) ×1. The remainder of the crystal stays buried where it was — it needs months to generate the next shard. This is your first research output.
  • Failure (within Safety Threshold): Materials consumed. You pressed too hard — the point of light dissipates. But you learned the correct amount of force. Retryable (DC –2).
  • Critical Failure (exceeds Safety Threshold):
  • The crystal shatters, releasing a blinding flash. You are thrown back three steps, your eyes burning. But worse — you hear movement coming from beneath the roots.
  • A Root Guardian, knotted from vines and roots, awakens. It is not here to attack you — it is the seal's guardian, but you triggered it.

Enemy: Root Guardian (★ Apprentice-grade threat, adjusted for solo)

Attribute Value
HP 20 (originally 25, solo –20%)
Armor Class (AC) 12 (bark)
Speed 20 ft
Attack (Lash) 2d10+2 bludgeoning, range 10 ft
Special Ability Root Bind — Craft DC 12 or be restrained for 1 round (cooldown 2 rounds)
Weakness Fire damage ×1.5

Tactical Hints: The Root Guardian is not an evil creature — it is a guardian that was startled awake. The player may use Theory or Erudition DC 12 to identify its nature and soothe it in a calm tone ("I know what you are protecting. I will not harm it."). On success the guardian stops attacking — it emits a low sound, then retreats underground, leaving behind a small "Earth" Shard (common) as a sign that "you have proven your understanding." - If chosen to defeat it: gain Light Shard ×1 + vine fiber (usable for crafting common materials)

Phase 5: Analysis (Theory DC 8, automatic success)

You place the point of light into the analyzer. The data is clear: a pure "Light" Element Shard — yet it carries a faint secondary characteristic line. That line points toward… "Life." The fireflies spent thousands of years turning this shard vein into the hub of their own magical metabolism. This means the forest itself is one vast, slowly operating magical ecosystem.

With a trembling hand you write in your notes: "First shard. Source: forest-ecosystem symbiotic vein. Quality: common. Potential: to be observed — possible Life-element crossover."

Act Three: The Call of Awakening

Scene 3-1: The Tower's First Crisis

Trigger: After the player returns to the tower, that night.

You wake in the middle of the night. Not from a sound — from the silence. The tower's magical wards are humming low, a deep thrum — the Defense System (Lv1) has activated on its own. You scan with a Perception filter: a magical surge is traveling from the direction of the old oak, moving toward your tower.

The seal beneath the old oak — it does not merely guard shards. It guards something far older. And your experiment has disturbed it.

Scene 3-2: The Truth of the Seal

Observe (Perception or Erudition DC 14):

You flip through Silas's notebook and find scrawled notes in the margins you had overlooked before — written in almost invisible ink. Only by using magic (lighting a small spell near the paper) can you read them:

"Selena (賽琳娜) — she was not the first mage. She was the seventh. The names of the six before her were deliberately erased from history. This forest — their laboratory — has something beneath it. I dare not touch it."

Silas discovered this secret, then fled.

Player Choices:

  1. Reinforce the tower's defenses and prepare for the surge's source: Craft DC 12 to strengthen the Defense System (defense value temporarily +3, lasting 1d4 rounds). When the surge arrives it is a weakened Light-Element echo (HP 10, does not attack) — a probe released by the seal's fae guardian. If the player does not attack it, it conveys an image: a woman in ancient robes stands beneath the old oak, speaking softly to the roots. Then it vanishes.

Reward: You gain a memory shard — "Selena's First Experiment." This memory grants a permanent +1 to Erudition (historical knowledge) checks. Also, the vein beneath the old oak stabilizes — future Light/Life element research there gains DC –1.

  1. Go directly to the old oak and face the surge's source: Will DC 13 to confront the seal's guardian — a fae creature rising from beneath the roots, shaped like a woman woven from firefly light. It does not speak — it shows you a memory directly: Selena's first shard experiment in this very forest. When the memory ends, the guardian dissipates. You gain Selena's memory shard — usable any time in the future; when used, it grants a Critical Success effect on one Theory check (one-time).

GM Secret: This thread leads to Scenario Five, "Echoes of the Weaver."

  1. Ignore the surge and continue research: The surge lingers outside the tower for a night, then dissipates. You lose an important worldview clue, but the tower is unharmed. If you experiment at the old oak again in the future, DC +2 (the guardian is more wary of you).
