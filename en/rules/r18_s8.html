Case File: The Life-Restoring Rite of Nine Nether Hall

Suggested Level Range: Levels 9–10 Difficulty (Case Urgency): Grade S (Catastrophe; full party + outside aid) Case Type: Flying Corpse + Fierce Ghost + Cultivated Zombie swarm + the Master (control source / finale boss) Case-Closing EXP: 900 Core Mystery: Recovers all S3 foreshadowing (the Master / Life-Borrowing register / Register of Life and Death tampering). Nine Nether Hall's main altar (underground on the opposite shore) launches the "Life-Restoring Rite" — batch-transferring Yang Lifespan via the Life-Borrowing register, seeking to refine an entire district into a dead-zone corpse-cultivating ground. The players join forces with the Guild + Bing + Uncle Nine to raid the main altar, confront the Master (Challenge Rating (CR) 7), destroy the register, and halt the catastrophe.

One-Line Hook: Cheng Guiyao's final rallying call: "Nine Nether Hall's opposite-shore main altar, tonight's Life-Restoring Rite. The names on the Life-Borrowing register are turning into living dead one by one — for this one, we throw in the whole team, together with Bing and Uncle Nine."

I. Take Case

The Guild's Three-Lineage Council mobilizes fully; Cheng Guiyao, Bing, and Uncle Nine join forces. Grade S coefficient ×2.5 budget.

Cheng Guiyao (in the council hall, with the three lineage elders seated): "The opposite-shore main altar opens the rite tonight. Grade S, coefficient ×2.5 budget — but this isn't about money, it's about a whole district's lives. Bing's Yin Constable network, Uncle Nine's funeral-home intelligence, and our Guild's three lineages — all together."

Bing (shaking the original Register of Life and Death): "The people on the register — I'm watching their death-dates arrive. But once the main altar opens, they'll turn into living dead — not even the Register of Life and Death can save them. Destroy that register, and only then can I collect their souls."

Uncle Nine (lighting a cigarette): "I'll supply the materials. Thunder-Struck Wood, Celestial Master Talismans — my turf has them. But once you're inside, it's on you."

Level-Design Hint: Roll Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check Difficulty Class (DC) 17 to parse the main altar's feng shui and the rite's ritual protocol; Social (Persuasion) check Difficulty Class (DC) 17 to coordinate the division of labor among the three lineages + Yin Constable + funeral home.

II. On-Site Investigation

Scene 1: Opposite-Shore Main Altar Periphery (Dead-Zone Edge)

The underground opposite-shore main altar — the air is like congealed ink, the Luopan Compass needle spins wildly, and a low rumble like distant thunder rises from beneath your feet. The moment you draw near, your skin frosts over and your breath turns to mist. - Environment Yin Value (YIN): Base 14 (Extreme Yin → dead-zone edge). - Witness: Uncle Nine. - Uncle Nine (lowering his voice): "This is an enlarged version of the corpse-cultivating cellar. I helped them move 'materials' back in the day, so I know — underground it's all array stakes and cultivated corpses; the Master opens the altar at the lowest level." (GM Note: Social (Interview) check Difficulty Class (DC) 17 to confirm the main altar = enlarged corpse-cultivating cellar.) - Spirit Identification: Roll Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check Difficulty Class (DC) 17 to see Flying Corpses (Challenge Rating (CR) 6, Hard-Skin Mitigation −8 requiring century-grade tool / Celestial Master Talisman) patrolling the altar periphery and Cultivated Zombie swarms (Challenge Rating (CR) 5) standing guard; Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check Difficulty Class (DC) 18 to identify the "Flying Corpse."

Scene 2: The Rite Altar and Life-Borrowing Register (Hidden Information)

  • Appraisal (Knowledge (KNW)) check Difficulty Class (DC) 18 to infiltrate the altar and seize the authentic "Life-Borrowing Register" (containing all living names from S3/S6) along with the original tampered Register of Life and Death.
  • Hidden Information: A page tucked in the register reads "Above the Master, there is another Register-Keeper" — leaving a loose end. Requires Intuition (INS) check Difficulty Class (DC) 18 to read the inserted page.

Combat / Non-Combat Options - Combat (Main Axis): Flying Corpses / cultivated corpses require century-grade tools; the Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 7, altar guardian) must be captured/destroyed; the key is to destroy the register + eliminate the Master (control source). - Non-Combat (Key to Breaking the Case): Seize the authentic register and halt the rite's ritual protocol.

GM Hint (Boss Non-Combat is the Correct Solution): The Flying Corpses and life-restoring vessels at the Life-Restoring Rite site have absurdly thick hard-skin (mitigation) too — you could swing ten times and still not draw blood. Remember, the goal here was never to kill, but to interrupt the life-restoring ritual. First use glutinous rice, Ink Line Reel warding, or Corpse-Still Talismans to control the scene, then deliver (Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check) or seal (Soul-Gathering Gourd / Corpse-Still Talisman); once the ritual breaks, the life-restoring vessels naturally dissipate. Forcing it wastes effort — use talismans, not blades. See the GM Book "Principles for Handling Hard-Skin Bosses."

Level-Design Hint (Scene Order): 1. Peripheral Luopan Compass direction-finding (Knowledge (KNW) check) → lock onto the main altar anomaly. 2. Spirit Sight confirms Flying Corpses and Cultivated Zombie swarm (Intuition (INS) check). 3. Infiltrate the altar and seize the register (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain the authentic copy and original register. 4. Read the inserted page (Intuition (INS) check) → leave the "Register-Keeper" loose end.

III. Prepare Rite (Required Spirit Tools / Materials / Rite Scale)

  • Materials: Century Peachwood Sword (required for Flying Corpse), Thunder-Struck Wood (ignores hard-skin), Celestial Master Talisman (Rare; exorcism effect +2), Coin Sword, Demon-Sealing Array (consumes Mana Pool (MP) 12), Soul-Gathering Gourd, Purification Talisman (Yin Value (YIN) −3), Ink Line Reel (Warding), Five-Thunder Palm (Level 3+), Invite Deity Possession (Level 3+), Deliverance materials.
  • Rite Scale: Epic. Full party + outside aid division of labor — Scholar breaks the rite's nodal eye, Warrior guards, Main Caster + Medium set the Demon-Sealing Array to trap the Fierce Ghost (maintained with Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 17), Bing suppresses the Yin Constable network, Uncle Nine supplies intelligence and materials.
  • Time Pressure: The rite's "life-restoring" countdown hits at Zi Hour; miss it and all living names on the register turn into cultivated corpses (Grade S district catastrophe).
  • Level Hint: Setting the altar requires Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check Difficulty Class (DC) 17; the Demon-Sealing Array requires Level 4+.

IV. Exorcise & Collect

Best Plan: Allied Assault to Sever the Source

  1. Scholar rolls Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check Difficulty Class (DC) 18 to break the rite's nodal eye.
  2. Warrior uses Thunder-Struck Wood / Century Peachwood Sword to suppress the Flying Corpse (Challenge Rating (CR) 6, roll Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check Difficulty Class (DC) 17), Cultivated Zombie swarm (Challenge Rating (CR) 5).
  3. Main Caster + Medium set the Demon-Sealing Array to trap the altar-guarding Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 7), break the warding with Five-Thunder Palm then capture with the Soul-Gathering Gourd.
  4. Key: Eliminate the Master (Challenge Rating (CR) 7, control source) — allied combined strike, destroy the authentic register, the rite collapses and the dead zone drops sharply.

Potential Risks

  • Flying Corpse Hard-Skin Mitigation −8: Non-century tools are ineffective.
  • Dead Zone (Yin Value (YIN) 16+): Each round pass a Composure (CMP) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15; two consecutive failures → Yin Assimilation.
  • If the Master escapes: If the register is not destroyed, the catastrophe continues.

Alternative Plan

  • Without Thunder-Struck Wood, force a breakthrough with the Celestial Master Talisman + Invite Deity Possession; if the Demon-Sealing Array fails, Bing uses Yin Constable authority to temporarily hold the dead souls and buy time.

V. Cleanup

  • Purification: Full main-altar purification requires Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check Difficulty Class (DC) 17, several Purification Talismans (Yin Value (YIN) −3), bringing Yin Value (YIN) back below 8 and lifting the dead zone.
  • Client Feedback: Cheng Guiyao: "Nine Nether Hall's Hong Kong vein — severed." Bing takes away the original Register of Life and Death; the Guild records a Legendary "Annihilated Nine Nether Hall Main Altar" reputation; Grade S case closure additionally grants the "Patriarch's Favor" passive (Advantage on Invite Deity Possession Composure (CMP) checks).
  • Case-Closing Reward: Nether Coin (COIN) 1200–1500; EXP = base 600 (Grade S) + investigation discoveries +20×5 + correct allied handling +100 + cleanup +60 + unresolved hook (Register-Keeper) +40 = total approx. 900.
  • Unresolved Mysteries (loose end, sequels possible):
  • Register inserted page "Above the Master, there is another Register-Keeper";
  • Living names still remain on the register, unremoved;
  • The source of the Register of Life and Death tampering — Bing continues the pursuit.

Closing Hook: In the firelight, the Master falls. But as you open the register's inserted page, a line of small text turns your spine cold — "Register-Keeper, quietly awaiting the next great enterprise." This battle is won; the war is not over.
