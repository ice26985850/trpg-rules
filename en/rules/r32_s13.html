Scenario Three: "Echo"

Item Content
Type Lost Traveler
Recommended Marks 3-6 (mid-level experience)
Player Count 3-5
Sessions 2-3
Core Attributes Traverse, Negotiation, Retrieval
Difficulty ★★★☆☆

Commission

A young man—A-Jie—has "vanished" in his apartment. But he hasn't truly disappeared—his roommates occasionally catch his shadow at the end of the hallway, and hear his footsteps at two in the morning. Food in the fridge goes missing, yet no one sees who ate it. The roommates called the police, but the police found no sign of kidnapping or suicide. One roommate's grandmother, after hearing about it, said: "He isn't missing. He's stuck 'on the other side' and can't get out." She gave them the contact of a Gap Walker.

A-Jie has been "half-missing" for three weeks. His condition is worsening.

Surface Map

  • A-Jie's Apartment: A shared rental near the university, fourth floor. A-Jie's room faces north; outside the window is a fire lane.
  • University Lab: A-Jie is a graduate student in physics. His research topic is "spatial folding"—theoretical.
  • Rooftop: The top floor of the apartment. A-Jie's roommate said he "mentioned going to the rooftop to smoke" the night before he vanished.

Gap Layer Map

The apartment's Gap Layer is almost identical to the Surface—which is the most dangerous part, because it is so easy to confuse them. The only difference: in the Gap Layer, the door to "A-Jie's room" is open (on the Surface, his roommates never opened it). Inside the room is an Artificial Threshold (Difficulty Class (DC) 12) that A-Jie "created"—he repeatedly stood at his doorway hesitating whether to go to the rooftop, creating this threshold.

Why Is A-Jie Trapped?

A-Jie's experiment was not entirely theoretical. He used a device of his own design—the "Resonator"—to create faint spatial resonance in his room. He did not know this was exactly how to open a Gap Threshold. He succeeded. But he got too absorbed—he stayed too long in the Gap, his Gap Endurance depleted, and he is now in the Middle Stage of Gap Sickness: he is no longer sure which layer he is in.

Key NPCs

NPC Info
A-Jie (Lost Traveler) 23-year-old physics graduate student. Smart, stubborn, obsessed with "the nature of reality." Currently in the Middle Stage of Gap Sickness—the GM does not tell the players whether he knows he is trapped.
Xiao-Mei (Roommate) A-Jie's roommate and close friend. The one most eager to save him. Can provide A-Jie's personal belongings as clues.
Advisor Professor A professor in the physics department. Knows A-Jie was researching "spatial folding," but when the players ask, he seems uneasy—"That research… should not have continued. I warned him."
Lost Item Collector A previous-generation Gap Walker who once lived in this apartment. Hoards items in the Gap. Knows where A-Jie is, but will not reveal it unless the players trade for it with a "new Lost Item."

Key Lost Items

Lost Item Quality Difficulty Class (DC) Location
A-Jie's Research Notes (Copy) Common Lost Item 8 University Lab Gap Layer
Resonator Gap Construct 18 A-Jie's Room Gap Layer
Mother's Necklace Memory Crystal 15 On A-Jie (brought into the Gap with him)

Key Scenes

  1. Explore the Apartment Gap Layer: Enter the Gap from the rooftop (Artificial Threshold DC 12). The apartment Gap Layer is identical to the Surface—the greatest horror is "nothing has changed." Traverse DC 14 to confirm whether you are truly in the Gap Layer.

  2. Find A-Jie's Tracks: A-Jie's room door is open. Retrieval DC 10 to discover traces of his recent activity in the Gap—he stuck a sticky note on the fridge: "I'm here. Find the Resonator."

  3. Negotiate with the Lost Item Collector: The Collector (Negotiation DC 14) knows A-Jie's location. He will demand a "new Lost Item" in exchange for the information. The players can find a Lost Item from another area and give it to him.

  4. Find A-Jie: He is on the apartment rooftop—but the Gap Layer's rooftop. He sits at the edge, looking at a false starfield (the starfield in the Gap is upside down). He is in the Middle Stage of Gap Sickness—unsure which layer he is in, trying to "derive" the dimension he is in using physics theory.

  5. Convince A-Jie to Leave: Negotiation DC 16. He will argue—"Everything here is more meaningful. The causality of the Surface World is wrong." The players need to:

  6. Present Surface items (Anchoring Items preferred) to let him "perceive" reality

  7. Or find his Anchoring Item—his mother's necklace (on him; Memory Crystal DC 15 to retrieve)

  8. Exit the Gap: After A-Jie is brought back to the Surface, he is in the Early Stage of Gap Sickness—requires Surface Rest for 7 days. Return Mark +1.

Ending Options

  • After A-Jie recovers, he becomes a potential Gap Walker (NPC acquaintance).
  • If the players fail to rescue him in time, he will, in the Late Stage of Gap Sickness, become a new Lost Item Collector.
  • The Resonator is recovered—may become a key item in future scenarios.
