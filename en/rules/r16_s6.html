S6: Respite

Level 11 — Boundless City | Milestones 6–7 | 7–10 hours | Intermediate (Social/Investigation) Applicable Mode: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

City Entrance

Streets, buildings, parked cars. The sky is gray—the clouds still, the light even. This is not a real sky, but your brain accepts it.

A BNTG trader pushing a cart passes by. "New here? From the suburb? You smell of the wheat field." He laughs—a genuine, weary human laugh. "Welcome to the Boundless City, friend."

Market and Trade

The BNTG trade center—a market converted from an abandoned warehouse. Stalls sell anything you can imagine: Almond Water (standard and variants), weapons (homemade and scavenged), maps, intelligence. Everything has a price.

Here you meet a familiar name—Lao Zhao himself. He is older than you imagined, with scars from Level 2 on his arm. He pats your shoulder: "You saw my messages. Good. Many others didn't."

Mira—the cartographer—shows her hand-drawn map of Level 11's blocks. Dr. Wu offers free medical care in a corner of the market. The city is a gathering place for Wanderers; here you find a strange sense of belonging.

The Infiltrator

But you begin to notice something wrong.

A Wanderer you know—who traded with you just a few days ago—has changed. His smile lags half a second. His fingers... the count seems off. He no longer comes to the market. His "friends" say he moved away.

Another Wanderer vanished. Then another. In the city, vanishing is not rare—people come and go. But this time is different. The vanished ones' belongings are still in their lodgings. As if they simply walked out—and never came back.

Investigation DC 18 reveals the truth: a Skin-Stealer has been lurking in the city for weeks. It has already replaced at least two people. It is seeking its next target. You may know it—it may be someone you trust.

Find it. Before it replaces the next person—and that person could be you.

Intelligence Gathering

While investigating the Skin-Stealer, you gather key intelligence about what lies deeper:

  • The Legend of Level !: Every veteran has heard of it. Ten kilometers of hospital corridor. Red alarms. Swarms of Entities. Those who pass through can reach "further out." Those who fail—no one has seen them since.
  • Entrance Location: The basement of an abandoned hospital at the edge of the Level 11 city. The elevator only goes down—to Level !.
  • M.E.G.'s Advice: "If you're going—wear your best running shoes. Abandon all unnecessary weight. This is not a fight. This is pure speed."

The Decision

You stand before the abandoned hospital at the city's edge. You have traded away all unneeded items for energy bars and Almond Water. Lao Zhao comes to see you off: "I've never been to Level !. But I know those who have—who came back alive. They say those ten kilometers changed them. Not their bodies—here." He taps his chest.

You walk into the hospital. The elevator doors open. There is only one button—marked "!".

You press it.
