Scenario S2: Jungle City Street War

Type: Multiplayer Squad | Suggested Level: 2 | Difficulty: ★★ | Estimated Duration: 3-5 hours

Story Synopsis

Sanhok — an abandoned resort city in the tropical jungle. Humid air, vine-covered buildings, and a gun barrel that could be hidden behind every window. Red Zone Bombing makes this dead city even more dangerous.

Battlefield Map

Sanhok (Jungle City) — 4 km × 4 km (Jungle + Urban Mixed)

Terrain Composition: Tropical Jungle (50%), Abandoned Urban Area (30%), River (20%)

Landmarks

  • Paradise Resort (urban|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): A multi-story building complex in the city center — suited for floor-by-floor combat
  • Training Camp (military|Loot: Military (Purple)): A military training facility in the north of the island, with multiple connected buildings
  • Cave (natural|Loot: Common (Green)): An underground cave system — a perfect ambush point
  • Riverside Dock (urban|Loot: Common (Green)): Warehouses and docks by the water

Enemy Configuration

A total of 6 enemy squads (including the player squad).

  • Rookie Squad (★): 3 squads, members each, drop point
  • Steady Squad (★★): 2 squads, members each, drop point
  • Assault Squad (★★★): 1 squad, members each, drop point

Battlefield Conditions

  • Circle: Building Lock — the final circle is within a building complex — forcing close-quarters street combat
  • Airdrop: 3 drops, ['During the 2nd circle contraction', 'During the 3rd circle contraction', 'Start of the 4th circle'] —
  • Red Zone Bombing: Yes
  • Vehicles: Off-road Vehicle, Jeep, Motorcycle
  • Weather: Overcast (possible showers)
  • Time of Day: Day

Scenario Phases

1. Drop & Site Selection (1 turn)

GM Note: The jungle canopy provides more cover for drops — but it also means you don't know who dropped in the same area.

2. Jungle Looting (4 turns)

GM Note: Loot points in the jungle are more scattered. Use hearing to determine if enemies are nearby.

3. Urban Combat (5-6 turns)

GM Note: Multi-story combat inside buildings is the core of this scenario. Teach players to use floor advantage — higher ground gives better vision, but windows expose your position. Introduce Red Zone Bombing — forcing players to move out of cover.

4. Final Street War (3-5 turns)

GM Note: The circle shrinks to within a single building — the climax of close-quarters firefight.

Victory Condition

Be the last surviving squad

Rewards

Achievement XP CR
Successfully eliminate an enemy contestant 80 30
Squad wipes an entire enemy squad (per member) 150 50
Survive to top 10 in a single match 150 100
Survive to top 5 in a single match 250 150
Achieve final victory (Chicken Dinner) 500 500
Successfully revive a downed teammate 80 20

The above are general reward baselines; scenario-specific objective rewards may be added by the GM as appropriate.
