Day of Awakening (S1)

Season: Season 1 "Awakening Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.1-5 - Difficulty: Simple - Expected Length: 1-2 sessions (4-8 hours) - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end of the document.

Core Conflict

Players awaken in Shinjuku, Tokyo—rifts open around them, and the System activates for the first time. They must survive the chaos and find other Awakeners.

Main Locations

  • Shinjuku Ruins: Shinjuku streets two weeks after D-Day. Abandoned cars, shattered glass, and the occasional roar of monsters echoing in the distance.
  • Shelter Underground Parking: A makeshift stronghold organized spontaneously by survivors, with basic supplies and a few injured people.

Key NPCs

  • Instructor Tanaka: A former Self-Defense Force member, now the protector of the survivor group. Gives the players their first quest.
  • Dr. Sato: Medical staff at the shelter, responsible for treating the wounded.

Key Scenes

  • The System's first activation—the awe of an attribute panel manifesting in one's vision
  • First battle against a monster (Void Rat)—learning attack checks
  • Rescuing survivors trapped in a collapsed building
  • Encountering signs of a D-Rank Rift, setting up S2

Enemy List

  • Void Rat (Quantity 3-5 | Lv.1-3, for tutorial purposes)
  • Lesser Rift Aggregator (Quantity 1 | Lv.5, scenario's final encounter)

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 200, Lesser Healing Potion ×2, White · Common equipment exchange voucher

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Void Rat count halved (2), Aggregator Hit Points (HP) −20%
