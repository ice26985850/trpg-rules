Scenario 4: The Lone Traveler's Expedition

Basic Info

Item Content
Difficulty ★★★★ High (Solo)
Suggested Reputation 40-70
Length 6-10 Turns
Core Theme Long journey, Legendary material, facing the past

Story Summary

A letter with no signature. The paper is very old—at least ten years.

It has only one line: "Do you still remember why you retired? If you're ready—come to the Sighing Plains. I'll be waiting there."

No one knows who sent it. But you know what the Sighing Plains are. The final battle of the Rift War was fought there. You were there too.

You can choose not to go. The shop will keep operating, earning steady money each week. No one will blame you.

But this letter will stay in the drawer. Every night after closing, you'll glance at it once more.

Key NPCs

NPC Role
The letter's mysterious sender Could be an old comrade, an old enemy, or your own past. Determined by the GM based on the player's retirement reason.
NPCs met on the journey Residents of villages along the way—they have their own stories. Some relate to the war.
Your companion Will they go with you? Leave the shop unattended for two months? Or stay to mind the shop?

Journey Design

Before Departure: The Choice (Turn 1)

  • Bring companion or not? Bring → no income while the shop is closed. Don't bring → companion minds the shop (base income), but solo travel is riskier.
  • How much supply to prepare? How to plan the route? → Player makes strategic decisions.

The Journey: Road to the Sighing Plains (Turns 2-5)

Route: your town → Verdantvale (1 week) → Watchkeep (2 weeks) → Sighing Plains (1 week). Total 4 weeks. Fast travel means allowed (relay stations, caravans).

Each stop along the way is a mini-scene:

Stop Scene
Verdantvale Meet an Elf veteran who retired here after the war. He recognizes you. He has something to return to you—an old badge, a broken sword, or an unsent letter.
Watchkeep The border has changed greatly. The outpost you knew is now a small town. The tavern keeper's daughter asks you: "Did you know my father? He was also a Rift War veteran. He's been missing for many years."
Sighing Plains The battlefield is covered with wildflowers. But some scars lie underground. You need to find the sender.

Sighing Plains: Facing the Past (Turns 6-7)

The GM determines the sender's identity based on the player's retirement reason (result of the d6 pre-retirement backstory table):

d6 Retirement Reason Sender's Identity What he/she wants
1-Wounded The enemy who wounded you back then—but he's no longer an enemy. He's old. He wants to apologize. Reconcile. Or at least, a drink.
2-Weary A young adventurer—the one you saved back then. Now he's retired too. He wants to say thanks. No chance back then.
3-Inherited An old acquaintance of the former shop owner. He has a relic left by the former owner—"On his deathbed he said, leave it for the next person to take over." Return the relic.
4-Love The one you loved. He/she heard about your shop. Wants to know if you're doing well. Farewell—a true farewell.
5-Mission A hunter from the Broken Blade League—the mission you gave up back then, he completed it for you. Hand you the mission's reward.
6-Disillusioned The person you wronged. He came. He forgives you—but wants to hear you say it in person. Forgiveness.

Core Principle: This scene is not a fight. It's a conversation. The GM need not arrange enemies—what's needed is an NPC with warmth, and a story that needs to be told.

Discovering the Legendary Material (Turn 8)

At the Sighing Plains, besides facing the past, the player also has a chance to discover a Legendary material that grows only on ancient battlefields—Ghost Orchid (Material Codex entry no.). It only appears under moonlight.

If the player has already found inner peace by now (completed the conversation with the past), Foraging Difficulty (DC) drops from 20 to 16. If the player is still struggling—Difficulty (DC) stays 20. This isn't game difficulty; it's a state of mind.

Return (Turns 9-10)

Return home with the Legendary material, a story that's been told, and a more complete self.

The shop is still that shop. But you are no longer the person who set out.

Rewards

Achievement Reward
Travel to the Sighing Plains Unlock a stable source of the Legendary material "Ghost Orchid" (may be foraged there once per season)
Reconcile with the past Permanently gain "Inner Peace"—Immunity to Inspiration burnout (the original rule's effect of 3 consecutive Inspiration failures → burnout no longer triggers)
Bring back the Legendary material May craft one Legendary-tier work (recipe of choice)
Companion who stayed behind earns merit Stay-behind companion Loyalty +3 (they proved they can stand on their own)
