Scenario Five: Rusted Factory Infiltration [Level (Lv.) 5]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 5 × 3-4 players | Duration: 4-5 hours | Map: Rusted Factory (Industrial Zone)

Commission

"The Blacksmith" needs a military-grade metal sample from inside the factory. Reward: 5,000 ₽ + Workbench Level (Lv.) 3 upgrade materials.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry — South Perimeter Wall Breach

Perception Target Number (TN) 17: an Iron Brotherhood watchtower above the wall. Stealth TN 14 can avoid it. Failure triggers the watchtower's warning fire (Iron Brotherhood Soldier ×2 + alarm summons patrol).

Scene 2: Foundry Workshop

A massive steel structure. Iron Brotherhood Patrol ×4 patrols the workshop (fixed route, loops every 45 minutes). - Primary Objective: Military-grade Metal Sample (near the furnace control console, Purple ×1) - Optional loot: Industrial Tools (Blue ×1) + Metal Scrap (White ×2)

Scene 3: Chemical Storage Zone

Toxic gas hazard zone (no gas mask: each round Courage TN 14 or become Poisoned 1d4/round). - Loot: Chemical Agent ×2 (Purple, 2 Slots each) + Industrial Acid (Blue) - Danger: Chemical Mutant ×1 lurking among the storage tanks

Scene 4: Railway Freight Yard

Multiple containers. Some sealed. Tech Target Number (TN) 17 (mechanical lock). - Iron Brotherhood Patrol ×3 + Patrol Captain ×1 pass by periodically (every 30 minutes) - Random inside containers: Military-spec supplies (d6≥5 = Purple ×1, 3 = Blue ×1)

Scene 5: Extraction

  • Route A: South Perimeter Wall Breach (Iron Brotherhood may have sealed it — blockade team arrives after 2d6 rounds)
  • Route B: Railway Freight Yard (random beacon)
  • Route C: Underground Pipeline Emergency Exit (safe but time-consuming)
  • Route D: Administration Building Rooftop (dangerous, requires Iron Brotherhood radio + flare gun)
