Scenario SA-3: "Echoing Bone Pile" — Echoes of the Bone Pile

Difficulty: Medium | Inquisitor: Ilya Cole (Radical)
Target Region: Abandoned Hive "Bone Pile", Level 2000, Chaos Cult stronghold
Recommended Attributes: Levels 3-4 | Challenge Rating (CR) Budget: 2-3 (Standard~Difficult)

Background

Ilya gave you an impossible mission — to infiltrate the Chaos Cult stronghold deep in the abandoned hive alone, and stop them before their ritual completes. He also gave you an assistant — a Psyker he does not trust.

NPC Companion

Xavier (Unregistered Psyker, Psi 2)

Item Content
One-line description A frail young man who can hear the voices of the Warp. He fears himself — and fears you
Motivation Prove he is not a monster. The Inquisitor said he would be released after 5 services — this is his 3rd
Bottom Line Never let psychic power harm the innocent
Initial Attitude −1 (Suspicion — he believes you were sent by the Inquisitor to monitor him)
Attributes Faith 2 / Body 2 / Mind 4 / Tech 2 / Fate 3
Key Skills Psyniscience (Trained), Investigation (Trained)
Equipment No weapon, no armor, Warp Resonance Stone (senses nearby Chaos contamination)
Special Ability Psyniscience: Automatically senses Chaos contamination and Warp anomalies within 30m. Psychic Blast: Once per combat, deals 2d6 mental damage to a target within 20m (automatically triggers a Warp phenomenon after use)

Internal Seduction Trigger

"Xavier is the same as that psyker. They are both unregistered. Can you trust him? Perhaps you should act — before he betrays you."
