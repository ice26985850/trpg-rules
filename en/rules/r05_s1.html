Dungeon Module D1 — Goblin Lair (Goblin Warren)

Recommended Rank: F / E (Challenge Rating (CR) 1–8)｜Theme: Wild cave, low-tier resource farming Acceptance: Besides the guild commission "F-For2 Goblin Patrol Clearance," players may also explore voluntarily (see GM Book 17.1). Map: maps/D1_哥布林巢穴.yaml ＋ maps/D1_哥布林巢穴.svg This game has no multiclass; solo entry follows the player-count scaling rules (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick).

How to Enter (Opening Beat)

  • Take a guild commission: Accept the F-tier "F-For2 Goblin Patrol Clearance" on the Adventurer's Guild bulletin board (the deep Throne Cavern also corresponds to E-For1 Goblin Lair Purge) (corresponds to Rank F/E, CL 1–8); or designated by the GM as a leveling ground.
  • Voluntary exploration: Players may also, after enrolling in S0, go explore the abandoned mine outside the city on their own (GM Book 17.1).
  • Leveling positioning: The top choice for familiar farming / resource farming after the prologue; suggested CL 1–4 to fight D1 + F/E commissions, grinding Rank from F to E (see "Prologue: Alno Holy City (Hub)").
  • Solo mode: Supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick; the three combat rooms come with 10×10 positioning diagrams (map/D1_戰鬥_*.yaml), entering a room triggers standard combat (Chapter 7), returning to the exploration round after clearing.

I. Background

In the depths of the abandoned mine on the northern foot of Dawn Village, a band of goblins has occupied the old mining tunnels, kidnapping village children to extract the location of the ore vein. The guild lists this as an F-tier subjugation target; deep in the lair lurks a chieftain who styles himself "Goblin King."

III. Key Room Details

  • R4 Trap Corridor (weak): Perception Difficulty Class (DC) 12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); trigger 1d6 Pierce. Disarm Difficulty Class (DC) 14.
  • R6 Sacrifice Pit (strong): Perception Difficulty Class (DC) 20; trigger 3d6 Bludgeon + Stun 1 round. Rockfall can block the R5↔R7 passage (GM ruling).
  • R3 Storage Cave: Open chest Difficulty Class (DC) 12; trap 30% (medium, 2d6). Contains: Goblin Earring ×2, Tattered Cloth ×3, Gold (G) 40~80.
  • R8 Escape Tunnel: Hostage (village child) imprisoned here; after rescue, clear the dungeon, return to guild for settlement EXP +102 / G +80 / IP +3 (equivalent to F-For1 commission).

IV. Minor Boss — Goblin King (CR5)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 Boss framework (simplified version):

  • Health (HP) 54 (CR5 formula)｜Attack Bonus (ATK) +2 (floor(1+0.25×5))｜Evasion (EVA) 10 (10+floor(0.15×5))｜Damage/hit 12 (floor(6+1.2×5))
  • Trait (1 stage): Brute Swing — once every 3 rounds, deals Damage ×1.5 to the front row and Knockback 1 grid.
  • Weakness: Fire ×1.5 (see monster bestiary fire-weakness note).
  • Drop: Goblin King Emblem (plot item, redeemable at guild for Influence Points (IP) +2); basic materials + low chance of Fine recipe.

V. Clear Rewards (full clear)

Item Value
Experience (EXP) ≈ 576 (R2 68 + R5 108 + R7 400, EXP accumulates to Character Level (CL) progress (Class Level ClassLv separately pools independent Class Experience (CEXP), not shared with EXP))
Gold (G) 150~250
Influence Points (IP) +3 (rescue hostage) / +2 (emblem redemption)
Drop Goblin Earring / Tattered Cloth / low chance of Fine recipe

Design tip: D1 is an early "farm EXP + G + materials" stable ground, preventing players from leveling only through the main Demon General battles and causing economic imbalance. Can be re-farmed daily (see Commission Catalog reset rules).
