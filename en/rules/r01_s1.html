Chapter 1: The Beginning of the Death Game

Scene Trigger: 5:30 PM

"A forced-teleport light envelops you. When you open your eyes, you find yourself back in the central plaza of the Town of Beginnings — together with every other player."

"The sky turns blood red. A giant, faceless figure in a red cloak appears above the plaza."

"Welcome to my world. I am Kayaba Akihiko."

Kayaba Akihiko's Declaration (GM should build as much oppressive dread as possible):

  1. Logout is impossible — NerveGear blocks the logout command
  2. In-game death = real-world death (NerveGear's high-power microwaves destroy the brain)
  3. The only escape — clear all 100 floors
  4. All players receive the "Hand Mirror" — auto-used, displays true appearance

Intro Conflict: Players' Reactions

The GM describes the varied reactions of the surrounding NPC players:

NPC Reaction Representative
Panic and crying Most players
Silent acceptance A few who stay calm
Immediate action Klein, Kirito (NPC), the few proactive ones
Loss-of-control rage Very few (attack NPCs, destroy items, etc.)

Choice for Players: 1. Calm down and listen to the information 2. Try to help panicking players 3. Look for others who are also acting proactively 4. Leave the crowd alone

Intro NPC Interaction

Klein — the red-scarf samurai who initiates conversation amid the chaos: - Personality: passionate, rough, has leadership but doesn't want the responsibility - First interaction: if he notices the player staying calm, he proactively proposes partying up - Provides info: basic combat tricks, SAO closed-beta experience

Argo — sells basic intel in the crowd: - Sells the "Strategy Guide booklet" - Contains: Floor 1 map, basic monster weaknesses, safe-zone markers - Price: free (in the chaos, she chooses to hand it out for free)

Chapter 6: Formation of the Clearers (Floors 11-15)

Background

After Floor 10 is cleared, the player population clearly splits: - Clearers: top players actively clearing floors (~300-500 people) - Middle Layer: capable but non-frontline players (~3,000-4,000 people) - Starting Layer: players who stay on Floor 1 and barely fight (~2,000-3,000 people)

Story Direction: players are now seen as "Clearer candidates" — NPCs start expecting them to join the frontline.

"I'm Asuna. Vice-Leader of the Knights of the Blood Oath. Heard you did well on Floor 10."

Silica Rescue Mission

"My familiar Pina... was taken!"

This is an emotional side story — on Floor 20 players meet the tamer girl Silica; her feathered-dragon familiar Pina was captured by a group of players (actually a precursor organization to the PK guild).

Mission Flow: 1. Track the kidnappers led by Silica 2. Cross Floor 20's dangerous forest region 3. Find the kidnappers' camp — sneak in or breach head-on 4. Rescue Pina (Pina is injured, needs potion healing) 5. Defeat the kidnapper boss (Lv.22, short-sword user)

Chapter 11: Meaning of Sacrifice (Floors 36-45)

Death Creeps Closer

On these floors, the Clearers begin to pay real sacrifices. The GM should, at this stage: - Let NPC Clearer members die in battle (non-player characters) - Describe the growing tally of strike-through lines on the Black Iron Palace monument - Let players feel "every Boss fight is a gamble"

Fate of the Moonlit Black Cats (if still extant)

This is a key node from the original work. Depending on Vol.1 player choices, several developments:

Route A: Player did not join / did not interfere → Moonlit Black Cats trigger a trap in the Floor 38 maze, wiped out. Keita dies. → Players may see their names struck through at the Black Iron Palace. → One of the saddest scenes in the campaign.

Route B: Player mentored them → Moonlit Black Cats members level up, but still take casualties in the Floor 40 Boss fight. → Keita survives, thanks the player. → He decides to stay in the middle layer and no longer join frontline clears.

Route C: Player joined Moonlit Black Cats → Keita and some members, led by the player, joined the Clearers. → All their fates bind to the player's guild.

Argo's Secret Intel

"There are some things... you might want to know. About Heathcliff."

Argo contacts the player alone on Floor 42. She provides the following intel:

  1. Heathcliff's HP has never dropped below yellow in combat — can't be coincidence
  2. Someone inside KoB mentioned Heathcliff "never sleeps"
  3. A player saw Heathcliff enter the maze alone late at night

Hint for Players: this is the seed of distrust. Players now have reason to suspect Heathcliff — but no proof.

Chapter 14: Threads of Truth (Floors 61-70)

Heathcliff's Slip

In the Floor 65 Boss fight, design a key scene:

The Boss's attack hits Heathcliff. His HP bar turns yellow — but only for an instant. The next second, the HP bar returns to green.

And — at the instant the HP bar turned yellow — a yellow system marker appeared at Heathcliff's feet. That's the "indestructible object" marker visible only to GM privileges.

Awareness Check: - All players present make an INT check (difficulty -15) - Success notices this instant - Great success (01-05) also notices more: Heathcliff's attack animation has a tiny delay — a trace of system-assist calibration

Follow-up: - If a player notices and confronts Heathcliff → he calmly denies, "You're too nervous" - Asuna defends Heathcliff — she noticed nothing wrong - But the seed of doubt is planted

If party strength is below preset: - Provide 1-2 extra NPC comrades - Boss HP -20% - Increase number of safe rooms

Important Reminders

  2. Daily life matters — reserve at least 15-20 minutes per session for non-combat scenes
