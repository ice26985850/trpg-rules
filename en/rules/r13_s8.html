Scenario SB-3: "Ruins of Choice"

Difficulty: Medium | Location: Adeptus Mechanicus abandoned facility "Thanos-7 Outpost"
Recommended Attributes: 3-4 growths

Background

Repairing the transport ship requires a critical part—it lies within the abandoned Adeptus Mechanicus facility. But when you arrive, you find someone is already here—an Eldar Ranger. He is looking for something too. He proposes a bargain.

NPC Companion

Kaela (continues from SB-1—if she survived SB-1) or Kara (continues from SB-2—if SB-2 was run)

If both previous companions are dead or absent, introduce a new companion:

Tech-Priest Omicron-7 (surviving Adeptus Mechanicus Tech-Priest)

Item Content
One-line description A half-mechanical Tech-Priest, trapped in this facility for 6 months
Motive To recover the Standard Template Construct (STC) fragment within the facility—his life's mission
Bottom Line Never damages ancient technology
Initial Attitude 0 (Neutral—he only cares about the tech)
Attributes Faith 2 / Body 3 / Mind 5 / Tech 5 / Fate 1
Key Skills Tech-Use (Expert +20), Lore (Imperial) (Trained)
Equipment Shock Maul (1d4+3 Energy), Heavy Carapace Armor (Armor Rating (AR) 4), Mechanicus diagnostic tool
Special Ability Machine Spirit Communion: Can interact with mechanical facilities without triggering defense systems. Tech Blessing: Once per scenario, grants a weapon +10 to-hit until the end of combat

Oath Conflict Point (Internalized)

The Eldar Ranger Elendil proposes a bargain—crystals in exchange for help repairing the ship.

  • The Emperor's Voice (high-priority oath—reject the xenos): "The xenos cannot be trusted. Reject him. You can find the part yourself—even if it takes a few more weeks."
  • The Voice of Conscience/Survival (low-priority oath—but more urgent now): "You are trapped on this planet. You need that ship. Accept his help—for yourself, for your brothers-in-arms."
