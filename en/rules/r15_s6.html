S6: The Final Assignment

Campaign: The Final Assignment (Campaign Five · Endgame) Recommended Level: 18–20 Difficulty Class (DC): 115 (Suicidal) Mission Type: Special — Endgame Mission Estimated Time: 150–180 minutes (recommended to play in two sessions)

Opening Description

The office lights have been on for days. How long since you last slept? The coffee cups on your desk are stacked three layers high.

Before you lies your retirement application — half filled out. Beside it, all the mission reports from your tenure. You signed every one. You remember every one.

Your comm terminal lights up. The last priority message. You could choose not to look — the signature line on the retirement application is waiting for you.

But you are the Director. You always look.

Endgame Choice — based on the player's cumulative choices across the campaign, triggers one of three endings:

Ending A: Legacy (Trigger: large Bureau scale, Reputation ≥ 70)

Scene: The Final Handover

You are not retiring — you are handing the Bureau to someone better than you.

Mission Content: Your personally chosen successor (an Elite Explorer you trained from scratch) will carry out his/her first "solo mission" — under your supervision. This is not a test, it is a legacy.

Check: DC 115. But this time — you stand behind him/her. You provide guidance (+15 bonus). You are not helping him/her complete the mission. You are teaching him/her: "When I am not here — you know what to do."

Success:

The successor returns from the mission. He/she walks into your office, mission report in hand. "Director — I did it." You take the half-filled retirement application from the drawer. This time, you hand the pen to him/her. You look around this office one last time — next to the previous Director's photo on the wall, there is now an empty frame. It is for you.

Critical Success:

The successor exceeds the mission. He/she brings back more than you expected. More importantly — he/she added a line at the end of the report: "Director... thank you for not giving up on me." You still remember his/her first day reporting for duty — a Trainee Explorer full of fear. Now before you stands a Director ready to take over. This is the legacy you leave behind.

Ending B: Dissolution (Trigger: excessive casualties, depleted resources, Reputation < 30)

Scene: The Final Inventory

The Bureau is dissolved. This is not failure — this is facing reality. You no longer have enough manpower and resources to keep operating.

But you do not just walk away. You have one last responsibility.

Mission Content: Find placement for every Explorer still on your roster.

This is not a check — it is a series of choices:

Explorer Option A Option B Option C
Veteran Explorer "Chen Gang" Recommend to M.E.G. frontline (he will keep fighting — but may die there) Assign to Outpost defense (safe but boring — he will hate you) Give him freedom (he will leave alone — you may never see him again)
Elite Explorer "Hye-yeon" Recommend to BNTG foreign affairs (stable, safe, high-paying) Persuade her to join an independent Outpost (dangerous but dignified retirement) Offer a final mission — find her missing mentor (DC 100, if successful she will be grateful forever)
Legendary Explorer (your best) Share the last cup of Almond Water with him/her ... ...

Final Scene:

You turn off the office lights — for the last time. The corridor is empty. Your footsteps echo through the hollow Outpost. Past the former dormitory area — now only empty beds. Past the mission briefing room — screen dark. Past the mission record board on the wall — one name, one name, one name. You walk out the Outpost's main gate. Level 1's corridor stretches before you. Fluorescent lights hum. You do not look back.

Ending C: Walking In Personally (Trigger: specific story clues or player's Guilt value reaches a critical point)

Scene: The Final Signature — On Your Own Dispatch Order

You have signed hundreds of mission orders. Every time, you sat in a safe place, signed, then waited for them to return.

But this time — this time is different.

Mission Content: You put on Explorer gear. You personally "cut into" the depths of The Backrooms.

Why? Maybe because you know something only a Director can handle. Maybe because you can no longer send someone else. Maybe — just maybe — you want to know what they experienced.

Special Rules — The Director's Explorer Attributes: - Experience Level: you have no experience level. Your bonus = (total missions completed during tenure ÷ 10), capped at +25 - Specialty: the knowledge you accumulated during your tenure — choose 1 primary specialty + 1 secondary specialty - Mental State: automatically starts from "Stable" (but the GM will give you Stress checks at key moments — because you know too much)

Check: DC 115. This is the hardest mission of your career — because this time, the dice are in your hand, but you are also beneath the dice.

Success:

You return. You push open the Outpost's main gate — your former Outpost. Your successor stands there, face full of surprise. You take off your gear, walk into your office (it is his/hers now), and take the retirement application from the drawer. You sign it. But this time — you know what they experienced. Because you walked it yourself.

Critical Success (Legendary Ending):

You succeeded. You completed the thing only you could complete. Maybe you closed The Unifier's passage. Maybe you found a clue to the exit. Maybe you only proved — a Director is not just a desk and a pile of documents. When you return, the whole Outpost is waiting for you. Not because you are the Director — because you came back.

GM Information

Ending Trigger DC Emotional Core
A: Legacy Reputation ≥ 70, has a successor candidate 115 (+15 guidance bonus) "I left the best behind"
B: Dissolution Excessive casualties or depleted resources None (series of choices) "I did my best"
C: Walking In Personally Specific clue or Guilt value triggers 115 "This time, I won't send someone else"

GM Final Recommendations: - This mission should not be rushed through in regular play time. Recommend arranging a special long session - Prepare tissues — this can be emotionally heavy - Endings B and C may require the player to write a final "Director's Log" as a roleplay wrap-up - Whichever ending — after the final scene, give the player a moment of silence. This is not a moment of victory cheers. This is the moment of ending.

If the player wants a "sequel": - Ending A's successor can be the protagonist of the next game - Ending C's retired Director can appear as an NPC in future games — that old hand who offers advice from the shadows
