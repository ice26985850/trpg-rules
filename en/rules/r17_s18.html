Scenario S8: Whispers of the Curtain [Level (Lv.) 8]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 8 × 1 player + 2 companions | Duration: 4-5 hours | Map: Core Zone Edge (Cult's Territory)

Commission

QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) special commission: investigate the Order of the Watchers' anomalous ritual activity at the Core Zone edge. Reward 15,000 ₽. Time limit: 180 minutes.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry — Abandoned Church Perimeter

2 Order of the Watchers patrols. Solo Option: Have the companion feint to draw attention (Intimidation/make noise), while the player sneaks into the church from the other side.

Scene 2: Church Interior — Gravity Anomaly

Localized gravity fluctuations. If a companion is a Technician, they can stabilize the gravity field (Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 17, success → movement unaffected by gravity for 10 minutes). - 1 cult guardian (solo weakening: Chest Hit Points (HP) 24→17) + 2 cultists

Scene 3: Crypt — Time Bubble Zone

Time bubble trap. The companion Scout can discover and mark a safe route (Awareness Target Number (TN) 14). - 1 Deep Diver (solo weakening: Threat Value 10→7, Chest Hit Points (HP) 24→17, Damage Reduction (DR) 32) - Solo-Specific Strategy: Use the time bubble — if the player is being chased, they can voluntarily enter the time bubble (1 round inside = 5 rounds outside), recovering and redeploying within the bubble, with enemies still in place when they exit.

Scene 4: Ritual Hall

1 cult leader (solo weakening: Chest Hit Points (HP) 24→15, second-stage time stasis canceled) + 1 weakened Deep Diver. - Optional: Disrupt the Ritual: 3 energy crystals (Hit Points (HP) 10, Damage Reduction (DR) 2). After destruction, the cult leader loses summoning ability. - Loot: Curtain Resonator (Gold) — removes the Disadvantage penalty of the Deep Laboratory in later scenarios

Scene 5: Extraction

  • QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) helicopter (flare gun + radio)
  • Or a hidden passage behind the church → Old Town edge (requires Perception (PER) Target Number (TN) 17 to discover)

Solo Adjustment

  • Too Hard: 1 Deep Diver does not join combat (cult leader's control fails); guardian replaced by 2 cultists
  • Too Easy: The Curtain Resonator attracts 1 Core Zone mutant (Threat Value 12) that appears during extraction
