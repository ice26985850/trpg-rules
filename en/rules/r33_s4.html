S4 · Patient Seventeen's Chart

This scenario is the turning point of the main story. For the first time, the player sees the trace that "Altruistic Hope" leaves at the system level — helping an Afterimage finish what it left undone spits out memory, and all those memory shards roll in the same direction: the Campfire. This is the campaign's pivotal revelation.

2. GM Summary

Storyline: Using the Memory Cards retrieved in S3, the player pieces together clues; Gilmar brings back a roster from the records room — 40 names, 39 crossed out, the 17th not. Some player recognizes "Seventeen." In Act Two, the asylum's Afterimage density is extremely high, each stuck in a repeated action; the player completes their unfinished business (+2 Hope Point (HOP), +2 Campfire Ember, spit out memory). Seventeen stands motionless at the records-room door; the player must enter the records room to find her chart — and the records room is a death trap (single exit, no Pallet, no Window). In Act Three, reading the chart: Seventeen was never a patient — she was a family visitor, wrongly admitted and held for three years.

Truth (GM-Exclusive): When the player helps an Afterimage finish what it left undone, the Altruistic Hope generated lowers Entity Hunger (EH), and the "warm amber things" an Afterimage releases on dissolving roll toward the Campfire — the Campfire is built from Ember, and Ember comes from "someone doing something for someone else." This is the campaign's pivotal revelation. The GM should not explain; let the player say "so the Campfire is…" themselves; if they can't, carry the question forward and S7 gives the answer.

How to Run: Fully enclosed, very short line of sight, Nurse (NRS) can Blink through walls — the most claustrophobic round. Blink crosses floors, so "running upstairs" is no longer safe. The records room is the only single-exit combat map; the GM should have the Nurse appear at the door only after the player enters, not chase in, just stand there, then slowly knock on the table.

3. Opening Text (read aloud)

Gilmar spreads a crumpled paper by the fire. It is a roster, forty names, the first thirty-nine struck through with thick black lines.

The fortieth — the seventeenth — has nothing. Not crossed out, not finished.

"This one," he says, "is still here."

You look at the empty space. Something moves in your chest. You can't say why, but you know this name. Or rather — you know the feeling of "knowing it."

Eloise flips the paper over. The back is blank, save for a deep crease, as if someone folded and unfolded it again and again.

"In the asylum," she says, "there's a list like this every year. The crossed-out ones forgot who they were. The not-crossed-out…"

She pauses.

"The not-crossed-out are the ones someone is still waiting to come home."

5. Encounter Configuration

Main Killer: Nurse (NRS). Avatar Menace Value (AVM) +3 (highest in the whole system). Challenge Rating (CR) = Prestige (PRS) + 3; this scenario's Prestige (PRS) is 7–10, so Challenge Rating (CR) 10–13. Occupies 60% of the Encounter Budget (EB). 6. NPC Appearances and Lines

Seventeen (§8-D, Afterimage) - Central to this scenario. Stands motionless at the records-room door, accepts no help. - After the chart is read aloud, she turns, pulls out her sister's chart, places it in the player's hands, then dissolves.

Sally Nkosi (§8-A): if she plucked the wrong pod in S3, here she "recognizes Seventeen's face" but can't say why.

Whisperer · the Chart Number (RC-02 variant) - The asylum Whisperer, intelligence-type, Encounter Budget Cost (EBC) 0. Spend 1 Hope Point (HOP) to ask "where is No. 17." - Line: "A number is only a number. A person is not. The one you ask about was never of this place."

Echo Companion · the Lace-Tier (§8-B, solo configuration) - Is two parts of the same person as the Afterimage "the Lace-Tier." If the player completes both, an extra +3 Campfire Ember.

7. Decision Points and Branch Results

Decision One: Help the Afterimages finish their unfinished business? - Each completed → +2 Hope Point (HOP), +2 Campfire Ember, spit out memory (possibly someone else's). - Branch: complete 3+ and read Seventeen's chart aloud → trigger the Ember Moment and the pivotal revelation.

Decision Two: Enter the records room (death trap)? - Entering = the only exit is blocked by the Nurse at the door; you must escape via the outer-wall crack (cp_crack) after Act Three. - Branch: don't enter → main story can't advance, only pure escape; enter → pivotal revelation + Memory Card.

9. Ending Branches and Rewards

Ending branch: Whether the pivotal revelation triggers (depends on Decisions One / Two). No multiple-ending branch.

Rewards

Item 1 uncommon item + 1 Add-on per person
Narrative Confirmed: the Campfire is made of Ember, and Ember is born of Altruistic Hope
New Ability "Afterimage Dialogue": from now on the player may attempt to communicate with Afterimages in any Realm (spend 1 Action Point (AP) + Stealth (STL) or Will (WIL) check)

Ember Moment trigger text:

When Seventeen dissolves, you notice something. The others, when they dissolve, turn to ash. She does not. She turns into a small handful of warm, amber things that fall to the ground, and then — you see it clearly — they do not stay where they landed. They roll in one direction. All in the same direction. You chase them out. You chase them to the crack in the asylum's outer wall. You watch those things pass through the crack, roll into the Fog, roll toward a place far off that you cannot see. But you know where it is. Because that direction is where you wake every time. They are rolling toward the Campfire.

10. Connections to Adjacent Scenarios

  • Forward (S3): the retrieved Memory Cards are the material for piecing together "Patient Seventeen's Chart"; if Sally plucked the wrong pod → she recognizes Seventeen here.
  • Backward (S5): at the end of Seventeen's chart "after her sister's chart number was written an address — the Swamp" → S5 Sunken Stair clue; this scenario's "Afterimage Dialogue" ability → S5 Void interaction with Afterimages.
  • Hook payoff points: ① the outer-wall crack (cp_crack) → hints something exists beyond the Realms; ② Afterimages spitting memory toward the Campfire → S7 Ember Vein and the Umbilical; ③ the asylum's symmetric layout → the prototype of S6 Léry's Memorial Institute's mirrored design.
