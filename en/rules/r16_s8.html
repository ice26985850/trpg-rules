S8: The Way Home

Applicable Mode: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

Ending Layer — The White Space | Milestone 8 (Legendary) | 2–3 hours | Finale

The White Space

You sit in the white void. Two doors before you.

This place has no time. How long have you sat—minutes? Hours? You don't know. But at last you have time. From the moment you opened your eyes in Level 0, you never truly stopped. Always moving, always the next exit, always the next threat.

Now—here there is only quiet. And two doors.

Recap

The GM guides the character through a recap of the entire campaign:

Do you remember?

The first bottle of Almond Water in Level 0. The sticky note on the bottle—"Keep walking. Don't stop.—Lao Zhao". You didn't yet know who Lao Zhao was.

The pipes of Level 2. The 35°C heat. In the darkness, a Hound's breathing right on the other side of the cubicle partition. Wang Jing's diary—the words on its last page.

The party hall of Level 4. The moment all the Partygoers turned to look at you at once. Their smiles vanished. That was more terrifying than the smiles.

The hotel of Level 5. The waiter in the tailcoat speaks your name. The hundreds of empty eyes in the banquet hall. The empty chair you did not sit in.

The absolute darkness of Level 6. You heard your mother's voice calling you in the darkness. It wasn't real—but your feet almost walked toward it.

The glass wall of Level 7. That black shape—larger than the whole building—swims slowly past.

The wheat field of Level 10. The scarecrows move when you aren't looking. You never figured out what they wanted.

Level 11. Lao Zhao himself. Scars from Level 2 on his arm. He pats your shoulder.

The ten kilometers of Level !. Your legs burning. The Willpower Check at the 9th kilometer. You almost gave up. But you didn't.

You never gave up.

The Two Doors

Doors are just doors. No ornate decoration. No mysterious runes. No gatekeeper.

The left door—"The Front Rooms." Home. Back to where you came from. Back to your bed, your job, your family. Back to that mundane life interrupted by your noclip.

The right door—"The Backrooms." Stay. Back to the streets of Level 11. Back to Lao Zhao, Mira, Dr. Wu. Back to those who just noclipped in, as afraid as you once were.

The Choice

There is no DC here. No Check. No GM deciding for you.

Only you.

You stand up. Your legs still tremble from Level !. You walk toward one of the doors.

Ending A: Return to the Front Rooms

You push open the left door.

Warm yellow light envelops you. Then your own bed. You open your eyes. Morning light beyond the window. Your phone on the nightstand—the date is the day you noclipped. You were gone only minutes.

You sit up. Your room is exactly as before—but it feels wrong. The room is too small. Too quiet. No hum of fluorescent lights—you almost miss that sound.

Your pocket is empty. Almond Water cannot exist in the real world. But you remember its taste. You remember everything—the yellow wallpaper of Level 0, the darkness in the pipes, the ten kilometers of Level !.

No one will believe you. You go to work. You go shopping. You continue your life. But on certain nights—at the edge of falling asleep—you will hear it. The hum of fluorescent lights. Coming from the other side of the wall. From beneath the floor. From the deepest part of your memory.

Sometimes you wonder—did you really come home? Or did The Backrooms give you a perfect replica, so you'd think you came home?

You will never know. But you are here. You are alive.

Campaign ends. The character returns to the Front Rooms.

Ending B: Stay in The Backrooms

You push open the right door.

Cold fluorescent light. The streets of Level 11. Gray sky. Still clouds.

Lao Zhao sits on the bench at the street corner—in the same spot as when you left. He sees you and smiles. Not the never-fading smile of the Partygoers. A real, weary, human smile.

"Back?"

You sit beside him. "Back."

In the days that follow—you are no longer a Wanderer. You are a guide. Every newcomer who falls into Level 0, you go to receive them. You give them their first bottle of Almond Water. You leave marks on the walls. You write your own sticky note—"Keep walking. Don't stop.—your name".

Some lived. Some didn't. But you were always there—at the outpost, in the pipes, in the city—helping everyone as afraid as you once were.

This is not home. But it is the place you chose. With the people you chose.

One day—perhaps years later—another Wanderer will find the entrance to Level !. Before setting out, he will ask you: "Ten kilometers—was it worth it?"

How will you answer?

Campaign ends. The character stays in The Backrooms, becoming a guide.

Whatever you chose, you survived. Not as a hero. As a person. A person who found their own answer in the infinite maze.

That is enough.

— Backrooms Walker TRPG, The End —
