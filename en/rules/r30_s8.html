S4: King of the Arena (Lv.10-15 · Noxus Arena)

Background: The purest form of combat — the arena deathmatch. The players participate in the Noxian underground arena league in 2v2 or 3v3 format, challenging increasingly powerful opponent combinations, ultimately facing the reigning champion.

Round Level Opponent
Qualifier Lv.10 Noxus Soldiers ×4
Quarterfinals Lv.11 Assassin Duo (2 Assassin-type NPCs)
Semifinals Lv.12 Beast Tamer + 2 War Bears
Finals Lv.13-14 Former Champion "Iron Fist" + Partner
Championship Challenge Lv.15 Darius (optional — defeating him allows you to invite him into your Bond)

Reward: "Champion's Belt" (Attack Damage (AD)+20, Health Points (HP)+200, +10% damage in arena), Darius Bond.
