Scenario 1: "Awakening"

Item Content
Type Character Origin (Tutorial)
Recommended Marks 0 (First session after character creation)
Sessions 1 session
Core Attributes All (tutorial checks)
Difficulty ☆ (Tutorial)

Opening

You didn't come because you took a Commission. You woke up.

This morning you walked through the office glass door as usual—but the other side of the glass was not the lobby. It was a corridor. A corridor you had never seen, three times longer than it should be. You looked back—the glass door was still there, but beyond it was a grayish-white light, no street, no people.

You are trapped in the Gap Layer.

NPCs

NPC Type Info
Old Mo Previous Walker / Mentor A retired Walker in his sixties. "Fishing" in the Gap Layer—using shards of a Memory Crystal as bait
the Mirror (player) the Mirror (non-hostile) Mimics your movements at the other end of the corridor—but it found the exit before you did

Scenes

  1. Awakening: The player wakes in the Gap Layer. The GM describes the Gap Layer's sensory feel (light coming from the wrong direction, footsteps delayed, air denser than the Surface). The player makes a first Passive Awareness (10+AW) to sense the spatial anomaly. (Ref: Player Rulebook §4.1 Passive Check)

  2. Meeting Old Mo: Old Mo sits on a folding chair in the middle of the corridor, a thermos beside him. "Newbie, huh? Don't panic. You're in the Gap now—the space between spaces. Take a deep breath first." Old Mo teaches the player three basic actions: Confirm Layer (Anchor DC 10), Move (Traverse DC 8), Observe the Surface (Awareness DC 10). (Ref: Player Rulebook §5.1 Action List)

  3. First Search: Old Mo points to the corridor corner—"See that glowing thing? That's a Lost Item. Go retrieve it." Retrieval DC 6 (after global -2) to find a Common Lost Item (a keychain). (Ref: Player Rulebook §5.1 Lost Item Quality Table)

  4. The Mirror Appears: The player's Mirror appears at the other end of the corridor. Old Mo: "Don't be afraid. The Mirror won't hurt you—at least not now. But you must watch it Exit the Gap. Never turn your back on the Mirror." The player must make a Negotiation DC 8 (non-verbal interaction with the Mirror—maintain eye contact, retreat slowly). (Ref: GM Rulebook §3.3.1-C Mirror Rules)

  5. Finding the Exit: Old Mo points out the Threshold—the office glass door (Natural Threshold DC 6). Traverse check. Success → return to the Surface. Failure → Old Mo steadies you for another attempt (Advantage). (Ref: Player Rulebook §5.1 Traverse Check)

  6. Return to the Surface: The player returns to the office. Only 3 minutes have passed. Coworkers didn't notice you were gone. But you did—a faint golden Residual glow on the glass door's frame. You can see the Gap now. Forever.

Rewards

  • Return Mark × 0 (not counted in tutorial scenario)
  • Old Mo becomes a Contact (can be visited during Interludes—Retrieval knowledge +2)
  • Obtain your first Lost Item: an old keychain (Common Lost Item—origin unknown)

Companion Recruitment

  • Old Mo: Cannot be recruited in this scenario (he is a mentor-type NPC; can become a Companion after Tier 3)
