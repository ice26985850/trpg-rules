Corpse-Nurturing Ground — The Whole Cemetery Awakens (S5)

7-9 | Hard | 2-3 | Jing-Chu (Hunan ancient battlefield ruins)

Synopsis

An anomaly appears at the mass grave below an ancient battlefield — corpses across the whole area are waking. From Fresh Corpses to White Jiangshi, Black Jiangshi, even a Leaping Jiangshi leads them. Local villagers have all evacuated. The Talisman sect issued an emergency call — at least three Earth Masters are needed to jointly set up the "Grand Sha-Suppressing Array." Beneath the mass grave lies a sealed ancient altar — a Warring States-era human-sacrifice site. A thousand years of human sacrifice accumulated unimaginable Yin-Sha energy. Someone recently broke the altar's seal.

Key NPCs

Old Daoist Chen (Client/Ally)

  • Info: Came here when young — the seal was intact then.

  • Stats: Maoshan Disciple·Talisman sect, Level 8.

Leaping Jiangshi — Warring States High Priest (Regional Boss)

  • Secret: The altar's last high priest — was sacrificed alive, then became a Leaping Jiangshi. Guards the altar but does not know what he is guarding.

  • Stats: Type: Leaping Jiangshi; cr: 7; hp: 70; def: 18; special: Retains partial language ability — may converse.

Tomb-Raider Survivor (Information Source)

  • Attitude: Mentally broken.

  • Info: The only survivor of the tomb-raiding team that broke the seal. Repeatedly says "the thing beneath the altar is not dead."

Warlord Scout (Potential Threat)

  • Info: One of Liu Dapao's men — will come for antiquities after the corpse horde is dealt with.

Tomb Structure

Type: Open Area + Underground Altar

Main Encounters

Cross the Corpse-Mutation Zone (Type: Exploration/Combat, DC 14, TTV 10)

Avoid or destroy patrol corpses. Sound and light attract more.

Setting Up the Grand Sha-Suppressing Array (Type: Ritual, DC 14)

Requires three Earth Masters cooperating. Interference may cause the array to fail.

Leaping Jiangshi High Priest (Type: Combat/Social, TTV 20)

Leaping Jiangshi ×1 + Black Jiangshi ×2. The high priest retains partial language ability — may converse.

Blood Pool (Type: Environment, DC 14)

Source of Sha — must be sealed or purified.

Altar Core (Type: Exploration/Ritual, DC 16)

Find and repair the suppression artifact. Must decipher ancient inscriptions.

Warlord Interference (Type: Social/Combat, DC 14)

The scout may strike at the worst moment.

Key Items

The Ancient High Priest's Bronze Dagger (Suppression Artifact)

  • Description: Key to sealing the altar. Can be taken away, but the seal weakens.

List of Human Sacrifices (Bamboo Slips)

  • Description: Records the victims of a thousand years of human sacrifice; extremely high historical value.

The Leaping Jiangshi High Priest's Mask

  • Description: Obtained if resolved peacefully. In combat can intimidate low-tier Jiangshi (White Jiangshi and below, Fear DC −4).

Multiple Endings

Perfect Seal

  • Condition: Repair the suppression artifact + purify the blood pool + soothe the high priest

  • Result: The corpse-nurturing ground returns to normal; gain "Mark of the Purifier" (Sha perception permanently +2).

Forced Purge

  • Condition: Destroy all corpse mutations but do not repair the altar

  • Result: Temporarily resolved; Sha re-condenses within 3-5 years.

High Priest Awakens

  • Condition: Help the high priest regain his reason

  • Result: Gain a very strong ally — the high priest voluntarily stays at the altar to guard the seal.
