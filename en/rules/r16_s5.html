S5: Trek

Level 8 → 9 → 10 | Milestones 5–6 | 8–12 hours | Intermediate-Advanced Applicable Mode: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

Level 8: The Glowing Caves

You climb ashore from the underground river. Above is not sky—but the cavern dome, dozens of meters high. The dome is covered with bioluminescent fungi, casting a soft blue-green glow like a false starry sky.

The cave floor is damp, overgrown with a strange fungal forest—mushrooms up to two meters tall, their caps glowing faint blue. Glowing spores drift in the air. Some mushrooms are edible (restores Calorie Reserve), some are deadly poisonous (Knowledge DC 14 to distinguish).

In the shadows, "Cave Dwellers" lurk—pale, eyeless humanoid creatures that sense prey through vibration. They fear light—a flashlight can drive them back. But they are many. Keep your light source. Keep moving.

The cave's exit is an upward-sloping tunnel. At its end is natural light—gray, uniform. You climb out of the tunnel.

Level 9: The Eternal-Night Suburb

You stand on a suburban street. On both sides are identical two-story houses—white fences, trimmed lawns, garages. The streetlights glow sodium-yellow.

It is raining. Not heavily—a fine, steady rain. It never stops.

The street stretches beyond sight. Every intersection looks the same. Every house is the same. If you mark a house—turn around and look back—the mark is gone, but the house remains.

Behind the curtains of certain houses there is light. Don't be fooled. The shadows behind the windows move, but wrong—too slow, too smooth. That is not human. Do not enter any house.

You need to cross the entire suburb—possibly several kilometers, possibly dozens of kilometers. Direction is hinted by the streetlights' blinking pattern (Perception DC 15 to interpret). Follow the correct streetlight sequence and you will find the boundary.

Level 10: The Golden Wheat Field

The suburb ends abruptly. Beyond the last house—not more streets. A wheat field.

An endless golden wheat field ripples under the warm sunlight. The sky is a clear blue—a real blue, not the false overcast of Level 11. The wind through the wheat ears makes a rustling sound. Too beautiful. Too comfortable. Too... wrong.

At the center of the wheat field stand scarecrows. They wear old farmer's clothes, broken hats. Their faces are empty cloth sacks.

You swear they were not in that position just now.

Every time you look away—even for a second—the scarecrows' positions change. They are approaching. Slowly, patiently. But they seem to bear no hostility—they are simply "watching." The paths through the wheat field vanish after you pass. Navigation DC 16.

At the edge of the wheat field is an asphalt road. Follow the road, and on the horizon the outlines of buildings begin to appear—skyscrapers, apartments, shops.

Ending

You step onto the asphalt. Looking back at the wheat field—the scarecrows all stand at its edge, like a row of silent sentinels. They will not cross this road.

Ahead: Level 11. The Boundless City.
