Scenario S3: The Brood Nest

Applicable Mode: Solo (Lone Wolf mode, 4 Action Points (AP) per round) / Multiplayer (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–5 players, 3 AP each) Difficulty: Hard Recommended Level: 3–6 Estimated Duration: 4–6 hours Mission Type: Sabotage Mission Player Count: 1–6

Mission Briefing

A Concord reconnaissance satellite discovered a Kithrak Brood Nest bio-ship in the border star system, "hatching" within the asteroid belt — the hull is attached to an asteroid, absorbing minerals to produce a new generation of combat creatures. Intelligence analysis shows the hatching cycle will complete within 12 hours, at which point thousands of brood warriors will be released into the star sector.

The Mothership Arbitrator dispatches a boarding assault team, with the objective of planting explosives at the bio-ship's central heart (the Broodmother Chamber) before hatching completes, utterly destroying the hatching ground.

Enemy Vessel Data

  • Vessel Name: Broodmother-class Bio-Ship (S3)
  • Vessel Type: Insectoid Bio-Ship
  • Affiliation: Kithrak Brood Nest
  • Scale: Medium (~400 m length, organic) / 3 organic layers (Muscle Layer, Circulation Layer, Core Layer)
  • Gravity: Standard Gravity (0.8G, the vessel has its own weak biological gravity field)
  • Self-Destruct System: None (a bio-ship will not self-destruct — but will trigger an immune response)

Advance Flow

Turning Point 1: Limits of the Breach Method

The bio-ship's hull is alive — cutting it with a Plasma Cutter (PC) causes the hull to spasm in pain (gravity fluctuation), but this is the only way. Hacking does not apply (no electronic systems).

Turning Point 2: Hatching Progress Bar

The GM counts down hatching progress starting from 8, −1 per round; at zero → all remaining incubation pods burst, releasing 2d6 brood larvae.

Turning Point 3: Immune Response

When the players go deep into the Muscle Layer, the bio-ship begins an immune response — releasing antibody creatures (small brood) and spraying digestive enzymes (biochemical damage).

Key Encounters

E1 Muscle Layer Entrance (Sphincter Passage)

  • Enemies: Brood Larva × 4 (CR 1, swarm attack — each additional larva present gives the whole swarm +1 attack)
  • EL: 4

E2 Circulation Layer (Lymph Channel)

  • Enemies: Brood Warrior × 2 (CR 2)
  • EL: 4
  • Environment: Submerged in lymph fluid — movement speed halved; the fluid is weakly acidic, dealing 1 acid damage per round (ignores Armor Integrity (ARMR))

E3 Core Layer — Broodmother Chamber

  • Enemies: Brood Guardian × 2 (CR 4) + Brood Brainbug (Boss) × 1 (CR 6)
  • EL: 14
  • Summon Frequency: The Brainbug summons 1d4 every 2 rounds
  • Notes: The Broodmother Chamber is the final battlefield; the Brainbug's mind control (Will check DC 18) can force one player to attack an ally for 1 round.

Rewards

  • Experience Points (XP): Primary Objective 12 / Secondary Objective 6 / Full Survival 3 / GM Generic 6
  • Reputation: 3
  • Special Loot: Brood Nest Pheromone Extract — a pheromone compound extracted from the Brainbug's corpse. Effect: while worn, brood larvae have Disadvantage on attacks against that character; Reputation +1.

Special Rules

  • Bio-Ship Environment: No doors — only sphincter passages (passing requires a Mobility (MOB) check DC 10); the floor is warm and damp, the hull has a heartbeat (low-frequency vibration); the air is full of spores (non-brood characters must pass a Resilience (RES) check DC 10 each hour or become nauseated).
  • Flame Advantage: Flame attacks deal double damage to bio-ship structures (brood weakness); the flamethrower is extremely effective in this mission.
  • Writhing Corridors: Every 1d6 rounds, the current corridor writhes and contracts — all characters must pass a Mobility (MOB) check DC 12 or be crushed (1d6 damage) and have movement restricted for 1 round.
