Introduction

Nation Building TRPG — Player Introduction

Reference Works Statement

This is an original nation-building TRPG. The setting, mechanics, and all content herein are original designs.

You Are Not an Adventurer — You Are a King.

This is a tabletop role-playing game built around "nation management": your Hit Points (HP) are your population, your "equipment" is infrastructure, and your "skill tree" is your technology and magic systems.

Worldview

The story unfolds on the New Continent of Elden. Five hundred years ago, the old world perished in a magical catastrophe called "The Sundering"; survivors arrived at this land strewn with ancient ruins and unknown magic. Magic flows as naturally as wind and water, and the ever-present "Ley Lines" run through the earth; Humans, Dwarves, Elves, Beastmen, and Dragonborn coexist. You will lead them from a camp of 50 people, onward to tribe, city-state, kingdom, empire — until the legendary age of conversing with gods.

What Do You Play?

You run a nation's day-to-day: plant wheat or potatoes this year? Ally with the neighbor or wage war? The game advances by "year," with four turns — spring, summer, autumn, winter — each year. Each turn you declare actions, make dice checks, and respond to crises, watching civilization grow walls and academies from wasteland. You can also dispatch "Hero" units (with nine attributes) to explore, negotiate, or fight, and unlock five schools of magic — Arcane, Divine, Nature, Rune, Alchemy — even dropping meteor showers or stopping time.

What Makes It Different?

  • The nation is your character: replace traditional character attributes with "National Six Dimensions (POP/RES/ECO/MIL/TEC/MOR)"; no levels or XP — growth comes from accumulated construction and era progression.
  • Crisis Deck: each turn may draw a crisis event, from famine and rebellion to neighboring extortion — management always has variables.
  • Race & Culture Fusion: choose single or dual races; each has independent satisfaction, and mixed races unlock unique fusion tech.

Who Is It For?

Those who enjoy strategic management, resource allocation, diplomatic maneuvering, and the satisfaction of building from nothing will find joy here. A little TRPG foundation helps, but you need not be a rules savant — the core mechanics are clear and easy to learn.
