Clan Intrigue (S10)

Season: Chapter 3 "Expedition Chapter" Basic Information - Suggested Level: Lv.46-53 - Difficulty: Normal - Expected Length: 2-3 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.

Core Conflict

The players learn of the internal conflict between Malkis (Black Serpent Chief-God) and Zephyros (Eagleman Chief-God). Malkis is willing to provide intelligence — but at a price.

Key Locations

  • Black Serpent Temple (hidden underground):
  • Eagleman Sky Fortress:

Key Scenes

  • Negotiate with Malkis's emissary — moral dilemma (accept the evil god's help?)
  • Infiltrate the Sky Fortress — obtain Zephyros's plan
  • Discover Iluna (Fox-God) maneuvering between both sides — which side is she really on?

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 5000, Clan Relations Intel, Purple · Unique Accessory

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) −10.
