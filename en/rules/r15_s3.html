S3: Abyssal Probe

Campaign: The Rift (Campaign Two · Core) Recommended Level: 5–6 Difficulty Class (DC): 90 (Hard) Mission Type: Recon Mission (REC) → Resource Recovery Mission (RRV) Estimated Time: 90–120 minutes

Opening Description

The Rifts are spreading. Starting from the initial anomaly signal at Level 3, now Level 4 and Level 7 have both reported similar phenomena. Physical laws begin to break down near the Rifts — gravity drift, abnormal time flow, spatial folding.

Your Intel Department's analysis points out: all the Rifts point in a common direction — the depths of Level 7, the "Deep Sea of Fear." If you want to know the source of the Rifts, you must send someone into that endless black water.

No one volunteered.

Scene Flow

Scene One: Mission Briefing — The Aquaphobic Team

Scene Description: Your best Explorers stand in the office. They are all here. But when you mention "Level 7," the air in the room changes.

Veteran Explorer "Chen Gang" (Specialty: Combat): "Director, I've been to many places. But Level 7... that's not for the living. That's for the drowned."

Decision Point: - Team Selection: Level 7 demands extremely high physical fitness — requires Survival Equipment (diving gear, protective suits) - Intel Purchase: Intel on Level 7 is scarce — even precise Intel has limitations. This is a nearly unexplored region - Special Equipment Needs: - High-capacity Almond Water canister (mandatory — no source of fresh water) - Sonar detector (mandatory — underwater visibility is near zero) - Holographic map projector (recommended — underwater space is distorted, easy to get lost without a map)

Doctrine Choice Dilemma: - Cautious: spend more time exploring, but consume more oxygen and Almond Water - Aggressive: reach the objective faster, but speeding in the dark = higher risk of surprise encounters

Scene Two: Level 7 Entrance — The Boundary of the Surface

Scene Description: Level 7's "entrance" is not a door, but the edge of a vast body of water. The black surface stretches to the end of vision. Above is a low concrete ceiling — only about 5 meters of space between the water and the ceiling. The only footholds are concrete platforms scattered across the water.

Environmental Rules: - All movement costs double (water resistance) - Lighting range halved (water absorbs light) - Every 1 hour underwater consumes 1 unit of Almond Water (dehydration accelerates) - Comm range limited to 50 meters (signal attenuates in water)

Phase One — Surface Patrol (DC 60): - Move between platforms, use sonar to mark the route - Without sonar: encounter probability +20% (cannot detect approaching things in the dark)

Scene Three: Underwater Abyss — The Core of the Rift

Scene Description: About 200 meters below the surface — sonar reveals an anomalous structure. Not naturally formed. A perfect sphere — about 30 meters in diameter — suspended in the water. It radiates low-frequency energy pulses, perfectly synchronized with the Rifts in other Levels.

Core Encounter — The Rift Source:

As the Explorers approach the sphere, they experience the following effects (in order): 1. Distortion of time sense — the second hand on the watch runs backward 2. Abnormal spatial perception — up, down, left, right lose meaning 3. "Sound" — not through the ears, but directly in the mind: "...not yet..."

Check: Final mission check DC 90.

Special Action — Sample Collection (optional, additional risk): - Attempt to extract an energy sample from the sphere: independent Tech check at DC 100 - Success: obtain the "Rift Core Sample" — a key item that unlocks the "Dimensional Theory" tech tree - Failure: energy backlash — the collector suffers severe wounds

Outcome Branches: - Critical Success: Fully record the Rift source data + successfully collect the sample. Unlock detailed information on the three Rift-handling routes (Destroy/Stabilize/Research) - Success: Determine the Rift source location and basic nature. May choose one route - Marginal Success: Recording incomplete — but at least the source is known to exist. A follow-up dispatch will be needed - Failure: Contact with the source triggers an energy surge — the team is scattered. Search and Rescue needed - Catastrophe: The sphere activates upon contact — a massive shockwave engulfs everything nearby

Scene Four: Return — Bearing the Mark of the Abyss

Whatever the result, those who return from Level 7 are no longer who they were when they left. What they saw in the dark — they will not say, but their eyes have changed.

If the Explorers return successfully, the GM narrates: "When they take off their diving gear, their hands are shaking. Not from the cold — Level 7's water temperature is constant. It is because something deeper than the water has floated up with them."

GM Information

Item Value
True DC 90
Intel Bias ±15% (detailed Intel); Level 7's Intel, even precise, can only reduce to ±10%
Minimum Personnel 3 (underwater safety requirement)
Recommended Specialty Engineering (operate sonar), Recon, Combat (underwater Entity defense)
Recommended Equipment Sonar detector (mandatory), high-capacity Almond Water canister (mandatory), holographic map projector
Encounter Probability 35% (underwater darkness + unknown Entities = high risk)
Time Limit Maximum 8 hours (oxygen and Almond Water limits)

Special Environmental Rules: - Without sonar: all non-visual perception −20% - Without underwater gear: DC +10 - After every 2 hours of underwater activity: forced Stamina check (d100 ≥ 50), failure → Fatigue (personal bonus −5)

Campaign Progression: After successfully completing this mission, unlock Campaign Two's "Route Choice" (Destroy/Stabilize/Research). The player must decide in the next month.
