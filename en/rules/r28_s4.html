Shadow of the Six Pillars (S4)

Season: Season 1 "Awakening Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.12-20 - Difficulty: Hard - Expected Length: 2-4 sessions (8-16 hours) - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end of the document.

Core Conflict

The first beastman clan vanguard appears in Tokyo—a reconnaissance team from the Werewolf Clan. The Guild urgently mobilizes all available Awakeners. The players will face "intelligent enemies" for the first time.

Main Locations

  • Shibuya Crossing: Once the busiest intersection in the world, now a battlefield against the beastman vanguard.
  • Werewolf Recon Outpost (Abandoned Department Store): A stronghold occupied by werewolves, containing the beastmen's otherworld camp within.

Key NPCs

  • Werewolf Scout Captain Gruk: The first hostile character with spoken lines. Disdainful of human weakness, yet curious about the System the players display.
  • Guild Chief of Staff Takeda: Tactical commander, providing intelligence and support.

Key Scenes

  • Street skirmish—first encounter with an organized enemy
  • Department store assault—different tactical challenges on each floor
  • Dialogue with Gruk—reveals the beastman perspective ("Your world is too easy to invade")
  • Discovering the beastmen are collecting "something"—setup for S5

Enemy List

  • Werewolf Scout (Quantity 3-5 | Lv.12-14, has tactical AI)
  • Werewolf Warrior (Quantity 2-3 | Lv.15-16, high damage)
  • Werewolf Scout Captain Gruk (Quantity 1 | Lv.18 Boss, can be spoken with)

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 1500, Blue · Rare equipment crate ×1, Guild Reputation +200, may obtain Werewolf Weapon Fragment (S5 key item)

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Scouts reduced to 2, Gruk Hit Points (HP) −25% and loses the ability to summon reinforcements
