Volume I: Chronicle of the Nine Realms — Complete Adventure Epic

Total Span: full coverage of levels 1-36 | Acts: 6 acts | Span per Act: about 6 levels (one major realm)

Design Philosophy: from the mortal fear of Qingshi Town (青石鎮) to the ultimate choice of the ascension heavenly tribulation, let the player feel "qualitative change" at every realm.

Act I: Mortal Awakening

Suitable Level: Qi Refining 1-4 → Breakthrough to Foundation Establishment

Core Theme: The true crossing from mortal to cultivator

Time Span (in-game): about 1-2 years

Plot Summary

After the battle at Qingshi Town, the player obtains from the corpse of the zombie puppet · Liu Yunfei his Qingyun Sect disciple token and last will. Whatever the player's origin, Liu Yunfei's story points in the same direction — the Qingyun Sect (青雲門). Following this clue, the player travels to the Qingyun Mountains of the Eastern Lands (東土) and sets foot on the true path of cultivation.

The Qingyun Sect is holding its quinquennial "Ascension Convention" — a public exam to recruit new disciples from outside. This is the golden chance for rogue cultivators and children of small families to enter the orthodox cultivation system. At the convention the player undergoes Spirit Root testing, Dao Heart trial, and practical sparring, and formally becomes an outer disciple of the Qingyun Sect (or keeps their original sect affiliation while building ties with the Qingyun Sect).

After entry, the player is assigned their first sect mission: investigate the recent abnormal demonic-beast disturbances at the "Mistveil Valley" (霧隱谷) at the northern foot of the Qingyun Mountains. This seemingly simple task leads the player to an ancient ruin — the "Taichu Hermitage" (太初別府), said to be the cultivation cave dwelling of the first human immortal, Taichu True Person (太初真人), during his Qi Refining days. Hidden within the cave dwelling is the first fragment of the secret of "Nine Realms to Immortality" — the "Secret of the Dao Foundation."

However, the Qingyun Sect is not monolithic. An inner disciple at the Foundation Establishment stage (a traitor) has secretly colluded with outside forces, intending to monopolize the treasures of the Taichu Hermitage. The player must defeat him in a duel, protect the ruin's secret, and complete the breakthrough from Qi Refining to Foundation Establishment in the process.

End of Act: The player breaks through to Foundation Establishment, truly stepping through the great gate of the cultivation world.

Act II: The Choice of the Dao

Suitable Level: Foundation Establishment 5-8 → Breakthrough to Golden Core

Core Theme: Choose your Dao — righteous/demonic/neutral, sect affiliation, cultivation direction

Time Span (in-game): about 3-5 years

Plot Summary

After successful Foundation Establishment, the player faces a far broader cultivation world. The Qingyun Sect nominates the player to participate in the Zhongzhou Debate Terrace's centennial "Sect Grand Tournament" — not only an honor battle, but a key event deciding the sect's resource allocation for the next century. Meanwhile, demonic-path infiltration becomes obvious: a secret organization calling itself the "Netherworld Dao" (幽冥道) is quietly recruiting disciples from the great sects.

During preparations for the tournament, the player accidentally contacts the "Five Elements Secret Realm" (五行祕境) — a cultivation holy land that opens once every hundred years, said to contain the origin power of the Five Elements Great Dao. However, entry slots are limited, and the great sects fight openly and secretly over them. The player must achieve good results in the tournament to win entry slots for the Qingyun Sect (or their own sect).

After the tournament ends, the true face of the demonic-path forces is gradually exposed — the leader of the Netherworld Dao is a Golden Core Perfected demon cultivator who intends to seize the "Five Elements Origin" within the secret realm to refine an evil artifact that can shake the continent's balance. The player goes deep into the realm and has the final confrontation with the Netherworld Dao.

In the deepest part of the secret realm, the player finds Taichu True Person's second Dao Seed — the "Secret of the Golden Core," grasping that the Golden Core is not simply solidified Spiritual Energy, but the condensation of a "seed of the Dao." In this confrontation and insight, the player breaks through to Golden Core.

Act III: Winds Rise and Clouds Surge

Suitable Level: Golden Core 9-12 → Breakthrough to Nascent Soul

Core Theme: Choices in the tide of the era — on the edge of the Demon Abyss crisis and continental war

Time Span (in-game): about 5-10 years

Plot Summary

With the Golden Core formed, the player's standing in the cultivation world is utterly transformed. The Qingyun Sect grants the player "True Inheritance Disciple" status, with independent disciple-accepting rights, elder-guidance rights, and more sect resources.

However, peace did not last long. The Western Domain's Demon-Suppressing Hall sent an urgent call for help — the Demon Abyss seal showed its most severe loosening in ten thousand years. One of the five Fusion-stage mighty ones who guard the Demon Abyss year-round was gravely wounded by an unknown force. Without enough high-stage cultivators going to support, the seal could collapse within a decade.

Meanwhile, on the border between the Eastern Lands and Western Domain erupted the "Spirit Vein Contest" (靈脈爭奪戰) — a newly discovered supreme-grade spirit vein lies at the two-domain border, and the Crimson Clouds Valley and the Western Domain Rogue Alliance refuse to yield. War is on the verge of breaking out. The Netherworld Dao fans the flames between the two domains, intending to provoke a full-scale war to weaken the righteous path's attention.

The player is drawn into this intricate situation. On the way to support the Western Domain, the player is ambushed by the Netherworld Dao and discovers the true cause of the Demon Abyss seal loosening — the Netherworld Dao is conducting a ritual called the "Opening Heaven Plan" (開天計劃) deep in the Demon Abyss, attempting to actively open the Demon Realm passage.

While tracking the Netherworld Dao deep into the Demon Abyss rift, the player finds Taichu True Person's third Dao Seed — the "Secret of the Nascent Soul." Taichu's lingering intent reveals a shocking truth: the Nascent Soul is not a simple "second life," but a "projection of the Great Dao" in the individual. The act of condensing the infant is essentially making oneself a tiny branch of the Great Dao.

Act IV: Heaven and Earth as the Board

Suitable Level: Nascent Soul 13-16 → Breakthrough to Spirit Transformation

Core Theme: From pawn to player — participate in reshaping the continental landscape

Plot Summary

With the Nascent Soul formed, the player is no longer just someone executing tasks — but someone qualified to help formulate strategy. At the initiative of the Demon-Suppressing Hall, the Five Sacred Lands convene a "Cultivator World Convention" (修仙界大會) to discuss forming an Anti-Demon Alliance. The player, as an outstanding Qingyun Sect True Inheritance Disciple, is invited to attend.

However, the convention does not go smoothly. The great sects each have their own thoughts: the Taixu Sword Sect advocates full-on attack; the Crimson Clouds Valley worries war will affect the pill supply chain; the Myriad Treasures Pavilion sees the war's business opportunity; the Tianji Manor calculates a "bloodshed omen" if the alliance is rushed. The player must mediate among the sides to push the alliance's formation.

Just as the alliance is about to be reached, shocking news rocks the cultivation world — an "ancient inheritance" (上古傳承) appears near Ascension City in Zhongzhou, preliminarily judged to be the complete inheritance of an innate divine demon (先天神魔) from the Divine-Demon Era (神魔紀). The news instantly shifts every sect's attention from the Demon Abyss to the inheritance contest.

While exploring the ancient inheritance, the player meets a mysterious being calling itself the "Ancient Remnant Clan" (上古遺族) — the last surviving descendant of an innate divine demon from the Divine-Demon Era. He reveals a deeper truth about the Demon Abyss crisis: the reason the Demon Realm passage can be opened is that the Hongmeng Continent's "Heavenly Dao" (天道) itself is in decline. And the cause of the decline relates to a flaw in the "Nine Realms to Immortality" system.

Meanwhile, the Netherworld Dao exploits the sects' chaos over the inheritance to accelerate the "Opening Heaven Plan" deep in the Demon Abyss. When the player returns from the inheritance, a first small "Demon Realm rift" has already appeared in the Demon Abyss.

In a battle to stop the Demon Realm vanguard from expanding the rift, the player faces the Nascent Soul-stage ultimate challenge — defeating the Demon Realm vanguard's commander, a Spirit Transformation-stage "Demon Lord." Between life and death, the player breaks through to Spirit Transformation.

After Spirit Transformation, the player already stands at the top tier of the Hongmeng Continent's cultivator pyramid. Yet the closer to the peak, the heavier the Heavenly Dao feels. Every major move of the player may affect the continental landscape.

The clue of the fifth Dao Seed points to the "Immortal Realm Trial Path" in the East Sea Abyss beyond the Eastern Lands — a trial passage the Immortal Realm set up for mortal cultivators in ancient times; those who pass gain the Immortal Realm's recognition and the blessing of Immortal Realm laws. But this trial path has had no one pass it for tens of thousands of years.

While exploring the trial path in the East Sea, the player discovers a shocking fact: the other end of the trial path leads directly to the "Heavenly Dao Core" (天道核心) — the hub of the Heavenly Dao's law operation. Scattered within the Heavenly Dao Core are "Heavenly Dao Fragments" (天道碎片) that fell away as the Heavenly Dao declined. Collecting these fragments can temporarily stabilize the Heavenly Dao and delay the Demon Realm invasion.

Meanwhile, the Netherworld Dao quietly accelerates its pace. Their cult leader — a terrifying existence at the Fusion stage — finally acts in person. The Netherworld Cult Leader's true identity is exposed: he is a fallen immortal who betrayed the Immortal Realm in the Immortal-Demon War ten thousand years ago. He betrayed the Immortal Realm in that war and surrendered to the Demon Realm, granted immortality by the Demon Realm's "Demon Ancestor." His goal is not simply to open the Demon Realm passage, but to "devour the Heavenly Dao" (吞噬天道) — to take the Heavenly Dao Core for himself and become the new master of the Hongmeng Continent.

The player must prove their qualification in the Immortal Realm Trial, collect the Heavenly Dao Fragments, then face the Netherworld Cult Leader in the final confrontation. In defeating the Netherworld Cult Leader, the player completes the two breakthroughs of Void Refining and Fusion, preparing for the ultimate duel of the final act.

Act VI: The Peak of the Nine Realms

Suitable Level: Fusion 25-28 → Mahayana breakthrough → Tribulation Crossing → Ascension

Core Theme: The ultimate choice — ascend to the Immortal Realm or remain in the mortal world

Time Span (in-game): about 100-500 years

Plot Summary

With Fusion achieved, the player stands at the very top of the Hongmeng Continent. The Heavenly Dao Fragments are gathered and the Heavenly Dao temporarily recovers stability. But the true ultimate threat is not yet removed — the "Demon Ancestor" (魔祖) deep in the Demon Realm, the Demon Realm's master sealed ten thousand years ago, is awakening.

Cang Wudao was only a prelude. The true Demon Ancestor is an existence beyond Tribulation Crossing — his status in the Demon Realm is comparable to the Immortal Realm's "Heavenly Immortal" (天仙). The ten-thousand-year seal has exhausted most of his power, but once he fully awakens, even the weakest Demon Ancestor is not something mortal cultivators can oppose.

The only way to stop the Demon Ancestor's awakening is to go deep into the Demon Realm before the seal fully collapses and apply a final layer of reinforcement on the seal — a "Heavenly Dao Seal" (天道封印) that requires "Ascension Power" (飛昇之力) to drive. This means the player must first complete the Mahayana and Tribulation Crossing breakthroughs before entering the Demon Realm, then trigger the Ascension Tribulation in the ultimate duel with the Demon Ancestor, using ascension power to seal the Demon Realm passage.

But ascension means leaving the mortal world forever. The player faces the ultimate choice: - Ascend to the Immortal Realm, entrusting the mortal world's future to those who come later - Give up ascension, remain in the mortal world as a "Scattered Immortal" (散仙) to keep guarding - A third path: at ascension, leave part of your Great Dao enlightenment in the mortal world, planting a new Dao Seed so later comers also have a chance to reach the Nine Realms

Above Ascension City, the Heavenly Tribulation descends. The eighty-one heavenly thunders, the ultimate Inner Demon trial, the Heavenly Dao's final question — all await the player.

End of Act: Ascension — or remaining — or forging a third path. The Nine Realms to Immortality becomes legend.
