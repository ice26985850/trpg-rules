Scenario 9: Rift Emergence (CL50–65 · Otherworld Outpost & Void Sentry)

Run Method: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. The player-count scaling rules are the same as the Prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / +1 concurrent event). This scenario is the CL50–65 finale chapter's first act, continuing from Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle's collapse and the Otherworld invasion.

Difficulty Guideline: Otherworld bosses are Legendary~Mythic Boss battles. Design Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ party total level × 0.6. After adjustment, ensure "lowest level (CL50 Otherworld invasion) can clear, highest level (CL65) has a mini-boss challenge". Otherworld enemy stats and traits are detailed in Monster Compendium Chapter 5.

I. Opening Description (links with Scenario 8)

Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle collapses amid cheers, and Demon Lord Vercellia's ending (subjugation or reconciliation) is settled. Just as everyone raises a toast, the ground splits without warning — a wind that does not belong to this world pours into Altia, and the sky is torn open by a violet-black "Rift". Otherworld vanguards pour from the fissure, distorting reality and nullifying rules wherever they go. You realize: the Demon Lord was never the end; the beings beyond the world are the true End.

Acceptance condition: Must clear Scenario 8 (any ending) and obtain the "Demon Lord Castle Fallen" story flag. The Otherworld threat does not ask about faction — both subjugation-route and reconciliation-route players must face it.

II. Scene Flow

Act 0: Accept Otherworld Defense (opening beat)

After the Rift emerges, cities fall into chaos. At the Adventurers' Guild (or the remnant Demon Lord Army's temporary joint command) you accept the "Otherworld Defense" commission — requires Character Level (CL) ≥50 to accept. This is not a voluntary expedition, but the world's last line of defense; the battle merit, Influence Points (IP), and equipment accumulated so far are all put to use in this decisive battle.

After accepting, you step into the first stable Rift node and begin exploring the Otherworld domain. Solo mode is supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, with no sidekick.

Act 1: CL50–59 Rift Emergence (outpost battle)

Otherworld Vanguards (CR55) pour from the fissure; reality distortion causes some terrain rules to fail (GM may rule "healing invalid in this area / +1 movement" and other Otherworld traits). Players must defend three "World Anchors", preventing the fissure from expanding. - Influence Points (IP) hook: Protect refugees evacuating the Otherworld front +6 Influence Points (IP); wanton killing of panicked civilians −5.

Act 2: CL60–65 Void Sentry (finale mini-boss)

After the fissure stabilizes, the depths of the Otherworld reveal the Void Sentry (CR65, mini-boss) — a forward command commander sent by the Singularity, responsible for consolidating the Rift passage. All its traits are active: reality distortion / rule nullification / beyond-world resistance / Rift Sync (see Monster Compendium). - Finale mechanic: The Sentry flips the positioning map every 2 rounds (reality distortion), and at Phase 2 activates rule nullification (ignores a player's certain Damage Reduction (DR)); players must punch through the core within a limited number of rounds, otherwise it summons Rift Vanguards for reinforcement. - Ending branch: After defeating the Sentry, the Rift passage is temporarily sealed, but the true Otherworld Lord still lurks in the depths — leading to Scenario 10.

III. NPC Dialogue Examples

Rift Watcher (surviving Otherworld scholar): "Your world, in our eyes, is also just 'the rift on the other side'. Understanding, or destruction — this was never a question that power could decide." Demon Lord Vercellia (if survives on the reconciliation route): "A world beyond even I have touched... Hero, this enemy is older than hatred."

V. Stage Design

  • Stage A World Anchor Defense: Social / Ritual check (DC 26), Otherworld invasion gate.
  • Stage B Rift Stabilization: Exploration check (DC 28), unlock Otherworld domain.
  • Stage C Void Sentry: Combat check (DC 30), finale mini-boss.

Design note: Scenario 9 is the first act of the max-level CL50+ finale stage, carrying the "beyond the world" thematic expansion; together with the Demon General battles of Scenario 1–8 it forms a two-layer narrative of "internal hatred → external End". Solo mode with no sidekick, difficulty scales per GM Book 13.1.
