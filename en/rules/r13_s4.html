Scenario SA-4: "The Sole Deep Hive" — The Sole of the Deep Hive

Difficulty: High | Inquisitor: Magnus Iron (Radical)
Target Region: Deep Hive (below Level 2300), a forgotten ancient subterranean temple
Recommended Attributes: Levels 4-5 | Challenge Rating (CR) Budget: 3 (Difficult)

Background

Magnus needs that ancient relic — he does not care about the cost. He gave you the minimum resources, and an assistant he would not mind losing.

NPC Companion

Veteran Talos (former Astra Militarum Sergeant, convicted of "Desertion")

Item Content
One-line description A 50-year-old gray-haired veteran, weary-eyed but with steady hands. He has seen too much
Motivation Retire after completing his penance — find a quiet corner of the Hive City to await death
Bottom Line Never leave a wounded comrade behind
Initial Attitude +1 (Trust — he has heard of your previous missions)
Attributes Faith 2 / Body 3 / Mind 3 / Tech 4 / Fate 2
Key Skills Ballistic Skill (Trained), Medicae (Trained)
Equipment Lasgun (1d6+1), Flak Armor (Armor Rating (AR) 2), old comrade's dog tags
Special Ability Veteran's Intuition: Once per scenario, the GM must reveal an impending danger ("Something about the path ahead feels wrong"). Field Medicae: When using a Medikit, restore an additional 1d4 Hit Points (HP)

Key Checks

Scene Check Success Failure
Cross the gas zone Endurance (Difficult), Talos shares a gas mask Pass safely Hit Points (HP) −1d4/round
Face the Blind Prophet Oratory (Standard) Receive warning and shortcut Cannot gain trust
Resist relic contamination Faith (Extreme) Corruption gain halved Corruption +1 per minute

Internal Seduction Trigger

At the deepest part of the temple, holding the relic:

"Talos is already old. He won't live much longer — he said so himself. If you need to sacrifice someone here to complete the mission — he is the reasonable choice. He might even volunteer."
