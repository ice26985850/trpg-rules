Scenario s02: The Time Rift Incident

Mode: Multiplayer (1 GM + 2–4 players, each playing a Supervisor) Source: Z Corporation TRPG, Chapter 3 Crisis Contract Estimated Duration: 5–9 hours Difficulty: ★★★★☆ (Advanced) Required Files: Z公司_玩家規則書.md, Z公司_GM規則書.md (Chapter 3)

I. Background

Time distortion phenomena are intensifying across the city, with "time rifts" appearing in multiple locations—fragments of the past and future seep into reality, and Cursed Spirits and Mutant Beasts surge forth in their wake. Each Supervisor's department is assigned to handle the rifts simultaneously, while the Board of Directors begins to reveal its true face; the Privileged Gold Member "Madam Time" makes her entrance, seemingly aware of the truth behind the "Eternal Pact."

This is a crisis in which multiple Supervisors stand side by side (or compete against one another)—you must both defend the city and strive for your own evaluations under the watchful eye of the Board.

II. Preparation

  • Each player brings their own team of Sorcerers (recommended ≥ 4 employees, including at least 1 member with high Intelligence or high Cursed Energy).
  • The GM prepares data cards for "Madam Time" and the "Board of Directors" (see the Employee & NPC Codex).

III. Core Mechanic: Time Distortion Effects Table (roll d6)

Each time a rift-related mission is entered, the GM rolls d6 to determine the temporal anomaly:

d6 Effect
1 Time Rewind: This round's dispatch results are voided
2 Acceleration: +1 available Time Slot this round
3 Afterimage: Enemy duplicates appear (threat increased)
4 Sluggishness: Computing Power (Sanli) -10
5 Foresight: That Supervisor's next Check +10
6 GM's discretion

IV. Optional Missions (T1–T4 + Support)

  • T1 Seal the Rift: d100 Cursed Energy Check; on success the rift closes.
  • T2 Evacuate Civilians: d100 Charisma/Intelligence Check; affects Satisfaction and public opinion.
  • T3 Trace the Source: d100 Intelligence Check; advances the "Source" progress bar.
  • T4 Deep Investigation: A high-risk mission; success reveals clues about the "Eternal Pact," setting up Chapter 4.
  • Support: Coordinate departments, soothe public opinion, provide mutual support.

V. Player Interaction

  • Supervisors may communicate with one another, trade/borrow employees, and execute missions jointly (after repeated joint operations, a Battle Rapport Check bonus applies, up to +20).
  • Competition: The ranking of each Supervisor's Satisfaction (Man Yi Du) affects the Board's final evaluation and subsequent resource allocation.
  • The GM adopts a round-robin approach to handling each Supervisor's phases, maintaining the pace.

VI. Ending Determination

  • Multiple Supervisors jointly achieve the T4 revelation → enter Chapter 4 "Truth Revealed."
  • Time Rifts Spiral Out of Control (negative situation escalates past a threshold) → triggers the preconditions for the final-choice ending.
  • A single Supervisor may choose to make private contact with Madam Time to obtain part of the truth ahead of time (affecting their Chapter 4 stance).

VII. GM Tips

  • Contact with Madam Time can reveal part of the truth behind the "Eternal Pact," as setup for Chapter 4; her stance may be that of an ally or an enemy.
  • When running multiple Supervisors in parallel, it is recommended to track with a dual-track system of "shared positive/negative progress bar" + "each Supervisor's individual Satisfaction."
  • For detailed scene text, dialogue templates, and departmental conflict escalation, see Chapter 3 of the GM Rulebook.
