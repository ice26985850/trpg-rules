Abyss Lord TRPG — Modules & Campaign Scenarios

Version: v2.1
Purpose: Standalone scenario manual for the GM — includes the Starter Module "First Incursion" and the full Lv1–15 campaign framework "From Cavern to Abyss Empire"
Prerequisite: Use together with the Abyss Lord Player Rulebook and GM Guide

Part One: Starter Module "First Incursion"

For: New GMs and players' onboarding guide
Dungeon Level: Lv1
Estimated Duration: 1–2 sessions

Scene 1: Establish the Initial Dungeon

GM narration:

"You are a newly ascended Dungeon Lord. The mana torches of the Throne Hall are lit — this is your first dungeon. For now there are only three chambers: your Throne Hall, and two undeveloped tunnels ending in pitch darkness. A few freshly hired monsters pace uneasily at your feet. From afar comes the faint sound of surface humans — are they coming?"

Player needs: Familiarize with attributes, assign starting monsters, plan which side to dig first.

GM prep: - Have players fill in the core of the character sheet (archetype, attributes, starting monsters) - Introduce the resource system — players' starting resources are listed per archetype configuration - Introduce the turn structure — this module has 5 scenes, each scene = 1 in-game turn

Scene 2: First Dig & Expansion

Player spends 1 action + Building Materials ×2, rolls IND + Engineering check for the first dig.

Check: IND + Engineering skill vs Difficulty 1 success

Result Narration
Failure The pick strikes hard rock strata — you only scrape off a few fragments. Materials spent, space not unlocked, but the GM may give a clue: "Something seems to be glowing behind the rock…"
1 Success Dug out a small cave. You raise the mana torch, illuminating the rough stone walls
2+ Success You not only dug out the space, but also discovered shimmering crystals in the rock wall — a Vein! That space's mine output permanently +50%

GM guidance: Narrate exploration discoveries — may strike a vein, may find a broken diary in ancient ruins. Let players choose what to build in this space (recommend: Trap Workshop or Mine).

Scene 3: First Wave of Invasion

Recon: If a Scout Outpost was built — automatically gain intel. Otherwise roll CUN + Espionage check.

Recon check reveals: "2 T1 adventurers approaching. A burly man carrying a shield, and a lithe, slender figure leaping along the rock walls."

Preset Adventurers:

Name Class CP Dice Pool HP Trait Background
Allen Warrior T1 1d6+2 5 Shield: Defense +1 die "Loyal and simple, saving up for his sister's medicine"
Mira Rogue T1 2d6+1 3 Can scout traps: Trap DC -2 "Impulsive and reckless, wants to prove herself"

Adventurer AI Behavior (roll 2d6):

2d6 First-floor behavior
2-4 Mira scouts traps first, Allen guards the rear
5-7 Both advance cautiously, Allen front, Mira to the side
8-10 Mira tries to sneak past monsters, Allen draws them head-on
11-12 Allen charges recklessly: "Mira, keep up!"

Invasion Route: Outer layer → Middle layer. GM guides players to deploy monsters and traps.

Combat tips: - This is a tutorial fight — let players make mistakes. If players forget to deploy traps, let Allen step on a rockslide that "fortunately didn't trigger" - If players are at a disadvantage — the dungeon structure itself triggers a "rockslide", dealing 1d3 damage to adventurers (GM may use the environment to help newcomers at any time) - If Mira's HP drops below 2 she'll try to retreat — this is a good moment to introduce the "capture vs escape" mechanic

Scene 4: First Captive & Interrogation

If adventurers are defeated (HP to 0 = unconscious): can be captured.

GM introduces interrogation mechanic: - Player chooses method: Interrogation (DOM) / Torture (WAR) / Charm (DOM + spend ◆) - Allen's weakness: "Mentions of his sister make his eyes flicker" — if player uses Charm and mentions family, DOM +1 die - Mira's weakness: "Impulsive" — if player makes her wait more than 2 turns before interrogating, WIL -1

Interrogation sample dialogue:

GM (Allen): "I won't talk… you monsters…"

If using Charm: "Your sister… she's still waiting for you out there, right? If the Adventurers' Guild learns you've been captured, will they send someone to rescue you — or abandon you? Which do you think is more likely?"

Allen's WIL opposed: WIL=3, roll 3d6. Lord's DOM + Interrogation vs 3d6.

First Captive Reward: Regardless of interrogation result, player gains the achievement "First Prisoner" — extra +1 Experience Point (EXP).

Scene 5: Post-battle Settlement & Second Expansion

Loot settlement (by defeated adventurer): - Each defeated T1 adventurer: ◆1d3 + ◇1 + ●1 - Each captive: no loot (the captive is the loot)

Experience: Repel T1 invasion +3 EXP, first capture +1 Experience Point (EXP).

Second Expansion: Guide players to use gained materials for the second dig. By now players should have experienced the full "dig → build → allocate → defend" loop.

Transition to next turn:

Second week after the battle. Allen is locked in the prison you just dug. Mira escaped — she'll bring people back. But you also have your first real resources. The dungeon's mana torches seem brighter than before.

Part Two: Campaign Framework — From Cavern to Abyss Empire

For: Full long-term campaign Lv1–15
Estimated total duration: 15–30 sessions

Act One: Establishment (Lv1–3) — 2~4 sessions

Theme: Survive. A freshly enthroned Dungeon Lord begins from the Throne Hall + 2 dark tunnels. Outside is the unknown — every sound of digging may draw the first adventurers.

Dungeon Lv Key Event GM Focus
1 First Incursion (see Part One). Repel 2 T1 adventurers, first taste of defensive combat Tutorial fight. Let players familiarize with combat phases, trap triggers, monster deployment
2 First dig expansion succeeds. Discover first natural resource (Vein / Mana Node). Neighboring dungeon NPC appears for the first time — a letter arrives at your Throne Hall Introduce expansion system. Introduce diplomacy concept (reply to the letter or not?)
3 "The Adventurers' Guild has noticed you". Two consecutive turns of invasion — the second wave is a revenge squad (led by the adventurer who escaped last time). Must decide: strengthen defense or accelerate expansion? Introduce consecutive invasion pressure. Players must make strategic trade-offs

Revenge Squad data (Lv3 second wave): - 2–3 T2 adventurers (dice pool 4–5d6), including the escaped adventurer + 1 reinforcement - Escaped adventurer gains "Revenge" trait — first-round attack +1 die

Act One climax: "Adventurers' Guild Wanted Notice" — after INF reaches 3, the Guild formally places your dungeon on the "Wanted List". GM introduces a physical wanted poster (printable prop).

GM wanted poster template:

Act Two: Growth (Lv4–7) — 3~6 sessions

Theme: Expansion and choice. The dungeon takes shape. The Abyss Embassy is built — you must choose one of the Four Alliances to join. The choice affects all later diplomacy.

Dungeon Lv Key Event GM Focus
4 Can build the Abyss Embassy. Two known NPC dungeons each invite you to join their alliance. Balzec, "Earth-Render"'s flame missive vs Malthus, "Iron-Bone Marquis"'s bone pigeon. Must choose — or stay neutral Alliance choice. This affects all subsequent diplomacy
5 Unlock elite monster recruitment. First elite invasion (T3, includes Boss captain). If allied before — allied dungeons may proactively send reinforcements Boss fight mechanic introduced (Phase 2 trigger). First experience of allied coordination
6 Archetype Lv6 advancement unlocked. After the dungeon reaches a certain scale — first discover conflicts exist between other dungeons. Moris, "Thousand-Faces" asks you to mediate his territorial dispute with Balzec Inter-dungeon politics. Player's choice affects the tripartite relationship

Two invitation letters:

Balzec's flame missive (a parchment with scorched edges, the writing seeming to burn):

"Newcomer — heard your dungeon is starting to take shape. The gates of the Abyssal Legion are always open to the strong. Come to Skyburn Keep, accept the trial. Pass — you're one of us. Fail — you won't leave even your bones. Awaiting your choice. — Earth-Render"

Malthus's bone pigeon (a bird formed of white bone, blue soul-fire burning in its eye sockets, landing on the armrest of your Throne Hall, clutching a pristine white letter in its claws):

"To the esteemed new neighbor: The Bone Mound Council has noted your activity in the Sea of Shadows. We appreciate lords who value order. On the day the Embassy is built, allow me to visit in person — to discuss matters of maintaining Abyssal order under the threat of the Order of Light. — Malthus, 'Iron-Bone Marquis' of the Eternal Slumber Corridor"

Act Two climax: "Abyssal Black Market" — Grix, "Goldtooth" (Ancient Wyrm) hosts the annual black market auction in the Scrap Canyon. All known NPC dungeon lords attend.

Black market scene framework: 1. Entry: Describe the lively scene of the Scrap Canyon. Describe each NPC lord's appearance/demeanor (vividly, per the players' known NPCs) 2. Social: Players may talk with any NPC. Each conversation is a small diplomacy check opportunity 3. Auction: 3–5 items auctioned in sequence (at least 1 legendary item, starting bid ◆50) 4. Assassination: At the auction's climax — intel from the Shadow Network shows an assassin has infiltrated. CG: players may choose to expose / condone / exploit the chaos to grab loot

Act Three: Threat (Lv8–11) — 4~8 sessions

Theme: War and betrayal. The dungeon becomes a regional power. The Order of Light begins organizing large-scale expeditions — no longer adventurer squads, but company-level offensives.

Dungeon Lv Key Event GM Focus
8 "Crusade Expedition" — the Order of Light sends 3–4 T3 squads invading simultaneously from different entrances! Must mobilize allied reinforcements. The dungeon's diplomacy check decides how many NPC dungeons are willing to send troops First large-scale multi-front defense. Tests players' resource allocation and diplomatic results
9 Archetype Lv9 ultimate advancement unlocked. Around this time — cracks appear within your alliance. Bone Mound Council: is Malthus being challenged? Abyssal Legion: does Balzec demand you take the flame trial? Free Artificers: tech-plagiarism dispute? Shadow Network: traitor leaks intel? Intra-alliance politics. Player's reaction decides the alliance's direction
10 Unlock first legendary monster. Moris, "Thousand-Faces"'s forbidden experiment suffers a severe accident — his dungeon is going out of control. If you were friendly with him — he sends you a desperate call for help Time-sensitive side quest. Rescue / exploit / ignore — three choices affect the whole game

Crusade Expedition scene:

Three preset invasion entrances: - Main Entrance (front): T3 elite squad ×2, includes Boss captain (Paladin) - Side Fissure (right): T3 standard squad ×1 - Abandoned Passage (left): T2 patrol ×1 (probing invasion)

Allied troop deployment check (per allied dungeon):

Roll DOM + Diplomacy check vs Difficulty 3. Success = that dungeon sends reinforcements (1d3 monsters, total CP ≤6) to help defend 1 entrance.

Act Three climax: "Breath of Nexis" — abnormal fluctuations in the Abyssal mana tide. Ethlin (Void-Weaver) warns: the mana tide may be a prelude to something "awakening".

Ethlin's whisper spreads through your Throne Hall: "Do you feel it? The Mana Crystals are trembling. Not just yours — the mana of the entire Abyss is trembling. I went to the Primordial Fissure… it is breathing."

GM introduces the first anomaly of the Primordial Fissure (D-16) after Lv10 — it made a sound. The ancient dungeons begin to awaken.

Act Four: Overlord (Lv12–15) — 6+ sessions

Theme: Fateful choice. Your dungeon has become a top-tier Abyssal power. The Order of Light prepares the "Crusade" — a full-scale offensive against the Abyss. And the sleeping entity in the Primordial Fissure is about to wake.

Dungeon Lv Key Event GM Focus
12 "Dungeon Covenant" — you convene (or are invited to) the Abyssal Council. All Lv8+ dungeon lords gather. Topic: unite against the Order of Light, or each fight alone? Your diplomacy check decides the strength of the united front Multi-NPC diplomatic negotiation. This is a complete social encounter scene
13 "Radiant Crusade" begins. T4–T5 adventurer legions attack all allied dungeons simultaneously. The forces assigned to your dungeon are decided by INF — high Infamy attracts the strongest enemies Ultimate defensive battle. Use all accumulated resources, monsters, allies, captive cannon fodder
14 Crusade continues. If defense succeeded last turn — adventurers suffered heavy losses but the Order keeps sending reinforcements. A turning point appears: Corwin, "Rusted Duke" (former Paladin) reveals a fatal weakness inside the Order — he needs your help to obtain the final piece of intel Surface infiltration mission. This may be the lord's last expedition

Dungeon Covenant scene:

Gathering point: Scrap Canyon, the mint hall of Grix's foundry. Attending NPCs (at least the following 8, possibly more depending on player's diplomatic results): - Malthus (Bone Mound Council), Balzec (Abyssal Legion) - Vera (Shadow Network), Wrench (Free Artificers) - Moris, Ethlin, Selathil (neutral attendance), Corwin

Debate structure: 1. Player's opening statement (DOM + Diplomacy, decides overall atmosphere) 2. Three rounds of debate — one topic per round (unite / independent action / resource allocation) 3. Final vote — each NPC dungeon's voting tendency depends on its relationship value with the player

🏁 Ultimate Event: "Awakening of Nexis"

On the 14th full-moon night of the 700th year of the Rift Era, the Primordial Fissure split open. What walked out was not a monster — but a consciousness. Nexis, the Mother of the Abyss, opened her eyes after ten million years of slumber. Her first words echoed through every dungeon in the Abyss:

"My children — what have you made of my underworld?"

GM scene framework:

Stage Content Check
Descent Nexis's voice rings throughout the Abyss. All dungeons' Mana Crystals resonate with light simultaneously. Dungeon mana temporarily ×2 — but may be withdrawn afterward No check. Narrative scene
Judgment Nexis examines each major alliance in turn. What she sees is not a united Abyss — but division, infighting, and compromise with the surface. Her question: "What have you built with my blood?" Player responds on behalf of their dungeon DOM check (respond to the question). ARC check (understand her true intent)
Choice Nexis gives two choices: A. Become her right hand — gain ultimate power, but the dungeon becomes her extension (lose some autonomy). B. Refuse — be exiled from the Abyss, become a wandering lord. C. Persuade her — the Abyss has evolved, no longer needs a single mother goddess (extremely hard challenge: DOM + Diplomacy ×3 multi-check, all must succeed) This is the ultimate check of the entire campaign. Players may use all accumulated intel, ally support, captive testimony to aid the debate
Consequence A = gain "Mark of Nexis" (dungeon MAG locked at 15, all monsters CP +2), but you can never again defy her will. B = dungeon reclaimed — but at the final moment you receive a joint letter from all allies: you are not alone. Become "Wandering Lord" — dungeon jointly kept by allies. C = Nexis smiles. "Perhaps this era truly no longer needs a mother goddess." She dissolves into mana filling the sky — the entire Abyss gains permanent +3 mana. Your dungeon becomes the new center of the Abyss

Multi-check details (Route C):

Check Goal Available supporting evidence
1st Dominion (DOM) + Diplomacy Prove the Abyss Lord deserves autonomy Alliance relationships with at least 3 NPC dungeons (each ally +1 die)
2nd Dominion (DOM) + Diplomacy Prove the Order of Light can be contained Corwin's inside Order intel (+2 dice), record of defeating the Crusade expedition (+1 die)
3rd Arcana (ARC) + Arcane Knowledge Prove Abyssal mana has evolved a new ecosystem Player's magic research results (+1 die), Ethlin's dimensional research (+1 die), dungeon MAG ≥12 (+1 die)

All three checks succeed = Route C achieved. Any single failure = Nexis unmoved — return to A/B choice.

Act Four Aftermath — Finale Events

Regardless of choice, the campaign ends with a launch ceremony of a New Abyssal Era:

  • If A chosen: Your dungeon becomes Nexis's temple. All NPCs come to pay homage. Your character becomes the Abyss's "Right Hand of God" — unmatched in power, but can never again say "no" to Nexis.

  • If B chosen: You lose your dungeon, but gain the identity of "Wandering Legend" — free to wander between any allied dungeon. A new adventure begins — you may assist allies against the Crusade, seek a way to build a new dungeon, or — turn to infiltrate the surface. A 24-hour counterattack-on-the-surface side event is playable.

  • If C chosen: Your dungeon is publicly recognized as the "New Center of the Abyss". The Order of Light is forced to sign a ceasefire agreement. From then on — dungeons are no longer monster nests. They are kingdoms.

Finale scene:

Three Abyssal months later. The day the ceasefire agreement is signed. You stand at the highest point of the Throne Hall, overlooking your dungeon — now it is no longer a string of caves, but a prosperous underground kingdom. Monsters and converted former adventurers coexist in peace. In the distance, the newly built Abyss Embassy blazes with light — those are envoy delegations from sixteen dungeons offering congratulations. The Abyss will never be the same again.
