Scenario 10: Otherworld Campaign (CL66–75 · Deep Realm Campaign and the Eclipse Sovereign)

Run Method: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count adjustment rules)," with a design baseline of a 4-person party. The player-count adjustment rules are the same as in the Prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / +1 concurrent). This is the second act of the endgame chapter for CL66–75, following the deep Otherworld campaign after Scenario 9's Voidward Sentry fell and the passage was temporarily sealed.

Difficulty Guideline: The Otherworld bosses are Mythic boss fights. Design Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ total party level × 0.6. After adjustment, ensure that "the lowest level (CL66) can clear it, and the highest level (CL75) faces a mid-boss challenge." For Otherworld enemy stats and traits, see Monster Compendium, Chapter 5.

I. Opening Description

Scenario 9's Voidward Sentry has fallen, and the rift passage is temporarily sealed, yet the skies of the Altia Continent remain torn by violet-black fissures. You follow the weakened passage in reverse, delving deep into the heart of the Otherworld—here there is no sky, only countless drifting "world fragments." The Eclipse Sovereign lurks at the core of the fragments, the Singularity's will made manifest as its direct agent in the Otherworld proper.

II. Scene Flow

Act 0: Undertaking the Otherworld Campaign (Opening Beat)

After the passage is sealed, the Joint Command authorizes you to "campaign in reverse"—you must have Character Level (CL) ≥66 to undertake it (clearing Scenario 9 is a prerequisite). This is the turning point from defense to offense: from "plugging the rifts" to "striking into the Otherworld's lair."

After undertaking it, you step into the heart of the Otherworld and begin the deep realm campaign. Solo mode is supported at Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5, with no allies.

Act 1: CL66–72 Deep Otherworld Realm Campaign (Outpost Boss)

Amid the drifting fragments of the Otherworld proper, you encounter the Void Apostle (CR70, boss fight)—the Eclipse Sovereign's lieutenant, who devours all existence. Trait "Void Assimilation" drains a portion of players' Health (HP) every 2 rounds (resist with Constitution (CON) save, Difficulty Class (DC) 24). - Influence Points (IP) Hook: Assist an Otherworld survivor scholar in repairing the World Anchor +8 Influence Points (IP).

Act 2: CL73–75 Eclipse Sovereign (Final Mid-Boss)

The deep campaign reaches the fragment core, where the Eclipse Sovereign (CR75, mid-boss) manifests—the Singularity's agent. Its traits are fully active: Rule Nullification (ignores multiple native Damage Reduction (DR)) / Reality Distortion (splits the positioning map) / Eclipse Domain (area-wide DoT + cover negated) / Rift Resonance (in phase three, recovers 9% Health (HP) per round). - Final Battle Mechanic: In phase one the Sovereign summons Void Apostle reinforcements; in phase two Rule Nullification is fully active, and players must break its defense using Otherworld-exclusive means (Beyond-the-World Resistance ×0.5); in phase three Rift Resonance self-heals, requiring a swift decisive battle. - Ending Branch: After defeating the Sovereign, the fragment core collapses, and the "Path of Finality" leading to the Singularity's true body is revealed—leading to Scenario 11.

III. NPC Dialogue Examples

Rift Watcher: "The Eclipse Sovereign is the shadow of the Singularity. If you can understand that the 'fragments' themselves are also devoured worlds… perhaps there is a third path." Demon Lord Vercellia (if alive on the reconciliation route): "I have warred for a thousand years, and never imagined the day I would stand shoulder to shoulder with heroes, fighting something that has no 'hatred' at all."

IV. Encounter Information

Encounter Enemy Challenge Rating (CR) Baseline Count (4 players) Player Adjustment
Otherworld Realm Void Apostle 70 ×1 (boss ×4) Boss fight; 1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) 67
Final Battle Eclipse Sovereign 75 ×1 (mid-boss ×4) Mid-boss; 1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) 72

For Otherworld enemy stats, see Monster Compendium, Chapter 5: Void Apostle CR70 (HP288/ATK+18/EVA20/CritTh19/EXP23,800), Eclipse Sovereign CR75 (HP1224/ATK+19/EVA21/CritTh19/EXP27,000). Theme traits: Reality Distortion / Rule Nullification / Beyond-the-World Resistance / Rift Resonance.

V. Stage Design

  • Stage A Deep Realm Campaign: Exploration check (DC 30), navigation of the Otherworld proper.
  • Stage B Void Apostle: Combat check (DC 32), outpost boss.
  • Stage C Eclipse Sovereign: Combat check (DC 34), final mid-boss; key is Otherworld-exclusive defense-breaking and a swift decisive battle.

Design Note: Scenario 10 is the endgame chapter's "counteroffensive" turning point, where difficulty leaps from Scenario 9's minor boss to a mid-boss; together with Scenario 11's final two-part consecutive battle, it forms the climax. Solo with no allies; difficulty scales per GM Book 13.1.
