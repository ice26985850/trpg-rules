Scenario S3: Echoes in the Depths of the Ruins

Growth Stage: Licensed Hunter (41-80 Experience Points (XP)) Duration: 4-5 hours Premise: Completed Scenario S2; the PC has independent-operation experience

Background

A priority message arrives from the Association's Intelligence Division—sent by Kira (commissioner from Scenario S1):

"I found that person's name. The one who bought the ruins creatures five years ago—his name is Aldridge. A former Association researcher." "He's now hiding in an abandoned research institute in the suburbs of Talaba City. That place—it's where he once experimented on ruins creatures." "Be careful. The Spy Plume says that place is 'still breathing'."

Scene One: Abandoned Research Institute — Entry

Duration: 45 minutes Type: Exploration

The institute is hidden in the suburbs' forest. Vines cover the outer walls; the main gate is sealed with chains. But you find a broken side window—just big enough for one person to squeeze through.

The interior is dark. The red glow of emergency lights reveals laboratories lining both sides of the corridor. You smell a strange odor—like a mix of hospital disinfect and a zoo.

A sign hangs on the first laboratory's door: "Spy Plume—Specimen #001".

Institute Exploration (Three Levels)

Floor Area Discovery
1F Reception hall + Archive room Find Aldridge's experiment logs (Nen Knowledge DC 16 → can decipher his Nen type—Manipulation, and his ability requires "pre-marking the target")
B1 Laboratory area Cages with abandoned specimens—Beast corpses (dead for years). One cage's door was broken from the inside—whatever was inside escaped on its own
B2 Main control room Aldridge is here. In the center floats a giant glass tank, inside which floats a human-sized... bird?

Scene Two: Aldridge — The Fallen Researcher

Duration: 30 minutes Type: Dialogue / Combat Prep

A white-haired man sits before the console. He hears footsteps but does not turn around.

"I knew you'd come. The moment the Spy Plume was taken, I knew—someday, someone would trace here."

He stands and turns. His left eye is not a human eye—it is a bird's eye.

"I gave myself the genes of the last specimen. Now I can see the flow of Nen—clearer than any Hunter. Do you know what that means?"

"It means—I can see your next move."

Aldridge (Specialization, CR 6)

Attribute Value
Hit Points (HP) 90
Spirit Points (SP) 60
Armor Class (AC) 14
Attack Nen Wave: d20+6 ranged, 2d8 Nen damage
Nen Ability Eye of Foresight (SP 3): Once activated, for 3 turns Aldridge can impose disadvantage on 1 of the PC's attacks each turn (he "sees" the attack trajectory). Requires marking the PC first (standard action, cannot dodge—so he will always mark you on the first turn of combat).
Weakness The Eye of Foresight cannot track multiple attack sources at once—if the PC can create a "feigned attack"—a feint. The PC can use Deception DC 16 (instead of an attack check) to waste Aldridge's foresight charges on fake moves; subsequent attacks cannot be foreseen.

PC's Optional Countermeasures: - Feint tactic (Deception DC 16 to first waste his foresight charges) - Use a hidden-trigger Nen ability (the Eye of Foresight cannot see hidden Nen) - Attrition war—Eye of Foresight lasts 3 turns, then cools down 2 turns

Scene Three: The Thing in the Glass Tank

Duration: 60 minutes Type: Combat / Resolution

After defeating Aldridge—

Aldridge kneels on the ground. His bird eye is bleeding. "It wasn't me... who made it." He points at the glass tank.

The liquid in the tank begins to boil. The glass cracks.

A giant bird-shaped creature steps out. It has three eyes, each glowing.

It does not attack—it speaks.

"You humans... locked my child in here... for ten years."

Mother of the Spy Plume (CR 7, Boss)

Attribute Value
Hit Points (HP) 120
Spirit Points (SP) 80
Armor Class (AC) 15
Attack Feather Blade: d20+7, 2d8+3 slashing (ranged 30m)
Special Third Eye: At the end of each turn, the Mother of the Spy Plume can "read" all abilities the PC used last turn. Repeatedly used abilities are adapted to—that type of damage is permanently halved against it. The GM informs the PC at the end of each turn: "It is observing your pattern."

Non-combat Option (PC may choose):

The Mother of the Spy Plume does not have to be killed. The PC can achieve reconciliation through Persuasion (DC 20) or by showing "you treated its child well" (if the PC carries the Scenario S1 Spy Plume). After reconciliation, the Mother of the Spy Plume will:

  1. Take away all surviving specimens from the institute
  2. Give the PC a "Mother Feather" (consumable: after use, gain foresight effect next turn—all enemy attacks at disadvantage)
  3. Reveal Aldridge's upstream buyer—"a person in the Kakin Kingdom. He calls himself the 'Collector'." (foreshadowing for Scenario S4)

Scenario S3 Rewards

Reward Details
Experience Points (XP) 12-15 XP
Items 12,000J, Mother Feather (foresight consumable), Aldridge's experiment logs (Nen Knowledge reference)
Intel Existence of the "Collector"—target of Scenario S4
Relationship Kira (deepened relationship), Mother of the Spy Plume (friendly—if reconciled)
