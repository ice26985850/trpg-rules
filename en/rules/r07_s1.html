First Cry — The Petty Landlord's Tomb (S1)

1-3 | Simple | 1-2 | Central Plains (Henan countryside) | 1925

The first tomb. The last rest.

Synopsis

A petty landlord's Third Concubine was recently buried. It should have been an ordinary funeral, but strange things began happening in the village — roosters stopped crowing, the well water turned bitter, and infants cried every night. The village chief hired the players to investigate. This is a small tomb and should hold no supernatural threat — yet the grass atop the grave withered yellow overnight. The Third Concubine did not die naturally — she was poisoned by the First Wife. Her resentment is fermenting in the tomb, and she is in the "Fresh Corpse" stage — the perfect live-combat training target for a new Earth Master.

Objectives

  • Investigate the source of the village's strange occurrences
  • Enter the Third Concubine's tomb
  • Deal with the supernatural threat in the tomb
  • Expose the truth

Key NPCs

Village Chief Old Zhou (Client)

  • Attitude: Anxious, half-doubting of Earth Masters

  • Secret: He knows there was conflict between the First Wife and the Third Concubine, but dares not speak of it

  • Stats: None (ordinary person)

The First Wife (Obstacle)

  • Attitude: Outwardly grieving, inwardly guilt-ridden

  • Secret: A maid witnessed her poisoning

  • Stats: None (ordinary person)

The Third Concubine's Lingering Spirit (Tomb Lord)

  • Attitude: Sorrowful, longing for justice

  • Secret: Willing to be released, but wants the truth exposed

  • Stats: Type: Lingering Spirit (Fresh Corpse stage); cr: 0.5; hp: 12; def: 5; attacks: Instinctive Swing: 2d10+1 vs DEF, Hit: 1d4; Weakness: Sunlight, Evil-Dispelling Talisman instant-kill, revealing the truth allows peaceful release

Little Maid (Witness)

  • Attitude: Terrified — fears the First Wife will silence her

  • Secret: Saw the First Wife put something in the soup

Tomb Structure

Type: Small Tomb

Passage

  • Length (meters): 8

  • Description: Narrow earthen tunnel, slight cave-in risk

  • Threats:

  • Cave-in risk (Agility DC 10 to avoid)

Single Chamber

  • Size: 3m square

  • Contents:

  • Wooden coffin (ordinary material)
  • A few burial goods (silver hairpin, jade bracelet, rouge box)
  • Corner jar (placed by the First Wife — contains poison residue)

Main Encounters

Investigate the Village (Type: Exploration/Social, DC 8)

Gather clues, interrogate villagers. Success yields info on the poison jar.

Open the Tomb (Type: Exploration, DC 10)

Find the correct passage location. Failure may dig wrong or trigger a cave-in.

Tomb Cave-in (Type: Exploration, DC 8)

The passage has cave-in risk. Passive perception — GM secretly rolls perception.

Third Concubine (Fresh Corpse) (Type: Combat/Social, TTV 2)

Opening Roll d6=3. Fresh Corpse ×1. Weak combat ability. If players show respect or mention the First Wife's poisoning, the Third Concubine's lingering spirit stops attacking and tries to communicate. Can be soothed through dialogue — no combat needed.

Truth Revelation (Type: Social, DC 10)

Make the First Wife confess or let the villagers know the truth.

Key Items

Rouge Box

  • Type: Obsession Vessel

  • Description: The Third Concubine's obsession vessel. If returned to her family, gain village reputation.

  • Value: Reputation +2

Silver Hairpin

  • Type: Clean Antique

  • Description: Can be sold normally.

  • Silver Dollar Value: 30

Poison Jar

  • Type: Key Evidence

  • Description: Evidence used against the First Wife.

Multiple Endings

Perfect Release

  • Condition: Expose the truth + soothe the Third Concubine

  • Result: Village reputation +3, Third Concubine rests, First Wife arrested.

Forced Suppression

  • Condition: Directly destroy the Third Concubine without resolving her obsession

  • Result: Reputation +1, but the Third Concubine's resentment transfers to the rouge box — may return in the future.

Compromise

  • Condition: Accept the First Wife's bribe, conceal the truth

  • Result: Gain 200 Silver Dollars, but reputation −2, Third Concubine's resentment grows.

Failure

  • Condition: Defeated by the Third Concubine / flee

  • Result: Village falls into panic, requires a higher-level Earth Master to handle.

Rewards

  • XP: 100

  • Money: 0-200 Silver Dollars (depends on ending)

  • Reputation: −2 to +3 (depends on ending)
