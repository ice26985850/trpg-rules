Ghost Market Deal — Not a Tomb, but a Ghost Market (S4)

5-7 | Special (Social-Dominated) | 1-2 | Jing-Chu (deep within an abandoned mine)

Synopsis

This is not a tomb — this is a Ghost Market. The players receive an invitation to enter a Ghost Market hidden deep within an abandoned mine. Tonight there is a special auction — the headline lot is a "sealed Han Dynasty jade coffin." What lies inside the jade coffin is not the tomb lord — it is an extremely powerful resentful spirit sealed within the jade (Leaping Jiangshi · Flying Jiangshi tier). Someone deliberately brought the jade coffin to the Ghost Market to auction, intending to let the resentful spirit find a new host.

Key NPCs

Gatekeeper

  • Attitude: Expressionless.

  • Secret: He does not need to breathe.

  • Info: Checks the invitation or collects an entry fee: an antique with a story.

"Old Abacus" (Senior Merchant)

  • Attitude: Shrewd, fond of Earth Masters.

  • Secret: Knows the jade coffin is wrong but dares not speak of it.

"Jade Face" (Buyer)

  • Attitude: Extremely interested in the jade coffin.

  • Secret: She is the younger sister of the resentful spirit inside the jade coffin in life — wants to free her elder sister.

  • Stats: Actually a Flying Jiangshi-tier entity.

Auctioneer

  • Attitude: Professional, neutral.

  • Secret: Owes a debt to the Market Master; not here willingly.

The Market Master's Emissary

  • Attitude: Mysterious.

  • Info: The Market Master never appears — the emissary's word is the final judgment.
