Mercury Maze — Outskirts of the Imperial Mausoleum (S3)

5-7 | Moderate | 2-3 | Guanzhong

Synopsis

The outer defense system of the Imperial Mausoleum is discovered — a maze encircled by a mercury river. The designers imitated the Qin Dynasty technique of "using mercury as the hundred rivers, lakes, and seas." The maze traps the resentful spirits of craftsmen who perished during construction. The players must cross the maze to find the true exit — this is only the outer wall of the Imperial Mausoleum.

Key NPCs

"The Mapmaker" Old Zhou (Information Source)

  • Info: Provides part of the maze map — only reached the second control room. Lost a leg and three companions in the maze.

  • Stats: Touch-Gold Clan·Mountain-Mover sect (disabled, retired).

The Master Craftsman's Resentful Spirit (Core Spirit)

  • Attitude: Angry — they were buried alive in the maze.

  • Secret: Knows the layout of all traps, but will only reveal it once players fulfill a dying wish (dying wish: bring news of their deaths to their descendants).

Contact of the Japanese East-Asia Archaeological Society (Competitor)

  • Attitude: Polite but dangerous.

  • Secret: They too have found the maze entrance — digging from another direction.

Main Encounters

Mercury Toxic Vapor (Type: Environment, DC 12)

Long-term exposure accumulates toxicity. Every 30 minutes a Constitution DC 12 check; failure means poisoning.

Sluice Gate Mechanism (Type: Exploration, DC 14)

Wrong operation → mercury floods the area. The three control rooms each control a different area.

Craftsmen's Resentful Spirits (Type: Social/Combat, TTV 8)

3-5 resentful spirits. Can be soothed by fulfilling the dying wish.

Trap Corridor (Type: Exploration, DC 12-14)

Linked false floors + crossbow bolts + quicksand.

False Exit Trap (Type: Exploration, DC 14)

Distinguish real from fake exits — beyond the fake exit lies a more dangerous area.

Japanese Team Competition (Type: Social, DC 12)

Possible cooperation (sharing maps) or conflict.

  • Condition: Soothe the craftsmen + complete control map + true exit
