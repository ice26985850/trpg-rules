B-Rank Threat (S8)

Season: Chapter 2 "Resistance Chapter" Basic Information - Suggested Level: Lv.33-40 - Difficulty: Hard - Expected Length: 3-4 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.

Core Conflict

The first B-Rank Rift appears in Tokyo Bay. Magma Guardian Kargnus guards the Rift's core. This is the first Rift the players face that must be closed by destroying its core.

Key Scenes

  • Progressively delve into the seven zones of the Rift — each zone a different environmental challenge
  • Strategic choice: destroy the core vs. fight the Boss first
  • Kargnus's phase transition — first experience of Boss phase mechanics

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 5000, B-Rank Equipment (Purple · Unique), Guild Reputation +500

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: monsters halved, Kargnus adjusted per solo rules.
