S6 · Therapy Room 13

This scenario exposes the campaign's cruelest truth: not all Killers are taken from outside. Some walk over from the Campfire. The person the player knows — Hilda Lane (§8-A) — is being Reforged into The Silent One (SIL). The player must decide: interrupt it, or watch it finish.

2. GM Summary

Storyline: At the Campfire, Hilda Lane is gone — but this time everyone remembers her. Eloise calmly repeats Hilda's words from the night before: "Does hate count as a kind of hope?" Fane's notes (S5) reveal the "internal-production" line — taking "those who lost hope but still hate" from the Campfire and processing them in the white place, "Room 13." In Act Two, The Silent One (SIL) has no heartbeat, emits Survivor voices to lure, and mid-Realm performs a "Mirror Swap" that invalidates map memory. Mid-scene the player chases "a teammate's voice" into a room and sees a crouched figure repairing a machine — call her name, and she turns around. In Act Three the player finds Therapy Room 13, where the Reforge is underway (Q&A, each answer removes a little). The player has three ways to interrupt.

Truth (GM-Exclusive): The Entity is a vast but clumsy system — it can detect anomalies but cannot locate causes. When the Reforge fails, the voice that asks questions "asks the same question again," and the things behind the twelve observation windows "verify" — because in their records this step has never failed. This gives the S7/S8 plan feasibility. The Silent One's (SIL) weakness is "hesitation": if the player has done an altruistic act for Hilda before, she has a chance to stop.

How to Run: The whole scene never knocks the table — the absence of a heartbeat is the greatest source of terror. The GM may imitate a teammate's voice / an Echo's movements; the player must judge true from false alone. In the first half the player believes it is a teammate; have the player shout the teammate's name at it, then it turns around. All three interruption methods have a cost; the third, "don't interrupt," is not a bad ending — it is a knowledge gain with a cost.

3. Opening Text (read aloud)

An empty space by the fire. But this time it's different — this time, everyone remembers her.

Hilda Lane. Here just yesterday. She said she was tired.

Eloise pokes the fire, her tone flat as if reading a shopping list.

"Yesterday she asked me, does hate count as a kind of hope."

"I said I didn't know."

"She said: 'Then I suppose I still have a little something left.'"

She pulls a page from her breast — Fane's notes. On it is written:

"Two production lines. External sourcing: from the Seep. Internal production: from the Campfire. Raw material: those who lost hope but still hate. Processing site: that white place. Room 13."

Eloise sets the page down.

"The white place," she says, "we've been there. Only back then, we thought only patients were inside."

5. Encounter Configuration

Main Killer: The Silent One (SIL) (story-exclusive avatar, the 9th, not in the 8-avatar standard pool). A former Survivor, incomplete because the Reforge is unfinished; moves like a Survivor (crouches, vaults windows, hides); no Terror Radius (TR) (no heartbeat, the greatest terror source); power direction "Mimicry" (emits Survivor voices to lure); weakness "Hesitation" (if the player has done an altruistic act for Hilda before, it has a chance to stop before attacking). Data block supplied separately by the combat designer as a simplified version; this scenario occupies 60% of the Encounter Budget (EB).

6. NPC Appearances and Lines

Hilda Lane (§8-A) → The Silent One (SIL) - Central to this scenario. In the first half she is "a teammate's voice"; turns around mid-scene to reveal herself. - Line (before turning): "…help me fix this one. Almost done." (Survivor voice) - Line (after turning, silent, only looking at you)

Sally Nkosi (§8-A): if she plucked the wrong pod in S3, here she speaks the Reforge's Q&A content (plucked a certain Killer's memory).

Eloise Finch (§8-A): reveals she too was once taken into Room 13, but "it didn't take" — her hate wasn't enough.

Echo Companion · Hilda (§8-B, solo configuration, after Act Three): if the Reforge is interrupted and she enters The Void, she appears as an Echo from S7 onward.

7. Decision Points and Branch Results

Core decision: How to interrupt Therapy Room 13 (choose one of three, all with a cost)

Method How Cost Result
Call her name Call her True Name + one thing only she would remember (requires recalling details from the first five scenarios) None She stops, Reforge fails, sent to The Void (recoverable later)
Give her a memory Spend one of your own Memory Cards and give it to her Permanently lose one Memory Card Reforge reversed, self preserved, returns to Campfire (best ending)
Don't interrupt Let it finish +5 Entity Hunger (EH); Hilda becomes a Killer appearing in S7/S8 Gain full knowledge of the Reforge process (needed for S8 "Takeover" ending)

GM hint: the third option is not a bad ending — it is a knowledge gain with a cost. Do not narratively punish the player who chooses it.

"Hesitation" made concrete: each time The Silent One (SIL) is about to attack the player character, if that player did at least one altruistic act for Hilda in S1–S5, make a check; the solo / duo success threshold is one tier lower than for three / four players. On success, The Silent One (SIL) stops for one round and says one word (first time "you," second time "don't," third time "run").

9. Ending Branches and Rewards

Ending branches (by core decision): - Call her name: Reforge fails, Hilda enters The Void (recoverable from S7). - Give a memory: best, Hilda returns to the Campfire. - Don't interrupt: Hilda becomes a Killer, appears as an enemy in S7/S8; the player gains Reforge-process knowledge (precondition for S8 "Takeover" ending).

Ember Moment trigger text (on calling her name / giving a memory):

The voice that asked questions stopped. Stopped for a long time. Then you hear something that has never happened — it asks the same question again. Everything behind the twelve observation windows twitched. They are confirming. They are verifying. Because in their records, this step has never failed. Fane's notes burn hot in your pocket: "It isn't that it can't be deceived. It's that it can't notice it's been deceived." You now know the second half of that sentence: it will notice "something is wrong." It just can't find where.

10. Connections to Adjacent Scenarios

  • Forward (S5): Hidden Workshop conduit (ly_drain ↔ ws_hidden) unlocked; Fane's notes "internal-production line" → this scenario's Hilda line; the "Spot the Loophole" ability → breaking the Mirror Swap.
  • Backward (S7): calling her name / giving a memory → Hilda assists in S7 or appears as an Echo; not interrupting → Hilda appears as a second Killer in S7 (Huntress (HTR) + The Silent One (SIL)). Reforge knowledge → precondition for S8 "Takeover" ending.
  • Hook payoff points: ① The Silent One (SIL) has no heartbeat → S1 Eloise's "the one who sat where you sit"; ② the twelve observation windows → a rehearsal of S8 the Nameless Heart's "Mouth Plain"; ③ the "Call Out the Name" ability → contact with The Entity's True Form in S8.
