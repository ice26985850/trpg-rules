Introduction

Hearthside Tales — A Small Shop Story of the Retired Adventurer

You were once an adventurer who swung a sword and slew dragons. Now your hearth-fire is no longer used to forge weapons, but to simmer a good pot of soup.

Hearthside Tales is a cozy, shopkeeping-style TRPG built around the theme of "retired adventurers returning home to open a shop." Here, your dragon is the chain merchant guild eyeing you from next door, and your treasure is a recipe forgotten by time.

Worldview

The story takes place on the fantasy continent of Ynserghard, in the "Post-Heroic Age." It has been twenty years since the Rift War that ended the Monster War; the Demon Lord is sealed away, yet adventurers are severely oversupplied — over thirty thousand sword-bearers compete for fewer than a thousand subjugation contracts each year. So the Kingdom launched the "Hoes for Swords" program, encouraging heroes to forge their swords into spatulas. Legend says every recipe a craftsperson discovers is one page of the Wanderer God's cookbook scattered across the mortal world; stepping into your shop is simply another way of continuing the adventure. Five great regions, the turning of four seasons, and six major factions make up this familiar yet gentle world.

Who Do You Play? What Do You Do?

You are a retired adventurer who opens a small shop with companions — a restaurant, a smithy, an apothecary, or a general store. Day to day you gather materials, develop recipes, and serve all kinds of customers; now and then you face rivals, seasonal crises, and border monsters. Your old combat instincts have become your eye for quality control; you earn gold, accumulate Reputation, and upgrade your stall step by step into a Legendary Shop.

System Highlights

· 3d6 Bell Curve: Check = 3d6 + Attribute + Skill ≥ Difficulty (DC). A skilled craftsperson won't mysteriously botch simple work, while a masterwork always needs a touch of fate's favor (a natural roll of 16–18 triggers a Critical Success).

· Management meets adventure: Five attributes (Craft, Appraisal, Social, Inspiration, Stamina), resources like Inspiration Points (IP) and Action Points (AP), tie together crafting, gathering, and shop upgrades (from Lv.1 to Lv.5 costs hundreds of G); you can also take a "Side Class" to learn a second profession and combine exclusive synergy effects.

· Create and inherit: A Researcher's "Reverse Engineering" can analyze others' products to steal their recipes; you can also invent exclusive dishes and forge masterworks — every recipe discovered is another page of the Wanderer God's book that you continue to write.

Who Is It For?

Players who enjoy management, crafting, life-simulation, and immersive roleplay will love it; the light strategy and resource-management barrier is friendly, and the 3d6 system is easy to pick up. Players chasing hardcore combat may find battle merely a garnish.

Play Style

· Combat weight: Supportive · Strategic depth: Moderate · Story-driven: Story-supportive · Resource management: Moderate · Character customization: High · Character-creation complexity: Medium · Rules density: Mid-weight · GM requirement: GM required

Estimated Play Time

· Single scenario: 3–5 hours (including character creation) · Full campaign: 30–50 hours (five connected scenarios, from unknown shop to Legendary Shop)

Basic Information

· Players: recommended 2–4 players + 1 GM (economy and cooperative crafting are balanced around a 3-person party) · Core dice: 3d6
