s05_Fury of the Sea Witch (Excerpted from the League of Legends TRPG Campaign Scenario Collection · Multiplayer Scenario · Side Story S1)

S1: Fury of the Sea Witch (Lv.3-6 · Bilgewater)

Background: Bounty hunters discover a giant sea witch appearing in the open sea off Bilgewater Harbor, threatening shipping lanes. The players are hired to hunt it down — but discover the sea witch was enraged by the illegal alchemical waste of the pirate king, Gangplank.

Chapter Level Type
Hunt Preparation Lv.3 Investigation + Ship Modification
Open-Sea Hunt Lv.4-5 Fleet Battle + Sea Witch Tracking
Abyssal Showdown Lv.6 Boss: the Abyssal Siren (Health Points (HP) 1,200, three phases: Surface/Shallow/Abyss)

Rewards: "Sea Witch Scale Armor" (Armor (AR)+30, Magic Resistance (MR)+20, underwater breathing)
