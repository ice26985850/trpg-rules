Scenario 11: Final End (CL76–99 · Personal Guard and the Singularity Beyond the World)

Run Method: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count adjustment rules)," with a design baseline of a 4-person party. The player-count adjustment rules are the same as in the Prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / +1 concurrent). This is the final act of the endgame chapter for CL76–99, the decisive battle following the fall of the Eclipse Sovereign in Scenario 10 and the appearance of the Path of Finality.

Difficulty Guideline: First fight the Final Herald (CR85, endgame BOSS personal guard), then the Singularity Beyond the World (CR99, endgame BOSS). Design Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ total party level × 0.6 (at 4 players CL99 it is about CR99). For Otherworld enemy stats and traits, see Monster Compendium, Chapter 5.

I. Opening Description

The Eclipse Sovereign of Scenario 10 collapses, and a "Path of Finality" splits open in the fragment core—leading straight to the Void Core where the Singularity's true body resides. At the journey's end awaits the Final Herald, the Singularity's personal guard and the prophesied "Herald of the Final Battle." Past it, you will face the Singularity Beyond the World—the true and final end of this journey.

II. Scene Flow

Act 0: Undertaking the Final End Decisive Battle (Opening Beat)

After the Path of Finality appears, the Joint Command entrusts you with the final expedition mandate—you must have Character Level (CL) ≥76 to undertake it (clearing Scenario 10 is a prerequisite). This is the eve of the last battle; all accumulated war merits, Influence Points (IP), gear, and companions (even Demon Generals from the reconciliation route) will be gambled here.

After undertaking it, you step onto the Path of Finality. Solo mode is supported at Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5, with no allies.

Act 1: CL76–84 Path of Finality

Along the path you encounter the "Prophecy Echo" illusions laid by the Final Herald (Reality Distortion + Psychic trial), as well as scattered remnants of the Void Apostle. You arrive at the antechamber of the Void Core.

Act 2: CL85 Final Herald (Endgame BOSS Personal Guard)

In the antechamber of the Void Core, the Final Herald (CR85, endgame BOSS personal guard) manifests. Its traits are fully active: Reality Distortion (flips / splits the positioning map) / Rule Nullification (ignores terrain cover and partial immunities) / Prophetic Judgment (AOE instant-death threat countdown) / Rift Resonance (in phase three, recovers 10% Health (HP) per round). Beyond-the-World Resistance ×0.5, Psychic immunity. - Guard Mechanic: After the Herald falls, the Void Core's outer shell shatters and the true body of the Singularity Beyond the World is revealed—this is a "consecutive battle" design, where the sustained-pressure of the guard fight carries directly into the endgame BOSS. - Influence Points (IP) Hook: Rescue a companion during the Prophetic Judgment countdown +10 Influence Points (IP).

Act 3: CL99 Singularity Beyond the World (Endgame BOSS)

The Singularity's true body appears, the Singularity Beyond the World (CR99, endgame BOSS). Its traits are fully active: Reality Distortion (continuously flips the positioning map) / Rule Nullification (ignores most native defenses) / World Collapse (area-wide AOE law collapse) / Rift Resonance (in phase three, recovers 12% Health (HP) per round). Beyond-the-World Resistance ×0.5, Psychic immunity, all-attribute Resistance ×0.5. - Endgame Mechanic: Each round the Singularity gains 1 stack of Damage Reduction (DR) via "Rift Resonance" (cap 30%), and players must break through the core with burst damage within a limited number of rounds; exceeding the round threshold triggers an area-wide Area Effect (AOE) instant-death threat countdown. - Ending Branch: - Influence Points (IP) ≥60 (True Peace route continuation): You may attempt the "Understand the Otherworld" ending—sealing the rifts rather than destroying the Singularity, forging cross-world peace and being granted the golden true Hero's Proof. - Influence Points (IP) <60: Only the "Subjugation Ending"—destroy the Singularity, the rifts forcibly close, the world is restored but memories of the Otherworld are erased.

Act 4: Epilogue (Triggerable at IP≥60)

If the True Peace route is achieved, an optional epilogue is available: a faint but stable link is established between the Altia Continent and the Otherworld, and as the emissary of both worlds you open a new chapter.

III. NPC Dialogue Examples

Final Herald: "The prophecy is already written—your world will ultimately fall into silence. This is not hatred; it is merely… inevitability." Singularity Beyond the World: (no language, only the echo of laws) "…" Demon Lord Vercellia (if alive on the reconciliation route): "A thousand years of hatred seem so small before this void. Heroes, this time, fight for all worlds."

V. Stage Design

  • Stage A Path of Finality: Exploration / Psychic check (DC 34), illusion trial.
  • Stage B Final Herald: Combat check (DC 36), endgame BOSS personal guard; upon falling, the Singularity's true body is revealed (consecutive battle).
  • Stage C Singularity Beyond the World: Combat check (DC 40), endgame BOSS; the ending branch is determined by total Influence Points (IP) (see Section III).
