Dungeon Module D6 — Volcano

Recommended Rank: C/B (Challenge Rating (CR) 19–26) | Theme: Lava ravine, high-tier resource farming Acceptance: Guild commission "C-Fire1 / B-Fire1" (corresponds to Rank C→B, CL 19–26); or designated by GM as a leveling ground. Map: maps/D6_火山.yaml ＋ maps/D6_火山.svg This work has no multiclass; solo entry follows the player-count scaling rule (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick).

How to Enter (opening beat)

  • Take guild commission: At the Adventurers' Guild bulletin board accept the C-tier "Volcano Subjugation" (C-Fire1), then after promotion accept B-Fire1 (corresponds to Rank C→B, CL 19–26).
  • Active exploration: After joining at S0, players may delve into the lava ravine from the mining town pass for exploration (GM Book 17.1).
  • Leveling role: The top choice for farming resources at CL 19–26; run alongside C/B commissions to grind Rank from C up to B.
  • Solo mode: Supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick; the mini-boss room has a 10×10 positioning map (maps/D6_戰鬥_炎魔領主.yaml), entering the room switches to standard combat (Chapter 7), clear the room to return to the exploration round.

I. Background

The lava ravine below the mining town has never truly gone quiet; the Balrog Lord rises from the magma lake, ruling over fire lizards and lava giants. Mining crews frequently vanish, so the Guild lists it as a C→B tier subjugation target; at the throne deep in the ravine, the Balrog Lord is this hell's mini-boss.

III. Key Room Details

  • R3 Lava Crevice (weak): Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); triggers 1d6 Burn. Disable DC14.
  • R8 Falling Rock Corridor (strong): Perception DC18; triggers 3d6 Bludgeon. Falling rocks can block the R7↔R13 passage (GM ruling).
  • R14 Geyser Trap Zone (strong): Perception DC20; triggers 3d6 Burn.
  • R5 Charred Mine: Open chest DC12; trick mine 30% (medium, 2d6). Contains Fire Crystal ×2, Glint Shard, Gold (G) 120~200.
  • R9 Sulfur Treasure Cave: Open chest DC14; contains Sulfur Ore ×2, Fine Material, Gold (G) 140~220.
  • R17 Exit Rift: After clearing, return to Guild to settle rewards and leave.
