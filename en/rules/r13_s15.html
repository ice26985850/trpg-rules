Scenario A-5: "Last Prayer at Black Dock" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Mortal · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map Reference: maps/map15_silent_tower.svg

Difficulty: Endgame (Challenge Rating (CR) Total 6-7) | Inquisitor: Victor Black (Fallen)
Target Region: Carcassonne—Tower of Silence bottom level → Black Dock
Recommended Player Attributes: Level 5 (at the limit of humanity)

Surface Goal

The final mission. Inquisitor Victor Black personally selected you to carry out this "ultimate trial": go to the abandoned prison area at the bottom level of the Tower of Silence, and purge a long-forgotten Chaos corruption.

True Goal

Victor Black has been corrupted by Chaos. This mission is his final test of the Penitents—not a test of loyalty, but a test of "fall potential". In his eyes, those who survive and complete this mission are qualified to become his Chaos followers. After completing the mission, he will privately offer each survivor a choice: accept Chaos's "gift" → gain freedom and power; refuse → be executed by him on the grounds of "proven unredeemable".

Known Threats

  • The bottom level of the Tower of Silence—the "Eternal Waiting Level"—has been abandoned for 200 years
  • There may still be "something" down there
  • No supplies or support whatsoever

Hidden Threats

  • At the bottom ruins is a sealed Warp rift, whose 200-year seal is failing
  • In the ruins are other Penitents trapped at the bottom level—they have been forgotten here for decades. Some still retain their sanity, some have been corrupted by Chaos
  • Victor's true motive

Resource Ration

Zero supplies. Only what you still have on you. Victor says: "If you still need my help by now, you are not worth redeeming."

Temptation Note Distribution

All notes are invalid in this mission—or transformed into Victor's private communications. The GM may give each player a "Victor's Whisper": a privately offered, tempting choice.

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Elder Erin, the "Forgotten" A former Penitent trapped for 47 years Faith 5 (even more steadfast in despair). Can provide a map of the bottom level and intel. If the players save her, she becomes the final witness against Victor
Mutated Penitent Marcus A Penitent who fell after being trapped for 23 years Has become a Mutant Tyrant. The Temptation Note he once received was Note 1—he chose to betray his teammates. But that did not save him
Victor Black's Avatar Final Boss The Inquisitor himself will not appear in person—but will await the "result" at Black Dock

Key Check Nodes

1 Searching the ruins for Erin Investigation (Hard) Mind×10+Investigation−20 Find and gain help Miss her
2 Facing Marcus's madness Oratory (Extreme) Faith×10+Oratory−40 Awaken his remaining humanity (give him a moment of clarity, he may choose self-destruction) Can only fight
3 Sealing the Warp rift Faith (Extreme)+full-team Group Test Faith×10−40 Rift purified, all present −5 Corruption Rift expands, Warp Shadow×2 appear
4 Refusing Victor's "gift" Faith (Hard) Faith×10−20 Refuse—gain chance at the Emperor's Mark Accept—permanent +10 Corruption + Chaos Mark, but gain freedom

Combat Encounters

Encounter Enemies Challenge Rating (CR) Tactics
Bottom Ruins Mutant Lurker×3 + Warp Shadow×1 1×3+2 Multiple threats in the darkness
Marcus's Domain Mutant Tyrant "Marcus" 2 (enhanced) If awakened→self-destruct (no combat). If combat→he will try to persuade players to join his "way of survival"
Rift Seal (if failed) Warp Shadow×2 2×2 Continuously pouring from the rift
Black Dock (endgame) If refuse Victor→Inquisition Assault Trooper×4 + Acolyte-level Chaos Cultist×2 1×4+1×2 Victor has already arranged the "silencing"

Endgame Branches

Choice Result
Refuse Victor + survive If Erin is rescued→her testimony can let other Inquisitors discover Victor's betrayal. The character gains true freedom... but what does that mean in the 40k world?
Refuse Victor + die in battle Glorious ending. Your names will not be remembered—but your choice proves the Emperor's faith is real
Accept Victor's "gift" Character becomes a Chaos follower. You are "free"—but no longer yourselves. GM takes over the character
Deceive Victor (pretend to accept, report to other Inquisitors) Extremely dangerous. Requires Deceive Test (Extreme) success to fool the Fallen Inquisitor
