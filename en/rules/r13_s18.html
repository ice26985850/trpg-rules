Scenario B-3: "Ruins of the Fateful Choice" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Astartes · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map: maps/map18_thanos7.svg

Difficulty: Intermediate | Location: Adeptus Mechanicus Abandoned Facility "Thanatos-7 Outpost" Recommended Attributes: 3–4 growths

Background

Following the evacuation coordinates, the squad arrives at an abandoned Adeptus Mechanicus research outpost. It has what they need—and perhaps a working transport ship—but an Adeptus Mechanicus facility is never simply "abandoned."

Scenario Structure

Phase Content
Arrival The facility appears bombed from the outside. The main building has collapsed, but the underground facility seems intact. The entrance is sealed—requires a Tech-Use test to bypass.
Underground Laboratory The Adeptus Mechanicus was researching... xenos technology here. A sealed Eldar comms crystal. The crystal is still active—it has been transmitting a signal all along.
Eldar Appear An Eldar Ranger squad (3) arrives, drawn by the crystal's signal. They want the crystal back. They offer a trade: the crystal in exchange for help repairing their transport ship (they have the skill but are unfamiliar with human machinery).
The Mechanicus "Guardian" The underground facility's automated defense systems are still active—the Machine Spirit has been twisted by Chaotic energy and will attack any intruder.

Oath Conflict Point

Conflict: Reject the Xenos vs. Survival Above All

This is the core dilemma of the scenario: - Accept the Eldar's help: repair the transport ship and gain an evacuation tool. But Corruption +3~5, and gain the "Debt to the Eldar". - Refuse and drive off the Eldar: remain pure. But you must find your own way to repair the transport ship—which could take weeks.

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Eldar Ranger "Elandil" Potential Ally / Trade Partner Calm, arrogant, but genuinely wants the crystal back. He bears no hostility toward humans—merely disdain. If refused, he will not attack, only leave.
Twisted Machine Spirit Environmental Threat The facility's defense turrets and servo-skulls have been polluted by Warp energy. They are not "evil," only "in pain"—they can be soothed with a Tech-Use test.
