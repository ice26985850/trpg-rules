Scenario Five: "Echoes of the Weaver"

Attribute Content
Suggested Quality ★★★★★ Legendary-grade
Player Count 1 mage (required) + Companion (required)
Estimated Sessions 5–8 (can serve as the campaign finale)
Core Output Multiple Legendary Shards, completion of the ultimate spell, world-level impact, a conversation with the Void God
Primary Threats The Void Rift, the Void God's lingering consciousness, the Watcher's ultimate trial, Pure Fire's intervention
Prerequisite Academic Reputation ≥ 60; possess at least 2 Legendary Shards; complete the theoretical preparation of your research goal; complete at least three of Scenarios One through Four; having a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 60 is strongly recommended
References [Solo Supplement Chapter 2] NPC Companion system, [Solo Supplement §1.2] Encounter difficulty scaling, [Player Book §3.11 Taboo Shards], [Player Book §2.10 Growth]

Background

Enough Legendary Shards have gathered in one place. Enough powerful spells have been created. The Starweave itself is beginning to destabilize.

Your tower's Observatory (requires Lv4+) reports an unusual magical surge — its source is the Void Rift. All Void spells resonate with an unwarranted echo when cast. The Watcher Council's highest alert is triggered — an alert level not seen since the Ashville Cataclysm (阿什維爾大爆炸) of Era Year 1102.

You also notice a personal anomaly: the shards you gained across Scenarios One through Four — those shards are echoing one another. As if they know each other's existence. As if they remember.

Valastra (瓦拉斯特拉)'s last message comes through your tower's scrying pool:

"This is no coincidence. Ignarath chose you because your shards are calling to him. Selena's memory shard is calling to you. Everything is converging. The Void God (虛無之神) — He is waiting for you."

Act One: The Eve of Convergence

Scene 1-1: Muster Your Strength

This is the preparation phase of the entire campaign. The player must integrate all available resources before the final action:

Action list (3 Action Points (AP) available for final preparation):

Action Action Point (AP) Effect
Study Ignarath's memory shard 2 Theory DC 18 — gain exact coordinates of the Void God's seal
Consult Valastra 1 The Dragons provide Legendary material support (one unique material, 50% discount)
Strengthen Companion Bonds 1 Conduct a bond conversation ([Solo Supplement §2.4]) — Loyalty +2d6. The Companion gains a "Bond Awakening" preview
Publish Final Thesis 2 Publish a paper on your research journey — Academic Reputation +3d6+10 (one-time)
Collect Void Shards 3 Travel to the Void Rift for a final Void research — gain the needed Void Legendary Shard (or confirm you already have it)
Build Final Defenses 1 Upgrade the Defense System to Lv5 (if not yet) — cost 4000 GP

GM Hint: This phase should carry emotional weight — it may be the last time you conduct ordinary research in the tower. Let the player have meaningful interaction with NPCs (apprentice, Companion, allies). Review the journey of the whole campaign.

Scene 1-2: Assembly of Key Companions

Depending on the player's choices in prior scenarios, the following NPCs may appear:

NPC Source Assistance Provided
Eira (艾拉) (Chief Apprentice) Scenario One Holds the tower, keeps research uninterrupted. She has grown into Chief Apprentice — her special skill may be a lifesaver in this scenario
Lena (莉娜) (Mercenary Companion) Scenario Two Fights alongside you. At Loyalty ≥ 60 she shows "Loyalty Awakening" — a special sacrificial protection move
Zephyrus (澤菲羅斯) (Dragonkin Companion) Scenario Four Fights alongside you. Can fly to transport the player, dragonflame assists in combat

Marcus (馬庫斯) (Taboo Mage) Scenario Three If freed/trusts you — provides a unique use of Void Shards (can transform one Void Shard into a Legendary Shard of any element, but requires Will DC 20)

GM Adjustment: Whether each NPC appears depends on the player's past choices. This is not a "default" roster — it is the consequence of the player's actions. If someone died or betrayed due to the player's choices — they will not be here.

Act Two: Creation of the Ultimate Spell

Scene 2-1: Collect the Last Legendary Shards

The player needs a Radiant Legendary Shard (◈) and a Void Legendary Shard (◈).

The Radiant Shard — "Selena's Legacy":

Location: the oldest node of the Starweave — in the depths of the Ethereal. Requires an Ethereal projection via Observatory Lv4+.

Challenge: Find the original shard left by Selena, the first mage. She sealed her first Legendary Shard in a memory space in the Ethereal — only those who prove they understand "the nature of seeking" may enter.

Enter the memory space (Theory + Perception dual check, DC 22, both must succeed):

  • Success: You enter Selena's memory. She stands beneath the old oak in the forest — the very tree you saw in Scenario One — performing her first shard experiment. She turns to you — no, to someone in her own era — and says, "If you are watching all this — then it means you have already traveled far enough. Take this."

Gain: Selena's original Radiant Shard (◈) ×1 + gain Selena's memory — permanent Theory +1 ([Player Book §2.10] deep-learning effect, but triggered by plot)

  • Failure: You are lost in the Ethereal for 2d4 hours. You wake on your tower floor with a splitting headache. Retryable, but DC +2.

The Void Shard — "Heart of the Seventh Hole":

Location: deep in the Void Rift — below the third level of the Ashville Ruins (same location as the original Scenario Two, but the rift is now more active).

Challenge: Enter the edge of the Void — maintain self-awareness in a place without concepts of time and space.

Will DC 22, 3 consecutive successes (each failure Corruption +2):

  • 1st success: You step into the rift. The world warps around you — color loses meaning, direction ceases to exist. But you stand firm. Because you remember who you are.
  • 2nd success: The Void's silence echoes in your mind. But your Companion — grabs your arm (physical/magical connection) — reminds you there are still others in the world.
  • 3rd success: At the rift's core, you see it — a crystal blacker than darkness, identical to the growing Void Shard you saw in Scenario Three — but a hundred thousand times larger. This is not a shard. This is a heart.

Key moment: You must choose — touch it, or take a small piece?

Choice Result
Touch Will DC 26. On success gain full understanding — but the Void God's consciousness may leave a mark within you (Corruption +5, but gain "Void Sense" — permanently sense Void magical surges)
Sample Craft DC 20. Safely take a small piece. Gain Void Legendary Shard (◈) ×1
  • 3 failures: You are expelled from the rift. Take 4d10 Void damage + Corruption +6. Retryable, but the next required consecutive successes increase to 4.

Scene 2-2: Create the Ultimate Spell

Back at the tower, the player performs the most important spell creation of the whole campaign ([Player Book §3.1]).

Ultimate Spell design requirements: - At least 5 shards — at least 2 of them Legendary-grade - Must include: Radiant + Void + one Life-element shard (or alternative — see below) - Optional: add Constraints and Mediums to adjust the final effect

Quality: automatically Legendary-grade (★★★★★) — because two Legendary Shards are used.

Spirit cost: about 32–64 (see [Player Book §3.7] Spirit cost table). Requires a ritual Medium (halves) or a Cooldown Constraint (diminishing) to cast.

GM Review ([Player Book §3.9]): This is the ultimate spell — give the player maximum creative freedom. The only requirement: this spell must align with the ultimate conception described in the player's research goal ([Player Book §2.8]).

Naming the spell: The player is asked to name this spell — and explain the meaning of the name. This will be the signature output of the entire campaign.

Act Three: The Final Choice

Scene 3-1: Four Choices

Before the Void Rift — all assembled Companions, allies, and some key NPCs (depending on the player's past choices) are present. Representatives of the Watchers and the Taboo Network have also come — they all await your decision.

The Void Rift unfolds before you — like a black wound across the sky. What comes from the rift is not sound, but a consciousness: ancient, vast, imprisoned for thousands of years.

"You have come. Bearing my shards. Bearing my memory. Bearing their fear."

The Void God's voice is not a threat — it is weariness. Three thousand and one hundred years of loneliness.

The player must choose:

Option A: Release the Void God

  • Use the ultimate spell to open the seal
  • The Void God returns — a fundamental change to the magical world
  • All mages gain the right to use "Void" element shards (no longer Taboo)
  • Your tower becomes the center of the new magical age
  • Academic Reputation rises directly to 100

Cost: The world is never the same. Pure Fire may launch a religious war. No one knows what the Void God will do after returning.

Option B: Fully Seal the Void God

  • Use the ultimate spell to permanently banish the Void God's lingering consciousness
  • All existing Void Shards lose activity (become common quality)
  • You gain the title "Guardian"
  • All factions (including the Watchers and the Taboo Network) briefly reconcile

Cost: Void research ends entirely. You lose all your Void Shards. No Void spell exists in the world ever again.

Option C: Merge with the Void God

  • The ultimate path of the Taboo style
  • Fuse the Void God's lingering consciousness into yourself
  • All attribute caps raised to 15
  • Gain the ability to create new shards (one per month, any rarity)

Cost: Other factions may become enemies. Your Corruption cap is removed — but you must make a weekly Will DC 20 or gain +1d6 Corruption. This is a point of no return.

Option D: Negotiated Coexistence

  • The hardest path
  • Not fully release, not fully seal
  • Establish a "dialogue window" — the Void God may communicate with the world in limited ways

Factions to persuade (at least 3 of 4):

Faction Persuasion DC Argument
Watcher Council DC 22 "Sealed knowledge is more dangerous than open knowledge"
Dragon Elders Council DC 18 (if Zephyrus assists: DC 14) "Valastra — Ignarath said: he is not evil"
Taboo Network DC 16 "Legalized Void research — isn't that what you always wanted?"
Pure Fire DC 28 "If we establish dialogue, there will never be another Ashville"

If you successfully persuade ≥ 3 factions: coexistence succeeds. The Void God becomes a "Cosmic Advisor." You gain the title "Conciliator" — Academic Reputation +20, Practical Reputation +20, all faction relations improved.

If you fail: you must choose a fallback among A/B/C.

Scene 3-3: The Ultimate Casting

Ultimate Spell casting ([Player Book §3.8]):

Because the spell is already Legendary-grade (★★★★★), the Spirit cost is extremely high. The player must pay using the following combination: - Ritual Medium (halves) - Overdraw (1d4 force damage per point of Spirit — see [Player Book §1.3]) - Companion Resonance (if Companion present and Loyalty ≥ 60 — the Companion voluntarily contributes half their Spirit)

Final check (Craft DC 22 to perform the ultimate spell casting):

  • Critical Success (double 10): The spell not only fully succeeds — it produces unpredictable positive effects. Depending on the chosen path — the GM describes the extra Legendary-grade effect.

Example (if Option D Negotiated Coexistence + Critical Success):

The seal is not opened — it becomes a door. A wisp of the Void God's consciousness flows into the world through the crack in the door — not a threat, but a voice: gentle, ancient, carrying three thousand years of weariness and three thousand years of wisdom.

"Thank you," the voice says. "It has been a long time since anyone spoke to me."

  • Success: The spell runs as designed. Goal achieved.

  • Failure (within Safety Threshold): The spell partially succeeds — effect reduced. But something goes wrong in the rite — you lose one non-critical shard. Retryable after 1d4 Long Rests.

  • Critical Failure (double 1):

Roll d6 to determine the consequence: | d6 | Consequence | |----|-------------| | 1–2 | Goal not achieved. All Legendary Shards destroyed. But you live — you can start over. | | 3–4 | Goal partially achieved but reversed (seal becomes release, release becomes seal — the exact opposite result) | | 5–6 | Spell loses control — you are teleported to an unknown demiplane. Your Companion is teleported with you. This is not an ending — it is a brand-new beginning. |

Ultimate Endings

A. Release the Void God

  • The Void God returns, the magical world welcomes a new age
  • Your tower becomes the center of the new age — the "Void Research Academy" is established there
  • But Pure Fire declares a "crusade" against you
  • You become a legend — but your story is only just beginning

B. Fully Seal

  • The Void God's threat is permanently eliminated
  • You become the "Guardian" — a new title, granted only to mages who make an ultimate contribution to the world
  • All factions briefly reconcile — an anonymous Watcher captain quietly says to you: "We owe you an apology"
  • Academic Reputation 90, Practical Reputation 90

C. Merge with the Void God

  • You become the new Void God — or at least, the new vessel of the Void God
  • Gain divine-level abilities (see [Player Book §2.10] Legendary-grade treatment + extra)
  • But all factions may become your enemies
  • GM option: the character becomes a legendary NPC existence in the world, and the player creates a new character to continue the journey. Or the player continues playing — now the goal changes to: find balance between divinity and humanity.

D. Negotiated Coexistence

  • The hardest to achieve, the wisest ending
  • The Void God becomes a "Cosmic Advisor" — an unprecedented form of existence
  • Void research is legalized, but regulated
  • You gain the title "Conciliator" — the only human in history simultaneously respected by the Watchers, the Taboo Network, the Dragons, and Pure Fire
  • Academic Reputation +20, Practical Reputation +20
