Scenario S9: Empire's Afterglow

Suggested Era Range: Empire Age → Legendary Age
Suggested Level: Mid-to-High
Estimated Turns: ~70 turns (25 in-game years)
Difficulty: ★★★★☆
Core Themes: Disaster, Survival, Unification, Ascension
Players: 2–5 players + 1 GM

I. Scenario Overview

Synopsis

The world steps into its darkest hour, as disasters sweep in like tides. The last few surviving empires slaughter one another in the afterglow, contesting the final resources and legitimacy. This is no glorious campaign, but a struggle of life and death—whoever can unify the world amid the great catastrophe will carve their reign into eternity, becoming the only beacon at the end of darkness.

When the bell of the imperial capital rings, it is either coronation or oblivion.

Scenario Goals

  1. Integrate the remnants of territory amid disaster, establish stable provinces
  2. Cope with natural disasters and famine, keep the nation alive
  3. Build the Eternal Monument, or hold the highest Imperial Might Score at the endgame
  4. Survive the iron-blooded unification and ascend the throne

II. Multiplayer Mechanics

  • Mode: Competitive (survival + conquest). Each player leads an empire, surviving in a disaster region.
  • Imperial Might Score: Stable Provinces×12 ＋ Wars Won×18 ＋ Miracles Built×25 ＋ Survivor Population×0.5.
  • Victory Condition: The first to complete the "Eternal Monument" immediately triggers the endgame and +35 points; otherwise, Imperial Might Scores are tallied at turn 70, and the highest ascends the throne.
  • Player Interaction: May forge temporary ceasefires, declare war, launch annihilation wars; during disasters may share refuge resources or prey on the afflicted.
  • GM Duties: Track stable provinces, miracles built, and survivor population; arrange disaster and refuge events.

IV. Ending Conditions

Victory (Highest Imperial Might Score)

  • Complete the Eternal Monument and hold the highest Imperial Might Score
  • Reward: All attributes +12, unlock the "Eternal Emperor" Legendary Deed; other players gain corresponding renown by score

Partial Success

  • Survive to the endgame but not ascended
  • Reward: Keep your empire, may enter Free Mode to continue

Failure Conditions

  • Imperial capital falls and population drops to zero
  • Remedy: Inheritance Mechanism—as a surviving minister, install a young lord, keeping 50% of attributes

VII. GM Tips

  1. Disaster pacing: The Empire Age is rife with disasters; use disaster events to create survival pressure
  2. Scarce resources: Emphasize resource management; make every decision bear on the nation's fate
  3. Mutual aid or plunder: Both are legitimate during disasters; encourage dramatic choices
  4. Miracle balance: Miracle construction is a key score source; avoid any single player monopolizing
  5. Endgame ceremony: Coronation should carry sufficient narrative weight
