S1: Routine Patrol

Campaign: The Rookie Director (Campaign One) Recommended Level: 1 Difficulty Class (DC): 40 (Very Easy) Mission Type: Recon Mission (REC) Estimated Time: 30–45 minutes

Opening Description

This is your second week on the job. The coffee on your desk is still steaming — synthetic coffee from the Outpost cafeteria, tasting like hot water that newspapers were steeped in, but at least it is hot.

Your comm terminal chimes. The M.E.G. Regional Dispatch Center has sent today's mission list. Only one item:

"Level 1, Corridor B, routine patrol. Standard route, no expected anomalies. Duration: 4 hours."

This is the simplest mission you have ever taken. You could almost sign it with your eyes closed.

But this is your first signature.

Scene Flow

Scene One: Office — Signing the Mission Order

Scene Description: Your office is small, but tidy. A photo of the previous Director hangs on the wall — you have not yet decided whether to take it down.

Decision Point: - Choose who to dispatch (select 1–2 from your initial team) - Decide whether to purchase Intel (basic Intel costs 50 budget — but for a routine Level 1 patrol that may be a waste)

Key NPC Dialogue:

If you choose to dispatch the Trainee Explorer "Lin Xiao": "Director, this is my first solo mission. I won't let you down." There is light in her eyes — the kind of light of someone who just graduated from training and has not yet seen the true face of The Backrooms.

Scene Two: Mission Execution — Patrol Begins

Scene Description: Level 1's corridors stretch under the hum of fluorescent lights. Grayish-white walls, pale yellow floor tiles, a fire extinguisher every thirty meters. Everything is normal — too normal.

Check: Roll d100 + Explorer Experience + Equipment bonus + Doctrine bonus, against DC 40.

Outcome Branches: - Critical Success: Not only is the patrol completed, but you also discover a previously unmarked small storage room in a corner — containing 2 units of Almond Water. Intelligence +2. - Success: Patrol completed. No anomalies. Intelligence +1, mission record is good. - Marginal Success: During the patrol, the comm headset briefly malfunctioned for fifteen minutes. No real problem — but that feeling of isolation is unsettling. Patrol completed, Intelligence +1. - Failure: Unlikely (DC 40, the base bonus alone is near passing) — but if it does fail, the patrol team briefly gets lost in the maze-like corridors and takes twice as long to finish. Mission still completed but consumes extra resources. - Catastrophe: Almost impossible (natural 1–5) — if it does happen, the patrol team encounters an unrecorded spatial anomaly. A door that should have been a storage room opens into pitch-black void. Fortunately no one is hurt — but this discovery requires further investigation.

Scene Three: Office — Mission Report

Scene Description: When the patrol team returns, you are eating your synthetic sandwich. Lin Xiao stands at the door, a bit dirty on the face, but in high spirits.

Settlement: - Intelligence +1 (or more) - No casualty risk - Mission completed successfully

Roleplay:

Lin Xiao: "Director, Sector B is all normal. ... Can I apply to go somewhere farther next time?" This should make you feel proud — but for some reason, you only feel a tinge of unease.

GM Information

Item Value
True DC 40
Intel Bias (basic Intel) ±25% (DC 30–50)
Minimum Personnel 1
Recommended Specialty Recon
Encounter Probability 5% (almost no Entity encounter)
Time Limit None

Difficulty Design Intent: This is a tutorial mission. Even with the minimum setup (1 Trainee Explorer +5 + Equipment 2 × 3 = +11), facing DC 40 there is a 66% base success rate. Only natural 1–5 triggers Catastrophe (5%). This is to let the player learn the basic flow, not a real challenge.

Hidden Reward: If the player actively chooses not to use basic Intel (saving 50 budget), the GM can hint: "You already know Level 1 Sector B very well — you could walk that route with your eyes closed." This teaches the player: buying Intel is a choice, not mandatory.
