Scenario B-5: "The Last Ship" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Astartes · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map: maps/map20_last_ship.svg

Difficulty: Final | Location: Mountain Evacuation Point Recommended Attributes: Levels 5–7 (approaching Chapter Master level)

Background

The orbital defenses are down. The rescue signal has been sent. An Imperial escort responded—but it can only dispatch one landing craft. The landing craft holds at most 6 people. And you have: - 4 Astartes (possibly fewer) - 20–30 rescued civilians (depending on earlier choices) - possible Eldar "allies"

Also—the black box data you obtained from B-4. If you take it back, it may overturn Exterminatus. But if you take the data... more people must be left behind.

Scenario Structure

Phase Content
Final Mustering All remaining characters, civilians, and allies gather at the evacuation point—an abandoned starport. The landing craft is expected to arrive within 6 hours.
Chaos's Final Assault The Chaos forces on the planet sense the evacuation attempt. A mixed force of Mutants, Warp entities, and a Chaos Space Marine (if Kassar is dead, a new Chaos Space Marine (CSM)) is advancing on the starport. They are not trying to stop the evacuation—they want that landing craft.
The Calculation The landing craft holds 6 people. Who boards? An Astartes takes 1 person per seat. 2 civilians count as 1 seat (they can squeeze). The Eldar is not human—he needs 1 seat but may be unwilling to board a human ship.
Those Who Stay Warriors who choose to stay gain the "Final Mission"—they do not stay in vain. They will hold the starport until the landing craft safely takes off, after which their ultimate fate will be decided jointly by the GM and the players.

Oath Conflict Point (Ultimate)

This is the final test of all oaths:

  • Oath to the Emperor: Bring back the black box data—overturning a wrongful Exterminatus may be the greater loyalty?
  • Oath to Comrades: Who boards? Who stays? Will the brothers who stay forgive you?
  • Oath to the Innocent: Civilians—how many can you take?
  • Oath to the Mission: A successful evacuation is mission complete—but what counts as "success"?

Final Conditions

Finale Condition Narrative
Full Evacuation (unlikely) Find some way to get everyone aboard—or the civilians choose to stay A miracle. But you know this is not the normal ending of 40k.
Partial Evacuation At least 1 Astartes aboard + black box data Standard ending. Those who stay gain a dignified "Final Mission."
No Evacuation Everyone chooses to stay, or the landing craft is destroyed A solemn ending. Your story ends here—but a legend is born on the planet from then on.
Someone Chooses Darkness An Astartes accepts Chaos's help—or uses the power of Chaos to ensure evacuation Nightmare ending. Among those who board, one is no longer himself.

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Landing Craft Pilot Lieutenant Vera Last Hope A young Imperial Navy pilot. She was assigned to this "suicide evacuation mission." She is afraid—but she is here.
Chaos's "Envoy" Final Temptation Before the Chaos assault begins, a voice echoes through the starport—not an enemy, but Chaos's offer: "Give us the landing craft. We will let all of you leave safely—so long as you promise... to do nothing."

Combat Encounters

Encounter Enemies Challenge Rating (CR) Tactics
Starport Perimeter Mutants ×8 + Tyrant ×2 1×8+2×2 Buy time
Control Tower Defense Warp Shadows ×2 2×2 They emerge from the walls
Landing Pad (finale) Chaos Space Marine ×1 (leader of the final wave) + Cultists ×4 4+0×4 Final boss fight. The landing pad must be cleared before the landing craft lands.

Final Scene

The landing craft takes off. The warriors who stayed stand on the starport's control tower.

"Emperor—if You are watching—tell my brothers I have no regrets."

GM Adjustment Suggestions

  • Shorten the campaign: Run only A-1, A-3, A-5 (Mortal) or B-1, B-3, B-5 (Astartes).
  • Increase difficulty: Add 1–2 extra enemies to all combat encounters.
  • Reduce difficulty: Give an extra Medikit before key fights, or halve enemy Hit Points (HP).
  • Mixed mode: Not recommended for regular mixing, but Mortals and Astartes may have a brief crossover at the A-5/B-5 level.
