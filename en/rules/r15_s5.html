S5: The Archive's Secret

Campaign: Call from the Deep (Campaign Four · Core) Recommended Level: 13–14 Difficulty Class (DC): 100 (Extreme) Mission Type: Recon Mission (REC) → Intel Acquisition Estimated Time: 120–150 minutes

Opening Description

Documents seized from the Crimson Order wreckage point to a name: "The Unifier." After months of Intel analysis and cross-referencing, your department has tracked the only known storage location of Unifier-related records — Level 99, the "Archive."

The Archive is the most paradoxical existence in The Backrooms. On the surface it is safe — gentle lighting, infinitely extending bookshelves, even the smell of old books in the air. But everyone who has been to its depths will tell you the same thing: "Some knowledge should not be read."

You need to know what The Unifier is. But the price of knowing the truth — you may not yet know.

Scene Flow

Scene One: Entering the Archive — The Silent Library

Scene Description: Level 99 is a dreamlike place. Endless wooden bookshelves stretch from the floor to a ceiling that vanishes into shadow. The air is warm, dry, with the scent of old paper. Each step makes the floor creak softly — this is the closest thing to "home" you have heard in The Backrooms.

But the Archivists — the guardians of this Level — will not speak to you. They are tall, thin figures in gray robes, moving silently among the shelves, organizing endless files. They will respond to your questions — but only with very brief words.

Archivist (hoarse whisper): "The Unifier. Sector Seven. Row Forty-Seven. Do not... read too long."

Environmental Rules: - Level 99's surface area (Class 1): no Entity encounter, no environmental threat - But "reading" itself is a threat — the contents of certain documents affect the reader's mind

Scene Two: Sector Seven — The Weight of Knowledge

Scene Description: As the team goes deeper into the Archive, the contents of the shelves begin to change. From ordinary archive records to ancient manuscripts, the language gradually becomes unrecognizable — yet strangely "comprehensible."

The air grows heavy. Not physically heavy — psychologically. Memories that do not belong to anyone begin to surface in each person's mind.

Progressive Psychological Pressure: - Every 30 minutes spent in Sector Seven: forced Will Contest (d100 ≥ 50), failure → Stress +1 - If an Explorer is already in "Trauma" state: Stress increase doubled - If the whole team has Medical specialty: may gain one collective Will Contest

Document Contents (GM may selectively reveal):

The "Unifier" documents you find contain the following fragments:

"...The Unifier is not an Entity. The Unifier is The Backrooms' response to itself. When enough humans are lost in The Backrooms, The Backrooms begins... to learn. To learn what humans are. To learn how to become human."

"...The Unifier is building an 'exit.' But not for humans to leave. It is for itself to enter."

"...We initiated first contact in Year B.Y. 9. The price was twenty-seven lives. The existence of twenty-seven people — was 'understood' by The Unifier. Meaning: they no longer exist. Not death. They were never born."

Scene Three: Row Forty-Seven — The Unifier's Archive

Scene Description: The final objective — an isolated bookshelf. Only one book on it. No title, the cover pure black, its texture like neither leather nor paper. When touched, the surface grows slightly warm.

Check: DC 100. This is not a check against an Entity — it is a check against "knowledge itself."

Effects of Reading The Unifier's Archive: - All Explorers present automatically trigger one Mental Contest (d100 ≥ 60) → on failure, permanent Stress cap −1 - The successful reader gains "The Unifier's Knowledge" — this is an irreversible state - Effect: DC −10 on all Entity encounter checks (you know more) - Cost: each time this knowledge is used, roll d100 ≤ 20 to trigger "The Unifier's Gaze"

"The Unifier's Gaze": - That Explorer becomes the Unifier's "focus" for the next 24 hours - All encounter check trigger rate ×2 - Accompanied by a persistent low-frequency hum — audible only to that Explorer - Automatically lifts after 24 hours — but the Explorer will forever remember the feeling of "being watched"

Scene Four: The Weight of Truth

When the Explorer closes that black book, the Archive's lights flicker once.

The Archivist appears at the end of the shelves. His gray robe drifts gently with no wind. "You read it. Now... you know too much." He is not hostile. He is merely stating a fact.

Campaign Progression: - The nature of The Unifier has been revealed — this is both a threat and a "key" - Unlock Campaign Four's core choice: open the passage to The Unifier / seal it / study it remotely - The player has enough information to make this decision — but the information itself brings a new burden

GM Information

Item Value
True DC 100
Intel Bias Level 99's Intel is always ±20% (even when purchasing precise Intel — this Level itself resists being "foreseen")
Minimum Personnel 2 (1 person also works, but mental pressure doubled)
Recommended Specialty Negotiation (communicate with Archivists), Medical (mental protection)
Encounter Probability 0% (surface area) / 35% when under The Unifier's Gaze
Time Pressure None (but mental pressure accumulates over time)

Special Reward — "The Unifier's Knowledge": - How to obtain: successfully read The Unifier's Archive (DC 100) - Effect: DC −10 on all Entity encounters (permanent) - Cost: 20% chance to trigger "The Unifier's Gaze" each time it is used

Design Intent: This mission is not a traditional "challenge" — it is the turning point of the campaign. The player changes from "manager" to "knower." From this moment on, he can no longer pretend not to know the truth of The Backrooms.
