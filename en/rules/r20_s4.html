Scenario S4: The Defector

Applicable Mode: Solo (Lone Wolf mode, 4 Action Points (AP) per round) / Multiplayer (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–5 players, 3 AP each) Difficulty: Hard Recommended Level: 4–7 Estimated Duration: 4–6 hours Mission Type: Decapitation Strike + POW Rescue (mixed) Player Count: 1–6

Mission Briefing

A senior Alliance officer, Colonel Victor Kane, contacted the Concord via an encrypted channel — he is willing to defect, offering the Alliance's complete defensive deployment in the star sector. But he has one condition: you must board his ship and extract him alive.

His loyal guard has grown suspicious of him and is hunting him aboard the ship. Time is short — Colonel Kane is being rounded up, and once the Alliance command confirms his defection, they will immediately initiate the self-destruct sequence.

Enemy Vessel Data

  • Vessel Name: Tyrant's Fist (S4)
  • Vessel Type: Alliance Command Vessel
  • Affiliation: Free Star Alliance (FSA)
  • Scale: Large (600 m length) / 4 decks
  • Gravity: Standard Gravity (1G); the defector may remotely manipulate the gravity system to assist the players
  • Self-Destruct System: Yes (10-round countdown)

Advance Flow

Turning Point 1: Finding Kane

Colonel Kane's last communication came from the escape pod area near the bridge. The players must cross the loyal guard's blockade line to find him. Kane's status is random: 1d6 = 1-2 lightly wounded, 3-4 currently engaged (1 marine hunting him), 5-6 already captured (being escorted to the bridge).

Turning Point 2: Moral Choice — The Database

During withdrawal, Kane points out that the Alliance's complete tactical database is in the adjacent room. Downloading takes 3 rounds — but the loyal guard is closing in. Download the data? Or abandon the data to ensure Kane's safety?

Turning Point 3: Double Betrayal

30% chance (GM hidden roll) — Kane's defection is a trap. He is actually a decoy for Alliance Intelligence, carrying a tracker. When the players arrive at the extraction point with him, an Alliance stealth assault vessel appears to intercept.

Key Encounters

E1 Blockade Line Before the Bridge

  • Enemies: Marine × 3 (CR 3) + Commander (Boss, Kane's Lieutenant) × 1 (CR 4)
  • EL: 13
  • Notes: The lieutenant commander will shout demanding the players hand over Kane; a tactical command contest (DC 18) may shake the defenders' morale.

E2 Escape Pod Area

  • Enemies: Marine × 2 (CR 3)
  • EL: 6
  • Notes: The loyal guard hunting Kane; Kane may already be wounded.

E3 Extraction Point — Hangar

  • Enemies: Defense Robot × 2 (CR 4)
  • EL: 8
  • Notes: If the trap triggers, 3 additional marines board from the stealth assault vessel.

Key NPC: Colonel Victor Kane

  • Hit Points (HP): 12 Armor Integrity (ARMR): 4
  • Weapon: Pistol (1d6)
  • Abilities: Tactical Intelligence (can reveal the enemy deployment on the next deck, similar to a Scout's tactical reconnaissance but requires no check); Gravity Manipulation (this mission only, 2 uses per mission, can remotely manipulate the gravity of one area)
  • Escort Rules: Kane can move 6 squares per round; if he falls grievously wounded, a player must drag him (costs 3 AP, moves 3 squares).

Moral Choices

  • MC1 Database: Download the data (XP +2, Reputation +2, but self-destruct countdown −1d4 rounds, additional EL 5 encounter) vs abandon the data (withdrawal route stays clear; Kane shows respect for the players)
  • MC2 Kane's Shortcut: Take Kane's suggested shortcut, 50% chance of a fast route (advance accelerated by 2 rounds), 50% chance of an ambush (EL 8).

Rewards

  • Experience Points (XP): Primary Objective 14 / Secondary Objective 8 / Full Survival 3 / GM Generic 6
  • Reputation: 3 (Kane survives and data downloaded, additional +2)
  • Special Loot: Alliance Senior Officer's Scepter (Kane's Scepter) — an encrypted data storage. Effect: permanently −2 to DC when breaching Alliance systems.

Special Rules

  • Hunt Level: The GM secretly tracks a "Hunt Level" (1–5). Each time Kane is spotted, the players trigger an alarm, or time passes, the level +1; +1 per advance phase. Level 5 → the commander personally leads an elite guard in pursuit (EL 10+).
  • Kane's Shifting Loyalty: The GM may secretly set "Loyalty" (1–10). How the players treat him affects this value — threaten (−1d4), protect (+1d4), respect (+2). Below 3 → Kane may betray at a critical moment; above 7 → he proactively offers extra intelligence.
