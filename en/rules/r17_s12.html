Scenario S2: Underground Lone Wolf [Level (Lv.) 2]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 2 × 1 Player + 1 Companion | Duration: 2-3 hours | Map: Ash Mall Underground Parking Garage

Commission

The Blacksmith received intel: deep in the underground parking garage there is a military supply vehicle, but he does not want to send too many people—"Last time I sent four; two came back." Reward: 1,800 ₽ + keep any weapons found. Time limit: 90 minutes.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry—Underground Parking Garage Entrance

Companion Scout warns: "Mutant Rat droppings—fresh. At least 3. We can take the maintenance passage on the left to go around them." - Option A: Sneak past (Stealth Target Number (TN) 11, +2 solo advantage → Target Number (TN) 9) - Option B: Clear Mutant Rats ×3 (Threat Value 3)

Scene 2: Maintenance Passage

Narrow space (only one person can pass). If the Companion is a Technician, they can spot an old map on the passage wall—marked with the supply vehicle's exact location (saves search time). - Mutant Rat ×1 (deep in the passage, ambush) - Perception Target Number (TN) 11: There is light at the end of the passage—an Enhanced Residual near the supply vehicle

Scene 3: Supply Vehicle Area

Enhanced Residual (former soldier) ×1 + Residual ×1. - Solo Tactic Suggestion: Have the Companion draw attention (suppressing fire) while the player flanks - Inside the supply vehicle: Military surplus pistol ×1 (1d8) + possible military SMG (d6 ≥5 = Blue SMG, ≤4 = ammunition) - Inside is also a soldier's corpse—in the pocket, a letter to his family (narrative item)

Scene 4: Extraction

  • Route A: Return the way you came (safe but time-consuming)
  • Route B: East Parking Lot (fixed/flare gun/1d3 Residuals)
  • Route C: Discover a stairwell to the surface (Perception Target Number (TN) 14 → unlock hidden extraction route, permanently recorded)

Enemies in This Scenario

Reference: Scenario Two (Secrets of the Underground Parking Garage)'s Mutant Rats ×3 (Threat Value 1×3) + Enhanced Residual ×1 (Threat Value 5, GM Rulebook §3.1 variant).

Solo Adjustment

  • Too Hard: Enhanced Residual replaced by ordinary Residuals ×2
  • Too Easy: Someone got to the supply vehicle first—encounter Scavengers ×2 (carrying supplies; can negotiate a split)
