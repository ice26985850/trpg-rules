Scenario S1: Maiden Voyage

Applicable Mode: Solo (Lone Wolf mode, 4 Action Points (AP) per round) / Multiplayer (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–5 players, 3 AP each) Difficulty: Tutorial Recommended Level: 1 Estimated Duration: 2–3 hours Mission Type: Data Raid Player Count: 1–6

Mission Briefing

The Intelligence Division of the Concord United Fleet (CUF) intercepted anomalous communications from a Free Star Alliance (FSA) patrol vessel, the Border Eye, in the border star system — the vessel appears to be conducting some kind of reconnaissance mission, carrying the latest data on Alliance fleet deployments.

The Mothership Arbitrator dispatches a rookie squad on their first real boarding assault: board, extract the tactical data, and withdraw safely. This is a mission designed for "controlled difficulty" — the Border Eye has no self-destruct system, and its defenders are mostly poorly trained crewmen rather than marines.

Enemy Vessel Data

  • Vessel Name: Border Eye (S1)
  • Vessel Type: Human Patrol Vessel
  • Affiliation: Free Star Alliance (FSA)
  • Scale: Small (85 m length) / 2 decks
  • Gravity: Standard Gravity (1G), uniform throughout
  • Self-Destruct System: None

Advance Flow

Turning Point 1: Breach Point Selection

The players may breach from the Cargo Hold (safe but farthest from the data center) or the Hull near the Bridge (fast but high risk). - Cargo Hold Breach: Starting point is far from the objective but lightly defended, suited to a stealth approach. - Bridge-Side Breach: Close to the objective, but meets 2 Guards in the first round.

Turning Point 2: Guards Between Decks

The main staircase to the second deck is patrolled by 2 Guards. - Frontal Engagement: Triggers an EL 3 encounter (2 Guards); continue advancing after winning. - Maintenance Conduit Detour: No combat, but requires an Engineering (ENG) check (DC 10) to open the maintenance conduit entrance.

Turning Point 3: Last Line at the Data Center

Outside the data center door are 1 automated turret and 1 marine (the squad's only elite enemy). - Tactical Breach: Triggers an EL 5 encounter; may directly use a system breach check (DC 15) to turn the turret to your side. - Feint to Lure: Use flashbangs or smoke to divert the turret's attention; Mobility (MOB) check (DC 13) to circle to its rear dead angle.

Key Encounters

E1 Cargo Hold (Node 2)

  • Enemies: Crewman (Mook) × 2 (CR 0.5)
  • EL: 1
  • Notes: Poorly trained defenders, tend to flee or surrender rather than fight to the death.

E2 Main Corridor Staircase (Node 4, Optional Encounter)

  • Enemies: Guard (Standard) × 2 (CR 2)
  • EL: 4
  • Notes: May be bypassed via the maintenance conduit to avoid combat.

E3 Data Center Entrance (Node 7)

  • Enemies: Auto-Turret (Standard) × 1 (CR 2) + Marine (Elite) × 1 (CR 3)
  • EL: 5
  • Notes: Last line of defense. The commander-type foe may shout, trying to intimidate the players into retreating.

Rewards

  • Experience Points (XP): Primary Objective 8 / Secondary Objective 3 / Full Survival 3 / GM Generic 6
  • Reputation: 1 (if completed without triggering the alarm, additional +1)
  • Special Loot: Alliance Patrol Vessel Tactical Chart — marks all Alliance patrol routes in the star sector. Effect: Reputation +1; Initiative (INIT) +1 when facing Alliance vessels in the future.

Special Rules

  • Tutorial Pacing: The GM should pause to explain in detail each time a new mechanic appears — the first firearm check, the first breach check, and the first gravity change (if any) should all be explained step by step.
  • No Self-Destruct: After completing the objective the players may withdraw at leisure; but once the alarm is triggered, enemy reinforcements will gradually increase.
  • Failure Buffer: Even if the data is not extracted, the mothership will still recover the squad (this is a training mission), but failure yields no XP or Reputation.
