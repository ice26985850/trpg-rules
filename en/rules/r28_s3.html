Depths of the Erosion Zone (S3)

Season: Season 1 "Awakening Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.7-14 - Difficulty: Normal - Expected Length: 2-3 sessions (8-12 hours) - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end of the document.

Core Conflict

The Guild detects a rapidly expanding D-Rank Rift; the players are dispatched inside to scout and seal it. Inside the rift are otherworld materials—but there is more.

Main Locations

  • Inside the D-Rank Rift: A small maze (5 zones) shrouded in otherworld law: Entrance Hall, Mycelial Corridor, Ruined Plaza, Antechamber to the Core, Rift Core.
  • Mycelial Corridor: A passage coated with glowing fungi, hiding otherworld minerals and lurking monsters.

Key NPCs

  • Sato (D-Rank Awakener), a companion: A veteran Awakener dispatched by the Guild, injured inside the rift and in need of rescue by the players.

Key Scenes

  • Crossing the mycelial forest—collecting otherworld materials (can be crafted into Green · Uncommon equipment)
  • Discovering the remains of a previous survey team—tension rises
  • Boss battle in the antechamber—first true D-Rank Boss
  • Strange phenomena after the rift closes—a hint from the Collector

Enemy List

  • Acid Slug (Quantity 4-6 | Lv.5-7, appears in groups)
  • Mycelial Parasite (Quantity 2-3 | Lv.8, control-type special ability)
  • Rift Aggregator (Quantity 1 | D-Rank Boss, Lv.10)

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 800, D-Rank Armor Crate (Green · Uncommon) ×1, Otherworld Crystal ×3 (can be crafted into Blue · Rare equipment), Guild Reputation +100

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Slugs halved (2-3), Parasites reduced to 1, Aggregator Hit Points (HP) −25% and damage −15%
