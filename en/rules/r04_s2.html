Frozen Wasteland TRPG — Solo Campaign Scenario: Lone Wolf's Path

For: 1 Player + 1 GM + 1 NPC Companion Campaign Length: 9 Sessions (Novice → Legendary, covering a complete growth arc) Rules Referenced: This scenario uses the NPC Companion system, solo combat scaling, and solo rescue alternatives from the Solo Mode Rules Supplement v1.0. Freeze Zones Referenced: Uses the Freeze Zones from the Sample Freeze Zone Expansion (noted per session).

Campaign Overview

Core Concept

You are a lone Cold Walker — not because you chose solitude, but because your team was wiped out in a Spasm Type Thaw. You survived, but at the cost of a guilt you can never shake. Now you have received a commission from the Lakeheart City Cold Walker Guild: venture deep into the Freeze Zones to find a key figure said to be "still alive" — a pre-Freeze scientist who may hold the truth behind the Freeze.

You must find a companion you can trust, traverse Freeze Zones ranging from Slumber Type to Spasm Type, confront your own Trauma in solitude, and make choices no one else can share the burden of.

Emotional Threads

  • Trauma: "Losing your teammates" — You survived; the others did not. Every decision carries their weight.
  • Trust: Can you trust a companion again? When you begin to trust, will you lose them once more?
  • "The One You're Still Searching For": That scientist — not merely the objective of the commission. His research may point toward a far greater truth.

Campaign Structure

Act Sessions Titles Difficulty Freeze Zone Core Theme
Act I: Scars 1–3 Start, Echo, Trust Novice Frostfall Supermarket → Frostbell Courtyard → Dawnlight Elementary Learning to walk alone, accepting a companion
Act II: Deeper 4–6 Silence, Pulse, Betrayal Intermediate St. Mary's Hospital → Permafrost Power Plant → Frosttide Harbor Skill growth, tests of trust
Act III: Truth 7–9 Ironfrost, Abyss, Choice High → Legendary Ironfrost Outpost → Time Laboratory → Genesis Corridor Confronting Trauma, the final choice

Act I: Scars

Session 1: Beneath the Frostfall

Suggested Play Count: Session 1 (first session after character creation) Difficulty: Novice Freeze Zone: Frostfall Supermarket (Slumber Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 01) Estimated Time: 2–3 hours

Briefing: Three days ago, your team was caught in a sudden Thaw within a Spasm Type Freeze Zone. You are the sole survivor. Today, a contact from the Lakeheart City Cold Walker Guild found you — they need you to confirm an intelligence report: whether there truly exists a lead pointing to "Dr. Orlov" the scientist, deep within the walk-in refrigerator of the Frostfall Supermarket. The payment is 2 Crystals + one free week of lodging. You have no reason to refuse — or rather, you lack the strength to refuse.

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Search the Frostfall Supermarket walk-in refrigerator and find a lead related to Dr. Orlov (a frozen cash-register record or a refrigerated shipping manifest) - Optional Objective: Bring back at least 3 days' worth of supplies (rations/medicine)

Scene Flow:

  1. Guild Office (Opening, 10 min) The contact Mira hands you the mission briefing and a spare key. She mentions: "If you need a hand… Old Tan says there's a newcomer looking for him. A girl named Lena. She's been waiting at the Cold Walker School for three days."
  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 10 to coax more of Lena's background from Mira (she is a former medical student whose mother is Frozen somewhere).
  3. Decision Point: Go meet Lena? (→ 2a) Or set out alone? (→ 2b)

2a. Cold Walker School (if you choose to meet Lena) Lena (age 26, former medical student) is waiting outside Old Tan's classroom. She speaks quickly, her fingers constantly twisting the hem of her coat. She needs Crystals to hire a guide to find her mother — but what you need is a helper. She is willing to go with you to the supermarket in exchange for 1 Crystal. - Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 12 to persuade her to accept lower pay (taking only a share of the supplies). - Companion: Lena joins the party. Trait: Medical Training. Attributes: Speed (SPD) 3, Perception (PER) 4, Guts (GUT) 3, Tech (TEC) 5, Negotiation (NEG) 3.

2b. Setting Out Alone (if you choose to go solo) At the supermarket entrance you meet a lingering scavenger — a gaunt middle-aged man who calls himself "Rusty Nail." He has no gear but knows the supermarket's layout. He proposes to lead the way in exchange for 20 Scrap Coins and one ration. - Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 10 to notice his hands trembling — he may be hiding a danger (frost-mist clumps in the refrigerator). - Companion: Rusty Nail joins the party. Trait: Keen Senses. Attributes: Speed (SPD) 4, Perception (PER) 5, Guts (GUT) 2, Tech (TEC) 2, Negotiation (NEG) 2.

  1. Supermarket Entrance (15 min) The supermarket's automatic doors are stuck halfway open. Your flashlight beam cuts inside; the shelves stand in the darkness like tombstones. The silence of the Freeze Zone hits you head-on.
  2. First-time Freeze Zone rules tutorial: the Thaw Die mechanic, Premonition Sense.

  3. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 8 to detect the faint time-gradient band at the entrance — the boundary is closer than it appears.

  4. Dry Goods Section Exploration (30 min) Cans and dried food are scattered across the shelves. Most have been taken by earlier Cold Walkers — but a careful search still yields rewards.

  5. Key Encounter: Discover the first frost-mist clump — a tutorial fight. Use solo scaling (Health (HP) reduced to 10).
  6. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 10 to search the shelves, gaining 1d4 rations. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to find a box of first-aid bandages hidden behind a toppled shelf.

  7. Loot: 1d4+2 rations, first-aid bandages ×2, 2d10 Scrap Coins.

  8. Walk-in Refrigerator (Climax, 40 min) The freezer door is half-open. It is far colder inside than the rest of the supermarket — your breath instantly condenses into white mist. At the deepest point of the refrigerator, a Frozen deliveryman lies slumped beside a cargo cart. In his hand he grips a shipping manifest.

  9. Environmental Hazard: The refrigerator is moderately cold (Guts (GUT) Difficulty (DC) 12 every 5 rounds, see Rulebook §1.8.1).
  10. Minor Encounter: If the companion is Rusty Nail, he stops at the refrigerator door — "I'm not going in. Something in there… isn't right." Requires Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 12 to persuade him to follow.
  11. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to spot the shipping manifest in the deliveryman's hand. Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 10 to decode the delivery address on the manifest — pointing to "St. Mary's Hospital, Attn: Dr. Orlov."
  12. Ethical Choice: The deliveryman's body begins to show signs of Thaw — the sound of ice crystals fracturing. He is a fully Frozen One. You have only 3 rounds to decide: risk thawing him (spending a Defrost Agent) for more information, or take the manifest and go.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Thaw the Deliveryman: Spend 1 Defrost Agent. The deliveryman wakes in deep confusion. He can tell you "Dr. Orlov was searching for a certain frequency — the frequency of time," but he needs you to bring him back to the safe zone. This means dragging a fully Frozen One across half the supermarket. Use the solo-drag rules (Speed (SPD) Difficulty (DC) 12, 3 rounds, Movement −5). - Option B — Take the Manifest and Leave: Quick and safe. But you will never know what he could have told you. Gain the "Stranger You Failed to Save" Trauma trigger (narrative layer only, no mechanical penalty — but record it).

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 0–1 (looting the refrigerator cargo crate) - Milestone: Survival Milestone (completing your first plunder) → Attribute +1 - Story Lead: The manifest points to St. Mary's Hospital + (if thawed) the "frequency of time" mystery lead

Ending Branches: - If you thawed the deliveryman and brought him to the safe zone → he becomes an NPC in Lakeheart City, able to provide internal hospital intel later - If you did not thaw → Guild contact Mira provides another source of intel — an old man in Frostbell Courtyard

Session 2: The Silent Bell

Suggested Play Count: Session 2 Difficulty: Novice Freeze Zone: Frostbell Courtyard (Slumber Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 02) Estimated Time: 3–4 hours

Briefing: The shipping manifest points to St. Mary's Hospital, but the hospital is a Pulse Type Freeze Zone — you need more preparation. Mira (or the deliveryman) tells you that Frostbell Courtyard is home to an old man — he refused to evacuate, preferring to remain in his home at the edge of the Freeze Zone. He is said to have worked at the hospital and knows its interior layout. Find him, get the hospital map.

But now you travel with a companion. The last mission made you realize — alone is not enough. The question is: can you trust this person?

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Find the old man's residence (Unit C-35) and obtain the St. Mary's Hospital interior map or verbal information - Optional Objective: Bring back the remaining medicine from the community clinic

Scene Flow:

  1. Community Entrance (10 min) The streets of Frostbell Courtyard are quiet as a painting — clothes hung on balconies frozen mid-flutter in the wind, a cat Frozen mid-pounce after a falling leaf. This is frozen everyday life.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 10 to notice the faint blue glow of the central fountain in the community park — a time-stable point.

  3. Community Clinic (25 min) The clinic's glass door is shattered halfway. In the waiting room two Frozen patients sit in chairs, a nurse frozen mid-handoff of a medicine bottle. The pharmacy is at the back of the clinic.

  4. Key Encounter: A Frozen pet (cat) in the clinic corridor — use "Frost Creature" statistics, Health (HP) reduced to 12.
  5. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 12 to pry open the pharmacy lock. Find 1d4 medical kits and 1 Defrost Agent.

  6. Loot: Medical kits ×1d4, Defrost Agent ×1 (50% chance).

  7. Unit C-35 (Core Scene, 50 min) A two-story little house. The door is unlocked. In the living room sits an old man — not Frozen. He sits on the sofa, a blanket over his lap, a radio playing static.

  8. The old man's name is Isaac. He is 78, a former night-shift janitor at St. Mary's Hospital. When the Freeze came he happened to be home. He refuses to leave because he is waiting for his wife — she was on shift at the hospital that day.
  9. Key Encounter: Isaac's mental state is unstable. Sometimes he is lucid (able to describe the hospital layout in detail), sometimes he slips into hallucinations ("I hear her calling me — from the basement").
  10. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 14 to obtain complete hospital map information while the old man is lucid. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to find a family photo on his coffee table — his wife in a nurse's uniform, the hospital emblem behind her.

  11. Ethical Choice: Isaac shows mild Time Poisoning symptoms — his body is slowly freezing. You may use a Defrost Agent to ease it (spend 1), or give him your warming pack. Or you can take the information and leave.

  12. Park Rest Stop (15 min) If your companion is injured or you have accumulated stress, the community park fountain provides a safe rest point — clears all Cold Fatigue.

  13. RP Scene: Your companion opens up here. Lena will speak of her mother. Rusty Nail will confess why he shrank back at the refrigerator door before — he once lost a partner there.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Help Isaac: Spend a Defrost Agent or provide Warming Gear. Grateful, Isaac tells you the hospital basement holds a "special storage area" — where Dr. Orlov's research data may be. He also tells you of a secret passage entering from the boiler room. - Option B — Take the Information and Leave: You get the basic hospital map, but learn nothing of the special storage area or the secret passage. Your companion watches you walk out of the house in silence.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 0–1 (clinic looting + the old man may gift one) - Milestone: Ethical Milestone (choice to help or refuse to help) → optional second Zone Experience - Story Lead: Hospital map (full or basic version) + special storage area intel (if you helped the old man)

Ending Branches: - Helped Isaac → obtain full hospital intel + secret passage. Companion's attitude toward you improves (Companion Loyalty +1, affecting Session 6). - Refused help → only the basic map. The companion may raise objections in Sessions 5–6.

Session 3: Echoes on the Blackboard

Suggested Play Count: Session 3 Difficulty: Novice → Intermediate transition Freeze Zone: Dawnlight Elementary (Pulse Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 03) Estimated Time: 3–4 hours

Briefing: With the hospital map in hand, you are one step closer to Dr. Orlov's research. But Mira stopped you before you left: people from the Silent Hand Church appeared near Frostbell Courtyard — they are searching for something. Stranger still, someone saw a chalk star chart drawn on a community wall — identical to what you might find at the school. The Church people are not after supplies. They are searching for "time-stable points."

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Find the school library, record the star chart on the gymnasium scoreboard (Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 1/3) - Optional Objective: Investigate traces of Church activity — what are they looking for?

Scene Flow:

  1. School Gate (15 min) The school gate stands half-open; the Frozen guard in the booth is frozen mid-pour of tea. The swing on the playground is held at its highest point — a child Frozen upon it. Fresh footprints on the ground — not old, recently left.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to analyze the footprints — at least 3 people, one wearing heavy boots. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 16 to find candle wax residue beside the footprints — ritual paraphernalia of the Silent Hand Church.

  3. First-Floor Classroom Area (30 min) The classrooms are like time capsules — the blackboards still bear the last lesson's writing from before the Freeze. In the third-grade classroom, an entire class is Frozen in their seats, all gazing out the window together.

  4. Key Encounter: Inside the classroom is an isolated "Time Echo" — a student's memory replayed as sound: "The teacher said the stars are wounds of time." Contacting the Time Echo requires Guts (GUT) Difficulty (DC) 12, else gain 1 Panic Value.
  5. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to find the teacher's log on the podium — inside is a draft star chart.

  6. Loot: Teacher's log (story item) + 1 medical kit can be found in the school infirmary.

  7. Gymnasium (Core Scene, 40 min) The gymnasium scoreboard flickers between 47:42 and 42:47. Time runs backward here. This is no malfunction — it is the physical manifestation of a Genesis Hypothesis fragment.

  8. Special Mechanic: The scoreboard's flickering follows the Pulse Type rhythm — every 13 flickers (about 47 seconds), it briefly displays a complete image: a panorama of the gymnasium at the Thaw Moment.
  9. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 15 to analyze the scoreboard's jumping pattern and find the correct observation timing. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 18 to spot, in the panorama, a person in the last row of the stands who does not belong to this school — a black greatcoat, watching the court. This figure is not Frozen.
  10. Key Encounter: As you begin recording the star chart, the gymnasium doors are pushed open. Not a Thaw — a living person. A monk of the Silent Hand Church walks in, sees you, and stops.

  11. Negotiation Scene: The monk calls himself Brother Silas. He tells you the Church seeks "time-stable points" to "help time rest." He does not want you to take the star chart data — but he will not attack you either. You may persuade him (Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 15) to share what he knows, threaten him (Guts (GUT) Difficulty (DC) 12), or circumvent him.

  12. Boiler Room / Underground Passage (if time allows, 20 min) On a blank page of the custodian's log is a line in pencil: "Heard the sound again last night. Coming from underground. Not mechanical. Like a heartbeat."

  13. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 14 to open the lock leading to the basement. The underground passage connects to nearby buildings — possibly a shortcut for future missions.

  14. Loot: Tool parts ×1d4, 2d10 Scrap Coins.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Cooperate with the Church: Share the star chart data with Brother Silas. He tells you an important piece of intel — "Time-stable points are not random. They form a network. Find all the nodes and, in theory, the Freeze can be shut down from within." Gain Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 1/3 + Church relation (future access to Church intel). - Option B — Refuse Cooperation: You record the star chart data alone. But Brother Silas leaves — you do not know what he will do next. Gain Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 1/3, but the Church may become a future obstacle.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 1–2 - Milestone: Tech Milestone (the technical challenge of recording the star chart) → gain a new Feat - Story Lead: Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 1/3 + black-coated figure image + (if cooperated) time-stable point network intel

Ending Branches: - Cooperated with the Church → obtain the specific locations of the time-stable point network (one of which points to the Permafrost Power Plant). The Church provides remote assistance in Session 5. - Refused cooperation → the Church becomes a future competitor. Your intel on time-stable points relies on your own observation.

Act II: Deeper

Session 4: Silence Under the Scalpel

Suggested Play Count: Session 4 Difficulty: Intermediate Freeze Zone: St. Mary's Hospital (Pulse Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 04) Estimated Time: 4–5 hours

Briefing: With the hospital map in hand and the star chart recorded, you know Dr. Orlov's research data lies in the hospital's underground special storage area. The hospital's Pulse Type Thaw means you can plan action windows — but also must be careful. Each floor has a different Thaw cycle; you must move precisely between floors. Most important — the hospital's patients. After so many years, some may still be alive.

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Enter the basement special storage area and find Dr. Orlov's research data - Optional Objective: Stabilize a Critical Frozen One (gain key intel)

Scene Flow:

  1. Hospital Lobby (15 min) The registration window is Frozen with a queued crowd. Half the ceiling fluorescent lights went dark at the Thaw Moment — the other half burn forever. You can hear a regular hum in the distance — the hospital's pulse.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 12 to decode the floor Thaw schedule posted at the registration window. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 10 to notice fresh blood on the floor — not from before the Freeze.

  3. Second-Floor ICU (40 min) Six patients lie in the ICU. Five show no sign of life — they were already Dying when Frozen. But the sixth — a middle-aged woman — her monitor still beats, agonizingly slow. A Critical Frozen One.

  4. Key Encounter: After thawing, the patient has only minutes of consciousness. She is the hospital's head nurse, and knows the nature of Dr. Orlov's experiments — "He said time is a song going out of tune. He was searching for the right note." She asks you to pass a message to her husband (if you meet him).
  5. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 10/12/12 three-round stabilization procedure (see Solo Rules Supplement §4.2, Difficulty (DC) −1). Each Crystal spent extends by 2 rounds.

  6. Ethical Choice: Stabilizing her takes time and resources — in a Pulse Type environment, every minute is a risk. And could her husband be the old man you met in Frostbell Courtyard?

  7. Operating Room Corridor (25 min) The operating room's time field is extremely unstable — all actions within this area cost an extra Minor Action. At the end of the corridor, on the floor, lies a Frozen security officer — his pistol still in its holster.

  8. Key Encounter: Mid-corridor, a ceiling fragment begins to Thaw — a small collapse (affecting a 3×3 area, 2d6 Physical, Speed (SPD) Difficulty (DC) 13 to dodge).

  9. Loot: Security pistol (1d8, 5 rounds), flashlight.

  10. Basement Special Storage Area (Climax, 50 min) Enter via the secret passage beside the boiler room (if obtained from Isaac) or the main stairwell. The storage door bears a faded label: "Orlov — Temporal Acoustics Experiment — Unauthorized Personnel Forbidden."

  11. The interior is Dr. Orlov's private lab — a storage room converted into a study. The walls are covered with handwritten equations and time-waveform diagrams. On the central table sits a device — Orlov's "Time Resonator" prototype.

  12. Key Item: Research log (Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 2/3) — records Orlov's theory: "The Freeze is not random. It follows specific frequencies — each Freeze Zone has its own time-resonance frequency. Find the resonance frequency and, in theory, the Freeze Zone's stability can be influenced from the outside."
  13. Special Encounter: The Time Resonator still holds faint energy — as you approach, you hear a low-frequency hum, similar to the scoreboard's flickering pattern. If activated (Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 18), the Resonator briefly "syncs" you with the hospital's time frequency — for the next 5 rounds you are unaffected by Pulse Type Thaw.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Stabilize the Head Nurse: Fulfill her request. You gain key intel (Orlov's musical metaphor, her husband's message). But is her husband Isaac? If you helped Isaac in Session 2, you can return to tell him — this may be the closest reunion to "The One You're Still Searching For." - Option B — Abandon Her, Head Straight to the Basement: You obtain the research data faster. But your companion falls silent for a long while afterward. If it is Lena (whose mother is Frozen), she will ask a question you cannot answer: "If that were my mother, what would you have done?"

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 2–3 - Milestone: Survival Milestone → Attribute +1 - Story Lead: Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 2/3 + Time Resonator prototype (optional to take) + the head nurse's message

Ending Branches: - If head nurse stabilized + helped Isaac → you can arrange their reunion. This is a major emotional moment — the GM should let the scene unfold slowly and with weight. - If not stabilized → the research data is your only gain. You feel the weight of "the Stranger You Failed to Save" accumulating.

Session 5: The Eternal Pulse

Suggested Play Count: Session 5 Difficulty: Intermediate → High Difficulty transition Freeze Zone: Permafrost Power Plant (Breath Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 06) Estimated Time: 4–5 hours

Briefing: Orlov's research points to a startling possibility — time can be "retuned." But your next lead lies at the lighthouse in Frosttide Harbor — a time-stable point overlooking the ocean. Before setting out, you decide to go to the Permafrost Power Plant. Two reasons: first, the power plant itself is a time-stable point and may relate to the star chart you recorded. Second, you need more Time Crystals — and the plant's control room is said to hold a considerable reserve.

If your companion's loyalty is high, they will proactively offer to help plan this mission. If loyalty is low, they will begin to question — "We've gathered enough money by now, right? Why keep risking it?"

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Obtain the Time Crystal reserve from the control room + confirm whether the power plant is a node of the time-stable point network - Optional Objective: Shut down the spreading Freeze (prevent surrounding areas from being swallowed)

Scene Flow:

  1. Plant Perimeter (15 min) The plant's cooling towers emit a faint blue glow. The entire plant is in the Breath Type "exhale" phase — time near normal flow. But you know this will not last.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to notice the anomalous growth patterns of plants around the plant — over the past years they have adapted to the Breath Type time rhythm.

  3. Control Room (Core Scene, 50 min) The control room is the plant's neural hub. The monitoring screens are still running — the power plant's heartbeat never stopped. An engineer is Frozen before the console, hands hovering above the keyboard. A certain link exists between him and the plant — his consciousness seems to have "merged" into the plant's time loop.

  4. Special Mechanic: The engineer is a Partial Frozen One — his consciousness is trapped in the plant's time loop, but his body can move within limits (eyes, lips).
  5. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 14 to read the time-stable point network diagram on the monitoring screens — the plant is indeed a node in the network. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 18 to find the message the engineer left in the notebook beneath the keyboard.

  6. Key Choice: To obtain the full Time Crystal reserve (3–5), you must shut down part of the system. But this affects the link between the engineer and the plant — no one knows what consequence it will have for him.

  7. Cooling Tower (30 min) From the top of the cooling tower you can overlook the frozen wasteland for kilometers around. If you obtained the time-stable point network intel in Session 3, you can use binoculars here to confirm the locations of at least two other nodes — including the Frosttide Harbor lighthouse.

  8. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to confirm the lighthouse's position and distance. Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 12 to mark node positions on the map.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Enforce Forced Cooling: Shut down the spreading Freeze. Gain the crystal reserve. But the engineer will cry out one last word at the moment of shutdown — he has time for only one word. The GM chooses a word with far-reaching narrative impact (suggestions: "Abyss," "Beware underwater," "The lighthouse is not a node — it's an entrance," etc.). - Option B — Do Not Shut Down the System: Protect the engineer. You can only take a small number of Crystals (1–2). But the engineer tells you a number with faint lip movements — a frequency value — which he says he calculated from "the plant's pulse." This frequency may prove useful in the Time Laboratory.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 3–5 (Option A) or 1–2 (Option B) - Milestone: Relationship Milestone (companion's loyalty reaching a high or low point in Sessions 4–5) → major story development - Story Lead: Time-stable point network node map + (Option B) mysterious frequency value

Ending Branches: - Companion loyalty high + chose to protect engineer → companion expresses trust and admiration. Companion gains a second Trait (GM and player negotiate the choice). - Companion loyalty low → companion may propose parting ways in Session 6. This triggers the next session's core conflict.

Session 6: The Lamp of the Frozen Sea

Suggested Play Count: Session 6 Difficulty: High Difficulty Freeze Zone: Frosttide Harbor (Chain Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 08) Estimated Time: 5–6 hours

Briefing: The Frosttide Harbor lighthouse. According to your map, this is a time-stable point — and a possible source of Genesis Hypothesis leads. But Frosttide Harbor is a Chain Type Freeze Zone — one Thaw may trigger a domino effect across the entire harbor. And you are not the only one searching for the lighthouse. The Collector's agents, the Church's monks, even other Cold Walkers — all have heard "there is something" in the lighthouse. This is a race against time and a competition with other factions.

If your companion's loyalty is low, they will propose leaving here — "This isn't the kind of job I signed up for."

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Reach the top of the lighthouse and speak with the lighthouse keeper (obtain Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 3/3 or an alternative lead) - Optional Objective: Reach the lighthouse before the competitors (affects the completeness of information obtained)

Scene Flow:

  1. Harbor Entrance (20 min) Frozen waves stand like blue-gray glass walls on either side of the harbor. Fishing boats are frozen at the moment of departure. On the dock you can see others' footprints — at least two different groups have already entered.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to identify the footprints — one set is the Church's ritual boots (you saw Brother Silas's), another is light Cold Walker boots. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 18 to find a third set of footprints — barefoot, very fresh — which makes no sense.

  3. Warehouse District (30 min) The warehouse is stacked with Frozen cargo. A Collector's agent is searching the same warehouse. He calls himself Valen — a well-dressed middle-aged man. He proposes a trade: his employer wants only "one thing" — the nautical log inside the lighthouse. Everything else is yours.

  4. Negotiation Options: You may accept the trade (gain warehouse looting rights + extra payment), refuse (Valen may cause trouble when you enter the lighthouse), or counter-propose (Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 16 to have him provide interior lighthouse intel in exchange).

  5. Loot: Warehouse supplies — 2d4 rations, 3d10 Scrap Coins, rope ×1.

  6. Lighthouse Base (25 min) The lighthouse's iron door stands ajar. Inside is pitch black. The lighthouse's spiral staircase climbs to the top. The air inside is colder than outside — the lighthouse itself is a time-stable point, but a stable point sometimes means "time operates here in a different way."

  7. Key Encounter: On the third landing, a Church monk lies across the stairs — not attacked, but Frozen. He is a member of the Silent Hand Church, recoiled upon trying to "touch the time-stable point." His notes read: "The lighthouse keeper — he is not Frozen. He refused. He made a bargain with time."

  8. Lighthouse Top (Climax, 50 min) The lighthouse keeper — an old sailor past 70. He is not Frozen. He sits by the window, gazing at the dark ocean. As you enter, he slowly turns his head.

  9. "I've been waiting for you." He does not look like he has waited days — his eyes carry the weight of decades of waiting.
  10. Core Dialogue: The keeper tells you the lighthouse's truth: it is not merely a time-stable point — it is physical proof of the Genesis Hypothesis. The lighthouse is built upon a "time-resonance point" — when the Freeze came, this point became one of the "origins" of the Freeze network. The keeper chose to remain here because "someone must remember."
  11. Hint: The keeper will tell you Dr. Orlov came here — in the week before the Freeze. He measured the lighthouse's frequency. If Genesis Hypotheses A/C are true, the keeper will say he has seen "a black tower rising from the seabed" — evidence for Hypothesis B.
  12. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 16 to have the keeper share his complete memories (gain the full Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 3/3). Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to find Orlov's handwritten notes on his desk — recording a set of frequency values and a location: "Time Laboratory, 7th Floor."
  13. Companion Crisis (story trigger): If companion loyalty is low, they will announce departure at the lighthouse base — "Sorry, I can't do this. It's too much." This triggers an emotional scene: will you hold them back or let them go? If companion loyalty is high, they will make a surprising choice at the lighthouse top — e.g., proactively offering to watch the lighthouse so the keeper can leave to rest in the safe zone.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Hold the Companion Back (when loyalty is low): Negotiation (NEG) Difficulty (DC) 18 to persuade. Success means the companion stays, gaining a overcoming of the "Betrayal" Trauma (or similar emotional breakthrough). Failure means the companion leaves — you lose the companion but gain an important realization: you finally understand why your former teammates made certain choices at the last moment. - Option B — Respect Their Choice: Let the companion leave. You complete the mission alone. Either way, you gain progress toward overcoming the "Losing Your Teammates" Trauma — you finally understand that losing them was not your fault.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 3–5 (the keeper may have reserves) - Milestone: Trauma Milestone (overcoming the "Losing Your Teammates" Trauma) → Trauma turns into a Mark - Story Lead: Genesis Hypothesis Fragment 3/3 + Time Laboratory 7th Floor location + Orlov's frequency value

Ending Branches: - Obtaining the complete Genesis Hypothesis fragments → your understanding of the Freeze reaches its peak. You can make wiser choices in Sessions 7–9. - Companion leaves → you begin Session 7 alone. But if you previously saved the deliveryman or the head nurse, one of them may be willing to become your new companion. - Companion stays and loyalty is high → the companion gains player-equivalent attribute growth in Sessions 7–9 ("We grew strong together").

Act III: Truth

Session 7: The Voice of Ironfrost

Suggested Play Count: Session 7 Difficulty: High Difficulty Freeze Zone: Ironfrost Outpost (Spasm Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 07) Estimated Time: 5–6 hours

Briefing: The three fragments of the Genesis Hypothesis are assembled. You now know the possible nature of the Freeze — a quantum anomaly, a supernatural intrusion, or time's own immune response. But whatever the truth, Dr. Orlov found the final lead, stored at the Ironfrost Outpost — a military communications station. In the last few hours before the Freeze, Orlov sent a message from there. This message may point to a safe path to the Time Laboratory's 7th Floor. Without it, entering the 7th Floor is nearly suicide.

The Ironfrost Outpost is Spasm Type — Thaws are random, lethal, unpredictable. You will have no time to think. You must go in fast and out fast.

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Obtain Dr. Orlov's final message from the communications room - Optional Objective: Obtain the outpost's weapons reserve (preparation for the finale)

Scene Flow:

  1. Outpost Perimeter (15 min) The military outpost is surrounded by barbed wire. A faded warning sign on the gate reads: "Military Zone — Trespassers Will Be Shot." Frozen raindrops hang on the wire — thousands of ice beads suspended in midair.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to find a cut in the barbed wire — someone entered before you. The footprints are fresh.

  3. Barracks (25 min) In the soldiers' barracks, the bunks are as neat as if never used. But under one bed you find a private diary — a letter a soldier wrote to his family. The last page holds only one line: "They said this isn't a drill. That scientist came — Orlov. He said he knew how to stop it. But no one listened."

  4. Key Encounter: In the barracks is a Frozen soldier — his rifle aimed at the ceiling. Extremely high Thaw risk (Spasm Type — may Thaw at any moment). If you choose to approach him, you need Speed (SPD) Difficulty (DC) 14 to avoid the line of fire at his Thaw Moment.

  5. Loot: Military rations ×3, pistol ammo ×10, first-aid kit ×1.

  6. Communications Room (Core Scene, 50 min) The communications room is the outpost's neural hub. The radio equipment is still running — some channel still sending a repeating signal. Orlov's final message is stored on the main console's hard drive.

  7. Special Mechanic: The communications room is under severe time interference — radio signals interweave with temporal anomalies. As you try to read the message, you may see images that do not belong to this timeline.
  8. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 16 to access the encrypted hard drive. Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 18 to decode the full message. Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to filter out the noise produced by time interference.

  9. Orlov's Final Message (read aloud by GM): > "This is Orlov. If anyone receives this message — the 7th Floor entrance is not inside the building. It is at the intersection of frequencies. Frequency 47.3Hz — it resonates with the lighthouse. Use the Resonator. Open the door. Trust no one who tells you the Freeze is a curse — but trust no one who tells you it is a gift, either. It is —" [noise] "— it is of our own making. The Themis Array. I was part of it. Forgive me."

  10. Outpost Fortress (30 min) At the top of the fortress is a lookout platform. From here you can overlook the wasteland leading to the Time Laboratory. If you have binoculars, you can scout threats along the path in advance.

  11. Key Encounter: On the lookout platform is a Frozen sniper — his rifle aimed at a distant road. If he Thaws (random Spasm Type trigger), he will complete the shot — hitting the first moving target on the path. You must disarm or redirect the rifle before he Thaws.
  12. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 14 to disarm the rifle. Speed (SPD) Difficulty (DC) 12 to redirect the muzzle at the Thaw Moment.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Destroy Orlov's Confession: His message contains his role in the Themis Anomaly. Destroying this message means protecting his reputation — but also concealing part of the truth. If you stabilized the head nurse in Session 4, she may ask you why. - Option B — Preserve the Full Message: The truth is cruel — Orlov is one of the scientists who caused the Freeze. But it is also the key to stopping it. If you meet Orlov in the future (Session 9), this truth will affect your dialogue.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 2–4 - Milestone: Tech Milestone → gain a new Feat - Story Lead: Orlov's final message + 7th Floor entrance key (frequency 47.3Hz)

Session 8: The Seventh Floor

Suggested Play Count: Session 8 Difficulty: High Difficulty → Legendary transition Freeze Zone: Time Laboratory 7th Floor (Spasm Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 09) Estimated Time: 5–7 hours

Briefing: Orlov's message told you the 7th Floor entrance is "at the intersection of frequencies." Using the Time Resonator (if you took it in Session 4), tuned to 47.3Hz, you found the entrance — a Time Rift hidden behind an ordinary wall. Passing through the rift, you enter a space that should not exist.

The Seventh Floor is not a laboratory. It is a prison. Or rather, a sanctuary — depending on how you look at it.

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Find Dr. Orlov (or his legacy) - Optional Objective: Understand the true nature of the Seventh Floor

Scene Flow:

  1. Frequency Gate (15 min) Passing through the Time Rift does not feel like moving — it feels like drowning. You are swallowed by time itself for a few seconds, then emerge in the Seventh Floor's entrance hall. The walls are translucent, as if made of condensed time. You can see faint light flowing inside them — a vascular network formed by Time Crystals.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Guts (GUT) Difficulty (DC) 14 to keep calm (the shock of crossing the Time Rift). Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 12 to notice a floating Time Crystal at the center of the hall — a core Crystal, palm-sized, a dodecahedron.

  3. Corridor of History (30 min) The corridor walls display images of the distant past — the birth of the world, the origin of life, the rise of civilization. The images move, but silently. Touching the wall lets you hear the sounds of the corresponding era.

  4. Key Encounter: In the "Themis Array Activation" image, you can see Dr. Orlov — a younger him operating a console. If you preserved his full message, this image helps you understand his guilt more deeply.

  5. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Tech (TEC) Difficulty (DC) 16 to record sound samples from the walls (Orlov's experiment logs). Perception (PER) Difficulty (DC) 14 to find the specific moment the Themis Array was activated — the precise time point of the Freeze's onset.

  6. Time-Bubble Cage (Core Scene, 60 min) At the deepest point of the Seventh Floor, a spherical time bubble floats in midair. Time flows extremely slowly within the bubble — a person inside appears to move in extreme slow motion. Dr. Orlov — he is not dead. He sealed himself in the time bubble, waiting for someone to find him.

  7. Special Mechanic: Entering the time bubble costs 2 Time Crystals. Time within the bubble flows at 1/100 of normal — a few minutes inside means days have passed outside.
  8. Dr. Orlov: He is old — but not as old as imagined. Around his sixties, wearing a lab coat. When he sees you, his expression is complex — both relieved and guilty.
  9. Core Dialogue: Orlov confirms the message's contents — he was the chief scientist of the Themis Array. "We tried to observe the quantized nature of time. We succeeded — and we failed. The Freeze was not the result we wanted. It is time's self-protection — it is trying to isolate the regions 'observed' by us." (Hypothesis A) But then he adds: "Yet — I have also witnessed phenomena that cannot be explained by quantum physics. At a certain frequency, I heard echoes — echoes that do not belong to our timeline." (hinting at the possibility of Hypothesis B)

  10. Hint: Orlov tells you the Freeze can be reversed — but it requires sacrifice. Reversing the Freeze means "returning" the observed time — and returning requires an anchor. A person who voluntarily remains in time. He is already prepared.

  11. Seventh Floor Core (30 min) The Seventh Floor's core is a gigantic Time Crystal — one of the "hearts" of the entire Freeze network. If you choose to reverse part of the Freeze, you can perform the final operation here.

  12. Key Encounter: Within the core Crystal you can see the image of the Freeze network — each Freeze Zone is a node. Some nodes are brighter than others — time-stable points. The lighthouse is the brightest of them.

Key Ethical Choice: - Option A — Release Orlov: Break the time bubble. Orlov leaves the Seventh Floor with you. He becomes a key figure in Session 9 — but you also take on the responsibility of protecting him. He is a fragile old man in a cruel wasteland. - Option B — Leave Orlov in the Time Bubble: This is the atonement he chose. You gain all his research data, but will never have a face-to-face conversation. As you leave the Seventh Floor, the time bubble slowly rotates behind you.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: 5–8 (the Seventh Floor's purity is extremely high) - Milestone: Ethical Milestone → gain special experience of the Seventh Floor (energy perception of the time bubble) - Story Lead: The full truth of the Themis Array + the method to reverse the Freeze + the core Crystal (story item)

Session 9: The Full Picture of Time

Suggested Play Count: Session 9 (finale) Difficulty: Legendary Freeze Zone: Genesis Corridor (Breath Type, see Sample Freeze Zone Expansion Sample 11) Estimated Time: 6–8 hours

Briefing: You stand at the entrance of the Genesis Corridor. This is the final of finals. You know the truth of the Freeze — or at least Orlov's version of the facts. You know the method to reverse the Freeze — offer a willing anchor at the intersection of time's frequencies. But you also know the Freeze may not be a catastrophe — it may be time's immune response, an intrusion from the far shore, or both.

Whatever your choice, you will change this world. No one can tell you what is right. Only you.

Mission Objectives: - Primary Objective: Make the final choice in the Genesis Corridor - All Optional Objectives: Complete the journey of "The One You're Still Searching For," and make a final farewell with your companion (or face the finale together)

Scene Flow:

  1. Corridor Entrance (20 min) The Genesis Corridor's entrance is not like a building's door — more like a rift in time itself. It lies at the core of a deep Freeze Zone, within a gigantic Time Crystal. Entering the Corridor, you see the full picture of time — past, present, possible futures, all presented before you at once.

  2. Difficulty (DC) Suggestion: Guts (GUT) Difficulty (DC) 18 to bear the shock of the time panorama (on failure gain 1d4+2 Panic Value). If you have a companion, the companion at your side reduces Difficulty (DC) by 2.

  3. Time Fragments (30 min) The Corridor's interior is countless time fragments — each fragment is the final moment of some life. You will see your former teammates — their last instant before Freezing. You cannot change the past, but you can see their final expressions — some fearful, some peaceful. One person — is looking at you. As if they knew you would come.

  4. Emotional Scene: This is the final scene for overcoming the "Losing Your Teammates" Trauma. What will you say to them? The GM gives the player space to freely roleplay.

  5. Frequency Intersection (Core Scene, 60 min) The Corridor's center is a perfect spherical space. The sphere's inner wall projects the complete map of the Frozen Wasteland — every Freeze Zone, every time-stable point, the location of every living person. At the sphere's center, a floating platform — the Frequency Intersection.

  6. Orlov (if you brought him) will explain here: to reverse the Freeze, you must stand on the intersection, tune the Resonator to the correct frequency, then — wait. Wait for time to recalibrate. In this process, your body becomes the new time anchor. You will Freeze — perhaps for hours, perhaps years, perhaps forever. You will not die. But you will congeal within time, until the world's time finds balance again.

  7. If you came alone — you know what to do. You have the Resonator (if you took it in Session 4). You have the frequency (47.3Hz). You have the courage (or the fear, or both).

  8. Final Choice (30 min) There are five possible endings:

Ending A — Sacrifice: Become the Anchor You step onto the platform and activate the Resonator. Your body begins to solidify — from the tips of your toes, slowly spreading upward. Your last thought is: what will your teammates think? You vanish into time. The Freeze begins to slowly recede — starting from the lighthouse, spreading outward. It may take decades, but the world begins to thaw.

Ending B — Covenant: Coexist with the Far Shore You determine the Freeze is not a problem to be "solved." It is a new existence — another form of time. You use the Resonator not to reverse the Freeze, but to make a covenant with the far shore: let the safe zones stay permanently stable, let the Freeze Zones remain as they are, let humanity learn to coexist with the new time.

Ending C — Destroy: Shut Down the Themis Array You find the remnants of the Themis Array — it is still running. You shut it down. All Freeze Zones Thaw instantly — time across the entire wasteland recovers at once. Millions of Frozen people awaken. But this also means all the dangers frozen in time — exploding grenades, collapsing buildings, bullets in crossfire — all trigger simultaneously. You saved many, and you killed many.

Ending D — Preserve: Let the Freeze Continue You choose to change nothing. The Freeze is the world's status quo — humanity has already begun to adapt. Your journey let you understand the truth, but truth is not necessarily the solution. You return to the safe zone and become a mentor, teaching the next generation of Cold Walkers how to survive among the fragments of time.

Ending E — Seek: Keep Walking On You make no final choice. You hand the frequency and the Resonator to someone worthy of trust — perhaps the lighthouse keeper, perhaps the institute, perhaps your companion. Then you keep walking into the Freeze Zone. They say that far in the deepest part of the Freeze, there is another Corridor — an entrance even Orlov does not know. There may be another answer there. Or just more questions.

Loot and Rewards: - Time Crystal: meaningless (finale) - Milestone: Final Milestone → character completes the growth arc - Story Lead: the ending itself - Character Ending: every thread of "The One You're Still Searching For" converges here

Afterword (read aloud by GM): Each choice has a unique afterword — the GM narrates the world's future based on the player's ending choice. This should be a slow scene, where the GM gives the player a last quiet moment to feel the weight of this world.
