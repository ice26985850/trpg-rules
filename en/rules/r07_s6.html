Unrotted Bone — Smarter Than You (S6)

9-12 | Extreme | 3-4 | Cross-region (Guanzhong → Central Plains → Yan-Zhao)

Synopsis

This is not a tomb — it is a manhunt. An Unrotted Bone disguised as a human is active in various places. It blends into society with a human identity — antique dealer, scholar, even military officer. This Unrotted Bone was a Tang Dynasty sorcerer — in life he researched how to reverse life and death. It spent a thousand years evolving its corpse into an Unrotted Bone. Its goal is to complete the unfinished research of its life — open a tomb that is "impossible to open," take out what lies within, and achieve true resurrection. This is a cat-and-mouse scenario — players must track it across three cities and be smarter than it to stop it.
