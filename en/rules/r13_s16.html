Scenario B-1: "Awakening" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Astartes · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map: maps/map16_awakening.svg

Difficulty: Introductory | Planet: Requiem Recommended Attributes: Character Creation

Background

You lost consciousness during the fall. When you awaken, you find yourselves lying in the wreckage of a crashed Thunderhawk gunship. The fleet is gone. Exterminatus is being carried out—the flames falling from orbit still burn in the distance.

You are the only survivors—or are there others?

Scenario Structure

Phase Content
Awakening Each of you describes your state as you wake amid the wreckage. Check your gear—the condition of your Power Armor (Step 7). Who is here? Who is not?
Search Search the crash site: find 1–2 Chapter Relics (Step 5), determine your current location, and discover a ruined city nearby.
First Encounter Low-tier Mutants ×2–3—these are the planet's native wildlife, twisted by Chaotic energy.
Discover the Signal The comms unit in the wreckage briefly receives an Imperial-channel signal—coming from the northern mountains. The signal is too weak to answer, but at least it is a direction.

Oath Conflict Point

None—this is the opening scenario, focused on establishing character relationships and the tone of the setting.

Key Checks

Scene Check Notes
Search the Wreckage Investigation (Standard) Find useful supplies
Determine the Signal's Source Lore (Standard) Determine what the signal might be
First Combat Normal Combat Test Adapt to the combat system

Ending

The squad regroups and sets out for the northern mountains. The silhouette of a ruined city is faintly visible in the darkness.
