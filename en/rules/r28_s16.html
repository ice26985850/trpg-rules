Descent of the Six Pillars (S16)

Season: Chapter 4 "Final Battle Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.75-80 - Difficulty: Extreme - Expected Length: 3-5 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

The projections of the Six Beastman Clans' chief gods descend simultaneously at six S-Rank Rifts around the globe. Each projection alone can annihilate a city. The players may only choose to face one of them — but which?

Main Locations

  • Six S-Rank Rifts (corresponding to the six clans; players choose one)

Key Scenes

  • Choosing an opponent — each clan's chief-god projection has different difficulty and rewards
  • S-Rank Rift assault — the highest-difficulty labyrinth challenge
  • The showdown with the chosen chief god — either you die or I die

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 20000, Red · Mythic equipment corresponding to the defeated chief god, World Reputation reaches its peak

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Projections are greatly weakened per the Solo Boss rules
