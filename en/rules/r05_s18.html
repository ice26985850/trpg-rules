Scenario 7: Charm General & Gel General Battle (Enchanting Hot Spring Village × Gel Archipelago)

Run Method: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. The player-count scaling rules are the same as the Prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / +1 concurrent event). This scenario is two-threaded (Southern Charm Demon Palace + Eastern Gel Archipelago), and may be run in sequence or split into two routes depending on party size.

Difficulty Guideline: A dual-boss fight is a Boss battle; design Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ party total level × 0.6 (at 4-person Character Level (CL) 44, the dual bosses are roughly CR 44 / CR 46). After adjustment, ensure "lowest level (CL42) can clear, highest level (CL44) is challenged". Charm General CR30→CR44, Gel General CR28→CR46; see Monster Compendium and GM Book 12.3.

I. Opening Description

Head south to "Enchanting Hot Spring Village (R3)", cross east to "Gel Archipelago (R4)". Aya, the Bard God's Emissary, links the two storylines: Charm General Lilith·Meiying grew tired of Demons being discriminated against and wants to prove that Demons too can be loved; Gel General Plum·Gel is a naive foodie who only follows "big-sis Demon Lord". Both threads apply pressure simultaneously, testing how the party divides its forces.

II. Scene Flow

Act 0: Accept the Subjugation Commission (opening beat)

At the Adventurers' Guild headquarters you accept the dual commission to "subjugate the Third Demon General, the Charm General (CR44)" and "subjugate the Fourth Demon General, the Gel General (CR46)" — requires Rank S (Influence Points (IP) ≥40 to accept; usually reached naturally by Character Level (CL) 40 at the end of leveling, matching the Rank S acceptance threshold). These two elite conscription orders are unlocked only after you cleared two Demon Generals in Scenario 5 / Scenario 6 consecutively and the Guild confirmed you possess Marshal-level combat strength.

After accepting, the Guild lays out both sets of military intelligence at once, and the two generals advance in parallel. Solo mode is supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, with no sidekick. After clearing both generals and collecting four beacons, Influence Points (IP) must accumulate to ≥40 to take on the Demon Lord Castle final battle (Scenario 8).

Act 1: Charm Demon Palace Exploration (first half)

Hot spring city infiltration (Charisma (CHA) / Disguise check Difficulty Class (DC) 20). You encounter an illusion maze; Dancer-type 【Illusion Dance】 Charm exception can counter it. Along the way you may trigger a Lilith affinity event (+3 IP).

Act 2: Gel General Battle (east route, may precede or follow)

Plum·Gel (CR 46, Boss battle) three phases: - Phase 1 "Split & Multiply" (100%~66%): After taking a hit, 【Split】 into two bodies; requires Area Effect (AOE) to clear the field (Crit-immune trait not yet active before crystallization). - Phase 2 "Fusion" (66% transition): Sub-bodies recombine to full HP and apply 【Acid Corrosion】 (equipment / armor Damage Reduction drops). - Phase 3 "Crystallization" (33% transition): 【Crit Immunity】 + high Damage Reduction, reversing the "high crit" feel; must break it with 【Blunt-shatter Crystal】 or a specific mechanic, then proceed to the fragile finish.

Act 3: Charm General Battle (south route, dual-boss convergence)

Lilith·Meiying (CR 44, Boss battle) three phases: - Phase 1 "Dance of Seduction" (100%~66%): 【Illusion Dance (Charm, lasts 1–3 rounds by level)】 controls the field, forcing players to fight each other. - Phase 2 "Essence Drain" (66% transition): Drains the Health (HP) of charmed targets to restore her own; 【Lust Bind】 Immobilizes. - Phase 3 "True Form Revealed" (33% transition): 【Inner Demon Split】 copies one party member's appearance and some skills, creating a trust crisis; after lifting the Charm, go all-out on damage.

III. NPC Dialogue Examples

Bard Aya: "The Charm Demon Palace's illusions almost got me too. Remember — when charmed, you attack your dearest companions." Charm General Lilith·Meiying: "I just want to be loved~ (charming smile) Tell me, don't Demons deserve a true heart?" Gel General Plum·Gel: "Plum wants to eat that shiny jelly! Big-sis Demon Lord said I can eat it... can I, can I?" (baby talk)

V. Stage Design

  • Stage A Charm Demon Palace Exploration: Social / Illusion puzzle (DC 20 Charisma).
  • Stage B Gel General Boss: Three phases; key is 【Blunt-shatter Crystal】 to break the crystallization Crit Immunity.
  • Stage C Charm General Boss: Three phases; key is 【Inner Demon Split】 trust crisis and dispelling Charm.
  • Chapter-end twist: After clearing both generals and collecting four beacons, accumulate Influence Points (IP) to ≥40 to unlock Demon Lord Castle (Scenario 8).

VI. Rewards

  • Experience (EXP): Dual-route skirmishes +1200; dual bosses ×4 total ≈ +21,608 (Gel General 11,224 + Charm General 10,384) / person; quest +~5,500.
  • Gold: 8,000 G total.
  • Items: Meiying's Heart Pact (beacon), Gel Core (beacon), Inner Demon Mirror (Charm-dispel item), Hot Spring Healing Voucher.

VII. Suggested Level & Difficulty

  • Suggested Character Level (CL) range: 44 (threshold).
  • Difficulty: Extreme (Difficulty Class (DC) 25; dual-boss budget ×1.2, splitting into two routes reduces per-fight pressure).
