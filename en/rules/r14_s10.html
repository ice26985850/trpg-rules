Scenario S10: War of the God-King

Suggested Era Range: Legendary Age
Suggested Level: High (system pinnacle)
Estimated Turns: ~75 turns (30 in-game years)
Difficulty: ★★★★★
Core Themes: Divinity, Miracles, Legendary Duels, Apotheosis
Players: 2–5 players + 1 GM

I. Scenario Overview

Synopsis

The threshold of legend is now before you. The mightiest rulers are no longer content with mortal thrones; they chase divinity, proving themselves worthy of godhood through miracles and conquest. When legends become enemies, the world will meet its finale in the War of the God-King—either ascend as a god and write your name into eternity, or see your divinity collapse into a fading echo lamented by later generations.

This is the final line between mortal and divine.

Scenario Goals

  1. Awaken divinity, establish a faith sanctuary
  2. Prove your divinity through miracles and legendary duels
  3. Achieve apotheosis, or hold the highest Legend Score at the endgame
  4. Survive the War of the God-King and become a god

II. Multiplayer Mechanics

  • Mode: Competitive (divinity + conquest). Each player leads a legendary power, chasing divinity.
  • Legend Score: Divinity Level×30 ＋ Sanctuaries×20 ＋ Legendary Battles Won×20 ＋ Faith Population×0.5.
  • Victory Condition: The first to achieve "Apotheosis" immediately triggers the endgame and +40 points; otherwise, Legend Scores are tallied at turn 75, and the highest becomes a god.
  • Player Interaction: May forge divine alliances, declare war, launch legendary-level duels; occupying a sanctuary draws divine power.
  • GM Duties: Track divinity level, sanctuaries, and faith population; arrange global threats such as the Godslayer.

III. Scenario Structure

Phase I: Legendary Rise (Turns 1-20, Years 1-8)

Key Events:

Turn Event Type
Turn 4 Divinity Awakens—the ruler first senses the divinity within Awakening
Turn 9 Sanctuary Foundation—establish the first faith sanctuary Construction
Turn 14 Oracle Dispute—all sides interpret the same oracle, accusing one another of usurpation Religious (check DC 20)
Turn 18 Covenant and Betrayal—forge a divine alliance, or plot betrayal in secret Diplomacy (choice)

Phase II: The Pursuit of Divine Wonders (Turns 21-42, Years 9-17)

Prove divinity through miracles, contest sanctuaries and faith.

Turn Event Type
Turn 25 Send Down a Miracle—send a miracle upon your lands, absorbing faith Miracle
Turn 30 Sanctuary Contest—a masterless sanctuary sparks a multi-sided divine war Competition (Military check DC 21)
Turn 36 The Godslayer Appears—the legendary Godslayer threatens all God-Kings Threat
Turn 40 War of Faith—launch a holy war for dominance over faith Religious War (Crisis, check DC 22)

Phase III: The Path of Slaying Gods (Turns 43-60, Years 18-24)

Legendary duels reach a fever pitch; God-Kings attack one another.

Turn Event Type
Turn 46 God-King Duel—two God-Kings clash head-on on the battlefield Legendary Duel (combined check DC 23)
Turn 51 God-Stealing Plan—seize others' divinity shards to strengthen yourself Relic
Turn 56 Heaven's Wrath—divine overload triggers heaven's wrath, testing will Divine Wrath (Morale check DC 22)

Phase IV: The Eternal Throne (Turns 61-75, Years 25-30)

The final apotheosis—ascend as a god, or vanish.

Turn Event Type
Turn 66 Apotheosis—those whose divinity level reaches its peak may declare apotheosis Milestone (triggers endgame +40 points)
Turn 70 The Final War—last standoff of remaining God-Kings Military (check DC 24)
Turn 75 The Moment of Apotheosis—tally Legend Scores, highest becomes a god Ending

IV. Ending Conditions

Victory (Highest Legend Score)

  • Achieve apotheosis and hold the highest Legend Score
  • Reward: All attributes +15, unlock the "Eternal God-King" Legendary Deed; other players gain corresponding legendary status by score

Partial Success

  • Survive to the endgame but not deified
  • Reward: Keep your legendary status, may enter Free Mode to continue

Failure Conditions

  • Divinity collapses and the power disintegrates
  • Remedy: Inheritance Mechanism—be reborn through reincarnation, keeping 50% of attributes

Name Role Relationship

VII. GM Tips

  1. Let loose: The Legendary Age is the system's pinnacle; let players wield their legendary abilities freely
  2. Divinity tracking: Closely track divinity level, sanctuaries, and faith population
  3. Dramatic tension: Divine alliances and betrayal are the core tension; encourage players to scheme
  4. Balance threats: The Godslayer, as a global threat, can balance overly strong players
  5. Apotheosis ceremony: Apotheosis is the ultimate goal; give it sufficient narrative weight and ceremony
