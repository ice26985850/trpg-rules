S2: Anomaly Signal

Campaign: The Rookie Director (Campaign One · Climax) Recommended Level: 3 Difficulty Class (DC): 85 (Hard) Mission Type: Recon Mission (REC) Estimated Time: 60–90 minutes

Opening Description

Three months now. Your desk is piled with mission reports, payroll sheets, and equipment lists. You have grown used to the taste of synthetic coffee. You have grown used to signing mission reports.

But you have not grown used to receiving this.

A priority message on the comm terminal — from the remote monitoring station at Level 3, the "Electrical Station":

"Anomalous energy fluctuation detected. Source unknown. Intensity: moderate. Location: deep within Sector C. Request dispatch of recon team to confirm. Note: this area has had no entry for four months."

Your Intel Officer adds a sentence — this is no routine matter.

Scene Flow

Scene One: Office — Intel Assessment

Scene Description: Your Intel Officer "Su Ming" (Veteran Explorer, Specialty: Intel Analysis) stands before you, data flickering on his tablet.

Su Ming: "Sector C's monitoring station started receiving intermittent signals last month. At first we thought it was equipment failure. But the signal's frequency — Director, this is not random noise. It's like something is transmitting."

Decision Point: - Purchase Intel: basic (50) → detailed (150, reveals signal pattern), or precise (300, reveals exact location and potential threats) - Dispatch Team Selection: recommend at least 2 people, preferably an Explorer with Engineering specialty - Equipment Configuration: Sector C's environment has high-voltage lines and exposed transformers — Survival Equipment (insulated gloves) is highly recommended

Scene Two: Level 3 Entrance — Descent

Scene Description: Level 3's entrance is a heavy metal door. Push it open, and you are met with the smell of ozone and a low hum. The fluorescent lights here are no longer a steady white — they flicker, leaning toward an unhealthy amber.

Environmental Threats: - Some floor areas have standing water — extremely dangerous when electrified - Some corridors are blocked by collapsed pipes, requiring detours or an Engineering check to clear - Temperature is about 10 degrees higher than Level 1 — uncomfortable but tolerable

Scene Three: Deep in Sector C — The Signal Source

Scene Description: As the team approaches the signal source, they begin to notice anomalies — wires on the walls tremble slightly with no external force, like they have a pulse. The readings on the gauges jump. There is a low-frequency vibration in the air, not sound, but one you can feel in your chest.

Key Encounter — The First Manifestation of the Rift:

In an abandoned control room, the Explorers discover the source of the signal — not equipment. It is a "Rift." A seam that should not exist appears on the wall, oozing faint blue light. The energy reading peaks here.

Su Ming (over the comm headset, voice breaking up): "Director... this is not... a normal Backrooms phenomenon. I've never seen anything like it in any database."

Check: Roll the mission check. DC 85 (actual value).

If an Explorer with Engineering specialty is in the team: they may attempt to install temporary monitoring equipment (additional +5 bonus).

Outcome Branches: - Critical Success: Successfully record the Rift's complete energy data and return safely. Gain excess Intel (+5). Unlock a key clue for Campaign Two "The Rift." - Success: Record basic data, return safely. Intelligence +3. Obtain a Rift sample — can be handed to the R&D Lab for analysis. - Marginal Success: Data recording completed, but an electrical explosion occurs during evacuation. One Explorer is lightly wounded. Intelligence +2. - Failure: The Rift suddenly expands during recording — an electromagnetic pulse destroys most of the equipment. The team is forced to retreat. Intelligence +1, but the key data is lost. Mission report: you need to send someone in again. - Catastrophe (First Death Trigger Point): The Rift erupts with an energy pulse at close observation. The nearest Explorer is struck directly.

Scene Four: Catastrophe — Death Notification

When the Catastrophe occurs:

A burst of harsh static comes over the comm channel, then —

"Director—! It— it's getting bigger—" The last sound is the hum of electricity. Then silence.

This is the emotional climax designed for Campaign One: - The GM should pause here. Give the player time to process - Do not immediately give the mission result. First describe the silence after the comm cuts out - Follow-up scene: other Explorers wait at the entrance. Time ticks by. Until it is confirmed — only one person walks out

Bereavement Compensation Handling: - Depending on the Contract type, the player must personally fill out a bereavement compensation application form - The GM may actually print a blank form for the player to fill out — increasing immersion - If the player chooses to inform the family in person (roleplay option): Reputation +1, but requires a heavy emotional scene

Aftermath Choices: 1. Immediately dispatch a second team: investigate the Rift and recover the body (new mission, DC 90) 2. Seal off Sector C: safe but loses the chance for further Intel 3. Report to M.E.G.: request support from superiors (cost: this may be viewed as a management failure)

GM Information

Item Value
True DC 85
Intel Bias ±15% (detailed Intel) or ±25% (basic)
Minimum Personnel 2
Recommended Specialty Engineering, Recon
Encounter Probability 25% (Sector C's electrical structure is itself a threat)
Time Pressure None (but the GM should create an atmosphere of "uncertainty")

Design Intent: This is the player's first encounter with a true Backrooms anomaly — not an Entity, but a malfunction of space itself. The Rift is the core element of Campaign Two, and S2 is its introduction.

Death Design: If the Catastrophe triggers, this is the scenario's designed "first Explorer death." The GM should not soften it — this is the reality the player, as Director, must face. The casualty system exists not to punish, but to give weight to decisions.

If the player is too conservative and never sends anyone into Sector C: the Rift will keep expanding, eventually affecting other areas of Level 3. M.E.G. will ask why it was not reported — the player cannot escape forever.
