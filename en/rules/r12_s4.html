Urban Survivors — Scenario Three: "The Final Gate" — Late Siege: The Final Choice

Difficulty: ★★★★☆ Suggested Players: 1 (expandable to multiplayer) Siege Day: Day 100+ Estimated Sessions: 7–10 Core Theme: The Final Ending Prerequisite: Complete Scenario Two "The Long Winter," or a character with a deep relationship network and substantial trauma

Scenario Overview

The siege has entered its hundredth day. The city is barely recognizable—former cafés have become graves, former schoolyards have become fortresses, former homes have become rubble.

The end is coming. But not in one way—rather, it approaches in multiple possible shapes at once.

The Federal Army plans a final breakout, the Black River Merchant Guild sells secret evacuation routes, the International Humanitarian Aid convoy negotiates an evacuation list, the Voice of Liberty prepares its final act of resistance, and the Church has opened its doors to welcome anyone in need of shelter.

The player must make the ultimate choice—and that choice will permanently change everything that remains.

This scenario supports both solo and multiplayer modes. In multiplayer, the ultimate divergence between players (who stays, who leaves, who is taken along) is the highest-tension dramatic core.

Final Offers from Multiple Factions

Faction Proposal Cost Mechanics Reward For Whom
Federal Army (KFA) Join the final breakout operation Extremely high risk of death (~30% survival rate) At least 3 high-DC checks (Stealth, Physique, Willpower) Success: freedom, reconnection with the outside world Those who crave battle or have no other choice
Black River Merchant Guild (BRS) Secret evacuation route—cross the front line to the neutral zone All supplies, a secret, or a favor owed Bargaining DC 16 can reduce the cost; route is not 100% safe (20% encounter chance) Safe evacuation Pragmatists who have something to trade (1–2 slots; NPC companions require a choice)
Voice of Liberty (VoL) Take part in the final resistance—sabotage within the city Near-certain sacrifice (survival rate <10%) Series of combat / Stealth checks DC 16–20 Even in death, may shift the course of the war Wraith, Dimitri, those who choose to atone with their lives
International Humanitarian Aid (IHA) Negotiate evacuation—leave as civilians via the humanitarian corridor Must prove civilian status (may have to abandon armed companions) Social DC 14 to persuade; must surrender all weapons Safest evacuation (survival rate >80%) Protectors with the vulnerable (children, elderly)—Dr. Elena can vouch
St. Michael's Church Stay in the city—guard those who cannot leave Endure the final phase—bombardment, street fighting, occupation No check required; daily Willpower DC 14 to maintain morale Guard those who cannot protect themselves—inner peace Father Andre, Grandma Marta, those who do not flee

Key NPCs' Endgame Choices

NPC Choice Impact
Father Andre (NPC #1) Stay and guard the church to the end. If the player also stays: receive the priest's final blessing (full recovery of Mental State, once)
Yana Novak (NPC #2) Hesitates—wants to lead the community in a breakout yet unwilling to abandon anyone. The player's choice may affect her; at relationship value REL ≥ 7 she follows the player
The Professor (NPC #3) Prepares his own evacuation plan—one step ahead of everyone. Good relationship (REL ≥ 5) may provide one extra evacuation slot
Dimitri (NPC #8) Final chance at redemption—join VoL to atone, or break down. Player support decides his path; at REL ≥ 7 he heeds the player's advice
Victor (NPC #12) His informant identity may be exposed at the endgame. How the player handles him sets the community's moral tone
Nico and Lena (NPC #9) The children's fate is the heaviest moral counterweight. Any choice involving children brings the greatest emotional impact and traumatic consequences

The Ultimate Moral Dilemma: "The Signal"

Trigger: The player obtains a radio frequency—the besieging side's command frequency (found by accident, obtained from VoL, or sold by the Professor).

Option Action Consequence Trauma
A Hand it to the Voice of Liberty VoL launches precision sabotage, possibly turning the tide, but the besieging side will inevitably retaliate with massacre +5 Trauma Accumulation
B Sell it to the Black River Merchant Guild The Professor trades it for a large quantity of supplies and 2 evacuation slots; you are safe—but you abandoned everyone else +3 Trauma Accumulation
C Use the frequency to negotiate personally Social DC 18 to negotiate safe passage; failure means being located, special forces come to "deal with" you Success +1 / Failure +4
D Destroy the frequency Do nothing, let fate take its course +1 (the torment of "what if…")

Ending Determination (for GM use)

Method: Weighted judgment across four factors

Factor Weight Description
World Setting 40% The siege cause chosen by the GM before the game starts (Setting A/B/C)
Player Influence 30% Cumulative choices across the three scenarios: how many people helped, what alliances built, what sacrifices made
Key Events 20% The final handling of "The Signal," relationship status with key NPCs, survival of the Shelter
Random 10% Roll 3d6: 3–7 worsens / 8–12 holds steady / 13–18 improves

Possible Endings

Ending Condition Tone
Liberation Friendly breakthrough + player assistance Hope and trauma intertwined
Siege Lifted Diplomatic negotiation succeeds + player did not break the peace Bitter relief
Fall Enemy's final offensive succeeds + player failed to stop or flee The price of survival is living under occupation
Escape Successful evacuation via Black Market / IHA / VoL routes Freedom—but at what cost?
Freeze and Starve to Death Resources exhausted, basic survival unsustainable The most ordinary ending of the siege
Open-ended Player and GM choose not to end the story The siege continues…

Post-Ending Handling (GM Guide)

  1. Character Summary: Write an ending narrative for each surviving character—based on their choices, relationships, and trauma
  2. Lingering Questions: Raise "what happened next?"—where did the characters go? Did relationships with NPCs endure?
  3. Group Reflection: Let players share their most unforgettable moments
  4. GM Closing Words: Bring this siege to a close

Completion Rewards

  • Experience Points (Experience): 4
  • Fate Points (Fate): restored to maximum (5 points)
  • Trauma Relief: upon completing the core mission, Trauma Accumulation −5 (one-time—a major breather in a long campaign)
  • Special: Unlock the "Siege Survivor" title—the character gains a permanent ending narrative
