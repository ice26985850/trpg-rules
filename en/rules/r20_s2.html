Scenario S2: Abyssal Corridor

Applicable Mode: Solo (Lone Wolf mode, 4 Action Points (AP) per round) / Multiplayer (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–5 players, 3 AP each) Difficulty: Hard Recommended Level: 2–4 Estimated Duration: 3–5 hours Mission Type: Data Raid Player Count: 1–6

Mission Briefing

An Concord carrier, the Eternal Watch, was damaged early in the Rift War and drifted into the asteroid belt. The crew evacuated entirely, but the shipboard AI was never fully shut down. Over fifty years, the AI gradually broke down in its solitude.

Recently an Alliance patrol vessel approached the ship and mysteriously vanished. Intelligence suggests the Eternal Watch may hold a key early-war database. The Arbitrator dispatches an assault team to board, with the objective of locating and recovering the tactical database at the bridge.

Enemy Vessel Data

  • Vessel Name: Eternal Watch (S2)
  • Vessel Type: Abandoned Carrier — originally Terran Stellar Concord (TSC)
  • Scale: Giant (1,200 m length) / 5 decks (only main decks explored)
  • Gravity: Standard Gravity (1G); some areas have failed gravity plates, resulting in Variable-G
  • Self-Destruct System: None (abandoned state) — but the reactor is unstable and may explode

Advance Flow

Turning Point 1: The Bridge Choice

There are two routes to the bridge: the main elevator (fast but may alert the AI) or the repair conduit (long but safe). The Scout's route-planning ability is critical here.

Turning Point 2: Awakening of the AI

When the data download reaches 50%, the shipboard AI "Watcher" awakens. It is not hostile — but it believes the players are the crew sent to rescue it fifty years ago, and begins reconfiguring ship systems to "assist," yet its assistance is extremely dangerous (opening vacuum hatches to "fight fire," activating defense turrets to "protect" the players).

Turning Point 3: Reactor Crisis

The AI attempts to restart the main reactor. If not stopped, the reactor will overload and explode after 10 rounds.

Key Encounters

E1 Hangar Deck

  • Enemies: Defense Robot (Elite) × 2 (CR 3, rusty and sluggish — 50% chance each round to malfunction and skip its action)
  • EL: 6

E2 Corridor Before the Bridge

  • Enemies: Auto-Turret (Standard) × 3 (may be breached via system breach check DC 15, or bypassed via the repair conduit)
  • EL: 6

E3 Reactor Chamber

  • Environment: Radiation zone — unprotected characters take 1d4 radiation damage per round
  • Challenge: Breach the AI console (Engineering (ENG) check DC 18) to cancel the reactor restart

Rewards

  • Experience Points (XP): Primary Objective 10 / Secondary Objective 4 / Full Survival 3 / GM Generic 6
  • Reputation: 2
  • Special Loot: Watcher AI Fragment — a data core holding early-war AI code. Effect: permanently +1 to Engineering (ENG) checks when breaching AI vessels.

Special Rules

  • Psychological Pressure: The GM should emphasize the horror atmosphere of the environment — footstep echoes in dark corridors, damaged broadcasts playing evacuation orders from fifty years ago, dried blood on the bulkheads.
  • AI Anomaly: The Watcher AI's behavior is unpredictable. Every 3 rounds the GM rolls 1d6 to determine its "assistance" action: 1–2 open/close nearby hatches, 3–4 broadcast meaningless messages, 5-6 activate nearby automated defenses.
