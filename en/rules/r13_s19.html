Scenario B-4: "The Traitor's Genetic Legacy" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Astartes · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map: maps/map19_justicar.svg

Difficulty: High | Location: Wreck of the Fallen Warship "Justicar" Recommended Attributes: 4–5 growths

Background

The transport ship is repaired (or the Eldar helped repair it)—but it cannot take off. The orbital defense system above the planet's atmosphere is still active and will shoot down any ship attempting to leave. The squad must travel to the wreck of the fallen Imperial cruiser "Justicar" to shut down the orbital defense system—or hijack the ship's comms array to call for rescue.

Scenario Structure

Phase Content
Arrival at the Wreck The Justicar was shot down by friendly fire while Exterminatus was being carried out—not by Chaos, but by its own people. The shipwreck is enormous, stretching for kilometers.
The Bridge Mystery The ship's black box recording reveals a truth: the "reason" for Exterminatus may have been a misjudgment. The Chaos contamination on the planet was exaggerated, or worse—it was a certain Inquisitor's personal vendetta.
Surviving Crew Some sections of the ship still have people alive—30 Imperial Navy crew members were trapped in sealed compartments when the ship fell. They have been trapped for 47 days. Half of them have begun to doubt the Emperor.
The Temptation of Chaos Deep in the wreck lies a Chaos Space Marine—a traitor who was active on this planet before Exterminatus began. He senses the presence of the Astartes and tries to persuade them to join him: "You have already been abandoned by the Imperium—why still fight for them?"

Oath Conflict Point

Dual Conflict: 1. Protect the Innocent vs. Obey Orders: If Exterminatus was a misjudgment—what should the squad do? Take the evidence back? Or continue to carry out the Imperium's sentence? 2. Oath to the Emperor vs. Survival: The Chaos Space Marine's question is real—you truly have been abandoned by the Imperium. How much longer can your faith hold?

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Chaos Space Marine "the Forgotten" Kassar Boss / Dialogue Target He was once a member of the Imperial Fists—left on this planet to die 300 years ago. His fall was not from ambition, but from despair. He can be a mirror—showing what an Astartes becomes if it loses all hope.
The Captain's Final Log Clue The truth the captain recorded before his death. He tried to stop Exterminatus—and was executed by the Inquisitor on charges of "treason."
The Ship's Machine Spirit Environment The Machine Spirit of the Justicar is still active—it remembers everything. It will play the captain's final prayer over the ship's internal broadcast system.

Key Checks

Scene Check Notes
Recover black box data Tech-Use (Hard) Obtain the truth
Dialogue with Kassar Oratory (Extreme) or Mind (Extreme) Reject his logic—or be shaken
Rescue the crew Command (Standard) Organize the crew's evacuation

Combat Encounters

Encounter Enemies Challenge Rating (CR) Tactics
Ship Corridors Low-tier Mutants ×4 (mutated crew) 1×4 Melee in confined spaces
Engine Room Warp Shadows ×2 2×2 Concentration of Warp energy
Bridge (optional finale) Chaos Space Marine Kassar 4 If he was not persuaded

Branch Endings

  • Persuade Kassar: He cannot be saved—his corruption runs too deep. But at the last moment he can choose self-destruction rather than fight the squad. His final words before death: "Tell the Emperor... I did not forget Him."
  • Shaken by Kassar: A warrior may gain a permanent +2 Corruption and receive the brand of "Suspicion of the Warmaster."
  • Obtain the Truth: The black box data becomes the key to the finale—it can be used to prove Exterminatus was wrong.
