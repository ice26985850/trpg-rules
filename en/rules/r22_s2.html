S2 — The Collapsing Tower

Difficulty: Intermediate | Suggested Memory Value: Memory 5-7, 3-6 cycles | Time Range: Seven days Core Skills: Insight (INS), Execution (EXE), Memory (MEM) Players: 1 player + 1 GM

Opening Description

Noon on the seventh day, and the bell of the Tower of Time will not ring. Instead, a white light — and then silence. The tower's central structure collapses in an instant, not downward, but inward — as if time itself were being sucked out at that point.

You have seen this scene three times now. Each time you drew one step closer to the truth, and each time you died more horribly. But this time — this time you know which floor to start searching from.

Scenario Summary

The Tower of Time is the tallest building in Ashfall City, and the center of temporal research. At noon on the seventh day, a "time explosion" will destroy the tower's central structure — not an accident, but deliberate sabotage. The saboteur comes from within the Core Circle, an undercover scholar planted by the Church. The player must traverse the tower's seven floors within seven days, find the explosive device, and expose the mastermind.

The Tower's Seven-Floor Structure

Seven-Day Timeline

Day Key Event Suggested Action
Day 1 Apprentice Kai voluntarily contacts the player, mentions "strange vibrations" in the tower Obtain a visitor pass, explore the ground floor and 2nd floor
Day 2 Chief Scholar Victor publicly claims "routine research fluctuation" Investigate the 3rd floor, discover anomalous instrument readings
Day 3 A researcher "dies accidentally" on the 4th floor Obtain a way to enter the 4th floor
Day 4 Eileen privately contacts the player — she saw a bad prophecy Explore the 5th floor, find evidence of internal contradiction
Day 5 Core Circle emergency meeting Enter the 6th floor, obtain Eileen's prophecy fragment
Day 6 The last calm day Enter the 7th floor, discover the explosive device
Day 7 Noon — Explosion (or successful prevention) Disarm the device, expose the undercover agent

Three Routes

Route A — Academic Route (Insight + Memory)

Gain clearance floor by floor as a visiting scholar, discovering anomalies by consulting documents and observing experiments.

Route B — Infiltration Route (Execution + Insight)

Use Shadow Guild resources to infiltrate the tower; each floor requires a different infiltration strategy, avoiding the temporal alarm system.

Route C — Church Route (Social + Will)

Intervene in the investigation in the Church's name, use Church clearance to enter some floors, while balancing the Church's demands.

Truth Layers

  • Surface: The explosion was a research accident out of control
  • Middle: Someone sabotaged it deliberately — from within the Core Circle
  • Deep: The undercover scholar was planted by the Church, to simultaneously destroy both the Tower of Time's research capacity and the Church's own criminal evidence

Clear Conditions

  • Main Objective: Prevent the seventh-day explosion
  • Perfect Clear: Expose the undercover scholar's identity and protect Eileen (key witness)
  • Minimum Clear: Successfully disarm the explosive device

Multiplayer GM Tips

This scenario is designed for solo play (1 player + 1 GM). If you start with 2-3 players per GM Rulebook 14-3, note:

  • Each player manages their own Rift Mark (RM) and Memory Fragments (MF); Encounter Budget (EB) = sum of all players' Memory + highest Execution value; Team Check (pass if more than half succeed) applies to obtaining clearance and cracking Time Locks.
  • S2's multi-floor structure is naturally suited to division of labor: assign players to different routes (academic / infiltration / church), or explore floors separately, then exchange intelligence at the daily gathering. Tower guards, Elemental Mages, and other combat encounters should follow 14-3 enemy scaling (each additional combat unit: HP ×1.5 or count +1).
  • Disarming the explosive device on the 7th floor is a decisive single action: assign one player as the lead disarmer, others provide cover (cover who pass relevant checks grant the lead Advantage), or convert to a Team Check.
