Scenario S8: Lone Wolf Trial

Type: Solo Lone Wolf | Suggested Level: 2.5 | Difficulty: ★★★★ | Estimated Duration: 4–5 hours

Synopsis

You are no longer a Rookie. You survived three matches—that alone is an achievement. But it also means you are now "valuable prey." The bounty hunters receive a list—every contestant who has survived more than three matches, each with a price on their head. Your name is on it. Your codename is on it. Attached is a photo of you from your last match—one you never knew was taken. An abandoned industrial park is your stage. Sixteen Lone Wolves—at least half of them after you. You are not the hunter. You are the prey. But you are not about to give up without a fight.

Battlefield Map

Rust Belt Industrial Park — 5 km × 5 km

Enemy Configuration

Total enemy squads (including the player's squad).

Battlefield Conditions

  • Zone: Edge Drift (southeast bias—forces the player to cross the iron bridge or the chemical zone) — the circle slowly drifts southeast. The final circle is at the south end of the iron bridge or an abandoned warehouse on the south bank. This means: players in the north must cross the bridge before the fourth circle—and the bounty hunters know it.

Flow Phases

1. Bounty Announcement (0 (pre-drop) rounds)

GM Note: Before the player parachutes, give them a special opening: "The transport plane's broadcast system crackles with static—then switches to a woman's voice. Not the standard Pandora announcer. This voice is colder, more professional. 'Attention all contestants. The Pandora Bounty Committee has listed the following contestants as "High-Value Targets" for this match. Contestants who eliminate the targets below will receive double the bounty. The list is as follows—' Your name is on the list. Your codename is on the list. The broadcast ends. The rear cargo door of the transport plane opens. Below is a rusted expanse of grey."

2. First Blood (3–4 rounds)

GM Note: Make the player feel pressure immediately after landing. The bounty hunters will not grant the luxury of looting. The first bounty hunter begins tracking within 2 rounds of landing. Give the player a choice: go to the chemical zone for a gas mask (high risk), or move stealthily through the dormitory area (safer but poor loot). The focus of this stretch is building the feeling of "being hunted": - a sniper-scope glint in the distance (a bounty hunter looking for you) - radio interference—bounty hunters jamming communications - a copy of the list on a killed Rookie—your name circled

3. Drone Game (4–5 rounds)

GM Note: Introduce the drone system. Let the player experience the fear of being watched. First drone scan—give the player a chance to react: "Buzzing—take cover!" Second drone scan—if the player has not learned, let a bounty hunter find them. Best design: have the drone appear when the player thinks they are safe—"You just finished bandaging your wound and caught your breath. Buzzing."

4. Banshee's Hunting Ground (4–5 rounds)

GM Note: Vera begins to take the initiative. She will not rush in like the other bounty hunters—she sets traps, creates false impressions, and uses other bounty hunters as her hounds. Techniques: - let the player find an "abandoned first-aid kit"—with a tripwire grenade nearby - let a sniper-scope glint appear in an impossible position—that is a mirror, not her - let the player defeat a bounty hunter and find a walkie-talkie on the body—Vera's voice comes through it: "You are harder to handle than I expected. Good—that makes it interesting."

5. Iron Bridge Deadlock (3–4 rounds)

GM Note: The circle forces the player to cross the bridge. This is the most dangerous stretch of the journey—because Vera is waiting. So are the other bounty hunters. Give the player multiple options: - frontal bridge rush (most dangerous)—but smoke grenades can provide cover - detour through the chemical zone to find a shallow ford—but toxin damage will weaken you - use a vehicle to rush the bridge at high speed—leaving bounty hunters only a 2-second sniper window - fake a bridge rush then retreat—draw out the sniper's position, then counter-snipe. Every choice has a cost and an opportunity—there is no "safe" option.

6. Final Circle: Bounty Settlement (2–3 rounds)

GM Note: The last survivors: you, Vera, and at most 1–2 other Lone Wolves. The circle shrinks to an abandoned warehouse. Vera finally confronts you face to face. Give the player a climactic scene. Let Vera speak—"Do you know what your bounty is? Five hundred thousand Credits. Enough for an ordinary family to live fifty years. Do you know why it is so high? Because you made me look like a Rookie." Her weakness: perfectionism. She cannot accept failure. When her plan is disrupted—when the player makes a move she did not anticipate—she makes mistakes. This is the player's only opening. The final confrontation is not just gunplay—it is psychological warfare. Let her seethe because the player will not follow her script. "You were not supposed to go left—every time in your last match you went right—" "You switched weapons? Why? Your data says you prefer—"

Victory Condition

Become the last surviving Lone Wolf (and defeat or shake off the bounty hunter "Banshee")
