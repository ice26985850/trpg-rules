Scenario 4: "The Windowless Room"

Item Content
Type Seal Investigation
Recommended Marks 3-4
Sessions 2 sessions
Core Attributes Awareness, Negotiation, Anchor
Difficulty ★★★

Prerequisite Reading: Original scenario (間隙行者TRPG_系列劇本.md, Scenario Two). This version is adjusted for solo play.

Opening

Architect Mr. Lin contacts you privately. His firm is renovating a century-old mansion, and the construction crew discovered a "nonexistent room" on the third floor — it appears on the blueprints, but its windows were bricked shut, and the only door was welded closed from the inside. The blueprint is annotated: "September 1923 — Sealed." Any worker who tries to open the door feels "unable to breathe."

NPCs

NPC Info
Mr. Lin The client. An architect in his forties, a pragmatist but unnerved by this room
Old Physician A retired physician in his eighties who interned here in the 1940s and remembers the unopenable third-floor room
Seal Watcher Stands guard by the Crack — not hostile, waiting for "someone worthy"

Lost Items (Solo DC)

Lost Item Quality Solo DC Location
Silver Box Benevolent Keepsake 10 Gap Layer of the first-floor consultation room
Deed of Transfer Memory Crystal 13 Inside the Sealed Space
The Mistress's Comb Common Lost Item 6 Gap Layer of the original owner's second-floor bedroom

Scenes

  1. Surface Investigation: Awareness DC 8 reveals the area around the doorframe is "too clean — no dust, no wear." [[MAP-04 scene1]]
  2. Gathering Clues: The old physician provides a lead — the silver box is in the first-floor consultation room. Enter the Gap (front door Natural Threshold, DC 6), Retrieval DC 10 to find the box. Inside is a brass key and an inscription: "Forgive me." [[MAP-04 scene2]]
  3. Seal Watcher: Negotiation DC 16 (solo). Present the silver box → the Watcher steps aside. Without it → force your way through with Traverse DC 20 (failure → the Crack is permanently sealed + Gap Sickness worsens). [[MAP-04 scene3]]
  4. Enter the Sealed Space: A 1923 study. A man's Residual holds his signing posture — motionless, his hesitation already complete. Retrieval DC 13 to find the Deed of Transfer (Memory Crystal) — the original owner transferred his property to his ailing wife and then took his own life. [[MAP-04 scene4]]
  5. Unsealing: After taking the document, the seal breaks — the bricks outside the window crumble and sunlight pours in. [[MAP-04 scene5]]
  6. Return: Tell Mr. Lin the truth. Choice: make it public (the room is preserved as an architectural feature) or keep it secret.

Map Reference: Scenario 4 references maps/MAP-04_century_mansion.html throughout (Century Mansion floor plan).

Rewards

  • Return Mark × 2 (returning the document + freeing the Watcher)
  • Sealed Space Knowledge Mark (future Advantage on Sealed Space Negotiation)
