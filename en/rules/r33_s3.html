S3 · Roots That Eat No Soil

This scenario is the first station of a playstyle shift: the player moves from "escape" into "escape + infiltration." Act Two is a standard Trial, but Act Three is breaking away from the main line mid-Trial and infiltrating a place that should not exist — the Fog Cellar. There the player retrieves the first Memory Card, and learns that Ember can serve as "something that unlocks."

2. GM Summary

Storyline: The player carries the S2 Scar #5 "The fire remembers" clue. Botanist Survivor Sally Nkosi (§8-A) says the Sacrifice Tree at the center of Coldwind Farm "grows its roots upward… it eats something else." In Act Two, the player must complete, while fleeing across the open farm, the three-step process of "opening the root passage" — and step two demands generating an Altruistic Hope as a key, making "rescue" go from a moral choice to a required condition for clearing. In Act Three the player descends into the Fog Cellar and plucks a memory pod from the inverted root system.

Truth (GM-Exclusive): The Fog Cellar is The Entity's memory archive — memories are not destroyed, only "filed." Before plucking a pod you must "recognize" that it is yours; the GM does not tell right from wrong. Plucking someone else's pod (including a Killer's) is an S6 hook. The Ember Moment reveals: the tree recognizes only one thing — the Altruistic Hope that The Entity itself cannot spit out.

How to Run: The open ground is Hillbilly's (HBY) home turf, the most painful round so far — only 1 Pallet, the player must use the cornfield for concealment and psychological play rather than looping. The Fog Cellar has no Killer and no objective; pressure comes from the shrinking countdown and the cognitive challenge of "recognizing yourself." The GM should arrange for at least one player to pluck the wrong pod (a Killer's memory).

3. Opening Text (read aloud)

In the firelight, Sally presses a finger to the dirt and draws an imaginary arc.

"The tree in the middle of Coldwind Farm," she says, "its roots grow upward. I've seen it, before the soil covered it."

She looks up at you, very serious.

"Roots don't eat soil. They eat something else."

Eloise reads the scar "The fire remembers" aloud, as if chewing on a word.

"If memory was taken," she says, "then it must have been put somewhere. And that place… isn't outside the Trial."

"So it's inside," you say.

"Inside," she nods. "But you have to slip in while the Trial hasn't ended yet."

She brightens the fire a little.

"This time, don't only think about escaping."

Three steps of the root passage: ① Find the pattern in the bark indentations (Focus (FOC) check, Difficulty Class (DC) Medium); ② Obtain "a segment of Altruistic Hope" as a key (triggered by rescuing / healing / body-blocking, no check); ③ Press the hand to the bark (Will (WIL) check, Difficulty Class (DC) Hard, can spend Hope Point (HOP) to lower the tier).

6. NPC Appearances and Lines

Sally Nkosi (§8-A, Botanist-type Survivor) - Provides the root clue. If she accompanies, she will pluck the wrong pod in Act Three, and from then on her personality changes (S6 hook). - Line: "Roots don't eat soil. They eat something else."

Whisperer · the Woman's Voice Beneath the Roots (RC-02) - The Fog Cellar Whisperer, intelligence-type, Encounter Budget Cost (EBC) 0. The only being that can tell the player "whose pod you plucked," at a price of 2 Hope Point (HOP). - Line: "Can you tell whose it is? If you can't, don't pluck. Pluck the wrong one and that person will see you."

Echo Companion · the Handwiper (§8-B, solo configuration) - Constantly wipes his hands, never getting them clean. His pod is also in the Fog Cellar; the player may pluck it for him. (+3 Campfire Ember, and he will recognize the player in the S5 Void)

Eloise Finch (§8-A): delivers the "The fire remembers" reasoning, guiding the infiltration.

7. Decision Points and Branch Results

Decision One: Generate Altruistic Hope to unlock? (core) - If the player looks out only for themselves the whole time, no key can be made, the Fog Cellar cannot be entered — only a pure escape to finish the Trial, but no Memory Card. - Branch: generate Altruistic Hope → roots loosen → enter the Fog Cellar (take Memory Card + trigger Ember Moment).

Decision Two: Pluck a pod in the Fog Cellar - Pluck your own pod → 1 Memory Card (linked with the S1 carried item). - Pluck another's pod → 1 someone-else's Memory Card (+1 Hope Point (HOP) cap, cannot be returned, the original owner will later notice). The GM should arrange for at least one player to pluck the wrong one (plucking a Killer's memory = S6 hook). - Fail to leave within the shrinking countdown → lose 3 Hope Point (HOP), force-returned to the Campfire by The Entity's "filing."

9. Ending Branches and Rewards

Ending branch: Whether to enter the Fog Cellar (depends on Decision One); pluck right / wrong pod in the Cellar (Decision Two). Plucking wrong is not failure — it is an S6 hook.

Rewards

Item Content
Bloodpoint (BP) Standard × (per player-count table)
Prestige (PRS) Rises to level 7 upon completion
Hope Point (HOP) +3
Campfire Ember Accrued during play (est. 5–9)
Memory Card 1–2 (per player-count table)
Item 1 uncommon item per person
Narrative Memory is archived, not destroyed; Ember can serve as a key
New Ability The player may spend 1 Hope Point (HOP) to "ask the Memory Card for help," rerolling one check related to that memory's theme

Ember Moment trigger text:

When you press your hand to the bark, the tree does not react. You try again. Still nothing. Then you remember — a moment ago, under the Hook, when you caught that person, the warm thing that dropped out of your chest. You reach into your pocket. It's there. You don't remember picking it up. You press it to the bark. The roots loosen like fingers. This tree eats no soil, and no blood. It recognizes only one thing — the thing it itself cannot spit out.

10. Connections to Adjacent Scenarios

  • Forward (S2): Scar #5 "The fire remembers" → Eloise reasons "memory is put somewhere" → this scenario's Fog Cellar.
  • Backward (S4): if Sally plucked the wrong pod → in S4 she will "recognize Patient Seventeen's face" but can't say why; the Memory Cards retrieved here are the material for piecing together "Patient Seventeen's Chart" in S4.
  • Hook payoff points: ① the Handwiper's plucked pod → recognizes the player in the S5 Void; ② plucking a Killer's memory → S6 Sally speaks the reforging Q&A; ③ the Fog Cellar "recognize yourself" mechanic → the error ratio in S7's Purification Sanctum.
