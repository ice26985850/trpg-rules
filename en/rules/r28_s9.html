Abyssal Expedition (S9)

Season: Chapter 3 "Expedition Chapter" Basic Information - Suggested Level: Lv.41-48 - Difficulty: Normal - Expected Length: 2-3 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.

Core Conflict

Press deep into the Beastman Forest and explore the heart of clan territory. Discover that the Beastmen are performing some kind of ritual at the World Heart — accelerating the Creeping Law Erosion.

Key Scenes

  • The dangerous ecology of the Beastman Forest — each zone a unique environmental challenge
  • Discover clues of a pre-civilization — before the Beastmen arrived in the otherworld, another civilization existed
  • The ritual site — must be interrupted but cannot be destroyed

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 4000, Purple · Unique Material ×2

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: monster swarm halved.
