Scenario: Shadow of the Wailing Plains

Scenario No.: S1 | Corresponding Campaign: Act 1 (Levels 1–4) | Suggested Players: 1–4 (compatible with solo and group play) | Estimated Playtime: ~3–5 hours | Difficulty: Novice

I. Background

The players have only recently been accepted into their school when their master dispatches them to the border village of Kagemi Village to investigate a series of disappearances.

The village sits on the edge of the Wailing Plains (Onikoehara)—a place where the lingering malice of a millennium-old battlefield has accumulated. Over the past three months, five villagers have vanished at night, leaving behind only traces of yokai aura at the scene.

II. Major Factions

Player Side (player_side)

  • Lord: School Grandmaster (determined by the GM, matching the players' school)
  • Allies:
  • Kagemi Village Chief (elderly Ox Clan, provides information and lodging)
  • Village Apothecary (Rabbit Clan, can provide healing and antidote pills)

Enemy Side (enemy_side)

  • Main Antagonist: Beastified Beast (a collective of vengeful spirits of samurai who fell in the Warring States era)
  • Minions:
  • Yokai-Eroded Bandits ×3 (CR 0.5)
  • Wraith Ashigaru ×5 (CR 0.25)

Neutral (neutral)

  • Passing Rōnin (Monkey Clan, Level 5)—can be persuaded to become a temporary companion or source of information

Involved Schools (involved_schools)

  • Player's School (player_school): The players' school—the master assigned this task as part of their training
  • Pure Land Style: The village apothecary is a mid-tier disciple of Pure Land Style and can assist in purifying yokai

III. Named-Blade Clues

Target Named Blade: None (this scenario features no predetermined named blade)

Clue Description: This scenario contains no named blade, but deep within the Wailing Plains a single Soul-Steel Shard can be found—a necessary material for forging a blade of Yōmono grade or higher. This is the first cornerstone for forging a named blade in the future.

IV. Scene Flow

Scene 1: Arrival at Kagemi Village

  • Type: Exploration / Social
  • Description: The players arrive at Kagemi Village. The village chief recounts the disappearances—five people have vanished at night over the past three months. An on-site investigation (Perception check DC 13) reveals faint traces of yokai aura pointing toward the Wailing Plains.

Checks | Skill | DC | Success Result | |------|----|----------| | Perception | 13 | Discover the yokai residue comes from the Wailing Plains to the east | | Persuasion | 15 | The chief reveals more detail: the last missing person was said to have heard "the dead calling his name" |

NPCs - Village Chief (Ox Clan)—information provider - Apothecary (Rabbit Clan)—healing and antidote support

Scene 2: Patrol at the Edge of the Wailing Plains

  • Type: Exploration / Combat
  • Description: The players enter the edge of the Wailing Plains. Along the way they encounter bandits eroded by yokai—they have been possessed by vengeful spirits. During combat, yokai continues to accumulate (Wailing Plains scenes: +1 Yokai Value (YK) per turn).

Environment - Yokai accumulation: +1 Yokai Value (YK) per turn - Description: A dim forest of dead trees, the ground covered in thin mist. Faint weeping can be heard in the distance.

Scene 3: Night of Wraiths

  • Type: Combat
  • Description: After sunset, five Wraith Ashigaru emerge from the mist. They are formed from the resentful souls of the missing villagers. Key point: Wraiths have Damage Reduction (DR) 3 against physical damage, but fire and Pure Land Style techniques deal full damage.

Tactical Note: If the players are of Pure Land Style or possess fire-based attacks, the difficulty of this fight drops sharply. Otherwise, the GM is advised to hint that the players use torches or seek the village apothecary's purification aid.

Scene 5: The Return and Reflection

  • Type: Giri Dilemma
  • Description: After defeating the Beastified Beast, the players find the belongings of the missing villagers. Among them is a letter left by the wife of the last missing person. The letter reveals: that missing person was no simple victim—he walked into the Wailing Plains of his own accord, hoping to use himself as a sacrifice to calm the vengeful spirits. Did he succeed? Or was it merely a futile sacrifice?

Giri Dilemma (Options) - Tell the chief the truth (Giri Value +2): His husband sacrificed himself willingly, but may not be seen as a hero - Conceal the truth, saying he was a victim killed by a monster (Persuasion check DC 15; success brings the village peace of mind): Is this protecting the living or deceiving them? - Conceal the truth but privately erect a grave marker for him (Giri Value +3): Respect for the dead, but unable to give the family true comfort

V. The Giri Dilemma

Theme: Truth vs. Mercy

Option Check Reward Consequence
Speak the truth — +2 Giri Value (GI) The chief is grief-stricken, but thanks you for your honesty. The apothecary considers this the true meaning of "purification"—facing the truth.
Conceal the truth Persuasion DC 15 Villagers' gratitude But your conscience is uneasy. Spirit (SPT) check at Disadvantage until the next long rest.
Privately erect a grave — +3 Giri Value (GI) No one knows the truth, but the spirit of the dead thanks you through dreams.

VI. Ending Branches

Did not obtain Soul-Steel Shard Mission complete, but with regret. The master reminds you: "Some things matter more than the mission objective—go find it."

VII. Solo Play Adjustments

The following adjustments apply to solo play (a single player). The GM should reduce difficulty accordingly:

  • Yokai-Eroded Bandit count: 3 → 1
  • Wraith Ashigaru count: 5 → 2
  • Beastified Beast Hit Points (HP): 35 → 26
  • Beastified Beast cannot summon Wraith Ashigaru (at HP < 50%, instead gains +1d4 attack)
  • Provide an NPC companion option: the passing Rōnin (Monkey Clan) can be persuaded to join
