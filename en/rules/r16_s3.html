S3: The Hotel

Level 4 → 5 | Milestones 3–4 | 7–10 hours | Intermediate Applicable Mode: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

Level 4: The Partygoers' Trap

In the corridors of Level 4 you meet two beaming "Wanderers." They invite you to a party on F5. If you accept—the party hall holds 15–30 forever-smiling people, with a transformation ritual on stage. If you refuse—they will invite you again and again, their tone growing more urgent.

After uncovering the truth, use the mirror room (F7, where Partygoers won't enter—their reflections have no faces) and the lightless room to escape.

During your flight, you blunder into an elevator that shouldn't be running. The golden doors close. The elevator descends.

Level 5: Horror Hotel

The elevator doors open.

A red carpet stretches from beneath your feet to the end of the corridor. Crystal chandeliers cast a warm light—too warm. Portraits hang on the walls—men and women in Victorian dress, their eyes moving as you pass.

A tall, thin man in a tailcoat and white gloves stands in the corridor. He smiles—a genuine, human smile—but you aren't sure which is more terrifying.

"Welcome back," he says. Then he speaks your name.

You never told anyone your name.

The Endless Corridor

The hotel's corridors have no end. Each door is carved with a name. You recognize some—Wang Jing. Lao Zhao. Chen. And... your own name.

You open the door carved with your name. Inside is your real-world furniture, your photos, your bed. Everything is there—except that beyond the window is not a street, but another hotel corridor.

The men and women in the portraits begin to whisper. They tell you the hotel's secret: there is no exit. Unless—the banquet hall.

The Banquet Hall

A long table. White tablecloth. Silver cutlery. Candlelight. The table is filled with people—in splendid gowns and suits, postures elegant. But their eyes are empty. Their mouths chew—though there is no food on the table.

At the end of the long table sits an empty chair.

"The Servant"—the faceless waiter in the tailcoat—appears at your side. Its gesture points to the empty chair. An invitation.

You may choose to sit. To join this eternal banquet. To become part of Level 5—immortal but forever trapped.

Or you may find the back door. Behind the banquet hall's curtain, an unremarkable service door—leading to Level 6.

Ending

You do not sit. You push open the back door. Behind you comes the sound of hundreds of empty eyes in the banquet hall turning to you at once.

The door closes behind you.

Complete darkness. Even the light source in your hand is struggling. You have entered Level 6.
