Case File: The Empty Coffin in the Feng Shui Woods

Suggested Level Range: Levels 6–7 Difficulty (Case Urgency): B-tier (Dangerous; standard 2–3 person combat, split operations) Case Type: Black Zombie + Resentful Ghost + Corpse-Cultivation practice point (Nine Nether Hall Hong Kong station outpost) Case EXP (EXP): 600 Core Puzzle: Following the S4 thread to the New Territories Walled Village, you discover "Seventh Uncle," head of Nine Nether Hall's Hong Kong station, has set up a "practice" corpse-cultivation point in the feng shui woods at the village edge — hiding a Black Zombie in an empty coffin, guarding a batch of "materials" awaiting refinement. Players encounter Seventh Uncle face-to-face for the first time, confirm the corpse-cultivation plan, and reach the S3 ancestral-house corpse-cultivating cellar directly.

One-line Hook: The village's Earth God temple keeper calls in urgency: "The feng shui woods has coffin-opening sounds every night, but the coffins are all empty... the Luopan points at the Walled Village ancestral house without stopping."

I. Take Case

Players bring the S4 account-book thread to the Guild for verification; Council Chair Cheng Guiyao refers them to the Walled Village temple keeper "Uncle Fuk" for help.

Cheng Guiyao (spreading the account book on the table, fingertip on the three characters "Seventh Uncle"): "The Sham Shui Po case — the account book points to the New Territories Walled Village. I've spoken with Uncle Fuk — he says the feng shui woods at the village edge has coffins opening every night. Take the thread to confront them, and see what Seventh Uncle is really about."

Uncle Fuk (voice trembling on the phone): "Ghost Collector, you really must come. The coffins in the feng shui woods... open then close, close then open, but inside they're empty. The Luopan, once placed inside, points at the ancestral house and the needle won't stop spinning."

Level Design Tip: A Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 compares the account book with the Walled Village feng shui layout, confirming "the great work" = the prototype of the ancestral-house corpse-cultivating cellar; a Social (Persuasion) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 soothes the temple keeper to come along.

II. On-Site Investigation

Scene 1: Feng Shui Woods and Earth God Temple (Oppressive Yin)

A deserted feng shui woods, wild graves and an Earth God temple surround it. As you approach, the air feels waterlogged, sound distorts, and the Luopan needle spins wildly toward the ancestral house. A smell of rotting earth mixed with incense ash drifts through the woods.

  • Environment Yin Value (YIN): base 8 (yin-cold).
  • Witnesses: temple keeper Uncle Fuk.
  • Uncle Fuk (grabbing your sleeve): "The coffin-opening sounds start after midnight. I peeked — no corpse inside, just some white-furred finger marks. Those three missing people from your S4... I heard Seventh Uncle tell his men they were shipped here to be 'materials.'" (GM Note: A Social (Interview) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 confirms the S4 missing were shipped here as materials.)
  • Spirit Identification: An Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 shows a Wandering Soul (Challenge Rating (CR) 3) drifting around the coffins in the woods; a Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 identifies "Wandering Soul" — can be delivered, not actively hostile.

Scene 2: The Empty Coffin Array and Seventh Uncle's Appearance (Hidden Info)

  • Appraisal (Knowledge (KNW)) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 opens a coffin, finding a Black Zombie (Challenge Rating (CR) 3, Hard-Skin Mitigation −3) standing still inside, with a "Materials" register pressed under the coffin bottom (including A-Ying and other S4 missing).
  • Seventh Uncle Appears: "Young one, seen too much — better not leave." First face-to-face encounter; combat optional — if you force an attack, a Prowess (PHS) + Tracking check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 is needed to catch up — mostly he escapes.
  • Hidden Info: The center of the coffin array is carved with the "Walled Village Ancestral House Acupoint-Eye Diagram" — pointing directly to the S3 corpse-cultivating cellar entrance. An Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 is needed to rub off the diagram.

Combat / Non-Combat Options - Non-Combat (Correct Solution): Black Zombie must be suppressed with sub-century tools; Wandering Soul can be delivered; Seventh Uncle is best driven off rather than fought head-on. - Combat (Second Choice): If you force an attack on Seventh Uncle, a Prowess (PHS) Tracking check is needed, or he escapes.

Level Design Tips (Scene Order): 1. Peripheral Luopan direction (Knowledge (KNW) check) → lock feng shui woods anomaly. 2. Spirit sight confirms Wandering Soul (Intuition (INS) check). 3. Open coffin to search "Materials" register (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain A-Ying and other threads. 4. Rub off the acupoint-eye diagram (Intuition (INS) check) → leads to S3; drive off Seventh Uncle when he appears.

III. Prepare Rite (Required Tools/Materials & Rite Scale)

  • Materials: Peachwood Sword, Coin Sword (immobilize, charge-up), Corpse-Still Talisman (costs Mana Pool (MP) 3), Glutinous Rice, Ink Line Reel (warding), Purification Talisman (Yin Value (YIN) −3), Deliverance materials.
  • Rite Scale: Medium-large. Breaking the coffin array requires Knowledge (KNW) to find the acupoint eye, Warrior to suppress the Black Zombie, Medium to deliver the Wandering Soul.
  • Time Pressure: Before Seventh Uncle escapes he lights "Corpse-Luring Incense"; within half an hour the Black Zombie horde wakes — must fight fast.
  • Level Tip: Altar setup uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14; Ink Line Reel array seal uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Warding check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14.

IV. Exorcise & Collect

Best Solution: Break Array, Sever Source

  1. Scholar makes Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 to find the coffin-array acupoint eye.
  2. Warrior uses Coin Sword to immobilize the Black Zombie (Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14), glutinous rice draws out corpse poison.
  3. Medium makes Intuition (INS) + Communication check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 to soothe the Wandering Soul, deliver (costs Mana Pool (MP) 8).
  4. Seize the "Ancestral House Acupoint-Eye Diagram," force back Seventh Uncle (drive off, not kill).

Potential Risks

  • Black Zombie Hard-Skin Mitigation −3: requires spirit tools; when the Corpse-Luring Incense burns out the Black Zombie horde wakes (Area-of-Effect (AOE) crisis).
  • Seventh Uncle if Cornered: may release a Resentful Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 3) to block the way.

Alternatives

  • If no Coin Sword, use Soul-Calming Bell (Rare, all spirits below mid-tier held still 3 rounds) to control the field.

V. Cleanup

  • Purification: Purification Talisman (Yin Value (YIN) −3) × several sheets + Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 pushes the feng shui woods' Yin Value (YIN) below 6.
  • Client Feedback: Uncle Fuk kowtows; Cheng Guiyao records reputation for "Defeating Nine Nether Hall Outpost."
  • Case-Closing Reward: Nether Coin (COIN) 500–700; Experience Points (EXP) = base 200 (B-tier) + investigation discoveries +20×5 + correct array-breaking handling +100 + cleanup +80 + unresolved hook (acupoint-eye diagram) +40 + Seventh Uncle confrontation/drive-off +60 = total about 600.
  • Unresolved Mystery (leads to S3):
  • Before escaping, Seventh Uncle sneers "The Walled Village ancestral house is the real great work; you'll come sooner or later";
  • The acupoint-eye diagram matches the S3 Luopan anomaly.

Ending Hook: You spread out the rubbed-off acupoint-eye diagram, overlapping with the urgent Walled Village ancestral-house plea the Guild received — the village chief said, whoever goes in doesn't come out.
