Fate of Two Worlds (S20)

Season: Chapter 5 "Godfall Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.100+ - Difficulty: Mythic - Expected Length: 4-6 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

The endgame. The players, with all their allies, equipment, and knowledge, enter the World Heart. There — not one, but the remaining five clan chief gods' true bodies await. This is the battle that decides the fate of two worlds.

Main Locations

  • World Heart (final labyrinth, 10 regions)

Key Scenes

  • Final Alliance — all recruited allies gather here
  • Depths of the World Heart — discover that the Rift can be permanently closed or permanently opened
  • The Final Choice — the divergence point of five endings

Rewards

Depends on ending (Experience Points (EXP) 50000+, Legendary Achievement)

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Each chief-god true body adjusted per solo rules, ally assistance increased
