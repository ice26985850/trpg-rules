Scenario SA-2: "Soliloquy of the Ecclesiarchy Chapel" — Rust Crown's Soliloquy

Difficulty: Low | Inquisitor: Selena (Puritan)
Target Region: Tarr-Morag Hive, Upper Hive (Level 350), abandoned Ecclesiarchy chapel
Recommended Attributes: Level 3 | Challenge Rating (CR) Budget: 2 (Standard)

Background

A small Ecclesiarchy chapel in the Upper Hive has fallen silent for three months. You are sent to investigate — alone. Selena says "Faith needs no witnesses." She also assigned an Ecclesiarchy acolyte to accompany you.

NPC Companion

Sister Eleanor (Ecclesiarchy Acolyte)

Item Content
One-line description A young Ecclesiarchy acolyte, devout in faith but who has never witnessed true Warp contamination
Motivation Prove her faith is steadfast enough to be elevated to a full Sister
Bottom Line Never question the Emperor
Initial Attitude +1 (Trust — she regards this mission as an honor)
Attributes Faith 4 / Body 2 / Mind 3 / Tech 2 / Fate 3
Key Skills Oratory (Trained), Lore (Imperium) (Trained)
Equipment Pistol (1d6), Flak Vest (Armor Rating (AR) 1), Emperor Icon
Special Ability Light of Faith: Once per scenario, restore 2 Faith to the player (through shared prayer). Deals 2d4 holy damage to low-rank Warp entities (like Holy Water)

Key Checks

Scene Check Success Failure
Search chapel diary Investigation (Standard) Find 3 pages of diary Find only 1 page
Soothe the guardian's shade Faith (Difficult) or Eleanor's assistance Shade dissipates, receive warning Shade attacks
Eleanor witnesses Warp phenomenon Player soothes her with Oratory (Standard) She maintains faith, Doubt Points do not increase Doubt Points +2

Internal Seduction Trigger

When facing Chaos items at the underground altar of the chapel:

"Eleanor's faith is fragile. Let her touch that thing — see what happens. A corrupted acolyte could become a useful tool."
