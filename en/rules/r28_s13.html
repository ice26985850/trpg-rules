Total War (S13)

Season: Chapter 4 — "Final Battle Chapter" Basic Info - Suggested Level: Lv.61-68 - Difficulty: Hard - Expected Length: 3-5 game sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

The fourth wave of invasion begins. A-Rank Rifts open simultaneously in five major cities across the globe. The players must choose a battlefield — or rush between multiple battlefields.

Main Location

Key Scenes

Rewards
