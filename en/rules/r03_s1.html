Scenario s01: Dessert Syndicate Crisis

Mode: Single-player (1 GM + 1 player acting as the Supervisor) Source: Z Corporation TRPG — Chapter 2 main Crisis Contract Estimated Duration: 4–8 hours Difficulty: ★★★☆☆ (Intermediate) Required Files: Z Corporation_Player Rulebook.md, Z Corporation_GM Rulebook.md (Chapter 2)

I. Background

The Dessert Syndicate is a group formed by dessert-related Sorcerers such as the Ice Cream Witch. Mr. Tian, a Platinum Member and monopolist in the dessert market, operates through shady but legal means, and is pushing hard to escalate this situation into a "Crisis Contract," using the corporation's systems to crush his competitors.

You (the Supervisor) have just found your footing in Chapter 2 when Fangfang reports an escalating crisis. The Board of Directors' eyes are on you — how you handle this dessert war will determine your standing in the corporation, and may also win you a powerful dessert Sorcerer as an ally.

II. Preparation

  • GM: Read thoroughly Z Corporation_GM Rulebook.md Chapter 2; prepare the data cards for "Ice Cream Witch" and "Mr. Tian" (see the Employee & NPC Codex).
  • Player: At least 3 Sorcerer employees (any grade), Satisfaction (Man Yi Du) ≥ 60, Capital (Zijin) ≥ 100.

III. Initial Situation State

Situation State Description
Positive 0/10 Weaken Mr. Tian's influence
Negative 2/10 Tensions escalating

IV. Flow (Three Phases)

Phase 1: Crisis Report

Fangfang reports the crisis, then Mr. Tian contacts you proposing cooperation. The GM reads the report text aloud, and the player chooses: - Accept Mr. Tian's cooperation: Ally with the Intelligence Department; the route leans toward maintaining stability. - Refuse cooperation: Handle it yourself, opening up more variables.

This phase is a narrative choice; no checks.

Phase 2: Strength Assessment

Fangfang determines the branching route based on "Squad Strength vs. Threat" (the GM assesses based on the total grade of employees and mission risk): - Strength > Threat → Enter [Branch 1] - Strength < Threat → Enter [Branch 2]

Branch 1 (Strength > Threat) — Normal Route

  • Choice A: Cooperate with the Intelligence Department to prop up Mr. Tian → Result: the Ice Cream Witch cannot be recruited (consolidates relations with Mr. Tian and the Intelligence Department).
  • Choice B: Take down Mr. Tian → Result: the Ice Cream Witch can be recruited after the crisis is successfully resolved.

Specific scenes (expanding on Choice B): 1. Sneak into the Warehouse: d100 Intelligence check (success if ≤ Intelligence value); failure triggers an alarm, Negative +1. 2. Sabotage the Production Line: d100 Stamina check; success grants Positive +2. 3. BOSS Battle · Ice Cream Witch: d100 Cursed Energy / Stamina check; victory grants Positive +3 and unlocks recruitment.

Branch 2 (Strength < Threat) — High-Risk Route

  • Choice A: Negotiate with the Advanced Training Department, then join forces to annihilate the Dessert Syndicate → Threat completely eliminated, but the chance to recruit a special Sorcerer is lost.
  • Choice B: Maintain the status quo, unlocking the "Reconciliation Route" → opens four parallel negotiations (Mr. Tian / Intelligence Department / Early-Warning Department / Dessert Syndicate each with independent progress bars).

V. Ending Determination

  • Positive reaches 10/10 → The Supervisor wins and advances to Chapter 3.
  • Negative reaches 10/10 → The crisis intensifies, advancing to the next phase (if Chapter 4 deployment ends without progress, directly compare Positive/Negative magnitudes).
  • Chapter 4 deployment ends without advancing → Directly compare Positive and Negative values to determine the result.

VI. Recruiting the Ice Cream Witch

  • Condition: The crisis is successfully resolved, and "Take down Mr. Tian" was chosen.
  • Method: Recruit via communication; conducting the onboarding communication unlocks the "Dessert Exclusive Bonus" — when dessert-related Sorcerers carry out the crisis's covert missions, grid occupation −1, and all attribute grades are treated as one level higher during checks.

VII. GM Tips

  • The narrative potential of the Ice Cream Witch's three modes (Aerial Flight / Materialization / Delicious Ice) is detailed in the GM Rulebook.
  • Failure penalty baseline: Silver-grade city contract Satisfaction −20; abandonment counts as double the failure penalty.
  • Details of the Dessert Exclusive Bonus and the Crisis Contract structure are found in Z Corporation_Player Rulebook.md and the GM Rulebook.
