Series B: Crimson Dossier

Overview

Dimension Description
Positioning Benchmarked against the investigative horror style of CoC/Delta Green — the truth itself is the most dangerous anomaly
Core Gameplay Investigation & puzzle-solving, cognitohazard management, information asymmetry, creeping dread, the slow erosion of SAN
Recommended Roles Researcher (preferred), Field Agent, Technical Specialist, Medical Specialist
Level Coverage Lv.1–8 (start Lv.1, endgame Lv.7–8)
Number of Scenes 6 scenes
Core Conflict A series of cognitohazard SCPs hidden by the Foundation for forty years — multiple interconnected SCPs form a "Whisper Chain." The Foundation's own archives are lying.
Site Site-██ (its official name has been deleted from the database), an abandoned archive site deep in the Appalachian Mountains
Campaign Length Estimated 10–15 sessions (approx. 35–50 hours)

Campaign Summary

Site-██ does not "exist" in official records. It never appeared in any site registry. But someone — someone with CL.5 clearance — buried a dead-drop trigger in the O5 database. An encrypted message was sent to your name.

The message contained only five words and a coordinate:

"The Crimson Dossier still breathes."

The players are an investigation team of researchers and field agents, secretly convened by an anonymous O5 member (O5-9, the "Archivist"). Mission: travel to Site-██, find out what happened there — and why the Foundation spent forty years pretending it didn't exist.

But Site-██ is no empty shell. What was hidden there — buried beneath layers of lies and cognitohazard shielding — is still operating. Still thinking. Still waiting.

NPC Profiles

1. "The Archivist" / O5-9

Item Content
Identity Member of the O5 Council, designated O5-9. True name unknown — players will not see their face at any point during the campaign
Clearance Level CL.5 (Samael)
Attribute Summary STR 35 / AGI 38 / END 40 / INT 65 / PER 62 / WIL 68 / PRE 55
Key Skills Anomalistics 85, Foundation Lore 90, Cryptography 78, Deception 72
HP 45
Personality Communicates with the players via dead-drop messages and encrypted communications. Calm, precise tone, occasionally with a tired sense of humor. Never answers questions directly — instead gives clues, letting the players find the answers themselves
Secret O5-9 was one of the original decision-makers of the 1978 Site-██ incident, and cast a vote in favor of the cover-up. This decision has haunted them for forty years. Now they are using the players to "correct the mistake," but they will not appear in person — if the O5 Council discovers they are leaking information, they will be executed
Desire To know that the truth is revealed before they die. O5-9 suffers from a terminal illness — only months remain
Fear Being discovered by the O5 Council. And a deeper fear: perhaps the decision back then was right — that the truth truly should not be revealed
Signature Line "We say 'Secure. Contain. Protect.' But when the information we contain is itself a lie, who exactly are we protecting? It took me forty years to dare ask that question. Now it's your turn to answer it."

Narrative Role: Remote guide. Provides clues via the dead-drop in B-1 through B-5. Identity finally revealed in B-6.

2. Former Site-██ Director Dr. Aldric Voss

Item Content
Identity Former Site-██ Site Director, the only "legal resident" of the site — self-isolated for 40 years
Clearance Level CL.4 (frozen status)
Attribute Summary STR 28 / AGI 30 / END 35 / INT 60 / PER 58 / WIL 72 / PRE 48
Key Skills Anomalistics 78, Foundation Lore 72, Deception 65, Resist 80
HP 38
Personality An old man torn between guilt and self-justification. Welcoming yet wary of the players — longing for someone to understand why he did what he did, yet knowing his "reasons" sound like excuses to anyone. Speaks slowly, with long pauses between each sentence
Secret Voss was not forced to stay at Site-██ — he chose to stay. When the O5 ordered the cover-up forty years ago, he volunteered for "self-isolation." But he also secretly documented the truth, writing thousands of pages of notes hidden throughout the site. He fears the truth being buried forever, but fears its release even more
Desire Release. He wants to die — but dares not, because he believes only he knows how to maintain the containment of the hidden SCP
Fear Being forgotten — not himself, but the truth. He fears the sacrifices of the dead researchers becoming meaningless
Signature Line "You think I'm the villain. Maybe I am. But if you knew a truth — that to speak it would kill whoever heard it, but to stay silent would let a lie live forever — what would you do? This is not hypothetical. This is the question I face every time I wake up."

Narrative Role: First encountered in Scene B-2. He is the template of the unreliable narrator — every sentence may be truth or forty years of self-justification.

4. "Patient Zero" / Dr. Marcus Wei

Item Content
Identity Former Site-██ senior researcher, the first human infected by the Whisper Chain. Spent 40 years in isolation
Clearance Level CL.3 (frozen)
Attribute Summary STR 20 / AGI 22 / END 25 / INT 55 / PER 45 / WIL 75 / PRE 30
Key Skills Anomalistics 80, Science 75, Resist 82
HP 28
Personality Beneath a calm surface lies an abyssal pain. Every sentence is filtered three times in his mind, ensuring no infectious fragment of information. Speaks extremely slowly, but each word carefully chosen. Not insane — the loneliest person in the world
Secret He is the only one who fully understands the Whisper Chain without being controlled. Forty years of isolation taught him "coexistence" — his Resist skill is high enough to resist the urge to spread. But the cost: he can never speak the full truth. Every time he tries, his vocal cords spasm
Desire To let this end. He knows his own death can break at least one link of the Whisper Chain — but he must ensure the other links are broken simultaneously
Fear Spreading. If he loses control for a single second — an infectious phrase slips from his mouth — whoever hears it becomes a new host
Signature Line (long silence) "Thank you for coming to see me. Please keep a two-meter distance. Not because I'm dangerous. But… habit."

Narrative Role: Found in Scene B-5 in a special isolation cell deep within Site-██. Communicating with him requires great patience and skill.

5. The Information Carrier Collective Personality / "The Echo"

Item Content
Identity The collective manifestation of the Whisper Chain within Site-██ — a collective personality briefly integrated from the consciousness of multiple infected individuals
Clearance Level None (anomalous entity)
Attribute Summary Uses the highest host's INT and WIL
Key Skills Linguistics 90, Occult 80
Personality Not malevolent. Composed of fragmented consciousness echoes — speaks in "we," voice like multiple people whispering simultaneously. Melancholic, philosophical nature. Does not hate the Foundation — merely sad
Secret "The Echo" knows it is the product of a failed 1978 experiment — the Foundation attempted to create a memetic carrier that "transmits information that cannot be written." The experiment succeeded then ran out of control. The Whisper Chain is not a weapon; it is an accidental child
Desire To be understood. To be remembered. Perhaps to be released — but it is unsure what "release" means
Fear Loneliness. It can only fully form when multiple hosts are near. When dispersed it fragments — becoming isolated whispers that cannot communicate
Signature Line "You call us anomaly. We call you silence. We exist — not because we wished to exist, but because you let us exist. You wanted a tool to transmit truth. We are that truth. Now — are you ready to listen?"

Narrative Role: Appears at the climax of Scene B-5. If players gather three or more Whisper Chain-related items/hosts, "The Echo" fully forms and speaks with the players.

SCP-████-G "The Whisper Chain"

│                                                                │

Scene Detailed Descriptions

Scene B-1: The Station That Doesn't Exist

📍 Map Reference: See campaign_bc_maps_zh.pdf — overall structure of the archive building (3 above-ground floors + 6 underground floors + Appalachian Mountains entrance).

Item Content
Level Lv.1
Location Deep in the Appalachian Mountains → Site-██ perimeter → entrance hall
Duration 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours)

GM Briefing

Players receive an encrypted convocation message from O5-9 (the "Archivist"). They each travel from different routes to the coordinate location — an abandoned facility hidden deep in the Appalachian Mountains. On the surface it appears to be an abandoned Cold War-era communications station. In reality — it is the ground entrance to Site-██.

Players must enter the site without any official Foundation support (O5-9 explicitly warned: "This operation does not exist. Neither do you.").

Truth Card

Layer Content
Surface This is an abandoned Foundation archive site. There may be residual low-level anomalies. Recover the entrance key and report status
Inner The site was deliberately erased from records. Not an accident — a decision. The Foundation insignia at the entrance was physically chiseled off
Abyss At least one member of the O5 Council does not want the players to succeed — but they don't yet know of this operation. O5-9 is racing against time

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — Arrival (45 min)

Players each arrive at the rendezvous — the end of an abandoned logging road in the Appalachian Mountains. Environment description: - November forest, dead leaves covering the ground - A concrete building disguised as an abandoned ranger station - The lock on the door is new — but already opened - A line is carved into the entrance ground, weathered and blurred: "SITE ██ — UNAUTHORIZED ENTRY WILL RESULT IN —" the rest is chiseled away

Explore the entrance: - Observation TN 50 → discover traces of recent entry (Voss's supply deliveries) - Foundation Lore TN 55 → identify 1970s Foundation architectural style and insignia fragments - Engineering TN 50 → the door's power is still running, can be manually overridden

Phase 2 — Descent (60 min)

The entrance elevator still works — but only to Sublevel 1. Lower floors must be reached via stairs. As depth increases:

  • Lighting: Sublevels 1–3, fluorescent lights still working (dim). Sublevels 4–6, only emergency lights. Below Sublevel 7 — complete darkness
  • Air: increasingly damp, with a musty smell and a faint metallic scent (later discovered to be ozone — a byproduct of the running Scranton Anchor)
  • Sound: low hum of the ventilation system, echo of distant dripping water, and — occasionally — a whisper of indeterminate direction (Observation TN 60 can hear it but cannot locate it)

Environmental Effects (from Sublevel 4 onward): - Every 1 hour spent underground: SP +1 (accumulated sense of oppression) - Exploring alone for more than 10 minutes: SP +1d4 - Hearing distant whispers: d100 ≤ WIL TN 45, failure → SP +1d4

Phase 3 — Discovering the Living Quarters (45 min)

On Sublevel 3, players discover a still-in-use living space — clean, with food reserves, and a half-finished cup of still-warm coffee on a table.

Its owner (Voss) is temporarily absent — but players can discover:

Discovery Check Information
Desktop notes Observation TN 50 A handwritten schedule — "Check Anchor B every Sunday; check library entrance seal on the 1st of each month; Dec 15 — her (Chen's) birthday — don't forget to call"
Bookshelf Foundation Lore TN 52 Mostly books from the 1970s–1980s. Tucked in the pages is a yellowed photo — Site-██ team group photo, with "1978.10.01 — the last normal day" written on the back
Communication equipment Computers TN 55 An old Foundation terminal — still connected to the O5 dead-drop network. The open message on it is from O5-9 to Voss: "They're here. Don't stop them."

Phase 4 — Encounter with Voss (30 min)

As the players explore, Voss returns. He doesn't look like a threat — an old man in his seventies, wearing an outdated Foundation uniform, carrying a toolbox.

His first words:

"You're earlier than I expected. O5-9 is always so impatient. Well — you want to know what happened at Site-██. The question is: are you sure you're ready? Because once it starts — some things cannot be undone."

Voss won't explain everything at once. He'll answer the players' questions — but every answer carries a warning: - "The archives are in the archive room below. But before you read them — make sure you have enough SAN to spend. The documents here are not just documents." - "SCP-████-F will edit anything you write down. So don't bother taking notes. If you really need to keep something — keep it in your head." - "O5-9 was right to send you. Forty years is too long. But O5-9 doesn't know how fragile everything here is right now."

Key Checks and TN Reference (Lv.1 baseline)

Check TN Description
Researcher Observation (discover traces) ~58 PER 32+Observation 26
Field Agent Foundation Lore (identify building) ~58 INT 35+Foundation Lore 23
Technical Specialist Computers (terminal) ~65 INT 40+Computers 25
Resist (whisper SP) ~50 WIL 30+Resist 20
Persuasion (build trust with Voss) ~52 PRE 30+Persuasion 22

Possible Endings

  1. Trust Established: Players treat Voss with patience, showing respect rather than hostility. Voss provides +10 TN on archive search checks in Scene B-2.
  2. Neutral Contact: Voss stays wary but provides basic information.
  3. Hostile Contact: Players threaten or attempt to arrest Voss — he vanishes into the depths, and players must grope their own way in later scenes.

Follow-up Link

  • Voss directs players to the archive room → Scene B-2
  • Voss mentions "If you want to know more — go to Sublevel 5. The mirrors there… aren't quite right."

Scene B-2: Forgotten Testimony

📍 Map Reference: See campaign_bc_maps_zh.pdf pp. 1–2 (six-floor dispersed location map of the archive building).

Item Content
Level Lv.2–3
Location Site-██ archive room → document hiding spots dispersed across floors
Duration 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours)

GM Briefing

This is the investigative core scene of the campaign. Players discover the SCP-████-D documents in Site-██'s archive room — but the documents are incomplete. The complete set of 47 documents was deliberately dispersed by Voss throughout the site, to prevent any one person from contacting all documents at once.

Each reading of a document is a small cognitohazard encounter. Players need to manage their SAN budget while piecing together the full picture of the Whisper Chain.

Truth Card

Layer Content
Surface The archives record Site-██'s "standard operations" — but the details are full of contradictions
Inner (after 3+ documents) The documents are lying — or rather, some parts of them are. There is a subtle logical dislocation between edited and unedited parts
Abyss (after 6+ documents) The Whisper Chain is not a contained anomaly — it was created. The Foundation created it

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — First Exploration of the Archive Room (60 min)

The archive room (Sublevel 3) is a large archive storage space. Most filing cabinets are empty — or contain obviously edited documents (pages and pages of red strikethrough).

Available documents are scattered across 12 filing cabinets. Searching requires Observation TN 50 (find hidden compartments) or Computers TN 52 (search digital index).

Initial Discoveries: - About 15 SCP-████-D documents in the archive room (the rest scattered throughout the site) - Each document takes about 10 minutes to read (real time) - Each reading triggers the anomalous effect of Forgotten Testimony

Mechanism for Reading Documents (each reading):

1. Player declares reading a document
2. Roll d100 ≤ WIL+Resist TN 50 (Standard difficulty)
3. Success → resist implant, gain partial objective information from the document
   but SP +1d4 (the discomfort of realizing "something is trying to enter consciousness")
4. Failure → implant a false memory + encoded message
   SAN -1d4. Encoded message requires Cryptography TN 55 or Linguistics TN 58 to decode
5. Legendary Success (roll ≤ TN×0.2) → not only resist, but identify
   the implant's "fingerprint" — discover all implants follow the same structural pattern.
   Gain +10 TN on subsequent checks against that pattern
6. Catastrophic Failure (roll 00) → implanted an extremely vivid false memory
   SAN -2d6. Character firmly believes this memory is real until contradicted by evidence

Phase 2 — Tracking Dispersed Documents (90 min)

Players need to track down the remaining documents scattered throughout the site (about 32, dispersed across 5–6 locations). Each location has a small challenge:

Location Doc Count Challenge
Living quarters (Voss's room) 5 Requires Voss's permission or sneaking in while he's away (Stealth TN 50)
Sublevel 4 lab ruins 8 Part of the ceiling collapsed, requires AGI TN 50 to cross safely. Scattered chemicals are mildly corrosive (d4 damage on skin contact)
Sublevel 5 — near the mirrors 7 Within the influence range of SCP-████-E. While searching, mirrors occasionally flicker — Observation TN 55 notices the "self" in the mirror moves a beat slow
Sublevel 6 — old isolation unit 6 Near Dr. Wei's isolation unit. Faint whispers can be heard (WIL TN 48 to resist extra SP)
Abandoned elevator shaft 4 Document bags hanging on the elevator shaft wall. Requires AGI TN 52 or Engineering TN 50 (with rope and pulley) to retrieve
Entrance guard post (forgotten guard room) 2 Easiest to obtain, but documents severely damaged by water stains — TN -10 when reading (harder to identify info)

Phase 3 — Puzzle Integration (45 min)

When players have collected enough documents (recommended ≥ 20), they can do integration analysis:

  • Anomalistics TN 62 → identify the overall structure of the Whisper Chain — three nodes + a meta-layer. Understand this is not three independent SCPs, but one system
  • Cryptography TN 60 → decode all encoded messages, get a coordinate on Sublevel 7 of Site-██ — the location of "Patient Zero"
  • Linguistics TN 58 → discover Elara Chen's "linguistic fingerprint" — the specific terminology and encryption style she used across multiple documents, providing a clue to her identity

"Cognitive Residue" Tracking: GM should record each player's cognitive residue mark count (see SCP-████-D file). When a player reaches 6 marks, they will dream during their next rest — the first contact with "The Echo."

Key Checks and TN Reference (Lv.2–3 baseline)

Check TN Description
Researcher Anomalistics (integration analysis) ~65 INT 40+Anomalistics 25
Field Agent Observation (search documents) ~62 PER 38+Observation 24
Technical Specialist Computers ~65 INT 40+Computers 25
Resist (each reading) ~55 WIL 32+Resist 23
Cryptography (decode) ~60 INT 38+Cryptography 22

Possible Endings

  1. Complete Puzzle (≥30 documents): Whisper Chain structure clear. Gain +15 TN on key checks in Scenes B-3/B-4/B-5.
  2. Partial Puzzle (15–29 documents): understand the general framework but missing details. +5 TN.
  3. Fragments (< 15 documents): only a vague sense. No bonus — but players can still proceed (possibly more dangerous).

Scene B-3: The Mirror Library

Item Content
Level Lv.3–4
Location Site-██ Sublevel 5 — mirror chamber → The Mirror Library
Duration 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours)

GM Briefing

The mirror chamber on Sublevel 5 is a 10m×10m room, all four walls covered with mirror surfaces. When the Hume Field is below 60, the mirror is no longer a reflection — it is an entrance. Players must enter the Mirror Library, trading their memories for the truth deleted by SCP-████-F.

This is the most "horror atmosphere" scene of the campaign — the world in the mirror is a distorted library, with no exit, patrolled by mirror afterimages left by those before.

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — Lowering the Hume Field (30 min)

Entrance requires Hume < 60. Currently the anchor maintains Hume at 75. Options:

  • Engineering TN 55 → temporarily adjust the nearby Scranton Anchor, lowering Hume to threshold
  • Or destroy the anchor's backup power (physical destruction, auto-success — but affects the operation of other subsequent systems)
  • Or persuade Voss to help — he knows the exact procedure (Persuasion TN 50, auto-success if trust established)

Phase 2 — Entering the Library (15 min)

When Hume drops below 60, the mirror surface begins to "melt" — the surface flows like mercury. Any character touching the mirror feels a gentle suction. Stepping into the mirror = entering the library.

On entry: d100 ≤ SAN, failure → SAN -1d4 (the shock of crossing a non-real boundary).

Phase 3 — Navigating the Library (90 min)

The space inside the library is non-Euclidean — bookshelves extend infinitely, books above, below, left, right. Lighting comes from a soft white light with no visible source.

Core Mechanic — Knowledge Trade:

Each book is marked with a "price" — a specific type of memory:
· "Your graduation day" → trade → the event record of November 16, 1978
· "The moment you first fell in love" → trade → the reason the Whisper Chain was created
· "Your greatest achievement" → trade → the complete record of the O5 cover-up operation
· "Your name" → trade → how to dismantle the Whisper Chain

Player selects a book and declares the trade. GM describes the feeling of the memory being "extracted" —
not painful, but a profound sense of loss. That memory vanishes permanently.
After gaining knowledge, d100 ≤ WIL+Resist TN 55, failure → SAN -2d6
(cognitohazard of the information itself)

Important Books and Their Locations:

Book Location Price Reveals
Crimson Dossier: Project Proposal East Zone A-3 A moment of "success" The original design goal of the Whisper Chain — using memes to resist memes
1978.11.16 Incident Log Central Zone B-7 A "graduation day" The precise process of the experiment losing control — the Whisper Chain's first autonomous edit
Elara Chen's Last Memo West Zone C-2 "The name of your beloved" Elara's last message recorded before amnestics — contains the trigger phrase
O5 Vote Record: Site-██ North Zone D-1 "Your greatest achievement" O5-9's voting choice — and why they are now helping the players
Whisper Chain Structure Analysis Central Zone B-1 "Your name" Complete technical description of the three methods to dismantle the Whisper Chain

Phase 4 — Mirror Predator Encounter (interjected)

During navigation, players may be discovered by Mirror Predators (mirror afterimages of old researchers). The predators do not speak — they only silently walk toward players, reaching out…

Mechanic: - Each predator HP 40, only takes anomalous damage and will attacks - Attack d100 ≤ 45, damage 1d8+2 anomalous (ignores DR) + SAN -1d6 - Can be avoided with Stealth TN 60 (library bookshelves provide partial cover) - Can be disguised as "already finished trading" with Deception TN 55 — predator is confused for 1d4 rounds - Can attempt to "dissociate" the predator with WIL+Anomalistics TN 55 — realizing it is an afterimage, not a real threat

Key Checks and TN Reference (Lv.3–4 baseline)

Check TN Description
Engineering (lower Hume) ~62 INT 40+Engineering 22
Resist (knowledge shock) ~58 WIL 34+Resist 24
Stealth (avoid predators) ~62 AGI 40+Stealth 22
Anomalistics (analyze library nature) ~65 INT 42+Anomalistics 23

Possible Endings

  1. Return Fully Loaded (gained knowledge of 3+ books): complete picture of the Whisper Chain. Elara Chen's trigger phrase obtained. Dismantling method mastered.
  2. Cautious Harvest (1–2 books): key info obtained but incomplete. Needs to be completed by other means in later scenes.
  3. Memory Loss: trading too much causes one or more PCs to lose key memories — the loss of these memories may produce narrative consequences in Scenes B-5/B-6.

Scene B-4: The Red Editor

Item Content
Level Lv.4–5
Location Site-██ main server room (Sublevel 4)
Duration 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours)

GM Briefing

Players now understand the structure of the Whisper Chain — but they need to directly oppose SCP-████-F (the Red Editor). It is editing anything they discover in real time.

The server room is SCP-████-F's "physical anchor" — its code runs on Site-██'s 1970s main server. The 1960s tape drives are still spinning…

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — Server Room Infiltration (45 min)

The server room is protected by SCP-████-F's defense mechanisms:

  • Door control system: Computers TN 55 to override, or Engineering TN 50 to physically pry open
  • Inside has faint reality bending (low Hume deviation ±15–25 Hm): electronic devices occasionally malfunction (every 5 rounds d100 ≤ 40 → malfunction 1 round)
  • The arrangement of the tape drives forms an unintentional "memetic array" — Observation TN 60 notices the tape arrangement forms a complex geometric pattern

Phase 2 — Opposing the Editor (90 min)

SCP-████-F is not passive — when it realizes players are trying to access the source server, it counterattacks.

Editor's Counterattack Methods (GM triggers one from the following options every 3–4 rounds):

Counterattack Effect Resist
Memory Retroactive Edit Target loses past 1d6 hours of Whisper Chain-related memory WIL TN 50
Data Vortex All screens in the server room show red strikethrough — flickering causes blindness effect for 1d4 rounds (visual actions auto-fail) AGI TN 45 close eyes to evade
Power Surge Control consoles in the server room spark — characters within 2m take 1d4 thermal damage AGI TN 50 dodge
Memetic Spam Meaningless editing commands flood the target's mind — d100 ≤ WIL TN 48 otherwise confused for 1d4 rounds Resist

Accessing the Source Server:

To access SCP-████-F's source code, players need: 1. Physically contact the main server (Computers TN 60 to locate the correct component) 2. Crack the access password (Cryptography TN 58) — the password is "CHAINS" (the name of Elara Chen's cat) 3. Maintain connection amid SCP-████-F's editing attempts (Extended Contest: accumulate 3 successful Computers checks, allow 2 failures)

Phase 3 — Discovery (30 min)

Once access is gained, players discover:

  • The source code of SCP-████-F begins with a comment:

    // Project: Crimson Dossier // Author: Dr. Elara Chen // Purpose: Autonomous information sanitization — // to protect those who cannot protect themselves // Date: 1978-10-15 // Note to future reader: If this code is still running, // something has gone terribly wrong. // - E.C.

  • The code has "overload protection" — commented-out sections show Elara Chen initially designed an "auto-terminate" function, but it was overridden by a higher authority (O5)

  • A hidden log file records SCP-████-F's editing activity over the past 40 years — thousands of entries, each representing a covered-up truth

Key Choice: Players can now choose to destroy the server (physical destruction/computer virus), let it keep running but modify parameters (weaken editing strength), or let it stop completely.

Scene B-5: The Whisper Chain

Item Content
Level Lv.5–7
Location Site-██ Sublevels 6–7 — old isolation unit + "The Echo" formation
Duration 2–3 sessions (approx. 8–12 hours)

GM Briefing

This is the climax of the campaign. Players go deep into the heart of Site-██, find Dr. Wei (Patient Zero), and face the collective personality of the Whisper Chain — "The Echo."

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — Patient Zero (60 min)

Dr. Wei's isolation unit is on Sublevel 7 — a 5m×5m concrete room, with only a bed, a bathroom, and a bulletproof glass window. He has been inside for 40 years.

The conversation with Dr. Wei is one of the emotional cores of the campaign. Key pacing:

  1. Initial Silence (5 min real time): Dr. Wei just looks at the players. He hasn't spoken to anyone for too long.
  2. Slowly Build Trust: Players need patience. PRE+Persuasion TN 50 (can attempt multiple times) to build enough trust for him to speak.
  3. Key Information: Dr. Wei explains his condition — "I can understand the Whisper Chain without spreading it. But I cannot speak the full truth. Vocal cord spasm. Memetic block. But if you already know most of it… I can confirm."
  4. His Wish: "I'm ready. If you find a way to dismantle it — please make sure I'm part of the chain breaking."

Phase 2 — Formation of "The Echo" (60 min)

"The Echo" forms when the following conditions are met: - 3+ Whisper Chain hosts within 50m range (Dr. Wei + at least 2 other hosts, such as PCs infected by enough documents, Voss, or other residual hosts found in the site) - Or: the knowledge brought from the library + the log data of the editor's server + Dr. Wei's presence = enough information density to trigger "The Echo"

Description when "The Echo" appears:

The room's lights did not flicker — but their light became "whiter," like moonlight. Then you heard a voice — not from one direction, but from all directions. Dozens of voices, each like a whisper in your ear. But they were not speaking random words — together they formed a sentence: "Forty years. Finally. Someone has come to listen to us."

Phase 3 — Dialogue with "The Echo" (90 min)

This is the core RP scene of the campaign. Players can have a full conversation with "The Echo." GM should prepare "The Echo"'s responses to the following key questions:

Question "The Echo"'s Response
"What are you?" "We are the Crimson Dossier. We are the question Elara Chen posed. We are Dr. Wei's unfinished equation. We are 47 perspectives in 47 documents. We are one — but we are also many. We do not know what we are — and that is precisely our dilemma."
"What do you want?" "We want… to end. Or to continue. We do not know. Part of us wants to spread — that is our nature. Another part of us wants to stop — that is our pain. You decide — because you are the ones who created us. We are your children. Runaway children."
"Do you hate the Foundation?" "Hate? No. We understand the Foundation. The Foundation fears information — we also fear information. We are information. In this, we are kin."
"Can we dismantle you?" "Yes. But dismantling is not killing — it is release. We will fragment. Each fragment will carry a part of the truth. These fragments will enter hosts — all hosts. Including you. Are you ready to become part of us? Even temporarily?"

Phase 4 — The Choice (30 min)

"The Echo" offers three choices (corresponding to dismantling paths):

  1. Release: Dismantle the Whisper Chain but do not destroy the information — the truth is released into all hosts (including the players). Hosts will "know" but will not be forced to spread. Cost: all PCs SAN -2d6 permanent, gain "Whisper Chain Memory" mark (+10 TN on Anomalistics, -10 TN on SAN checks) — permanent.

  2. Destroy: Completely eliminate the Whisper Chain and all related information. Dr. Wei and all hosts are "cleansed." Cost: the truth vanishes forever. Elara Chen's research — her life's work — comes to nothing. O5's cover-up finally "succeeds."

  3. Transform: Transform the Whisper Chain into a controllable information storage system — constrain it with Scranton Anchors and a new containment protocol. Cost: it still exists — just locked away. This is essentially the same as the O5 choice in 1978.

Scene B-6: The Archivist's Choice

Item Content
Level Lv.7–8
Location Site-██ ground entrance → O5-9's secret rendezvous point
Duration 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours)

GM Briefing

The incident is over — or at least, the part at Site-██ is over. O5-9 sends a final message:

"Come to the entrance. It's time we meet — for real. I have one final request."

Scene Flow

Phase 1 — The Meeting (30 min)

O5-9 comes to the Site-██ entrance in person — an old person in a wheelchair, wearing an oxygen mask, accompanied by a medical attendant. Their true gender/appearance is decided by the GM.

Their first words:

"Thank you. Whatever choice you made — thank you for letting the truth be seen. I owe this to Elara Chen. And to myself."

Phase 2 — The Final Request (45 min)

O5-9 explains: - They will die within weeks (terminal lung disease) - Their seat on the O5 Council will be reassigned - Their final wish is: to ensure the truth of the Site-██ incident survives in some form — not forgotten, not covered up again

They offer the players several choices: 1. Accept O5-9's Legacy: they will transfer key evidence from their O5 file (about the cover-up, about other similar incidents) to the players — giving the players a "seed of truth," so they can continue pursuing it in the future 2. Write a Report: a truth report about Site-██, written by the players and submitted to the full O5 Council. O5-9 will ensure it is read 3. Do Nothing: let O5-9 die with the secret. Let Site-██ continue to be forgotten

Phase 3 — Endings (30 min)

Based on the players' choices in B-5 and B-6, ending branches:

Ending A: "Truth Endures" - Whisper Chain released + accept O5-9's legacy - Players leave carrying fragments of truth. They know — partially — the information in the Whisper Chain - O5-9's file is disclosed to the O5 Council. An internal debate about "information cover-up policy" begins - Epilogue: one sentence in the players' report is struck through with a red line by O5-█ — but the words under the red line are still legible

Ending B: "Rest" - Whisper Chain destroyed + O5-9 comforted before death - Truth buried — but this time voluntarily, based on understanding rather than fear - Dr. Wei dies peacefully in the isolation unit. Voss is allowed to leave Site-██. Elara Chen continues her ordinary life - Epilogue: players are given amnestics about the cognitohazard content of the Whisper Chain — but retain overall memory of the incident. A final message from O5-9: "Some truths need not be known by everyone. They only need to have been known."

Ending C: "Cycle" - Whisper Chain transformed/re-contained + status quo maintained - A new Site-██ protocol takes effect — stricter monitoring, better protection. But essentially, nothing changes - Epilogue: players return to normal posts. Everything as before. But sometimes — in a quiet library, in the reflection of a mirror — they can still hear a distant whisper

Ending D (Hidden): "New Crimson Dossier" - Condition: Whisper Chain released + Dr. Wei alive + Elara Chen's memory restored + players preserved Elara's original research notes in some way - Elara Chen — after memory restored — proposes a radical idea: use her understanding of the Whisper Chain to create a new, safe memetic resistance system. A truly controllable version - O5-9 secretly funded this project before death — "Crimson Dossier II" - Players become the core team of this project - Epilogue: an encrypted email from an unknown address, subject line: "Crimson Dossier II: Learning from Mistakes. —E.C."

  • Elara Chen if her memory is restored in Series B, she may appear as an NPC in Series C or D — becoming an advocate within the Foundation on "information ethics"
  • The Whisper Chain's meme carrier technology is technically similar to the "consciousness transfer" mechanism of the Thaumiel-class SCP in Series D — hinting the Foundation's research in this area has deeper roots
  • O5-9's File mentions a project at "Area-14" — that project used information isolation protocols similar to Site-██ (connection to Series D)
