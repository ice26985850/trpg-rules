Scenario 10: "The Final Seal"

Item Content
Type Ultimate Sealed Space
Recommended Marks 9-10+
Sessions 3-4
Core Attributes All
Difficulty ★★★★★★ (Legendary)

Opening

The oldest Sealed Space in the world has developed cracks.

Not from external damage—but from within. Something inside is actively trying to get out.

Its location is marked on the Millennial Collector's map as "the First Seal." No records show who sealed it, when, or why. But every Walker's Anchoring Item is resonating at once—all pointing in the same direction.

This is no Commission. It is a summons. Every living Gap Walker has felt it.

Prerequisites

  • Must have completed at least 6 prior scenarios (recommended 7-9)
  • Return Mark ≥ 8
  • Must possess an Anchoring Item
  • Strongly recommended to have a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 3 accompanying you
  • Recommended to choose the most complementary Companion recruited from Scenarios 3/5
  • If Scenario 8 was completed and "Fusion" was chosen—all Anchor-check Advantages will save you multiple times in this scenario

NPC

Scenes

  1. Assembly: Every NPC you know—Mrs. Chen, A-Kai, Xiao-Xia, A-Jie, Old Mo, and even Mr. Chen's Residual that you liberated in Scenario 1—appears in different forms. They cannot enter in your place, but they guard your retreat. Each provides a faint advantage (GM's choice: +1 Negotiation, +1 Anchor, or one extra safety net). [[MAP-10 Scene 1]]

  2. Arrival at the Seal: The Seal lies at the edge of the Deep Gap—a place with no space. Space itself "stops" here. Awareness DC 10 to confirm the Cracks—they are widening at a visible rate. [[MAP-10 Scene 2]]

  3. the First Watcher: The oldest Seal Watcher—composed of pure "waiting." Negotiation DC 18 (solo). It will not attack—it has waited too long to care about waiting one more. But it will ask, "Why do you want to open it?" The answer must be true. The Watcher can tell lies apart.

  4. Honest answer: The Watcher steps aside. And reveals the nature of what is inside—"It is not evil within. It is the denied. You denied its existence, so it was sealed here." [[MAP-10 Scene 3]]

  5. Lie or violence: The Watcher never unseals. The player must use the Key of the First Seal (Negotiation DC 12 to obtain the memory from the Watcher—the Watcher's memory is the key).

  6. Entry: The inside of the Sealed Space is not a room—it is a whirlpool of memories. All denied fragments of history swirl here. Anchor DC 16 (with -2) to maintain self-awareness. Companions must each make an Anchor check (DC 12). [[MAP-10 Scene 4]]

  7. Facing the "Denied One": Its form depends on the player's past choices. The GM shapes it based on the player's decisions across the first 9 scenarios—it is the aggregate of all "options not chosen."

  8. Negotiation DC 20 (ultimate Negotiation). Not combat—conversation. It wants to be acknowledged. Not to be loved, not to be forgiven—to be acknowledged as having existed. [[MAP-10 Scene 5]]

  9. Final Decision:

  10. Acknowledge and release: You acknowledge the existence of the "Denied One." The Seal opens. It is no longer trapped—but it is also no longer "denied." It merges into reality, becoming a new possibility. Return Mark +3, permanent Legendary Mark.
  11. Reseal but remember: You do not open it, but you remember it. You leave your Anchoring Item outside the Sealed Space—not as a seal, but as a "witness." From then on this Sealed Space is no longer "forgotten"—because you remember. Return Mark +2. You lose your Anchoring Item—but gain something stronger: you no longer need an Anchoring Item.
  12. Refuse: You judge it too dangerous. You reinforce the Seal. The Watcher is grateful—it can continue to exist without having to keep waiting. Return Mark +1. Other Walkers may come to open it in the future—but that is no longer your story. [[MAP-10 Scene 6]]

Legendary Ending

Whichever ending is chosen—your character completes the transformation from "Gap Walker" to "legend."

  • Return Mark 10+: All Traverse checks gain permanent Advantage. Can sense all Thresholds. May spend all Return Marks to reverse a Terminal-stage character's Gap Sickness.
  • Companion Loyalty effect: The Companion who accompanied you to the end gains +2 Loyalty. Their story also completes a stage.
  • World effect: The GM describes how your decision changed the Gap Walker community—they felt it. Every one of them. This is not the end—it is the beginning of a new era.

Safety Net

All safety nets in this scenario may be used only once, in the final scene. If the GM judges the players need it, "Companion Sacrifice" may trigger (a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 4 takes the Lost condition in their place).

Rewards

  • Return Mark × 1-3 (depending on ending)
  • Legendary Mark (full effect at Return Mark 10+)
  • Character completes the full journey—this character may appear as an NPC in the GM's future campaigns
