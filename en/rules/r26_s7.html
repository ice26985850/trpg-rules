Scenario 7: Kakin Throne — Inheritance of Blood and Nen

Player Character (PC) stage: Legendary (251+ Experience Points (XP)) Duration: 8-12 hours Prerequisite: After Scenario 5, the Player Characters (PCs) already have a foothold in the Dark Continent

Background

The Kakin Kingdom's succession war among the fourteen princes has entered its white-hot stage. The current king, Nasubi Hui Guo Rou, has declared: whoever brings back the most valuable "proof" from the Dark Continent shall become the next king.

The expeditionary forces of the fourteen princes enter the Dark Continent simultaneously. The Player Characters (PCs) are hired — or forced to participate — in this throne struggle by one of the princes (or princesses).

Choosing an Employer (GM offers the PCs the following choices)

Prince / Princess Number Reward for PCs Personality Morality
Benjamin First Prince Territory + command of an army Arrogant but upright Gray
Camilla Second Prince (Princess) Unlimited wealth Willful but innocent Light
Toiz Fourth Prince Knowledge and ancient heritage Cold but rational Dark Gray
Woble Fourteenth Prince (Princess, only 8 years old) Nothing at all — only a request Naive but brave Pure White
"?" No employer Complete freedom — PC-determined

Game Rules of the Throne Struggle

Rule Content
Proof Bring back any item or intel from the Dark Continent that "no one has seen before"
Means Unlimited — may trade, seize, cooperate, or assassinate
Time Limit Three months
Winner The king's "pot" will judge whose proof is most valuable (criteria unknown)
Loser Historical precedent: not exile, but execution

Key Event Table (triggers every 2 days, roll d10)

d10 Event
1 Letter from a Prince: The employer sends new instructions (or a desperate plea for help)
2 Hostile Expedition: Another prince's team encounters the PCs (may fight / trade / ally)
3 Manifestation of the Pot: One of the PCs dreams of the "pot" — it gives a vague hint (GM picks a secret Dark Continent location)
4 Kakin Infighting: Two princes' teams fight before the PCs; the PCs decide who to help — or stand by
5 Dark Continent Threat: Traces of one of the Five Calamities appear (roll d5 to pick the calamity)
6 Betrayal: An NPC guide within the PCs' team is bought off by another prince
7 Unexpected Discovery: Discover an unrecorded Dark Continent ruin (GM designs on the spot)
8 King's Messenger: The Kakin king sends a message directly — he is watching
9 Double Encounter: Two events trigger at once (roll d8 then d8)
10 Visit from the Gatekeeper: Ou or his successor appears, giving a warning or advice

Ending Routes

Ending Condition Result
Prince's Victory The prince the PCs assisted is elected Gain Kakin's permanent protection and resources
Truth of the Pot The PCs discover the pot's true nature (it is an ancient Dark Continent relic) May choose to destroy the pot → end the throne succession system
Independent Power The PCs help no prince and establish their own Dark Continent foothold Become a third power outside of Kakin
All Survive The PCs engineer a way for all princes to "fail the proof" yet avoid execution (specific method below) Gain the secret favor of all princes

Specific Method for the "All Survive" Route

The Game Master (GM) offers the PCs the following optional paths (PCs choose one or combine several):

Method Rule Difficulty
Substitute Proof The PCs sneak into the king's "Pot Chamber" and replace the proof the pot reads during judgment with a fake. Requires Stealth DC 22 + Sleight of Hand DC 20. When the pot judges, all proofs are "unqualified" — but equally unqualified = all spared execution (the king cannot tell who is worst). ★★★★
Persuade the King The PCs meet King Nasubi directly and persuade him to change the rules. "Let all princes live, let them owe each other a life — that is worth more than a dead heir." Persuasion DC 25 (each prince who vouches for the PCs lowers DC by 2). ★★★
Reveal the Pot's Truth The PCs publicly reveal at the succession ceremony that the pot is an ancient Dark Continent relic, and its judgment standard is "degree of threat to humanity" rather than "value." Once revealed, the throne succession system itself loses legitimacy — all princes are victims. Requires Intelligence (Nen Knowledge) DC 22 to analyze the pot + Charisma DC 20 to publicly persuade. ★★★★
Mutual Cancellation The PCs privately arrange for the two strongest princes to each declare: "If he fails, I will not inherit either." When the two strongest princes bind their fates together, the king cannot execute either without triggering civil war. Requires Persuasion DC 22 (once for each prince). ★★★
Gatekeeper Intervention If one of the PCs has already become a Gatekeeper (Scenario 8), the Gatekeeper can declare that the Dark Continent's "Gate" recognizes only one proof: "Spare your brother." Before the Gatekeeper, the pot respects the will of the Gate — all princes automatically fail. Requires becoming a Gatekeeper
