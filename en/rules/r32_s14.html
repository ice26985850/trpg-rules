Scenario Four: "The Spreading Crack"

Item Content
Type Gap Flooding
Recommended Marks 5-8 (advanced experience)
Player Count 3-5
Sessions 3-4
Core Attributes Awareness, Traverse, Anchor
Difficulty ★★★★☆

Commission

This is not a commission—it is an emergency.

An old community in the Eastside is "disappearing"—not being demolished, but space itself is collapsing. Residents report: walking into one alley and coming out in a completely different neighborhood; opening a home door to find it is not their own living room; descending the basement three levels and still not reaching the bottom. Three residents have already vanished. Local media reports it as "mass hallucination." The city government has sealed off three blocks of the area.

A veteran Gap Walker sent a warning through the acquaintance network: "Someone put something that shouldn't be in a Sealed Space. The Crack is growing. If it isn't dealt with within three days, the entire area will become a permanent Gap Layer."

Surface Map

  • Sealed-Off Zone: Three blocks, roughly 200 meters square. Mostly old apartments and low shops.
  • Core Area: The center of the sealed-off zone—an abandoned traditional medicine shop, closed for years. Behind the shop is a storage room, once sealed by a previous-generation Gap Walker (now missing).
  • Surrounding Community: Residents outside the sealed-off zone have been evacuated.

Gap Layer Map

The Gap Layer of the sealed-off zone is expanding—normal space is being "devoured" into the Gap. The Crack at the core grows outward like a tree, expanding about 10 meters per day. At the center of the Crack is a former Sealed Space—the Seal was forcibly broken.

Cause of Expansion

A previous-generation Gap Walker ("Old Chen") sealed this storage room ten years ago—because a "Forgotten" was locked inside: a little girl who vanished in an air raid during the 1940s. Old Chen could not liberate her (her memories were already completely fragmented), and could only Seal the space to prevent her from affecting the outside world.

A week ago, an unaware construction worker drilled into the storage room's wall while working next door. The Seal broke. The little girl's "Forgotten" state began to spread outward—not out of malice, but her essence of forgetting is "infecting" the surrounding space.

Key NPCs

NPC Info
Old Chen's Notes Records of the Sealed Space left by the previous-generation Gap Walker. Scattered across three locations: his old residence (Surface), his private Threshold (Gap Layer), and a letter mailed to "any Gap Walker" (undelivered at the post office)
The Little Girl's Residual The Forgotten—Retrieval DC 22 to "recover" her. She is not evil—she is forgetting itself
Path Guardian At the edge of the core area where the Crack gradually widens. A horse that can never cross the Threshold (from the old stable next to the medicine shop), blocking the only path to the core area. Negotiation DC 16
Local Residents A few residents inside the sealed-off zone refuse to evacuate. One of them, an old man, is the little girl's younger brother—now in his 80s. He is the only "anchor point"

Key Lost Items

Lost Item Quality Difficulty Class (DC) Location
The Little Girl's Schoolbag Memory Crystal 15 Sealed-Off Zone Gap—the Residual of a school
The Brother's Photo Common Lost Item 8 Sealed-Off Zone Gap—an old mansion
Old Chen's Sealing Notes Gap Construct 18 Assembled from three locations

Key Scenes

  1. Assess the Crack: The players arrive at the edge of the sealed-off zone. Awareness DC 12 reveals the spatial anomaly—the perspective of the alley is wrong. Traverse DC 18 to safely enter the Crack zone; failure results in being repelled by "spatial shock," losing 1d4 Gap Endurance.

  2. Fight the Expansion: The Crack expands daily. The players must resolve it within three days. Each day that passes: the Crack expands 10 meters, more residents are forced to evacuate, and the space in the Gap Layer becomes more unstable (Traverse DC +2).

  3. Path Guardian: The horse-shaped guardian on the outskirts of the core area. Negotiation DC 16; "waiting" is a strategy—after each negotiation attempt, wait 3 turns, DC -2 (the guardian's patience accumulates). Speaking its True Name auto-succeeds (the True Name clue is hidden in Old Chen's notes).

  4. Find Old Chen's Notes: Three puzzle-like notes record the sealing method, the little girl's name, and Old Chen's failed liberation attempts. After reading them in full, gain the "Knowledge Mark about this Sealed Space."

  5. The Little Girl—the Forgotten: Enter the core area to find her. She crouches in a corner, humming a song repeatedly. Retrieval DC 22 to "recognize" who she is. But she has already forgotten herself. Needs:

  6. The brother's photo (to remind her)
  7. Her schoolbag (Memory Crystal—to see the moment she was forgotten)

  8. Her name (found in Old Chen's notes)

  9. Liberate vs. Seal:

  10. Liberate (Negotiation DC 22, drops to DC 16 with the three items): Help her "remember herself"; she smiles and disappears. Return Mark +1. The Crack begins to heal.
  11. Re-Seal (Anchor DC 20): If liberation is impossible, re-seal. But this only delays—the Crack will expand again after several months.

  12. Failure: The Crack swallows the entire block by the end of the third day. The little girl's forgetting spreads—everyone in the sealed-off zone begins to forget they ever lived there.

  13. The Brother Who Assists Liberation: An old man in his 80s is willing to come into the Gap to see his sister. If he is present, the Liberation DC drops to 10.

Ending Options

  • Liberation success: The Crack heals. The brother and sister's Residuals meet briefly, then part. Return Mark +1, special achievement.
  • Seal success: Temporarily safe. May become a setup for other scenarios in the future.
  • Liberation failure: The players witness the block swallowed by the Gap—this may trigger a Gap Sickness check.
