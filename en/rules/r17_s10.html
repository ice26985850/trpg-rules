Scenario Ten: The Deep Laboratory [Level (Lv.) 10]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 10 × 4 players | Duration: 6-8 hours | Map: Core Zone edge — Ashila Facility perimeter

Commission

This is the finale. QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission), Scavengers' Compact, and Smuggler's Ring joint tri-faction commission: enter the Ashila Research Facility's deep laboratory and close the Dimensional Rift — or at least obtain data sufficient to understand how to close it. Reward: choice of Gold-tier items ×3 + 100,000 ₽ + full Hideout upgrade + Legendary status.

Prerequisite: Level (Lv.) 10, complete Scenario Eight or Nine, Reputation Friendly or higher with at least two factions.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry — Core Zone Perimeter Wall

QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) grants a special permit to temporarily open an entrance. Gravity has already begun to show obvious anomalies — in some directions gravity is only 30%, in others up to 200%. Each character needs Perception Target Number (TN) 14 to adapt to the anomalous environment (failure grants Stress 1).

Scene 2: Facility Perimeter — Research Campus

The above-ground building complex of the Ashila Facility. Distorted by anomalous energy — walls warp, time flow is unstable. - Deep Diver ×3 + Core Zone mutant ×2 - Inside the abandoned lab you can scavenge: research logs (Purple ×2) + energy cell (Gold ×1) + prototype equipment (Gold ×1, random weapon or armor)

Scene 3: Deep Entrance — Elevator Shaft

Gravity anomaly becomes extreme. Requires Tech Target Number (TN) 20 to restart the backup elevator (or climb with TN 17 through the elevator shaft). During the process you encounter: Teleporting Mutant ×2 (can teleport to any location).

Scene 4: Deep Laboratory

Here the anomalous energy intensity peaks. All checks suffer Disadvantage (unless wearing the "Curtain Resonator" — from Scenario Eight). - Core Zone mutant ×4 + Dimensional Guardian (Boss) ×1

Scene 5: Control Room

The ultimate trial to close the rift: - Tech Target Number (TN) 23 × 3 successes (team may cooperate) - During each attempt: Dimensional Guardian releases an energy wave (all 2d6 damage + Courage TN 20) - After three successes: the rift closes. Core Zone energy begins to decay. Extract.

Scene 6: Extraction — Final Escape

The rift closing triggers a chain collapse. The players have 10 turns to reach the surface extraction point. Random encounters with Residuals along the path (each turn d6: 5-6 = Residuals ×1d3). - QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) helicopter waits on the surface (arrives in the last 3 turns).
