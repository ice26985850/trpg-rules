Scenario S1: Solo Entry [Level (Lv.) 1]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 1 × 1 Player + 1 Companion | Duration: 2-3 hours | Map: Ash Mall (Edge Zone)

Commission

Owl needs someone to retrieve an encrypted file from the management office on the 3rd floor of the Ash Mall. He says, "Going alone is actually safer—two people are too conspicuous." Reward: 1,200 ₽. Time limit: 75 minutes.

Companion Recommendation

Preferred: Scout "Wraith"—she can help you spot threats early and avoid unnecessary combat.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry—East Service Entrance

Perception Target Number (TN) 11 (+2 solo stealth advantage): The Scout whispers, "There are at least two Residuals in the parking lot—but they're deep inside; we can go around them." - Option A: Detour (Stealth Target Number (TN) 11; success → go directly to Scene 2; failure → encounter Residuals ×1) - Option B: Frontal clearance of Residuals ×2 (Threat Value 6, suited for solo + Assault Companion)

Scene 2: Food Court (1st Floor)

Behind an abandoned fast-food counter there is a First Aid Kit ×1 (White). Perception Target Number (TN) 8 can reveal bandages in the corner. - Optional Encounter: An injured Newbie Scavenger (Hit Points (HP) 0%, can be rescued) - Rescue (First Aid Target Number (TN) 11): He will tell you that the spare key to the management office is hidden in the electronics store (free, no Lockpicking check required)

Scene 3: Electronics Store

The back warehouse requires Lockpicking (Target Number (TN) 14), or Companion Technician "Wrench" can handle it directly. - Inside the warehouse: Electronic Parts (Blue ×1) + management office spare key (if learned from Scene 2) - Without key: Going upstairs requires Perception Target Number (TN) 14 to find the ventilation duct entrance

Scene 4: Management Office (3rd Floor)

Without key: Lockpicking Target Number (TN) 14. Inside the office: - Primary objective: Encrypted data (Purple, 2 slots) - Random loot (d6): 1-2 = pistol ammunition (White), 3-4 = First Aid Kit (White), 5 = radio module (Blue), 6 = rooftop key

After obtaining the data: Perception Target Number (TN) 14 reveals that a Lone Wolf Veteran is coming up the stairs—he also has his eye on this data. - Solo-only Choice: Hide under the desk (Stealth Target Number (TN) 17; success → the Lone Wolf comes in, searches briefly, then leaves without engaging) - Or engage head-on (Threat Value 6; high but feasible for a solo Level (Lv.) 1)

Scene 5: Extraction

  • Route A: East Parking Lot (fixed/flare gun/attracts 1 Residual)—easiest
  • Route B: Behind the gas station (fixed/requires waiting d6 rounds)
  • Route C: Rooftop (hidden/requires key + flare gun/safest and grants the "Rooftop View" intel reward)

Enemies in This Scenario

Residuals (used throughout all scenes)

Reference: GM Rulebook §3.1 (full Residuals data). Count in this scenario: 1-2.

Lone Wolf Veteran (optional in Scene 4)

Reference: GM Rulebook §3.4 (Lone Wolf Veteran). Solo mode: Hit Points (HP) unchanged; a combined 4 Action Points (AP) from player + Companion can handle it.

Solo Adjustment

  • Too Hard: The Lone Wolf Veteran does not engage (you slip past directly); gas station wait rounds halved
  • Too Easy: During extraction, encounter Newbie Team ×2 (contesting the Extraction Point—negotiate or fight)
