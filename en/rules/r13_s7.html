Scenario SB-2: "Signal in the Ashes"

Difficulty: Low | Location: Ruined city "Eldenfell"
Recommended Attributes: 1-2 growths after character creation

Background

Following the transport ship's coordinates, you arrive at the ruined city. Here you find not a transport ship, but a group of civilian survivors. One person makes you stop in your tracks—a former Imperial Guard officer, bearing the mark of your Chapter.

NPC Companion

Kara Val (former Imperial Guard Lieutenant, once served under your Chapter)

Item Content
One-line description A resilient middle-aged female officer. She knows your Chapter—her company held a bridge with your support
Motive To protect the civilians who remain with her. She does not believe the Empire will return—but she believes in you
Bottom Line Never abandons civilians
Initial Attitude +2 (Eve of loyalty—she recognized your Chapter's mark)
Attributes Faith 3 / Body 3 / Mind 3 / Tech 3 / Fate 3
Key Skills Command (Trained), Ballistic Skill (Trained)
Equipment Autogun (1d8+1), Carapace Armor (Armor Rating (AR) 3), torn Chapter banner fragment (kept from that campaign)
Special Ability Veteran's Command: Provides the player one free Advantage (once per combat). Memory of the Banner: While holding the torn banner, she and the player gain +5 to Faith checks' Target Number (TN)

Oath Conflict Point

Kara asks you to protect the civilians and take them to the transport ship. But the transport ship cannot hold everyone—if you take the civilians, it may mean you cannot evacuate.

  • Choice A: Agree with her—the Oath "Vow to the Innocent" is fulfilled (Faith +2), but in later scenarios the fate of the civilians will weigh on your heart
  • Choice B: Refuse her—the Oath "Vow to the Mission" is fulfilled (a more efficient evacuation), but Kara's attitude −2
