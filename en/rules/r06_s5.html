S5: The Final Evolution

Suggested Evolution Nodes: 13-20 nodes Difficulty: Lethal (★★★★★★) Estimated Playtime: 6-8 hours Players: 1 / expandable to 2-4 Multiplayer (2-4 players): GM should multiply enemy count by ×1.5 (2 players) or ×2 (3-4 players); the party shares the scenario's Evolution Points and Remnant Coins rewards, while individual absorption gains remain each player's own. All other rules unchanged. Prerequisite Suggestion: Complete at least S1 + any two of S2/S3/S4

Plot Summary

This is the final journey. The Abyssal Rift—the giant fissure that has torn a 300-kilometer gash across the continent—is expanding at an unstoppable speed. Within a month, the Rift will swallow White Tower City, the Gene Forge, the Arena—and everything you know.

The only solution lies in the Rift's deepest depths. There sleeps the origin of everything—the residual consciousness of the Void Abyss Worm.

You must go down. Face the source of it all. Then make a decision: absorb it? Destroy it? Or find a third path between the two?

And this decision—will forever change who you are.

Scene 1: The Rift Entrance

Opening Description (GM reads aloud)

You did not come here alone.

At the edge of the Abyssal Rift gather all the people you met along the way—if they are still alive. Ryan (if survived from S1), Lina or Gros or Zero (if released from S2), your chosen faction allies (if from S3), Vera or Greg (if survived from S4), even Zarlax (if peacefully resolved).

They all look at you. Each wears a different expression—some with hope, some with fear, some with reverence.

"You really going down?" someone asks.

The Rift stretches beneath your feet. From the abyss comes not wind—but a low, rhythmic pulse. Like a heartbeat. Like breathing.

The Void Abyss Worm is not dead. It sleeps. And the Rift—is the trace of it turning in its dreams.

Only a life form evolved to a certain degree can withstand the pressure of the Rift's depths. Your evolution node total is the highest—this task can only be completed by you.

Scene Objectives

  • Say farewell to past allies/enemies (or summon the party)
  • Decide whether to bring companions down (each companion adds risk but also provides assistance)
  • Begin the descent

Possible Companions

Companion Source Assistance Provided Risk
Ryan S1 Combat support, emotional support during Humanity Value crisis (+1 Will YZ check) He may be unable to withstand the Rift's mental pressure (needs Will YZ DC 14 each round)
Zarlax S4 Strong combat ability (HP 120)—can share the final BOSS's damage with you He fights for his child—if the final choice involves sacrifice, he will oppose it
Zero S2 Knowledge of the Void Abyss Worm (reveals extra truth fragments) He may choose to absorb the fragments for himself at the last moment—becoming your enemy
Alden S3 Evolution Meditation technique—can maintain your Humanity Value in the Rift (recover 1 each round) Advanced in age, HP only 30

Scene 2: The Rift Descent

Opening Description

The descent itself is a battle.

The Rift's rock walls are covered with countless mutant creatures—the Rift's earliest inhabitants, living here for thousands of years, absorbing the faint evolution energy emitted by the Void Abyss Worm.

The deeper you go, the more distorted the creatures' mutations. At 100 meters, you see a seven-headed giant serpent. At 300 meters, a swarm of transparent, formless creatures flows across the rock walls. At 500 meters—what you see has surpassed your understanding of "life."

But you keep going down. Because you know—the Rift's expansion will not stop. Unless you do something.

Encounter Design

Phase One: 100 meters—Seven-Headed Serpent (Elite-tier)

Name HP Defense Skills
Seven-Headed Serpent 60 (each head 12 HP) 13 Each of the seven heads has an independent attack (each 1d6+3). Each head destroyed, attack count -1

Phase Two: 300 meters—Void Fluid (Elite-tier ×3)

Name HP Defense Skills
Void Fluid 25 each 8 (Disadvantage on physical attacks—fluid is hard to hit) Devour Touch (melee target makes Will YZ DC 15 or loses 1d4 Humanity Value), Split (when HP below 10, splits into two individuals of 10 HP each)

Phase Three: 500 meters—Abyss Gatekeeper (Boss-tier)

Name HP Defense Attributes Energy
Abyss Gatekeeper 100 16 LIL 10, MJ 8, TZ 12, GZ 10, YZ 14 18

This is an evolved giant beast that has completely lost its original form—the Void Abyss Worm's "watchdog." Thousands of years ago it tried to absorb the Worm's fragments, failed, and became this.

Skills: - Void Tentacle (Mid-tier, 3 Energy): Mid-range, 2d8+5 mental damage + Humanity Value -1 (cannot resist) - Worm's Roar (High-tier, 5 Energy): All present make Will (YZ) DC 18, on failure lose all actions this round - Abyssal Gaze (Ultimate, all Energy): Target makes Will (YZ) DC 22, on failure Humanity Value halved directly

Absorption Trait Sample (if defeated and absorbed): ①Void Resistance (Passive: void-related damage -3) ②Abyssal Sense (Passive: can sense nearby Void Abyss fragments) ③Abyssal Gaze (High-tier Active—but Humanity Value -2 after use)

Scene 3: The Hall of Fragments

Opening Description

You reach the Rift's deepest depths. There are no rock walls here—only endless darkness. In the darkness, light points float. Hundreds, thousands of light points—each one a memory. Memories of the Void Abyss Worm.

When you approach a light point, it unfolds before you:

—The Original Loneliness. The Void Abyss Worm was the earliest life in the universe. It evolved alone in the endless void for hundreds of millions of years, with no companion, no contrast. Finally—it devoured itself, because that was the one thing it had never experienced.

—The Separation of Fragments. Each fragment of the Worm became a new form of life. But the fragments did not know they were one. They began devouring each other—this was the original "evolution competition."

—The Birth of Humanity. The largest fragment entered the genes of early humans. This is why humans evolve faster than other species—not because humans are superior, but because humans are closest to the fragments.

A light point floats before you. This memory is different—it speaks to you directly:

"You have finally come. One of my countless children."

"You know—you are not here to destroy me. You are here to decide my fate."

Scene Objectives

  • Explore the Hall of Fragments and obtain the complete truth
  • Dialogue with the Void Abyss Echo
  • Face the three final choices

The Void Abyss Echo

The Void Abyss Echo appears in the form of the person the player trusts most—perhaps Ryan, Alden, or a lost loved one from the player's background. But it is not malicious—it has no concept of good or evil. It is merely a memory, waiting to be released.

Truth Fragments (collected by the player in the hall):

  1. Evolution is not a gift—it is a side effect of the Worm's fragments
  2. Humanity Value is not "humanity"—it is "the part outside the fragments." When Humanity Value hits zero, the fragments take complete control
  3. The Rift expands because the Worm is "awakening." If it fully awakens, the Rift will devour the entire continent
  4. There are three ways to stop the expansion—but each has a cost

Scene 4: The Final Choice

Opening Description

At the hall's center floats an object—not a light point, but a fist-sized crystal constantly shifting form. The Void Abyss Worm's core fragment.

As you gaze at it, it gazes back. You see your reflection—not the you of now, but the you of before. Human. Or Beast. Or Insect. Or something else.

Then you see your Evolution Tree—every node, every absorption, every drop in Humanity Value. The fragment remembers everything. Because the fragment has always been inside you.

The Void Abyss Echo's voice:

"You have three choices. You are already strong enough to bear any of them—but only one is correct. Not for me. For you."

The Three Choices

Choice One: Absorb—Become the New Void Abyss Worm

Item Content
Method Fully absorb the core fragment into your body. You will gain the Void Abyss Worm's full evolution potential
Immediate Effect Strength LIL +10, Constitution TZ +10, Will YZ +10. Evolution capacity becomes infinite. You can freely use any evolution skill you've witnessed. Humanity Value hits zero—permanently
Long-term Consequence You become the new "Devourer." The Rift stops expanding—because the Worm no longer sleeps, it is inside you. But you are no longer "you." The character transforms into an NPC/Ultimate Villain. The world's fate depends on your new identity's choices
Humanity Value Hits zero, cannot be restored
Suited For Players pursuing ultimate power who don't mind losing themselves

Choice Two: Destroy—End the Curse of Evolution

Item Content
Method Use your own evolution energy as the trigger to detonate the core fragment
Immediate Effect The fragment is destroyed. All life's evolution potential in the world vanishes—including yours. All your evolution nodes permanently fail. Your attributes revert to your Base Form's initial values. You become an ordinary "normal" life form again
Long-term Consequence The Rift fully closes. The Ultimate Evolution Cult loses its power source and collapses. The Humanity Guardians' mission is complete. No new Evolvers in the world—existing Evolvers gradually degenerate. Peace—at the cost of sacrificing a miracle
Humanity Value Restored to 100 (losing evolution means you become "yourself" again)
Suited For Players who value "humanity" and are willing to sacrifice power for the world

Choice Three: Merge—Control the Fragment with Humanity

Item Content
Method Fuse the core fragment into your body, but bind it to your Humanity Value with Will. You are not controlled by the fragment—you control the fragment with your Will
Immediate Effect Make an Ultimate Will (YZ) check DC 30. Success→gain the "Ultimate Form"—can freely switch all unlocked forms, and Humanity Value no longer decreases from evolution (already-lost not restored). All attributes +5. Gain Active: "Fragment Resonance"—can temporarily stimulate nearby creatures' evolution potential
Failure Consequence Check fails→the fragment goes out of control. Roll d6: 1-3→the fragment destroys you (character death); 4-5→the fragment partially controls you (you become a new Void Abyss Worm larva—character becomes an NPC); 6→the fragment refuses to merge but is weakened (Rift shrinks 50%, but not fully closed. Gain small attribute bonuses and a new story goal)
Long-term Consequence You are the first being to successfully tame the Void Abyss fragment. You become living proof of the balance between evolution and humanity
Humanity Value Check success→no longer decreases. Check failure→depends on the die result
Suited For Players pursuing the "third path," who believe in balance

If Companions Are Present

Companion Reaction to Choice
Ryan Choice One→he leaves sadly ("I don't want to watch you become that"). Choice Two→he breathes a sigh of relief ("You made the right choice"). Choice Three→he stays to help you with the Will check (provides +3 bonus)
Zarlax Choice One→attacks you (he cannot let another monster be born). Choice Two→understands ("For the child, this is best"). Choice Three→assists you with his Will (check DC -3)
Zero Choice One→tries to seize the fragment for himself before you absorb it (requires a contested Will YZ check). Choice Two→agrees ("Evolution was a mistake"). Choice Three→while he tries to seize the fragment, you discover his intent (can prevent it beforehand)
Alden Any choice→accepts calmly ("300 years—I finally get to see the ending"). On Choice Three provides "Evolution Meditation" assistance (check DC -5—greatest aid)

Scene 5: Aftermath

Opening Description

The light dissipates. The darkness recedes.

The Rift's rock walls begin to heal—or rather, shrink. You hear voices from above—the cheers of those gathered at the Rift's edge. Or terrified screams. Depending on your choice.

You feel different. Whatever you chose—you are no longer the person you were before.

You look at the fragment in your hand—if it still exists. If it is already inside you—you can feel it.

The Void Abyss Echo's last words echo in your mind:

"Thank you. I am no longer alone."

Then the voice vanishes. Permanently.

Campaign Endings

Absorb Ending—"A New God Descends"

You rise from the Rift. All creatures present—whether Evolver or ordinary—are forced to their knees by an irresistible force. Your body is no longer bound by physical law. You look down upon the world—this continent is yours. These lives are yours. You are the rebirth of the Void Abyss Worm.

But occasionally—very occasionally—you hear a faint voice in your mind. It is your old name. The people you once cared for. You are unsure where that voice comes from. But you know—something in the fragments still remembers.

Possible sequel adventure: The player character's old friends try to awaken the remnant humanity within him.

Destroy Ending—"The Ordinary Hero"

You crawl out of the Rift—weak, ordinary, but whole. You have lost all evolution ability, but your friends still recognize you. Ryan rushes up to support you (if still alive). The bells of White Tower City ring—not an alarm, but celebration. The age of evolution is over.

But you find yourself occasionally glancing at your hands without thinking—searching for those scales, claws, or antennae that no longer exist. You will evolve no more. But you will also forever remember—what it felt like to become something "not yourself."

Possible sequel adventure: A few Evolvers in the world who refuse to degenerate become a new threat.

Merge Ending—"The Balance Guardian"

You succeeded. The fragment is tamed under your Will. You are the first—perhaps the only—being able to remain fully self-aware while completely evolved. You become the spiritual leader of the Symbiosis Accord—living proof that evolution and humanity are not opposed.

But you know—you are not the only one with this potential. There are other Evolvers in the world. Perhaps one day, they too will need to walk the path you walked.

Possible sequel adventure: Teach a new generation of Evolvers how to coexist with the fragments within them.

Final Settlement

Action Reward
Complete scenario (any ending) +5 Evolution Points
Choose "Absorb" Ultimate Evolution—become a Legendary-tier being (character retires, can serve as an NPC/BOSS in future scenarios)
Choose "Destroy" "Hero's Mark"—all survivors remember you forever. Gain a brand-new, ordinary adventure starting point
Choose "Merge" and succeed "Ultimate Form" + "Fragment Resonance" skill. Will (YZ) permanently +5. Humanity Value no longer decreases
Choose "Merge" and fail but survive Gain Passive "Echo" — Will (YZ) +2, can sense nearby evolution energy

GM Notes

Difficulty Warning

S5 is the finale of the entire campaign. Difficulty is set to "Lethal"—meaning the player character may die or be permanently changed at the final choice. The GM should tell the players before the scenario begins: this is the last stop, and some endings are irreversible.

The Meaning of DC 30

The Ultimate Will check DC 30 is the highest DC in the entire system. A character with attribute 16+ plus d20 has about a 35% success rate. With companion assistance (up to -8 DC), the success rate can reach 75%. The GM should ensure the player has accumulated enough allies and preparation by the time they arrive here.

Alternative Path

If the GM or player finds DC 30 too harsh, it can be lowered to DC 25—but the cost is the "Ultimate Form"'s effect is halved.

Later Play

After S5, the player character may retire. But the world continues. The GM can design new scenarios around the consequences of the choice—such as "Absorb route→old friends' rebellion," "Destroy route→threat of remaining Evolvers," "Merge route→teaching the new generation."
