Scenario 2: Voices on the Corner

Basic Info

Item Content
Difficulty ★★ Basic (Solo)
Suggested Reputation 8-20
Length 3-5 Turns
Core Theme Accumulating Reputation, facing competition for the first time

Story Summary

Your shop is starting to have a name—the neighborhood is starting to call it by name, instead of "that new shop."

But with Reputation comes not only customers.

The old grocery at the corner, open for ten years, run by Colin, has recently turned unfriendly toward you. He lowered his prices. He spread some words in the market. He said, "Those new arrivals won't last a year."

You need to prove it: one person, plus your companion, is enough.

Key NPCs

NPC Role
Colin Owner of the corner grocery. Not a villain—an old man afraid of being replaced
Mayor's Secretary About to hold a "Neighborhood Choice"—a good chance to earn Reputation
Your repeat customers Those who come every week—they are your strongest weapon in this competition

Turn Design

Turns 1-2: Friction

  • Colin starts cutting prices. Some of your customers drift away (Foot Traffic −1d2, lasting 2 weeks).
  • You have three ways to respond:
  • Quality War: Make at least one Fine-quality item and let customers choose for themselves (no Check needed; quality speaks).
  • Social War: Visit Colin proactively (Social Difficulty (DC) 14) to understand why he's doing this.
  • Differentiation: Launch a product Colin doesn't have (Inspiration Difficulty (DC) 12, use your companion's Class to develop a new product line).

Turns 3-4: Neighborhood Choice

The mayor holds a "Best Small Shop of the Year" contest. You may enter. Criteria: Quality + Reputation + Neighborhood recommendation.

  • Submit one work (quality determines the base score)
  • Customer recommendation letters: how many repeat customers do you have? (each +1 point)
  • Colin also entered. His work is steady Standard quality. But you have a chance to make Fine—even Superior.

Turn 5: Reconciliation or Rupture

If your Social succeeds: Colin admits he was afraid. He proposes cooperation—your high-quality goods enter his distribution, his steady Foot Traffic drives customers to you. Win-win.

If you defeat him: Colin's shop closes after the contest. You may choose to buy out his storefront and inventory. But you may also choose not to—because you don't lack that shop. You only need your own.

Rewards

Achievement Reward
Win the contest Reputation +2d4, Foot Traffic permanently +1
Reconcile with Colin Unlock "Partner Store": the grocery distributes your Standard-quality goods (passive income 10G/week)
Launch a new product line Gain an advanced recipe (determined by companion's Class)
