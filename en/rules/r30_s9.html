Campaign Linkage Guide

Main Campaign Linkage: The Runeterra War Epic

White Wall Rift(Lv.1-3) → Shadow Spread(Lv.1-5) → Winter's Awakening(Lv.6-10) → Ascension Trial(Lv.11-16) → The Dead Return(Lv.17-20)
    Intro              Part 1            Part 2            Part 3            Finale

If players start from White Wall Rift, Shadow Spread may skip the Lv.1-3 portion and begin directly from the Lv.3 Noxus Outpost.

Inserting Side Stories

Player Level Suitable Side Story

Level Calibration

If the players' level exceeds the main campaign's recommended value after completing a side story, the GM may: - Increase the difficulty of main campaign encounters (HP and AD each +10-20%) - Let players clear the main campaign faster (retaining the sense of challenge) - Or use the GM Difficulty Adjustment Quick Reference Table (Rulebook §4.11)

Jump-In Entry

New players who already have high-level characters may enter directly based on the table below:

Current Level Recommended Starting Point
Lv.1-5 Shadow Spread or White Wall Rift
Lv.6-10 Winter's Awakening
Lv.11-16 Ascension Trial
Lv.17-20 The Dead Return
