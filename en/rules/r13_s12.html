Scenario A-2: "Silence of the Rust Crown" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Mortal · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map Reference: maps/map12_chapel.svg

Difficulty: Low (Challenge Rating (CR) Total 3-4) | Inquisitor: Selena (Puritan)
Target Region: Tarr-Morag Hive City, Upper Hive (Levels 300-400), abandoned Ecclesiarchy chapel
Recommended Player Attributes: Level 3 range

Surface Goal

Investigate an Ecclesiarchy chapel in the Upper Hive—no one has come out of it for nearly three months. Recover the relics inside.

True Goal

Selena suspects the chapel has been corrupted by Chaos. She wants to know the source of the corruption—and whether the Ecclesiarchy priest inside is still alive.

Known Threats

  • Uncertain—the interior situation of the chapel is unknown
  • Possibly Mutants

Hidden Threats

  • The priest died in his own ritual—he was backlashed while attempting to purify a Chaos item with forbidden arts
  • The Chaos item still remains at the chapel's underground altar, continuously releasing Warp energy—those who approach gain +1 Corruption per turn (minor contamination)
  • The priest's corpse has become a low-ranking Mutant

Resource Ration

Standard ration. Selena specifically provides 2 extra Holy Waters.

Temptation Note Distribution

  • 1 player receives Note 2 ("Observe and report who has been corrupted")
  • 3 players receive Note 4

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Priest Illyon's Diary Clue Diary pages scattered throughout the chapel. Records his process of discovering the Chaos item → attempting purification → gradually falling into despair
Chapel Guardian's Ghost Warp Phenomenon Not a true ghost—a memory afterimage projected by Warp energy. Can be soothed by a Faith Test

Key Check Nodes

# Scene Check Target Number (TN) Success Failure
1 Search the chapel for the diary Investigation (Standard) Mind×10+Investigation Find 3 diary pages Find only 1 page
2 Soothe the guardian's afterimage Faith (Hard) Faith×10−20 Afterimage dissipates, gain the guardian's final warning from life Afterimage attacks (mental damage)
3 Face the underground altar Faith (Standard)+Psyniscience Faith×10 Discover the Chaos item Influenced by the Chaos item

Combat Encounters

Encounter Enemies Challenge Rating (CR) Tactics
Chapel Nave Mutated Priest Illyon 1 Low-ranking Mutant template + 1 special psychic ability
Underground Altar Warp Energy Release Environment +1 Corruption per turn, requires destroying the Chaos item (Faith Test, Hard)
