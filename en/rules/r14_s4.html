Scenario S4: The Darkest Age

Suggested Era Range: Kingdom Age → Empire Age
Suggested Level: High
Estimated Turns: ~70 turns (17 years in-game)
Difficulty: ★★★★☆
Core Themes: Survival crisis, sacrifice, the spark of civilization
Player Count: 1 player + 1 GM

I. Scenario Overview

Synopsis

A green meteor streaks across the night sky and falls at the edge of the known world. At first people thought it was a divine omen — until those who touched it began to cough up blood, the land began to wither, and a plague-green mist spread on the wind.

This is not a war between you and your neighbors, but the shared survival of all civilization. You must not only build your nation — you must ensure that "civilization" itself survives.

Scenario Objectives

  1. Endure the chaos of the disaster's early stage
  2. Find the cause of the meteor blight and its cure
  3. Choose between "seal off and self-protect" and "open aid"
  4. Lead or join a multinational coalition expedition
  5. Decide the direction of civilization's rebuilding

II. Scenario Structure

Phase One: Disaster Falls (Turns 1–10)

Opening Narration (read aloud by the GM):

The night the meteor fell, half the continent's sky was green. Three days later, villages a hundred li from the impact began to echo with the sound of coughing blood.

Your Scholar (SCH) stares into the microscope (or crystal ball), paler than the patients: "This is no plague… it is a 'blight' that spreads along life force. Common herbs are useless."

Refugees have already begun to flood the kingdom's border. You must decide: the gate — open, or not?

Key Events:

Turn Event Type
Turn 1 Meteor falls, regional panic — Morale (MOR) unsettled Disaster
Turn 3 First blight case appears in a border village — quarantine needed (Morale check) Crisis
Turn 5 First choice of seal or aid: close the border (self-protect) or open shelter (Morale + but risk) Moral Choice
Turn 7 Food supply chain hit by disaster — Food (FOOD) output plummets Economic Crisis
Turn 9 Neighbors each protect themselves, trade breaks off — Economy (ECO) hit Diplomatic Crisis
Turn 10 Form an "Emergency Committee" to coordinate the fight against the blight Milestone

GM Notes: - This phase heavily uses the Crisis Deck (natural disaster / economic crisis / internal crisis); difficulty scales dynamically with National Power. - The blight can act as an amplified version of the "Poisoned (PSN)" status affecting NPCs and heroes (see GM Guide Ch. 24).

Key Events:

GM Notes: - Expedition combat combines hero personal combat with military units (if a guard detachment is brought). - The "price" choice can let a hero sacrifice (fall in battle) for the global cure — the most emotionally weighty moment.

Turn 70 Civilization Rebuilt — you shape the new post-disaster world order Ending

III. Ending Conditions

Success Ending (Savior)

  • Find the cure and save a large population (including other nations)
  • Reward: International prestige +50, all attributes +10; unlock the legendary deed "Spark of Civilization"

Partial Success (Fortress)

  • Seal off and self-protect; your nation survives but is internationally isolated
  • Reward: All attributes +5

Failure Conditions

  • Population falls below the survival line due to the blight (Population POP < 50)
  • Remedy: Play as survivors founding a new nation (retain 50% of attributes)

VI. GM Tips

  1. Oppression: The first 30 turns should make players feel resource tightening and helplessness, highlighting the scale of the disaster.
  2. Weight of Sacrifice: Make the sacrificer a named NPC / hero with bonds.
  3. Possibility of Cooperation: Though single-player, the plot encourages "cooperating with neighbors"; the GM may let neighbors become temporary allies.
  4. Linked New System: The blight can demonstrate status effects (Poisoned) and Mana (MP) consumption in the plot.
