Scenario S7: Hunter and Prey

Type: Solo Lone Wolf | Suggested Level: 2 | Difficulty: ★★★ | Estimated Duration: 3–4 hours

Synopsis

You came for the money—you need it for something important. A mid-sized desert map—at its center sits an abandoned military base hiding the best loot. But fifteen Lone Wolves all know about it. One of them—a retired special forces soldier—has the same reason for entering as you do. For money. For family. He needs the money more than you do. Will you let him have it?

Battlefield Map

Salt Flats — 4 km × 4 km

Terrain composition: Desert (50%), Abandoned Military Base (20%), Dry Riverbed (15%), Mining Site (15%)

Landmarks

  • Abandoned Military Base (military | loot: Military (Purple)): map center—the best loot on the map, but everyone knows it
  • Radio Relay Station (special | loot: -): a communications facility on a hilltop
  • Mine Entrance (industrial | loot: Common (Green)): an underground mine—a perfect stealth route
  • Salt Lake (natural | loot: Scarce (Gray)): a dried-up salt lake—open terrain; crossing it is suicide

Enemy Configuration

Total enemy squads (including the player's squad).

Battlefield Conditions

  • Zone: Jump Reversal — suddenly changes direction mid-way—forcing the player to cross the open salt lake
  • Airdrop: 2 times, ['Second Circle', 'Fourth Circle'] —
  • Red Zone Bombing: Yes
  • Vehicles: Off-road Vehicle, Motorcycle, Jeep
  • Weather: Clear (high heat) + Sandstorm (second half)
  • Time: Daytime

Flow Phases

1. Drop over Salt Flats (1 round)

GM Note: "The desert wind is hot—it is only nine in the morning, yet the heat off the ground already warps the horizon. You see the grey outline of the military base below. Everyone is flying toward it."

2. Base Raid (4–5 rounds)

GM Note: The fight inside the military base is the core of this act. Multiple Lone Wolves enter the base to loot at the same time—create layered encounters (A hears footsteps on the first floor while looting on the second, and at the same time spots someone approaching outside the window).

3. The Nemesis's Message (3–4 rounds)

GM Note: The player finds clues left by the nemesis on the enemies they kill. This is not mere combat—it is a silent conversation between two people with the same motive. Every letter, every clue, makes the player hesitate: "Should I keep going? He still has a daughter waiting for him."

4. Salt Lake Crossing (3 rounds)

GM Note: The circle suddenly reverses—the Safe Zone jumps to the far side of the salt lake. The player is forced to cross open terrain—this is when the ambush teams are most dangerous. Give the player options to use smoke grenades, vehicles, or detour through the mine.

5. Final Circle: The Choice (2–3 rounds)

GM Note: You and the Phantom—face to face among the ruins. Offer the player a "lower your gun" option—they can choose not to fire. (But the Phantom will fire—he has a reason he cannot lose.) Make the choice meaningful—even if the outcome is the same, the player made the choice.

Victory Condition

Become the last surviving Lone Wolf (or make your choice)

Rewards

Achievement XP CR
Eliminate one enemy contestant 80 30
Squad wipes out an entire enemy squad (per member) 150 50
Survive to top 10 in a single match 150 100
Survive to top 5 in a single match 250 150
Achieve final victory (Chicken Dinner) 500 500
Successfully revive a Downed teammate 80 20

The above are general reward baselines; scenario-specific objective rewards are added by the GM as the situation warrants.
