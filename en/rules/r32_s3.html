Scenario 3: "The Late-Night Commission"

Item Content
Type Lost-Item Commission + First Negotiation
Recommended Marks 1-2
Sessions 1-2 sessions
Core Attributes Negotiation, Traverse
Difficulty ★★

Opening

The owner of the "Night Voyage" Café—A-Kai—contacts you late at night. A part-time worker (Xia Xia, 19) left her grandmother's silver bracelet at the café when closing up. But the bracelet is no longer on the Surface—"it fell down." A-Kai can sense the bracelet in the Gap Layer, but he cannot enter it himself.

NPCs

NPC Type Info
A-Kai the Sensitive (potential Companion) Owner of the Night Voyage Café. In his fifties, knows all the local Walkers
Xia Xia Client (potential Companion) A 19-year-old part-timer. Just awakened—can see the Gap but doesn't know what it is
Lost Item Collector Gap Creature "Foraging" in the café's Gap Layer tonight—drawn by the silver bracelet's resonance

Lost Items

Lost Item Quality Solo DC Location
Silver Bracelet Benevolent Keepsake 10 Café Gap Layer—under Xia Xia's seat
Grandmother's Old Recipe Common Lost Item 6 Café Gap Layer—kitchen
A Suicide Note Memory Crystal 13 On the Collector (requires trade)

Gap Creatures

Creature Negotiation DC Location Demand
Lost Item Collector 12 Café Gap Layer Wants "a new story"—the player must tell of one of their own regrets

Scenes

  1. Surface Investigation: Awareness DC 8 to confirm the bracelet is not on the Surface. A-Kai describes the feeling of "falling down."
  2. Enter the Gap: The café's main door is a Natural Threshold (DC 6). After traversing, the café is twice as large in the Gap Layer as on the Surface—an entire extra row of nonexistent seats appears at the back.
  3. Search: Retrieval DC 6 to find the grandmother's recipe (clue: the bracelet's meaning to Xia Xia). Tucked in the recipe is a note—written by the grandmother: "To Xia Xia, this bracelet was given to me by my mother. Now it's yours. Don't lose it." But the bracelet is not under the seat.
  4. Encounter the Collector: The Collector is placing the bracelet into its "collection." Negotiation DC 12. It doesn't want money—it wants "a new story. A story about regret." The player must role-play, telling of one regret of their character. The GM adjusts the Negotiation DC based on sincerity (sincere -2, perfunctory +2).
  5. Gain a Clue: After a successful trade, the Collector hands the player the bracelet and says, "This bracelet didn't fall on its own. Someone stood by it for a long time—putting it on and taking it off again and again. Hesitation wore reality thin."
  6. The Truth: Xia Xia's grandmother passed away last month. Every night after closing, Xia Xia sits wearing the bracelet for a long time—unaware she is "wearing reality thin." She is close to awakening.

Ending Options

  • Return the Bracelet + Tell Xia Xia the Truth: Xia Xia becomes a Sensitive Companion (Loyalty 3). Return Mark +1.
  • Return the Bracelet Only: Return Mark +1. Xia Xia continues her routine—may awaken uncontrollably in a future scenario.
  • Keep the Bracelet: You may use the bracelet (Benevolent Keepsake effect: Endurance Cost recovers 1d6+2 Gap Endurance (GE)). But Xia Xia will keep looking for you.

Companion Recruitment

  • A-Kai: After this scenario, can become a Sensitive Companion (Type: Sensitive, Awareness 5 / Negotiation 4, special ability: Local Intel)
  • Xia Xia: If told the truth, can become an Apprentice Walker Companion (Type: Apprentice Walker, Traverse 3 / Retrieval 4, special ability: Keen Smell)

Rewards

  • Return Mark × 1-2
  • Can recruit 1-2 NPC Companions

Tier 2: Skilled (Return Mark 3-5)
