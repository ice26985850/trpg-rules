Part Three: Ascension Trial (Lv.11-16)

Main Campaign Act Three · Shurima Desert

Background

The fall of the Frostborn revealed the true purpose of "the Obsidian Circle" — collecting Forbidden Magic Stone on the surface while searching underground for the Shards of the Sun Disc. Legend says that gathering all the shards can restart the Ascension ritual and create a new generation of Ascended. The leader of the Obsidian Circle has already gone to Shurima, deep into the Emperor's Tomb. The players must search the desert for ancient clues and find their place in the inheritance struggle between Azir and Xerath.

Boss Battle: the Forbidden Ascended (Lv.16)

Attribute Value
HP 3,200
AD 120
AR/MR 140/140
Special Ascension Aura: recovers 3% of Health Points (HP) each turn

Three-Phase Mechanics: - P1 (100-70%): Sunbeam (straight line 4 cells, Ability Power (AP)×1.5) + summons Sand Soldiers (Health Points (HP) 400×3, Attack Damage (AD) 40) - P2 (70-35%): Ascended Form — size increases by 50%, attack range +2 cells; number of Sand Soldiers doubles - P3 (35-0%): Forbidden Ritual — drains the life of all combatants each turn (Health Points (HP)×3% restored to self); weakness exposed (back +50% damage)

Optional Redemption Route: If the players saved Renekton in 3.3, he will appear in P3 to assist, breaking the Boss's Ascension Aura.

  • Scenario-Exclusive Augment: "Sun Legacy" — gain 1 random Legendary-tier Augment Device
