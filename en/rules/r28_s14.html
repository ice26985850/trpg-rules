Dragon Isle Expedition (S14)

Season: Chapter 4 — "Final Battle Chapter" Basic Info - Suggested Level: Lv.66-73 - Difficulty: Hard - Expected Length: 2-3 game sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

To stand against the Clan Chief-Gods, greater power is needed. The players travel to Dragon Isle to seek the aid of the Dragon God Angerwide — but not everyone can pass the Dragon God's trials.

Main Location

  • Dragon Isle: A volcanic island, the territory of the Dragons. Extreme heat, toxic gas, draconic-magic traps.
  • Dragon God Temple: The entrance to Angerwide's divine realm, filled with elemental trial grounds.

Key Scenes

  • The Dragons' trial: the five-element trial of Fire, Ice, Thunder, Dark, and Holy
  • Dialogue with Angerwide — the Dragon God's condition (demands the players prove humanity is worth saving)
  • Receiving the Dragon God's blessing — but at a cost

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 10000, Dragon God's Blessing (all attributes +5, permanent), Dragon Scale Material (Red · Mythic)

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Trial Difficulty Class (DC) −15, number of guardians halved
