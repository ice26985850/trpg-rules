Scenario Eight: The Final Slate (Finale)

Applicable Level: Player Seq 2 or above, or equivalent influence level | Duration: 5–8 game sessions (epic finale) Recommended Players: Team of 3–5, or Solo + all surviving NPC companions

Background

Four tablet fragments are in your hands. Decoding progress 4/9. You already know:

  • Doomsday countdown: estimated from the first tablet — 15–30 years remain
  • Barrier weak points: one of seven has been patched (Scenario Six), six still need repair
  • The Ancient Sun God's final command: gather all nine fragments, read the tablet's final page
  • The weapon against the Outer Deities: Tablet 4/9 contains an incomplete "design" — the remaining fragments are needed to complete it

But now a problem arises — you do not need to find the remaining fragments. The remaining fragments are finding you.

A "resonance" exists between tablet fragments — when enough fragments are held by the same person, the others automatically "converge" toward the bearer. This is the Ancient Sun God's design — He knew the human who gathered all the fragments would need help. So when creating the tablet, He buried a "convergence" mechanism within it: once the bearer collects more than half the fragments, the others gradually draw near the bearer.

But this also means — all the factions holding other fragments now know your location.

Act One: Convergence

Within the following week, three things happen at once:

Event One: The Fifth Tablet Fragment (from the Church of Knowledge)

The Wisdom Angel of the Church of Knowledge (whom you met in Scenario Six) visits in person. He brings a tablet fragment — the Church of Knowledge has safeguarded it for two thousand years. Now he hands it to you:

"The church voted. Rather than keep locking it in the basement hoping it never proves useful — better to give it to someone already proven able to use it. One condition: the final tablet's contents — all of it. We want a copy."

This is a vote of confidence. The Church of Knowledge becomes your formal ally.

Event Two: The Sixth Tablet Fragment (from the Twilight Hermit Order)

The Twilight Hermit Order sent no one. What you received was a message transmitted by Adam himself. Not a voice — words written directly into your consciousness:

"The sixth fragment lies upon the altar of the Tree of the Desire Mother. We cannot retrieve it — because none of us can approach an Outer Deity's altar without being corrupted."

"But you can — your tablet fragment can suppress Outer Deity corruption (as you proved in Scenarios Five and Six). We offer an assault team of five — Seq 4–3, absolutely loyal. You lead them in, retrieve the fragment. Afterward — the tablet's contents — one sixth is ours."

(Then, a long pause.)

"Or — you may refuse. I will not force you. I am not like Him (whom Adam refers to, you vaguely guess)."

Event Three: The Tree of the Desire Mother's Full Invasion

The Tree of the Desire Mother no longer sends followers to attack you. This time — He comes in person.

The sky over Backlund turned a sickly purple in a single night. Trees began to move without wind — branches and leaves twisting into shapes that belong to no plant. A sweet smell of decay could be detected in every corner of the city. Beyonders began to experience "collective hallucinations" — they saw dead relatives standing at street corners, beckoning to them.

The Tree of the Desire Mother does not intend to destroy the city — He intends to "convert" everyone. Just as He converted Lightseeker (if you chose to let him sacrifice himself in Scenario Six). Only this time — the scale is the entire Backlund.

This is not a battle. It is a race against time.

Act Two: The Tree of the Desire Mother's Altar

The altar lies at the base of an abandoned church on the outskirts of Backlund. The church's floor was dug open — beneath it was not soil, but the roots of the Tree of the Desire Mother. Those roots are alive — they will entwine any Beyonder who approaches, injecting Outer Deity corruption into their bodies.

Scene Structure: Three Layers of Defense

Outer Layer — the Defense Line of the Desire's Followers

The Tree of the Desire Mother's Seq 4–5 followers (about 15–20 people) form multiple layers of defense around the church. A frontal breakthrough is not impossible — but every minute of delay expands the area of Backlund's corruption.

Twilight Hermit Order assault team (if you accept Adam's deal): - Five-member squad, Seq 4–3 - Tacit coordination: no one speaks — all communicate via the mental transmission of the Spectator Pathway - Tactics: They automatically handle the outer defense, opening a path for the player - Limitation: The assault team cannot approach within 50 meters of the altar (Outer Deity corruption would immediately corrupt them) — the final stretch must be walked by the player alone

Middle Layer — the Labyrinth of Desire

The tunnel leading underground was transformed by the Tree of the Desire Mother's power into a "Labyrinth of Desire." As the player passes through the tunnel, they see their deepest desires projected onto the walls — not hallucinations, but "you truly can see what would happen if you chose a different life."

Mind+Will (Extreme, once per layer, three layers total): - Layer One: Power — you see yourself seated on Backlund's throne, all bowing before you - Layer Two: Peace — you see yourself retreating to the countryside, never again taking part in the Beyonder world's conflicts. The laughter of family. Afternoon sunlight - Layer Three: Answers — you see yourself meeting the Ancient Sun God in person. He tells you the ultimate truth of everything

Each failure → the player is seduced by desire, wasting 1d3 hours (in-game time) in the hallucination. For every hour wasted, Backlund's corruption spread area increases by 10%. Failing all three → the entire city of Backlund is corrupted, Beyonder conversion rate reaches 40% — but the player can still continue the mission.

Core — the Heart of Roots of the Tree of the Desire Mother

The altar is not a stone platform. It is the Tree of the Desire Mother's "Heart of Roots" on Earth — a sphere about 3 meters tall, formed of dark purple roots. The sixth tablet fragment is embedded at the very center of the sphere.

When you reach out to take the fragment — the Tree of the Desire Mother speaks to you in person.

Not with a voice. Not with thought. With "longing." You suddenly feel an intense sense of belonging you have never experienced — as if you are not facing an Outer Deity, but facing a being that "wants to protect you." He wants you. Not to kill you. To — possess you.

"You need not suffer. You need not choose. You need not become a hero. Put down the tablet — put down this burden — come to me. I will make you never be alone again."

Mind+Will (Legendary −60): Resist the Tree of the Desire Mother's direct recruitment. The tablet fragment blazes with full light at this moment — each of your fragments uses its Ancient Sun God power to oppose the Tree of the Desire Mother's consciousness. Each fragment held grants +10 to the check (4 fragments = +40). This means the actual difficulty is −20.

The Tree of the Desire Mother's Recruitment vs the Tablet Fragment's Protection:

Number of Tablets Held Actual Will-check Difficulty Additional Effect
1–2 fragments Legendary −40 The tablet fragment provides only minimal protection
3–4 fragments Legendary −20 The tablet fragments begin to resonate — the Ancient Sun God's ancient echo rings in the player's ears (increasing resolve)
5–6 fragments Legendary −20 (gains an extra +20 from the tablet) The resonance becomes a chorus — the whispers of all the fallen angels of the Ancient Sun God gather in the player's consciousness

Act Three: The Tablet's Final Page

You retrieved the sixth fragment. The six tablets in your hands begin to arrange themselves — the resonance between them grows so strong it is visible to the naked eye — six white light-bands connect them, forming an incomplete ring.

You can now see the tablet's final page — incomplete, but enough to understand the Ancient Sun God's ultimate plan:

"The method against the Outer Deities — is not battle. It is sealing. You cannot merely seal the barrier's rift — you must permanently close all the weak points on the barrier. To do this — a ritual is required. A ritual requiring all twenty-two pathways' Uniquenesses activated simultaneously. None but I — can perform this ritual. And I — am no longer here."

"Unless — someone can gather enough pathway power. Not become Seq 0 — there is no time. It is a temporary borrowing — through the tablet fragment as a medium, borrowing the Uniquenesses held by the Seven Churches and the secret organizations. Only a quarter-hour is needed. A quarter-hour is enough."

"But the medium must be one who belongs to no single pathway — one who, in the course of growing up, absorbed the knowledge and power of multiple pathways. In other words — one who has gathered the tablet fragments."

You are that person. You need not become a god. You only need to hold the tablet, stand between the barrier and the Outer Deities, borrow all the Uniquenesses — and patch the final rift.

The Cost: Borrowing the power of twenty-two Uniquenesses will burn the medium — not a physical burning. It is your "pathway identity." After the ritual, you will lose all Beyonder abilities. You will become an ordinary person again.

Act Four: The Final Choice

From now on — no more fragments to gather. No more enemies to defeat. Only one choice: Are you willing?

Choice Requires Consequence Ending
Perform the ritual The Seven Churches agree to lend their Uniquenesses (if you built good relations with enough churches in the first seven scenarios — at least four churches — they will agree) You stand upon the barrier's seven weak points, tablet in hand, borrowing the power of twenty-two Uniquenesses. The barrier is permanently reinforced. The Outer Deities' invasion is delayed by at least a thousand years. You lose all Beyonder abilities. But you saved a world. Ending A: "The Nightwatcher" — you return to Gray Mist City and find a job at the Drowned Man Tavern. Every day you see Beyonders come and go, discussing their adventures. They do not know who you are. You pour drinks for them. You smile. You do not speak. On the darkest nights — the tablet fragment in your pocket glows faintly. Not power. Just — good night.
Awaken the Ancient Sun God's residual will Tablet fragments reach 6/9 or above, and you obtained the Ancient Sun God's relic at the Aurora Order's holy site (Scenario Seven) The Ancient Sun God's residual will is temporarily awakened — He takes over the ritual, you as the "body" rather than the "medium." The ritual succeeds — the barrier is repaired. But your personality will conflict with the Ancient Sun God's will. You will not vanish — but you are no longer wholly "you" Ending B: "Dawn" — your body remains, your memories remain. But when you look in the mirror — the reflection sometimes looks at you with another kind of gaze. Like a weary old man who has lived ten thousand years. You inherited the Sun God's last will — but you lost a part of yourself. All your friends say: "You look — a little different."
Refuse the ritual — seek an alternative Spend a great deal of time (months to years in-game) seeking a method that requires no sacrifice During the search — the barrier continues to deteriorate. Before you find an alternative, one of the weak points is breached by an Outer Deity. A smaller-scale Outer Deity invasion occurs — recorded in history as the "First Rift War." You did not stop the apocalypse — but you bought time for humanity Ending C: "Haven" — you organize a Beyonder coalition and establish a defense line at the invasion point. You did not save the world. You saved Gray Mist City and the surrounding region. Millions lived — because you were there. You do not regret this choice — but you can never be certain: if you had performed the ritual then, would more people have been saved?
Scatter the tablet fragments — let nine people each safeguard one Scatter your tablet fragments (if you have more than four) among your most trusted allies — one each The nine tablet bearers form a secret network — the "Stone-Bearer Council." Each learns from the tablet, each prepares for the apocalypse. No one can read the complete final page — but humanity now has nine "sparks" rather than one. In case one fails — there are others Ending D: "The Spark" — you buy a small cottage on the outskirts of Gray Mist City. No electricity. No gaslight. Only a table, a chair, and your tablet fragment. You do not know who will ultimately close the barrier — perhaps you, perhaps your companion, perhaps the next person to pick up the tablet. But you believe — humanity does not need a savior. Humanity needs many who refuse to give up.

Loss of Control Risk — Finale

In scenes where the ritual is performed or the tablet fragment runs at full power:

Risk Point Value
Tablet fragment at full resonance +3d10 (one-time)
Borrowing Uniquenesses (per each) +1d6
The Tree of the Desire Mother's recruitment attempt +2d10 (if resistance succeeds); if it fails, Loss of Control triggers directly
Spirit Exhaustion +2d5
Total (full finale) 40–80+ (depending on choice)

GM Note: In the finale — Loss of Control is not failure. Loss of Control is the cost. The player will likely reach the Edge of Loss or even trigger Loss of Control during the ritual. If the player loses control during the ritual — their loss-of-control form will be temporarily suppressed by the tablet fragment's power (because the tablet fragment will complete the ritual at any cost). After the ritual succeeds — the player's loss-of-control state is adjudicated by the GM: they may be sealed by the Nighthawks, cured, or survive permanently in a half-lost state.
