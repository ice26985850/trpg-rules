Introduction

Otherworld Invasion TRPG — When Gunfire Fails, You Awaken

【One-Line Pitch】 Modern cities are invaded by otherworld beastmen; bullets lose all meaning before the rifts — but you receive a "System" visible only to you, and every battle makes you stronger.

【World Setting】 One day, the otherworld's six great beastman clans tore open the Main Rift at the World Heart, and secondary portals spread across the globe. Humanity soon discovered: bullets that pass through a rift turn to dust, missiles fizzle near monsters, and electronics short-circuit on approach — because when two systems of physics collide, the otherworld's laws take priority. Most people could only retreat, but a very few "Awakened": a floating interface visible only to you appears in your vision — the survival add-on the gods wrote for you. Your starting point is just an ordinary person, but the ceiling is yours to decide.

【Who Do You Play? · Core Gameplay】 You are an "Awakener," commissioned by Osaikus, the God of Light, and Collector, the God of Play, to shoulder a gamified System and face the rifts. The loop is intuitive: accept a commission or quest → step into an Erosion Zone and purge monsters → gain Experience Points (EXP) and level up → grow stronger, loot equipment, and close the rift. Most distinctive is "Lawful Creep": the invasion seeps in like a tide, making the world more dangerous — you must grow stronger before the next wave, for stagnation equals death.

【System Highlights】 - This work uses Percentile Dice (d100) for resolution: attack rolls hit on a low roll, while skill checks challenge the Difficulty Class (DC) on a high roll. A roll of 1–5 is a Critical Success, 96–100 a Critical Failure — every action keeps you holding your breath. - Six core attributes — Strength (STR), Dexterity (DEX), Constitution (CON), Intelligence (INT), Wisdom (WIS), Spirit (SPI) — plus a dual-class progression: choose one of 8 Combat Classes and one of 8 Life Classes, then pair it with a randomly awakened signature talent (draw "Cheat Death" and you can forcibly keep 1 Hit Point (HP) when you hit zero). - Six tiers of rarity for equipment: from White · Common to Red · Mythic, strength readable at a glance. Otherworld resources deep in the rifts are the only source for forging top-tier gear, pushing you into peril.

【For What Kind of Players】 Players who love the progression thrill of "growing from an ordinary person all the way to a legend" and enjoy point allocation and resource planning will be hooked; meanwhile, rich story branches (five ending divergences) also serve immersive players. A little TRPG foundation helps, but the core rules are clear.

【Game Style Tags】 - Combat Weight: Core (combat is the primary gameplay) - Strategic Depth: Moderate Strategy (requires considering skill combinations and resource timing) - Story-Driven: Story-Supported (clear plot, but high player freedom) - Resource Management: Moderate Management (must watch key resources, but not too punishing) - Character Customization: High (many options to create a unique character) - Character Creation Complexity: Moderate (simple steps, quick to finish) - Rule Density: Moderate Rules (core rules clear, not cumbersome) - GM Requirement: Optional GM (supports both groups and solo play)

【Estimated Play Time】 - Single Scenario: about 3–6 hours - Full Campaign (five seasons, 20 scenarios advancing in sequence, level rising from Lv.1 all the way past 100): about 80–160 hours

【Basic Information】 - Players: 1–5 players + 1 GM (built-in solo-mode adjustment allows single-player) - Core Dice: d100 (Percentile Dice)
