Scenario B-2: "Signal Amid the Ashes" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)

Mode: Astartes · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　Map: maps/map17_eldenfell.svg

Difficulty: Low | Location: Ruined City "Eldenfell" Recommended Attributes: 1–2 growths after character creation

Background

The squad arrives at the bombed-out ruined city. The signal source lies in the administrative tower at the city's center—but there is something else in the city.

Scenario Structure

Phase Content
City Edge Survivors—some 40–50 civilians hiding in the city's sewers. Their leader is a former local Arbites Enforcer, "Kara". The civilians regard the Astartes with a mix of awe and fear.
City Interior Mutant nest—about 5–8 low-tier Mutants + 1 Mutant Tyrant occupy the main streets. You may choose to go through or around.
Administrative Tower The comms device is on the top floor. But the tower has been occupied by Chaos Cultists—a remnant cult that was active here before Exterminatus (about 8–10 followers + 1 Acolyte).
Signal Source The comms device is still operating—but the signal is not a distress call. It is an automatically looping Imperial evacuation-coordinate broadcast. This means that before Exterminatus, an evacuation point was set up in the northern mountains.

Oath Conflict Point

Conflict: Protect the Innocent vs. Obey Orders

The squad must decide how to handle these civilians: - Take them along (but they will slow you down and consume resources) - Leave them here (but the Mutants will find them) - Give them weapons to defend themselves (but that amounts to admitting the Imperium cannot protect them)

Key NPCs

NPC Role Details
Kara Civilian Leader Pragmatic, resilient. Does not trust the Astartes, but knows she cannot survive without them.
Data-Slate Record Clue Records in the administrative tower reveal the "reason" for Exterminatus—a suspected but unconfirmed Chaos contamination report.

Combat Encounters

Encounter Enemies Challenge Rating (CR) Tactics

Administrative Tower Chaos Cultists ×8 + Acolyte ×1 0×8+1 Floor-by-floor clearing inside a multi-level building
