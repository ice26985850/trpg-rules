Introduction

Backrooms Walker TRPG — Player Introduction

Reference Works Statement

This work is an original TRPG rules design inspired by the internet urban legend "The Backrooms" and its community settings (such as M.E.G., BNTG, and other factions). It is not an official licensed adaptation of any work.

What Kind of World Is This?

You have "clipped through" from the real world into the Backrooms — a parallel labyrinth of infinite Levels, where yellow fluorescent lights hum forever, the carpet is always damp, and every wall looks exactly the same. There is no manual, no quest, no way back. The Backrooms do not care about you; they bear no malice, only an absence of kindness.

Who Do You Play?

An ordinary person. Maybe an office worker pulling a late night, a delivery driver lost in a parking garage, a student who took the wrong door. Your only goal: survive, and find the next exit. The Backrooms hold "Entities" — but most are not monsters you can defeat; they are part of the environment. Most of the time, you should run, not fight.

What Makes This Ruleset Special?

First, combat is the last resort. Entities resist physical damage; a spear's value lies in keeping distance, not dealing harm — your real weapon is understanding the environment's rules. Second, an original Sanity (SP) system — the Backrooms constantly erode your mind; at zero Sanity you do not die, you become "The Lost." Third, character growth uses no levels, but "Milestones" — the important moments you live through in the Backrooms, each one changing who you are.

Who Is It For?

Players who love survival horror, resource management, and atmospheric immersion will adore it. If you enjoy the moral dilemmas of This War of Mine, the wilderness survival of S.T.A.L.K.E.R., or simply want to experience the loneliness of seeking meaning in an endless maze, this is your game. No deep TRPG experience needed — if you can roll a twenty-sided die (D20), you can play.
