Scenario S1: Novice Trial

Type: Multiplayer Squad | Suggested Level: 1 | Difficulty: ★ | Estimated Duration: 2-3 hours

Story Synopsis

Your first drop. Erangel (Newbie Island) — an abandoned Soviet military island. The sea breeze carries the smell of salt and rust. Twenty contestants, four squads — including yours. No one expects you to win. But you don't plan to let them have their way either.

Battlefield Map

Erangel (Newbie Island) — 4 km × 4 km (condensed version)

Terrain Composition: Coastal Plains (40%), Hills (30%), Abandoned Military Facilities (20%), Small Town (10%)

Landmarks

  • Abandoned Radar Station (military|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): An abandoned radar station atop a hill — excellent view, but easily sniped
  • Porto Noma Fishing Village (residential|Loot: Common (Green)): A fishing village by the coast, a dozen wooden huts with narrow alleys
  • Sakura Farm (rural|Loot: Scarce (Gray)): Open farmland and barns — good for vehicles, poor for hiding
  • Military Outpost (military|Loot: Military (Purple)): A small military outpost in the northeast corner — the best loot on the map, but remote

Enemy Configuration

A total of 4 enemy squads (including the player squad).

  • Rookie Squad (★): 3 squads, 4 members each, drop point Random
  • Steady Squad (★★): 1 squad, 4 members each, drop point Military Outpost

Battlefield Conditions

  • Circle: Central Contraction — a predictable standard circle — suitable for beginners
  • Airdrop: 1 drop, during the 2nd circle contraction — M24 Sniper Rifle + 4x Scope + .300 Ammo ×30
  • Red Zone Bombing: None
  • Vehicles: Jeep, Motorcycle
  • Weather: Clear
  • Time of Day: Day (noon)

Scenario Phases

1. Drop (1 turn)

GM Note: Describe the parachute drop scene: "The transport plane's rear cargo door opens — sea breeze rushes in, engines roar. Twenty contestants stand at the doorway, looking down at the island. Some are already praying." Let the players choose their drop point.

2. Looting Phase (5 turns)

GM Note: First circle — give players ample looting time. Describe the environmental atmosphere. In the distance, occasional gunshots ring out (encounters between NPCs). Tip: "Listen while looting — footsteps are the earliest warning."

3. First Battle (3-4 turns)

GM Note: Arrange for the players to encounter a Rookie Squad. This is the players' first battle — let the Rookies make some obvious mistakes (poor reload timing, unsteady aim), so players can experience the full loop of "hit → damage → down."

4. Circle Pressure (4 turns)

GM Note: The 2nd circle contracts — the safe zone center is near the players' current position. This means other squads are moving toward the players. Introduce the Steady Squad — tougher than the Rookies, and they use smoke grenades.

5. Final Battle (3-5 turns)

GM Note: 2-3 squads remain (including players). The circle shrinks to 200m. Ensure the final battle has enough cover and tactical choices. Victory condition: eliminate all enemies or be the last surviving squad.

Victory Condition

Be the last surviving squad
