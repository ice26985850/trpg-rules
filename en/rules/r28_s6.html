Through the Rift (S6)

Season: Chapter 2 "Resistance Chapter" Basic Information - Suggested Level: Lv.25-32 - Difficulty: Normal - Expected Length: 2-3 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.
