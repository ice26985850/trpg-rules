Scenario 5: Dragon General Battle (Dragonridge Peak · Proof of the Flamescale)

How to Run: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + party-size adjustment rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. Party-size adjustment rules are the same as the Prologue (see table below).

Players Enemy Challenge Rating (CR) Enemy Count Resource Supply Note
1 (Solo) Baseline −3 ×0.5 (at least 1) +50% Resource supply +50% (no assist, see Chapter 8)
2 Baseline −1 ×0.75 +20% —
3 Baseline ±0 ×1 Baseline —
4 (Baseline) Baseline ×1 Baseline —
5–6 Baseline +2 ×1.5 −10~−20% Concurrent events +1

Difficulty Rule: The Dragon General Boss is a boss battle, designed Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ party total level ×0.6 (at 4 PCs Character Level (CL) 40, about Challenge Rating (CR) 40); the GM may fine-tune by formula. After adjustment it must ensure "lowest level (CL40 threshold) can clear, highest level (CL42 leading to S6) has challenge." The Dragon General has been adjusted to a late-phase elite (originally CR12→CR40), see Monster Bestiary and GM Book 12.3.

I. Opening Description

The call-up order to subjugate the First Demon General is issued. Players head north to "Dragonridge Peak (R1 Northern Ice Plains)," gazing from the cold wind at the volcano crater breathing crimson flames. Ironforge City Lord Durin Anvil serves as guide and equipment supplier, hinting: Dragon General Ignisia Flamescale honors strength; only true power can make her face humanity.

II. Scene Flow

Act 0: Take on the Subjugation Commission (Opening Beat)

You ground through the leveling phase across the six themed dungeons (D3 Forest→D8 Ancient Ruins) and themed guild commissions, grinding all the way to Character Level (CL) 40, guild rank raised to Rank S (CR40 cap), only then can you accept the "Subjugate the First Demon General · Dragon General" call-up order—requires Rank S (Influence Points (IP) ≥40 to accept; Character Level (CL) 40 end-of-leveling-phase usually naturally meets it, consistent with the Rank S acceptance threshold). This call-up order did not exist during the leveling phase: it was the battle merits you accumulated through dungeons and commissions during your CL1–40 leveling phase that pushed your standing from F to S, only then could the guild issue it.

After acceptance, the call-up order is formally issued: the military order to subjugate the First Demon General has arrived; you pack up and head north to Dragonridge Peak. Solo mode is supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist. Whether you subjugate or persuade the Dragon General at chapter end, the guild acknowledges the battle merits and issues the "Dragon General Beacon," leading to Scenario 6.

Act 1: Investigate the Dragon General's Trail

Gather evidence at the foothill village of Dragonridge Peak (Knowledge / Perception check Difficulty Class (DC) 14). Discover a burned watchtower and dragon-scale fragments; you may choose to soothe the frightened villagers (Influence +4) or forcibly interrogate (negative, wanton killing −5). - Influence Points (IP) Hook: Protect the refugee village caught in dragon breath +4 Influence Points (IP).

Act 2: Explore the Dragon's Lair

Climb into the volcano cavern (terrain: lava fissures, movement slowed by terrain, Woodland origin exempt). Encounter dragon outposts and young dragons. Cavern width suggested 10 squares (20m), front/back row spacing 2 squares.

Act 3: Dragon General Boss Battle (Three Phases)

Ignisia Flamescale (CR 40, Boss battle), shifts phase by Health (HP) threshold: - Phase 1 "Whelp Form" (HP 100%~66%): Scale armor Damage Reduction 30%; normal dragon claw melee (Physical Slash). - Phase 2 "Complete Form" (HP 66% shift): Unlocks [Dragon Breath (cone Area Effect (AOE) Fire, radius 3 squares)] and [Aerial Assault (Charge + Fear)]. - Phase 3 "Berserk Form" (HP 33% shift): [Scale Armor Shattered] DR weakens, enters critical-hit window; [Apocalyptic Breath (large AOE)] once every 2 rounds, paired with player's high crit to finish.

III. Non-Player Character (NPC) Sample Dialogue

Ironforge City Lord Durin: "Dragon whelps only respect strength—whoever has the harder fist, they listen. Don't think of talking your way out; survive one of her dragon breaths first." Dragon General Ignisia Flamescale: "Human? Come to die again. Let this General see... whether your 'Hero' title is hot enough to handle." (tsundere, battle-loving) Jeno (lurking): "(In secret) The Dragon General is straightforward; a head-on clash is actually easier to handle... if we can avoid killing, that dumb dragon might even become an ally."

IV. Encounter Info

Encounter Enemy Challenge Rating (CR) Baseline Count (4 PCs) Party-size Adjustment
Dragon Lair Outpost Dragon Soldier 8 ×3 General rule: 1 PC→CR−3/×0.5; 5–6 PCs→CR+2/×1.5
Dragon Lair Outpost Young Dragon Hatchling 6 ×2 Same as above
Dragon General Boss Ignisia Flamescale 40 ×1 Boss battle; 1 PC→CR 37; 5–6 PCs→CR 42
Boss Guard Dragon Soldier 8 ×2 (Phase 2 summon) Same as above

Dragon Soldier: Health (HP) ~109, Attack Bonus (ATK) +6, Evasion (EVA) 16; Resistance Fire ×0.5. Young Dragon Hatchling: Health (HP) ~72, Attack Bonus (ATK) +3, Weakness Frost ×1.5.

V. Stage Design

  • Stage A Investigation: Social / Exploration (DC 14 Knowledge).
  • Stage B Dragon Lair Exploration: Combat + terrain (lava DoT threat).
  • Stage C Dragon General Boss: Three-phase boss battle, includes "critical-hit finishing window."
  • Chapter-end Twist: After subjugating or persuading the Dragon General, the guild acknowledges the battle merits and promotion, issuing the "Dragon General Beacon."

VI. Rewards

  • Experience (EXP): Outpost +600; Dragon General Boss ×4 ≈ +8,800/person; quest settlement +~1,734.
  • Gold (G): total 1,200 G.
  • Items: Flamescale Shard (smithing material), Dragon General Beacon (Beacon, proof to open the subsequent Demon General reckoning), Flame Resistance Charm.

VII. Recommended Level & Difficulty

  • Recommended Character Level (CL) range: 40 (threshold).
  • Difficulty: Hard (Difficulty Class (DC) 23; Boss battle budget ×1.2).
