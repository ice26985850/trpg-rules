Scenario 1: Crimson Ball in the White Room

Recommended Level: Sequence 9 | Recommended Players: 3-5 | Duration: 2-3 sessions

Background

For three months, a rumor has spread through the artist circles of Gray Mist City: receive a white invitation embossed with a red rose, and you will attend a once-in-a-lifetime ball in the "White Room." Those who returned spoke of their works suddenly bursting with astonishing vitality. After the most recent ball, a painter vanished—leaving an unfinished painting in his studio: six people dancing, but the sixth dancer's face is blurred beyond recognition.

Hooks (choose 1 of 3)

  1. Commission: The missing painter's family hires the players to investigate.
  2. Rumor: The players hear the ball rumor at the Drowned Man Tavern, triggering a Spiritual intuition.
  3. Coincidence: One player receives a white invitation.

Act 1: Investigation

Clue Point 1 — The Missing One's Home (edge of the Crown District) - Surface clue: The face of the sixth dancer on the painting keeps changing. - Spiritual clue: Spirit Sight → residual Spectator Pathway; Divination → "Dance intertwined with death." - Risk: Spirit Sight pushed too deep triggers +1d3 Loss of Control.

Clue Point 2 — 13 Moran Street (Iron Bridge District) - Surface clue: Neighbors say a black carriage comes to collect people every Friday night. - Spiritual clue: Divination shows the carriage comes from the direction of the abandoned theater in the Iron Bridge District.

Clue Point 3 — Drowned Man Tavern (Ash Harbor District) - Social challenge: Must succeed at Negotiation/Bribery against an informant. - Info: Lady White was once a prodigy dancer, "miraculously surviving" the Royal Theater fire five years ago. - Failure consequence: The informant tips off Lady White, increasing the final encounter's difficulty.

Clue Point 4 — Mistgrave University (Academy District) - Academic challenge: Reading old newspapers reveals the fire report; all follow-up reports vanish. - Mysticism: A Spectator Pathway Sequence 6 can cause large-scale effects in highly emotional environments.

Act 2: Conflict

Scene 1 — Infiltrating the Ball (basement of the abandoned theater, Iron Bridge District) - Environment: A lavish but dilapidated ballroom; over 20 guests dancing. - Key discovery: One of the six dancers is already dead, but Lady White refuses to admit it. - Path A (Social): Talk with Lady White; discover she lives inside a hallucination of her own making. - Path B (Stealth): Sneak backstage to find the Sealed Artifact. - Path C (Combat): Triggered once discovered.

Scene 2 — Confrontation - Lady White activates her power: all guests become "dancers" under emotional control. - Objective options: defeat her head-on, recall the controlled guests, or destroy the Sealed Artifact.

Antagonist Data: - Lady White | Spectator Pathway Sequence 6 "Hypnotist" - Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 55, Mind (MND) 50, Body (BOD) 35, Agility (AGI) 45, Charisma (CHA) 65 - HP: 115 / Spirit Pool (SP): 170 / Loss of Control (LoC): 45 / Loss of Control Threshold (LoCT): 110 - Powers: Dance Hypnosis (1 AP / 5 Spirit / 20m / area Charm), Emotional Storm (2 AP / 10 Spirit / whole area), Tempo Control (Reaction / controls hypnotized targets' actions) - Weakness: −20 Mind when confronted with the truth; actively showing her the fire memories can shake her power. - Tactics: Places controlled guests in the front line while she controls. Below 50% HP triggers "Eternal Waltz"—a berserk state.

Sealed Artifact Data: - "Eternal Metronome" | Tier 2 | Spectator Pathway Sequence 6 - Positive: Controls the emotions of those around her. - Side effect: Obsessed with the perfect rhythm, thinks of everything in counts of eight. - Hidden: At the stroke of midnight it sways on its own and summons dancers.

Act 3: The Choice

Revelation: Five years ago, Lady White lost control during the fire and bound the spectators within a dance hallucination. She has been "reholding" the ball, convinced that if they dance perfectly enough, everything can begin again. That dead dancer—was herself, as she sees herself in the hallucination.
