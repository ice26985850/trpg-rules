Gate of the Gods (S17)

Season: Chapter 5 "Godfall Chapter" Basic Information - Recommended Level: Lv.81-88 - Difficulty: Extreme - Expected Length: 2-3 sessions - Play Mode: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

Core Conflict

Defeating a projection is only the beginning. The path to the God-Realm opens — the players must traverse the God-Realm boundary, a route fraught with Law Turbulence and Ancient Guardians.

Main Locations

  • God-Realm Boundary (Law Turbulence zones across 5 regions)

Key Scenes

  • Law Turbulence — the physical laws of each region differ (one region has reversed gravity, another has anomalous time flow, etc.)
  • Encounter with the Ancient Guardians — beings that have existed since the birth of the World Heart
  • Collector's Message — the God of Play left clues along the way

Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 15000, God-Realm passage rights

Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Guardians halved, Law Turbulence frequency reduced
