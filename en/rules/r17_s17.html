Scenario S7: Under the Iron Fist [Level (Lv.) 7]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 7 × 1 player + 2 companions | Duration: 4-5 hours | Map: Rusted Factory Administration Building

Commission

Scavengers' Compact bounty 12,000 ₽: eliminate "Iron Fist" Viktor. Old Ke says: "One person might manage after all — but you'll need to be clever. Do you have two reliable people?" (This scenario recommends bringing 2 companions)

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry — Underground Pipeline Secret Entrance

Old Ke provides the secret pipeline entrance to the Iron Brotherhood Administration Building (requires Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 14 to locate; if the companion is a Technician, automatically succeeds).

Scene 2: Pipeline → Boiler Room

Encounter Mutant Rats ×3 inside the pipeline. The exit is the Administration Building's boiler room — an Iron Brotherhood soldier is resting here (can be ambushed; preemption rule triggered: first strike aims at the head).

Scene 3: Command Center (5th Floor)

Viktor + 2 guards. - Solo Boss Weakening: Viktor's Chest Hit Points (HP) drop from 26 to 18 (×0.7), Damage Reduction (DR) drops from 5 to 4 (−1). Reinforcements canceled. - Solo Tactics: Deal with the guards first (Threat Value 7×2=14), then focus fire on Viktor. Use cover and draw fire with companion firepower. - Optional Stealth Assassination: If the player enters the command center with Stealth Target Number (TN) 20, they can aim directly at Viktor's head (5% Instant Death + 30% aimed = 35%). Failure leads to normal combat.

Scene 4: Extraction

Zone-wide alarm. Viktor's death automatically triggers the alarm → all regular Extraction Points are locked. - Only route: Boiler Room → Pipeline → Scrap Yard → flare gun to call helicopter (4 rounds wait) - During the wait: Iron Brotherhood reinforcements +1 soldier per round (max accumulates to 3)

Solo Adjustment

  • Too Hard: 1 guard. Viktor Hit Points (HP) ×0.5.
  • Too Easy: Viktor activates self-destruct before dying (3-round countdown, 2d6/5m) — must extract quickly
