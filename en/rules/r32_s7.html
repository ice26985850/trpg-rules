Scenario 7: "The Spreading Crack"

Item Content
Type Gap Flooding (Emergency Event)
Recommended Marks 6-8
Sessions 2-3
Core Attributes Awareness, Traverse, Anchor
Time Pressure If not handled within three days → the entire area permanently becomes the Gap Layer
Difficulty ★★★★

Opening

The old Eastside neighborhood is "vanishing" — the alleys show wrong perspective, and when you open your front door it isn't your own living room; the basement goes three levels down and still isn't at the bottom. Three residents have disappeared. The city government has sealed off three blocks. You receive an urgent warning: a Crack in a Sealed Space is expanding. Someone put something inside it that shouldn't be there.

Key NPCs

NPC Info
The Little Girl's Residual the Forgotten (Retrieval Difficulty Class (DC) 20, solo). A child lost in an air raid in the 1940s. Not evil — she is trapped by being forgotten itself
The 80-Year-Old Younger Brother The little girl's younger brother. The only one who knows her name and the truth
Path Guardian (Horse) Guarding the key path. Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 14

Lost Items

Lost Item Quality Solo Difficulty Class (DC) Location
The little girl's schoolbag Memory Crystal 13 the Sealed-Zone Gap — the school's Residual
The younger brother's photo Common Lost Item 6 the Sealed-Zone Gap — the old house
The Old Walker's Sealing Notes Gap Construct 16 Pieced together from three locations

Scenes

  1. Assess the Crack: Awareness Difficulty Class (DC) 10 to see the spatial anomaly. Traverse Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to enter safely (solo already -2).[[MAP-07 場景1]]
  2. Path Guardian: Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 14. Each 3 turns waited: Difficulty Class (DC) -2. Speaking your True Name auto-succeeds.[[MAP-07 場景3]]
  3. Find the Old Walker's Notes: Three puzzle-like notes record the sealing method and the girl's name.[[MAP-07 場景4]]
  4. Liberate vs. Seal: Find the little girl — she crouches in a corner humming the same song. Requires three items (photo + schoolbag + name) to liberate.
  5. Liberate: Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 20 (with the three items, Difficulty Class (DC) 14). Success → she smiles and vanishes, the Crack begins to heal.[[MAP-07 場景5]]
  6. Seal: Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 18. Temporarily safe; will expand again months later.
  7. Failure: On the third day the Crack swallows the block. All residents begin to forget they ever lived there.
  8. Siblings' Reunion: If the 80-year-old brother is willing to enter the Gap to see his sister — while present, the Liberate Difficulty Class (DC) drops to 8 (at the edge of automatic success).[[MAP-07 場景6]]

Safety Net Trigger

Map Reference: Scenario 7 refers throughout to maps/MAP-07_community_crack.html (Eastside Community Crack map).

If a player's Gap Endurance (GE) hits zero in this scenario — Intuitive Dodge ejects you to the nearest safe Threshold. The Crack expands half a day more — but no disaster. (Reference: Solo Expansion Rules §S2.3)

Rewards

  • Return Mark × 2 (liberation + note return)
  • Knowledge Mark: long-term advantage regarding Sealed Spaces
