Scenario S2: Prey in the Shadows

Growth Stage: Apprentice Hunter (16-40 Experience Points (XP)) Duration: 3-4 hours Premise: Completed Scenario S1 or equivalent experience

Background

Three months have passed. You've independently completed a few small commissions. The Association is starting to give you weightier tasks.

This morning, a coded message arrived from the Association's Intelligence Division:

"Eastern Bartokia Continent—three days ago, a Bounty Hunter went missing while tracking a target. The last reported coordinates show him at an abandoned logging site." "The missing Hunter is named Carl. His tracking target is called the 'Phantom'—a Nen user who specializes in poaching rare Beasts." "The Phantom has never been seen. Those who have seen him—cannot speak of it."

Mission: Find Carl. Arrest or eliminate the Phantom.

Scene One: Abandoned Logging Site — Carl's Trail

Duration: 45 minutes Type: Exploration

The logging site has been abandoned for ten years. Rusted machines half-buried in weeds, the air smelling of wood shavings and decaying leaves. You find Carl's backpack—opened, dumped on the ground, but no blood.

Inside the backpack is his notebook. The last page reads: "The Phantom is not one person—but a pair. Twins. One lures you away, the other strikes from behind. Remember—do not look in only one direction."

Exploration Objectives

Objective Check Discovery
Inspect backpack Investigation DC 14 Carl's notebook + 1 SP recovery potion (minor)
Search logging site Perception DC 16 Two sets of footprints on the ground—but in opposite directions
Track footprints (Group A) Tracking DC 14 Group A heads deep into the forest—a decoy
Track footprints (Group B) Tracking DC 18 Group B loops around to a cellar entrance behind the logging site—the real target

Scene Two: The Hunt — Who Hunts Whom

Duration: 60 minutes Type: Chase / Combat

The PC follows Group B's footprints into the forest. Roll a d6 every 10 minutes:

d6 Event
1-2 Beast trap—a bear trap set by the Phantom (Reflex DC 14, failure 1d6+2 damage + Restrained)
3-4 Find an injured Beast (rare species, wounded by the Phantom's trap—option to heal for intel, Medicine DC 14)
5 Encounter a forest Hunter (NPC, willing to trade intel—learns the Phantom's lair location)
6 Ambushed from the side by one of the Phantom (CR 3, initiative advantage—sneak attack!)

Phantom · Elder Brother (CR 3, Transmutation)

A figure drops from the tree—light as a leaf. He does not introduce himself—his fists are the introduction.

Attribute Value
Hit Points (HP) 60
Spirit Points (SP) 30
Armor Class (AC) 14 (Dexterity 16)
Attack Assassin Fist: d20+5, 1d8+3 bludgeoning
Nen Ability Shadow Step (SP 2): Reaction—before being attacked, teleport to any position within 3m; that attack automatically misses (twice per combat)
Tactics Disappears after a combo—this is not a fight to the death, but a test of the PC's strength

Scene Three: The Cellar — Lair of the Twins

Duration: 90 minutes Type: Combat / Infiltration

The cellar entrance is hidden at the bottom of a dry well behind the logging site. You find Carl—tied in a corner, tape over his mouth, but alive.

Then you hear two overlapping voices from behind: "You're far more interesting than Carl."

Phantom · Twins (CR 4 ×1 + CR 3 ×1 twin battle)

Phantom · Younger Brother (CR 3)—same stats as the elder, but different Nen ability:

Attribute Value
Nen Ability Mirror Shadow (SP 2): Create an illusory duplicate identical to itself (HP 1, disappears when attacked). Once per combat.

Twin Tactics: - Turn 1: Younger brother creates the duplicate (PC must make Perception DC 16 to judge which is real), elder brother attacks head-on - Turn 2: Elder brother uses Shadow Step to dodge the PC's counterattack, younger brother flanks - Turn 3: Twins attack simultaneously (PC must choose to defend or counter one that turn)

How to Win the Fight: 1. Defeat younger brother (CR 3, weaker) → elder stops: "Enough. You win. Take Carl." 2. Defeat elder brother (CR 4) → younger brother surrenders 3. Persuade the twins (Persuasion DC 18) → "We only hunt Beasts, not kill people. Carl was an accident—he fell into the trap we set for Beasts. We were about to let him go."

If the PC chooses to persuade rather than kill the twins: The twins give the PC a token—a coin engraved with the character for "Shadow". In Scenario S4 (Hunter in the Dark), this token gives the PC advantage on negotiations with the underground world.

Scene Four: The Report — Carl's Gratitude

Duration: 15 minutes

Carl rubs his wrists, bound for two days: "Thanks. The Phantom told the truth—I fell into the trap they set for Beasts; they actually saved me."

He pulls a small pouch from his pocket: "This is what they didn't confiscate. I found it in a ruins up north—the 'Tracker's Eye'. More useful to you than me."

Scenario S2 Rewards:

Reward Details

Items 8,000J, Tracker's Eye (equipment: advantage on Tracking checks, once per day)
Relationship Carl (Bounty Hunter—future intel source), Phantom Twins (non-hostile)
Token If twins persuaded: "Shadow"-engraved coin (used in Scenario S4)
