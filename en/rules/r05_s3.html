D3 Forest

Recommended Rank: F / E (Challenge Rating (CR) 1–6) | Theme: Verdant dense forest, low-mid tier resource grinding Accept: Guild commission "F-For1 / E-For1" (corresponds Rank F→E, CL 1–6); or designated by GM as leveling field. Map: maps/D3_森林.yaml + maps/D3_森林.svg This work has no multiclass; solo entry follows party-size adjustment rules (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist).

How to Enter (Opening Beat)

  • Take guild commission: At the adventurer guild bulletin board accept F-tier "Forest Subjugation" (F-For1), after promotion accept E-For1 (corresponds Rank F→E, CL 1–6).
  • Active exploration: After players join in S0, they may actively explore from the forest edge outside the city (GM Book 17.1).
  • Leveling positioning: CL 1–6 veterans' / resource-grinding first choice; parallel with F/E commissions, grind Rank from F to E (see "Prologue · Alno Holy City Hub section").
  • Solo mode: Supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist; mini-boss room includes 10×10 positioning diagram (maps/D3_戰鬥_哥布林王.yaml), entering switches to standard combat (Chapter 7), clearing returns to exploration round.

I. Background

Deep in the verdant dense forest outside Dawnlight Village, a tribe of goblins built a stronghold beneath the ancient tree's roots; the chief who calls himself "Goblin King" rules the surrounding hunting trails with vine armor and brute force. The guild lists this as an F→E tier subjugation target; at the end of the dense forest, the ancient tree throne hosts this mini-boss.

III. Key Room Details

  • R4 Pitfall Trail (Weak): Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); trigger 1d6 Pierce. Disarm DC14.
  • R8 Poison Bee Corridor (Strong): Perception DC18; trigger 2d6 Poison + Stun 1 round. Disturbing the bee nest can block R3↔R7 passage (GM ruling).
  • R5 Mossy Stone Vault: Open chest DC12; trap 30% (mid, 2d6). Contains: herbs ×3, ragged cloth ×2, Gold (G) 30~60.
  • R9 Relic Stone Chamber: Open chest DC14; contains forest fruit ×2, Fine materials, Gold (G) 40~80.
  • R11 Exit Trail: After clearing return to guild to settle reward and leave.

IV. Mini-boss — Goblin King (CR5)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 Boss framework (simplified):

  • Health (HP) 54 (CR5 formula) | Attack Bonus (ATK) +2 (floor(1+0.25×5)) | Evasion (EVA) 10 (10+floor(0.15×5)) | Damage/hit 12 (floor(6+1.2×5))
  • Trait (1 phase): Brute Swing—once every 3 rounds, deals damage ×1.5 to Front Row and Knockback 1 square.
  • Weakness: Fire ×1.5 (see Monster Bestiary fire-weakness note).
  • Drop: Goblin King Insignia (story item, redeemable at guild for Influence Points (IP) +2); basic materials + low chance Fine recipe.

Design hint: D3 is a stable CL 1–6 area for "grinding EXP+G+materials," preventing players from leveling only through main-story Demon General battles and causing economic imbalance. Can be re-cleared daily (see "Commission Catalog" opening, reset rules).
