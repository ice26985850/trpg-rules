s02_Winter's Awakening (Excerpted from the League of Legends TRPG Campaign Scenario Collection · Multiplayer Scenario · Main Campaign Part Two)

Part Two: Winter's Awakening (Lv.6-10)

Main Campaign Act Two · Freljord Tundra

Background

After destroying the Obsidian Forge, the players discover that the secret backer of "the Obsidian Circle" comes from Freljord — an ancient bloodline known as the "Frostborn". They are attempting to use the Forbidden Magic Stone to awaken the Ancient Iceborn sealed beneath the Howling Abyss. The players must traverse the tundra, broker alliances among the three tribes, and stop the Frostborn's final ritual.

Scenario Structure

Chapter Title Level Type Core Encounter
2.1 Tundra Journey Lv.6 Survival Extreme cold environment trials, Icefield Behemoth encounter, search for Ashe's tribe
2.2 Tribal Trials Lv.7 Combat/Trial Complete three trials to earn Ashe's trust: Hunting/Wrestling/Tracking
2.3 Tri-Tribe Council Lv.8 Social Convince Sejuani's Winter's Claw and Lissandra's Frostguard to join the alliance (Difficulty Class (DC) 18-22 negotiation)
2.4 Howling Abyss Lv.9-10 Epic Dungeon/Boss Descend into the abyss, five-layer dungeon, final Boss "Ancient Iceborn — Father of Winter"

Key NPCs

NPC Role Bond Opportunity
Ashe Empress of the Avarosa, primary ally Bond levels 1-6
Sejuani Leader of the Winter's Claw, a swing faction that can be won over Bond levels 1-3 (must prove your strength)
Braum Legendary hero, provides support and guidance Bond levels 1-6
Lissandra Leader of the Frostguard — friend and foe alike May become a rival or a reluctant ally

Boss Battle: Ancient Iceborn — Father of Winter (Lv.10)

Attribute Value
HP 1,800
AD 85
AR/MR 120/90
Immune Frost, Slow

Three-Phase Mechanics: - P1 (100-70%): Frost Breath (3-cell cone in front, Attack Damage (AD)×1.5 + Freeze 1 turn) + summons Ice Elementals (Health Points (HP) 150×2) - P2 (70-35%): Adds "Winter Storm" (all-field Attack Damage (AD)×0.3 frost damage + Move Speed (MS) -2 each turn); Ice Elementals upgrade to Frost Giants (Health Points (HP) 300) - P3 (35-0%): Abyss Rupture — the ground randomly generates 2-cell fissures each turn (those who step in take Attack Damage (AD)×1.0 True Damage + fall for 1 turn); Boss enrages (damage +40%)

Rewards

  • EXP: Total 5,200 (level up from Lv.6 to Lv.10)
  • Gold: Total 6,000-9,000
  • Exclusive Equipment: "Heart of Winter" (Armor (AR)+50, Frost immunity, enemies within 2 cells at the start of each turn lose Move Speed (MS) -1)
  • Scenario-Exclusive Augment: "Iceborn Blessing" — gain the passive ability of Ashe or Sejuani
