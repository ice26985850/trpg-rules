Urban Survivors — Multiplayer Scenario: "In the Same Boat" — Mid-Siege: Division of Labor and Trust

Suggested Level: Play immediately after character creation (can follow or stand alone) Difficulty: ★★★☆ (Moderate) Suggested Players: 2–4 (multi-character mode) Player Mode: Multiplayer — each player portrays a different survivor in the same Shelter Siege Days: D+5 to D+20 (about 6–8 game sessions) Core Theme: Division of labor, trust, and collective cost

Scenario Overview

The siege has entered its second week. Several strangers who never met before have been thrown together by taking shelter in the basement of the same half-ruined apartment building.

With water and power cut off and supplies running thin, they must decide: scrape by each on their own, or share a single Shelter and divide the risks.

This scenario is designed specifically for the multi-character mode—each player controls one survivor, sharing the Shelter's resource pool, yet each carries their own secrets, Trauma, and moral bottom line. Collective survival is never cheap: every ration saved comes from someone swallowing their grievances.

This scenario is built exclusively for "multi-character mode": 2–4 players each portray a different survivor in the same Shelter, sharing the resource pool and dividing the risks, with interpersonal conflict and division of labor as the core driving forces. For solo play, use S1 "The First Day" instead.

Group Setup

  • Shared Shelter: Old Town apartment basement
  • Initial State: Structural Integrity 4 / Sealing 3 / Comfort 2
  • Shared Resources: Canned food ×4, bottled water ×3, first-aid kit ×1, fuel ×2
  • Starting Challenge: On the first night, footsteps and muffled voices echo from upstairs—another survivor, or a Federal Army patrol? The group must vote on whether to investigate, and how to respond.
  • GM Tip: Let players experience "collective decision-making" right from the start—there is no single protagonist, and every choice is a tug-of-war among multiple people.

Multiplayer-Exclusive Mechanics

  • Shared Resource Pool: One Shelter ledger recording food/water/medicine/fuel; each member consumes 1 unit of food and water per day.
  • Role Assignment: Suggest division of labor by each character's highest attribute—Scavenger (Agility/Craft), Caregiver (Willpower/Social), Guard (Physique/Agility), Repairer (Craft/Physique).
  • Group Vote: Decisions affecting everyone are made by majority; ties are broken by the GM or the highest-Willpower member (coordinator).
  • Relationships and Secrets: Each player has a "Relationship value REL" toward other players; each player has 1 "secret (SEC)". Revealing a secret → related REL drops to zero, but may buy the team's trust (GM's judgment).
  • Individual Trauma: Same as solo rules; "witnessing a companion make a cruel choice" also counts as a Trauma event (see Trauma table "forced choice" +3, "companion death" +7).
  • Conflict Resolution: When two players' actions conflict, first attempt a group vote; if still deadlocked, a Social contest (3d6+Social) is held, the loser backs down, or holds firm with a Costly Success (bearing the cost).

Key NPCs (recommended to appear in the scenario)

  • Granny Marta (NPC #7): Provides Old Town intel and folk wisdom. When players move through the Old Town, someone calls from a second-floor window: "Hey! Young folks!"
  • Marco (NPC #15): Black market trade introducer; source of the D+11 Black Market Comes Calling and one of the D+20 evacuation invitations.
  • Yana Novak (NPC #2): Organizer of the Civilian Self-Defense Committee (CDC); source of the D+20 evacuation invitation.
  • Father Andrei: Witness to moral events (optional); offers solace or trials to struggling players, the church near the Shelter still glowing with a faint light.

Ending Conditions

  • Shelter Stable: Structure + Sealing + Comfort ≥ 8
  • Group Survives: No player character is abandoned by the group (total REL not all zeroed), and at least 3 members survive to D+20
  • Survival: The whole group makes it through D+20

Clearance Rewards

  • Experience Points (Experience): 3
  • Fate Point (Fate): 1
  • Special: Unlock the "Collective Trauma Relief" option—group support can reduce a single Trauma by −2, requires unanimous consent
