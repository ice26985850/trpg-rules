Case File: The Thirteenth Tenant of Cheung Sang Store

Suggested Level Range: Levels 5–6 Difficulty (Case Urgency): B-tier (Dangerous; standard 2–3 person combat, split operations) Case Type: White Zombie (multiple) + Resentful Ghost (Nine Nether Hall peripheral "Life-Borrowing" workshop) Case EXP (EXP): 500 Core Puzzle: An old-building store covertly sells "life-extension folk remedies"; tenants who sign the contract grow young then vanish. The truth: a retired geomancer (Nine Nether Hall peripheral) drains the tenants' life force with "Life-Borrowing," refines their bodies into White Zombies, and hides a small "Longevity Furnace" in the back room — a prototype of Nine Nether Hall's corpse-cultivation art.

One-line Hook: The Guild refers a complaint — "An old-building store in Sham Shui Po sells life-extension medicine; tenants who bought it vanished, and past midnight there's bone-sawing from the back room."

I. Take Case (A Phone Call)

Referred by the Guild's Three-Lineage Council; property manager "Sister Ping" calls: the store owner downstairs, "Uncle Chuen," sells "life-extension folk remedies," and three tenants who signed contracts in recent months have evaporated.

Sister Ping (TV noise in the background, and a Cantonese longevity-health ad playing on the downstairs radio): "Ghost Collector? Did the Guild pass on my number? I manage that old Sham Shui Po building; downstairs Uncle Chuen runs a store, says he sells 'life-extension folk remedies,' eat it and you grow young again. Lately three tenants signed contracts, each a few years younger, then... evaporated. Past midnight there's bone-sawing from the back room; the cleaner went to check and never came back. Can you take a look?"

You: "Life-extension remedies? Did he draw anyone's blood, or get people to sign something strange?" Sister Ping: "Signed a contract. The paper said 'borrow' life — I can't make sense of it. But... three people signed, their faces went white, then they just moved out. Moved out or vanished, I wouldn't dare say."

Level Design Tip: In the Take Case phase, a Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 judges that the "life-extension folk remedy" resembles the forbidden art "Life-Borrowing"; a Social (Persuasion) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 settles the fee, success yields an extra 20% Nether Coin (COIN), failure means Uncle Chuen lowballs the price.

II. On-Site Investigation

Scene 1: Old-Building Lobby and Store (Yin Emerges)

The old six-story building has no elevator; the iron gate is rusted. Downstairs, the store hangs a "Cheung Sang Store" neon sign; behind the counter Uncle Chuen beams, but can't hide a trace of grey qi in his eyes. The moment you step inside downstairs, the hairs on your neck stand up, water seeps from the wall corners, and you smell something like long-stale incense ash mixed with glutinous rice — something is wrong here.

  • Environment Yin Value (YIN): base 6 (faint yin → yin-cold, worse in the back room).
  • Witnesses: same-building resident "Uncle Chan," manager Sister Ping.
  • Uncle Chan (opens the door a crack): "Uncle Chuen's store? Sigh, that A-Ying who was here first was the first to talk back to him. She said 'life-extension' was a scam; the day after signing she went green-faced, then... never saw her again." (GM Note: A Social (Interview) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 can draw out that A-Ying was the first resister.)
  • Spirit Identification: An Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 reveals white-furred claws seeping from the back-room floor (White Zombie (Challenge Rating (CR) 2, Hard-Skin Mitigation −2)). A Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 identifies "White Zombie": fears talismans, fears glutinous rice.

Scene 2: The Store Back Room's "Longevity Furnace" (Hidden Info)

  • Appraisal (Knowledge (KNW)) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 searches the back room, finding a clay furnace (small corpse-cultivation device, Challenge Rating (CR) 2 environmental encounter), beside it an account book noting "Received life ×3, handed to Seventh Uncle / Walled Village goods."
  • Hidden Info: The last page of the account book "Received by Seventh Uncle above, for the Walled Village ancestral house great work" — the key thread leading to S5/S3. Requires an Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 to notice ink bleeding through the paper's back.

Combat / Non-Combat Options - Non-Combat (Correct Solution): White Zombies need physical suppression; A-Ying died resisting, her soul became a Resentful Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 3), can be communicated with and delivered. - Combat (Second Choice): If suppression fails, the White Zombie erupts, must suppress head-on with talismans + spirit tools.

Level Design Tips (Scene Order): 1. Peripheral interviews (Uncle Chan / Sister Ping) → obtain A-Ying background. 2. Spirit sight confirms White Zombie (Intuition (INS) check). 3. Search back room (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain Longevity Furnace and account book. 4. At Zi Shi the Longevity Furnace is most active; proceed to Exorcise & Collect phase.

III. Prepare Rite (Required Tools/Materials & Rite Scale)

  • Materials: Peachwood Sword (+2 extra vs spirits/zombies), Corpse-Still Talisman (placed on forehead to immobilize, costs Mana Pool (MP) 3), Glutinous Rice (Burn, draws out corpse poison), Soul-Calming Talisman (soothes Resentful Ghost, costs Mana Pool (MP) 2), Purification Talisman (Area-of-Effect (AOE) radius 3 meters, Yin Value (YIN) −3), Incense, Candle & Hell Bank Notes.
  • Rite Scale: Medium. White Zombie physical suppression + Corpse-Still Talisman; Resentful Ghost communication + Deliverance (costs Mana Pool (MP) 10, 2 per round in segments).
  • Time Pressure: At Zi Shi the Longevity Furnace is most active; miss it and must wait another night.
  • Level Tip: Altar setup uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13; if out of glutinous rice, buy on the spot from a same-building store (Knowledge (KNW) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12).

IV. Exorcise & Collect

Best Solution: Split Handling (Suppress + Communicate)

  1. Warrior closes in with Peachwood Sword, Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to grapple the White Zombie, after a hit place Corpse-Still Talisman to immobilize; scatter glutinous rice on the floor to form a 3-meter-radius Burn zone.
  2. Medium makes Intuition (INS) + Communication check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 (or first calm with Soul-Calming Talisman) to hear A-Ying's last wish → turn into deliverable state.
  3. Destroy the Longevity Furnace (Dao Attainment (DOW) + Warding or Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 to smash the furnace); the White Zombie loses its supply source and collapses.

Potential Risks

  • White Zombie Hard-Skin Mitigation −2: unarmed ineffective, must use spirit tools + talismans; if Corpse-Still Talisman fails, the White Zombie erupts (Area-of-Effect (AOE) crisis) alarming other tenants.
  • Resentful Ghost Communication Fails: then trends toward Fierce Ghost, requires Soul-Gathering Gourd.

Alternatives

  • If no Medium, use Summon Spirit Rite (Level 2+, costs Mana Pool (MP) 6) to force three questions.

V. Cleanup

  • Purification: Purification Talisman (Yin Value (YIN) −3) or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 pushes the back room's Yin Value (YIN) below 4.
  • Client Feedback: Sister Ping breathes a sigh of relief; the Guild records reputation for "Tracking Nine Nether Hall Threads."
  • Case-Closing Reward: Nether Coin (COIN) 400–600; Experience Points (EXP) = base 200 (B-tier) + investigation discoveries +20×4 + correct split handling +100 + cleanup +80 + unresolved hook (account book) +40 = total about 500.
  • Unresolved Mystery (leads to S5):
  • The account book "Seventh Uncle / Walled Village goods" points to Nine Nether Hall's Hong Kong station and the Walled Village great work;
  • Uncle Chuen is only peripheral — who is the real "one above"?

Ending Hook: The Guild Council warns: the New Territories Walled Village has recently had frequent sounds of coffins opening; the Luopan points to the same area — Seventh Uncle is setting up the "great work."
