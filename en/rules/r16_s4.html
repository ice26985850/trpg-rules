S4: Blackout

Level 6 → 7 | Milestones 4–5 | 6–9 hours | Intermediate-Advanced Applicable Mode: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

Level 6: Absolute Darkness

Darkness. Not the darkness of "no light"—but the darkness of "light devoured." Your flashlight can only illuminate a circle with a one-meter radius at your feet. The darkness beyond the beam has weight—you can feel it pressing on your skin.

There are no fluorescent lights here. No emergency lights. No windows. Only damp concrete floor, the smell of peeling paint, and your own heartbeat growing louder.

Every 15 minutes, SP −1. The darkness is eroding you.

The Sound Corridor

You advance along one wall. The only way to navigate is by listening to the dripping water—the rhythm of the drips differs in each area. In some areas, the dripping sounds like Morse code.

In the darkness, you hear whispers. Not your hallucination—at least, you aren't sure. The voices call your name. In your mother's voice. In the voice of a friend you lost.

Perception DC 16 to tell true from false. Failure—you follow the voice deeper in.

Shadows

Something moves in the darkness. Not Hounds—Hounds have claws. This thing makes no sound. You only feel the airflow change. The temperature drops a few degrees.

"Shadows"—amorphous Entities unique to Level 6. They do not attack—they simply "are there." But the shadow of any character they touch vanishes. Permanently. What that means—no one knows.

Level 7: The Deep Sea

The texture of the floor suddenly changes—no longer concrete. Your footsteps sink into icy water. The water rises rapidly—within seconds it reaches your chest. Then the floor vanishes entirely.

You fall into the deep water.

When you open your eyes, you are in a corridor flooded with seawater. The fluorescent lights are still on—casting a sickly blue-green glow underwater. This is the wreckage of some research facility. Oceanography charts are pinned to the walls. Schools of fish swim overhead.

You need to find diving equipment—in the lockers there are oxygen tanks (30 minutes).

Leviathan

At the end of the underwater corridor is an entire wall of glass. Beyond the glass—not a corridor.

The deep sea.

At first you see nothing. Only deep blue—then deeper blue—then black.

Then it passes.

A black shape. Vast—larger than the whole building. Slowly, majestically, it swims past outside the glass wall. You cannot see its shape—only its silhouette blocking the faint light from above. That light comes from the surface—hundreds of meters above.

Do not look directly at it. Willpower DC 20. Failure—SP permanently −3.

The Drainage Pipe

At the bottom of the facility is a large drainage pipe. The pipe mouth is barred—and corroded. You can pry it open.

Swim into the pipe. Darkness surrounds you again. The water temperature begins to rise. The pipe turns upward.

Your head breaks the surface. Air—warm, damp, smelling of earth and fungus. You are on the bank of an underground river. Above you, a "starry sky" formed of fluorescent fungi.

Level 8.
