Honkai: Star Rail TRPG — Solo Campaign: Lone Star Journey (Solo Stellaris)

Campaign Name: Lone Star Journey (Solo Stellaris)
For: 1 PC + 1 NPC companion + 1 GM
Level Range: 1–20
Estimated Duration: 15–25 sessions (2–3 hours each)
Dependencies: Requires the Solo Mode Rules Supplement (solo rules.md); core rulebook v1.0; economy system v1.0
References: Rulebook §2.1 (character creation), §2.4 (Path), §3.1–3.8 (combat); Economy system §6 (consumables), §11 (wealth curve); Solo rules §2 (companions), §3 (encounter adjustment), §6 (death escape)

Campaign Overview

You are a lone Trailblazer. No squad, no backup — only a travel companion whose fate is intertwined with yours, and that navigator aboard the Astral Express who is forever brewing red tea.

Your journey begins with a distress signal on the ice plains of Belobog, passes through the steam-cloaked labyrinth of Rustcrown, the Aeon battlefield of the Xianzhou, and finally points toward the being in the depths of the Void that was once forgotten by the cosmos — the last shard of the Voice of Annihilation. Someone is collecting these shards. Someone wants to resurrect it. And you are the only one standing between it and the universe.

Arc 1: Frozen Signal (Lv 1-4)

Module Overview

Item Content
Theme A lone start, first real combat, discovery of a greater threat
Recommended Level 1-4
Estimated Sessions 4-5
Suggested Path Any, but non-Preservation/Abundance recommended (companion can fill the gap)

Story Background

It has been months since you received the Astral Express's invitation to become one of the Nameless, but you have had no formal mission — until today. Himeko finds you in the observation car, her tone more serious than usual: "Belobog sent an encrypted signal. Not an official distress call — a private channel, using old Trailblazer cipher. That means the sender knows the Express, but doesn't want the Great Guardian to know."

She pauses: "I'm sending you alone."

Chapter Structure

Act 1: Signal Source on the Ice Plains (Lv 1)

Setting: Outskirts of Belobog, ice plains

Plot: Himeko provides the approximate coordinates of the signal source, located at an abandoned research station on the outskirts of Belobog. The signal uses an ancient Trailblazer encryption protocol — only the Nameless can decode it.

Exploration Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 12 Survival: Navigate through the blizzard to the abandoned research station (failure → one extra random Fragmentum creature encounter, Challenge Rating (CR) 1/2) - Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Technology: Decode the signal contents from the abandoned terminal — the sender is an old acquaintance of Himeko's, a reclusive former Nameless who calls himself "Frostwhisperer"

Combat Encounter (solo scale):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Research Station Outskirts Fragmentum Wraith ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 1/2

Enemy Data Reference: Fragmentum Wraith → Enemy Excel "Arc 1 - Belobog" worksheet, Hit Points (HP) 20→24 (solo ×1.2), other stats unchanged. See enemy card for full data.

Reward: 50 Experience Points (XP), basic healing spray ×1

Companion Entrance: Deep within the research station, you find the trapped NPC companion (the one you chose). After a brief conversation, they join the party.

Act 2: Frostwhisperer's Commission (Lv 2)

Setting: Sub-level of the abandoned research station

Plot: Frostwhisperer — an aged former Nameless — appears. He explains: the Fragmentum activity in Belobog's lower districts is abnormal; the source is not a natural Stellaron rift, but "someone is deliberately drawing the Fragmentum down into the lower levels." He suspects someone among Belobog's nobility is exploiting the Fragmentum for profit. He needs you to investigate the lower districts, and in exchange, he will tell you a secret about a "Shard of the Voice of Annihilation."

Social Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 13 Insight: Judge whether Frostwhisperer is holding something back (partially — he knows about the shard but won't reveal everything at this time) - Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Persuasion (optional): Demand more payment → gain 1 piece of 3-star Relic

Combat Encounter:

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Underground Passage Icebound Guard ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 1
Abandoned Lab Fragmentum Wraith ×2 (no adjustment) 1

Enemy Data Reference: Icebound Guard → Enemy Excel "Arc 1 - Belobog", Hit Points (HP) 30→36, weakness reference §1.6 (Fire). Fragmentum Wraith → same as above, weakness reference: Physical, Quantum.

Reward: 100 Experience Points (XP), 3-star Relic ×1

Act 3: The Truth of the Lower Districts (Lv 3)

Setting: Belobog lower districts — abandoned mine

Plot: The investigation points to a noble merchant named "Orlov" — he uses Fragmentum rifts as a pipeline for the illegal dumping of industrial waste, profiting enormously from it. The Fragmentum has thus been polluted and begun expanding toward the residential areas. You must infiltrate his warehouse to obtain evidence, or confront him directly.

Two Routes: 1. Infiltrate: Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Stealth → avoid the guards, obtain the account books → hand them to the Great Guardian 2. Confront: Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Persuasion/Intimidation (Orlov's Social Fortitude 3, weakness: Intimidation, resistance: Persuasion) → force him to turn himself in

Combat Encounter (triggered on infiltration failure or when choosing confrontation):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Noble's Retainers ×2 Mercenary Thug (Challenge Rating (CR) 1/2, Hit Points (HP) 12) 1

Enemy Data Reference: Mercenary Thug uses the generic template — Armor Class (AC) 12, Hit Points (HP) 12, attack +2 (1d6), refer to rulebook §1.6 enemy types for Challenge Rating (CR) 0-1/2.

Reward: 150 Experience Points (XP), 300 Credit Points (CP) (if evidence is successfully obtained).

Act 4: Frostwhisperer's Secret (Lv 4)

Setting: Abandoned research station — Frostwhisperer's residence

Plot: After completing the investigation, Frostwhisperer honors his promise. From a lead-sealed crate he takes out a half-glowing crystal — a "Shard of the Voice of Annihilation." This shard came from the planet Rustcrown, and its other half is still there. Frostwhisperer says: this shard is calling to the other half — it is singing. And you, the moment he touched the shard, also heard a faint, elusive melody.

Story Choices: 1. Accept the Shard: Keep the shard by your side → gain the "Shard Resonance" ability (once per day → in combat, sense all invisible/hidden enemies within 30m Perception) 2. Hand it to Himeko for study: Himeko analyzes the shard from afar → gain detailed intelligence on Rustcrown (advantage on all Lore/Investigation checks in Arc 2 ×2) 3. Destroy the Shard: Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Constitution saving throw → on success, permanently gain resistance (halved) to Quantum damage; on failure, take 2d10 Quantum damage

Reference: For the choice mechanic, see rulebook §1.1 Degrees of Success (optional rule); the initial information volume of Arc 2 is determined by the choice made.

Reward: 200 Experience Points (XP), Lv 4 Ability Score Improvement (ASI) node (+2 attribute), companion unlocks first Technique

Arc 2: Steam Labyrinth (Lv 5-8)

Module Overview

Item Content
Theme Infiltrate a hostile city alone, trust and betrayal
Recommended Level 5-8
Estimated Sessions 4-5

Story Background

The shard points to Rustcrown — a steampunk planet industrialized at an accelerated pace by its Stellaron. Silver Wolf sends a message: "I heard you caused a bit of a stir in Belobog. Rustcrown — the Duke's informants are everywhere in this city. You need an identity. I can help you, but you help me look into one thing: why is Rustcrown's Stellaron still resonating — by my calculations, it should have been depleted a year ago."

Chapter Structure

Act 1: City in the Smoke (Lv 5)

Setting: Rustcrown — steam-shrouded upper districts

Plot: Silver Wolf forges an identity for you — "a metal trader from the Xianzhou." Your task is to infiltrate the banquet the Duke is about to hold and find out why the Stellaron is still resonating. The companion must coordinate with your disguise (the companion may optionally lurk in the lower districts as backup).

Social Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Deception: Maintain the forged identity, extract information from the nobility - Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Perception: Notice Chairman Colwyn privately conferring with a mysterious figure (an envoy of the Rustworn Prophet) at the banquet

Exploration Challenge: - Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Engineering: Analyze the banquet hall's steam power system, discover an energy pipeline leading underground — to a deep-level zone not on the map

Reward: 500 Experience Points (XP), forged documents provided by Silver Wolf (quality: medium, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to see through) → reference economy system §10.6 (standard forged documents: 200-600 Credit Points (CP)). Lv 5 Proficiency Bonus (PB) = +3.

Act 2: The Underground Furnace (Lv 6)

Setting: Rustcrown deep underground — the Perpetual Furnace

Plot: The energy pipeline leads you to the furnace. The furnace's core — the Stellaron — is still running, but it is being "fed": missing workers from the lower districts are sent into the Stellaron as fuel. The Duke trades human lives for energy. The guards here far exceed expectations.

Combat Encounter (solo scale):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR) Reference
Furnace Entrance Steam Guard ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.5) 3 Enemy card "Arc 2 - Rustcrown", Hit Points (HP) 60→90, weakness: Lightning, Wind
Pipeline Layer Fragmentum Derivative ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 4 Enemy card "Arc 2 - Rustcrown", Hit Points (HP) 70→84, weakness: Quantum, Imaginary

Encounter Adjustment Rule Reference: Solo rules §3.1 (halve numbers, Hit Points (HP) adjustment), §3.2 (enemy Hit Points (HP) ×0.6→original value ×1.2~1.5 conversion).

Story Choices: After discovering the truth of the furnace's operation — 1. Shut down the furnace immediately: Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Technology. Success → Rustcrown loses power, Duke immediately dispatches troops to hunt you. Failure → trigger alarm 2. Record evidence and retreat: Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Stealth. Bring the evidence to the resistance, take the collective overthrow route 3. Stage an accident: Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Engineering → make the furnace "appear" about to go out of control (without actually shutting it down). Buy yourself 24 hours to escape

Reward: 800 Experience Points (XP), evidence document (can be given to the resistance or Silver Wolf), Lv 6 Trace node (Energy Point (EP) +5)

Act 3: The Gearmaiden's Choice (Lv 7)

Setting: Lower districts — resistance secret stronghold

Plot: Silver Wolf arranges a meeting between you and the resistance leader, "the Gearmaiden." After looking at the evidence, she is expressionless, only saying: "I need you to help me kill a person — my father."

Social Challenge: Gearmaiden's Social Fortitude 4 (weakness: sincerity — Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Insight reveals she is not persuading but pleading for help; resistance: Intimidation)

Social Fortitude Rule Reference: GM guide optional rule (Social Combat); weakness/resistance mechanic see that chapter.

Routes: 1. Accept the assassination: Infiltrate the Duke's manor, face the Steam Duke alone (Lv 8 Boss fight) 2. Persuade her to give up: Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Persuasion (two consecutive successes) → she agrees to switch to the public trial route 3. Seek a third path: Propose a plan to overthrow the Duke without killing him → requires launching an uprising in the lower districts as cover

Act 4: The Duke's End (Lv 8)

Setting: The Duke's manor (or the streets of the public uprising)

Combat Encounter (assassination route chosen):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Manor Guards Steam Knight ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 6
The Duke Himself Steam Duke (Boss, Hit Points (HP) ×0.6, Toughness ×0.6) 8

Enemy Data Reference: Steam Duke data based on Gear Knight-Captain Orek (NPC Excel "Arc 2 - Rustcrown") adjusted — Hit Points (HP) 150→90 (solo ×0.6), attack +8 (2d8+5 slashing), Armor Class (AC) 19→17. Weakness: Lightning, Wind.

Duke Special Mechanic (Solo Boss Suppression): - Suppression Value system (see solo rules §3.3) - The Duke's "Steam Burst": every 3 rounds deals 3d8 Fire damage in a 10m radius

Reward: 1,500 Experience Points (XP), the Duke's vault (1,000 Credit Points (CP) + 4-star Destruction/Preservation Light Cone ×1), Lv 8 Ability Score Improvement (ASI) node, companion unlocks second Technique

Arc 3: Aeon Battlefield (Lv 9-12)

Module Overview

Item Content
Theme Glimpse the true face of the Aeons, echoes of the past
Recommended Level 9-12
Estimated Sessions 4-5

Story Background

Silver Wolf discovered another shard of the Voice of Annihilation on Xianzhou Luofu — sealed in the deepest part of an ancient battlefield. She cannot enter alone — the Cloud Knights have sealed off the entire area, and the Erudition Society is also competing for exploration rights. Silver Wolf needs a "middleman" — not a Stellaron Hunter, not a Cloud Knight, not a scholar — a Nameless.

"You go in alone," she says, "too many people going in will alert it. The shard remembers those who enter, and the last one it remembered was an Aeon."

Chapter Structure

Act 1: The Cloud Knights' Blockade Line (Lv 9)

Setting: Xianzhou Luofu — outskirts of the ancient battlefield

Plot: The Cloud Knight guard captain stops you before the blockade line. He is cautious, but you may choose one of three paths: 1. Persuade: Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Persuasion (Cloud Knight guard captain's Social Fortitude 4, weakness: Lore argument, resistance: Intimidation) → gain legal entry permission, with Cloud Knight support (can call support once in Act 3) 2. Infiltrate: Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Stealth + Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Technology (crack the Quantum encrypted door) → enter silently but trigger partial defense systems 3. Silver Wolf's backdoor: Accept the encrypted channel Silver Wolf provides → quick entry but the Cloud Knights investigate afterward

NPC Data Reference: Cloud Knight guard captain → NPC Excel "Arc 3 - Stellaron Hunter", Strength (STR) 16, Constitution (CON) 16, Perception +10.

Reward: 1,000 Experience Points (XP), ancient battlefield schematic (reduces Act 3 exploration Difficulty Class (DC) by 2)

Act 2: Graveyard of Data Wraiths (Lv 10)

Setting: Ancient battlefield — Quantum Memory Graveyard

Plot: Drifting through the graveyard are "Data Wraiths" — residual Quantum consciousness of ancient fallen warriors. They are not hostile creatures, but they replay the final memory fragments from before their deaths. One wraith shows a stunning scene: before the Voice of Annihilation was shattered, it was not "defeated by the combined Aeons" — but split voluntarily. It actively shattered itself, scattering across the cosmos, to ensure the "Melody of the End" could be heard in every corner of the universe.

Exploration Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Lore: Identify the Aeons shown by the wraith — you see Lan (Hunt), Nanook (Destruction), even Akivili (Trailblaze) - Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Resonance (Charisma): Attempt to communicate with the wraith → on success, gain a "Memory Key" (required to open the final tomb chamber)

Combat Encounter (if exploration fails and triggers wraith berserk):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR) Reference
Berserk Wraith Data Wraith ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 6 Enemy card "Arc 3 - Stellaron Hunter", Hit Points (HP) 90→108, weakness: Quantum, Imaginary

Enemy Ability Reference: Data Wraith — Data Plunder (Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Perception saving throw or cannot use Technology skills), Incorporeal (non-magical attack damage halved). Full data in enemy Excel.

Reward: 1,500 Experience Points (XP), Memory Key, Lv 10 Trace (Path resource +1)

Act III: Memory in the Mirror (Lv 11)

Scene: The deepest part of the graveyard—the Mirror Burial Chamber

Story: At the center of the burial chamber stands a giant mirror—not for reflection, but for "storage." Imprisoned within the mirror is the last complete memory from the fragments of the Voice of Annihilation: a lone existence standing at the beginning of the universe, singing the first song about "the end." Its song gave the first dead planet meaning in the void—because it was remembered.

Boss Battle:

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Guardian of the Burial Chamber Memory in the Mirror ×1 (solo adjustment) 10

Enemy Data Reference: Memory in the Mirror → enemy Excel "Arc 3—Stellaron Hunters," solo adjustment: Hit Points (HP) 200→120 (×0.6), Toughness 104→83 (×0.6/type multiplier ×0.8). Armor Class (AC) 19, attack +9 (2d8+6 mental), Memory Plunder Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Perception save. Full data in enemy card.

Solo Boss Suppression (solo rules §3.3): Suppression value +1 each round; upon reaching 5, splits off a Minor Memory Construct (Challenge Rating (CR) 5, Hit Points (HP) 30).

Story Choice: After victory— 1. Absorb the Fragment: Fuse the fragment with the half you already possess → fragment completeness 2/7 (affects the final battle) 2. Purify the Fragment: Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Resonance (Charisma) → the fragment loses its power but your Perception permanently +1 3. Return to the Express: Hand the fragment to Himeko for research → intel on the third fragment (preparation for Arc 4)

Reward: 2,000 Experience Points (XP), Memory Core Fragment, Lv 11 Trace (Hit Points (HP)+5)

Act IV: Silver Wolf's Farewell (Lv 12)

Scene: Xianzhou port

Story: After the mission, Silver Wolf waits for you at the port. This is the first time she speaks to you with her true face (rather than an encrypted channel). "I'm going somewhere—might be a long time. If you still need intel... go to the World's End Tavern. Say my name. The bartender will help you."

She hands you an encrypted chip: "The data inside is the location of the Imaginary Echo—it's on the Azure Abyss. It's collecting fragments, faster than any of us. If it assembles all seven first—the Voice of Annihilation will be reborn. Not as an Aeon, but as... something worse."

Reward: 2,500 Experience Points (XP), Silver Wolf's encrypted chip (essential intel for Arc 4), Lv 12 Ability Score Improvement (ASI)

Arc 4: Whispers of the Abyss (Lv 13-16)

Module Overview

Item Content
Theme The cost of sacrifice, the weight of memory
Recommended Level 13-16
Estimated Sessions 4-5

Story Background

Azure Abyss—a planet covered by oceans, where two factions stand at daggers drawn over "Memory Pearls." But the true threat lies deep beneath the sea: an activated fragment of the Voice of Annihilation is devouring this planet's collective memory. Sea creatures begin to forget migration routes, fishermen forget how to sail, and even Tide Maiden Selene—high priestess of the Tide of Recollection Sect—forgets herself from the day before upon waking each morning.

The Imaginary Echo has already been here. It drew power from the fragment and moved on to the next one. And you must obtain this fragment before it returns—if you still wish to stop it.

Chapter Structure

Act I: World's End Tavern (Lv 13)

Scene: World's End Tavern (rulebook §4.3 — Masked Fools hideout)

Story: Before heading to the Azure Abyss, you need intel. Silver Wolf said the bartender will help you—but you must first prove you have the "qualification." The bartender smiles and slides forward three drinks: "The Fool's Challenge. Three drinks. Finish them, or complete them. I'm not picky."

Three Challenges (complete any two): 1. "Absurdity": Use a song to persuade a stranger in the tavern to give you a coin—Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Performance, or trade with a secret 2. "Memory": Place your most precious memory into the bartender's glass bottle—he will return it to you, but the memory in the bottle turns blue (side effect of fragment resonance—a −2 to some Difficulty Class (DC) in Arc 4) 3. "Truth": Tell the bartender a lie about yourself—if the bartender doesn't see through it (Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Deception), you pass

Scene Reference: Detailed description of the World's End Tavern in rulebook §4.3 "World's End Tavern (Masked Fools)"—location, appearance, rules.

Reward: After completing the challenge, the bartender provides detailed intel on the Azure Abyss (advantage ×1 on all Perception/Survival checks in Arc 4), as well as a "Fool's Coin" (can ignore one failed check below Difficulty Class (DC) 20—permanently consumed after use).

Act II: Deep-Sea Rift (Lv 14)

Scene: Azure Abyss—the edge of the sunken city

Story: The scholar Aruna (Erudition Society) provided diving equipment and the approximate location of the fragment. The sunken city "Aquilos" was destroyed by a Stellaron thousands of years ago—and within its ruins, the fragment of the Voice of Annihilation still operates, like a music box that never stops.

Exploration Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Perception: Find the correct route to the fragment among the ruins (failure → encounter an extra random battle) - Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Endurance: Resist the Fatigue caused by deep-sea pressure (failure → Level 1 Fatigue)

Combat Encounter:

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR) Reference
Deep-Sea Rift Amnesiac Deep Diver ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 10 Enemy card "Arc 4—Imaginary Echo," Hit Points (HP) 150→180, weakness: Imaginary
Ruins Guardian Memory Devourer ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×0.6) 13 Enemy card "Arc 4—Imaginary Echo," Hit Points (HP) 250→150, Toughness 169→135

Companion Story: At the deepest part of the rift, the companion is struck by the fragment's memory storm and begins to see memories that aren't their own—lost family, unfulfilled dreams, a different version of themselves. RP moment: the companion opens their heart to you (Difficulty Class (DC) 14 Insight can sense their vulnerability, Charisma (Resonance) can soothe them).

Reward: 3,000 Experience Points (XP), Aquilos civilization record (usable in Arc 5—see Act III), Lv 14 Trace (Energy Point (EP)+10)

Act III: The Maiden's Choice (Lv 15)

Scene: Grand Cathedral of the Tide of Recollection Sect

Story: You return to the surface with the fragment—but it still resonates. Tide Maiden Selene tells you that to fully seal this fragment, you need "something of equal value to the fragment"—a most precious memory from your life. Not merely to remember it—but to completely remove it from your memory. You will never recall it again.

Sacrifice Choice (irreversible): 1. Sacrifice Memory: Choose a memory to be deleted → the fragment is purified, gain the "Weight of Memory" ability (usable once in the final battle: cause the Imaginary Echo to lose one action—because it "remembers the forgotten pain") 2. Refuse Sacrifice: The fragment remains in activated state (in Arc 5 the Imaginary Echo's data will be stronger—Challenge Rating (CR) +1, as it keeps drawing power from this fragment) 3. Seek a Substitute: Difficulty Class (DC) 20 Resonance (Charisma) → substitute the personal memory with the "collective memory" of the Aquilos civilization (requires the civilization record found in Act II). Success → fragment purified with no personal cost. Failure → return to option 1 or 2

Reference: The sacrifice mechanic references rulebook §1.1 critical success/critical failure rules—the choice here is irreversible, akin to the "negative side effect" level of a critical failure.

Reward: 3,500 Experience Points (XP), purified fragment (affects the Arc 5 final battle), Lv 15 Trace (Armor Class (AC)+1)

Act IV: Envoy of the Imaginary Echo (Lv 16)

Scene: Azure Abyss sea surface—amid a storm

Story: As you prepare to leave, the envoy of the Imaginary Echo descends—it already knows you have collected three fragments. It comes to "retrieve" them. This is the climax battle of Arc 4, and your first time facing the power of the Imaginary Echo head-on—though it is only an envoy.

Boss Battle:

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Envoy of the Imaginary Echo Fragment Avatar ×1 (solo adjustment) 15

Enemy Data Reference: Fragment Avatar → enemy Excel "Arc 4—Imaginary Echo," solo adjustment: Hit Points (HP) 310→186 (×0.6), Toughness 208→166. Armor Class (AC) 21, attack +12 (3d8+8 + elemental addition), Memory Storm (area mental damage). Full data and phase transition in enemy card.

Story: After defeating the envoy, you find a star chart in its wreckage—marking the location of the Imaginary Echo's true body: the Void Fragmentum, at the edge of the galaxy. That is where the last fragment lies. And where the final battle will take place.

Reward: 4,500 Experience Points (XP), Void Fragmentum star chart, Lv 16 Ability Score Improvement (ASI)

Arc 5: Lone Star Finale (Lv 17-20)

Module Overview

Item Content
Theme One person's end—or beginning
Recommended Level 17-20
Estimated Sessions 4-5

Story Background

Void Fragmentum. The core of the Imaginary Echo. It has already absorbed the power of five fragments, with only the last one remaining—it is waiting for you. Silver Wolf's final message was only six words: "It's waiting for you. Hurry."

Before seeing you off, Himeko said: "The essence of the Nameless is not traveling together, but—walking alone through the darkness without Star Tracks, laying the first path for those who come after. That is why I chose you to go alone."

Chapter Structure

Act I: Entrance to the Void Fragmentum (Lv 17)

Scene: Void Fragmentum—Memory Wasteland

Story: With your first step into the Fragmentum, you see the memories of the universe—the birth of Aeons, the end of civilizations, the moment the Voice of Annihilation was shattered. You realize these are not illusions—they are living memories, able to perceive your presence.

Exploration Challenges: - Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Resonance (Charisma): Establish a connection with the Memory Wasteland and gain route guidance (failure → fall into the Memory Maze, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Perception save or suffer Level 1 Fatigue)

Combat Encounter:

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR) Reference
Memory Wasteland Aeon Residue ×1 (Hit Points (HP) ×1.2) 14 Enemy card "Arc 5—Symphony of Finality," Hit Points (HP) 200→240, weakness: varies by fragment type

Enemy Ability Reference: Aeon Residue—an alienated energy entity, attack +10 (2d10+5 by random element), switches element type each time it is hit. Full data in enemy Excel.

Reward: 5,000 Experience Points (XP), Resonance of the Memory Wasteland (in the final battle you may summon a forgotten Aeon memory to assist you—effect see Act IV), Lv 17 Trace (second Technique enhancement)

Act II: The Companion's Trial (Lv 18)

Scene: Void Fragmentum—Mirror Sanctum

Story: In the sanctum, you and your companion each face a mirror—reflecting the version of yourself "if you had not chosen this path." The companion's mirror-image speaks—attacking them with their deepest fear. This is the companion's personal trial, and you must help them pass it.

RP Core Scene: The companion's mirror-image reveals their secret (GM designs based on companion background)—the companion must confront it. You may choose: 1. Face It for the Companion: You enter the companion's mirror world → take 3d10 mental damage, but the companion gains "Trial Passed"—immune to one Charm/Fear in the final battle 2. Encourage the Companion to Face It Themselves: Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Charisma (Persuasion) → on success the companion passes the trial and loyalty permanently +5. Failure → companion loyalty −3

Combat Encounter (if option 1 chosen):

Encounter Monster Challenge Rating (CR)
Companion's Mirror Image Shadow Duplicate (stats equal to companion, Hit Points (HP) ×0.6) equal to companion's Challenge Rating (CR)

Act III: The Pristine Fragment (Lv 19)

Scene: Void Fragmentum—Memory Sanctum (the legend of the Voice of Annihilation cited in rulebook §4.3, realized)

Story: At the center of the sanctum, you meet it—the last "Pristine Fragment" of the Voice of Annihilation. It is the only fragment not distorted by a Stellaron, sealed here the moment it was shattered. It has been waiting for someone who could understand it.

Conversation with the Pristine Fragment (NPC data reference: Pristine Fragment → NPC Excel "Arc 4-5—Finality," non-combat NPC):

It asks you three questions (your answers determine the final battle conditions): 1. "Why do you collect fragments? For power? Or for understanding?" 2. "If saving the universe requires being forgotten—are you willing to be forgotten?" 3. "Do you believe there is a beginning after the end?"

Based on your answers, the Pristine Fragment grants different "Finality Blessings": - Leaning "understanding" → recover 10 Energy Point (EP) each round in the final battle - Leaning "power" → attack +3 in the final battle - Leaning "hope" → death saving throw Difficulty Class (DC) −5 in the final battle

Reference: NPC data in NPC Excel "Pristine Fragment"—Intellect (INT) 20, Perception (PER) 22, Charisma (CHA) 22, Resonance +16. Non-combat NPC, will not participate in the final battle.

Reward: 7,000 Experience Points (XP), Finality Blessing (by answer content), fragment completeness 5/7 (Pristine Fragment + previously collected), Lv 19 Ability Score Improvement (ASI)

Scene: Void Fragmentum—Core of Birth

Story: The Imaginary Echo has fused five fragments. It stands at the center of the core, greeting you in a near-complete form—an aberrant Aeon composed of shattered memories, Stellaron energy, and the pain of being forgotten for millions of years.

"You've come," its voice transmits simultaneously from all the fragments, "alone—just like me back then."

Ending:

When the Imaginary Echo falls, it no longer roars. Its last words are faint: "Thank you... for remembering me."

The core fragment of the Voice of Annihilation begins to crumble—all the fragments you collected resonate, emitting a final melody. You have three choices:

  1. Let the Fragments Return to Nihility: All fragments dissolve into starlight and vanish → the Voice of Annihilation is utterly annihilated, leaving no trace in the universe. You gain the title "Watcher of the End"
  2. Inherit the Fragment's Melody: Absorb the core energy of the fragments → your Path undergoes a permanent transformation (you may reselect your Path while retaining 3 self-chosen abilities from your original Path). You become "the Newborn Melody"
  3. Return the Fragments to the Universe: Scatter the purified fragments across the universe → the Voice of Annihilation is reborn in pristine form, becoming a witness that will never be forgotten again. A new Star Track is added to the universe—"Memory"

Reward: 10,000 Experience Points (XP), Lv 20 ultimate Trace, campaign victory
