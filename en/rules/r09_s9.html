Scenario Four: "Where the Dragon Sleeps"

Attribute Content
Suggested Quality ★★★★ Master-grade
Player Count 1 mage (required) + Companion (strongly recommended)
Estimated Sessions 4–5
Core Output Legendary-grade Fire Shard (◈) + Dragon ally + Dragonkin Companion (possible)
Primary Threats The ancient dragon's trials, polar environment, a rival's shadow, the limits of your own power
Prerequisite Academic Reputation ≥ 30; possess at least 1 Rare-or-higher Fire Shard; having a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 50 is recommended
Companion Opportunity A Dragonkin Companion may be gained in this scenario
References [Solo Supplement §2.1] Bound Creature Companions, [Solo Supplement §2.4] Loyalty system, [Solo Supplement §1.2] Encounter difficulty scaling, [Player Book §3.2 Fire element]

Background

Deep in the Everwinter Tundra (永冬冰原), an ancient Fire Dragon named Ignarath (伊格納瑞斯) is growing old. This is no ordinary dragon — he is a second-generation dragon who saw Selena (賽琳娜) herself and witnessed the sealing of the Void God (虛無之神) firsthand.

Valastra (瓦拉斯特拉), envoy of the Dragon Elders Council (龍族長老議會) — a silver dragon three thousand years old — has issued a rare invitation: to permit one human mage to attend Ignarath's "Death Rite."

A dragon's death is not an end — they condense a lifetime of magical essence into a Legendary Shard, then their body returns to element and back to the Starweave. Ignarath is willing to give his shard to "the one person who proves worthy."

Valastra's invitation message is brief:

"Ignarath asked specifically to see you. Not your team. Not your school. You — alone. He says he saw 'something interesting' in your last Fire-element research. I don't know what he means. But he has little time left — come."

Act One: Crossing the Everwinter

Scene 1-1: Valastra's Trials

The Dragon Elders Council's Ice Palace:

The Ice Palace is not built on the ground — it is carved from a living glacier. Faint light shows through blue ice walls, and the air is so cold every breath turns to white mist. Valastra waits at the palace entrance in human form — an old white-haired woman.

"Ignarath has only seven days. Before you see him, you must prove you did not come to plunder. A dragon's legacy does not belong to the greedy."

Trial contents (the solo mage must complete two of the three trials, not all three as in the original):

Trial Check Success Reward Failure Consequence
Knowledge Trial Erudition or Theory DC 16 Dragon-Speaker Mark — +1 spell attack when using Fire shards Conviction shaken — Disadvantage on Will checks when first facing Ignarath
Craft Trial Craft DC 16 Dragon Scale Charm — Armor Class (AC) +1 (stacks with robes) Feels colder on the icefield — Fatigue check DC +2
Will Trial Will DC 16 Millennial Memory — gain a key memory fragment about the Void God's seal Nightmare — next two Long Rests recover half

Solo adjustment: In the original, each trial was borne by a different player. Here the same person faces all three in succession — the GM may allow two Short Rests (between trials) to recover Spirit.

Scene 1-2: Crossing the Icefield

Environmental effect (stacking): - Base: every 4 hours, Will DC 12 or gain one level of Fatigue ([Player Book §1.5]) - If Craft Trial failed: every 4 hours, Will DC 14 - If a Companion travels along: the Companion can help build camp (automatic success on camp DC, [Solo Supplement §2.3])

Crossing event (roll 1d20 per travel day):

d20 Event
1–8 Nothing — proceed smoothly
9–12 Ice Storm — Will DC 14 or lose 1/4 of max HP (frostbite)
13–15 Ice Elemental ×2 (★ threat, solo-adjusted — Hesitation rule in effect)
16–17 Discover a Dragon ruin — Erudition DC 14 to interpret runes, gain a historical record (Academic Reputation +1d6)
18–19 Ancient dragon lair entrance — a small dungeon (2–3 scenes, with Rare materials/shards)
20 Dragon patrol — young dragons salute you (they know you are going to see Ignarath)

If a Companion is present: - Lena (mercenary): She helps find safe campsites and keeps watch for night threats. Daily camp DC automatically –2. - Chief Apprentice: Not recommended to bring an apprentice into such extreme conditions — if insisted, the apprentice needs an extra daily Will DC 12 or falls ill (cannot assist, becomes a burden — but this may be an interesting narrative challenge)

Act Two: Ignarath's Lair

Scene 2-1: The Lair Entrance

Narration:

The lair lies on the slope of an active volcano — incongruously steaming amid the Everwinter Tundra. The cave mouth is large enough for a tower to pass through. Dragon runes cover the walls — each rune a memory, a dragon carving the most important moments of his long life into stone.

From deep in the cave comes a breathing — deep, like the shifting of the earth's crust.

Entrance guardian: A young Fire Dragon — Zephyrus (澤菲羅斯) — guards the lair entrance.

"Ignarath will only see those who prove they understand fire." Zephyrus's voice echoes through the cave. "Show me — your Fire spell."

Trial: The player must demonstrate a spell containing the Fire element ([Player Book §3.2]). Zephyrus's judgment:

Spell Demonstrated Result
★ Apprentice-grade Fire spell Barely passes — Zephyrus is unimpressed but lets you through ("At least you know the shape of fire")
★★ or above, a Fire spell with element crossover Zephyrus approves — future Fire experiments here gain DC permanent –1
★★★ or above, a Fire spell containing a Legendary Fire Shard Zephyrus is stunned — he flies you personally to Ignarath (skip Scene 2-2)
No Fire spell Zephyrus sighs — "Do you truly understand why you came here?" Lets you through, but Ignarath's first impression of you is disappointment

Scene 2-2: Facing Ignarath

Ignarath (伊格納瑞斯): - An ancient Fire Dragon over two hundred feet long. His scales have faded from crimson to grey-white, each breath accompanied by surging magma. But his eyes — still burning gold. - He does not waste words.

Ignarath's voice does not come from his mouth — it sounds directly in your mind, warm with fire:

"I saw Selena create the first shard with her own hands. I saw the Void God sealed. I saw humans walk out of caves, learn fire — then learn to tame it. For three thousand years, I have watched you."

His great eye turns to you.

"Now, tell me. Why do you want my fire?"

The player must give an answer — not an excuse, not flattery. Ignarath sees through lies (Will contest DC 25, almost impossible to deceive him).

Answer Type Ignarath's Reaction
Seeking Knowledge ("I want to understand the nature of fire") His eyes narrow slightly — satisfied. Final Trial DC –2
Power ("I need power to complete my research") "Power is not rare. Wisdom is." — neither disappointed nor pleased. DC unchanged
Legacy ("I want to carry your fire to the next generation") A long silence. Then — "Selena said such words too." Final Trial DC –4 + gain Ignarath's secret memory
Deception "Did you think I would not see it?" Final Trial DC +4. Ignarath will not send you away — but he will make you prove you are worthy of his trust.

Scene 2-3: Final Trial — Stabilizing the Death Shard

Ignarath begins the rite:

The great dragon closes his eyes. His body begins to emit light — not fire, but pure golden magic. His scales one by one turn to points of light, drifting into the air, forming a vortex. At the vortex's center — a fist-sized point of light brighter than the sun — is condensing.

Ignarath's voice sounds again, but this time weary:

"Stabilize it. With your magic — your method — your understanding. Prove your fire is not merely burning — but creating."

Challenge mechanic (time limit: 5 rounds — Ignarath's life counts down):

Each round, the player makes one Craft check (DC depends on the player's answer and prior trial results); cumulative successes determine the outcome.

Prior Performance Final Trial DC
Legacy answer + passed all trials DC 14
Seeking-Knowledge answer + passed all trials DC 16
Power answer or other cases DC 18
Deception exposed DC 22

Round 3 event (regardless of current success count):

Ignarath's body suddenly shakes violently — not in pain. In memory. A large portion of his scales turn to light at once, the death shard surges — magic release goes out of control. The whole lair's temperature spikes to searing — area Fire damage 3d6 (Craft DC 14 for half).

You must choose: 1. Protect yourself: Use a defensive spell or seek cover (take half damage) 2. Protect Ignarath's legacy: Shield the shard with body or spell — take full damage, but gain +2 on subsequent checks ("You chose protection — not protecting yourself")

If a Companion is present: - Lena: She instinctively dodges the blast ("Sorry — I'm no mage!"), but helps you up afterward. Loyalty –1d6 (she feels ashamed). - If Loyalty ≥ 60: She rushes up to shield the shard with you — takes equal damage. Loyalty +2d6. ("Damn — I must be crazy.")

Round 5 resolution:

Cumulative Successes Result
5 Perfect — the shard stabilizes in your hand. Ignarath's last consciousness smiles. "Selena would be proud of you." Legendary Fire Shard (◈) ×1 + Ignarath's Mark (permanent Fire spell attack +2)
3–4 Success — the shard stabilizes. Ignarath is satisfied. Legendary Fire Shard (◈) ×1
2 Incomplete — the shard stabilizes but is downgraded. Rare Fire Shard (◆) ×1 + Dragon Scale (Legendary material precursor)
0–1 Failure — the shard dissipates. But Ignarath does not blame you. "You tried. That is enough." Dragon Scale ×3 (Legendary material precursor) + Ignarath's respect

Act Three: The Dragon's Legacy

Scene 3-1: Ignarath's Last Gift

Whether success or failure, after the rite ends:

Ignarath's body has almost entirely dissipated, only a pair of golden eyes still burning.

"There is one thing — I always wanted to tell Selena — but had no chance. The Void God is not evil — He is merely lonely. The seven Weavers (守織者) — they were meant to be brothers and sisters. But the seventh — he saw things the others did not. He saw —"

His voice grows fainter.

"— perfect magic is not magic without flaws. It is… magic that accepts flaws."

The last point of light dissipates. Ignarath no longer exists — but the shard he left, in your hand, is warm as a heartbeat.

The player gains Ignarath's memory shard — a key thread pointing directly to Scenario Five, "Echoes of the Weaver": the Void God does not want to destroy magic — He wants to "complete" it. And "complete" means: let all magic accept imperfection.

Scene 3-2: Zephyrus's Proposal

Narration (as the player prepares to leave the lair):

Zephyrus stands at the lair entrance. He is no longer the judge-like figure from before — his dragon eyes hold something young dragons rarely show. Respect.

"I have never seen a human do what you just did. Ignarath — he rarely gives the judgment 'Legacy.' In fact, only twice in three thousand years. The first was Selena. The second was you."

He lowers his dragon head.

"If you are willing — I wish to follow you. Not as a mount. As… a student. I want to learn how humans understand magic. And at the same time —" his voice gains a trace of humor — "— I can also make sure Ignarath's shard is not wasted on boring fireball spells."

Player Choices: 1. Accept Zephyrus as a Bound Creature Companion: Gain a Bound Creature Companion (Master-grade, attribute total 30, Dragonkin — Fire immunity, flight, young-dragon form). He normally stays in the tower as a small dragon (about dog-sized), and can revert to full form to assist or guard. Loyalty 70 ("I respect Ignarath's choice"). 2. Decline but keep friendship: Zephyrus remains in Dragon territory but becomes your permanent ally. You may contact him anytime via scrying pool for Dragon knowledge and intelligence. 3. Refuse: Zephyrus is disappointed but understanding. "Then — sorry to disturb. May your research go well."

Scene 3-3: The Return Journey

Return Journey event table (d10):

d10 Event
1–3 Smooth return — nothing happens on the icefield
4–5 Encounter a spy sent by a rival — they want to steal Ignarath's shard (resolve by social or combat)
6–7 Discover ancient Dragon ruins in the icefield — containing Rare Frost materials
8–9 Valastra appears on the return journey — she gives you a Dragon token ("If you need the Dragons' help — anytime — show this")
10 Blizzard — trapped on the icefield for 1d3 days. An extra survival challenge, but also a chance to build a deeper bond with your Companion

Ending Branches

Ending Condition Reward
The Dragonfire Heir Perfectly stabilize the shard Legendary Fire Shard (◈) ×1 + Ignarath's Mark (permanent Fire spell attack +2)
The Inheritor 3–4 successes Legendary Fire Shard (◈) ×1
The Dragonkin Student Accept Zephyrus Dragonkin Bound Creature Companion ×1 + Fire-immune Companion + Dragon knowledge
Friendship Maintain ally relation only Dragon favor + Ignarath's memory shard (Scenario Five key thread)
The Incomplete Flame Only 2 successes Rare Fire Shard (◆) ×1 + Dragon Scale ×1 (Legendary material precursor)
The Respected Gain no shard but Ignarath approves Dragon Scale ×3 + Ignarath's Mark (no Fire attack +2, but gain Dragon favor)
