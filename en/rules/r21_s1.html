Scenario 1: The Elevator (Novice · 1 Day / 24 Minutes)

Scenario Overview

Item Content
Difficulty ★☆☆☆☆ Novice
Loop Length 24 minutes (strict)
Suggested Players 2-3
Suggested Loop Cap 10
Daily Action Points (AP) 2 (extremely compressed: each Action Point (AP) ≈ 12 minutes)
Tone Tags Denial / The elongation of death-bound time / Five lives compressed into 24 minutes

Loop Setting

  • Loop Endpoint: The elevator falls from the 30th floor to the ground (at the 24-minute mark)
  • Starting Moment: 8:00 AM; the instant the elevator doors close
  • Loop Boundary: Inside the elevator car (approx. 2m × 2m)
  • Opening Narration: "The elevator doors closed behind you. Five people are packed into this cramped space. The numbers on the panel begin to flicker—30. You catch a faint whiff of cologne and last night's alcohol. Everything is perfectly normal. Except... one of these people shouldn't be here."

Core Truth

Anchor Event: Five people enter the elevator, but one of them had already died of a heart attack in the office before entering—except no one noticed. The Rift did not form when the elevator fell, but at the very moment "five people walked into the elevator." Reality cannot process the contradiction of "a dead person walking into the elevator."

Surface Story: The elevator malfunctions, the five are trapped, and it ultimately crashes.

True Story: The deceased is Zhang Mingde, a 52-year-old finance director. He suffered a heart attack while working late alone at 5:00 AM and died at his desk. At 7:55 AM, his "body" stood up and walked into the elevator—but it was no longer him. That was the Rift's first anomalous manifestation.

Rift-Healing Conditions: - Required: The living recognize that one person in the elevator is already "gone" - Required: The living say "farewell" to Zhang Mingde—acknowledging his death - Optional: Find the suicide note in Zhang Mingde's pocket (written at 3:00 AM, to his daughter)

Eight Truth Fragments

  1. The Stopped Watch (Scene / on Zhang Mingde / Easy / Loop 1): Zhang Mingde's watch stopped at 4:58 AM—but he doesn't look like a man wearing a stopped watch. He checked the time twice more inside the elevator.
  2. No Breathing Sounds (Scene / All / Medium / Loop 2): In the instant the elevator fell silent, you counted the breaths—only four people's. But you saw five chests rising and falling. You're certain you saw them.
  3. The Suicide Note (Item / inner pocket of Zhang Mingde's coat / Medium / Loop 3): A letter Zhang Mingde wrote to his daughter at 3:00 AM: "Xiaowen, Daddy might not make it through tonight. If I'm gone, your tuition is in the third drawer of the desk." But he never sent it.
  4. The Medicine Bottle (Item / Zhang Mingde's briefcase / Easy / Loop 1): Fast-acting heart pills; the cap is open, a few pills missing. The date is this morning.
  5. The Colleague's Memory (Conversation / Passenger B—Li Na / Medium / Loop 4): Li Na is Zhang Mingde's colleague. If you ask her what impression she has of Zhang Mingde today, she'll say: "He was... very quiet today. Usually he'd complain the elevator is too slow. Today he didn't say anything." Then she'll pause: "Wait... did he... get on the elevator today? I don't remember him coming in."
  6. The Blind Spot (Scene / elevator ceiling / Hard / Loop 5): The angle of the elevator's surveillance camera—if you look carefully, you'll notice Zhang Mingde is standing precisely in the camera's blind spot. From reality's perspective, there are only four people in this elevator.
  7. The Cold (Scene / near Zhang Mingde / Easy / Loop 2): If you get close to Zhang Mingde, you feel cold. Not the air-conditioning cold—a chill seeping out from the bones. Only those near him can feel it.
  8. Farewell (Conversation / triggered / Special / Loop 6+): When all the living acknowledge that "Zhang Mingde is already gone" and someone says words of farewell to him—he smiles in the next second, then from where he stands, his body slowly dissolves into light. The elevator stops falling. The doors open. Beyond the doors is not the first-floor lobby—but a field of white light.

Key NPCs

Zhang Mingde "The Deceased" (52): Emotion baseline N/A (he is essentially gone, but his "afterimage" repeats some everyday motions—checking his watch, straightening his tie, smiling). Sensitivity "Extremely High." He does not speak. But he listens.

Li Na "The Colleague" (34): Emotion baseline 4/10 (fatigue after working late). Sensitivity "Medium." Secret: She knew Zhang Mingde hadn't been well lately, but thought nothing of it. When she saw Zhang Mingde's "afterimage" walk into the elevator this morning, she thought it was him in person.

Wang Jianguo "The Stranger" (45): Emotion baseline 6/10 (an ordinary office worker). Sensitivity "Low." Secret: He is sneaking looks at the stock market on his phone inside the elevator, completely unaware.

Chen Xiaoyu "The Intern" (23): Emotion baseline 7/10 (nervous on her first day). Sensitivity "High" (young people are sensitive). Secret: She is a classmate of Zhang Mingde's daughter.

Degradation Roadmap (Brief)

  • Loops 1-2: Normal. The elevator runs smoothly. Players first experience the "normal" elevator environment. Reset after the fall.
  • Loops 3-4: First anomaly: the floor display on the number panel occasionally skips a number. Zhang Mingde checks his watch more frequently.
  • Loops 5-6: The elevator occasionally blacks out briefly—1 second of total darkness. After it relights, Zhang Mingde's position has changed once.
  • Loops 7-8: Zhang Mingde begins to "lose focus"—others occasionally "can't see" him (as if he weren't there). Players approach the truth via Fragments 5 and 7.
  • Loops 9-10: The elevator lights turn pale blue—the same as the lights in Zhang Mingde's office before dawn. The last chance.

Three Endings

A - The Doors Open (Perfect): Obtain all fragments + say farewell to Zhang Mingde. The elevator stops at the 1st floor, the doors open—beyond is the white light of a hospital. Zhang Mingde smiles, turns, and walks out of the elevator. The remaining four look at one another as the elevator doors slowly close.

B - One Person Remembers (Normal): At least one person says farewell but the others fall silent. The elevator stops falling, suspended. Zhang Mingde's afterimage dissipates. The elevator lights go out—then relight. The four walk out, but one of them looks back at the empty corner of the elevator, knowing that something had once been there.

C - Loop Exhausted (Bad): No one says farewell. The final fall—the elevator does not stop at the 1st floor. It passes through the ground, continuing to fall, until the lights go out completely and even the panel numbers no longer light up.
