S4: Apex of the Food Chain

Suggested Evolution Nodes: 10-15 nodes Difficulty: Extreme (★★★★★) Estimated Playtime: 5-6 hours Players: 1 / expandable to 2-4 Multiplayer (2-4 players): GM should multiply enemy count by ×1.5 (2 players) or ×2 (3-4 players); the party shares the scenario's Evolution Points and Remnant Coins rewards, while individual absorption gains remain each player's own. All other rules unchanged. Prerequisite Suggestion: S1 + any one of S2/S3

Plot Summary

The "Arena"—a continent sealed by ancient power. Centuries ago, a being that had evolved to near-divine heights created this space. There is only one rule: only the strongest Evolver may leave. All Evolvers trapped here can only survive by ceaselessly hunting and absorbing one another.

You are trapped here now. There are only two ways to leave: become the apex of the food chain, or break the Arena's seal.

Scene 1: The Reluctant Entrant

Opening Description (GM reads aloud)

You are unsure how you got in. One moment you were outside—the next you stand in this bizarre forest.

The trees in the forest are not trees—they are plant life that evolved halfway, some trunks bearing eyes, some branches actively avoiding approaching creatures. The ground is soft like the skin of a living thing.

A voice sounds in your mind—not language, but a pure understanding:

"Welcome to the Trial Grounds. The rules are simple: devour or die. Become the apex, or become nourishment."

"Current participant count: 47."

You look around. At the forest's edge, you see a symbol floating in mid-air—a number: 15.

That is your rank. Out of 47 Evolvers, you are ranked 15th.

Scene Objectives

  • Understand the Arena's rules
  • Choose a strategy: hunt other Evolvers (rapid rank rise but consumes resources), ally (high risk but shareable intel), or seek a way to break the seal (highest difficulty but saves everyone)
  • Survive the first encounter

Scene 2: Hunter and Prey

Opening Description

You have little time to adapt. The other 46 Evolvers in the Arena are all moving—you can see it through the ranking panel in your mind. Numbers are jumping. Some dim.

As you watch, rank 12 turns gray. Then 12 vanishes. Your rank shifts from 15 to 14.

Ahead, from the bushes comes a voice:

"New here?" A female voice, with obvious weariness. "Don't worry, I don't feel like fighting right now. Name's Vera. Rank 28. Been in the Arena three weeks."

She steps out from behind the bushes. Her left arm has completely become some creature's tentacle—but she waves with her right hand, making a peace gesture.

"I can tell you the Arena's rules. Free. But if you want to leave alive—we need to talk."

NPC: Vera (Arena Survivor)

Item Content
Rank 28 (once 5th, dropped after a crushing defeat)
Base Form Human
Evolution Nodes About 12—including tentacle organ, color-shifting skin, venom gland
Humanity Value 30% (alien stage—fine scales have grown on her face)
Attitude Pragmatic survivor. Trusts no one, but knows she cannot survive alone
Can Provide Complete Arena map (including safe zones, water sources, high-risk zones), known info on other participants, a secret—the Arena's seal may be breakable
Need An ally. She was wounded in the last fight (current HP 25/50), needs help to survive

Arena Mechanics

Ranking System:

Rank Title Effect
1-5 Apex Predator Gain extra +2 Evolution Points per opponent eliminated. All creatures in the arena stay wary of you (you are easier to detect)
6-15 High-Rank Hunter Normal
16-35 Mid-Rank Survivor May choose to lie low, but fewer resources
36-47 Bottom Prey Cannot actively challenge higher-ranked Evolvers (Arena law enforced). But may counterattack if attacked

Ways to Rise in Rank: - Kill or absorb another Evolver (rank rises depending on the opponent's rank) - Declare a challenge against a higher-ranked opponent before the Arena's "Challenge Stele" (forced 1v1, others cannot interfere) - Defeat wild mutant creatures in the Arena (but rank rises less)

Scene 3: Alliance and Betrayal

Opening Description

Led by Vera, you arrive at the Arena's central area—a natural stone platform called the "Gathering Stone." This is the only neutral zone (Arena law forcibly prohibits combat here).

Several Evolvers are already on the platform. A burly figure with a rock-like body dozes against the stone. A thin silhouette—likely an insect-form Evolver by build—crouches at the platform's edge, watching everyone warily.

But what catches your attention is the person standing at the platform's center—he is unmutated. Completely human appearance. Confident smile.

"Newcomer!" He spreads his arms. "Welcome! Name's Marcus. Was a newbie once—now ranked 3rd."

Vera whispers: "Watch this one. His evolution path is… mind control."

Key Encounter: Choosing Allies

Possible Allies:

Evolver Rank Style Reliability Join Condition
Vera 28 Stealth/Poison ★★★★ Provide healing and protection
Rock—Greg 8 Defense/Strength ★★★★★ Help him find his missing younger brother (also in the Arena)
Bug-Whisperer—Qi 19 Perception/Insect ★★★ Share your memories from outside the Arena (she wants to know the outside world)
Marcus 3 Mind Control ★ (will surely betray) Accepts any ally on the surface—but will mind-control you at a critical moment to fight the other opponents

Branch Events

Event A: Marcus's Betrayal (if the player allies with Marcus) - When the player and another high-ranked Evolver fight each other to Near Death, Marcus appears - He uses mind control to force the player to attack their own teammate (resist with Will YZ DC 18) - If resistance fails→teammate defeated, Marcus absorbs both Near Death opponents, rank rises to 1st

Event B: Greg's Brother (if the player helps Greg) - Greg's brother "Little Rock" has already been absorbed by another Evolver—Greg doesn't know, but Vera does - Tell him the truth→Greg goes berserk (attacks any nearby Evolver)→but if the player soothes him successfully (Will YZ DC 16), he becomes the player's most loyal ally - Hide the truth→Greg keeps searching→may learn the truth at the final battle, reacting even more violently then

Event C: Qi's Question - Qi was still a child before being trapped in the Arena. She slowed her aging through evolution, but her mind remains incomplete - If you tell her the outside world has become worse→Humanity Value -2 (because you destroyed a child's hope) - If you tell her there is still something worth returning to outside→Humanity Value +2 (you gave her hope)

Scene 4: Regional Overlord

Opening Description

The Arena is divided into four regions, each with a "Regional Overlord"—a top-five-ranked Evolver. To reach the apex of the food chain, you must defeat at least one of them.

Your target is ranked 2nd, "Wilderness King" Zarlax—an Evolver once of Beast form, now evolved into a near-dragonic giant beast. His territory is the Arena's eastern volcanic plateau.

According to Vera's intel, Zarlax has absorbed over 30 Evolvers. His Humanity Value may have long since hit zero. But he never leaves his territory—perhaps he is guarding something?

BOSS Fight: Wilderness King Zarlax (Boss-tier)

Name HP Defense Attributes Energy
Wilderness King Zarlax 120 18 LIL 14, MJ 8, TZ 12, GZ 10, YZ 8 22

Phase One (HP > 60):

Skill Tier AP Energy Effect
Massive Jaw Bite Low-tier 1 1 Melee 2d8+7 piercing
Tail Sweep Mid-tier 2 3 Cone 3 squares, 2d6+7 bludgeoning + Agility MJ DC 16 or knocked prone
Lava Breath High-tier 3 5 Cone 5 squares, 3d10 fire + terrain becomes lava (1d6/round)
Dragon Scales Passive — — All damage -3

Phase Two (HP ≤ 60)—"Territory Guardian":

Zarlax retreats into his nest. In the nest is a giant egg—his child. In phase two:

  • Zarlax's attacks grow fiercer (all damage +1d6)
  • But he will not leave the nest's bounds (protecting the egg)
  • The player may choose: ①keep attacking Zarlax ②attack the egg to distract him (Humanity Value -5) ③attempt to communicate with Zarlax (Will YZ DC 20; success means Zarlax is willing to negotiate)

If the player chooses communication (third path): - Zarlax reveals the truth: the Arena's creator holds his child hostage—if he is not the last survivor, his child dies - The player may choose to help Zarlax break the seal—harder than breaking it alone, but with two powerful Evolvers joining forces

Loot: - Kill: 8 Evolution Points + 15 Biomass - Absorb: Trait sample (choose 2 of 5)—①Dragon Scales (Passive: all damage -2) ②Lava Breath (High-tier Active) ③Beast Heart (Organ: HP +15, Strength LIL +2) ④Territory Sense (Passive: Perception GZ advantage within a specific region) ⑤Paternal Instinct (Passive: Defense +2 when protecting a specific target)—only selectable on peaceful resolution - Peaceful resolution: gain no Evolution Points, but gain Zarlax's assistance—he appears as an ally in the final battle

Scene 5: The Final Showdown

Opening Description

Only the two of you remain. You and rank 1st.

The Arena's ground begins to tremble. Trees recoil, earth rises, forming a natural arena at the Arena's center—a circular space sealed by an energy barrier.

A figure walks from across.

Rank 1st—the "Champion." Winner of the previous Arena. He has been here 50 years. He could have left—but chose to stay. He waits for a truly worthy opponent.

BOSS Fight: Champion—Immortal Kane (Boss-tier)

Name HP Defense Attributes Energy
Immortal Kane 150 20 LIL 12, MJ 14, TZ 12, GZ 14, YZ 16 28

Kane has evolved over 30 nodes, including at least 3 Form Nodes. He can freely switch forms in combat.

Skills (Form Shift—switches to the next form at the end of each round, cycling):

Form Bonus Skills
Beast Form Strength LIL +3, HP +20 Earthquake Smash (High-tier), Devour (Mid-tier—absorb 2d6 HP from target to heal self)
Spirit Form Physical attacks ineffective against him, mental damage +3 Psychic Storm (High-tier), Soul Rend (Ultimate—Will DC 20 or lose consciousness 1 round)
Elemental Form Fire immunity, Frost immunity Elemental Storm (Ultimate—mixed 4d10 fire+frost+lightning), Elemental Shield (Passive: negates the first attack each round)

Tactical Hint (GM): Kane's form cycle is fixed: Beast→Spirit→Elemental→Beast. The player can predict and prepare countermeasures. But note—in Spirit form physical attacks are completely ineffective, in Elemental form elemental attacks are completely ineffective.

Special Mechanic—Arena's Blessing: In the final battle, both sides recover 3 Energy each round (instead of 2). The Arena is enjoying this duel.

Special Choice—Lay Down Arms: At the start of battle or any moment, either side may choose to lay down weapons. If the player lays down weapons: - Kane will not attack—he waited 50 years not to slaughter an unwilling opponent - Kane reveals: the Arena's seal can be broken by the combined will of two apex Evolvers - Instead of the final battle→both sides make a joint Will (YZ) check (each DC 20)→if both succeed, the seal shatters and all surviving participants are freed - If the player succeeds but Kane fails (or Kane won't cooperate)→breaking the seal alone requires Will (YZ) DC 28

Ending Settlement

Action Reward
Complete scenario +5 Evolution Points
Defeat Kane +10 Evolution Points + absorb Kane (gain the Ultimate Form Node "Champion's Body"—can switch form once a month at no cost)
Break the seal together with Kane All freed. Gain Passive "Seal Breaker" (Will YZ +3, gain Advantage against any seal/barrier thereafter)
Kill Wilderness King (peaceful resolution) Zarlax appears as an ally in S5
Kill Wilderness King (by force) Gain Evolution Points and Biomass

GM Notes

Arena Ranking Tracking

The GM should prepare a simple ranking table to track the survival status of other Evolvers in the Arena. Suggested: of the initial 47 participants, after the player experiences 3-4 encounters, naturally reduce to about 20.

Time Pressure

The Arena shrinks its bounds every 24 hours (boundary contracts inward 500 meters), forcing participants closer together. If the player spends too much time resting in the safe zone, the GM can trigger "boundary contraction" to push progress.

Solo Mode Hints

  • Both Greg and Vera can serve as NPC companions
  • In the Champion fight, Kane's HP drops to 100
  • The Arena's boundary contraction speed is halved
