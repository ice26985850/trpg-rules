Urban Survivors Scenario One: "The First Day" — Early Siege: Chaos and Adaptation

Suggested Level: Play immediately after character creation Difficulty: ★★☆ (Low–Medium) Suggested Players: 1 (expandable to multiplayer) Siege Day: Day 3 to Day 14 (about 5–7 game sessions) Core Theme: Chaos and Adaptation

Scenario Overview

The siege has only just begun. The artillery thunder is still distant, but the power and water are already unstable, and the shops have been looted bare.

The city has fallen into organized chaos—the Federal Army broadcasts that "the situation is under control," yet the streets are full of fleeing crowds and abandoned vehicles.

The players must find their footing amid the ruins of order: establish a Shelter, search for the first supplies, and decide whom to trust.

This scenario supports both solo mode (1 player + 1 GM) and multiplayer. In multiplayer, the players share a Shelter and manage resources together, with interpersonal conflict and division of labor becoming the core driving force.

Starting Scenarios (choose one, by player or GM)

A. Own Apartment

  • Location: Residential apartment in Old Town
  • Starting Resources: Household reserves (canned food ×3, bottled water ×2, first-aid kit ×1); personal belongings per character creation Step Eight
  • Starting Challenge: A neighbor knocks asking for help—a family with a child requests shelter
  • GM Tip: Make the player face the first moral choice immediately: open the door or refuse?

B. Workplace

  • Location: City Center office or University District classroom
  • Starting Resources: Office emergency reserves (cookies ×2, bottled water ×1); a colleague / classmate NPC at your side (GM chooses 1–2)
  • Starting Challenge: The building was damaged in a recent shelling and must be evacuated before the aftershocks
  • GM Tip: Good for guiding the player through their first stealth / evacuation action

C. The Streets

  • Location: Random district street (GM's choice)
  • Starting Resources: Only what is on your person (starting equipment on the character sheet)
  • Starting Challenge: Immediately encounter the first (light) shelling or a Patrol Soldier—must find cover within 3 turns
  • GM Tip: The highest-difficulty start—for players who enjoy a challenge

Special Events Table (triggered by day count)

Day Event Mechanic
Day 3 First Shelling Shelling strikes somewhere in the city; the whistling of shells grows ever closer. All characters make a Physique (PHY) check DC 10; on failure, a Minor Wound (grazed by shrapnel); those outdoors use DC 14 instead
Day 4 Water Crisis Tap water stops. From this moment, water becomes a scarce resource (trade value ×2); if the player has no stored water, the dehydration countdown begins
Day 5 A Neighbor's Request A neighbor knocks (GM rolls: 1–3 a family with a child, 4–5 an injured elderly person, 6 a suspicious young man). Take them in → that NPC becomes a Shelter member, food and water consumption +1/day; refuse → Willpower (WIL) check DC 10, on failure Mental State −1
Day 7 Airdropped Leaflets A plane flies low and drops leaflets—the besiegers' surrender demand vs. friendly forces' "hold on" message. Gives the player a plot clue and hope (or fear)
Day 10 First Black Market Contact Marco (NPC #15) approaches the players and offers a "deal." A Bargaining (Social) check DC 12 may be attempted; success yields better terms; accepting establishes initial contact with the Black River Merchant Guild; refusing lowers future trade starting attitude by −1
Day 14 Sniper Appears Someone is shot by a sniper on Republic Avenue. From this moment, crossing Republic Avenue in Daylight triggers the Sniper threat (Threat Level 4); the first time the player hears the gunshot: Willpower check DC 10, on failure Mental State −1

Key NPCs (recommended for Scenario One)

  • Grandma Marta (NPC #7): Provides Old Town intelligence and folk wisdom. As the player moves through Old Town, someone calls from a second-floor window: "Hey! Youngster!"
  • Marco (NPC #15): The Black Market introducer. Appears during the Day 10 Black Market Contact special event.
  • Yana Novak (NPC #2): Organizer of the Civil Defense Committee (CDC). The player encounters a group of residents organizing patrols in the neighborhood.

Completion Conditions

  • Shelter Stable: Structural Integrity + Sealing + Comfort ≥ 5
  • Build a Relationship: Relationship value (Relationship, REL) with at least 1 NPC ≥ 3
  • Survive: Successfully survive 7 days (i.e., complete at least 7 game sessions)

Clearing Rewards

  • Experience Points (Experience): 2
  • Fate Point (Fate): 1
  • Special: Unlock Shelter Improvement option—Reinforce Structure (Structure +1, requires Materials)

Transition to Scenario Two

Time jump of 2–3 weeks. The GM describes the changes during this period:

  • Power and water are still not restored
  • There are fewer people on the streets—some evacuated, some died
  • Black Market trade has become more frequent
  • Winter is approaching (or has already arrived)

The player's Shelter is still running, but the supply reserves are dwindling.
