Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart — Solo Scenario: "Lone Sword Questions Heaven"

Suitable for: 1 GM + 1 Player | Scope: Qi Refining → Tribulation Crossing (Lv.1-36) Depends on: This scenario is based on the "Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart" main rulebook + solo_rules.md Solo Rules Supplement. Citation format: → §chapter cites the main rulebook. → solo §section cites the solo rules. NPC Companions: see character_cards_Excel/NPC_Companion_Cards.xlsx

GM Prep: Before reading this scenario, it is recommended to first familiarize yourself with Chapter 4 (Worldbuilding) of the main rulebook and the Solo Rules Supplement.

Scenario Overview

"Lone Sword Questions Heaven" is the complete legend of a lone cultivator (Cultivator) from mortal to ascension. The player (Player) enters the Hongmeng Continent (鴻蒙大陸) alone, befriends close friends, apprentices under renowned masters, fights the demonic path, and finally faces the Heaven's Mechanism.

The scenario is divided into five acts, each covering several realms, with progress adjustable flexibly:

Act Realm Range Theme Est. Sessions
Act I Qi Refining Lv.1-3 Mortal Enters the Dao · First Taste of the Jianghu 2-3
Act II Foundation Establishment Lv.4-6 Apprenticeship · First Gleam of the Blade 3-4
Act III Golden Core Lv.7-9 Golden Core Perfected · First Sign of the Demonic 3-5
Act IV Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation Lv.10-18 Nascent Soul Seeks the Dao · Righteous-Demon Clash 5-8
Act V Void Refining → Tribulation Crossing Lv.19-36 Path to Ascension · Choice of the Heavenly Dao 5-10

Act I: Mortal Enters the Dao · First Taste of the Jianghu

Realm: Qi Refining Lv.1-3 | Location: Qingshi Town (青石鎮), Black Pine Ridge (黑松嶺) Main NPCs: Chen Bonian (陳伯年, old town chief), Yao Buran (藥不然, Crimson Clouds Valley disciple), Bai Ling (白靈, fox spirit · companion candidate) Reference: Worldbuilding → §4.5 (Qingshi Town)

Prologue: The Missing Herb Gatherers

For their own reasons, the players (Players) come to Qingshi Town (青石鎮) — an ordinary small town on the outskirts of the Taixu Sword Sect (太虛劍宗). On the night of arrival, the old town chief Chen Bonian sighs in the teahouse: another herb gatherer from the town has vanished — the third this month.

GM Check Points: - Asking townsfolk for information (Social Check, → §2): Difficulty (DC) 40; success reveals the missing all last headed toward Black Pine Ridge. - Searching the last missing person's residence (Observation/Divine Sense (SHS) Check): Difficulty (DC) 50; discover a bundle of undelivered herbs and half a map.

Solo Tip: The player may freely choose the investigation method. If bringing an NPC companion (see below), the companion can provide assistance (Check +5).

Obtaining NPC Companion: Bai Ling

During the investigation, the player encounters an injured white fox kit at the edge of Black Pine Ridge — its hind leg caught in a hunter's trap. If the player chooses to help (no check required), the kit reveals spiritual intelligence after healing: it is a spirit fox that has just awakened its intellect, named Bai Ling, who can become the player's first NPC companion.

Bai Ling (Qi Refining stage) Data
Health Points (HP) 20
Damage Claw Strike 1d6+2
Special Perception: can detect Spiritual Energy anomalies and hidden enemies within 50 zhang
Personality Curious, loyal; cannot speak human words but understands simple commands

Excel Card: see character_cards_Excel/NPC_Companion_Cards.xlsx → "Bai Ling" sheet

First Encounter: Black Pine Ridge Exploration

Encounter Type Data
Shadow Cat ×2 Mook Health Points (HP) 12, Claw Strike 1d6+1, Night Stealth +10
Injured Herb Gatherer NPC Trapped at the ancient cave entrance, Health Points (HP) 5/30 (critical)

In combat the player must protect the critically wounded herb gatherer (→ solo §3.2). If the herb gatherer dies, subsequent quest rewards are halved. Once rescued, the gatherer reports: someone deep in the ancient cave is conducting "heretical experiments."

Second Encounter: Deep in the Ancient Cave

After entering the ancient cave, the player discovers a crude alchemy room. A cultivator (Cultivator) expelled from the Qingyun Sect (青雲門), Liu Yunfei (柳雲飛), is using the herb gatherers' vital blood to refine "Yin-Gathering Pills."

Encounter Type Data
Liu Yunfei Boss Health Points (HP) 35, Corpse Claw 1d8+3 + Corpse Poison (Health Points (HP) -3 per round, 3 rounds), at half HP Corpse Qi Burst AoE 1d10

Weakness: Fire attribute +30% damage. Yang-attribute Cultivation Techniques counter corpse poison.

Tactical Suggestion (Solo): Use the ancient cave terrain (stone pillars provide cover) and fire-type attacks. Bai Ling can draw attention from the flank (assist action → solo §2.1).

Rewards and Follow-up

Reward Description
Cultivation +800 (can reach Lv.2-3)
Items Fragment of the Yin-Gathering Pill recipe (clue), Spirit Stones ×200, Mortal-grade weapon ×1
Reputation Qingshi Town +10
Follow-up Hook Liu Yunfei mentioned "Netherworld Dao (幽冥道)" before dying; deep in the ancient cave is a sealed stone gate (opens in Act II)

Act II: Apprenticeship · First Gleam of the Blade

Realm: Foundation Establishment Lv.4-6 | Location: Taixu Sword Sect, Sword-Washing Pool Main NPCs: Qin Yu (秦羽, guide → NPC card), Xuanzhenzi (玄真子, apprenticeship candidate → §15.2) Reference: Apprenticeship system → §14, §15

Prologue: A Letter of Recommendation

The deed of righteousness in Qingshi Town reaches the Taixu Sword Sect. Inner-sect disciple Qin Yu (→ character_cards_Excel/NPC_Card_QinYu.xlsx) personally comes to invite the player to the Taixu Sword Sect — "The Sword-Washing Pool needs a new sword-art teaching assistant; perhaps you could come take a look."

Branch Choice: - Accept the Invitation → Enter the Taixu Sword Sect storyline (recommended route) - Decline → May choose to go to the Crimson Clouds Valley (丹霞谷, Ge Yun storyline) or the Northern Frontier (Zhenbei True Lord storyline); the GM adjusts the follow-up based on the choice.

Apprenticeship (→ §14)

After entering the sect, the player may apprentice under Xuanzhenzi (→ §15.2) — see the main rulebook for specific conditions and trials. After apprenticeship, unlocks: - Monthly resource supply (→ §14.4) - Cultivation Technique transmission (oral and heart-to-heart teaching → §14.4) - Dao Heart trial (→ §14.3 promotion conditions)

Main Encounter: Sword-Washing Pool Trial

Encounter Order Type Data
1. Basic Sword Puppet Elite Health Points (HP) 60, Basic Sword Art 1d8+4, Armor Class (AC) 12
2. Intermediate Sword Puppet Elite Health Points (HP) 80, Green Lotus Sword Formula 2d8+5, Armor Class (AC) 14, 3rd strike ×1.5
3. Mirror Trial Boss Health Points (HP) = player's Health Points (HP), copies all of player's Martial Techniques (Damage −20%), Armor Class (AC) = player's Armor Class (AC)

Mirror Trial Mechanic: The mirror uses the player's Divine Abilities on rounds 3 and 6 (if the player has learned them). Defeating the mirror counts as passing the sword trial.

Reward: Foundation Establishment Pill ×1 + Green Edge Sword (Spirit-grade, 1d10+2+Constitution (GUG)/8, Hit +5)

Act II Follow-up Hooks

  • Xuanzhenzi mentions "the Netherworld Dao has been active frequently in the Northern Frontier lately"
  • Qin Yu reports: the stone gate deep in the ancient cave may lead to a Netherworld Dao stronghold
  • Bai Ling begins to speak simple human words by the end of this act ("Master... be careful...")

Act III: Golden Core Perfected · First Sign of the Demonic

Realm: Golden Core Lv.7-9 | Location: Northern Frontier front line, outskirts of the Bloodfiend Valley Main NPCs: Zhenbei True Lord (鎮北真君, → §15.3), Bloodfiend Demon Lord (血煞魔君, → §15.3) Reference: Combat system → §3, Monster templates → combat_system_design.md §6

Prologue: Border Beacon Fires

With the Golden Core formed, the player's standing in the cultivation world is utterly transformed (→ world_expansion.md, Golden Core chapter). The Taixu Sword Sect grants the "True Inheritance Disciple" status. At this time, urgent military intelligence comes from the Northern Frontier: Netherworld Dao demon cultivators have established an outpost on the Northern Frontier border.

The player's master (Xuanzhenzi or another) suggests: go to the Northern Frontier to assist Zhenbei True Lord (→ §15.3) — both a chance to train and to make a name.

NPC Companion Evolution: Bai Ling

Bai Ling (Golden Core stage) Data
Health Points (HP) 60
Damage Spirit Claw 2d6+5 + "Spiritual Energy Burn" (1 round Health Points (HP) -5)
Special Transformation: can briefly become human-shaped (1 shichen/day), for infiltration or social use
Personality Now speaks fluently, with deep loyalty to the player

Encounter One: Border Patrol

Encounter Type Data
Netherworld Dao Scout ×2 Mook Health Points (HP) 45, Shadow Bolt 1d10+3 (ranged), Stealth +15
Demonized Beast ×1 Elite Health Points (HP) 90, Demon Claw 2d8+8, takes +30% fire/light damage

GM Hint: Netherworld Dao scouts will try to flee to report — the player must eliminate or stop them within 3 rounds.

Encounter Two: Outpost Raid

Encounter Type Data
Netherworld Dao Steward · Ghost Face (鬼面) Boss Health Points (HP) 150, Netherworld Palm 2d10+6, at half HP summons 2 mooks, every 4 rounds releases "Dark Veil" (whole-field vision blocked, Hit −15 for 2 rounds)

Ghost Face (→ character_cards_Excel/Boss_Card_Collection.xlsx → Ghost Face sheet) is a mid-level cadre of the Netherworld Dao. Drops upon defeat: - Netherworld Token (pass to enter Netherworld Dao strongholds) - A secret letter: indicates the Netherworld Dao's next target is the "Bloodfiend Valley"

Encounter Three (Optional): The Choice at Bloodfiend Valley

Tracking the secret letter brings the player to the outskirts of Bloodfiend Valley. The player may choose:

Choice Consequence
Infiltrate and Investigate Stealth Check Difficulty (DC) 55; on success discovers Bloodfiend Demon Lord (→ §15.3) is negotiating with the Netherworld Dao — but he has not fully sided with them
Frontal Assault Triggers combat with Bloodfiend Valley guards (3 Golden Core demon cultivators, → solo §3.1 quantity adjustment)
Contact Bloodfiend Demon Lord Social Check Difficulty (DC) 60; can persuade Bloodfiend Demon Lord to provide intel (he is discontent with Netherworld Dao control)

Branch Hint: If the player builds a connection with Bloodfiend Demon Lord in this act, a demonic-path route opens in Act IV. But it does not affect main-story progression.

Rewards

Reward Description

Items Demon-Breaking Crossbow (→ §5 Equipment), Spirit Stones ×3,000
Reputation Northern Frontier +15
NPC Favor Zhenbei True Lord affinity +15 (if assisted Northern Frontier); Bloodfiend Demon Lord affinity +10 (if chose contact route)

Act IV: Nascent Soul Seeks the Dao · Righteous-Demon Clash

Realm: Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation Lv.10-18 | Location: major sects of Zhongzhou, Netherworld Dao main altar Main NPCs: Tianyazi (天涯子, → §15.4), Dugu Wuji (獨孤無極, → §15.4), Fallen Immortal · Cang Wudao (墮仙·蒼無道, Boss)

Prologue: The Grand Situation Under Heaven

With the Nascent Soul formed, the player is now a renowned powerhouse. But at this time, the Netherworld Dao cult leader Cang Wudao (→ §15.4, late Fusion stage) announces shocking news to the cultivation world — he has discovered an ancient immortal-mansion ruin deep in the Northern Frontier Demon Abyss, containing the "Dao Ancestor's Inheritance." The great sects stir with unrest.

The player's master suggests: "Go to the Tianji Manor (天機府) and find Tianyazi (→ §15.4) — he may know the truth."

Visiting Tianyazi

Tianyazi (→ character_cards_Excel/NPC_Card_Tianyazi.xlsx) receives the player at the Tianji Manor. He reveals: - The "immortal-mansion ruin" Cang Wudao spoke of does exist - But what is inside is not the Dao Ancestor's Inheritance, but a sealed ancient Demon Ancestor — Devourer of the Dao (→ Boss card) - Cang Wudao intends to unseal the Demon Ancestor and use its power to rule the Hongmeng Continent

Tianyazi gives the player a task: before Cang Wudao unseals it, gather the three "Demon-Sealing Keys" — held respectively by the Taixu Sword Sect, the Crimson Clouds Valley, and Tianyazi himself.

NPC Companion Evolution

At this point the player may choose to swap companions or keep Bai Ling. Optional new companions:

Companion Acquisition Condition Trait
Bai Ling (Nascent Soul stage) Keep New "Spirit Fox Illusion Array": in combat, 1/day, befuddle 1 enemy for 2 rounds
Tianji Manor Disciple · Qingxuan (青玄) Recommended by Tianyazi Healer type; each round restores player's Health Points (HP) ×5%

Encounter One: The Taixu Sword Sect's Trial

Back at the Taixu Sword Sect, Dugu Wuji (→ §15.4) agrees to hand over the Demon-Sealing Key — but on one condition: the player must prove themselves in the "Taixu Sword Debate."

Encounter Type Data
First Match (Golden Core opponent) Elite Health Points (HP) 150, Green Lotus Sword Formula 2d8+8, 3rd strike ×1.5
Second Match (Nascent Soul opponent) Elite Health Points (HP) 220, Heaven-Rending Sword Gang 3d10+6, ignores 10 defense
Third Match (Spirit Transformation opponent · Dugu Wuji at 30% power) Boss Health Points (HP) 350, simplified Myriad Swords Return to Origin 3d12+8, Armor Class (AC) 24

Solo Tip: Dugu Wuji's battle is not a must-win — simply surviving 5 rounds earns recognition. → solo §3.2 elite battle design.

Encounter Two: Demon Abyss Decisive Battle

Encounter Type Data
Netherworld Demon General · Black Cliff (黑崖) Mid-Boss Health Points (HP) 280, Demon Abyss Rift 3d10+10, summons 3 mooks (HP 60), berserk at half HP
Fallen Immortal · Cang Wudao Final Boss Health Points (HP) 500, Fallen Immortal Sword 4d12+12, every 3 rounds releases "Heavenly Dao Collapse" (AoE 3d12+Spirit Power (LIL)), Weakness: Light +25%, Chaos +20%

Cang Wudao Combat Mechanic (→ character_cards_Excel/Boss_Card_Collection.xlsx → Fallen Immortal · Cang Wudao sheet, solo-adjusted data): 1. Phase One (HP 500-300): uses Fallen Immortal Sword and ranged spells 2. Phase Two (HP 299-150): summons Demon Abyss rifts, dealing 1d6 dark-erosion damage to the player each round 3. Phase Three (HP 149-0): sacrifices own lifespan to unleash "Fallen Sky Strike" (single target 6d12, ignores defense; after use Cang Wudao is weakened for 1 round)

GM Hint: This is the highest-difficulty battle in the solo scenario. It is recommended to ensure before battle that the player has enough pills and at least 1 Robe-and-Bowl-level aid (if any).

Act V: Path to Ascension · Choice of the Heavenly Dao

Realm: Void Refining → Fusion → Mahayana → Tribulation Crossing Lv.19-36 Location: entire Hongmeng Continent, Gate of the Heavenly Dao Main NPCs: One Thought of Reincarnation (輪迴一念, Heavenly Dao trial), the player's Robe-and-Bowl master

Prologue: After the Sealing

Cang Wudao is defeated and the seal on the Demon Ancestor Devourer of the Dao is temporarily stabilized. But the player learns a cruel truth from Tianyazi: the seal is not permanent — every thousand years a Mahayana-stage cultivator must reinforce it with their own lifespan. The previous cultivator to reinforce the seal has less than a hundred years of life left.

Branch Choice: - Seek a Permanent Sealing Method → go to the Tianji Manor, ancient ruins, and visit Yichen (羿塵, ancient remnant clan → world_expansion.md) - Prepare to Ascend, Leave This World → accelerate cultivation to ascend before the seal collapses (selfish route) - Become the Next Sealer → accept Zhenbei True Lord's Robe-and-Bowl Trial (→ §14.3 promotion conditions)

Act V Structure

This act is an open-ended conclusion; the GM advances it based on the player's choices. The three main routes are:

Route A: Seek the Permanent Seal (Recommended)

Step Encounter/Challenge
1. Visit Yichen Yichen (Spirit Transformation-stage power) tells: the Demon Ancestor's true body hides in the "Void Rift," and it must be utterly destroyed within the rift
2. Enter the Void Rift The laws within the Void Rift are chaotic; each round requires passing a Dao Heart (DAX) Check (DC 60) or become lost for 1 round
3. Face the Demon Ancestor · Devourer of the Dao Boss: Health Points (HP) 1000, Devour 4d20+15, devours 1 buff effect from the player each round, Weakness: Chaos Origin

Devourer of the Dao Battle: The final battle in the Void Rift. The player must first defeat the Devourer's "Seven Emotions Incarnations" (7 consecutive battles, each representing one emotion: joy/anger/worry/thought/sorrow/fear/surprise); a short rest is allowed after each (→ §1.2). After all are defeated, the Devourer's true body appears.

Route B: Ascension (Speedrun Route)

After the Tribulation Crossing stage, the player may choose to ascend directly. But Tianyazi will tell them: after ascension they can no longer intervene in this world — meaning that when the seal collapses, the Hongmeng Continent will lose its last guardian.

If still choosing ascension: undergo the final Heavenly Tribulation (→ §1.5, Difficulty (DC) raised to 80, but Robe-and-Bowl inheritance may assist). On success, the player becomes part of the Heavenly Dao and the scenario ends.

Route C: Become the Sealer (Sacrifice Route)

If choosing to accept Zhenbei True Lord's Robe-and-Bowl Trial ("Will you seal the Demon Abyss at the cost of your lifespan?" → §14.3), then: - Player's lifespan −500 years (about 100-200 years remaining) - Seal stabilized for 1000 years - Gain the title "Continent Guardian," reputation permanently +50 - During this time may take disciples, found a sect, and seek the next guardian — the scenario turns into an endgame of "seeking a successor"

Ending: The Gate of the Heavenly Dao

No matter which route is chosen, the destination of Act V is the same door — the Gate of the Heavenly Dao. Here the player looks back on a lifetime of choices:

GM Narration Example: "Before the Gate of the Heavenly Dao, you see your whole life: the herb gatherer of Qingshi Town that year, the sword-song of the Sword-Washing Pool, the wind and snow of the Northern Frontier, Cang Wudao's Fallen Sky Strike, the choice before the seal. Every choice is engraved upon the Gate of the Heavenly Dao." "Then, the door opens."

Ending Determination:

Ending Condition
Ascend to Immortality Route B complete, Tribulation Crossing success
Guardian Sage Route C complete, becomes the sealer
God-Slayer Route A complete, permanently destroy the Demon Ancestor
Dao Ancestor's Inheritance Route A complete + Robe-and-Bowl affinity 100 + Dugu Wuji's recognition → obtain the Dao Ancestor's Inheritance
