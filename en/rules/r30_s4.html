s04_Return of the Dead (Excerpted from the League of Legends TRPG Campaign Scenario Collection · Multiplayer Scenario · Main Campaign Finale)

Finale: Return of the Dead (Lv.17-20)

Main Campaign Finale · Shadow Isles

Background

The failure of the Ascension ritual forced the Obsidian Circle to launch its final desperate plan — go to the Shadow Isles, where death is not the end and the power of the dead is inexhaustible. Their leader, Malzahar, the Void Prophet (the true mastermind), has already prepared to open a Void Rift deep within the Shadow Isles, connecting the world of the living to the Void. The players must find allies in the unlikeliest of places — even including Thresh and Hecarim — and stop the Void's descent on the night of the Harrowing.

Scenario Structure

Chapter Title Level Type Core Encounter
4.1 Sea of Mists Lv.17 Survival/Combat Cross the black mist, undead ship encounter, search for a landing point
4.2 Night of the Harrowing Lv.18 Epic Social Negotiate with Thresh/Kalista/Hecarim to form the Undead Coalition
4.3 Void Rift Lv.19-20 Ultimate Boss Battle Three-phase Malzahar + Void summon legion

NPC Role Bond Opportunity

Hecarim Shadow of War — must be subdued by force before negotiation Rival → reluctant ally
Malzahar Ultimate Boss —

Ultimate Boss Battle: Malzahar, the Void Prophet (Lv.20)

Attribute Value
HP 8,000
AD/AP 150/180
AR/MR 160/160
Special Void Shield: gains Ability Power (AP)×0.5 shield at the start of each turn

Four-Phase Mechanics (special treatment for the finale Boss): - P1 (100-75%): Void Summon (summons 3 Void Wraiths each turn: Health Points (HP) 300, Attack Damage (AD) 50) + Void Grasp (single target Ability Power (AP)×1.5 + Suppress 1 turn) - P2 (75-50%): Opens the first Void Rift — all combatants take Health Points (HP)×2% True Damage each turn; Void Wraiths upgrade to Void Beasts (Health Points (HP) 600, Attack Damage (AD) 80) - P3 (50-25%): Void Descent — all players gain the "Void Corruption" debuff (Health Points (HP)×3% True Damage each turn, can be resisted with a Difficulty Class (DC) 22 Constitution (CON) save); summons a Void Eye (Health Points (HP) 1,200, deals Ability Power (AP)×0.5 gaze damage to all each turn) - P4 (25-0%): Final Prophecy — Malzahar channels for 3 turns to open the ultimate rift. If not killed within 3 turns: instant death for all (scripted death). During this, the Undead Coalition (Thresh/Kalista/Hecarim) grants the players 1 extra action each turn.

Multiple Endings

Ending Condition Consequence
Perfect Victory Kill Malzahar within 3 turns of P4 + at least 1 player survives The Void Rift is sealed, Runeterra gains peace. The players become legendary heroes.
Pyrrhic Victory Kill Malzahar but the whole party dies The rift is sealed, but the players become the new guardians of the Shadow Isles (undying form).
Void Descent P4 channel completes unstopped The world enters the Void Era. Opens a new campaign line — "Void Counterattack".

Rewards

  • Total Campaign EXP: accumulate from Lv.1 to Lv.20
  • Total Campaign Gold: 50,000-70,000
  • Finale-Exclusive Equipment: "Heart of the Void" (Attack Damage (AD)+80/Ability Power (AP)+100, killing an enemy restores 20% of max Health Points (HP), revives in Void form 2 turns after death — once per battle)
  • Finale-Exclusive Augment: "Child of the Void" — may choose any 1 Legendary-tier Augment Device
  • Campaign Completion Achievement: "Runeterra's Guardian" title (all attributes +5%)
