s06_Zaun's Abyss (Excerpt from League of Legends TRPG Campaign Script Collection · Multiplayer Scenario · Side Story S2)

S2: Zaun's Abyss (Lv.8-12 · Zaun)

Background: A series of toxic gas leaks has struck the lower levels of Zaun, with victims exhibiting strange "alchemy mutations." The players' investigation reveals — Viktor is conducting Glorious Evolution experiments, fusing living humans with machinery.

Chapter Level Type
Source of the Poison Fog Lv.8-9 Investigation + Infiltration
Glorious Evolution Lv.10-11 Laboratory Dungeon + Mutant Combat
Viktor Lv.12 Boss + Moral Choice: Destroy or Understand?

Reward: "Glorious Evolution Core" (transforms 1 piece of equipment into a mechanical version: stats +20%, but no longer affected by healing spells)
