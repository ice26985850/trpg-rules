Scenario Eight: The Cult's Secret [Level (Lv.) 8]

Suitable: Level (Lv.) 8 × 3-4 players | Duration: 5-6 hours | Map: Industrial Zone edge / Core Zone perimeter

Commission

QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) Special Mission (Rare!): investigate the Order of the Watchers' activity at the edge of the Core Zone. Someone reported the cult conducting some kind of ritual. Reward 15,000 ₽ + QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) Reputation +20 + Level (Lv.) 5 Intelligence Center unlocked.

Scene Sequence

Scene 1: Entry — Abandoned Church at the Edge of the Core Zone

The cult converted a church from before the Collapse into a holy site. Cult Patrol ×3 on the perimeter.

Scene 2: Church Interior

Gravity anomaly zone. In some areas gravity is halved (jump distance ×2), in others gravity is doubled (movement halved). - Cult Guardian ×2 + Cult High Priest ×1

Scene 3: Crypt

An ancient structure excavated by the cult — possibly some facility from before the Collapse. Time bubble zone. - Time bubble: upon entering, time flows abnormally. 1 turn inside the bubble = 5 turns outside. - Deep Diver (Core Zone mutant) ×2

Scene 4: Ritual Hall

The ritual the cult is performing. Cult Leader ×1 + Deep Diver ×4. If you can interrupt the ritual (destroy 3 energy crystals), you obtain the "Curtain Resonator" (Gold).

Scene 5: Extraction

  • Route A: Return the same way (church → edge → QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) checkpoint — but requires 20 minutes on foot)
  • Route B: QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) helicopter (flare gun + radio, requires unobstructed line of sight)
