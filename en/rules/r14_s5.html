Scenario S5: War of Succession

Suggested Era Range: Empire Age → Legendary Age
Suggested Level: High (Finale)
Estimated Turns: ~60 turns (15 years in-game)
Difficulty: ★★★★★
Core Themes: Internal rupture, legendary finale, the empire's fate
Player Count: 1 player + 1 GM

I. Scenario Overview

Synopsis

The founder of the empire has reached the end of his life. There is no clear law of succession — three heirs, three visions, three armies. When the old man closes his eyes, the empire splits from within.

You play one side in this storm — perhaps the designated heir, perhaps the underestimated one. Diplomacy, betrayal, and hero duels on the battlefield will all decide whether the empire steps into the Legendary Age, or shatters into scrap.

Scenario Objectives

  1. Survive and grow strong amid the succession crisis
  2. Handle the other heirs and the noble factions
  3. Decide the empire's final form
  4. Open or refuse the Legendary Age

II. Scenario Structure

Phase One: The King's Death (Turns 1–8)

Opening Narration (read aloud by the GM):

The old king's hand loosens its grip on the scepter. The royal physician bows his head; the hall falls silent. Then, the three heirs turn their eyes, all at once, to the chair that symbolizes the imperial throne.

Your strategist leans close to your ear: "Declare now, and you decide whether you are hunter or prey."

The eldest son grips his sword; the second daughter unfolds a draft of policy; in the youngest son's hand, a wisp of magic flows at his fingertips.

Key Events:

Turn Event Type
Turn 1 The old king's health worsens; undercurrents stir at court Narrative
Turn 3 Nobles declare — who supports which heir? (military / economic / magic factions) Internal Politics
Turn 5 Designate heir: eldest son (Military +, Morale −) / second daughter (Economy +, Military −) / youngest son (Technology +, Economy −) Major Choice
Turn 7 Unchosen heirs may break away — prevent defection (General GEN loyalty) Internal Crisis
Turn 8 The mourning period ends; the power vacuum appears Milestone

GM Notes: - The heir choice directly changes national attributes (see the choices effects in the scenario YAML) — let players feel the positioning. - Hero loyalty (especially the General) is critical in this phase; low loyalty may defect to another side.

Key Events:

GM Notes: - Hero duels are the spotlight moment, using hero Hit Points (HP) / Defense (DEF) and status effects (see GM Guide Ch. 24). - War drains the National Six Dimensions (Population / Resources / Military); avoid letting the player lose it all at once.

Turn 60 Finale — the empire's fate is decided here; the legendary chapter opens or closes Ending

III. Ending Conditions

Success Ending (United Legend)

  • A single heir wins and opens the Legendary Age
  • Reward: The empire endures, all attributes +15; unlock the legendary deed "Empire Founder"

Partial Success (New System)

  • Triarchy or abolition of the monarchy; the nation transforms
  • Reward: Morale (MOR) +20; unlock a unique policy

Failure Conditions

  • The civil war spirals out of control; the empire fully splits
  • Remedy: Choose one successor state to continue the game (retain 70% of attributes)

VI. GM Tips

  1. Special Nature of Civil War: The enemy is "one of our own"; war damage should be highlighted as national internal drain (Morale plummets).
  2. Hero Spotlight: S5 is the biggest stage for hero personal combat and magic — give more duel and casting scenes.
  3. Multiple Endings: All four endings (unification / triarchy / reform / split) are reasonable; let the player's choice shape the finale.
  4. Legendary Threshold: Opening the Legendary Age requires Technology (TEC) > 150 or a magic breakthrough — guide players to sprint for it.
