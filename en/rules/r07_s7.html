Imperial Mausoleum — The Tomb Lord Waited a Thousand Years (S7)

12-15 | Epic | 4-6 | Guanzhong (Imperial Mausoleum core)

Synopsis

The end of everything. All clues — the Mercury Maze control map, the Corpse-Nurturing Ground altar inscriptions, the Unrotted Bone's research notes — point to the same Imperial Mausoleum. The tomb lord is an ancient emperor who sought immortality. He succeeded but also failed — his body is undying, yet his soul is trapped in eternal loneliness. For a thousand years he has lain awake in his coffin, unable to die, unable to leave. He waits not for tomb-raiders — but for someone who can end his eternal loneliness. Two forces exist in the Imperial Mausoleum: the tomb lord (yearning to end) and the "Power of Immortality" (resisting ending). This is not a traditional boss fight — it is helping the tomb lord fight against himself.

Multiple Endings

Rest

  • Condition: Help the human side defeat the divine side

  • Result: The tomb lord finally dies. The Imperial Mausoleum collapses but knowledge is preserved. The players become legends.

Sealing

  • Condition: Do not destroy the Power of Immortality; seal it permanently

  • Result: The Imperial Mausoleum stays intact but is sealed forever. Gain the title of Guardian.

Inheritance

  • Condition: Accept the Power of Immortality (very high Will check DC 22)

  • Result: The player becomes a new Undying One — at the cost of eternal loneliness.

Destruction
