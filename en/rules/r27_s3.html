S3: Shadow of Tenka Fubu

Scenario Code: S3 | Campaign: Act Three (Levels 11–14) | Recommended Players: 1–4 (compatible with solo and group play) | Estimated Playtime: approx. 5–8 hours | Difficulty: Advanced | Prerequisite: S2

I. Background Setting

The "Tenka Fubu" campaign of Tora Arashi-zai, the Tiger Clan Daimyō, reaches its climax. Total war erupts.

The players have been granted command authority within their chosen faction (the Giri Alliance or the Tenka Fubu League). The core of this scenario is a pivotal Mass Battle — the Battle of the Wailing Plains.

Meanwhile, the trail of the famed blades points to "Onikiri-maru," one of the Five Swords Under Heaven — it lies in Arashi-zai's hands, and its yokai is eroding the wielder's Spirit (SPT).

III. Scene Flow

Scene 1: War Council — Pre-Battle Preparation

  • Type: Social / Strategy

The players take part in the faction's war council. They must make three pre-battle decisions:

  1. Choose your role in the Mass Battle: Commander type (directs troops) or Assault Captain type (charges directly into the enemy formation)
  2. Allocate limited resources — among Cavalry main force / Archer cover / Monk-soldier medical support, only one may be committed at full strength
  3. Whether to use spy intelligence (using it grants Advantage (Adv) in the Mass Battle, but may expose your position within the Rat Clan intelligence network)

Checks

Skill Difficulty (DC) Success Effect
Military Strategy 18 Gain enemy deployment intelligence; your entire army gains Advantage (Adv) in the first round of the Mass Battle
Persuasion 20 Persuade a neutral faction (such as the Jōdo Temple) to provide support

Scene 2: Battle of the Wailing Plains

  • Type: Mass Battle (large-scale combat)

The two sides join decisive battle on the Wailing Plains.

Mass Battle Scale - Our side: 3,000–5,000 troops (depending on the players' chosen faction) - Enemy: 4,000–6,000 troops - Mass Battle rounds: 5–8 rounds

Player Characters - If Commander type: use Command Value (CV) to direct troops - If Assault Captain type: personally cut into the enemy formation

Midway through the Mass Battle, the young enemy commander (your nemesis) leads an assault team charging into our main camp. His target is you — he demands a Single Combat (Duel).

Scene 3: Single Combat — A Fateful Duel

  • Type: Single Combat (Duel between commanders)

At the center of the Wailing Plains. Both armies cease fighting temporarily; all eyes turn to you.

The enemy commander declares:

"[Player's school name]'s [Player Name]! I am [Enemy Commander Name], heir of [Enemy Commander's School]. From the moment we first met, I knew — only one of us will become a legend. Today, here — let our blades decide whose Giri is true."

Enemy Commander Information - Name: Depends on the nemesis the players met in Act One (set by the GM) - Beast Clan: Has a symmetrical or countering relationship to the players - School: Different from the players' but of equal tier - Level: Same as the players - Blade: Wazamono or Yoki-wazamono

Single Combat Rules (duel_rules)

  • Rounds (rounds): 3
  • Special Rules (special):

    In the third round, the vengeful spirits of the Wailing Plains are roused by both sides' yokai, and the battlefield is enveloped in a yokai storm (both sides' Yokai Value (YK) increases by +10). Whoever lands the final blow within the storm — will be recorded in the war chronicles of the Sengoku era.

Three Choices — Post-Duel Outcome (post_duel)

Option Consequence
Slay Gain the nemesis's blade (Wazamono). Enemy Morale (MOR) −5. But Giri Value (GI) −5. One more blade waits upon the Wailing Plains for two hundred years.
Release Giri Value (GI) +5. The nemesis owes you a debt of Giri. Enemy Morale (MOR) −2. The nemesis says: "I will repay this life you spared — when repayment is no longer needed."
Recruit Make a Spirit (SPT) Contest Check (DC 18). On success, the nemesis becomes your companion NPC (CR 6). This is the noblest victory.

Scene 4: Shadow of Onikiri-maru

  • Type: Exploration / Revelation

After the Mass Battle ends, the players obtain unexpected intelligence — from the Rat Clan intelligence network. The intelligence reveals: the "Onikiri-maru" that Arashi-zai wields, one of the Five Swords Under Heaven, is not the holy sword it appears to be. It is a Cursed Blade, and its yokai is eroding Arashi-zai's Spirit (SPT). Behind Arashi-zai's "Tenka Fubu" — drives the will of Onikiri-maru.

Key Discoveries - Every samurai Onikiri-maru cuts down, it absorbs their Spirit (SPT) to empower itself - Arashi-zai is losing control over his own actions - The Black Moon Society is somehow connected to this process

The Players Now Face a Choice - Report the intelligence to your liege (may trigger an assassination plot against Arashi-zai) - Keep it secret and investigate on your own (act alone, but with limited resources) - Try to report the intelligence to Arashi-zai himself (extremely dangerous, but may save him)

Moral Dilemma: Truth vs. Strategy (moral_dilemma)

Option Check Consequence
Report to Liege — The liege plots to assassinate Arashi-zai. The players are asked to take part — but Arashi-zai is a victim, not a villain.
Investigate Alone — Gain Giri Value (GI) +3 (you chose to face the truth rather than exploit it). But you lack support.
Tell Arashi-zai Infiltrate Owari Province (Stealth DC 22) + Persuade Arashi-zai to believe you (Persuasion DC 25) If successful — Arashi-zai may voluntarily surrender Onikiri-maru, averting further slaughter. This is the only path that simultaneously saves Arashi-zai, ends the war, and obtains Onikiri-maru. But it is also the most difficult.

IV. Rewards & Aftermath

Rewards (reward)

  • Experience Points (XP): Mass Battle: 800 × your unit's performance coefficient. Single Combat: 300. Intelligence revealed: 200.
  • Gold: 1000
  • Items: The nemesis's blade (if Slay was chosen). Fragments of intelligence on Onikiri-maru.

Aftermath (follow_up)

  • The players' faction's victory or defeat in the Mass Battle shapes the faction landscape of Act Four
  • The intelligence on Onikiri-maru directly leads into the plot of Act Four
  • The nemesis's fate (Slay / Release / Recruit) has major repercussions
