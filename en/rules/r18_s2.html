Case File: The Seventh Drawer in the Mortuary

Suggested Level Range: Levels 4–6 Difficulty (Case Urgency): B-tier (Dangerous; standard 2–3 person combat, split operations) Case Type: Fresh Corpse / White Zombie + Resentful Ghost (husband-and-wife dual stack) Core Puzzle: A man who died from a medical dispute has turned into a White Zombie; his wife's Resentful Ghost lingers in the home seeking revenge over the loss of her husband — and behind the misdiagnosis, someone deliberately orchestrated it, burying a thread leading to Nine Nether Hall.

I. Opening (A Phone Call)

At 2 a.m., Uncle Nine from Shi Chu Shi funeral home calls, voice hushed.

Uncle Nine (head down, lighting another cigarette): "Young one, I've been in this line thirty years — I've seen plenty of bodies roll over, but one sitting up on its own... first time. The Fresh Corpse in Drawer Seven sat up in the middle of the night. And... I hear his wife's voice in the hallway, but his wife passed last month."

You: "What was the cause of death? Any family came to identify?" Uncle Nine (eyes wary): "Traffic accident — the kind where you can't sleep alone. His wife said he was on his phone all night before he died, like waiting for someone's message. Family? A young woman came, burst into tears at the sight of the body, fainted, signed the papers and left without even checking his trouser pockets." (GM Note: Hidden info — in the trouser pocket is an unsent phone / suicide note, key to communicating with the husband's resentment; requires a Social or Intuition (INS) check to notice Uncle Nine deliberately didn't mention it.)

Uncle Nine: "I don't care what sect you're from, but this is my turf. If you can handle it, handle it; if you can't — don't make it bigger. There's a funeral at dawn, I need it settled before first light."

Level Design Tip: Taking the case immediately enters time pressure. The whole scene must be resolved "before first light" (about four hours) or the funeral home covers up the failure, alarms the media, and cleanup difficulty spikes.

II. On-Site Investigation

Scene 1: Mortuary Corridor (Oppressive Yin)

The AC is too strong, the fluorescent lights a sickly white, drawers arranged like a honeycomb. The moment you enter, your skin crawls with goosebumps, and the air carries a smell of preservative mixed with rust.

  • Environment Yin Value (YIN): base 7 (range 5–10, stacked by multiple corpses), fluorescent lights flicker occasionally.
  • Witnesses: Uncle Nine, on-duty orderly "Sai Cheong," the deceased husband's daughter "Aki" (already left, reachable by phone).
  • Sai Cheong (cowering in a corner): "Drawer Seven... his hand reached out, nails all black. And there's a woman's voice singing a Cantonese nursery rhyme in the hallway, but there's no woman here — Aki's mom was cremated last month." (GM Note: The nursery rhyme is the Resentful Ghost's mark; an Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 locks onto the ghost's location.)

Scene 2: Drawer Seven (Spirit Identification)

  • Spirit Identification: An Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 shows the Fresh Corpse inside with dilated pupils and open mouth, already turned White Zombie (white fur over the body). A Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 identifies it as "Fresh Corpse → White Zombie (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 2)," Hard-Skin Mitigation −2, fears talismans and glutinous rice.
  • Simultaneously an Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 catches the Resentful Ghost in the hallway — identified as "Resentful Ghost (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 3)," cause of death the grief of losing her husband, locked hatred target is "the hospital / surgeon that killed him."
  • Hidden Info (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15, searching the deceased husband's trouser pocket): a mobile phone, the last message to his wife: "I found out the surgeon took money; they let me die on purpose. If anything happens to me, avenge me." — exposes that the "misdiagnosis" was deliberate, burying the Nine Nether Hall thread.

Combat / Non-Combat Options

  • Non-Combat (Correct Solution): Split up — Warrior / Assistant physically suppress the corpse mutation with Peachwood Sword + Corpse-Still Talisman; Medium uses Intuition (INS) to communicate and soothe the Resentful Ghost, then deliver the couple after learning the truth.
  • Combat (Second Choice): If suppression fails, the White Zombie erupts; must suppress head-on with talismans + spirit tools; if the Resentful Ghost won't talk, it trends toward Fierce Ghost, requiring capture or elimination.

Level Design Tips (Scene Order & Division of Labor): 1. Corridor perception (Intuition Intuition (INS) check) → confirm dual spirits. 2. Search Drawer Seven's trouser pocket (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain phone clue. 3. Split: Group A suppresses corpse mutation (Prowess Prowess (PHS) suppression check), Group B soothes Resentful Ghost (Intuition Intuition (INS) Communication check). 4. Reunite for deliverance, wrap up before first light.

III. Prepare Rite (Required Tools/Materials & Rite Scale)

  • Materials: Peachwood Sword (+2 extra vs spirits/zombies), Corpse-Still Talisman (placed on forehead to immobilize, costs Mana Pool (MP) 3), Glutinous Rice (scattered on floor for Burn, draws out corpse poison), Soul-Calming Talisman (soothes Resentful Ghost, costs Mana Pool (MP) 2), Incense, Candle & Hell Bank Notes (for deliverance).
  • Rite Scale: Medium. White Zombie needs physical suppression + Corpse-Still Talisman immobilization; Resentful Ghost needs Medium communication + Deliverance Rite (costs Mana Pool (MP) 10, 2 per round in segments).
  • Time Pressure: Must resolve before first light (about four hours); delay causes White Zombie corpse poison to spread, Resentful Ghost turns Fierce Ghost.
  • Level Tip: Altar setup uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13; if out of glutinous rice, request from Uncle Nine on the spot (funeral home stock, free).

IV. Exorcise & Collect

Best Solution: Split Handling (Suppress + Communicate)

  1. Suppress Corpse Mutation: Warrior closes in with Peachwood Sword, Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to grapple the Fresh Corpse; after a hit, place Corpse-Still Talisman to immobilize. Scatter glutinous rice on the floor to form a Burn zone of 3-meter radius, forcing the White Zombie to stay.
  2. Communicate & Soothe: Medium makes Intuition (INS) + Communication check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 (or first calm with Soul-Calming Talisman) to let the Resentful Ghost hear the husband's phone last words "I don't blame you, come keep me company" — resentment dissolves, turning into a deliverable state.
  3. Deliver the Couple: Set up altar and chant; the deceased husband (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 2, progress threshold 4) and the Resentful Ghost wife (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 3, progress threshold 6) delivered separately or on a shared altar.

Potential Risks

  • White Zombie Hard-Skin Mitigation −2: any attack reduced by 2; unarmed ineffective, must use spirit tools + talismans. If suppression fails, corpse mutation spreads to other drawers (Area-of-Effect (AOE) crisis), triggering chain corpse mutations of multiple Fresh Corpses.
  • Resentful Ghost turns Fierce Ghost: if communication fails and players attack, the Resentful Ghost (before negotiation willingness WILL 0) may intensify toward Fierce Ghost, requiring Soul-Gathering Gourd / Demon-Sealing Array / Five-Thunder Palm.
  • Time Pressure Failure: unresolved at first light → funeral home demands a low-key cover-up of failure, alarms media, cleanup difficulty spikes, reputation deducted.

Alternatives

  • If no Medium: use Summon Spirit Rite (Level 2+, costs Mana Pool (MP) 6) to force the deceased husband to appear for three questions, indirectly soothing the Resentful Ghost.
  • If White Zombie out of control: use Coin Sword (hit immobilizes 1 round) or Soul-Calming Bell (all spirits below mid-tier held still 3 rounds, Rare) to control the field.

V. Cleanup

  • Purification: After deliverance, a Purification Talisman (Yin Value Yin Value (YIN) −3) or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 pushes the mortuary's Yin Value (YIN) back below 5.
  • Client Feedback: Uncle Nine nods: "Drawer Seven's settled. For this one, I owe you a favor." (If completed before first light, add: "The funeral goes ahead at dawn, no one knows.")
  • Reward: Nether Coin (COIN) 200–400 (B-tier baseline); Experience Points (EXP) 200 (B-tier base) + investigation discoveries +20×2 + correct split handling +80, totaling about 300.
  • Possible Drop: Soul-Calming Bell (Rare, all spirits below mid-tier held still 3 rounds) — if handled well and Uncle Nine is grateful, gifted as a token.
  • Unresolved Mystery (Sequel Thread):
  • The deceased husband's phone last words point to "the surgeon took money, let me die on purpose" — behind the deliberate misdiagnosis someone orchestrated it; the thread points to a retired geomancer selling "life-extension folk remedies."
  • Uncle Nine whispers: "This kind of 'intentional death,' I've seen it elsewhere... across the harbor there's a place that specially collects these 'materials.'" (Referring to Nine Nether Hall.)

Ending Hook: As you leave, Uncle Nine slips you a note — an urgent cry for help from a Walled Village village chief in the New Territories: the entire ancestral house has been declared a Haunted House, with chain fatal deaths. The Luopan Compass reads an abnormal feng shui layout underground.
