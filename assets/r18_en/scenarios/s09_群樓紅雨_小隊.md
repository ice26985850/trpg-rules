# Case File: Red Rain over the Block · Squad

> **Suggested Level Range**: Levels 3–6
> **Difficulty (Case Urgency)**: Grade B/A (three adjacent Tong Lau erupt in Red Raincoat hauntings simultaneously; a solo collector can't cover all, squad coordination required)
> **Case Type**: Bound Spirit (mirror-reflection) / multi-player cooperation
> **Core Mystery**: Three buildings simultaneously show the same "Red Raincoat" figure repeatedly falling — this is not three ghosts, but one unfulfilled last wish "mirror-reflected" by similar feng shui into three linked images; if not handled in sync, the resentment will conduct and surge between the buildings.
> **Player Mode**: Multi-player scenario (Squad mode, 2–4 players + 1 GM)

---

## I. Opening (A Group Call)

The Guild group chat explodes late at night. Three adjacent Tong Lau in Sham Shui Po — Fu Hing Lau, Yi Shing Mansion, and Fu Lam Lau — report simultaneously: every night at 3 a.m., the same woman in a red raincoat appears in the stairwells of all three buildings, repeating "going downstairs to mail a letter." A solo Ghost Collector could only hold one building for three straight nights; the other two buildings' resentment surged, and tenants began showing premonitory signs of sleepwalking off the building.

Guild dispatch order: send 2–4 Ghost Collectors as a squad, split up to occupy the three buildings, and perform a synchronized deliverance at 3 a.m.

(GM Note: This scenario is designed for multiple players; if only 1 player, lower the overall Difficulty Class (DC) by 1–2 and enemy Challenge Rating (CR) by −1, but at least two people dividing labor is still recommended.)

---

## II. On-Site Investigation (Split by Building)

The three buildings share the layout of S1 "Tong Lau Stairwell" (six floors, no elevator; letters hidden in the mailbox slots). Upon arrival the squad divides labor:

- **Fu Hing Lau (Player A)**: Interview the property manager "Strong Kee," check the hidden letters in the mailbox slots.
- **Yi Shing Mansion (Player B)**: Interview the cleaning lady "Sister Ping," investigate the fall background.
- **Fu Lam Lau (Player C, may be vacant and covered by A/B)**: Check the old feng shui drain shared by the three buildings, confirm the "mirror-reflection" shares one source.

Each player's flow mirrors S1: Spirit Sight confirms the Bound Spirit (Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check Difficulty Class (DC) 12), check the mailbox slots (Appraisal / Intuition check Difficulty Class (DC) 14).

- **Key Discovery (requires full squad to assemble)**: Each building's mailbox slots hide a "Red Raincoat" suicide note, with a shared handwriting — but only the Fu Lam Lau letter bears the true name "Marites" and a complete address; the other two are mirror-reflected afterimages. The true letter paper also bears the "Shi Chu Shi" Funeral Home letterhead (leads to S2).
- **Environment Yin Value (YIN)**: Base 5 per building; if a building is left unguarded that night, its Yin Value (YIN) rises +2 per round and conducts +1 to neighboring buildings.

---

## III. Prepare Rite (Squad Scale)

Three small deliverance rites need coordination:

- **Materials**: Per building — Yellow Paper + Cinnabar, Incense & Candle (1 set), Hell Bank Notes (1 stack), a keepsake from her lifetime (the true letter) as a medium.
- **Rite Scale**: In squad mode, one player may "lead the altar" to chant scriptures while the others "guard" (maintain wards, watch for Ghost Wall, and use Soul-Calming Talismans to calm neighboring buildings' images when needed).
- **Sync Window**: The three Red Raincoats only appear clearly together at 3 a.m.; miss tonight and you wait another night, with resentment already accumulated.
- **Level Hint**: Leading the altar requires Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check Difficulty Class (DC) 12 to set the altar; guarding requires Composure (CMP) + Warding check Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to maintain the three buildings' wards in sync.

---

## IV. Exorcise & Collect (Synchronized Deliverance)

### Best Plan: Three-Building Synchronized Deliverance (Non-Combat, Correct Solution)

1. Three players take position and use Soul-Calming Talismans to calm the three buildings' Red Raincoats respectively (spirit static for 2 rounds).
2. The altar leader chants; all three buildings perform the deliverance rite simultaneously: each round Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check Difficulty Class (DC) = 12 + target Challenge Rating (CR) 3 (mirror-reflection boost), so Difficulty Class (DC) 15; completion threshold total progress ≥ 6 (CR×2).
3. Key: The three buildings' progress must reach the threshold "in the same round," otherwise the lagging building's image goes berserk, Yin Value (YIN) +3, requiring the guard to immediately remedy with a Purification Talisman (Area-of-Effect (AOE) Yin Value (YIN) −3).
4. Mail the letter on Marites' behalf at the "Shi Chu Shi" Funeral Home (or burn it for distant sending); the three images dissipate together.

### Potential Risks

- **Ghost Wall (optional)**: Any building's stairwell may trigger spatial distortion, trapping the guard. Composure (CMP) + Will check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to see through it, then Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Warding check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to break it.
- **If out of sync**: The lagging building's resentment surges and summons a Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 5), turning into combat — here the squad's advantage shows: Warrior up front, Caster at range, Talisman user supplements control, responding per the combat rules (see Player Rulebook Chapter 3).
- **If an Exorcism Talisman is misused to scatter a single building**: That building's image collapses but resentment remains, conducting Yin Value (YIN) +3 to the other two buildings, Guild reputation −1.

### Alternative Plan

- Communication failure: Summon Spirit Rite (Level 2+, consumes Mana Pool (MP) 6) forces three truthful answers, wringing out the true name before deliverance.
- Insufficient hands: Can switch to "rotating guard" — one player carries a Pathfinding Talisman to soothe building by building, but time drags on and Yin accumulation risk is high.

---

## V. Cleanup

- **Purification**: Three buildings synchronized Purification Talisman (Area-of-Effect (AOE) Yin Value (YIN) −3) or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check Difficulty Class (DC) 12, bringing the three buildings' Yin Value (YIN) back below 2.
- **Client Feedback**: The three buildings' property managers jointly thank you; Strong Kee: "This time with a few more people, it was way faster."
- **Reward**: Nether Coin (COIN) 300–400 (Grade B baseline); complete investigation + proper synchronized deliverance unlocks the squad reputation "Block-Cooperation." EXP approx. 600 (Grade B base 200 + investigation discoveries +100–200 + synchronized handling +200).
- **Unresolved Mysteries (sequel clues)**:
  1. The true letter's "Shi Chu Shi" Funeral Home letterhead — why do all three Tong Lau hauntings point to the same funeral home? (Leads to S2.)
  2. A bronze bell engraved "Shi Chu Shi" found in Fu Lam Lau's feng shui drain, seemingly deliberately buried — who is cultivating this resentment?

> **Closing Hook**: You tuck the three letters into a document envelope. A few days later, Uncle Nine of the Shi Chu Shi Funeral Home calls — the fresh corpse in Drawer No. 7 sat up on its own in the middle of the night, and... this time, more than one.
