# Case File: The Corpse-Cultivating Cellar beneath the Walled Village

> **Suggested Level Range**: Levels 7–10
> **Difficulty (Case Urgency)**: A/S-tier (Lethal to catastrophic; full party of 3–4 + outside help; regional threat)
> **Case Type**: Cultivated Zombie + Fierce Ghost + Haunted House (deliberately refined by Nine Nether Hall)
> **Core Puzzle**: "Seventh Uncle," head of Nine Nether Hall's Hong Kong station, has dug a corpse-cultivating cellar beneath the Walled Village ancestral house, refining Cultivated Zombies that obey him, and using the resentment of the village's deceased over the years to cultivate a Fierce Ghost as a "gatekeeper"; the Haunted House itself is a living nest. The key is not to fight, but to find and destroy Seventh Uncle — the Cultivated Zombies collapse once they lose their source of control.

---

## I. Opening (An Urgent Plea)

In the New Territories Walled Village, the entire ancestral house was declared a "Haunted House," with two villagers dying in a chain within three days. The village chief sent an urgent plea through the Guild's Three-Lineage Council, personally referred by Council Chair Cheng Guiyao.

**Village Chief (on the call, voice trembling)**: "Ghost Collector... our Walled Village ancestral house — whoever goes in doesn't come out. The Luopan Compass hasn't stopped spinning since it went in, pointing underground. The elders say... something underground is being 'cultivated.' Come quickly, or the whole village is finished."

**You**: "Anyone outside see anything?"
**Village Chief**: "There's a retired geomancer, 'Seventh Uncle,' always wandering around the house, saying he's helping us 'read the feng shui.' But his eyes... aren't human eyes." (GM Note: Seventh Uncle is the head of Nine Nether Hall's Hong Kong station — seemingly helping, but actually the one who set it up.)

**Level Design Tip**: A/S-tier requires team operation. Taking the case immediately sets an extremely high budget cap (S-tier coefficient ×2.5), but division of labor is still needed, or the Haunted House's death-domain assimilation will pick off players one by one.

---

## II. On-Site Investigation

### Scene 1: Walled Village Periphery and Ancestral House (Haunted House Appears)
A deserted ancestral house, surrounded by wild graves and a feng shui woods. As you approach, the air feels waterlogged, sound distorts, and the Luopan Compass needle spins wildly pointing underground.

- **Environment Yin Value (YIN)**: Haunted House whole domain 10 (death-domain level), the underground corpse-cultivating cellar can reach 12–16.
- **Witnesses**: the village chief, surviving villager woman "Second Aunt," and the seemingly "helpful" Seventh Uncle.
  - **Second Aunt** (grabbing your sleeve): "The ones who went in... when they came out, their faces were green, couldn't speak a word, and the next morning they stopped breathing. The sound inside the house is like many people crying at once." (GM Note: proof that resentment cultivates a Fierce Ghost; an Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 lets you hear the muffled groans underground.)
  - **Seventh Uncle** (forced smile): "Young one, feng shui is complicated, you can't handle it. Better leave it to me, I'll 'deal with it' for you." (GM Note: A Social (Persuasion) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16 can see through his stalling — the delay is to let the Cultivated Zombies take form.)

### Scene 2: The Corpse-Cultivating Cellar under the Ancestral House (Spirit Identification & Hidden Info)
- **Spirit Identification**: An Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16 shows two Cultivated Zombies (White Zombie level, Challenge Rating (CR) 3) standing still in the underground cellar, obeying an invisible command; a Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 5) coils on the roof beam, wrapped in protective black qi. A Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16 identifies the "Cultivated Zombie" trait — only by destroying the corpse-refiner do they lose control.
- **Hidden Info** (Scholar Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check at Difficulty Class (DC) 17, to break the feng shui layout and find the acupoint eye): The Luopan's abnormal movement originates from an underground "acupoint eye" — a yin-energy vent sealed under the village dead's Spirit Tablets, the very core of the corpse-cultivating cellar. Also found at the cellar bottom: carved Spirit Tablets and a torn page of the "Life-Borrowing Register" (see Unresolved Mystery).

### Combat / Non-Combat Options
- **Combat (Main Axis)**: The Haunted House and Fierce Ghost cannot be negotiated with (Fierce Ghost negotiation willingness WILL 0), must be captured/eliminated; Cultivated Zombies must first sever the source of control.
- **Non-Combat (Key to Breaking the Situation)**: Scholar breaks the feng shui layout and finds the acupoint eye; Main Caster opens altar + Demon-Sealing Array to imprison the Fierce Ghost; Warrior suppresses Cultivated Zombies; the key is to find and destroy Seventh Uncle.

**Level Design Tips (Scene Order & Team Division)**:
1. Peripheral Luopan compass direction (Knowledge Knowledge (KNW) check) → lock underground anomaly.
2. Haunted House encounter inside ancestral house (Composure Composure (CMP) check each round, death-domain assimilation risk).
3. Split groups: Scholar finds acupoint eye (Knowledge Knowledge (KNW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 17) / Warrior suppresses Cultivated Zombies (Prowess Prowess (PHS) suppression) / Medium + Main Caster Demon-Sealing Array to trap Fierce Ghost (Dao Attainment Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to maintain).
4. Track Seventh Uncle (Pathfinding Talisman or Luopan) → final battle to destroy the source of control.

## III. Prepare Rite (Required Tools/Materials & Rite Scale)

- **Materials**: Century Peachwood Sword (×2 damage vs spirits, required against Black Zombie and above), Thunder-Struck Wood (ignores Hard-Skin Mitigation vs zombies, strongest tool), Coin Sword (immobilize, charge-up), Demon-Sealing Array (coin-sword array + talisman, costs Mana Pool (MP) 12 in segments), Deliverance Rite materials, Ink Line Reel (warding), Purification Talisman (Yin Value Yin Value (YIN) −3).
- **Rite Scale**: Large. Requires team division — Scholar breaks the situation, Warrior protects the rite, Main Caster opens altar + Demon-Sealing Array (costs Mana Pool (MP) 2 per round to maintain, Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15), Medium maintains spirit sight.
- **Time Pressure**: The Haunted House death-domain requires an assimilation check each round (two consecutive failures → assimilated by yin energy), must fight quickly; if Seventh Uncle escapes, the Cultivated Zombies lose control and spread to the whole village (S-tier disaster).
- **Level Tip**: Altar setup uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16; Demon-Sealing Array requires Level 4+.

## IV. Exorcise & Collect

### Best Solution: Team Division, Sever the Source of Control
1. **Find the Acupoint Eye**: Scholar makes Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check at Difficulty Class (DC) 17 to break the feng shui layout, locating the corpse-cultivating cellar's acupoint eye and Seventh Uncle's position.
2. **Suppress Cultivated Zombies**: Warrior closes in with Century Peachwood Sword / Coin Sword (immobilize), Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to suppress the two Cultivated Zombies (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 3).
3. **Seal the Fierce Ghost**: Main Caster + Medium set the Demon-Sealing Array to trap the Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating Challenge Rating (CR) 5), Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 each round to maintain; or use Five-Thunder Palm (Level 3+, 3d6 ignores Hard-Skin, breaks the Fierce Ghost's protection) to break protection then capture with Soul-Gathering Gourd.
4. **Key: Destroy Seventh Uncle**: Track down and crush the corpse-refiner. Cultivated Zombies lose their source of control → mitigation fails, actions chaotic, collapse; the Haunted House core (acupoint eye) broken → whole-domain Yin Value (YIN) drops sharply.
5. **Whole-House Great Purification**: Team works together with Purification Talisman + Deliverance Rite, sending off the Fierce Ghost and the village dead's resentment.

> **GM Tip (Boss Non-Combat is the Correct Solution)**: Cultivated Zombies (Hard-Skin (Mitigation) by base tier, Black Zombie and above can reach −3 to −5) are not solved by brute force with blades — the true key of this case is to destroy the source of control "Seventh Uncle"; the Cultivated Zombies collapse once they lose control (see Best Solution step 4). The correct solution is first to use glutinous rice, Ink Line Warding, or Corpse-Still Talisman to control the field, then open altar for deliverance (Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check) or seal (Soul-Gathering Gourd / Corpse-Still Talisman); only the Hard-Skin (Mitigation)-ignoring Thunder-Struck Wood and Five-Thunder Palm are the few tools that can break through by force. Don't fight the corpse with muscle — fight the mastermind with brains. See GM Book "Hard-Skin Boss Handling Principles."

### Potential Risks
- **Fierce Ghost Cannot Be Negotiated**: Negotiation willingness (WILL) = 0, can only be captured/eliminated; if you underestimated and force-attack, prioritize attacking the target with lowest Health Points (HP) / most isolated.
- **Haunted House Death-Domain (YIN 16+)**: Pass a Composure (CMP) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 each round; two consecutive failures → assimilated by yin energy.
- **Seventh Uncle Escapes**: If not destroyed in the final battle, the Cultivated Zombies lose control and spread to the whole village, escalating to S-tier disaster, cleanup difficulty maxed.

### Alternatives
- If no century-tier tools: break the Cultivated Zombie Hard-Skin with Five-Thunder Palm / Celestial Master Talisman (Rare, Exorcism Talisman effect +2).
- If Demon-Sealing Array maintenance fails: use Invite Deity Possession (Level 3+, attribute +2) to forcibly suppress the Fierce Ghost, but requires a Composure (CMP) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 at the end to avoid losing control.

## V. Cleanup

- **Purification**: Whole-house great purification uses Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16, Purification Talisman (Yin Value Yin Value (YIN) −3) × several sheets, pushing the ancestral house's Yin Value (YIN) below 5, lifting the Haunted House state.
- **Client Feedback**: The village chief kneels: "The village is saved... thank you. I never want to speak of these things again." The Guild records a major reputation for "Defeating Nine Nether Hall."
- **Reward**: Nether Coin (COIN) 800+ (S-tier baseline, plus intel and tool conversion); Experience Points (EXP) 350 (A-tier) to 600 (S-tier), depending on whether Seventh Uncle was destroyed and the disaster avoided, totaling about 600.
- **Drops**: Thunder-Struck Wood (Legendary, ignores Hard-Skin vs zombies) / Celestial Master Talisman (Rare, finished Exorcism effect +2) — choose one, Guild rewards by merit.
- **Unresolved Mystery (Sequel Threads, four)**:
  1. **Master**: Before Seventh Uncle escaped (or in his belongings) he left a line "The Master awaits the great work's completion" — who is the "Master" behind Nine Nether Hall?
  2. **Spirit Tablet**: At the bottom of the corpse-cultivating cellar was found a Spirit Tablet carved with the name related to some character of the players "the case you couldn't handle" (GM fills in per character-sheet foreshadowing).
  3. **Life-Borrowing Register**: A torn page marked "Life-Borrowing Register," listing a few celebrities who should be dead but are still alive — the forbidden art "Life-Borrowing" was never rooted out.
  4. **Yin Constable Accountability**: The Yin Constable "Ox-Head Bing" comes to hold account — the souls you delivered were on the Underworld register; who is tampering with the Book of Life and Death?

> **Ending Hook**: You walk out of the ancestral house as dawn approaches. Bing stands on the field ridge, flicking the torn page of the Book of Life and Death in his hand: "This case isn't over. The names on the Life-Borrowing Register will pay, sooner or later."
