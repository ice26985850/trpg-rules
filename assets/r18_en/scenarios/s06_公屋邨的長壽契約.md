# Case File: The Longevity Contract of the Public Estate

> **Suggested Level Range**: Levels 7–8
> **Difficulty (Case Urgency)**: Grade A (Lethal; full party of 3–4)
> **Case Type**: Fierce Ghost + Resentful Ghost swarm + Living Haunted House (Nine Nether Hall "Life-Borrowing" rite)
> **Case-Closing EXP**: 700
> **Core Mystery**: Recovers S3 foreshadowing ④ (Bing's accountability) and ③ (Life-Borrowing register). Yin Constable Ox-Head Bing comes knocking — an entire floor of residents in a public housing estate has had their "Yang Lifespan carved away" yet remains registered as alive. The truth: Nine Nether Hall lured wealthy tenants with a "Longevity Contract" to borrow lifespan, siphoning the Yang Lifespan of same-floor tenants; the drained become Resentful Ghosts, and one resister became a Fierce Ghost gatekeeper.

**One-Line Hook**: Bing kicks the door in, waving a torn page of the Register of Life and Death: "You exorcised those souls — they were on my register. The people on this floor had their Yang Lifespan carved away, but they're still alive. The names on the Life-Borrowing register will have to pay it back, sooner or later."

---

## I. Take Case

Yin Constable Ox-Head Bing comes to the door, presents a torn page of the Register of Life and Death, and names a public housing estate in Kwun Tong for investigation. Coordinated by the Guild Council.

**Bing** (kicks the door open, slaps the torn register page onto the table): "You exorcised those few souls before — they were on my register, their time was up. But the register says their Yang Lifespan was carved away, yet their household registration shows them still alive. In this public housing estate, something's deceiving even the dead — and it landed on my head."

**You**: "Yang Lifespan carved away yet still alive? That's Life-Borrowing."
**Bing**: "Exactly. I won't make trouble for you, but you help me investigate. Report only after you've investigated — don't go exorcising the ones that shouldn't be exorcised again." (GM Note: The agreement must be "investigate first, report later," or Bing holds you accountable and deducts reputation.)

**Level-Design Hint**: Roll Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check, Difficulty Class (DC) 15, to read the "Yang Lifespan transfer" mark on the torn register page; Social (Persuasion) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to reach an "investigate first, report later" agreement with Bing.

---

## II. On-Site Investigation

### Scene 1: Estate Corridor and the Wealthy Household's Door (Oppressive Yin)
The long corridor of the public estate glows with sickly white fluorescent tubes, water beads seeping from the walls. The moment you step onto that floor, the air feels soaked in ice; same-floor tenants have ashen, greenish faces yet come and go as usual — the eeriness of a living haunted house lies in "people still thinking they're alive."
- **Environment Yin Value (YIN)**: Base 9 (Cold → edge of Extreme Yin).
- **Witness**: Same-floor tenant "Sister Bing."
  - **Sister Bing** (lowering her voice): "The Longevity Contract? Yeah, Brother Fat's door is always open; people who go in come out white-faced. He says signing the contract means a long, long life — but why do my kids always cry that 'there's a smell in the house'?" (GM Note: Social (Interview) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to draw out the "Longevity Contract" rumor.)
- **Spirit Identification**: Roll Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to see Resentful Ghosts (Challenge Rating (CR) 4) drifting in the corridor and the gatekeeping Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 5, warded black aura); Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to identify the "Fierce Ghost" as unnegotiable — must be captured/destroyed.

### Scene 2: The Wealthy Household's "Longevity Contract Altar" (Hidden Information)
- **Appraisal (Knowledge (KNW)) check Difficulty Class (DC) 16** searches the residence and finds the contract altar uses the birth data (eight characters) of seven same-floor households as a conduit to siphon Yang Lifespan into Brother Fat; beneath the altar lies a torn page of the "Life-Borrowing Register" (including Brother Fat and others).
- **Hidden Information**: The last page of the register reads "Life-Restoring Rite · Opposite-Shore Main Altar" — leading to S7/S8. Requires Intuition (INS) check Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to notice the layered ink.

**Combat / Non-Combat Options**
- **Non-Combat (Key to Breaking the Case)**: The Resentful Ghost swarm can be delivered; the Fierce Ghost must be captured/destroyed; the key is to destroy the contract altar and liberate the siphoned Yang Lifespan.
- **Combat (Main Axis)**: If attacking by force, the Fierce Ghost prioritizes locking onto the lowest Health Points (HP) target.

**Level-Design Hint (Scene Order)**:
1. Peripheral interview (Sister Bing) → obtain the contract rumor.
2. Spirit Sight confirms Resentful Ghost swarm and gatekeeping Fierce Ghost (Intuition (INS) check).
3. Search the contract altar (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain torn Life-Borrowing Register page.
4. Read the last page of the register (Intuition (INS) check) → leads to S7/S8.

## III. Prepare Rite (Required Spirit Tools / Materials / Rite Scale)

- **Materials**: Century Peachwood Sword (×2 damage vs spirits; required for Fierce Ghost), Coin Sword (Paralysis), Demon-Sealing Array (consumes Mana Pool (MP) 12 in segments), Soul-Gathering Gourd (capture Fierce Ghost), Purification Talisman (Yin Value (YIN) −3), Deliverance materials.
- **Rite Scale**: Large. Scholar breaks the contract altar, Warrior guards, Main Caster + Medium set the Demon-Sealing Array to trap the Fierce Ghost (consumes Mana Pool (MP) 2 per round to maintain; Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15).
- **Time Pressure**: Brother Fat "collects lifespan" every night at Zi Hour; miss it and another household is drained, raising Extreme Yin assimilation risk.
- **Level Hint**: Setting the altar requires Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check Difficulty Class (DC) 15; the Demon-Sealing Array requires Level 4+.

---

## IV. Exorcise & Collect

### Best Plan: Break the Altar and Liberate
1. Scholar rolls Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge check Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to break the altar's nodal eye.
2. Warrior uses the Century Peachwood Sword to suppress the Resentful Ghost swarm (roll Prowess (PHS) + Melee Combat check Difficulty Class (DC) 15).
3. Main Caster + Medium deploy the Demon-Sealing Array to trap the Fierce Ghost (Challenge Rating (CR) 5), maintaining with a Dao Attainment (DOW) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15 each round; or use Five-Thunder Palm (Level 3+, 3d6 ignores hard-skin, breaks warding) then capture with the Soul-Gathering Gourd.
4. Deliver the Resentful Ghost swarm; Brother Fat suffers Yang Lifespan backlash (non-combat ending — Brother Fat falls into a coma awaiting arrest).

### Potential Risks
- **Fierce Ghost is unnegotiable**: Underestimating and forcing an attack will prioritize locking onto the lowest Health Points (HP) target.
- **Extreme Yin Environment**: Each round pass a Composure (CMP) check Difficulty Class (DC) 15; two consecutive failures → Yin Assimilation.

### Alternative Plan
- Without the century-grade tool, break through with the Celestial Master Talisman (Rare; exorcism effect +2).

## V. Cleanup

- **Purification**: Full-floor purification requires Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check Difficulty Class (DC) 16, several Purification Talismans (Yin Value (YIN) −3), bringing Yin Value (YIN) back below 6 and lifting the living haunted house.
- **Client Feedback**: Bing takes away the torn register page; the Guild records a major "Assisted the Yin Constable" reputation gain.
- **Case-Closing Reward**: Nether Coin (COIN) 700–1000; EXP = base 350 (Grade A) + investigation discoveries +20×5 + correct altar-breaking handling +100 + cleanup +80 + unresolved hook (Life-Restoring Rite) +40 + stopping the lifespan-siphon rite +30 = **total approx. 700**.
- **Unresolved Mysteries (leads to S7)**:
  1. Register's last page "Life-Restoring Rite · Opposite-Shore Main Altar";
  2. Brother Fat names his superior "the Master's Agent."

> **Closing Hook**: Bing folds the torn page: "Something's opening a path on the opposite shore. The cross-harbour tunnel — lately it hasn't been peaceful."
