# Case File: The Red Raincoat in the Stairwell

> **Suggested Level Range**: Levels 1–3
> **Difficulty (Case Urgency)**: D/C (Harassment at an old Tong Lau in Sham Shui Po; can be handled solo, but escalates to a C-tier threat if mishandled)
> **Case Type**: Bound Spirit
> **Core Puzzle**: The last wish of a Filipina domestic helper who fell to her death — "deliver a letter to Mama" — was never fulfilled. Every night she repeats the act of going downstairs to mail the letter in the stairwell... but the letter is stuck at the bottom of the mailbox.

---

## I. Opening (A Phone Call)

At half past midnight, your phone buzzes. The screen lights up: a call from "Keung Kee," a Sham Shui Po property manager whose name appears on the Guild's referral list.

**Keung Kee** (TV noise and distant barking in the background): "Ghost Collector? Are you a Ghost Collector? I manage that old Tong Lau on Tai Po Road — can't rent the units out lately. Every night around three, there's a woman in a red raincoat in the stairwell, walking down then back up, down then back up... The cleaner saw her three times and quit. Do me a favor, keep it low-key, just sort it out quietly."

**You**: "Anyone gotten hurt? Who lived on that floor before?"
**Keung Kee** (a pause): "Hurt? ...No. It's an old building, just haunted. Come take a look first, see how much it'll cost. Keep things clean and I'll send more jobs your way." (GM Note: Keung Kee deliberately conceals a fatal fall three years ago; must be exposed through an On-Site Investigation Social (Interview) or Intuition (INS) check.)

**Level Design Tip**: In the Take Case phase, a Social (Persuasion) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 can settle the fee; success yields an extra 20% Nether Coin (COIN), failure means Keung Kee lowballs the price.

---

## II. On-Site Investigation

### Scene 1: Outside the Tong Lau and the Ground-Floor Lobby
The old six-story Tong Lau has no elevator; the iron gate is rusted, and the stairwell is plastered with yellowing talisman paper. The moment you step in, the hairs on your neck stand up, your phone signal drops two bars, water seeps from the wall corners, and you smell something like long-stale incense ash — something is wrong here.

- **Environment Yin Value (YIN)**: base 5 (range 3–8; a fatal fall occurred here, on the high side).
- **Witnesses**: the cleaner "Sister Ping" (already quit, reachable by phone), and same-floor resident "Uncle Chan."
  - **Uncle Chan** (opens the door a crack): "Red raincoat? Yeah, paces the stairs all night. I said she looks like that Filipino woman from before, but she's long since... ah, never mind, no use talking about it." (GM Note: Uncle Chan knows of the old fall; a Social (Interview) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 can draw out that three years ago a Filipina helper named Marites was wrongly accused of theft, fired, and fell to her death on a rainy night.)
- **Spirit Identification**: an Intuition (INS) + Spirit Perception check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 lets you see a wisp of red drifting at the stair landing, feet not touching the ground. A Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 identifies it as a "Bound Spirit" — fixed to the place of death, repeating its pre-death behavior, not actively hostile.

### Scene 2: The Third-Floor Mailbox and Stairwell (Hidden Information)
The red-raincoat woman repeats "go downstairs to mail the letter" every night. You check the mailbox of the floor she lived on — an Appraisal (KNW) or Intuition (INS) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 14 reveals an unsent letter wedged at the bottom of the mailbox, addressed "To Mama, Cebu, Philippines"; the envelope is soaked through with rain but the handwriting is legible.
- **Hidden Information** (requires a check to reveal): The letterhead on the inner page bears the name of a funeral home — "Shi Chu Shi." This is a thread leading to S2; the GM should quietly hint to players reading the letter, "You'll hear that name again."
- The letter reads: "Mama, I didn't steal anything; the madam framed me. I can't come home, but I want you to know I did nothing wrong. I'm sorry, Mama."

**Level Design Tips (Scene Order)**:
1. Peripheral interviews (Uncle Chan / Sister Ping) → obtain the fall background.
2. Spirit sight confirms the Bound Spirit (Intuition Intuition (INS) check).
3. Search the mailbox (Appraisal Knowledge (KNW) check) → obtain the suicide note and the "Shi Chu Shi" letterhead.
4. At Zi Shi (1 a.m.) the Red Raincoat appears; proceed to the Exorcise & Collect phase.

### Combat / Non-Combat Options
- **Non-Combat (Correct Solution)**: Communicate with the Bound Spirit and perform a Deliverance Rite to send her to the afterlife.
- **Combat (Wrong Option)**: If you disperse her directly with an Exorcism Talisman, the Bound Spirit scatters but her resentment lingers; the Tong Lau's Yin Value (YIN) rises by 2, Keung Kee files a complaint afterward, and the Guild deducts reputation.

---

## III. Prepare Rite (Required Tools/Materials & Rite Scale)

The non-combat deliverance requires a simple altar:
- **Materials**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar (to draw a Soul-Calming Talisman), Incense & Candle (1 set), Hell Bank Notes (1 stack), and a personal belonging from life (that letter) as a medium.
- **Rite Scale**: Small — 1 round to set up the altar + multiple rounds of deliverance ritual. Altar Opening requires a Censer + Incense Stick; without them, the Altar Opening (Dao Attainment Dao Attainment (DOW)) check is at −3.
- **Time Pressure**: The Bound Spirit only appears clearly at Zi Shi (1–3 a.m.); miss tonight and you must wait another night.
- **Level Tip**: An Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 sets up the altar; the talisman costs Mana Pool (MP) (Soul-Calming Talisman 2 points). If materials are insufficient, immediately make a Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 to buy extra glutinous rice and incense from a store in the same building.

---

## IV. Exorcise & Collect

### Best Solution: Deliverance Rite (Non-Combat)
1. Use the Soul-Calming Talisman to calm the Red Raincoat (the spirit is held still for 2 rounds, can communicate).
2. An Intuition (INS) + Communication or Knowledge (KNW) + Folklore check at Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to hear the last wish clearly: "Help me mail a letter to Mama, tell her I didn't steal anything."
3. Set up the altar and chant scriptures, performing the deliverance ritual: each round an Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check at Difficulty Class (DC) = 12 + target Challenge Rating (CR) = 1, thus Difficulty Class (DC) 13; completion threshold total progress ≥ 2 (CR×2). Success means the Bound Spirit departs in peace.
4. Mail the letter on her behalf via the "Shi Chu Shi" funeral home (or burn it as a distant offering), fulfilling the last wish.

### Potential Risks
- **Ghost Wall**: The stairwell is vertically narrow; when the Red Raincoat is agitated she may trigger spatial distortion. An Composure (CMP) + Will check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 sees through it; failure means wandering in circles, Movement (MOVE) invalid, Yin Value (YIN) +1. After seeing through it, make another Knowledge (KNW) + Lineage Knowledge or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Warding check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15, and apply a Pathfinding Talisman / draw an Ink Line to break the trap.
- If the Main Caster leaves the altar more than 3 meters during the deliverance ritual, progress resets to zero.
- If players mistakenly disperse her with an Exorcism Talisman: resentment lingers, the Tong Lau turns permanently cold and yin (Yin Value Yin Value (YIN) rises), Keung Kee refuses final payment, Guild reputation −1.

### Alternatives
- If communication fails: use Summon Spirit Rite (Level 2+, costs Mana Pool (MP) 6) to force three truthful answers, extract the last wish, then deliver.
- If no altar can be set up: use Hell Bank Notes to soothe + erect a temporary Spirit Tablet, temporarily pacifying her, but this is not a formal resolution and she may resurface later (GM may keep as a side quest).

---

## V. Cleanup

- **Purification**: After deliverance, a Purification Talisman (AOE radius 3 meters, Yin Value (YIN) −3) or Dao Attainment (DOW) + Purification check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 pushes the stairwell's Yin Value (YIN) back below 2.
- **Client Feedback**: After acceptance, Keung Kee breathes a sigh of relief: "As long as it's done... I'll come to you for future jobs." (If she was dispersed with residue, he complains, "There's still a smell.")
- **Reward**: Nether Coin (COIN) 50–100 (D-tier baseline); if investigation is complete + deliverance done well, additionally grant an investigation bonus and unlock the "First Deliverance" reputation. Experience Points (EXP) 50 (D-tier base) + 10–20 per investigation discovery + 50 for correct handling, totaling about 100.
- **Unresolved Mystery (Sequel Thread)**:
  1. The funeral-home letterhead "Shi Chu Shi" on that letter — why would a fallen Filipina helper's suicide note be written on a funeral home's stationery?
  2. Uncle Chan hesitates: "That woman worker from before... didn't fall on her own." (Whether there's more to it awaits S2.)

> **Ending Hook**: You pocket the handwritten letter. A few days later, Uncle Nine from Shi Chu Shi funeral home calls — the Fresh Corpse in Drawer No. 7 sat up on its own in the middle of the night.
