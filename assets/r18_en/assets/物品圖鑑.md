# Item Codex (Ghost Collector TRPG)

> This codex compiles the spirit tools, talismans, materials, and miscellaneous items commonly used by Ghost Collectors, for purchasing, carrying, and reference.
> Values follow Chapter 5 of the Character System Draft (character-builder equipment economy) and the compilation memo (_compile_brief).
> Hard rule on format: All game terms use the "English Full Name (ABBR)" format; the full name is used every time the term appears in body text.

---

## Currency and Rarity Notes

- **Currency**: The in-game currency is **Nether Coin (COIN)** — the underground tender of the Ghost Collector circle, used to settle everything from Hell Bank Note exchanges to sect accounting. Hong Kong dollars serve only as background narrative reference and are not part of the game mechanics.
- **Rarity Tiers**: Common / Fine / Rare / Epic / Legendary.
- **Carrying Capacity**: Carrying capacity limit = Prowess (PHS) × 5 kg; exceeding it imposes −1 on Prowess (PHS)-related checks. Materials carried under the Assistant / Apprentice "Material Management" trait do not count toward carrying capacity.
- **Talisman Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: This codex adopts the authoritative values from Section 2 of the compilation memo (_compile_brief) and Section 6 of the combat design. Talismans consume Mana Pool (MP) and materials "at the moment of drawing"; wearing / using them does not consume further.

> Note: The Mana Pool (MP) column for talismans in the equipment table of Chapter 5 of the Character System Draft differs slightly from the compilation memo. This codex uniformly adopts the final values from the memo / combat design: Exorcism Talisman 2, Corpse-Still Talisman 3, Warding Talisman 2, Eye-Opening Talisman 3, Pathfinding Talisman 1, Purification Talisman 2, Soul-Calming Talisman 2, Door-Sealing Talisman 2.

# Chapter 1　Basic Spirit Tools

> Commonly found on the market and taught by sects, affordable in price — the starter gear of every Ghost Collector.

## ① Peachwood Sword（Peachwood Sword）

- **Type**: Melee spirit tool
- **Attribute**: Dao Attainment (DOW)
- **Damage / Effect**: 1d6 + Prowess (PHS) modifier; +2 additional against spirits / zombies (spirit tool trait)
- **Weight**: 1.5 kg
- **Price**: 30 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: Carved from evil-warding peachwood, it is the standard spirit tool effective against both spirits and zombies. Nearly every Hong Kong Ghost Collector carries one, and it is essential for temple rites.

## ② Coin Sword（Coin Sword）

- **Type**: Melee spirit tool
- **Attribute**: Dao Attainment (DOW)
- **Damage / Effect**: 1d6 + Prowess (PHS) modifier; inflicts "Paralysis" charge on zombies (Paralysis 1 round after a hit)
- **Weight**: 2.0 kg
- **Price**: 50 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: Copper coins of past dynasties strung and bound into a sword; its metal energy counters yin. Especially good against moving zombies — a strike pins them in place, making it easy to follow up with talismans.

## ③ Bagua Mirror（Bagua Mirror）

- **Type**: Worn spirit tool
- **Damage / Effect**: Reflects evil energy; +1 on Spirit Perception / detection checks
- **Weight**: 0.5 kg
- **Price**: 40 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: A convex Bagua mirror hung at the chest or held in hand can reflect one supernatural attack (retaliation 1d6 Burn). It can also reveal the outline of invisible spirits.

## ④ Three Pure Bell（Three Pure Bell）

- **Type**: Implement spirit tool
- **Damage / Effect**: Stuns low-tier spirits (Fierce Ghosts and below: −2 attack for 1 round); −2 on Intuition (INS) Fear checks Difficulty Class (DC)
- **Weight**: 0.3 kg
- **Price**: 35 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: Its clear ringing soothes souls and suppresses evil. Most effective against low-tier beings like Bound Spirits and Wandering Souls, keeping them from approaching for a time.

## ⑤ Ink Line Reel（Ink Line Reel）

- **Type**: Implement spirit tool
- **Damage / Effect**: Snaps an ink line into a 10-meter ward; spirits / zombies may not cross (Flying Corpses can fly over, but are still trapped upon landing)
- **Weight**: 0.5 kg
- **Price**: 20 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: The master's ink-line-snapping skill. Snap a black line along the ground boundary and it becomes an invisible wall. Breaking Ghost Walls and guarding doors and altars all rely on it.

## ⑥ Luopan Compass（Luopan Compass）

- **Type**: Implement spirit tool
- **Damage / Effect**: Detects environmental yin-yang energy fields; +2 on Yin Perception (Intuition (INS)) checks
- **Weight**: 0.8 kg
- **Price**: 60 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Fine
- **Description**: A modified feng shui compass whose needle trembles where yin energy is heavy. Only a Fine-grade one reads accurately, making it the detection core of haunted-house investigation.

## ⑦ Talisman Kit（Talisman Kit）

- **Type**: Talisman-drawing three-piece set
- **Damage / Effect**: Essential for drawing talismans (cannot draw without any one piece)
- **Weight**: 0.5 kg (all three combined)
- **Price**: 25 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: Yellow paper as the base, cinnabar as the ink, wolf-hair brush for the strokes. Without this set, no matter how high your Dao Attainment, you cannot draw a single talisman. Consumable yellow paper / cinnabar are listed separately in the Materials chapter.

## ⑧ Censer & Incense（Censer & Incense）

- **Type**: Altar-opening essential
- **Damage / Effect**: The "power source" of Altar Opening; without censer and incense, Altar Opening (Dao Attainment (DOW)) checks suffer −3
- **Weight**: 1.0 kg
- **Price**: 30 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: A burner of incense is the primer of the altar — if the incense does not burn, the altar does not open. Deliverance rites and Invite Deity Possession are impossible without it.

# Chapter 2　Rare Spirit Tools

> Not obtainable through ordinary channels, high in price, mostly appearing as rewards from high-tier cases or through sect inheritance.

## ⑨ Century Peachwood Sword（Century Peachwood Sword）

- **Type**: Melee spirit tool (rare)
- **Attribute**: Dao Attainment (DOW)
- **Damage / Effect**: 1d8 + Prowess (PHS) modifier; ×2 damage against spirits
- **Weight**: 1.5 kg
- **Price**: 400 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Description**: Peachwood grown for over a century and consecrated by generations of Taoists. Damage against pure spirits is doubled outright, making it a potent weapon against Fierce Ghosts and Resentful Ghosts.

## ⑩ Celestial Master Talisman（finished, Celestial Master Talisman）

- **Type**: Consumable spirit tool (finished talisman)
- **Damage / Effect**: Takes effect without drawing; Exorcism Talisman effect +2 (treated as a reinforced Exorcism Talisman)
- **Weight**: 0.1 kg
- **Price**: 380 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Description**: A finished talisman left by a patriarch-level master, ready the moment you tear it open. Against hard-skinned (mitigation) beings like Hopping Corpses and Flying Corpses (−5 / −8), the Celestial Master Talisman's exorcism power is enough to break their defense; its effect equals a reinforced Exorcism Talisman, a rare-grade trump card against highly resistant spirits.

## ⑪ Soul-Calming Bell（Soul-Calming Bell）

- **Type**: Implement spirit tool (rare)
- **Damage / Effect**: Ring it: all spirits below mid-tier (related to Challenge Rating (CR), GM's discretion) are frozen still for 3 rounds
- **Weight**: 0.3 kg
- **Price**: 350 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Description**: A stronger bell than the Three Pure Bell. One ring freezes all low- and mid-tier spirits in the area, buying the team a golden three rounds to set up formations or perform deliverance.

## ⑫ Thunder-Struck Wood（Thunder-Struck Wood）

- **Type**: Melee spirit tool (strongest spirit tool)
- **Attribute**: Dao Attainment (DOW)
- **Damage / Effect**: 2d6 + Prowess (PHS) modifier; ignores hard-skin mitigation against zombies, devastating in a single blow
- **Weight**: 1.0 kg
- **Price**: 600 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Legendary
- **Description**: Peachwood struck by heavenly thunder yet unburned, holding thunder energy within. It ignores all tiers of hard skin on White Zombies, Black Zombies, Hopping Corpses, and Flying Corpses alike — a legendary-level zombie bane.

## ⑬ Soul-Gathering Gourd（Soul-Gathering Gourd）

- **Type**: Implement spirit tool (rare)
- **Damage / Effect**: Seals low-tier spirits (Challenge Rating (CR) ≤ 2) into temporary containment (capacity 1); subdue check Dao Attainment (DOW) vs Difficulty Class (DC) = Challenge Rating (CR) × 3
- **Weight**: 0.5 kg
- **Price**: 380 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Description**: Open the gourd mouth and low-tier Wandering Souls and Bound Spirits are sucked in and temporarily sealed. Capacity is only one, but rather than destroying them, capturing often keeps a living witness to question about the cause of death.

# Chapter 3　Core Talismans (Eight Standard Talismans)

> Talismans consume Mana Pool (MP) and materials "at the moment of drawing"; tearing to activate is a Bonus Action (BA), takes automatic effect, no hit check required.
> Mana Pool (MP) cost, casting time, Area of Effect (AOE), and effect values follow the compilation memo and Section 6 of the combat design.

## ⑭ Exorcism Talisman（Exorcism Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (tear talisman)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Touch / close range 5 meters
- **Effect**: Deals 1d6 Burn to spirits / zombies (1d4 per round, stackable up to 3 layers, lasts 3 rounds) + repulse (Slowed, movement halved for 1 round); automatically hits spirits.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 10 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ⑮ Corpse-Still Talisman（Corpse-Still Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 3
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (paste on forehead)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Touch (paste on zombie's forehead)
- **Effect**: Paralysis, until forcibly removed; target cannot move or use Main Action (MA), may break free with a Composure (CMP) check Difficulty Class (DC) 12 as a Bonus Action (BA).
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + mana infusion
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 12 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ⑯ Warding Talisman（Warding Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2 (consumed at drawing, passive while worn)
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (wear)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect**: Blocks 1 supernatural attack (absorbs equal to that attack's damage die); consumed upon trigger, single-use.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 15 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ⑰ Eye-Opening Talisman（Eye-Opening Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 3
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect**: Perception Range (PR) +10 meters, can see invisible spirits; lasts 10 minutes.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + mana
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 18 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Fine

## ⑱ Pathfinding Talisman（Pathfinding Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 1
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (ignite)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Drifts toward the direction of heaviest yin energy after ignition
- **Effect**: Navigation; floats to guide toward the yin energy source; lasts until reaching the yin source or burning out (about 5 minutes).
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 8 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ⑲ Purification Talisman（Purification Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Area of Effect (AOE) radius 3 meters
- **Effect**: Purify; Yin Value (YIN) −3 (instant, no duration); clears area yin-energy interference.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + glutinous rice flour
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 12 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ⑳ Soul-Calming Talisman（Soul-Calming Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Touch
- **Effect**: Spirit is calmed and still for 2 rounds (Calmed status, can communicate during this period); commonly used to force out possession or soothe Wandering Souls.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 10 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common

## ㉑ Door-Sealing Talisman（Door-Sealing Talisman）

- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (paste on door)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Door / passage
- **Effect**: Spirits may not pass through (until the talisman is damaged or its mana severed); used to seal escape routes or protect a retreat.
- **Material**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + Black Dog Blood
- **Weight**: 0.05 kg
- **Unit Price**: 14 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Fine

# Chapter 5　Healing Consumables

> Consumables that restore Health Points (HP) during combat and exploration. Using them is a Bonus Action (BA), no hit check required, in line with the player rulebook's "Bonus Action (BA) drink medicine" mechanic.

## ㉛ Purifying Talisman Water（Purifying Talisman Water）

- **Type**: Healing consumable
- **Effect**: Drinking restores 1d6 + 1 Health Points (HP); also clears 1 layer of "Yin Erosion"-type negative status (GM's discretion)
- **Weight**: 0.2 kg
- **Price**: 15 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: Talisman water blended from talisman ash, warm and calming to the spirit, dispelling filth and restoring focus. Suitable for both minor wounds and yin discomfort — a basic healing item every Ghost Collector carries.

## ㉜ Golden Wound Salve（Golden Wound Salve）

- **Type**: Healing consumable
- **Effect**: Applied externally, restores 2d4 Health Points (HP)
- **Weight**: 0.3 kg
- **Price**: 25 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Description**: A bruise-and-trauma external salve stocked by temple keepers and funeral homes. Best for physical trauma (blades, clubs, falls); for yin wounds it only restores Health Points (HP), not dispelling yin energy.

# Chapter 6　Armor (Ritual Robe)

> Armor provides an "armor bonus" added directly into the Armor Class (AC) formula: `Armor Class (AC) = 10 + Prowess (PHS) modifier + Dodge + armor bonus`. With no armor, the armor bonus is treated as 0. See the player rulebook's Attributes and Defense chapter for details; fill this value into the character sheet's "armor bonus" field.

## ㉝ Coarse Ritual Robe（Coarse Ritual Robe）

- **Type**: Armor (light)
- **Effect**: Armor Class (AC) +1 (armor bonus)
- **Weight**: 1.0 kg
- **Price**: 100 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Fine
- **Description**: The coarse cloth Taoist robe issued to Ghost Collectors at initiation, embroidered with simple evil-warding talisman patterns. It does not block blades, but slightly deflects yin claw-scratches and evil-energy impacts — a novice's first piece of armor.

## ㉞ Tailored Ritual Robe（Tailored Ritual Robe）

- **Type**: Armor (light)
- **Effect**: Armor Class (AC) +2 (armor bonus); and +1 on Composure (CMP) checks against Area of Effect (AOE) yin-energy impacts
- **Weight**: 1.2 kg
- **Price**: 280 Nether Coin (COIN)
- **Rarity**: Rare
- **Description**: A tailor-made robe dyed with cinnabar and lined with talisman cloth, hard to find on the market. Beyond raising defense, it steadies the mind and slows yin-energy impacts — standard issue for high-tier Ghost Collectors.
