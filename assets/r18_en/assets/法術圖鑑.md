# Spell Codex (Ghost Collector TRPG)

> This codex compiles the talismans and advanced spells a Ghost Collector can cast, for reference and casting preparation by Main Casters and Scholars.
> Values are based on Section 6 of the combat design draft (combat-designer spell system) and the compilation memo (_compile_brief).
> Format rule: all game terms use the "English Full Name (ABBR)" format; full names are used every time they appear in the body text.

---

## Mana Pool (MP) and Common Casting Rules

- All talismans / incantations / mudras / altar openings / wards consume **Mana Pool (MP)**; talismans additionally require materials.
- When Mana Pool (MP) is depleted, rest is required: resting 1 hour restores Dao Attainment (DOW) points (Liuren lineage restores Dao Attainment (DOW) × 2 points); a full night's sleep restores to full.
- Casting time is divided into: Bonus Action (BA, tear talisman / equip), Main Action (MA, melee or primary spell), full round (concentration), multi-round concentration (ritual).
- Range is annotated with **Area of Effect (AOE)** radius (meters).

## Chapter One: Spell Type Overview

A Ghost Collector's methods fall into five major categories, differing in nature and resource cost:

| Type | Description | Typical Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talisman** | Drawn with cinnabar on yellow paper; takes effect the moment it is torn. Attack/control types auto-hit (area or contact), no hit check needed. | Mana Pool (MP) 1–3 + materials | See Chapter Two, the eight core talismans. |
| **Incantation** | Reciting secret spells to channel power, often combined with talismans or mudras. | Mana Pool (MP) per spell | Five-Thunder Palm and Summon Spirit Rite are extensions of this type. |
| **Mudra** | Forms a seal to amplify the "next" spell/talisman. Cast as a Bonus Action (BA), costs Mana Pool (MP) 1. | Mana Pool (MP) 1 | Choose one of three effects: Damage +1d4 / Contest +2 / Deliverance check +1. |
| **Altar Ritual** | A large ritual of setting up the altar and burning incense while chanting scriptures, requiring a censer and incense as its "power source". | Mana Pool (MP) in stages + sustained Composure (CMP) | Deliverance Rite and Summon Spirit Rite belong to this. Without a censer and incense, the Altar Opening (Dao Attainment (DOW)) check suffers −3. |
| **Ward** | Uses ink-line reel / coin arrays to mark forbidden zones that trap spirits or block passage. | Mana Pool (MP) to set array + sustain each round | Demon-Sealing Array and ink-line reel belong to this. Spirits/zombies within the ward cannot cross (flying corpses can fly over, but remain trapped once they land). |

## Chapter Two: The Eight Core Talismans (Standard Talismans)

> General: drawing consumes Mana Pool (MP) and materials on the spot; casting by tearing the talisman is a Bonus Action (BA) and takes effect automatically, no hit check needed. Level requirement is "none" (a novice can learn).

### ① Exorcism Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (tear talisman)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Contact / Close 5 meters
- **Effect Values**: Deals 1d6 Burn to spirits/zombies (1d4 Burn at the start of each round, stacks up to 3 layers, lasts 3 rounds) + Forceback (Slowed, movement halved for 1 round); auto-hits spirits.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar

### ② Corpse-Still Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 3
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (stick to forehead)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Contact (stick to zombie's forehead)
- **Effect Values**: Paralysis, until forcibly removed; target cannot move or use Main Action (MA), may attempt a Composure (CMP) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 12 on a Bonus Action (BA) to break free.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + Mana Infusion

### ③ Warding Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2 (consumed when drawn, passive when equipped)
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (equip)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect Values**: Blocks 1 supernatural attack (absorbs damage equal to that attack's damage dice); consumed on trigger, one-time use.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar

### ④ Eye-Opening Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 3
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect Values**: Perception Range (PR) +10 meters, can see invisible spirits; lasts 10 minutes.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + Mana

### ⑤ Pathfinding Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 1
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (ignite)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: After ignition, drifts toward the direction of strongest Yin
- **Effect Values**: Navigation; the floating guide points toward the Yin source; lasts until reaching the Yin source or burning out (about 5 minutes).
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar

### ⑥ Purification Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Area of Effect (AOE) radius 3 meters
- **Effect Values**: Purifies; Yin Value (YIN) −3 (instant, no duration); clears area Yin interference and aids ritual stability.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + Glutinous Rice Flour

### ⑦ Soul-Calming Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Contact
- **Effect Values**: Calms and freezes a spirit still for 2 rounds (Calmed state; can communicate during it); commonly used to force out a possession or soothe a wandering soul.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar

### ⑧ Door-Sealing Talisman

- **Spell Type**: Talisman
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 2
- **Casting Time**: Bonus Action (stick to door)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Door / passage
- **Effect Values**: Spirits cannot pass through (until the talisman is damaged or its mana breaks); used to seal escape routes or protect a retreat.
- **Level Requirement**: None
- **Materials & Cost**: Yellow Paper + Cinnabar + Black Dog Blood

## Chapter Three: Six Advanced Spells

> Advanced spells consume massive Mana Pool (MP), mostly requiring concentration, ritual, or sustained Composure (CMP) support, and must be cast with caution.

### ⑨ Five-Thunder Palm

- **Spell Type**: Incantation / Melee
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 8
- **Casting Time**: Main Action (MA)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Contact fist (melee)
- **Effect Values**: 3d6 lightning damage, **ignores** Zombie Hard-Skin Mitigation; can break the defense of a fierce ghost's protective aura.
- **Level Requirement**: Level 3 or above
- **Materials & Cost**: Five-Thunder Talisman + large amount of mana; after casting, backlash 1d4 Yin Value (YIN).

### ⑩ Summon Spirit Rite

- **Spell Type**: Incantation / Altar Ritual
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 6
- **Casting Time**: Full round (concentration)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Ritual radius 5 meters
- **Effect Values**: Summons a departed soul to manifest, forcing it to answer three truthful questions (asking cause of death, source of resentment, and weakness is extremely useful).
- **Level Requirement**: Level 2 or above
- **Materials & Cost**: Spirit-Banner + item from life (as the anchor for manifestation).

### ⑪ Deliverance Rite

- **Spell Type**: Altar Ritual
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 10 (2 per round, staged casting)
- **Casting Time**: Multi-round concentration (1 round to set up altar and prepare, then a Dao Attainment (DOW) + Altar Opening check each round)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Altar range
- **Effect Values**: Sends the spirit away; completion threshold is total progress ≥ Challenge Rating (CR) × 2 (Infant Spirit ×3, as it is harder to send off). Each failed round adds Yin Value (YIN) +1 to the environment; critical failure reduces progress by 1.
- **Level Requirement**: Level 3 or above (Infant Spirit requires Level 4 or above)
- **Materials & Cost**: Altar + Incense & Candle + Hell Bank Notes + large Mana Pool (MP) + sustained Composure (CMP) to hold the field.

### ⑫ Demon-Sealing Array

- **Spell Type**: Ward / Array
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 12 (1 round to set array, 2 to sustain each round)
- **Casting Time**: 1 round to set array + sustain each round
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Area of Effect (AOE) radius 5 meters
- **Effect Values**: Seals an extremely dangerous spirit (traps it within the array); each round requires a Dao Attainment (DOW) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to maintain; failure breaks the array.
- **Level Requirement**: Level 4 or above
- **Materials & Cost**: Coin Array + Talisman + sustained Mana Pool (MP) input.

### ⑬ Invite Deity Possession

- **Spell Type**: Incantation / Possession
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: 7
- **Casting Time**: Main Action (MA) + sustained
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect Values**: During possession, all attributes +2, can cast spells normally unavailable, spell power doubled (treated as possession buff); after it ends, must pass a Composure (CMP) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to avoid losing control (failure causes Confusion for 1d4 rounds).
- **Level Requirement**: Level 3 or above
- **Materials & Cost**: Incense + Talisman + Composure (CMP) check.

### ⑭ Life-Borrowing (Forbidden Art)

- **Spell Type**: Forbidden Art / Self
- **Mana Pool (MP) Cost**: All Mana Pool (MP)
- **Casting Time**: Main Action (MA)
- **Range / Area of Effect (AOE)**: Self
- **Effect Values**: All spell power ×2 for 3 rounds.
- **Level Requirement**: None (forbidden for righteous factions)
- **Materials & Cost (aftermath)**: **Forbidden Art**. Costs Lifespan −1 year (irreversible) + permanent Composure (CMP) −1; Yin Value (YIN) accumulation accelerates. For practitioners of strict-discipline lineages such as Maoshan, afterward Composure (CMP)-related checks take an additional −2, and Health Points (HP) / Mana Pool (MP) cannot grow by crossing the line.

> **Warning**: Life-Borrowing is the forbidden line for righteous Ghost Collectors. Though it buys three rounds of power, the lost lifespan and Composure (CMP) are irreversible, and it easily invites Yin corruption and descent into the heretical path. Never use it lightly except in dire straits.
