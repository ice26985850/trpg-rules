# Nation Building TRPG — Spell Codex

---

## Usage Notes

This codex collects the Spell list of *Nation Building TRPG*. Heroes cast Spells using **Mana (MP)**; the Casting Check is `1d20 + relevant hero attribute (raw value) ≥ Spell Difficulty Class (DC)`. On failure, Mana (MP) is wasted with no effect.

> **Critical Success (natural 20)**: Spell effect ×1.5, with no extra Mana (MP) spent.
> **Critical Failure (natural 1)**: Mana (MP) backlash deals 1d6 self-damage.
> **Cooldown (CD)**: Spells marked with a CD must wait that many turns after casting before they can be cast again; unmarked spells may be cast every turn (limited by the Mana (MP) cap). High-tier Spells (Meteor Strike, Time Stop) are CD-limited to prevent a single Hero from endlessly clearing the field.

Spells are divided into six types by **Magic Type**, listed below by category.
