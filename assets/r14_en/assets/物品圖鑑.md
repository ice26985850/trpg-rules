> **Hero Carrying Cap**: The cap on consumables a hero carries (Healing Potions and Mana Potions combined) = 5 + FLOOR(Constitution (CON) ÷ 2) vials. This limit prevents a flood of high-tier potions from diluting combat, while preserving the healthy trade-off between "buying potions vs. saving up for equipment."
