# Scenario S8: Kings' Strife

> **Suggested Era Range:** Kingdom Age → Empire Age  
> **Suggested Level:** Intermediate  
> **Estimated Turns:** ~60 turns (20 in-game years)  
> **Difficulty:** ★★★☆☆  
> **Core Themes:** Conquest, Ruins, Intrigue, Coronation  
> **Players:** 2–5 players + 1 GM

---

## I. Scenario Overview

### Synopsis

After the old empire collapsed, its vast territory turned to wasteland, with only the scattered Imperial Ruins still glimmering with former glory. Several kings founded realms upon the wastes—nominally each holding their own ground, yet all coveting the empty crown. Whoever can control the legendary Imperial Ruins and awaken their slumbering power will become the empire's successor.

This is a war of land, faith, and betrayal. Your vassal may raise the rebel banner tomorrow.

### Scenario Goals

1. Found a realm and establish territory and the line of succession
2. Expand outward by conquest, controlling strategic strongholds and Imperial Ruins
3. Control 2 or more Imperial Ruins, or hold the highest Royal Authority Score at the endgame
4. Survive the holy war and intrigue, and be crowned

---

## II. Multiplayer Mechanics

- **Mode:** Competitive (conquest + intrigue). Each player leads a kingdom, founding it from a different region.
- **Royal Authority Score:** Territories×10 ＋ Ruins Controlled×20 ＋ Vassal Nations×8 ＋ Wars Won×15.
- **Victory Condition:** The first to control 2 or more "Imperial Ruins" in the region immediately triggers the endgame and +30 points; otherwise, Royal Authority Scores are tallied at turn 60, and the highest is crowned.
- **Player Interaction:** May forge vassal relations, declare war, launch conquest wars and court intrigue; capturing ruins unlocks special bonuses.
- **GM Duties:** Track each nation's territory count, ruins controlled, and vassal relations; arbitrate conquest and intrigue checks.

## III. Scenario Structure

### Phase I: The Throne First Established (Turns 1-15, Years 1-5)

**Key Events:**

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 3 | Coronation Ceremony—formally crowned king, proclaim sovereignty | Ceremony |
| Turn 7 | Ruins Expedition—send an expedition into the Imperial Ruins to seek relics | Exploration (Technology check DC 15) |
| Turn 11 | Succession Dispute—inheritance dispute erupts within the royal house | Intrigue (choice) |
| Turn 14 | Frontier Vassal—subdue a weak neighboring nation as a vassal | Vassal |

**GM Notes:**
- In the Kingdom Age the focus is on founding the realm and territorial foundations; guide players to explore ruins in the early game
- Scatter kingdoms across different regions, leaving a conquest buffer zone

## IV. Ending Conditions

### Victory (Highest Royal Authority Score)
- Control 2 or more Imperial Ruins and hold the highest Royal Authority Score
- **Reward:** All attributes +10, unlock the "Imperial Successor" Legendary Deed; other players gain corresponding renown by score

### Partial Success
- Survive to the endgame but not crowned
- **Reward:** Keep your kingdom, may enter Free Mode to continue

### Failure Conditions
- Royal capital falls and no vassal to inherit
- **Remedy:** Inheritance Mechanism—flee as exiled royalty and attach to the victor, keeping 45% of attributes

## VII. GM Tips

1. **Pacing:** The Kingdom Age moves at a steady pace; focus on founding the realm and exploration in the early game
2. **Ruins core:** Ruins are the core strategic resource; the GM should highlight their scarcity
3. **Mix in intrigue:** Assassination and betrayal are soft solutions, used alongside frontal war
4. **Relic balance:** The Relic Awakening event should be arranged with care, to avoid any single player monopolizing too early
5. **Balance of power:** Maintain a multipolar balance; avoid an absolute hegemon emerging too early
