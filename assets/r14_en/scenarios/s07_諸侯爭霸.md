# Scenario S7: Lords' Struggle

> **Suggested Era Range:** City-State Age → Kingdom Age  
> **Suggested Level:** Beginner to Intermediate  
> **Estimated Turns:** ~50 turns (15 in-game years)  
> **Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆  
> **Core Themes:** Diplomacy, Alliance, War, Hegemony  
> **Players:** 2–5 players + 1 GM

---

## I. Scenario Overview

### Synopsis

Several city-states rise in the same region, scheming openly and secretly over resources, faith, and dominance. The Holy City looms at the center; whoever controls faith and the council holds the voice of authority. When the honeymoon of diplomacy passes, steel will turn on steel at last—whoever can unite the lords and build a regional hegemony will be the next legendary monarch.

This is a chess game of power. Allies and enemies are often but two faces of the same mask.

### Scenario Goals

1. Establish city-state standing in the region, contest strategic resources and the faith center
2. Expand influence through diplomacy (marriage, council, blockade) and war (declare war, siege, decisive battle)
3. Control 3 or more major city-states, or hold the highest Hegemony Score at the endgame
4. Survive the shifting alliances and have the last laugh

---

## II. Multiplayer Mechanics

- **Mode:** Competitive (diplomacy + war equally weighed). Each player leads a city-state, rising from a different region of the map.
- **Hegemony Score:** Population (POP)×1 ＋ City-States Controlled×15 ＋ Allied Nations×5 ＋ Wars Won×12.
- **Victory Condition:** The first to control 3 or more major city-states in the region immediately triggers the endgame and +25 points; otherwise, Hegemony Scores are tallied at turn 50, and the highest wins.
- **Player Interaction:** May forge defense/offense alliances, declare war, launch sieges and economic blockades; capturing neutral city-states expands the sphere of influence.
- **GM Duties:** Track each nation's controlled city-states and alliance relationship map; arbitrate cross-national conflict checks; arrange global events such as the Oracle Dispute.

## III. Scenario Structure

### Phase I: Tripartite Standoff (Turns 1-12, Years 1-4)

**Key Events:**

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 2 | Holy City Pilgrimage—visitors may raise Morale | Diplomacy |
| Turn 5 | Salt-Iron Dispute—a salt-iron mine attracts the covetous eyes of many | Competition (Economy check DC 13) |
| Turn 8 | Marriage Proposal—another nation proposes a royal marriage to forge an alliance | Diplomacy (choice) |
| Turn 11 | Border Outpost—build an outpost to expand the sphere of influence | Construction |

**GM Notes:**
- In the City-State Age the focus shifts from survival to power play; encourage players to make contact from the start
- Scatter city-states across different regions of the map, leaving a diplomatic buffer zone

---

### Phase II: Vertical and Horizontal Alliances (Turns 13-28, Years 5-9)

Diplomatic maneuvering reaches a fever pitch; factions constantly regroup.

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 15 | Regional Council—multiple players may form a formal league, sharing defense | Diplomacy |
| Turn 19 | Economic Blockade—impose a trade blockade on a rival city-state | Economic War |
| Turn 23 | Seduce a Defector—send spies to turn a rival's border commander | Espionage (Morale check DC 15) |
| Turn 27 | Oracle Dispute—two great faiths contest control of the Holy City | Religious (global Morale) |

---

### Phase III: Years of Flaring Beacons (Turns 29-42, Years 10-13)

Diplomacy breaks down; war becomes the main theme.

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 31 | Declare War—formally declare war on a rival city-state | Military (Crisis) |
| Turn 35 | Open-Field Decisive Battle—two armies clash on open ground | Military (check DC 16) |
| Turn 39 | Siege Falls—breach a rival's capital and bring it under control | Military (control +15 points) |

---

### Phase IV: The Final Chapter of Hegemony (Turns 43-50, Years 14-15)

The final consolidation—unite the lords, or perish on the battlefield.

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 45 | Hegemony Declaration—control 3 or more major city-states to proclaim hegemony | Milestone (triggers endgame +25 points) |
| Turn 48 | Final Battle—last standoff of remaining powers | Military (check DC 18) |
| Turn 50 | Lord of the Lords—tally Hegemony Scores, highest becomes the regional hegemon | Ending |

---

## IV. Ending Conditions

### Victory (Highest Hegemony Score)
- Control 3 or more major city-states and hold the highest Hegemony Score
- **Reward:** All attributes +8, unlock the "Lord of the Lords" Legendary Deed; other players gain corresponding renown by score

### Partial Success
- Survive to the endgame but fail to unify the region
- **Reward:** Keep your power, may enter Free Mode to continue

### Failure Conditions
- Capital falls and no inheritable power remains
- **Remedy:** Inheritance Mechanism—attach to the victor as a vassal, keeping 40% of attributes

## VII. GM Tips

1. **Pacing:** The City-State Age power play moves at a faster pace; push diplomatic contact from the early game
2. **Relationship map:** Use a whiteboard to draw each nation's controlled city-states and alliance relations, update each phase
3. **Soft power:** Economic blockades and espionage are important options; don't let war be the only solution
4. **Oracle influence:** The Oracle Dispute can affect global Morale; arrange victory and defeat with care
5. **Faction fluidity:** Keep factions fluid; avoid solidifying strong/weak early on
