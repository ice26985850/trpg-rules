# Scenario S3: Empire's Legacy

> **Suggested Era Range:** Kingdom Age  
> **Suggested Level:** Mid-High  
> **Estimated Turns:** ~50 turns (12 years in-game)  
> **Difficulty:** ★★★☆☆  
> **Core Themes:** Exploration, moral choice, the temptation and danger of ancient legacy  
> **Player Count:** 1 player + 1 GM

---

## I. Scenario Overview

### Synopsis

An expedition discovers a giant ruin beneath the border that belongs to no known civilization — metal that repairs itself, lights that never go out, runes that move carved on the walls. Scholars say it is the legacy of the "First Empire," holding technology far ahead of its time. But deep within the ruin, something is breathing.

What you face has never been "can we take it," but "should we open it."

### Scenario Objectives

1. Explore the ruin and decipher its technology
2. Choose between "power" and "safety"
3. Respond to the consequences of the ruin's awakening
4. Decide the legacy's final fate (nation / international co-management / seal)

## II. Scenario Structure

### Phase One: Discovery (Turns 1–10)

**Opening Narration (read aloud by the GM):**

> When the miner broke through the third rock layer, his pick struck a warm metal wall. Behind the wall there was no ore vein, only a corridor descending into darkness, glowing with faint blue light.
>
> The Scholar (SCH) kneels before the door, fingers tracing the runes, voice trembling: "This was not built in our age. This is… the First Empire."
>
> You stand at the entrance, behind you the hope of the whole kingdom, before you the unknown.

**Key Events:**

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 1 | Form the expedition — send the Scholar (SCH) and guard (General GEN) in | Exploration |
| Turn 3 | Entrance rune mechanism — needs rune knowledge (Craft CRF or Knowledge KNW) to unlock | Puzzle Check |
| Turn 5 | Discover self-repairing metal and perpetual light sources — tech far beyond the age | Tech Discovery |
| Turn 7 | Ruin guardian puppets awaken (Monster Manual CR 4), guard responds | Combat |
| Turn 9 | First legacy tech "self-repair coating" may be copied — used to reduce building decay | Gain |
| Turn 10 | Neighbor detects anomalous energy and begins to watch this place | External Threat |

**GM Notes:**
- Combat inside the ruin uses hero personal combat rules (Player's Handbook Ch. 11 / GM Guide Ch. 24).
- Guardian puppets and other defenders use the Monster Manual Challenge Rating (CR) for their stats (GM Guide Ch. 27).

## III. Ending Conditions

### Success Ending (Prudent Inheritance)
- Decipher the legacy tech without awakening destruction
- Choose co-management or sealing; region stable
- **Reward:** Technology (TEC) +15; unlock the legendary deed "Legacy Guardian"

### Partial Success (Price of Power)
- Take the core for great power, but trigger later supernatural crises
- **Reward:** All attributes +10, but crisis difficulty (DC) permanently +2

### Failure Conditions
- Gatekeeper loses control and damages the ruin and surroundings (Resources RES −30)
- **Remedy:** Rebuild after sealing, retaining research records

## VI. GM Tips

1. **Atmosphere:** The ruin should inspire "awe over greed"; describe supernatural details in moderation.
2. **Hero Stage:** The Scholar casting, the General guarding, the Spy infiltrating can all shine within the ruin.
3. **No Wrong Answer:** All three endings are reasonable; the point is to let players bear the consequences of their choice.
4. **Linked Crisis:** If the core is connected, draw more "Supernatural Crisis" cards in later turns.
