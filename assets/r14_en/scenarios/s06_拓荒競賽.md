# Scenario S6: Pioneer Contest

> **Suggested Era Range:** Tribal Age → City-State Age  
> **Suggested Level:** Beginner (newbie-friendly)  
> **Estimated Turns:** ~40 turns (10 in-game years)  
> **Difficulty:** ★☆☆☆☆  
> **Core Themes:** Race, Diplomacy, Raiding  
> **Players:** 2–4 players + 1 GM

---

## I. Scenario Overview

### Synopsis

Several pioneer bands land on the same shore. They do not know one another, yet all eye the same land. Whoever can take root and grow first, and ascend to a city-state before the others, will become the coastal hegemon of this new continent.

This is no solo struggle—your neighbor is both a potential ally and an inevitable rival.

### Scenario Goals

1. Establish and develop your own settlement
2. Interact with other players (trade, alliance, or raiding)
3. Push Population past 250 and enter the City-State Age (or hold the highest Hegemony Score at the endgame)
4. Survive the competition and emerge victorious

---

## II. Multiplayer Mechanics

- **Mode:** Competitive (temporary alliances allowed). Each player leads a pioneer band, landing from different edges of the map.
- **Hegemony Score:** Population (POP)×1 ＋ Buildings×5 ＋ Wars Won×10 ＋ Active Diplomatic Pacts×3.
- **Victory Condition:** Reaching "Population > 250 and entering the City-State Age" first immediately triggers the endgame and +20 points; otherwise, Hegemony Scores are tallied at turn 40, and the highest wins.
- **Player Interaction:** Can forge/break pacts, declare war, trade. Successfully raiding another nation's village seizes Resources (RES) and food, but your own Morale (MOR)−5.
- **GM Duties:** Record each nation's Hegemony Score on a whiteboard, update every phase; arbitrate cross-national conflict checks.

## III. Scenario Structure

### Phase I: Landing Race (Turns 1-8, Year 1 Spring → Year 2 Spring)

**Key Events:**

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 1 | Choose camp location—riverside (resources＋flood risk) or highland (defense＋fewer resources) | Exploration |
| Turn 3 | Discover another band landed miles away—make contact or keep distance | Diplomacy |
| Turn 5 | Resource point contest—a rich mineral site discovered by two bands at once, first come first served | Competition |
| Turn 8 | First permanent building completed—first to build gains renown | Milestone |

**GM Notes:**
- Lower DC by 2 for the first 5 turns, letting players learn the multiplayer mechanics
- Have each band land from different edges of the map, avoiding immediate head-on conflict

---

### Phase II: Territory Contest (Turns 9-20, Years 2-4)

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 10 | Border friction—two bands clash at a hunting ground, negotiate boundaries or drive them off by force | Territorial Dispute |
| Turn 13 | Refugee wave—a wave of refugees flows toward all villages at once; whoever takes them in gains population and burden | Moral Choice |
| Turn 16 | Trade network—establish trade routes with neighboring bands, exchanging goods | Diplomacy |
| Turn 19 | Raid the vulnerable—a neighboring band weakened by plague; seize the chance? | Military |

---

### Phase III: Alliance and War (Turns 21-32, Years 5-7)

In this phase the weak ally for self-defense, while the strong try to pick them off one by one.

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 22 | Forge alliance—two players may form a defense pact, sharing intelligence | Diplomacy |
| Turn 25 | Moment of betrayal—an allied side may switch sides mid-battle | Betrayal |
| Turn 28 | Siege—a great power lays siege to a weaker nation | Military |
| Turn 31 | Hero duel—both sides' heroes duel on the battlefield, affecting morale | Hero Duel |

---

### Phase IV: The City-State Contest (Turns 33-40, Years 8-10)

| Turn | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| Turn 34 | Population milestone—players whose population exceeds 200 take the lead | Milestone |
| Turn 37 | Final trade—a resource allocation window before the endgame | Diplomacy |
| Turn 39 | Era-upgrade prep—meet the final City-State Age condition (Population > 250) | Construction |
| Turn 40 | **Coastal Hegemon**—tally Hegemony Scores, highest rules the coast | Ending |

---

## IV. Ending Conditions

### Victory (Highest Hegemony Score)
- Be first to Population > 250 and enter the City-State Age (immediately triggers endgame +20 points)
- Or hold the highest Hegemony Score at turn 40
- **Reward:** All attributes +5, unlock the "Coastal Founder" Legendary Deed; other players gain corresponding renown by score

### Partial Success
- Survive to the endgame but not come out on top
- **Reward:** Keep progress, may enter Free Mode to continue

### Failure Conditions
- Population drops to 0 (nation destroyed) or Morale drops to 0 causing a full revolt
- **Remedy:** May use the "Inheritance Mechanism"—play a survivor rebuilding elsewhere (keeping 50% of attributes)

## VII. GM Tips

1. **Pacing:** The first 5 turns are tutorial mode—slow down, explain the multiplayer mechanics
2. **Interaction-driven:** Push at least one player-to-player action (diplomacy/trade/war) each turn
3. **Transparent scoring:** Hegemony Score is publicly recorded by the GM on a whiteboard, updated each phase
4. **Cost of betrayal:** Temporary alliances may be broken, but there should be dramatic consequences (e.g., loss of renown)
5. **Don't steal the show:** The GM does not directly punish players; let player interaction and competition drive the story
