# Urban Survivors — Monster / Enemy Bestiary

> Complete statistics for 6 enemy unit types, using the v1.1 3d6 system.
> All values are aligned with the core dice mechanics.

---

## Threat Level System

| Threat Level | Meaning | Civilian Solo Survival Rate |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| **Threat 1** | Minor threat — easily avoided through stealth | ~95% |
| **Threat 2** | Moderate threat — requires careful handling | ~70% |
| **Threat 3** | High threat — extremely hard to escape once spotted | ~30% |
| **Threat 4** | Lethal threat — almost certainly fatal on encounter | ~10% |
| **Threat 5** | Devastating threat — beyond an individual's ability to resist | ~1% |

## Enemy Units

### Patrol Soldier — Threat 2

```
【Patrol Soldier (Patrol)】
Threat Level: 2
Attributes: Physique 4 / Agility 3 / Willpower 2
Awareness: +2 (during routine patrol +0)
Movement Speed: 1 Distance Band / turn (foot patrol)
Attacks:
  • Assault Rifle (+3 Shooting, range 100 m, Damage: at least Serious Wound)
  • Rifle Butt (+2 Melee, Damage: Minor Wound)
  • Bayonet (+3 Melee, Damage: Serious Wound)
Special Abilities:
  • Radio Call: after 1 turn, 1–2 reinforcements arrive within 2d4 turns
  • Flashlight: can illuminate a 30 m area at Night
Weaknesses:
  • Fixed patrol route — predictable
  • Fatigue — Awareness −2 after prolonged duty
  • May hesitate against civilians — if a character pleads (Social DC 14), 30% chance to go easy
Behavior Pattern:
  Patrols along a route, pausing to observe every 3 turns (~18 seconds).
  Reports anomalies before investigating. Will not necessarily open fire unless attacked — may shout "Halt!" first.
Survival Tip: Observe the route, move during pauses. If spotted — run. Do not attempt to fight.
```

### Sniper — Threat 4

```
【Sniper (Snipe)】
Threat Level: 4
Attributes: Physique 3 / Agility 5 / Willpower 4
Awareness: +5 (visual) / +1 (hearing)
Movement Speed: 0 (fixed at sniping position)
Attacks:
  • Sniper Rifle (+5 Shooting, range 500 m, Damage: at least Fatal Wound; exceeding Defense Value → instant death)
  • Pistol (+2 Shooting, range 30 m, Damage: Serious Wound)
Special Abilities:
  • Aimed Shot: if spending 1 turn aiming, +3 to next turn's shot
  • Optical Scope: during Daytime, ignores cover bonuses within 100 m
  • Concealed Position: only discoverable with passive Awareness DC 18
Weaknesses:
  • Narrow field of view — covers only a specific area (usually a street or square)
  • Most active by day — efficiency drops sharply at Night (without night-vision optics, view drops to 50 m, no aiming bonus)
  • Exposes position after firing (gunshot + muzzle flash) — though usually too late by then
Behavior Pattern:
  Holds high ground during the day, scanning the assigned area. Prioritizes shooting armed targets.
  Will not necessarily fire on a lone civilian — depends on orders (GM decides).
  Once it fires, relocates after 2d4 turns.
Survival Tip: Never cross a known sniper zone during Daytime. Wait for Night. Use a mirror or reflective object to confirm position.
```

### Officer — Threat 3

```
【Officer (Officer)】
Threat Level: 3
Attributes: Physique 3 / Agility 3 / Willpower 5
Awareness: +4
Movement Speed: 1 Distance Band / turn
Attacks:
  • Pistol (+3 Shooting, range 30 m, Damage: Serious Wound)
  • Saber (+3 Melee, Damage: Serious Wound)
Special Abilities:
  • Command: nearby Patrol Soldiers' Awareness +2, will not hesitate to fire
  • Interrogation: Social Contest DC 16 — may extract intel from captives
  • Tactical Thinking: not easily fooled by feints or diversions
Weaknesses:
  • Arrogance — may underestimate the civilian threat
  • Self-preservation — retreats first after taking damage
  • Has radio — but may be reluctant to call for support (fears appearing incompetent)
Behavior Pattern:
  Leads 2–4 Patrol Soldiers. Gives orders rather than searching personally.
  When encountering civilians, interrogates first ("Who are you? Why are you here? Who else is with you?").
  If a character provides useful intel (Social DC 16), may trade for safe passage.
Survival Tip: The Officer is a negotiable opponent — if you have something he needs. But do not underestimate his vigilance.
```

### Deserter — Threat 2

```
【Deserter (Deserter)】
Threat Level: 2
Attributes: Physique 4 / Agility 4 / Willpower 2
Awareness: +3 (especially sensitive to threats)
Movement Speed: 1 Distance Band / turn (cautious movement)
Attacks:
  • Assault Rifle (+2 Shooting, range 100 m, Damage: Serious Wound — but limited ammo, only d6 rounds)
  • Dagger (+3 Melee, Damage: Serious Wound)
Special Abilities:
  • Desperation: gains Advantage on attacks when surrounded or cornered
  • Wilderness Survival: +3 to scavenging checks
  • Knows patrol routes: can help characters avoid enemy forces (if willing to cooperate)
Weaknesses:
  • Limited ammo — firing is a last resort
  • Paranoid — hard to trust anyone (Social DC 16 to establish initial trust)
  • Being hunted by his own army — if found with a Deserter, the character is also treated as an enemy
Behavior Pattern:
  Hides, scavenges, avoids everyone. When meeting civilians, first judges whether they pose a threat.
  May trade — Deserters have military knowledge and weapons, but need food and shelter.
  If a character offers shelter, the Deserter may become a powerful companion — but may also betray the character to save himself.
Survival Tip: The Deserter is a potential powerful ally, but also a ticking time bomb. Trust takes time to build.
```

### Looter — Threat 2–3

```
【Looter (Looter)】
Threat Level: 2 (alone) / 3 (group of 3 or more)
Attributes: Physique 3 / Agility 3 / Willpower 2
Awareness: +2
Movement Speed: 1 Distance Band / turn
Attacks:
  • Club/Pipe (+2 Melee, Damage: Minor Wound → Serious Wound)
  • Knife (+3 Melee, Damage: Serious Wound)
  • Pistol (+1 Shooting, range 20 m, Damage: Serious Wound — only 30% chance of having a gun)
Special Abilities:
  • Strength in Numbers: when 3 or more, +1 to attack checks (flanking)
  • Looting: knows which locations may still have supplies
Weaknesses:
  • Disorganized — may turn on each other
  • Bullies the weak — if met with firm resistance (character's counterattack causes any Wound), Willpower DC 12 or retreat
  • Loud — the Looters themselves may attract enemy forces
Behavior Pattern:
  Scavenges empty houses, robs other Survivors. When meeting a lone civilian, may demand "hand over all your supplies."
  Not necessarily immediate violence — intimidation first. If the character complies, may only take part of the supplies.
  If the character resists or flees, chases for 2d4 turns then gives up (fear of drawing the army).
Survival Tip: Looters are bullying opportunists. Sometimes handing over part of your supplies is safer than fighting.
```

### Feral Dog — Threat 1–2

```
【Feral Dog (Feral Dog)】
Threat Level: 1 (single) / 2 (pack of 3 or more)
Attributes: Physique 2 / Agility 4 / Willpower 1
Awareness: +4 (smell) / +2 (hearing)
Movement Speed: 2 Distance Bands / turn
Attacks:
  • Bite (+3 Melee, Damage: Minor Wound + infection risk — Physique DC 10)
  • Pack Rushing: 3 or more attacking the same target simultaneously → attack gains Advantage
Special Abilities:
  • Scent Tracking: can track a scent trail from within 24 hours
  • Night Vision: sight unaffected in darkness
Weaknesses:
  • Afraid of fire — flames or loud noises can scatter them (Willpower DC 8 or flee)
  • Hungry — can be lured away with food (throw food, automatically succeeds at diverting attention)
  • Retreats after injury — a single Feral Dog flees upon taking any Wound
Behavior Pattern:
  Driven by hunger, seeks anything edible. May attack injured / weakened Survivors.
  Packs surround prey. Barks a warning first; if the target does not retreat, attacks.
  Will not chase a target that enters a building (unless extremely hungry).
Survival Tip: Feral Dogs fear fire and can be lured away with food. But treat any bite wound — the infection risk is real.
```

## GM Encounter Design Advice

### Threat Combination Reference

| Encounter | Total Threat | Suitable Scenario |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 patrol squad (2 soldiers) | 4 | S1 early |
| Feral Dog pack (3) | 6 (pack) | S1–S2 |
| Patrol Soldier (2) + Officer (1) | 7 | S2 |
| Looter (3) | 9 (group) | S2 |
| Sniper (1) + Patrol Soldier (2) | 8 | S2–S3 |
| Officer (1) + Patrol Soldier (4) | 11 | S3 |

### Solo Player Tips

- Always provide the option to sneak around
- Encounters should not be "obstacles that must be cleared," but "risks to be assessed"
- Let players see patrol routes and hear footsteps — give them time to prepare
- Even if spotted, fleeing is always a valid option
