## Trade Reference

The following is a **rough guide** for barter (not fixed prices; the GM adjusts based on actual demand):

| Item | Roughly Exchanges For |
|------|-----------|
| 1 liter of clean drinking water | 2–3 cans of food, 1 roll of bandage, 1 hour of skilled labor |
| 1 dose of antibiotics | 3–5 cans of food, a small tool, a service, or information |
| 1 can of canned food | 1 liter of fuel (gasoline), 2–3 candles, a small tool |
| 1 pack of cigarettes | 1–2 cans of food, 1 small bottle of liquor (trade between luxuries is extremely flexible) |
| 1 medical service | Depending on the wound: 2–10 cans of food, valuables, a long-term favor |
