# Urban Survivors Scenario 2: "The Long Winter" — Mid-Siege: Resource Depletion and Moral Trials

> **Difficulty**: ★★★☆
> **Suggested Players**: 1 player (expandable to multiplayer)
> **Siege Days**: Days 30–90
> **Estimated Sessions**: 14–21
> **Core Theme**: Depletion and Choice
> **Prerequisite**: Complete Scenario 1 "The First Day" or possess a character with a stable Shelter

---

## Scenario Overview

The siege has entered its second month. Winter has arrived. Most easily reachable locations have already been picked clean.

Water and power have never been restored. The Black Market has become a necessary evil for survival, and every transaction carries a hidden cost.

Cracks begin to show among the people in the Shelter—some want to break out, some want to surrender, and some simply want to sleep and never wake up.

The moral choices the player faces grow sharper: steal a neighbor's reserves to feed your own child, or watch your child go hungry?

> This scenario supports solo and multiplayer modes. In multiplayer, resource distribution, division of labor, and trust conflicts within the group become even sharper.

| NPC Mortality | Mortality of elderly NPCs and child NPCs increases (GM's discretion) |

## Four-Act Structure

### Act 1: The Coming of Deep Winter (Days 30–40)
- **Theme**: Fuel crisis, Shelter reinforcement
- First snow—Warmth begins to drop rapidly
- Fuel reserves run low—must go out to find more fuel
- The Shelter must be reinforced against the severe cold (Sealing must be ≥ 3, otherwise Warmth drops an extra –1 per day)
- Grandma Marta (NPC #7) shares herbal knowledge—how to make a simple warming poultice

### Act 2: Resource Depletion (Days 40–55)
- **Theme**: Must scavenge at high-risk locations
- Old Town scavenge DC raised from 10 to 12 (looted many times over)
- The player must travel to dangerous zones like the City Center or North Suburb to find enough supplies
- First encounter with the Black River Merchant Guild's "special deal"—the Professor (NPC #3) appears in person
- Disease begins to spread through the community (dysentery or pneumonia)

### Act 3: The Test of Trust (Days 55–70)
- **Theme**: NPC secret reveals, betrayal or loyalty
- An NPC's secret is accidentally exposed (GM picks a suitable NPC)
- Victor (NPC #12) proposes an "information trade"—but you know he is an informant
- Dmitri's (NPC #8) PTSD is triggered during an artillery shelling—he may act dangerously
- A conflict breaks out in the Shelter—someone is accused of stealing reserve food

### Act 4: The Dark Bargain (Days 70–90)
- **Theme**: The pivotal deal with the Black River Merchant Guild
- The Professor makes an "offer you can't refuse"—a large cache of supplies in exchange for a "small favor"
- Voice of Liberty (VoL) makes contact with the player—Ghost (NPC #6) asks for help
- The International Humanitarian Corps announces its final evacuation—but slots are limited
- An NPC makes an extreme choice (e.g., Victor sells someone out, Carlo's con is exposed)

## Key NPCs Appear

- **The Professor (NPC #3)**: Introduced through Marco's referral. Waits in an abandoned warehouse—looks less like a Black Market tycoon and more like a weary university lecturer. Makes a dark bargain.
- **Dr. Elena (NPC #4)**: Encountered at a temporary clinic or hospital in Old Town. Performs surgery under extremely limited conditions. Medical support (but drugs are limited).
- **Ghost (NPC #6)**: Voice of Liberty contacts the player at night—a masked figure leaves a message. Provides military training and weapons, but demands participation in dangerous missions.
- **Carlo (NPC #10)**: Approaches the player disguised as a "Federal Army Officer," offering "inside intel"—but it is Carlo in disguise. The player must see through the lie.

## Ending Conditions

- **Survive the Winter**: Warmth has not hit zero for more than 7 consecutive days
- **Deep Bonds**: Establish meaningful relationships with at least 3 NPCs (Relationship value REL ≥ 5)
- **Moral Choice**: Make at least 2 major moral choices (recorded by GM)
- **Shelter Survival**: The Shelter has not collapsed (Structural Integrity > 0)

## Completion Rewards

- Experience Points (Experience): 3
- Fate Points (Fate): 1
- Special: Unlock one Adaptive Talent (chosen based on Trauma Accumulation value)

## Transition to Scenario 3

Time jumps to Siege Day 100. The GM describes:

- The city is unrecognizable. Empty streets, burned-out buildings, silence everywhere.
- Everyone still alive is preparing—preparing to break out, preparing to negotiate, or preparing to die.
- The player receives final invitations from multiple factions. Each faction offers an ending—but every ending has a cost.
- This is the final phase of the siege. Everything is about to end.
