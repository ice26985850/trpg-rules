# Earth Master TRPG

You are no Touch-Gold Captain — you are an Earth Master: reading Feng Shui, discerning Yin-Yang, opening coffins and locating acupoints, and negotiating terms with the things in the tomb. Some tombs should never be opened — yet you opened them anyway.

## What Kind of World Is This?
The Early Republican Era: warlords clashing, ancient roads and deserted temples, and things buried underground for a thousand years. Feng Shui is no mysticism — it is the structure of the tomb and the flow of Sha (Malevolent Energy). Talismans are no magic — they are a borrowed sliver of life-preserving light from heaven and earth. True Qi lets you hold on for one more breath; glutinous rice and cinnabar make the Jiangshi (Hopping Corpse) retreat a step — for the rest, you rely on your wits and your nerve.

## Who Do You Play? What Do You Play?
You are an Earth Master, hired or forced into the tomb to seek what the predecessors left behind. The core loop is Divide-Gold Fix-Point, cracking mechanisms and traps, dealing with corpse mutations, then negotiating with the supernatural. Opening the coffin is not the end — what stirs inside is where it begins: an Opening Roll table decides whether you face burial treasures, a low-tier White Jiangshi, or a spirit tablet in an empty coffin bearing your name.

## Three Most Distinctive Mechanics
- 2d10 Bell-Curve Check: opposed as "2d10 + Attribute + Skill vs Difficulty Class (DC)". Extreme values occur only 1% of the time — a seasoned Earth Master is never betrayed by luck; every point of modifier counts.
- Heritage × Sect dual-track: Heritage decides the source of your martial art (Orthodox Daoist Transmission / Folk Martial Master / Touch-Gold Clan / Maoshan Disciple); Sect decides your organization and resources (Dragon-Shaker / Xuan Kong / Talisman / Mountain-Mover) — sixteen combinations, each with its own way to survive.
- Fear System: witnessing a Jiangshi's corpse mutation accumulates Fear Value; exceeding the Fear Threshold (FT) of your Courage sinks you into disadvantage, panic, even breakdown — nerve is the most real resource in the tomb.

## Is It For You?
Players who enjoy folk horror, tomb-puzzle solving, resource management, and supernatural negotiation will dive right in. Combat here is plain and lethal; low-martial True Qi will not let you fly or tunnel through the earth — you need not be a battle maniac. Beginners have character templates to get started fast; veterans can finely calculate Heritage pairings and every ounce of True Qi and talisman spent.
