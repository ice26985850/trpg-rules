# Corpse-Nurturing Ground — The Whole Cemetery Awakens (S5)

> 7-9 | Hard | 2-3 | Jing-Chu (Hunan ancient battlefield ruins)


## Synopsis

An anomaly appears at the mass grave below an ancient battlefield — corpses across the whole area are waking. From Fresh Corpses to White Jiangshi, Black Jiangshi, even a Leaping Jiangshi leads them.
Local villagers have all evacuated. The Talisman sect issued an emergency call — at least three Earth Masters are needed to jointly set up the "Grand Sha-Suppressing Array."
Beneath the mass grave lies a sealed ancient altar — a Warring States-era human-sacrifice site. A thousand years of human sacrifice accumulated unimaginable Yin-Sha energy.
Someone recently broke the altar's seal.
