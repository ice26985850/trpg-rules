# Ghost Market Deal — Not a Tomb, but a Ghost Market (S4)

> 5-7 | Special (Social-Dominated) | 1-2 | Jing-Chu (deep within an abandoned mine)


## Synopsis

This is not a tomb — this is a Ghost Market. The players receive an invitation to enter a Ghost Market hidden deep within an abandoned mine.
Tonight there is a special auction — the headline lot is a "sealed Han Dynasty jade coffin."
What lies inside the jade coffin is not the tomb lord — it is an extremely powerful resentful spirit sealed within the jade (Leaping Jiangshi · Flying Jiangshi tier).
Someone deliberately brought the jade coffin to the Ghost Market to auction, intending to let the resentful spirit find a new host.
