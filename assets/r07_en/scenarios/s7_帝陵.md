## Synopsis

The end of everything. All clues — the Mercury Maze control map, the Corpse-Nurturing Ground altar inscriptions, the Unrotted Bone's research notes — point to the same Imperial Mausoleum.
The tomb lord is an ancient emperor who sought immortality. He succeeded but also failed — his body is undying, yet his soul is trapped in eternal loneliness.
For a thousand years he has lain awake in his coffin, unable to die, unable to leave. He waits not for tomb-raiders — but for someone who can end his eternal loneliness.
Two forces exist in the Imperial Mausoleum: the tomb lord (yearning to end) and the "Power of Immortality" (resisting ending). This is not a traditional boss fight — it is helping the tomb lord fight against himself.

## Multiple Endings

### Rest

- **Condition**: Help the human side defeat the divine side

- **Result**: The tomb lord finally dies. The Imperial Mausoleum collapses but knowledge is preserved. The players become legends.

- **Result**: The Imperial Mausoleum stays intact but is sealed forever. Gain the title of Guardian.

### Inheritance

- **Condition**: Accept the Power of Immortality (very high Will check DC 22)

### Destruction

- **Condition**: Defeated by the divine side / external forces invade

- **Result**: The Imperial Mausoleum collapses, the Power of Immortality leaks out — a world-setting-level major event.
