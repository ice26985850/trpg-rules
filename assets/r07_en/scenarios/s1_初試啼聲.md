# First Cry — The Petty Landlord's Tomb (S1)

> 1-3 | Simple | 1-2 | Central Plains (Henan countryside) | 1925

_The first tomb. The last rest._


## Synopsis

A petty landlord's Third Concubine was recently buried. It should have been an ordinary funeral, but strange things began happening in the village —
roosters stopped crowing, the well water turned bitter, and infants cried every night. The village chief hired the players to investigate.
This is a small tomb and should hold no supernatural threat — yet the grass atop the grave withered yellow overnight.
The Third Concubine did not die naturally — she was poisoned by the First Wife. Her resentment is fermenting in the tomb,
and she is in the "Fresh Corpse" stage — the perfect live-combat training target for a new Earth Master.


## Objectives

- Investigate the source of the village's strange occurrences
- Enter the Third Concubine's tomb
- Deal with the supernatural threat in the tomb
- Expose the truth
