> 5-7 | Moderate | 2-3 | Guanzhong


## Synopsis

The outer defense system of the Imperial Mausoleum is discovered — a maze encircled by a mercury river. The designers imitated the Qin Dynasty technique of "using mercury as the hundred rivers, lakes, and seas."
The maze traps the resentful spirits of craftsmen who perished during construction. The players must cross the maze to find the true exit — this is only the outer wall of the Imperial Mausoleum.
