# General's Mound — The Broken-Tail Tiger (S2)

> 3-5 | Moderate | 2-3 | Guanzhong (Shaanxi mountains)


## Synopsis

A Ming Dynasty general's tomb is discovered at a perilous mountain location. Dragon-Shaker sect Feng Shui records mark this site as the "Broken-Tail Tiger" layout.
A tomb-raiding gang has broken through the outer passage; the tomb's contents begin affecting the surroundings — hunters report seeing "armored figures" patrolling on moonlit nights.
The tomb lord is a Ming Dynasty provincial commander betrayed by his subordinates, stabbed in the back by his most trusted vice-general. His obsession is not revenge — it is "wanting to know why."
The vice-general was also buried alive in a side chamber — as a sacrificial retainer.
