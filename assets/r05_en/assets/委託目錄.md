# Commission Catalog (Adventurer's Commission Catalog)

> This catalog is a stable leveling source of **side content** (non-main-quest) in *The Hero's Proof*. Running parallel to the main-quest Demon General battles (S1–S4), players may pick up commissions at the Adventurer's Guild bulletin board at any time to farm **Experience (EXP) / Gold (G) / Influence Points (IP)** and unlock dungeons (see GM Book Chapter 17).
>
> Companion rules: Guild Rank (Rank F→S) and acceptance caps — see **GM Book 12.4**; Influence Points (IP) accumulation and ending thresholds — see **GM Book Chapter 14**; structured dungeon play — see **GM Book Chapter 17**.
>
> This game **has no multiclassing / multiple classes** (promotion = a single-class advancement at ClassLv 11/21); solo mode **provides no assist characters (no helpers)**, and commission difficulty scales per the party-size adjustment rules (GM Book 13.1).

---

## 0. General Commission Rules

1. **Acceptance Method**: Adventurer's Guild bulletin board (Alno Holy City headquarters or branch offices in each city). You may only accept commissions whose **target Challenge Rating (CR) ≤ current Rank cap** (see table below).
2. **Reward Settlement**:
   - **Experience (EXP)**: EXP granted by a commission accumulates toward Character Level (CL) progression (Class Level (ClassLv) draws from a separate Class Experience (CEXP) pool and does not share EXP); Skill Level (SkillLv) does not accumulate from this EXP (Skill Points (SP) come only from CL increases).
   - **Gold (G)**: may be spent at temples / the guild to exchange for magical gear, or on smithing purchases (Player Book Chapter 4).
   - **Influence Points (IP)**: accumulate per GM Book 14.1, determining the Reconciliation Ending (≥40 Peace / ≥60 True Peace), affinity and defection, and some guild / temple side-quest rewards.
3. **Reset & Repeat**: Basic commissions **refresh daily**, bounty-type **refresh weekly**; they may be repeatedly accepted to grind resources, but only a **first clear** of the same commission grants full IP — reruns grant only EXP + G (IP halved).
4. **Failure / Abandonment**: You may abandon mid-way without losing Rank; if unfinished, no EXP / G / IP is awarded. A failed assessment (see 12.4 Promotion) may be retaken, with the interval set by the GM.
5. **Party-size Adjustment**: Solo → enemy Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5 (minimum 1), no helpers; multi-player per GM Book 13.1.

## 8. GM Custom Commission Quick Reference

The GM may quickly generate new commissions using the following benchmarks:

- **Single-monster EXP** = `floor(15 × CR × (1 + CR/15))` (consistent with GM Book 10.4; Boss ×4).
- **Total commission EXP** ≈ single-monster EXP × expected encounters (multi-battlefield commissions add ~10–20%).
- **Gold (G)** ≈ EXP × 0.8 (bounties / escorts higher, recon / exploration lower); commission settlements also deduct a **5% guild tax** (actual received = listed G × 0.95, deducted by the GM before paying out; see GM Book §4.4 Gold Sink).
- **Influence Points (IP)** by rank: F +3~4 / E +5 / D +6~8 / C +7~8 / B +10 / A +12 / S +15~20; pure-social / reconciliation-oriented commissions double IP.
- **Target CR must not exceed the acceptor's Rank cap** (see quick-reference table above); anything exceeding it counts as an "over-tier commission," requiring GM approval and reduced EXP.

> Design note: Commissions are the main source of **IP and equipment economy**, while the main-quest Demon General battles emphasize **EXP and story**. Combining the two avoids the imbalance of "only fighting bosses yet unable to afford gear / gather enough IP."
