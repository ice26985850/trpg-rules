# Enemy Stat Cards — D1/D2 Combat Room Quick Reference

> **Stat Source**: GM Book 10.4 Enemy Challenge Rating (CR) Formula (system finalization locked):
> - Health (HP) = `floor(40 × (1 + 0.09 × (CR − 1)))`
> - Attack Bonus (ATK) = `floor(1 + 0.25 × CR)`
> - Evasion (EVA) = `10 + floor(0.15 × CR)`
> - Damage/Hit = `floor(6 + 1.2 × CR)`
> - Experience (EXP) = `floor(15 × CR × (1 + CR/15))`; Boss EXP ×4
> - Critical Threshold (CritTh) fixed at 20; high Challenge Rating (CR) may lower the threshold by 1–2 (this card uses 19 for CR13+)
>
> **Usage**: `Damage/Hit` is the "base damage" of each monster's Normal Attack, applied directly; `Attack Method` only describes the damage type (Pierce / Slash / Bludgeon / Shadow, etc.) without listing separate weapon dice (consistent with the full bestiary, where weapons are shown for illustration). `Damage Reduction (DR)` marked "suggested" is estimated from the same-family template and may be fine-tuned by the GM. Solo Mode follows GM Book 13.1 (Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, minimum 1 each) for quick calculation at the bottom of each card.
>
> Corresponding positioning combat maps: D1 → `maps/D1_戰鬥_前廳／寢窟／王座窟.yaml`; D2 → `maps/D2_戰鬥_前室／停靈堂／巫妖密室.yaml`.
