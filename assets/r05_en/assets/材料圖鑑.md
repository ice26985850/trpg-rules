# The Hero's Proof — Material Catalog

> **Material-attribute Driven**: This catalog does not enumerate finished goods; it only lists **Materials** and their **8 unified fields**; finished-product values are determined by material fields × item templates (see `圖鑑/物品圖鑑.md` §1～§2 and Player Book Chapter 4), combining freely by the player.
> **8 Unified Fields**: ① Damage Base (DmgBase) ② Damage Reduction Base (DRBase) ③ Stat Bonus (StatBonus) ④ Durability Base (DurBonus) ⑤ Effect Tags (Tags) ⑥ Level Requirement & Rarity tier (ReqLv/Rarity) ⑦ Heal Base (HealBase) ⑧ Buff Type & Value (BuffType/BuffVal). A blank "—" means the material does not use that field.
> **White-template Principle (brief)**: Town shops only sell basic (white-template) gear + low-tier healing/mana potions + basic food; mid-to-high-tier potions, bombs, scrolls, and buff dishes must be self-crafted via "gathering → crafting" or dropped from adventures, and are not stocked on shop shelves (see the general white-template principle at the top of `圖鑑/物品圖鑑.md`).
> **Numeric Constraints**: All field values must fall within the legal ranges of Section 3 "Rarity → Attribute Cap" in `DesignDoc/05_Manufacturing_System_Value_Framework.md`; finished-product Rarity = max(material Rarity), and finished-product Level Requirement (ReqLv) = max(material ReqLv).
> **Related Documents**: Examples of finished-product templates are in `圖鑑/物品圖鑑.md`; crafting/gathering rules are in Player Book Chapter 4 and the appendix of `圖鑑/材料圖鑑.md` (gathering life-skill design).

## Appendix A: Verification Anchors (preserve)

Material Rarity strictly determines the weapon power-tier cap; the anchor relationship is as follows (Expected Damage (EvD) ascending, no inversion allowed):

```
Common Wood 1d2 (EvD 1.5)
  < Ironwood 1d5 (EvD 3.0)
    < white-template · Training Longsword 1d8 (EvD 4.5, shop baseline)
      < Fine Steel Ingot 1d10 (EvD 5.5, Fine)
        < Epic 2d6 (Dragonscale Plate / Dragonblood Wood, EvD 7.0)
          < Legendary 3d8 (Dragon King Scale, EvD 13.5)
```

→ Lower-tier material creations fall below the white-template (early transition / secondary materials), while higher-tier material creations exceed the white-template (Rarity gap holds), consistent with the white-template principle in `圖鑑/物品圖鑑.md`.

> **Appendix A Supplement · Stat Bonus (StatBonus) Tiers (CHA / LUK series, anchored per 05 §3.4)**: accessory-material stat bonuses strictly follow Rarity, no inversion allowed:
>
> ```
> Common +1 (Charmglow Stone CHA+1 / Strength Crystal STR+1)
>   < Fine +2 (Rainbow Echo Shard CHA+2 / Lucky Coin Ore LUK+2 / Agility Wardstone DEX+2)
>     < Rare +3 (Enchantwhisper Crystal CHA+3 / Lucky Stardust LUK+3 / Mana Crystal MAG+3)
>       < Epic +4 (Heartflame Pupil CHA+4 / Fourleaf Phantom Crystal LUK+4 / Critical Eye CritTh−2)
>         < Legendary +5 (Phantom Shadow Heart CHA+5 / Fate Core LUK+5 / Dragonpupil Stone HP+30)
> ```
>
> → Charisma (CHA) and Luck (LUK) now both have complete Stat Bonus tiers from Common → Legendary, on par with other accessory materials, no over-tuning.
