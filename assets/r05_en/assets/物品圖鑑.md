# The Hero's Proof — Item Catalog

> Data source: System finalization (Chapter 8, Equipment & Economy), Traditional Chinese, terms use the "Chinese full name (abbreviation)" format.
> Price unit: Gold (G).　Base Price = `floor(20 × CL^1.3)` (CL = Recommended Level / Level Requirement ReqLv).
> White-board Price = Base Price × (1 + 0.15 × (ReqLv − 1)); final price of magic equipment = White-board Price × Rarity Multiplier (Common ×1 / Fine ×3 / Rare ×8 / Epic ×20 / Legendary ×35).
> Durability (Dur): Weapons 40 (Heavy 60) / Armor 50 (Heavy Armor 80) / Accessories 60. At 0 Durability it becomes unusable; Repair Cost = Sale Price × 5% × (Max − Current)/Max.
> Penalty for not meeting the Equipment Level Requirement (ReqLv): Hit Bonus (Hit) −2, Effect −10%.
>
> [Basic Shop General Principle] Town shops sell only "basic (white-board) items": basic equipment (Common, no special effects) + basic consumables (Beginner Healing Potion, Mana Potion) + basic food (Holy Light Wheat-tier recovery type).
> Consumables and dishes **also follow** this principle — mid/high-tier potions, bombs, scrolls, and buff dishes **are not stocked on shop shelves**, and must be self-crafted via Life Skills (Farming → Alchemy / Cooking) or obtained from adventure drops.
> The Basic Shop offers white-board items matching the player's Character Level (CL) with corresponding ReqLv (ReqLv floats ±2 levels), so high-CL players can purchase suitable basic gear, addressing Audit Item 40.
> [Crafting System] All equipment / consumables / dishes use "Material-attribute Driven" design: finished product values are determined by the **Material Table** (see Section III, 8 unified fields) + **Item Template**, freely combined by players. This catalog does not exhaustively list finished products, only materials tables, templates, and a few examples.

### Item Templates and Material → Finished Product Conversion Formulas (Audit Gap Filled)

> Players freely combine "materials (8 unified fields) × item templates" to produce finished goods. This catalog does not exhaustively list finished products, only defining how each template maps material fields to finished-product values. There are six template types:

| Item Template | Material 8 Fields → Finished Product Value Mapping |
|---|---|
| Weapon (Weapon) | `DmgBase` accumulates into base weapon damage (damage die); `DRBase` converts to Damage Reduction (Sunder / Block); `StatBonus` converts to attribute bonus (STR/DEX/MAG or accuracy); `DurBonus` converts to Durability (Weapon 40 / Heavy 60); `Tags` convert to special effects (Elemental / Critical, etc.); `ReqLv/Rarity` determines level requirement and price multiplier. |
| Armor (Armor) | `DRBase` converts to Damage Reduction (Physical reduction %); `StatBonus` converts to attribute bonus (MAG+1, CON≥12, etc.); `DurBonus` converts to Durability (Armor 50 / Heavy Armor 80); `Tags` convert to resistances / special (Fire resist, reduced critical damage taken); `ReqLv/Rarity` determines requirement and multiplier. |
| Accessory (Accessory) | `StatBonus` converts to attribute bonus (STR/DEX/MAG/LUK/HP/MP/CritTh/EVA, etc.); `DurBonus` converts to Durability (60); `BuffType/BuffVal` converts to passive buff (Critical damage +%, drop rate +%); `Tags` convert to resistances; `ReqLv/Rarity` determines requirement and multiplier. |
| Potion (Potion) | `HealBase` → healing amount (restores HP/MP die); `DmgBase` → damage (offensive potion); `Tags` → added status (Burn / Purify); `BuffType/BuffVal` → temporary buff; `ReqLv/Rarity` determines level and base price. |
| Bomb (Bomb) | `DmgBase` → area damage die (AOE radius 1); `Tags` → element and added status (Fire / Frost + Chill); `ReqLv/Rarity` determines level and base price; `DurBonus` not applicable (consumables have no durability). |
| Scroll (Scroll) | `DmgBase`/`Tags` determine area and status (AOE radius, EVA−%, Blind / Burn, etc.); `HealBase` converts to purify / cleanse effect; `ReqLv/Rarity` determines level and base price; `DurBonus` not applicable. |

> General rule: unused fields are left blank with "—"; `ReqLv` determines the finished product's level requirement and base price (see top formula and Section IV quick reference), `Rarity` determines the rarity multiplier (Common ×1 / Fine ×3 / Rare ×8 / Epic ×20 / Legendary ×35). Finished white-board price = Base Price × (1 + 0.15 × (ReqLv − 1)) × Rarity Multiplier.

## V. Economy: Buying/Selling and Discount Rates (Player Sell / NPC Buyback)

> Audit supplement: originally only dishes had an NPC buyback formula; now a general "Player Sell Discount Rate" and "NPC Buyback Discount Rate" are added, closing the cash-out and buyback loop for equipment / consumables / materials / dishes.

- **Player Sell Discount Rate (Player → Town Shop Resale)**: When a player sells equipment / white-board basic consumables / materials back to a town shop, recovery = current sale price × **50%** (rounded down with floor). Basic Shops buy back at this price to avoid infinite money farming and stay consistent with market recovery; **Alchemy products (potions / bombs / scrolls) are not purchased by shops**.
- **NPC Buyback Discount Rate (Player → NPC Buyer Sale)**:
  - General-goods NPC buyback multiplier **0.6** (player receives 60% of base price);
  - Specialty merchant (corresponding field, e.g., a dish shop owner buys dishes, a smith buys equipment/materials) buyback multiplier **0.8** (80% of base price); **Alchemy products (potions / bombs / scrolls) do not apply to this 0.8 buyback multiplier — they are for personal use or delivery of commissions/quests only, and may not be resold at high price (cutting the farm loop).**
  - **Dish Special Case**: Self-crafted dishes traded between players or bought by NPCs have sale price = main ingredient base price × (1 + 0.4 × buff level) (buff level 1~3); this formula is a special case of the "specialty merchant buyback price" and takes priority over the 0.8 multiplier (Audit Item 41).
- **Base Price Definition**: Equipment / Accessories = White-board Price (Base Price × (1+0.15×(ReqLv−1)) × Rarity Multiplier); Consumables = Crafting Cost Baseline; Materials = Material Base Price; Dishes = the main-ingredient formula above.
- **Player-to-Player Trade**: Both sides freely negotiate, not limited by discount rates; dishes traded to other players reference the NPC buyback formula as pricing baseline.
- **Repair and Resale are Mutually Exclusive**: Equipment at 0 Durability can only be refurbished (sale price ×2 refurbish cost, see Durability rules at top) and does not apply the sell discount.
