# Scenario 6: Necromancer General Battle (Silent Burial Wetlands · Proof of the Dead Souls)

**How to Run**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + party-size adjustment rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. Party-size adjustment rules are the same as the Prologue (1 PC → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 PCs → baseline; 4 PCs → baseline; 5–6 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / concurrent events +1).

> **Difficulty Rule**: The Necromancer General is a boss battle, designed Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ party total level ×0.6 (at 4 PCs Character Level (CL) 42, about Challenge Rating (CR) 42). After adjustment it must ensure "lowest level (CL40) can clear, highest level (CL42 threshold) has challenge." The Necromancer General has been adjusted to a late-phase elite (originally CR25→**CR42**), see Monster Bestiary and GM Book 12.3.

---

## I. Opening Description
Head west to "Silent Burial Wetlands (R2)," where poison-mist swamp and ancient barrow intertwine. Temple cleric apprentice Lina Gentlelight accompanies, assisting with revival matters and secretly investigating the church's dark secrets. The Necromancer Demon General Mourvana Silentgrave is not evil, but a gentle being who guards the dead and fights the undead curse—this chapter reveals the "resurrection economy" contradiction and foreshadows the Fifth's lineage.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act 0: Take on the Subjugation Commission (Opening Beat)
You accept the "Subjugate the Second Demon General · Necromancer General" commission at the adventurer guild headquarters—**requires Rank S (Influence Points (IP) ≥40 to accept; Character Level (CL) 40 end-of-leveling-phase usually naturally meets it, consistent with the Rank S acceptance threshold)**. The ticket to this battle is your push to Rank S during the leveling phase, and after subjugating the Dragon General (CR40) in Scenario 5, the guild's next elite call-up order.

After acceptance, the guild hands you the coordinates of the Necromancer General's territory. Solo mode is supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist. Upon clearing, it issues the "Necromancer General Beacon," opening the Twin Generals line (Scenario 7).

### Act 1: Graveyard / Undead Domain Exploration
Cross the poison mist (Constitution (CON) check Difficulty Class (DC) 16, resist Poison), unlock the ancient barrow seal (Knowledge DC 18). Along the way meet cursed dead souls; you may choose to release them (Influence +4) or dispel them (no IP).
- **Revelation Obligation**: The GM must at least hint here at "the Fifth, Jeno's" lineage and the church's dark secrets (discretionary revelation). Exposing the hidden truth +2 Influence Points (IP).

### Act 2: Necromancer General Boss Battle (Three Phases)
**Mourvana Silentgrave (CR 42, Boss battle)**, shifts phase by Health (HP) threshold:
- **Phase 1 "Summon" (100%~66%)**: Each round [Undead Summon] replenishes minions, draining player Area Effect (AOE).
- **Phase 2 "Corpse Tide" (66% shift)**: [Life Siphon] drains HP from corpses and allies; [Death Domain (full-field DoT)].
- **Phase 3 "Lich-form" (33% shift)**: [Critical Hit Immunity] invincible until core exposed; must break the "Life-preserving Coffin (breakable object)" to lift immunity; [Final Dirge] countdown mechanic (break the core within the limit).

### Act 3: The Fifth's Lineage Foreshadowing
During/after the battle, Jeno appears. If the player strikes his loneliness with "You are not one of the Four Heavenly Kings, you are the wronged Fifth," it triggers a defection side quest (large Influence + Scenario 8 assist), this being the comedy / potential turncoat point.

## III. Non-Player Character (NPC) Sample Dialogue
**Cleric Lina Gentlelight**: "Resurrection costs money... I always felt something was wrong. This investigation, please let me accompany you to find the truth."
**Necromancer General Mourvana Silentgrave**: "The dead crave rest, not to be roused for war. If you understand this gentleness... perhaps we need not be enemies." (gloomy, gentle)
**Jeno**: "The Fifth, remember the Fifth! ... W-what wronged, I don't know what you're talking about!" (flustered tsukkomi)
