# Scenario 2: Abyssal Echo (CL13–26 · Dead Souls, Raging Tides, and Lava)

> ※ This chapter is the leveling-period overview; actual combat relies on the D1–D8 modules and is not a missing page.

**Run Method**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)," with design baseline = a 4-person party. Player-count scaling rules are the same as the prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → CR −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → CR +2 / ×1.5 / concurrent +1).

> **Positioning Note**: Scenario 2 is the CL13–26 leveling-period overview, stringing three mid-tier dungeons and themed commissions into the leveling rhythm of "from the undead catacomb to the depths of the volcano." Specific rooms, positioning diagrams, and minor boss stats are detailed in each `D*` dungeon module.

---

## I. Opening Description
With Rank pushed to C, you are no longer a novice at the village gate. The Slumbering Tomb sealed beneath the Haunted Port, the Sea Siren's domain in the eastern raging tides, the Balrog Lord deep in the northern volcano — the world's mid-tier threats begin to take shape. On this road, you will first touch the foreshadowing of "the Fifth, Jeno's" origins (see D2 and later Scenario 6).

## II. Scene Flow
### Act Zero: Accept Leveling-Period Commissions (Opening Beat)
This scenario covers **CL13–26**, composed of three dungeons and their corresponding themed commissions:
- **D2 Slumbering Tomb** (Rank C/D, CL 13–18): Requires first taking commission C-Sea1 Ghost Ship Expedition to unlock the entrance; undead type.
- **D5 Ocean** (Rank D/C, CL 13–18): The raging-tide Sea Siren domain.
- **D6 Volcano** (Rank C/B, CL 19–26): The Balrog Lord deep in the lava.
> Solo mode supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick. Each dungeon's entry method is in its module's "How to Enter (Opening Beat)."

### Act One: D2 Slumbering Tomb (CL13–18)
Unlock the Haunted Port, delve into the undead catacomb, defeat the minor boss **Graveyard Lich (CR18)**. See `scenario/D2_沉眠墓窖.md` and `map/D2_*.yaml`.

### Act Two: D5 Ocean (CL13–18)
The eastern raging tides, the Sea Siren's archipelago caves; defeat the minor boss **Sea Siren (CR18)**. See `scenario/D5_海洋.md` and `map/D5_海洋.yaml` / `map/D5_戰鬥_海妖.yaml`.

### Act Three: D6 Volcano (CL19–26)
The volcanic underground palace ruled by the Balrog Lord; defeat the minor boss **Balrog Lord (CR26)**. See `scenario/D6_火山.md` and `map/D6_火山.yaml` / `map/D6_戰鬥_炎魔領主.yaml`.

### Act Four: Eve of the Demon General Approach
At the end of CL26, Rank pushed to B→A; Influence Points (IP) approach 40, foreshadowing the Twin Generals reckoning (Scenario 7) is imminent.

## III. Stage Design
- **Stage A Catacomb**: D2 undead exploration + unlock (CL13–18).
- **Stage B Raging Tides**: D5 Ocean minor boss (CL13–18).
- **Stage C Lava**: D6 Volcano minor boss (CL19–26).
- **Chapter-end Turn**: Rank pushed to A; confirm the "Fifth's origins" foreshadowing is laid; foreshadow the Demon General reckoning beginning after CL40.

### Interlude Cutscenes and Side Characters (pure narrative, no mechanical effect)
- **[Cutscene] The Necromancer General's Requiem**: When delving to the deepest level of D2 Slumbering Tomb, the Necromancer General Morvana does not strike immediately, but soothes the resentful spirits in the cave with a Requiem — she whispers "I am only gathering the dead for this land," planting a personality foreshadowing for the "reconciliation route" of the Scenario 6 Necromancer General Battle (pure cutscene, no combat triggered).
- **[NPC] Run, Night-sparrow Spy**: An information broker dispatched by the temple of Night-sparrow Shadow-seam, the Thief Deity, often lurking in the dark corners of the Haunted Port tavern. He sells the player the inside story of the "resurrection economy": the guild advocates charging for resurrection, the temple advocates free relief, and the two sides are secretly wrestling — echoing this game's "Resurrection Fee Controversy" main line, which players can later take a stance on in the 12.4 Temple Favorability system.
