# Dungeon Module D8 — Ancient Ruins

> **Recommended Rank**: A/S (Challenge Rating (CR) 35–40) | **Theme**: Lost civilization ruins, top-tier resource farming
> **Access**: Guild commission "A-Ruin1 / S-Ruin1" (corresponding to Rank A→S, CL 35–40); or designated by the GM as a leveling ground.
> **Map**: `maps/D8_古代遺跡.yaml` ＋ `maps/D8_古代遺跡.svg`
> This work has **no multiclass**; solo entry follows the player-count adjustment rules (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no allies).

### How to Enter (Opening Beat)
- **Take a guild commission**: At the Adventurers' Guild bulletin board, accept the Rank A "Ruins Subjugation" (A-Ruin1); after promotion, accept S-Ruin1 (corresponding to Rank A→S, CL 35–40).
- **Active exploration**: After joining at S0, players may delve deep into the lost ruins from the Archaeological Camp (GM Book 17.1).
- **Leveling role**: The top choice for resource farming at CL 35–40; run alongside A/S commissions to grind Rank from A up to S, and face the Demon Generals (CL40+).
- **Solo mode**: Supported at Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5, no allies; the minor-boss room includes a 10×10 positioning map (`maps/D8_戰鬥_遺跡守護者.yaml`). Entering the room switches to standard combat (Chapter 7); after clearing, return to the exploration round.

## I. Background

The ruins of a lost civilization lie buried beneath the yellow sands, where gargoyles and golems still patrol the royal tomb from a thousand years past, and the Ruin Guardian reflects all incoming attacks with its Guardian Stance. Expedition parties seldom return, and the guild lists this as an A→S rank subjugation target; the Ruin Guardian within the sanctuary is this ruin's minor boss.

## III. Key Room Details

- **R3 Collapsed Staircase (weak)**: Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); triggers 1d6 Bludgeon. Disable DC14.
- **R8 Trap Courtyard (strong)**: Perception DC18; triggers 3d6 Pierce. The crossbow wall can block the R7↔R14 passage (GM discretion).
- **R14 Rune Trap Hall (strong)**: Perception DC20; triggers 3d6 Arcane. Runes can block the R13↔R15 passage (GM discretion).
- **R18 Falling-Rock Rear Hall (strong)**: Perception DC20; triggers 3d6 Bludgeon.
- **R5 Priest Burial Vault**: Open chest DC12; trap 30% (moderate, 2d6). Contains Ancient Coins ×2, Glint Shard, Gold (G) 300~480.
- **R9 Golden Reliquary**: Open chest DC14; contains Gold Ornaments ×2, Fine materials, Gold (G) 320~500.
- **R21 Exit Gateway**: After clearing, return to the guild to settle rewards and leave.

## IV. Minor Boss — Ruin Guardian (CR40)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 boss framework (simplified version):

- **Health (HP)** 180 (CR40 formula) | **ATK Bonus (ATK)** +11 (floor(1+0.25×40)) | **Evasion (EVA)** 16 (10+floor(0.15×40)) | **Damage/hit** 54 (floor(6+1.2×40))
- **Trait (1 phase)**: `Guardian Stance`—once every 3 rounds, reflects 30% of damage taken back to the attacker.
- **Weakness**: Lightning ×1.5 (see Monster Compendium note).
- **Drop**: `Heart of the Ruins` (story item, redeemable at the guild for Influence Points (IP) +5); Fine materials + low chance of Fine recipe.
