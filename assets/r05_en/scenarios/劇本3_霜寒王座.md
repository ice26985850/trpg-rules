# Scenario 3: Frost Throne (CL27–34 · the Aloof Queen of the Ice Plains)

> ※ Note: This is the leveling-phase master chapter; actual play relies on the D1–D8 modules and is not a missing page.

**How to Run**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + party-size adjustment rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. Party-size adjustment rules are the same as the Prologue (1 PC → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 PCs → baseline; 4 PCs → baseline; 5–6 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / concurrent events +1).

> **Positioning Note**: Scenario 3 is the master chapter of the CL27–34 leveling phase, centered on D7 Ice Plains. Specific rooms, positioning diagrams, and mini-boss stats are detailed in `劇本/D7_冰原.md`.

---

## I. Opening Description
Rank A, you stand at the northern edge of the world. Upon the ice plains throne, the Frost Queen rules this silent snow realm with absolute zero. This is the last stretch of "pure level-grinding" before the Demon General reckoning—when you emerge from the ice plains, your Rank will be pushed to S, and the Demon Generals' call-up orders already await you at guild headquarters.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act 0: Take on the Leveling-phase Commission (Opening Beat)
This scenario covers **CL27–34**, centered on:
- **D7 Ice Plains** (Rank B/A, CL 27–34): frozen snow realm, the Frost Queen's territory.
> Solo mode is supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist. See `劇本/D7_冰原.md` "How to Enter (Opening Beat)" for entry.

### Act 1: Ice Plains Outskirts (CL27–30)
Cross the blizzard patrol line and purge the frost minions (Frost Wolves / Ice Crystal Golems), accumulating battle merits to push to Rank A.

### Act 2: Ice Plains Depths (CL31–34)
Delve into the throne ice cavern and defeat the mini-boss **Frost Queen (CR34)**. See `劇本/D7_冰原.md` and `maps/D7_冰原.yaml` / `maps/D7_戰鬥_冰霜女王.yaml`.

### Act 3: Eve of the Demon General Threshold
At CL34 end, Rank A→S; Influence Points (IP) accumulate to the 40 threshold. Guild headquarters officially opens the elite "Four Demon Generals Subjugation" call-up order to you—the main-story reckoning from Scenario 5 begins.

## III. Stage Design
- **Stage A Snow Line**: Ice Plains outskirts patrol battle (CL27–30).
- **Stage B Throne**: D7 Ice Plains mini-boss Frost Queen (CL31–34).
- **Chapter-end Twist**: Rank pushed to S; IP reaches 40; unlocks Scenario 5 Dragon General Subjugation order.

### Interlude Cutscenes & Side Characters (purely narrative, does not affect mechanics)
- **[Cutscene] Dragon General's Messenger**: Around CL30, a silver-armored messenger bursts into the Frost Throne battlefield, delivering Dragon General Ignisia Flamescale's taunt letter—"To survive this long on the ice plains shows some skill; but the winds of Dragonridge Peak will scatter your arrogance." This is a prelude to Scenario 5 Dragon General Battle, hinting the Dragon General already knows the players' whereabouts.
- **[NPC] Wind-displacement Hunting Lodge Guide, Elano**: An elf devotee of the Azure Wolf, Displacement (Hunter deity), stationed at the hunting lodge on the D7 frost-forest edge. She marks the snowfield blind spots for players with the "Eye of Displacement," and in casual talk spreads the Azure Wolf Deity's doctrine of "respecting the wilderness, not wanton killing," echoing the Hunter bonus of the 12.x Guardian Deity Grace.
