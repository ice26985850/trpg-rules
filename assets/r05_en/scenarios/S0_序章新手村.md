# S0 Prologue: Tutorial Village (Tutorial Village Trial)

**Run Method**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)" — instead of writing a separate version for each player count, the GM scales difficulty and event volume on the spot using the table below. Design baseline = a 4-person party.

### Player-Count Scaling Rules (for N players)
| Players | Enemy Challenge Rating (CR) | Enemy Count | Resource Supply | Concurrent Events | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (solo) | Baseline −3 | ×0.5 (min 1) | +50% | 1 | Resource supply +50% (no sidekick, see Chapter 8) |
| 2 | Baseline −1 | ×0.75 | +20% | 1 | — |
| 3 | Baseline ±0 | ×1 | Baseline | 2 | — |
| 4 (baseline) | Baseline | ×1 | Baseline | 2–3 | — |
| 5–6 | Baseline +2 | ×1.5 | −10~−20% | 3–4 | Can add +1 concurrent event |

> **Solo Exclusive (no sidekick)**: In this game's solo mode, **no assisting characters are provided** (no Sidekick; see Chapter 8); difficulty is instead supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5, and players must self-assess their role using the aptitude table.
> **Difficulty Guideline**: After adjustment, ensure "the lowest-level character can clear it, the highest-level has a challenge" — do not let enemy Challenge Rating (CR) exceed Character Level (CL) by too much. All adjustments compare "total player levels" against the encounter difficulty budget (Easy ×0.5 / Standard ×1.0 / Hard ×1.5 / Boss fight ≈ Boss CR ≈ party total level ×0.6).

> **★ This is the first step of the journey ★**
> This chapter is the opening move of the entire campaign. **Act One (Temple Class Selection)** and **Act Four (Guild Enrollment)** are two mandatory checkpoints that cannot be skipped — without a class, you cannot fight monsters; without a guild badge, you cannot take commissions. The GM must clearly tell players at the start: first the temple, then the guild, then adventure. See the full flow in "Prologue: Alno Holy City (Hub)".

## I. Opening Description
In the Demon Lord Calendar year 1024, at "Dawn Village" on the border of the Altia Continent (Eltyria). The player plays a traveler who calls themselves a Hero-in-Self-Declaration, arriving at the village entrance with a pack on their back. The Village Chief tells them: the northern Demon General stirs, and the Adventurer's Guild is recruiting newcomers. To become a Hero recognized by the world, one must first go to the Class Temple in Alno Holy City, where a deity personally grants a Class.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act One [Mandatory]: Self-declared Hero → Obtain Class at the Temple (Narrative Ritual)
The player travels to the Class Temple "Seven-Oracle Hall" in Alno Holy City, and through a Conviction check obtains a class, binding their Guardian Deity and Temple Favorability. This is the true first step of the journey — without a class, you cannot pass the later guild enrollment exam.

**★ Top Clarification A (must emphasize verbally and in writing)**: This game has **no multiclass**. "Promotion" refers to stage advancement within a single class — when Class Level (ClassLv) reaches 11 and 21, you advance within that class, rather than stacking a second class. The GM must explain this clearly at the ritual site.

#### Scene Description: Seven-Oracle Hall
Seven shrines surround a central circular ritual platform, with a seven-pointed star pattern inlaid on the floor, each point aimed at a shrine. Within each shrine stands a statue of that class's Guardian Deity, holding an unlit class emblem in its hands. When a candidate steps onto the platform, the corresponding shrine grows slightly warm — the deity is "choosing the person," walking along with your heart. Presiding over the ritual is the guide-priest Old Mel: "Child, the gods do not look at how strong you are, only at why you raise this emblem."

#### Seven Class Grant Phrases and Core Roles
| Class | Guardian Deity | Divine Grant Phrase | Core Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Gordan, Shield of Iron Oath | "The shield stands where the man stands. Before you trust your back to a comrade, first make sure you stand firm enough." | Front-row tank / high Health (HP) and Armor Class (AC), absorbs damage at the very front |
| **Monk** | Ran, Tempest Fist Sovereign | "The wind needs no reason to strike your face. Neither do your fists." | Melee burst / unarmed or light weapons up close, chaining hits for instant high damage |
| **Mage** | Eryon, Starlight Sage | "The stars have long since written the answer; you need only learn to speak it." | Ranged spell turret / large area, high burst, but fragile |
| **Cleric** | Lia, Merciful Light Matron | "Wounds speak; I teach you to listen." | Healing and holy support / the core of party sustainability |
| **Thief** | Vera, Shadow-fingered Trickster | "Locks never refuse a gentle hand, only a reckless one." | Agile sneak attacks and lockpicking / stealth, sneak attacks, and trap disarming |
| **Hunter** | Tucker, Eye of the Wild | "The forest remembers every footstep — including yours." | Ranged physical and traps / bow and crossbow fire, tracking, trap-laying |
| **Dancer** | Serphi, Chaotic Sound | "One turn of the step, and the battle turns with it." | Crowd control and party buffs / dance steps apply crowd-control states and party-wide buffs (Buff) |

> Role tags are only for players to quickly understand "what I do in the party," not hard limits. Class Level (ClassLv) 11/21 promotion branches further refine the feel (e.g., Warrior → Guardian / Berserker), but still within the same class.

#### Class Selection Ritual Flow
1. **Motivational Dialogue (Influence Points (IP) hook entry)**: The GM asks the player to state "why you raise this emblem." If they choose the motivation "to protect others rather than for power," the GM records **+2 Influence Points (IP)** (can carry into Act Four or later side stories).
2. **Conviction Check**: Make a **Difficulty Class (DC) 12 Conviction check**, bonus = Charisma Modifier (M_CHA) + Proficiency (Prof).
   - **Success**: The corresponding shrine lights up, the class emblem falls from the statue's hands, caught by the player; at the same time, that class's Guardian Deity grace is bound.
   - **Failure**: The emblem falls back dark onto the platform; Old Mel smiles: "Try again; the gods do not rush anyone." You may **immediately reroll** — it does not kill, nor does it make the situation worse.
   > **Design Intent**: The DC12 threshold is deliberately low, ensuring almost everyone obtains a class within 1–2 tries. The pacing is left to "which one to choose," not "whether you can choose."

#### Post-selection State
- **Class Emblem**: The class field on the character sheet is filled in, gaining that class's level 1 (ClassLv 1) abilities.
- **Guardian Deity Grace**: The passive grace takes effect immediately; the active ability is once per day, triggered/recovered during "rest" (see GM Book 12.x).
- **Temple Favorability baseline**: Temple Favorability (0–100) starts at **40–50 (neutral, baseline)**, affecting later resurrection fee discounts and temple side-story unlocks (see GM Book 12.5).
- **★ Lifetime Single-class Declaration**: Once selected, it is locked; **you cannot multiclass into other classes**; later at ClassLv 11 and 21 you branch and deepen in the form of "promotion," a road choice within the same class.

#### GM Tips
- **Guiding hesitant players**: First ask "how do you want to help the party?" rather than "what do you want to play." Let them hear the seven divine grant phrases, and use "which phrase moved you" as the decision anchor.
- **No difference in solo mode (no sidekick) class selection**: Solo class selection is exactly the same as multiplayer, purely based on player preference; the selection mechanic itself does not change by player count.
- **Link with Act Four "Guild Enrollment"**: The class emblem obtained at selection is one of the enrollment credentials; the Influence Points (IP) earned at selection (protection motivation +2) can be carried directly into Act Four. At the end of selection, the GM reminds verbally: "Your emblem and these 2 Influence Points will follow you to the guild."

### Act Two: Tutorial Village Exploration
Dawn Village can be freely explored: the smithy (Basic Shop for entry-level gear), the herb garden (gathering), the tavern (intel: hearsay of the "Fifth" legend), and villager commissions (side stories).
- Side story "Lost Lamb": Search for a lost child (Exploration check Difficulty Class (DC) 10). Completion grants +3 Influence Points (IP), Experience (EXP) reward.
- Side story "Night Thief": Drive off mountain rats stealing vegetables (minor combat, Mountain Rat Challenge Rating (CR) 1 ×2).

### Act Three: Goblin Raid Encounter
At night, a Goblin squad attacks the village warehouse. This is the player's first real combat, verifying hit determination (d20 + Hit Bonus ≥ Evasion (EVA)) and Action Point (AP) economy.

### Act Four [Mandatory]: Adventurer's Guild Enrollment Exam (must be written)
After the battle, guild apprentice officer "Jeno" appears, presiding over the F→E promotion exam: a combat simulation (training dummies and phantom enemies) + commission settlement. The examiner is in fact disguised as "the Fifth, Jeno," planting the foreshadowing of his defection. Upon passing, you receive the Apprentice (F) rank, officially setting foot on the path of subjugation / reconciliation.

## III. Non-Player Character (NPC) Dialogue Examples
**Guild Master Grace Silversign**: "Self-declared Hero? Hah, these days anyone dares to shout that. But… I charged in the same way back then. Go to the temple, get your class, then come back to me."
**Apprentice Officer Jeno (disguised)**: "The exam rules are simple — don't die. By the way, I'm not one of the Four Demon Generals; I'm the Fifth. Got it?" (correcting with a straight face)
**Villager Auntie**: "Young one, help me find my little granddaughter, will you? She ran off toward the herb garden…"

## V. Stage Design
- **Stage A Temple Ritual**: Social / check, no combat (DC 12 Conviction check).
- **Stage B Village Exploration**: Exploration, includes 2 side stories (can run in parallel).
- **Stage C Goblin Night Raid**: Combat, map 8×6 grid, front/back row spacing 2 grids (4m).
- **Stage D Guild Exam**: Combat + social settlement (F→E gate).

## VI. Rewards
- Experience (EXP): First battle +120/person; exam passed +300/person; side stories total +200.
- Gold: 80 G total.
- Items: Wooden practice sword (weapon), Healing Potion ×3, Apprentice Guild Badge (entry credential).

## VII. Next Step After Clearing
You have chosen your class and hung the guild apprentice badge (Rank F). There are two paths ahead, and the GM can let players choose the order:
1. **Return to the holy city guild to take F/E commissions** (see Commission Catalog F-For1/F-For2, E-For1/E-San1), accumulating battle merits and Influence Points (IP).
2. **Spontaneously explore D1 Goblin Lair** (the abandoned mine outside the city, Rank F/E, Challenge Rating (CR) 1–8), farming resources and levels.
Goal: grind Rank from F to E, push Character Level (CL) to 5+, and only then will the guild issue the "Subjugate the First Demon General · Dragon General" conscription order (Scenario 5 Dragon General Battle). In solo mode, support with CR −3 and enemy count ×0.5, and **provide no assisting characters**. See the full flowchart in "Prologue: Alno Holy City (Hub)".
