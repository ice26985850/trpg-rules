# Scenario 8: Demon Lord Castle (Abyssal Oath Castle · Proof of the End)

**Run Method**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. The player-count scaling rules are the same as the Prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / +1 concurrent event).

> **Difficulty Guideline**: The Demon Lord is a Legendary~Mythic Boss battle; design Challenge Rating (CR) ≈ party total level × 0.6 (at 4-person Character Level (CL) 50, roughly Challenge Rating (CR) 50). After adjustment, ensure "lowest level (CL46 after clearing the four generals) can clear, highest level (CL50) gets a Legendary challenge". Demon Lord CR50 maintained; the four Demon Generals' thresholds have all been raised to ≥40 (Dragon 40 / Necro 42 / Charm 44 / Gel 46), see GM Book 12.3.
> **Ending unlock condition**: Before the battle begins, settle total Influence Points (IP). **Total IP ≥ 40** → unlocks "Reconciliation Ending"; IP 40–59 basic reconciliation, IP ≥ 60 true reconciliation (all generals defect, golden Hero's Proof awarded). IP < 40 → only "Subjugation Ending".

---

## I. Opening Description
Collecting the four Demon General beacons (whether by subjugation or reconciliation), the barrier of Demon Lord Castle "Abyssal Oath Castle (R5)" lifts and the castle officially opens. Above the floating fortress, a Demon Realm rift whispers. Demon Lord Vercellia·Nornheim sits upon the throne, unexpectedly sneaking a bite of dessert — the proud yet lonely contrast is the narrative premise for possible reconciliation.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act 0: Accept the Final Subjugation Commission (opening beat)
At the Adventurers' Guild headquarters you accept the final commission to "subjugate the Demon Lord" — **requires Influence Points (IP) ≥40**, and you must first accept the two S-tier prerequisite commissions S-1 / S-2 (Rank S, IP≥40 to begin) before proceeding. This condition is the grand tally of the road fought so far: joining in the Prologue, Scenario 5 Dragon General, Scenario 6 Necro General, Scenario 7 dual generals — every battle's merit converts to IP, and only after reaching 40 do you get to stand before Demon Lord Castle's gate.

After accepting, the dark shadow of Demon Lord Castle looms, and the finale begins. Solo mode is supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, with no sidekick. The ending is determined by final IP: ≥40 peace route, ≥60 true-peace route (see Chapter 14).

### Act 1: Open Demon Lord Castle
Embed the four beacons into the barrier hub (Difficulty Class (DC) 24 ritual check). If a Demon General was recruited (defected), they become a Scenario 8 ally unit (lowering the chapter's Challenge Rating (CR) or providing special support).
- **Influence Points (IP) settlement point**: The GM formally settles total Influence Points (IP) here, determining the ending branch (see Section VII).

### Act 2: Final Boss — Demon Lord (three phases)
**Vercellia·Nornheim (CR 50, Boss battle)**:
- **Phase 1 "Sovereign" (100%~66%)**: 【Four Generals' Echo】 rotates Dragon / Charm / Necro / Slime power, changing attribute each phase.
- **Phase 2 "True Demon Lord Form" (66% transition)**: 【Sovereign Domain (field-wide debuff)】 + 【Four Elements Annihilation (rotating AOE)】.
- **Phase 3 "The End" (33% transition)**: 【Judgment of the End】 field-wide instant-death threat countdown; players must punch through the core within the limit, creating the "rightful path" final climax.

### Act 3: The Fifth's Reversal (influenced by IP)
**Jeno·Gojo (CR 45, hidden / reversal boss)** intervenes at the End phase:
- **Branch · Atonement**: If early affinity / Influence Points (IP) are sufficient (recommended having triggered a defection sidequest + total IP met), turns into 【Atonement Burst】 ally support, weakening the Demon Lord.
- **Branch · Despair**: Otherwise 【Despair Burst】 fully opens, becoming the hardest fight (GM may put them on the field together with the Demon Lord to apply pressure).

## V. Stage Design
- **Stage A Castle-opening Ritual**: Social / Ritual check (DC 24), Influence Points (IP) settlement gate.
- **Stage B Demon Lord Boss**: Three phases; key is punching through the core within the 【Judgment of the End】 limit.
- **Stage C The Fifth's Reversal**: Branch difficulty and ending direction determined by Influence Points (IP).
- **Dual-track ending**: Subjugation (defeat) or Reconciliation (total Influence Points (IP) ≥ 40 unlocks).
