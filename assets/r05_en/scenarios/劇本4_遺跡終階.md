# Scenario 4: Ruin Apex (CL35–40 · Ancient Guardian and the Demon General Threshold)

> ※ Note: This is the leveling-phase master chapter; actual play relies on the D1–D8 modules and is not a missing page.

**How to Run**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + party-size adjustment rules)", with a design baseline = 4-person party. Party-size adjustment rules are the same as the Prologue (1 PC → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) −1 / ×0.75; 3 PCs → baseline; 4 PCs → baseline; 5–6 PCs → Challenge Rating (CR) +2 / ×1.5 / concurrent events +1).

> **Positioning Note**: Scenario 4 is the final chapter of the CL35–40 leveling phase, centered on D8 Ancient Ruins, the bridge from "level-grinding" into "Demon General reckoning." Specific rooms, positioning diagrams, and mini-boss stats are detailed in `劇本/D8_古代遺跡.md`.

---

## I. Opening Description
Rank S, you stand at the entrance of an ancient ruin. This structure, older than the kingdom, sleeps the "Ruin Guardian" who protects the embers of civilization. Break through it, and you are not just a powerful adventurer—but a "subjugator" acknowledged by the guild, worthy to stand before the Four Demon Generals. The Demon Generals' presence already closes in from all corners of the map.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act 0: Take on the Leveling-phase Final Commission (Opening Beat)
This scenario covers **CL35–40**, centered on:
- **D8 Ancient Ruins** (Rank A/S, CL 35–40): ancient civilization ruins, guardian guards the deepest part.
> Solo mode is supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist. See `劇本/D8_古代遺跡.md` "How to Enter (Opening Beat)" for entry.

### Act 1: Ruin Outer Corridor (CL35–37)
Pass the trap corridors and spirit-guard legion, decode the ancient inscriptions (Knowledge check Difficulty Class (DC) 22).

### Act 2: Ruin Deep Core (CL38–40)
Deep-core shrine, defeat the mini-boss **Ruin Guardian (CR40)**—the strongest non-Demon-General enemy of the leveling phase, strength already near the Demon General threshold. See `劇本/D8_古代遺跡.md` and `maps/D8_古代遺跡.yaml` / `maps/D8_戰鬥_遺跡守護者.yaml`.

### Act 3: Step into the Main Story (leads to Scenario 5)
CL40 maxed, Rank S stable, Influence Points (IP) ≥40. Guild headquarters issues you the first Demon General call-up order—**"Subjugate the First Demon General · Dragon General."** The leveling phase officially ends; Scenario 5 main-story reckoning begins.

## III. Stage Design
- **Stage A Outer Corridor**: Ruin traps + spirit-guard legion (CL35–37).
- **Stage B Deep Core**: D8 mini-boss Ruin Guardian (CL38–40).
- **Chapter-end Twist**: CL40 maxed; leads to Scenario 5 Dragon General Subjugation order; prologue to the Four Demon Generals reckoning.

### Interlude Cutscenes & Side Characters (purely narrative, does not affect mechanics)
- **[Cutscene] Guardian's Whisper**: Arriving at the deepest part of D8 Ancient Ruins, the Ruin Guardian does not block but whispers in an ancient tongue its reason for guarding—"This ruin seals the 'true history' after the Demon Lord War, not the version spread by the Seven Temples." This whisper sets up the identity reveal of S4 (Ancient Ruins depths / Ruin line).
- **[NPC] Ironforge City Lord, Durin**: Dwarf King Durin sees players off at the ruin exit, leaving parting words "The guide north of Dragonridge Peak is an old acquaintance of mine; if in danger, invoke my name." This pre-establishes the bond between players and the Scenario 5 Dragonridge Peak guide, making the D8 graduation and Scenario 5 handoff more coherent.
