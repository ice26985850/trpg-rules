# Scenario 1: Border Trial (CL1–12 · From Novice Leaving the Village to First Glimpse of the Demon General's Presence)

> ※ This chapter is the leveling-period overview; actual combat relies on the D1–D8 modules and is not a missing page.

**Run Method**: This scenario uses "Method A (one scenario + player-count scaling rules)," with design baseline = a 4-person party. Player-count scaling rules are the same as the prologue (1 player → Challenge Rating (CR) −3 / enemy count ×0.5; 2 players → CR −1 / ×0.75; 3 players → baseline; 4 players → baseline; 5–6 players → CR +2 / ×1.5 / concurrent +1).

> **Positioning Note**: Scenario 1 is the CL1–12 leveling-period overview; it does not write independent combat itself, but strings the already-built dungeons and guild commissions into a rhythm of "leave the village → farm levels → approach the Demon General threshold." Specific rooms, positioning diagrams, and minor boss stats are detailed in each `D*` dungeon module.

---

## I. Opening Description
In the prologue (S0) you chose your class, enrolled, and received your first F-tier commission. The world outside Alno Holy City has just opened to you — goblins in the abandoned mine still bind children, a self-styled king lurks deep in the woods, and an ancient ruler sleeps beneath the Yellow Sands Ruins. This is not the beginning of a heroic epic, just the path every adventurer has walked: farm levels, change ranks, grow stronger.

## II. Scene Flow
### Act Zero: Accept Leveling-Period Commissions (Opening Beat)
You take F / E tier commissions on the Adventurer's Guild bulletin board, or go explore outside the city yourself. This scenario covers **CL1–12**, composed of three dungeons and their corresponding themed commissions:
- **D1 Goblin Lair** (Rank F/E, CL 1–8): The top choice for familiar farming after the prologue.
- **D3 Forest** (Rank F/E, CL 1–6): Green woods low-to-mid tier leveling.
- **D4 Desert** (Rank E/D, CL 7–12): Yellow Sands Ruins mid-tier leveling.
> Solo mode supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick. Each dungeon's specific entry method is in its module's "How to Enter (Opening Beat)."

### Act One: D1 Goblin Lair (CL1–8)
Subjugate the abandoned-mine goblins, rescue the children, and defeat the minor boss **Goblin King (CR5)**. See `scenario/D1_哥布林巢穴.md` and `map/D1_*.yaml` / `map/D1_戰鬥_*.yaml`.

### Act Two: D3 Forest (CL1–6)
The ancient-tree throne of the green woods; defeat the minor boss **Goblin King (CR5)** (forest tribe version). See `scenario/D3_森林.md` and `map/D3_森林.yaml` / `map/D3_戰鬥_哥布林王.yaml`.

### Act Three: D4 Desert (CL7–12)
The underground palace of the Yellow Sands Ruins; defeat the minor boss **Sand King (CR12)**. See `scenario/D4_沙漠.md` and `map/D4_沙漠.yaml` / `map/D4_戰鬥_沙王.yaml`.

### Act Four: Approaching the Demon General Threshold
At the end of CL12, guild battle merits push your rank to Rank E→D; only when you accumulate Rank S (Influence Points (IP) ≥40) does the Dragon General subjugation order (Scenario 5) unlock (the CL40 leveling-period end usually meets it naturally, consistent with the Rank S acceptance threshold). This scenario ends at "establishing the leveling rhythm and clarifying the next-stage goal."

## III. Stage Design
- **Stage A Leave the Village**: F/E commission + D1 exploration (CL1–8).
- **Stage B Woods**: D3 Forest minor boss (CL1–6).
- **Stage C Ruins**: D4 Desert minor boss (CL7–12).
- **Chapter-end Turn**: Rank pushed to D; confirm the "CL1–40 leveling period" route, foreshadowing the Demon General reckoning beginning after CL40.

### Interlude Cutscenes and Side Characters (pure narrative, no mechanical effect)
- **[Cutscene] The Fifth's Strange Song**: Around CL3, in the depths of D3 Forest the player hears a song belonging to no known traveler — Ayane Rainbow-echo, the Bard Deity, plays a off-key strange melody on the Rainbow-echo Lute, seemingly hinting at "a seventh, the 'Fifth' who is not among the Seven Gods." The song ends with a light laugh, leaving the player a foreshadowing of the mystery of origins (revealed in the latter part of Scenario 6).
- **[NPC] Old Mother of Dawn Village**: The actual commissioner of the D1 Goblin Lair commission, the oldest weaver in Dawn Village. With her sun-spoiled wheat ale and the words "young one, don't be like those adventurers who only care about money," she gives the player their first substantial commission, the first impression many novice players have of "civilian commissions outside the guild."
