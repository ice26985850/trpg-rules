# Dungeon Module D4 — Desert

> **Recommended Rank**: E/D (Challenge Rating (CR) 7–12) | **Theme**: Golden-sand ruins, mid-tier resource farming
> **Acceptance**: Guild commission "E-Sand1 / D-Sand1" (corresponds to Rank E→D, CL 7–12); or designated by GM as a leveling ground.
> **Map**: `maps/D4_沙漠.yaml` ＋ `maps/D4_沙漠.svg`
> This work has **no multiclass**; solo entry follows the player-count scaling rule (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick).

### How to Enter (opening beat)
- **Take guild commission**: At the Adventurers' Guild bulletin board accept the E-tier "Desert Subjugation" (E-Sand1), then after promotion accept D-Sand1 (corresponds to Rank E→D, CL 7–12).
- **Active exploration**: After joining at S0, players may actively explore the desert ruins by the trade road (GM Book 17.1).
- **Leveling role**: The top choice for farming resources at CL 7–12; run alongside E/D commissions to grind Rank from E up to D.
- **Solo mode**: Supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick; the mini-boss room has a 10×10 positioning map (maps/D4_戰鬥_沙王.yaml), entering the room switches to standard combat (Chapter 7), clear the room to return to the exploration round.

## I. Background

In the golden-sand ruins crossing the trade road, a band of sand pirates occupies an ancient altar; their chief, the "Sand King", can trap people in quicksand and manipulate sun-scorch. Past caravans often perished in the sea of sand, so the Guild lists it as an E→D tier subjugation target; deep in the altar, the Sand King is this ruins' mini-boss.

## III. Key Room Details

- **R3 Quicksand Shallows (weak)**: Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); triggers 1d6 Slow. Disable DC14.
- **R8 Sun-scorched Path (strong)**: Perception DC18; triggers 2d6 Burn. Mirror reflection can block the R4↔R12 passage (GM ruling).
- **R5 Merchant Remains**: Open chest DC12; trick mine 30% (medium, 2d6). Contains Waterskin ×2, Glint Shard, Gold (G) 50~90.
- **R9 Pottery Vault**: Open chest DC14; contains Spice ×2, Fine Material, Gold (G) 60~110.
- **R13 Exit Sand Path**: After clearing, return to Guild to settle rewards and leave.

## IV. Mini-boss — Sand King (CR12)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 boss framework (simplified version):

- **Health (HP)** 79 (CR12 formula) | **Attack Bonus (ATK)** +4 (floor(1+0.25×12)) | **Evasion (EVA)** 11 (10+floor(0.15×12)) | **Damage/hit** 20 (floor(6+1.2×12))
- **Trait (1 phase)**: `Quicksand Cage` — once every 3 rounds, imprisons our Back Row for 1 round and deals 2d6 Bludgeon.
- **Weakness**: Water / Ice ×1.5 (see Monster Compendium note).
- **Drop**: `Sand King Crown` (story item, redeemable at Guild for Influence Points (IP)+3); Fine Material + low chance of Fine recipe.
