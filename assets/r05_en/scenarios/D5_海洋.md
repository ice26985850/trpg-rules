# Dungeon Module D5 — Ocean

> **Recommended Rank**: D/C (Challenge Rating (CR) 13–18) | **Theme**: Blue-wave reefs, mid-high-tier resource farming
> **Acceptance**: Guild commission "D-Sea1 / C-Sea1" (corresponds to Rank D→C, CL 13–18); or designated by GM as a leveling ground.
> **Map**: `maps/D5_海洋.yaml` ＋ `maps/D5_海洋.svg`
> This work has **no multiclass**; solo entry follows the player-count scaling rule (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick).

### How to Enter (opening beat)
- **Take guild commission**: At the Adventurers' Guild bulletin board accept the D-tier "Ocean Subjugation" (D-Sea1), then after promotion accept C-Sea1 (corresponds to Rank D→C, CL 13–18).
- **Active exploration**: After joining at S0, players may take a small boat from the harbor to the reef waters for exploration (GM Book 17.1).
- **Leveling role**: The top choice for farming resources at CL 13–18; run alongside D/C commissions to grind Rank from D up to C.
- **Solo mode**: Supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no sidekick; the mini-boss room has a 10×10 positioning map (maps/D5_戰鬥_海妖.yaml), entering the room switches to standard combat (Chapter 7), clear the room to return to the exploration round.

## I. Background

Countless merchant ships lie sunk among the blue-wave reefs; the Sea Siren lurks in a tidal pool that emerges only at high tide, capturing passing crew with her enchanting song. Coastal fishing villages have seen repeated disappearances, so the Guild lists it as a D→C tier subjugation target; deep in the tidal pool, the Sea Siren is this sea area's mini-boss.

## III. Key Room Details

- **R3 Undercurrent Corridor (weak)**: Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); triggers 1d6 pull displacement. Disable DC14.
- **R8 Venom-sting Jellyfish Swarm (strong)**: Perception DC18; triggers 2d6 Poison. The jellyfish swarm can block the R3↔R13 passage (GM ruling).
- **R5 Shipwreck Cargo Hold**: Open chest DC12; trick mine 30% (medium, 2d6). Contains Pearl Shell ×2, Glint Shard, Gold (G) 80~140.
- **R9 Shell Mound Vault**: Open chest DC14; contains Coral ×2, Fine Material, Gold (G) 90~150.
- **R14 Exit Waterway**: After clearing, return to Guild to settle rewards and leave.

## IV. Mini-boss — Sea Siren (CR18)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 boss framework (simplified version):

- **Health (HP)** 101 (CR18 formula) | **Attack Bonus (ATK)** +5 (floor(1+0.25×18)) | **Evasion (EVA)** 12 (10+floor(0.15×18)) | **Damage/hit** 27 (floor(6+1.2×18))
- **Trait (1 phase)**: `Tidal Charm` — once every 3 rounds, Charms our Back Row for 1 round (loses action).
- **Weakness**: Lightning ×1.5 (see Monster Compendium note).
- **Drop**: `Sea Siren Scale` (story item, redeemable at Guild for Influence Points (IP)+3); Fine Material + low chance of Fine recipe.
