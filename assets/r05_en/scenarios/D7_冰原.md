# Dungeon Module D7 — Ice Plains (Tundra)

> **Recommended Rank**: B/A (Challenge Rating (CR) 27–34) | **Theme**: Perpetual Frozen Tundra, high-tier resource farming
> **Access**: Guild commission "B-Ice1 / A-Ice1" (corresponding to Rank B→A, CL 27–34); or designated by the GM as a leveling ground.
> **Map**: `maps/D7_冰原.yaml` ＋ `maps/D7_冰原.svg`
> This work has **no multiclass**; solo entry follows the player-count adjustment rules (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no allies).

### How to Enter (Opening Beat)
- **Take a guild commission**: At the Adventurers' Guild bulletin board, accept the Rank B "Ice Plains Subjugation" (B-Ice1); after promotion, accept A-Ice1 (corresponding to Rank B→A, CL 27–34).
- **Active exploration**: After joining at S0, players may delve deep into the Perpetual Frozen Tundra from the Northern Outpost (GM Book 17.1).
- **Leveling role**: The top choice for resource farming at CL 27–34; run alongside B/A commissions to grind Rank from B up to A.
- **Solo mode**: Supported at Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5, no allies; the minor-boss room includes a 10×10 positioning map (`maps/D7_戰鬥_冰霜女王.yaml`). Entering the room switches to standard combat (Chapter 7); after clearing, return to the exploration round.

## I. Background

Beneath the blizzards of the Perpetual Frozen Tundra, the Frost Queen has frozen the entire valley with her Eternal Winter Domain, with yetis and ice wolves as her subjects. Northern villages are gradually frozen and abandoned, and the guild lists this as a B→A rank subjugation target; enthroned above, the Frost Queen is this tundra's minor boss.

## III. Key Room Details

- **R3 Thin Ice Fracture (weak)**: Perception DC12 (Dexterity (M_DEX) / Luck (M_LUK)); triggers 1d6 ice damage. Disable DC14.
- **R8 Ice Spike Corridor (strong)**: Perception DC18; triggers 3d6 Pierce. Ice spikes can block the R7↔R13 passage (GM discretion).
- **R14 Blizzard Trap Zone (strong)**: Perception DC20; triggers 2d6 Frost damage.
- **R5 Frozen Remains**: Open chest DC12; trap 30% (moderate, 2d6). Contains Cold Iron ×2, Glint Shard, Gold (G) 200~320.
- **R9 Ice Crystal Vault**: Open chest DC14; contains Ice Crystal ×2, Fine materials, Gold (G) 220~340.
- **R18 Exit Ice Path**: After clearing, return to the guild to settle rewards and leave.

## IV. Minor Boss — Frost Queen (CR34)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 boss framework (simplified version):

- **Health (HP)** 158 (CR34 formula) | **ATK Bonus (ATK)** +9 (floor(1+0.25×34)) | **Evasion (EVA)** 15 (10+floor(0.15×34)) | **Damage/hit** 46 (floor(6+1.2×34))
- **Trait (1 phase)**: `Eternal Winter Domain`—once every 3 rounds, all allies −2 ATK for 2 rounds.
- **Weakness**: Fire ×1.5 (see Monster Compendium note).
- **Drop**: `Frost Crown` (story item, redeemable at the guild for Influence Points (IP) +4); Fine materials + low chance of Fine recipe.
