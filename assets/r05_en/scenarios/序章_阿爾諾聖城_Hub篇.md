# Prologue: Alno Holy City (Hub) (Town Base Chapter)

> **GM Opening Handbook.** This chapter does not replace any existing content from S0–S4, D1–D2, or the Commission Catalog; it only clarifies "where to start and how each scene connects," so the entire campaign can run from beginning to end.
>
> Companion rules: Temple and Guild see GM Book 12.4; Temple Favorability see GM Book 12.5; Influence Points (IP) endings see Chapter 14; dungeon exploration see Chapter 17; commissions see the Commission Catalog.
>
> **Two Top Clarifications (applied throughout)**: A. This game has **no multiclass**, promotion = single-class ClassLv 11/21 stage advancement; B. Solo mode **provides no assisting characters (no sidekick)**, difficulty supported by Challenge Rating (CR) −3 and enemy count ×0.5 (min 1).

---

## (I) Holy City Overview

Alno Holy City (Arno Sanctum) is the neutral hub of the Altia Continent (Eltyria) — the Class Temple and the Adventurer's Guild headquarters are both in this city. Whether you are a village child just down from the mountains or a survivor who escaped the Demon General's pursuit, the story begins here.

(One-line positioning: the temple grants classes, the guild issues badges, and two dungeons hide outside the city.)

---

## (II) Three Major Facilities and Their Corresponding Story Beats

### 1. The Class Temple
- **Function**: The ritual site where players "choose a class." Aligns with S0 Act One — the seven classes are granted by deities; after passing a Difficulty Class (DC) 12 Conviction (M_CHA) check, the deity appoints one.
- **Beat positioning**: The true first step of the journey. Without this step, you cannot pass the guild enrollment exam.
- **Clarification A (no multiclass)**: This game has **no multiclass**. "Promotion" refers to stage advancement within a single class — when Class Level (ClassLv) reaches 11 and 21, you advance within that class, rather than stacking a second class. The GM must explain this clearly at the ritual site, to avoid players later asking "can I multiclass another one."
- **Related mechanics**: Temple Favorability (GM Book 12.5) 0–100, affects later divine oracle and supply.

### 2. The Adventurer's Guild
- **Function**: Take commissions from the Commission Catalog on the bulletin board, Rank promotion, enrollment exam. Aligns with S0 Act Four — the enrollment exam starts at Rank F, and players must disguise themselves as "the Fifth, Jeno" to pass.
- **Beat positioning**: Immediately come here after choosing a class to hang your badge and get the apprentice emblem, only then are you a formal adventurer.
- **Rank and Acceptance Cap (Character Level (CL))**:

  | Rank | Max Commission CR | Corresponding CL Threshold | Unlocked Content |
  |------|---------------------|-------------|----------|
  | F Apprentice | 1–4 | CL 1–4 | Basic commissions, Basic Shop, temple entry |
  | E | 5–8 | CL 5–8 | Regional subjugation commissions, guild warehouse |
  | D | 9–12 | CL 9–12 | Escort / scout high-tier commissions |
  | C | 13–18 | CL 13–18 | Subjugation bounties, guild discount 5% |
  | B | 19–28 | CL 19–28 | Demon General-related frontline commissions, discount 10% |
  | A | 29–40 | CL 29–40 | Elite bounties, exclusive smithing quota |
  | S | 41+ | IP≥40 | Demon Lord Castle subjugation / full peace authority (requires IP≥40) |

  > Influence Points (IP) also see Chapter 14 "Influence Points (IP) Endings"; Scenario 8 finale requires IP≥40 to accept S-tier commissions.

### 3. Dungeon Gates
- **Abandoned Mine outside the city → D1 Goblin Lair**: Can be spontaneously explored at Rank F/E, Challenge Rating (CR) 1–8, the top choice for low-tier resource farming. 8 rooms including 3 combat rooms (10×10 positioning diagram attached).
- **Haunted Port → D2 Slumbering Tomb**: Requires commission C-Sea1 Ghost Ship Expedition to unlock the entrance, Rank C/D, CR 13–18, undead type. 9 rooms including 3 combat rooms (positioning diagram attached).
- **Six Themed Dungeons (CL1–40 leveling period)**: Run in parallel with the main Demon General battles, serving as a stable leveling source during the leveling period, corresponding to the themed commissions in the Commission Catalog:
  - **D3 Forest (Whispering Woods)**: Rank F/E, CR 1–6, minor boss Goblin King (CR5). Corresponding commissions F-For1 / E-For1.
  - **D4 Desert (Yellow Sands Ruins)**: Rank E/D, CR 7–12, minor boss Sand King (CR12). Corresponding commissions E-San1 / D-San1.
  - **D5 Ocean (Shipwreck Trench)**: Rank D/C, CR 13–18, minor boss Sea Siren (CR18). Corresponding commissions D-Sea1 / C-Sea1.
  - **D6 Volcano (Molten Core Vein)**: Rank C/B, CR 19–26, minor boss Balrog Lord (CR26). Corresponding commissions C-Fire1 / B-Fire1.
  - **D7 Ice Plains (Eternal Winter Throne)**: Rank B/A, CR 27–34, minor boss Frost Queen (CR34). Corresponding commissions B-Ice1 / A-Ice1.
  - **D8 Ancient Ruins (Lost Sealed Sanctum)**: Rank A/S, CR 35–40, minor boss Ruin Guardian (CR40). Corresponding commissions A-Ruin1 / S-Ruin1.
- **Exploration rules**: See GM Book Chapter 17 "Dungeon Exploration".
- **Leveling Period Suggested Table (GM at-a-glance)**:

  | CL Range | Where to Go | Target Rank / CL | Notes |
  |---------|----------|---------------|------|
  | 1–6 | D3 Forest + F/E commissions | F→E (CL 7+) | Familiarize, farm materials |
  | 7–12 | D4 Desert + E/D commissions | E→D | Bridge to D5 |
  | 13–18 | D5 Ocean + D/C commissions | D→C | Includes D2 Slumbering Tomb |
  | 19–26 | D6 Volcano + C/B commissions | C→B | Fire-type leveling |
  | 27–34 | D7 Ice Plains + B/A commissions | B→A | Ice-type leveling |
  | 35–40 | D8 Ancient Ruins + A/S commissions | A→S | End of leveling period, leads to Scenario 5 |
  | 40+ | Scenario 5 Dragon General Battle (CR40) onward, Four Demon Generals reckoning → Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle → Scenario 9–11 Beyond the World | S | Main story finale |

## (III) Full Flowchart (run from beginning to end)

> Rank promotion ladder at a glance: F (S0) → E (D3 Forest) → D (D4 Desert) → C (D5 Ocean) → B (D6 Volcano) → A (D7 Ice Plains) → S (D8 Ancient Ruins, IP≥40) → Scenario 5 Dragon General (CR40) main reckoning → Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle (CR50) → Scenario 9–11 Beyond the World (CL50–99 finale, three chapter finale bosses: Void-realm Sentinel CR65 / Eclipse Sovereign CR75 / Eschaton Prophet CR85 + Singularity CR99).
> Each node includes four items: **How to start / Prerequisites (Rank・CL) / Next step after clearing / Solo mode tip**.

### Node 0 — S0 Prologue: Tutorial Village
- **How to start**: Upon entering the city, first go to the Class Temple to choose a class (Act One), then go to the Adventurer's Guild to enroll (Act Four).
- **Prerequisites**: None (CL 1–4, Rank F after enrollment).
- **Next step after clearing**: Return to the holy city guild to take F/E themed commissions, or spontaneously explore D3 Forest to level up, preparing to farm along the six themed dungeons all the way to CL40 (Scenario 1–4 leveling period).
- **Solo mode tip**: CL 1–4 stage supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; **no sidekick**, GM should not be tempted to insert NPCs.

### Node 1 — Leveling Period Overview (Scenario 1–4 · D1–D8 + themed commissions)
- **How to start**: Take the corresponding themed commission on the guild bulletin board (F-For1/E-For1 → E-San1/D-San1 → D-Sea1/C-Sea1 → C-Fire1/B-Fire1 → B-Ice1/A-Ice1 → A-Ruin1/S-Ruin1), or explore the corresponding dungeon yourself; advance along Scenario 1 (CL1–12) → Scenario 2 (CL13–26) → Scenario 3 (CL27–34) → Scenario 4 (CL35–40).
- **Prerequisites**: Rank F→S (CL 1–40), accepting according to the dungeon's themed corresponding Rank.
- **Next step after clearing**: Accumulate battle merits so the guild recognizes your strength, push Rank from F all the way to S, and accumulate Influence Points (IP) to ≥40, only then can you accept the Dragon General subjugation order (Scenario 5).
- **Solo mode tip**: Each themed dungeon's combat rooms adjusted by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick.

### Node 2 — Scenario 5 Dragon General Battle (CL40)
- **How to start**: Push Rank to S (CL40, IP≥40) during the leveling period, then you can accept the "Subjugate the First Demon General · Dragon General (CR40)" conscription order, heading north to Dragoncrest Peak.
- **Prerequisites**: Rank S (CL 40, IP≥40).
- **Next step after clearing**: After subjugating / persuading the Dragon General, issue the "Dragon General Beacon," opening the promotion path to Scenario 6.
- **Solo mode tip**: This chapter supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick.

### Node 3 — Scenario 6 Necromancer General Battle (CL42)
- **How to start**: Accept the "Subjugate the Second Demon General · Necromancer General (CR42)" commission from guild headquarters (requires Rank S).
- **Prerequisites**: Rank S (CL 42, IP≥40).
- **Next step after clearing**: Issue the "Necromancer General Beacon," opening the Twin Generals line (Scenario 7).
- **Solo mode tip**: CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick.

### Node 4 — Scenario 7 Charm General and Gel General Battle (CL44)
- **How to start**: The guild issues the dual-line commission "Subjugate the Third Demon General · Charm General (CR44) / Fourth Demon General · Gel General (CR46)" (requires Rank S).
- **Prerequisites**: Rank S (CL 44, IP≥40).
- **Next step after clearing**: After clearing both generals, collect the four beacons, accumulate Influence Points (IP) to ≥40, unlock Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle.
- **Solo mode tip**: CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick.

### Node 5 — Scenario 8 Demon Lord Castle (CL46–50, Finale)
- **How to start**: Requires Influence Points (IP) ≥40 to accept S-1 / S-2 and the final commission "Subjugate the Demon Lord (CR50)" (Rank S, CL 46+) at the guild; the Fifth, Jeno (CR45, female human traitor · the Fifth Demon General) appears in reversal at the eschaton stage.
- **Prerequisites**: Rank S (CL 46+) or IP≥40 to start accepting S-tier.
- **Next step after clearing**: Ending determined by IP — IP≥40 peace route, IP≥60 true peace route (see Chapter 14); after Demon Lord Castle collapses, the ground splits open, the Otherworld invades, leading to Scenario 9–11.
- **Solo mode tip**: CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick. For the final battle, be sure to confirm players can sustain without assisting characters.

### Node 6 — Scenario 9–11 Beyond the World (CL50–99 Finale Chapter)
- **How to start**: After clearing Scenario 8, the Otherworld rift appears; accept the "Otherworld Defense" commission at the guild (or the Demon Lord Army temporary joint command HQ) (requires CL≥50).
- **Prerequisites**: Cleared Scenario 8 and CL≥50.
- **Next step after clearing**: Scenario 9 (CL50–65) forward skirmish + finale minor boss Void-realm Sentinel (CR65) → Scenario 10 (CL66–75) deep domain expedition + finale mid-boss Eclipse Sovereign (CR75) → Scenario 11 (CL76–99) first fight the close-body guard Eschaton Prophet (CR85) then the finale boss Beyond the World · Singularity (CR99). Influence Points (IP) ≥60 can trigger the true peace epilogue.
- **Solo mode tip**: CR−3, enemy count ×0.5; no sidekick. Otherworld traits (reality distortion / rule nullification) per GM ruling.

## (IV) GM Opening Monologue Example (can be read aloud)

> "Welcome to Alno Holy City, nobodies. This city is the neutral hub of the Altia Continent, and the headquarters of both the temple and the Adventurer's Guild are right here — which means your legend begins at this city gate.
>
> The opening move is only three steps, don't panic:
> **First, go to the Class Temple.** The deity there will take a look at your soul and throw you a DC12 Conviction check — pass it, and one of the seven classes is appointed by the god, and you do that for life (reminder: this game has no multiclass; once chosen, it's chosen, later you advance within the same class, not stack another).
> **Second, go to the Adventurer's Guild.** Take the deity's recommendation to enroll and hang your badge, starting at Rank F apprentice. The exam is a bit troublesome — you have to disguise yourself as 'the Fifth, Jeno' to slip through; the guild has never actually seen the man anyway.
> **Third, there are six themed dungeons outside the city waiting for you to farm experience**: from the Whispering Woods (D3, accessible at F/E) all the way to the Lost Sealed Sanctum (D8, only enterable at A/S), with the Yellow Sands Ruins, Shipwreck Trench, Molten Core Vein, and Eternal Winter Throne in between. Each dungeon corresponds to the guild's "themed commission," farming levels, gear, and Influence Points (IP) all rely on them. Also, the Goblin Lair (D1) in the abandoned mine and the Slumbering Tomb (D2) in the haunted port remain as extra leveling grounds.
>
> Once you've chosen your class and joined the guild, you're no longer passersby. Whether to take themed commissions to level up, or go straight to the woods to beat goblins, is up to you — but remember, the Dragon General's subjugation order won't be issued until you've farmed through the six themed dungeons, pushed Rank to S, and saved up 40 Influence Points (IP). So, temple first, or guild first?"
