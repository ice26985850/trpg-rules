# D2 Slumbering Tomb (Silent Crypt)

> **Recommended Rank**: C / D (Challenge Rating (CR) 13–18) | **Theme**: Undead tomb, links to S2 Necromancer line
> **Unlock**: Complete guild commission "C-Sea1 Ghost Ship Expedition" to discover the entrance; or opened by GM per story.
> **Map**: `maps/D2_沉眠墓窖.yaml` + `maps/D2_沉眠墓窖.svg`
> This work has **no multiclass**; solo entry follows party-size adjustment rules (GM Book 13.1: Challenge Rating (CR) −3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist).

### How to Enter (Opening Beat)
- **Unlock prerequisite**: First push Rank to C (CL 13+), accept commission **C-Sea1 Ghost Ship Expedition** (recon / exploration) at the guild, investigate the haunted port and discover the Slumbering Tomb entrance; or opened directly by GM per story.
- **Leveling positioning**: Mid-late undead-themed area (CR 13–18), suggested CL 13+; clearing it cuts S2 Necromancer General's outer support and obtains the "Crypt Key" to unlock side quests (see "Prologue · Alno Holy City Hub section").
- **Solo mode**: Supported by CR−3, enemy count ×0.5, no assist; the three combat rooms already include 10×10 positioning diagrams (maps/D2_戰鬥_*.yaml).

## I. Background

The source of the coastal haunted port (C-Sea1 commission) is an ancient tomb beneath a cliff. The Necromancer General **Mourvana (S2 Boss)**'s subordinate "Graveyard Lich" lurks here, building defenses from the dead. Clearing the tomb cuts S2 Necromancer General's outer support and obtains the "Crypt Key" to unlock subsequent side quests.

## III. Key Room Details

- **R4 Spike Trap Room (Mid)**: Perception DC16; trigger 2d6 Pierce. Disarm DC18.
- **R6 Phantom Corridor (Strong)**: Perception DC20; trigger 3d6 Bludgeon + Stun 1 round; phantom can make players misjudge connections (GM may temporarily hide 1 link, investigate DC16 to see through).
- **R3 Burial Corridor**: Open chest DC12; trap 30% (mid, 2d6). Contains: bone amulet ×2, decaying goldware ×3, Gold (G) 200~400.
- **R8 Tomb Owner's Coffin Chamber**: Open coffin DC14; obtain **Crypt Key** (story item, unlocks S2 related side quests) + Epic recipe chance 20%.

## IV. Mini-boss — Graveyard Lich (CR18)

Per GM Book 10.4 CR formula and 10.7 Boss framework (simplified):

- **Health (HP)** 101 (CR18 formula) | **Attack Bonus (ATK)** +5 (floor(1+0.25×18)) | **Evasion (EVA)** 12 (10+floor(0.15×18)) | **Damage/hit** 27 (floor(6+1.2×18))
- **Trait (1 phase)**: `Necro Torrent`—once every 3 rounds, deals damage ×0.8 Shadow AOE to whole party; `Undead Summon`—at Health (HP) ≤50% summons Skeleton Warrior ×2 (CR13).
- **Weakness**: Holy ×1.5 (Cleric's "Holy Purify" can weaken its summon).
- **Drop**: `Crypt Key` (story item) + basic materials + Epic recipe chance 20%.
