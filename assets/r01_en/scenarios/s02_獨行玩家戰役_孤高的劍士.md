# SAO TRPG — Solo Player Campaign: The Lonely Blade

> *"Fighting alone isn't because I don't want a party. It's because — I'm afraid of losing anyone else."*
>
> A solo (1 player + 1 GM) full-campaign path, Lv.1→100. This scenario requires the **Solo Mode Rules Supplement**.

# Vol.I: Town of Beginnings

> **Level**: Lv.1 → Lv.10 | **Floor**: Floors 1-10 | **Sessions**: 4-6

## Chapter 1: Link Start

### Intro (GM reads aloud)

> *"November 6, 2022, 1 PM. You put on the NerveGear and speak the spell that changed everything —"*
> *"Link Start."*
> *"When you open your eyes, you stand in the central plaza of the Town of Beginnings. The sky is a perfect blue. Players around you cheer, run. This is your first time in Aincrad."*

Player creates a Lv.1 character (use Rulebook/02_Character Creation.md + Solo Mode Rules Supplement S2.3 Skill Slot=3).

### Scene 1: First Encounter

Step out of the Town of Beginnings gate; two **Frenzied Boars** appear on the meadow (ref Monster Catalog Lv.2). One charges the player — the other charges a nearby red-scarf player.

**Klein appears**. He swings his blade to repel the boar, turns to the player with a grin:
> *"Yo! You just came out of town too? Name's Klein. Wanna team up? Two's safer than one — though I'm not great at this either."*

**Klein becomes the first companion** (use Solo Mode Rules Supplement §S3.4 Klein data).

### Scene 2: The Death Game

5:30 PM, forced teleport. Blood-red sky. Kayaba Akihiko's declaration.

> *"This isn't a game. The moment your HP hits zero, your brain is fried by the NerveGear."*

GM should build an atmosphere of dread. Klein's reaction: shocked, angry, but not broken — he turns to the player:
> *"Hey... we gotta get out of here. Now. Everyone's panicking — in the chaos anything can happen."*

### Scene 3: First Night

Klein and the player find an abandoned hut at the edge of the Town of Beginnings. No inn — but at least they won't be attacked by other panicked players.

> *"I was thinking... should we form a party? No — not a guild, just... watch each other's backs. What do you say?"*

(This is Klein's formal party invitation to the player.)

### Scenes 4-6: Exploring the Starting Floors

Chapters 2 through 5 use the following simplified flow (GM designs each session from this):

| Session | Content | Monsters | Boss |
|---------|---------|----------|------|
| 1st | Create char, intro, Klein encounter, death-game declaration | Frenzied Boar ×2 | — |
| 2nd | Floor 1 maze first half, learn Sword Skill system | Kobold ×3 (in batches) | — |
| 3rd | Floor 1 Boss prep, meet Argo | Forest encounter | — |
| 4th | **Floor 1 Boss: Illfang the Kobold Lord** | — | HP×0.30, see data below |

### Floor Boss: Illfang (Solo Version)

> Original reference: Scenario/Tale of the Floating Castle Heroes.md Vol.I Ch.3

```
Lv.8 | HP: 180 (orig 600×0.30) | ATK: 20 | DEF: 18
[Phase 1] HP>60%: single Slash ATK×1.5
[Phase 2] HP≤60%: Scimitar Whirlwind ATK×1.8 single (AOE→single adjusted)
Drop: Coat of Midnight (LA reward)
```

**Tactical Tip**: after the player uses a Sword Skill, Klein performs a Switch to cover, forming a solo Switch loop.

# Vol.II: Solo on the Frontline

> **Level**: Lv.10 → Lv.30 | **Floor**: Floors 11-30 | **Sessions**: 6-10

## Meeting and Farewell of the Moonlit Black Cats

> *"We're the Moonlit Black Cats. Small, but everyone's great."* — Keita

### Key Story: Floor 20

Klein temporarily leaves due to Fuurinkazan's founding (becomes a non-traveling companion, providing logistics support from town). On Floor 20 the player meets a small guild — **Moonlit Black Cats**.

**Keita** invites the player to join their guild:
> *"We're short on someone experienced. Wanna join? Nothing required — just occasionally clear with us."*

**Keita becomes the second companion** (Lv.15, one-handed sword user, uses generic companion template: HP = VIT×8+Lv×2, weapon ATK+Lv÷3).

### Floor 27 Maze (Important Story Node)

GM designs a maze exploration. On Floor 27, Moonlit Black Cats members enter an unknown area without the player — triggering a hidden trap.

> *This scene is SAO's most heartbreaking moment. Keita and the others... didn't make it out.*

**GM Choice**:
1. **Original Route**: Moonlit Black Cats wiped out. Player sees their names struck through at the Black Iron Palace → character growth: never joins a guild lightly again.
2. **Rewritten Route**: Player arrives in time, saves Keita but other members die → Keita withdraws from frontline but survives.

(After this, the player returns to solo status, until meeting her at the strategy meeting —)

## Asuna's Debut

> *"You're that solo player? I'm Asuna. Vice-Leader of the Knights of the Blood Oath."*

**Asuna becomes a long-term companion** (ref Solo Mode Rules Supplement §S3.4 Asuna data). From cold at first to gradually opening up to the player — this is the campaign's core emotional line.

### Floor 30 Boss (Solo Version)

Ref original scenario Vol.II, Boss HP × 0.30, AOE→single ×1.5. Player + Asuna cooperate to subjugate.

# Vol.III: Dual Blades

> **Level**: Lv.30 → Lv.55 | **Floor**: Floors 31-55 | **Sessions**: 6-10

## Unique Skill Unlock

During an encounter on Floor 45 — the player is surrounded by three elite monsters. Asuna is separated on the other side. At the critical moment —

> *Your left hand moved. Not your will — but something faster than will.*
> *A second sword appears in your hand.*

**Dual Blades Unlock** (ref Rulebook/06_GM Guide.md §6.11 Unique Skill). This ability can be held by only one person in the entire campaign — and you are that person.

### Dual Blades Rules (Solo Version)

| Skill | SP | Lag | Effect |
|-------|-----|------|--------|
| Double Slash | 10 | 0 | ATK×1.3 × 2 hits |
| Cross Slash | 20 | 1 | ATK×1.8 × 2 hits |
| Starburst Stream | 40 | 2 | ATK×1.0 × 8 hits |
| Eclipse | 60 | 3 | ATK×1.2 × 12 hits (finisher) |

## Lisbeth and the Legendary Weapon

Asuna introduces the player to her friend — blacksmith Lisbeth.

**Lisbeth becomes a logistics companion** (doesn't fight, but provides equipment enhancement, story interaction, and forging services — ref Solo Mode Rules Supplement §S3.4).

**Key Story**: the player helps Lisbeth find rare ore and commissions her to forge a weapon that can bear Dual Blades. She ultimately crafts **Dark Repulser** (ref Equipment Catalog §1.1 ★★★★ one-handed sword).

# Vol.IV: Knights of the Blood

> **Level**: Lv.55 → Lv.75 | **Floor**: Floors 56-75 | **Sessions**: 6-8

## Heathcliff and the Knights of the Blood Oath

At the Floor 60 strategy meeting, a man who has never been wounded stands before everyone.

> *"I am Heathcliff. Leader of the Knights of the Blood Oath."*

GM gradually lays the groundwork for Heathcliff's anomaly:
- His HP never drops to the yellow region
- He knows every Boss's behavior pattern inside out
- Asuna maintains unquestionable loyalty to him — creating rare tension between her and the player

### Floor 74: Gleam Eyes (Solo Version)

Ref original scenario Vol.IV. Boss HP × 0.30. **This is the moment Dual Blades first publicly appear before the Clearers.**

> *When the light of Dual Blades bursts in the Boss room, everyone freezes.*
> *Including Heathcliff. He looks at you — a nameless expression flickers in his eyes. Not surprise. It was... recognition.*

### Floor 75: Truth Revealed

During the Skull Reaper fight (ref original scenario §Boss data, HP × 0.30), Heathcliff blocks a lethal blow with an impossible move.

His HP bar doesn't change. A yellow system marker appears at his feet.

> *"You are... Kayaba Akihiko."*
> *"—Caught, are you."*

GM should fill this moment with tension. Heathcliff / Kayaba proposes the final showdown — Floor 100 Ruby Palace.

Asuna's reaction: shock, sense of betrayal — she once trusted this man so deeply.

# Vol.V: The End

> **Level**: Lv.75 → Lv.96 | **Floor**: Floors 76-100 | **Sessions**: 6-8

## Final Assault

All companions return here:

- **Klein** leads Fuurinkazan into the final assault
- **Agil** provides the strongest supplies
- **Argo** gathers Kayaba's final intel
- **Asuna** steps out of KoB loyalty, stands by the player
- **Lisbeth** does final enhancement for everyone's weapons

(This is not one person's fight. It is the sum of two years of bonds.)

### Floor 100: Ruby Palace

> *"Welcome, lonely blade. No — now you're not alone."*
>
> *Kayaba Akihiko stands at the center of the Ruby Palace. Behind him is the void — the door to the real world.*

**Final Boss data ref**: original scenario Tale of the Floating Castle Heroes.md Ch.17.

**Solo Version adjustment**: HP × 0.35 (6300), each phase one companion joins the fight (Phase 1: Asuna, Phase 2: Klein, Phase 3: all).

### Ending

> *"You defeated me. SAO — cleared."*
> *White light swallows everything.*
> *When you open your eyes again, you see the real sky.*

**Aftermath** (GM writes freely): the player wakes in reality. Those companions — they too wake somewhere. In some hospital, some room, some city. And you know — you will meet again.

### GM Map Update Rules

1. **On reaching a new floor**: send that floor's MAP_UPDATE, mark the floor's town and maze zone
2. **After clearing Boss**: update Boss node to `explored=true boss=false` (cleared)
3. **Before entering Boss room**: show warning — "The gate is about to close, crystal-null zone about to activate"
4. **Key story point**: mark special-area node (e.g., Kayaba reveal, Dual Blades awakening)
5. **After clearing full campaign**: mark all 100 floor nodes, Ruby Palace shows clear animation
