## Chapter 1: The Beginning of the Death Game

### Scene Trigger: 5:30 PM

> *"A forced-teleport light envelops you. When you open your eyes, you find yourself back in the central plaza of the Town of Beginnings — together with every other player."*
>
> *"The sky turns blood red. A giant, faceless figure in a red cloak appears above the plaza."*
>
> *"Welcome to my world. I am Kayaba Akihiko."*

**Kayaba Akihiko's Declaration** (GM should build as much oppressive dread as possible):

1. Logout is impossible — NerveGear blocks the logout command
2. In-game death = real-world death (NerveGear's high-power microwaves destroy the brain)
3. The only escape — clear all 100 floors
4. All players receive the "Hand Mirror" — auto-used, displays true appearance

### Intro Conflict: Players' Reactions

The GM describes the varied reactions of the surrounding NPC players:

| NPC Reaction | Representative |
|--------------|---------------|
| Panic and crying | Most players |
| Silent acceptance | A few who stay calm |
| Immediate action | Klein, Kirito (NPC), the few proactive ones |
| Loss-of-control rage | Very few (attack NPCs, destroy items, etc.) |

**Choice for Players**:
1. Calm down and listen to the information
2. Try to help panicking players
3. Look for others who are also acting proactively
4. Leave the crowd alone

### Intro NPC Interaction

**Klein** — the red-scarf samurai who initiates conversation amid the chaos:
- Personality: passionate, rough, has leadership but doesn't want the responsibility
- First interaction: if he notices the player staying calm, he proactively proposes partying up
- Provides info: basic combat tricks, SAO closed-beta experience

**Argo** — sells basic intel in the crowd:
- Sells the "Strategy Guide booklet"
- Contains: Floor 1 map, basic monster weaknesses, safe-zone markers
- Price: free (in the chaos, she chooses to hand it out for free)

### Prerequisite: Strategy Meeting

> *"The Floor 1 Boss room has been found. The strategy meeting is convened in Tolbana."*

**Location**: Floor 1 town "Tolbana" plaza  
**Participating NPCs**: ~40 players (including later KoB and DDA precursor members)  
**Main Speaker**: Agil — tall bald axe-user, volunteers to chair the meeting

**Meeting Content** (GM performs):
1. Confirm Boss room location and route
2. Share Boss intel (Illfang the Kobold Lord)
3. Party composition (tank / damage / support assignment)
4. First public lesson on Switch tactics

> *but everyone knows the real weapon is the scimitar at his waist."*

**Tactical Tips Provided** (Argo's intel):
- "Phase 1 shield defense is high. Attack from the side and back."

### Post-Clear Setup

After Illfang falls:
1. A teleport gate appears at the center of the Boss room
2. Whole party teleported to **Floor 2 main town "Urbas"**
3. The first player to touch the gate earns the "Pioneer" title
4. System server-wide announcement: "Floor 1 Cleared"

## Chapter 6: Formation of the Clearers (Floors 11-15)

### Background

After Floor 10 is cleared, the player population clearly splits:
- **Clearers**: top players actively clearing floors (~300-500 people)
- **Middle Layer**: capable but non-frontline players (~3,000-4,000 people)
- **Starting Layer**: players who stay on Floor 1 and barely fight (~2,000-3,000 people)

**Story Direction**: players are now seen as "Clearer candidates" — NPCs start expecting them to join the frontline.

### Asuna's Debut

> *"I'm Asuna. Vice-Leader of the Knights of the Blood Oath. Heard you did well on Floor 10."*

**Scene**: Floor 15 main town, KoB recruitment site.

Asuna's attitude toward the players depends on their prior performance:
- If players performed excellently in the Floor 10 Boss fight → Asuna personally invites them to join KoB
- If players performed averagely → Asuna observes but doesn't act immediately
- If players have negative rumors → Asuna stays wary

## Chapter 7: Shadow of PK (Floors 16-20)

### Orange Player Incident

> *"The frontline hasn't been peaceful lately. Some players... are attacking other players."*

**Event Chain**:
1. Players find a murdered NPC-player corpse in the maze (not monster-caused)
2. Investigation reveals **Orange Players** are responsible
3. Track clues — requires info gathering and negotiation checks
4. Encounter Orange Player squad (3 people, ~Lv.20)
5. Choice: fight, persuade them to stop, or report to the Clearers

**Orange Player Encounter Data** (if fight chosen):
```
Orange Player ×3 | Lv.20 | different weapons each
HP: 250/person | ATK: 28 | DEF: 18 | HIT: 42
Tactics: outnumber, ambush, use smoke bombs to flee
```

### Silica Rescue Mission

> *"My familiar Pina... was taken!"*

This is an emotional side story — on Floor 20 players meet the tamer girl **Silica**; her feathered-dragon familiar Pina was captured by a group of players (actually a precursor organization to the PK guild).

**Mission Flow**:
1. Track the kidnappers led by Silica
2. Cross Floor 20's dangerous forest region
3. Find the kidnappers' camp — sneak in or breach head-on
4. Rescue Pina (Pina is injured, needs potion healing)
5. Defeat the kidnapper boss (Lv.22, short-sword user)

**Reward**: Silica's friendship, taming knowledge (if party has a taming-skill player, gain Taming EXP+2)

| Silver Flag | — | ~8 | Elite squad |

### Guild Conflict Event: Floor 30 Rare Boss

A rare field Boss appears on Floor 30 — two guilds discover it simultaneously.

**Decision Point**:
1. Cooperate with the other guild to subjugate (split reward)
2. Compete — whoever downs it first gets the LA reward
3. Yield — give the other side the clear, in exchange for a favor

This event should show the complexity of guild politics while giving players an important social choice.

## Chapter 11: Meaning of Sacrifice (Floors 36-45)

### Death Creeps Closer

On these floors, the Clearers begin to pay real sacrifices. The GM should, at this stage:
- Let NPC Clearer members die in battle (non-player characters)
- Describe the growing tally of strike-through lines on the Black Iron Palace monument
- Let players feel "every Boss fight is a gamble"

### Fate of the Moonlit Black Cats (if still extant)

> **This is a key node from the original work. Depending on Vol.1 player choices, several developments:**

**Route A: Player did not join / did not interfere**
→ Moonlit Black Cats trigger a trap in the Floor 38 maze, wiped out. Keita dies.
→ Players may see their names struck through at the Black Iron Palace.
→ One of the saddest scenes in the campaign.

**Route B: Player mentored them**
→ Moonlit Black Cats members level up, but still take casualties in the Floor 40 Boss fight.
→ Keita survives, thanks the player.
→ He decides to stay in the middle layer and no longer join frontline clears.

**Route C: Player joined Moonlit Black Cats**
→ Keita and some members, led by the player, joined the Clearers.
→ All their fates bind to the player's guild.

### Argo's Secret Intel

> *"There are some things... you might want to know. About Heathcliff."*

Argo contacts the player alone on Floor 42. She provides the following intel:

1. Heathcliff's HP has never dropped below yellow in combat — can't be coincidence
2. Someone inside KoB mentioned Heathcliff "never sleeps"
3. A player saw Heathcliff enter the maze alone late at night

**Hint for Players**: this is the seed of distrust. Players now have reason to suspect Heathcliff — but no proof.

## Chapter 12: Relay Point — Floor 50 (Floors 46-50)

### Vitality of Algade

Floor 50 main town "Algade" is the largest player gathering place in all of Aincrad:
- Largest player market (all kinds of gear, materials, intel trading)
- Intel exchange center
- Contact offices for each guild
- Restaurants, inns, entertainment facilities

**Setup Scene**: GM should let players experience "daily life" on Floor 50 — their last rest before the second-half assault.

## Chapter 14: Threads of Truth (Floors 61-70)

### Heathcliff's Slip

In the Floor 65 Boss fight, design a key scene:

> *The Boss's attack hits Heathcliff. His HP bar turns yellow — but only for an instant. The next second, the HP bar returns to green.*
>
> *And — at the instant the HP bar turned yellow — a yellow system marker appeared at Heathcliff's feet. That's the "indestructible object" marker visible only to GM privileges.*

**Awareness Check**:
- All players present make an INT check (difficulty -15)

**Follow-up**:
- If a player notices and confronts Heathcliff → he calmly denies, "You're too nervous"
- Asuna defends Heathcliff — she noticed nothing wrong

# GM Usage Guide

## Adjusting Difficulty

If party strength is above preset, add the following adjustments:
- Monster count +50%
- Boss HP +20%
- Add 1 elite monster to the encounter

If party strength is below preset:
- Provide 1-2 extra NPC comrades
- Boss HP -20%
- Increase number of safe rooms

## Important Reminders

1. **Death is real** — don't let players feel the GM is protecting them
2. **Daily life matters** — reserve at least 15-20 minutes per session for non-combat scenes
3. **Music** — suggest using the SAO soundtrack as background music
4. **Character development** — track players' emotional arcs, reward them later
