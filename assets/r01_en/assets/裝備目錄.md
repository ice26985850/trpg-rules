# SAO TRPG — Equipment Catalog

> *"This sword is called 'Elucidator.' She is my partner."* — Kirito

---

## Equipment System Overview

- Each character may equip: **Main Weapon ×1, Sub Weapon ×1 (requires specific skill), Head Armor ×1, Body Armor ×1, Accessories ×2**
- Weapons have durability and require repair after prolonged use (can be repaired for a fee at weapon shops or by players with Smithing skill)
- Equipment Level Restriction: Character Level must be ≥ the equipment's required Level to equip it
