# SAO TRPG — Solo Mode Rules Supplement

> *"What does solo play mean in a death game? — It means you can only rely on yourself. And those willing to stand by your side."*
>
> This supplement module enables SAO TRPG to support **1 Player + 1 Game Master (GM)** solo/duet mode (Duet Play).

## S1. Necessity of This Supplement

The original SAO TRPG rulebook (規則書/01_基礎系統.md through 06_GM指南.md) and the three DLCs are designed by default for a **4–6 Player** group game. The following systems are completely inoperable in solo mode:

| Original System | Problem | Reference |
|-----------------|---------|-----------|
| **Switch (SW)** | Requires at least 2 Players to alternate entering the field; cannot be triggered solo | 規則書/03_戰鬥系統.md §3.2.3 |
| **Boss Battle Balance** | Boss HP and damage are designed for 8–20 person parties | 規則書/06_GM指南.md §6.2 |
| **Combat Role Division** | Tank/DPS/Support must be filled by different characters | 規則書/02_角色創建.md §2.2.3 |
| **Skill Slot Limit** | 2 slots cannot cover all survival + DPS + exploration needs | 規則書/02_角色創建.md §2.8 |
| **Permanent Death** | No ally rescue; death = campaign over | 規則書/01_基礎系統.md §1.5 |

> **Conclusion**: The original system is unsuitable for solo play. This supplement provides a complete alternative.

## S2. Core Difficulty Adjustments

### S2.1 Solo HP Bonus (Revised from §1.3.2)

Original HP formula (規則書/01_基礎系統.md §1.3.2):

```
HP = VIT × 8 + Level × 2
```

In solo mode it becomes:

```
HP = VIT × 12 + Level × 5
```

> A solo Player's HP is about 1.5× the original, to withstand damage that would normally be shared across a party.

### S2.2 Solo SP Adjustment

The original SP formula remains unchanged (`STR × 2 + AGI`), but the following is added:
- **SP Auto-Recovery**: Recover 3 SP at the end of each turn (compensating for the lack of an ally to cover Recovery Lag gaps)
- Reference: original rulebook/01_基礎系統.md §1.3.2 SP formula

### S2.3 Solo Skill Slot Expansion

Original skill slots (規則書/02_角色創建.md §2.8):

| Orig. Lv | Orig. Slots |
|----------|-------------|
| 1 | 2 |
| 10 | 3 |
| 20 | 4 |

Solo mode changes to:

| Lv | Slots | Note |
|----|-------|------|
| 1 | 3 | +1 slot (essential for survival skills) |
| 5 | 4 | Unlocked early |
| 10 | 5 | |
| 20 | 6 | |
| 30 | 7 | |
| 50 | 8 | |
| 70 | 9 | |
| 90+ | 10 | Same as original maximum |

### S2.4 Monster Count Adjustment

| Original Encounter (4 players) | Solo Encounter |
|-------------------------------|----------------|
| Monster × 4 | Monster × 1 |
| Monster × 6 | Monster × 2 |
| Monster × 8+ | Monster × 2–3 (arrive in waves) |
| Elite Monster × 2 | Elite Monster × 1 |
| Boss at full strength 8–20 players | Boss HP reduced to 25–35% (see S6) |

## S3. NPC Companion System

> *"I am not alone. I still have my companions."* — this is the core of solo mode.

### S3.1 Companion Types

The GM controls 1–2 NPC Companions who accompany the Player. Companions are obtained in the following ways:

| Acquisition Method | Companion Strength | Example |
|--------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Story Recruitment** | Medium–High (equal to Player Level) | Klein, Asuna, Lisbeth |
| **Hired NPC** | Low–Medium (Player Level −5) | Mercenary NPC in town |
| **Guild Member** | Medium (Player Level −3) | Recruited after the Player establishes a guild |

### S3.2 Companion Control Rules

- In combat: The Player may direct the companion's actions (choose attack targets, use skills)
- During exploration: The GM role-plays the companion's dialogue and personality
- Companions use a simplified stat card (see template below) — a full character sheet is not required
- At most 2 companions may be carried at once (more would make encounters too easy)

## S4. Solo Switch System

The original Switch requires two Players to alternate (規則書/03_戰鬥系統.md §3.2.3). Solo mode changes this to **"Companion Switch"**:

### S4.1 Companion Switch Rules

```
1. Player uses a Sword Skill → enters Recovery Lag
2. Declare "Switch!" as an immediate action → designate one companion
3. The companion acts immediately (uses its main action to attack)
4. The monster's target switches to that companion
5. After the companion acts, on the Player's next turn after recovering from Recovery Lag, they may Switch back
```

### S4.2 Solo Combo Bonus (Revised from original §3.2.3 Switch table)

| Consecutive Switches | HIT Bonus | Damage Bonus | Note |
|----------------------|-----------|--------------|------|
| 1st | +5 | ×1.0 | |
| 2nd | +10 | ×1.15 | |
| 3rd | +15 | ×1.25 | |
| 4th+ | +20 | ×1.35 | Solo maximum (no need for 5+ chain) |

## S5. Death and Revival Adjustments

### S5.1 Near-Death Protection (Revised from original §1.5.3)

Original near-death rule: at HP 1–5, the check is −30.

Solo mode adds:
- When HP drops to 0, the first trigger instead sets HP = 1 + Near-Death Protection (once per battle)
- When a companion is present: the companion may take the lethal hit in the Player's place (companion HP drops to 0 → companion is severely wounded and withdraws, but the Player survives)
- Reference: original rulebook/01_基礎系統.md §1.5.3 Near-Death State

### S5.2 Companion Death

- Companion HP reaches 0 → that companion is "severely wounded and withdraws", and cannot fight for the rest of this adventure
- Automatically recovers upon returning to town (no revival item needed)
- Story-critical NPC companions do not suffer Permanent Death (e.g., Klein, Asuna) — this is to maintain narrative continuity in solo mode
- Reference: original rulebook/02_角色創建.md §2.12 Character Inheritance mechanic (applies to Player characters only)

### S5.3 Player Death

If the Player character dies after Near-Death Protection has been consumed:
- Use the original rule's inheritance mechanic (規則書/02_角色創建.md §2.12)
- New character Level = death Level − 10 (minimum 1)
- The GM should have the new character "inherit" the old character's storylines and relationships

## S6. Encounter Design Formulas

### S6.1 Monster Count Formula (Revised from original §6.2.2)

Original formula: 規則書/06_GM指南.md §6.2.2 Monster Stat Reference Table.

Solo mode encounter design:

| Difficulty | Normal Monsters (orig. ×4 →) | Elite Monsters (orig. ×2 →) | Boss HP |
|-----------|------------------------------|------------------------------|---------|
| Easy | 1 | None | Orig. HP × 0.20 |
| Normal | 1–2 | None | Orig. HP × 0.25 |
| Hard | 2 | 1 | Orig. HP × 0.30 |
| Boss | 1–2 (in waves) | 0–1 | Orig. HP × 0.35 |

> Original Boss HP source: 規則書/06_GM指南.md each Boss's data + 劇本/浮遊城英雄譚_完整戰役.md

### S6.2 Boss Damage Adjustment

The Boss's area attacks (AOE) are designed in the original to hit 3–6 Players. In solo mode:

- **Area Attack**: changed to single-target attack (ATK × 1.5)
- **Multi-Target Attack**: at most 2 targets (Player + 1 companion)
- **Summon Skill**: number of summons halved (rounded down)

### S6.3 EXP and Col Adjustment

In solo mode, the Player does not need to split EXP, therefore:
- The original EXP formula (規則書/01_基礎系統.md §1.4.2) remains unchanged
- The original Col formula (規則書/06_GM指南.md §6.6.2) remains unchanged
- The Player keeps all rewards (no need to split with party members)

## S8. Map System Support

In solo mode, since there are no allies to share information gathering and exploration, the map system is especially important.

### S8.1 Solo Exploration Map Bonus

Solo players enjoy the following map exploration advantages:

| Item | Solo Version | Standard Version |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| Enemy Detection Check | AGI + INT (standard) | Sum of whole party |
| Trap Awareness | No need to assign scout duties | Requires a dedicated person |
| Maze Memory | Player can directly record all explored areas | Requires leader coordination |

### S8.2 GM Map Update

The GM should provide the following each time the Player explores a new area:

```
[MAP UPDATE]
You have arrived at [Floor/Area name].
Visible in sight:
- [Visible landmark 1] (distance: approx. [X] meters)
- [Visible landmark 2] (direction: [East/West/South/North])

Maze depth: [X]% explored
Markers: ⬜ Unexplored  ⬛ Explored  🔴 Boss Room
```

### S8.3 Map-Driven Events

Some events require the Player to trigger them at the correct map location:

| Moonlight Flower | Floor 24 Moonlight Grass Patch (night) | Lv.30+ | `lone_swordsman` Vol.2 (before Asuna's debut) |
