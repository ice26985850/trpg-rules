# SAO TRPG — Sword Skill Catalog

> *"The system guides your body. All you must do is entrust your will to the blade."*

---

## How to Use Sword Skills

- Each weapon has its own Sword Skill tree (see Rulebook Chapter 4)
- Using a Sword Skill consumes the corresponding SP, and you cannot act during the Recovery Lag
- Some Sword Skills have prerequisite learning conditions (e.g., requiring a specific skill level or completing a quest)
- In the catalog below, bold entries are signature/original Sword Skills

## XI. Conditions for Unlocking Special Weapon Types

### Learning the Katana
- Must complete the "Soul of the Samurai" quest line from a specific NPC
- Or be taught by a player who has unlocked the Katana (takes one in-game week)
- Katana unlock quests usually appear on Floor 10+

### Learning Dual Blades
- Unique Skill, only one person per campaign can learn it
- The GM decides unlock conditions; suggested options include:
  - Highest reaction speed (AGI+SPD total) in the party
  - Best performer in a key Boss battle
  - Complete the hidden quest "Trial of Twin Blades"
  - Story trigger (e.g., the dire straits of the Floor 74 Boss battle)

---

## XII. Sword Skill Combo Rules (Combo System)

When a player uses a basic Sword Skill and does not enter Recovery Lag (0-turn Recovery Lag), they may choose:

1. **Follow with a normal attack**: HIT-5, but extends the combo
2. **Follow with another basic Sword Skill**: SP cost as normal, HIT-5
3. **Stop the combo**: proceed to next turn normally

After using basic Sword Skills 3 or more times consecutively, from the 4th use onward each incurs HIT-10.

> **Tactical Tip**: Basic Sword Skill combos suit clearing mobs; mid-to-high tier Sword Skills + Switch suit Boss battles.
