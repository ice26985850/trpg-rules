# SAO TRPG — Monster Compendium

> *"There are three Frenzy Boars ahead. Lv.2. Be careful."*

---

## How to Use

This compendium provides stat templates for monsters of each Floor. The GM may modify them as needed.

### Symbol Legend
- **N** = Normal Monster
- **E** = Elite Monster
- **R** = Rare Monster
- **B** = Boss

## Quick Monster Generation

Use the following formulas to quickly generate:

```
HP = Level × 15 (N) / Level × 30 (E) / Level × 100+ (Boss)
ATK = Level × 1.2 + 5
DEF = Level × 0.9 + 4
HIT = 25 + Level × 0.5
EVA = 15 + Level × 0.35
SPD = 20 + Level × 0.4
EXP = Level × 15 (N) / Level × 40 (E) / Level × 120 (Boss, party total)
Col = Level × 8 (N) / Level × 20 (E) / Level × 120 (Boss, party total)
```
