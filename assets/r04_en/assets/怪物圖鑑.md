# Monster / NPC Bestiary (Frozen Wasteland TRPG)

> This is a standalone bestiary compiling monster / NPC stat templates, examples, and the complete database. The core rulebook retains only cross-reference guidance.

## Monster / NPC Stat Template

## 3.9 Monster/NPC Stat Template

### Example: Freezing Hound

```
[Freezing Hound] Medium Mutant Beast
Attributes: Speed (SPD) 8(+3) Perception (PER) 7(+2) Guts (GUT) 6(+1) Tech (TEC) 2(−3)
Health (HP) 22 | Defense (AC) 13 | Movement (MV) 11 squares | Initiative +3

Attacks:
  Lunge Bite: d20+3 vs Defense (AC), 2d6 Physical + Speed Difficulty (DC) 12 or else Prone
  Claw Strike: d20+3 vs Defense (AC), 1d6+3 Physical
  Time Howl: 5×5 area, Guts Difficulty (DC) 14 or else Rattled

Special Abilities:
  Thaw Tracking: senses nearest thaw location within 10 squares
  Freeze Resistance: duration halved when re-frozen
```

### Example: Gray Rat — Lone Plunderer

```
[Gray Rat] Human Plunderer
Attributes: Speed (SPD) 7(+2) Perception (PER) 6(+1) Guts (GUT) 4(−1) Tech (TEC) 6(+1) Negotiation (NEG) 3(−2)
Health (HP) 18 | Defense (AC) 13 | Movement (MV) 10 squares | Panic Threshold 13

Equipment: Light Armor +1 | Pistol 1d8 | Knife 1d4+2 | Time Crystal ×3

Combat Style: Opportunist — will not attack a group larger than its own.
              When injured (Health (HP) <50%), attempts to negotiate or flee.

Negotiation: Offering 2+ Time Crystals can buy information; mentioning "the outside" softens attitude.
```

#### Time Echo

[Time Echo] Threat Level 3 — Environmental Hazard

**Basic Information**
- **Time Echo**: Environmental Hazard
- **Threat Level: 3**: High-density Time Crystal areas

**Description**
A "time memory" within the Freeze Zone — an energy afterimage left by past thaw events. It has no physical form, but can replay the lethal fragments of past Thaw Moments.

**Attacks**
| Attack Name | Hit Formula | Damage | Range | Special Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Echo Surge | — | 1d8 Time Damage | Touch range (5 squares) | Ignores armor |
| Memory Replay | — | Special | Area | Replays the most dangerous event from the area's last thaw |

**Special Abilities**
Unattackable: Time Echo cannot be harmed by physical attacks
Time Crystal Dispel: spend 1 Crystal to make it vanish for 1d4 turns
Omen Sense: Perception Difficulty (DC) 15 to predict its next movement direction

**Spoils**
Leaves 1d3 Time Crystals after dissipating

**Tactical Behavior**
Auto-spawns where Time Crystals are dense
Moves 3 squares toward the nearest creature every 1d4 turns
Players feel temporal perception distort when it approaches
