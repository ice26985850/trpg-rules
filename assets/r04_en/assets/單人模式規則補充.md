# Frozen Wasteland TRPG — Solo Mode Rules Supplement v1.0

> **Intended for:** 1 player + 1 GM (duo game / one-on-one session)
> **Reference base:** This document is a supplementary module to the *Frozen Wasteland TRPG Core Rulebook v1.0*. All unmodified rules carry over from the core rulebook.

---

## I. Solo Mode Overview

The standard rules of Frozen Wasteland assume a plundering party of 2–5 players. Many of the core mechanics rely on the presence of "teammates" — rescue actions, panic soothing, combat burden-sharing, skill complementarity, and so on. This supplement module provides all the adjustments needed to make solo play possible.

### Design Goals of Solo Mode
- Preserve the "taut, nervy" core tone of Frozen Wasteland — isolation only amplifies fear
- Allow a single player character to complete plundering missions independently
- Fill skill gaps through NPC companions, without replacing the player's decision-making authority
- Maintain the weight of ethical choices — facing the Frozen alone means no teammate to share the responsibility

## II. NPC Companion System

### 2.1 The Role of Companions

NPC companions are not a "second player character." They are:

- **Functional supplements:** Fill gaps in the player's attributes
- **Narrative catalysts:** Interactions with the player create room for character growth
- **Tactical resources:** Provide assistance in combat and rescue
- **Sources of risk:** Companions can also be injured, panic, and need protection — they are not invincible

### 2.2 Companion Creation

#### Attribute Allocation
Companions use a simplified attribute system, with **12 points freely distributed** across the five attributes (range 1–6):

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Speed (SPD) | Movement and reaction |
| Perception (PER) | Observation and anticipation |
| Guts (GUT) | Calm and willpower |
| Tech (TEC) | Device operation |
| Negotiation (NEG) | Social and negotiation |

The modifier formula is the same as for players: **Modifier = Attribute Value − 5** (range −4 to +1)

#### Derived Attributes
- Health (HP) = Guts × 2 + 8 (slightly lower than players)
- Movement = Speed + 2 squares
- Panic Threshold = Guts × 2 + 3
- Time Resistance = Tech modifier
- No Resolve Points or Insight Points

#### Companion Traits
Each companion selects **1 trait** (cannot be changed):

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Keen Senses** | Advantage on Perception-related checks. May spend a Reaction to assist the player on a Perception check. |
| **Iron Grip** | Counts as two people when carrying heavy loads. Melee damage +1. |
| **Medical Training** | Using a first-aid kit restores 1d6+3 Health (HP). May perform first aid on the player (Major Action, restores 1d6+2). |
| **Mechanical Aptitude** | Advantage on Tech checks. May assist the player in operating devices (grants Advantage). |
| **Social Lubricant** | Advantage on Negotiation checks. May provide the player a −10% price when negotiating at base. |
| **Steady Composure** | Panic Threshold +3. When the player panics, may spend a Reaction to soothe them (Negotiation Difficulty (DC) 10; on success, player's Panic −1 per round). |
| **Spacetime Sensitivity** | Innate sense for temporal anomalies. Player gains +2 bonus on Premonition Perception checks. Carrying crystals does not increase Thaw Die risk. |
| **Tactical Cover** | When the player is adjacent to the companion, the player gains +2 Armor Value. The companion may use a Reaction to block one attack for the player (the companion takes that damage). |

#### Companion Equipment
Companions carry basic equipment (same as the player's starting gear) but do not carry weapons (except with GM permission).

### 2.3 Companion Action Rules

#### Action Economy
In combat / a Freeze Zone, a companion may perform each round:
- **1 Major Action** or **1 Move + 1 Minor Action** (cannot do both Major + Move)
- **1 Reaction** (per round)

#### Companion Commands
The player may use a **Free Action** to issue simple commands to the companion ("go move that crate," "take cover behind the barricade," "use the first-aid kit on me"). The GM judges whether the command is carried out based on the companion's personality and traits.

#### Companion Autonomous Actions
When the player issues no command, the GM controls the companion to take reasonable self-preservation actions:
- In combat: seek cover or assist the player
- At the Thaw Moment: prioritize self-preservation (hide), unless a trait dictates otherwise
- Freeze Zone exploration: follow the player, assist with carrying

#### Companion Panic
Companions panic just like players. When a companion panics:
- Disadvantage on all checks
- Movement −2
- Cannot execute the player's commands
- The player may soothe them with Negotiation Difficulty (DC) 12 (spending a Major Action)

#### Companion Down
When a companion's Health (HP) drops to 0, they enter Dying (same rules as players). The player must perform first aid within 3 rounds, or the companion dies. A companion's death causes the player a permanent Panic Value +3 (cannot be removed).

### 2.4 Recommended Companion Pairings

| Player Type | Recommended Companion Trait | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| High Speed, low Tech (e.g., scout) | **Mechanical Aptitude** | Fills the Tech gap |
| High Tech, low Speed (e.g., technician) | **Iron Grip** or **Tactical Cover** | Fills mobility and defense |
| High Guts, low Negotiation (e.g., hard-ass) | **Social Lubricant** | Fills social gap |
| Balanced (beginner) | **Medical Training** or **Keen Senses** | Improves survival rate |

## III. Solo Combat Adjustments

### 3.1 Enemy Count Scaling

| Original Encounter (multiplayer) | Solo Adjustment |
|---|---|
| 3–4 enemies | 1–2 enemies |
| 2 enemies | 1 enemy (Health (HP) −25%) |
| 1 strong enemy (Boss) | Health (HP) −30%, damage −1 die |
| Swarm of Frozen | Soothing Difficulty (DC) −3 |

### 3.2 Retreat Difficulty Adjustment
When retreating solo, the boundary-crossing check Difficulty (DC) is **−2** (no pressure of waiting for teammates).

### 3.3 Self-Rescue from Dying
When the player's Health (HP) drops to 0 and no companion is nearby:
- May spend 1 Resolve Point (if available) for **Adrenaline Self-Rescue**: recover to Health (HP) = 1 and immediately gain 1 Move action
- This ability may be used only once per game
- Using it grants 1 level of Exhaustion (Disadvantage on all checks, until next rest)

## IV. Solo Rescue Alternatives

### 4.1 Carrying the Fully Frozen
The original rule requires a minimum of 2 people. In solo mode:

| Method | Requirement | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Dragging** | Speed Difficulty (DC) 12, spends 3 rounds | Use a tarp/coat as a makeshift stretcher to drag. Movement −5 |
| **Lever Carry** | Tech Difficulty (DC) 12, requires a crowbar or similar tool, spends 2 rounds | Use environmental objects as leverage points. Movement −3 |
| **Time Crystal Assist** | Spend 1 crystal, spends 1 round | Briefly reduce the Frozen One's "temporal weight." Movement −2 |

### 4.2 Stabilizing the Critical Freeze
The original 3-round process is unchanged, but in solo mode:
- A companion may assist (grants Advantage)
- If no companion, the Difficulty (DC) of each round is **−1**

### 4.3 Self-Rescue from Freeze
The original rule states Full Freeze (level 3) cannot be self-released. Solo mode adds:

> **Willpower Thaw:** Spend 2 Resolve Points and make a Guts Difficulty (DC) 18 check. On success, Freeze Level immediately drops to level 1. This ability may be used only once per campaign.

## V. Solo Panic Management

### 5.1 Panic Release Without Teammates
The original rule's "teammate soothing" is unavailable. Add alternatives:

| Method | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Self-Calm** | Spend 1 round on deep breathing / focusing. Guts Difficulty (DC) 12; on success, Panic Value −2 (limited to 2 times per game) |
| **Use Sedative** | Panic Value −3, but next Guts check is at Disadvantage (drug dependency) |
| **Time Crystal Meditation** | Spend 1 crystal, spend 1 round. Guts Difficulty (DC) 10; on success, Panic Value −1d4 |
| **Companion Soothing** | Companion spends a Major Action, Negotiation Difficulty (DC) 10 (automatically succeeds if the companion has the "Steady Composure" trait) |

### 5.2 Loneliness Pressure
When exploring a Freeze Zone solo, the environmental panic accumulation every 3 rounds increases to **+2** (replacing the original +1). Isolation makes the oppressive weight of time heavier.

## VI. Attribute Coverage Suggestions

A solo character cannot divide labor like a team. The GM, when designing a solo campaign, should ensure:

| Player's Primary Attribute | Challenges the GM Should Reduce | Alternatives the GM Should Add |
|---|---|---|
| Low Speed | Reduce time-pressure chase scenes | Add stealth detour routes |
| Low Perception | Reduce instant-death traps relying on Omen detection | Add obvious visual cues |
| Low Guts | Reduce high-pressure scenes of continuous thawing | Provide more breathing windows |
| Low Tech | Reduce demands for precision device operation | Provide "brute-force alternatives" (smash open rather than unlock) |
| Low Negotiation | Reduce forced social checks | Allow actions to replace words (show strength / threaten) |

## VII. Solo Mode Feat Adjustments

Some original feats require adjustment in solo mode:

| Original Feat | Solo Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Teammate Guardian** | Changed to "Companion Guardian": companion gains +3 Armor Value (originally +2) |
| **Pace Synchronizer** | Changed to "Lone Step": own Movement +2, no Disadvantage from acting solo while sneaking |
| **Chain Coordinator** (Experience) | Experience intuition changed to "own Guts check +1" (originally teammate +1) |
| **Rearguard Expert** | On retreat, own retreat check Advantage + Armor Value +3 (originally teammate gains Advantage) |
