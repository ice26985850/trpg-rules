# Frozen Wasteland TRPG Feat List (17)

## Design Notes

The original 8 feats remain unchanged (#1–#8), and 9 new feats are added (#9–#17), covering all specified directions: cross-attribute combination prerequisites (#9, #11), advanced Freeze Zone Experience (#10, #17), Frozen rescue (#11), team coordination (#12), Time Crystal application (#13), Trauma-related (#14), retreat/survival-oriented (#15, #16).

All values are internally consistent with the system baseline: attribute range 1–10, modifier = attribute − 5, Panic Threshold = Guts × 2 + 5, Health (HP) = Guts × 2 + 10, Movement = Speed + 3 squares, Disadvantage = 2d20 take lower, Advantage = 2d20 take higher.

## Feat Acquisition Rules

- **At character creation:** Each character may choose 1 feat that meets its prerequisites.
- **Milestone reward:** After every 2–3 games, may choose to gain a new feat or attribute +1 (cap 10).
- **Feat chains:** Some feats form advancement chains (Time Adaptation → Time Debtor, Time Reader → Rhythm Tamer), which must be acquired in sequence.
- **Trauma feats:** Feats with Trauma prerequisites cannot be selected at character creation — they must be "earned" in play.
