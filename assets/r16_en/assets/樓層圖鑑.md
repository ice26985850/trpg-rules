# Backrooms Walker TRPG — Level Index

> The Backrooms is composed of infinitely many "Levels". The following is a complete reference to all Levels (15+) involved across the 8 scenarios.

---

## Level 0 — Tutorial Level (Front Hall)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Scenario** | S1: Awakening |
| **Type** | Yellow office cubicles — the classic entrance to the Backrooms |
| **Spatial Stability** | Mildly unstable (minor spatial shifts) |
| **Main Threats** | Sense of isolation (SP erosion), occasional Hounds |
| **Starting Gift** | Within 5 cubicles there is always a bottle of Almond Water |
| **Exits/Entrances** | Exit: Fire door→L1, Dark staircase→L2, Elevator→L4 |
