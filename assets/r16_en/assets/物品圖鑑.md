> **Rarity Scavenge DC Reference**: Common DC 8 / Scarce DC 12 / Uncommon DC 15 / Rare DC 18
> **Shelf Life (Soft Cap)**: Almond Water has no explicit expiration, but long-term storage (over one month of in-game time) has a low chance (about 5% per week) of becoming contaminated — drinking contaminated Almond Water requires a Physique DC 12 check, otherwise an extra -1D4 SP. This is a soft throttle to prevent early hoarding from causing a surplus later; the GM may adjust or disable it based on campaign length.
