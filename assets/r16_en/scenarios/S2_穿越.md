# S2: Traverse

> **Level 1 → 2 → 3** | Milestones 2–3 | 6–9 hours | Beginner → Intermediate
> **Applicable Mode**: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

---

## Level 1: Cornerstone Outpost

M.E.G. outpost "Cornerstone"—a makeshift community of shelves and shipping containers, more than 20 humans. Intelligence Officer Chen hands you a hand-drawn map of Level 2.

"Bring enough light sources. Three times more than you think. Level 2's pipes—no matter what you hear, don't stop."

Recruitable NPC companions to travel with: Karl (ex-firefighter, high Physique), Lena (college student, high Perception), Marco (delivery driver, Expert in Spatial Memory).

---

## Level 2: Pipe Nightmare

The maintenance stairs descend. With every step down, the temperature rises one degree. The light shifts from cold white to blood red. 35°C. Sweat begins to bead.

Your flashlight beam is weak in the narrow pipes. The metal surfaces are covered in claw marks. Sound is absorbed—you can't hear the Hounds in the distance, and they can't hear you. Encounters are sudden, close-range.

At a pipe junction you find a shelter left by those before—Wang Jing's diary. She stayed here for 47 days. The last page: "I'm going to the heart of the nest. There are answers there. Or an end."

The red valve room—turning the valves changes the pipe flow. The right combination may trigger a hidden event.

---

## Level 3: Power Station

You tumble from the pipe exit into a vast space. An abandoned power facility—exposed wires spark blue in the darkness. Malfunctioning equipment emits an irregular hum. The noise of old generators is deafening.

Between the machine rooms, something moves. "The Electrician"—a former maintenance worker, now a half-human, half-machine Entity. Its hands are bare electrodes. Touching them releases a high-voltage current (2D6 electric damage).

The exit is a heavy maintenance door—but the circuit is damaged. You must restart three generator units to restore power and open the door. Each generator unit sits in a different corner of the facility—along "The Electrician's" patrol route.

When the maintenance door finally hisses open, beyond it is a bright corridor.

---

## Ending

You pass through the maintenance door. Behind you, the noise of the power station gradually fades. The corridor ahead is clean and bright—leading to Level 4. Your hands still tremble from the memory of the electric shock. But you're alive. Still walking. That's enough.
