# S1: Awakening

> **Level 0 — Tutorial Level (Front Hall)** | Milestone 1 | 4–6 hours | Beginner
> **Applicable Mode**: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

---

## Opening

You open your eyes.

The first sensation is the ground—not the warm floor of your home, but damp, rough carpet. You try to stand, pressing your palm against the carpet, and feel a faint, wet stickiness that shouldn't be there.

You look around. Yellow. Yellow everywhere—faded mustard-yellow wallpaper running from floor to ceiling. The fluorescent lights on the ceiling emit a low hum. The lights flicker. There is a smell in the air—not foul, just... old. Like a file room that hasn't been opened in decades.

What is your last memory? Working late into the night. Searching for your car in the underground parking garage. Getting up in the middle of the night for a drink.

An ordinary moment in an ordinary, mundane day—and then you were here.

---

## Scene One: Eyes Open

In the cubicle there is a desk and an empty chair. Scattered across the desk are expired reports and blank forms. In the drawer are pens, paper clips, and a pair of scissors.

On the low partition wall of one cubicle, written in black marker: "Keep walking. Don't stop.—Lao Zhao"

Within the first five cubicles, you are certain to find a sealed bottle of Almond Water. This is the only free kindness The Backrooms grants you.

---

## Scene Two: Marks of Those Before

Faint chalk arrows mark the floor. They all point the same direction—toward the brighter fluorescent corridor. Follow them. You pass a computer left on; its screen shows only one line: "If you're reading this, you've been walking for 47 minutes. Keep going. Don't stop.—Lao Zhao"

You begin to understand the rule: light = safety. Those who came before left marks to help those who follow. You are not alone—even if you are alone right now.

---

## Scene Three: The Darkness Spreads

The fluorescent lights at the far end of the corridor begin to go out, row by row. The darkness spreads toward you. From the depths of the now-darkened corridor comes the sound of claws scraping against concrete.

Choice: Hide in a cubicle (Stealth DC 12), stay quiet and wait (Willpower DC 14), or turn and run back toward the light.

If you choose to hide—the Hounds' breathing is right on the other side of the cubicle partition. Heavy, damp, with the stench of rotting flesh. But you stay quiet. It passes. The lights return.

---

## Scene Four: The Door

At the end of the corridor is a wall—but set into the wall is a door. An unmarked fire door, slightly lighter in color than the surrounding wallpaper. On the floor before the door sits the last bottle of Almond Water.

From the other side of the door comes a sound. A human voice.

You push the door open.

---

## Ending

An M.E.G. member greets you: "New here? Came from Level 0? Congratulations—you survived your first day. Most don't. Here, sit down. There's water, there's food."

You receive the M.E.G. newcomer care package. But you see ahead—deep within the warehouse, maintenance stairs lead downward. Level 2 is waiting. But today, you only need to breathe.
