# S7: Sprint

> **Level ! — Run If You Don't Want to Die!** | Milestones 7–8 | 4–6 hours | Advanced
> **Applicable Mode**: Solo (1 Player + 1 GM) / Multi (2–5 Players + 1 GM, see GM Rulebook for Multiplayer Mode adjustments)

---

## The Siren

The elevator doors open. You step into a corridor.

Hospital style—white walls, white floor, fluorescent lights. Looks almost normal. Then the red alarm light comes on.

A shrill siren rips through the entire corridor, its frequency making your teeth ache.

At the far end of the corridor—behind you—comes a sound from the darkness. Not one Entity—dozens. Claws, footsteps, low growls. They are running.

Your brain doesn't need to think: **Run.**

---

## Ten Kilometers

This corridor is exactly ten kilometers long. Not a metaphor.

Along the way are obstacles—overturned hospital beds, collapsed ceiling debris, rusted wheelchairs. Every kilometer, a supply point on the wall: energy bars, Almond Water. You can only grab them while running—you cannot stop.

**Stamina Challenge Mechanic** (one Check per kilometer, 10 in total):
- Physique (Endurance) DC 12 + km/2: maintain steady speed
- or Agility (Acrobatics) DC 12 + km/2: vault obstacles
- Each failure: accumulate 1 level of Fatigue. Fatigue affects subsequent Checks (−1 per level)

The 5th kilometer—the supply point has enough Almond Water to partially restore stamina. The 7th kilometer—your legs are burning. Your lungs are searing. The footsteps behind grow closer.

The 9th kilometer—Willpower Check DC 18. Failure: speed slows, a Hound's claw-tip grazes your backpack. Success: you clench your teeth, squeeze out your last strength.

---

## The Finish

The 10th kilometer.

End of the corridor. A door. The door reads EXIT.

You burst through the door—and tumble into a pure white space. The door closes automatically behind you. The siren is cut off. The Entities' roars are sealed away. Only your breathing remains—violent, scorching, but still present.

You lie on the ground, not knowing how long has passed. Maybe minutes. Maybe hours.

Then you lift your head. This white space has no walls, no floor, no ceiling. Only white.

And two doors.

The left door—warm yellow light seeps from the gap.

The right door—cold fluorescent light seeps from the gap.

But now you are too exhausted. You can only sit here for a while. Looking at these two doors. Remembering how you came to be here.
