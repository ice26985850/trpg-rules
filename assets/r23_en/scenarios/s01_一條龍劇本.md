## Design Rationale

The One-Dragon Campaign gives the GM a complete adventure path they can "pick up and run." It is not a strictly linear script — PCs will always do something the GM did not expect — but a narrative framework that is **"trunk, branches, and toolbox."** The GM can use it as a default route, or lift chapters, encounters, and scenes into their own original sessions.

This scenario follows these design principles:
- **Realm-driven**: each chapter corresponds to one realm breakthrough, six chapters from First Glimpse to Return to Simplicity
- **Interwoven factions**: the "Four Clouds of the Age" form four story threads, unfolding across the six chapters
- **Ample branching**: each chapter end offers "optional GM variants" covering PCs who stray from the preset route
- **Citation-first**: martial arts / equipment / NPCs all reference named entries from existing rules, reducing GM improvisation burden

### Premises This Scenario Depends On

1. **PC count**: 4-6 people (this is the default difficulty baseline for all encounters)
2. **Sect distribution**: no specific sect required, but recommend at least 1 Righteous and 1 Neutral/Gray PC. An all-Evil party requires the GM to adjust some Righteous NPCs' initial attitudes
3. **PC level curve**: at the start of each chapter the PCs sit at the peak of the previous chapter's realm. If the PCs fall behind the scenario's expected pace, the GM should insert extra sessions or adjust encounter difficulty
4. **NPC character cards**: core NPC data referenced here is detailed in `11_NPC角色卡.md`. If an NPC has not yet appeared in the character cards, mark them "New NPC" in the scenario

### 1.1 Chapter Overview

#### Realm Positioning

This chapter is the PCs' transformation from "an ordinary person who knows a little martial art" to "a true Jianghu person." In this chapter the PCs complete the initiation trial, begin to understand the Jianghu's rules and dangers, and at the chapter's end break through to the Second Realm — Crossing the Threshold.

**Core conflict**: The PCs gather in the same place, at the same time, for different reasons, facing a crisis beyond their current ability. This crisis forces them to **cooperate** and **transcend themselves**.

**Recommended PC realm**: First Realm · First Glimpse (first session after character creation)

### 1.2 Hook / Hooks

> **The GM picks one hook, or mixes them. The goal is to bring 4-6 PCs together in one scene.**

#### Hook A: Tea House Encounter (suits all sects)

**Scene**: Three li outside Luoyang City stands the "Sihai Tea House" — a rest stop run by the Sihai Alliance. Today it is unusually lively: a mysterious storyteller is recounting the "open and secret struggles among the Tianjian Alliance's five sword factions."

**Suggested GM text**:
> "The tea house is packed. By the window, several young people in different sect attire each occupy a table. In the corner the storyteller strikes his waking-wood: 'The Tianjian Alliance Leader Lu Chenyuan, past sixty, with no son. In three years the succession arrives, and which of the five sword factions — Qingshuang, Lieyang, Luomei, Jinglei, Duanshui — is not eyeing it hungrily? Today's tale: how Lieyang Sword Master Duan Tianya shook all four with one move, "Blazing Sun Burns the Sky," on the sword-debate platform...'"

**Conflict trigger**: Before he finishes, a group of masked men in black bursts into the tea house. "By order of the Blood Moon Cult — righteous disciples present, hand over your weapons and you may keep your life." The NPC passersby panic; only the sect PCs have the ability — and the reason — to step forward.

**Why the PCs act**:
- Righteous sect PCs: the men in black are the Blood Moon Cult (Evil); righteous disciples are obliged to protect the innocent
- Neutral sect PCs: the men in black threaten "everyone hand over weapons" — that already touches their own interests
- Gray-Evil PCs (Poison Sect): the men in black don't know you and may beat you as a righteous disciple too

#### Hook B: Sect Field Mission (suits Righteous / Skill Sect / Medicine Sect)

**Scene**: The PC's master / elder gives the first real task — "go to Longquan Ferry outside Luoyang to retrieve an item / receive a person." On arrival, they find more than one sect sent disciples for the same item.

**Sects issuing the field mission**:
| Sect | Task |
|------|------|
| Sword Sect | Huashan Sect Master orders you to Luoyang for a sealed Tianjian Alliance letter (envelope reads "For the Alliance Leader's eyes only") |
| Qi Sect | A Taixu Palace Taoist orders you to Luoyang to receive a Taixu Palace envoy (actually a disciple sent by Ziyang) |
| Vajra Sect | The abbot orders you to escort a monk from Wanfo Temple's lower courtyard at Longquan Ferry |
| Skill Sect | A Gaibang elder orders you to Luoyang to ask around "who has been mass-buying medicinals lately" |
| Medicine Sect | Yaowang Valley orders you to receive a batch of rare herbs shipped from Jiangnan at Longquan Ferry |
| Shadow Sect | The master only says "go to Luoyang, act as circumstances dictate" (you don't even know what for) |
| Poison Sect | Tang Sect has you secretly observe a mechanism master from Qianji Pavilion (resting at the tea house) |
| Independent | You took an escort job — guard a merchant to Luoyang. Decent pay, but you don't know the merchant |

**Conflict trigger**: When all PCs arrive at Longquan Ferry, they happen upon a band of mountain bandits who seized the ferry. The boat is burned, goods scattered. Worse, the bandit chief is no ordinary bandit — the "Black Wind Stronghold Chieftain," said to have studied a demonic-cult fragment and brushed the edge of the Second Realm.

#### Hook C: Calamity from the Sky (suits Gray-Evil / Shadow Sect / Independent)

**Scene**: The PCs each walk the highway thirty li outside Luoyang. A horse's neigh — a spooked carriage careens wildly, the driver unconscious. From inside come a woman's sobs and a child's screams.

**Conflict trigger**: The carriage belongs to a nearby village; inside is a wounded villager. The rescuing PC discovers the carriage was not accidentally spooked — someone tampered with it. A mechanism was placed on the wheel. And the one who set it stands coldly on a nearby hillside, watching.

This person is "New NPC: Yin Jiuling, male, Qianji Pavilion defector, peak Second Realm, obsessed with using mechanism arts to test 'the limits of human nature'."

### 1.3 Key Scenes

#### Scene 1: Meeting in the Chaos (all hooks)

**Summary**: This is the PCs' first meeting in battle. Each shows their strength — Sword Sect draws blades, Qi Sect circulates Qi, Vajra Sect meets force with force, Poison Sect supports from range.

**GM description text**:
> "At the tea house / ferry / highway, all is chaos. You see a young swordsman draw his blade — his sword still carries a rookie's clumsiness, but his bearing says he won't retreat. You see a Taoist robed figure take a deep breath, hands slowly circling — that is the opening form of Taiji Fist. You see a bald monk say nothing, simply planting himself between the tea house / carriage / wounded and danger. And you — you look back the way you came, and decide your stance."

#### Scene 2: The Master's Comment (post-battle)

**Summary**: After the fight, a middle-aged man who sat in the corner without acting stands and brushes the dust from his clothes.

**Who is this person** (by hook):
| Hook | This person's identity |
|------|------------------------|
| Hook A (tea house) | The storyteller himself. He removes his disguise — a Gaibang elder of the Skill Sect. Having witnessed all, he says to the acting PCs: "Not bad, youngsters. Interested in doing this old beggar a favor?" |
| Hook B (field mission) | The ferry boatman. He lifts his bamboo hat — actually a Tianjian Alliance spy. "The Tianjian Alliance is looking for reliable newcomers. I saw what you just did." |
| Hook C (highway) | The "crying woman" in the carriage. She stops crying, lifts the curtain — a young masked woman. She only leaves "Not bad" and goes. If the PCs press, she says her name is "Liu Shengyan" (see 11_NPC角色卡.md §Yaowang Valley / Liu Shengyan) |

#### Scene 3: The First Choice

**Summary**: This elder / NPC gives the PCs a choice: "Black Wind Stronghold / the villains have noticed you. Within ten days they will come for revenge. You can each go your own way — or go together to find them."

This is the PCs' **first team decision**. The GM should not rush here — let the players make their choice. If they decide to split, jump to "Optional GM Variant B."

#### Scene 4: Black Wind Stronghold (combat scene, see 1.4 Encounter Design)

#### Scene 5: Breakthrough (realm breakthrough scene)

**Summary**: After defeating the Black Wind Stronghold Chieftain, the PCs find the demonic-cult fragment he studied in the stronghold. Only half a page — but the martial art recorded on it carries an evil air. More critically, at the fragment's end is a line in small characters: "The remaining seven pages are stored at the Shen Family Pawnshop in Luoyang City."

Meanwhile, the PCs also find the captured villagers, the looted goods, or the sect mission's target item in the stronghold.

**GM description text (realm breakthrough)**:
> "You sit cross-legged beneath an ancient pine behind Black Wind Stronghold. The mountain wind passes; you feel a warm current slowly circulating in your dantian — not a surge of Qi, but a subtle change: you begin to understand the 'rhythm' in martial arts. Not every move must be full force, not every strike a life-or-death gamble. True martial art is — knowing when to exert, when to hold back. You open your eyes and realize, without noticing, you already stand on the threshold of the Second Realm."

**Breakthrough condition check**:
- Experience (Martial Truth / XP) requirement: cumulative ≥ 20 Experience (Martial Truth / XP) (GM assesses by 1-3 sessions' progress)
- Martial requirement: master at least 1 initiation-level inner manual + 1 basic technique ✓ (satisfied at sect start)
- Story lock: complete the initiation trial — defeat the Black Wind Stronghold Chieftain and make the choice "Uphold the Righteous Path / Uphold Yourself" ✓

### 1.7 Realm Breakthrough Point

> **Break through to Second Realm · Crossing the Threshold**

**Trial description**: After defeating the chieftain, the PC feels for the first time between life and death the true meaning of "martial" — not move sets, but the ability to stay calm under pressure and find the opening in a desperate situation.

**GM guidance**: During the post-battle rest, give each PC a "personal epiphany" moment. Ask each player: "After this battle, what new understanding of 'martial' does your character have?"

**Breakthrough rewards**:
- Attribute +3 (max +2 single)
- Qi realm bonus +3
- Attribute cap unlocked to 6
- Dice modifier: Advantage Die vs lower-realm foes
- Martial Proficiency ×1: Second Realm gains passive "First Sense of Qi" — short rest recovery +2

### 1.9 Optional GM Variants

#### Variant A: PCs Decide Not to Party Up

If the PCs choose to go separate ways in Scene 3, the GM should:
1. Still give each PC one chance — after deciding to leave, each meets someone needing help (that someone is another PC's related NPC)
2. If still not partying up — jump to a "few days later" transition. A major event in Luoyang (see Chapter 2 Hook) draws everyone back to one place

#### Variant B: PCs Crush It Easily

If the PCs' build is strong or dice luck excellent, Black Wind Stronghold becomes too simple:
- The chieftain fires a signal flare before dying — "Black Wind Stronghold is finished, but you won't live either!"
- The flare means "someone in Luoyang" now knows — leaving a dangling thread
- Add an escape scene: Black Wind Stronghold begins to collapse / catch fire, the PCs must rescue hostages and flee within limited turns

#### Variant C: PCs Are Defeated

If the PCs are defeated in the Boss battle, do not wipe them out:
- The chieftain does not kill them — he needs live captives to ask "who sent you"
- The PCs are locked in the woodshed with other captured villagers
- This becomes a **prison-break scene** — the PCs must cooperate to escape, and during escape rediscover Scene 2 (save villagers) and Scene 5 (find the demonic fragment)
- After escape they may re-challenge the chieftain (the chieftain, due to demonic-art backlash, has only half his previous HP)

## Chapter 2: Crossing the Threshold

### 2.1 Chapter Overview

#### Realm Positioning

The PCs are no longer rookies fresh from the sect. They have their first fight side by side, built initial fame (Renown level 1: Minor Reputation). Now they step into the wider Jianghu — Luoyang City.

The core of this chapter is **"first taste of Jianghu"** — the PCs begin to learn the Jianghu is more than dueling; there is commercial interest, human relations, and undercurrents beneath the surface. The Sihai Alliance and Yaowang Valley are the main allies / NPC focus.

**Core conflict**: Someone in Luoyang is mass-buying rare medicinals, and these medicinals happen to be key ingredients of a specific poison / banned drug. When the PCs investigate, they find more than one buyer — both Righteous and Evil are scrambling for it. And behind it, the legend of "Wanxiang Grotto" seems involved.

**Recommended PC realm**: Second Realm · Crossing the Threshold (recommended break through to this chapter after at least 2 sessions)

#### Six-Attribute Baseline (GM reference)

|-----------|------------|-----------------------------------|

| Comprehension / Willpower / Charisma | 3-6 | Difficulty (DC) 10-15 |
| Qi Pool | 15-25 pts | — |
| Health Points (HP) | 25-40 | — |

### 2.2 Hook / Hooks

#### Hook A: Shen Wanjin's Invitation (suits Righteous / Neutral PCs)

**Scene**: The PCs receive a gilded invitation at an inn in Luoyang City. Sender: Shen Wanjin, chief manager of the Sihai Alliance (see 11_NPC角色卡.md §Sihai Alliance / Shen Wanjin).

**Invitation content** (GM may read aloud):
> "Valiant heroes, I am Shen Wanjin, chief manager of the Sihai Alliance. I have heard of your righteous deed at Black Wind Stronghold and admire you greatly. Tomorrow at dusk, a humble banquet awaits at Yangzhou Tower — matter of importance. If you honor me, I am deeply grateful. — Shen Wanjin"

**Dialogue at the banquet** (GM may narrate):
> Shen Wanjin is a plump middle-aged man, smiling like Maitreya. But his eyes — you notice — are frighteningly sharp. "Young heroes, I am a merchant, I speak plainly. Someone in Luoyang has been mass-buying several medicinals lately: Dragon-Saliva Herb, Cold-Stone Gall, Seven-Star Begonia. Each alone is ordinary bruise medicine, but together — together they are the main ingredients of 'Hua Gong San.' Hua Gong San temporarily seals cultivated Qi. What kind of person needs large amounts of Hua Gong San, tell me?"

**What Shen Wanjin wants**: He wants to know who is buying. His caravan was recently robbed outside Luoyang — not of gold, but a batch of medicinals. This bruises his pride and puts him on guard — if someone stirs trouble in Luoyang, it hurts the Sihai Alliance's business.

**Resources he provides**:
- Sihai Alliance intelligence network (rumor-gathering Difficulty (DC) -2)
- Shen Wanjin's private funding (200 taels as startup capital)
- A letter of introduction — if the PCs need Yaowang Valley help on medicinal uses

#### Hook B: Liu Shengyan's Plea (suits Medicine Sect / Neutral / Wandering PCs)

**Scene**: In Luoyang the PCs chance upon a young woman beset by local thugs. After helping, she calls herself "Liu Shengyan" (see 11_NPC角色卡.md §Yaowang Valley / Liu Shengyan), chief healer of Yaowang Valley's Jishi Hall. She came to Luoyang chasing a batch of banned medicinals leaked from Yaowang Valley.

**Liu Shengyan's dialogue**:
> "Thank you for stepping in. My surname is Liu, Liu Shengyan, I study medicine at Yaowang Valley. Three months ago, Yaowang Valley's pharmacy was tampered with — someone used fake accounts to hide the loss of thirty jin of Dragon-Saliva Herb. Dragon-Saliva Herb itself is unremarkable, but my master (Su Qingnang) says, if Dragon-Saliva Herb is paired with Cold-Stone Gall and Seven-Star Begonia — that is the Hua Gong San formula. Wandering heroes, you know what happens if Hua Gong San floods the black market?"

**Help Liu Shengyan can provide**:
- Medical treatment (free healing for PCs)
- Pill supply (provides 3 Minor Restoration Pills + 2 Detox Powders, see 08_Equipment & Pills)
- Professional appraisal of poisons / medicinals

#### Hook C: Shen Family Pawnshop Clue (continues Chapter 1 foreshadow)

**Scene**: Bearing the demonic-cult fragment from Black Wind Stronghold, the PCs come to the "Shen Family Pawnshop" in Luoyang. The pawnshop's clerk, "Shen San," a thin sharp-eyed middle-aged man, turns pale when he sees the fragment.

**Shen San's dialogue**:
> "Honored guests... where did you get this? It's not that I won't do business — I can't accept this, dare not accept it. Fine, I'll tell you plainly. Three months ago, someone pawned an identical fragment. Got a hundred taels. But the next day, that person was killed outside the city. A day later, someone came to redeem it — not the same person. I didn't ask. In this line, the less you know the safer. That person... I didn't see his face. But I remember a tattoo on his wrist — a black scorpion."

**Clue points to**: black scorpion tattoo. The PCs, via Jianghu Lore Difficulty (DC) 12 (Comprehension), may recognize it as the mark of a Blood Moon Cult subordinate faction in the Western Regions.

### 2.3 Key Scenes

#### Scene 1: Luoyang City Exploration (open scene)

**Summary**: Luoyang is the largest city in the Central Plains; the PCs may explore freely. Key interactive locations:

| Location | Description | Info / Resources obtainable |
|----------|-------------|------------------------------|
| **Yangzhou Tower** (Sihai Alliance) | Luoyang's most lavish restaurant, where merchants from all over rest. Shen Wanjin often banquets here | Hook A; meet NPCs here |
| **Longquan Pavilion** (sword forge) | Branch of the realm's most famous sword forge. An old smith is forging a sword — says it is for "a Taixu Palace bigshot" | Buy / upgrade weapons (see 08_Equipment & Pills); learn Ziyang is due in Luoyang for a sword |
| **Yaowang Jishi Hall** | Yaowang Valley's free clinic in Luoyang. Liu Shengyan sits here | Healing; buy medicine (pill supply); Liu Shengyan may be here |

| **Six Doors Luoyang Branch** | Government force, hall master "New NPC: Tie Jingxin, male, Six Doors Luoyang Branch master, Fourth Realm · Perfecting the Craft" | Learn court attitude toward Jianghu; Tie Jingxin pursues the fleeing demonic remnants |
| **Mixed Riffraff Tea House** | Small tea house east of Luoyang, where Gaibang disciples meet. Gather all intel here | Rumor-gathering Difficulty (DC) -2 (another -1 for Skill Sect / Gaibang PCs) |

#### Scene 2: Tracing the Medicinals (investigation chain)

**Summary**: The PCs must trace the buyer behind the medicinal purchases. Investigation chain from clue to conclusion:

```
Clue 1: Black Wind Stronghold fragment → Shen Family Pawnshop → scorpion tattoo → Blood Moon Cult (Hook C)
Clue 2: Site of Shen Wanjin's robbed caravan → on-site search → a scrap of cloth (special weave, Western Regions style) → Western Regions caravan → Blood Moon Cult or Western Regions force
Clue 3: Liu Shengyan's medicinal loss → find forged-account handwriting → done by an insider skilled in pharmacology → Yaowang Valley Netherworld Hall?
Clue 4: Recent transaction records of major pharmacies → three buyers → ① Western Regions merchant (Blood Moon Cult link) ② a mysterious Bashu buyer (Qianji Pavilion?) ③ a local Luoyang rich man
```

**Truth** (GM knows, PCs must discover):
- The Blood Moon Cult is indeed buying Hua Gong San ingredients — to weaken the Righteous before acting in the Northern Frontier (see Chapter 5)
- But there is a third batch of buyers — "New NPC: Chu Huaisha, male, Jianghu Independent, Third Realm · Complete Mastery." He belongs to no faction; he buys medicinals to brew something called "Po Feng San" — said to open certain mechanism seals in the outer Wanxiang Grotto
- The three batches of buyers don't know of each other — but they all scramble for the same medicinals, causing abnormal market fluctuation

#### Scene 3: Yangzhou Tower Night Banquet — Information Gathering

**Summary**: After some investigation, Shen Wanjin invites the PCs to Yangzhou Tower again. Liu Shengyan is present too (if the PCs haven't met her, Shen Wanjin introduces). This is an **information puzzle** scene.

**GM description text**:
> "In the private room on the second floor, Shen Wanjin unfolds a Luoyang map. 'This is what we know.' He taps three spots. 'Western Regions merchant — they haunt Camel Alley in the west of the city. The mysterious Bashu buyer — his men stayed at Ping'an Inn north of the city, leaving for Bashu in three days. And that local Luoyang rich man —' he pauses, 'this one is interesting. Name's Chu Huaisha, never heard of him before. But he holds something — several former-dynasty ancient maps. My men checked; even the Qianji Pavilion can't read what places those maps depict.'"

**Shen Wanjin's proposal**:
> "'My meaning is simple. The Western Regions merchant — leave to the Six Doors, Tie Jingxin will handle it. The Bashu buyer — our Sihai Alliance can tail him. But this Chu Huaisha —' he looks at the PCs, 'you want to know what's on his ancient map, and so do I. But I can't ask openly — I'm a merchant, business leaves a line. You are Jianghu people, fewer rules.'"

#### Scene 4: Meeting Chu Huaisha (social / stealth scene)

**Summary**: The PCs go to Chu Huaisha. He lives in a secluded courtyard north of Luoyang. On the outer wall — if the PCs look closely — some decorations that are actually mechanisms.

**Chu Huaisha's entrance**:
> "The courtyard gate opens. The man who steps out is around thirty, in a washed-pale blue robe, a plain wooden hairpin. Not handsome, but his eyes hold a strange light — as if he has seen too much he shouldn't. 'Looking for me?' He smiles. 'Or for what I hold?'"

**Chu Huaisha's attitude** depends on how the PCs approach:
- Direct force for the treasure: he delays with mechanisms and lightfoot, then flees (he is Third Realm, the PCs aren't his match yet)
- Frank dialogue: he tells the truth — "Po Feng San" he stole-learned from a man named Gongshu Xuan. He is not a bad man, but he needs into Wanxiang Grotto — because his sister was poisoned, and the antidote lies only in a certain secret chamber of Wanxiang Grotto
- Propose cooperation: if the PCs help him into Wanxiang Grotto, he will share the ancient map and Po Feng San formula. He also reveals important info — "You're not the only one who truly wants into Wanxiang Grotto. Qianji Pavilion has already deduced the entrance's rough location. At most half a year, the whole Jianghu will know."

#### Scene 5: Camel Alley — Shadow of the Blood Moon Cult (combat scene)

**Summary**: Whether or not the PCs meet Chu Huaisha, something happens in Camel Alley west of Luoyang: the Blood Moon Cult's Western Regions merchant tries to destroy evidence before leaving Luoyang, blocked by the Six Doors. But the Six Doors are short-handed — the fight has already started in Camel Alley.

**GM description text**:
> "When you reach Camel Alley, the alley is already chaos. Six Doors officers and Western Regions saber masters are tangled together. You see Tie Jingxin — the stern Six Doors hall master — solo against three men in black. His art is fierce and sharp, but they are many, and he is already panting. At the other end of the alley, a wagon is being loaded — that is the evidence; if not stopped, the trail goes cold."

**PC choices**:
1. Help the Six Doors fight the Western Regions merchant → Six Doors favor +1, Tie Jingxin owes the PCs a favor
2. Intercept the wagon in the chaos → obtain Blood Moon Cult document evidence (records their Luoyang activities and a blurry map)
3. Help neither, only watch → after the fight the Six Doors win but most evidence is destroyed

### 3.1 Chapter Overview

The PCs are now recognized good hands in the Jianghu. They leave Luoyang for Bashu — where Wanxiang Grotto lies. Along the way they enter the Jianghu's most complex layer: grudges and entanglements.

**Core conflict**: Wanxiang Grotto's entrance is locked onto by multiple forces — Tianjian Alliance (Duan Tianya leads), Qianji Pavilion, Blood Moon Cult, and various independents. ### 3.2 Hook / Hooks

#### Hook A: Duan Tianya's Invitation (suits Sword Sect / Righteous PCs)

**Scene**: On the way to Bashu, passing the Sword Pavilion, the PCs meet a team of swordsmen in Tianjian Alliance attire. The leader is a man in his early thirties — his gaze like a drawn sword, sharp but slightly flamboyant.

**Duan Tianya's entrance** (see 11_NPC角色卡.md §Tianjian Alliance / Duan Tianya):
> "'You're the Luoyang bunch?' He sits on his horse, looking down at you. 'I am Duan Tianya, Tianjian Alliance Lieyang Sword Master. I hear you crossed blades with the Blood Moon Cult in Luoyang.' He dismounts, walks up, sizes you up. 'How's your sword?' he asks the swordsman directly. No pleasantries, no courtesy. That is Duan Tianya's style — straight to the point."

**Duan Tianya's purpose**:
> "'You've heard of the succession. In three years Lu Chenyuan steps down. Of the five factions, my Lieyang faction holds — the Tianjian Alliance should not guard only the Central Plains. A sword rusts in its sheath. But Alliance Leader Lu and the Qingshuang conservatives disagree.' He pauses. 'I came to Bashu for Wanxiang Grotto. What's inside, whoever takes it owns it. I don't want it falling to the Blood Moon Cult or Qianji Pavilion. But the Tianjian Alliance is too big, too slow. You have no sect baggage — I want to work with you.'"

**Duan Tianya's sincerity**:
- If his men can't beat the PC swordsman (agrees to a spar), he respects the PCs more
- If the PCs accept cooperation — Duan Tianya teaches the Sword Sect PC a move "Cold Plum Sword Art · Frosty Sword Qi" (see 09_Martial Arts Codex §17)
- But Duan Tianya has his own aim — he wants to find in Wanxiang Grotto a "Sword-Intent Stone" said to raise sword-art realm, to gain advantage in the leadership contest

#### Hook B: Mo Ran's Plight (suits Medicine Sect / Poison Sect / Neutral PCs)

**Scene**: In a Bashu town, the PCs meet a man surrounded by villagers. He wears black, pale, with an uncomfortable aura — but the villagers surround him not because he did wrong, but because "he looks like a bad man."

**Mo Ran's entrance** (see 11_NPC角色卡.md §Yaowang Valley / Mo Ran):
> "The man stands surrounded, no expression. 'I'm just passing through, buying dry rations.' His voice is light but with strange penetrating power. A villager charges with a hoe — without moving, the villager's wrist is pinned by a silver needle, the hoe clatters down. 'I said, just passing through.' He takes silver from his robe, places it on the tea stall. Then looks at you — first time he meets anyone's eyes. 'You. You smell of medicine. Yaowang Valley people? Or studied medicine?'"

**Mo Ran's plight**:
- Mo Ran is Yaowang Valley Netherworld Hall Master — studies poisons. Liu Shengyan told you in Chapter 2 "the medicinals leaked from inside" — and Mo Ran is everyone's suspect
- But Mo Ran is not the real insider. The real insider is a Netherworld Hall disciple — Mo Ran came to Bashu to find evidence
- He needs the PCs' help — "You're not Yaowang Valley people, I can trust you. If I go to Junior Sister Liu (Liu Shengyan), she'll believe others saying I'm the insider. But if you help me find the evidence — I'll tell you a secret of Wanxiang Grotto"

**Secret Mo Ran knows**:
- In Wanxiang Grotto grows a spirit herb called "Pure-Heart Lotus" — called "the world's number-one antidote"
- The poison on Chu Huaisha's sister may only be cured by Pure-Heart Lotus
- This is why Mo Ran came to Wanxiang Grotto — not for himself, but to prove Netherworld Hall doesn't only harm

#### Hook C: Bashu Jianghu Rumors (suits all sects)

**Scene**: However the PCs arrive in Bashu, on arrival they hear two rumors:

**Rumor one**: Behind Emei Mountain, every full moon a blue light appears — lasting about an hour then vanishing. Locals call it a "fox immortal," but Jianghu people know — it is the sign of a formation opening.

**Rumor two**: Qianji Pavilion issued a "Qianji Invitation" — inviting skilled mechanism masters and ancient-script readers from across the realm to jointly crack Wanxiang Grotto's entrance seal. Time: three months. Place: Qianji Pavilion Headquarters.

**Truth of the rumors** (GM knows): Rumor two is Gongshu Xuan's setup — he does know Wanxiang Grotto's entrance, but needs more people to probe the seal's dangers.

### 3.3 Key Scenes

#### Scene 1: Sword Pavilion Pass — Duan Tianya's Spar (social / combat scene)

**Summary**: At the Sword Pavilion Pass, Duan Tianya proposes a bout with the PC swordsman. Not a fight to the death — a "sword debate" between swordsmen (see 07_GM Guide §atmosphere of sword debate).

**GM description text**:
> "At the Sword Pavilion Pass, the mountain wind is biting. Duan Tianya draws his Lieyang Sword — the blade glows gold in sunlight. 'Three moves.' he says. 'I only use three. If you take them, I admit you're a swordsman worth partnering. If not — I won't kill you, but you go your way, I go mine.' He holds the sword before him, tip slightly down — the opening form of Lieyang Sword Art, 'Sunrise.'"

**Spar rules** (non-lethal combat):
- Duan Tianya uses only three moves (three turns)
- The PC need not defeat him — only survive three moves with HP still > 0
- Duan Tianya's data adjusts to the PC's current realm for fairness

**Duan Tianya · spar brief data**:
| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Health Points (HP) | 50 (but each move deals at most 40% of PC max HP — holding back) |
| Accuracy | +7 (Third Realm has realm advantage over PC, normally very high, but halved holding back ≈ +4) |
| Damage | 2d6+2 (Lieyang Sword Art · Sunrise) / 3d6+2 (Lieyang Sword Art · Midday) / 2d8+3 (Lieyang Sword Art · Sunset — third move +2 accuracy if PC HP > 50%) |
| Special | No pursuit, no chasing the defeated. Sheathes after three moves |

**Result**:
- PC takes three moves: Duan Tianya nods. "Not bad. You're qualified to be my partner." → gain Duan Tianya's trust, Tianjian Alliance line opens
- PC fails to take: Duan Tianya sheathes. "You're not there yet. But I don't withdraw the invitation — I only suggest more practice."

#### Scene 2: Tang Sect Town — Mo Ran's Wrongful Accusation (social / investigation scene)

**Summary**: Mo Ran brings the PCs to a town under Poison Sect (Tang Sect) control. The tea here carries a faint numbness — Tang Sect habit. Mo Ran says the insider he tracked is hiding here.

**GM description text**:
> "In the town tea house, every cup is black — to hide the tea's color and avoid fear. Mo Ran sits in the corner, wiping a silver needle with a black cloth. 'See that one?' He jerks his chin at the pharmacy across. 'Gray robe. He's a disciple of my Netherworld Hall, named Meng Chuan. Three months ago he stole the Dragon-Saliva Herb outflow record. I've chased him two thousand li.' Mo Ran pauses. 'I can't move here. Tang Sect territory. If I move, every Tang Sect disciple in town comes for me. So — you draw him out. Say someone waits outside town, has Wanxiang Grotto news to sell him.'"

**Investigation choices**:
- Do as Mo Ran says (draw the snake) → leads to the truth later
- PC asks Meng Chuan directly → Meng Chuan says he stole the herbs not for the Blood Moon Cult, but to save his poisoned mother — and the poison was placed by another in Netherworld Hall. This becomes a Rashomon of "who tells the truth"
- PC asks around town → Tang Sect disciples tell the PC — "that Mo Ran, three years ago he nearly poisoned his own brother"

**Truth** (GM holds):
- Meng Chuan **did steal the medicinals**, but **not sold to the Blood Moon Cult** — he was coerced by "New NPC: Nangong Hui, male, former Yaowang Valley Netherworld Hall vice-master, Fourth Realm · Perfecting the Craft" using his mother's life
- Nangong Hui is the real insider. He has secretly defected to the Blood Moon Cult (or more complex — he is a Qianji Pavilion double agent planted in Yaowang Valley)
- Mo Ran did not betray Yaowang Valley, but he does have a dark side — "three years ago nearly poisoned his own brother" — that was his youthful recklessness losing control in a poison duel. This is his lifelong stain

#### Scene 3: Behind Emei Mountain — Blue Light at Full Moon (exploration / puzzle scene)

**Summary**: On the full moon, the PCs come behind Emei Mountain. The blue light's source is a stone cave halfway up. At the mouth is an ancient stele reading: "All phenomena return to origin, the mind is the key." A palm print on the face.

**GM description text**:
> "Moonlight falls on the stele, blue light glows deep in the cave. The palm print is exactly an adult hand's size. But when you place your hand, the stele does not react. You try circulating Qi — the stele glows faintly, then dims. Not that Qi is insufficient. Something is missing. Mo Ran / Chu Huaisha (whichever the PC brought) looks: 'This is no ordinary mechanism.' He points at fine lines on the face, 'This is — emotion? Or rather, mindset.' The stele demands — a sincere person who truly wants into Wanxiang Grotto, not force, not wisdom, but heart."

**Solution**:
- A PC must sincerely state why they enter Wanxiang Grotto (roleplay — no dice, but GM judges by sincerity whether the stele reacts)
- If the PC tries force → the stele releases a sword qi, all present take 2d6 internal injury
- If the PC tries mechanism arts → **new mechanism** Difficulty (DC) 18 (Comprehension) to parse the palm-print mechanism principle

**After the stele opens**: the cave mouth opens. But this is not Wanxiang Grotto's main door — only the outer area. Inside is a long corridor ending in a door. On the door is a circular groove missing a third of its arc. This is Wanxiang Grotto's "key" — three fragments form a complete circle to enter. The PC finds the first fragment deep inside.

**Summary**: When the PCs return to the Qianji gathering with the first fragment, Gongshu Xuan reveals the truth — Wanxiang Grotto's main door needs three fragments forming a key. Gongshu Xuan holds the second. And the third's location — Gongshu Xuan points at someone present.

**GM description text**:

##### Trial One: Sword's Extreme ("Heavenly Dao Sword")

**Description**:
> "You enter the first trial room. Empty except a sword floating in air. The blade is transparent, glowing pale blue. As you approach, a voice sounds in your mind: 'What is the sword?'"

**Trial content**: The PC must answer "what is the sword" with martial art (not only sword art) or wisdom. Not a judgment question — no standard answer. The trial tests the depth of the PC's understanding of their own martial path.

**Solution**:
- Direct answer (Oratory / Willpower check Difficulty (DC) 18): articulate your martial philosophy in words. Needs sincerity — false answers are detected by the sword
- Answer with action (spar the sword): enter a mental duel — the PC's spirit projection fights a sword-form-only opponent. Uses the PC's real data for 3 turns. Opponent data: HP 40, accuracy +8, damage 3d8. But the opponent mimics the PC's first three moves — the PC must see through their own pattern
- Ziyang's hint (if present): Ziyang outside says: "The sword is not you. You are the sword. The sword is not a sword. You are not you."

**Reward**: On pass gain "Sword-Intent Stone" — raises one sword art's proficiency one level (e.g. Complete Mastery → Perfecting the Craft). Also the PC gains a small inheritance of the ancient martial pulse — any one attribute permanent +1 (only for the passer)

##### Trial Two: Qi's Source ("Origin Return")

**Description**:
> "Second trial room. A round pool at the center. Water crystal clear — but you see no reflection. Beneath, countless fine air currents swim, alive. The voice returns: 'What is Qi?'"

**Trial content**: The PC must calm the pool's currents — or merge with them. Tests understanding and control of the Qi / Inner Strength system.

**Solution**:
- Force suppression (Constitution + Willpower combined check Difficulty (DC) 20): cover the whole pool with your Qi, suppress all currents. High-Constitution / high-Willpower Qi Sect PCs can
- Guide fusion (Comprehension Difficulty (DC) 18 + 8 Qi cost): understand the currents' motion, guide them to new balance. High-Comprehension PCs' first choice
- Transform-absorb (risk plan): draw the chaos into yourself — each current absorbed recovers 2 Qi, but needs Willpower check Difficulty (DC) 15 to avoid Qi Deviation (fail takes 2d6 internal injury)

**Reward**: On pass gain "Origin Stone" — holds the ancient Qi essence. Usable 3 times: each costs a main action to recover 3d6+4 Qi (like a Qi-Gathering Pill, no pill needed). Or choose one-time absorb — Qi cap permanent +10.

##### Trial Three: Life and Death ("medicine-poison same source")

**Description**:
> "Third trial room. Split in two — left full of rare flowers, fragrance thick; right a patch of withered black soil, stench of decay. At center a stone platform with a half-dead sapling. The voice says: 'What is the difference between medicine and poison?'"

**Solution**:

- Transform with poison (special plan): not heal it, but let it live in another form — treat poison as nourishment. Needs Poison / Alchemy Difficulty (DC) 16 + willingness to accept "poison is also part of nature"
- Philosophical answer (Oratory / Willpower Difficulty (DC) 18): answer "medicine and poison have no difference, both are nature's use. Medicine can kill, poison can save." — if roleplayed sincerely, the trial accepts

**Reward**: On pass gain two items:
1. "Pure-Heart Lotus Seed" — can grow new Pure-Heart Lotus (if not obtained before)

##### Trial Four: Mind's Mirror ("face the true self")

**Inner-demon projection**: The GM, by the PC's background, chooses and creates the scene the PC fears most. Examples:

### 5.2 Hook / Hooks

#### Hook A: Lu Chenyuan's Summons (Righteous PCs)

**Scene**: The PCs receive a Tianjian Alliance flying-pigeon letter — Lu Chenyuan asks the PCs to the Tianjian Alliance HQ "for a talk." This is not an invitation — this is the **Sword Alliance Order**, the highest summons in the Tianjian system.

**GM description text**:
> "When you reach the Tianjian Alliance HQ, no ceremonial pageantry greets you. Lu Chenyuan waits in a private chamber — not the spirited alliance leader on the debate platform, but a weary old man. His sword lies on the table. A new crack on the scabbard — not from battle, but quietly split by inner force. 'Someone wants my life.' Lu Chenyuan says it flatly, as if commenting on the weather. 'More than one. But I can't die — not yet. If I die, the Tianjian Alliance splits. Duan Tianya is radical but capable, the conservatives hidebound but have reason. I need them united before the barbarians come. And you —' he lifts his eyes, still sharp despite weariness, 'you walked Wanxiang Grotto. You broke up fights at the assembly. You're one of the few young people both factions respect. ' He slides something across the table — an envelope. 'This is Plan B's detail. Our spy in the Blood Moon Cult risked everything to send it. Read it, then tell me what to do.'"

**Envelope contents** (GM summary, may provide directly):
> Ye Xiuluo's Plan B has three steps:
> 1. **Hua Gong San attack**: put Hua Gong San in the Tianjian Alliance HQ drinking water (using a modified version of Nangong Hui's formula — tasteless, slow-acting, but once triggered seals all below Fourth Realm's Qi within 12 hours)
> 2. **Assassinate Lu Chenyuan**: as the alliance's combat power halves, Ye Xiuluo infiltrates personally, beheads Lu Chenyuan
> 3. **Total war**: once Lu Chenyuan dies, twelve Blood Moon Cult branch altars attack simultaneously, aiming to take three of the five sword peaks within three days

#### Hook B: Ye Xiuluo's Envoy (Evil / Neutral PCs)

**Scene**: The PCs receive a letter. No signature, only a blood-colored crescent moon. It says — "Our business in Wanxiang Grotto isn't finished. I wait at the old place."

**The "old place"**: ten li outside Luoyang, a ruined roadside shrine. The PCs once "negotiated terms" with a Blood Moon Cult spy there — if the PCs chose the cooperation route in Chapter 3.

If the PCs come:
> "In the shrine, a man stands with his back to you. Dark-red robe — not bright red, but dried blood's color. When he turns, you see his face — surprisingly young. Early thirties. Handsome, but his eyes hold a strange light — as if always looking far, not at you. 'I am Ye Xiuluo.' He says it as lightly as introducing what he ate today. 'Thank you — or rather, the thing you may help me do.'"

**Ye Xiuluo's dialogue**:
> "'Plan B — I know Lu Chenyuan already knows. His spy is good, followed the vine to my belly. But it doesn't matter. The real Plan B isn't Hua Gong San. The real Plan B is —' he smiles, a bitter smile, not a grin. 'I must break the final layer of the Blood Moon Art within three months. If I can't, I die. If I can, the Blood Moon Cult will have a leader who needn't rule by fear. Do you understand? Hua Gong San, assassination — these are distractions. The real Plan B is to negotiate with the Tianjian Alliance. But I need someone to believe the Blood Moon Cult is still that terrifying cult — otherwise the Righteous won't give me negotiation leverage.' He takes a vial — the Hua Gong San antidote. 'If you're willing — you're the only one who can deliver this to Lu Chenyuan.'"

**Ye Xiuluo's truth** (GM understanding — Ye Xiuluo's multi-faceted image):
- What he says is true — but he is also using the PCs
- His "negotiation" plan: let the Tianjian Alliance tacitly accept the Blood Moon Cult's legal existence in the Central Plains, in exchange the Blood Moon Cult withdraws from the Western Regions to open Righteous trade routes; and the Blood Moon Cult sends troops in the Northern Frontier
- Lu Chenyuan won't accept — but Duan Tianya might
- This creates a dilemma for the PCs — trust Ye Xiuluo (possibly a trap), or refuse him (miss a possibly peaceful chance)

#### Hook C: Chance to Create Own Technique (all PCs)

At some moment this chapter — before a battle's eve, on the road, in a desperate stand against a strong foe — the PC's self-created technique "solidifies."

**GM guidance** (insert at a fitting moment):

The Sixth Realm has no "breakthrough to the Seventh Realm" — the Sixth Realm itself is humanity's endpoint. But the Sixth Realm has a **finale choice** — more important than any breakthrough.

**GM final narration** (after the finale soliloquy — differs by the PC's choice):

**If the PC chooses to guard the Jianghu**:
> "You founded no sect, nor retired to the mountains. You walk the Jianghu far and wide — not as a disciple of some sect, but as yourself. Every autumn you sit a while beneath the ancient pine at Black Wind Stronghold. The pine is old now. But it still stands. So do you."

**If the PC chooses to found a sect**:
> "Your sect is not large — only a dozen-odd disciples. But each was picked by you one by one. Not the most gifted — the most upright of heart. You teach them martial art. But more often you teach them — 'The sword art is to protect people, not to prove you are stronger than them. Remember that.' You watch them practice in the courtyard, sunlight pouring down. Not bad."

**If the PC chooses to retire**:
> "Occasionally someone in the Jianghu mentions your name. 'That person who was at XXX back in the day — have you heard of them? Yes, him/her. They say he/she is gone now. Not dead. Hidden away in some mountain. Some say they saw a fisherman in the East Sea, with a line of writing on the rod — too faded to read now.'"

**If the PC chooses to travel west**:
> "You crossed the Great Wall, stepped into the Western Regions. You learned much martial art you had never seen. You ate much wind and sand. You remember the day you left the Central Plains — you looked back once, then walked on. You will not return. But you will write letters. One each year — to every person who fought at your side in those tea houses / ferry crossings / official roads."

**If the PC chooses Meng Po's path** (the most special ending):
> "Wangchuan Tower has a new Lord. The Jianghu still fears the name — but slowly they find Wangchuan Tower no longer takes any contract indiscriminately. The new Lord has a strange rule: 'Do not kill those who change the world. Not the wicked, not the good — only those who give up choosing.' No one knows what it means. But there is a rumor — they say in the secret chamber of Wangchuan Tower's headquarters, there is a window. Before it, forever, rests a sword. That sword was never used. But on it is carved a line: 'I walked my whole life with it. I no longer need it now.'"

### 6.9 GM Optional Variants

#### Variant A: The PC Leaves a Hook at the Finale

If the table wants the story to continue in this world — the GM may say after the finale:
> "Three years after the war. You are in Luoyang City — no longer the small place with a single tea house. On a notice at the city gate you see a strange message: 'Qianji Pavilion is about to begin analyzing a second Wanxiang Grotto. Gongshu Xuan says: this time it is different — this grotto is not in Bashu. It is beneath the East Sea.' You look at that notice. Then you look at those beside you — of those who fought side by side, some remain, some have gone their own ways. Enough people — to form a team again?"

**Sequel teaser**: If the whole table wants to continue — the story goes on.

#### Variant B: The PCs Diverge at the Finale

If the PCs' finale choices are irreconcilably split — say one wants to found a sect, another to retire:
- The GM should give each their own finale narration — let each choice mean something
- May arrange a "reunion years later" scene as closure — "Though each walks their own road, the bond of fighting side by side remains"

#### Variant C: Bad Ending — The World Did Not Improve

If the whole table's tone leans dark, the GM may choose a grimmer ending:
- The barbarians are driven back, but the Tianjian Alliance / Righteous forces are badly wounded; the Jianghu becomes a more chaotic place
- The PC's martial art is Number-One-Under-Heaven — but "What good is Number-One-Under-Heaven? You cannot save everyone"
- This is not a "punishment" of the PC — but a "real Jianghu" — the world does not improve because of one person's effort. But — precisely because such people exist, the world did not grow worse

## Appendix C: GM Quick-Run Checklist

Before each session, spend 3 minutes checking the following:

### Opening (10%)
- [ ] Announce Jianghu rumors (generated from last chapter's events, or use 06_江湖模擬.md §3.2, roll d10)
- [ ] Review last session's key events (let players tell it, not the GM recap)
- [ ] Record current PC realm, Heroism, Renown

### Exploration / Social (30%)
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 NPCs (use 07_主持指南.md §2.2 quick-build table)
- [ ] Confirm key clues already given to the PC
- [ ] Prepare 1–2 scenes where a specific sect / skill PC can shine

### Encounter / Conflict (40%)
- [ ] Verify enemy data (does realm match the PC? is count reasonable?)
- [ ] Confirm battle terrain / environment
- [ ] Prepare difficulty adjustment plan (too easy → reinforcements / too hard → turning point)

### Climax (15%)
- [ ] The session's core event or turning point
- [ ] Whether there is an "information dividend" for players
- [ ] Whether there is a character spotlight moment

### Wrap-up (5%)
- [ ] Update Renown and grudge records
- [ ] Award Experience (XP) and rewards
- [ ] Plant next session's hooks (1–2)
