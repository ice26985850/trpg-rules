## Chapter One: Black Wind Stronghold · A Rookie's First Step

> **PC Realm**: First Realm · First Glimpse  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter One (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter One)

### Hook

Your master / sponsor hands you your first solo task: "The mountain bandits at Black Wind Stronghold, outside Luoyang City, have been getting bolder lately. They've also got some villagers locked up inside. Go rescue them — you don't need to wipe out the whole stronghold, just get the people out."

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Encounter | Original | Solo Version |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Encounter 1: Mountain-road Ambush | 4–6 Mountain Bandit Mooks (14_#2) | 2 Mountain Bandit Mooks (one on the mountain road, one lying in ambush in a tree) |
| Encounter 2: Stronghold Gate Guard | Mountain Bandit Chief (14_#3) + 2 Mooks | Mountain Bandit Chief ×1 (guarding the gate alone — "the others all went drinking") |
| Encounter 3: Boss Han Bao | Health Points (HP) 30, Berserk +3 | Health Points (HP) 20, Berserk cap +2; when wounded >15 the Fear effect triggers (see `20_單人規則.md` §2.3) |

### Non-Combat Options

- **Stealth Route**: If the PC has the Stealth skill — can bypass the gate guard and go straight to the woodshed to rescue the villagers. Stealth DC 12 (solo version DC 10 — the defense is looser than the original).
- **Intimidate Route**: Strength/Charisma check DC 12, posing as "an advance scout sent by the Six Doors," to have the gate guard lead the way to the chief — skipping Encounter 2.

### Rewards

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| Experience (XP) 15 (including rescue XP) | — |
| Heroism +8 | Rescuing the villagers |
| Black Wind Stronghold treasure vault | 1d20+10 taels of silver, Jin Chuang Medicine ×2 |
| Demon Cult remnant fragment | On Han Bao's corpse — a blurred Hua Gong Powder formula; unusable but useful in Chapter Two |
| 3 free nights at a Luoyang inn | Thanks from the rescued villagers |
| Renown +1 (foundation of becoming known) | — |

## Chapter Two: Luoyang · A Storm is Brewing

> **PC Realm**: Second Realm · Crossing the Threshold  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter Two (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Two)

### Scene Reskin

In the original, the PC party deals with three factions — Shen Wanjin, Tie Jingxin, and Chu Huaisha. The solo version changes this: the PC becomes **Shen Wanjin's temporary agent** — he needs "someone who isn't a merchant" to investigate where the Hua Gong Powder ingredients are flowing.

> Arriving in Luoyang, you find the city more complicated than you imagined. The price of Dragon Saliva Herb at the apothecaries has inexplicably tripled. Six Doors constables have stepped up checks at the city gate. Shen Wanjin — the chief manager of the Sihai Alliance — finds you in a teahouse. "Young friend, help me look into something. It's inconvenient for me to show my face — I'm a merchant, and investigating a colleague would draw gossip. Find out who's buying up the Dragon Saliva Herb, and the reward is negotiable."

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Original Encounter | Solo Version |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Shenji Pawnshop info exchange | Unchanged — pure social |
| Six Doors Camel Alley battle (original: PC helps Tie Jingxin) | Solo version: by the time the PC arrives, the fight is nearly over. Tie Jingxin faces 2 Western Regions saber masters alone (Health Points (HP) 15 each). The PC may choose: join the fight (2v2) or stand by (Tie Jingxin wins in the third round but is wounded) |
| Chu Huaisha's courtyard | Original had "stop Chu Huaisha from fleeing." Solo version: changed to dialogue — under the PC's questioning, Chu Huaisha reveals part of the truth ("I bought the Dragon Saliva Herb to save someone"), but won't say who. If the PC passes a Read Intent check DC 14, they notice Chu Huaisha's voice changes when mentioning "sister" |

### Key NPC: Liu Shengyan

The PC first meets Liu Shengyan at Yaowang Valley's Charity Hall (a Luoyang free clinic). She is bandaging an injured villager — one of the villagers the PC rescued from Black Wind Stronghold.

> Liu Shengyan looks up at you: "You're the one who saved them? The medicine fee is on me — consider it repaying the favor they owe you. Oh —" she digs a cloth-wrapped bundle from her medicine box, "you carry the scent of the Demon Cult remnant from Black Wind Stronghold. You're the first person to take free Jin Chuang Medicine from my hands without thinking it beneath you. I'll remember you."

**Liu Shengyan's join condition**: PC Heroism ≥ 8 and has helped at least one commoner NPC.

**Liu Shengyan · NPC Companion stats** (simplified; see `20_單人規則.md` §4.1):
- Health Points (HP) 20 / 2 move uses per fight
- Move: "Medicine King Needle Art" (heal 2d6 Health Points (HP), costs 1 use)
- Passive: when the PC is adjacent to Liu Shengyan, Dying check DC −2
- Items held: Jin Chuang Medicine ×3 (may be given to the PC outside combat)

### Rewards

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| Experience (XP) 18 (includes investigation + assisting Tie Jingxin) | — |
| 30 taels of silver | Shen Wanjin's investigation fee |
| Six Doors favor +1 | Helping Tie Jingxin |
| Sihai Alliance favor +1 | Helping Shen Wanjin |
| Hua Gong Powder formula analysis report | Liu Shengyan helps analyze — confirms the buyer is not only the Blood Moon Cult (see original Variant D — the Western Factory's shadow, `12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Three Variant D) |

## Chapter Three: Yaowang Valley · The Mole

> **PC Realm**: Third Realm · Complete Mastery  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter Three (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Three)

### Scene Reskin

In the original, the PC party investigates the loss of medicines in Yaowang Valley. In the solo version: Liu Shengyan (or another companion) accompanies you to the Yaowang Valley entrance, but what happens inside the valley you must handle alone — Yaowang Valley's rule: non-Medicine Sect disciples entering the valley must alone face the "Three Questions of the Medicine King."

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Original Encounter | Solo Version |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Yaowang Valley entrance · mechanism gatekeeper | Comprehension DC 14 (solo version DC 12 — you're alone, so Su Qingnang went easy on you) |
| Netherworld Hall exploration | Originally might need multiple people splitting up. Solo version: Mo Ran proactively offers to show you around ("Heard you're investigating Hua Gong Powder? Follow me.") — is this a trap or sincerity? In the plot Mo Ran is innocent (see NPC entry), but the atmosphere here should make the PC suspicious |
| Nangong Hui Boss fight | Standard Health Points (HP) 45 → solo version Health Points (HP) 30 (`20_單人規則.md` §2.3). Liu Shengyan may stay outside the valley — if brought in, she can detoxify the PC in combat (passive: automatically clears one Poisoned condition on the PC each round) |

### Solo Extra Encounter: Mo Ran's Test

> "I heard you saved people from my hall in Luoyang." Mo Ran looks at you oddly — not like an interrogation, more like confirmation. "Why did you save someone you didn't know?"

Mo Ran decides their attitude based on the PC's answer. If the PC's answer is sincere (GM's judgment) — Mo Ran gives the PC a "Netherworld Hall outer-hall pass" — allowing free movement in Yaowang Valley without restriction, and hints the PC should inspect Nangong Hui's private pharmacy (which holds tampered accounts and leftover Hua Gong Powder ingredients).

### Solo Version: Chu Huaisha's Ending

In the original, Chu Huaisha is a multi-threaded NPC. The solo version simplifies: Chu Huaisha is surrounded and attacked by Blood Moon Cult spies outside Yaowang Valley; the PC may choose to save or abandon him.

- **Save Chu Huaisha**: Heroism +5. Chu Huaisha tells you the secret of the "Po Feng Powder formula" (see original) and promises the "mysterious symbol" clue from the Chapter Two report (used in Chapter Four). Chu Huaisha will not join as a companion — "I owe you my life, but there are things I must finish alone."
- **Do not save**: Chu Huaisha is captured by the Blood Moon Cult. Infamy +3. He will appear in Chapter Five as a "brainwashed Blood Moon Cult elite disciple" — the PC must snap him out of it rather than kill him (new combat reaction: "Do you remember me? — Chu Huaisha's saber pauses for 0.1 seconds").

### Rewards

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| Experience (XP) 20 | — |
| Mo Ran's poison-needle pouch ×10 | If passed Mo Ran's test (hidden weapon, poison-coated) |
| Po Feng Powder formula | Saving Chu Huaisha |
| Nangong Hui's secret letter | Looted after the Boss fight — "Chief Supervisor Cao has approved. No leaks within three months." (confirms the Western Factory secret line) |

## Chapter Four: Tianjian Alliance · Wanxiang Grotto

> **PC Realm**: Fourth Realm · Perfecting the Craft  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter Four (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Four)

### Scene Reskin

The Tianjian Alliance succession ceremony is the largest social scene in the original. Solo version: the PC is invited as "the key clue to unraveling the fragment mystery" — because in the first three chapters you obtained at least two Wanxiang Grotto fragments.

Duan Tianya becomes a possible companion at this point. In the succession struggle he chooses "to bear it alone" — siding with no faction. If your PC chose the righteous path in earlier chapters (Heroism ≥ 20), Duan Tianya will proactively offer to travel with you.

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Original Encounter | Solo Version |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Tianjian Alliance succession debate | Original: the PC party mediates among the factions. Solo version: the PC is invited by Lu Chenyuan to "say one thing" — what do you want to say about the cracked Tianjian Alliance? Based on your answer, influence the ceremony's ending (Charisma DC 16 can mend some of the rift) |
| Wanxiang Grotto's four great trials | Original: four people each pass one trial in the grotto. Solo version: all four trials must be completed alone by the PC — but between each trial the "Spirit of the Grotto" (played by the GM) grants one recovery (Health Points (HP) fully restored, Qi +5) |
| Duan Tianya "spar" | Original: the spar is friendly exchange. Solo version: changed to "Duan Tianya, by Lu Chenyuan's order, tests whether you are worthy to enter the grotto" — must fight Duan Tianya (non-lethal). After defeating him, Duan Tianya says: "You're stronger than me. I'll go in with you — not to protect you, but because you need someone to help you hold off the grotto's minions." |

### Solo Version Grotto Trials

| Trial | Test | Solo DC | Pass Reward |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| Trial of Strength | Strength DC 16 | Lift the gate-guarding stone lion | Strength permanently +1 (not counted toward cap) |
| Trial of Wisdom | Comprehension DC 18 | Solve the Three-Talents formation | Gain "Formation-Breaking Insight" — thereafter Technique Reading DC −2 |
| Trial of Focus | Willpower DC 18 | Face your greatest fear in the illusion | Gain "Mind Like Still Water" — thereafter Qi Deviation check DC −3 |
| Trial of Clarity | Introspection (no DC — GM judges by the PC's story arc) | Face your deepest regret | Unlock self-created martial art condition (realm met is enough to self-create, no story-lock) |

### Rewards

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| Experience (XP) 25 | — |
| Heavenly Sword Art · Beginner's Volume | Taught personally by Lu Chenyuan (uses Strength or Comprehension — the weapon is your martial comprehension, not a specific sword art) |
| Ancient Map · Complete Edition | The four fragments combined — reveals the "ancient martial vein" lies beneath the Blood Moon Cult Main Altar |
| Duan Tianya (optional companion) | Join condition: Heroism ≥ 20 and PC passed at least two trials in the grotto |
| Named Sword "Chengying" (replica · 1d8+2) | Gift from Lu Chenyuan — "This sword is worthy of you" |

## Chapter Five: Blood Moon Cult · The Peak of the Demon Cult

> **PC Realm**: Fifth Realm · Peak of Perfection  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter Five (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Five) + Western Factory secret-line Variant D

### Scene Reskin

In the original this is a large-scale frontal conflict. The solo version changes it to: the PC infiltrates the Blood Moon Cult Main Altar — relying on the connections built in the first three chapters (Liu Shengyan's antidotes, Duan Tianya's sword cover, or Mo Ran's intelligence) rather than a full army battle.

Han Tieyi appears at this point — if the PC previously contacted the Western Factory secret line or has good relations with the Six Doors.

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Original Encounter | Solo Version |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Stronghold outskirts | Original: great battle Blood Moon Cult elite ×3 + mooks ×6. Solo version: Han Tieyi's Imperial Guard draws most of the forces outside — the PC only needs to face 2 Blood Moon Cult elites and 1 minor chief. Han Tieyi can provide ranged fire support (once per day — "Move! My archers have him locked!") |
| Li Canyang Boss fight | Standard Health Points (HP) 60 → solo version Health Points (HP) 40 (`20_單人規則.md` §2.3). Li Canyang's saber art is limited in the narrow corridor — cannot use the area mode of "Setting-Sun Slash" |
| Ye Xiuluo Boss fight | Standard version Health Points (HP) 85 → solo version Health Points (HP) 55 (`20_單人規則.md` §2.3). If Duan Tianya is present — Duan Tianya can clash with Ye Xiuluo for one round at the start of combat, buying the PC an extra "initiative action" (one extra major action) |

### Ye Xiuluo's Dialogue

The solo-party Boss fight shouldn't be a pure numbers clash — Ye Xiuluo says a passage to the PC:

> "I've been watching you since five years ago. From that greenhorn at Black Wind Stronghold, to the nameless investigator in Luoyang City, to the one in Wanxiang Grotto who faced your deepest fear without flinching — I always thought the Jianghu would never see another like you. They either died too early, or changed to become too much like us."  
> "Make your move. Let me see — are you the kind who changes like them, or are you truly different."

### Solo Version Ending Branches

| PC's performance in combat | Ye Xiuluo's ending |
|----------------------------|--------------------|
| Go all-out, frontal confrontation | Ye Xiuluo is defeated but refuses capture — leaps into the abyss below Wanxiang Grotto (the entrance to the "ancient martial vein"). Leaves last words: "The thing below... go see it yourself. I'm no longer worthy." |
| Had the chance to "abandon pursuit" in combat and chose to spare | Ye Xiuluo survives the battle. In Chapter Six he appears on the Northern Frontier battlefield as "an uninvited reinforcement" — "I'm not here to help you. I'm here to kill Jin Ridi — it just happens you're here too." |
| PC uses the Infamy route (Infamy ≥ 25) | When Ye Xiuluo's Health Points (HP) drop to 10%, he stops fighting. "Enough. You're not fit to be my enemy — you're fit to take my place. The Blood Moon Cult needs a new cult leader. You come — I'll go." (PC gains the status "Blood Moon Cult cult-leader candidate" — Chapter Six's optional route changes drastically) |

### Western Factory Secret-Line Settlement

See original Chapter Five Variant D (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Five Variant D). In the solo version, Zhuque appears after the Ye Xiuluo fight — leaving only one line: "Chief Supervisor Cao is very interested in you. Next time we meet — it won't be on the battlefield." This line foreshadows Chapter Six.

### Rewards

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| Experience (XP) 30 | — |
| Star-Absorbing Great Method · Fragment | Found in his study after defeating Ye Xiuluo |
| Ye Xiuluo's token (Asura Mask) | Optional — when worn, intimidates same-realm enemies (Willpower DC 18, on failure hit −2) |
| Han Tieyi (optional companion) | If cooperated with the Imperial Guard in combat |

## Chapter Six: Northern Frontier · All Under Heaven

> **PC Realm**: Sixth Realm · Return to Simplicity  
> **Citation**: Original Chapter Six (`12_一條龍劇本.md` §Chapter Six)

### Scene Reskin

The barbarians breach Yanmen Pass. The Imperial Court collapses entirely. The PC need not command vast armies — the solo version's finale is: **on the battlefield, find Jin Ridi and stop him from using the "ancient martial vein" to activate Wanxiang Grotto's final mechanism (an ancient weapon capable of destroying the Central Plains' martial world).**

### Solo Encounter Adjustments

| Original Encounter | Solo Version |
|--------------------|--------------|
| Northern Frontier battlefield melee | Original: PC army clashes head-on with the barbarian army. Solo version: the PC's small squad (companions + 3–5 Tianjian Alliance disciples / Imperial Guard light cavalry) is responsible for infiltrating behind enemy lines, going straight for Jin Ridi's command post. The accompanying NPC holds off the minions — the PC need not roll dice against every foot soldier |
| Jin Ridi Boss fight | Standard Health Points (HP) 100 → solo version Health Points (HP) 70 (`20_單人規則.md` §2.3). Jin Ridi's "Wolf Pack Summon" drops from 3 to 1 (Phantom Wolf, Health Points (HP) 15) |

### Jin Ridi's Weakness

In the solo version, Jin Ridi has an extra weakness: his "Beast-King's Blood" depends on the energy supplied by the ancient martial vein. If the PC destroys the connection between the martial vein and the grotto mechanism before (or during) combat (Trapcraft DC 20 or Comprehension DC 22), Jin Ridi loses 1d6 Health Points (HP) per round thereafter ("His power is being cut off...").

### Peace Route

The solo version keeps the peace route from the original — if the PC proposes a concrete provisions / trade plan (Charisma DC 20), Jin Ridi may be willing to retreat. The solo version triggers the peace route more easily than the original — because the PC has accumulated enough "Jianghu credibility" in earlier chapters.

### Finale Cutscene (CG)

After combat ends (regardless of win/loss or route), the PC stands alone on the ancient battlefield. The following recollection appears — GM reads aloud:

> The wind from the Northern Frontier blows, no longer carrying the smell of blood. You remember that night at Black Wind Stronghold — back then you only thought of saving a few villagers, never imagining this road would lead here.  
> You remember the medicine scent when Liu Shengyan handed you your first dose of Jin Chuang Medicine.  
> You remember Duan Tianya's words at the Wanxiang Grotto entrance: "You're stronger than me."  
> You remember Ye Xiuluo's final expression at the edge of the abyss.  
> This road you walked alone — but you were never truly alone.
