## I. Weapon Tables

> **Usage Notes**:
> - **Damage**: base damage dice; final damage = weapon damage + move bonus + attribute modifier + other bonuses
> - **Damage Type**: the damage type the weapon itself deals; a move may override this type (e.g. using a sword with an internal-Qi move may deal Internal Injury)
> - **STR Requirement**: if unmet, hit check **−2** and damage dice halved (rounded up)
> - **Hidden-Weapon type**: uses **CMP** instead of STR for hit; STR requirement becomes CMP requirement
> - **Hit Modifier**: light weapons +1 hit, heavy weapons −1 hit, reflecting handling differences

## II. Armor Tables

> **Usage Notes**:
> - **External Reduction**: subtract this value from each External Injury taken (minimum reduced to 1)
> - **AGI Penalty**: when wearing armor, Initiative, evasion, and lightfoot-related checks all take this penalty
> - **CON Requirement**: if unmet, AGI penalty doubles, and from the 3rd round of each battle the wearer is Exhausted (all checks −1 until battle ends)
> - **Defense (DC)**: the base Defense DC bonus armor provides, unified into External Reduction (see Combat System §4.4.1: Defense DC = 11 + AGI modifier + armor bonus)
> - **Internal Injury / Qi damage is completely unaffected by armor reduction** (see Core Mechanics damage types)
> - **Special Property**: some armor has extra bonuses or special effects

## III. Pill Tables

> **Usage Notes**:
> - **Rarity**: Common / Rare / Precious / Peerless. Affects purchase price, acquisition difficulty, and GM drop frequency
> - **Price**: reference market price in silver taels. Actual price varies by region, seller, and plot (±50%)
> - **Alchemy DC**: the Alchemy check DC a player (requires Medicine Sect background or Alchemy skill ≥2) must pass to craft this pill
> - **Cooldown**: combat-use cooldown for the same pill (usually 1 per battle; "No Cooldown" means it can be taken repeatedly)
> - **Consumption**: taking a pill in combat costs a **major action**

## 4.5 Forging & Alchemy Rules (Player-Optional)

The following rules are for player characters with relevant skills to use between sessions.

### Forging Weapons

Forging a weapon requires: a smithy (or own tools), materials, time, and craftsmanship.

| Weapon Quality | DC (Trapcraft/STR) | Material Cost | Time | Source |
|----------------|--------------------|---------------|------|--------|
| Common (shop quality) | 10 | Price × 50% | 1 day | Ordinary iron ore |
| Fine (+1 damage or hit) | 15 | Price × 100% | 3 days | Hundred-refined steel |
| Superior (+1 damage and +1 hit) | 22 | Price × 200% | 7 days | Cold iron / black iron |
| Divine (+2/+1 and special property) | 30 | Price × 500% + fortuitous material | 30 days | Meteoric iron and other legendary materials |

> Critical Failure (double 1): materials scrapped, money not refunded. Critical Success (double 10): weapon quality +1 extra or time halved (player's choice).

### Brewing Pills

Alchemy requires: an alchemy furnace (bronze furnace 80 taels or borrow Yaowang Valley's alchemy room), herbs, a pill formula, and time.

| Pill | DC (Alchemy) | Material Cost | Time | Yield |
|------|--------------|---------------|------|-------|
| Jin Chuang Medicine | 10 | 2 taels | 2 shichen | 1d3+1 doses |
| Minor Restoration Pill | 12 | 8 taels | 4 shichen | 1d2 pills |
| Qi-Returning Powder | 14 | 10 taels | 4 shichen | 1d2 doses |
| Detox Pill | 14 | 5 taels | 4 shichen | 1d2 pills |
| Life-Continuing Pill | 18 | 30 taels | 1 day | 1 pill |
| Great Restoration Pill | 24 | 80 taels | 3 days | 1 pill |
| Nine-Turn Reviving Pill | 30 | 300 taels + millennium lingzhi | 7 days | 1 pill |

> **Obtaining Formulas**: basic pills (Jin Chuang Medicine, Minor Restoration Pill, Qi-Returning Powder, Detox Pill) starter formulas are gifted by the sect. Rare formulas require fortuitous encounter, trade, or quests.
>
> **Herb Gathering**: basic herbs can be gathered in the wild (CMP check DC 12 gathers 1d4 common herbs, offsetting 5 taels material cost). Gathering Critical Failure (double 1) means mistakenly picking poison grass — wastes half a day and the gatherer must make a CON check DC 12 for poison.
>
> **Alchemy Failure**: failed check wastes materials (silver not refunded). Critical Failure (double 1) scraps materials and damages the furnace (10 taels repair, or next batch DC +2). Critical Success (double 10) doubles yield. Rare herbs (lingzhi, snow lotus, ambergris) only obtainable via specific plots or high-difficulty fortuitous encounters.

## V. Design Notes

### 5.1 Weapon Design Principles

1. **Light vs Heavy**: Light weapons (Short Sword / Short Saber / Throwing Dart etc.) have lower damage but +1 hit, suiting AGI builds or initiates; Heavy weapons (Black Iron Heavy Sword / Horse-Cutting Saber / Zhangba Long Spear etc.) have high damage but −1 hit, suiting STR-focused characters. Once STR reaches a threshold the hit penalty is removed (e.g. Black Iron Heavy Sword at STR ≥ 8), reflecting the "strength to lift a cauldron" growth arc.

2. **Named / Divine positioning**: Named weapons (Azure Frost Sword / Blood-Fang Saber / Coiling Dragon Staff etc.) provide tactical bonuses on top of standard weapons (lower crit threshold, attached conditions), targets for mid-game characters. Divine weapons (Purple Lightning Sword) outclass ordinary weapons across the board, reliably triggering crits with high attributes — the end-game gear.

3. **STR Requirement Gradient**: STR requirement ranges 2 (initiate) to 8 (Grandmaster), covering an equipment-upgrade path across all realms. The −2 hit penalty when unmet is harsh enough (~15% lower success) yet doesn't fully prevent use ("barely swinging a heavy sword" is a classic wuxia scene).

4. **Hidden Weapons as an independent system**: hidden weapons use CMP for hit (reflecting "mind to eye to hand") and are mostly long-range (three-step/far bands), giving Poison Sect / high-CMP characters an independent weapon curve not reliant on STR. Hidden-weapon carry count is set by AGI (AGI experts carry and draw more quickly).

5. **Strange Weapons' tactical variety**: Judge's Brush boosts acupoint-sealing, Iron Fan blocks hidden weapons, Dust Whisk provides control, Nine-Section Whip adds attack range — each strange weapon offers a unique tactical dimension, encouraging off-meta builds.

### 5.2 Armor Design Principles

1. **Defense vs Agility tradeoff**: armor provides External Reduction (2→6) at the cost of AGI (−1→−4). Heavy-armor characters are nearly immune to mob attacks under high reduction, but slow to act and hard to evade. The extreme Bright Light Armor even forbids evasion — only hard-tank or parry/block.

2. **CON Requirement Gradient**: CON requirement ranges 0 to 6, forming a "double-attribute threshold" with STR — a character in heavy armor wielding a heavy sword must invest in both STR and CON, naturally sacrificing AGI/CMP/CHA, creating attribute-level build tradeoffs.

3. **Tactical value of under-armor wear**: Golden-Silk Soft Armor and Heavenly Silk Robe can be worn unseen under clothing while providing considerable protection. This matters greatly in social/infiltration scenes — a character in fine robes at a banquet with hidden soft armor won't be exposed.

4. **Armor's distinction by damage type**: External Injury is reduced, Internal Injury fully penetrates — this ensures inner-art masters (Qi Sect) and Poison Sect retain advantage against heavy-armor characters. There is no "universal defense."

### 5.3 Pill Design Principles

1. **Resource scarcity drives decisions**: pills are limited by price, rarity, and cooldown. In combat each type is limited to 1–2 uses per battle — you cannot win by pill-popping, you must use them at key moments. Peerless pills have cross-session cooldowns, ensuring they are "last resort" not "routine consumable."

2. **Alchemy as a sub-system**: Alchemy DC spans DC 10→30, aligned with the Core Mechanics DC gradient. Only Medicine Sect characters (Alchemy skill ≥2) may brew Rare-and-above pills, giving Medicine Sect a unique economic and team-contribution channel.

3. **Moral dimension of side effects**: buff pills like Great-Strength Pill and Light-Body Powder have "post-use attribute penalty" — short-term gain for a cost, preventing "pops pills before battle" from being optimal. Using some poisons/illegal items lowers Heroism, tying resource choice to character identity.

4. **Cautious role of breakthrough pills**: Realm-Breaking Pill and Foundation-Building Pill provide limited check bonuses rather than auto-success — they lower failure risk without removing challenge. The "Epiphany Pill" is 1-use only and Peerless, ensuring it is a true highlight moment.

### 5.4 Synergy with Existing Systems

| System Module | Synergy Point |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Core Mechanics (01)** | Attribute modifier formula (attribute−4) determines hit; 2d10+modifier vs DC determines alchemy success/failure; HP/Qi formulas set the design baseline for pill recovery amounts |
