## Reading Notes

This chapter provides **20 general enemies**, grouped by realm and scene, for the GM to grab on the fly for impromptu encounters. Enemy data is simplified — no need to track Qi-cost details; just deduct the single-use Qi cost noted on each move.

### Data Format Notes

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Attributes** | The six attribute values; modifier in parentheses (attribute value − 4) |
| **HP** | CON × 5 + realm bonus |
| **Qi** | CON × inner-art coefficient + realm bonus |
| **Defense (DC)** | 11 + AGI modifier + armor bonus |
| **Initiative** | AGI + WIL |
| **Evasion** | AGI modifier (base evasion-check bonus if no lightfoot) |

### Realm Quick Reference

| Realm | HP Bonus | Qi Bonus | Defense DC Base | Mob Reference |
|-------|----------|----------|-----------------|---------------|
| First Realm · First Glimpse | +0 | +0 | 11 | ordinary commoners, mooks |

## I. Human Force Tier (First to Third Realm, 12 enemies)

### 1. Street Hooligan (First Realm · Weak)

> "Hey, new guy? This street belongs to Tiger Lord. If you wanna pass — you know what's expected."

The lowest rung of street scum. They go around in groups of three or five collecting protection money, harassing decent folk, and bullying out-of-towners. Their fists aren't hard but their guts are big — yet at the first sight of blood their knees go soft.

| Attribute | STR 3(−1) | AGI 3(−1) | CON 3(−1) | CMP 2(−2) | WIL 2(−2) | CHA 2(−2) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 15 | Qi 3 | Defense DC 10 | Initiative +5 | Evasion −1

**Martial Arts**:
- **Hooligan Fist** — hit +0, damage 1d4 (min 1), Qi 0. Formless street brawling moves.
- **Grab a Weapon** — hit +1, damage 1d6, Qi 0. Picks up a bench, wooden club, wine jug, or the like to smash. After use, loses the weapon (needs a minor action to pick up again).

**Tactics**: 3–6 surround a lone target (flanking bonus +1~+2). Morale extremely low — once someone drops, the rest must make a WIL check DC 10 or scatter. Morale +2 when a chief is present.

**Drop/Reward**: 2d6 copper coins, broken dagger (weapon, damage 1d4)

**Scenes**: city back-alleys, outside gambling dens, dock warehouses, urban slums

### 2. Mountain Bandit Mook (First Realm · Weak)

> "This mountain is mine, this tree I planted. If you want to pass this way — you know."

Impoverished folk driven to banditry, or idle good-for-nothings. Rough saber work but many in number, holding the mountain passes to rob merchants. Starving, they'll fight desperate — but against a real martial artist they collapse at a touch.

| Attribute | STR 4(0) | AGI 3(−1) | CON 4(0) | CMP 2(−2) | WIL 3(−1) | CHA 2(−2) |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 20 | Qi 4 | Defense DC 10 | Initiative +6 | Evasion −1

**Martial Arts**:
- **Bandit Saber** — hit +1, damage 1d8, Qi 0. Wild hacking, all brute force.
- **Thrown Stone** — hit −1, damage 1d4, Qi 0. Picks up a stone to throw, usable in the three-step band.

**Tactics**: 4–8 swarm at once, no coordination. Prioritizes attacking the weakest-looking target. Disperses after the chief is defeated or half are down (WIL DC 12).

**Drop/Reward**: scattered copper coins 2d10, inferior single saber (weapon, damage 1d8), one ration

**Scenes**: mountain pass, forest trail, bandit-stronghold outskirts, abandoned temple

### 3. Mountain Bandit Chief (Second Realm · Mid)

> "Brothers, don't panic! Just a few who know a little kung fu — slaughter them for the wine!"

Leader of a small bandit gang, a cut above the mooks. Usually a retired old soldier, a sect reject, or a naturally brutish sort. Knows a little crude inner art — can throw his weight around locally but never earns a true jianghu practitioner's notice.

| Attribute | STR 5(+1) | AGI 4(0) | CON 5(+1) | CMP 3(−1) | WIL 4(0) | CHA 4(0) |
|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 30 | Qi 13 (Initiate mental-method coefficient 2) | Defense DC 12 (leather +1) | Initiative +8 | Evasion 0

**Martial Arts**:
- **Mountain-Cleaving Saber** — hit +2, damage 2d6+1, Qi 1. Weighty forehand chop.
- **Barbarian Bull Charge** — hit +1, damage 1d8+1, Qi 2. Charges forward; on hit target must make a STR check DC 12 or be pushed back one band.
- **Hunyuan Stance (Inner Art)** — passive: inner-art coefficient 2; Qi recovery 1/round (out of combat recovers 1 WIL point per 10 minutes).

**Tactics**: Lets the mooks wear down the enemy, then strikes from the flank. When HP drops below half, tries to negotiate or flee. Yells "Tough customer — retreat!" to order withdrawal.

**Drop/Reward**: silver 3d10, fine-steel single saber (weapon, damage 1d8+1), stronghold treasure map (leads to a sidequest), inferior Jin Chuang Medicine ×1

**Scenes**: stronghold main hall, mountain-road ambush point, bandit feast

### 4. Six Doors Constable (Second Realm · Mid)

> "Six Doors is on a case — civilians stand clear. Are you coming with us, or do we invite you with iron chains?"

The court's tentacle into the jianghu. Properly trained, with disciplined fist, foot, and saber work, plus a little tracking and investigation know-how. A single constable is no master, but a group working with Six Doors authority and yamen resources is enough to make ordinary jianghu folk wary.

| Attribute | STR 4(0) | AGI 5(+1) | CON 4(0) | CMP 4(0) | WIL 5(+1) | CHA 4(0) |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|----------|-----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 25 | Qi 11 (Initiate mental-method coefficient 2) | Defense DC 13 (leather +1) | Initiative +10 | Evasion +1

**Martial Arts**:
- **Six-Door Swift Saber** — hit +2, damage 1d8+1 (AGI bonus), Qi 1. Issue saber art of fighting slow with fast, designed for capture.
- **Grappling Hand** — hit +2, damage 1d4+1 (STR bonus), Qi 2. On hit target must make an AGI check DC 12 or have one arm locked (that arm cannot attack/block next round).
- **Iron Ruler Block** — spend 1 Qi (reaction), this round +3 Defense DC vs single-target attacks.

**Tactics**: 2–4 coordinate to surround and capture. One pins from front, another grapples from the flank. Prioritizes control moves over killing. Shouts "Stand down!" (minor action, target WIL check DC 12 or next-round hit −1).

**Drop/Reward**: Constable waist tablet (plot item), issue single saber, silver 2d6, wanted poster (leads to bounty quest)

**Scenes**: town streets, crime scene, yamen main hall, during escort

### 6. Jianghu Independent Cultivator (Second Realm · Mid)

> "This one is but a humble independent, wandering the four seas. Righting wrongs when met — but mood permitting."

A solitary cultivator with no sect. May be a lucky one who found a fragment manual somewhere, or the last heir of some fallen sect. Broad but not refined in martial arts, yet extremely survivable — after all, surviving alone in the jianghu is itself a skill.

| Attribute | STR 4(0) | AGI 5(+1) | CON 5(+1) | CMP 5(+1) | WIL 4(0) | CHA 4(0) |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 30 | Qi 13 (Initiate mental-method coefficient 2) | Defense DC 12 | Initiative +9 | Evasion +1

**Martial Arts** (GM may pick any two to shape different cultivator styles):
- **Scattered Palm** — hit +2, damage 2d6, Qi 1. Flexible close-quarters palm art, hard to predict for having no fixed form.
- **Roaming Body Sword Art** — hit +3, damage 1d8+1 (AGI bonus), Qi 2. Sword moves driven by nimble footwork; +1 extra hit this round when evading.
- **Stele-Shattering Fist** — hit +1, damage 2d6+1, Qi 2. Fierce single punch; double damage vs obstacles.
- **Flying Over Grass (Lightfoot)** — spend 2 Qi, this round can move 2 distance bands; Initiative +2.

**Tactics**: Acts on circumstance, never lingers in battle. Opens with lightfoot advantage, probes one or two moves to judge strength. If can't win, runs — independents are best at leaving alive.

**Drop/Reward**: Fragment manual (random Initiate art), healing pill ×1, jianghu lore (provides an info lead)

**Scenes**: teahouse, tavern, wilderness inn, small-town lodging

### 7. Demon Cult Outer Disciple (Second Realm · Weak)

> "May the Cult Leader reign ten thousand years, uniting the jianghu! Do you know who you face?"

An outer disciple of the Blood Moon Cult or another Demon Cult. Brainwashed into absolute loyalty, proud to devote themselves to the cult — though most have never even seen the Cult Leader. Their arts are quick-result evil paths: fast to show but unstable at root.

|-----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|-----------|-----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 25 | Qi 11 (Initiate mental-method coefficient 2) | Defense DC 11 | Initiative +7 | Evasion 0

**Martial Arts**:
- **Blood Demon Palm** — hit +2, damage 2d4, Qi 2. Palm carries dark-red baleful Qi; on hit target CON check DC 10, on failure next-round hit −1 (slight Qi-blood turmoil).
- **Heart-Devouring Curse** — hit +0, no direct damage, Qi 3. Minor action, whispers a cult incantation at a target in line of sight. Target must make a WIL check DC 11 or becomes distracted — all checks −1 next round.

**Tactics**: 2–4 coordinate, one pins front, another casts Heart-Devouring Curse. Shout cult slogans to bolster courage. Morale greatly rises when an elite disciple (Third Realm) is present.

**Drop/Reward**: Demon Cult token (tablet marked with blood moon/other symbol), evil-path secret medicine ×1 (briefly boosts attribute but with side effects)

**Scenes**: Demon Cult branch altar, underground sacrificial altar, village infiltrated by the cult, deep-forest midnight gathering

### 8. Tang Sect Outer Disciple (Second Realm · Mid)

> "Sichuan Tang Sect — hidden weapons or poison, pick one? Actually — no difference."

The most junior formal disciple of Tang Sect. Raised since childhood among poisons and hidden weapons, they scoff at the morality of "using poison is dishonorable" — in Tang Sect, poison and hidden weapons are the Sword Sect's sword and the Qi Sect's Qi; no distinction of means.

| Attribute | STR 3(−1) | AGI 5(+1) | CON 4(0) | CMP 5(+1) | WIL 4(0) | CHA 3(−1) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 25 | Qi 11 (Initiate mental-method coefficient 2) | Defense DC 12 | Initiative +9 | Evasion +1

**Martial Arts**:
- **Poison-Coated Sleeve Arrow** — hit +2, damage 1d6, Qi 1. Three-step short-crossbow hidden weapon; on hit stacks Poisoned (1d4/round, 2 rounds).
- **Tang Sect Free Hand** — hit +1, damage 1d6, Qi 1. Seems ordinary fist and foot, but fingertips are coated with poison (on hit CON check DC 12 or Poisoned 1d4, 2 rounds).
- **Smoke Bomb** — hit +2 (area), Qi 2. Minor action, throws a smoke bomb. Creatures in the landing band must make a CON check DC 12 or be Blinded 1 round, all checks −2.

**Tactics**: Holds the three-step band, kites with poison arrows. After enemies close in, throws a smoke bomb to retreat or switches to Tang Sect Free Hand for melee. Never fights head-on — Tang Sect disciples are never bound by "fair and square."

**Drop/Reward**: Tang Sect antidote pill ×1, Poison-Coated Sleeve Arrow ×2, Smoke Bomb ×1

**Scenes**: Bashu forest of the Shu Road, Bashu market, hidden-weapon range, Tang Sect outer sentry post

### 9. Shaolin Monk (Third Realm · Mid)

> "Amitabha. Benefactor, put down the butcher's knife and turn back — if you will not heed, this poor monk's brows-level staff will show no mercy."

A formally ordained Shaolin martial monk. Solid foundation, trained in both fist and staff, with a body of hardening art. Does not draw weapons lightly, but once he acts, he holds nothing back. Righteous and awe-inspiring, naturally counters evil-path arts.

### 10. Six Doors Bureau Chief (Third Realm · Strong)

> "This official has handled cases for twenty years — what kind of river-and-lake outlaw have I not seen? You — are just another trying tricks."

The presiding officer of a Six Doors provincial branch. Excellent martial skill, rich case-handling experience, with a team of constables to command. Knows jianghu rules inside out — can judge in court and seize men in dark alleys. Most hates jianghu folk who offense with martial force.

| Attribute | STR 5(+1) | AGI 6(+2) | CON 5(+1) | CMP 6(+2) | WIL 6(+2) | CHA 6(+2) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 35 | Qi 21 (Orthodox mental-method coefficient 3) | Defense DC 15 (chainmail +2) | Initiative +12 | Evasion +2

**Martial Arts**:
- **Six-Door Soul-Chasing Saber** — hit +4, damage 2d8+1, Qi 3. Orthodox Court-Sect saber art; against "already wounded" targets, +2 extra hit (pressing advantage).
- **Sinew-Splitting Bone-Dislocating Hand** — hit +3, damage 2d4+1, Qi 2. On hit, one of target's arms randomly sealed (1 round), and must make a CON check DC 12 or suffer excruciating pain — that arm unusable until short rest.
- **Solve Case Like a God** — no cost. Minor action, observes a single target — next round +2 hit against that target ("This official sees your flaw").

**Tactics**: First minor action "Solve Case Like a God" to break the move, then Soul-Chasing Saber strikes. If enemies close in, Sinew-Splitting Hand disables the arm. Saves Third-Realm Advantage Die (2 per scene) for key attacks or arrest checks. Directs on-site constables to coordinate the surround.

**Drop/Reward**: Six Doors gold tablet (important plot item), official silver 5d10, classified case file (contains info lead)

**Scenes**: prefectural yamen, provincial arrest house, pursuit scene, secret interrogation room

> "That you stand alive before me already speaks well of you. Next, let me see your true skill."

A formally selected full member of the Blood Moon Cult. No longer the brainwashed cannon fodder of the outer ring — these are warriors truly tempered in blood and fire. Absolutely loyal to the Cult Leader, their skill far beyond the outer disciples.

| Attribute | STR 5(+1) | AGI 6(+2) | CON 5(+1) | CMP 5(+1) | WIL 5(+1) | CHA 5(+1) |

### 12. Lone Assassin (Third Realm · Strong)

> "…"

A shadowy night killer who comes and goes without trace. Not of Wangchuan Tower — may be the avenger of an exterminated sect, a wanted fugitive, or simply someone who lives by it. Never shows by day; strike always aims at the vital point.

| Attribute | STR 5(+1) | AGI 7(+3) | CON 5(+1) | CMP 6(+2) | WIL 5(+1) | CHA 4(0) |

**Martial Arts**:
- **Life-Taking Stab** — hit +5, damage 2d6+3 (AGI bonus), Qi 2. Lethal stab with short sword/dagger. If striking from Stealth/Hidden this round, damage doubled (assassinate).

## II. High-Martial Tier (Fourth to Sixth Realm, 8 enemies)

### 13. First-Rate Swordsman (Fourth Realm · Strong)

> "The Way of the sword lies not in killing, but in — severing attachment. What is your attachment?"

A solitary swordsman wandering the jianghu, reached the first-rate realm. May be a reject of some sword sect, or a self-taught genius. Refined sword art, composed bearing, treating each duel as cultivation of the sword-way.

| Attribute | STR 6(+2) | AGI 7(+3) | CON 6(+2) | CMP 7(+3) | WIL 6(+2) | CHA 6(+2) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 45 | Qi 34 (Superior mental-method coefficient 4) | Defense DC 14 | Initiative +13 | Evasion +3

**Martial Arts**:
- **Autumn Water Sword Art** — hit +5, damage 3d6+2, Qi 3. Sharp and swift sword; if acting before the target this round, +2 extra hit.
- **Qi-Breaking Form** — hit +4, damage 2d8+2, Qi 4. A sword specialized in breaking inner-family Qi — on hit target loses 1d6 Qi.
- **Autumn Water Mental Method** — passive: inner-art coefficient 4; Qi recovery 3/round; Fourth-Realm Steady Die (all non-combat checks 3d10 take middle two dice).

**Tactics**: Seizes initiative to attack, suppressing with Autumn Water Sword Art. Against inner-art masters, switches to Qi-Breaking Form to drain their Qi. Observes the opponent's sword style (move-reading check), finds the flaw, then strikes precisely. Respects worthy opponents — may critique their sword art after the fight.

**Drop/Reward**: Autumn Water Sword (fine long sword, damage 1d8+1, special: sword-art moves +1 hit), sword-manual fragment (can learn the first form of Autumn Water Sword Art)

**Scenes**: lakeside sword practice, famous-sword tournament, mountaintop duel, chance encounter while wandering

### 14. Demon Cult Guardian (Fourth Realm · Boss)

> "The righteous call me mad? Ha — once you've seen the filthy deeds they do in secret, you'll know who the real demon is."

A high-ranking Demon Cult officer, just below the Cult Leader. Formidably strong and unscrupulous. Scorns the "righteous" hypocrisy, holding his own understanding of cult dogma — not necessarily a fanatic, but absolutely a terrifying opponent.

**Martial Arts**:
- **Demon Fiend Palm** — hit +5, damage 3d6+3, Qi 4. Palm-wind carries baleful Qi that disturbs the mind — on hit target WIL check DC 15 or next-round hit −2 and cannot use Qi-infusion (mind disturbed by baleful Qi).

### 16. Jianghu Old Eccentric (Fourth Realm · Mid)

> "Lad, I see your bones are extraordinary — care to learn a move or two from this old man? Of course, not without a meeting gift."

A martial eccentric hermiting in deep mountains, ruined temples, or deserted villages. Unfathomable skill, eccentric and changeable temperament. May be a master in hiding after being hunted by enemies, or a half-mad half-lucid strong one after Qi Deviation. Meeting them is fortune or disaster depending on your words.

| Attribute | STR 5(+1) | AGI 7(+3) | CON 6(+2) | CMP 8(+4) | WIL 7(+3) | CHA 4(0) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 45 | Qi 34 (Superior mental-method coefficient 4) | Defense DC 14 | Initiative +14 | Evasion +3

**Martial Arts** (different eccentrics have different combinations, GM picks 2–3):
- **Strange Move · Plucking Leaf Flying Flower** — hit +5, damage 2d8+3 (CMP bonus), Qi 3. Uses whatever leaf/pebble at hand as hidden weapon, usable in three-step/far bands. Completely ignores armor External Reduction.
- **Strange Move · Four Ounces Deflect a Thousand Pounds** — deals no damage, Qi 4. Major action, makes a special roll against a single target — if result exceeds target's hit value, the target's attack this round is redirected at another target (including an ally). "You hit the wrong person."
- **Strange Move · Secret Voice Transmission** — no cost. Minor action, whispers a word only the target can hear. Target must make a WIL check DC 16 or next round can only move (mind disturbed by the words).
- **Strange Art · Turtle-Breath Great Method** — passive: automatically recovers 2 HP per round; when HP below 25%, doubles to 4 HP/round.

**Tactics**: Plays by no rule. May open with Secret Voice Transmission to disturb, then Four Ounces Deflect a Thousand Pounds to create chaos, finally Plucking Leaf Flying Flower to finish. May also just stand still letting you hit — then suddenly strike. Combat style varies by eccentric's personality: some jovial, some gloomy-silent.

**Drop/Reward**: Incomplete manual (random Superior or Advanced art), strange pill ×1 (random effect), eccentric's "secret" (provides important plot lead)

**Scenes**: deep-mountain ruined temple, ancient-tomb entrance, deserted-village old house, sitting alone at a cliff edge

### 17. Tianjian Alliance Elder (Fifth Realm · Boss)

> "The Tianjian Alliance has stood in the jianghu a century — how can you alone shake it? Stand down — for old Alliance Leader's sake, I will not kill you."

A deeply senior elder of the Tianjian Alliance. Cultivation reached Peerless; sword art divine. Values the Alliance's honor above life; any provocation to the Alliance is seen as a profound humiliation. Rigid but not unfeeling.

| Attribute | STR 7(+3) | AGI 8(+4) | CON 8(+4) | CMP 7(+3) | WIL 8(+4) | CHA 7(+3) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 60 | Qi 47 (Peerless Divine-Art coefficient 5) | Defense DC 17 (fine robe +1) | Initiative +16 | Evasion +4

**Martial Arts**:
- **Heavenly Sword Art · Starfall** — hit +7, damage 4d8+3, Qi 7. Condenses sword-intent into a point, a stab like a meteor falling — ignores 50% of target's armor External Reduction. On hit may choose to knock target back 1 band.
- **Heavenly Sword Bodyguard** — spend 3 Qi/round (upkeep), Defense DC +3, and after each time attacked may immediately counterattack with a minor action (damage 1d6+3).
- **Sword-Intent Pressure** — spend 2 Qi. Minor action, all enemies on the field must make a WIL check DC 16 or next-round hit −2 ("This old man's sword is not for your direct gaze").
- **Heavenly Sword Heart Sutra** — passive: inner-art coefficient 5; Qi recovery 4/round; Fifth-Realm Inner-Strength Infusion (1 per battle, may take 18 on a check directly).

**Tactics**: Dignified and composed. May open with Sword-Intent Pressure to suppress the whole field, then enter attack-defense mode with Heavenly Sword Bodyguard. Starfall used to pick off the most threatening target. Inner-Strength Infusion usually saved for a key strike or self-rescue. Will not actively pursue flee-ers — "The Tianjian Alliance's sword does not chase down the fleeing."

### 18. Blood Moon Cult Elder (Fifth Realm · Boss)

> "The righteous hypocrites crown themselves with sword andxia — but in this old man's eyes, your sword isn't even fit to lift the Blood Moon."

One of the highest core members of the Blood Moon Cult. Decades cultivating evil-path arts, hands stained with countless lives. Worships power almost pathologically — in the Blood Moon Cult, strength is everything. Unlike the Demon Cult Guardian (Fourth Realm), the elder has evolved from "fond of battle" to "proving the Way through martial arts."

| Attribute | STR 6(+2) | AGI 8(+4) | CON 7(+3) | CMP 8(+4) | WIL 9(+5) | CHA 6(+2) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 55 | Qi 50 (Peerless Divine-Art coefficient 5) | Defense DC 17 (embroidered fine robe +1) | Initiative +17 | Evasion +4

**Martial Arts**:
- **Blood Moon Art · Erosion** — hit +7, damage 3d8+4 (CMP bonus), Qi 6. Profound-yin inner Qi erodes the opponent's meridians — while dealing damage, target loses Qi equal to half the damage (rounded down). Armor cannot reduce.
- **Blood-Transforming Demon Art** — hit +6, damage 4d6+2, Qi 8. On hit target must make a CON check DC 18 or HP maximum −5 until long rest (essence-blood transformed away). This move limited to 2 uses per battle.
- **Blood Moon Illusion** — spend 4 Qi. Minor action, casts a mental attack on all enemies in sight — each must make a WIL check DC 16 or fall into "Blood Moon Illusion" (next round can only choose: attack the nearest ally, or do nothing).
- **Blood Moon Demon Sutra** — passive: inner-art coefficient 5; Qi recovery 4/round; when HP below 25%, all damage +2d4 (dying counterattack).

**Tactics**: Opens with Blood Moon Illusion to create chaos, then Blood-Transforming Demon Art to cut a key target's HP maximum (this damage is especially terrifying — not HP damage but maximum reduction!). Finally Blood Moon Art · Erosion to steadily drain enemy Qi. Uses Fifth-Realm Inner-Strength Infusion to ensure Blood-Transforming Demon Art hits. He enjoys enemies' fear — describing in battle how "your Qi is being devoured by my Blood Moon power."

**Drop/Reward**: Blood Moon Demon Sutra fragment (cannot learn the full version, but provides 1 core Blood Moon Cult secret), Demon-Origin Pill ×1 (restores 20 Qi, but requires WIL check DC 16 or Qi Deviation), Blood Moon Elder Ring (key plot item)

**Scenes**: Blood Moon Cult Main Altar secret chamber, front line of the righteous-evil great war, forbidden land shrouded by Blood Moon power

### 19. Barbarian Warrior (Fifth Realm · Strong)

A top warrior from a Northern Frontier barbarian tribe. Cultivates no inner art, knows no acupoints, reads no manuals — relies on born divine strength and an iron body. Believes the "strength is justice" barbarian creed; each totem tattoo is said to mark a beast he hunted.

### 20. Hidden Grandmaster (Sixth Realm · Boss)

> "Thirty years ago I stopped concerning myself with the jianghu. You insist on testing? Very well — use your strongest move. I use but one. Within one move, if you still stand, you win."

A hidden master who has stepped into the Sixth Realm · Return to Simplicity. May be the founder of some long-forgotten sect, or a broom-sweeping old monk who fused all his cultivation into daily life. Outwardly no different from ordinary folk — no sharp aura, no piercing gaze, but it is precisely this "ordinariness" that is most terrifying.

| Attribute | STR 8(+4) | AGI 9(+5) | CON 10(+6) | CMP 10(+6) | WIL 10(+6) | CHA 9(+5) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|------------|------------|-----------|----------|

**Combat Data**: HP 75 | Qi 70 (Peerless Divine-Art coefficient 5) | Defense DC 21 (cloth, but Sixth-Realm realm modifier +5 equivalent) | Initiative +19 | Evasion +5

**Martial Arts** (GM picks 2–3 based on the master's background):
- **Return-to-Simplicity · One Sword** — hit +9, damage 5d8+6 (whichever is highest among STR/AGI/CMP modifier), Qi 5. A seemingly plain sword — but completely ignores armor, block, and Protective Qi; only evade or parry can answer. If the target cannot evade or parry, this sword surely hits.
- **Wuji Bodyguard** — no cost (passive permanent). Whole body wrapped in formless Qi-field — +4 Defense DC vs all attacks (already included in data); automatically recovers 5 HP and 5 Qi per round.
- **Enlightenment** — deals no damage, Qi 3. Major action, makes a special roll against a single target (hit +10). On success, target cannot act this round — not controlled, but awed motionless by the martial truth the master displays.
- **Unity of Heaven and Man** — passive: Sixth-Realm privilege — 3 Inner-Strength Infusions per battle (may take 18 on a check); Advantage Die range expanded to all checks.

**Tactics**: This master is not meant to be beaten — he is the GM's "ceiling." The Hidden Grandmaster in battle rarely strikes first: he may use "Return-to-Simplicity · One Sword" to show the PCs true sword-way, or "Enlightenment" to calm a berserk PC. Beating him is not by numbers — but by plot. What he recognizes is not your martial art, but your character, your choices, the "Martial Truth" you show across the whole campaign line.

**Drop/Reward**: If the master's recognition is won — exclusive martial-art inheritance (GM creates a Peerless move), Martial Truth ×10 XP, "Proof of Grandmaster" (Renown permanently +10 and may take disciples)

**Scenes**: mist-shrouded mountain peak, the broom-sweeping old monk in the ancient temple's back courtyard, the old man practicing sword alone under a waterfall, the jianghu legend's "that place"
