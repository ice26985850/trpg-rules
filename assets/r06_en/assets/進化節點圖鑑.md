## Ultimate Active Nodes (6)

Ultimate Active Nodes are the pinnacle power of the Evolution Tree. They consume "all remaining Energy (min 5)", and can only be used once per battle. To unlock an Ultimate Node, you must have accumulated considerable Evolution Depth (usually 15+ Evolution Nodes).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U01 | Form Shift | Ultimate | 3 | All Remaining (min 5) | Once per battle | Self | Immediately activate a "Form Node" whose unlock requirement you know, gaining all its effects, lasting the entire battle (disappears after battle, not retained) | 5 Evolution Points | Know that Form Node's unlock requirement, Will (YZ) 10+ |
| U02 | Domain Expansion | Ultimate | 3 | All Remaining (min 5) | Once per battle | Self radius 6 squares | Deploy exclusive domain lasting entire battle: within domain all your attacks +2 damage, all enemy recovery -50%, at start of each round enemies take 1d6 damage (damage type based on your strongest element or psychic) | 5 Evolution Points | Own at least 1 high-tier active node |
| U03 | Ultimate Evolution | Ultimate | 3 | All Remaining (min 5) | Once per battle | Self | Within this battle, all owned active node damage +1d6, passive node effects ×1.5, attribute nodes additional +1. Lasts entire battle | 6 Evolution Points | Own 15+ Evolution Nodes |
| U04 | Self-Destruct Rebirth | Ultimate | 3 | All Remaining (min 5) | Once per battle | Self radius 5 squares | Deal 5d10+YZ×2 pure damage to all enemies in range (ignores all resistance), then you "die" and are reborn as "Newborn Form" after 1d4 rounds: HP full, randomly gain one temporary organ node effect (lasts until battle ends) | 5 Evolution Points | Constitution (TZ) 8+ |
| U05 | Gene Collapse | Ultimate | 3 | All Remaining (min 5) | Once per battle | Single target | Will (YZ) DC 20: on failure target randomly loses 1-2 activated active/passive node effects (lasts entire battle), and takes 3d10+YZ×2 pure damage | 5 Evolution Points | Will (YZ) 12+ or have absorbed "Prototype Ω" |
