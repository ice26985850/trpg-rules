# Devour Gene: Chronicle of Evolution — Monster Codex

> This codex compiles all enemy and monster data that appear in every scenario. The GM can quickly reference it for encounter design.
>
> **Defense Value and Attack Bonus (v1.1 balance fix):** Monsters no longer use the "10 + Agility" formula; instead they are set based on the tier of their scenario (see Section 9 "Tiered Defense and Attack Table" of the GM Rulebook). The Defense Values in the table below are aligned to the corresponding tier; the "+X" after the attack dice is that tier's attack bonus. If used in a higher-tier scene, for each tier above, Defense +2 and Attack Bonus +2.
>
> | Tier | Minion Defense/+X | Elite Defense/+X | Boss Defense/+X | Ultimate Boss Defense/+X |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | S1 | 14-15 / +4~+5 | 16-17 / +5~+6 | 19-20 / +6~+7 | — |
> | S2 | 15-16 / +5~+6 | 18-19 / +6~+7 | 20-21 / +7~+8 | — |
> | S3 | 17-18 / +6~+7 | 21-22 / +8~+9 | 23-24 / +9~+10 | — |
> | S4 | 19-20 / +8~+9 | 23-24 / +10~+11 | 25-26 / +11~+12 | 28-30 / +14~+16 |
> | S5 | 21-22 / +9~+10 | 23-24 / +11~+12 | 25-27 / +12~+14 | 30-32 / +16~+18 |
