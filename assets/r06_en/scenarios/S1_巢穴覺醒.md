# S1: Nest Awakening

> **Suggested Evolution Nodes:** 0-5 nodes (Novice)
> **Difficulty:** Easy (★★)
> **Estimated Playtime:** 2-3 hours
> **Players:** 1 (Solo Mode default) / expandable to 2-4
> **Multiplayer (2-4 players):** GM should multiply enemy count by ×1.5 (2 players) or ×2 (3-4 players); the party shares the scenario's Evolution Points and Remnant Coins rewards, while individual absorption gains remain each player's own. All other rules unchanged.

---

## Plot Summary

You awaken in a nest deep underground. Around you lie the corpses of others like you—some already rotting, some seemingly dead only recently. You don't remember who you are, don't remember where this is, don't even remember what you once were.

The only clear thing is a hunger from deep within your body. Not hunger for food—but a hunger for **Evolution**.

Your body tells you: climb upward. Devour everything that blocks your path. And then—perhaps—you will remember who you are.

---

## Scene 1: Awakening

### Opening Description (GM reads aloud)

> Darkness. Boundless darkness.
>
> The first thing to return to your consciousness is touch—you lie upon some damp, sticky surface. Then smell—a putrid, sweet stench fills your nostrils. Then hearing—the echo of water dripping in the distance, and some kind of… moving sound.
>
> You open your eyes. The darkness is so thick you cannot tell if you've truly opened them.
>
> But you can feel your body. Your limbs—are they as you remember them?
>
> Your fingertips touch the ground. Rough rock. Then you touch what lies beside you.
>
> A hand. A cold, stiff hand.
>
> Around you lie a dozen or so corpses. Some resemble you. Some… no longer do.

### Scene Objectives
- Create your character (using the character creation flow, including selecting a Root Node)
- Explore the nest and understand the basic environment
- Experience your character's amnesiac state

### Scene Details

**Nest Map:** see `maps/巢穴覺醒_覺醒巢穴.yaml` and `.svg`

The nest is a natural underground cave system containing the following areas:
- **A: Hall of Awakening**—the central hall where the player awakens, scattered with 12 corpses. Among the bodies can be found 1 Healing Potion and 1 rusty dagger (1d4 damage)
- **B: Water Source**—a gathering point of underground spring water. Clear water. Resting here restores 1d4 HP (one-time)
- **C: Nest Passage**—a narrow passage leading to the surface. Some glowing fungi provide faint illumination

### Trigger Events
- When the player touches a corpse, Perception (GZ) DC 12 reveals: some corpses bear gnawing marks—not from animals, but from some conscious presence
- When the player touches their own body, Perception (GZ) DC 10 reveals: old wounds on their own body have healed—at a rate far beyond normal

## Scene 2: The First Meal

### Opening Description

> A rapid scratching sound comes from ahead in the passage. Same as the tooth marks you saw on the corpses—but those weren't marks on a corpse. These were left by something alive.
>
> From the shadows, a pair of red eyes lights up. Then a second pair. A third.
>
> Mutant rats. Their bodies are three times the size of ordinary rats, with bony spikes growing from their spines that should not exist on rodents. They were once human food—but in this nest, they evolved into predators.
>
> They are gnawing on a fresh corpse. Upon seeing you, they stop.
>
> A silent understanding fills the air: between you and them, only one side can keep living.

### Encounter Design

**Combat: Mutant Rats ×3 (Minion-tier)**

| Name | HP | Defense | Attributes | Attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mutant Rat A | 8 | 9 | MJ 5 | Bite 1d4+2 (carries disease: Constitution TZ DC 10 or else poisoned) |
| Mutant Rat B | 8 | 9 | MJ 5 | Bite 1d4+2 |
| Mutant Rat C | 10 | 10 | MJ 5, TZ 4 | Reinforced Bite 1d6+2 (alpha rat, slightly larger) |

**Tactical Hint (GM):** Mutant rats prioritize attacking the nearest target. If a rat is Near Death, the others grow more ferocious (Attack +1).

**Loot:**
- Kill: 3 Evolution Points (1 each Minion-tier) + 3 Biomass (1 each)
- Absorb (any one): Trait sample—①Night Vision (Passive) ②Burrowing Stealth (low-tier Active) ③Disease Resistance (Passive)

### Key Choice

This is the player's first encounter with the "Kill vs Absorb" choice. The GM may guide here:
- If the player chooses Absorb, describe the feeling of "merging the enemy's essence into yourself"—both thrilling and unsettling
- If the player chooses Kill, describe the surge of power in the body when gaining Evolution Points

## Scene 3: Voices in the Dark

### Opening Description

> As you continue climbing upward, you hear a voice. Not the scratching of rats—but a human voice. Or at least, it once was human.
>
> "Is anyone there? Please… I need help…"
>
> The voice comes from a hollow in the rock wall. Inside, a figure is curled up. His body has already begun to mutate—rough scales grow on his skin, one eye has become a reptile's vertical pupil. But his gaze… his gaze is still human.
>
> "You too… escaped?" he asks, voice hoarse. "Or… are you like those things, only wanting to devour me?"

### NPC: Ryan (Evolved Survivor)

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| **Base Form** | Human |
| **Status** | Has evolved 2 nodes (Hardened Skin + Sharp Claws). Humanity Value 55%. Struggling over whether to continue evolving |
| **HP** | 20 (originally 25, injured) |
| **Attitude** | Cautious—longs for companionship but fears betrayal. He has witnessed too many Evolvers devouring each other |
| **Can Provide** | Information about the nest (he knows the upward passage, knows of the "Gatekeeper"'s existence), willing to fight alongside |
| **Need** | Healing (his wound is bleeding, needs 1 Healing Potion or a bandaging Perception DC 12 check), answers—he is also amnesiac, but remembers awakening in a laboratory |

### Branch Options

| Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Heal and ally** | Ryan joins as an NPC companion (HP 20, Defense 12, Sharp Claws 1d8+3). Assists the player in later combat. If the player is Near Death, Ryan prioritizes protecting the player |
| **Refuse help** | Ryan is disappointed but understanding. He leaves on his own. Will not appear in later scenes—but may be encountered again in S2 (Lab Escape) (now a stronger Evolver or… now a monster) |
| **Absorb him** | (Optional) If the player's Humanity Value is below 40%, the system allows attacking and absorbing Ryan. After absorption gain trait sample: ①Hardened Skin (Passive) ②Sharp Claws (low-tier Active) ③Human Will (Passive: Will YZ +1). Humanity Value -10 |

## Scene 4: The Gatekeeper

### Opening Description

> At the end of the passage is a door. Not natural rock—but a man-made steel gate. Carved on the door is a symbol: a serpent devouring its own tail.
>
> Before the door, a creature sleeps. It may once have been human—but now is more like a hybrid of lizard and insect. Two pairs of compound eyes cover half its face, sharp bone plates grow from its spine, and at the end of the tail dragging on the ground is a bone spike.
>
> The Nest Guardian—placed here to ensure no one leaves the depths alive.
>
> Its eyes snap open. All six pupils lock onto you at once.
>
> No dialogue. Only combat.

### Encounter Design

**BOSS Fight: Nest Guardian (Elite-tier)**

| Name | HP | Defense | Attributes | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nest Guardian | 40 | 14 | LIL 7, MJ 5, TZ 6, GZ 7 | 12 |

**Skills:**
- **Tail Spike** (low-tier, 1 AP, 1 Energy): Melee 1d8+3 piercing + poison (Constitution TZ DC 13)
- **Bone Plate Charge** (low-tier, 1 AP, no cost): Melee 1d6+3 bludgeoning + target pushed back 2 squares (Strength LIL DC 14)
- **Compound Eye Lock** (Passive): Advantage on Perception (GZ) checks. No Disadvantage against stealth targets

**Tactical Hint (GM):**
The Guardian stays put at the door, prioritizing targets near the doorway. If the player tries to bypass it to open the door—it goes berserk (Attack +2 until the player leaves the doorway). Suggested tactics: weaken it before opening the door, or use ranged attacks to pin it down then open the door quickly.

**Combat Map:** see `maps/巢穴覺醒_守門者之戰.yaml` and `.svg`

**Loot:**
- Kill: 3 Evolution Points + 5 Biomass
- Absorb: Trait sample—①Compound Eye Prototype (Organ Node hint) ②Hardened Skin (Passive) ③Tail Spike (low-tier Active) ④Bone Plate Defense (Passive: physical damage -1) ⑤Insect Affinity (Passive: natural Perception of insect-type creatures)

## Scene 5: The Surface

### Opening Description

> The steel gate closes behind you. Or rather—after you tore it apart.
>
> You stand at the nest's exit. Sunlight stings your eyes—you can no longer remember the last time you saw sunlight.
>
> Outside lies a wasteland. In the distance are three directions:
>
> **North**—a white tower stands on the horizon, surrounded by orderly farmland. Human civilization.
>
> **East**—smoke rises. The ruins of some industrial or experimental facility. Possibly a source of clues.
>
> **South**—wilderness. Traces of mutant creatures everywhere. The Evolver's hunting ground.
>
> You have no memories. But you have a choice.

### Scene Objectives
- Let the player make their first decision about "what am I"
- Connect to later scenarios (North→S3 Evolution War, East→S2 Lab Escape, South→S4 Apex of the Food Chain)
- Settle the scenario's evolution gains

### Ending Settlement

| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| Complete scenario | +3 Evolution Points |
| Absorbed at least 1 enemy | Extra +1 Evolution Points |
| Helped Ryan (healed and not betrayed) | Gain passive: "Bond Memory"—Will (YZ) checks get +1 (only when protecting others) |
| Found traces of old wounds on yourself | GM may reveal a clue about the player's background (e.g., experiment number, brand mark, etc.) |
