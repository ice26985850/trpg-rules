# S3: Evolution War

> **Suggested Evolution Nodes:** 6-12 nodes
> **Difficulty:** Hard (★★★★)
> **Estimated Playtime:** 4-5 hours
> **Players:** 1 / expandable to 2-4
> **Multiplayer (2-4 players):** GM should multiply enemy count by ×1.5 (2 players) or ×2 (3-4 players); the party shares the scenario's Evolution Points and Remnant Coins rewards, while individual absorption gains remain each player's own. All other rules unchanged.
> **Prerequisite Suggestion:** S1 Nest Awakening (recommended), S2 Lab Escape (optional)

---

## Plot Summary

The Ultimate Evolution Cult and the Humanity Guardians have arrived at the Gene Forge Ruins simultaneously—this ancient facility has the ability to accelerate evolution, and whoever controls it gains a decisive advantage in this war.

You are the focal point both sides are fighting over. You have already proven your evolution potential—the Cult sees you as an "ideal Evolver," while the Guardians consider you a "deluded one who can be saved." Both sides have made their offers.

But you have a third choice: shut down the Forge so neither side gets it.

## Scene 1: Invitations from Both Sides

### Opening Description (GM reads aloud)

> The Gene Forge Ruins stand at the center of a wasteland known as the "Scorched Plain." This structure is older and vaster than any facility you've seen—its towers pierce the clouds, its foundations reach deep into the crust.
>
> Before the ruins' entrance, two camps have already set up.
>
> On the left—the crimson tents of the Ultimate Evolution Cult. The Evolvers before the tents display their "achievements": six arms, transparent skin, metallic bones. They smile when they see you—but it is the smile of a predator seeing prey.
>
> On the right—the white camp of the Humanity Guardians. Neat tents, patrolling soldiers, checkpoints. They eye you with wary gazes, assessing your Humanity Value.
>
> Between the two, a figure in a gray robe stands in the no-man's-land. An observer of the Symbiosis Accord—neither here to fight, nor to negotiate. Just to "observe."
>
> The ruins' great gate is tightly shut. Only a living being with sufficient evolution energy can open it.
>
> And the evolution energy radiating from your body—is enough to make the gate tremble.

### Scene Objectives
- Speak with representatives of both factions and understand their respective terms
- Make a faction choice—or decide to take the third path

## Scene 2: The Forge Gate

### Opening Description

> The gate is alive.
>
> Literally—the Gene Forge's gate is not made of metal or stone, but a layer of organic membrane. It pulses with your approach, like the valve of some giant creature's heart.
>
> Text appears on the membrane's surface—the language of the ancient gene civilization:
>
> **"Only an Evolver can open this gate."**
> **"The one who enters must prove the worth of evolution."**
>
> The air around the gate begins to distort. Your body involuntarily begins to respond—your unlocked evolution nodes activate one by one, each mutation glowing.
>
> The more evolution nodes you have, the stronger the gate's reaction.

### The Gate's Trial

Opening the gate requires injecting evolution energy. Handling differs by choice:

**If you choose the Cult route:**
- The Cult provides 5 volunteer Evolvers to inject energy with you
- The process is fast but painful—each injector loses 1 Humanity Value

**If you choose the Guardian route:**
- The Guardians provide a technological device—the "Energy Simulator," which can mimic evolution energy
- Requires Perception (GZ) DC 16 to operate it correctly. Failure→device explodes, costs 1d4 rounds

**If you choose the third path:**
- You inject energy alone. Will (YZ) DC 18
- Success→the gate opens only for you (both sides are locked out, you gain first-strike advantage)
- Failure→the gate opens for everyone (both sides pour in simultaneously) and your energy is drained

## Scene 3: The Forge Contest

### Opening Description

> The gate opens.
>
> The sight beyond defies imagination. The Gene Forge's interior is a vast biomechanical space—pipes cover the walls like blood vessels, at its center a pool of glowing "Evolution Primordial Fluid." Half-formed organisms float in the liquid—some look like organs, some like complete embryos.
>
> The air is saturated with evolution energy. You can feel every cell in your body stirring.
>
> The "Evolution Primordial Fluid"—one sip grants a free evolution. But the cost is—it accelerates your Humanity Value loss.
>
> You have no time to hesitate. The Cult's and Guardians' forces are already pouring in through other entrances.

### Three-Way Melee Design

This is not a traditional turn-based combat, but a dynamic melee scene. The following events occur in the Forge interior each round:

| Round | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cult vanguard (3 Evolver Minions) vs Guardian soldiers (3 Minions) melee. The player may choose to join either side or bypass the battlefield |
| 2 | The Forge's defense system activates—two "Bio-Mechs" (Elite-tier) peel off the walls, attacking all creatures present |
| 3 | The Primordial Fluid pool begins to boil. Any creature within 2 squares of the pool takes 1d6 fire damage. But drinking the fluid grants a free evolution (Humanity Value -5) |
| 4 | BOSS appears—the Cult's "Thousand-Faced Bishop" descends in person, or the Guardians' "Purification Marshal" Elena appears (depending on the player's hostile faction) |
| 5 | The Forge core begins to overload—if not controlled, the entire facility explodes in 5 rounds |

### Key Encounter

**Bio-Mech ×2 (Elite-tier)**

| Name | HP | Defense | Attributes | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bio-Mech A | 45 | 15 | LIL 8, TZ 8 | Bio-Cannon (ranged 2d8+3), Tentacle Bind (mid-range, Agility DC 15) |
| Bio-Mech B | 45 | 15 | LIL 8, TZ 8 | Energy Absorption (recovers 5 HP from the fluid pool each round), Charge |

**BOSS: Thousand-Faced Bishop (enemy BOSS of the Cult route)**

| Name | HP | Defense | Attributes | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thousand-Faced Bishop | 90 | 17 | LIL 7, MJ 8, TZ 7, GZ 9, YZ 12 | 20 |

**Skills:**
- **Form Shift** (Passive): The Bishop can shift form once per round (human/tentacle/flying), each form having a different skill set
- **Thousand-Faced Touch** (High-tier, 5 Energy): Touch a target—the target must make Will (YZ) DC 18, on failure the Bishop can copy one of the target's evolution nodes (usable this battle)
- **Evolution Deprivation** (Ultimate, all Energy): Target makes Will (YZ) DC 20, on failure randomly loses one evolution node (permanently—but can be restored with absorption points after the battle ends)

**BOSS: Purification Marshal Elena (enemy BOSS of the Guardian route)**

| Name | HP | Defense | Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purification Marshal Elena | 80 | 18 | LIL 9, MJ 7, TZ 6, GZ 8, YZ 10 |

**Skills:**
- **Purification Strike** (Mid-tier): Melee 2d10+4 slashing + target loses all Energy (suppressed by her "anti-evolution ability")

- **Humanity Judgment** (Ultimate, all Energy): Target with Humanity Value below 40% loses consciousness for 1 round directly (cannot resist)

## Scene 4: The Core Choice

### Opening Description

> You stand before the Forge's core control console. Three options appear on the screen:
>
> **① Maximize Output—channel the Forge's energy into yourself.**
> → Gain a chance: in 30 seconds, absorb however much evolution energy you can—gain that much. Theoretically you could unlock multiple nodes at once. But can your body withstand it?
>
> **② Stabilize—use the Forge's energy to transform rather than absorb.**
> → Gain no new evolution nodes, but permanently increase evolution capacity +5 and all unlocked node effects +20%.
>
> **③ Shut Down—cut off the Forge's energy core.**
> → The Forge is permanently closed. No one gets it. Gain the Accord faction's highest favor.

### Endings and Consequences

| Choice | Immediate Effect | Long-term Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Maximize Output** | Will (YZ) DC 20: Success→gain 10 Evolution Points (usable immediately); Failure→evolution goes out of control (roll d6 on the loss-of-control table). Either way, Humanity Value -15 | The Cult sees you as "God's Chosen"—but their "God's Chosen" usually don't survive three months. Your body begins to reject itself—afterward each new node's Humanity Value loss ×2 |
| **Stabilize** | Evolution capacity +5, all node effects +20%. Humanity Value -5 | Your body reaches a perfect state of balance. Afterward, DC to absorb enemies -2 |
| **Shut Down** | Gain the "Heart of Balance" Passive Node. No Humanity Value loss | The balance of the three factions is maintained. You become an important ally of the Accord. But both the Cult and the Guardians will remember you |

### Scenario Settlement

| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| Complete scenario | +5 Evolution Points |
| Defeated the hostile faction's BOSS | Extra +3 Evolution Points + BOSS absorption opportunity |
| Completed all terms of the chosen faction | Faction-exclusive reward (see Scene 1) |
| Protected or released any captive | Humanity Value +2/person |

## GM Notes

### Dynamic Adjustment of Faction Routes
- If the player chooses the Cult route but later repents, they can choose "Shut Down" at the Forge core—at which point the Cult becomes hostile
- If the player chooses the third path but is discovered during infiltration, they can temporarily defect to either side (but trust is lowered)
- The Thousand-Faced Bishop or Elena's BOSS fight is already set to "Extremely Hard"—if the player loses 2 consecutive checks, the GM should consider providing environmental advantages (such as cover at the fluid pool, fallen enemies' weapons, etc.)

### Later Connections
- S3 is the prerequisite for S5—the clues about the "Evolution Primordial Fluid" and the "ancient gene civilization" revealed in the Forge are resolved in S5's Abyssal Rift
- The faction choice made in this scenario affects S5's finale—a Cult ally provides one path, a Guardian ally provides another, an Accord ally provides a third
