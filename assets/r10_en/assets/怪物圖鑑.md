# Starship Convoy TRPG — Bestiary

> This compendium catalogs all hostile units and space creatures a convoy may encounter in the Glass Sea Sector. The combat rules and the Threat Level (TL) system are detailed in Chapter 1 of the Game Master (GM) Rulebook.

---

## Threat Level (TL) System

| Threat Level (TL) | Attribute Pool | Weapon Loadout | Suitable Convoy |
|:--:|:-----:|------|------|
| 1 | 10 points | 1 Light | Starting convoy can handle |
| 2 | 14 points | 1 Medium + 1 Light | Can be defeated with partial damage |
| 3 | 18 points | 1 Heavy + 1 Medium | Requires full coordination |
| 4 | 22 points | 1 Heavy + 1 Medium + Special | Possible paralysis/crippling |
| 5 | 26 points | 2 Heavy + Torpedo + Special | Retreat is reasonable |

### Quick Generation Steps

1. Set Threat Level (TL) → allocate attributes (Engine/Armor/Firepower/Sensors/Special)
2. Calculate derived attributes: Hull Points (HP) = Armor (ARM) ×10+20 | Evasion Class (AC) = 10+Engine (ENG-S) | Energy = Engine (ENG-S) ×5+10 | Damage Reduction (DR) = Armor (ARM) ×2
3. Pick weapons → grant 1–2 special abilities → define tactical script

## Pirate Ships

### Light Raider (TL 1)

```
Hull Points (HP):25 | Armor (ARM):1 (Damage Reduction (DR) 2) | Engine (ENG-S):3 | Firepower (WPN):2 | Sensors (SEN):2
Evasion Class (AC):13 | Energy:15 | Initiative:3
Weapons: Light Laser Cannon (2d6, Precision +1), Jamming Missile (2d4, -2 to target's next-turn attacks on hit)
Special: None
Tactics: High-speed approach, close-range dogfight; flee at HP<50% (Engineering (ENG) Difficulty Class (DC) 12)
Loot: 1d4×100 Credits (Cr), 1d4-1 Ammunition, 1d3 Parts
```

### Medium Marauder (TL 2)

```
Hull Points (HP):40 | Armor (ARM):2 (Damage Reduction (DR) 4) | Engine (ENG-S):2 | Firepower (WPN):3 | Sensors (SEN):3
Evasion Class (AC):12 | Energy:20 | Initiative:2
Weapons: Medium Plasma Cannon ×2 (2d8), Boarding Torpedo (4d6 + boarding action)

Loot: 2d6×100 Credits (Cr), 2d6 Ammunition, 2d4 Parts, 1d3 Fuel, 1d2 Medicine
```

### Heavy Pirate Flagship (TL 3)

```
Hull Points (HP):60 | Armor (ARM):4 (Damage Reduction (DR) 8) | Engine (ENG-S):1 | Firepower (WPN):5 | Sensors (SEN):3
Evasion Class (AC):11 | Energy:15 | Initiative:1

Loot: 3d10×100 Credits (Cr), 3d10 Ammunition, 4d6 Parts, 3d6 Fuel, 2d4 Medicine, 1 rare modification
```

## Space Creatures

### Interstellar Jellyfish Swarm (TL 1, Non-hostile)

```
Hull Points (HP):30 (swarm) | Armor (ARM):0 (Damage Reduction (DR) 0) | Engine (ENG-S):4 | Firepower (WPN):1 | Sensors (SEN):5
Evasion Class (AC):14 | Energy:30 | Initiative:4
Attacks (self-defense only): Tentacle Lash (1d8, close range), Bio-Electric Pulse (3 energy, all ships within mid range -1d6 Energy and halved shields)
Special: Passive Stealth (undetectable beyond long range if Sensors (SEN)<4), Migration Instinct (non-hostile, auto-leaves after 3 rounds), Symbiosis (convoy detour → +2 to next voyage event roll)
Tactics: Does not attack first; releases electric pulse then flees when attacked
Reward: 2d6 Biomass (convertible to 1d3 Fuel or Medicine)
```

### Void Hunter (TL 2, Hostile)

```
Hull Points (HP):45 | Armor (ARM):3 (Damage Reduction (DR) 6, natural carapace) | Engine (ENG-S):3 | Firepower (WPN):4 | Sensors (SEN):4
Evasion Class (AC):13 | Energy:25 | Initiative:3
Weapons: Bio-Acid Spray (3d6, Piercing 3, -1 Armor (ARM) on hit, stacks to -3, restored by repair), Tentacle Grapple (2d8, close range, target cannot move on hit; 2d4 auto damage per round until escaped)
Special: Tractor Gel (Reaction, 5 energy: attacker's weapons unusable next turn), Predatory Frenzy (attacks twice per round at HP<50%), Subspace Stealth (7 energy: teleport from long to close range, ambush)

Reward: 3d6 Biomass, 1 Void Carapace Fragment (+1 Armor (ARM) hull add-on)
```

## Hyperspace Entities

### Hyperspace Anomaly (TL 4)

```
Hull Points (HP):80 | Armor (ARM):5 (Damage Reduction (DR) 10, non-material protection) | Engine (ENG-S):5 | Firepower (WPN):4 | Sensors (SEN):6
Evasion Class (AC):15 | Energy:40 | Initiative:5
Special Rule — Non-Euclidean Entity: not bound by distance; roll d4 each round to determine phase (1=Material, can attack and be attacked / 2=Energy, immune to energy weapons, physical weapons ×1.5 / 3=Void, cannot be attacked but sensor overload can force manifestation / 4=Chaos, attacks ×2 and damage taken ×2)
Attacks: Reality Tear (3d10, ignores armor, Intuition (INT) Difficulty Class (DC) 15 on hit → crew collective hallucination, unable to act), Time Corridor (8 energy, target ship skips next round)
Special: Dimensional Rift (randomly changes all ships' distance bands each round end), Info Erosion (highest-Sensors (SEN) ship requires Sensors (SEN) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 16, failure → sensors damaged), Immortal Entity (collapses into a stable dimensional rift at HP=0, can be studied/utilized)
Tactics: Unpredictable; behavior changes by phase each round; the Game Master (GM) should create an eerie atmosphere
Reward: 1 Dimensional Shard (consumable: next Jump auto-succeeds, no risk)
```
