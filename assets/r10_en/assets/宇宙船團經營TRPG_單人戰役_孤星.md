# Starship Convoy TRPG — Solo Campaign: Lone Star

> **Mode:** Solo (1 Player + 1 Game Master)
> **Level Range:** Lv1 - Lv10
> **Estimated Length:** 4 Acts, 3-5 Sessions each (approximately 15-20 Sessions total)
> **Prerequisites:** Player Manual + Game Master (GM) Manual + Solo Mode Rules Supplement

---

# Campaign Overview

## Who Are You?

You are not the leader of the convoy. You are not the commander of the fleet.

**You are the only one who survived.**

Your convoy — the Resonance Convoy (共鳴號船團) — was betrayed during a routine exploration of the Ash Belt (灰燼帶). When the Pirate Flagship appeared on radar, your comm channel filled with panic. You heard your friends' voices disappear one by one.

You are the only one who got out.

Now, you drift alone in the unknown region at the edge of the Ash Belt. You have just enough fuel for one Jump. Your food won't last two weeks. Your four crew members look at you, waiting for your decision.

**This is where the Lone Star campaign begins.**

# Act I: Drift (Level (Lv) 1-3)

> **Theme:** Finding a reason to keep going after losing everything
> **Key Mechanics:** Crew Council, solo survival, starting ally's debut
> **Estimated Sessions:** 3-4

## Chapter 1: Among the Wreckage (Level (Lv) 1)

### Opening — "They're All Dead"

**Scene:** The player's ship drifts silently at the edge of the Ash Belt. The engine is lightly damaged (-10 HP). The comm channel carries only static — occasionally punctuated by recorded fragments of the convoy members' final moments. The crew gathers on the bridge.

**Crew Council (first):**
> Your First Mate, Engineer, Navigator, and Comms Officer each voice their opinion. This is the first time, without the convoy's voting mechanism, that they need you to make the final decision.

| Crew | Opinion |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "We should return to the Abyss Gate (深淵門). There we can resupply, sell supplies, and find a new convoy." |
| Engineer | "The engine won't make it to the Abyss Gate. We must find a place for emergency repairs first." |
| Navigator | "There's an abandoned outpost deep in the Ash Belt — C-7. The star charts show it has basic repair facilities." |
| Comms Officer | "I intercepted an encrypted message — sounds like pirates discussing 'the traitor got paid.' Someone is pulling the strings behind this attack." |

**Options and Consequences:**

| Decision | Path | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| Head to the Abyss Gate | → Chapter 2A | Engine may fail en route (Engineering (ENG) DC 15 to maintain) |
| Head to C-7 Outpost | → Chapter 2B | Outpost may be abandoned or already occupied |
| Investigate the encrypted message first | → Chapter 2C | Trace the signal source — may encounter danger |

### Voyage Event

No matter which path is chosen, trigger the following events during the first leg of the voyage (Game Master (GM) selects 1-2):

1. **Floating Debris** — You recognize one of the ships: it was your friend's. You may hold a 1-hour mourning (roleplay), or search the wreckage (1d4 Parts + a personal memento).

2. **Distress Signal** — From a small civilian ship with engine failure. Help them? (+1 Reputation progress, costs 1d4 Fuel) or ignore? (Your crew may remember this choice).

### Key XP: Completing Chapter 1 → 300 XP (approaching Lv2)

---

## Chapter 2A: The Long Way Home (Head to the Abyss Gate)

If the player chooses to return to the Abyss Gate:

**Challenge 1: Engine Crisis**
- Engineering (ENG) DC 15 to keep the engine running. Failure → engine damaged (System Damage Table `[GM §1.7]`), forced to divert to the nearest safe location (Class C port).

**Challenge 2: Border Patrol**
- Approaching the edge of the Abyss Gate sector, you encounter a patrol ship of the Skyhook Syndicate (天鉤集團). They are suspicious of "ships operating alone."
- Diplomacy (DIP) DC 13 to explain the situation → smooth passage; failure → escorted (adds +1 day) or fined 500 Credits (Cr).

**Arriving at the Abyss Gate:**
- At the port you meet your **Starting Ally** (determined by background `[Solo Rules §6.3]`). They heard of the convoy's fate and came specifically to find you.
- The Starting Ally provides: supply support (1,000 Credits (Cr) worth of goods) + one piece of intel — "The pirate flagship that attacked you was recently sighted at the Nephthys System (奈菲斯星系), on the edge of the Ash Belt."

---

## Chapter 2B: The Abandoned Outpost (Head to C-7)

If the player chooses to head to the C-7 outpost:

**Scene:** C-7 is a small orbital platform — abandoned but structurally intact. Inside are basic repair facilities.

**Exploring C-7 (use the Derelict Facility Table `[GM §4.12]`):**

| Area | Discovery |
|------|------|
| Docking Bay | Safe. Can dock and perform initial inspection |
| Engineering Bay | 2d4 Parts + usable spare engine parts (Engineering (ENG-S) repair DC -2) |
| Habitation | Logs of the outpost personnel — last entry two years ago: "They said rescue was coming. We're still waiting." |
| Comms Room | Encrypted message receiver — can trace the "traitor" lead from Chapter 1. Decrypt DC 18 Engineering (ENG) |

**Potential Danger:** The outpost's life support is unstable. For every 2 areas explored, make an Engineering (ENG) DC 12 check to avoid oxygen interruption.

**On departure:** The outpost's comm log contains an encrypted contact — codename "Ghost." This is the first lead pointing to the traitor.

---

## Chapter 2C: Tracing the Signal

If the player chooses to trace the encrypted message:

**Scene:** The signal source is an abandoned military ship at the edge of the Ash Belt. The signal is auto-replaying — a timed broadcast set to "send after the attack is complete."

**Military Ship Exploration `[GM §4.12 Derelict Ship Interior]`:**

| Area | Discovery |
|------|------|
| Bridge | Navigation log shows: this ship communicated with the pirate flagship one day before the attack |
| Comms Room | An encrypted recording — "Cargo delivered. Payment as agreed. Ghost." |
| Cargo Hold | Empty — but scans show traces of rare ore residue (from the Starfall Sector (星墜扇區)). Who is buying and selling Starfall ore? |

**Crew Council:** Your crew reacts differently to the codename "Ghost." The Comms Officer thinks it's someone's handle; the First Mate thinks it may be an organization.

## Chapter 3: The First Ally (Level (Lv) 2-3)

> **Transition:** No matter which path in Chapter 2, the player now knows "Ghost" is the key lead to the betrayal. The Starting Ally proposes cooperation.

### The Starting Ally's Proposal

The Starting Ally (based on your background) proposes a specific help plan:

| Background | Starting Ally's Help |
|------|-------------|
| Bankrupt Merchant | Echo helps you negotiate a transport contract — deliver a batch of medical supplies to a remote colony, reward 5,000 Credits (Cr) |
| Defected Officer | Kane knows a secret supply depot of the Imperial Navy — can "borrow" some military parts |
| Academic Exile | Dr. Orion provides an unpublished study — on anomalous energy readings in the Starfall Sector |
| Interstellar Smuggler | Shadow gives you the address of a black-market contact — can buy rare modification parts |
| Colonist Survivor | Leah's colony needs help — if you help them transport emergency supplies, they provide permanent free resupply |
| Mercenary Captain | Iron Fist knows a merchant who needs an escort — 8,000 Credits (Cr) round trip |
| Religious Pilgrim | Brother Ezekiel tells you of a pilgrim gathering place — can resupply and recruit crew there for free |
| Explorer | Kasha found an incomplete star chart of the Starfall Sector — if you help her complete it, share the discovery |

### Key Encounter: First Solo Combat

On the way to accepting the ally's mission, the player faces their **first true solo battle**:

**Encounter: 1d2 light raider craft (Threat Level (TL) 1)** `[GM §2.3]`

This is the player's first fight without convoy support. The Game Master (GM) should:
- Ensure the player understands the pacing of solo combat (no coordinated strike bonus)
- Remind the player they can use Energy abilities and tactical actions
- If the player gets into trouble, the Starting Ally can appear as a **plot wingmate** (they "just happened to pass by")

### Level-Up Node

After completing Chapter 3, the player should reach **Level (Lv) 3** (approximately 1,200 XP cumulative).

**Rewards:**
- Hull Upgrade Point +1
- First Feat
- Starting Ally's Reputation +2

# Act II: Connection (Level (Lv) 4-6)

> **Theme:** From lonely survival to building your own support network
> **Key Mechanics:** Recruit NPC wingmates, mid-scale combat, faction interaction
> **Estimated Sessions:** 4-5

---

## Chapter 4: The Genius and the Fugitive (Level (Lv) 4)

### Marcus Chen — Recruitable Engineer

At the Abyss Gate, the player hears a rumor: a young genius engineer works at a port machine shop, but he "doesn't belong there."

**Recruitment Mission:** Marcus Chen `[NPC_Card_FullSet:Marcus Chen]` is being harassed by a low-level manager of the Skyhook Syndicate — they want him to work on a secret project. He refuses, but has no ship to leave on.

**Crew Council:** Your current Chief Engineer may feel threatened — "We don't need another engineer." You need to handle the internal tension within the team.

**Recruitment Conditions:**
- Provide Marcus a position (he'll accept — initial Loyalty 8)
- His joining doubles your repair efficiency (Engineering (ENG) 10 = genius-level)
- Original Chief Engineer Loyalty -1 (can be restored through personal attention)

### Leah Starwhisper — Intelligence Network

At the Crossroads Outpost, the player meets Leah Starwhisper `[NPC_Card_FullSet:Leah Starwhisper]`, liaison of the Drifter Alliance.

She reacts to the codename "Ghost" — "That's the comm code of some pirate boss. I can help you trace it. But you need to do something for me first."

**Leah's Mission (choose one):**
1. Transport emergency medicine to a colony at the edge of the Ash Belt
2. Investigate an observation post that has gone silent for two weeks
3. Escort an important Drifter Alliance courier through pirate-active territory

Upon completion, Leah becomes your steady source of intelligence (you receive one free piece of intel at each port).

## Chapter 5: Hunter and Prey (Level (Lv) 5)

### The Identity of Ghost

Leah's intelligence network traced "Ghost" — real name **Victor Dorn**. He was the comms officer of your original convoy.

> He was captured by pirates two weeks before the attack. The pirates gave him a choice: provide the convoy's route intel, or watch his family be killed. He chose the former. After the attack, the pirates didn't keep their promise — his family remains imprisoned.

**This is not a simple revenge story.** Victor is both a traitor and a victim.

### Key Decision (Crew Council)

When you find Victor's whereabouts, your crew will have differing opinions:

| Crew | Opinion |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "He's a traitor. Whatever the reason, our shipmates died because of him." |
| Comms Officer | "If I were in that situation... I don't know what choice I'd make." |
| Engineer | "I don't care about the reason. What I care about is his family — they're still in pirate hands." |

**Your Options:**

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| **Hunt down Victor** | → Chapter 6A: Battle with Victor and pirate guards (Threat Level (TL) 2). After victory, can choose to kill him, hand him to the Drifter Alliance, or forgive him |
| **Rescue his family** | → Chapter 6B: Infiltrate the pirate stronghold. Stealth > combat. After success, Victor turns himself in and asks for forgiveness |
| **Do both** | → High-difficulty challenge. Must first infiltrate and rescue (without being detected), then find Victor before the pirates transfer him |

### Key Battle (Level (Lv) 5 Milestone)

No matter which path is chosen, this chapter includes a **mid-scale solo battle**:

- **1 medium raider ship (Threat Level (TL) 2)** or **2-3 light raider craft**
- If a Starting Ally serves as wingmate, can add 1 light enemy
- Victory reward: Level (Lv) 5 level-up + second Feat

---

## Chapter 6: Ripples of Choice (Level (Lv) 5-6)

### Victor's Fate

Based on the choice in Chapter 5, this chapter has three branches:

**Branch A: You Killed Him**
- Your crew's reaction is polarized — some relieved, some silent
- Victor says one thing before dying: "The pirate flagship... is in the Starfall Sector. There's a bigger plan."
- Obtain: Coordinates of the pirate flagship + an encrypted data module left by Victor

**Branch B: You Saved Him and His Family**
- Victor provides complete intel on the pirate network
- He cannot return to the convoy — but there's a safehouse in the Starfall Sector, "if you need a hiding place, it's there"
- Obtain: Pirate intelligence network + a future safehouse + Starting Ally Reputation +3 (they admire your forgiveness)

**Branch C: You Handed Him to the Drifter Alliance**
- The Alliance's trial: Victor is sentenced to life exile (rather than death) — partly due to your testimony
- The Alliance's repayment: access to a high-level intelligence network + a decommissioned patrol craft as a wingmate (usable in the key battle of Act III)

### Level-Up Node

After completing Act II, the player should reach **Level (Lv) 6** (approximately 6,000 XP cumulative).

**Rewards:**
- Attribute +1
- Reputation event triggered — your name begins to spread through the sector
- Can recruit a new Bridge Crew member (chosen from existing NPCs, or created by the GM)

# Act III: Rise (Level (Lv) 7-8)

> **Theme:** From survivor to legend — facing the true enemy
> **Key Mechanics:** High-difficulty solo combat, legendary NPC interaction, major moral choices
> **Estimated Sessions:** 3-4

---

## Chapter 7: The Secret of Starfall (Level (Lv) 7)

### Heading to the Starfall Sector

The coordinates of the pirate flagship point deep into the Starfall Sector. Getting there requires **one cross-sector Jump** (20 Fuel `[GM Resource Consumption Quick Reference]`) — ensure ample supplies before departure.

**Voyage Event (en route):** GM rolls d100 on the Encounter Table `[GM §4.9]`, selects 1-2 events. Prefer Starfall Sector themes (alien signals, ruins-related).

### Carlo Vega — An Unexpected Encounter

At the edge of the Starfall Sector, the player receives a faint distress signal — it's Carlo Vega `[NPC_Card_FullSet:Carlo Vega]`.

> If you recall the introductory module *The Last Broadcast* (S01) `[Rulebook §4.9]` — Carlo's plot can serve as a parallel event here. If *The Last Broadcast* (S01) has already occurred in your campaign, Carlo may already be on the player's ship (or already dead). Adjust the following accordingly.

**Carlo's Intel:** While exploring the Starfall Sector he discovered the pirate flagship's activity — "They're not plundering. They're digging. Something deep in an alien ruin."

**Crew Council:** Carlo wants to join your ship — but your Navigator warns: "Carlo Vega's reputation is half legend, half madness. His information may be unreliable."

---

## Chapter 8: The Pirate Flagship (Level (Lv) 7-8)

### Approaching the Pirate Operations Base

The Pirate Flagship — a heavily modified destroyer — is moored above an alien ruin. Surrounded by 2-4 light escort ships.

**This is the hardest battle of the entire campaign.** The player must make tactical choices:

| Strategy | Method | Difficulty |
|------|------|:---:|
| **Frontal Assault** | Directly attack the flagship, face all escort ships | Very High |
| **Sneak Sabotage** | Use cloaking (if available) or asteroid belt cover to approach, disable the escorts' sensors first | High |
| **Call Wingmate** | Summon the Starting Ally as wingmate support (requires Reputation 4+) | Medium |
| **Diplomatic Deception** | Disguise as a pirate supply ship to approach, then sudden attack (Diplomacy (DIP) DC 18 + forged ID) | Medium |

### Flagship Battle Data

**Heavy Pirate Flagship (Threat Level (TL) 3)** `[GM §2.3]` — full data in the GM Manual.

**Solo Adjustments:**
- If player has 1 wingmate → use standard Threat Level (TL) 3 data
- If player fights alone → flagship's escorts reduced to 1-2 ships, and the flagship's Hull Points (HP) reduced by 20%
- If player successfully infiltrates → flagship cannot act on the first round (caught by surprise)

### Crew Council in Combat

At the most intense moment of battle, the player's Bridge Crew each react:

| Crew | Reaction |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "Captain, we need to retreat—" then sees your expression and amends: "—retreat is not an option. I understand. All hands, battle stations." |
| Engineer | Voluntarily performs a free power overload — "This one's on me." |

**After Victory:**

In the flagship's wreckage, the player discovers the pirates' true objective: an **alien ruin core** — an energy device still operational. The pirates intend to use it to blackmail the entire sector.

**Crew Council (Major):** How to dispose of this core?

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| Hand to the Relic Watchers | Gain the Watchers' permanent respect + ancient star chart + unlock Act IV-A |
| Hand to the Drifter Alliance for research | May develop new energy — easing the sector's fuel crisis + unlock Act IV-B |
| Keep for yourself | Can provide a permanent energy bonus for your ship — but draws the attention of all factions + unlock Act IV-C |
| Destroy it | No faction gains it. But its destruction releases a signal — transmitted into the depths of the Still Sea (靜海) |

# Act IV: Legacy (Level (Lv) 9-10)

> **Theme:** How does your story end?
> **Key Mechanics:** Endgame choices, Lv9-10 advanced abilities, campaign wrap-up
> **Estimated Sessions:** 2-3

---

## Chapter 9: Echoes of the Star Sea (Level (Lv) 9)

### Based on how the alien core was disposed of, Chapter 9 has three branches:

### Branch A: The Watchers' Path (core handed to the Relic Watchers)

The Relic Watchers invite you into their inner circle — a place very few outsiders have seen. They share a secret: deep in the Still Sea lies a "Pioneer Archive" — a knowledge repository built in the age of the Far Leap Project, forgotten for 100 years. They want you to find it.

**Final Mission:** Find the archive, decide whether to bring its knowledge back to the sector.

### Branch B: The New Energy Era (core handed to the Drifter Alliance)

The Drifter Alliance's research succeeded — they found a way to replicate the alien core's energy. The Abyss Gate's fuel crisis is expected to be resolved. But the Skyhook Syndicate won't sit by and watch their monopoly broken.

**Final Mission:** Protect the new energy technology from being sabotaged or stolen by the Skyhook Syndicate.

### Branch C: The Lonely Guardian (core kept for yourself)

Your ship is now the most powerful single vessel in the sector. All factions want to negotiate with you — or eliminate you. You must decide: build your own faction, or continue alone.

**Final Mission:** Under pressure from multiple factions, decide how to use this power.

---

## Chapter 10: Finale — Your Legacy (Level (Lv) 10)

### Final Decision (no Crew Council — this is your own decision)

| Choice | Ending |
|------|------|
| **A. Rebuild the Convoy** | You recruit new captains and found the "Lone Star Convoy" — named after your ship. You are no longer the only survivor. |
| **B. Join a Faction** | You accept an invitation from a faction to become their flagship captain. Your name will be remembered alongside that faction. |
| **C. Keep Sailing** | You belong to no convoy or faction. Your ship is your home. The Still Sea still holds much unknown — you haven't seen enough. |
| **D. Become a Legend** | You release the alien core's energy into the Abyss Gate — solving the fuel crisis, but forever changing your ship. You "vanish" in the explosion. A legend spreads through the sector: deep in the star sea, there is a ship that never needs fuel, whose captain is an immortal ghost. |

### Final XP

Complete the campaign → Level (Lv) 10 (20,000 XP cumulative). Obtain:
- Attribute +1
- Flagship ability unlocked (GM and player negotiate a unique legendary ability)
- Reputation locked at 5 (living legend)
