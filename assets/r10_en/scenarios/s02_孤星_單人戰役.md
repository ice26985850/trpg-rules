# Starship Convoy TRPG — Solo Campaign: Lone Star

> **Mode:** Solo (1 Player + 1 Game Master)
> **Level Range:** Lv1 – Lv10
> **Estimated Length:** 4 Acts, 3–5 sessions each (roughly 15–20 sessions total)
> **Prerequisites:** Player Manual + GM Rulebook + Solo Mode Rules Supplement

---

# Campaign Overview

## Who Are You?

You are not the leader of a convoy. You are not the commander of a fleet.

**You are the only one who survived.**

Your convoy — the "Resonance" Convoy — was betrayed during a routine Ash Belt expedition. When the pirate flagship appeared on radar, your comm channel filled with panic. You heard your friends' voices vanish one by one.

You are the only one who escaped.

Now you drift alone in an unknown region at the edge of the Ash Belt. Fuel is only enough for one Jump. Food will not last two weeks. Your four crew watch you, waiting for your decision.

**This is where the Lone Star campaign begins.**

## Campaign Structure

| Act | Title | Level | Core Theme |

# Act One: Drift (Level (Lv) 1–3)

> **Theme:** Finding a reason to keep going after losing everything
> **Key Mechanics:** Crew Council, solo survival, the Starting Ally appears
> **Estimated Sessions:** 3–4

## Chapter One: Among the Wreckage (Level (Lv) 1)

### Opening — "They're All Dead"

**Scene:** The player's ship drifts silently at the edge of the Ash Belt. The engine is slightly damaged (−10 HP). The comm channel carries only static — occasionally broken by recorded fragments of the convoy's final moments. The crew gather on the bridge.

**Crew Council (first):** > Your First Mate, Engineer, Navigator, and Comms Officer each voice their opinion. This is the first time, without the convoy voting mechanic, that they need you to make the final call.

| Crew | Opinion |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "We should return to the Abyss Gate. There we can resupply, sell our cargo, find a new convoy." |
| Engineer | "The engine won't make it to the Abyss Gate. We have to find somewhere to make emergency repairs first." |
| Navigator | "Deep in the Ash Belt there's an abandoned outpost — C-7. The star charts show it has basic repair facilities." |
| Comms Officer | "I intercepted an encrypted message — sounds like pirates discussing 'the traitor got paid.' Someone is pulling the strings behind this attack." |

**Options and Consequences:**

| Decision | Path | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| Head to the Abyss Gate | → Chapter 2A | Engine may fail en route (Engineering (ENG) Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to maintain) |
| Head to Outpost C-7 | → Chapter 2B | The outpost may be abandoned or already occupied |
| Investigate the encrypted message first | → Chapter 2C | Trace the signal's source — may encounter danger |

### Voyage Events

Whichever route you choose, trigger the following events during the first leg of the voyage (GM selects 1–2):

1. **Drifting Wreckage** — You recognize one of the ships: it was your friend's. You may hold a 1-hour memorial (roleplay), or search the wreckage (1d4 Parts + a personal keepsake).

2. **Distress Signal** — From a small civilian ship with engine failure. Help them? (+1 Reputation progress, costs 1d4 Fuel) or ignore them? (Your crew may remember this choice).

### Key XP: Complete Chapter One → 300 XP (close to Lv2)

## Chapter Two A: The Long Way Home (Head to the Abyss Gate)

If the player chooses to return to the Abyss Gate:

**Challenge 1: Engine Crisis**
- Engineering (ENG) Difficulty Class (DC) 15 to keep the engine running. Failure → engine damaged (System Damage Table `[GM Rulebook §1.7]`), forced to divert to the nearest safe location (Class-C port).

**Challenge 2: Border Patrol**
- Approaching the edge of the Abyss Gate sector, you encounter a Skyhook Group patrol ship. They are suspicious of a "ship operating alone."
- Diplomacy (DIP) Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to explain the situation → pass through smoothly; failure → escorted (costs +1 day) or fined 500 Credits (Cr).

**Arriving at the Abyss Gate:**
- At the port you meet your **Starting Ally** (determined by background `[Solo Rules §6.3]`). They heard of the convoy's fate and came looking for you.
- The Starting Ally provides: supply support (1,000 Credits (Cr) worth of goods) + one piece of intel — "The pirate flagship that attacked you was recently sighted near the Nephys system, at the edge of the Ash Belt."

---

## Chapter Two B: The Abandoned Outpost (Head to C-7)

If the player chooses to head to Outpost C-7:

**Scene:** C-7 is a small orbital platform — abandoned but structurally intact. It has basic repair equipment inside.

**Exploring C-7 (use the Abandoned Facility Table `[GM Rulebook §4.12]`):**

| Zone | Discovery |
|------|------|
| Docking Bay | Safe. Can dock and perform an initial inspection |
| Engineering Bay | 2d4 Parts + usable spare engine parts (Engine (ENG-S) repair Difficulty Class (DC) −2) |
| Habitation | Logs of the outpost's personnel — last entry recorded two years ago: "They said rescue is coming. We're still waiting." |
| Comms Room | Encrypted message receiver — can trace the "traitor" lead from Chapter One. Decrypt Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Engineering (ENG) |

**Potential Danger:** The outpost's life-support is unstable. For every 2 zones explored, make an Engineering (ENG) Difficulty Class (DC) 12 check to avoid an oxygen interruption.

**On Leaving:** The outpost's comm log contains an encrypted contact — codename "Ghost." This is the first lead pointing to the traitor.

---

## Chapter Two C: Tracking the Signal

If the player chooses to trace the encrypted message:

**Scene:** The signal's source is an abandoned military ship at the edge of the Ash Belt. The signal is on auto-replay — a timed broadcast set to "send after the attack is complete."

**Military Ship Exploration `[GM Rulebook §4.12 Derelict Ship Interior]`:**

| Zone | Discovery |
|------|------|
| Bridge | Nav log shows: this ship communicated with the pirate flagship one day before the attack |
| Comms Room | An encrypted recording — "Cargo delivered. Payment as agreed. Ghost." |
| Cargo Hold | Empty — but a scan shows traces of rare ore residue (from the Starfall Sector). Who is trading Starfall ore? |

**Crew Council:** Your crew react differently to the codename "Ghost." The Comms Officer thinks it's someone's handle; the First Mate thinks it may be an organization.

## Chapter Three: First Ally (Level (Lv) 2–3)

> **Bridge:** Whichever route Chapter Two took, the player now knows "Ghost" is the key lead to the betrayal. The Starting Ally proposes cooperation.

### The Starting Ally's Proposal

The Starting Ally (based on your background) offers a concrete plan of help:

| Background | The Starting Ally's Help |
|------|-------------|
| Bankrupt Merchant | Echo brokers you a transport contract — deliver medical supplies to a remote colony, reward 5,000 Credits (Cr) |
| Defected Officer | Kane knows a secret Imperial Navy supply depot — can "borrow" some military parts |
| Academic Exile | Dr. Orion provides an unpublished study — on anomalous energy readings from the Starfall Sector |
| Interstellar Smuggler | Shadow gives you a black-market contact's address — where you can buy rare modification parts |
| Colonist Survivor | Leah's colony needs help — if you run emergency supplies for them, they provide permanent free resupply |
| Mercenary Captain | Iron Fist knows a merchant who needs an escort — 8,000 Credits (Cr) round trip |
| Religious Pilgrim | Brother Ezekiel tells you of a pilgrim gathering place — where you can resupply and recruit crew for free |
| Explorer | Kasha found an incomplete Starfall Sector star chart — if you help her complete it, share the discovery |

### Key Encounter: First Fight Alone

En route to accepting the ally's job, the player faces their **first real solo combat**:

**Encounter: 1d2 Light Raider Craft (Threat Level (TL) 1)** `[GM Rulebook §2.3]`

This is the player's first fight without convoy support. The GM should:
- Make sure the player understands the pace of solo combat (no synergy bonus)
- Remind the player they can use energy abilities and tactical actions
- If the player gets stuck, the Starting Ally can appear as a **plot wingmate** (they "just happened to be passing by")

### Level-Up Node

After completing Chapter Three, the player should reach **Level (Lv) 3** (cumulative ≈ 1,200 XP).

**Rewards:**
- Hull upgrade point +1
- First Feat
- Starting Ally Reputation +2

# Act Two: Connection (Level (Lv) 4–6)

> **Theme:** From lonely survival to building your own support network
> **Key Mechanics:** Recruit NPC wingmates, mid-scale combat, faction interaction
> **Estimated Sessions:** 4–5

---

## Chapter Four: The Genius and the Fugitive (Level (Lv) 4)

### Marcus Chen — Recruitable Engineer

At the Abyss Gate, the player hears a rumor: a young genius engineer works at a port machine shop, but he "doesn't belong there."

**Recruitment Mission:** Marcus Chen `[NPC_Card_Full: Marcus Chen]` is being harassed by a low-level Skyhook Group manager — they want him for a secret project. He won't comply, but has no ship to leave on.

**Crew Council:** Your current Chief Engineer may feel threatened — "We don't need another engineer." You need to handle the tension within the team.

**Recruitment Conditions:**
- Offer Marcus a position (he'll accept — initial Loyalty 8)
- His joining doubles your repair efficiency (Engineering (ENG) 10 = genius-level)
- Original Chief Engineer Loyalty −1 (recoverable through personal attention)

### Leah Starwhisper — Intelligence Network

At the Crossroads Outpost, the player meets Leah Starwhisper `[NPC_Card_Full: Leah Starwhisper]`, liaison of the Drifters' Alliance.

She reacts to the codename "Ghost" — "That's a comm code for some pirate boss. I can help you track him. But you need to do something for me first."

**Leah's Mission (choose one):**
1. Deliver emergency medicine to a colony at the edge of the Ash Belt
2. Investigate an observation post that has gone silent for two weeks
3. Escort an important Drifters' Alliance courier through pirate-active space

On completion, Leah becomes your steady source of intel (one free piece of intel at each port).

---

## Chapter Five: Hunter and Prey (Level (Lv) 5)

### The Identity of Ghost

Leah's intelligence network traces "Ghost" — real name **Victor Dorn**. He was your original convoy's Comms Officer.

> He was captured by pirates two weeks before the attack. The pirates gave him a choice: hand over the convoy's route intel, or watch his family die. He chose the former. After the attack, the pirates didn't keep their promise — his family remains imprisoned.

**This is not a simple revenge story.** Victor is both traitor and victim.

### Key Decision (Crew Council)

When you find Victor's whereabouts, your crew will have divided opinions:

| Crew | Opinion |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "He's a traitor. Whatever the reason, our shipmates died because of him." |
| Comms Officer | "If I'd been in that situation... I don't know what I would have chosen." |
| Engineer | "I don't care about the reason. What I care about is his family — they're still in pirate hands." |

**Your Options:**

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| **Hunt Victor** | → Chapter 6A: combat with Victor and pirate guards (Threat Level (TL) 2). After victory you may choose to kill him, hand him to the Drifters' Alliance, or forgive him |
| **Rescue his family** | → Chapter 6B: infiltrate the pirate stronghold. Stealth > combat. After success Victor turns himself in, asking for forgiveness |
| **Do both** | → High-difficulty challenge. Must infiltrate and rescue first (undetected), then find Victor before the pirates move him |

### Key Combat (Level (Lv) 5 milestone)

Whichever route, this chapter contains a **mid-scale solo battle**:

- **1 Medium Raider Ship (Threat Level (TL) 2)** or **2–3 Light Raider Craft**
- If the Starting Ally is present as a wingmate, add 1 light enemy
- Victory reward: Level (Lv) 5 upgrade + second Feat

---

## Chapter Six: Ripples of Choice (Level (Lv) 5–6)

### Victor's Fate

Based on the Chapter Five choice, this chapter has three branches:

**Branch A: You kill him**
- Your crew react in extremes — some relieved, some silent
- Victor says one thing before dying: "The pirate flagship... in the Starfall Sector. There's a bigger plan."
- Gain: coordinates of the pirate flagship + an encrypted data module Victor left behind

**Branch B: You save him and his family**
- Victor provides complete intel on the pirate network
- He cannot return to the convoy — but there's a safe house in the Starfall Sector, "if you ever need a hideout, it's there"
- Gain: pirate intelligence network + a future safe house + Starting Ally Reputation +3 (they admire your mercy)

**Branch C: You hand him to the Drifters' Alliance**
- The Alliance's judgment: Victor is sentenced to life exile (not death) — partly thanks to your testimony
- Alliance reward: access to a high-level intelligence network + a decommissioned patrol craft as a wingmate (usable in Act Three's key battle)

### Level-Up Node

After completing Act Two, the player should reach **Level (Lv) 6** (cumulative ≈ 6,000 XP).

**Rewards:**
- Attribute +1
- Reputation event triggers — your name begins to spread through the sector
- May recruit a new Bridge Crew member (chosen from existing NPCs, or created by the GM)

# Act Three: Rise (Level (Lv) 7–8)

> **Theme:** From survivor to legend — facing the real enemy
> **Key Mechanics:** High-difficulty solo combat, legendary NPC interaction, major moral choices
> **Estimated Sessions:** 3–4

---

## Chapter Seven: The Secret of Starfall (Level (Lv) 7)

### Heading to the Starfall Sector

The pirate flagship's coordinates point deep into the Starfall Sector. Reaching it requires **one cross-sector Jump** (20 Fuel `[GM Rulebook §Resource Consumption Quick Reference]`) — make sure you resupply before departing.

**Voyage Events (en route):** The GM rolls the d100 Encounter Table `[GM Rulebook §4.9]`, selects 1–2 events. Prefer Starfall Sector themes (alien signals, ruins-related).

### Carlo Vega — An Unexpected Meeting

At the edge of the Starfall Sector, the player receives a faint distress signal — it's Carlo Vega `[NPC_Card_Full: Carlo Vega]`.

> If you remember the starter module "The Last Broadcast" `[Rulebook §4.9]` — Carlo's plot can serve as a parallel event here. If "The Last Broadcast" has already happened in your campaign, Carlo may already be on the player's ship (or already dead). Adjust the following accordingly.

**Carlo's Intel:** While exploring the Starfall Sector he discovered the pirate flagship's activity — "They're not raiding. They're digging. For something deep in an alien ruin."

**Crew Council:** Carlo wants to join your ship — but your Navigator warns: "Carlo Vega's reputation is half legend, half madness. His info may be unreliable."

---

## Chapter Eight: The Pirate Flagship (Level (Lv) 7–8)

### Approaching the Pirate Base

The pirate flagship — a heavy modified destroyer — is anchored above an alien ruin. Around it cruise 2–4 light escort ships.

**This is the hardest fight of the entire campaign.** The player must make tactical choices:

| Strategy | Method | Difficulty |
|------|------|:---:|
| **Frontal Assault** | Attack the flagship directly, facing all escorts | Very High |
| **Stealth Sabotage** | Use cloaking (if available) or asteroid-belt cover to approach, disable the escorts' sensors first | High |
| **Call Wingmate** | Summon the Starting Ally as wingmate support (requires Reputation 4+) | Medium |
| **Diplomatic Deception** | Disguise as a pirate supply ship to approach, then suddenly attack (Diplomacy (DIP) Difficulty Class (DC) 18 + forged ID) | Medium |

### Flagship Combat Data

**Heavy Pirate Flagship (Threat Level (TL) 3)** `[GM Rulebook §2.3]` — full data in the GM Rulebook.

**Solo Adjustments:**
- If the player has 1 wingmate → use standard Threat Level (TL) 3 data
- If the player fights alone → the flagship's escorts drop to 1–2 ships, and the flagship's Hull Points (HP) are reduced by 20%
- If the player succeeds at stealth → the flagship cannot act on the first round (caught by surprise)

### Crew Council in Combat

At the height of the battle, the player's Bridge Crew each react:

| Crew | Reaction |
|------|------|
| First Mate | "Captain, we need to pull back —" then, seeing your expression, amends: "— retreat is not an option. I understand. All hands, battle stations." |
| Engineer | Voluntarily performs one free power overload — "This one's on me." |

**After Victory:**

In the flagship's wreckage, the player discovers the pirates' true objective: an **Alien Ruins Core** — an energy device still operational. The pirates intend to use it to extort the entire sector.

**Crew Council (major):** What to do with this core?

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| Hand to the Relic Watchers | Gain the Watchers' permanent respect + an ancient star chart + unlock Act Four A |
| Hand to the Drifters' Alliance for research | May develop a new energy source — eases the sector's fuel crisis + unlock Act Four B |
| Keep it for yourself | Can provide a permanent energy bonus to your ship — but draws the attention of all factions + unlock Act Four C |
| Destroy it | No faction gains it. But its destruction releases a signal — transmitted into the depths of the Still Sea |

# Act Four: Legacy (Level (Lv) 9–10)

> **Theme:** How does your story end?
> **Key Mechanics:** Endgame choices, Lv9–10 high-level abilities, campaign resolution
> **Estimated Sessions:** 2–3

---

## Chapter Nine: Echoes of the Star Sea (Level (Lv) 9)

### Based on how the alien core was disposed of, Chapter Nine has three branches:

### Branch A: The Watchers' Path (core handed to the Relic Watchers)

The Relic Watchers invite you into their inner circle — a place few outsiders have seen. They share a secret: deep in the Still Sea lies a "Pioneer Archive" — a knowledge repository built in the Far-Leap Project era, forgotten for 100 years. They want you to find it.

**Final Mission:** Find the archive, decide whether to bring its knowledge back to the sector.

### Branch B: The New Energy Era (core handed to the Drifters' Alliance)

The Drifters' Alliance's research succeeds — they find a way to replicate the alien core's energy. The Abyss Gate's fuel crisis stands a chance of being solved. But the Skyhook Group will not sit by while its monopoly is broken.

**Final Mission:** Protect the new energy technology from Skyhook Group sabotage or theft.

### Branch C: The Lonely Guardian (core kept for yourself)

Your ship is now the most powerful single vessel in the sector. Every faction wants to negotiate with you — or eliminate you. You must decide: build your own faction, or keep traveling alone.

**Final Mission:** Under pressure from multiple factions, decide how to use this power.

---

## Chapter Ten: Finale — Your Legacy (Level (Lv) 10)

### Final Decision (no Crew Council — this is your own call)

| Choice | Ending |
|------|------|
| **A. Rebuild the Convoy** | You recruit new Captains and found the "Lone Star Convoy" — named after your ship. You are no longer the only survivor. |
| **B. Join a Faction** | You accept an invitation from some faction, becoming their flagship captain. Your name will be remembered alongside that faction's. |
| **C. Keep Sailing** | You belong to no convoy or faction. Your ship is your home. The Still Sea still holds much unknown — you haven't seen enough. |
| **D. Become a Legend** | You release the alien core's energy into the Abyss Gate — solving the fuel crisis, but forever changing your ship. You "vanish" in the explosion. A legend spreads through the sector: deep in the star sea sails a ship that never needs fuel, commanded by an undying ghost. |

### Final XP

Complete the campaign → Level (Lv) 10 (cumulative 20,000 XP). Gain:
- Attribute +1
- Flagship ability unlocked (GM and player negotiate a unique legendary ability)
- Reputation locked at 5 (the living legend)
