# S01 The Last Broadcast

> **Scenario Role:** Starter module — the convoy's first adventure after departing the Abyss Gate
> **Recommended Length:** 2–3 sessions (approx. 8–12 hours)
> **Recommended Level:** Level (Lv) 1–2
> **Players:** 2–5 (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–4 Captains)
> **Core Showcase:** Resource management, voting decisions, voyage events, crew interaction

---

## Adventure Background

The Abyss Gate's public channel received an encrypted broadcast: "To all ships that have left — this is Navigator Carlo Vega. My ship is stranded in orbit around Nephys-4. I have something you want: a complete Ash Belt navigation chart. Come. Or don't."

Carlo Vega is a legendary navigator who set out alone three years ago to explore the depths of the Ash Belt and was presumed dead. Nephys-4 lies on the edge of the Ash Belt sector and requires a medium-range Jump to reach.

This broadcast was received simultaneously by multiple convoys — including the Skyhook Group's tracker.

---

## Act One: The Hook — "You've Got a Friend in Deep Space"

### Scene Briefing

The convoy has only just left the Abyss Gate. Carlo Vega's broadcast plays repeatedly on the public channel.

**Key Information:**
- Carlo Vega is a legendary navigator of the Abyss Gate, presumed dead three years ago
- Nephys-4 requires 1 medium-range Jump (Engine Level (Lv).2+, 10 Fuel)
- The broadcast signal was sent recently — but why now?
- Erin Colt, the Skyhook Group's tracker, is also monitoring this channel

---

## Act Two: The First Vote — "Go or Stay?"

### Vote Type
Simple Majority — the convoy votes on whether to head to Nephys-4.

### GM Secret Motive Cards (each Captain draws one at random)

1. **Your engine needs rare parts:** Carlo is said to have once found a military repair ship. You badly need those parts to upgrade your engine.
2. **You owe a debt to an Abyss Gate information broker:** If you don't go, he can stall for a while. If you go, the debt comes due.
3. **One of your crew was once Carlo's navigation apprentice:** That crew member privately begs you to mount a rescue. Refusing will affect Loyalty.
4. **The Skyhook Group is also tracking this signal:** You privately know they want that navigation chart. If you don't go, the Skyhook Group will get there first.

### Debate Points
- Route risk vs. the value of the navigation chart
- Fuel cost (medium-range Jump consumes 10 Fuel)
- Credibility of the intel — is Carlo really still alive?
- Competition with other convoys — if you don't go, an NPC convoy may beat you to it

---

## Act Three: The Voyage — "The Road Through the Ashes"

### Jump Phase

The convoy makes one medium-range Jump to Nephys-4. Roll on the d100 Voyage Event Table (see GM Rulebook §4.9); the GM selects 1–2 events.

### Recommended Voyage Events

| d20 Range | Event | Details |
|:------:|------|------|
| 1–8 | Smooth Jump | Arrive in Nephys-4 orbit, but fuel consumption exceeds expectations (+5 units) |
| 9–12 | Derelict Fleet | Encounter a drifting formation of old military warships en route — may spend 1 day scavenging, but with the risk of triggering automated defenses |
| 13–16 | Distress Signal | A smaller convoy's ship has engine failure — help them? They may know the situation at Nephys-4 |
| 17–20 | Anomalous Interference | Approaching Nephys-4, communications are hit by strong electromagnetic interference. The signal seems to originate from the planet's surface |

---

## Act Four: Arrival — "Nephys-4: The Forgotten Research Station"

### Scene Description

Nephys-4 is a gray-brown rocky planet with a thin atmosphere. Orbit is empty — no ships, no signals. But on the planet's surface, lights flicker from a pre-war research station.

### Exploration Challenges (three scenes)

#### Scene A: Orbital Debris (Sensors (SEN) check, Difficulty Class (DC) 12)

- Carlo's ship, the "Wanderer's Eye," drifts in orbit — its hull torn open from within
- Successful scavenging: partial navigation data fragments + an encrypted personal log module
- Clue: "He's not on the ship. He went down. The log mentions — there are 'things they need to know' in the research station"

#### Scene B: Research Station — Surface Level (Exploration)

- The research station is partially operational, lights flickering
- An abandoned laboratory — research subject: **alternative energy for hyperspace jumps**
- Discovery: Carlo's makeshift camp
- Final log entry: "I finally know why the Jump Gates were destroyed. Not war. It was them. They're in hyperspace. I touched their nest —"

#### Scene C: Research Station — Deep Level

- **Carlo Vega himself** — alive, but mentally unstable
- He claims to have made contact with "Hyperspace Entities" during a hyperspace jump — beings that live in the hyperspace dimension
- He inadvertently led them near Nephys-4 — **and now they are closing in**
- The navigation chart is on his data pad — but he demands the convoy take him away

---

## Act Five: The Second Vote (Life or Death) — "Take Him, or Take the Chart?"

### Vote Type
Two-Thirds Majority — this concerns the convoy's charter and moral bottom line.

### Situation

The research station's sensors show — a massive hyperspace anomaly is approaching Nephys-4, arriving in about 2 hours. Carlo demands to be taken away, but first must go to the station's core area to "shut down what he activated."

### Four Options

| Option | Risk | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **A: Take Carlo to the core to shut down the device, then evacuate together** | Tight on time, possible encounter with hyperspace entities | Complete navigation chart + Carlo's knowledge + secrets of the hyperspace entities |
| **B: Forcefully take the data pad and leave immediately** | Hyperspace entities may track the jump trajectory | Navigation chart + safe evacuation — but an unknown threat remains |
| **C: Take Carlo but skip the core** | Device left running, consequences unknown | Navigation chart + Carlo — but the threat may spread across the entire sector |
| **D: Kill Carlo, take the data pad** | Moral cost; condemnation by the other Captains | Navigation chart — the "safest" but darkest option |

---

## Act Six: Endings and Consequences

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| **A** | The core-area exploration is a tense scene — may trigger a combat encounter with a small hyperspace entity (Threat Level (TL) 2). After a successful shutdown, gain the complete navigation chart, Carlo Vega (as an NPC crew member), and valuable intel on the hyperspace entities. Sequel module: track the origin of the hyperspace entities. |
| **B** | Roll d20 when jumping away: result ≥ 12 successfully breaks pursuit. On failure, encounter hyperspace entities on the next jump. The navigation chart is incomplete — missing safe-route markers. |
| **C** | Gain the navigation chart and Carlo. But the GM secretly records "the hyperspace anomaly is spreading." In later sessions, more hyperspace entity events may appear in other sectors. |
| **D** | Gain the complete navigation chart. All crew Loyalty drops, convoy relations grow tense. If the Relic Watchers learn of this, the convoy is marked "Untrustworthy." |

## Key NPCs

### Carlo Vega
- **Role:** Legendary navigator / source of the distress signal
- **Attributes:** Command (CMD) 7 | Engineering (ENG) 4 | Navigation (NAV) 6 | Diplomacy (DIP) 5 | Intuition (INT) 8
- **Description:** In his fifties, mentally damaged but deeply knowledgeable. Knows the sector's secrets — but cannot always express them clearly.
- **Role Function:** Information source, core of a moral dilemma, a "living star chart"

### Erin Colt
- **Role:** Skyhook Group tracker
- **Attributes:** Command (CMD) 5 | Engineering (ENG) 4 | Navigation (NAV) 5 | Diplomacy (DIP) 7 | Intuition (INT) 6
- **Description:** A professional, cold-eyed Skyhook Group agent. Wants the navigation chart and Carlo. May appear in the mid-to-late part of the scenario.
- **Role Function:** Third-party pressure; may become a recurring rival or ally in sequels

### The "Gate-Bottom" Informant
- **Role:** Wandering information broker
- **Attributes:** Command (CMD) 3 | Engineering (ENG) 4 | Navigation (NAV) 5 | Diplomacy (DIP) 6 | Intuition (INT) 7
- **Description:** An information broker who latched onto the convoy back at the Abyss Gate. May provide leads through encrypted channels.
- **Role Function:** Information source, a connecting point between scenarios

## GM Notes

- The core of this scenario is **showcasing the convoy voting mechanic** — make sure both votes carry genuine dramatic tension
- The second vote (life or death) is the climax of the entire scenario — give each Captain time to state their position; don't rush the pacing
- Carlo Vega can become a long-term NPC — if the convoy chooses to rescue him, he may later provide unique navigation knowledge and plot leads
- The introduction of hyperspace entities paves the way for sequel modules — they are one of the Glass Sea Sector's primary threats
- The stance of Erin Colt, the Skyhook Group tracker, can shift based on the convoy's actions — she is not necessarily a pure enemy
