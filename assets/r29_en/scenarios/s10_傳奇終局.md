# Scenario S10: Legendary Endgame

> **Type**: Solo Lone Wolf | **Suggested Level**: 5 | **Difficulty**: ★★★★★ | **Estimated Duration**: 6–8 hours

## Synopsis

You won your last match. You thought it was over. Then that letter came. A black envelope, gold wax seal—Pandora Entertainment Group's highest-level invitation. "Dear Champion: your performance has been flagged in our files as 'Legendary Tier.' For this, we cordially invite you to a private event—limited to five legendary champions, and you." The prize is not money—you already have more than enough. The prize is "freedom." Permanent removal from Pandora's lists. You will never again receive an invitation, a threat letter, or a "special opportunity." You are free. But the opponents are five legends who have never lost. One of them—"the Reaper"—holds the record of 25 wins. Do you think you can become the 26th? he asks.

## Flow Phases

### 1. Invitation (0 (opening) rounds)

**GM Note**: This is not a parachute drop—it is a show. The player is driven to the arena by a private car. Walking down a long corridor—holographic projections of past champions line both sides. At the end of the corridor—a door. Carved into it: "Beyond this door, legends are born—or ended." The Pandora CEO's voice comes over the arena-wide broadcast: "Distinguished guests—welcome to the 47th Legendary Invitational. Tonight's challenger is—(your codename). And he will face—five champions who have never lost. Who will be left standing? Let us—begin."

### 2. First Blood (3–4 rounds)

**GM Note**: The five champions each land (or enter) on different floors. The player starts on the third floor (residential zone)—a neutral zone, with time to adjust. The first encounter should be "the Mirage" or "the Bulwark"—let the player feel the pressure of a champion-level opponent, but not be instantly killed. Key point: make the player realize "these are not ordinary enemies—their AI is serious."

### 3. Vertical Chase (5–6 rounds)

**GM Note**: The circle begins to contract downward. The champions begin to move. This is the core passage of vertical combat: - the player may meet "the Hurricane" charging up the stairwell - "the Red Fox" may snipe from an upper-floor window at the player climbing the outer wall - "the Bulwark" may occupy the bottom of the mandatory elevator shaft—like an unavoidable tower Use the vertical space to create tension—"You hear footsteps overhead—but the elevator shaft's echo makes it impossible to tell if it is one floor up or two."

### 4. Audience Pressure (3–4 rounds)

**GM Note**: Pandora's executives begin using the "audience vote" system—they can vote on the type and position of the next arena trap. The GM announces: "A line flashes across the screen—audience vote: flamethrower, third quadrant." This gives the player a warning—but also adds pressure. Use the broadcast system to play "the Reaper's" commentary on the player: "You know what—he switched weapons in his last match. Why? Because he was afraid. Afraid to use the gun he knows best—because that gun reminds him of someone."

### 5. Final Circle—You and the Reaper (3–5 rounds)

**GM Note**: The central arena. All other champions have been eliminated or taken by Out-of-Zone Damage. Only you and the Reaper remain. The arena lights focus on you both. Pandora's executives look down from the panoramic control room. The whole arena falls silent. The Reaper speaks: "Twenty-five. You are the third person I have seen reach this point. The first two—both fell here. Do you know why? Not because I am stronger than them—because they wanted to win too much. What about you? Why are you here?" Give the player a choice: A. "For freedom." (make a Persuade contest—may make the Reaper hesitate) B. "To beat you." (go straight into combat—but the Reaper's respect for you +1) C. "I don't know." (the most honest answer—the Reaper laughs, then says "That is the best answer I have ever heard.") Then—the duel begins. No Safe Zone in the final circle. 6d4/round. Not a contest of gunplay—a clash of wills between two legends.

### 6. Ending (0 (epilogue) rounds)

**GM Note**: Depends on the outcome: **Player wins:** The Reaper falls. The arena is silent for three seconds—then erupts in thunderous applause. The Pandora CEO's voice: "Ladies and gentlemen—a new legend." The control room door opens. They hand you a document—the permanent removal agreement. You sign it. Then walk out of the arena—sunlight on your face. Freedom. **Player loses:** The Reaper stands beside you—he does not finish you off. "You were stronger than the first two. Rest a year—then come back. I'll wait." The screen goes dark. You did not lose—you were just not strong enough. But you know he will wait. A year later—you will return.

## Victory Condition

Defeat "the Reaper"—or prove your worth before him
