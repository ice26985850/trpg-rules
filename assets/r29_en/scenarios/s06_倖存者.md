# Scenario S6: The Survivor

> **Type**: Solo Lone Wolf | **Suggested Level**: 1 | **Difficulty**: ★ | **Estimated Duration**: 1.5–2 hours

## Synopsis

This is your first match. A small night island—only 2 km across. Ten contestants. No teammates. No airdrop. Just you, your gun, and nine strangers closing in through the dark. You are not here to win—you are here to prove you did not come to die.

## Battlefield Map

**Night Mist Island** — 2 km × 2 km

**Terrain composition:** Coast (30%), Forest (50%), Abandoned Fishing Village (20%)


### Landmarks

- **Abandoned Lighthouse** (tactical | loot: Abundant (Blue)): the island's highest point, but anyone who goes up is visible to the entire island
- **Fishing Village Pier** (residential | loot: Common (Green)): an abandoned fishing village—alleys well suited to Silent Move
- **Radio Tower** (special | loot: -): the island's only radio tower. Its green indicator light stands out sharply at night

## Flow Phases

### 1. Solo Descent (1 round)

**GM Note**: "The transport plane's cargo door swings open—below is a black sea and a dark island. You take a deep breath and leap. The parachute blossoms in the night sky—you see nine other chutes scattering through the darkness like ghosts."

### 2. Night Looting (4 rounds)

**GM Note**: Darkness is both your friend and your enemy. You cannot see them, and they cannot see you. But footstep sounds carry clearly on a quiet night. Guide the player to use their hearing—'You hear a rustle in the bushes to your front-left.'

### 3. First Blood (3–4 rounds)

**GM Note**: Stage the player's first solo kill. It should mean something—after the kill, let the player find an item on the enemy's body (a family photo, an unsent letter), a reminder that every contestant is a person.

### 4. The Radio's Temptation (2 rounds)

**GM Note**: The lighthouse or the radio tower is a strategic high ground—but also extremely exposed. Offer the player a choice: gamble on activating the radio (gain intel), or play it safe on the ground?

### 5. Final Circle: The Veteran (2–3 rounds)

**GM Note**: The circle shrinks to a single fishing-village shack. You and the veteran. "He steps out of the shadows—fifty-something, with a grizzled grey beard, an SKS scarred all over in his hands. He looks at you and nods slightly. Not a challenge—recognition. 'Not bad—your first match, and you made it this far.' Then he raises his gun."

## Victory Condition

Become the last surviving Lone Wolf
