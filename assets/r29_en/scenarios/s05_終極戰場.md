# Scenario S5: The Ultimate Battlefield

> **Type**: Multiplayer Squad | **Suggested Level**: 5 | **Difficulty**: ★★★★★ | **Estimated Duration**: 6-8 hours

## Story Synopsis

"Ragnarök" — the ultimate experimental ground where four terrains converge. 52 contestants, 12 of the world's strongest squads. Resources are extremely scarce — 80% of gear can only be plundered from enemies. Sandstorms and blizzards rage in turn. The final circle has no safe zone — whoever breaks first loses.

## Battlefield Map

**Ragnarök (Mixed Ultimate Map)** — 8 km × 8 km

**Terrain Composition:** Urban (25%): abandoned skyscraper clusters, Jungle (25%): dense tropical vegetation, Desert (25%): open sandy ground, Snowfield (25%): snow-covered area


### Landmarks

- **Central Bridge** (tactical|Loot: Military (Purple)): A giant bridge connecting the four zones — the core of the final circle's waterway showdown
- **Skyscraper** (urban|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): A 30-story abandoned commercial building — elevators stopped, can only take the stairs
- **Underground Bunker** (military|Loot: Military (Purple)): An underground military command center — the only shelter on the map
- **Energy Center** (industrial|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): An abandoned nuclear facility — full of radiation warning signs

## Enemy Configuration

A total of 12 enemy squads (including the player squad).

- **Assault Squad** (★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Ambush Squad** (★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Professional Squad** (★★★★): 4 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Champion-tier** (★★★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **GM-Custom Boss-tier** (★★★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 

## Battlefield Conditions

- **Circle**: Watery End — the final circle is in the waterway near the bridge — forcing a bridge-top showdown or a standoff in the water
- **Airdrop**: 2 drops, — only produces special gear (thermal imaging scopes, self-revive syringes, etc.)
- **Red Zone Bombing**: Yes
- **Vehicles**: Full Vehicle Pool
- **Weather**: Disaster Weather (Sandstorm/Blizzard alternating every 10 turns)
- **Time of Day**: All day (from noon to dawn)

## Scenario Phases

### 1. Desperate Beginning (2 turns)

**GM Note**: 52 people drop at once. The sky is obscured by parachutes. Players must decide strategy immediately: go to the 20% loot zones to grab (facing fierce competition), or start from zero in no-man's-land (then plunder the first enemy they meet).

### 2. Weather War (6-8 turns)

**GM Note**: Sandstorms and blizzards alternate — each weather switch is a signal to flee or hunt. Those who adapt to the weather survive; those who don't are eliminated by it. Use weather changes to create tactical opportunities.

### 3. Loot Plundering (4-6 turns)

**GM Note**: Every enemy you kill is a walking supply crate. But sometimes you open the crate — and find it empty too. The pressure of scarce resources should show in every turn.

### 4. Bridge Endgame (3-5 turns)

**GM Note**: The final circle — on the bridge. Out-of-Zone Damage mercilessly erodes everyone's HP. Not a contest of marksmanship — but of whose will breaks first. Med kits, adrenaline, Calm checks — in the death countdown of 6d4/turn, who can hold out to the end?

## Victory Condition

Be the last surviving squad (surviving is victory)
