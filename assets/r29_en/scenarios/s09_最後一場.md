# Scenario S9: The Last Match

> **Type**: Solo Lone Wolf | **Suggested Level**: 3 | **Difficulty**: ★★★★★ | **Estimated Duration**: 5–7 hours

## Synopsis

This is your last match. Win and you retire—lose and you stay here forever. An abandoned city trembles in the storm. Twenty elite Lone Wolves drop into this dead city. And your nemesis—your former partner—has signed up too. He survived. And he has a score to settle with you.

## Battlefield Map

**Drowned City** — 6 km × 6 km

**Terrain composition:** Abandoned Urban (70%), Underground Pipes (20%), Water (10%)


### Landmarks

- **Skyline Tower** (urban | loot: Abundant (Blue)): a 60-floor abandoned skyscraper—panoramic view but extremely exposed
- **Subway System** (urban | loot: Common (Green)): abandoned subway tunnels—dark passages connecting the whole city
- **Broadcast Tower** (special | loot: -): one of the city's three tallest radio towers
- **City Hospital** (urban | loot: Abundant (Blue)): an abandoned hospital—plenty of medical supplies, but perfect for ambush
- **Harbor Authority Building** (urban | loot: Military (Purple)): an administration building by the port—once a military command center

## Flow Phases

### 1. Back to the Start (1 round)

**GM Note**: "In the storm, the transport plane's cargo door opens—below is a city you know. You and Kai fought three matches here. But this time is different—this time you are here to end it all."

### 2. The City's Memory (4–5 rounds)

**GM Note**: Guide the player to explore the city—every landmark is a place where you once fought side by side. Skyline Tower—you held the bridge there together. City Hospital—where you first saved his life. Let memory and reality intertwine—let the player feel "this city knows us."

### 3. The Nemesis's Game (4–5 rounds)

**GM Note**: The nemesis will not appear directly. He leaves clues—each clue a fragment of your shared past. The player begins to hesitate, to remember, to question. This is psychological warfare—the nemesis is "preparing" the player to face him. Meanwhile, the other elite Lone Wolves keep applying pressure. Make the player struggle between "tracking the nemesis's clues" and "dealing with the threat in front of them."

### 4. Hunt in the Storm (4–5 rounds)

**GM Note**: The storm is the perfect stage—blurred vision, deafened hearing. Every corner may be a duel. Let the environment itself become an opponent—flooded streets slow you down, collapsed buildings block routes, lightning illuminates a target for an instant.

### 5. Final Circle: Us (2–3 rounds)

**GM Note**: The top floor of Skyline Tower—where you first worked together. The circle shrinks to just this floor. You and Kai. "Rain comes in through the broken window. Lightning illuminates his face—he has aged, but his eyes have not changed. In his hands is that gun—the one you gave him." Give the player a real choice: - Fire—end it all, take the bounty, retire - Lower your gun—say "I'm sorry" Whatever the choice, the outcome is decided by the dice—but the choice itself is the player's.

## Victory Condition

Become the last surviving Lone Wolf—or find something more important than victory
