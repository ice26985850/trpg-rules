# Scenario S3: Desert Military Base

> **Type**: Multiplayer Squad | **Suggested Level**: 3 | **Difficulty**: ★★★ | **Estimated Duration**: 4-6 hours

## Story Synopsis

Miramar — an endless desert. Heat haze distorts the horizon. The abandoned military base hides the best gear on the island — but all eight squads know it. You didn't come here to loot — you came to take it by force.

## Battlefield Map

**Miramar (Desert)** — 6 km × 6 km

**Terrain Composition:** Desert (60%), Abandoned Industrial Zone (20%), Military Base (15%), Highway (5%)


### Landmarks

- **Military Base** (military|Loot: Military (Purple)): The best loot on the map — and the most dangerous place on the map
- **Bridge Checkpoint** (tactical|Loot: Common (Green)): The only bridge connecting the east and west zones — a favorite spot for Ambush Squads
- **Abandoned Factory** (industrial|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): A multi-story industrial building with heavy cover
- **Gas Station Town** (urban|Loot: Common (Green)): A small town by the desert highway

## Enemy Configuration

A total of 8 enemy squads (including the player squad).

- **Steady Squad** (★★): 3 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Assault Squad** (★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Ambush Squad** (★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Professional Squad** (★★★★): 1 squad,  members each, drop point 

## Battlefield Conditions

- **Circle**: Edge Drift (South) — drifts south each circle — the final circle is inside the military base
- **Airdrop**: 4 drops, — 
- **Red Zone Bombing**: Yes
- **Vehicles**: Off-road Vehicle (Desert Specialist), Jeep, Motorcycle, Armored Vehicle (Airdrop)
- **Weather**: Clear (high temperature)
- **Time of Day**: Day

## Scenario Phases

### 1. Drop Site Selection (1 turn)

**GM Note**: Desert visibility is extremely high — you can see other squads' parachutes when dropping. Choice: rush straight into the military base and gamble, or play it safe and develop on the outskirts?

### 2. Airdrop Contest (5-6 turns)

**GM Note**: The airdrop's red smoke is especially conspicuous in the desert. Multiple squads move toward the airdrop at once — you face a choice: go grab it (high risk, high reward), or snipe the airdrop grabbers from afar?

### 3. Bridgehead Ambush (3-4 turns)

**GM Note**: The Ambush Squad will wait for you at the bridgehead. Let players experience the pressure of "being ambushed" — and give them a chance to "counter-ambush" (use smoke grenades for cover, swim under the bridge to flank).

### 4. Military Base Decisive Battle (4-6 turns)

**GM Note**: The circle shrinks inside the military base. Multi-story buildings, heavy cover — the Professional Squad will show its true strength here.

## Victory Condition

Be the last surviving squad
