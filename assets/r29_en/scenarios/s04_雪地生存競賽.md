# Scenario S4: Snowfield Survival Contest

> **Type**: Multiplayer Squad | **Suggested Level**: 4 | **Difficulty**: ★★★★ | **Estimated Duration**: 5-7 hours

## Story Synopsis

Vikendi — a snow-covered abandoned ski resort. Every step leaves an indelible mark on the snow. A Champion-tier squad is on this map — they're not looking for you, they're hunting you. After nightfall, anyone without night-vision goggles is blind.

## Battlefield Map

**Vikendi (Snowfield)** — 6 km × 6 km

**Terrain Composition:** Snowfield (45%), Abandoned Town (25%), Forest (15%), Frozen Lake (15%)


### Landmarks

- **Snow Castle** (urban|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): A resort converted from an abandoned medieval-style castle
- **Cosmodrome** (military|Loot: Military (Purple)): An abandoned space research facility — with labs underground
- **Frozen Lake Village** (residential|Loot: Common (Green)): A small village by the frozen lake — moving on the ice is risky
- **Cement Plant** (industrial|Loot: Abundant (Blue)): A large industrial building — abundant heavy cover

## Enemy Configuration

A total of 10 enemy squads (including the player squad).

- **Steady Squad** (★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Assault Squad** (★★★): 3 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Ambush Squad** (★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Professional Squad** (★★★★): 2 squads,  members each, drop point 
- **Champion-tier** (★★★★★): 1 squad,  members each, drop point 

## Battlefield Conditions

- **Circle**: Jump Reversal — suddenly shifts sharply midway — breaking everyone's plans
- **Airdrop**: 4 drops, — 
- **Red Zone Bombing**: Yes
- **Vehicles**: Snowmobile, Jeep, Armored Vehicle (Airdrop)
- **Weather**: Light Snow → Blizzard (night)
- **Time of Day**: Day → Night (night falls after the 4th circle)

## Scenario Phases

### 1. Silence of the Snow (3 turns)

**GM Note**: The snowfield is unusually quiet — footsteps are absorbed by the snow. This silence is unnerving. Use the footprint system to give players clues: "There are two sets of tracks in the snow — one is very fresh."

### 2. Shadow of the Champion-tier (3-4 turns)

**GM Note**: Let players "feel" the presence of the Champion-tier — without direct contact. "A figure on a distant slope — standing still. You can see him through your sniper scope, and he is looking back at you through his. Then he turns and vanishes — not fleeing, but making a pact."

### 3. Blizzard Arrives (3 turns)

**GM Note**: The weather changes abruptly — the blizzard drops visibility to 50m. Hearing fails. This is the best time for the Champion-tier to hunt. Also give players a counter chance — set traps in the blizzard, or use the blizzard to move hidden.

### 4. Night Final Battle (4-6 turns)

**GM Note**: Night — squads without night-vision goggles can barely fight. Let the final circle be at a lit location (such as the lights inside Snow Castle), or force everyone to grope in the dark — the most desperate yet thrilling scene.

## Victory Condition

Be the last surviving squad (or defeat the Champion-tier)
