# Series A: Daybreak Protocol

## Overview

| Dimension | Description |
|------|------|
| **Positioning** | Benchmarked against D&D's tactical dungeon-crawl style — firepower and tactics in the SCP universe |
| **Core Gameplay** | Tactical combat, suppressive fire, containment assaults, squad coordination |
| **Recommended Roles** | Mobile Task Force Operative (primary), Field Agent, Security Agent, Medical Specialist |
| **Level Coverage** | Lv.1–10 (start Lv.1, endgame Lv.9–10) |
| **Scene Count** | 6 main scenes + 1 optional interlude |
| **Core Conflict** | A large-scale Chaos Insurgency infiltration of Site-19, simultaneously triggering multiple SCP containment breaches — this is not an accident, it is a planned attack |
| **Site** | Site-19 (the largest site globally, ████ containment cells) |
| **Campaign Length** | Estimated 12–18 sessions (approx. 40–60 hours) |

### Campaign Summary

At 03:47, Site-19's Hume Field monitoring array lit up with 12 red alerts at once. Three Safe-Class SCP containment cells failed within the same second, immediately followed by anomalous readings from three Euclid-Class objects. This is not a system failure — someone has taken control of the containment protocol from the inside.

A Chaos Insurgency infiltration squad, disguised as Foundation security personnel, has been moving through Site-19's corridors for at least 72 hours. Their goal is not to steal a particular SCP — they intend to collapse this site from within, to prove that the Foundation's ideal of "Containment" is a failure.

And deep in the Heavy Containment Zone, a Keter-Class SCP that was never publicly documented is waking — because the Chaos Insurgency severed its Scranton Anchor.

The players take on the role of a newly trained MTF squad (codename "Hammerbone"), living through Site-19's longest night — through combat, choices, and survival.

**Combat Strategy**: Kale never fights head-on. He uses the CI infiltration squad as cover, alters the battlefield terrain with pre-placed demolition charges, and triggers an escape protocol when severely wounded (HP < 25%). He carries a Scranton Anchor jammer fitted with anomalous components — destabilizing the Hume Field around him (all players within 30m take TN -20 on checks involving Anomalistics).

### Scene A-1: Red Alert

> **📍 Map Reference**: Site-19 overall structure recommended to reference the "Generic Site Layout" (shared with Campaign C).
> **Key Areas**: D-Class Containment Wing → Main Corridor → Security Command Center.

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.1 |
| **Location** | Site-19, D-Class Personnel Containment Wing → Main Corridor → Security Command Center |
| **Length** | 1 session (approx. 3–4 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

03:47. The player characters ("Hammerbone" squad) have just completed six weeks of training and are at Site-19 for their "shakeout stationing." They are awakened in their barracks by the alarm — not a drill.

The control center's voice sounds over the broadcast: "Code: Red. Code: Red. Site-19 is entering full Lockdown. All personnel report to designated safe zones. This is not a drill. Repeat: this is not a drill."

#### Truth Card

| Layer | Content |
|----|------|
| **Surface** (player start) | Multiple containment cells fail simultaneously. Possibly a system failure. Security channel chaotic, some communications already cut |
| **Inner** (discoverable in scene) | The failure pattern is too precise — three Safe-Class unlocked in the same second. This is not a malfunction, it is deliberate sabotage (Computers TN 55 or Anomalistics TN 50) |
| **Abyss** (revealed later) | The Chaos Insurgency has infiltrated Site-19's security team. They have at least 3 insiders,embedded for weeks |

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Wake Up and Arm (15 minutes)**

The GM guides players to describe their characters waking in the barracks to the alarm. Each player may make a brief RP action — grabbing gear, checking their communicator, comforting (or nervously ignoring) the others in the barracks.

Equipment issue: each player draws standard issue from the equipment locker — sidearm (1d8, 15 rounds), tactical vest (DR 2/2/1), communicator, and first-aid kit ×1. MTF additionally draws an assault rifle (1d10, 30 rounds).

**Phase 2 — Corridor Advance (45 minutes)**

The squad moves from the barracks area toward the main control room. Passing through the D-Class Containment Wing corridor, they encounter:

- **First Obstacle**: the corridor blast door has dropped. Engineering check d100 ≤ INT+Engineering TN 50 to manually override; or Computers TN 55 to bypass the door control system; or STR check TN 60 (three assisting) to pry open.
- **Environment**: corridor lights flicker at irregular intervals. Every 1d6 rounds, all go dark for 1 round (total darkness: visual actions automatically fail, unless equipped with flashlight/night vision).

**Phase 3 — Encounter SCP-████-A "Iron Maiden" (60 minutes)**

At the T-junction leading to the main control room, a suit of medieval plate armor blocks the passage. It is attacking two security agents trying to flee — one already down, severely wounded.

**Combat Parameters Quick Reference**:
- SCP-████-A HP 55 / Piercing DR 5 / AGI 35
- Attack: d100 ≤ 55, damage 1d10+2 Slashing
- Two NPC security agents: HP 45/15 each (one severely wounded), sidearm d100 ≤ 60

**Resolution Paths**:

| Path | Method | Key Checks |
|------|------|---------|
| **Combat** | Frontally defeat SCP-████-A | Firearms TN 75 (MTF Lv.1: AGI 40 + Firearms 35), requires approx. 6–8 hits to pierce DR |
| **Knowledge** | Identify the phrase | Linguistics d100 ≤ INT+Linguistics TN 60 → discover inscriptions are Old French; Foundation Lore d100 ≤ INT+Foundation Lore TN 55 → recall archive records the phrase; or directly attempt to guess the phrase (PRE check, TN 40, three failures trigger Berserk) |
| **Weakness** | Exploit thermal weakness | Engineering TN 55 to make an improvised incendiary (fire extinguisher + lighter → thermal cone); or find a fire hydrant/thermal equipment |
| **Stealth** | Bypass rather than fight | Stealth TN 60 (armor PER 25, but it has echo-location-like vibration sensing — requires removing shoes or moving extremely slowly) |

**Phase 4 — Arrive at Command Center (30 minutes)**

Upon entering the main control room, the players meet Director Harlow (via video — he is in a deep secure room). He briefly explains the situation:
- Three Safe-Class SCPs breached containment
- Three Euclid-Class show anomalous activity
- The main corridor blast doors were locked by "internal command" — someone is using security codes
- MTF Nu-7 is responding, but their deployment is being jammed

Harlow assigns the "Hammerbone" squad two objectives: (1) clear the corridor from the D-Class area to the Medical Wing; (2) find and escort survivors.

**Key Check Reference (Lv.1 baseline)**:
- MTF PC: Firearms TN ~75 (AGI 40+Firearms 35), Dodge TN ~55 (AGI 40+Dodge 15)
- Field Agent PC: Stealth TN ~65 (AGI 45+Stealth 20), Observation TN ~60 (PER 35+Observation 25)
- Security Agent PC: Observation TN ~65 (PER 40+Observation 25), Melee TN ~60 (STR 35+Melee 25)
- Medical PC: Medicine TN ~65 (INT 40+Medicine 25)

#### Possible Endings

1. **Efficient Pass**: Players handle Iron Maiden non-combat (phrase/weakness), saving significant ammunition and HP. Harlow impression +1.
2. **Hard-Won Pass**: Frontal combat but successful. Consumes approx. 30–40% HP and ammunition. Harlow forms a preliminary assessment of squad combat capability.
3. **Near Failure**: Iron Maiden enters Berserk Mode, squad suffers heavy losses. GM may insert MTF Nu-7's vanguard squad to intervene — but at the cost of the squad's reputation.

#### Follow-Up Connections

- Successfully clear corridor → Scene A-2 (Corridor War)
- If squad performed exceptionally (Legendary Success handling Iron Maiden) → Harlow may reveal more information, skipping an encounter
- If squad suffered heavy losses → GM inserts a brief rest scene (Medical Wing 30-minute rest, recover small amount of HP)

### Scene A-2: Corridor War

> **📍 Map Reference**: See `campaign_a_maps_zh.pdf` pages 1–2 (Site-19 main corridor system T-junction three-branch diagram).
> This scene involves the MTF squad's three-way tactical choice at the T-junction.

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.2–3 |
| **Location** | Site-19 Main Corridor System → D-Class Containment Wing → Medical Wing |
| **Length** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The "Hammerbone" squad has cleared the first corridor. But Site-19's corridor system is a maze — and the Chaos Insurgency is systematically releasing anomalies to divide the Foundation's forces. Now the players must advance down three parallel corridors, each leading to a different entrance of the Medical Wing, but each blocked by a different problem.

This is the campaign's core "tactical advance" scene — testing the players' mastery of suppressive fire, squad commands, cover use, and resource management.

#### Truth Card

| Layer | Content |
|----|------|
| **Surface** | CI is releasing SCPs to divert attention — their real target is the deep containment zone |
| **Inner** | CI infiltrators disguised as Foundation security. They know all security protocols and codes (Observation TN 65 to spot that the "security" personnel's gear is worn contrary to Foundation standards) |
| **Abyss** | The person commanding this infiltration knows Vasquez — he came for her |

#### Scene Flow

**Encounter 2A — East Corridor "Fire Suppression Line" (90 minutes)**

Three CI infiltrators (disguised as security) have set up a makeshift cover at the end of the corridor and block the passage with a light machine gun.

**Enemy Data**:
- CI Infiltrator ×3: HP 48 each / tactical vest DR 2/2/1 / assault rifle d100 ≤ 62 (AGI 38+Firearms 24), damage 1d10 Piercing
- Light MG emplacement: d100 ≤ 55 (fixed mount ADV), damage 2d8 Piercing, 100-round belt. Sustained suppressive fire (see rulebook §3.5)

**Cover Layout**:
```
[CI Cover] ← 15m → [Overturned Cart/Half Cover TN -20] ← 10m → [Pillar/Partial Cover TN -10] ← 10m → [Player Start]
```

**Resolution Paths**:

| Path | Method | Key |
|------|------|------|
| **Suppressive Fire** | Players use suppressive fire to cover the CI emplacement (d100 ≤ firing skill), then alternate advance | At least 2 hold automatic weapons; squad command "Suppress and Advance" |
| **Demolitions** | Use the fire-suppression pipes above the corridor — Demolitions TN 55 to detonate pipes, flooding CI position (MG malfunction + CI movement impeded) | Requires Demolitions skill or alternative explosive source |
| **Flank** | Field/Stealth character circles behind CI via adjacent ventilation duct (Stealth TN 60, Engineering TN 50 to remove vent grille) | If discovered, caught in crossfire |
| **Negotiation** | After identifying CI disguise, shout via loudspeaker. Deception TN 55 to pretend to be CI high-command support, or Interrogation TN 60 to get password from captured CI | Requires capturing a CI first |

**Tactical Teaching Moment**: The GM should demonstrate suppressive fire rules, squad commands (focus fire / alternating cover), and the cover system here. This is the players' "tutorial boss" for learning core tactical combat mechanics.

**Encounter 2B — South Corridor "Phase Hunt" (75 minutes)**

This corridor looks empty — but SCP-████-B has escaped containment and is moving through the walls. Players must deal with it while advancing.

**Innovative Mechanic — Chase Encounter**: This is not a stand-and-fight battle. SCP-████-B moves through the walls, paralleling the players at 10m/round. It appears to attack at random moments, then re-phases. Players must respond while moving.

Key check chain:
1. Observation TN 60 → spot crystal glint in wall (1-round advance warning)
2. Phase Weaver anchors → attack window opens
3. Focus-fire command → all ADV on marked target
4. Phase Web → Dodge TN 55 or be restrained
5. Medical → First Aid TN 55 for poisoned character

**Optional Element**: Midway down the corridor, the players find a wounded D-Class — "Rusty" (D-4815). He hides in a ventilation duct, armed with a homemade shiv. He provides key intel: he recognized the face of one of the CI infiltrators — that person's real name is Jensen, a CI peripheral weapons dealer.

**Encounter 2C — Medical Wing Entrance "Decision Point" (45 minutes)**

The Medical Wing entrance door is locked from the inside. Screams and gunfire come from within. Two methods:

1. **Break Door**: Demolitions TN 50 or STR (three assisting) TN 55 to break down — but the situation beyond is unknown (possibly an ambush)
2. **Detour**: Enter via adjacent maintenance passage (longer, but safer — Stealth TN 50)

The truth beyond the door: three medical staff and five wounded security are being coerced by a CI infiltrator. The infiltrator demands "hand over the complete file on SCP-████-B" — he thinks the Medical Wing has a backup database. In reality this is a dead end — CI intelligence was wrong.

**Decision Point**: If players break in forcefully, the infiltrator uses a medical staff member as a human shield (-20 attack TN to avoid hitting the hostage). If players negotiate, the infiltrator demands safe passage — he releases the hostages but takes the database terminal.

#### Key Check and TN Reference (Lv.2–3 baseline)

| Check | TN | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| MTF Firearms (suppressive fire) | ~75 | AGI 42+Firearms 33 |
| Field Stealth (vent) | ~68 | AGI 46+Stealth 22 |
| Observation (spot CI disguise) | ~65 | PER 36+Observation 29 |
| Squad Command (focus fire) | ~60 | PRE 30+Leadership 30 |
| First Aid (poison treatment) | ~62 | INT 38+Medicine 24 |

#### Possible Endings

1. **Tactical Mastery**: All three corridors cleared efficiently, at least one CI captured, Rusty and Medical Wing hostages rescued. Squad gains +2 Morale, +200 bonus XP.
2. **Tactical Pass**: Objectives met but with losses. One or more PCs enter moderate-wound range. Rusty's assistance depends on whether he was rescued.
3. **Strategic Retreat**: One or more corridors abandoned. Medical Wing hostages partially dead. Director Harlow says "understood" but his tone betrays disappointment.

#### Follow-Up Connections

- Rusty rescued → gains CI infiltrator identification info in Scene A-3
- CI captured → gains interrogation opportunity in Scene A-3
- Medical Wing secure → provides better medical support in later scenes (more HP recovery)

### Scene A-3: Breaking Point

> **📍 Map Reference**: Site-19 Security Center (second floor) + deep elevator shaft.

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.4–5 |
| **Location** | Site-19 Security Center → Deep Elevator → (branch) |
| **Length** | 2–3 sessions (approx. 8–10 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This is the campaign's "information turning point." The players discover clues to the Chaos Insurgency's true objective, Vasquez's secret is threatened, and — a key moral choice: who to trust?

The squad successfully clears the path to the Security Center. Here, they can access full surveillance records, interrogate the captured CI infiltrator, and decide on next steps.

Meanwhile, Kale (CI commander) speaks to the entire site for the first time via the hijacked broadcast system — he says Vasquez's full name.

#### Truth Card

| Layer | Content |
|----|------|
| **Surface** | Kale publicly accuses Vasquez of having been a CI member. Vasquez does not deny it |
| **Inner** (discovered after investigation) | Vasquez was a double agent the Foundation planted inside CI. Her "defection" was authorized. But the authorization record is CL.5 — only O5 can see it (Computers TN 65 + overclearance modifier CL -40 = TN 25, Very Hard) |
| **Abyss** | Harlow's experiment — his testing of SCP-████-C energy utilization — is the indirect cause of this attack. CI used the Hume fluctuations from the experiment to find the exact positions of the anchors they needed to sever |

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Information Gathering (90 minutes)**

The Security Center has multiple interactive intelligence sources:

| Source | Check | Reveals |
|--------|------|---------|
| Surveillance replay | Computers TN 55 | CI infiltrators' movement trails over the past 72 hours, including footage of them approaching the Scranton Anchors |
| CI infiltrator interrogation | Interrogation TN 60 (or Deception TN 55 pretending to be CI high command) | CI's communication frequencies and part of the action plan. "Kale said to 'let everyone see the truth'" |
| Vasquez's personal terminal | Computers TN 60 (overclearance CL.3→4: -20 = TN 40) or persuade Vasquez to show voluntarily | Her CI-era operation codename "Copperhead," a deleted-but-recoverable mission authorization — from O5-7 |
| Rusty's assistance (if rescued) | Automatic | Identifies the true identities of three infiltrators, all linked to CI's European division |
| Harlow's comms log | Computers TN 65 | Discovers he authorized a "Hume Field experiment" three months ago, whose data aligns with the CI attack timeline |

**Phase 2 — Confrontation with Vasquez (60 minutes)**

Kale's broadcast triggers a chain reaction. Other Foundation personnel in the Security Center begin to cast suspicious glances at Vasquez. A tense scene unfolds:

- A young security agent (NPC) publicly challenges Vasquez, demanding an explanation
- Harlow orders over comms to "temporarily relieve Vasquez of command"
- Players must decide: obey Harlow's order? Support Vasquez? Or stay neutral?

**Key Line — Vasquez to players**:
> *"Yes, I spent three years in CI. As a Foundation undercover agent. My mission was to infiltrate their command structure and acquire intel on the '███ Project.' I did — at the cost of watching people I knew die. On both sides. If you want to relieve me of command, do it now. But after that — I'm going to the Heavy Containment Zone, to stop Kale from blowing up this site. Whether or not you follow."*

**Truth-Reveal Checks**:
- Trust Vasquez and help prove her innocence: Computers TN 65 (overclearance CL.3→CL.5 to access O5 files: -40 = TN 25, Very Hard) — but if successful, find partially decrypted files of "Operation Copperhead," confirming her account
- Or: intuition/read people — PER+Observation TN 60 opposed by her Deception (PRE+Deception TN 55), to judge whether she is lying

**Phase 3 — Route Selection (30 minutes)**

Players gain intel on two action routes and must choose their main thrust:

| Route | Objective | Risk | Reward |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Deep Elevator → Heavy Containment Zone** | Repair Scranton Anchors, stop SCP-████-C from waking | Directly face Kale's main force, SCP-████-B, possible Keter threat | Resolve the problem at its root |
| **Vent System → CI Command Center** | Capture/kill Kale, paralyze CI command chain | Narrow spaces, ambush risk, longer route | Leaderless, CI retreats |

The squad must make a choice — or split up (high risk, high reward).

#### Key Check and TN Reference (Lv.4–5 baseline)

| Check | TN | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| MTF Computers (surveillance) | ~68 | INT 36+Computers 32 |
| Field Interrogation | ~62 | PRE 32+Interrogation 30 |
| Computers (Harlow log) | ~72 | INT 40+Computers 32 |
| Read People (Vasquez) | ~68 | PER 38+Observation 30 |
| Overclearance (O5 files) | ~30 | INT+Computers-40 (Very Hard) |

#### Possible Endings

1. **Trust Vasquez + Find Evidence**: Squad stays united. Vasquez's effect as mentor doubles — in later scenes, she provides extra tactical advice (equivalent to ADV on one key check hint).
2. **Trust Vasquez but No Evidence**: Squad follows her, but internal cracks exist. In Scene A-5, certain NPCs may disobey her commands.
3. **Distrust Vasquez**: Squad chooses independent action or follows Harlow's orders. Vasquez goes to the Heavy Containment Zone alone — in Scene A-5, her survival depends on the players' later choices.
4. **Discover Harlow's Responsibility**: Players may choose to confront Harlow directly here. This leads to a shift in the political landscape in Scene A-6.

#### Follow-Up Connections

- Choose route → Scene A-4 (Heavy Containment) or Scene A-X (The Rats' Path → ultimately also reaches A-4, but via a different path)
- Vasquez's loyalty status affects Scene A-5

### Scene A-4: Heavy Containment

> **📍 Map Reference**: See `campaign_a_maps_zh.pdf` pages 3–5 (Site-19 underground five-level vertical layout L12–L18).

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.5–6 |
| **Location** | Site-19 Heavy Containment Zone (underground levels 12–18) |
| **Length** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The Heavy Containment Zone — Site-19's deepest, most secure area. Three Scranton Anchors arranged in a triangle stabilize SCP-████-C. But two anchors have been sabotaged by CI, and the third is failing.

This is the campaign's most challenging tactical map-push scene: multi-level, multi-path, friend-or-foe indistinguishable, resources tight.

#### Truth Card

| Layer | Content |
|----|------|
| **Surface** | Two anchors physically destroyed (demolitions), third anchor's control panel locked. Need to repair at least two anchors to maintain Hume Field |
| **Inner** | CI installed a "reverse protocol" on the third anchor — any standard reboot attempt accelerates Hume Field consumption. Must crack the protocol first (Cryptography TN 60) |
| **Abyss** | SCP-████-C itself is not entirely passive. It is "waiting" — and becomes active when the Hume Field drops below a certain threshold. Clues in Harlow's experiment log: the crystal produces "resonance" at certain frequencies |

#### Scene Flow

**Map Levels**:

```
Level 12 (Elevator Lobby) —— CI Defense Position
  ↕ Stairs / Elevator Shaft
Level 14 (Maintenance Corridor) —— occupied by SCP-████-B
  ↕
Level 16 (Anchor Control Rooms A/B/C) —— three anchors, three independent encounters
  ↕
Level 18 (Heart of the Void Containment Cell) —— SCP-████-C
```

**Level 12 — Elevator Lobby Breakthrough (60 minutes)**

CI has established a defensive position in the elevator lobby — six infiltrators, one heavy machine gun, portable cover. This is the ultimate test of the squad's tactical ability.

**Enemy Data**:
- CI Infiltrator ×6: HP 48 / assault rifle d100 ≤ 62 / tactical vest DR 2/2/1
- CI Heavy Weapons Gunner ×1: HP 55 / light MG d100 ≤ 58 (AGI 34+Heavy Weapons 24), damage 2d8 / body armor DR 3/2/2
- CI Engineer ×1: HP 42 / sidearm d100 ≤ 52 / setting up C4 trap

**Environmental Elements**:
- Four concrete pillars in the elevator lobby (full cover)
- CI behind some cover (overturned equipment crates, TN -10)
- Ceiling fire-sprinkler system can be activated (Computers TN 50) — creating a "wet" environment, CI electronics DIS

**Resolution Paths**:

| Path | Method |
|------|------|
| **Frontal Breakthrough** | Suppressive fire + alternating advance + focus fire. MVP skills: Suppressive Fire (MTF), Squad Command (Leadership) |
| **Tech Hack** | Hack elevator control system (Computers TN 60), stop elevator at Level 14, squad circles behind via maintenance corridor. Or activate sprinklers + cut CI comms |
| **Psychological Warfare** | Broadcast (fake) Foundation reinforcements-arrival message via loudspeaker — Deception TN 55 to make CI partially retreat or waver. Or use CI comms code obtained in Scene A-3 to send fake orders |
| **Demolitions** | Set directional charge on ceiling (Demolitions TN 55), dropping concrete debris on CI position — non-lethal but causes chaos (all DIS 2 rounds) |

**Level 14 — Maintenance Corridor (45 minutes)**

SCP-████-B occupies this area. CI's Phase Stabilizer runs here — they are trying to "tame" it. The stabilizer emits a high-pitched hum that sets your teeth on edge.

**Special Rule**: Due to the Phase Stabilizer, SCP-████-B is always in high phase (can be attacked), but attack frequency doubles (2 Phase Strikes per round).

**Extra Choice**: Players may destroy the Phase Stabilizer (direct attack, HP 20, DR 2) — this returns SCP-████-B to normal behavior (50% chance low phase), easier to dodge but harder to predict.

**Level 16 — Anchor Control Room (120 minutes)**

Three control rooms, three challenges:

**Anchor A (fully destroyed)**:
- Requires complete replacement of core components
- Engineering TN 60 (standard) + Cryptography TN 55 (recalibrate) + requires spare parts (borrowed from Anchor B or C, or using CI engineer's leftover toolkit)
- Also needs someone on watch (every 5 rounds, CI reinforcements may arrive — d100 ≤ 40)

**Anchor B (partially destroyed)**:
- Control panel locked, with CI reverse protocol
- Cryptography TN 60 to crack protocol → then Engineering TN 50 to reboot
- If Cryptography fails: attempt forced reboot (Engineering TN 70, Very Hard — failure triggers reverse protocol, Hume Field -20)

**Anchor C (running but failing)**:
- Still running but output declining (Hume -5/10 min)
- Can "overload" Anchor C (Engineering TN 55) to double output × 1d6 rounds — risk is the anchor may burn out
- Or directly maintain (Engineering TN 45) to keep current output stable

**Hume Field Tracking**: The GM should use a public or hidden Hume counter. Starting Hume = 65.

| Action | Hume Change |
|------|-----------|
| Every 10 minutes (approx. 30 min real play) | -5 |
| Anchor A repaired | +20 (one-time) |
| Anchor B repaired | +15 (one-time) |
| Anchor C overload success | +10 (one-time, then -15) |
| CI destroys new anchor | -25 |
| Hume drops to 40 → Final Whisper activates |
| Hume drops to 20 → ZK Scenario |

#### Key Check and TN Reference (Lv.5–6 baseline)

| Check | TN | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| MTF Firearms (heavy breakthrough) | ~80 | AGI 44+Firearms 36 |
| Engineering (anchor repair) | ~68 | INT 40+Engineering 28 |
| Cryptography (reverse protocol) | ~62 | INT 38+Cryptography 24 |
| Computers (elevator override) | ~65 | INT 38+Computers 27 |
| Demolitions (ceiling directional) | ~60 | INT 36+Demolitions 24 |

#### Possible Endings

1. **All Three Anchors Repaired**: Hume Field stabilizes and recovers to 85+. SCP-████-C returns to dormant state. The door to Level 18 can be safely opened.
2. **Two Anchors Repaired**: Hume Field stabilizes at 50–65. Enough to prevent immediate collapse, but Final Whisper already activated — all characters entering Level 18 must continuously deal with the cognitohazard.
3. **Only One Anchor Repaired or All Failed**: Hume Field drops below 40. Final Whisper at full power. Scene A-5 becomes "emergency evacuation" rather than "frontal containment."

#### Follow-Up Connections

- Anchors repaired ≥ 2 → Scene A-5: directly face SCP-████-C + Kale
- Anchors repaired < 2 → Scene A-5 becomes "survival mode" — evacuate before total collapse and activate Protocol 42-Solomon

### Scene A-5: Heart of the Void

> **📍 Map Reference**: See `campaign_a_maps_zh.pdf` pages 6–7 (80m spherical containment chamber top-down view + Scranton Anchor array + breach routes).

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.7–8 |
| **Location** | Site-19 Deep Level — Heart of the Void Containment Cell (80m-diameter spherical chamber) |
| **Length** | 2–3 sessions (approx. 8–12 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This is the campaign's climax. The players enter the Heart of the Void's containment cell — a giant spherical chamber, the black crystal suspended at center, surrounded by a complex Scranton Anchor array. Kale and his elite guard are already waiting.

Kale's plan has entered its final stage: he does not intend to destroy SCP-████-C — he intends to turn it into a gun. By focusing the Heart of the Void's reality erosion into a beam, he plans to hit the O5 Council's location during their emergency meeting.

#### Truth Card

| Layer | Content |
|----|------|
| **Surface** | Kale is inside the containment cell. He has a "Phase Focusing Array" — a device modified with technology CI extracted from SCP-████-B research |
| **Inner** (Science/Anomalistics reveal) | SCP-████-C is not pure destructive force. It is — in a sense — "digesting" reality. The reality it consumes does not vanish, but is converted into a guidable form of energy. Kale's focusing array is theoretically feasible |
| **Abyss** | There is "something" in the crystal — not a consciousness, but echoes of fragmented multiple consciousnesses. Each is the last trace of a reality swallowed by the Heart of the Void. If players attempt to communicate with it (see Negotiation Path), the voices they hear are multiple, broken — yet at the same time filled with a strange, non-malicious quality. It simply... is eating. It does not know (or care) what its eating means |

#### Scene Flow

**Stage Setup**

The containment cell is a giant spherical space, approx. 80m in diameter. SCP-████-C floats at center, surrounded by three Scranton Anchors (at least two of which should have been repaired by the players). Multiple metal walkways extend from the walls to the central platform. The walkways have no rails — fall = 20m drop (3d6 Bludgeoning damage + critical wound check).

**Environmental Effects** (based on Scene A-4 Hume Field state):
- Hume ≥ 50: all physical checks DIS; every 5 rounds d100 ≤ SAN-10 or SAN -1d6
- Hume ≥ 30: above + every 3 rounds Void Rift auto-spawns 1 Void Shard
- Hume < 30: above + every round Final Whisper (d100 ≤ SAN-20, SAN -3d8)

**Phase 1 — Confrontation (30 minutes)**

Kale stands on the central platform, his elite guard (4 CI elites) beside him. In his hand he holds a remote detonator — he has placed backup explosives on the three Scranton Anchors.

**Kale's Opening Line**:
> *"You finally made it. Where's Vasquez? She didn't come? What a pity. I thought she'd want to see for herself — the consequences of her choices. You know why she left CI? Not because we were wrong. But because she couldn't face the fact that we were right."*

Vasquez's response (if still alive and in squad):
> *"I left because you started enjoying it, Kale. Anomalies aren't weapons — they're prisoners. We both used to believe that."*

**Phase 2 — Multi-Layer Combat (90–120 minutes)**

This encounter has three simultaneous layers:

**Layer A: Combat (vs CI)**
- Kale (commander data see NPC profile) + 4 CI elites (HP 55, Firearms TN 68, tactical vest DR 3/2/2 with ceramic plates)
- Kale's tactic: keep distance, use walkways as maneuver space, activate explosives to divert attention
- If Kale HP < 30% → he activates the Phase Focusing Array prototype (gives himself 3 rounds ADV on Dodge, but -1d6 HP per round)

**Layer B: Containment (stabilize crystal)**
- Explosives on the three anchors must be defused (Demolitions TN 60 each, or Engineering TN 55 physical removal)
- Void Shards keep spawning — need someone dedicated to handling them ("clear the mobs" role)
- Optional: exploit Harlow's experiment data — if players obtained it, they can attempt to "tune" the anchor frequencies (Anomalistics TN 65), halving the crystal's Hume consumption rate

**Layer C: Communication (surprise option)**
- If the researcher/high-INT character in the squad tries to study rather than attack the crystal: Anomalistics TN 60 or Science TN 65 → discover the crystal has a "response mode" at specific frequencies
- By adjusting the Scranton Anchors' Hume output frequency (Engineering+Anomalistics joint check TN 55), players can attempt to communicate with the consciousness fragments in the crystal
- Communication is not a conversation — more like receiving fragmented images and emotions. Content is improvised by the GM, but the core message is: (1) the crystal doesn't want to hurt anyone, (2) it doesn't even truly understand what "hurt" means, (3) it is very lonely, (4) it wants to go home — back to where it came from
- If players successfully convey the intent "you must stop consuming the Hume Field": PRE+Persuasion TN 45 (non-verbal communication, difficulty downgraded) → crystal temporarily lowers consumption rate (Hume consumption drops to -5 per 30 minutes)

**Phase 3 — Endgame (30 minutes)**

When Kale's HP drops to 0 or most CI elites are defeated, the endgame triggers:

Kale activates his final chip — the Phase Focusing Array aimed at the Heart of the Void. He laughs maniacally:

> *"If I can't prove CI was right — then I'll prove the Foundation was wrong. Everything you protect — will see the truth."*

The focusing array begins emitting pulses — dealing damage to the crystal each round (direct HP attack), while the crystal's Hume consumption rate spikes (Hume -10 per round).

**Endgame Choices**:

| Choice | Method | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| **Kill Kale** | Focus-fire attack (he cannot Dodge now — focused on maintaining the array) | He may complete the focusing before dying — crystal HP drops to 50% |
| **Destroy Array** | Attack the device (HP 30, DR 5), or Demolitions TN 55 to overload | Kale turns to full attack on players |
| **Redeem Kale** | PRE+Persuasion TN 55 (requires Vasquez to speak with him first, evoking his former belief) | Kale drops his weapon, but CI elites may not |
| **Cooperate with Crystal** | If players have established communication → crystal actively absorbs the array's energy beam (Hume consumption instead falls) | Crystal becomes more "active" — may become a more complex entity in future |
| **Sacrifice** | A character manually overloads a Scranton Anchor, resonating with its own Hume Field frequency — character's SAN permanently -30 and gains "Reality-Sensitive" mark | Crystal immediately dormant, all anomalous effects cease |

#### Key Check and TN Reference (Lv.7–8 baseline)

| Check | TN | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| MTF Firearms (Kale combat) | ~82 | AGI 46+Firearms 36 |
| Demolitions (defuse explosives) | ~65 | INT 38+Demolitions 27 |
| Anomalistics (crystal analysis) | ~70 | INT 42+Anomalistics 28 |
| Persuasion (redeem Kale) | ~62 | PRE 34+Persuasion 28 |
| Resist (Final Whisper) | ~58 | WIL 38+Resist 20 |

#### Possible Endings

1. **Perfect Containment**: Crystal stabilized + Kale captured/redeemed + all anchors repaired. O5 impressed. Squad earns "Defender of Site-19" honor.
2. **Costly Containment**: Crystal stabilized but at heavy cost — one PC or key NPC sacrificed / permanently impaired.
3. **Pyrrhic Victory**: Crystal destroyed (rather than contained). Foundation loses a potentially priceless research resource. O5 disappointed but acknowledges it was necessary.
4. **Retreat Protocol**: Squad cannot stabilize the crystal, initiates emergency evacuation. Site-19 partially abandoned, SCP-████-C sealed in the collapsed deep-level area — but this only postpones the problem.

### Scene A-6: Settling the Cost

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.9–10 |
| **Location** | Site-19 Conference Room → O5 Virtual Hearing → Each Character's Ending |
| **Length** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The incident is over. Site-19's corridors still smoke, but containment has been restored. Now — the reckoning begins.

The O5 Council has convened a closed-door hearing to review where responsibility lies. Harlow's secret experiment, Vasquez's CI background, Kale's confession (if alive), and all the players' key decisions — all will be laid on the table.

This is not a battle. It is a narrative trial — the players' Persuasion, evidence presentation, and moral stance will determine the ending.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Prepare Testimony (45 minutes)**

Players have time to review the incident and decide what they will say. Each key decision is on record:

- Scene A-1: whether Iron Maiden was handled non-combat (showing wisdom vs pure force)
- Scene A-2: whether Rusty and the Medical Wing hostages were rescued
- Scene A-3: whether Vasquez was trusted, whether Harlow's secret was discovered
- Scene A-4: Hume Field state, how many anchors repaired
- Scene A-5: Kale's fate, the crystal's fate, whether any character sacrificed

**Phase 2 — O5 Hearing (60–90 minutes)**

The GM plays the O5 Council (via video screen — O5 members never appear in person, only voice and silhouette). The hearing proceeds in this order:

1. **Harlow testifies**: He admits the experiment, but argues it was "for better containment"
2. **Vasquez testifies**: Her CI background is formally discussed. O5-7 intervenes and decrypts "Operation Copperhead" files (if players failed to do so earlier)
3. **Rusty testifies**: If alive, he provides testimony as an "outside perspective" — his statement is unexpectedly powerful
4. **Players testify**: Each player may make one statement (PRE+Persuasion or PRE+Leadership check, TN 60)

**Phase 3 — Verdict and Ending (30 minutes)**

O5 renders the verdict. Result depends on:

| Factor | Influence |
|------|------|
| Whether SCP-████-C was successfully contained | Biggest bonus |
| Whether Kale was taken alive | + provides intelligence value |
| Whether Harlow's experiment was exposed | Deduction (Foundation embarrassment), but players exposing it → shows integrity |
| Whether Vasquez was supported | Affects future of MTF Nu-7 |
| Handling of D-Class Personnel | Quality of Rusty's testimony affects D-Class reform discussion |
| Civilian/low-rank casualties | Lower is better |

#### Ending Branches

**Ending A: "New Dawn"**
- Condition: Crystal successfully contained + Kale taken alive + Harlow's responsibility exposed + Vasquez's innocence proven
- O5 transfers Harlow out, appoints Vasquez as Site-19 Acting Director (or recommends a reformist director)
- Players receive "O5 Commendation" — CL promotion +1 (cap Lv.4)
- Rusty granted C-Class personnel status
- Epilogue: Site-19 begins implementing containment-protocol reforms

**Ending B: "Stability Is Victory"**
- Condition: Crystal contained (any method), but gray areas exist
- O5 maintains status quo — Harlow stays but under stricter supervision, Vasquez continues as MTF commander
- Players receive standard commendation — XP +500, no CL promotion
- Epilogue: everything returns to normal... but what does "normal" mean at the Foundation?

**Ending C: "Burning Lesson"**
- Condition: Crystal destroyed or escaped, major losses
- O5 launches full investigation. Harlow dismissed and possibly subjected to Amnestics. Vasquez suspended pending review
- Player squad disbanded, members assigned to different sites — but each is marked "not fully trustworthy"
- Epilogue: a helicopter flies the players out of Site-19. No one speaks. Below them, on the ruins of the Heavy Containment Zone, construction crews have already begun work

**Ending D (Hidden): "Copperhead's Children"**
- Condition: Kale redeemed + crystal communicated with (rather than forcibly contained) + Vasquez alive
- O5-7 privately contacts the players — you demonstrated a different way of containing. Perhaps the Foundation needs more people "who can communicate with non-human entities"
- Players invited to join a brand-new project: "SCP Communication Initiative" — an experimental program attempting dialogue with sentient SCPs rather than mere containment
- Vasquez and (redeemed) Kale become advisors to the project
- Epilogue: a black helicopter lands on Site-19's helipad. O5-7's voice comes over the communicator: "Come to Area-██. We need to talk — about the future."

### Optional Scene A-X: The Rats' Path

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Level** | Lv.3–5 (branch scene) |
| **Location** | Site-19 D-Class Containment Wing depths → abandoned maintenance tunnels → CI logistics outpost |
| **Length** | 1 session (approx. 4 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This optional scene appears in Scene A-3's branch choice — if players choose the "Vent System → CI Command Center" route. It uses Rusty (or another D-Class NPC) as guide, leading players through Site-19's "underworld" — passages secretly known to D-Class personnel, abandoned areas, and unofficial shortcuts.

This is a stealth infiltration scene, not a frontal combat one.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — "The Rats' Corridor" (45 minutes)**

Rusty leads the players into a set of abandoned maintenance tunnels deep in the D-Class Containment Wing. These tunnels are not on the official map — they were forgotten during the 1970s expansion.

Rusty explains:
> *"D-Class call these 'rat runs.' Every old site has them. The directors don't know — or don't want to know. Here you learn one thing: the Foundation is built on its own mistakes. Under every layer of containment protocol, there's a layer it tries to forget."*

**Environment**: Tunnels dark, narrow (single-file passage, cannot deploy combat formation), damp. Walls bear marks carved by former D-Class — some are counts (days survived), some names, others blurred by time beyond recognition.

**Phase 2 — "The Ghost" (60 minutes)**

In the tunnels, the players discover an abandoned small containment cell. The label on the door is rusted, but a Computers TN 50 check (portable terminal scan) reveals it once contained a Safe-Class anomaly — SCP-████-D, reclassified as "Neutralized" and removed in 1983.

But the file says "Neutralized"... yet the cell bears fresh traces. Someone was here recently.

**Discovery**: This is a CI infiltrator's hideout. Rusty recognizes his belongings — a contact he knew during his CI days. This person should now be at the CI logistics outpost. This provides key intel: CI has a hidden outpost in the tunnel system.

**Phase 3 — CI Logistics Outpost Infiltration (90 minutes)**

CI has set up a small outpost in an abandoned 1970s maintenance workshop. Here are three CI logistics personnel (not combat elites — HP 35, sidearm d100 ≤ 50) and a large amount of equipment.

**Objectives**:
- Obtain CI's comms log (Computers TN 55)
- Find CI's complete personnel list at Site-19 (with cross-reference table of all disguised identities)
- Optional: steal CI's Phase Stabilizer prototype (makes SCP-████-B easier to handle in Scene A-4)

**Resolution Paths**:

| Path | Method |
|------|------|
| **Stealth** | Whole squad Stealth TN 60 to infiltrate, obtain intel then silently withdraw |
| **Disguise** | Rusty pretends to be a CI courier (Deception TN 55), players hide and support from shadows |
| **Raid** | Quick combat — exploit narrow-space advantage (CI cannot deploy simultaneously) |
| **Tech** | Remotely hack CI's portable server (Computers TN 65) without entering the outpost |

#### Follow-Up Connections

- Successful infiltration → obtain CI complete personnel list → in Scene A-4, players can identify and bypass certain CI defenses (knowing their shift-change times)
- Obtain Phase Stabilizer → SCP-████-B returns to normal behavior mode in Scene A-4 (easier)
- Comms log → exposes Kale's communications with CI high command — there is an "external support" plan to receive them after a successful attack. In Scene A-5, this can be used to shake CI elites' morale
