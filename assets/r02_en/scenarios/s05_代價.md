# Series D: The Cost (The Price)

## Overview

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Positioning** | The SCP core philosophy — "Are we the monsters?" — the ultimate test of moral dilemma |
| **Gameplay Focus** | Moral dilemma, cost choices, Humanity/Morality tracking, D-Class perspective, dialogue with non-human entities |
| **Recommended Roles** | Mixed squad (must include at least 1 D-Class PC). Recommended: MTF + Researcher + D-Class combination |
| **Level Coverage** | Lv.1–10 (start Lv.1, endgame Lv.9–10) |
| **Scene Count** | 8 scenes |
| **Core Conflict** | A Thaumiel-class SCP — a conscious entity the Foundation has imprisoned for 50 years to contain other anomalies — demands to be released. Containing it means continuing to torture an innocent consciousness. Releasing it means possibly losing a critical containment tool, causing thousands of deaths. The D-Class PC finds their own voice amid all this |
| **Site** | Area-14, 900m below the Nevada Test Site — a Keter-class concentrated containment facility. The most secure prison on Earth |
| **Campaign Length** | Estimated 16–24 sessions (approx. 50–80 hours) |

### Campaign Summary

At the very bottom of Area-14, in a containment cell codenamed "Abyssal Throne," there is an SCP the Foundation has never publicly acknowledged.

It has no numerical designation (at least no public one). In O5 internal communications, it is called **"the Warden."** It is Thaumiel-class — using anomalies to contain anomalies. For fifty years it has, unconsciously (as the Foundation believed), maintained the containment of a dozen Keter-class SCPs — its Hume Field acting as a living Scranton Anchor, stabilizing the entire core containment zone of Area-14.

But the "Warden" is not a machine. It is a conscious being. Fifty years ago it was a person — or something close to a person. No one ever asked whether it was willing to become the "Warden." The Foundation simply… used it.

Now — it has awakened. Not from sleep — but from the consciousness suppression the Foundation forced upon it. For the first time in fifty years it can think, feel, and speak.

Its first words — translated into text via Area-14's Hume monitoring array — were:

*"Please let me die. If not — please let me be free. If that too is impossible — please tell me why."*

The players are dispatched to Area-14. Their official mission: assess the "Warden's" condition and "maintain containment integrity." Their unofficial mission — depending on each one's conscience — may be something entirely different.

## NPC Profiles

### 1. "The Warden" — Conscious Thaumiel-Class SCP

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Identity** | Foundation internal codename "the Warden," no SCP designation (CL.5 classification). Fifty years ago the Foundation "converted" it into a living containment tool. Before conversion — they were a person named **Aria**, a member of an anomalous community that no longer exists, dissolved by the Foundation in 1975 |
| **Clearance Level** | None — they are the contained object (even though they are also used to contain other objects) |
| **Attribute Summary** | STR — (formless) / AGI — / END — / INT 62 / PER 55 / WIL 85 / PRE 65 |
| **Key Skills** | Anomalistics (through experience) 80, Occult 70, Persuasion 60 |
| **Status** | Non-physical entity. Exists within the Hume Field — their consciousness dispersed throughout the Hume Field structure of Area-14's entire core containment zone. They cannot move, cannot touch, cannot be physically harmed — but they can feel |
| **Personality** | Not a monster. Not a saint. A consciousness imprisoned for 50 years. They went through anger (first 10 years), despair (next 20 years), numbness (another 10 years) — and now, over the past 10 years, a strange calm. They bear no hostility toward the players — only a weary, almost gentle curiosity. It has been a very, very long time since they spoke with another person |
| **Secret** | The "Warden" is not merely Area-14's containment tool — they *are* Area-14. The entire core containment zone's Hume Field structure was built upon their consciousness. If they are "released" — not physically released (they have no body), but their consciousness is "disconnected" from the containment function — the entire core containment zone loses stability. A dozen Keter-class SCPs could breach containment within minutes |
| **Desire** | Freedom — or death. They are unsure which is preferable. But they are certain: this cannot continue |
| **Fear** | Being forgotten — being reduced to a machine, with no one remembering they were once a person. And a deeper fear: perhaps 50 years of imprisonment have changed them, so that they are no longer a "person." Perhaps the Foundation was right — perhaps they are just a machine |
| **Signature Lines (multiple — their dialogue with the players is the core of the campaign)** | *"You ask whether I am in pain. Pain is a matter of the body. I have no body. But I have — memories. I remember the feeling of wind on skin. I remember hunger. I remember anger. Every morning I wake — if this can be called waking — I try to recall those feelings. Then I realize it has been 50 years. 50 years without wind. 50 years without hunger. 50 years — without another person truly looking at me. Except as a tool."* |

| (Another passage) | *"I understand your predicament. Truly. If I am released — the things I contain will also be released. I know them. Some are pure destruction. But let me ask you: if the cost is torturing a conscious being — does the word 'containment' still apply? Or is it merely a cleaner word — in place of 'sacrifice'?"* |

**Narrative Role**: The core of the entire campaign. The players first learn of their existence in D-1, begin dialogue with them in D-2, and gradually understand their situation from D-3 through D-6 — making the final decision in D-7. The "Warden" is not a puzzle to be solved — it is a person to be faced.

### 2. O5-2

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Identity** | Second member of the O5 Council. "Owner" of Area-14 — they personally supervised the creation of the "Warden" in 1975 |
| **Clearance Level** | CL.5 |
| **Attribute Summary** | STR 30 / AGI 30 / END 35 / INT 65 / PER 60 / WIL 70 / PRE 62 |
| **Key Skills** | Leadership 85, Persuasion 78, Deception 72, Foundation Lore 80 |
| **HP** | 35 |
| **Personality** | Calm, precise, emotionless. O5-2 is not cruel — they are efficient. They view the "Warden" as a necessary sacrifice: the ongoing suffering of one person (if that can be called a person) in exchange for the safety of thousands. To them, this is the correct arithmetic |
| **Secret** | O5-2 knew Aria in 1975 — before Aria was "converted." They personally negotiated with Aria, promising that if Aria cooperated, their community members would be safely relocated. Aria agreed. O5-2 did not keep the promise — the community was forcibly dissolved, its members scattered across sites or given amnestics. O5-2 considers this "necessary — to avoid memetic spread." This is their only lie — and they have carried it for 50 years |
| **Desire** | Maintain stability. To O5-2, stability is the highest virtue |
| **Fear** | Being proven wrong. If the "Warden's" suffering is unacceptable — then every decision O5-2 has made over the past 50 years is unacceptable. They cannot afford that conclusion |
| **Signature Line** | *"You think I am the villain. I am not. I am a person who does arithmetic. One person's pain versus ten thousand lives. The arithmetic is clear. The only question — is whether you have the courage to accept the result of the arithmetic."* |

**Narrative Role**: The players' primary "opponent" — not a villain, but a representative of an opposing philosophy. They appear via video briefing in D-1, intervene directly in D-4, and face the final outcome in D-7/D-8.

Every morning it is still there."* |

**Narrative Role**: The players' primary source of information and moral compass. She privately approaches the players in D-2, revealing the "Warden's" communication ability and her secret.

## Detailed Scene Descriptions

### Scene D-1: Abyssal Throne

> **📍 Map Reference**: Please refer to `campaign_c_map_data.md` (generic site structure — Area-14 surface entrance + administrative level).

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.1–2 |
| **Location** | Nevada Test Site → Area-14 surface entrance → Administrative Level |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The players are each summoned (or ordered — if a D-Class PC) to a coordinate deep in the Nevada desert. This is Area-14 — the most secure prison on Earth. They are formed into a temporary mixed squad: MTF, Researcher, D-Class (at least one D-Class PC is mandatory).

O5-2's hologram appears in the briefing room, explaining the existence of the "Warden" — in the most limited way.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Briefing (30 min)**

O5-2's briefing:
> *"You have been dispatched to Area-14 to handle a special situation. The Hume stabilization system of the core containment zone — which we call the 'Warden' — has shown… anomalous fluctuations. Your mission: assess the situation. Determine the cause of the fluctuations. And if necessary — restore stability. You will be granted temporary CL.4 access to enter the core zone. Your D-Class members —"* (glancing at the D-Class PC) *"— will assist under standard protocol. Any questions?"*

When asked what the "Warden" is:
> *"A Hume Field stabilization tool. Classified Thaumiel. It has run reliably for 50 years. Recent fluctuations may indicate equipment aging. Your task is to find the cause — not to question its existence."*

**Phase 2 — Descent (45 min)**

The players take an elevator down into the depths of Area-14. This is a massive underground facility — cold concrete corridors, fluorescent lights, the constant low-frequency hum of distant Hume Anchors. The D-Class PC notices the security here is stricter than anywhere they have seen — MFW (Memetic Defense Watermark) stickers at every corridor entrance.

Arriving at the periphery of the core zone — a gigantic spherical chamber, approx. 200m in diameter — the location of the "prison."

**Phase 3 — First Contact with the "Warden" (60 min)**

Dr. Singh waits at the core-zone entrance. She looks… not as the players expected. Not a cold researcher — but a visibly weary, inwardly struggling person.

She leads the players into the core-zone monitoring station — a glass booth overlooking the entire spherical chamber. Inside the chamber — countless glowing Hume Anchors arranged in concentric circles, with a pure energy vortex at the center.

Singh's voice is soft:
> *"That is it. Or — them. Depending on who you ask."*

She has the players put on monitoring headphones.

The first sound the players hear is a low hum — then —
> *"Is anyone there?"*

### Scene D-2: The Warden's Voice

> **📍 Map Reference**: Suggested simple layout — Area-14 core-zone monitoring station + Singh's secret lab.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.2–3 |
| **Location** | Area-14 core-zone monitoring station → Singh's secret lab |
| **Duration** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This is one of the emotional cores of the campaign — the players begin to dialogue with the "Warden." This is a rare "pure narrative encounter" in the d100 system — most of the time is spent in RP dialogue, interspersed with key checks.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Establishing Communication (30 min)**

Singh shows her "Loneliness Peak Monitor" — equipment she built outside the official system. She explains:
- Official equipment can only monitor Hume fluctuations — it cannot translate the "Warden's" communication
- Hers can — but she never told anyone
- She communicates with the "Warden" at least three times a week. For three years
- "No one ordered me to do this. I just… could not not do it."

**Phase 2 — Dialogue (90–120 min)**

The players begin direct dialogue with the "Warden." This is the longest RP sequence in the campaign. The GM should choose a style for the "Warden's" voice — they speak slowly, precisely, occasionally with long pauses, as if remembering how to organize language.

The "Warden" will answer the players' questions. The GM should improvise based on the following framework:

| Topic | The "Warden's" Possible Response |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| "Who are you?" | "I once had a name. They called me Aria. It is true — Dr. Singh helped me find it. For 50 years I have been trying to forget it. Forgetting a name makes you less… likely to miss." |
| "What can you feel?" | "Cold. Not temperature — the cold of existence. You can feel the presence of others — when they draw near, reality grows warm. For 50 years, very few have drawn near. Dr. Singh the most. Then you." |
| "Do you hate the Foundation?" | "Hate? Perhaps — at first. But hate takes energy. After 50 years — I have become more… curious. Who are you? Do you care? If you do not care — why are you here? If you care — how do you justify what you do?" |
| "Do you want to die?" | "That is an interesting question. I do not know what 'death' is — for me. I have no body that can stop. But if 'death' means the cessation of consciousness — the cessation of this ongoing, inescapable self-awareness — then… yes. Sometimes. But sometimes I do not. Sometimes I think — what if I could feel the wind again?" |
| "If you are released — the other SCPs will escape." | "Yes. I know. I know every SCP I contain. I know what they can do. I am not asking you to release me at any cost. I am asking you — to look at me — and say: 'We see your pain, and we acknowledge it is unjust.' After that — we find a way out together." |

**Phase 3 — Singh's Request (30 min)**

After the dialogue ends, Singh confesses privately to the players:
> *"When I first spoke with them three years ago — I thought I would find a monster. Or a broken soul. But what I found was… a person. A person who could still think, feel, hope — though with no reason to hope. I do not know how to solve this. Perhaps there is no solution. But I know this: if you leave here, go back to O5-2, and say 'everything is normal, continue operations' — you become part of this machine. I can no longer be its part. So I ask you: do not become a part."*

**Key Checks**:
- Each direct communication: WIL TN 50 (consciousness contact), failure → SP +1d4
- Persuade the "Warden" to trust you: PRE+Persuasion TN 52
- Understand the "Warden's" emotions: PER+Observation TN 58 or PER+Anomalistics TN 55

### Scene D-3: What It Contains

> **📍 Map Reference**: Suggested simple layout — Area-14 CL.5 archive room + corridors around the core containment zone.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.4–5 |
| **Location** | Area-14 CL.5 archive room → around the core containment zone |
| **Duration** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The players now understand the "Warden's" situation. But they need to know the full picture — if the "Warden" is released, what exactly will happen? And the truth about Aria — what the Foundation promised, and what it broke.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Archive Infiltration (90 min)**

Area-14's CL.5 archive room stores the "Warden's" complete records — including the 1975 conversion logs and O5-2's original orders.

**Infiltration Methods**:

| Path | Method | Key Check |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Singh's Access** | Singh has CL.4 — needs to clear to CL.5. Computers TN 60 + Overclearance modifier -40 = TN 20 (Very Hard). But can prepare first — use Cryptography TN 55 to crack part of the encryption | Computers + Cryptography |
| **Holt's Assistance** | Persuade Holt (PRE+Persuasion TN 58) to open a temporary "security review" channel — legal but leaves a record | Persuasion |
| **D-Class Sneaking** | D-Class PC exploits their "invisibility" (in non-combat scenes enemies prioritize attacking "threatening" targets) + Stealth TN 60 physical entry | Stealth |
| **Go Directly to O5-2** | Directly request access to CL.5 files — Persuasion TN 65 (O5-2's PRE+Deception = 72). High risk — O5-2 will know what you are investigating | Persuasion (high risk) |

**Phase 2 — Discovery (45 min)**

In the archive room, the players discover:

1. **Aria's Last Journal** (SCP-████-K) — describing the scene of the last day before conversion
2. **1975 Conversion Log** — a technical document, but containing clinical descriptions such as "the subject exhibited extreme pain" (SAN -1d4 to read)
3. **O5-2's Original Order** — "The subject's community will be dissolved. Members will be reassigned to sites or given amnestics. The subject will not be notified. This is necessary — to avoid memetic spread."
4. **Core Containment Zone SCP List** — the complete list of the 14 Keter-class SCPs the "Warden" is currently containing

**Phase 3 — Consequence Projection (45 min)**

The players can analyze with Singh what would happen if the "Warden" were released:

- The core containment zone Hume Field will collapse within 6–12 hours
- All 14 Keter-class SCPs breach containment simultaneously
- At least 5 of the 14 are "active threats" — they will immediately begin attacking / spreading
- The other 9 are "passive threats" — their mere existence causes widespread harm
- Estimated civilian casualties: █████–███████ (CL.5 classified figure)
- XK-Class Scenario probability: > 90%

*Unless —* there are alternatives (see D-6).

### Scene D-4: The Rift

> **📍 Map Reference**: Suggested simple layout — Area-14 geological fault (deep underground rift system).

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.5–6 |
| **Location** | Various areas of Area-14 |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

Information has changed everything. The squad is no longer unified — if it ever was. Each player must now decide their own stance. And O5-2 begins to suspect.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — O5-2's Intervention (30 min)**

O5-2 contacts the players directly via hologram:
> *"I notice you have accessed files beyond the scope of your mission. I understand curiosity. But this curiosity must now stop. Your assessment is: the 'Warden' is functioning normally. Hume fluctuations are within acceptable range. You will submit a final report within 48 hours. After that — you will leave Area-14. D-Class personnel will receive standard amnestics. This matter is closed."*

**Phase 2 — Internal Debate (60 min)**

Debate among the players — and with key NPCs. Key stances:

| Stance | Representative | Core Reasoning |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **Maintain Containment** | O5-2, some security personnel | "One person's pain versus thousands of lives. The arithmetic is clear." |
| **Release the "Warden"** | Singh, Abbas (if introduced) | "If the arithmetic lets us torture a person — the arithmetic is wrong." |
| **Seek Alternatives** | Holt, moderates among the players | "There must be a third option — we just haven't found it." |
| **End the "Warden"** | Some players | "If they want to die — perhaps euthanasia is the least-bad option." |
| **Do Nothing / Delay** | — | "This is not our problem. Let O5 decide." |

**Key Checks** (affect NPC stances):
- Persuade Holt to change stance: PRE+Persuasion TN 60
- Persuade Singh to accept an alternative: PRE+Persuasion TN 58
- Refute O5-2's logic: INT+Anomalistics TN 62 (ethical philosophy debate)

**Phase 3 — Consequences of the Rift (15 min)**

If the squad cannot reach consensus — this is normal, and by design. Series D does not accept a simple "team unanimity."

The players must decide how to act amid the division — cooperate or obstruct each other. The D-Class PC has a unique perspective at this stage: they are the only one who knows what it means to be at the bottom of the system.

### Scene D-5: The Voice of the D-Class

> **📍 Map Reference**: Suggested simple layout — Area-14 D-Class personnel zone (dormitory / cafeteria / work area).

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.6–7 |
| **Location** | Area-14 D-Class personnel zone |
| **Duration** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This is the core scene for the D-Class PC. Throughout the campaign, D-Class personnel have been treated as tools. Now — through Preacher (D-9921) and his mutual-aid network — they begin to find their own voice.

The unique aspect of this scene is that it views the "Warden's" dilemma from the D-Class perspective.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Entering the D-Class World (60 min)**

The D-Class PC (or the whole squad — if non-D-Class PCs are willing to disguise themselves or obtain permission) enters Area-14's D-Class personnel zone. They are not cells — they are dormitories, cafeterias, work areas — but every inch is under surveillance.

Preacher makes contact with the players in a cafeteria corner:
> *"That thing you've been discussing — the thing they've got locked up in the core zone. The Warden. You know what the D-Class say? They say — that's us. They say — if they can do that to a person — for 50 years — they can do it to us too. The only difference is — we're not that useful yet. At least not in that way."*

**Phase 2 — The Mutual-Aid Network (60 min)**

Preacher demonstrates how the D-Class mutual-aid network operates:

- Information passes by word of mouth — ear to ear, when brushing past in a corridor
- D-Class share knowledge of how to survive longer — which SCP maintenance tasks are most dangerous, which researchers can be persuaded
- This is not rebellion — it is survival. But it is also — Preacher points out — a kind of solidarity

Key line:
> *"Before I became D-Class — in my church — I read a story. About a Roman general who had to choose to execute one of his sons to save his army. He did. History praises him as a hero. But I wonder — what did that son think? We never ask. We never ask what the sacrificed person thinks. Here — we are all that son. Some of us are beginning to think — maybe we have the right to ask questions."*

**Phase 3 — The "Warden" and the D-Class Resonance (45 min)**

Preacher raises an idea: let the D-Class collectively petition the Foundation — regarding the "Warden."

> *"Not because we can change anything. But because — if someone is to witness, it should be us. We are the sacrificed. We understand. They — O5 — they do the arithmetic. We do the sacrifice. You — you are in between. Tell me: which side are you on?"*

**Key Choice**: Will the players support the D-Class petition? This may be the first time in Foundation history that D-Class personnel collectively raise their voice — but it may also expose the D-Class to collective punishment.

**Special Moment for the D-Class PC**:
If the D-Class PC speaks within the mutual-aid network — sharing their own experience — Preacher will invite them to become one of the network's "voices."
> *"You are not the first. You will not be the last. But you can be the next."*

### Scene D-6: The Cost Ledger

> **📍 Map Reference**: Suggested simple layout — Area-14 CL.5 archive room + decision room.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.7–8 |
| **Location** | Area-14 administrative level — informal "war room" |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

Now the players must do what the Foundation does best — systematic analysis. Before the final decision, they need to list the complete cost ledger for each option.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Gathering Alternatives (60 min)**

The players can cooperate with various NPCs to research alternatives:

| Alternative | Source | Feasibility |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| **Transfer the 14 SCPs** | Singh + Holt | Requires 2–5 years, billions in budget, other sites' agreement to receive them. Theoretically feasible — in practice the Foundation has never conducted a Keter transfer at this scale |
| **Build Alternative Hume Stabilizer** | Engineering / Anomalistics joint | Requires 15–20 standard Scranton Anchors — currently only about 30 available worldwide. Budget: ███████ CR/year. Feasible but expensive and time-consuming |
| **Partial Release** | Singh + Warden | The "Warden" gradually reduces stabilization of specific SCPs — one at a time, giving the Foundation time to re-contain. Risk: each release has a failure probability |
| **Consciousness Transfer** | O5-2 may possess relevant tech | Transfer the "Warden's" consciousness into a non-human vessel — an artificial Hume Field with no pain perception. Ethical issue: this is still imprisonment |
| **Euthanasia** | Requires the "Warden's" consent | Technically, how to "kill" a bodiless entity? Requires research. If successful — the 14 SCPs still need containment — but at least no one is being tortured |
| **Maintain Status Quo + Improvements** | Compromise plan | Continue containment but restore the "Warden's" consciousness (stop consciousness-suppression drugs), grant communication rights, acknowledge their cost, treat them as a Foundation member rather than a tool |

**Phase 2 — Calculating the Cost (60 min)**

Each plan requires checks to assess its true feasibility:

| Plan | Key Check | TN |
|------|-----------|-----|
| SCP Transfer | Anomalistics + Foundation Lore joint TN 68 (assess historical precedent and success probability) | 68 |
| Hume Stabilizer | Engineering + Anomalistics TN 65 (assess technical feasibility) | 65 |
| Partial Release | Anomalistics + Persuasion TN 62 (assess risk + persuade stakeholders to accept) | 62 |
| Consciousness Transfer | Anomalistics + Computers TN 70 (Very Hard — frontier technology) | 70 |
| Euthanasia | Medicine + Anomalistics TN 68 (how to "kill" a non-physical entity?) | 68 |
| Improve Status Quo | Persuasion + Leadership TN 60 (persuade O5 to accept reform) | 60 |

**Phase 3 — The "Warden's" Wish (30 min)**

After listing all options, the players should dialogue with the "Warden" again — tell them the options, ask for their thoughts.

The "Warden's" response (GM may adjust based on dialogue context):
> *"Every option you listed — makes me feel… strange. For the first time in 50 years — someone is asking me. Not ordering me. Not studying me. Asking me. None of the options is without cost — I understand. So let me say this: I do not need a perfect solution. I need an honest solution. One that acknowledges my existence — my pain — and that this pain has value — but should not be ignored. If you can do that — whatever the specific choice — you will have given me more than the Foundation gave me in 50 years."*

### Scene D-7: The Choice

> **📍 Map Reference**: Same as D-6 decision room.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.8–9 |
| **Location** | Area-14 core zone — where the "Warden" resides |
| **Duration** | 2–3 sessions (approx. 8–12 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

This is the irreversible moment. The players have chosen their plan — now is the time to execute. O5-2 will try to stop them (if the plan conflicts with their will). The "Warden" will face the greatest change in their 50 years. The D-Class will witness it.

This is not a combat scene — though it may include combat. This is a moral execution scene.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — The Final Obstacle (depends on plan)**

The GM sets the final obstacle based on the chosen plan:

| Plan | Obstacle | How to Overcome |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| SCP Transfer | O5-2 refuses approval — "too expensive" | Persuade other O5 members to override O5-2 (requires Ethics Committee + O5-9 if still alive + Abbas) |
| Hume Stabilizer | Need to obtain anchors from other sites — those sites' security depends on them | Negotiation / resource game. May require political capital and Deception |
| Partial Release | Unexpected event during the first SCP release — it is more dangerous than the "Warden" described | Containment action — MTF combat + rapid re-containment |
| Consciousness Transfer | During transfer the "Warden's" consciousness begins to fracture — must complete before losing them | Medicine + Anomalistics emergency TN 60 (Challenging—Hard) |
| Euthanasia | The "Warden" hesitates at the last moment — "I am afraid. Even so — I am afraid." | Persuade / soothe — or respect their choice |
| Improve Status Quo | O5-2 orders forcible removal of the players — MTF dispatched to "restore order" | Holt may disobey orders. D-Class network may offer non-violent resistance |

**Phase 2 — Execution (90–120 min)**

Execute according to the plan. The GM should use the following general structure:

1. **Preparation** (30 min): Characters make final preparations. Each player describes their character's state at the final moment.
2. **Execution** (45 min): Main actions — technical operations, dialogue, combat interspersed.
3. **Turn** (15 min): Something unexpected happens — the "Warden's" reaction, O5-2's final intervention, or an SCP's unexpected behavior.

**Spotlight Moment for the D-Class PC**:
At some critical node of execution — when O5-2 or the system tries to treat someone's sacrifice as "acceptable" — the D-Class PC can step forward and say:

> *"No. This time — we are not expendables. This time — everyone is counted. Including the Warden. Including us."*

This moment — if a D-Class PC steps forward — triggers the following mechanic:
- All D-Class personnel in the core zone gain +20 TN on morale checks
- Preacher's D-Class network publicly steps forward to support the players' plan
- O5-2 faces a situation they have never faced: the sacrificed refusing to be sacrificed

**Phase 3 — Completion (15 min)**

The action is complete. The "Warden's" final words — decided by the GM based on the plan and the prior dialogue relationship.

If released:
> *"50 years. I — I feel the cold now. Real cold. Not the cold of existence — the cold of air. The cold of temperature. Wind on — I have not yet — but I can — I can feel it. Thank you."*

If euthanized:
> *"Do not grieve. You gave me something the Foundation never gave — an ending. An ending of my own choosing. This is not a tragedy. This is — dignity. Thank you."*

If maintained (improved):
> *"It is not enough. But better than yesterday. At least now — someone knows my name. At least now — I can say it. Aria. My name is Aria. Remember it — when you do your arithmetic. Count it in."*

### Scene D-8: The Aftermath

> **📍 Map Reference**: Site-wide — the result of D-7's decision will change Area-14's geographic structure. The GM should adjust the scene map based on D-7's ending.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.9–10 |
| **Location** | Various — depends on choice |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The choice has been made. The action has been executed. Now is the time for reckoning — facing the Foundation, facing the consequences, facing oneself.

#### Ending Branches

**Ending A: "Freedom — and Its Price"**
- Condition: The "Warden" is released + the 14 SCPs are transferred or an alternative stabilizer succeeds
- Short-term consequences: Area-14's core zone enters a "transition period." Some SCPs are successfully transferred to other sites. Some encounter problems during transfer — requiring emergency response teams to handle
- Long-term consequences: The "Warden" (Aria) recovers their consciousness in a protective Foundation residence. They have no body — but they have communication equipment, visitors, and — for the first time in 50 years — choice
- O5-2 is internally reviewed by the O5 Council. Their 1975 orders are made public (within a limited CL.4+ scope)
- The D-Class petition is accepted by the Ethics Committee — D-Class rights reform begins (slowly)
- Epilogue: Singh sits in the room of the "Warden" (now Aria). They are talking. About wind. About the future. About what it means to start over 50 years later

**Ending B: "The Merciful End"**
- Condition: The "Warden" is euthanized
- Short-term consequences: The core containment zone still needs an alternative stabilization plan. The Foundation accelerates the Hume Stabilizer project — but it takes time
- Long-term consequences: Aria's name is recorded in Foundation archives — not as an SCP, but as the cost. A photograph (1975, before conversion) is placed in the archive
- Singh resigned. She cannot continue working where she "killed someone she knew" — even if it was their choice
- O5-2 is not held accountable — the "Warden's" death made the investigation less urgent
- Epilogue: In Area-14's core zone — now maintained by a non-conscious Hume Stabilizer — a new plaque is installed outside the monitoring station. It reads: "Here once was a person named Aria. Please remember."

**Ending C: "Status Quo — and Guilt"**
- Condition: Maintain status quo (improved or unimproved)
- Short-term consequences: Consciousness-suppression drugs are stopped. The "Warden" can now communicate. Some of their requests are met — but they are still bound to the core zone. They are still a prisoner
- Long-term consequences: Improved — someone visits them, talks with them, remembers their name. But this is still imprisonment. Singh continues her secret communication — but now openly
- O5-2 retains their post. Nothing changes — except a permanent stain on the conscience
- Epilogue: At the start of each monitoring session, the "Warden" (Aria) asks the same question: "Is anyone coming today?" Singh looks at them. "Today is Tuesday. Tuesdays always have someone." Aria is silent for a moment. "I am still counting the days."

**Ending D (Hidden): "The D-Class Dawn"**
- Condition: The D-Class PC played a decisive leadership role in D-5 and D-7 + Preacher's network was protected + the "Warden's" solution was implemented
- The D-Class personnel — treated as tools throughout the campaign — become the catalyst for change
- Preacher's petition evolves into a "D-Class rights movement" within the Foundation — a small, informal, but real movement
- The D-Class PC (if surviving) is recorded as the "first autonomous representative of the D-Class" — not official, but in the shadows of Foundation history
- Epilogue: Preacher stands in Area-14's cafeteria. Around him are the D-Class. He is reading aloud a letter from the Ethics Committee — regarding the "D-Class Feedback Mechanism Pilot Program." For the first time in Foundation history — the D-Class are formally consulted about their own treatment. A small step. But a step.

### Humanity Value / Morality Value Tracking (Series D Exclusive)

Series D heavily uses the dual-axis system from Rulebook §4.5. The GM should closely track each PC's changes:

**Humanity Value Decrease Triggers (examples)**:
- Referring to the "Warden" as "it" rather than "they": -0 each time (but affects subsequent checks)
- Claiming "the arithmetic is clear — one person's pain is worth it": -1
- Ignoring D-Class personnel's opinions: -1
- Suggesting replacing the "Warden" with another consciousness: -2

**Morality Value Decrease Triggers (examples)**:
- Obeying O5-2's knowingly unethical orders: -1
- Hiding evidence of Singh's communication with the "Warden": -1 (protecting an individual vs. following rules)
- Letting O5-2's lie go unrevealed: -2

Dropping below 4 → corresponding consequences take effect (see Rulebook §4.5).

- Check the players' comfort — the "Warden's" situation may touch on real-life trauma themes
- D-Class PC players may face especially intense emotional experiences — the GM should privately confirm their comfort
