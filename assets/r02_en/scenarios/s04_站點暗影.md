# Series C: Site Shadow (Shadow of the Site)

## Overview

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Positioning** | Site politics + resource management + internal threats — the horror of the Foundation's bureaucratic system |
| **Gameplay Focus** | Organization Management Subsystem, Departmental Politics, Site Event response, Resource Allocation |
| **Recommended Identities** | Researcher, Security Agent, Technical Specialist, Medical Specialist (requires CL.2+ at start) |
| **Level Coverage** | Lv.3–10 (start Lv.3, endgame Lv.9–10) |
| **Scene Count** | 7 core scenes + Site Event system |
| **Core Conflict** | A newly appointed Site Director attempts to reform the Containment Protocol and cut costs — but his "optimization" is releasing things that should not be released. Departments pass the buck, and the Ethics Committee closes in. |
| **Site** | Site-██ (newly built), located underground in the Nevada desert. It shares the same designation as the abandoned Site-██ from Series B — not a coincidence, but a covert "atonement" political project. |
| **Campaign Length** | Estimated 14–20 sessions (approx. 45–70 hours) |

### Campaign Summary

Site-██ is a brand-new Foundation Site — at least on the surface. The underground facility is state-of-the-art, its Containment Cells equipped with the latest Scranton Anchor arrays, and its personnel are carefully selected "elites" drawn from various Sites.

But this Site's designation — ██ — is a cursed name within the Foundation. Forty years ago, a Site of the same name (see Series B: Crimson Dossier) was erased from the records. Who approved this new Site-██? And why does someone within the O5 Council insist on "restoring the honor of this designation"?

The players are assigned to Site-██ as mid-level backbone staff across departments. Their direct superiors are the department heads — and above them all is **Dr. Solomon Cross**, a newly appointed Site Director known for his "efficiency first" motto.

Cross's reforms began on day one: reducing the frequency of Containment Cell maintenance, consolidating redundant security posts, and canceling "unnecessary" D-Class Personnel health checks. His reasoning is impeccable — limited budget, resources need optimizing. But when the first SCP begins to destabilize due to delayed maintenance, the players must decide: support the Director's "big-picture view"? Or become an internal whistleblower?

The Shadow of the Site is not a sword — it is a net slowly tightening.

## Detailed Scene Descriptions

### Scene C-1: New Site, Old Shadows

> **📍 Map Reference**: See `campaign_c_map_data.md` — general site structure. Scene involves the second-floor administrative level.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.3 |
| **Location** | Site-██ Administrative Level → department offices |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 4–6 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The players arrive at Site-██ in the Nevada desert. It is their onboarding day. Cross personally welcomes them — with his signature smile and a well-prepared speech. But beneath the surface of this gleaming new facility — something is off.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Arrival & Onboarding (45 min)**

The surface entrance to Site-██ is an unremarkable desert research station. After a series of security checks, the players are led into an elevator — then descend underground.

Cross's welcome speech (GM may read aloud):
> *"Welcome to Site-██ — the Foundation's most advanced, most efficient Containment facility. You were chosen not only for your skills — but for your attitude. We need people who can think outside the box. People who ask 'why have we always done it this way?' People who understand that Containment is not about keeping everything the same — but about finding a better way."*

**Phase 2 — Department Assignment & Relationship-Building (60 min)**

Each player is assigned to a different department (based on identity), but regularly convenes with the squad. They meet their direct superiors:

| Department | Superior | First Impression | Exclusive Info in First Scene |
|-----------|----------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Security | Yuki Tanaka | Cold, terse | Privately warns players: "Keep an eye on the maintenance schedule. It's changed recently." |
| Research | Orlov | Warm but nervous | Accidentally mentions "there are some readings from Sublevel 9 I'm not quite sure about... but I'm still analyzing them" |
| Technical | — | — | Cross directly manages the Technical Department — he eliminated the Technical Director post to "reduce redundant layers" |
| Medical | — | — | Medical Director absent ("administrative leave" — later found to be a critic sidelined by Cross) |

**Phase 3 — Environmental Reconnaissance (45 min)**

The players have time to explore the Site. The GM should provide subtle anomaly clues:

| Discovery | Check | Clue |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Maintenance Log | Observation TN 58 | The inspection frequency of several Containment Cells was changed from "daily" to "weekly" — crossed out in black pen, stamped with Cross's electronic signature |
| Personnel Roster | Computers TN 52 | Multiple key positions vacant — "to be filled after optimization" |
| Old Photo | Observation TN 60 | A photo on the break-room bulletin board from when Site-██ was under construction — the ruins of the old facility are visible in the background |
| Interdepartmental Note | Observation TN 55 | A note from Tanaka to Cross (returned): "Request to restore daily inspection of Cell 9-B. Last inspection was 12 days ago. —YT." Handwritten reply at the bottom: "Noted. Maintain current schedule. —SC" |

| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The first anomalous readings appear. The Hume-affected radius of SCP-████-H is expanding. A temperature control system in a Containment Cell mysteriously went offline for 4 hours overnight. No one was hurt — but it is the first concrete sign: Cross's optimization is having consequences.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Anomaly Report (30 min)**

Orlov (or Tanaka's alternate channel) privately contacts the players: the monitoring array on Sublevel 9 has detected a Hume Field fluctuation. It is not at alarm level — but definitely abnormal. Problem: Cross has already been informed. His response: "Before a formal alarm triggers, don't escalate. We need to avoid unnecessary panic."

**Phase 2 — Field Investigation (90 min)**

The players go to Sublevel 9 to investigate. They find:

- **SCP-████-H's Containment Cell**: Only one Scranton Anchor is running (there should be two). The maintenance log shows the second anchor was reassigned to Cell 12-C two weeks ago.
- **Hume-affected radius**: Expanded to about 18m. Walls within the radius feel "not quite right" — the touch is like through a thin film. Observation TN 55 → notice that items at the edge of the affected radius seem to have lost some detail.
- **Temperature control offline**: Engineering TN 58 to inspect the system → discover it did not malfunction — its maintenance budget was cut, and maintenance staff switched it from "daily automatic detection" to "weekly manual detection."

**Phase 3 — Confronting Cross (45 min)**

The players may choose to directly question Cross — or stay silent and gather more evidence.

If questioned:
> *Cross's smile doesn't waver. "I understand your concerns. But let's look at the data. The Hume fluctuation is within acceptable range. The temperature control was offline for 4 hours — that's not good, but no SCP was substantively affected either. You see a fracture — I see a system operating within acceptable parameters. The question isn't 'is there a fracture.' The question is 'are these fractures within the safety margin.' By every objective standard — yes, they are."*

Persuasion TN 60 can get him to agree to restore one anchor — but not change overall policy.

#### Key Checks

| Check | TN | Description |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Anomalistics (Hume reading analysis) | ~68 | INT 42 + Anomalistics 26 |
| Engineering (temp control diagnostics) | ~65 | INT 42 + Engineering 23 |
| Observation (affected radius edge) | ~62 | PER 38 + Observation 24 |
| Persuasion (questioning Cross) | ~62 | PRE 34 + Persuasion 28 |

### Scene C-3: Departmental War

> **📍 Map Reference**: Same as C-1, site administrative level (second floor), involving department offices and conference rooms.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.5–6 |
| **Location** | Site-██ department offices → administrative conference room |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The fractures are widening — not only physically, but organizationally. Departments begin to clash openly. Security accuses Research of "hiding readings." Research accuses Technical of "not fixing the second anchor." Technical accuses Administration of "cutting our budget."

The players — as mid-level staff — find themselves at the eye of the storm. They must choose which side to stand on.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Department Meeting (60 min)**

Cross convenes a site-wide department meeting. The GM should set a tense round-table scene. Key attendees:

- **Cross**: chairs the meeting, trying to maintain the facade that "everything is under control"
- **Tanaka**: directly challenges — "The second anchor was moved. Why?"
- **Orlov**: avoids direct conflict — "I think we need more data..."
- **Magpie (O5 Observer)**: quietly observes, occasionally smiling
- **Cross's response**: "The second anchor was reassigned to where it's needed more. This is the efficiency principle we discussed — dynamic resource allocation."

**Phase 2 — Corridor Politics (90 min)**

After the meeting, each faction separately approaches the players:

- **Tanaka**: asks the players to help her gather evidence of the security impact of Cross's decisions. Offers access to internal security reports.
- **Orlov**: privately admits he had early readings of SCP-████-H but didn't report them. Asks the players not to tell Tanaka — "it would only make things worse."
- **Magpie**: probes how the players view Cross's performance — "I just want to understand the grassroots perspective. Completely informal."
- **Cross**: if the players have a good relationship with him, he proposes a "special project" — helping him optimize the Containment Protocol. "Your field experience is exactly what we need."

**Phase 3 — Choosing a Stance (30 min)**

The players must make a key decision: do they support Cross's reforms, support Tanaka's opposition, or stay neutral and gather more evidence?

Each choice has consequences:

| Stance | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Support Cross** | Gain temporary CL +1. But in later scenes, SAN Checks suffer −10 TN due to cognitive dissonance |
| **Support Tanaka** | Gain access to internal Security resources. But Cross begins restricting players' access to sensitive information |
| **Neutral / Independent Investigation** | Neither side trusts you — but both provide information (hoping to win you over) |

### Scene C-4: The Thing Released

> **📍 Map Reference**: See `campaign_c_map_data.md` (general site structure — underground Containment wing). Need to sketch the layout of multiple corridors and isolation doors for the breach.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.6–7 |
| **Location** | Site-██ Sublevel 9 — SCP-████-H Containment Cell |
| **Duration** | 2 sessions (approx. 6–9 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The worst has happened. The Hume-affected radius of SCP-████-H suddenly expands — jumping from 22m to 45m. The radius swallows three adjacent Containment Cells and two maintenance personnel. One of those cells contains a Safe-class anomaly — now it has "vanished."

This is the first substantive failure of Cross's "efficiency model."

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Emergency Response (30 min)**

The alarm finally sounds. The entire Site enters yellow alert. Tanaka takes over tactical command — Cross temporarily retreats to the administrative level ("I need to contact the O5").

The players are deployed to Sublevel 9: assess damage, search for missing personnel, and attempt to stabilize SCP-████-H.

**Phase 2 — Affected Zone Exploration (90 min)**

Entering SCP-████-H's affected radius is a unique experience:

> *The world looks... normal. But normally wrong. The colors are too uniform. The sounds too flat. As if everything is missing a layer — a layer of detail you never noticed before. There's a desk in the corner. A coffee cup sits on it. The cup is empty — but it's steaming. No one is here. But the coffee is fresh.*
> *Then you realize: it's not "no one is here." Someone was here — and then they vanished. Not fleeing. Not dying. Vanished — from the records, from memory. You try to recall the two maintenance personnel's names. You can't. You check your notes — the notes say "Today inspected Cell 9-B with ██ and ██." Their names are right there — in black ink. But you can't read them. They're right in front of you — but you can't read them.*

**Mechanic — Exploration within the affected radius**:
- Every 10 minutes within the affected radius: d100 ≤ SAN-10, failure → SAN -1d6 + brief memory blur (lose specific details)
- Observation TN 60 → find the missing maintenance personnel's toolkit at the edge of the affected radius — phone still there, but the entries for them in the contact list are blank
- Anomalistics TN 65 → determine the expansion pattern of the Hume-affected radius — it is "searching" for more resources. If uncontrolled, it will swallow the entire Sublevel 9 within 72 hours

**Phase 3 — Stabilization Operation (60 min)**

Restoring SCP-████-H's stability requires:

1. **Reactivate the second Scranton Anchor**: Engineering TN 62. Needs to be retrieved from Cell 12-C (or allocated from storage).
2. **Restore maintenance personnel quota**: requires authorization from Cross or Tanaka.
3. **Temporarily reinforce the Hume Field**: Anomalistics TN 65, overload the existing anchor to 120% (effect lasts 2d6 hours, after which the anchor must cool down for 24 hours).

**Phase 4 — Cross's Reaction (30 min)**

After the incident, Cross convenes the players. His reaction depends on the players' stance in C-3:

- If players supported Cross: he shows rare vulnerability — "Maybe I was wrong. Maybe some things can't be optimized." This can be the turning point of his transformation.
- If players opposed Cross: he is defensive — "This is an isolated incident. The data doesn't support a full reversal of the reforms."
- If neutral: he probes — "What do you think I should do?"

**Key — Fate of the Missing Personnel**:
The two maintenance personnel within the affected radius remain "missing" — in no records, in no one's memory. Only the players (because they were present) can vaguely "feel" that someone should be there. This becomes the key emotional anchor driving Scenes C-5/C-6: the Foundation system can erase two people's existence — and no one will remember them. Except the players.

### Scene C-5: Ethics Committee

> **📍 Map Reference**: Same as C-1, site administrative level (second-floor conference room + Ethics Committee office).

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.7–8 |
| **Location** | Site-██ Administrative Level → Ethics Committee hearing room |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The SCP-████-H incident triggered an automatic Ethics Committee review. Cassandra Vela formally launches the investigation. She will interview the relevant personnel individually — including the players.

This is the political climax of the campaign: the players must decide in formal testimony what to say, how to say it, and whom to protect.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Vela's Individual Interview (30 min per player)**

Each player faces Vela alone. The GM should run the interview scene separately for each player. Vela's questions include:

1. "When did you first notice there was a problem with the Containment Protocol?"
2. "Did Director Cross at any point block your safety concerns?"
3. "Who do you think should be held responsible for the SCP-████-H incident?"
4. "Do you believe Director Cross is still fit to serve as Site Director?"

**Key Checks**:

| Response Strategy | Check | Consequence |
|-------------------|-------|-------------|
| **Fully Honest** | Automatic | Vela obtains full evidence. Cross may be removed. But if Cross's allies discover you were the informant — department relations suffer |
| **Protect Cross** | Deception TN 62 | Vela notices (PER 58 + Interrogation 68), but she may not expose you immediately. She marks you as "possibly coerced" |
| **Limited Honesty** | Persuasion TN 60 | Provide partial truth but withhold judgment. Vela understands — but needs more evidence |
| **Shift Blame to Orlov** | Deception TN 58 | Orlov bears primary responsibility. Cross is temporarily safe — but is that fair? |

**Phase 2 — Joint Hearing (60 min)**

All relevant parties are summoned to a joint hearing. Cross, Tanaka, Orlov, Magpie, Vela — all present.

Cross's defense:
> *"Site-██ is an experiment. All experiments carry risk. Our risk budget was calculated — I admit the calculations proved overly optimistic on certain parameters. But 'overly optimistic' is not a crime — it is science. I will adjust the model. I will restore certain maintenance cycles. But a full reversal of the reforms — abandoning everything we've learned — would be an even greater waste of Foundation resources."*

Vela's response:
> *"The problem isn't the model. The problem is that when the model began to show fractures — you chose to ignore them. 'Acceptable risk' is only acceptable when you accept the risk. Your maintenance personnel did not accept the risk. They vanished — from all records. Tell me, Director Cross: do you remember their names?"*

**Phase 3 — Provisional Ruling (15 min)**

Vela will not issue a final ruling that day — but she will give a preliminary opinion:
- Suspend further resource cuts until the investigation concludes
- Restore SCP-████-H's Containment resources to 100%
- Cross remains in post temporarily — but under "supervised observation"

### Scene C-6: Whistleblower

> **📍 Map Reference**: Same as C-1, site administrative level (second floor). Scene mainly involves corridor hiding and office negotiation.

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.8–9 |
| **Location** | Site-██ D-Class Personnel area → secret meeting point |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–7 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

D-7844 ("Key") contacts the players through an unexpected channel — he slipped a note into the maintenance log on Sublevel 9. The note reads: "I know what your Director is hiding. Corridor 3B, tomorrow 02:00. I'll come alone — hope you do too."

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Secret Meeting (45 min)**

2 AM. Corridor 3B is a dead-end used for ventilation duct maintenance — no surveillance (the D-Class discovered this blind spot). "Key" is waiting there.

His opening:
> *"Good. You came. I've got 90 seconds — then patrol passes. Listen: your Director Cross — I've seen his type before. In my old job — before I was framed — I dealt with dozens of corner-cutting builders. The pattern is always the same. It starts with small, reasonable cuts. Then they find the cuts don't cause immediate problems — so they double down. By the time the problem shows — the building is about to collapse."*

**Phase 2 — Evidence Exchange (60 min)**

"Key" provides three pieces of evidence:

| Evidence | Content | Impact on Cross |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Photo of Maintenance Log** | Shows the gradual reduction of maintenance frequency for 6 cells over the past three months — from daily to every two weeks | Proves the cuts are systematic, not "temporary adjustments" |
| **Scranton Anchor Transfer Record** | Shows the anchor was transferred from SCP-████-H to a "priority project" — signed by Cross | Proves he knew of and approved the resource transfer |
| **Cross's "Personal Notes" (partial)** | "Key" accidentally entered Cross's office during maintenance. A handwritten note mentions "if the Ethics Committee intervenes — emphasize the influence of SCP-████-I on decisions, reducing personal responsibility" | Suggests Cross planned to shift part of the blame onto anomalous influence (if he knew SCP-████-I's effects in advance) |

**Phase 3 — Protecting the Whistleblower (45 min)**

Once "Key"'s identity is exposed, he will face danger — not from Cross (Cross won't retaliate directly), but from the system itself. A D-Class Personnel holding CL.4 evidence = a security risk. Security Protocol may require amnestics or "reassignment" (transfer to another Site — or worse) for him.

The players must decide how to protect him:
- Hide him within the Site (Stealth TN 58 + Deception TN 55 to forge a transfer order)
- Have Tanaka place him in protective custody (Persuasion TN 60)
- Contact Vela — the Ethics Committee can authorize the D-Class Personnel as a "protected witness"
- Or — do nothing (the safest but also the most cowardly choice)

### Scene C-7: Reckoning

> **📍 Map Reference**: Same as C-1, site-wide (administrative level + helicopter pad as final exit).

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Level** | Lv.9–10 |
| **Location** | Site-██ → O5 virtual hearing |
| **Duration** | 1–2 sessions (approx. 5–8 hours) |

#### GM Briefing

The final reckoning. Vela completes the investigation. The O5 Council will make the final ruling. All relevant parties — Cross, Tanaka, Orlov, Vela, Magpie, the players, even "Key" (if protected) — are summoned.

#### Scene Flow

**Phase 1 — Final Testimony (60 min)**

The O5 Council (via video screens) hears testimony in turn. Each player gets one chance to make a final statement (PRE+Persuasion or PRE+Leadership, TN 65).

Key question: "Should the Site-██ experiment — Cross's efficiency model — be terminated, reformed, or expanded?"

**Phase 2 — Cross's Final Address (15 min)**

Cross's address depends on the players' actions in the campaign:

- If exposed: he admits error but defends — "I made mistakes. But if we stop innovating because of mistakes, the Foundation will never progress."
- If protected but evidence is substantial: he tries to shift blame onto SCP-████-I — "I may have been influenced by the anomaly."
- If protected and evidence is insufficient: he stays confident — "This is a valuable experiment. The data supports continuing optimization."

**Phase 3 — Ruling & Endings (30 min)**

The O5's ruling depends on key factors:

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|-------|
| Final state of SCP-████-H | Core — if stable, Cross's model is "partially viable" |
| Whether the missing personnel are "restored" (memory/records) | Emotional core — the O5 is highly concerned about "the two erased by the system" |
| Consistency of player testimony | If all players stand on the same stance, the O5 is more likely to adopt it |
| Magpie's behind-the-scenes maneuvering | His report may favor or harm Cross — depending on whether the players exposed his bias earlier |

**Ending A: "The Brakes Engage"**
- Condition: Cross is held accountable + SCP-████-H re-stabilized + reforms reversed
- Cross is transferred out — to a non-management post. Tanaka is promoted to Acting Site Director
- Site-██'s resource allocation restored to conservative standards
- Players receive commendation from the Ethics Committee

**Ending B: "Limited Reform"**
- Condition: Cross partially held accountable + reforms adjusted rather than canceled
- Cross remains in post but under Vela's supervision
- Resources restored to 85% — a compromise

**Ending C: "The System Wins"**
- Condition: Magpie successfully covers up + insufficient evidence + O5 chooses to protect the reform model
- Cross transferred to another Site — to expand his model
- Site-██ continues optimizing. The next failure is only a matter of time

**Ending D (Hidden): "New Shadow of the Site"**
- Condition: Players reveal in testimony the link between SCP-████-I and old Site-██ + the crossover with Series B is noticed by the O5
- One of the O5 (possibly O5-9, if surviving in Series B) proposes a full review of "organizational anomalies"
- Players are invited to join a special project team dedicated to investigating systemic Foundation failures
- Epilogue: a new file is created — "SCP-████-I-EX: Detection and Prevention of Institutional Anomalies" — the players are the primary contributors
