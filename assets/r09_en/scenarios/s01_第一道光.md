# Scenario One: "The First Light"

| Attribute | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Suggested Quality** | ★ Apprentice-grade |
| **Player Count** | 1–3 starting mages |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 1–2 |
| **Core Output** | 1 Light-element Shard + possible rare Shard |
| **Primary Threat** | Rival mages, minor environmental hazards |
| **Prerequisite** | None |

## Background

The players' tower stands at the edge of a suburban forest. Its previous occupant—an old mage named **Silas (塞拉斯)**—vanished mysteriously three years ago, leaving behind a tower that is dilapidated but still functional. Lately, the fireflies in the forest have displayed anomalous magical activity: their flight paths form perfect geometric patterns.

## Act One: Establishing the Tower

### Scene 1-1: Arrival

**Narration**: The players arrive at their newly acquired tower. Vines crawl over its outer walls, the telescope on the observation deck is askew, and the flasks in the laboratory are coated in dust. But the basic infrastructure is still intact—Laboratory Lv1, Library Lv1, Observation Deck Lv1, Containment Room Lv1, Defense System Lv1.

**What the players do**:
- Name the tower
- Choose the tower's location (suburbs or wilderness recommended—this scenario is built around the suburbs)
- Inspect each facility
- Allocate character attributes, choose a research style, and create starting spells

### Scene 1-2: Discovering Silas's Research Notes

**Narration**: While cleaning the library, the players find a dust-covered notebook. The cover reads "Silas Duskstar—Forest Magical Ecology Research, Volume Three." The final pages record a baffling observation:

> *"The fireflies' light—not bioluminescence. I observed it through a Perception filter for three nights. Their spectrum contains a line that belongs to no known bioluminescent reaction. The wavelength of that line… this is wrong. It's as if—the Shard is being excreted. But a Shard cannot occur naturally inside a living organism. Unless… no, this is absurd."*

The notes break off here. The date on the last page matches the day before Silas disappeared.

**GM Hint**: The notebook is a research lead, not an answer. It tells the players "something is anomalous in the forest," but not what the cause is. This is the first step of the research loop: Observe.

## Act Two: The First Research Loop

### Phase 1: Observe (Perception check, Difficulty Class (DC) 12)

**Success**:
> You kept watch in the forest for three nights. On the third night—just as you were about to give up—you saw it. The fireflies' flight paths are not random. They are drawing circles—perfect mathematical circles. And every time they pass a certain old oak, their light briefly shifts from yellow-green to pure white. There is something beneath that tree.

**Failure**:
> You spent three nights in the forest battling mosquitoes. You certainly saw very pretty fireflies—but they looked like ordinary fireflies. Maybe Silas was simply too old and had started hallucinating? (GM secret note: the next Observe check is at Difficulty Class (DC) –2—you are beginning to notice that something is off.)

### Phase 2: Hypothesize (Theory check, Difficulty Class (DC) 10)

**Success** (Difficulty Class (DC) –2 to Experiment):
> A thought suddenly strikes you: the fireflies' light is them expelling excess mana—like humans sweating. That means the nearby magic concentration is abnormally high. Only one thing can sustain such a high background magic concentration: an unexcavated Shard vein. Right beneath that old oak.

**Failure** (Difficulty Class (DC) +2 to Experiment):
> You wrote three pages of exquisite hypotheses—about firefly mating rituals and the evolution of light signals. The only problem is: it is most likely wrong. But you are so invested that you decide to run the experiment based on this hypothesis anyway.

### Phase 3: Preparation

Cost: 5 Gold Pieces (GP) (basic reagents), one collection container, one shovel. No special materials required.

### Phase 4: Experiment—Excavation Beneath the Old Oak (Craft check, Difficulty Class (DC) 12)

**Success (within Safety Threshold)**:
> Your shovel hits something hard. After carefully clearing away the soil, you see a fist-sized crystal—it gives off a soft milky glow in the fireflies' light. You carefully extract a small fragment with a reagent vial—the crystal trembles slightly in your hand, then breaks into a perfect little point of light. **You have obtained a "Light" element Shard (Common-grade).**

**Failure (within Safety Threshold)**:
> You dug for two hours—and found nothing. Did you dig in the wrong spot? Your materials are spent, but you may try again. (Materials consumed, no Shard, may retry.)

**Critical Failure (exceeds Safety Threshold)**:
> Your shovel hits the crystal—but your hand is too heavy. The crystal shatters, releasing a blinding flash. You are thrown back three steps, your eyes burning. (If a roll of 14–15 on the Accident Table occurs—a small Light-element being is accidentally summoned to the experiment site.)

### Phase 5: Analyze (Theory check, Difficulty Class (DC) 8, automatic success)

> You place the Shard into the analyzer. The data is clear: this is a pure "Light" element Shard—but there is a faint second characteristic line. This line points toward… "Life"? The fireflies have spent thousands of years turning this Shard vein into their own mana-metabolic core. Incredible.

**Shard Record**: Light-element Shard ×1 (Common-grade). If the Accident Table triggered an accidental summon, additionally obtain a small Light-element containment creature.

## Act Three: Academic Socializing

### Scene 3-1: The Uninvited Guest—Victor

**Narration**: The morning after returning to the tower, the visitor bell rings on the observation deck. A middle-aged mage, **Victor Page (維克託·佩吉)** (Bibliographic style, specializing in the Light element), stands at the tower door holding an open notebook.

> "Sorry to disturb you. I'm Victor. I—er—read a few reports in the 'academic journals' about magical phenomena in this forest. I noticed you recently moved into Silas's old tower. Have you… discovered anything?"

**NPC: Victor Page**
- Research style: Bibliographic
- Attributes: Perception 4, Theory 6, Craft 3, Will 4, Erudition 7
- Academic Reputation: 12
- Specialty: Light element
- Trait: Jealousy—cannot bear anyone being better than himself
- Secret: He corresponded with Silas three years ago about the anomalies in the forest—he is here to find Silas's research results

**Player Choices**:
1. **Share the discovery**: Tell Victor about the Shard vein → Victor proposes cooperation (leads to the "Collaborative Research" branch)
2. **Keep it secret**: Be evasive and send him away → Victor will explore the forest on his own (leads to the "Rivalry" branch)
3. **Deceive**: Tell him a false location → Victor will discover the deception after 1d3 rounds (leads to the "Conflict" branch)

### Scene 3-2: The Village Crisis

**Narration**: Regardless of how the players handle Victor, the village chief visits that evening. The village well has begun to emit a strange glow—villagers who drank the well water "can see things that aren't there."

**Problem Diagnosis** (Perception or Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 10):
> The glow of the well and the glow of the fireflies share the same wavelength. The mana from the Shard vein has seeped into the groundwater—long-term consumption may cause hallucinations and mana poisoning.

**Solutions**:
1. **Purify the well** (Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 12, 1 Action Point (AP)): Use basic reagents to neutralize the mana in the well water
2. **Seal the vein** (Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 14 + Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 12, 2 Action Points (AP)): Seal the vein's mana leak—a more thorough solution, and may expose a larger extent of the vein
3. **Ask Victor for help** (if relations are good): Victor can provide a Rare-grade filtration rune (worth 30 Gold Pieces (GP))—but this means owing him a favor

**Reward**: Regardless of the solution, Practical Reputation +1d3. If sealing the vein is a Critical Success, discover a second layer beneath the vein—a "Rare" Light Shard!

### Scene 3-3: The Battle Over the Vein (Victor branch)

**Cooperation branch** (if the players chose cooperation):
- Victor provides a Rare-grade extraction device (worth 50 Gold Pieces (GP))
- The players and Victor conduct a joint experiment together (Difficulty Class (DC) –3, due to cooperation)
- Success: each side obtains one Light Shard (Collaborative style may obtain two) + Academic Reputation +2 each
- Critical Success: a "Light" Rare Shard is also found in the vein—distribution must be negotiated

**Rivalry branch** (if the players kept it secret):
- After 1d3 rounds, the players hear an explosion from the forest
- Victor is digging on his own—he triggers the Accident Table (d12+8—his Bibliographic-style Craft check is at Disadvantage)
- The players may: rescue (+Academic Reputation and ally) or stand by (rival is weakened but this may lead to a larger incident)

**Conflict branch** (if the players deceived him):
- After Victor discovers the deception, he publicly accuses the players of "academic misconduct" at the next academic conference
- Academic Reputation –1d6

## Ending Branches

| Ending | Condition | Reward |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Independent Discovery** | Complete the research loop alone | Light Shard ×1, Academic +1d3, Practical +1d3 |
| **Win-Win Cooperation** | Cooperate with Victor | Light Shard ×1~2 (depends on style), Academic +2, future ally |
| **Vein Master** | Critical Success on sealing the vein | Light Shard ×1 + Rare Light Shard ×1, Academic +2d6 |
| **Academic Stain** | Deception of Victor exposed | Light Shard ×1, Academic –1d6 |
| **Elemental Companion** | Accidental summon from Accident Table, tamed Light element | Light Shard ×1 + Containment creature: small Light element (can assist research) |
