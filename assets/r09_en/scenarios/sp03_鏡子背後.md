## Background

A sensation rocked the academic world of **Arcadia City (阿卡迪亞城)**: an Empirical-style mage named **Marcus Flintlock (馬庫斯·燧發槍)** — who was also a secret member of the **Taboo Researchers' Underground Network (禁忌研究者地下網絡)** — lost control during a Void Shard experiment. He did not trigger the Accident Table — he triggered something worse.

His tower was sealed. The **Watcher Council (守望者議會)** declared it a "research accident," but their response was suspiciously fast — fast enough to look like a cover-up. Stranger still, they did not arrest Marcus — they placed him in **protective custody** at Watcher headquarters.

The Taboo Network contacted you through your scrying pool (if you have one) or an anonymous letter left on your laboratory desk: "Marcus found something he should not have. We need someone — a reputable Independent Mage — to enter Watcher headquarters and see him. Payment: a Rare Void Shard."

## Act One: A Two-Faced Mission

### Scene 1-1: Two Missions

Two requests arrive at once:

**The Watcher Council's formal letter:**
> "Respected colleague: The Watcher Council cordially invites you to join the academic assessment panel for the 'Marcus Flintlock Incident.' As an independent expert in the field, your opinion will help the Council judge the potential danger of the research. The assessment panel will receive part of Marcus's research materials and one opportunity to meet him."

**The Taboo Network's anonymous message:**
> "The Watchers are lying. Marcus did not lose control — he found the right thing. The Watchers hid it. Go see him. Ask him about 'the Mirror' (鏡子). He will know it is us. Bring back the answer, and the Void Shard is yours."

**The player must choose a primary stance** (but may conceal or change it during execution):

| Stance | Gain | Risk |
|--------|------|------|
| **Accept the Watcher invitation** | Legal entry to headquarters + 200 GP stipend + Watcher favor | Must report findings to the Watchers — if concealed, consequences are yours alone |
| **Accept the Taboo commission** | Promise of a Rare Void Shard + deeper trust from the Taboo Network | Must find your own way into headquarters + risk of discovery |
| **Double agent** | Both sides' rewards (possibly) | If discovered by either side — worst case: your tower is sealed |
| **Publicly refuse both** | Remain neutral — but lose both sides' trust and opportunities | Miss key plot threads + Marcus's secret remains forever unknown |

### Scene 1-2: Entering Watcher Headquarters

**Watcher Headquarters** — a grey-white granite fortress at the center of Arcadia City. All visitors must pass three checks:

**First: Identity Verification** (automatic pass — if you accept the Watcher invitation; if sneaking in secretly, Craft DC 18 to forge documents or Social DC 16 to persuade the guard)

**Second: Magical Security Scan** (Will DC 14 to resist — the scan detects your Corruption value. If Corruption ≥ 5, the Watchers grow suspicious of you — all subsequent social checks have Disadvantage)

**Third: Item Inspection** (all magical items must be registered. If contraband Taboo materials are found — confiscated on the spot + a warning)

**GM Hint:** If the player has a Companion (such as Lena), the Watchers do not allow the Companion into interior areas — the Companion must wait in the reception room. This means the player is **alone** in the following scenes. This is intentional — to heighten tension.

### Scene 1-3: Meeting Marcus

**Marcus Flintlock (馬庫斯·燧發槍):**
- Research style: Empirical (but secretly dabbles in Taboo research)
- Attributes: Perception 5, Theory 7, Craft 6, Will 6, Erudition 5 (total 29)
- Status: Locked in an anti-magic cell, wearing magic-suppression shackles. He does not look afraid — he looks angry.
- His words (if the player mentions "the Mirror"):
  > Marcus's eyes sharpen suddenly. "The 'Mirror' sent you? Then fine. Listen — I have no time to explain it all. The Watchers found a **Void Shard** in my tower. An ordinary Void Shard. But this one is different — it is *growing*. Not absorbing magic — growing, getting bigger and stronger on its own. That's impossible. A Void Shard is a fragment torn from the Starweave — it is dead magical residue. It should not grow."
  >
  > "Unless… there is a living source feeding it. On the other side of the Veil."

**Key intelligence Marcus reveals:**
1. The growing Void Shard is still in his tower — the Watchers could not bring it out ("It's resisting — they dare not touch it")
2. His tower is now sealed by the Watchers, but it holds a secret passage — through a hidden scrying pool
3. He believes this shard connects to the lingering consciousness of the Void God (虛無之神) — "He is not dead. He is sleeping. And something — or someone — is waking Him."
4. The Watchers conceal this because: if the public knew the Void God might awaken, it would trigger panic — and a new round of witch-hunts

## Act Two: Infiltrating the Sealed Tower

### Scene 2-1: The Secret Passage

**Marcus's instructions:**
> "My tower is in the East District, near the old city wall. Watchers guard the front. But I built a hidden scrying pool in the cellar — it connects directly to an abandoned mill outside the city. Go to the mill's basement, find a wall carved with three concentric circles — that is the door."

**Finding the passage (Perception DC 14):**

- **Success:** Find the mill + activate the scrying pool (requires spending 5 Spirit — the pool's far end is still operating)
- **Failure:** Waste hours among the mill's ruins. Watcher patrol frequency increases (later stealth DC +1)

### Scene 2-2: Marcus's Tower

**Environment:** The tower interior is sealed by magic — all spell Spirit costs +1 (seal interference). Light is dim — only emergency runes emit a faint blue glow. The tower's HP is already damaged by the accident (current HP = half of max HP).

**Core challenge — approaching the Void Shard:**

The tower has three floors:

**First floor (Laboratory):**
- Condition: Most equipment is damaged. Black cracks from Void damage scar the walls.
- Discoverable (Perception DC 12): Marcus's emergency notes — hastily written on the wall: "It is not a shard. It is a seed."

**Second floor (Library):**
- Condition: A **Void Echo (★ threat)** roams the library — a condensate of energy leaked from the Void Shard.

  #### Enemy: Void Echo
  | Attribute | Value |
  |-----------|-------|
  | HP | 20 (solo-adjusted) |
  | Armor Class (AC) | 14 |
  | Speed | 0 (fixed position — center of the library) |
  | Attack (Void Pulse) | 2d10+3 Void, range 30 ft, on hit target Will DC 12 or Corruption +1 (temporary — cleared after a Long Rest) |
  | Special Ability | Void Drain — at the start of each round, if the player is within 20 ft, Spirit –1 |
  | Weakness | Radiant damage ×1.5; a Life spell can dispel it (Craft DC 14 — "you can neutralize it with the Life element") |

  **Tactical Hints:** Do not linger in battle — use Radiant or Life spells to dispel it quickly. Direct attacks waste Spirit and time.

**Third floor (Containment Room — where the shard is):**
- The core room. At its center floats a fist-sized black crystal — deep purple light pulsing slowly within, like a heartbeat.
- **Environmental effect:** Each round spent in the room, Spirit –1. Will DC 15 — on failure you hear whispers ("Come… touch me… I can give you… knowledge…")

**Methods to obtain the Void Shard:**

| Method | Check | Effect |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Careful Extraction** | Craft DC 16 + requires a specialized reagent | Safely obtain the Void Shard (Rare) + the shard stops growing |
| **Theory Taming** | Theory DC 18 (3 consecutive successes, each costing 1 Action Point (AP)) | Tame the shard — obtain the Void Shard (Rare) + research insight into the Void Shard |
| **Forced Capture** | Craft DC 20 (one-time) | On success obtain the shard; on failure take 3d10 Void damage + Corruption +3 |
| **Study Instead of Capture** | Theory + Erudition DC 16 | Do not extract the shard, but conduct one full on-site observation — gain unique insight into the Void Shard's growth mechanism (Academic Reputation +3d6, future Void research DC –5) |

**If you choose to study rather than capture:**
> You spend two hours in the Containment Room, recording data on every pulse of the shard. Then you see it — the pulse pattern is not random. It is responding. Responding to some source on the other side of the Veil.
>
> The Void God is not dead. He is not asleep. His lingering consciousness is scattered across dozens of Void Shards — and these shards are **reconverging**. The reason this shard grows is that other Void Shards are drawing near it — like fragmented consciousness trying to reassemble.
>
> This is not a scientific problem. It is a countdown.

## Act Three: The Price of Trust

### Scene 3-1: Report and Choice

After returning to Watcher headquarters (or the Taboo Network's contact point), the player must decide what to tell whom:

**If the player reports all findings to the Watchers:**
- Watcher Captain **Olivia Ironwall (奧莉薇亞·鐵壁)** (Will 8, zero tolerance for Taboo): She is silent a long time. Then says, "Thank you for your honesty. But now you know too much. We have two choices — you become our formal advisor, sign a confidentiality agreement, and handle this with us. Or…"
- Choose to become an advisor: Watcher favor + future tower gains Watcher protection (monthly 10% chance of attack reduced to 0) + 100 GP monthly stipend — but you can no longer freely conduct Void research
- Choose to refuse: The Watchers place your tower on a "watch list" — 20% chance per month of inspection. But you keep your freedom

**If the player hands the shard to the Taboo Network:**
- "The Mirror" appears via a magic mirror-image — a blurry humanoid outline, voice of indeterminate gender. "You did well. The Void Shard is yours — that was the agreed payment. But the intelligence you brought back is more valuable. The Void God is reassembling? That changes everything."
- Taboo Network trust rises sharply. You gain "Core Member of the Network" status — 20% off at the black market, priority purchase rights on rare Taboo materials. But Watcher suspicion of you increases.

**If the player chooses to be a double agent (concealing from both and giving each partial truth):**
- This requires extremely high skill. Conceal the shard's existence from the Watchers: Social DC 18. Conceal your cooperation with the Watchers from the Taboo Network: Social DC 16.
- If both succeed: gain double the reward — but this is only the beginning. You now hold inside-contact status in two opposing organizations. In any future scenario, your choices may alter both organizations' fates.

### Scene 3-2: Watcher or Taboo Network Follow-up

**Watcher route follow-up:**
- Olivia privately gives you a file — on all reports of Void Shard "anomalous behavior" over the past fifty years. Extremely valuable research material.
- You gain a new long-term research goal: "Understand the structure of the Void God's lingering consciousness" — completing this goal requires gathering more threads in Scenarios Four and Five.

**Taboo Network route follow-up:**
- "The Mirror" gives you a task: find the locations of other "growing Void Shards." There are seven in total — corresponding to the seven Weavers. Each shard connects to a different part of the other side of the Veil.
- This is a complete thread-chain running through to Scenario Five.

## Ending Branches

| Ending | Condition | Reward |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **The Watcher's Advisor** | Report all to the Watchers + accept the advisor post | Watcher protection + stipend + research file + loss of freedom to conduct Void research |
| **Core Member of the Network** | Hand the shard and intelligence to the Taboo Network | Void Shard (Rare) ×1 + Core Member status + black-market 20% discount + Watcher watch list |
| **The Double-Agent Master** | Successfully conceal from both sides | Double reward (stipend + shard) + double-agent status (long-term plot) |
| **The Keeper of Knowledge** | Choose to study the shard rather than capture it | Academic Reputation +3d6 + Void research DC permanent –5 + the shard may still be obtained later |
| **The Caught Spy** | Double-agent fails (either side) | Lose the shard + blacklisted by that organization + Academic Reputation –2d6 |
| **The Void Seed** | Successfully capture the Void Shard | Void Shard (Rare) ×1 + the shard is still growing (may upgrade to Legendary-grade later — but requires more research) |

**If the player has a Companion:** the Companion's role throughout the scenario depends on type —

- **Lena (mercenary):** While waiting in the reception room she chats with other Watcher soldiers, picking up some Watcher internal gossip — she passes it to you (extra intelligence ×1)
- **Chief Apprentice:** While left guarding the tower, independently completes a small experiment — gains a common material (a pleasant surprise)
- **Ally mage (such as Victor):** If relations are good, he can help analyze Marcus's shard data — DC –2
