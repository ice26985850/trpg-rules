# Scenario Five: "Rift of the Starweave"

| Attribute | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Suggested Quality** | ★★★★★ Legendary-grade |
| **Player Count** | 1–4 mages |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 4–6 (can serve as the campaign finale) |
| **Core Output** | Multiple Legendary Shards, completion of a Legendary spell, world-level impact |
| **Primary Threat** | The lingering consciousness of the Void God, the Watchers' ultimate trial, the players' own research goals |
| **Prerequisite** | At least one player with Academic Reputation ≥60; and at least the theoretical preparation of their own research goal completed |

## Background

If enough Legendary Shards gather in one place—if a sufficiently powerful spell is created—the Starweave (星辰之織) itself becomes unstable. As the players approach completion of their ultimate research goal, they begin to notice some anomalies:

- The Observation Deck Lv5 reported an unexplained magical fluctuation—the source is at the Void Rift
- All spells containing Void Shards resonate unusually when cast
- The Watcher Council's highest alert was triggered—an alert that had only been triggered once before, at the last Abyssal Gate incident

Something on the other side of the veil has awakened.

## Act One: The Call from the Void

### Scene: The Players' Tower

A consciousness—ancient, powerful, imprisoned for millennia—sends a message to the players through the rift in the Starweave:

> *"Help me. I can give you ultimate knowledge."*

This is no ordinary Void entity. This is the lingering consciousness of the **seventh Weaver—the Void God (虛無之神)**. When He was sealed, the other six Weavers (守織者) failed to completely destroy Him—a part of His consciousness was scattered across dozens of Void Shards. Now these Shards are being activated by the players' research, and His consciousness is beginning to reassemble.

### Key Sources of Information

**Valastra (Dragon Elder)**:
> "The Void God is not evil—He is simply misunderstood. What He tried to do—unite all the holes into one—is not destruction in essence, but creation. The creation of a state of 'perfect magic.' The problem is, perfect magic means the end of magic—because magic is magic precisely because of its imperfection."

**The Void God's consciousness** (contacted through dreams or a scrying pool):
> "I gave you the Shards. Without My void-holes, your magic is only half real. What you call 'spell combinations'—are merely patching together My legacy. I can teach you true magic."

## Act Two: The Key Choice

The players must make the most important choice of the entire campaign:

### Option A: Release the Void God
- Travel to the Void Rift
- Use the Legendary spell the players created to open the seal
- The Void God returns—the world changes

### Option B: Seal the Void God Completely
- Collect the remaining Void Shards
- Use a special Legendary spell (must contain the three elements Radiant + Void + Life)
- Permanently banish the Void God's lingering consciousness

### Option C: Merge with the Void God
- The Taboo style's ultimate path
- Fuse the Void God's lingering consciousness into oneself
- Gain near-divine power—but lose humanity

### Option D: Negotiate Coexistence
- Neither fully release nor fully seal
- Establish a "dialogue window"—the Void God can communicate with the world in a limited way, but cannot act freely
- The most difficult path—requires persuading all factions (Watchers, Academy, Dragons) to accept this plan

## Act Three: Converging Power

Whatever path is chosen, the players need to converge enough power:

### Support That Must Be Gained (at least 3/5)

| Faction | How to Gain Support | Difficulty Class (DC) / Condition |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Watcher Council | Prove your plan is the safest | Persuade Olivia (opposed Will/Social check, Difficulty Class (DC) 22) |
| Dragon Elders Council | Prove you understand the essence of the Starweave | Show Valastra your Legendary spell design |
| Arcadia Academy | Publish a Legendary-grade paper on your discovery | Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 24 |
| Taboo Network | Promise Void research will be legalized | Persuade "The Mirror" (opposed Social, Difficulty Class (DC) 20) |
| Pure Fire | This is the only moment they might ally with you—if you can persuade them of "complete sealing" | Extremely difficult (Difficulty Class (DC) 28)—but a Critical Success gains powerful anti-magic support |

### Prerequisite Quest: Collect the Final Legendary Shards

The players need one **Void Legendary Shard (◈)** and one **Radiant Legendary Shard (◈)** to complete the ultimate spell.

**Obtaining the Void Shard—"Heart of the Void"**:
- Location: the core of the Void Rift—requires crossing the rift into the Void Domain
- Challenge: maintain self-awareness in a domain with no concept of time or space
- Will check, Difficulty Class (DC) 22, three times in a row (each failure: Corruption +2)
- Success: obtain the ultimate Void Shard + a unique insight into the Void Domain

**Obtaining the Radiant Shard—"The First Light"**:
- Location: the oldest node of the Starweave—requires entering the deep Spirit Realm
- Challenge: find the legacy left by Selena (賽琳娜), the first mage in legend—her original Shard
- Combined Theory + Perception check, Difficulty Class (DC) 24, both must succeed
- Success: obtain Selena's original Radiant Shard ◈ + Selena's memory (permanent Theory +1)

## Act Four: The Final Rite

### Scene: The Void Rift—the Edge of the Starweave

All faction representatives are present. The players stand before the rift, holding all the Legendary Shards they collected throughout the campaign.

**Ultimate spell combination** (created by the players with the GM's assistance):
- Minimum requirement: 5 Shards—at least 2 Legendary-grade
- Quality: ★★★★★ Legendary-grade
- Spirit Pool cost: estimated 64+ (ritual halves it to 32+)
- GM note: this is the final spell creation—give the players maximum creative freedom. Any reasonable combination should be allowed.

### Ritual Phases

**Phase 1: Establish Resonance** (Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 20, all assistants may provide bonuses)
- Build a resonance array using all the players' Legendary Shards
- Success: the rite formally begins, the Shards begin to glow in sync

**Phase 2: Weave the Spell** (Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 22)
- Actually execute the ultimate spell's Shard combination
- Failure: the connections between Shards become unstable—requires an additional Will check, Difficulty Class (DC) 20 to maintain (failure: the Shards begin to crumble—each Shard has a 30% chance of being destroyed)

**Phase 3: Release** (Will, Difficulty Class (DC) 24)
- Release the ultimate spell—your entire Spirit Pool + possibly overdraft
- Critical Success (double 10): the spell's effect reaches 1.5× the design—not only achieves the goal, but has an extra positive side effect
- Critical Failure (double 1): the spell goes out of control—roll d6:
  1–2: goal not achieved, all Legendary Shards destroyed
  3–4: goal partially achieved, but the player gains permanent Corruption +5
  5–6: goal reversed—seal becomes release, release becomes seal—but the effect is weakened

### Ultimate Endings

**A. Release the Void God**:
- The Void God returns, the magical world undergoes fundamental change
- All mages gain the right to use "Void" element Shards (no longer Taboo)
- The players' tower becomes the center of a new magical era
- Academic Reputation directly rises to 100
- But the world is no longer the same—the GM may describe the appearance of the new world

**B. Seal Completely**:
- The Void God's threat is permanently eliminated
- The players gain the title "Guardian"
- All participating factions permanently reconcile—an unprecedented cooperation
- A memorial hall may be built on the original site of the Void Rift
- Academic Reputation rises to 90, Practical Reputation rises to 90

**C. Merge with the Void God**:
- The player becomes the new Void God—or rather, the new vessel of the Void God
- All attribute caps raised to 15
- Gains the ability to create new Shards (may create one Shard of any rarity per month)
- But all other factions may become enemies—you are now the object they need to "guard"
- Risk of becoming an NPC—if Corruption exceeds 20 points (even if not reached before)

**D. Negotiate Coexistence**:
- The hardest to achieve, but the wisest path
- The Void God gains limited communication ability—becoming a kind of "cosmic advisor"
- Void research is legalized—but must proceed under supervision
- Academic Reputation +20, Practical Reputation +20, relations with all factions improve
- Gains the title "Reconciler"—jointly respected by all factions
