| Attribute | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Suggested Quality** | ★★★–★★★★ Expert-grade to Master-grade |
| **Player Count** | 2–4 mages |

## Background

Deep in the eternal-winter ice plains of the north, an ancient fire dragon named **Ignarath (伊格納瑞斯)** is growing old. An emissary of the Dragon Elders Council—**Valastra (瓦拉斯特拉)**—has issued a rare invitation: to allow human mages to participate in the dragon's "death rite."

A dragon's death is not an end—they condense a lifetime of magical essence into a Legendary Shard, then their body returns to the elements and to the Starweave (星辰之織). Ignarath is willing to give this Shard to "a human who can prove themselves worthy."

But the news has leaked. At least three mage teams are rushing to the eternal-winter ice plains—including the players' rivals.

## Act One: Valastra's Trials

### Scene: The Ice Hall of the Dragon Elders Council

Valastra is a silver dragon three thousand years old, who receives the players in human form (an old white-haired woman).

> "Ignarath's Shard may be the purest Flame Shard ever known. But the legacy of the dragon does not belong to the greedy. To prove your worth—you must first prove your knowledge."

**Trial contents** (each player must complete at least one):

1. **Knowledge Trial** (Erudition or Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 18): answer three of Valastra's questions about dragon history and magical theory
2. **Craft Trial** (Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 18): stabilize an unstable element sample by hand, without using magical tools
3. **Will Trial** (Will, Difficulty Class (DC) 18): gaze directly into a dragon illusion—Ignarath's millennia of memory—without losing yourself

**Success reward**: players who pass the trial gain Valastra's recognition and the "Dragon-Speaker Mark"—+1 to spell attack when using Flame Shards.

**Failure consequence**: players who fail the trial may still participate, but all their checks are at Disadvantage in the challenges ahead ("the dragons are watching you").

## Act Two: Crossing the Ice Plains

### Zone 1: Icewind Canyon

**Threat**: Extreme cold weather and ice-element creatures.

**Environmental effect**: for every 4 hours marching in the ice plains, Constitution, Difficulty Class (DC) 12 or gain one level of Fatigue.

**Discoverable items** (Perception, Difficulty Class (DC) 15):
- Ice-element crystal (precursor material for an Uncommon/Rare Frost Shard)
- Ancient dragon rune-stone (records dragon history—Erudition, Difficulty Class (DC) 14 to decipher, worth 150 Gold Pieces (GP) or usable for an academic paper +1d6 Reputation)

### Zone 2: Entrance to Ignarath's Lair

**Narration**: A gigantic cave entrance. Dragon runes are carved all over the cave walls, emitting a faint red glow. From deep in the cave comes a deep breathing sound—like the earth's crust moving.

**Second trial**: a dragon guardian stands before the cave entrance—a young fire dragon, **Zephyros (澤菲羅斯)**.

> "Ignarath will only see those who can prove their understanding of fire. Show me—your spells."

**Trial**: each player must display a spell containing the Fire element. Zephyros judges based on the spell's characteristics and creativity:

| Judging Criterion | Passing Threshold |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Apprentice-grade Flame spell | Barely passes—Zephyros is unimpressed but lets you through |
| Spell with cross Fire-element | Zephyros is pleased—future Fire, Difficulty Class (DC) –1 |
| Spell created with a Legendary-grade Flame Shard | Zephyros is shocked—takes you directly to Ignarath |

## Act Three: The Dragon's Death Rite

### Scene: Ignarath

Ignarath is an ancient fire dragon over two hundred feet long. His scales have faded from bright red to ashen white, and every breath is accompanied by the surging of magma. But his gaze is still sharp.

> "I have seen a thousand winters, a thousand summers. I watched humans walk out of caves, learn fire—then learn to tame it. Now, only one of you may carry away my flame."

### Competition—Other Mage Teams

Ignarath permits all mage teams that arrive to participate. The players will face:

**Team A: Academy Delegation of Albert Aurelian**
- Elite researchers of the Arcadia Royal Academy of Magic
- Leader: **Lydia Flameheart (莉迪亞·炎心)** (Empirical style, Theory 8, Craft 7, specializes in Fire)
- Advantage: top-tier research equipment and academic resources
- Weakness: over-reliance on standardized processes, lacks creativity

**Team B: Elite of the Taboo Researchers**
- A two-person team sent by the underground network
- Leader: **"Ashes" (灰燼)** (Taboo style, Will 8, Theory 7, Corruption 12)
- Advantage: stops at nothing—their experiments are at Difficulty Class (DC) –4 and use Taboo acceleration
- Weakness: high Corruption value may trigger catastrophic consequences at any time

**Team C: The players' rivals** (imported from character sheets)

### Core Challenge—Stabilizing Ignarath's Death Shard

After Ignarath begins the rite, his magical essence is released in an unstable form. Who can stabilize and capture it within the time limit?

**Challenge mechanic** (time limit: 5 rounds):

Each round, all teams simultaneously make one Craft check (with their own style modifier). The team with the most cumulative successes obtains the Shard. But—

**Round 3 event**: Ignarath's magic release goes out of control—area-wide Fire damage 3d6 (Dexterity, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 for half). At this point "Ashes" will attempt to forcibly stabilize the Shard using Taboo methods—this may cause the rite to collapse.

**Player choices**:
1. **Compete to the end**: continue your own stabilization attempt (check, Difficulty Class (DC) 20)
2. **Stop Ashes**: use spells to oppose and block the Taboo operation (spell collision or opposed Will, Difficulty Class (DC) 18)
3. **Assist Ignarath**: use Life or Radiant spells to help the dragon stabilize itself (Theory, Difficulty Class (DC) 16)

**Critical Success branch**: the player uses a spell containing both Radiant and Fire elements—perfectly balancing the dragon's chaotic flame. Ignarath recognizes the player's skill and wisdom. The Legendary Shard automatically belongs to the player, and the other teams are convinced.

**"Ashes" Corruption Eruption branch** (if Ashes is not stopped in Round 3): the Corruption value hits the 15-point threshold—Ashes's Shard is replaced with a corrupted version, the rite collapses, and the entire area takes 6d6 Fire + 2d8 Void damage. Ignarath forcibly interrupts the rite—the Shard is incomplete (downgraded to Rare-grade).
