# Scenario Four: "The Academic Trial"

| Attribute | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Suggested Quality** | ★★★–★★★★ Expert-grade to Master-grade |
| **Player Count** | 2–5 mages (may be filled by non-player mage NPCs) |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 3–4 |
| **Core Output** | Large amounts of Academic Reputation, school advancement abilities, political influence |
| **Primary Threat** | Academic politics, reputation attacks, possible ostracism |
| **Prerequisite** | At least one player with Academic Reputation ≥30 |

## Background

The Arcadia Royal Academy of Magic (阿卡迪亞皇家魔法學院) is about to hold its quinquennial **Grand Council**—the most important academic conference, which will decide the research directions, funding allocation, and—most importantly—**the three seats of the Watcher Council** for the next five years.

This Grand Council is especially tense because a controversial issue will be put to a vote: whether to comprehensively ban independent mage towers. The Academy faction argues that "all magical research should proceed under Academy supervision," while the independent mages—including the players—will bear the brunt.

## Act One: Pre-Council Maneuvering

### Scene: One Week Before the Council

The players receive notice that the Grand Council is about to convene. At the same time they receive:

- **The Academy faction's open letter**: calling for "all independent research to be brought into the Academy review system, for the safety of magical research and academic standards"
- **The independent mages' joint letter**: requesting the players join the "Free Alliance of Independent Research"—a loose coalition formed by independent mages

**Preparation phase** (players have 2–3 Action Points (AP) to use before the Grand Council):

| Action | Action Point (AP) | Effect |
|--------|-----|--------|
| Write a position paper | 2 | Academic +1d3; the paper will be cited at the Grand Council |
| Visit key NPCs | 1–2 | Build alliances, learn each side's stance |
| Gather blackmail on rivals | 2 | Erudition or Social, Difficulty Class (DC) 15—obtain leverage over rivals |
| Prepare a live demonstration | 2 | Prepare a spell for the conference presentation |
| PR campaign | 1 | Practical +1d3—gain public support for independent mages |

### Key NPC Stances

| NPC | Stance | Can Be Won Over |
|-----|--------|-----------------|
| Headmaster Aurelian | Neutral, leans Academy | Can be won with a major research achievement |
| Duo School representative | Supports independent mages | Natural ally |
| Classical Society representative | Academy faction | Almost impossible to win over |
| Watcher representative | Undecided—depends on who can guarantee safety | Can be won with the argument "independent research is harder to collectively conceal accidents" |
| Shard Hunter Guild | Strongly supports independent mages | Natural ally—they live off independent mages |
| Noble patron | Pragmatist—who can provide military value? | Can be won with Practical Reputation and results of military commissions |

## Act Two: The Grand Council (Three Days)

### Day One—Opening and Papers

**Core mechanic**: each paper earns "Academic Influence Points"; accumulated influence decides the final vote.

The players may:
- **Present a paper** (Theory or Erudition check): Difficulty Class (DC) depends on the paper's topic and Shard quality. Success earns influence.
- **Challenge others' papers** (opposed Theory check): winner gains influence, loser loses it.
- **Live spell demonstration** (Craft check): high risk high reward—success doubles reputation, failure doubles the loss.

**Calculating influence**:
- Each successful paper: +1d3 influence (Legendary-grade paper +3d6+5)
- Challenge victory: +1d6 influence
- Successful live demonstration: +2d6 influence
- Opponent's paper fails: equivalent influence transfers to your name

### Day Two—Debate and Intrigue

**Core event: the academic debate**—"Independent Mage Towers: Bastions of Freedom or Hotbeds of Danger?"

Each side sends three representatives. Players may voluntarily become debaters for the independent mages' side.

**Debate mechanic** (best-of-three format):
- Each round: opposed Theory check (debate skill + Academic Reputation modifier)
- Extra bonus: prepared position paper (+1 per paper); gathered blackmail (may be revealed when the opponent speaks, giving the opponent Disadvantage)
- Each round's winner gains +2 influence for their side

**Intrigue event** (evening of Day Two):

Roll d6 to determine:
1. The players' tower is infiltrated during the conference—lose 1 random Shard
2. A rival's apprentice tries to tamper with the players' spell-demonstration materials
3. Anonymous letter—"If you publicly support X tomorrow, you will find something interesting"
4. Private Watcher interview—"We know what you did in Ashville last month"
5. Noble patron offers a huge donation—on condition you vote for the Academy faction
6. An unexpected ally appears—an NPC the players thought neutral actually supports independent mages

### Day Three—Voting and the Decisive Battle

**Final sprint before the vote**: each side makes its last lobbying effort.

**Influence conversion**:

| Total Influence | Voting Result |
|-----------------|---------------|
| <10 | Comprehensive ban on independent mage towers—all independent towers must join the Academy system within 6 months |
| 10–19 | Compromise plan—independent mages must accept annual Watcher inspections |
| 20–29 | Maintain the status quo—freedom of independent research unrestricted |
| 30+ | Strengthen protection of independent research—the Academy may not restrict independent mages' research freedom, and must open library resources to independent mages |

**If the vote fails** (influence <10): this is not the end—but the starting point of an academic war. Transition to the "Academic War" scenario framework.

**If the vote succeeds** (influence ≥30): a total victory. The players become iconic figures of the independent mages' movement.

## Act Three: Consequences

**Chain reactions of the voting result**:

**If independent research is successfully protected**:
- Academic Reputation surges (+2d6+10)
- May create an "Independent Research Association"—gain school rally ability (even without reaching 60+ Reputation)
- Watcher seat—players may be nominated to the Watcher Council
- Rivals' reaction: rivals may publicly apologize and seek cooperation, or hate them more and turn to Taboo research to try to surpass them

**If the compromise plan**:
- Academic Reputation grows moderately (+1d6+3)
- Must accept a Watcher inspection once every 4 rounds in the future
- Relations with the independent mages' extremist faction may become tense

**If comprehensive ban**:
- The players face a choice: submit (join the Academy system, lose some autonomy) or resist (go underground, effectively becoming Taboo researchers)
- May trigger the serial scenario "Academic War"
