# Scenario Two: "The Abyssal Gate"

| Attribute | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| **Suggested Quality** | ★★–★★★ Standard-grade to Expert-grade |
| **Player Count** | 2–4 mages |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 2–3 |
| **Core Output** | Void Shard (Rare) + Space Shard (Uncommon/Rare) |
| **Primary Threat** | Scrutiny by the Watcher Council, Taboo-research residue, extraplanar creatures |
| **Prerequisite** | At least one player with Academic Reputation ≥10 or of the Taboo style |

## Background

The Ashville (阿什維爾) Ruins—site of the great explosion in Year 1102 of the Era—have recently shown new magical fluctuations. The Watcher Council (守望者議會) is secretly organizing an expedition under the nominal pretext of an "academic survey," but its real purpose is to investigate whether Taboo researchers are conducting illegal experiments within the ruins.

But before that, an anonymous message spread through the Taboo Research Network: the Void rift deep in the ruins is still active. Occasionally it "spits out" extremely rare Void Shards—if one can reach them before the Watchers do.

## Act One: Two Invitations

The players receive two messages at the same time:

**Message A** (Watcher Council): A formal letter inviting "mages of good academic standing" to join the academic expedition to the Ashville Ruins. Participants receive a research grant of 200 Gold Pieces (GP) and priority access to the expedition's findings.

**Message B** (Taboo Network): A unsigned parchment appears on the players' laboratory table. A single line is written on it: "Third level of the ruins. Night of the new moon. Bring an empty Shard container."

**Player Choices**:
- Accept the Watcher invitation → Official route, low risk low reward
- Follow the Taboo lead → High risk high reward, but may be discovered by the Watchers
- Participate in both → Double agent—highest risk, but also highest reward (though the consequences are severe once discovered)

## Act Two: Into the Ruins

### Zone 1: Ruins Surface—Watcher Camp

If the players choose the official route:

**Watcher Expedition NPCs**:
- **Captain Olivia Ironwall (奧莉薇亞·鐵壁)**: veteran Watcher, Will 8, zero tolerance for Taboo research
- **Researcher Tiberius (提比略)**: young Academy mage, Theory 7, excessive curiosity
- **Mercenary Captain Garrett (加雷特)**: responsible for protecting the expedition, Craft 7

At the camp, the players are assigned the task of mapping the magical residue of the ruins using Perception and Theory checks.

**Hidden Intelligence** (Perception, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 or Erudition, Difficulty Class (DC) 14):
> While drawing the map you notice an anomaly: the magical residue pattern on the third level of the ruins does not match a standard explosion residue. The explosion radiated outward from the center—but the magical traces on the third level **converge from the outside inward**. This is not the mark of an explosion—it is the residue of some kind of **summoning ritual**.

If the players report this discovery to Olivia, she becomes abnormally nervous and forbids anyone from approaching the third level. If the players keep it secret, it becomes valuable intelligence.

### Zone 2: First Level—Magical Residue Zone

**Threat**: Residual magical energy has formed unstable elemental traps.

**Advance check** (each advance into a new zone requires one check):
- Perception, Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to detect traps
- Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to disarm or bypass
- Failure: random elemental damage 1d6 (roll d6: 1–2 = Fire, 3–4 = Frost, 5 = Lightning, 6 = Force)

**Discoverable items** (Perception, Difficulty Class (DC) 14):
- A fragment of Ashville's experiment log (Common-grade lore Shard)—contains records of failed attempts to stabilize the Void Shard
- A time-eroded but still usable rare material (Phoenix Ash—worth 100 Gold Pieces (GP))

### Zone 3: Second Level—Underground Laboratory

**Narration**: This was Ashville's main laboratory before the explosion. Charred runes are still embedded in the walls. In the drawers of the experiment tables are some sealed sample vials.

**Core challenge**: An **animated magical guardian**, activated by residual magic, guards the laboratory's core area.

#### Enemy: Animated Magical Guardian
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Hit Points (HP) | 40 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 14 |
| Attack | 2d10+4 Force, range 30 ft (spell attack) |
| Special Ability | Mana Absorption—whenever it takes spell damage, it recovers HP equal to half the caster's Theory value |
| Weakness | Physical attacks (non-magical weapons deal normal damage); can be disabled by Craft, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 (dismantle the core rune) |

**Tactical Hint**: The animated guardian is a test for mages—pure spell attacks will feed it. The players need to cooperate: one draws the guardian's attention while another finds and disables the core rune.

**Defeat reward**:
- Core Rune (Uncommon/Rare Constraint Shard—can be used as a "Cooldown" Constraint)
- Laboratory Log (Uncommon/Rare lore Shard—contains the correct method for extracting the Void Shard, Difficulty Class (DC) –2)

### Zone 4: Third Level—the Void Rift

**Narration**: The deepest part of the underground laboratory. Half the ceiling has collapsed, and moonlight pours in through the fissure. On the ground is a black crack about three feet long—the Void rift. It looks as if space itself has been torn open. The matter at the rift's edge is slowly "flowing"—inward, exactly as the players discovered at the camp.

**Core challenge**—obtaining the Void Shard:

**Method A: Extraction** (recommended for Empirical/Intuitive styles)
- Craft check, Difficulty Class (DC) 16 + rare material (Phoenix Ash or Dragon Scale)
- Success: obtain Void Shard (Rare-grade) + Space Shard (Uncommon/Rare-grade)
- Failure (within Safety Threshold): materials consumed, the Void rift becomes unstable (Difficulty Class (DC) +2 on next attempt)
- Critical Failure (exceeds Safety Threshold): the rift expands, releasing an extraplanar creature (see enemy below)

**Method B: Direct Grasp** (recommended for Taboo style)
- Will check, Difficulty Class (DC) 15 + Craft check, Difficulty Class (DC) 12 (Taboo style: Difficulty Class (DC) –4 = Difficulty Class (DC) 8)
- Success: obtain Void Shard (Rare-grade) + trigger for Taboo-style legend acquisition rate → may obtain 2
- Failure (within Safety Threshold): take 2d10 Void damage, Corruption +1
- Critical Failure (exceeds Safety Threshold): Corruption +3 + extraplanar creature released

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Hit Points (HP) | 60 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 16 |
| Speed | 40 ft (can short-range teleport) |
| Attack | 2d10+5 Void (spell attack, melee) |

**Tactical Hint**: This is the first truly dangerous enemy. The Rift Stalker prioritizes attacking the caster with the highest Theory value ("smells strong magic"). The players need to use Radiant damage to limit its mobility and use Force spells to deal real damage.

## Act Three: Consequences

### Watcher Route
- Olivia "thanks" the players for their discovery—but demands they hand the Void Shard to the Watchers for "safe keeping"
- The players may: comply (lose the Shard but gain the Watchers' trust and 300 Gold Pieces (GP)) or refuse (keep the Shard but relations with the Watchers deteriorate)
- If the players' Taboo affiliation is discovered: Olivia issues a formal warning (Academic Reputation –2d6, placed on a watch list)

### Taboo Route
- The Taboo Network makes contact, stating "you have passed the test"
- Gain access to the black-market network—can purchase Taboo materials at 20% off in the future
- But the Watchers begin to suspect you (10% chance per month of a Watcher inspection)

### Double-Agent Route
- If successful in obtaining Shards from both sides without being discovered: double Shards + Taboo Network trust + Watcher stipend
- If discovered by the Watchers: worst case—formally prosecuted for "endangering public safety," the tower may be seized
- If discovered by the Taboo Network: placed on the "Untrustworthy List"—permanently lose access to the Taboo Network's channels

## Ending Branches

| Ending | Condition | Reward |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Academic Hero** | Official route + discovered the secret + complied with the Watchers | Watcher stipend 300 Gold Pieces (GP), Academic +2d6, Watcher favor |
| **Taboo Scholar** | Taboo route + successfully obtained the Shard | Void Shard (Rare) + Space Shard (Uncommon/Rare), black-market access, Taboo Network trust |
| **Double-Agent Master** | Succeeded on both sides and undiscovered | Double Shards + double trust + Academic +2d6, Practical +1d6 |
| **Rift Releaser** | Experiment Critical Failure triggered extraplanar creature | May lose Shards, but defeating the Rift Stalker yields the "Void Core" (a precursor to a Legendary material) |
| **Watcher Wanted** | Double agent discovered | Lose all Shards, tower may be sealed, must flee into the wilderness |
