## 2.5 GM Spell Review Guidelines

### Review Checklist

1. **Combination Reasonableness**: encourage "Yes, but the effect is..."
2. **Effect Cap**:

| Quality | Damage Cap |
|---------|------------|
| ★ | 15 |
| ★★ | 30 |
| ★★★ | 50 |
| ★★★★ | 80 |
| ★★★★★ | No cap |

3. **Side Effect Determination**: internally contradictory combinations (Life + Void) → require first side effect; 3+ rare shards → require a check
4. **Differentiation Encouragement**: differs from existing spells in ≥2 dimensions → Spirit –1
5. **Naming Reward**: creative naming → first casting Spirit –1 or attack/DC +1

## 2.6 HP and Death (GM Reference)

### Player HP

- Max HP = 15 + Will
- HP increases in tandem when Will increases; at attribute 8, +5 HP; at Laboratory Lv5, +5 HP
- Max HP is roughly 35

### Death Saving Throw

When player HP drops to 0, they enter Dying. Each turn 2d10 (no modifier):

- ≥10: one success. Accumulate 3 → Stabilized
- <10: one failure. Accumulate 3 → Dead
- Double 1: two failures. Double 10: recover to 1 HP and awaken

### Rest Adjudication

- **Short Rest** (1 hour, safe environment): recover HP equal to Will + half Spirit. At most 2 Short Rests per Long Rest.
- **Long Rest** (8 hours, safe location): recover all HP + all Spirit + remove 1 level of Fatigue.
- **Wilderness Camping**: Perception or Craft DC 10. Failure → Long Rest effect halved. Critical Failure → nighttime encounter.

### Fatigue

Each 24 hours without a Long Rest → +1 Fatigue level: 1 = all checks –1; 2 = speed halved; 3 = all checks at Disadvantage; 4 = HP halved; 5 = unable to act; 6 = death.

### Mage Tower HP

```
Mage Tower Max HP = 30 + Defense System Level × 5
```

- When Mage Tower HP drops to 0: all facilities disabled, requires major repair (cost = total facility levels × 200 Gold Pieces (GP), 2d4 turns)
- Mage Tower maintenance action recovers 1d6 Mage Tower HP
- The Defense System's defense value (5/10/15/20/30) is damage reduction — damage dealt by intruders is reduced by the defense value first, then subtracted from Mage Tower HP

## 2.7 Unified Five-Attribute System

### Enemies and NPCs Use the Same Attributes

All creatures (players, NPCs, enemies) uniformly use the five attributes: **Perception, Theory, Craft, Will, Erudition**.

Old-version enemy attribute conversion:

- Strength → **Craft** (physical strength and combat skill merged)
- Dexterity → **Craft** (agile movement and precise operation merged)
- Constitution → **Will** (physical resilience and mental resilience merged)

### Attitude Changes

- Social check success: attitude rises one level (cap per single interaction is Friendly)
- Critical Success: attitude rises two levels + gains extra intel
- Failure: attitude unchanged, next attempt DC +2
- Critical Failure: attitude drops one level, cannot attempt again that day
- Complete the other party's commission: attitude rises one level
- Deception exposed: attitude drops to Hostile

### Academic Debate Adjudication

Best-of-three contested Theory check:

- Each round: 2d10 + Theory + Proficiency
- Each position paper +1 (max +3)
- Expose a weakness: opponent at Disadvantage
- Winner +1d6+4 Academic Reputation; loser –1d6

## 2.13 Multiplayer Game Management

### Tower Network

By default each player owns an independent tower. The GM should decide before the campaign begins:

**Option A: Independent**

- Each player one tower, at different locations
- Communication: Scrying Pool Lv1+ allows mutual calls (10 Spirit per use)
- Teleport Gate: both sides' Scrying Pool Lv2+ can open (20 Spirit/hour)

**Option B: Joint Tower**

- All players share one tower
- Facility level: take the highest corresponding facility level among players
- Action Points: calculated independently per player
- Costs: shared by players

**Option C: Adjacent Towers**

- Each player builds towers near each other (e.g., all in the mage district of the same city)
- Normal individual management, but meetings have no travel cost

### Collective Research Rules

When players conduct research in the same tower:

- Each stage of the research loop can be assigned to different players (leverage each one's strengths)
- Experiment stage executed by highest Craft; Observe by highest Perception
- Assistants still spend half the Action Points (AP) of that stage (rounded up)
- Results (shards) distributed by team negotiation

### NPC Mages as Teammates

If there are not enough players, NPC mages can fill in. NPCs use the same attribute system as players, with total attributes 22–28 points, starting Reputation set appropriately.

## 2.17 Five Scenario Frameworks

### Lone Tower Research

Research milestones (3–5) → narrative events interspersed each turn → monthly settlement

### Collaborative Project

Lead check + assistant bonus → methodology conflict roleplay → team contribution bar

### Shard Hunter

Three-layer ruin: entrance (guards) → middle (puzzles) → core (legendary shard)

### Tower Defense

Four phases: detect (observation deck) → delay (defense system) → confront (spell battle) → consequences

### Academic War

Four phases: public divergence → resource contest → decisive battle (debate/demonstration) → consequences

> **GM Philosophy**: The core of Arcane Origins is "the pursuit of knowledge." Failure is not punishment; it is fuel for advancing the story. The best game moments often come from a catastrophic experiment accident. Let the players feel — they are not "grinding monsters to level up," but "doing research."
