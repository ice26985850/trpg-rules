# Scenario 2: Town Judgment Day (Standard · 3 Days)

## Scenario Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★☆☆ Standard |
| **Loop Length** | 3 days |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-4 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 13 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 8 + Action (ACT) Modifier (3 days × Action Points (AP)) |
| **Tone Tags** | Collective guilt / Complicit silence / The delay of justice |

## Loop Setting

- **Loop Endpoint:** Midnight on the third day; all townsfolk vanish collectively
- **Starting Moment:** 6:00 AM on the first day; the players wake up in the town's inn
- **Loop Boundary:** The entire town and its surrounding 3 km (old mine, forest edge)
- **Opening Narration:** "You wake up. Sunlight streams through the inn's old curtains. There's a smell of autumn in the air—dry leaves, woodsmoke in the distance. From downstairs comes the sound of the innkeeper's wife preparing breakfast. Everything is perfectly normal. Everything is perfectly peaceful. Except... you don't quite remember how you came to this little town."

## Core Truth

Fifteen years ago (1994), an outsider teacher named "Chen Yan" was lynched by the townsfolk. He was accused of "corrupting the town's youth." The real reason: Chen Yan had uncovered the mayor's financial scandal—the mayor and several merchants had embezzled government grants. His death was to silence him. For fifteen years, the entire town became an accomplice in silence.

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: At least one townsperson publicly admits Chen Yan was wrongly accused
- Required: The players find Chen Yan's diary from that year (hidden in the old schoolhouse basement)
- Optional: The players persuade the mayor to tell the truth at the gathering

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **The Last Lesson (Document / second floor of the old schoolhouse / Medium / Loop 2):** A letter Chen Yan wrote to his students the day before he died: "Tomorrow I'm going to tell you something important, about the future of this town."
2. **The Mayor's Alibi (Document / township office archives / Hard / Loop 4):** Phone records from October 1994—the mayor was not at the lynching scene that night.
3. **The Old Tailor's Dream (Conversation / Grandma Lin's tailor shop / Easy / Loop 1):** "I dreamed that young teacher came back again, standing in the square."
4. **The Marks Beneath the Square (Scene / town square / Medium / Loop 3):** At sunset on the third day, dark stains in the seams of the stone slabs.
5. **The Merchant's Ledger (Item / back room of the grocery / Medium / Loop 5):** Records of "special compensation" the grocery owner received after Chen Yan's death.
6. **The Student's Silence (Conversation / town high school / Hard / Loop 6):** Lin Xiao, Chen Yan's student from that year—a now high-school teacher—kept Chen Yan's diary.
7. **The Basement Diary (Item / old schoolhouse basement / Hard / Loop 7):** Chen Yan's diary, recording the process of uncovering the financial scandal. The last page: "They know. Something may happen tonight."
8. **The Truth of the Ritual (Memory / town square / Special / Loop 8+):** During the silent ritual, if ≥4 fragments have been obtained, a collective Déjà Vu is triggered—all players simultaneously see a flashback of that night fifteen years ago.

## Three Key NPCs

**Mayor Zhou Zhengyi (56):** Emotion baseline 4/10 (repressed anxiety), sensitivity "High." He knows Chen Yan was innocent, but believes telling the truth would destroy the town. Key shift: during Loops 7-9 he may say "I should have stood up back then."

**High School Teacher Lin Xiao (30):** Emotion baseline 5/10, sensitivity "Extremely High." Chen Yan's favorite student, who secretly reads the diary once a week in the basement. Key shift: in Loop 6 the first memory residue—unconsciously writing Chen Yan's name on the blackboard.

**Grocery Owner Wang Fugui (62):** Emotion baseline 6/10 (forcing cheerfulness), sensitivity "High." He took part in the lynching and took the compensation. Key shift: in Loop 8+ he may break down sobbing "I dream of him every night."

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-2 (Mirror):** A normal town. Let the players feel the "goodness." First glimpse of total wipe at midnight on the third day.
- **3 (Final Mirror):** The last normal loop. Fragments 3+4 can be obtained.
- **4-5 (Ripple):** The inn clock runs 3 minutes slow. The mayor forgets to order coffee with lunch. Fragments 2+5 can be obtained.
- **6 (Ripple→Crack):** Lin Xiao's first memory residue. Fragment 6 can be obtained.
- **7-9 (Crack):** A reflection from fifteen years ago appears in the old schoolhouse windows. The fountain water goes still. Fragments 1+7 can be obtained.
- **10-12 (Fracture):** Spatial jumps. Lin Xiao may become half-awakened. Fragment 8 can be obtained.
- **13 (Collapse):** The last chance. NPCs may tell the truth or remain silent forever.

## Three Endings

**A - Justice Delayed (Perfect):** 8 fragments + the mayor reveals the truth publicly + the diary is made public. No townsperson vanishes—the Rift heals.

**B - One Person's Farewell (Normal):** ≥6 fragments + Lin Xiao reads out the diary. The town vanishes but Lin Xiao remains. The Rift heals halfway.

**C - The Fifteenth Autumn (Bad):** Loops exhausted. The residents turn to statues, forever encircling the square. The players remember everything—and to remember is, in itself, meaning.
