# Scenario 4: The Infinite Meeting (Expert · 1 Day)

## Scenario Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★★★ Expert |
| **Loop Length** | 1 day (an 8-hour meeting) |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-4 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 10 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 4 (highly compressed: each Action Point (AP) ≈ 2 hours) |
| **Tone Tags** | The sins of capital / The price of silence / Individual responsibility within a power structure |

## Loop Setting

- **Loop Endpoint:** 4:00 PM; the CEO suffers a fatal heart attack, the meeting is forcibly ended
- **Starting Moment:** 8:00 AM; the players sit down in the meeting room, the meeting just beginning
- **Loop Boundary:** The entire 30-floor office building (the meeting room is on the 28th floor)
- **Opening Narration:** "The meeting room door closed behind you. Eight people in suits sit along the long table. Someone is looking at their phone. Someone is drinking coffee. CEO Chen Hongyuan taps the table—'Everyone, let's begin. Today's agenda: the final vote on the HuiDa acquisition.' He flips open the folder in front of him. You don't know what's inside. But you know—at four in the afternoon, he will die."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** CEO Chen Hongyuan's heart attack was no accident—he had been given a slow-acting poison by Director Zhao Yongchang before the meeting. Motive: the HuiDa acquisition three years ago—Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang joined forces to cook the books, leading to a small company called "HuiDa" being maliciously acquired and then dismantled. HuiDa's founder jumped to his death as a result. Zhao Yongchang is now the only one who knows the whole truth. He wants to kill Chen Hongyuan to silence him—and then bury the truth together with Chen Hongyuan in the grave.

The Rift formed at the instant Chen Hongyuan died. Not because he died—but because the truth died with him. The loop traps all who know, asking the same question: this time, will someone tell the truth?

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: Someone (any director) publicly points out the fraud in the acquisition before Chen Hongyuan dies
- Required: Expose the fact that Zhao Yongchang poisoned him
- Optional: Chen Hongyuan himself confesses and repents

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **The Original HuiDa Case Files (Document / 28th-floor archive room / Medium / Loop 2):** The real financial data of the HuiDa acquisition—off by 80 million from the public figures. Bearing the signatures of Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang.
2. **Zhao Yongchang's Medicine Bottle (Item / Zhao Yongchang's office / Hard / Loop 4):** A slow-acting toxin undetectable by routine autopsy. Zhao Yongchang "imported" it from abroad three days before the meeting. The shipping invoice is hidden in his office.
3. **Chen Hongyuan's Diagnosis (Document / Chen Hongyuan's office / Medium / Loop 3):** Chen Hongyuan's physical exam report from three months ago—his heart was perfectly healthy. He should never have had a heart attack.
4. **HuiDa Founder's Suicide Note (Item / HuiDa's old office / Special / Loop 5+):** A letter the HuiDa founder wrote before jumping. On the 30th floor (HuiDa's original office floor—cleared after the acquisition). It lists everything.
5. **The Directors' Secrets (Conversation / the various directors / Hard / Loop 6):** Every director knows a piece of the truth—but no one knows all of it. You need to persuade them individually: the CFO knows about the fake books, the Legal Director knows of procedural violations, the HR Director knows HuiDa's employees were illegally fired.
6. **Zhao Yongchang's Schedule (Document / front-desk computer / Easy / Loop 1):** Zhao Yongchang was still in his office at 11:00 PM the night before the meeting—surveillance shows he went to the pantry. In the pantry was a bottle with its label torn off.
7. **Chen Hongyuan's Letter of Repentance (Item / Chen Hongyuan's briefcase / Hard / Loop 7+):** A half-written letter from Chen Hongyuan: "I know I did wrong. The HuiDa matter will stay with me for life. I plan to, after this meeting—" The letter was never finished. What did he plan to do after the meeting? Turn himself in? Or keep hiding?
8. **The Meeting Room Clock (Scene / meeting room / Special / Loop 8+):** 3:58 PM—the clock stopped. All the clocks stopped. When all the truth fragments are assembled, the GM freezes time for 5 minutes (real time). Within those 5 minutes, the players may say anything to any NPC. After 5 minutes, time resumes—and Chen Hongyuan has his heart attack, or... doesn't.

## Five Key NPCs

**CEO Chen Hongyuan (62):** Emotion baseline 7/10 (forced authority), sensitivity "Extremely High"—he is the Anchor character. He is actually wracked with guilt, but must maintain his image in front of the directors. Key shift: in Loop 5+ he may suddenly pause mid-sentence, staring at his own hands.

**Director Zhao Yongchang (55):** Emotion baseline 8/10 (very confident—he knows Chen Hongyuan will die today), sensitivity "High." He is the only one who does not want the loop to end—every reset means he has to kill Chen Hongyuan again. Key shift: in Loop 4+ he begins to grow anxious ("Why won't he just die?").

**CFO Lin Meiling (45):** Emotion baseline 5/10 (nervous), sensitivity "Medium." She knows about the fake books but dares not speak—she needs this job. Secret: she secretly kept a copy of the original data.

**Legal Director Wang Jianguo (50):** Emotion baseline 6/10 (formulaic calm), sensitivity "Low." He knows of the procedural violations but tells himself "it's legal." Key shift: in Loop 6+ he begins to flip through the HuiDa case files—he knows what he's looking for but won't admit it.

**HuiDa Founder Zhou Ming (Deceased):** Emotion baseline N/A, sensitivity N/A. He does not exist in the meeting room. But he is the heaviest presence in this building. On the 30th floor (HuiDa's old office), you can occasionally see a figure in an old suit standing at the window. He does not speak. But he left that suicide note.

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-2 (Mirror):** A normal meeting. Everything proceeds by agenda. Chen Hongyuan has his heart attack right on schedule at 4:00.
- **3-4 (Ripple):** The conversations in the meeting begin to show subtle changes—something a director says differs from Loop 1. Zhao Yongchang starts fidgeting at 3:50.
- **5-6 (Crack):** The light in the meeting room begins to change—sometimes it suddenly turns to afternoon warmth (though it's clearly still morning). Once, Chen Hongyuan paused for 10 seconds mid-speech—everyone thought he'd lost his train of thought, but his expression was fear.
- **7-8 (Fracture):** The 30th floor begins to "seep into" the 28th—occasionally you can hear footsteps from above (the 30th floor has been cleared for three years). Zhao Yongchang's office door opens on its own—inside, a photo of the HuiDa founder sits on the desk (Zhao Yongchang doesn't remember placing it).
- **9-10 (Collapse):** The clock stops. All clocks freeze at 3:58—the moment two minutes later never arrives, unless someone tells the truth. Fragment 8 triggers. This is the last chance.

## Four Endings

**A - The Truth Comes Out (Perfect):** Expose Zhao Yongchang's poisoning + all directors publicly admit the fraud + Chen Hongyuan repents before death (or survives). Chen Hongyuan does not have the heart attack—or he does but is rescued in time. Zhao Yongchang is arrested. The HuiDa founder's suicide note is made public. The Rift heals.

**B - Justice Delayed (Normal):** The truth of the fake books is exposed but Chen Hongyuan's death could not be prevented. Chen Hongyuan says "I'm sorry" before dying. Zhao Yongchang's crime is exposed—but the judicial process takes time. The Rift heals halfway—the HuiDa founder's suicide note is found, but no one can apologize to him.

**C - Mutual Destruction (Hidden):** If the players choose to have Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang confront each other face to face—in anger and fear the two expose each other's crimes. Chen Hongyuan has his heart attack. Zhao Yongchang, after seeing Chen Hongyuan collapse, opens the window and jumps. Both die, but the truth is revealed. The Rift heals—at the cost of two lives.

**D - Everything As Before (Bad):** Loops exhausted. The meeting proceeds as usual. Chen Hongyuan dies as usual. Zhao Yongchang escapes as usual. The only difference—the players remember everything. The moment they step out of the office building, they see a faded slip of paper taped to the 30th-floor window: "No one will remember. No one will care." It is the HuiDa founder's last words.
