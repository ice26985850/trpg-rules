# Scenario 3: The Last School Festival (Advanced · 7 Days)

## Scenario Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★★☆ Advanced |
| **Loop Length** | 7 days |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-5 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 15 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 8 + Action (ACT) Modifier (7 days × Action Points (AP), roughly 56-77 Action Points (AP) total) |
| **Tone Tags** | The end of youth / Unspoken words / Doing what must be done even knowing the ending |

## Loop Setting

- **Loop Endpoint:** 3:00 PM on the seventh day (the day of the School Festival); a gas incident occurs at the school and the entire school dies
- **Starting Moment:** 7:00 AM, seven days before the School Festival (Monday); the players wake up in the classroom
- **Loop Boundary:** The entire school campus (including dorms, gymnasium, and the hill behind)
- **Opening Narration:** "You wake up at your desk. Your cheek is pressed against the cool desktop, a trace of drool still at the corner of your mouth. Written on the blackboard: '7 days until the School Festival.' The cherry blossoms outside the window are in bloom—but you remember the cherry trees shouldn't bloom until next week. Yet you can't recall what happened yesterday. Or the day before. Or why you're slumped over your desk."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** The true cause of the gas incident was not an accident, but a chain reaction triggered by the chemistry teacher to cover up a fatal lab accident. But the instant the Rift formed was not the gas—it was one student's regret in the final second before death: she/he never confessed to the person they had a crush on.

**Surface Story:** On the day of the School Festival, gas leaks from the lab—an accident; the whole school perishes.

**True Story:** Chemistry teacher Jiang Xue, during a secret experiment three days before the School Festival, caused a student who had stayed late (her teaching assistant) to inhale toxic gas through an operational error. She hid the accident, but the leak kept spreading. On the day of the School Festival, enough toxic gas had accumulated in the lab to spread throughout the school via the ventilation system.

But the Rift was not caused by the chemistry teacher. The Rift was caused by a student named "Su Mian"—the instant she collapsed in the gas, her only thought was: "I still haven't told Classmate Lin I like him." This regret tore reality apart.

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: Su Mian (or with the players' help) confesses to Classmate Lin
- Required: Expose the truth of the chemistry teacher's accident
- Optional: Prevent the gas from spreading on the day of the School Festival

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **Su Mian's Diary (Item / Su Mian's desk / Easy / Loop 1):** A diary written, torn up, and rewritten again and again. Every page has only one sentence: "Didn't say it today, either."
2. **The Locked Lab (Scene / chemistry lab / Medium / Loop 3):** The chemistry lab has been locked since three days before the School Festival—normally it should be open. A faint smell of bitter almonds seeps from the door gap.
3. **The Missing Assistant (Conversation / students / Easy / Loop 2):** The senior student "Zhou Yang" suddenly took leave four days before the School Festival—and was never seen again. The teachers say he "transferred."
4. **Jiang Xue's Overtime Records (Document / faculty office / Hard / Loop 4):** Chemistry teacher Jiang Xue was in the lab from three days before the School Festival until the night before. Her signature is on the attendance records—but it grows messier with each entry.
5. **The Ventilation System Map (Item / school administration office / Medium / Loop 5):** The school's ventilation system blueprint. The annotations show the chemistry lab's vents connect to the entire school—including the gymnasium (the main venue of the School Festival).
6. **Classmate Lin's Notebook (Item / library / Easy / Loop 1):** Classmate Lin borrowed a poetry collection five days before the School Festival. Tucked inside is a slip of paper: "Want to read this poem under the cherry tree to someone, on the day of the School Festival. If I have the courage."
7. **Su Mian's Final Moment (Memory / gymnasium / Special / Loop 7+):** At the instant the gas spreads, if a player is near Su Mian, the GM triggers a flashback: Su Mian's point of view—as she falls, she looks across the gymnasium at Classmate Lin, her lips moving but no sound coming out. The shape of her lips forms: "I like you."
8. **Jiang Xue's Confession (Conversation / triggered / Hard / Loop 8+):** If you confront Jiang Xue with evidence from Fragments 2+3+4+5, she breaks down on the seventh day: "I just didn't want one accident to ruin everything. Zhou Yang was a good student. He was only helping me. He shouldn't have died. And I shouldn't have let him die for nothing."—but her cover-up killed the whole school.

## Five Key NPCs

**Su Mian "The Girl Who Never Confessed" (17):** Emotion baseline 5/10 (introverted, nervous), sensitivity "Extremely High"—she is the Anchor character. In every loop, she walks toward Classmate Lin at 2:55 PM on the seventh day—but never makes it. If you tell her "you should go confess" before 2:50 PM on the seventh day, she will go—this directly triggers the Rift-Healing.

**Classmate Lin "The Crush" (17):** Emotion baseline 7/10 (cheerful), sensitivity "High." He actually likes Su Mian too—but his note was never seen by her. Key shift: in Loop 6+ he may unconsciously doodle Su Mian's name on his desk.

**Jiang Xue "The Chemistry Teacher" (34):** Emotion baseline 3/10 (extreme anxiety), sensitivity "High." She is not a bad person—just an ordinary person who made a mistake and then tried to cover it up. Key shift: in Loop 8+ she may voluntarily go to the principal's office to confess.

**Zhou Yang "The Vanished Assistant" (18):** Emotion baseline N/A (he is already gone). Sensitivity N/A. His "afterimage" occasionally appears near the lab. He is the only one who died before the loop began—he is the Rift's "omen."

**The Principal "The One in the Know" (58):** Emotion baseline 6/10 (outwardly calm), sensitivity "Medium." He knows what Jiang Xue is hiding, but chose to trust her. His secret: his daughter was also at the school on the day of the School Festival.

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-3 (Mirror):** Normal school life. Build familiarity with Su Mian and Classmate Lin. Let the players experience "everyday youth."
- **4-6 (Ripple):** The cherry blossoms wither early. Jiang Xue begins to make mistakes—misstating formulas in class. The number of pages in Su Mian's diary changes daily.
- **7-9 (Crack):** The school broadcast occasionally plays songs from an era that doesn't belong to this one. The length of the corridors is changing. Su Mian may briefly "realize" the loop.
- **10-12 (Fracture):** The chemistry lab door disappears—but the gas inside keeps spreading. Zhou Yang's afterimage begins to interact with other NPCs.
- **13-15 (Collapse):** The School Festival decorations change on their own. The gymnasium clock runs backward. Su Mian will automatically walk toward Classmate Lin on the seventh day—but the flow of time is slowing.

## Four Endings

**A - The Confession (Perfect):** Expose Jiang Xue's truth + prompt Su Mian to confess before the gas spreads + prevent the gas from spreading. The School Festival proceeds normally. Su Mian finishes reading that poem under the cherry tree. The whole school is saved. The Rift heals.

**B - Regretful Yet Whole (Advanced-Good):** The truth is exposed but the gas could not be stopped. Su Mian confessed at the last moment. The whole school dies—but the loop stops. Su Mian's regret is filled. The Rift heals—at the cost of everyone's dissipation.

**C - Su Mian's Choice (Hidden):** If Su Mian "half-awakens" in a later loop—she realizes she is the cause of the loop. She can choose: continue the loop (she can see Classmate Lin over and over) or confess (the loop ends, but she will die). She leaves this choice to the players.

**D - The Last School Festival (Bad):** Loops exhausted. The school vanishes from the map. The only thing left is two characters carved into the cherry tree: "Su Mian."
