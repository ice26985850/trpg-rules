# Time Loop TRPG — Item Codex (Overview of Equipment and Anchored Objects)

> This system has no traditional weapon / armor shops. A character's personal belongings are mainly "things you would carry in real life";
> the real resources are **information** and **Memory Anchors**, not equipment tiers.
> ▶ Fill in the "Starting Equipment" field on the character sheet by referring to this codex.

---

## I. Cross-Loop Item Rules

When the loop resets, different types of items have different fates:

| Item Type | Behavior at Loop Reset |
|------|------|
| Default Equipment | Returns to the state at the loop's start |
| Ordinary Items Obtained During a Loop | Disappear — not on you when you return to the loop's start |
| **Anchored Item** | Retained — spend 1 Memory Anchor Point (MAP) to lock it, carried back across loops |
| Key Plot Item | Decided by the GM |

**Anchored Item:** Spend 1 Memory Anchor Point (MAP) to anchor one item you are currently holding. After anchoring, the item appears on you when you wake in the next loop. There is a resource trade-off between anchoring items and anchoring information — your Anchors are never enough.

---

## II. Common Equipment (Every Character Has)

- Everyday clothing
- Mobile phone (can take photos / record audio / take notes; cannot reach outside)
- Wallet / ID
- Keychain

---

## III. Background Bonus Equipment

Based on the background chosen at character creation (see the Player Rulebook "Step 1: Background"), gain additionally:

- **Law Enforcer:** badge / ID, handcuffs, notebook, walkie-talkie
- **Seeker:** notebook and pen, voice recorder / camera
- **Caregiver:** basic first-aid kit, work ID
- **Laborer:** toolbox, gloves, utility knife (not a weapon)
- **Outsider:** sketchbook or journal, telescope or old camera, a small meaningful object
- **The Connected:** an item related to the Core Relationship NPC (group photo, letter, keepsake)

---

## IV. Anchored Item Mechanic Explained

- Anchoring is performed during the "Loop Settlement Phase," sharing the Memory Anchor Point (MAP) pool with information anchoring.
- One item occupies 1 Memory Anchor Point (MAP); information also occupies 1. The two crowd each other out.
- An anchored item will "appear on you" at the start of the next loop — as if you had carried it from the beginning.
- Good anchoring targets: objects that carry information across loops (journals, recordings, photos), or key plot props.

---

## V. Key Plot Items

- Issued by the GM per scenario, usually tied to a "Truth Fragment" or "Core Relationship."
- Cross-loop ownership is adjudicated individually by the GM (may be auto-retained as the story progresses, or may reset each time).
- Examples (vary by scenario): Zhang Mingde's suicide note, Chen Yan's trial record, the surveillance footage from the day of the School Festival, the resolution document from the meeting room.

---

## VI. Design Notes (GM Cheat Sheet)

- Equipment itself barely affects check values; its value lies in **carrying information** and being an **emotional anchor**.
- Encourage players to treat "the recording on the phone" and "the journal" as anchoring targets — this makes "carrying information across loops" concrete.
- If a player wants to use an item for aggressive purposes (e.g., a utility knife), handle it per the "Violent Conflict" rules; no separate weapon stat table is provided.
