# Scenario One: The Elevator (Beginner · 1 day / 24 minutes)

## Scenario Summary

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Difficulty** | ★☆☆☆☆ Beginner |
| **Loop Length** | 24 minutes (strict) |
| **Suggested Players** | 2-3 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 10 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 2 (extremely compressed: each Action Point (AP) ≈ 12 minutes) |
| **Tone Tags** | Denial / elongation of near-death time / five lives condensed into 24 minutes |

## Loop Setup

- **Loop Endpoint:** The elevator falls from the 30th floor to the ground (at minute 24)
- **Starting Moment:** 8:00 AM, the instant the elevator doors close
- **Loop Boundary:** Inside the elevator car (about 2m × 2m)
- **Opening Narration:** "The elevator doors close behind you. Five people are crammed into this narrow space. The numbers on the panel begin to jump—30. You smell a faint hint of cologne and last night's alcohol. Everything is normal. Except... there's one person who shouldn't be here."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** Five people enter the elevator, but one of them had already died of a heart attack in the office before entering—only no one noticed. The rift did not form when the elevator fell, but at the moment "five people walked into the elevator." Reality could not process the contradiction of "a dead person walking into the elevator."

**Surface Story:** Elevator malfunction, five people trapped, eventual crash.

**True Story:** The deceased is Zhang Mingde, a 52-year-old finance director. He suffered a heart attack while working overtime alone at 5 AM, and died at his desk. At 7:55 AM, his "body" stood up and walked into the elevator—but that was no longer him. It was the rift's first anomalous manifestation.

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: The living discover that one person in the elevator has already "left"
- Required: The living say "farewell" to Zhang Mingde—acknowledge his death
- Optional: Find the suicide note in Zhang Mingde's pocket (written at 3 AM, to his daughter)

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **Stopped Watch (scene/on Zhang Mingde/ Easy / loop 1):** Zhang Mingde's watch stopped at 4:58 AM—but he doesn't look like someone wearing a stopped watch. He checked the time twice in the elevator.
2. **No Breathing Sound (scene/all/Easy-Medium/ loop 2):** The instant the elevator goes quiet, you count the breathing sounds—only four people's. But you saw five chests rising and falling. You're certain you saw it.
3. **Suicide Note (item/inside Zhang Mingde's coat/Medium/ loop 3):** A letter Zhang Mingde wrote to his daughter at 3 AM: "Xiaowen, Dad may not make it through tonight. If I'm gone, your tuition is in the third drawer of the desk." But he never sent it.
4. **Pill Bottle (item/Zhang Mingde's briefcase/Easy/ loop 1):** Fast-acting heart-relief pills, cap open, a few missing. The time is this morning.
5. **Colleague's Memory (dialogue/passenger B—Li Na/Medium/ loop 4):** Li Na is Zhang Mingde's colleague. If you ask her what impression she has of Zhang Mingde today, she'll say: "He was... very quiet today. Usually he'd complain the elevator is too slow. Today he said nothing." Then she'll pause: "Wait, did he... get on the elevator today? I don't remember him walking in."
6. **Blind Spot (scene/elevator ceiling/Hard/ loop 5):** The angle of the elevator's surveillance camera—if you look carefully you'll find Zhang Mingde's position is exactly the camera's blind spot. From reality's perspective, there are only four people in this elevator.
7. **Cold (scene/near Zhang Mingde/Easy/ loop 2):** If you get close to Zhang Mingde, you feel cold. Not the air-conditioning cold—but a chill seeping from the bones. Only those near him can feel it.
8. **Farewell (dialogue/triggered/Special/ loop 6+):** When all the living acknowledge "Zhang Mingde is already gone" and someone says farewell to him—he smiles the next second, then from his position his body slowly dissolves into light. The elevator stops falling. The doors open. Beyond the doors is not the first-floor lobby—but a field of white light.

## Key NPCs

**Zhang Mingde "the deceased" (52):** Emotional baseline N/A (he is essentially gone, but his "afterimage" repeats some daily motions—checking his watch, adjusting his tie, smiling). Sensitivity "Extreme." He does not speak. But he listens.

**Li Na "colleague" (34):** Emotional baseline 4/10 (fatigue after working late). Sensitivity "Medium." Secret: she knew Zhang Mingde hadn't been well lately, but didn't think much of it. When she saw Zhang Mingde's "afterimage" walk into the elevator this morning, she thought it was him.

**Wang Jianguo "stranger" (45):** Emotional baseline 6/10 (ordinary office worker). Sensitivity "Low." Secret: he sneaks looks at the stock market on his phone in the elevator, utterly unaware.

**Chen Xiaoyu "intern" (23):** Emotional baseline 7/10 (nervous on her first day). Sensitivity "High" (young people are sensitive). Secret: she is a classmate of Zhang Mingde's daughter.

## Degradation Roadmap (Brief)

- **Loops 1-2:** Normal. The elevator runs smoothly. Players first experience the "normal" elevator environment. Reset after the fall.
- **Loops 3-4:** First anomaly: the floor display on the number panel occasionally skips a number. Zhang Mingde checks his watch more frequently.
- **Loops 5-6:** The elevator occasionally briefly loses power—1 second of total darkness. After relighting, Zhang Mingde's position changed once.
- **Loops 7-8:** Zhang Mingde begins to "lose focus"—others occasionally "can't see" him (as if he weren't there). Players approach the truth through fragments 5 and 7.
- **Loops 9-10:** The elevator lights turn pale blue—the same as the lights in Zhang Mingde's office before dawn. Last chance.

## Three Endings

**A - The Elevator Doors Open (Perfect):** Obtain all fragments + say farewell to Zhang Mingde. The elevator stops at the 1st floor, doors open—beyond the doors is the white light of a hospital. Zhang Mingde smiles, turns and walks out of the elevator. The remaining four look at each other as the elevator doors slowly close.

**B - One Person Remembered (Ordinary):** At least one person says farewell but the others stay silent. The elevator stops falling, suspended. Zhang Mingde's afterimage dissipates. The elevator lights go out—then relight. The four walk out, but one of them looks back at the empty elevator corner, knowing something was once there.

**C - Loops Exhausted (Bad):** No one says farewell. The final fall—the elevator does not stop at the 1st floor. It passes through the ground, keeps falling, until the lights go out completely and even the panel numbers stop glowing.

# Scenario Two: Town Judgment Day (Standard · 3 days)

## Scenario Summary

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★☆☆ Standard |
| **Loop Length** | 3 days |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-4 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 13 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 8 + Action (ACT) modifier (3 days × Action Points (AP)) |
| **Tone Tags** | Collective guilt / silent complicity / delayed justice |

## Loop Setup

- **Loop Endpoint:** Midnight of the third day, all townsfolk collectively vanish
- **Starting Moment:** 6:00 AM on the first day, players wake in the town's hotel
- **Loop Boundary:** The whole small town and surrounding 3 km (old mine, forest edge)
- **Opening Narration:** "You wake up. Sunlight streams through the hotel's old curtains. The air smells of autumn—dry leaves, distant cooking smoke. From downstairs comes the sound of the hotel proprietress preparing breakfast. Everything is normal. Everything is peaceful. Except... you don't quite remember how you came to this small town."

## Core Truth

15 years ago (1994), an outsider teacher named "Chen Yan" was lynched by the townsfolk. He was accused of "corrupting the town's youth." The true reason: Chen Yan discovered the mayor's financial scandal—the mayor and several merchants had embezzled government grants. His death was to silence him. For 15 years, the whole town became an accomplice in silence.

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: At least one townsperson publicly admits Chen Yan was wrongly accused
- Required: Players find Chen Yan's diary from that year (hidden in the old schoolhouse basement)
- Optional: Players persuade the mayor to speak the truth at the gathering

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **Last Lesson (document/old schoolhouse 2nd floor/Medium/ loop 2):** A letter Chen Yan wrote to his students the day before he died: "Tomorrow I'm going to tell you something important, about this town's future."
2. **The Absent Mayor (document/town hall archive room/Hard/ loop 4):** Phone records from October 1994—the mayor was not at the lynching scene that night.
3. **The Old Tailor's Dream (dialogue/Grandma Lin's tailor shop/Easy/ loop 1):** "I dreamed that young teacher came back again, standing in the square."
4. **Traces Beneath the Square (scene/town square/Medium/ loop 3):** At sunset on the third day, dark stains in the stone seams.
5. **The Merchant's Ledger (item/grocery back room/Medium/ loop 5):** Records of the "special compensation" the grocery owner received after Chen Yan's death.
6. **The Student's Silence (dialogue/town high school/Hard/ loop 6):** Chen Yan's student from back then—now a high school teacher, Lin Xiao—kept Chen Yan's diary.
7. **The Basement Diary (item/old schoolhouse basement/Hard/ loop 7):** Chen Yan's diary, recording the discovery of the financial scandal. Last page: "They know. Something may happen tonight."
8. **The Truth of the Ritual (memory/town square/Special/ loop 8+):** During the silent ritual, if ≥4 fragments obtained, triggers a collective Déjà Vu—all players simultaneously see a flashback of that night 15 years ago.

## Three Key NPCs

**Mayor Zhou Zhengyi (56):** Emotional baseline 4/10 (repressed anxiety), sensitivity "High." He knows Chen Yan was innocent, but believes speaking the truth would destroy the small town. Key shift: loops 7-9 may say "I should have stood up back then."

**High School Teacher Lin Xiao (30):** Emotional baseline 5/10, sensitivity "Extreme." Chen Yan's favorite student, secretly reads the diary once a week. Key shift: loop 6 first memory residue—unconsciously writes Chen Yan's name on the blackboard.

**Grocery Owner Wang Fugui (62):** Emotional baseline 6/10 (forced cheerfulness), sensitivity "High." Participated in the lynching and took compensation. Key shift: loop 8+ may collapse in tears "I dream of him every night."

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-2 (Mirror):** Normal small town. Let players feel the "goodness." First full-wipe at midnight of the third day.
- **3 (last Mirror):** Last normal loop. Fragments 3+4 obtainable.
- **4-5 (Ripple):** Hotel clock 3 minutes slow. Mayor forgets to order coffee at lunch. Fragments 2+5 obtainable.
- **6 (Ripple→Crack):** Lin Xiao's first memory residue. Fragment 6 obtainable.
- **7-9 (Crack):** Old schoolhouse window shows a reflection from 15 years ago. Fountain water still. Fragments 1+7 obtainable.
- **10-12 (Fracture):** Spatial jumps. Lin Xiao may half-awaken. Fragment 8 obtainable.
- **13 (Collapse):** Last chance. NPC may speak the truth or stay silent forever.

## Three Endings

**A - Justice Delayed (Perfect):** 8 fragments + mayor publicly reveals truth + diary made public. No townsperson vanishes—the rift heals.

**B - One Person's Farewell (Ordinary):** ≥6 fragments + Lin Xiao reads the diary aloud. The town vanishes but Lin Xiao stays. Rift half-healed.

**C - The Fifteenth Autumn (Bad):** Loops exhausted. Residents turn to statues forever surrounding the square. The player remembers everything—and remembering itself is meaning.

# Scenario Three: The Last School Festival (Advanced · 7 days)

## Scenario Summary

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★★☆ Advanced |
| **Loop Length** | 7 days |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-5 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 15 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 8 + Action (ACT) modifier (7 days × Action Points (AP), total about 56-77 Action Points (AP)) |
| **Tone Tags** | The end of youth / words left unsaid / things to do even knowing the ending |

## Loop Setup

- **Loop Endpoint:** 3:00 PM on the seventh day (the day of the school festival), a gas incident occurs at the school, whole school dies
- **Starting Moment:** Seven days before the school festival (Monday) 7:00 AM, players wake in the classroom
- **Loop Boundary:** The entire school campus (including dorms, gym, back hill)
- **Opening Narration:** "You wake on the desk. Your cheek is against the cool desktop, a trace of drool at the corner of your mouth. On the blackboard is written '7 days until the school festival.' The cherry blossoms outside are blooming—you remember the cherry blossoms should bloom next week. But you can't recall what happened yesterday. Or the day before. Or why you're slumped on the desk."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** The true cause of the gas incident is not an accident, but a chain reaction triggered by the chemistry teacher to cover up a fatal lab accident. But the moment the rift formed was not the gas—it was a student's regret in the last second before death: she/he never confessed to the person they secretly loved.

**Surface Story:** On the day of the school festival, a gas leak in the lab, an accident, the whole school perishes.

**True Story:** Chemistry teacher Jiang Xue, in a secret experiment three days before the school festival, caused a student (her teaching assistant) who stayed at school to inhale toxic gas due to operational error. She hid the accident, but the leak kept spreading. On the day of the school festival, enough concentration of toxic gas had built up in the lab and spread throughout the school via the ventilation system.

But the rift was not caused by the chemistry teacher. The rift is a student named "Su Mian"—in the instant she fell in the gas, her only thought was: "I still haven't told Classmate Lin I like him." This regret tore reality apart.

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: Su Mian (or with the players' help) confesses to Classmate Lin
- Required: Expose the truth of the chemistry teacher's accident
- Optional: Stop the gas spread on the day of the school festival

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **Su Mian's Diary (item/Su Mian's desk/Easy/ loop 1):** A diary written, torn, and rewritten again and again. Every page has only one sentence: "Didn't say it today either."
2. **The Lab Lock (scene/chemistry lab/Medium/ loop 3):** The chemistry lab has been locked since three days before the school festival—normally it should be open. A faint bitter-almond smell seeps from the door seam.
3. **The Absent Assistant (dialogue/student/Easy/ loop 2):** A senior, "Zhou Yang," suddenly took leave four days before the school festival—and was never seen again. The teachers say he "transferred."
4. **Jiang Xue's Overtime Records (document/faculty office/Hard/ loop 4):** Chemistry teacher Jiang Xue was in the lab from three days before the school festival to the day before, late into the night. Her signature is on the attendance record—but the signature grows messier.
5. **Ventilation System Diagram (item/administration office/Medium/ loop 5):** The school's ventilation system blueprint. Annotations show the chemistry lab's vent connects to the whole school—including the gym (the main venue of the school festival).
6. **Classmate Lin's Notes (item/library/Easy/ loop 1):** Classmate Lin borrowed a poetry collection five days before the school festival. Inside is a slip of paper: "Want to read this poem to someone under the cherry tree on the day of the school festival. If I have the courage."
7. **Su Mian's Last Moment (memory/gym/Special/ loop 7+):** At the instant the gas spreads, if players are near Su Mian, the GM triggers a flashback: Su Mian's perspective—as she falls she looks at Classmate Lin on the other side of the gym, her lips moving but no sound comes out. The mouth shape is: "I like you."
8. **Jiang Xue's Confession (dialogue/triggered/Hard/ loop 8+):** If you confront Jiang Xue with evidence from fragments 2+3+4+5, she will collapse on the seventh day: "I just didn't want one accident to ruin everything. Zhou Yang was a good student. He was only helping me. He shouldn't have died. I shouldn't have let him die for nothing."—but her cover-up killed the whole school.

## Five Key NPCs

**Su Mian "the unconfessed girl" (17):** Emotional baseline 5/10 (introverted, nervous), sensitivity "Extreme"—she is the Anchor Person. Each loop, at 2:55 PM on the 7th day she walks toward Classmate Lin—but never makes it. If before 2:50 PM on the 7th day you tell her "you should go confess," she will go—this directly triggers the rift healing.

**Classmate Lin "the secretly loved one" (17):** Emotional baseline 7/10 (cheerful), sensitivity "High." He actually also likes Su Mian—but his note was never seen by Su Mian. Key shift: loop 6+ may unconsciously draw Su Mian's name on the desk.

**Jiang Xue "chemistry teacher" (34):** Emotional baseline 3/10 (extreme anxiety), sensitivity "High." She is not a bad person—just an ordinary person who made a mistake and tried to cover it up. Key shift: loop 8+ may proactively go to the principal's office to confess.

**Zhou Yang "the vanished assistant" (18):** Emotional baseline N/A (he is already gone). Sensitivity N/A. His "afterimage" occasionally appears near the lab. He is the only one who died before the loop began—he is the rift's "omen."

**Principal "the Insider" (58):** Emotional baseline 6/10 (calm surface), sensitivity "Medium." He knows Jiang Xue is hiding something, but chooses to believe her. His secret: his daughter is also at school on the day of the festival.

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-3 (Mirror):** Normal school life. Build familiarity with Su Mian and Classmate Lin. Let players experience "ordinary youth."
- **4-6 (Ripple):** Cherry blossoms wither early. Jiang Xue begins to err—misstates a formula in class. Su Mian's diary has a different number of pages each day.
- **7-9 (Crack):** The school broadcast occasionally plays songs from another era. The length of the hallway changes. Su Mian may briefly "realize" the loop.
- **10-12 (Fracture):** The chemistry lab door disappears—but the gas inside keeps spreading. Zhou Yang's afterimage begins to interact with other NPCs.
- **13-15 (Collapse):** The school festival decorations change on their own. The gym clock runs backward. Su Mian will automatically walk toward Classmate Lin on the 7th day—but time flows slower.

## Four Endings

**A - Confession (Perfect):** Expose Jiang Xue's truth + prompt Su Mian to confess before the gas spreads + stop the gas spread. The school festival proceeds normally. Su Mian finishes that poem under the cherry tree. Whole school saved. Rift heals.

**B - Regret but Complete (Advanced Good):** Truth exposed but gas not stopped. Su Mian confesses at the last moment. Whole school dies—but the loop stops. Su Mian's regret is filled. Rift heals—at the cost of everyone dissipating.

**C - Su Mian's Choice (Hidden):** If Su Mian "half-awakens" in later loops—she realizes she is the cause of the loop. She can choose: continue the loop (she can see Classmate Lin repeatedly) or confess (loop ends, but she dies). She leaves this choice to the players.

**D - The Last School Festival (Bad):** Loops exhausted. The school disappears from the map. All that remains is two characters carved on the cherry tree: "Su Mian."

# Scenario Four: The Infinite Meeting (Expert · 1 day)

## Scenario Summary

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★★★ Expert |
| **Loop Length** | 1 day (8-hour meeting) |
| **Suggested Players** | 3-4 |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 10 |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 4 (highly compressed: each Action Point (AP) ≈ 2 hours) |
| **Tone Tags** | The sin of capital / the cost of silence / individual responsibility in power structures |

## Loop Setup

- **Loop Endpoint:** 4:00 PM, CEO dies of heart attack, meeting forcibly ends
- **Starting Moment:** 8:00 AM, players sit down in the meeting room, meeting just begins
- **Loop Boundary:** The whole 30-floor office building (meeting room on the 28th floor)
- **Opening Narration:** "The meeting room door closes behind you. Eight people in suits sit on either side of the long table. Someone is looking at their phone. Someone is drinking coffee. CEO Chen Hongyuan taps the table—'Everyone, let's begin. Today's agenda: the final vote on the HuiDa acquisition. He opens the folder in front of him. You don't know what's inside. But you know—at four o'clock, he will die."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** CEO Chen Hongyuan's heart attack was no accident—he was given slow-acting poison by director Zhao Yongchang before the meeting. Motive: the HuiDa acquisition three years ago—Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang colluded to cook the books, causing a small company called "HuiDa" to be maliciously acquired and dismantled. HuiDa's founder jumped to his death. Zhao Yongchang is now the only one who knows the whole truth. He wants to kill Chen Hongyuan to silence him—then bury the truth with Chen Hongyuan in the grave.

The rift formed at the instant of Chen Hongyuan's death. Not because he died—but because the truth died with him. The loop traps all who know, asking the same question: this time, will someone tell the truth?

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: Someone (any director) publicly points out the falsification in the acquisition before Chen Hongyuan dies
- Required: Expose the fact that Zhao Yongchang poisoned him
- Optional: Chen Hongyuan himself confesses and repents

## Eight Truth Fragments

1. **HuiDa Case Original Documents (document/28th floor archive room/Medium/ loop 2):** The real financial data of the HuiDa acquisition—differing from public data by 80 million. Signed by both Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang.
2. **Zhao Yongchang's Pill Bottle (item/Zhao Yongchang's office/Hard/ loop 4):** A slow-acting toxin undetectable by routine autopsy. Zhao Yongchang "imported" it from abroad three days before the meeting. The shipping receipt is hidden in his office.
3. **Chen Hongyuan's Diagnosis (document/Chen Hongyuan's office/Medium/ loop 3):** Chen Hongyuan's physical exam report from three months ago—heart completely healthy. He should not have had a heart attack.
4. **HuiDa Founder's Suicide Note (item/HuiDa's old office/Special/ loop 5+):** The letter HuiDa's founder wrote before jumping. On the 30th floor (HuiDa's original office floor—emptied after the acquisition). It lists everything.
5. **The Directors' Secrets (dialogue/each director/Hard/ loop 6):** Each director knows a bit of the truth—but no one knows it all. You need to persuade them separately: the CFO knows the fake books, the legal director knows the procedural violations, the HR director knows HuiDa's employees were illegally fired.
6. **Zhao Yongchang's Schedule (document/front desk computer/Easy/ loop 1):** Zhao Yongchang was still in the office at 11 PM the night before the meeting—surveillance shows he went to the pantry. In the pantry is a bottle with its label torn off.
7. **Chen Hongyuan's Letter of Remorse (item/Chen Hongyuan's briefcase/Hard/ loop 7+):** A half-written letter from Chen Hongyuan: "I know I did wrong. The HuiDa matter will stay with me for life. I plan to, after this meeting—" The letter is unfinished. What did he plan to do after this meeting? Confess? Or keep hiding?
8. **The Meeting Room Clock (scene/meeting room/Special/ loop 8+):** 3:58 PM—the clock stops. All clocks stop. When all truth fragments are pieced together, the GM freezes time for 5 minutes (real time). In those 5 minutes, players can say anything to any NPC. After 5 minutes, time resumes—Chen Hongyuan has his heart attack, or... doesn't.

## Five Key NPCs

**CEO Chen Hongyuan (62):** Emotional baseline 7/10 (forced authority), sensitivity "Extreme"—he is the Anchor Person. He is actually full of guilt, but must maintain his image in front of the directors. Key shift: loop 5+ may suddenly pause while speaking, staring at his own hands.

**Director Zhao Yongchang (55):** Emotional baseline 8/10 (very confident—he knows Chen Hongyuan will die today), sensitivity "High." He is the only one who doesn't want the loop to end—each reset means he must kill Chen Hongyuan again. Key shift: loop 4+ begins to be anxious ("why does he never die?").

**CFO Lin Meiling (45):** Emotional baseline 5/10 (nervous), sensitivity "Medium." She knows the fake books but dares not speak—she needs this job. Secret: she secretly kept a copy of the original data.

**Legal Director Wang Jianguo (50):** Emotional baseline 6/10 (formulaic calm), sensitivity "Low." He knows the procedural violations but tells himself "it's legal." Key shift: loop 6+ begins to flip through the HuiDa case files—he knows what he's looking for but won't admit it.

**HuiDa Founder Zhou Ming (deceased):** Emotional baseline N/A, sensitivity N/A. He does not exist in the meeting room. But he is the heaviest presence in this building. On the 30th floor (HuiDa's old office), you can occasionally see a figure in an old suit standing by the window. He does not speak. But he left that suicide note.

## Degradation Roadmap

- **1-2 (Mirror):** Normal meeting. Everything by agenda. Chen Hongyuan has his heart attack on time at 4:00.
- **3-4 (Ripple):** Dialogue in the meeting begins to show subtle changes—something a director says differs from loop 1. Zhao Yongchang grows restless at 3:50.
- **5-6 (Crack):** The meeting room light begins to change—sometimes suddenly turns to afternoon warmth (though it's clearly still morning). Once, Chen Hongyuan paused 10 seconds while speaking—everyone thought he stumbled over words, but his expression was fear.
- **7-8 (Fracture):** The 30th floor begins to "seep" into the 28th—occasionally you hear footsteps from above (the 30th floor was emptied three years ago). Zhao Yongchang's office door opens on its own—inside, a photo of HuiDa's founder sits on the desk (Zhao Yongchang doesn't remember placing it).
- **9-10 (Collapse):** The clock stops. All clocks stop at 3:58—that moment two minutes later never arrives, unless someone tells the truth. Fragment 8 triggers. This is the last chance.

## Four Endings

**A - Truth Revealed (Perfect):** Expose Zhao Yongchang's poisoning + all directors publicly admit the falsification + Chen Hongyuan repents before death (or survives). Chen Hongyuan does not have a heart attack—or he does but is rescued in time. Zhao Yongchang arrested. HuiDa founder's suicide note made public. Rift heals.

**B - Justice Delayed (Ordinary):** Expose the fake-books truth but fail to stop Chen Hongyuan's death. Chen Hongyuan says "I'm sorry" before dying. Zhao Yongchang's crime is exposed—but the judicial process takes time. Rift half-healed—HuiDa founder's suicide note is found, but no one can apologize to him.

**C - Mutual Destruction (Hidden):** If players choose to have Chen Hongyuan and Zhao Yongchang confront each other face to face—in anger and fear they expose each other's crimes. Chen Hongyuan has a heart attack. Zhao Yongchang, after seeing Chen Hongyuan fall, opens the window and jumps. Both die, but the truth is exposed. Rift heals—at the cost of two lives.

**D - Everything as Usual (Bad):** Loops exhausted. The meeting proceeds as usual. Chen Hongyuan dies as usual. Zhao Yongchang escapes as usual. The only difference—the players remember everything. The moment they walk out of the office building, they see a faded slip of paper stuck on the 30th-floor window: "No one will remember. No one will care." That was HuiDa founder's last words.
