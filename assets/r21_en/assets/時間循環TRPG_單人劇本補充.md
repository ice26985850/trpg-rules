# Part One: Single-Player Adaptation of Existing Scenarios

The following adjustments adapt the four original scenarios to single-player mode. Each adjustment notes the corresponding **original scenario location** and **referenced rules**.

---

## Adjustment One: The Elevator (★ Beginner · 24 min)

> **Original scenario:** Time Loop TRPG — Series Scenario Collection § Scenario One

### Single-Player Adaptation

| Adjustment | Content |
|------|------|
| **Recommended Companion** | **Chen Xiaoyu (intern, 23)** — Attributes: Physique 2 / Alertness 3 / Will 2 / Affinity 3. She was a classmate of Zhang Mingde's daughter, and in later loops may trigger a Déjà Vu of "I think I knew your father." |
| **Fragment Difficulty** | All fragment acquisition difficulties unchanged. The 24-minute enclosed space naturally suits solo play — the player need not split up. |
| **Special Adjustment** | Loop 1: Chen Xiaoyu initiates conversation with the player ("You look nervous too — first day on the job?"), naturally building a connection. |
| **Ending** | Ending B "One Person Remembered" hits harder in solo mode — because "one person" is the player. |

> **Reference:** NPC Companion rules are in the Single-Player Rule Supplement §III; companion attributes in §3.3; companion Empathy Soothing in §3.4.

## Adjustment Two: Town Judgment Day (★★★ Standard · 3 days)

> **Original scenario:** Time Loop TRPG — Series Scenario Collection § Scenario Two

### Single-Player Adaptation

| Adjustment | Content |
|------|------|
| **Recommended Companion** | **Lin Xiao (high school teacher, 30)** — Attributes: Physique 2 / Alertness 3 / Will 3 / Affinity 2. He is himself the key figure of Fragment 6. Once he becomes a companion, Fragment 6's difficulty drops from "Hard" to "Moderate." But the cost is: if the player fails to protect him during a loop, his death adds +2 extra fatigue (because he is both a companion and a Core Relationship NPC candidate). |
| **Fragment Distribution Adjustment** | Fragment 2 (the absent mayor / the town hall archives room) — may be handled by the companion's "divide-and-conquer action": the player spends 2 Action Points (AP) + the companion rolls 2d6 + Alertness (AWA) (3) → +1. Full Success = the companion independently finds the phone records. The player may conduct other investigations in the meantime. |
| **Combat Adjustment** | The Threat Level of guards in scenes originally requiring multi-player cooperation (e.g., infiltrating the town hall) is lowered by 1. |
| **Time Adjustment** | The loop cap changes from 13 to 15 (solo play needs more loops to compensate for the missing teammates' efficiency). |

> **Reference:** Companion divide-and-conquer action is in the Single-Player Rule Supplement §3.4; solo combat balance in §VI; threat level adjustments in §6.1.

## Adjustment Three: The Last School Festival (★★★★ Advanced · 7 days)

> **Original scenario:** Time Loop TRPG — Series Scenario Collection § Scenario Three

### Single-Player Adaptation

| Adjustment | Content |
|------|------|
| **Recommended Companion** | **Su Mian (the girl who never confessed, 17)** — Attributes: Physique 1 / Alertness 2 / Will 3 / Affinity 3. She is an Anchor figure — making the Anchor figure a companion is the most narratively impactful design of this scenario in solo mode. # Part Two: A Brand-New Single-Player Scenario — Echo Hotel (★→★★★★★)

**Design note:** This is a scenario designed entirely for solo play. Rather than adapting an existing scenario, it is built from the loop structure to fragment acquisition entirely around "one person investigating." The player takes the role of a traveler staying at a remote mountain hotel, trapped by a blizzard, and discovers that every night at midnight the hotel resets to the previous evening — and every room hides the secret of a former guest.

Difficulty curve coverage: Loops 1–3 (beginner exploration) → 4–6 (standard investigation) → 7–9 (advanced puzzle) → 10–12 (expert truth) → 13+ (ultimate choice).

---

## Scenario Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Difficulty** | ★→★★★★★ progressive (beginner-friendly entry, expert-level truth) |
| **Loop Length** | 1 day (blizzard night: 6:00 PM evening → next day 12:00 AM midnight) |
| **Daily Action Points (AP)** | 9 + Action (ACT) modifier (6-hour effective window) |
| **Suggested Loop Cap** | 15 |
| **Tone Tags** | Persistence in loneliness / every room holds a story / the past never truly passes |

## Loop Setup

- **Loop Endpoint:** Midnight 12:00 AM, the bell in the hotel lobby rings, all guest room doors lock simultaneously — then reset.
- **Start Moment:** 6:00 PM evening, you wake up by the fireplace in the hotel lobby. The blizzard howls outside. The hotel keeper is wiping a glass behind the bar.
- **Loop Boundary:** The hotel building + 50 meters around it (beyond 50 meters survival is impossible in the blizzard — automatic reset).
- **Opening Narration:** "The fireplace crackles. You open your eyes and find yourself curled up on the old sofa in the lobby. Outside the window is a blizzard — a vast white expanse, no road in sight. Behind the bar, an old man is wiping a glass. 'Awake?' he says without looking up. 'The blizzard will last a while yet. The rooms are upstairs — pick any. No other guests anyway.' As he says 'no other guests,' your gaze inadvertently sweeps across the register — a dozen-odd names densely written on it. But all crossed out."

## Core Truth

**Anchor Event:** This hotel belongs to no era. It is a "seam in time" — everyone who gets lost in the blizzard and dies in these mountains, their final consciousness before death comes to this hotel. They stay one night, then dissipate at midnight. The hotel keeper is the only one who is here forever — he is not a living person, he is the guardian of this seam. He cannot remember any guest, because in the seam of time, a "person" is merely a passerby.

Ten years ago, one guest did not dissipate. Her name was **Lin Suwen**, an archaeologist who fell off a cliff trying to save a companion in the blizzard. Her regret was — she never told her daughter "Mom loves you." This regret was too heavy, too heavy for the seam to digest — it formed a Rift. Since then, every guest who comes to the hotel has been trapped in a six-hour loop. Lin Suwen herself lives in Room 204, but she does not remember who she is — she only remembers "I am waiting for someone."

**Rift-Healing Conditions:**
- Required: The player finds Lin Suwen's keepsake in Room 204 (an unsent letter to her daughter)
- Required: The player tells Lin Suwen the truth — "You are no longer alive, but your daughter still lives. She needs to know you died to save someone. She needs this letter."
- Optional: The player visits other guest rooms and understands the other guests' stories — these stories are not necessary, but they let the player understand the nature of the loop (and may provide Cognition Points (CP) and clues).

## The Eight Truth Fragments

**Fragment 1: The Secret of the Register (scene / lobby bar / Easy / Loop 1)**
- Look closely at the crossed-out names in the register — beside each name is a line of small text in a different handwriting: "Died on Month X, Day X, Year XXXX." The last entry is ten years ago. After that the register is blank — until you appear. Your name is not crossed out. Beside it only three words are written: "Still alive."

**Fragment 2: The Crying in Room 204 (scene / second-floor corridor / Moderate / Loop 2)**
- At 10:00 PM, if you pass Room 204, you hear someone crying inside. The door is locked — but if you wait until 11:00 PM, the door briefly opens for 5 minutes. In the room is a woman, sitting by the window, watching the blizzard. She will not speak to you on her own. But if you ask her "Who are you waiting for," she answers: "My daughter. She said she would come pick me up. But the snow is too heavy. She may not make it back."

**Fragment 3: The Other Guests' Stories (dialogue / various rooms / Moderate / Loop 3+)**
The following 3 small stories are distributed across the other rooms of the hotel. At least 2 must be understood to piece together the nature of the loop:
- **Room 201 - The Old Hunter (73):** Lost in the blizzard and froze to death five years ago. Every night he polishes a hunting rifle — but it is not actually his. It is a relic he found in a cave before dying; until his death he wondered "whose rifle is this."
- **Room 203 - The Young Mountaineer (24):** Died three years ago. Every night he writes a letter — but each time he writes halfway, he forgets what he was writing. The letter begins: "Dear Mom, I'm sorry, I shouldn't have come alone. The mountain is beautiful, but —" then the handwriting breaks off.
- **Room 205 - The Truck Driver (41):** Died in an avalanche eight years ago. Every night he studies a map, trying to find a road that does not exist. If you look at his map — you discover that road once existed. But it was abandoned ten years ago.

**Fragment 4: The Keeper's Diary (item / behind the bar / Moderate / Loop 4)**
- The hotel keeper's diary. The first page is dated "date unknown." The last page is ten years ago: "Today a woman came. She is not like the others — she remembers who she is. She asked me a question: 'Who are you waiting for?' I answered: 'I wait for no one. I am just here.' She said: 'So am I.' Then she went upstairs. Since then, time here has been broken. The snow never stops. The guests never leave. I cannot remember their faces — but tonight, I wrote down this sentence: 'So am I.'"

**Fragment 5: Lin Suwen's Archaeological Notes (item / under the bed in Room 204 / Hard / Loop 5)**
- An old backpack containing an archaeology notebook. It records the archaeological project from ten years ago. The last page is a photo torn from a newspaper — a little girl, with the headline "Mountain Rescue Team Searches for Missing Archaeologist." On the back is written: "My daughter asked when I'd be home. I said next Wednesday. Was that a Wednesday? I no longer know."

**Fragment 6: The Unsent Letter (item / under the pillow in Room 204 / Hard / Loop 6)**
- The letter Lin Suwen wrote to her daughter, never sent. Full text:

> "Dear Duoduo:
> Mom is trapped in a place. Not a cave — a hotel. There's a fireplace, a warm bed, and snow that never stops. But Mom is not afraid — because it's safe here. The only thing Mom fears is that you don't know Mom loves you. That morning before I left we argued. You said 'You never cared about me' — that was the most wrong thing you ever said. Mom cares about you. Cares so much that in this blizzard, the only thing Mom thinks about is that next Wednesday is your birthday. I'm sorry Mom can't come back. If one day someone brings you this letter — please remember: Mom died to save someone. That matters more than any archaeological discovery."

**Fragment 7: The Keeper's True Name (dialogue / bar / Special / Loop 8+)**
- The hotel keeper is not a real hotel keeper. He is the mountain itself — or rather, the mountain's apology to all the dead. If you directly ask him "Who are you exactly," he falls silent. But after Loop 8+, if you have already collected at least 4 of Fragments 1–6 — he answers: "What my name is doesn't matter. What matters — that woman called Lin Suwen. She shouldn't be here. You should help her." Then he gives you the key to Room 204 (before this, the door only opened 23:00–23:05).

**Fragment 8: Delivering the Letter (action / triggered / Loop 9+)**
- At the end of this loop, you make a choice: whether to "anchor" that letter. Anchoring the letter costs **3 Anchors** (full-recreation-level anchoring). If you anchor it — when you wake in the next loop, the letter is clutched in your hand. The paper is warm. The ink not yet dry. Then you realize: this letter was not written for you to read. You must leave this hotel. You must find Lin Suwen's daughter. And when the loop ends — you find the hotel door, for the first time, opens outward.

## Five Key NPCs

**Lin Suwen "The Waiting Mother" (42):** Emotional baseline 3/10 (calm within sorrow), sensitivity "Extremely High" — she is an Anchor figure. Every night she watches the window in Room 204. If you don't speak to her, she won't speak first. If you do, she asks you: "Have you seen a girl named Duoduo?"

**The Hotel Keeper "The Mountain" (appears 60, actually ∞):** Emotional baseline 5/10 (eternal calm), sensitivity N/A (he is not human — he does not degrade or shift). At the start of every loop he says the same thing: "Awake? The blizzard will last a while yet. The rooms are upstairs." Throughout the scenario he does not shift, does not experience Déjà Vu — he is the one unchanging anchor.

**The Old Hunter (Room 201):** Emotional baseline 4/10 (calm sorrow). He does not speak — his throat was damaged when he froze to death. He only nods, shakes his head, or points at something. His rifle points at a map — marking where Lin Suwen fell off the cliff. If the player understands what he is pointing at: gain an extra 1 Cognition Point (CP).

**The Young Mountaineer (Room 203):** Emotional baseline 2/10 (regret). Every night he writes a letter. If you help him finish the ending before he forgets, he smiles before he vanishes — and at the start of the next loop, an extra key appears on his door, which can open Room 204 (an alternative to Fragment 7's key acquisition). This is the reward for "completing what others left unfinished."

**The Truck Driver (Room 205):** Emotional baseline 6/10 (stubborn optimism). He believes he can find a way out. Every loop he rushes out of the hotel with his map — then gets scared back by the blizzard at the door. If you tell him "this road was abandoned ten years ago," he falls silent, then hands you a note: "Lin Suwen. A passenger from ten years ago. I drove her. Where she was going is at that cliff. The coordinates are —"

## Recommended NPC Companion

**Candidate Companion: The Young Mountaineer (Room 203) —** Attributes: Physique 3 / Alertness 2 / Will 2 / Affinity 1. He can become a companion after Loop 3+ (after helping him finish his letter). He is young and action-oriented; Physique 3 is a high value among companions — suited to combat and action support. Cost: he has a deep fear of "leaving home"; if the player mentions "going home," he falls into dejection — all checks in that loop suffer Disadvantage.

## Degradation Roadmap (Single-Player Exclusive)

| Loop | Degradation Stage | Key Event |
|:--:|:--:|------|
| 1-2 | Mirror | Normal hotel. The player explores the lobby, guest rooms, and gets familiar with NPCs. Fragments 1+2 obtainable. |
| 3-4 | Mirror → Ripple | Room 203's door begins to open on its own (the mountaineer's letter half-written). The stories in Fragment 3 begin to "play" on loop — NPCs' repeated behaviors become obvious. |
| 5-6 | Ripple | The fireplace no longer crackles — it becomes a silent flame. The crying in Room 204 is clearer than before — then suddenly stops (Lin Suwen at that moment nearly remembers something). Fragments 4+5+6 obtainable. |
| 7-8 | Crack | Faces appear in the blizzard — flashing past the window. All the faces of those who died in these mountains ten years ago. The keeper for the first time says something different: "Today? What day is it today?" Fragment 7 obtainable. |
| 9-10 | Fracture | Room 204's door no longer auto-locks. Lin Suwen begins to speak full sentences — but she is not speaking of the present. She is speaking — but the content is what she said to her daughter at the moment she fell off the cliff ten years ago. Fragment 8 trigger condition met. |
| 11-12 | Fracture → Collapse | The hotel's walls begin to turn transparent — you can see the mountain in the blizzard outside the corridor. But the mountain is not the present one. It is the mountain of ten years ago — no abandoned roads, no collapsed bridges. |
| 13-15 | Collapse | The final loop. All the hotel room doors open at once. All the "guests" stand at the doorways — not waiting for you, but waiting for Lin Suwen. They know who she is. They are waiting for her to make a choice. |

## Three Endings

**A - The Letter Is Delivered (Perfect · Anchored Ending)**
Trigger: Fragments 1–7 all obtained + anchored Fragment 8 (spent 3 Anchors to anchor the letter) + persuaded Lin Suwen to accept the truth.
Ending narrative: When you wake in the next loop, the letter is in your hand — warm. The hotel door is open. Outside is not a blizzard — but a mountain path, sunny. You know where you're going: the town below, to find someone named "Duoduo." The hotel vanishes behind you — but it is not erased. It simply no longer needs to loop. That woman Lin Suwen finally waited for the one she was waiting for. Though that person was not her daughter — it was enough.

**B - You Told Her (Ordinary · Non-Anchored Ending)**
Trigger: Fragments 1–6 obtained + told Lin Suwen the truth but did not anchor the letter.
Ending narrative: After hearing the truth, Lin Suwen is silent for a long time. Then she smiles. "Tell him — or her. I don't need to send that letter. I know what it says." She walks to the window. The blizzard stops. She pushes the window open — outside the window is not the mountain. It is her daughter's room. She walks in. The window closes. The loop stops.

**C - Stay in the Blizzard (Bad Ending)**
Trigger: Loops exhausted (15 times) without making Lin Suwen face the truth.
Ending narrative: The hotel no longer resets. The blizzard stops — but all the guest rooms are empty. In Lin Suwen's room only a letter remains, pressed under the pillow. The letter has yellowed — as if placed there for ten years. You pick up the letter. Walk out of the hotel. There are no footprints on the mountain path — only your own. You must walk to the foot of the mountain. You must find the person named Duoduo. But you don't know how old she is now. You don't even know if she is still waiting.
