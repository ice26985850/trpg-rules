# Time Loop TRPG — Monster Codex (Overview of Loop Anomaly Entities)

> This codex collects the "anomalous entities," "environmental crises," and "temporal anomalies" in the Time Loop world that pose a threat to players.
> The vast majority of them **cannot be defeated** — they can only be dispelled through understanding the truth, correct action, or endurance.
> ▶ Use the entity stat cards together with `sheets/敵人角色卡合集.xlsx`; this codex is for the GM's quick reference.

---

## I. Threat Classification and Threat Levels

- **Anomalous Entity:** Humanoid afterimages / phantoms made visible by rifts; their gaze triggers a Will (WIL) check and Panic.
- **Environmental Threat:** Ownerless area crises (e.g., gas leaks) that cannot be attacked and can only be resolved through action.
- **Temporal Anomaly:** Collapses of the world's rules themselves (e.g., Time-Jump Collapse); cannot be opposed, only endured while ending the loop as quickly as possible.

Threat Levels: 2 (Common) / 3 (Dangerous) / 4 (Critical) / 5 (Lethal — must escape)

> Note: This system has no traditional "grind monsters for levels" design. These entities are "symptoms of the world," not a source of XP.
> Treat them as puzzles and atmosphere, not as combat objectives.

## II. Entity Entries

### Entity One: The Phantom of the Elevator
- **Type:** Anomalous Entity / Temporal
- **Threat Level:** 2 (Common)
- **Scenario:** The Elevator
- **Appearance:** Inside the elevator car — from Loop 6 onward
- **Combat Attributes (equivalent):** Physique (Action (ACT) equivalent) 2 / Will (WIL) equivalent 4 / Alertness (Insight (INS) equivalent) 3 / Social Resistance 5
- **Attack Method:** No direct attack — it does not harm the living
- **Base Damage:** 0 (but it makes you see things you shouldn't → triggers a Panic check)
- **Removal Method:** Cannot be defeated — can only be dispelled through "farewell"
- **Special Abilities:**
  1. Gaze Fear: When nearby, make a Will (WIL) check (Normal +0); failure → Mild Panic
  2. Time Distortion: Clocks near it accelerate — bringing you closer to the loop endpoint
  3. Truth Mapping: If the player says to Zhang Mingde "He is no longer here," it dissipates and leaves behind a fragment clue
  4. Degradation Boost: After Loop 9 its presence grows stronger — the Will (WIL) check becomes Hard (−1)
- **GM Note:** This is not an enemy; it is the "manifestation" of a rift. It does not want to harm the player; it is waiting for the player to understand the truth.

### Entity Two: The Afterimage at the Town Square (Chen Yan's Afterimage)
- **Type:** Anomalous Entity
- **Threat Level:** 3 (Dangerous)
- **Scenario:** Town Judgment Day
- **Appearance:** The town square — from Loop 7 onward, on the third day at dusk
- **Combat Attributes (equivalent):** Physique (Action (ACT) equivalent) 1 / Will (WIL) equivalent 5 / Alertness (Insight (INS) equivalent) 4 / Social Resistance 5
- **Attack Method:** Gaze — no contact needed
- **Base Damage:** Mental Damage (fatigue +2, if the Will (WIL) check fails)
- **Removal Method:** Cannot be attacked — can only be released by exposing the truth
- **Special Abilities:**
  1. Silent Gaze: Locking eyes for more than 3 seconds → Will (WIL) check (Hard −1); success grants a Flashback, failure +2 fatigue
  2. Memory Projection: If the player has obtained ≥5 fragments, it directly projects images into their mind
  3. Time Freeze: Loop 10+, when it appears nearby NPCs briefly freeze (5–10 seconds)
  4. Truth Release: When the mayor publicly confesses his crime — it smiles, then dissipates
- **GM Note:** Chen Yan's afterimage. This is not a vengeful ghost; it is the structure of reality reminding the living: the truth has not yet been spoken.

### Entity Three: Gas Leak in the Chemistry Lab
- **Type:** Environmental Threat
- **Threat Level:** 4 (Critical)
- **Scenario:** The Last School Festival
- **Appearance:** Around the chemistry lab → spreads to the whole school on Day 7
- **Combat Attributes (equivalent):** all 0 (ownerless)
- **Attack Method:** Area continuous damage
- **Base Damage:** 1 round exposed → Minor Wound; 2 rounds → Moderate Wound; 3 rounds → Severe Wound; 4 rounds → Near-Death
- **Removal Method:** Cannot be defeated — can only be stopped by sealing the source
- **Special Abilities:**
  1. Area Spread: Each round the spread radius +1 area (lab → entire floor → whole school)
  2. Odorless and Colorless: Detection requires an Insight (INS) check (Hard −1); failure means you don't realize you've been exposed
  3. Time Pressure: Each round the countdown −2 grid (high pressure)
  4. Sealing Window: Seal the vents before it spreads to the whole school — only requires 1 standard action (2 Action Points (AP)) + Action check (Hard −1)
- **GM Note:** An environmental threat is not an enemy; it is a crisis that needs to be "resolved." Combat cannot stop it — only correct action can.

### Entity Four: Time-Jump Collapse
- **Type:** Temporal Anomaly
- **Threat Level:** 5 (Lethal — must escape)
- **Scenario:** All Scenarios (Loop 10+)
- **Appearance:** Any scene — triggered when a d10 roll comes up 1
- **Combat Attributes (equivalent):** all 0 (world rules)
- **Attack Method:** Time jump — the character is thrown into a random future / past moment
- **Base Damage:** The jump itself causes no damage, but may place the character in a lethal scene
- **Removal Method:** Cannot be defeated
- **Special Abilities:**
  1. Time Jump: Triggered on d10=1, jumps 1d3 rounds
  2. Causal Disorder: After returning from the jump, the character may find that some things have happened / not yet happened — inconsistent with teammates' memories
  3. Fatigue Accumulation: Each time experiencing a time jump → fatigue +2
  4. Accelerated Loop Cap: Loop 12+, the d10=1 trigger threshold changes to d10 ≤ 2
- **GM Note:** This is the loop structure itself collapsing, not an enemy — it is a world rule. It cannot be opposed; it can only be endured while ending the loop as quickly as possible.

## III. Design Notes (GM Cheat Sheet)

- All entities' "threat" comes from **information and time pressure**, not stat-crushing.
- Removal conditions are always tied to "truth" or "correct action" — treat entities as gears that drive the scenario forward.
- Loop 10 is the watershed: Time-Jump Collapse begins to intervene, and difficulty spikes significantly (see the GM Core Rulebook's "Loop Degradation Roadmap").
- If players try to "fight" these entities, remind them: death only resets the loop; the problem remains.
