# S5 · The Return Costs More Than the Going

> This scenario is the second station of the playstyle shift: the player **exploits a system loophole for the first time** — deliberately falling into The Void and then climbing back out. From passive survival to actively manipulating the rules. This is the quietest, most psychological round of the whole campaign.

## 2. GM Summary

**Storyline**: At the Campfire, someone is gone — suggested to pick a Campfire companion the player is emotionally bonded to, Gilmar Sato (§8-A), or in a solo game the Echo Companion the player has rescued most often. Only the player remembers him (because of the Memory Card). Patient Seventeen's chart (S4) gives the key: after her sister's chart number was written an address — the Swamp. In Act Two, the player must find the Sunken Stair and confirm where it leads. In Act Three the player descends the Sunken Stair to The Void — no Killer, no objective, Hope Point (HOP) continuously drains, but the only exit is "regenerate one genuine emotion." The player is flung into the Hidden Workshop (Fane's lab).

**Truth (GM-Exclusive)**: Ember generated in The Void does not roll toward the Campfire — because The Void is not on that road. The Entity has a **vague instinct** about Ember: it cannot tell the two kinds of hope apart, but by instinct isolates "those who no longer produce self-hope." This is its immune system, not intelligence. This makes the eventual "feed The Entity to death" plan **feasible but dangerous** — providing the tension for S7 and S8.

**How to Run**: Swamp movement is restricted, Hag's (HAG) traps can teleport — "space cannot be trusted." The Void scene should **clear the table, shut off the heartbeat, and stretch the pauses** — the only scene in the whole campaign with no heartbeat; that absence is itself the pressure. The solo configuration gives an 8-round Void cap and a full 3 trigger objects (error tolerance).

## 3. Opening Text (read aloud)

> One person is missing from the fire.
>
> Not dead — "not here." You ask Eloise; Eloise tilts her head at you, like you spoke a language she doesn't understand.
>
> "Gilmar?" she says. "Who?"
>
> You open your mouth. You aren't sure you aren't mad. But you have his Memory Card in your pocket. If he never existed, whose is this card?
>
> Eloise looks at the fire, for a long time.
>
> "If you're right," she says, "if he really existed once… then he's in The Void now."
>
> She lifts her eyes.
>
> "And The Void has one entrance, no exit."
>
> You spread out Patient Seventeen's chart. You read the last line aloud — that address, the Swamp.
>
> "Then walk into the entrance," you say, "and find the exit."
>
> For the first time, Eloise shows something like fear.
>
> "The return," she says, "costs more than the going."

## 6. NPC Appearances and Lines

**Gilmar Sato (§8-A, Campfire companion who fell into The Void)**
- Emotional core. In The Void he is almost entirely empty, doesn't recognize the player. **The player does one unrewarded thing for him** (feed him, hold him, say his name) → can be rescued.
- Line (Void): "…a name? I think I had one. But that was long ago. You… know me?"

**Fane the Gleaner (§8-D, Hidden Workshop master)**
- Provides key S7 knowledge and the truth of the Hatch.
- Words carved on the table: "It isn't that it can't be deceived. It's that it can't notice it's been deceived."

**Whisperer · the Voice Under the Water (RC-02 variant)**
- The swamp Whisperer, intelligence-type, Encounter Budget Cost (EBC) 0.
- Line: "The return costs more than the going. The going: a little hope. The return: you must find something you want, down there."

**Echo Companion · the Handwiper** (if its pod was plucked in S3): an Afterimage in The Void, **it recognizes the player** (+5 Campfire Ember).

## 7. Decision Points and Branch Results

**Decision One: Descend the Sunken Stair? (actively exploit the loophole)**
- Don't go → pure escape completes the Trial, but misses the main story (no Fane's notes, can't rescue Gilmar).
- Go → enter The Void (Act Three), actively manipulate the rules.

**Decision Two: Void trigger-object choice**
- Do something unrewarded for Gilmar → rescue (in S8 he will remember the player's name).
- Acknowledge / make amends to a sacrificed Echo → emotional closure.
- Remember what the carried item is → memory closure.

**Decision Three: Rescue Gilmar?**
- Rescue → he returns to the Campfire (empty, recovers a little each scenario, remembers the player's name in S8).
- Don't rescue → emotional vacancy, but the player keeps more Hope Point (HOP).
