# S8 · The Nameless Heart

> Capstone scenario. S8 is **not a harder Trial** — it is a **rule flip**: the Generator is an igniter, being Hooked is the only way to contact the Entity (The Entity) Proper, escape is surrender, and Hope Points (HOP) are ammunition. The player chooses one of three endings: Conflagration / Takeover / Ember.

## 2. GM Summary

**Storyline**: Act One plans at the campfire (the only time in the whole campaign the "player is in charge"). All known conditions are laid out: total Campfire Ember, the first ember (The First Ember), the Ember Vein (`ember_vein`), the Lone Tower, the Umbilical, the Mirror-Bearer's intel, Fane's device, Memory Cards. The player decides three things: which path to take, who gets Hooked, and which ending to choose. Act Two is the Forsaken Boneyard trial + tower climb (Generator = Ember Vein charge; the Endless Stair needs the first ember to ascend; the Statue Ring lets you "deliver a eulogy" to replenish Campfire Ember). Act Three passes through the Sky-Rift to reach the Nameless Heart — an organ with an empty hole at its center (the Entity Core). The Entity acts in person only when a hook is involved; the player must manufacture one hook-contact with it, then ignite (Conflagration) / enter the hole (Takeover) / not ignite (Ember).

**Truth (GM only)**: the Entity Core is empty — "it was empty all along. That is why it has been eating." The embers came out of it, are the same kind of thing as it, so fire travels back from its own body. The Ember Moment reveals the "Nameless" is the gathering of all who looked back — the first ember was not dropped by him, but by them, including the player.

**How to Run**: **The GM must clearly tell the player in Act One that the rules are flipped** (empowerment, not a surprise). Solo configuration: have the player state the plan once each to Eloise, Fane, and the Mirror-Bearer; each asks one question (three moments of self-examination). In Act Three, turn off all the lights, leaving only a single candle by the glass jar; clear the table, placing only the Campfire Ember glass jar at the center.

## 3. Opening Text (read-aloud)

> This time, no one wakes you by the fire. You are sitting there yourself.

> Eloise pushes the glass jar toward you. There are more beads in it than ever — that is all of you, the residue of every look-back, every rescue, every time you handed over the last thing in your hand, across eight scenarios.

> "Take inventory," she says. "See if we have enough."

> Fane spreads out that diagram: the Lone Tower, the Umbilical, the Ember Vein, a route map to the same wound.

> Ari the Mirror-Bearer rarely speaks: "The Entity acts in person when a hook is involved. That is its only exposed moment."

> You take a deep breath. In the firelight, there is no script. No word "survive."

> Only you, and how you all decide to end it.

> Or decide not to.

## 5. Encounter Setup

**Main Killer**: Legion (LGN). Avatar Menace Value (AVM) +1 (N≥3) / −1 (N=1). Challenge Rating (CR) = Prestige (PRS) + AVM; this scenario's Prestige (PRS) is 18–20, so Challenge Rating (CR) 19–21 (17–19 at N=1). Occupies 60% of the Encounter Budget (EB). ## 7. Decision Points and Branch Outcomes

**Core decision (Act One, made by the player)**: ① which path to take (Tower / Umbilical / both); ② who gets Hooked (someone must contact the Entity at the moment it acts); ③ which ending to choose.

**Act Three ending divergence**:

| Ending | Trigger | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Conflagration** | Execute ignition; fire tier reaches threshold | Fire burns into the Entity; the Realms collapse (see fire tiers) |
| **Takeover** | Enter the hole (**requires the S6 "Don't interrupt" choice for process knowledge**) | The player becomes the new Entity, writing the first rule: "no more hooks" |
| **Ember** | Open an exit gate and escape in Act Two, or do not ignite in Act Three | Return to the campfire; the fire is a little bigger; the cycle continues |

**Ignition steps (Conflagration)**: ① the hooked one takes the ember; ② press into the Entity's hand (Will (WIL) check, Difficulty Class (DC) Legendary; may spend Hope Points (HOP) to lower the tier, 2 points per tier down); ③ fire spreads automatically; ④ the remaining players contribute Hope Points (HOP) and Memory Cards (each 1 HOP = 1 fire tier, each Memory Card = 3 fire tiers); ⑤ Campfire Ember surges up from the Umbilical (= accumulated total); ⑥ compare against the Conflagration threshold table.

## 9. Ending Branches and Rewards

**Ending branch (choose one of three)**:

- **Conflagration**: three tiers by fire level —
  - Crippled (Campfire Ember reaches the Crippled threshold in the §9 player-count table; solo 17): the Entity is heavily wounded, most Realms collapse, half the players return to reality and half remain in the residual fog (players decide who stays), with seepage hints suggesting a return.
  - Full (reaches the Full threshold; solo 27): all Realms collapse, all players return to reality, with incomplete memories (remember "there was something important" but cannot say what).
  - Blazing (reaches the Blazing threshold; solo 41): in addition to the above, may specify one character who fell into the Void / was Reforged to bring back as well (Gilmar / Hilda / a sacrificed Echo / any player character), waking in some street in reality holding something warm.
- **Takeover**: requires the S6 "Don't interrupt" knowledge. The player becomes the new Entity, writing "no more hooks" — opens a "you write the rules" variant campaign.
- **Ember**: escape or do not ignite. Return to the campfire, the fire a little bigger, the cycle continues — a new player character wakes, the old player becomes an NPC guide.

**Rewards**

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Blood Point (BP) | Standard × (per player-count table) |
| Prestige (PRS) | **Level 20 (capstone)** |
| Narrative | One of the three endings |
| **Campaign complete** | — |
| **Sequel hook** | "Ember" continues into a new campaign (old character becomes an NPC); "Takeover" opens a variant campaign; "Conflagration" opens a modern-metropolis sequel (seepage investigation) |

**Ember Moment trigger text** (always triggers, regardless of ending):
> In the Nameless Heart you see a human shape, very faint, standing at the edge of that hole. His back is to you. His posture is the looking-back posture. He has stood there for who knows how long.
> You recognize him. Not by his face — you recognize the **posture**.
> It is the posture of the twelve statues at the Forsaken Boneyard's Tower Base. It is the posture of the skeleton by the Ember Niche. It is the posture of everyone who stopped at a doorway and looked back.
> He is not one person. He is **everyone**.
> The first ember was not dropped by him. It was dropped by them. Every one. Including you.
