# S1 · First Awakening

> This scenario is the prologue of the *Dead by Daylight (DbD)* TRPG adaptation. The player wakes by the Campfire for the first time, not knowing who they are or where they are. This scenario's job is to **establish the baseline of fear** and **teach the core rules**, while planting four campaign-spanning hooks.

## 2. GM Summary

**Storyline**: The player character wakes by the Campfire with no memory. Campfire chronicler Eloise Finch (§8-A) gives two rules — "Fix five Generators and the gate opens" and "Get Hooked three times and you're done" — and, contradiction-style, says in Act One "you live longer if you don't rescue people," yet personally goes to rescue someone during the Trial. The player enters a standard Trial and learns Repair, the heartbeat and Terror Radius (TR), Chase, the Pallet, the Window, the Hook and rescue. Midway, the GM lets the player witness firsthand the opposite effects of two kinds of Hope Point (HOP) on the Entity Hunger (EH) track. After returning to the Campfire, the first Ember Moment triggers, and the player sees "Ember" for the first time.

**Truth (GM-Exclusive)**: Ember is what The Entity cannot spit out — it is the crystallization of Altruistic Hope. The altruistic hope generated by rescuing someone makes Entity Hunger (EH) drop (The Entity "frowns"); the self-hope generated by repairing makes Entity Hunger (EH) rise (The Entity is "fed"). This contrast is the core hook of the campaign-spanning mechanic, but S1 does not explain it — it only plants a question in the player's mind. The Campfire is safe precisely because it is built from countless Embers (revealed only in S7).

**How to Run**: This is a "tutorial Trial," but do not teach by lecturing. Let the rules emerge from experience. Deliberately keep a pure chase-and-escape experience — S1 places no Realm Construct, letting the player first meet "the Killer" as a threat. The first Chase **must let the player escape** (see §5-5 Pacing Control). In the solo configuration (N=1), the Echo Companion "47" is the metronome and emotional anchor.

## 3. Opening Text (read aloud)

> You open your eyes.
>
> The first thing you feel is heat. A fire burns in front of you, its light stretching your shadow long across the rusted metal floor. The air tastes of iron rust and cold moonlight.
>
> You don't know your own name. You reach into your pocket — there is a small thing inside, not yours, but you can't yet recall whose. Maybe a key, maybe a torn ticket stub, maybe a button. You close your hand around it.
>
> Someone sits across the fire. She looks up at you, as if she already knew you would wake.
>
> "Another one," she says. Not gentle, but not unkind. "Don't panic. You're safe for now."
>
> She points at the fire.
>
> "As long as you're by the fire, it's safe. Out there, no guarantees."
>
> You want to ask a lot more, but she is already standing.
>
> "Listen to two things first. One — this place is full of machines. Fix five and the door opens. Two — if that thing catches you and Hooks you three times, you're gone."
>
> She hesitates, as if recalling something she'd rather not.
>
> "And — you'll live longer if you don't go rescuing people."
>
> When she says it, she doesn't meet your eyes.
>
> "I'm Eloise. Just find a way to survive till dawn — though here, it never seems to get light."

## 6. NPC Appearances and Lines

**Eloise Finch (§8-A, Campfire Chronicler)**
- Act One opening: delivers the rules and hooks: "Fix five Generators and the door opens." "Hooked three times and you're gone." "You'll live longer if you don't rescue people." — then she personally goes to rescue someone during the Trial.
- Hook line: "The one who sat where you sit vanished. Three weeks later, in a Trial, I saw a thing — it walked exactly like him." (points to S6 The Silent One (SIL))
- Closing: "Haven't you noticed the fire is a little bigger than when you first came?"

**Echo Companion · 47 (§8-B, solo configuration)**
- Keeps counting. **Stops at 100 to look at you.**
- Solo-game line (fixed line at the end of each round, as a metronome): "…ninety-seven, ninety-eight, ninety-nine, a hundred. See? I'm still counting."

**Whisperer · Well Chorus (RC-02, the Suffocation Pit Whisperer)**
- Intelligence-type construct (Encounter Budget Cost (EBC) 0). Can trade one Trial piece of info: spend 1 Hope Point (HOP) for "the relative bearing of one Generator this round."
- Line (from the bottom of the pit, overlapping chorus): "Come down. Come see. We all forgot, but you still remember."

## 7. Decision Points and Branch Results

**Decision One: Rescue or not (core tutorial)**
- The player sees an Echo Companion (or, in group play, a teammate) Hooked in E4.
- Choose to rescue: triggers Altruistic Hope, Entity Hunger (EH) −1, gain 1 Hope Point (HOP), and unlock the Ember Moment. **This is the most important decision in S1** — the GM should, midway, show the player the track contrast of "self-hope raising Entity Hunger (EH) +1, altruism lowering Entity Hunger (EH) −1."
- Choose not to rescue: the character is safe, but no Ember Moment triggers this round, and Campfire Ember accrues less.

**Decision Two: Optional exploration nodes (solo / duo)**
- The player may spend time exploring `mm_opt_shack` or `mm_opt_locker`.
- Explore `mm_opt_shack`: gain a Memory Card clue pointing to S3 "Roots That Eat No Soil" (a note reading "tree").
- Explore `mm_opt_locker`: gain a common item, and words carved into the inside of the iron cabinet (pointing to S2 "Scars in the Iron Valley").

## 9. Ending Branches and Rewards

**Ending branch**: S1 is a linear prologue with no multiple endings. The only branch is "whether the Ember Moment triggers" — as long as the player rescues at least one companion (including an Echo Companion) during the Trial, it is guaranteed to trigger after returning to the Campfire.

**Rewards**

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Bloodpoint (BP) | Standard (per character-builder formula) |
| Prestige (PRS) | Rises to level 3 upon completion |
| Hope Point (HOP) | +2 (scenario complete) + accrued during play |
| Campfire Ember | Accrued during play (est. 3–6) |
| Memory Card | 0 (S1 grants no Memory Card) |
| Item | 1 common item per person (from Chest or Campfire offering) |
| Narrative | First sighting of "Ember" (unexplained) |

**Ember Moment trigger text** (GM reads aloud):
> The second you let go of the Hook, something very small drops out of your chest. It lands on the ground, warm.
> You bend to pick it up, and it is gone.
> After you return to the Campfire, at the edge of the fire you see one exactly like it.

## 10. Connections to Adjacent Scenarios

- **Forward**: None (prologue). The un-personal item (key / ticket stub / button) the player gets in S1 becomes the anchor of the S3 Memory Card.
- **Backward (S2)**: Eloise's contradiction in S1 — saying "don't rescue people" yet personally rescuing during the Trial — is the lead-in to S2 "Scars in the Iron Valley"; in S2 the player discovers some of those scars were carved by Eloise herself.
- **Hook payoff points**: ① Eloise's "the one who sat where you sit" → S6 The Silent One (SIL) Hilda Lane; ② the carved words in `mm_opt_locker` → S2 scar #8; ③ the "tree" clue in `mm_opt_shack` → S3 the Sacrifice Tree.
