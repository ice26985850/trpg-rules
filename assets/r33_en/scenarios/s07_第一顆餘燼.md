# S7 · The First Ember

> This scenario is the campaign's **arming scenario**: the player obtains the first ember (The First Ember) here, and comes to understand what the Campfire is, and what the Ember Vein and the Umbilical are. The player gains all the knowledge and tools needed to fight the Entity (The Entity).

## 2. GM Summary

**Storyline**: At the campfire the player studies Fane's note wall (S5); five slips of paper assemble into a conclusion — the Entity builds its Realms from people's memories, and some of what it devours it spits back out, and those things roll toward the campfire. Eloise adds the linchpin line: "So what if… we make it spit up more?" In the second-act trial, the deep leaf litter of Red Forest makes "dying-crawling is actually safe" — the most counterintuitive design in the whole campaign. In the third act the player descends three levels of the Purification Sanctum, reaches the Ember Niche, reads the engraved plaque of the "Nameless," and takes the first ember.

**Truth (GM only)**: The Ember Moment reveals — the ember is pulling the player home, past the Sanctum, the forest, the fog, to see the campfire; but from beneath the campfire a thin, red, *living* thing stretches upward, threading through every Realm, connecting to the Entity itself — that is the **Umbilical**. The embers came out of it, so they are "the same kind of thing" as it, and between the same kind of thing, "fire spreads." The red line that has been on the world map since the very beginning finally has a name.

**How to Run**: Open woodland + Huntress (HTR) ranged throwing — "distance is no longer safe." The deep leaf litter is a double-edged sword: a dying crawl is completely concealed (the Killer cannot find you by line of sight), but standing and sprinting leaves a 3-round trail, while footstep sounds are absorbed. The GM should make every Sanctum description contain one thing that is *off* (wrong proportions). If the player's Campfire Ember reaches the threshold, they take it directly; otherwise a bonus trial triggers (survive inside the Sanctum).

## 3. Opening Text (read-aloud)

> Five papers lie spread by the fire. You didn't write them — they're Fane's hand.

> You turn them over one by one.

> First: "Building a Trial costs it effort. It keeps accounts."

> Second: "That's why it gives a way out. Prey with no hope has no taste, and prey with no taste isn't worth the effort."

> Third: "But I found something it doesn't know. Some of what it eats, it spits back out."

> Fourth: "I followed those things for three years. They all roll the same direction."

> Fifth: "Every night you sit warming yourselves next to that pile of things."

> You lay the five in a row. They read like one sentence — but the last half is missing.

> Eloise brightens the fire and looks at you.

> "So what if," she says, "we make it spit up more?"

> The fire doesn't answer. But the firelight jumps once, as if listening.

## 6. NPC Appearances and Lines

**The "Nameless" (§8-D, skeleton / first survivor)**
- Core of this scenario; exists only through an engraved plaque.
- Plaque text: "I had already gotten out. The door was open. One foot of mine was already outside. Then I heard someone calling from behind. I didn't know him. When I looked back I wasn't thinking anything. I just felt that leaving alone didn't seem right. Later I was hoisted up. Something fell out of me. It was warm. I picked it up. Because it was the only thing left that was still my own. I left it here. If you're reading this far — then you probably looked back too. Take it."

**Eloise Finch (§8-A)**: speaks the linchpin line "what if we make it spit up more?"

**Ari the Mirror-Bearer (§8-D)**: appears for the first time (deep in the Sanctum, through the Memory-Gleaning Mirror). Provides the S8 path: the Lone Tower.

**Hilda / The Silent One (SIL) (§8-A)**: depends on the S6 choice — if Reforged, appears as a second Killer; if rescued, assists the player.

## 7. Decision Points and Branch Outcomes

**Decision One: whether to deliberately drop prone (deep leaf litter)**
- Standing and fleeing leaves a 3-round trail and footstep sounds; a dying crawl is fully concealed but slow.
- Branch: deliberately giving up standing = the safest movement method in the whole campaign, and also the psychological preparation for S8's "deliberately give up escape."

**Decision Two: whether Campfire Ember reaches the threshold**
- Reaches threshold → take the first ember (The First Ember) directly.
- Does not reach → triggers the bonus trial (survive inside the Sanctum for a number of rounds, see player-count table), then take it.
