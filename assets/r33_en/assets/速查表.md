# Quick Reference (Dead by Daylight TRPG)

> **Purpose**: A cheat-sheet to print and lay on the table. Prioritizes information density and scannability — **tables first, prose second**.
> **Naming Iron Rule**: All game terms use the "English Full (ABBR)" format; parentheses are always full-width. English variable names inside Excel formulas are an iron-clause exception.
> **Grid & Time**: 1 square = a 2-meter grid cell; the only time unit in the system is the "Round", roughly 10–15 seconds; a Trial is about 24 rounds ≈ 48 minutes.
> **Printing Suggestion**: Print Sections 1–5 on one sheet (player side), Sections 6–9 on one sheet (Game Master (GM) side), Sections 10–12 on one sheet (status & solo).
> **Companion Reference**: For skill details see the *Skill List*; for status details see the *Status Effect Catalogue*; for Killer and Creation stat blocks see the *Monster Catalogue*.

## 1　Check Fundamentals

### 1-1 Core Check Formula

```
Check Total (CT) = 2d10 + Attribute Bonus (AB) + Skill Level (SL) + Situation Modifier (SM)
Margin of Success (MoS) = Check Total (CT) − Difficulty Class (DC)
```

In full: **Check Total (CT) equals the sum of two ten-sided dice, plus Attribute Bonus (AB), Skill Level (SL), and Situation Modifier (SM); Margin of Success (MoS) equals Check Total (CT) minus Difficulty Class (DC).**

### 1-2 Four-Tier Outcome Criteria

| Result | Criterion | One-liner |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical Success** | Margin of Success (MoS) ≥ +5 **or** Natural Double-Ten | You did it, and earned a bonus |
| **Success** | Margin of Success (MoS) +0 ~ +4 | You did it |
| **Failure** | Margin of Success (MoS) −1 ~ −4 | You didn't, usually with a noise cue |
| **Critical Failure** | Margin of Success (MoS) ≤ −5 **or** Natural Double-One | You didn't, and something went wrong |

- **Natural Double-Ten** (both ten-sided dice show 10; 1%): **always a Critical Success**, overriding Margin of Success (MoS).
- **Natural Double-One** (both ten-sided dice show 1; 1%): **always a Critical Failure**, overriding Margin of Success (MoS).

### 1-3 Opposed Checks

```
Both sides roll 2d10 + their own modifiers; higher total wins. Tie → Killer wins.
```

**"Tie goes to Killer" is the single most important ruling in this system.** Survivors cannot stall by tying — only terrain resources let them.

**Five cross-faction opposed axes (every opposed check must fall under one of these five rows)**

| Opposed Context | Killer Attribute | Survivor Attribute | Typical Scene |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chase** | Hunt (HNT) | Agility (AGI) | Catch up / open Distance Band (DB) |

### 2-1 Difficulty Class (DC) is dynamic

```
Live Difficulty Class (DC_live) = Base Difficulty Class (DC_base) + Fear Pressure (FP) + INT(Challenge Rating (CR) ÷ 4)
```

In full: **Live Difficulty Class (DC) equals the action's Base Difficulty Class, plus the current Fear Pressure (FP), plus Challenge Rating (CR) divided by 4 rounded down.**

### 3-3 【Authoritative Ruling】 Semantics of Attribute Point (ATP / KAP)

> **Attribute Point (ATP) and Killer Attribute Point (KAP) = "the sum of the five attributes' final values", NOT "free allocation points above a base of 1".**

```
Survivor: Agility (AGI) + Technique (TEC) + Stealth (STL) + Focus (FOC) + Will (WIL) = Attribute Point (ATP)
Killer    : Brute (BRU) + Hunt (HNT) + Perception (PER) + Dread (DRE) + Occult (OCC) = Killer Attribute Point (KAP)

Attribute Point (ATP)     = 15 + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 2)     single attribute range 1–10, at creation 1–5 each
Killer Attribute Point (KAP) = 8 + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 2)   single attribute range 1–5
```

In full: **the sum of the Survivor's five attribute final values exactly equals Attribute Point (ATP); the sum of the Killer's five attribute levels exactly equals Killer Attribute Point (KAP). Attribute Point (ATP) equals 15 plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 2 rounded down; Killer Attribute Point (KAP) equals 8 plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 2 rounded down.**

**Both sides mean exactly the same thing — at the table just remember one line: the sum equals that number.**

| Faction | Legal example at Prestige (PRS) 1 | Five summed | Legal example at Prestige (PRS) 20 | Five summed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Survivor | 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 | **15** | 10 / 6 / 4 / 3 / 2 | **25** |
| Killer | 3 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 1 | **8** | 5 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 2 | **18** |

**⚠ Common Misconception**: The Killer's "8 points" at Prestige (PRS) 1 is **not** "five attributes each start at 1, then freely allocate 8 more". Under that reading the five-attribute sum would be 13, and at cap would approach 23/25 — the eight Avatars' attribute shapes would be flattened and the Occult (OCC)-driven differentiation axis would fail entirely. **This ruling is authoritative; both rulebooks and the character sheet implement it exactly as written.**

## 4　Distance Band (DB) Reference

**1 square = 2 meters. Chase is always resolved by Distance Band (DB); the grid is only used for short-range tactical movement on the encounter map.**

| Band # | Distance Band (Abbr) | Meters | Squares | Tactical meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Contact (CON)** | 0–2 m | 0–1 sq | Killer can basic attack, can carry a Dying survivor; you can drop a Pallet to stun |
| **1** | **Near (NEA)** | 2–8 m | 1–4 sq | Chase bites hard; Killer can lunge; Pallet can stun |
| **2** | **Mid (MID)** | 8–16 m | 4–8 sq | Chase maintained; can vault to open distance; Killer can use thrown Powers |
| **3** | **Far (FAR)** | 16–32 m | 8–16 sq | inner edge of Terror Radius (TR); heartbeat audible; can use Aura abilities |
| **4** | **Escape (ESC)** | beyond 32 m | beyond 16 sq | Chase auto-ends; no heartbeat; safe to repair |

### 4-1 Chase Opposed Check Distance Band (DB) Resolution

| Difference (Killer total − Survivor total) | Distance Band (DB) change |
|---|---|
| ≥ +10 | **−2** (rapid close) |
| +1 ~ +9 | **−1** |
| **0 (tie)** | **−1** (Killer advantage ruling) |
| −1 ~ −9 | **unchanged** |
| ≤ −10 | **+1** (you opened distance) |

> **Key**: Winning a normal check only holds the line — you must win by 10+ to actively open distance. To change the Distance Band (DB), use terrain resources.

### 4-2 Terrain Resources (the Survivor's only active comeback tool)

| Resource | Action Point (AP) | Effect | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drop Pallet (Pallet)** | 1 | Killer at Near (NEA) or Contact (CON): stunned **2 rounds**, Distance Band (DB) **+2** | Pallet is destroyed once dropped; Killer may spend 2 AP to break an undropped Pallet |
| **Vault Window (Window)** | 1 | Distance Band (DB) **+1**; Killer must spend 2 extra AP to vault | After a window is vaulted 3 times it is permanently sealed by The Entity |
| **Loop** | 2 | Chase opposed check **+4** Situation Modifier (SM); win → Killer's Bloodlust resets to 0 | each cover usable 2× per match |
| **Juke** | 2 | Make a Track opposed check; Critical Success → **immediately break Chase** | requires a line-of-sight blocker |
| **Vault Sprint** | 2 | After vaulting, add a sprint same round, Distance Band (DB) extra **+1** | triggers Exhausted 2 rounds |

**Resource value**: one Pallet ≈ +3.48 Distance Band ≈ offsets **4.7 rounds** of pursuit; one Window vault ≈ +1.5 Distance Band ≈ offsets **2.0 rounds**.

### 4-3 Bloodlust & Terror Radius (TR)

| Bloodlust tier | Trigger | Chase check bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Tier I | continuous Chase ≥ 3 rounds | +1 |
| Tier II | continuous Chase ≥ 5 rounds | +2 |
| Tier III | continuous Chase ≥ 7 rounds | +3 |

**Reset conditions**: hit a survivor, break a Pallet, Chase ends. **Decay**: after Chase is interrupted, −1 tier per round.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squares | 16 sq | 12 sq | 10 sq | 0 sq |

### 5-4 Med-Kit Charge Baseline (Core-Mechanics ruling)

The Med-Kit is measured in **Charge (CHG)** durability; each 1 AP spent consumes 1 Charge (CHG). Self-heal costs 4 CHG / use, healing others costs 3 CHG / use; healing one Health State (HS) requires 12 accumulated Progress Points (PP). The table below is the core-mechanics designer's ruling (old → new), fixing the original "even Common can't finish one tier" over-tight problem:

| ID | Name | Rarity | Charge (CHG) old → new | Self-heal check count (new ÷ 4) | Tiers healable (self, typical build) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IT-01 | Camp First-Aid Kit | Common (C) | 4 → 16 | 4 | exactly 1 tier (self-build M≥6) |
| IT-02 | Household First-Aid | Uncommon (U) | 6 → 20 | 5 | 1 tier with margin |
| IT-03 | ER Med-Kit | Rare (R) | 4 → 24 | 6 | ~1.5 tiers (1 tier with margin) |
| IT-04 | Ranger Med-Kit | Very Rare (VR) | 8 → 28 | 7 | 2 tiers |

> **Ruling model**: Self-heal check = 2d10 + Focus (FOC) bonus + First Aid (MED) skill level vs self-heal DC 14. At Prestige (PRS) 1 / 5 / 10 under a typical self-build (heal efficiency M = 4 / 6 / 8), self-heal success ≈ **64% / 79% / 90%**, expected +2.4 / +3.6 / +4.6 Progress Points (PP) per check; with the progress floor (0 / 8), healing one tier (12 PP) expects about **5 / 4 / 3** self-heal checks. The new values ensure Common finishes exactly one tier and Very Rare finishes two tiers under a typical build. The *Item Catalogue* (IT-01~IT-04) is updated in sync by the item designer.

### 6-1 【Authoritative Ruling】 Settlement player-count multiplier — now table-lookup

The Bloodpoint (BP) settlement player-count multiplier is **the player-count compensation multiplier of the core economy**, not a Perk effect modifier, so it is exempt from the "Perks forbid percentages" iron rule. But to keep players from doing mental math at the table, **always use the table below — no percentages, no formulas written out**:

| N (player Survivor count) | **1** | **2** | **3** | **4** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Settlement player-count multiplier** | **× 1.75** | **× 1.5** | **× 1.25** | **× 1.0** |
| Equivalent fraction (for Excel) | 7 ÷ 4 | 6 ÷ 4 | 5 ÷ 4 | 4 ÷ 4 |

**Settlement order (order affects the total, do not swap)**

```
① Categorize in-match scores by category, each capped at its per-category cap (per-category cap = 2000 + 400 × Prestige (PRS))
② Sum the four categories → in-match base Bloodpoint (BP)
③ Multiply by the settlement player-count multiplier (table above)
④ Add Offering bonuses (not subject to the per-category cap)
⑤ Final single round-down ROUND(…, 0) → match final Bloodpoint (BP)
```

> **Plain version**: **For each missing survivor, settlement pays a quarter more.** One player carrying the whole match earns one and three-quarters shares.

## 7　Pressure Point (PRE) Economy Table

**Pressure Point (PRE) is the Killer's at-table resource pool, fully independent of Action Point (AP). Survivors have no equivalent resource — your resource is terrain.**

```
Pressure Point cap (PRE_max)   = 4 + 2 × N + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 4)
Pressure Point regen (PRE_reg) = 2 + INT(N ÷ 2)
```

In full: **Pressure Point (PRE) cap equals 4 plus twice the player Survivor count (N), plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 4 rounded down; Pressure Point (PRE) regen per round equals 2 plus player Survivor count (N) divided by 2 rounded down.**

| N | Prestige (PRS) 1 cap | Prestige (PRS) 10 cap | Prestige (PRS) 20 cap | Regen/round |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 6 | 8 | 11 | **2** |
| **2** | 8 | 10 | 13 | **3** |
| **3** | 10 | 12 | 15 | **3** |
| **4** | 12 | 14 | 17 | **4** |

### 7-1 Additional gains

| Event | Pressure Point (PRE) |
|---|---|
| Each Hook | **+3** |
| Each Down (survivor enters Dying) | **+2** |
| Each Pallet break | **+1** |

### 7-2 Spend costs

| Use | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Check bonus** | 1 point = +1 | declare before roll, cap +3 per use |
| **Sudden Skill Check** | 2 points | force a survivor mid-continuous-action to make an extra skill check immediately, DC +3 |
| **Force reroll opposed check** | 3 points | use when unhappy with a Chase result, take new result |
| **Use Power** | 2–5 points | per Avatar, see *Monster Catalogue* |
| Survivor Dread Pressure backlash | **−2 points** | when a survivor wins a Dread Pressure opposed check by ≥ +10, the Killer loses 2 points |

> **One line for the GM**: The "+2" in the Chase baseline modifier gap (Δ = +2) — **+1 of it is the attribute gap, +1 is the Pressure Point (PRE) you invested this round.** The Killer's edge is not innate; it is bought with resources. Spend it all and the Survivor turns the table.

## 8　Hook & Rescue Flow

### 8-1 Full flow from Down to Sacrifice

```
[Down] Killer hits a Dying survivor
   │
   ├─ Survivor: may spend 1 AP to crawl 1 sq (Will (WIL)); ally may spend 3 AP to pick up
   ▼
[Carry] Killer spends 1 AP, must be at Contact (CON) band with the Dying survivor
   │   ★ Carry cap 4 rounds, must drop if exceeded
   │   ★ Survivor: each round auto Grasp opposed check (no AP)
   │       └─ 3 cumulative successes → break free to ground, enter Injured
   ▼
[Hook] Killer spends 1 AP, must be adjacent to an unbroken Hook → Killer +3 Pressure Point (PRE)
   ▼
┌─[Stage 1 · Summon] time limit (8 − N) rounds ────────────────────────┐
│  Self-unhook: 1× per round, Focus (FOC) check vs DC 20 − (4 − N)       │
│        max 3 per match; each failure: this stage time limit −2 rounds  │
│  Success → off Hook, enter Injured, gain Unhook Protection              │
│  Timeout → enter Stage 2                                               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ▼
┌─[Stage 2 · Struggle] time limit (8 − N) rounds ──────────────────────┐
│  Forced Will (WIL) skill check once per round                          │
│  DC = 12 + rounds already on Hook (increases each round)               │
│  Failure → this stage time limit −1 round                              │
│  Two consecutive Crit Failures → immediately Stage 3                   │
│  Timeout → Stage 3                                                     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ▼
[Stage 3 · Sacrifice] immediate elimination. The Entity's claw pierces through; the character's match ends.
```

### 8-2 Player-count scaling comparison

| N | Stage rounds | Self-unhook DC | Single success rate (total mod +3) | Three-cumulative success rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **7** | **17** | **28%** | **62.7%** |
| 2 | 6 | 18 | 21% | 50.7% |
| 3 | 5 | 19 | 15% | 38.6% |
| **4** | **4** | **20** | **10%** | **27.1%** |

### 8-3 Stage-memory rules

| Situation | Next hook starts from |
|---|---|
| Rescued in Stage 1 | **Stage 2** |
| Rescued in Stage 2 | **directly Stage 3 (Sacrifice)** |
| Self-unhook success AND remaining time > half | still from Stage 1 |
| Third time hooked / you are last alive survivor and hooked | **immediate Sacrifice** |

### 8-4 Rescue check (Rescue Window)

**Rescue action: 3 AP, baseline is Insight (INS) check vs DC 9.** The closer the Killer is to the Hook, the stricter the check:

| Killer-to-Hook Distance Band (DB) | Check |
|---|---|
| **Escape (ESC)** | **Auto success**, no check |
| **Far (FAR)** | Insight (INS) check vs DC 9 |
| **Mid (MID)** | Insight (INS) check vs DC 9, plus a Sneak (SNK) check vs DC 12. Failing Sneak still rescues, but **you are immediately locked by the Killer and enter Chase** |
| **Near (NEA) / Contact (CON)** | Sneak (SNK) **opposed** against Killer Perception (PER) check.<br>Rescuer wins → rescue succeeds, rescued gains Unhook Protection<br>**Killer wins → Hook Grab**: rescuer enters Dying directly and is carried, the hooked one stays on the Hook |

**Unhook Protection**: a survivor who is rescued or self-unhooks successfully immediately gains **5 rounds** of Endurance + Haste.
**Cancel condition**: performing any conspicuous action (repair, heal, cleanse a Totem, sabotage a Hook, open an Exit Gate) cancels it immediately.

### 8-5 Anti-Camp Mechanic

If the Killer stays **continuously in the hooked survivor's zone for 2 rounds**, the hooked survivor gains **Resolve**:

- In Stage 2 they may also attempt self-unhook; success criterion becomes rolling a **Natural 18–20** (15%).
- The hook-struggle DC escalation **pauses**.

**【Solo Trial】** This rule is a hard protection in a solo match: in solo, if the Killer camps the Hook the player is permanently stuck, so the Anti-Camp mechanic must trigger. The GM may not skip it for "story reasons".

## 9　Endgame Flow

```
[Stage 1] Complete the Generatortarget count
          N=1: 2 │ N=2: 3 │ N=3: 4 │ N=4: 5
          (field spawn count = target + 2; the Killer cannot guard them all at once)
   ▼
[Stage 2] Exit Gates powered, start opening
          Gate segments: N=1: 3 │ N=2: 4 │ N=3: 4 │ N=4: 5
          each segment 3 AP + Mechanics (MEC) check vs DC 10
          CritS +2 seg / Success +1 seg / Failure +0 seg and noise / CritF +0 seg and noise and AP zeroed
          ★ Each segment pushed triggers a noise notification
          ★ "Hold at 99" tactic: stop before the last segment, does NOT trigger Endgame Collapse
   ▼
[Stage 3] Endgame Collapse triggers
          Trigger: any Exit Gate fully opened OR Killer actively closes the Hatch
          Countdown rounds: N=1: 12 │ N=2: 10 │ N=3: 8 │ N=4: 6
          During: all survivors gain Suffocating — heal progress zeroed each round
                Fear Pressure (FP) extra +2; Killer gains Haste
          Slowdown: while any survivor is Dying or hooked, the countdown drops 1 every 2 rounds
   ▼
[End] Countdown to 0 → all survivors still in the realm are immediately reaped by The Entity, Sacrificed out
        Through an Exit Gate (1 AP) or jump into the Hatch (1 AP) → escape success
```

### 9-1 Hatch Quick Reference

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Open threshold** | MAX(1, Generatortarget count − 1) → N=1: 1 │ N=2: 2 │ N=3: 3 │ N=4: 4 |
| **N=1 open condition** | Completed ≥ threshold **and** all Echo Companions eliminated |
| **N ≥ 2 open condition** | Alive players = 1 **or** completed ≥ threshold and alive players ≤ INT(N ÷ 2) |
| **Location** | GM decides randomly among the realm's 3 preset Hatch points (roll 1d6: 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6) |
| **Discovery** | Auto-discovered on entering the zone (clear wind sound); adjacent zone may use Search (SRC) check vs DC 12 to judge direction |
| **Key** | Holding a Key can open a closed Hatch: 1 AP, auto success |
| **Jump in** | 1 AP, auto escape success |
| **Killer closes** | 1 AP. After closing, all Generators count as complete, Exit Gates power on, **Endgame Collapse triggers immediately** |

### 11-3 Growth Formula Overview

```
Attribute Point (ATP)      = 15 + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 2)      ← sum of five attribute final values
Skill Point (SKP)          = 2 + Prestige (PRS)
Killer Attribute Point (KAP) = 8 + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 2)     ← sum of five attribute levels
Killer Skill Point (KSP)   = 2 + INT(Prestige (PRS) × 0.6)
Perk slots                 = 1 + (PRS≥5) + (PRS≥10) + (PRS≥15)
Hope Point cap (HOP)        = 3 + INT(Prestige (PRS) ÷ 4) + Memory Cards held
Bloodpoint cap (BP_max)     = 20000 + 2000 × (PRS − 1) + 200 × (PRS − 1)²
BP to reach level N         = 2000 × N   (level 1→20 totals 418,000 BP)
Per-category in-match BP cap = 2000 + 400 × Prestige (PRS); in-match total cap = four times this
```

In full: **Attribute Point (ATP) equals 15 plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 2 rounded down; Skill Point (SKP) equals 2 plus Prestige (PRS); Killer Attribute Point (KAP) equals 8 plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 2 rounded down; Killer Skill Point (KSP) equals 2 plus Prestige (PRS) times 0.6 rounded down; Perk slots are 1 plus 1 for each of three Prestige (PRS) thresholds that is met; Hope Point (HOP) cap equals 3 plus Prestige (PRS) divided by 4 rounded down, plus Memory Cards held; Bloodpoint (BP) cap is a quadratic in Prestige (PRS).**

### 11-4 Skill Point (SKP) Upgrade Cost

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **This step's Skill Point (SKP) cost** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| **Cumulative cost from 0** | 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 | **9** |

The 22 Skill Points (SKP) at Prestige (PRS) 20 exactly equal the total cost of the "5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1" five-tier ladder (9 + 6 + 4 + 2 + 1 = 22).

### 12-3 Echo Companion Three-Line Behavior Table

> **Usage: read top to bottom each round; execute the first row whose condition holds. No rolls, no judgment, no roleplay.**
