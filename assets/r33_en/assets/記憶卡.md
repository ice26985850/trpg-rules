# Memory Card Catalogue

> **System Role**: The Memory Card is a cross-scenario resource spanning the entire campaign. Players can obtain it from S3 onward, for: ① spending 1 Hope Point (HOP) to "ask the Memory Card for help" — reroll one check related to that memory's theme (unlocked at S3); ② serving as "ammunition" in the S8 Conflagration ending — each Memory Card = 3 fire intensity; ③ acting as the narrative voucher to trigger the campaign abilities "Spot the Gap," "Call the Name," and "Follow the Ember."
> **Distribution Principle**: A single campaign usually yields 8–13 cards (by player-count table and choices); this catalogue enumerates all 20 possible types. The GM distributes them based on the situations the players actually trigger in each scenario — not every card is obtained in the same playthrough.
> **Back-Side Usage**: The back of each card is left blank for the player to write by hand "what this sentence means to me (the character)" — a name, a scene, a line only they understand. The GM does not predefine the back content; the "Back-Side Blank Note" hints below only suggest fill-in directions.

---

## MEM-01 · The Tree Does Not Eat Earth

- **Front Text**: "Roots don't eat soil. They eat something else."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: At the opening of S3, when Sally Nkosi speaks this line, and the player chooses "produce Altruistic Hope (lockpick)" in Choice One and successfully sneaks into the Fogcellar.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "the first moment I stopped for someone else"; or note the name of the person you saved in S3.

## MEM-02 · I Had Already Gotten Out

- **Front Text**: "I had already gotten out... then I heard someone calling from behind. I didn't know him. When I turned back, I wasn't thinking anything."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In the S3 Fogcellar, when reading the Nameless's carved slab, and picking the memory pod that belongs to you (picked correctly).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "if I hadn't turned back then"; the S7 Ember Niche and S8 Nameless Heart retrieve this card.

## MEM-03 · If You Can't Tell, Don't Pick

- **Front Text**: "Can you tell? If you can't, don't pick. If you pick the wrong one, that person will see you."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In the S3 Fogcellar, spend 2 Hope Points (HOP) with the Whisperer's "female voice beneath the roots" to trade for the intel "whose pod you picked."
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: If you once picked the wrong pod (picked another's / a Killer's memory), note here "whose I picked wrong" — this is the anchor for the S6 foreshadowing.

## MEM-04 · Whose Did You Pick?

- **Front Text**: "You see it very clearly — that face, one you don't recognize at all. But it knows you."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S3, the GM arranges "at least one player picks the wrong pod" (picks a certain Killer's memory). If it happens, issue this card as the wrong-pick voucher.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "who that face might be"; if Sally also picked wrong in S6, she speaks the reforging Q&A here, and this card is the fuse.

## MEM-05 · The Fortieth Name

- **Front Text**: "Forty names. The first thirty-nine are crossed out. The fortieth — the seventeenth — is nothing at all."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: At the opening of S4, issued when Gilmar Sato unfolds the medical-records-room roster.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "that empty slot — why do I recognize it"; both the S4 pivot reveal and the S7 Mirror-Bearer path use this as a lead.

## MEM-06 · She Was Never From Here

- **Front Text**: "A number is just a number. A person is not. The one you asked about — she was never from here."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S4, spend 1 Hope Point (HOP) with the Whisperer's "medical record number" to ask "where is Number 17."
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Note Number Seventeen's true identity (a visiting family member, wrongly admitted and held for three years); retrieved after reading the record in S4.

## MEM-07 · They Roll Toward the Campfire

- **Front Text**: "They didn't stay where they were. They rolled in one direction. All in the same direction."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: Triggered at the S4 Ember Moment (completed 3+ Afterimage unfinished-business + read Number Seventeen's record).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "that direction is... the Campfire"; this is the campaign-wide pivot reveal, retrieved when the S7 Umbilical is named.

## MEM-08 · The Writer

- **Front Text**: "He wrote on the wall with his fingernails, until his fingers bled. When he finished, he laughed."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S4, after completing the Afterimage "the Writer" (using the nails from S2) unfinished-business, given before the Afterimage dissipates.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Note the line you carved with sheet metal and nails in S2; this card proves the "message" mechanic runs through the whole campaign.

## MEM-09 · The Return Costs More Than the Going

- **Front Text**: "The return costs more than the going."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: At the opening of S5, when Eloise speaks this line and the player decides to actively descend the Sunken Stair (actively exploit the gap).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "why I went back to find him"; can note the motive for saving Gilmar or any sacrificed Echo in S5.

## MEM-10 · It's Not That It Can't Be Deceived

- **Front Text**: "It's not that it can't be deceived. It's that it won't notice it's been deceived."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S5, issued when entering the Hidden Workshop and obtaining Fane's notes.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: The feasibility of the S6 reforging failure and the S8 Takeover ending both hinge on this; write "who is it we mean to deceive."

## MEM-11 · Down There Is Something You Want

- **Front Text**: "The going: a sliver of hope. The return: you must find, down there, something you want."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S5, in the Void, triggered when you do something unrewarded for Gilmar (feed him, hold him, say his name) and the rescue fires.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "the thing I found down there"; in S8 Gilmar will remember your name, and this card is the voucher.

## MEM-12 · Does Hatred Count as Hope?

- **Front Text**: "Does hatred count as a kind of hope?"
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: At the opening of S6, issued when Eloise relays Hilda Lane's words from the previous night.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Note the altruistic acts you performed for Hilda (affects The Silent One's (SIL) "hesitation" probability); retrieved at the S6 core choice.

## MEM-13 · Two Production Lines

- **Front Text**: "Outside harvest: grab from the Seep. Internal production: grab from the Campfire. Raw material: those who've lost hope but still have hatred. Processing site: that white place. Room 13."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S6, issued when obtaining the Fane note's "internal production" line paragraph.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "who was the one who walked out from the Campfire"; points to the truth of Hilda being reforged into The Silent One (SIL).

## MEM-14 · It Asked Again

- **Front Text**: "It asked the same question again. They are confirming. They are checking."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S6, triggered at the Ember Moment (core choice "call the name" or "give the memory").
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Proves The Entity is a clumsy system — it can detect anomalies but cannot locate their cause; prerequisite knowledge for the S8 Takeover ending.

## MEM-15 · Make It Spit Up More

- **Front Text**: "Then what if we make it spit up more?"
- **Front Text (cont.)**: Eloise Finch, looking at the conclusion pieced together from five pages in Fane's hand, says this.
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: At the opening of S7, when players study the Fane note wall and piece together "part of what It swallowed it spat back out, rolling toward the Campfire."
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: This is the starting point of the Conflagration plan; write "how much we mean to make it spit up."

## MEM-16 · The Same Kind of Thing

- **Front Text**: "They and It are the same kind of thing. And between the same kind of thing — fire spreads."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S7, issued when obtaining the First Ember and triggering the Ember Moment.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Note the Umbilical and the Ember Vein; the core voucher for the S8 fire-carrying path and the Conflagration ending.

## MEM-17 · The Fire Needs Someone to Feed It

- **Front Text**: "That's not a campfire. That's a fire someone kept alive for another, after that other had gone."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S7, issued when exploring the Hearth of the Mother's Dwelling (rf_hearth).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: The core of the S8 Ember ending — "the fire needs someone to feed it"; retrieved when an old character becomes an NPC guide.

## MEM-18 · If You've Read This Far

- **Front Text**: "If you've read this far — then you probably turned back too. Take it."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S7, issued when exploring the optional node the Nameless Grave (rf_opt_grave) (echoes the S8 Nameless carved slab).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Write "why I turned back"; retrieved when the S8 Nameless Heart appears.

## MEM-19 · That Hole Is Empty

- **Front Text**: "That hole is empty. That's why It has been eating all along."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S8 Act Three, arriving at the Nameless Heart, issued at The Entity's core reveal.
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Proves The Entity was empty all along; all three S8 endings (Conflagration / Takeover / Ember) rest on this.

## MEM-20 · Take It

- **Front Text**: "If you've read this far — then you probably turned back too. Take it."
- **Suggested Distribution Timing**: In S8, at the ending — issued when the Stone Circle (fb_ring) delivers its address, or when any of the three endings is achieved (campaign-level closing card).
- **Back-Side Blank Note**: Note "the First Ember wasn't dropped by him, it was dropped by them — including you"; one of the three endings decides the direction of the continuing campaign.
