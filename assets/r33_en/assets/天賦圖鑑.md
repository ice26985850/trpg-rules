# Perk Catalogue

> **Purpose**: A table-side reference for both Players and the Game Master (GM). The book holds **66 entries** total — 36 Survivor Perks and 30 Killer Perks.
> **Numeric Iron Rule**: All effects use integer add/subtract or fixed counts; no percentages.
> **Time & Distance**: The only time unit in the system is the "Round (Round)", roughly 10–15 seconds; 1 cell = a 2-meter square.
> **Balance Iron Rule**: No Perk may make Setup 1 (Solo Trial, 1 Player + 1 GM) a guaranteed win or guaranteed loss. Any effect that depends on teammate count must include a line "Echo Companion Counting Ruling".

> The three Killer trees each have 10 entries (30 total), above the 24-entry floor; the three trees are balanced in count, so no tree is short on options when picking.

### 1-2 Perk Slots and Tier Thresholds

Every character (Survivor and Killer alike) has 1–4 Perk slots, unlocked by Prestige (PRS). Perks are configured before a Trial begins, cannot be changed mid-Trial, and the same Perk cannot be equipped twice.

| Prestige (PRS) | Available Perk Slots |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 1 |
| 5–9 | 2 |
| 10–14 | 3 |
| 15–20 | 4 |

Each Perk has three tiers: Tier I, Tier II, Tier III. Buying a higher tier automatically replaces the lower tier. **The tier threshold is a hard limit** — a Prestige (PRS) 6 character cannot equip Tier II even if they can afford it.

| Tier | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost | Minimum Prestige (PRS) to Equip |
|---|---|---|
| Tier I | 2,500 | 1 |
| Tier II | 3,250 | 7 |
| Tier III | 4,000 | 14 |

Maxing one Perk across three tiers costs **9,750 Bloodpoints (BP)**. Unlocked tiers are non-refundable; Perk loadouts can be freely changed before each Trial at no cost.

### 1-3 Trigger Condition Tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Passive** | Always on, no declaration, no Action Point (AP) cost. |
| **Once per round** | Auto or optional on condition, at most once per round. |
| **Once per match** | Spent once, then inert until Trial end. |
| **Costs Action Point (AP)** | Actively declared and pays Action Point (AP). |
| **[Exhaustion type]** | Cannot activate while you have Exhausted (ST-05); applying it imposes Exhausted. |
| **[Hex type]** | Bound to a Totem on the field; if the Totem is purified the Perk is permanently nullified. Hex-type effects are stronger than same-tier non-Hex Perks. |

### 1-4 Prestige Resonance

Perks are "Universal" or "Exclusive". Exclusive Perks are initially usable only by that Archetype or Avatar; when you reach Prestige (PRS) 5 / 10 / 15 with a character of that Archetype / Avatar, that Archetype's / Avatar's Exclusive Perks unlock to Tier I / II / III for all your other same-faction characters, after which they count as Universal.

| Prestige (PRS) reached by that character | Tier unlocked to other characters |
|---|---|
| 5 | Tier I |
| 10 | Tier II |
| 15 | Tier III |

### 1-5 Distance Band (BAND)

| Distance Band | Distance |
|---|---|
| Contact (CON) | 0–2 meters |
| Near (NEA) | 2–8 meters |
| Mid (MID) | 8–16 meters |
| Far (FAR) | 16–32 meters |
| Escaped (ESC) | beyond 32 meters |

### 1-6 Setup 1 (Solo Trial) General Rules

Setup 1 is 1 Player + 1 GM, with **2 Echo Companions** on the field — weakened NPC Survivors operated by the GM.

- Where an entry says "other Survivor" or "teammate", in the Solo Trial it means an Echo Companion.
- Echo Companions have a flat +2 to their skill-check totals, and their Progress Point (PP) contribution is halved (rounded down).
- For Perks that depend on teammate count, the "Echo Companion Counting Ruling" line is the formal rule under the Solo Trial; **no Perk writes "invalid solo" without giving an alternative.**
- If all Echo Companions are down, the separately written "when no Echo Companion" clause takes effect.

### 1-7 Common Check Baselines (referenced by Perk entries)

| Action | Action Point (AP) | Baseline DC | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repair Generator | 2 (once per round) | 10 | 2d10 + Technique (TEC) bonus + Mechanical Repair (MEC) Skill Level |
| Heal another | 3 | 10 | 2d10 + Focus (FOC) bonus + First Aid (MED) Skill Level |
| Self-heal | 4 | 14 | same as above (requires Med-Kit or Perk SP-07) |
| Rescue hooked | 3 | 9 | 2d10 + Focus (FOC) bonus |
| Vault window / Drop Pallet | 1 | 9 | 2d10 + Agility (AGI) bonus + Parkour (PRK) Skill Level |
| Open Chest | 3 | 9 | 2d10 + Technique (TEC) bonus + Search (SRC) Skill Level |
| Disarm trap | 2 | 12 | 2d10 + Technique (TEC) bonus |
| Hook self-rescue | 0 (auto each round) | 20 − (4 − N) | 2d10 + Agility (AGI) bonus + Escape (ESQ) Skill Level |
| Wiggle | 0 (auto each round) | 12 + rounds already on hook | same as above |
| Chase contest check | 0 (auto each round) | contest | both sides roll |

**Four-Outcome Judgment**: Margin of Success (MoS) = Check Total − DC. Critical Success is MoS ≥ +5 or natural double-ten; Success is 0 to +4; Failure is −1 to −4; Critical Failure is ≤ −5 or natural double-one.

## III. Survival Tree (SP-01 ~ SP-09)

> This tree answers "how do I stay alive, how do I run, how do I resupply." The first pillar of the Solo Trial.

### SP-01 | Windows of Opportunity

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: You automatically know the position and state (intact / broken / sealed by The Entity) of all Pallets, Windows, and breakable walls within a set range around you. The GM tells you this at the start of each round.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — automatic at round start; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — the GM directly tells you all targets and states in range.
- **Tier Values**: Tier I 1 Distance Band / Tier II 2 Distance Bands / Tier III 3 Distance Bands.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: This Perk's effect does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions are neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. With no teammate scouting for you solo, this is the steadiest entry-level solution for the Solo Trial's "B | Break Chase".

### SP-02 | Hope

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 5 | Once per match (activates when Exit Gates power) | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When the Exit Gates power (Generators done), this Perk activates — you gain Haste (ST-09), raising your per-round Distance Band cap +1.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — the instant Exit Gates power; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — immediately gain Haste, duration by tier.
- **Tier Values**: Duration: Tier I 6 rounds / Tier II 9 rounds / Tier III until Trial ends.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. In a solo Endgame Collapse no teammate draws aggro; Tier III of this Perk is nearly a survival floor.

### SP-03 | Sprint Burst　[Exhaustion]

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 1 | [Exhaustion] Once per round | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When you start moving from a standstill you may declare it — this move's Distance Band cap +2, and the move does not trigger the chase contact check. After activating you gain Exhausted (ST-05).
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — you have not moved this round and have no Exhausted; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP), imposes Exhausted after; Check — none needed; Result — this move's Distance Band cap +2, no contact check.
- **Tier Values**: Exhausted duration: Tier I 8 rounds / Tier II 6 rounds / Tier III 5 rounds.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. This is the standard solution for the Solo Trial's "B | Break Chase", the highest-coverage Survival Perk.

### SP-04 | Adrenaline　[Exhaustion]

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 10 | [Exhaustion] Once per match | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: The instant the Exit Gates power, you immediately recover one Health State (HS) (Dying → Injured, or Injured → Healthy) and gain one extra move at once. If you are being carried or hooked at the time, the effect delays until you leave that state. This Perk **ignores** any existing Exhausted (ST-05) and forces activation, then imposes Exhausted.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — the instant Exit Gates power (or when you leave carried / hooked state); Cost — 0 Action Point (AP), imposes Exhausted after; Check — none needed; Result — recover one Health State (HS) + one extra move.
- **Tier Values**: Extra move: Tier I 1 Distance Band / Tier II 2 Distance Bands / Tier III 2 Distance Bands and immune to this Exhausted.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. Solo, no teammate heals you; this free Health State (HS) recovery is often the difference between escape and Sacrifice.

### SP-05 | Balanced Landing　[Exhaustion]

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 1 | [Exhaustion] Once per round | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When you fall from a height you take no recovery stun and make no sound, and immediately gain one extra move. After activating you gain Exhausted (ST-05).
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — you fall from a height with no Exhausted; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP), imposes Exhausted after; Check — none needed (waives the normal landing check); Result — no stun, no sound, and extra move.
- **Tier Values**: Extra move: Tier I 1 Distance Band / Tier II 1 Distance Band / Tier III 2 Distance Bands; Exhausted duration: Tier I 8 rounds / Tier II 6 rounds / Tier III 5 rounds.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. Available terrain depends on the Realm; when picking the Realm Shard (see Item Catalogue IT-55) confirm the field has elevation.

### SP-06 | Dead Hard　[Exhaustion]

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 5 | [Exhaustion] Once per match | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When you are in a Chase and about to take a hit that would worsen your Health State (HS), you may declare it — that damage instead applies **Endurance (ST-16)**, your Health State (HS) unchanged. After activating you gain Exhausted (ST-05).
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — in Chase, the Killer's attack check has confirmed a hit on you, and you have no Exhausted; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP), imposes Exhausted after; Check — none needed, auto; Result — this hit does not worsen Health State (HS), you gain Endurance and follow-up compensation by tier.
- **Tier Values**: After activating you additionally gain: Tier I none / Tier II Haste (ST-09) 1 round / Tier III Haste 2 rounds.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. **Balance Note**: This Perk is once per match and eats Exhausted, so it won't make solo a guaranteed win — it buys "one mistake's forgiveness", not damage immunity.

### SP-07 | Self-Care

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 1 | Costs Action Point (AP) | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: You may self-heal **without a Med-Kit**. Additionally, your Med-Kit Charge (CHG) use is reduced.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — you are in Injured or Dying Health State (HS) and not in Chase; Cost — 4 Action Points (AP); Check — 2d10 + Focus (FOC) bonus + First Aid (MED) Skill Level vs this Perk's DC; Result — CritS +6 Progress Points (PP); Success +4; Failure −1 and noise cue; CritF −3, noise cue, remaining Action Points (AP) this round lost. Accumulate 12 Progress Points (PP) to recover one Health State (HS).
- **Tier Values**: Self-heal DC: Tier I 18 / Tier II 16 / Tier III 14; Med-Kit Charge (CHG) use: Tier I −0 / Tier II −0 / Tier III −1 (minimum 1).
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. **This is one of the core pillars of the Solo Trial** — Setup 1 has no teammate healing; the Solo Trial's "A | Self-Recovery" otherwise relies on SP-30 Unbreakable.

### SP-08 | Plunderer's Instinct

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: Your rarity roll when searching Chests is raised, and you automatically know the position of all Chests and ground items in a set range. If you also have Pathfinder (SCT) Tier II "Scavenging Expert", the two **take only the higher**, no stacking. Same "take the highest" with the Offering "Chalk Dust Pouch" (see Item Catalogue IT-48).
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — when you search a Chest, and at round-start position reveal; Cost — chest search 3 Action Points (AP), reveal 0; Check — chest search is 2d10 + Technique (TEC) bonus + Search (SRC) Skill Level vs DC 9, reveal needs no check; Result — CritS obtain item and rarity +1 tier; Success obtain item; Failure no item; CritF no item and noise cue. Rarity boost by tier.
- **Tier Values**: Rarity boost: Tier I +1 tier / Tier II +1 tier / Tier III +2 tiers (cap Ultra Rare (UR)); detection range: Tier I 3 Distance Bands / Tier II 4 Distance Bands / Tier III whole field.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: Does not depend on teammate count; Echo Companions neither counted nor affect it.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. Solo, no teammate shares items; Chest output is all your supply, so this Perk's real value under Setup 1 is higher than under Setups 2–4.

### SP-09 | Left Behind

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 10 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When you become the last living Survivor on the field, you automatically know the Hatch's Distance Band (BAND); if the Hatch has not yet spawned, you instead learn the Distance Band (BAND) of the highest-progress Generator.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — all other Survivors have escaped, died, or been Sacrificed; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — the GM keeps telling you the Hatch's Distance Band (BAND), precision by tier.
- **Tier Values**: Tier I only Distance Band (BAND) / Tier II Distance Band (BAND) and bearing / Tier III Distance Band (BAND) and bearing, and your Distance Band (BAND) cap +1 when moving toward the Hatch.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Echo Companions count toward the living count** — this Perk activates only after both Echo Companions are down. Deliberate: if Echo Companions didn't count, the solo player would get full effect from the start, equal to a guaranteed win.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged, but activation condition as above. **Balance Note**: This Perk is the solo player's "can't-lose fallback", not a "win-from-the-start" opener — it only lights up after you've lost two companions.

## IV. Cooperation Tree (SP-10 ~ SP-18)

> This tree answers "how do I help others, how do others help me." **All nine entries involve teammates, so each states the Echo Companion Counting Ruling and solo alternative rule.**

### SP-10 | Kindred

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When any Survivor is hooked, you learn the Distance Band (BAND) of all other Survivors. If the hooked one is **you**, this info is also given to all other Survivors. Additionally, if the Killer stays within 1 Distance Band of the hooked one, the GM must actively reveal it.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — any Survivor on the field is hooked; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — the GM directly tells the Distance Band (BAND) of all Survivors in range, and whether the Killer is guarding the hook.
- **Tier Values**: Reveal range: Tier I 2 Distance Bands / Tier II 3 Distance Bands / Tier III whole field.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Counted**. Kindred triggers normally when an Echo Companion is hooked, and the Echo Companion's position is revealed to you normally.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. Solo, this Perk's real value is the line "is the Killer guarding the hook" — your only reliable intel for deciding whether to rescue an Echo Companion. If both Echo Companions are down, this Perk changes to: **when you are hooked, learn the Killer's Distance Band (BAND)**, range by tier.

### SP-11 | Prove Thyself

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: When another Survivor is in the same Distance Band co-repairing the same Generator, you and all Survivors in range gain a bonus to the repair check, up to 3 allies counted.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — you and at least 1 other Survivor co-repair the same Generator in-band; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — bonus folded into the original repair check; Result — bonus by ally count, rest per standard repair (CritS +10 Progress Points (PP) / Success +6 / Failure −2 and noise / CritF −5, noise, remaining Action Points (AP) lost).
- **Tier Values**: Per ally: Tier I +1 / Tier II +2 / Tier III +3 (cap 3 allies).
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Counted, but bonus halved (rounded down)**. One Echo Companion gives Tier I +0 / Tier II +1 / Tier III +1.
- **【Solo Trial】**: **If no Echo Companion is available to co-repair**, this Perk changes to — when you repair a Generator **alone**, your repair check gains a flat Tier I +1 / Tier II +2 / Tier III +3 bonus. This alternative guarantees the Perk never idles in solo.

### SP-12 | Bond

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: You automatically know the Distance Band (BAND) and current Health State (HS) (Healthy / Injured / Dying) of all other Survivors in a set range.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — automatic at round start; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — the GM directly tells each other Survivor's Distance Band (BAND) and Health State (HS).
- **Tier Values**: Range: Tier I 2 Distance Bands / Tier II 3 Distance Bands / Tier III 4 Distance Bands.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Counted**. Echo Companions' Distance Band (BAND) and Health State (HS) reveal normally.
- **【Solo Trial】**: **If no other Survivor is on the field** (both Echo Companions down), this Perk changes to — you learn the Distance Band (BAND) of the **highest-progress Generator** on the field, range by tier unlimited.

### SP-13 | Shoulder the Burden

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 10 | Once per match · Costs Action Point (AP) | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: Activate on a hooked Survivor — you take one of their hook stages onto yourself (their hook count −1, yours +1), and free them. You then gain Exposed (ST-06). **You cannot activate while at your last hook stage.**
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — you and a hooked Survivor are in Contact (CON) band, and you are not at your last hook stage; Cost — 2 Action Points (AP), once per match; Check — none needed, auto success; Result — target off hook, hook count −1, your hook count +1, you gain Exposed.
- **Tier Values**: Exposed duration: Tier I 6 rounds / Tier II 4 rounds / Tier III 2 rounds.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Counted**. Can activate on an Echo Companion, identical effect.
- **【Solo Trial】**: **If no other Survivor is on the field**, this Perk changes to — once per match, when you are about to be hooked, your hook count does not increase (The Entity grabs a wrong Afterimage). After activating you gain Exposed, duration by tier (6 / 4 / 2 rounds).

### SP-14 | Botany Knowledge

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: All your healing checks (self or other) gain a bonus, and your Med-Kit healing effect is raised one tier (e.g., a Camp Med-Kit's heal-other bonus of +1 counts as +2).
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — whenever you make any healing check; Cost — heal other 3 Action Points (AP) / self-heal 4 Action Points (AP); Check — bonus folded into the original healing check; Result — per standard healing (CritS +6 Progress Points (PP) / Success +4 / Failure −1 and noise / CritF −3, noise, remaining Action Points (AP) lost).
- **Tier Values**: Healing check bonus: Tier I +1 / Tier II +2 / Tier III +3.
- **Echo Companion Counting Ruling**: **Counted**. Bonus applies normally, undiminished, when healing an Echo Companion.
- **【Solo Trial】**: Effect unchanged. The bonus also applies to **self-heal**, so even with both Echo Companions down this Perk stays fully active, never idling.

### SP-15 | Empathy

| Tree | Unlock Prestige (PRS) | Trigger | Bloodpoint (BP) Cost (I / II / III) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | 1 | Passive | 2,500 / 3,250 / 4,000 |

- **Effect**: All other Survivors in Injured or Dying Health State (HS) have their Distance Band (BAND) continuously revealed to you, **with no range limit**. Additionally, you learn whether they are being chased.
- **Trigger / Cost / Check / Result**: Trigger — another Survivor on the field is Injured or Dying; Cost — 0 Action Point (AP); Check — none needed; Result — the GM continuously tells their Distance Band (BAND), Chase status reveal by tier.

## XI. Perk Loadout Rules Quick Reference

### 11-1 Prestige (PRS) Reference

| Prestige (PRS) | Available Perk Slots | Max Equippable Tier | Add-on Cap | Offering Slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1 | Tier I | 0 | closed |
| 4 | 1 | Tier I | 1 | closed |
| 5–6 | 2 | Tier I | 1 | closed |
| 7 | 2 | Tier II | 1 | closed |
| 8–9 | 2 | Tier II | 1 | open (1 / match) |
| 10–13 | 3 | Tier II | 2 | open (1 / match) |
| 14 | 3 | Tier III | 2 | open (1 / match) |
| 15–20 | 4 | Tier III | 2 | open (1 / match) |

Below is a formula block; variables use English abbreviations. Chinese note: available Perk slots = 1 plus whether Prestige reaches 5, 10, 15; max equippable tier is Tier III at Prestige 14+, Tier II at 7+, else Tier I.

```
Available Perk Slots   = 1 + (PRS>=5) + (PRS>=10) + (PRS>=15)
Max Equippable Tier     = IF(PRS>=14, 3, IF(PRS>=7, 2, 1))
```

The variable PRS above is Prestige (PRS).

### 11-2 Rules for Changing Perks

| Situation | Rule | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Change Perk loadout **before** Trial | Free, unlimited | 0 Bloodpoints (BP) |
| Change Perk **during** Trial | **Not allowed** | — |
| Buy a Perk's Tier I | Needs Prestige (PRS) ≥ 1 | 2,500 Bloodpoints (BP) |
| Upgrade to Tier II | Needs Prestige (PRS) ≥ 7 to equip (may buy early) | 3,250 Bloodpoints (BP) |
| Upgrade to Tier III | Needs Prestige (PRS) ≥ 14 to equip (may buy early) | 4,000 Bloodpoints (BP) |
| Refund unlocked tier | **Not refundable** | — |
| Reset a character's Survival specialization | Resettable | 5,000 Bloodpoints (BP) |

**Bloodpoints (BP) are shared**: Bloodpoints (BP) are not bound to a character. Bloodpoints (BP) earned by Survivor A can unlock Survivor B's or a Killer Avatar's Perks.

### 11-3 Perk General Rules (Eight)

1. **No stacking**: Same-Perk effects don't stack. In multiplayer, same Perk takes only the highest tier.
2. **Exhaustion lock**: Tagged [Exhaustion], you cannot activate while Exhausted (ST-05); during Exhausted, any Action Point (AP)-spending action does not advance the Exhausted countdown.
3. **Cooldown by round**: All cooldowns use "round", not seconds.
4. **One-time Perks**: Tagged "once per match" are spent until Trial end.
5. **Hex type**: Bound to one Totem on the field. Purify Totem is 2 Action Points (AP), 2d10 + Technique (TEC) bonus + Search (SRC) Skill Level vs DC 12. Totem purified → Perk permanently nullified.
6. **DC stack cap**: Killer-Perk Technique-check DC bonuses cap at **+6** total on the same check.
7. **Echo Companion Counting Ruling is a formal rule**: Each Perk's "Echo Companion Counting Ruling" line has rule force, not a suggestion.
8. **Solo need-not-win, need-not-die**: Where an entry has a 【Solo Trial】 alternative rule, that rule replaces the original under Setup 1, taking priority.
