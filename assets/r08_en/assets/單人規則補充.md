# Solo Rules Supplement

> **Applicable Scenario**: 1 GM + 1 Player (Duet Play)
> **Depends on Rules**: This document is a supplement to the *Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart* rulebook. All unmodified rules follow the main rulebook (`rulebook.md`) as written.
> **Citation Format**: `→ §Section` indicates a reference to the corresponding section of the main rulebook.

---

## I. Core Changes Overview

| Main Rules | Solo Adjustment |
|-----------|----------|
| Combined Strike rules (→ §6.2) | **Disabled**. Replaced by "Combo System" (see §1.2) |
| United Casting (→ §6.3) | **Disabled**. Replaced by "Charge Casting" (see §1.3) |
| Formation Tactics (→ §6.4) | **Disabled**. Replaced by NPC Companion Tactics (see §2.3) |
| Assist Action (→ §6.1) | **Changed to NPC Companion performing the action** (see §2.1) |
| Encounter Scale | Enemy count −50%; single Boss retained but with adjusted stats (see §3) |

---

## I.1 Turn Structure (unchanged → §3.1)

Unchanged. The player still has **3 Action Points** per turn (one each of Major / Move / Minor).

---

## I.2 Combo System (replaces Combined Strike → §6.2)

Solo play cannot perform combined strikes; instead use "Combo" — under specific conditions the player may launch multiple consecutive attacks:

| Combo Trigger Condition | Effect |
|-------------|------|
| Took no Damage at all in the previous turn | First attack this turn deals +1d6 Damage |
| Enemy is in an abnormal Status Effect (Frozen / Paralyzed / Burning / Immobilized) | First attack against that enemy deals additional Damage equal to 1 Minor action (Martial Techniques only, does not consume the Minor action) |
| Dealt Damage to the same target for 3 consecutive turns | The 4th turn's Damage against that target automatically crits (Damage ×1.5) |

---

## I.3 Charge Casting (replaces United Casting → §6.3)

The player may spend 1 turn "charging": take no attack this turn; next turn's Divine Ability or earth-grade-and-above Martial Technique Damage ×1.5. If hit during charging, the charge is interrupted (Damage bonus canceled but no backlash).

---

## II. NPC Companion System

In solo play, the player may bring **1 NPC Companion** along. The companion has a simplified stat card, and the player issues commands during Combat.

> **Reference**: The companion's detailed character card is in `character_cards_excel/NPC_companion_card.xlsx`.
> **Design Principle**: The companion supplements abilities the player lacks (e.g., if the player is a Sword Cultivator, the companion may be a Pill Cultivator providing healing).

### II.1 Companion Action Rules

Each turn the companion has **2 Action Points** (1 Major + 1 Move), commanded by the player. The companion cannot use Divine Abilities (simplified NPCs have no Divine Ability slots).

| Companion's Available Major Actions | Description |
|---------------------|------|
| Attack | Use the companion's Martial Technique attack (Damage see companion card) |
| Assist | Grant the player +10 to hit or +5 to Defense this turn (→ §6.1 simplified version) |
| Heal | Use pills or healing-type Martial Techniques |
| Defense | This turn Defense Value (AC) +10, but cannot attack |

### II.2 Companion Growth

The companion does not gain cultivation independently. Each time the player breaks through a **major realm**, the companion automatically advances to the **Early** stage of the same realm.

| Player Breakthrough | Companion Advancement |
|----------|----------|
| Qi Refining → Foundation Establishment | Companion rises to Foundation Establishment Early; Health Points (HP) +20; Damage die +1d |
| Foundation Establishment → Golden Core | Companion rises to Golden Core Early; unlocks second Martial Technique slot |
| Golden Core → Nascent Soul | Companion rises to Nascent Soul Early; Health Points (HP) +40; may perform 3 actions / turn |

### II.3 Companion Tactics (replaces Formation → §6.4)

| Tactic | Condition | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| **Pincer Attack** | Player and companion in different zones (melee + mid-range) | Both sides' attack hit against enemies in the middle zone +10 |
| **Covering Retreat** | Companion in melee zone, player in mid/long-range zone | Player's Evasion +15 this turn; companion bears the next attack |
| **Focus Fire** | Both attack the same target | The second attack ignores 5 points of the target's Defense |

## III. Encounter Scale Adjustment

Monster/NPC data in the main rules (→ §3.9) is benchmarked for a 3-4 person party. Adjust by the following ratios in solo play:

### III.1 Enemy Count Adjustment

| Main-Rules Encounter | Solo Adjustment |
|-----------|----------|
| 1 Boss | Unchanged (Boss Health Points (HP) −20%, Damage −1d) |
| 2-3 mooks | Reduced to 1-2 |
| 4+ mooks | Halved (rounded down) |
| Large-scale battlefield | Handle as narrative; only roll key checks |

### III.2 Solo Encounter Design Principles

| Encounter Type | Suggestion |
|----------|------|
| Mook fight | 1-2, used to drain resources, generally not lethal |
| Elite fight | 1, Health Points (HP) = player's Health Points (HP) ×0.8~1.2, requires strategic response |
| Boss fight | 1, Health Points (HP) = player's Health Points (HP) ×1.2~1.8, multi-phase or multi-mechanic |
| Chase / Escape | Primarily Agility checks, Combat not forced |

## IV. Other Rule Changes

### IV.1 Rest (→ §I / main-rule addenda)

Unchanged. NPC Companion rests in sync with the player.

### IV.2 Social (→ §II / main-rule addenda)

Unchanged. NPC Companion may participate in Social but does not make independent checks (the companion's attitude modifier may be adjudicated by the GM).

### IV.3 Crafting and Gathering (→ §III / main-rule addenda)

Unchanged. May use companion assistance (pill-refining / artifact-refining check +5).

### IV.4 Master-Apprenticeship System (→ §XIV / main-rule addenda)

Unchanged. The NPC Companion does not take a master independently, but may gain indirect guidance through the player's master.

### IV.5 Reputation and Relationships (→ §IV / main-rule addenda)

Unchanged. The NPC Companion's reputation is not tracked independently.

### IV.6 Death and Revival

When the player dies, if the NPC Companion survives and holds a "Protective Jade Talisman" or similar item, the companion may spend that item to block the player's lethal blow (while the companion is present, the Protective Jade Talisman's effect is triggered by the player). The companion itself does not truly die — when Health Points (HP) reach zero it enters a "Gravely Wounded" state, requiring a long rest to recover.

| Level Range | Realm | Encounter Difficulty Baseline |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 1-3 | Qi Refining | Mooks 1-2 (HP 15-30) |
| 4-6 | Foundation Establishment | Mooks 1-2 (HP 30-50) + optional elite |
| 7-9 | Golden Core | Mooks 1-3 (HP 40-70) + elite ×1 |
| 10-12 | Nascent Soul | Elite ×1 (HP 80-150) + Boss fight |
| 13-18 | Spirit Transformation | Mostly Boss (HP 200-400), multi-phase |
| 19-24 | Void Refining | Boss with multiple mechanics (HP 400-700) |
| 25-30 | Fusion | Epic Boss (HP 700-1200) |
| 31-36 | Mahayana / Tribulation Crossing | Heavenly-Dao-level challenge + Inner-Demon Tribulation |
