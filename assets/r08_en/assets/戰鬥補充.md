## 5.6 Encounter Table Usage Guide



### Rolling Rules



```

Random Encounter Frequency: Roll d100 once per day of travel (or trigger when the GM deems appropriate)

Encounter Chance:
 - Safe Area (near a sect / main road): roll d100, triggers encounter on ≤30
 - Normal Area (wilderness / mountain path): roll d100, triggers encounter on ≤50
 - Dangerous Area (Demon Beast territory / ancient battlefield): roll d100, triggers encounter on ≤70
 - Extreme-Danger Area (Demon Domain / depths of a Secret Realm): roll d100, triggers encounter on ≤90

Encounter Difficulty Adjustment: The GM may adjust the number/strength of encounters based on the party's actual power. Easy encounters: may increase number by 50%. Extreme encounters: may reduce enemy count or lower Challenge Rating (CR) by 1–2 levels

```



### Encounter Type Descriptions

| Type | Description | GM Handling Suggestion |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Combat** | Directly enter Combat | Handle per standard Combat rules |
| **Social** | Involves NPC interaction | May develop into trade / commission / intel gathering / alliance / hostility |
| **Exploration** | Involves scene exploration | Use exploration skill checks (Divine Sense perception / Comprehension puzzle-solving / Agility traversal, etc.) |
| **Fortuitous Encounter** | Special event | Usually requires a check (mainly Fortune); result may be reward / Combat / mission |
| **Disaster** | Environmental danger | Requires team coordination to respond; usually no "enemy" but has environmental checks |



### Cross Encounters



GM may mix encounter tables from different regions or trigger multiple encounters simultaneously:

- Example 1: Central Province encounter "Heaven-Earth Treasure Emerges" + Eastern Seas encounter "cultivators from all sides rush over" = a scramble battle

- Example 2: Southern Wilderness encounter "barbarian tribe" + Western Regions encounter "Bloodfiend Sect" = Bloodfiend Sect is bewitching the barbarians

- Example 3: Combat encounter + Disaster encounter = fighting in a blizzard / sandstorm (environmental penalties stack)
