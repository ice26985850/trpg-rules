# Chapter One: Core Rules

## 1.1 d100 Percentage Check

### Core Formula

```
Check success condition: d100 ≤ Target Number (TN)
Target Number (TN) = Attribute Value + Proficiency Bonus + Technique Bonus + Circumstance Modifier − Difficulty Modifier (DC)
```

### Margin of Success Levels (MoS = Target Number (TN) − d100 result)

| Level | Margin of Success (MoS) Range | Effect Description |
|------|---------|---------|
| Minor Success | 1-20 | Barely succeeds; effect is discounted |
| Major Success | 21-40 | Standard success; stable performance |
| Perfect Success | 41-60 | Excellent success; extra benefit |
| Transcendent | 61-80 | Stunning performance; significant bonus |
| Heavenly | 81+ | Skill approaching the Dao; legendary effect |

### Critical Success · Serendipity

The range expands as the realm grows:

```
Critical Success Range = 3 + floor((Level−1) / 3)
```

| Realm | Critical Success Range | Probability |
|------|-----------|------|
| Qi Refining Early (Lv.1) | 01-03 | 3% |
| Foundation Establishment Early (Lv.5) | 01-04 | 4% |
| Golden Core Early (Lv.9) | 01-05 | 5% |
| Nascent Soul Early (Lv.13) | 01-07 | 7% |
| Spirit Transformation Early (Lv.17) | 01-08 | 8% |
| Void Refining Early (Lv.21) | 01-09 | 9% |
| Fusion Early (Lv.25) | 01-11 | 11% |
| Mahayana Early (Lv.29) | 01-12 | 12% |
| Tribulation Crossing Early (Lv.33) | 01-13 | 13% |
| Tribulation Crossing Perfected (Lv.36) | 01-14 | **14%** |

### Critical Failure · Qigong Deviation

- **Range**: Fixed 96-00 (5%); consequences worsen with realm
- **Triggers**: Critical failure on a check / technique backlash / overdose of pills / Inner Demon attack

### Opposed Check

| Situation | Result Determination |
|------|---------|
| Both succeed | Compare Margin of Success (MoS); higher wins |
| One succeeds, one fails | The one who succeeds wins |
| Both fail | Compare Margin of Failure (MoF); lower (less severe failure) wins |
| Both critical success | Compare Margin of Success (MoS); tie → Serendipity Collision (both gain buffs) |
| Both critical failure | Catastrophic result |

## 1.6 Qigong Deviation

### Trigger Conditions
1. Any check rolls 96-00
2. Forcibly cultivating a technique above one's stage
3. Accumulated toxin from pill overdose
4. Inner Demon attack

### Resistance and Consequences

Resistance Check: `d100 ≤ Dao Heart − Inner Demon Value`

| Level | Name | Dao Heart Check | Consequence |
|------|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Qi Reversal | +0 | Qi Pool −20%, lasts 1 round |
| 2 | Meridian Damage | −5 | All attributes −5, lasts 1 day |
| 3 | Blood and Qi Surge | −10 | Health Points (HP) −30%, random attribute −1d5 (permanent) |
| 4 | Dao Foundation Shaken | −15 | Realm regresses one minor stage, Dao Heart −1d10 |
| 5 | Primordial Spirit Wounded | −20 | Divine Sense/Spirit Power halved, lasts 1 month |
| 6 | Inner Demon Entanglement | −25 | Inner Demon Value +20, critical failure expands to 94-00 |
| 7 | Death and Dao Dissolution | −30 | Character death (GM may introduce a revival arc) |

### Inner Demon Value System
- **Accumulation**: Qigong Deviation / Inner Demon Tribulation / cultivating demonic arts / killing karma
- **Threshold**: When Inner Demon Value ≥ Dao Heart Value, "Inner Demon Entanglement" (all checks −10, critical failure 94-00)
- **Removal**: Secluded meditation ( −1d5 per month), Heart-Clearing Pill (清心丹), special serendipity

# Chapter Three: Combat and Conflict

## 3.1 Round Structure

Each round = **1 Major Action + 1 Move Action + 1 Minor Action + unlimited Free Actions + 1 Reaction per round**

| Action Type | Count | Typical Use |
|----------|------|---------|
| Major Action | 1 | Martial technique attack, release divine ability, activate magic treasure, full defense, set up formation |
| Move Action | 1 | Cross 1 distance tier, jump, rise from prone |
| Minor Action | 1 | Switch weapon/treasure, consume pill, crush talisman, divine sense scan |
| Free Action | Unlimited | Brief speech, activate sustained inner art, circulate qi |
| Reaction | 1 per round | Evade, block, opportunity counter, protective qi burst |

---

## 3.2 Initiative: Divine Sense Determines All

```
Initiative Check: d100 ≤ Divine Sense + floor(Agility/5)
```

- Critical Success (difference ≥ 40): +1 to action order, extra minor action on first round
- Critical Failure (d100 ≥ 96): last in action order, loses minor action on first round

| Situation | Initiative Modifier |
|------|---------|
| Ambush/Sneak Attack | Divine Sense check +30 |
| Divine Sense Sealed | Divine Sense value halved |
| Heavy Wound (HP<25%) | −20 |
| Inner Art "Heaven's Mechanism Deduction" | Roll twice, take better |

## 3.4 Two-Layer Defense

```
Layer One: Protective Qi (passive absorption)
  → Qi serves as temporary Health Points (HP) to absorb damage; once depleted, damage pierces to the body

Layer Two: Active Evasion/Block (consumes Reaction)
  → Evasion: d100 ≤ Agility skill value → fully avoid
  → Block: d100 ≤ Melee skill value → damage halved, remainder absorbed by qi
  → Protective Burst: spend extra qi, protective efficiency ×3

Passive Defense (no Reaction used): Physique/5 + Armor Value + Inner Art passive bonus
```

- Critical Success → second-stage damage; realm growth to 8d12 + Extreme range

**Scorch Heaven, Boil Sea (焚天煮海)** (Immortal-Grade, Fire)
- 2-round charge-up + Major action, Qi 60, every 3 long rests
- All targets at Long range (including allies): 10d10 Fire + create fire-sea environment (5 rounds)
- Critical Failure → 5d10 backlash, spirit power depleted for 3 rounds after use

**Heaven-Earth Dharma Form (天地法相)** (Heaven-Grade, transformation)
- Major + sustain Minor each round, Qi 30+10/round
- Attributes +15, temporary Health Points (HP) +50%, damage die up 2 levels, awe aura
- After end, weakened 3 rounds (checks −30)

## 3.7 Special Abilities System

### Magic Treasures / Spirit Artifacts

**Equipment Slots**: Main-hand weapon, off-hand, armor, headgear, accessory ×2, footwear, inner core slot (Golden Core unlocks Life-Bound Treasure)

**Grades**: Mortal Artifact → Spirit Artifact → Earth Artifact → Heaven Artifact → Immortal Artifact → Divine Artifact (damage 1d4 → 6d12)

**Compatibility System**:

```
Compatibility = Base Value (Mortal 30% ~ Divine 80%) + Nurturing (+5%/year, cap +30%) − Grade Penalty
Grade Penalty = (Treasure Grade − User Realm Tier) × 10%
```

| Compatibility | Effect |
|--------|------|
| ≥ 80% | ×1.5 |
| 50-79% | ×1.0 |
| 30-49% | ×0.7 |
| < 30% | ×0.5 + 10% backlash chance |

**Durability**: −1 per use, shatters at zero, permanent −10% max after repair

### Pills / Elixirs

**Consumption**: Minor action, at most 1 per round, same-type effects do not stack

**Toxin Accumulation**:
- Each pill consumed → toxin + grade value (Mortal 1d3 → Immortal 4d10)
- Toxin 0-20: no effect
- Toxin 21-40: effect −20%
- Toxin 41-60: effect −40% + 30% vomiting
- Toxin 61-80: effect −60% + 50% vomiting + checks −10
- Toxin 81-100: toxin attacks the heart, −1d10 Health Points (HP) per round

**Clearing**: Short rest −1d10, long rest −3d10, seclusion fully clears

**Pill Examples**:

| Name | Grade | Effect | Toxin |

| Rejuvenation Pill (回春丹) | Mortal-Grade | Restore 1d10+5 Health Points (HP) | +1 |

| Realm-Breaking Pill (破境丹) | Heaven-Grade | Breakthrough check +15 | +20 |

### Formation Array

**Setup**: Requires formation flags / formation disk / formation eye, 1 round (small) to several rounds (large)

**Breaking**: Destroy formation eye / formation master killed/interrupted / formation opposed check

**Sample**: "Three Talents Immortal-Trapping Formation (三才陷仙陣)" (Earth-Grade) — 2 zones; enemy movement ×2 / attack −15 / 1d8 Metal damage per round; ally attack +10

**Core Positioning**: Pre-prepared "one-time spell" — crush with Minor action, no qi cost, no check needed, single use

**Spirit Beast Participation**: Acts after master's initiative, 1 Major + 1 Move action per round

# I. Rest and Recovery System

## 1.1 Rest Types

| Type | Duration | Health Points (HP) Recovery | Qi Recovery | Toxin Reduction | Inner Demon Reduction | Limit |
|------|------|---------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| **Short Rest** (Breathing Regulation) | 1 shichen (≈2 hr) | Recover `Physique×1` | Recover `Spirit Power×1` | — | — | Max 2 per day |
| **Long Rest** (Meditation) | 8 shichen | Recover `Physique×Realm Health Coefficient` | Recover `Spirit Power×Realm Qi Coefficient` | -5 | -3 (if Dao Heart check succeeds) | Requires safe environment |
| **Seclusion** | 7+ days | Full recovery | Full recovery | -20 | -10 | Requires seclusion site; max 1 per month |

## 1.2 Short Rest · Breathing Regulation (1 shichen)

```
Condition: Non-combat state, can be interrupted (if interrupted, only 50% recovered)
Effect: Health Points (HP) recover Physique×1; Qi recover Spirit Power×1
Cost: Each short rest consumes 1 Low-Grade Spirit Stone (provides spiritual energy)
Daily cap: 2 times
```

## 1.3 Long Rest · Meditation (8 shichen)

```
Condition: Safe environment (no enemy threat), cannot be interrupted
Effect:
  - Health Points (HP) recover Physique × Realm Health Coefficient
  - Qi recover Spirit Power × Realm Qi Coefficient
  - Toxin value -5
  - Inner Demon value: Dao Heart check d100 ≤ Dao Heart, success → -3, failure → no change
  - All "once per day" skills reset
Limit: Only 1 long rest per 12 shichen
```

## 1.4 Seclusion (7+ days)

```
Condition: Seclusion site (cave mansion / quiet chamber / secret realm)
Cost: Consume spirit stones daily (Low-Grade ×3 or Mid-Grade ×1)
Effect:
  - Health Points (HP) / Qi fully recovered
  - Toxin value -20
  - Inner Demon value -10
  - Can perform 1 technique cultivation check
  - Can attempt 1 realm breakthrough
Limit: Max 1 seclusion per month; cannot participate in external events during seclusion
```

## 1.5 Special-Case Recovery

| Situation | Effect |
|------|------|
| Pill-assisted short rest | Short rest recovery ×1.5 (requires consuming 1 Spirit-Returning Pill (回靈丹) or Wound-Healing Pill (療傷丹)) |


# Fourteen. Master-Apprentice and Mentorship System

In the cultivation world, the master-disciple relationship is the core bond for inheriting the Dao methods. A good master lets a disciple avoid a hundred years of detours; a rebellious disciple can also ruin a master's integrity in old age.

---

## 14.1 Conditions for Taking a Master

### Basic Conditions

| Condition | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Realm Gap** | Master's realm must be ≥ disciple's current realm + 2 stages (e.g., a Qi Refining disciple cannot apprentice to Foundation Establishment; needs Golden Core or above) |
| **Spirit Root Compatibility** | Master and disciple share at least one same or mutually-generating Spirit Root (otherwise power transmission is impossible) |
| **Dao Heart Harmony** | Master-disciple Dao Heart value gap does not exceed 30 (too large a gap means incompatible ideals) |
| **Sect Permission** | If the master is a sect member, the sect must permit taking disciples (usually Golden Core or above grants the right to take disciples) |

### Master-Acceptance Ceremony

To conduct a formal "Master-Acceptance Ceremony," one of the following must be satisfied:
- Held in the sect's Ancestral Hall (DC auto-passes)
- Rogue cultivators require a witness of Golden Core or above

After the ceremony, perform the **Master-Disciple Affinity Check**: `d100 ≤ 50 + Fortune (JIY)/5 + master favorability modifier`

| Check Result | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| Critical Success (d100 ≤ Critical Success value) | Initial master-disciple intimacy +20; master gifts an initiation gift (Earth-grade item) |
| Success | Formally establish the master-disciple relationship; initial intimacy = 10 + Fortune (JIY)/10 |
| Failure | Master temporarily declines, but gives a trial mission (retry after completion) |
| Critical Failure | Master deems the affinity not yet arrived; may not apprentice again within a short period (1 year) |

| Grade | Realm Range | Max Disciples | Max Techniques Taught | Resource Support Level | Breakthrough Guardian Bonus | Example NPC |

## 14.7 Becoming a Master (Advanced Gameplay)

When a player reaches **Golden Core Middle** and has sect permission (or Rogue Cultivator reputation ≥ 30), they may take disciples.

### Disciple-Recruiting Process

1. Discover a promising NPC / other player (NPC aptitude secretly rolled by GM)
2. Conduct the Master-Acceptance Ceremony
3. Perform the Master-Disciple Affinity Check

### Master Benefits

| Disciple Achievement | Master Benefit |
|----------------------|----------------|
| Each disciple breakthrough | Master gains cultivation = disciple's realm experience × 10% (Heaven's Dao feedback) |
| Disciple renowned throughout the world (reputation ≥ 60) | Master reputation +1d10 |
| Disciple founds a school | Master gains "Founder" title; reputation +20 |
| Disciple ascends | Master lifespan +100 years; may glimpse a corner of the Heaven's Dao (Dao Heart +1d10) |

---

> **Design Note**: The master-disciple system is one of the most core social relationships in the cultivation world. This system balances: ritual sense (Master-Acceptance Ceremony), numerical balance (intimacy linked to benefits), risk-reward (higher master grade means heavier obligations), and long-term play (new goals after becoming a master).
