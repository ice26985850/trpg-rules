# Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart — Bestiary

> This is a standalone compendium containing the Spirit Beast Codex and a quick-reference for common enemies. The rulebooks only cross-reference it.

## Chapter 3: Spirit Beast Codex

## 3.1 Spirit Beast Rules Overview

```
General Spirit Beast Rules:
- Action Order: Acts immediately after its master's Initiative.
- Actions per Round: 1 Main Action + 1 Move Action.
- Command: The master may use a Support Action to command the Spirit Beast (otherwise it acts on instinct / under GM control).
- Loyalty: 0–100; affects whether it obeys dangerous orders.
- Death: A Spirit Beast dies when its Health Points (HP) reach 0 (special Spirit Beasts may undergo Nirvana / be reborn).

Instinct Action Table (when the master does not command it):
- Valiant: Attacks the nearest / most threatening enemy.
- Guardian: Stays in the master's melee zone and blocks blows for the master.
- Cunning: Prioritizes attacking the enemy with the lowest / weakest Health Points (HP).
- Timid: Stays away from combat, attacking only from a safe distance.
- Independent: Judges the optimal target autonomously (requires the Spirit Beast's Divine Sense (SHS) ≥ 40).

Taming Rules:
- Tame Difficulty (DC) = Base DC + Spirit Beast Grade modifier + Spirit Beast current-state modifier.
- Tame Check: d100 ≤ Taming skill value (or Divine Sense (SHS) + Taming Proficiency).
- Failure Consequence: The Spirit Beast attacks the tamer (depending on its temperament).

Evolution Rules:
- Devour the Inner Core of a same-grade or higher-grade Spirit Beast.
- May evolve alongside its master when the master breaks through a major Realm.
- Fed with special heavenly treasures and earthly wonders (e.g., Dragonblood Grass, Phoenix Fruit).
```

### Spirit Beast and Master Synergy Mechanics

```
Coordinated Attack (master uses a Main Action to command):
- Master and Spirit Beast attack the same target simultaneously.
- Target's Evasion against that attack is −20 (caught off guard).
- If both hit, each deals +1d6 damage.

Spirit Beast Fusion (requires Spirit Transformation or above, special Divine Ability):
- Master and Spirit Beast briefly fuse.
- All attributes + Spirit Beast Grade × 3.
- Gains the Spirit Beast's special abilities.
- Lasts 3 rounds; afterward the Spirit Beast is weakened for 2 rounds.

Soul Contract (Life-Bound Spirit Beast, only 1):
- Master and Spirit Beast share life perception.
- When the Spirit Beast's Health Points (HP) reach 0, the master may substitute with their own Health Points (HP) (1:1).
- When the master dies, the Spirit Beast is freed (contract dissolved).
- Dissolving the contract: Both sides suffer 3d10 soul damage.
```
