# Volume I: Core World-Building Setting

## I. Creation Myth: The Primordial Era of the Dao

Before the chaos was split, there was no heaven and no earth, no life and no death — only a mass of undifferentiated Primordial Qi. Within the Primordial Qi was gestated a wisp of consciousness, later revered as the **Dao Ancestor**. The Dao Ancestor, with his supreme power, clove the chaos asunder: the clear qi rose to become the heavens, and the turbid qi sank to become the earth. Yet when heaven and earth were first divided, they constantly threatened to collapse back into chaos. The Dao Ancestor therefore transformed his body into the Dao — his two eyes became the sun and moon, his bones became the mountains and ranges, his blood became the rivers and streams, and his meridians became the **Dragon Veins**, spreading through the depths of the great earth.

The Dao Ancestor's final breath became the **Spiritual Energy** between heaven and earth, and from then on all living spirits could breathe and cultivate. The place where the Dao Ancestor fell became the **Dao Ruins** — the ultimate Secret Realm pursued by cultivators in legend, which to this day no one has been able to reach.

After the Dao Ancestor transformed into the Dao, the first generation of living beings was born from the Spiritual Energy. Born with great divine abilities, they were later honored as the **Primordial Divine Demons**. The Primordial Divine Demons carved up heaven and earth, established the first order of cultivation, and set down the cultivation ladder of the **Nine Realms to Immortality** — they believed the Great Dao was divided into nine layers, and that cultivators must break through them one by one in order to transcend the mortal dust and ascend to the immortal realm.

## III. The Laws of Heaven-and-Earth Spiritual Energy

### The Nature of Spiritual Energy

Spiritual Energy is the lingering resonance of life after the Dao Ancestor became the Dao. It is everywhere, yet its density varies enormously. The essence of Spiritual Energy is **"Dao Resonance"** — the projection of the Great Dao's laws in the material world. The closer a place is to the fundamental nature of the Dao, the denser its Spiritual Energy.

### Patterns of Spiritual Energy Distribution

1. **Where Dragon Veins Converge**: At the junctions of the Dragon Veins deep in the earth, Spiritual Energy gushes up from below, forming **Spirit Eyes** or **Spirit Springs**. Most sects are sited atop Dragon Veins.

2. **Spirit Vein Grades**:
   - **Low-Grade Spirit Vein**: Spiritual Energy density 1.5× the base value; can support a small sect
   - **Mid-Grade Spirit Vein**: Spiritual Energy density 2×; can support a mid-sized sect
   - **High-Grade Spirit Vein**: Spiritual Energy density 3×; can support a great sect
   - **Top-Grade Spirit Vein**: Spiritual Energy density 5×; can support a Holy Land
   - **Dragon Vein Main Root**: Spiritual Energy density 10×; said to be where the Dao Ruins lie

3. **Spirit-Exhausted Zones**: Far from Dragon Veins, in lands that endured the ancient great wars, lie the **Spirit-Exhausted Lands** — places where Spiritual Energy is so thin cultivation is impossible. Demonic cultivators often operate in these regions, because demonic qi is unaffected by Spiritual Energy.

4. **Spiritual Energy Tides**: Every thousand years, the Spiritual Energy of heaven and earth undergoes a **Spiritual Energy Tide** — its concentration rises markedly over several decades and then falls back. During the tide, the success rate of breaking through bottlenecks improves, Secret Realm entrances open more easily, and it is also a period when heavenly materials and earthly treasures emerge in great numbers. We are currently in the rising phase of a Spiritual Energy Tide.

### The Relationship Between Spiritual Energy and Realms

Each cultivation realm corresponds to the maximum density of Spiritual Energy a cultivator can absorb and wield. A Qi Refining cultivator can only make use of the thin surface-layer Spiritual Energy, while a Mahayana grandmaster can mobilize the power of an entire Dragon Vein. This is also why high-realm cultivators generally need to occupy higher-grade Spirit Veins — otherwise their cultivation speed will be extremely slow.

## IV. The Three-Realms System

### The Immortal Realm

The destination of cultivators after Tribulation Ascension. The Immortal Realm is not the blissful pure land of legend, but another cultivation world with an even stricter hierarchy. An ascender is only a **Heavenly Immortal** in the Immortal Realm — its lowest rank. The Immortal Realm influences the mortal world indirectly through the **Heavenly Dao Laws**; normally immortal cultivators do not directly interfere in mortal affairs (restrained by the Heavenly Dao). But the Immortal Realm will, at critical moments, grant guidance to the mortal world through means such as **Heavenly Edicts** and **dream-visions**.

### The Mortal World (Hongmeng Continent)

The primary stage of the game. Spiritual Energy is abundant but insufficient to sustain true immortals — after a successful Tribulation Crossing one must ascend within a set time, or the physical body cannot withstand the Heavenly Dao's backlash.

### The Demon Realm

A dimension opposed to the Immortal Realm. The essence of the Demon Realm's Spiritual Energy is **demonic qi** — polluted, distorted Spiritual Energy. Demon Realm beings cultivate by devouring and plundering, utterly unlike the cultivation world's breathing-and-refining. The Demon Realm passage is sealed by an ancient seal, but the seal is weakening. Some demonic-cultivator sects secretly maintain contact with the Demon Realm, attempting to draw power from it.

### The Underworld / Reincarnation

After death, a person's soul returns to the Underworld and is reborn via the **Reincarnation Platform**. A cultivator's soul is stronger than a mortal's and can, through secret methods, retain part of its past-life memories (**"Past-Life Wisdom"**). Certain grandmasters, upon falling, can escape into the Underworld in the form of a **Nascent Soul / Primordial Spirit**, seeking a chance at revival or rebirth.

# Volume II: The Sect System

## I. Sect Grade System

The sects of the Hongmeng Continent are divided into four grades by strength and influence:

| Grade | Minimum Realm Requirement (Sect Master) | Max. Total Disciples | Spirit Vein Requirement | Typical Count (Continent) |
|------|-------------------|-------------|---------|----------------|
| **Small Sect** | Golden Core | ≤100 | Low-Grade Spirit Vein | Several thousand |
| **Mid-Sized Sect** | Nascent Soul | ≤500 | Mid-Grade Spirit Vein | Several hundred |
| **Great Sect** | Spirit Transformation | ≤2000 | High-Grade Spirit Vein | Dozens |
| **Holy Land** | Fusion or above | ≤5000 | Top-Grade Spirit Vein | 5–7 |

### Grade Promotion Conditions

- **Small Sect → Mid-Sized Sect**: Sect Master reaches Nascent Soul + possesses at least one Mid-Grade Spirit Vein + disciples ≥50 + at least one Golden Core elder
- **Mid-Sized Sect → Great Sect**: Sect Master reaches Spirit Transformation + possesses at least one High-Grade Spirit Vein + disciples ≥300 + at least three Nascent Soul elders + wins a Great Sect seat through the **Sect Grand Competition**
- **Great Sect → Holy Land**: Sect Master reaches Fusion + possesses a Top-Grade Spirit Vein + has a Fusion-stage Grand Elder in residence + earns the recognition of at least three Holy Lands

## III. Sect Creation Rules (GM Customization Guide)

### Steps to Create a Sect

#### Step One: Determine the Sect Concept

1. **Dao Lineage Positioning**: What "Dao" is the sect centered on? (Sword Dao / Alchemy Dao / Artifact Dao / Formation Dao / Beast-Taming / Talisman Dao / Body Cultivation / Righteous Path / Demonic Path / Miscellaneous Cultivation)
2. **Sect Style**: Righteous / Demonic / Neutral? Radical / Conservative? Closed / Open?
3. **Core Trait**: What unique feature does this sect have that no other sect can replace?

#### Step Two: Determine Sect Grade and Scale

Reference the table below:

| Grade | Disciple Cap | Number of Elders | Golden-Core-and-above Disciples | Territory Scope | Spirit Vein Grade | Technique Library Capacity |
|------|---------|---------|------------|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Small Sect** | 20–100 | 1–3 | 1–5 | 1 mountain / 1 Spirit Vein branch | Low-Grade | 5–10 works |
| **Mid-Sized Sect** | 100–500 | 3–10 | 5–30 | 1–3 mountain ranges | Mid-Grade | 10–30 works |
| **Great Sect** | 500–2000 | 10–30 | 30–100 | Entire region | High-Grade | 30–80 works |
| **Holy Land** | 2000–5000 | 30–50+ | 100+ | Cross-region power | Top-Grade | 80–200 works |

#### Step Three: Sect Resources and Output

| Grade | Monthly Spirit Stone Output | Pill Output (monthly) | Magical Tool Output (monthly) | Spirit Herb Field Scale |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Small Sect** | 100–500 low-grade | 10–30 Grade-1 pills | 1–3 Magical Tools | 1–3 mu |
| **Mid-Sized Sect** | 500–3000 low-grade | 50 Grade-1 + 10 Grade-2 pills | 5–10 Magical Tools + 1 Spirit Artifact | 5–10 mu |
| **Great Sect** | 3000–20000 | 30 Grade-2 + 5 Grade-3 pills | 5–10 Spirit Artifacts | 20–50 mu |
| **Holy Land** | 20000+ | 20 Grade-3 + 3 Grade-4 pills | 20 Spirit Artifacts + possible Quasi-Immortal Artifact | 100+ mu |

#### Step Four: Sect Development Rules

**Sect Upgrade Condition Table**:

| Upgrade Path | Conditions |
|---------|------|
| **Found a Sect** | Golden Core cultivator + at least one Low-Grade Spirit Vein + 5 or more disciples |
| **Small → Mid** | Sect Master Nascent Soul + Mid-Grade Spirit Vein + disciples ≥50 + at least one Golden Core elder + sect reputation ≥500 |
| **Mid → Great** | Sect Master Spirit Transformation + High-Grade Spirit Vein + disciples ≥300 + three Nascent Soul elders + wins the Sect Grand Competition + reputation ≥2000 |
| **Great → Holy Land** | Sect Master Fusion + Top-Grade Spirit Vein + Fusion-stage Grand Elder + recognition by three Holy Lands + reputation ≥10000 |

**Sect Decline**:
- Sect Master dies with no qualified heir → grade may drop
- Spirit Vein destroyed or exhausted → resource output halved; if no new Spirit Vein is found within a year, demote
- Loss in a sect war → cede land and pay reparations; reputation greatly reduced
- Mass loss of disciples → if below the current grade's minimum, demote

#### Step Five: Sect Diplomacy System

**Diplomatic Relation Levels**:

| Relation | Description | Game Effect |
|------|------|---------|
| **Blood Alliance** | Share life and death | Joint defense, resource sharing, techniques may be mutually transmitted |
| **Alliance** | Offensive-defensive alliance | Mutual aid in war, trade discount (−30% cost) |
| **Friendly** | Benign interaction | Disciples may visit and exchange; normal trade |
| **Neutral** | Each keeps to its own | No special interaction |
| **Cool** | There is friction | Trade restricted (+50% cost); disciple travel requires application |
| **Hostile** | Like fire and water | Trade forbidden; disciples in the other's territory may be attacked |
| **Mortal Enemy** | Fight to the death | State of total war |

**Sect War Rule Framework**:
- **Declaration of War**: Requires a just cause (treasure seizure / revenge / territorial dispute / Righteous vs. Demonic); declaring war without cause lowers reputation
- **Forms of War**: Total war / timed decisive battle (elder duel) / disciple sparring (avoids large-scale casualties)
- **War Outcome**: Victor gains resources / territory / technique reparations; loser cedes land and pays reparations, reputation greatly reduced
- **Third-Party Mediation**: A Holy Land or neutral Great Sect may intervene to mediate

# Volume IV: GM Running Guide

## I. Narrative Pacing Control for Cultivation Campaigns

A cultivation-type TRPG differs from a traditional dungeon crawl; it has its own pacing characteristics. The GM needs to manage two core time scales:

### Two Time Scales

| Scale | Description | Applicable Scenes |
|------|------|---------|
| **Adventure Time** | Advances minute by minute / hour by hour | Combat, exploring Secret Realms, character dialogue, urgent events |
| **Cultivation Time** | Advances day by day / month by month / year by year | Secluded cultivation, pill and artifact refinement, learning techniques, sect tasks |

### Principles of Pace Switching

1. **Adventure → Cultivation**: After completing an adventure / dungeon, give characters time to "digest their spoils" — cultivate new techniques, refine pills, upgrade treasures. This period can be skipped quickly ("Three months later…").

2. **Cultivation → Adventure**: Give the characters a clear external push —
   - A sect task
   - A Secret Realm entrance about to close
   - Enemies coming to their door
   - A heavenly material or earthly treasure coming into the world (Spiritual Energy Tide fluctuation)
   - Receiving a distress signal

3. **Golden Ratio**: It is recommended to arrange a longer cultivation period (1–6 months) after every 3–4 adventures, giving characters time to digest their growth.

### Handling Secluded Cultivation

Seclusion is one of the core experiences of cultivation. The GM should follow these principles:

- **Short Seclusion (1–7 days)**: Handle quickly; just roll a Cultivation Check
- **Mid Seclusion (1–3 months)**: Intersperse 1–2 small events — Inner Demon attack / a fellow cultivator's visit / Spiritual Energy disturbance
- **Long Seclusion (half a year or more)**: Arrange at least 1–2 interruption events, and describe the changes in heaven and earth during the seclusion

## II. Narrative Handling of Realm Breakthroughs

Realm breakthroughs are the "highlight moments" of a cultivation campaign. Every major-realm breakthrough should let players feel a qualitative change. The following are narrative suggestions for breakthroughs at each stage:

### Qi Refining → Foundation Establishment
**Narrative Focus**: The first qualitative change — the true threshold from mortal to cultivator.
- **Sense of Ritual**: Spiritual Energy flooding the body, the entire meridian network rebuilt
- **Check**: Foundation Establishment Pill + Comprehension (WUX) Check (d100, Target Number 70 − current realm modifier)
- **Failure Consequence**: Fall back to Perfected Qi Refining; must cool down one month and retry
- **Description Suggestion**: "You feel the Spiritual Energy in your dantian begin to condense from gaseous to solid — like mist turning to water, drop by drop… finally gathering into a pool of spirit-liquid at the bottom of your dantian. This is your Dao Foundation — the true starting point of cultivation."

### Golden Core → Nascent Soul
**Narrative Focus**: From "refining external force" to "creating life" — the Golden Core shatters, the Nascent Soul is born.
- **Sense of Ritual**: Another "self" is gestated within the Golden Core, breaking out of the core
- **Check**: Special breakthrough ritual + temperament (Disposition) Check
- **Failure Consequence**: Golden Core damaged (severity depends on the margin of check failure); in the worst case may cause cultivation regression
- **Description Suggestion**: "A fine crack opens on the surface of the Golden Core, light shooting from within. More and more cracks appear, until the Golden Core shatters entirely — but this is not destruction, it is rebirth. A tiny figure identical to you sits at the center of the shattered Golden Core; he opens his eyes and meets your gaze. That is your Nascent Soul."

### Spirit Transformation → Void Refining
**Narrative Focus**: From "the formed" to "the formless" — begins to grasp spatial laws.
- **Sense of Ritual**: Primordial Spirit leaves the body, senses the void of heaven and earth
- **Check**: Comprehension (WUX) + spatial-perception Check (high difficulty)
- **Failure Consequence**: Primordial Spirit damaged; possible amnesia or mental confusion
- **Description Suggestion**: "Your Primordial Spirit detaches from your flesh and for the first time truly senses the nature of the void. Space is no longer a distant concept — it is a net, and you have learned how to pluck one of its threads."

### Tribulation Crossing → Ascension
**Narrative Focus**: The ultimate trial — the Heavenly Tribulation descends.
- **Sense of Ritual**: Eighty-one heavenly thunderbolts + Inner Demon Tribulation + Dao Heart Inquiry
- **Check**: Multiple consecutive rounds of checks (Physique [RSS] endurance / Inner Demon resistance / Dao Heart [DAX] steadfastness)
- **Failure Consequence**: Turned to ash (physical body destroyed) / switch to cultivating as a Loose Immortal (Nascent Soul escapes but body is destroyed) / cultivation regression
- **Description Suggestion**: "Dark clouds gather above your head, lightning flickering within the clouds. The first heavenly thunder falls — not ordinary lightning, but the Heavenly Dao's own trial of you. Each bolt contains a thread of Great Dao law; as you are struck you are also tempered by the Heavenly Dao. This is the ultimate trial where destruction and rebirth happen at once."

### Minor-Stage Breakthroughs (Early → Middle → Late → Perfected)
- **Narrative Suggestion**: Relatively lightweight; may be handled with montage technique
- **Check**: Cultivation Check (d100 + Comprehension modifier vs Target Number)
- **Description Suggestion**: Use brief imagery — "After three months of bitter cultivation, you feel the Spirit Power pool in your dantian expand by a notch"

## IV. Encounter Design Guide

### Encounter Types and Design Points

| Encounter Type | Design Points | Check Focus |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Mortal** | Reflect the gap between cultivator and mortal — let players feel their own power. But mortals may also convey key information or emotional resonance | Charisma / reputation check; spell display (auto-success) |
| **Rogue Cultivator** | Wild cultivators of varied personalities. May be ally, competitor, swindler, or robber. Rogues are usually craftier than same-realm sect disciples but have unstable foundations | Social check; power-assessment check |
| **Sect Disciple** | Represents the sect's face; behavior constrained by sect rules. May serve as ally, opponent, or information source | Sect reputation check; social check |
| **Demonic Beast** | From Tier 1 (Qi Refining level) to Tier 9 (Tribulation Crossing level). Demonic beasts have territorial awareness and do not necessarily attack. High-intelligence beasts can communicate or even trade | Combat check; beast-taming / communication check |
| **Demonic Cultivator** | Cultivators of demonic techniques. May be stronger than same-rank but riskier. Some demonic cultivators are not evil — merely chose a different Dao | Will-resistance check; combat check |
| **Ancient Remnant Race** | Races left over from the Divine-Demon Era — descendants of the Primordial Divine Demons, ancient demon clans, spirit clans, etc. Powerful and mysterious, may possess abilities beyond the current cultivation system | Knowledge (Ancient) check; special negotiation |

### Golden Rules of Encounter Design

1. **Not every encounter is combat**: Give players the choice to flee, negotiate, sneak, or trade
2. **Environment is encounter**: Spiritual Energy storms, spatial rifts, heavenly fire descending — natural phenomena are themselves challenges
3. **Difficulty gradient**: Encounters within the same adventure should have a clear difficulty curve
4. **Information first**: Give players the chance to gather intelligence — scouting, asking around, divination

## V. Heavenly Tribulation and Inner Demon Narrative Rules

### Heavenly Tribulation Trigger Conditions

The Heavenly Tribulation mainly triggers under the following circumstances:
1. **Major-realm breakthrough** (Golden Core → Nascent Soul and above): definitely triggers
2. **Refining heaven-defying treasures** (Quasi-Immortal Artifact level): may trigger a minor Heavenly Tribulation
3. **Touching Heavenly Dao taboos**: cultivating forbidden arts, leaking Heaven's Mechanism, reversing life and death, etc.

### Heavenly Tribulation Check Framework

Taking the Heavenly Tribulation at the Golden Core → Nascent Soul breakthrough as an example (standard Heavenly Tribulation):

| Stage | Check Content | Check Method |
|------|---------|---------|
| **First Wave: Lightning Tribulation (3 bolts)** | Physique endurance | Physique Check (d100 + Physique (RSS) modifier); each bolt increases in difficulty (50/60/70) |
| **Second Wave: Fire Tribulation** | Spirit Power control | Spirit Power Check (d100 + cultivation modifier); control the Spiritual Energy within to resist the heavenly fire |
| **Third Wave: Wind Tribulation** | Will steadfastness | Will Check (d100 + Will modifier); resist the gang-wind that can scatter the soul |
| **Fourth Wave: Inner Demon Tribulation** | Dao Heart | See Inner Demon rules below |

**Heavenly Tribulation Intensity Adjustment**:
- Solid foundation (cultivating righteous-path techniques, progressing step by step): Heavenly Tribulation difficulty −10
- Unstable foundation (pill-stacking, demonic-speed cultivation): Heavenly Tribulation difficulty +10
- Heavy karmic debt (slaughtering many innocents): Heavenly Tribulation difficulty +20
- Merit-protected body (saving the world and people): Heavenly Tribulation difficulty −5

### Inner Demon Tribulation Narrative Rules

The Inner Demon Tribulation is the most dangerous part of the Heavenly Tribulation — it tests not the body, but the Dao Heart.

**GM Operating Procedure**:
1. Review that character's backstory; find their greatest regret / fear / obsession
2. The Inner Demon takes the concrete form of these regrets / fears / obsessions
3. The player must face the Inner Demon — not through combat, but through choice / dialogue / enlightenment
4. Use a Will Check to support the narrative — but final success or failure should be decided by the player's roleplay

**Inner Demon Examples**:
- A dead relative asks, "Why do you cultivate?"
- Another self mocks, "Your Dao Heart is nothing but hypocritical self-deception"
- Slain enemies come to claim your life collectively
- The Great Dao itself asks, "What is the Dao?"

**Inner Demon Check**:
- **Pass** (Will Check success + reasonable roleplay): Inner Demon disperses, Dao Heart grows firmer (gain permanent Will bonus)
- **Barely Pass** (Check fails but roleplay excellent): Inner Demon not dispersed but temporarily suppressed (gain a debuff, requires later plot to resolve)
- **Fail** (Check fails + shaken by the Inner Demon): cultivation regression, or develop an "Inner Demon Mark" (long-term negative trait)

### Consequences of Heavenly Tribulation Failure

| Degree of Failure | Consequence |
|---------|------|
| Minor Failure (failed to endure 1–2 bolts) | Breakthrough fails; cultivation regresses one minor stage; cool down half a year |
| Moderate Failure (failed 3+ bolts of lightning/fire/wind) | Cultivation regresses one major stage; physical body damaged; requires heavenly materials and earthly treasures to repair |
| Severe Failure (Inner Demon Tribulation fails) | Develop an Inner Demon Mark; long-term debuff; requires special plot to resolve |
| Fatal Failure (Heavenly Tribulation fully fails) | Physical body destroyed. Nascent Soul may escape (if present) → switch to Loose Immortal path; or die directly |

### Act I: Prologue — "The Missing Herb Gatherers"

**Scene 1: Qingshi Town**

The player characters meet in Qingshi Town (preset: all characters are Qi Refining cultivators who have just left their sect / master's mountain, or local rogue cultivators). Qingshi Town is a mortal hamlet on the edge of the Qingyun Mountain Range, with a marketplace teahouse frequented by cultivators called the "Qingfeng Residence."

**Key NPC — Old Mayor · Chen Bonian**:
- A mortal elder, venerated in the town
- Anxiously seeking cultivator help — three of the town's herb gatherers went into the mountains to gather herbs and have not returned for seven days
- Location of the disappearance: "Black Pine Ridge" north of Qingshi Town

**Information Gathering (Check Points)**:
- Ask the townsfolk: Charisma Check; learn that Black Pine Ridge recently had "yin winds blowing, even the demonic beasts fled"
- Examine the herb gatherers' lodgings: Perception Check; discover the herb gatherers' gathering records — they planned to go to the "ancient cave" deep in Black Pine Ridge to collect "Ghostlight Mushrooms"
- Ask around at the marketplace teahouse: Social Check; from an old rogue cultivator learn that "the ancient cave in Black Pine Ridge is said to be an abandoned cultivator's cave-dwelling, but it was emptied out ten years ago"

**Enter the Adventure**: The players head for Black Pine Ridge.

---

### Act II: Development — "The Ancient Cave of Black Pine Ridge"

**Scene 2: Black Pine Ridge Wilderness**

Environmental Features: Pine forest blocks out the sky and sun; dim light; a faint rotting stench hangs in the air.

**Encounter A: A Startled Demonic Beast (Check Point)**
- A wounded Tier-1 demonic beast, the "Shadow Cat," darts out from the bushes
- Check Choice: Combat (gain beast materials on defeat) / Soothe (Beast-Taming Check; gain intelligence — the Shadow Cat fled from the direction of the ancient cave)
- From the beast's wounds: discover faint black Spiritual Energy residue on the claw marks (Spirit Power Perception Check — identify this as "corpse qi")

**Encounter B: The First Corpse**
- Discover the corpse of one herb gatherer near the ancient cave
- The body is withered, as if drained of moisture
- Check: Medicine Dao / Spirit Power Perception Check — discover faint yin-evil Spiritual Energy remains in the deceased; cause of death is "essence-qi drained dry"

**Scene 3: Ancient Cave Entrance**

The ancient cave entrance is shrouded in a faint gray mist.

**Check**: Spirit Power Perception — the mist contains faint "yin-evil qi"; prolonged exposure for a Qi Refining cultivator causes discomfort (but is not fatal).

**Environmental Puzzle**: The ancient cave entrance has a broken restriction — a simple "Maze Array" (mostly failed but with residual might).
- Array-breaking Check: Formation-Array Knowledge Check; or simply force through (Will Check against the confusion effect)

**Scene 4: Ancient Cave Interior — Front Hall**

After entering the ancient cave, it is confirmed this was once a cultivator's cave-dwelling. Faint writing is carved on the stone wall of the front hall:
> "…I am Liu Yunfei, a disciple of Qingyun Sect, expelled from the master's sect for touching a taboo. This is my place of seclusion… If a later comer reaches this point, remember: at the deepest part of the cave-dwelling is what I sealed in my time — do not open it…"

**Check**: Knowledge (Cultivation History) Check — Liu Yunfei was a Qingyun Sect disciple about fifty years ago, said to have vanished after being expelled for "cultivating evil techniques."

**Scene 5: Ancient Cave Interior — Alchemy Room / Artifact Room**

This place has already been looted; all valuables taken. But careful searching reveals:

- Alchemy Room corner: an incomplete alchemy notebook (found via Perception Check) — records a pill formula called the "Yin-Gathering Pill," whose effect is "accelerates yin-attribute Spiritual Energy absorption," with the side effect "long-term use leads to impure Spiritual Energy"
- Artifact Room: some faint runes carved on the wall (Knowledge Check — these are not righteous-path runes, but belong to the forbidden "Corpse Dao" art's runes)

**Encounter C: The Other Two Missing Herb Gatherers**
- In a passage deep in the ancient cave, discover the second herb gatherer — still alive, but incoherent
- He mutters: "The cave… deepest inside… that seal… broke… a black hand… grabbed Old Third…"
- The third herb gatherer is nowhere to be found

---

### Act III: Climax — "Beneath the Seal"

**Scene 6: Ancient Cave Deepest — Sealing Chamber**

This is different from before — clearly a carefully arranged restriction chamber. A complex sealing array is carved on the floor, but now more than half the array patterns are destroyed. At the center of the seal is a bottomless fissure, from which black Spiritual Energy seeps out like smoke.

**Boss Encounter — "Corpse Puppet · Liu Yunfei"**

What crawls out of the seal is Liu Yunfei himself — or rather, Liu Yunfei's corpse. Fifty years ago, while cultivating the Corpse Dao forbidden art he fell into Qigong Deviation, and before death sealed himself here. Now the seal was accidentally broken by the herb gatherers (they wanted to pry open the sealing stone for treasure); after Liu Yunfei's corpse absorbed the herb gatherers' essence-qi, it transformed into a "Corpse Puppet."

| Boss Attribute | Value |
|----------|------|
| **Name** | Corpse Puppet · Liu Yunfei |
| **Power** | Equivalent to Perfected Qi Refining (slightly above Levels 1–3 players) |
| **Health Points (HP)** | 45 |
| **Attack Method** | Corpse Claw (d100 attack, damage 1d8+3, with corpse-qi erosion) |
| **Special Ability** | "Corpse Qi Burst" (3-round cooldown) — area yin-qi damage; resist via Will Check |
| **Weakness** | Fire-attribute attacks deal +50% damage; restrained by yang-attribute techniques; slow movement |
| **AI Behavior** | Prioritizes attacking the nearest living thing; uses Corpse Qi Burst every 3 rounds |

**Combat Design Notes**:
- For 3–5 Early Qi Refining players, this Boss is challenging but not lethal
- If there is a Fire Spirit Root player or prepared fire-attribute talismans, the fight is much easier
- If the fight is too difficult, the GM may let Liu Yunfei's lingering consciousness briefly sober up, hesitating one round at a critical moment (giving players a breather)

## Act I: Mortal Dawn

**Suitable Levels**: Qi Refining Levels 1–4 → Breakthrough to Foundation Establishment

**Core Theme**: The true crossing from mortal to cultivator

**Time Span (in-game)**: ~1–2 years



### Plot Summary



After the battle of Qingshi Town, the players obtained Liu Yunfei's Qingyun Sect disciple token and last letter from his relics. Whatever their origin before, Liu Yunfei's story points in the same direction — Qingyun Sect. Following this thread, the players travel to the Qingyun Mountain Range in East Land, stepping onto the true path of cultivation.



Qingyun Sect is holding its once-every-five-years "Ascension Gathering" — a public examination open to the outside world for recruiting new disciples. This is the golden opportunity for rogue cultivators and children of small families to enter the orthodox cultivation system. At the gathering the players undergo Spirit Root testing, Dao Heart trials, and practical combat, formally becoming Qingyun Sect outer disciples (or keeping their original sect identity but establishing a connection with Qingyun Sect).



After joining, the players are assigned their first sect task: investigate the recent frequent demonic-beast disturbances in the "Misty Hidden Valley" on the northern foot of the Qingyun Mountain Range. This seemingly simple task leads the players to an ancient ruin — "Tai Chu's Hermitage," said to be the cultivation cave-dwelling of True Person Tai Chu, the first Human Immortal Venerable, when he was at Qi Refining. Hidden within the cave-dwelling is the first fragment of the secret of the "Nine Realms to Immortality" — the **"Mystery of the Dao Foundation."**



However, Qingyun Sect is not monolithic inside. A Foundation Establishment inner disciple (a traitor) secretly colludes with outside forces, attempting to monopolize the treasures in Tai Chu's Hermitage. The players must defeat him in a confrontation, protect the ruin's secret, and in the process complete their breakthrough from Qi Refining to Foundation Establishment.



**Act End**: The players break through to Foundation Establishment, truly stepping across the threshold into the cultivation world.



### Key Scenes



#### Scene 1: Ascension Gathering — Spirit Root Platform



**Location**: Qingyun Sect mountain-gate plaza, a giant "Spirit Root Stele" stands at the center

**Event**: Thousands of seekers gather here; the Ascension Gathering is presided over by a Qingyun Sect Dharma-Teaching Elder

**Procedure**:

1. **Spirit Root Test**: Place your hand on the Spirit Root Stele; the stele lights up in the corresponding Spirit Root color — Check: no check needed; Spirit Root is determined at Character Creation — Narrative focus: the GM describes the stele's reaction based on Spirit Root quality — a Heaven Spirit Root makes the stele blaze with light, the whole crowd in an uproar; a low-grade Spirit Root makes it dim, drawing whispered gossip

2. **Dao Heart Inquiry**: The Dharma-Teaching Elder asks each person three questions — "Why do you cultivate?" — "If the Great Dao is hopeless, what will you do?" — "What are you willing to sacrifice for the Dao?" — Check: Dao Heart Check (d100 ≤ Dao Heart); success means the elder nods in approval

3. **Entry Spar**: Random pairing, first-blood-only — Check: best of three, using Martial Technique opposition — Opponent example: other Early Qi Refining seekers (Health Points (HP) 25, attack skill value 35)



**Key NPCs**:

3. **Encounter B — Black Shadow in the Mist**: — A mysterious black-robed figure appears from the valley-floor direction — If the players challenge him, the black-robed figure says nothing, releases a black mist, and vanishes — Spirit Power Perception Check (d100 ≤ Spirit Power (LIL) −10) identifies faint "restriction" qi within the black mist

## Act II: The Choice of the Dao Path



**Suitable Levels**: Foundation Establishment Levels 5–8 → Breakthrough to Golden Core

**Core Theme**: Choose your Dao — Righteous / Demonic / Neutral, sect affiliation, cultivation direction

**Time Span (in-game)**: ~3–5 years



### Plot Summary



After succeeding at Foundation Establishment, the players face a far broader cultivation world. Qingyun Sect nominates the players to participate in the once-a-century "Sect Grand Competition" at the Central Region Debate Platform — not only a battle of honor, but a key event deciding the sect's resource allocation for the next century. At the same time, demonic-path infiltration becomes ever more evident: a secret organization calling itself the "Youming Dao (Netherworld Path)" is quietly recruiting disciples within the great sects.



During preparations for the Sect Grand Competition, the players unexpectedly make contact with the "Five Elements Secret Realm" — a cultivation holy land that opens once every hundred years, said to contain the origin power of the Five Elements Great Dao. However, entry slots are limited, and the great sects scheme openly and covertly for them. The players must perform well in the Sect Grand Competition to win entry slots for Qingyun Sect (or their own sect).



After the Sect Grand Competition ends, the true face of the demonic-path forces is gradually exposed — the leader of Youming Dao is a Golden Core Perfected demonic cultivator who intends to seize the "Five Elements Origin" within the Five Elements Secret Realm, to refine a heretical artifact that can shake the continent's balance. The players go deep into the Secret Realm and wage a final showdown with Youming Dao.



At the deepest part of the Secret Realm, the players find True Person Tai Chu's second Dao Seed — the **"Mystery of the Golden Core"** — realizing that the Golden Core is not simple solidified Spirit Power, but the condensation of a "seed of the Dao." In this showdown and enlightenment, the players break through to Golden Core.

## Act Three: Rising Winds and Surging Clouds


**Suitable Level**: Golden Core (金丹) 9–12 → Breakthrough to Nascent Soul (元嬰)

**Core Theme**: Choices in the Torrent of the Age—the Demon Abyss Crisis and the Brink of Continental War

**Time Span (In-Game)**: Approximately 5–10 years


### Plot Summary


With the Golden Core formed, the player's standing in the cultivation world is no longer what it once was. The Qingyun Sect grants the player the status of "Legacy Disciple" (真傳弟子), with the right to take on disciples independently, receive guidance from the elders, and access greater sect resources.


However, peace did not last long. An urgent plea for help arrived from the Demon-Suppressing Hall (鎮魔殿) in the Western Regions—the seal of the Demon Abyss has shown its most severe loosening in ten thousand years. Among the five Fusion (合體)-stage grandmasters who guard the Demon Abyss year-round, one was struck by an unknown force and gravely wounded. If enough high-rank cultivators do not go to reinforce it, the seal could collapse within a decade.


At the same time, a "Spirit Vein (靈脈) Contest" erupted at the border between the Eastern Lands (東土) and the Western Regions—a newly discovered supreme-grade Spirit Vein lies at the boundary of the two domains, and Danxia Valley (丹霞谷) and the Western Regions Loose Cultivator (散修) Alliance refuse to yield to one another. Battle is on the verge of breaking out. Meanwhile, the Nether Path takes the opportunity to stir up trouble between the two domains, attempting to provoke a full-scale war to divert the Righteous Path's attention.


The player is drawn into this intricate situation. On the way to reinforce the Western Regions, the player is ambushed by the Nether Path and discovers the true cause of the seal's loosening—the Nether Path is performing a ritual called the "Genesis Plan" (開天計劃) deep within the Demon Abyss, attempting to actively open a passage to the Demon Realm (魔界).


While tracking the Nether Path through the rifts of the Demon Abyss, the player finds Taichu the True Person's (太初真人) third Dao Seed—**"The Secret of the Nascent Soul"** (元嬰之祕). Taichu's lingering intent reveals a startling truth: the Nascent Soul is not a simple "second life," but a "projection of the Great Dao within the individual." The process of condensing the infant (凝嬰) is essentially making oneself a tiny branch of the Great Dao.


In the deepest part of the Demon Abyss rift, the player engages in a life-and-death duel with a Nascent Soul-stage Demon General of the Nether Path. The outcome of this battle will decide the short-term fate of the Demon Abyss seal—and also opens the door for the player to the Nascent Soul realm.

## Act Four: Heaven and Earth as the Board


**Suitable Level**: Nascent Soul (元嬰) 13–16 → Breakthrough to Spirit Transformation (化神)

**Core Theme**: From a chess piece to a chess player—participating in the reshaping of the continental order

**Time Span (In-Game)**: Approximately 10–30 years


### Plot Summary


With the Nascent Soul formed, the player is no longer merely someone who executes tasks—but someone qualified to participate in strategy-making. The Five Sacred Lands, at the initiative of the Demon-Suppressing Hall, convene the "Cultivation World Assembly" (修仙界大會) to discuss forming the Anti-Demon Alliance. The player, as one of the outstanding Legacy Disciples of the Qingyun Sect, is invited to attend the assembly.


However, the assembly does not go smoothly. Each great sect harbors its own thoughts: the Taixu Sword Sect (太虛劍宗) advocates a full-scale offensive, Danxia Valley worries that war will affect the pill supply chain, the Wanbao Pavilion (萬寶閣) sees the business opportunity brought by war, and the Tianji Manor (天機府) calculates a Heaven's Mechanism (天機) warning that "if the alliance is hastily formed, there will be a bloodshed disaster." The player must mediate among all sides to push for the alliance's formation.


Just as the alliance is about to be reached, a major piece of news shakes the cultivation world—an "ancient inheritance" (上古傳承) emerged from the void near Ascension City in the Central Continent, preliminarily judged to be the complete inheritance of a Primordial Divine Demon (先天神魔) from the era of gods and demons. As soon as the news breaks, every sect's attention immediately shifts from the Demon Abyss to the inheritance contest.


While exploring the ancient inheritance, the player encounters a mysterious existence calling itself the "Ancient Remnant Clan" (上古遺族)—he is the last surviving descendant of a Primordial Divine Demon from the era of gods and demons. He reveals a deeper truth about the Demon Abyss crisis: the reason the Demon Realm passage could be opened is because the "Heavenly Dao" (天道) of the Hongmeng Continent (鴻蒙大陸) itself is in decline. And the cause of the decline is related to a certain flaw in the "Nine Realms to Immortality" (九境登仙) system.


Meanwhile, the Nether Path uses the chaos of the sects fighting over the inheritance to accelerate the "Genesis Plan" deep in the Demon Abyss. When the player returns from the inheritance, a first "small rift" connecting to the Demon Realm has already appeared in the Demon Abyss—the Demon Realm's vanguard army begins to infiltrate the mortal world.


In a battle to stop the Demon Realm vanguard from expanding the rift, the player faces the ultimate Nascent Soul challenge—defeating the commander of the Demon Realm vanguard, a Spirit Transformation-stage "Demon Lord" (魔君). Between life and death, the player breaks through to Spirit Transformation.

## Act Five: The Stairway to Immortality


**Suitable Level**: Spirit Transformation (化神) 17–20 → Void Refining (煉虛) Breakthrough → Fusion (合體) Breakthrough

**Core Theme**: Crossing the gulf between mortal and immortal—facing the truth of the Demon Realm

**Time Span (In-Game)**: Approximately 50–100 years


### Plot Summary


After Spirit Transformation, the player already stands at the upper layer of the Hongmeng Continent cultivator pyramid. However, the closer to the peak, the more one can feel the weight of the Heavenly Dao. Every major action of the player may affect the continental order.


The clue of the fifth Dao Seed points to the "Immortal Realm Trial Path" in the East Sea Abyss—this is a trial passage established by the Immortal Realm for mortal cultivators in ancient times; those who pass may gain the Immortal Realm's recognition and the blessing of Immortal Realm laws. But this trial path has had no one pass it for tens of thousands of years.


While exploring the trial path in the East Sea, the player discovers a startling fact: the other end of the trial path leads directly to the "Heavenly Dao Core" (天道核心)—the hub where the Heavenly Dao's laws operate. Scattered within the Heavenly Dao Core are "Heavenly Dao Fragments" that have broken off due to the Heavenly Dao's decline. Collecting these fragments can temporarily stabilize the Heavenly Dao and delay the Demon Realm invasion.


Meanwhile, the Nether Path accelerates its pace in the shadows. Their Cult Master—a terrifying Fusion-stage existence—finally acts in person. The true identity of the Nether Path Cult Master is revealed: he is a fallen immortal from the Immortal-Demon War ten thousand years ago. He betrayed the Immortal Realm in that war, defected to the Demon Realm, and was granted immortal life by the Demon Realm's "Demon Ancestor" (魔祖). His goal is not simply to open the Demon Realm passage, but to "devour the Heavenly Dao" (吞噬天道)—to claim the Heavenly Dao Core as his own, thereby becoming the new master of the Hongmeng Continent.


The player must prove their qualification in the Immortal Realm trial, collect the Heavenly Dao Fragments, and then face the Nether Path Cult Master in the final duel. In the process of defeating the Nether Path Cult Master, the player completes the two breakthroughs of Void Refining and Fusion, preparing for the ultimate duel of the final act.

## Act Six: The Peak of the Nine Realms


**Suitable Level**: Fusion (合體) 25–28 → Mahayana Breakthrough → Tribulation Crossing → Ascension

**Core Theme**: The Ultimate Choice—ascend to the Immortal Realm or remain in the mortal world

**Time Span (In-Game)**: Approximately 100–500 years


### Plot Summary


With Fusion achieved, the player stands at the very top of the Hongmeng Continent. The Heavenly Dao Fragments are gathered, and the Heavenly Dao has temporarily regained stability. But the true ultimate threat has not yet been removed—the "Demon Ancestor" deep in the Demon Realm, the master of the Demon Realm sealed ten thousand years ago, is awakening.


Cang Wudao was merely a prelude. The true Demon Ancestor is an existence beyond the Tribulation Crossing stage—his status in the Demon Realm is equivalent to the Immortal Realm's "Heavenly Immortal" (天仙). The ten-thousand-year seal has already exhausted most of his power, but once he fully awakens, even the weakest Demon Ancestor is not something mortal cultivators can oppose.


The only way to stop the Demon Ancestor's awakening is to delve into the Demon Realm before the seal completely collapses and apply a final layer of reinforcement to the seal—the "Heavenly Dao Seal" (天道封印) that requires "Ascension Power" (飛昇之力) to drive. This means the player must complete the Mahayana and Tribulation Crossing breakthroughs before entering the Demon Realm, then trigger the Ascension Tribulation in the ultimate duel with the Demon Ancestor, using the Ascension Power to seal the Demon Realm passage.


But Ascension means leaving the mortal world forever. The player faces an ultimate choice:

- Ascend to the Immortal Realm, entrusting the future of the mortal world to those who come after

- Give up Ascension, remaining in the mortal world as a "Loose Immortal" (散仙) to continue guarding

- A third path: upon ascending, leave a portion of one's Great Dao comprehension in the mortal world, planting a new Dao Seed, so that later comers also have a chance to ascend the Nine Realms


Above Ascension City, the Heavenly Tribulation (天劫) descends. Eighty-one Heavenly Thunders, the ultimate trial of the Inner Demon, the Heavenly Dao's final question—all await the player.


**End of Act**: Ascension—or remaining—or forging a third path. The Nine Realms to Immortality has become legend.

**Event**: The location of Taichu's seventh Dao Seed finally appears—it lies on the other side of the Demon Realm seal. To obtain it, one must briefly cross the seal into the outer Demon Realm.


**Flow**:

1. **Crossing the Seal**: — The seal is now fragile enough that Mahayana-stage power can briefly pass through without breaking it — Spirit Power Check (d100 ≤ LIL−20) to open a temporary passage — After crossing, must return within 12 hours (時辰), otherwise the passage closes

2. **Outer Demon Realm**: — A distorted world shrouded in a dark-purple sky — The air is filled with demonic energy—every 2 hours a Dao Heart Check; on failure, Inner Demon Value +2d10 — Mahayana-stage Cultivation lets the player barely hold on here

3. **The Truth of the Demon Realm**: — The Demon Realm is not purely "evil"—it is a "mirror world" whose laws are completely different from the mortal world — The Demon Realm's "demonic energy" is distorted Spiritual Energy—its essence is the embodiment of "emotion" and "desire" — The Demon Ancestor is an ancient existence trapped in this world—he was once also a cultivator, but chose "devouring" instead of "ascension"

4. **The Seventh Dao Seed—"The Secret of Tribulation Crossing"** (渡劫之祕): — The Dao Seed is at the "Seal Foundation" directly below the Seal Gate — Taichu the True Person entered the Demon Realm ten thousand years ago—he left this Dao Seed and a prophecy — Information: "Tribulation Crossing is not resisting the Heavenly Tribulation, but becoming one with it. The Heavenly Tribulation is not punishment, but the final door the Heavenly Dao opens for you. In the Eighty-One Thunder Tribulations, each thunder contains a thread of Great Dao law. Accept, do not resist—this is the true meaning of Ascension." — The Dao Seed also contains a warning: "The Demon Ancestor is not evil—he is another kind of choice. Ascend or devour—you will face the final choice before the gate."


**Reward**: Taichu's seventh Dao Seed (Tribulation Crossing Check +25), the truth of the Demon Realm


#### Scene 3: Tribulation Crossing—Eighty-One Heavenly Thunders


**Location**: Central Continent · Ascension City · Ascension Platform

**Event**: After returning from the Demon Realm, the player steps onto the Ascension Platform, welcoming the ultimate trial—Tribulation Crossing


**Flow**:

1. **Great Heavenly Tribulation—9 Heavenly Thunders**: — Mahayana→Tribulation Crossing must first pass the "Great Heavenly Tribulation" (大天劫): 6 rounds — Thunder / Fire / Wind / Inner Demon (Dao Heart) / Primordial Unity (all attributes) / Reincarnation (Fortune) — Need ≥4 rounds succeeded

2. **After Tribulation Crossing Breakthrough—Preparing for the Ascension Tribulation**: — After succeeding at Tribulation Crossing, the player enters the Tribulation Crossing stage — At this point the Ascension Platform gives a choice: challenge the Ascension Tribulation immediately, or postpone (up to 100 years)

3. **Ascension Tribulation—9 Rounds of Ultimate Trial** (if the player chooses to challenge immediately):

| Round | Name | Check Attribute | Difficulty |
|------|------|---------|------|
| 1 | First Heavenly Thunder | Physique | d100 ≤ RSS |
| 2 | Second Heavenly Thunder | Spirit Power | d100 ≤ LIL |
| 3 | Third Heavenly Thunder | Divine Sense | d100 ≤ SHS |
| 4 | Heavenly Fire Burning the Body | Physique + Spirit Power | d100 ≤ (RSS+LIL)/2 |
| 5 | Astral Wind Shattering the Soul | Divine Sense + Dao Heart | d100 ≤ (SHS+DAX)/2 |
| 6 | Ultimate Inner Demon Trial | Dao Heart | see below |
| 7 | Spacetime Turbulence (時空亂流) | Comprehension + Agility | d100 ≤ (WUX+SHF)/2 |
| 8 | Gate of Reincarnation (輪迴之門) | Fortune + Dao Heart | d100 ≤ (JIY+DAX)/2 |
| 9 | Heavenly Dao's Question | All Attributes | see below |

**Ultimate Inner Demon Trial (Round 6)**:

- The GM reviews the character's entire experience from Act One to Act Six

- The Inner Demon tests the player with the "other possibility" of every important choice

- Every illusion of "if you had chosen another path back then…"

- Dao Heart Check (d100 ≤ DAX−20), need ≥3 successes

- RP performance may offset the Check difficulty


**Heavenly Dao's Question (Round 9)**:

- The Heavenly Dao itself questions the player—this is the final trial of the Nine Realms to Immortality

- Three questions (the GM may customize based on the character's experience): 1. "What is the Dao?" 2. "What is your Dao?" 3. "If the Great Dao conflicts with all living beings, what will you do?"

- There is no standard answer—the player's answer determines the "Immortal Grade" (仙格) after ascension

- Check: Dao Heart + Fortune + Comprehension, composite Check (d100 ≤ (DAX+JIY+WUX)/3)


**Tribulation Adjustment Factors**:

- Solid Foundation (根基穩固): −10 difficulty

- Merit Protects the Body (功德護體): −5 difficulty (merit of saving the world the player accumulated)

- Cang Wudao's Repentance (if Branch B): Dao Heart Check +20

- Seventh Dao Seed: all rounds Check +5

- Heavy Karmic Debt (業力深重): +20 difficulty (if large-scale slaughter of the innocent)


**Failure Handling**:

- ≥6 rounds succeeded: Ascension succeeds

- 4–5 rounds succeeded: Ascension fails but survives—may choose to remain in the mortal world as a "Loose Immortal" (realm locked at Tribulation Crossing Perfected, Lifespan 30000 years, cannot ascend again)

- ≤3 rounds succeeded: physical body destroyed. May choose Nascent Soul escape to cultivate as a Loose Immortal, or die directly


**Reward**: Ascend to the Immortal Realm (success) / Loose Immortal (partial failure) / character ends (failure)


#### Scene 4: The Awakening of the Demon Ancestor—The Ultimate Duel


**Location**: The boundary between the Demon Realm and the mortal world—the "Chaos Gate" (混沌之門)

**Event**: During the Tribulation Crossing (or after ascending successfully / as a Loose Immortal), the Demon Ancestor finally fully awakens. He tears open the seal and appears above Ascension City.


**Flow**:

1. **Demon Ancestor · Shidao (噬道)**: — The Demon Ancestor's form is a giant humanoid black hole—he has devoured too much, to the point that he himself has become "devouring" itself — "Ten thousand years… I have finally waited for this moment. A cultivator about to ascend—your Ascension Power will be the key to restoring my strength!" — The Demon Ancestor's goal is not to destroy the mortal world—but to devour the player's Ascension Power

2. **Ultimate Boss Battle—Demon Ancestor · Shidao**:

| Boss Attribute | Value |
|----------|------|
| **Name** | Demon Ancestor · Shidao |
| **Realm** | Beyond Tribulation Crossing (Weakened state: Challenge Rating (CR) 24) |
| **Health Points (HP)** | 800 (devoured ten thousand years of power, but weakened by the seal) |
| **Demon Essence** | 600 |
| **Attack Skill** | d100 ≤ 95 (crushing level) |
| **Evasion Skill** | d100 ≤ 85 |

**Phase 1—Devouring Aura (HP 100%–70%)**:

- The Demon Ancestor is surrounded by a "Devouring Aura": each round absorbs 1d20 True Qi from all targets within melee range

- **Touch of Devouring** (Immortal-grade Middle Tier): Melee~Long-range, 6d12 Chaos, absorbs 50% of the damage dealt, converting it to his own Health Points (HP)

- **Law Crush**: those below the Demon Ancestor's realm suffer −20 to all Checks

**Phase 2—Summoning of Ten Thousand Demons (HP 70%–40%)**:

- The Demon Ancestor summons from within himself the afterimages of the powerful existences he has devoured over ten thousand years

- Including: the afterimage of Cang Wudao (if killed), and various strong figures devoured by the Demon Ancestor throughout history

- Each round 1d3 afterimages appear (HP 50–100, Challenge Rating (CR) 8–12)

**Phase 3—Ultimate Devouring (HP 40%–0%)**:

- The Demon Ancestor begins devouring the entire space—the battlefield shrinks by one zone each round

- If the player is devoured: instant death (unless passing a Fortune Check—"not destined to die")

- The only chance of victory: **Ascension Power**—if the player has passed the Ascension Tribulation, they may use the Ascension Power to oppose the devouring

**Ascension Power (Special Mechanic)**:

- If the player ascended successfully: gains "Ascension Power"—×2 damage to all Demon Realm creatures, immune to devouring

- If the player is a Loose Immortal: may use "Heavenly Dao Fragments" to temporarily gain partial Ascension Power (effect halved)

- If the player failed Tribulation Crossing but survived: must rally the power of all Fusion-stage and above cultivators in the mortal world for a "Power of All Living Beings" (衆生之力) opposition

**Tactical Points**:

- This is not a battle in the conventional sense—the Demon Ancestor's devouring can hardly be opposed by conventional means

- The player must use the Ascension Power (or alternative) to engage the Demon Ancestor in a "law-level opposition"

- In this battle, the comprehension of the seventh Dao Seed is crucial: "The Heavenly Tribulation is not punishment, but the door of the Great Dao."—the Ascension Power itself can oppose the Demon Ancestor

3. **Three Endings**:


**Ending A—Ascension Seal**:

- The player defeats the Demon Ancestor with the Ascension Power, then applies the "Heavenly Dao Seal" with the Ascension Power

- The Demon Realm passage is permanently sealed—requires an ascendant to re-reinforce it every ten thousand years

- The player ascends to the Immortal Realm, becoming a new legend of the Hongmeng Continent

- At the final moment of ascension, the player leaves a "Dao Seed"—so that later comers also have a chance to walk the path of the Nine Realms to Immortality

**Ending B—Loose Immortal's Guard**:

- The player gives up Ascension (or chooses to stay after failing Ascension), defeating the Demon Ancestor as a Loose Immortal

- Uses Heavenly Dao Fragments and their own Cultivation to reinforce the seal

- The seal can hold for three thousand years—after three thousand years, a new hero is needed

- The player travels, takes disciples, and guards the continent as a Loose Immortal

- Can never ascend, but becomes the guardian deity of mortal cultivators

**Ending C—Sacrifice**:

- If unable to defeat the Demon Ancestor, the player chooses to detonate the Ascension Platform with all their Cultivation

- The Ascension Platform, having accumulated ten thousand years of Ascension Power, releases it all at once—enough to re-seal the Demon Ancestor for ten thousand years

- The price is the player's complete disappearance (Nascent Soul / Primordial Spirit also dissipates)

- But ten thousand years later, the seal will loosen again…

- The player's name is engraved on the Ascension Platform—becoming a legend looked up to by cultivators of the next ten thousand years


**Reward**: Depends on the ending—ascend to the Immortal Realm / become a Loose Immortal guardian / become an eternal legend


#### Scene 5: Finale—The Dao Seed Endures


**Location**: Ascension City · Ascension Platform (Ending A) / the player's chosen cave dwelling (Ending B)

**Event**: Regardless of the ending, the player has completed the full journey of the "Nine Realms to Immortality"


**Epilogue Narrative**:

- The player's story is written by the "Hearsay Daoist" into the final chapter of the Hearsay Records (風聞錄)

- The great sects engrave the player's name into their sect histories

- All nine of Taichu the True Person's Dao Seeds are found—they now scatter across the Hongmeng Continent, waiting for the next destined person

- The complete system of the Nine Realms to Immortality—from Qi Refining to Tribulation Crossing—becomes more complete because of the player's exploration


**Open Ending**:

- If the player ascended: the GM may describe the first impression of the Immortal Realm—a larger, more complex world awaits

- If the player remained: new talented juniors appear on the continent—they look up to the player's legend and begin their own cultivation paths

- If the player sacrificed: centuries later, a young cultivator discovers a residual Dao Seed fragment on the Ascension Platform…

## A. Major Events of the Cultivation World (20 Major Events of the Past Millennium)


### 1. The Ascension of the Taixu Sword Venerable (approx. 980 years ago)

Taixu the True Person (太虛真人), the previous-previous Sect Master of the Taixu Sword Sect, successfully crossed the tribulation at the Ascension Platform of Ascension City and became the first ascendant in a thousand years. At the moment of ascension, ten-thousand-zhang golden light descended from the Nine Heavens (九天), visible across the entire Central Continent. Before ascending, Taixu the True Person left behind a "Taixu Sword Intent" (太虛劍意) to guard the Sword Sect—to this day no one has fully comprehended this sword intent. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: It is said that when Taixu the True Person ascended, he could not be bothered to seriously face the Eighty-One Heavenly Thunders, only looking back at the mortal world once at the final thunder, sighing "still have attachments"—and then ascended. This person… is probably a thorn in the Immortal Realm too.


### 2. The First Disturbance of the Demon Abyss Seal (approx. 950 years ago)

After nearly ten thousand years of silence, the Demon Abyss seal showed its first significant loosening. Three Fusion-stage grandmasters went to reinforce it, taking ten years, one of whom exhausted himself and perished. After this event, the Demon-Suppressing Hall system was established—five Fusion-stage grandmasters take turns guarding the Demon Abyss. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The fallen grandmaster said only one sentence before death: "It is waking." To this day no one knows who that "It" is.


### 3. The Alchemy Revolution of Danxia Valley (approx. 880 years ago)

Ge Xuanzhen (葛玄真), Sect Master of Danxia Valley, improved the pill formula of the "Foundation Establishment Pill" (築基丹), raising the refining success rate from 30% to 70%, and halving the material cost. This made the Foundation Establishment Pill no longer the exclusive privilege of great families and major sects; loose cultivators and children of small families also had a chance to break through. For this, Ge Xuanzhen was called the "Friend of Loose Cultivators." **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: Old Ge appears selfless on the surface, but behind the scenes he hoarded all the materials for the old formula; once the new formula came out and the old materials' prices crashed—he turned around and earned half a Spirit Vein. A philanthropist and a shrewd merchant can coexist; learn from this, everyone.


### 4. The Emergence of the Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Palace of the Northern Abyss (approx. 800 years ago)

After the Northern Abyss (北冥) glacier melted, a palace ruin from the era of gods and demons was discovered—the "Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Palace" (萬年冰宮). The ruin yielded large numbers of ancient technique fragments and Magic Treasures, among them a broken "Immortal Artifact" (仙器). The ruin's appearance triggered a fifty-year struggle between the Northern Abyss loose cultivators and the Xuanyin Sect. In the end the Xuanyin Sect gained control of the ruin, but at the cost of three Nascent Soul elders perishing. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: It is said that at the deepest part of the Ice Palace is an "Ice Coffin" (冰棺), whose contents remain unopened to this day. The Xuanyin Sect keeps tight-lipped about it.


### 5. The Covenant of the Ten-Thousand Demons Ridge of the Southern Wilderness (approx. 750 years ago)

The Demon Race (妖族) of the Southern Wilderness established a formal tribal alliance at the Ten-Thousand Demons Ridge (萬妖嶺), and sent an envoy to the Central Continent to sign the "Human-Demon Non-Aggression Treaty" (人妖互不侵犯條約) with the Five Sacred Lands. This was the first time in history that human cultivators formally acknowledged the Demon Race's territorial sovereignty. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: On the day of signing, the Taixu Sword Sect sent a Golden Core-stage disciple to sign—while the Demon Race sent a seventh-rank Demon King. This diplomatic etiquette… tsk tsk.


### 6. The Great Uprising of the Eastern Lands Loose Cultivators (approx. 680 years ago)

Dissatisfied with Danxia Valley's monopoly on high-grade pills, the Eastern Lands loose cultivators erupted in a large-scale protest. Thousands of loose cultivators besieged the Danxia Mountains for three months. Ultimately Tianji Manor mediated; Danxia Valley agreed to release a certain quota of low-price pills to the loose cultivator market each year. This event also spurred the institutionalization of the Eastern Lands Loose Cultivator Market. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: I saw with my own eyes the disciples of Danxia Valley trembling behind their mountain gate—alchemists really are not much in combat. But three months later the loose cultivators found they could not do without Danxia Valley's pills either, so both sides found a way down.


### 7. Tianji Manor's "Heavenly Mechanism Ban" (approx. 600 years ago)

After Xuan Jizi, the contemporary Manor Master of Tianji Manor, deduced a Heaven's Mechanism called the "Omen of Destruction" (破滅之兆), he ordered Tianji Manor to seal itself for fifty years, ceasing all divination services to the outside. This caused a huge shock in the cultivation world—all sides speculated what exactly Xuan Jizi had seen. After fifty years the ban was lifted, and Xuan Jizi said not a word about it. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: I spent three hundred years trying to dig out this secret; to this day I only know it relates to the "Nine Realms" (九境) and "Ascension." Xuan Jizi's mouth is tighter than any seal I have ever seen.


### 8. The Great Schism Within Tiandao Sect (approx. 550 years ago)

Tiandao Sect—the continent's oldest sacred land—underwent an internal great schism. Some elders believed Tiandao Sect should actively participate in cultivation-world affairs, while others insisted on the ancient tradition of "transcending the world" (超脫物外). The schism split Tiandao Sect into the "Engaged Faction" (入世派) and the "Withdrawn Faction" (出世派); though not formally split into two sects, the internal differences persist to this day. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The granddaughter of the Engaged Faction leader later married the son of the Wanbao Pavilion Master—giving Wanbao Pavilion a powerful ally within Tiandao Sect. Political marriages work just as well in the cultivation world.


### 9. The Explosion of Wanbao Pavilion's "Heaven-Casting Furnace" (approx. 480 years ago)

The Heaven-Casting Furnace (鑄天爐)—the town-pavilion treasure of Wanbao Pavilion, said to be able to refine half-Immortal Artifacts—exploded while refining a "Pseudo-Immortal Artifact" (僞仙器). The explosion affected a hundred-li radius, leveling half of Heaven-Casting City (鑄天城). It took Wanbao Pavilion three hundred years to rebuild. The cause of the accident remains a mystery—the official story is "unstable materials," but those in the know hint at sabotage. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: It is said that before the explosion, someone saw an "eye" inside the Heaven-Casting Furnace—not a material, but the eye of a living thing. This sounds like the handiwork of the Nether Path, but the Nether Path was not yet active publicly back then.


### 10. The "Both Righteous and Wicked" Controversy of the Xuanyin Sect (approx. 420 years ago)

In a conflict, the Xuanyin Sect used demonic-path techniques to defeat the provocation of a small Righteous Path sect. Afterwards, the Righteous Path sects demanded that the Xuanyin Sect explain its "permission to use demonic-path techniques." Xuanyin Sect Sect Master Leng Yueji (冷月姬) delivered the famous "The Dao Has No Righteous or Wicked" (道無正邪) speech: "Power is a blade; whether it is righteous or wicked is determined by the hand that holds it." The speech won the understanding of some sects, but also deepened the hostility of others. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: Leng Yueji told me privately that she did not care at all about the righteous-wicked debate—she just wanted the Xuanyin Sect to survive. In that godforsaken place, the Northern Abyss, you simply cannot survive without demonic-path techniques.


### 11. The "Beast King Trial" of Barbarian Beast Mountain (approx. 380 years ago)

A seventh-rank ancient exotic beast (equivalent to Fusion stage) appeared deep in the Southern Wilderness. Man Cang (蠻蒼), Sect Master of Barbarian Beast Mountain, went alone deep into the Southern Wilderness, and after three months, subdued the exotic beast through the "Beast King Trial" (獸王試煉). This event catapulted Barbarian Beast Mountain from obscurity to a leader among the middle sects. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: When Man Cang returned, he was covered in wounds, but his eyes were glowing. Someone asked how he subdued it; he said: "It was not I who subdued it, but it that chose me." I do not quite understand this beast-obsession—but it is admirable.


### 12. The Great Fire of the Central Continent Market (approx. 300 years ago)

The largest cultivator trading market of the Central Continent suffered a bizarre great fire—the fire used Spiritual Energy as fuel; the more one used Spirit Power to extinguish it, the fiercer it burned. In the end it was the Formation Array masters of Tianji Manor who drained the Spiritual Energy from the fire scene with a "Spirit-Severing Formation" (絕靈陣) to put it out. Post-incident investigation found the fire was deliberate, but the mastermind was never found. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: Among the shops burned, three happened to have account books recording large numbers of shameful transactions. The fire was no accident—it was destroying evidence.


### 13. The Controversy Over Qingyun Sect's "Teach Without Discrimination" (有教無類) Policy (approx. 250 years ago)

Qingyun Sect formally announced a "Teach Without Discrimination" policy of no apprenticeship fees, no mandatory tasks, and any Spirit Root may join. This measure attracted large numbers of loose cultivators and mortal children, and the Qingyun Sect's disciple count quintupled within ten years. But it also led to severe resource shortages and uneven cultivation quality. Other sects sneered: "Qingyun Sect is not taking disciples, it is running a charity hall." **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: Qingyun Zi's idealism is admirable, but reality is cruel—Qingyun Sect remains a middle sect to this day, largely because its resources are too scattered. That said, their outer disciples are all over the continent, so their intelligence network is actually the strongest.


### 14. The Appearance of Penglai Immortal Island (approx. 200 years ago)

Above the East Sea, the legendary overseas sacred land "Penglai Immortal Island" (蓬萊仙島) took the initiative to establish contact with the continental sects for the first time. Penglai Immortal Island sent an envoy to participate in the sect grand competition at the Central Continent's Debate Platform, demonstrating a cultivation method completely different from the continental system—"Entering the Dao through the Sea" (以海入道). **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The Penglai envoy was an old man who spoke with a fishy smell, but his strength—Spirit Transformation Perfected, half a step from Void Refining. The unfathomable depth of overseas cultivators may be no less than that of the continent.


### 15. The Expansion of the Western Regions' Spiritless Lands (approx. 150 years ago)

The Western Regions' "Spiritless Lands" (絕靈之地)—areas polluted by demonic energy where cultivation is impossible—have expanded by about thirty percent over the past hundred and fifty years. This means more and more Western Regions land is becoming barren wasteland where one cannot cultivate. The Loose Cultivator Alliance has repeatedly appealed to the sacred lands for help, but the response has been limited. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The expansion rate of the Spiritless Lands is directly proportional to the degree of loosening of the Demon Abyss seal. If you notice someone around you hoarding Western Regions land deeds—they definitely know something.


### 16. The Nether Path's First Public Activity (approx. 100 years ago)

A mysterious organization calling itself the "Nether Path" (幽冥道) first became publicly active in the cultivation world. Initially it mainly ran underground auctions, intelligence trading, and forbidden-technique sales. The great sects did not at first regard it as a threat—until they later discovered the Nether Path had developed large numbers of secret members within each sect. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The Nether Path's infiltration ability far exceeded the sects' imagination. Three of my own informants in the intelligence network were confirmed to be Nether Path moles. This organization is far older than we thought—its roots may trace back to the Immortal-Demon War ten thousand years ago.


### 17. The Discovery of Taichu's Secondary Manor (approx. 80 years ago—but not deeply explored then)

Qingyun Sect disciple Liu Yunfei (柳雲飛) discovered a ruin suspected to be Taichu the True Person's cultivation cave at the northern foot of the Qingyun Mountains. But after contacting forbidden corpse-dao (屍道) necromancy within the ruin, he was expelled from the sect, and exploration of the ruin was halted. Fifty years later, this ruin was rediscovered during the "Greenstone Town Anomaly" (青石鎮異變) event. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: Liu Yunfei is a tragedy. He was not bad at heart, just too eager to prove himself. In an environment like Qingyun Sect's "Teach Without Discrimination," mediocre disciples are often more anxious than geniuses.


### 18. Confirmation of the Rising Phase of the Spiritual Energy Tide (approx. 60 years ago)

Tianji Manor formally confirmed that the current period is the rising phase of the millennium Spiritual Energy Tide. This means that over the coming decades to centuries, Spiritual Energy concentration will continue to rise, Secret Realm entrances will open more easily, and heavenly materials and treasures will emerge in large numbers. After this news was announced, the sects began actively expanding—the frequency of Secret Realm exploration and Spirit Vein contests increased significantly. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: The Spiritual Energy Tide is a double-edged sword. More Spiritual Energy does aid cultivation, but more Secret Realms also means more danger. The last rising phase of the Spiritual Energy Tide—that is, a thousand years ago—saw the first disturbance of the Demon Abyss seal. Ponder it yourselves.


### 19. The Wounding of the East Tower Guardian of the Demon-Suppressing Hall (approx. 10 years ago)

One of the five guardians of the Demon Abyss seal—Zhendong the True Lord (Fusion Middle)—was attacked by an unknown force during a seal disturbance and has lain gravely wounded and unconscious ever since. The Demon-Suppressing Hall was forced to issue an urgent plea to the sects. This event is regarded as the beginning of the Demon Abyss seal entering its "danger period." **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: As Zhendong the True Lord lay unconscious, he repeatedly muttered one word—"Shidao" (噬道). To my knowledge, no known Demon Realm creature or Demon Cultivator is called "Shidao." This means… something on the other side of the seal is something we have never seen.


### 20. The Greenstone Town Anomaly and the Beginning of the "Chronicle of the Nine Realms" (Recent)

A Qi-Refining cultivator resolved the corpse-puppet (屍傀) incident in Greenstone Town (青石鎮) and rediscovered Taichu's Secondary Manor. This event was recorded by the Hearsay Daoist, who opened a new chapter in the Hearsay Records—"Contemporary Annals" (當代紀事). A small town's anomaly may become the first domino that changes the fate of the Hongmeng Continent. **Hearsay Daoist's Note**: I have seen too many "small events" become "great history." These few young people of Greenstone Town—if they are still alive—perhaps one day what I write will not be "Greenstone Town Anomaly," but "Chronicle of the Nine Realms" (九境登仙錄).

## B. Gossip About the Major Sect Powers


### Taixu Sword Sect


**Public Image**: Sacred land of the sword dao, the holy temple in the hearts of sword cultivators across the world. Cold, proud, and arrogant beyond compare.


**Private Gossip**:

1. **The Sword Venerable's Regret**: When Dugu Wuji was young, he had a Dao Companion (道侶)—a female cultivator of mediocre aptitude with a Water Spirit Root (水靈根). During the thirty years Dugu Wuji spent in seclusion assaulting the Mahayana stage, her Lifespan was exhausted and she passed away. Upon emerging from seclusion and learning this, Dugu Wuji sat alone in the heavy snow for seven days and nights. It is said that for those seven days, the snow within a hundred li of Taixu Mountain was black. Since then he has never smiled at anyone—becoming "naturally cold-faced."

2. **Internal Factions—"Sword-Purity Faction" (劍純派) vs "Sword-Utility Faction" (劍用派)**: The Sword-Purity Faction believes the sword dao should be pure—abandoning all non-sword techniques and Magic Treasures; the Sword-Utility Faction believes the sword is a tool, and Magic Treasures and pills may all serve the sword dao. The struggle between the two factions has lasted a thousand years. The current Sect Master Dugu Wuji belongs to the Sword-Purity Faction, but the Vice-Sect Master (also his junior fellow disciple) belongs to the Sword-Utility Faction. What outsiders do not know is that the leader of the Sword-Utility Faction has been secretly refining a "sword that is not a sword"—a new type of Magic Treasure fusing Wanbao Pavilion technology with the Taixu Sword Intent.


### Danxia Valley


**Public Image**: Foremost in alchemy, compassionate in relieving the world. The cultivation world's "pharmacy" and "hospital."


**Private Gossip**:

1. **The Pill King's Ambition**: Ge Xuanzhen publicly claims no interest in power, but in fact he is secretly pushing

for Danxia Valley to be upgraded from an "upper sect" to a "sacred land." To achieve this goal, he needs to refine a fifth-grade-or-above pill to prove Danxia Valley's strength—a pill considered "impossible to refine in the mortal world": the "Immortal Spirit Pill" (仙靈丹, which lets a Nascent Soul cultivator directly skip a minor stage). According to insiders, he has failed 37 times, but the 38th experiment is underway.

2. **The Pill-Formula Black Market**: Danxia Valley publicly forbids leaking pill formulas, but in fact has an internal black market—Legacy Disciples and elders privately trade "unofficial" pill formulas. The most valuable is the improved formula for the "Face-Preserving Pill" (駐顏丹)—said to let a cultivator permanently keep the appearance they had at breakthrough. This formula is monopolized by a female elder, who earns tens of thousands of Spirit Stones from it each year.


### Wanbao Pavilion


**Public Image**: Foremost in artifact refining, honest with all, young and old alike. "Every Magic Treasure, lifetime responsibility."


**Private Gossip**:

1. **The Truth of the Heaven-Casting Furnace Explosion**: The Heaven-Casting Furnace explosion four hundred eighty years ago was no accident, but a victim of internal power struggles within Wanbao Pavilion. At the time, an elder tampered with the refining of a rival to eliminate him. That rival perished in the explosion, but his remnant soul (his Nascent Soul-stage Primordial Spirit escaped) still lurks somewhere in Heaven-Casting City to this day—occasionally possessing artifact-refining apprentices and altering their work.

2. **The Illegitimate Son of Gongshu Ye (公輸冶)**: The current Sect Master Gongshu Ye had a romantic indiscretion in his youth—he had an affair with the daughter of a mortal blacksmith. It is said she bore him a son, but Gongshu Ye never publicly acknowledged it. That child is now a Golden Core-stage cultivator—working in a small artifact-refining workshop under Wanbao Pavilion… under a surname not Gongshu's. Someone has seen Gongshu Ye appear late at night outside that artifact-refining workshop, watch from afar for a while, then leave.

### Tianji Manor (天機府)



**Public Image**: Number one in Formation Arrays, neutral and impartial. A cold, mysterious organization that "calculates all of Heaven's Mechanism."

**Private Gossip**:

1. **Xuanji Zi's (玄機子) Secret**: The current Manor Master of Tianji Manor, Xuanji Zi, deduced a Heaven's Mechanism that is unfavorable to himself — his lifespan is only eighty years remaining. For a Fusion-realm cultivator (lifespan 8,000 years), this is almost equivalent to "dying tomorrow." He tells no one this secret, but ever since then he has begun frantically cultivating successors — he selected three disciples and separately imparted to each the three core secret arts of Tianji Deduction. No one masters all three at once.

2. **Tianji Manor's Business Acumen**: Tianji Manor professes "neutrality" on the surface, but in reality two-thirds of every sect's Mountain-Protecting Grand Array have a built-in Tianji Manor "remote shutdown" backdoor. Tianji Manor publicly insists this is a "technical channel required for maintenance," but rumors say: if one day some sect offends Tianji Manor, their Mountain-Protecting Grand Array might "happen to need maintenance" at a critical moment.



### Green Cloud Sect (青雲門)



**Public Image**: A righteous and proper sect, teaching without discrimination, charging no apprenticeship fees. A paradise for loose cultivators.

**Private Gossip**:

1. **Qingyun Zi's Economic Crisis**: The "teach without discrimination" policy keeps Green Cloud Sect's spirit-stone reserves perpetually hovering on the edge of bankruptcy. Qingyun Zi · Su Daoxuan (青雲子·蘇道玄) must personally go to the Central Continent market each month to "beg for alms" — selling the employment services of Green Cloud Sect disciples to various powers. His most awkward moment was when an outer disciple of Danxia Valley recognized him, and that disciple said: "Sect Master Su, you still haven't repaid last month's loan for pill-refining materials." They say Qingyun Zi's expression at that moment was uglier than surviving a tribulation.

2. **The Inside Story of the Han Feng Incident**: The betrayal of the Foundation Establishment traitor Han Feng (Act I Boss) was not an isolated case. Because Green Cloud Sect has a low entry threshold, over the past fifty years at least a dozen disciples have been turned by outside powers. Qingyun Zi knows this, but he believes "rather than closing the door, open it even wider" — letting more disciples receive righteous education is the fundamental way to resist the temptation of the demonic path. The effect of this strategy — is currently still questionable.



### Profound Yin Sect (玄陰宗)



**Public Image**: A gray-path sect that is both righteous and evil. Guardian of the bitter cold lands of Beiming.

**Private Gossip**:

1. **Leng Yueji's (冷月姬) Past**: Leng Yueji, the Sect Master of Profound Yin Sect, was in her youth an outer disciple of the Taixu Sword Sect. She was eliminated because her Spirit Root attributes were unsuited to the sword dao, and in a fit of anger went to Beiming and founded Profound Yin Sect. To this day there remains a file on her in the Taixu Sword Sect, reading "mediocre talent, not worth cultivating." Leng Yueji had a copy of this file framed and hung in her cave dwelling — to look at once before cultivating each day.

2. **The Secret of the Demonic Alliance**: Profound Yin Sect publicly "allows cultivation of demonic arts but forbids wanton killing," but in reality several elders within the sect maintain secret contact with the Netherworld Dao. Leng Yueji knows of this — she chooses not to interfere, because she too has planted her own informants within the Netherworld Dao. This is a dangerous balancing game — Profound Yin Sect is simultaneously suspected by the righteous path and tested by the demonic path.



### Savage Beast Mountain (蠻獸山)



**Public Image**: A beast-taming sect, symbiotically coexisting with demonic beasts. Guardian of the southern wilderness jungles.

**Private Gossip**:

1. **Man Cang's (蠻蒼) Secret — He Is Not Pure-Human**: It is said that Man Cang, the Sect Master of Savage Beast Mountain, has within him a trace of ancient exotic-beast bloodline. This explains why he can "resonate" with that seventh-rank exotic beast. Man Cang has never publicly acknowledged this, but those who know his secret have noticed: when enraged, his pupils briefly turn into vertical slits.

2. **The Ambiguous Relationship with Wanyao Ridge**: On the surface, Savage Beast Mountain and the demon tribe alliance of Wanyao Ridge in the southern wilderness maintain a "mutual non-aggression" peaceful relationship. But privately, each year Savage Beast Mountain ships a certain amount of "spirit-beast rations" to Wanyao Ridge — in exchange for the demons not attacking Savage Beast Mountain's gathering teams in the southern wilderness. Some righteous sects accuse Savage Beast Mountain of "tribute-paying"; Man Cang's response is: "This is not tribute-paying, this is — good-neighborly friendship."



### Loose Cultivator Alliance (散修聯盟)



**Public Image**: A loose mutual-aid organization of free cultivators. The backbone of the western-region loose cultivators.

**Private Gossip**:

1. **The "Dictator" Within the Alliance**: Although called an "alliance," the Loose Cultivator Alliance is in fact controlled by a three-person core group — Alliance Master "Iron-Mask Wanderer" (Void Refining Early), Vice Alliance Master "Mobei Blade Wanderer" (Golden Core Perfected), and Strategist "Poison Lady" (Nascent Soul Late). These three control eighty percent of the alliance's resource allocation. The lower-ranked loose cultivators have much to complain about but no power to resist — after all, the alliance is their only

mainstay in the western regions.

2. **Deals with the Netherworld Dao**: Some mid-level cadres of the Loose Cultivator Alliance secretly deal with the Netherworld Dao — trading information and materials for demonic-path techniques. In the western regions, where the Spirit-Exhausted Land keeps expanding, more and more loose cultivators are beginning to think "demonic arts at least work, better than waiting to die." The alliance leadership is fully aware of this trend but powerless to stop it.

## C. Gossip on Famous Cultivators (15 People)



### 1. Dugu Wuji (獨孤無極) (Taixu Sword Sect Master, Mahayana Early)

**Dao Title**: Sword Venerable  
**Realm**: Mahayana Early

**Anecdotes**:

In his youth, Dugu Wuji was a chatterbox — yes, you read that right. His current cold face was cultivated later. According to his fellow disciples back then, when Dugu Wuji was at the Foundation Establishment stage he got so excited at the sect's Grand Tournament that after knocking his opponent's sword flying he launched into an impromptu speech — half a shichen (two hours) on "the sword dao and life." Later his master truly could not bear it and punished him with ten years of wall-contemplation — no speaking allowed. From then on he became what he is now. His former Dao companion (that water-Spirit-Root female cultivator) said while alive: "I still preferred the talkative you of before." For those words, he sat alone in the heavy snow for seven days and seven nights.



### 2. Ge Xuanzhen (葛玄真) (Danxia Valley Master, Spirit Transformation Late)

**Dao Title**: Pill King  
**Realm**: Spirit Transformation Late

**Anecdotes**:

Ge Xuanzhen has a hobby: undercover incognito visits. Every few years he dresses as an ordinary Qi-Refining loose cultivator and wanders the various markets. Once at a Dongtu loose-cultivator bazaar he saw someone selling fake pills — in his name. Ge Xuanzhen on the spot pointed out the pills were fake; the other mocked him: "What would a Qi-Refining loose cultivator like you know of the Pill King?" Ge Xuanzhen smiled faintly and demonstrated the pill-refining process for the same pill on the spot — taking only the time of one cup of tea. That fake-pill peddler knelt on the spot. From then on, a legend appeared in the Dongtu markets: "the Pill King's Shadow" — an apparently ordinary loose cultivator who happens to appear precisely when you most need pill knowledge.



### 3. Gongshu Ye (公輸冶) (Wanbao Pavilion Master, Spirit Transformation Middle)

**Dao Title**: Artifact Venerable  
**Realm**: Spirit Transformation Middle

Every true-inheritance disciple of Wanbao Pavilion, on their first Spirit Artifact refinement, Gongshu Ye makes them touch this scar. His mortal-born illegitimate son has the same scar at the same spot — the brand of a blacksmith's career. Every time Gongshu Ye sees that scar, he falls silent for a long while.

They say every time the Taixu Sword Sect elders receive the flowers they collectively fall silent for the time of one incense stick.



### 5. Qingyun Zi · Su Daoxuan (青雲子·蘇道玄) (Green Cloud Sect Master, Nascent Soul Late)

**Dao Title**: Qingyun Zi  
**Realm**: Nascent Soul Late

Su Daoxuan is the poorest sect master on the entire continent — an open secret. No one exposed him — because everyone did rather admire the thickness of his face.

## D. Regional Introductions and Information



### Central Continent (中州) — Core of Cultivation Civilization

**Spiritual Energy Concentration**: Highest on the continent (location of the Dragon Vein Main Root, average concentration 3×)  
**Specialties**: Spirit veins, technique secret manuals, cultivation talent  
**Major Powers**:

Taixu Sword Sect (Holy Land), Tiandao Sect (Holy Land), Wanbao Pavilion (Upper Sect), Tianji Manor (Upper Sect)

**Recent Developments**:

- The Debate Platform is preparing the next Grand Tournament; sects have begun (secretly) jockeying for position
- Abnormal spiritual-energy fluctuations at the Ascension Platform of Ascension City — possibly related to Heaven's Dao decline
- A batch of "demonic artifacts" of unknown origin appeared in the Central Continent market — (suspected) the Netherworld Dao testing market reaction

**Dangerous Areas / Forbidden Lands**:

- **Dao Ruins (Legend)**: Where the Dao Ancestor became the Dao; location unknown, no one can reach it
- **Beneath Ascension City**: Legend says the place closest to the Immortal Realm — also the place closest to the "Heaven's Dao Core." Entry forbidden below Mahayana
- **Central Continent Underground Dark River**: Where the Dragon Vein Main Root lies; extremely dense spiritual energy but full of natural prohibitions

**Loose Cultivator Survival Guide**:

- Central Continent has the most resources but also the fiercest competition. Loose cultivators are advised to join a market guild or find a great sect as backing
- In the market there is a "Loose Cultivator Mutual-Aid Society" — membership fee is not low but at least guarantees you won't be bullied
- Don't draw your sword on Taixu Sword Sect territory — Sword Sect disciples are very sensitive to "displaying sword qi without permission"



### Dongtu (東土) — Alchemy Holy Land and Overseas Immortal Mountains

**Spiritual Energy Concentration**: Relatively high (wood/water Spirit-Vein enriched, average 2×)  
**Specialties**: Spirit medicines, pills, loose-cultivator culture  
**Major Powers**: Danxia Valley (Upper Sect), Green Cloud Sect (Middle Sect), Penglai Immortal Island (Overseas Holy Land)

**Recent Developments**:

- Danxia Valley is secretly advancing a plan to elevate to Holy Land — needs to refine a fifth-grade pill
- Green Cloud Sect is actively seeking new spirit veins to ease resource pressure
- A new power appeared in the Dongtu loose-cultivator bazaar — self-styled "East Sea Merchant Guild," (suspected) a commercial organ of Penglai Immortal Island

**Dangerous Areas / Forbidden Lands**:

- **Depths of the Hundred Herbs Mountain Range**: Abundant wild spirit medicines but also high-rank demonic beasts
- **Black Pine Ridge and Surroundings**: After the Liu Yunfei incident, yin energy in some areas has not fully dissipated
- **East Sea Abyss**: Entrance to the Immortal Realm trial path — entry forbidden below Spirit Transformation

**Loose Cultivator Survival Guide**:

- Dongtu is the most loose-cultivator-friendly region — Green Cloud Sect teaches without discrimination, Danxia Valley has abundant pills
- Recommended to master a pill-refining or herb-gathering skill — this is the main livelihood of Dongtu loose cultivators
- The cultivation resources of the East Sea cannot be ignored — Penglai Immortal Island's influence is expanding toward the continent



### Western Regions (西域) — Border of the Demon Abyss and Land of Austerity

**Spiritual Energy Concentration**: Low and unstable (polluted by Demon Abyss demonic energy, some areas are Spirit-Exhausted Lands)  
**Specialties**: Ancient-battlefield artifact fragments, demonic-path techniques, ascetic talent  
**Major Powers**: Demon-Suppressing Hall (Holy Land直属/directly subordinate), Loose Cultivator Alliance, various ascetic minor sects

**Recent Developments**:

- The Demon Abyss seal keeps loosening — Zhendong True Lord (鎮東真君) gravely wounded, not yet recovered
- The Spirit-Exhausted Land accelerates its expansion — the Loose Cultivator Alliance has sent its fifth appeal for help to the Holy Land
- Netherworld Dao activity frequency in the western regions has surged — possibly preparing for the "Opening-Heaven Plan"
- A batch of new immortal-artifact fragments discovered in ancient-battlefield ruins — triggering a new wave of exploration

**Dangerous Areas / Forbidden Lands**:

- **Demon Abyss**: The continent's most dangerous forbidden land, guarded by five Fusion grandmasters, but the seal keeps loosening
- **Spirit-Exhausted Land**: Cannot cultivate; demons and demonic cultivators active
- **Ancient Battlefield Core Zone**: Residual prohibitions and curses of the Immortal-Demon War — scavengers beware

**Loose Cultivator Survival Guide**:

- The western regions are the hardest region for loose cultivators — thin spiritual energy + demonic-energy pollution
- Recommended to join the Loose Cultivator Alliance — surviving alone in the western regions is nearly impossible
- Ancient-battlefield scavenging can earn big money but is extremely risky — hundreds of loose cultivators vanish there each year
- Maintain good relations with the Demon-Suppressing Hall — they are the only order-keepers in the western regions



### Beiming (北冥) — Secrets of the Extreme Cold Lands

**Spiritual Energy Concentration**: Medium but special (extremely heavy yin energy, abundant moonlight spiritual energy)  
**Specialties**: Yin-attribute technique materials, ice-attribute heavenly treasures, ancient ruins  
**Major Powers**: Profound Yin Sect (Middle Sect), Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Palace (occupier of ancient ruins), various exiled loose cultivators

**Recent Developments**:

- Abnormal tremors from the "Ice Coffin" of the Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Palace — Profound Yin Sect urgently reinforced guards
- The Polar Night Secret Realm will open in three years — sects have begun contesting entry quotas
- Some "abnormal" freezing phenomena appeared at the edge of Beiming — possibly related to an ancient existence sealed in ice

**Dangerous Areas / Forbidden Lands**:

- **Deep Layers of the Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Palace**: Where the Ice Coffin lies — Profound Yin Sect strictly forbids outsiders approaching
- **Polar Night Secret Realm**: Opens during each three-year polar night — opportunity and danger coexist
- **Beneath the Beiming Ice**: Legend says a frozen city from the Divine-Demonic Era — of ten explorers, none return

**Loose Cultivator Survival Guide**:

- Beiming suits those cultivating yin-attribute techniques — cultivators of other attributes will suffer

## B. Combat Round Structure Quick Reference

|-----------|--------|
| 1× | **Main Action** — Martial Technique / Divine Ability / Artifact Activation / Full Defense / Array Setup |
| 1× | **Move Action** — Cross 1 zone / lateral zone-switch / break from melee (needs check) |
| 1× | **Auxiliary Action** — Switch Weapon / Take Pill / Pinch Talisman / Divine Sense Scan / Command Spirit Beast |
| Unlimited | **Free Action** — Speak / Activate Inner Art / Qi Circulation |
| 1× per round | **Reaction Action** — Evasion / Block / Body-Burst / Substitute Technique |

**Defense Flow**:

1. Attacker declares → rolls Attack Check
2. Critical Success → defender cannot Evade, only relies on body-protecting qi

4. No reaction → passive defense = RSS/5 + Armor + Inner Art passive

**Initiative Modifiers**: Ambush +30 / Gravely Wounded −20 / Critical Success → extra auxiliary + action order +1 on first round / Critical Failure → last + lose auxiliary
