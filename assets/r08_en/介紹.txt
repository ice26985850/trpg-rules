Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart — A Hongmeng Continent Xianxia TRPG

Have you ever wondered: if there truly were a Great Dao leading to immortality, would you choose to take up the sword and slay demons, or burn incense and inquire of the Way? Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart is a tabletop role-playing game (TRPG) set in an Eastern xianxia (cultivation) world. Here you are no spectator reading a story — you are a cultivator seeking the Dao upon the Hongmeng Continent, starting as a mere Qi Refining mortal and step by step knocking upon the heavens, until you Cross the Tribulation and Ascend.

WORLDVIEW

The Hongmeng Continent was born after the Dao Ancestor dissolved into the Dao; spiritual energy (the lingering resonance of the Great Dao between heaven and earth) nourishes all living things. Cultivators advance tier by tier along the staircase of the "Nine Realms to Immortality": Qi Refining, Foundation Establishment, Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Spirit Severing, Void Refining, Unity, Mahayana, and Tribulation Crossing. In the present age, the seal left by the Immortal-Demon War ten millennia ago is loosening, a demonic tide is about to rise, and secret realms appear one after another — a time when winds and clouds converge and heroes emerge in multitudes.

WHO DO YOU PLAY? WHAT DO YOU PLAY?

You play a cultivator just beginning the path. The core gameplay revolves around the word "cultivation": choose a Spirit Root, practice a Cultivation Technique, raid secret realms, refine pills and forge artifacts, and seek out a famed master to take you as a disciple (the game features seven legendary figures you may apprentice under, each with their own unique lineage and trials). Whether you wish to become a sword cultivator whose single strike astounds the immortals, an alchemist who delves deep into the Dao of Pills, or a strategist who commands formations — it is entirely your choice.

SYSTEM HIGHLIGHTS

First, an original d100 percentage check: roll a hundred-sided die; if the result does not exceed the Target Number (TN), you succeed, and by Margin of Success (MoS) the outcome is graded into five tiers — Minor, Major, Perfect, Transcendent, and Heavenly. The lower you roll, the more dazzling your performance.

Second, the trinity of Cultivation Techniques: three great categories — Inner Arts, Martial Techniques, and Divine Abilities — with over fifty schools in total, freely combined to forge a combat style uniquely your own. A low roll may also trigger an "Opportunity" critical success, or a "Qi Deviation" critical failure, making every throw pound with excitement.

Third, the Master-Disciple Inheritance system: from the entry trial to the "Robe-and-Bowl Trial," the master-disciple interplay runs through the entire growth journey, so the character is not merely numbers, but a path of cultivation.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Players who enjoy Eastern fantasy, savor character progression and technique-building, and delight in making trade-offs within the story will fall in love with it. Whether you are a strategy-minded tactician who prefers tactical combat, or a storytelling type immersed in plot and relationships, you can find your own Dao. Basic TRPG experience is helpful, but the core rules are clear, and newcomers can pick it up step by step.
