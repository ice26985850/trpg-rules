# Volume I: Chronicle of the Nine Realms — Complete Adventure Epic

> **Total Span**: full coverage of levels 1-36 | **Acts**: 6 acts | **Span per Act**: about 6 levels (one major realm)

> **Design Philosophy**: from the mortal fear of Qingshi Town (青石鎮) to the ultimate choice of the ascension heavenly tribulation, let the player feel "qualitative change" at every realm.

## Act I: Mortal Awakening

**Suitable Level**: Qi Refining 1-4 → Breakthrough to Foundation Establishment

**Core Theme**: The true crossing from mortal to cultivator

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 1-2 years

### Plot Summary

After the battle at Qingshi Town, the player obtains from the corpse of the zombie puppet · Liu Yunfei his Qingyun Sect disciple token and last will. Whatever the player's origin, Liu Yunfei's story points in the same direction — the Qingyun Sect (青雲門). Following this clue, the player travels to the Qingyun Mountains of the Eastern Lands (東土) and sets foot on the true path of cultivation.

The Qingyun Sect is holding its quinquennial "Ascension Convention" — a public exam to recruit new disciples from outside. This is the golden chance for rogue cultivators and children of small families to enter the orthodox cultivation system. At the convention the player undergoes Spirit Root testing, Dao Heart trial, and practical sparring, and formally becomes an outer disciple of the Qingyun Sect (or keeps their original sect affiliation while building ties with the Qingyun Sect).

After entry, the player is assigned their first sect mission: investigate the recent abnormal demonic-beast disturbances at the "Mistveil Valley" (霧隱谷) at the northern foot of the Qingyun Mountains. This seemingly simple task leads the player to an ancient ruin — the "Taichu Hermitage" (太初別府), said to be the cultivation cave dwelling of the first human immortal, Taichu True Person (太初真人), during his Qi Refining days. Hidden within the cave dwelling is the first fragment of the secret of "Nine Realms to Immortality" — the **"Secret of the Dao Foundation."**

However, the Qingyun Sect is not monolithic. An inner disciple at the Foundation Establishment stage (a traitor) has secretly colluded with outside forces, intending to monopolize the treasures of the Taichu Hermitage. The player must defeat him in a duel, protect the ruin's secret, and complete the breakthrough from Qi Refining to Foundation Establishment in the process.

**End of Act**: The player breaks through to Foundation Establishment, truly stepping through the great gate of the cultivation world.

### Key Scenes

#### Scene 1: Ascension Convention — Spirit Root Platform

**Location**: Qingyun Sect mountain-gate plaza, a giant "Spirit Root Stele" stands at the center

**Event**: Thousands of Dao-seekers gather here; the Qingyun Sect's Dharma-Transmission Elder presides over the Ascension Convention

**Flow**:
1. **Spirit Root Test**: Place hand on the Spirit Root Stele; the stele lights up in the corresponding Spirit Root color — Check: no check required; Spirit Root is determined at character creation — Narrative focus: GM describes the stele's reaction based on Spirit Root quality — those with Heavenly Spirit Root make the stele blaze brilliantly, the crowd in an uproar; those with Low-grade Spirit Root have a dim glow, drawing whispers.
2. **Dao Heart Inquiry**: The Dharma-Transmission Elder asks each three questions — "Why cultivate the Dao?" — "If the Great Dao is hopeless, what will you do?" — "What are you willing to sacrifice for the Dao?" — Check: Dao Heart (DAX) Check (d100 ≤ DAX); success earns the elder's approving nod.
3. **Entry Spar**: Random pairing, first touch ends the bout — Check: best of three, using Martial Technique contests — Example opponent: another Qi Refining early-stage Dao-seeker (HP 25, attack skill value 35)

**Key NPCs**:
- Dharma-Transmission Elder · Qingxu True Person (清虛真人, late Nascent Soul) — the most respected elder of the Qingyun Sect, eyes like torches
- Various Dao-seekers — future sect siblings, rivals, or friends

**Reward**: Qingyun Sect outer-disciple status, Qingyun Sect Dao robe, Spirit Stones ×20, "Qingyun Breathing Art" (Mortal-grade upper-stage Inner Art)

#### Scene 2: Mistveil Valley — Demonic Beast Disturbance

**Location**: Mistveil Valley at the northern foot of the Qingyun Mountains, shrouded in mist year-round

**Event**: The player's first task as an outer disciple — investigate the recent anomaly of demonic beasts leaving their habitats and attacking mortal villages

**Flow**:
1. **Investigate Demonic Beast Tracks**: — Divine Sense (SHS) Check (d100 ≤ SHS) reveals the beast tracks are abnormally chaotic, not foraging behavior, more like... flight — Spirit Power (LIL) Perception Check (d100 ≤ LIL) discovers the valley's Spiritual Energy flows in one direction — deep in the valley floor
2. **Encounter A — Startled Demonic Beast Pack**: — 3-5 first-stage demonic beasts "Mist Wolves" flee from the valley floor — combat or soothe (Beast-Taming Check) — each Mist Wolf: Health Points (HP) 15, Attack 1d6+1, hit rate 40%
3. **Encounter B — Black Shadow in the Mist**: — a mysterious black-robed figure appears from the valley-floor direction — if the player challenges, the black-robed figure says nothing, releases a black mist, then vanishes — Spirit Power (LIL) Perception Check (d100 ≤ LIL-10) identifies a faint "restriction" aura within the black mist

**Key NPC**: Black-robed figure (true identity: Qingyun Sect inner disciple · Han Feng (韓楓), early Foundation Establishment, already bought off by demonic-path forces)

**Reward**: Sect Contribution Points +50, Spirit Stones ×30, outer-disciple monthly stipend increased

#### Scene 3: Taichu Hermitage — Ruin Entrance

**Location**: Floor of Mistveil Valley, an ancient stone gate covered in vines

**Event**: Tracking the source of the Spiritual Energy disturbance, discover an ancient ruin hidden in the mountainside

**Flow**:
1. **Crack the Stone Gate Restriction**: — faint inscription on the gate: "Dwelling of Taichu True Person in his Qi Refining days; later comers knock with Spirit Power" — Spirit Power (LIL) Check (d100 ≤ LIL) activates the gate — or Comprehension (WUX) Check (d100 ≤ WUX) to understand the inscription's meaning
2. **Ruin Antechamber**: — Taichu True Person's cultivation notes carved on the walls — Comprehension (WUX) Check (d100 ≤ WUX) lets you grasp this immortal's unique understanding of the "Dao Foundation" during his Qi Refining days — "The Dao Foundation is not fixed; the secret of the Nine Realms to Immortality lies in re-cultivating the foundation at every realm" — this is the first fragment of the great secret of "Nine Realms to Immortality"
3. **Discover Signs of Disturbance**: — Divine Sense (SHS) Check (d100 ≤ SHS) reveals someone arrived before the player — some items taken, but the deepest chamber's mechanism is intact — footprints point to the chamber deep in the ruin

**Reward**: First fragment of "Secret of the Dao Foundation" (permanent passive: +5 to Breakthrough Check when advancing from Qi Refining to Foundation Establishment)

#### Scene 4: Chamber Duel — Traitor Han Feng

**Location**: The deepest chamber of the Taichu Hermitage

**Event**: Tracking the intruder into the chamber, discover Qingyun Sect inner disciple Han Feng attempting to forcibly crack the seal at the chamber's center

**Flow**:
1. **Confrontation**: — after being exposed, Han Feng sneers: "You think the Qingyun Sect is some pure land? This world is changing; the Demon Abyss seal won't hold much longer. Smart people should pick a side early." — the player may choose to persuade (Social Check) or act directly

#### Scene 5: Dao Foundation Formed — Foundation Establishment Breakthrough

**Location**: Beside the Spirit Spring in the Taichu Hermitage chamber (Spiritual Energy concentration ×2) or return to the Qingyun Sect

**Event**: Using the insight from Taichu's manuscript and the aid of the Foundation Establishment Pill, the player attempts the Foundation Establishment breakthrough

**Flow**:
1. **Preparation Stage**: — may take the Foundation Establishment Pill (must be refined first or purchased from Crimson Clouds Valley (丹霞谷); if not refined, Breakthrough Check −20) — meditate three days (cultivation Check) — may choose a guardian companion

In that instant, you feel your entire meridian network washed anew, physique and Spiritual Energy merging as one. This is Foundation Establishment — the true starting point of cultivation." — failure: cultivation reverts to Qi Refining Perfected; must cool down one month and retry

**Reward**: Breakthrough to Early Foundation Establishment (Lv.5), all attributes +5, free attribute points +5, Health Points (HP) +20, Qi +30, lifespan 150→250

#### Scene 6: Sect Merit Review — End of Act I

**Reward**: inner-disciple status, monthly Spirit Stones ×100, full "Qingyun Carefree Art" (Earth-grade mid-stage Inner Art), Taichu Dao Seed (carried, Dao Heart (DAX) +2)

## Act II: The Choice of the Dao

**Suitable Level**: Foundation Establishment 5-8 → Breakthrough to Golden Core

**Core Theme**: Choose your Dao — righteous/demonic/neutral, sect affiliation, cultivation direction

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 3-5 years

### Plot Summary

After successful Foundation Establishment, the player faces a far broader cultivation world. The Qingyun Sect nominates the player to participate in the Zhongzhou Debate Terrace's centennial "Sect Grand Tournament" — not only an honor battle, but a key event deciding the sect's resource allocation for the next century. Meanwhile, demonic-path infiltration becomes obvious: a secret organization calling itself the "Netherworld Dao" (幽冥道) is quietly recruiting disciples from the great sects.

During preparations for the tournament, the player accidentally contacts the "Five Elements Secret Realm" (五行祕境) — a cultivation holy land that opens once every hundred years, said to contain the origin power of the Five Elements Great Dao. However, entry slots are limited, and the great sects fight openly and secretly over them. The player must achieve good results in the tournament to win entry slots for the Qingyun Sect (or their own sect).

After the tournament ends, the true face of the demonic-path forces is gradually exposed — the leader of the Netherworld Dao is a Golden Core Perfected demon cultivator who intends to seize the "Five Elements Origin" within the secret realm to refine an evil artifact that can shake the continent's balance. The player goes deep into the realm and has the final confrontation with the Netherworld Dao.

In the deepest part of the secret realm, the player finds Taichu True Person's second Dao Seed — the **"Secret of the Golden Core,"** grasping that the Golden Core is not simply solidified Spiritual Energy, but the condensation of a "seed of the Dao." In this confrontation and insight, the player breaks through to Golden Core.

### Key Scenes

#### Scene 1: Preparations for the Sect Grand Tournament

**Location**: Zhongzhou · markets around the Debate Terrace

**Event**: The player arrives in Zhongzhou, finding cultivators from all over the continent gathered around the Debate Terrace. The sects set up encampments in the markets, probing each other.

**Flow**:
1. **Market Exploration**: free exploration, may conduct: — buy equipment/pills/talismans (Zhongzhou market is the continent's largest cultivator trading market, fully stocked) — buy opponent intel from an informant ("Wind-Heard Daoist" (風聞道人), Spirit Stones ×50 per copy) — spar with other sect disciples (Social Check to gain friendship or enmity)
2. **Opponent Preview**: — Taixu Sword Sect (太虛劍宗): sword-cultivator genius · Ling Shuang (凌霜, Foundation Establishment Perfected, cold and taciturn) — Crimson Clouds Valley: pill-and-sword dual cultivator · Yao Buran (藥不然, late Foundation Establishment, known for trading pills for intel) — Myriad Treasures Pavilion (萬寶閣): artifact-cultivator elite · Gongshu Ce (公輸策, late Foundation Establishment, many treasures) — Xuanyin Sect (玄陰宗): mysterious girl · Ye Wuhen (夜無痕, mid Foundation Establishment, unknown origin) — Barbarian Beast Mountain (蠻獸山): beast-taming youth · Mang Ya (莽牙, mid Foundation Establishment, with a second-stage spirit beast White Tiger)
3. **Discover Anomaly**: — Divine Sense (SHS) Check (d100 ≤ SHS) senses several unusual auras in the crowd — demon cultivators lurking — Tracking Check: Agility (SHF) Check to trail the suspect to a market back-alley, overhearing a snippet: "...the Netherworld Dao lord said, cause chaos during the tournament..." — if discovered: ambushed by 2 Foundation Establishment demon cultivator thugs (CR 3×2)

**Key NPCs**:
- Ling Shuang (Taixu Sword Sect)
- Yao Buran (Crimson Clouds Valley): see NPC character card
- Wind-Heard Daoist — cultivator-world informant, calls himself "Know-It-All," interested in all intel

**Reward**: intel advantage, potential ally/rival relationship network

#### Scene 2: Sect Grand Tournament — Arena Matches

**Location**: Zhongzhou · Debate Terrace — a giant arena floating above a ten-thousand-zhang abyss

**Event**: The Sect Grand Tournament officially begins, using an elimination format. The player represents the Qingyun Sect.

**Flow (three key duels)**:

**First Match — Preliminaries (vs rogue)**: — opponent: early Foundation Establishment rogue cultivator (HP 45, attack skill value 45) — low difficulty, lets the player show strength — Check: best of three contests, normal combat flow

**Second Match — Elimination (vs Myriad Treasures Pavilion · Gongshu Ce)**: — Gongshu Ce uses many magic treasures: protective spirit shield (absorbs 20 damage), flame flying sword (2d8 fire), immobilization talisman ×2 — tactical hint: Gongshu Ce's own martial skill is average (attack skill value 48) but has many treasures; can be countered with disarm/break-magic techniques — if victorious: Gongshu Ce says admiringly "Your strength has nothing to do with treasures; I lose," and gifts a "Realm-Breaking Talisman" (Breakthrough Check +5, one-time) as a sign of respect — if defeated: not lethal (first touch ends), but the Qingyun Sect loses qualification for the next round

**Third Match — Semifinals (vs Netherworld Dao's puppet contestant)**: — the opponent appears to be a disciple of some small sect, but is actually controlled by the Netherworld Dao — mid-battle the opponent suddenly erupts with abnormal demonic qi power — attack Check +15, damage +2d6 (exhausted after 3 rounds) — post-match investigation: Spirit Power (LIL) perception Check discovers a "heart-controlling gu" planted in the opponent's body — this is one of the Netherworld Dao's plans to cause chaos in the tournament

**Final Tournament Ranking**:
- If all wins: Qingyun Sect gets top three, wins Five Elements Secret Realm slots ×3
- If reaches semifinals: ranks 5-8, slots ×1
- If loses in elimination: no direct slots, must obtain through other means

**Reward**: sect reputation (depends on record), Five Elements Secret Realm slots, participants' respect/hostility

#### Scene 4: Five Elements Secret Realm — Entry

**Flow**:

**Key NPCs**:

When the five elements lose control, the Spiritual Energy tide will become a tide of destruction — that is what our lord desires."
2. **Alliance and Choice**: — disciples of various sects also arrive one after another (Ling Shuang, Yao Buran, Ye Wuhen, Mang Ya, etc.) — the player may choose: join righteous-path disciples against the Netherworld Dao / seize the Five Elements Origin amid the chaos / make a deal with the Netherworld Dao

#### Scene 6: Core Formation — The Choice of the Dao

## Act III: Winds Rise and Clouds Surge

**Suitable Level**: Golden Core 9-12 → Breakthrough to Nascent Soul

**Core Theme**: Choices in the tide of the era — on the edge of the Demon Abyss crisis and continental war

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 5-10 years

### Plot Summary

With the Golden Core formed, the player's standing in the cultivation world is utterly transformed. The Qingyun Sect grants the player "True Inheritance Disciple" status, with independent disciple-accepting rights, elder-guidance rights, and more sect resources.

However, peace did not last long. The Western Domain's Demon-Suppressing Hall sent an urgent call for help — the Demon Abyss seal showed its most severe loosening in ten thousand years. One of the five Fusion-stage mighty ones who guard the Demon Abyss year-round was gravely wounded by an unknown force. The player is drawn into this intricate situation. ## Act IV: Heaven and Earth as the Board

**Suitable Level**: Nascent Soul 13-16 → Breakthrough to Spirit Transformation

**Core Theme**: From pawn to player — participate in reshaping the continental landscape

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 10-30 years

### Plot Summary

With the Nascent Soul formed, the player is no longer just someone executing tasks — but someone qualified to help formulate strategy. At the initiative of the Demon-Suppressing Hall, the Five Sacred Lands convene a "Cultivator World Convention" (修仙界大會) to discuss forming an Anti-Demon Alliance. The player, as an outstanding Qingyun Sect True Inheritance Disciple, is invited to attend.

However, the convention does not go smoothly. The great sects each have their own thoughts: the Taixu Sword Sect advocates full-on attack; the Crimson Clouds Valley worries war will affect the pill supply chain; the Myriad Treasures Pavilion sees the war's business opportunity; the Tianji Manor calculates a "bloodshed omen" if the alliance is rushed. The player must mediate among the sides to push the alliance's formation.

Just as the alliance is about to be reached, shocking news rocks the cultivation world — an "ancient inheritance" (上古傳承) appears near Ascension City in Zhongzhou, preliminarily judged to be the complete inheritance of an innate divine demon (先天神魔) from the Divine-Demon Era (神魔紀). The news instantly shifts every sect's attention from the Demon Abyss to the inheritance contest.

While exploring the ancient inheritance, the player meets a mysterious being calling itself the "Ancient Remnant Clan" (上古遺族) — the last surviving descendant of an innate divine demon from the Divine-Demon Era. He reveals a deeper truth about the Demon Abyss crisis: the reason the Demon Realm passage can be opened is that the Hongmeng Continent's "Heavenly Dao" (天道) itself is in decline. And the cause of the decline relates to a flaw in the "Nine Realms to Immortality" system.

Meanwhile, the Netherworld Dao exploits the sects' chaos over the inheritance to accelerate the "Opening Heaven Plan" deep in the Demon Abyss. When the player returns from the inheritance, a first small "Demon Realm rift" has already appeared in the Demon Abyss.

In a battle to stop the Demon Realm vanguard from expanding the rift, the player faces the Nascent Soul-stage ultimate challenge — defeating the Demon Realm vanguard's commander, a Spirit Transformation-stage "Demon Lord." Between life and death, the player breaks through to Spirit Transformation.

## Act V: The Steps to Ascension

**Suitable Level**: Spirit Transformation 17-20 → Void Refining breakthrough → Fusion breakthrough

**Core Theme**: Cross the gap between mortal and immortal — face the truth of the Demon Realm

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 50-100 years

### Plot Summary

After Spirit Transformation, the player already stands at the top tier of the Hongmeng Continent's cultivator pyramid. Yet the closer to the peak, the heavier the Heavenly Dao feels. Every major move of the player may affect the continental landscape.

The clue of the fifth Dao Seed points to the "Immortal Realm Trial Path" in the East Sea Abyss beyond the Eastern Lands — a trial passage the Immortal Realm set up for mortal cultivators in ancient times; those who pass gain the Immortal Realm's recognition and the blessing of Immortal Realm laws. But this trial path has had no one pass it for tens of thousands of years.

While exploring the trial path in the East Sea, the player discovers a shocking fact: the other end of the trial path leads directly to the "Heavenly Dao Core" (天道核心) — the hub of the Heavenly Dao's law operation. Scattered within the Heavenly Dao Core are "Heavenly Dao Fragments" (天道碎片) that fell away as the Heavenly Dao declined. Collecting these fragments can temporarily stabilize the Heavenly Dao and delay the Demon Realm invasion.

Meanwhile, the Netherworld Dao quietly accelerates its pace. Their cult leader — a terrifying existence at the Fusion stage — finally acts in person. The Netherworld Cult Leader's true identity is exposed: he is a fallen immortal who betrayed the Immortal Realm in the Immortal-Demon War ten thousand years ago. He betrayed the Immortal Realm in that war and surrendered to the Demon Realm, granted immortality by the Demon Realm's "Demon Ancestor." His goal is not simply to open the Demon Realm passage, but to "devour the Heavenly Dao" (吞噬天道) — to take the Heavenly Dao Core for himself and become the new master of the Hongmeng Continent.

The player must prove their qualification in the Immortal Realm Trial, collect the Heavenly Dao Fragments, then face the Netherworld Cult Leader in the final confrontation. In defeating the Netherworld Cult Leader, the player completes the two breakthroughs of Void Refining and Fusion, preparing for the ultimate duel of the final act.

## Act VI: The Peak of the Nine Realms

**Suitable Level**: Fusion 25-28 → Mahayana breakthrough → Tribulation Crossing → Ascension

**Core Theme**: The ultimate choice — ascend to the Immortal Realm or remain in the mortal world

**Time Span (in-game)**: about 100-500 years

### Plot Summary

With Fusion achieved, the player stands at the very top of the Hongmeng Continent. The Heavenly Dao Fragments are gathered and the Heavenly Dao temporarily recovers stability. But the true ultimate threat is not yet removed — the "Demon Ancestor" (魔祖) deep in the Demon Realm, the Demon Realm's master sealed ten thousand years ago, is awakening.

Cang Wudao was only a prelude. The true Demon Ancestor is an existence beyond Tribulation Crossing — his status in the Demon Realm is comparable to the Immortal Realm's "Heavenly Immortal" (天仙). The ten-thousand-year seal has exhausted most of his power, but once he fully awakens, even the weakest Demon Ancestor is not something mortal cultivators can oppose.

The only way to stop the Demon Ancestor's awakening is to go deep into the Demon Realm before the seal fully collapses and apply a final layer of reinforcement on the seal — a "Heavenly Dao Seal" (天道封印) that requires "Ascension Power" (飛昇之力) to drive. This means the player must first complete the Mahayana and Tribulation Crossing breakthroughs before entering the Demon Realm, then trigger the Ascension Tribulation in the ultimate duel with the Demon Ancestor, using ascension power to seal the Demon Realm passage.

But ascension means leaving the mortal world forever. The player faces the ultimate choice:
- Ascend to the Immortal Realm, entrusting the mortal world's future to those who come later
- Give up ascension, remain in the mortal world as a "Scattered Immortal" (散仙) to keep guarding
- A third path: at ascension, leave part of your Great Dao enlightenment in the mortal world, planting a new Dao Seed so later comers also have a chance to reach the Nine Realms

Above Ascension City, the Heavenly Tribulation descends. The eighty-one heavenly thunders, the ultimate Inner Demon trial, the Heavenly Dao's final question — all await the player.

**End of Act**: Ascension — or remaining — or forging a third path. The Nine Realms to Immortality becomes legend.

**Ending A — Ascension Seal**:
- The player defeats the Demon Ancestor with ascension power, then applies the "Heavenly Dao Seal" with ascension power
- The Demon Realm passage is permanently sealed — requires an ascender to re-reinforce it every ten thousand years
- The player ascends to the Immortal Realm, becoming a new legend of the Hongmeng Continent
- At the final moment of ascension, the player leaves a "Dao Seed" — giving later comers a chance to walk the Nine Realms to Immortality path

**Ending B — Scattered Immortal Guardian**:
- The player gives up ascension (or chooses to stay after ascension failure), defeating the Demon Ancestor as a Scattered Immortal
- Uses Heavenly Dao Fragments and own cultivation to reinforce the seal
- The seal can hold three thousand years — after that, a new hero is needed
- The player travels, takes disciples, and guards the continent as a Scattered Immortal
- Can never ascend, but becomes the guardian deity of mortal cultivators

**Ending C — Sacrifice**:
- If unable to defeat the Demon Ancestor, the player chooses to detonate the Ascension Platform with all their cultivation
- The Ascension Platform's ten-thousand-year accumulated ascension power releases at once — enough to re-seal the Demon Ancestor for ten thousand years
- The cost is the player's complete disappearance (Nascent Soul/primordial spirit also dissipates)
- But ten thousand years later, the seal will loosen again...
- The player's name is engraved on the Ascension Platform — becoming a legend gazed upon by cultivators of the next ten thousand years

**Reward**: depends on ending — ascend to Immortal Realm / become Scattered Immortal guardian / become an eternal legend

#### Scene 5: Final Chapter — The Dao Seed Endures

**Location**: Ascension City · Ascension Platform (Ending A) / the player's chosen cave dwelling (Ending B)

**Event**: Whatever the ending, the player has completed the full journey of "Nine Realms to Immortality"

**Epilogue Narrative**:
- The player's story is written by "Wind-Heard Daoist" into the final chapter of the "Wind-Heard Records" (風聞錄)
- The great sects engrave the player's name into their sect histories
- All nine of Taichu True Person's Dao Seeds are found — they now lie scattered across the Hongmeng Continent, awaiting the next destined one
- The complete system of the Nine Realms to Immortality — from Qi Refining to Tribulation Crossing — becomes more complete because of the player's exploration

**Open Ending**:
- If the player ascended: the GM may describe first impressions of the Immortal Realm — a larger, more complex world awaits
- If the player stayed: new gifted juniors appear on the continent — they gaze up at the player's legend and begin their own cultivation path
- If the player sacrificed: centuries later, a young cultivator discovers residual Dao Seed fragments on the Ascension Platform...
