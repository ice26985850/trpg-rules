# Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart — Solo Scenario: "Lone Sword Questions Heaven"

> **Suitable for**: 1 GM + 1 Player | **Scope**: Qi Refining → Tribulation Crossing (Lv.1-36)
> **Depends on**: This scenario is based on the "Tianji: Cultivation of the True Heart" main rulebook + `solo_rules.md` Solo Rules Supplement.
> **Citation format**: `→ §chapter` cites the main rulebook. `→ solo §section` cites the solo rules.
> **NPC Companions**: see `character_cards_Excel/NPC_Companion_Cards.xlsx`
>
> **GM Prep**: Before reading this scenario, it is recommended to first familiarize yourself with Chapter 4 (Worldbuilding) of the main rulebook and the Solo Rules Supplement.

---

## Scenario Overview

**"Lone Sword Questions Heaven"** is the complete legend of a lone cultivator (Cultivator) from mortal to ascension. The player (Player) enters the Hongmeng Continent (鴻蒙大陸) alone, befriends close friends, apprentices under renowned masters, fights the demonic path, and finally faces the Heaven's Mechanism.

The scenario is divided into five acts, each covering several realms, with progress adjustable flexibly:

| Act | Realm Range | Theme | Est. Sessions |
|:--:|----------|------|:-----------:|
| Act I | Qi Refining Lv.1-3 | Mortal Enters the Dao · First Taste of the Jianghu | 2-3 |
| Act II | Foundation Establishment Lv.4-6 | Apprenticeship · First Gleam of the Blade | 3-4 |
| Act III | Golden Core Lv.7-9 | Golden Core Perfected · First Sign of the Demonic | 3-5 |
| Act IV | Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation Lv.10-18 | Nascent Soul Seeks the Dao · Righteous-Demon Clash | 5-8 |
| Act V | Void Refining → Tribulation Crossing Lv.19-36 | Path to Ascension · Choice of the Heavenly Dao | 5-10 |

# Act I: Mortal Enters the Dao · First Taste of the Jianghu

> **Realm**: Qi Refining Lv.1-3 | **Location**: Qingshi Town (青石鎮), Black Pine Ridge (黑松嶺)
> **Main NPCs**: Chen Bonian (陳伯年, old town chief), Yao Buran (藥不然, Crimson Clouds Valley disciple), Bai Ling (白靈, fox spirit · companion candidate)
> **Reference**: Worldbuilding → §4.5 (Qingshi Town)

## Prologue: The Missing Herb Gatherers

For their own reasons, the players (Players) come to Qingshi Town (青石鎮) — an ordinary small town on the outskirts of the Taixu Sword Sect (太虛劍宗). On the night of arrival, the old town chief Chen Bonian sighs in the teahouse: another herb gatherer from the town has vanished — the third this month.

**GM Check Points**:
- Asking townsfolk for information (Social Check, → §2): Difficulty (DC) 40; success reveals the missing all last headed toward Black Pine Ridge.
- Searching the last missing person's residence (Observation/Divine Sense (SHS) Check): Difficulty (DC) 50; discover a bundle of undelivered herbs and half a map.

> **Solo Tip**: The player may freely choose the investigation method. If bringing an NPC companion (see below), the companion can provide assistance (Check +5).

### Obtaining NPC Companion: Bai Ling

During the investigation, the player encounters an injured white fox kit at the edge of Black Pine Ridge — its hind leg caught in a hunter's trap. If the player chooses to help (no check required), the kit reveals spiritual intelligence after healing: it is a spirit fox that has just awakened its intellect, named **Bai Ling**, who can become the player's first NPC companion.

| Bai Ling (Qi Refining stage) | Data |
|----------------|------|
| Health Points (HP) | 20 |
| Damage | Claw Strike 1d6+2 |
| Special | Perception: can detect Spiritual Energy anomalies and hidden enemies within 50 zhang |
| Personality | Curious, loyal; cannot speak human words but understands simple commands |

> **Excel Card**: see `character_cards_Excel/NPC_Companion_Cards.xlsx` → "Bai Ling" sheet

## First Encounter: Black Pine Ridge Exploration

| Encounter | Type | Data |
|------|------|------|
| Shadow Cat ×2 | Mook | Health Points (HP) 12, Claw Strike 1d6+1, Night Stealth +10 |
| Injured Herb Gatherer | NPC | Trapped at the ancient cave entrance, Health Points (HP) 5/30 (critical) |

In combat the player must protect the critically wounded herb gatherer (→ solo §3.2). If the herb gatherer dies, subsequent quest rewards are halved. Once rescued, the gatherer reports: someone deep in the ancient cave is conducting "heretical experiments."

## Second Encounter: Deep in the Ancient Cave

After entering the ancient cave, the player discovers a crude alchemy room. A cultivator (Cultivator) expelled from the Qingyun Sect (青雲門), **Liu Yunfei (柳雲飛)**, is using the herb gatherers' vital blood to refine "Yin-Gathering Pills."

| Encounter | Type | Data |
|------|------|------|
| Liu Yunfei | Boss | Health Points (HP) 35, Corpse Claw 1d8+3 + Corpse Poison (Health Points (HP) -3 per round, 3 rounds), at half HP Corpse Qi Burst AoE 1d10 |

**Weakness**: Fire attribute +30% damage. Yang-attribute Cultivation Techniques counter corpse poison.

**Tactical Suggestion (Solo)**: Use the ancient cave terrain (stone pillars provide cover) and fire-type attacks. Bai Ling can draw attention from the flank (assist action → solo §2.1).

# Act II: Apprenticeship · First Gleam of the Blade

> **Realm**: Foundation Establishment Lv.4-6 | **Location**: Taixu Sword Sect, Sword-Washing Pool
> **Main NPCs**: Qin Yu (秦羽, guide → NPC card), Xuanzhenzi (玄真子, apprenticeship candidate → §15.2)
> **Reference**: Apprenticeship system → §14, §15

## Prologue: A Letter of Recommendation

The deed of righteousness in Qingshi Town reaches the Taixu Sword Sect. Inner-sect disciple **Qin Yu** (→ `character_cards_Excel/NPC_Card_QinYu.xlsx`) personally comes to invite the player to the Taixu Sword Sect — "The Sword-Washing Pool needs a new sword-art teaching assistant; perhaps you could come take a look."

**Branch Choice**:
- **Accept the Invitation** → Enter the Taixu Sword Sect storyline (recommended route)
- **Decline** → May choose to go to the Crimson Clouds Valley (丹霞谷, Ge Yun storyline) or the Northern Frontier (Zhenbei True Lord storyline); the GM adjusts the follow-up based on the choice.

## Apprenticeship (→ §14)

After entering the sect, the player may apprentice under **Xuanzhenzi** (→ §15.2) — see the main rulebook for specific conditions and trials. After apprenticeship, unlocks:
- Monthly resource supply (→ §14.4)
- Cultivation Technique transmission (oral and heart-to-heart teaching → §14.4)
- Dao Heart trial (→ §14.3 promotion conditions)

## Main Encounter: Sword-Washing Pool Trial

| Encounter Order | Type | Data |
|----------|------|------|
| 1. Basic Sword Puppet | Elite | Health Points (HP) 60, Basic Sword Art 1d8+4, Armor Class (AC) 12 |
| 2. Intermediate Sword Puppet | Elite | Health Points (HP) 80, Green Lotus Sword Formula 2d8+5, Armor Class (AC) 14, 3rd strike ×1.5 |
| 3. Mirror Trial | Boss | Health Points (HP) = player's Health Points (HP), copies all of player's Martial Techniques (Damage −20%), Armor Class (AC) = player's Armor Class (AC) |

**Mirror Trial Mechanic**: The mirror uses the player's Divine Abilities on rounds 3 and 6 (if the player has learned them). Defeating the mirror counts as passing the sword trial.

**Reward**: Foundation Establishment Pill ×1 + Green Edge Sword (Spirit-grade, 1d10+2+Constitution (GUG)/8, Hit +5)

## Act II Follow-up Hooks

- Xuanzhenzi mentions "the Netherworld Dao has been active frequently in the Northern Frontier lately"
- Qin Yu reports: the stone gate deep in the ancient cave may lead to a Netherworld Dao stronghold
- Bai Ling begins to speak simple human words by the end of this act ("Master... be careful...")

The Taixu Sword Sect grants the "True Inheritance Disciple" status. At this time, urgent military intelligence comes from the Northern Frontier: Netherworld Dao demon cultivators have established an outpost on the Northern Frontier border.

The player's master (Xuanzhenzi or another) suggests: go to the Northern Frontier to assist **Zhenbei True Lord** (→ §15.3) — both a chance to train and to make a name.

# Act IV: Nascent Soul Seeks the Dao · Righteous-Demon Clash

> **Realm**: Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation Lv.10-18 | **Location**: major sects of Zhongzhou, Netherworld Dao main altar
> **Main NPCs**: Tianyazi (天涯子, → §15.4), Dugu Wuji (獨孤無極, → §15.4), Fallen Immortal · Cang Wudao (墮仙·蒼無道, Boss)

## Prologue: The Grand Situation Under Heaven

With the Nascent Soul formed, the player is now a renowned powerhouse. But at this time, the Netherworld Dao cult leader **Cang Wudao** (→ §15.4, late Fusion stage) announces shocking news to the cultivation world — he has discovered an ancient immortal-mansion ruin deep in the Northern Frontier Demon Abyss, containing the "Dao Ancestor's Inheritance." The great sects stir with unrest.

The player's master suggests: "Go to the Tianji Manor (天機府) and find Tianyazi (→ §15.4) — he may know the truth."

## Visiting Tianyazi

Tianyazi (→ `character_cards_Excel/NPC_Card_Tianyazi.xlsx`) receives the player at the Tianji Manor. He reveals:
- The "immortal-mansion ruin" Cang Wudao spoke of does exist
- But what is inside is not the Dao Ancestor's Inheritance, but a sealed ancient Demon Ancestor — Devourer of the Dao (→ Boss card)
- Cang Wudao intends to unseal the Demon Ancestor and use its power to rule the Hongmeng Continent

Tianyazi gives the player a task: before Cang Wudao unseals it, gather the three "Demon-Sealing Keys" — held respectively by the Taixu Sword Sect, the Crimson Clouds Valley, and Tianyazi himself.

| Encounter | Type | Data |
|------|------|------|

**Cang Wudao Combat Mechanic** (→ `character_cards_Excel/Boss_Card_Collection.xlsx` → Fallen Immortal · Cang Wudao sheet, solo-adjusted data):

2. **Phase Two** (HP 299-150): summons Demon Abyss rifts, dealing 1d6 dark-erosion damage to the player each round

> **GM Hint**: This is the highest-difficulty battle in the solo scenario. It is recommended to ensure before battle that the player has enough pills and at least 1 Robe-and-Bowl-level aid (if any).

# Act V: Path to Ascension · Choice of the Heavenly Dao

> **Realm**: Void Refining → Fusion → Mahayana → Tribulation Crossing Lv.19-36
> **Location**: entire Hongmeng Continent, Gate of the Heavenly Dao
> **Main NPCs**: One Thought of Reincarnation (輪迴一念, Heavenly Dao trial), the player's Robe-and-Bowl master

## Prologue: After the Sealing

Cang Wudao is defeated and the seal on the Demon Ancestor Devourer of the Dao is temporarily stabilized. But the player learns a cruel truth from Tianyazi: the seal is not permanent — every thousand years a Mahayana-stage cultivator must reinforce it with their own lifespan. The previous cultivator to reinforce the seal has less than a hundred years of life left.

**Branch Choice**:
- **Seek a Permanent Sealing Method** → go to the Tianji Manor, ancient ruins, and visit Yichen (羿塵, ancient remnant clan → world_expansion.md)
- **Prepare to Ascend, Leave This World** → accelerate cultivation to ascend before the seal collapses (selfish route)
- **Become the Next Sealer** → accept Zhenbei True Lord's Robe-and-Bowl Trial (→ §14.3 promotion conditions)

## Act V Structure

This act is an open-ended conclusion; the GM advances it based on the player's choices. The three main routes are:

### Route A: Seek the Permanent Seal (Recommended)

| Step | Encounter/Challenge |
|------|----------|
| 1. Visit Yichen | Yichen (Spirit Transformation-stage power) tells: the Demon Ancestor's true body hides in the "Void Rift," and it must be utterly destroyed within the rift |
| 2. Enter the Void Rift | The laws within the Void Rift are chaotic; each round requires passing a Dao Heart (DAX) Check (DC 60) or become lost for 1 round |
| 3. Face the Demon Ancestor · Devourer of the Dao | Boss: Health Points (HP) 1000, Devour 4d20+15, devours 1 buff effect from the player each round, Weakness: Chaos Origin |

**Devourer of the Dao Battle**: The final battle in the Void Rift. The player must first defeat the Devourer's "Seven Emotions Incarnations" (7 consecutive battles, each representing one emotion: joy/anger/worry/thought/sorrow/fear/surprise); a short rest is allowed after each (→ §1.2). After all are defeated, the Devourer's true body appears.

### Route B: Ascension (Speedrun Route)

After the Tribulation Crossing stage, the player may choose to ascend directly. But Tianyazi will tell them: after ascension they can no longer intervene in this world — meaning that when the seal collapses, the Hongmeng Continent will lose its last guardian.

If still choosing ascension: undergo the final Heavenly Tribulation (→ §1.5, Difficulty (DC) raised to 80, but Robe-and-Bowl inheritance may assist). On success, the player becomes part of the Heavenly Dao and the scenario ends.

### Route C: Become the Sealer (Sacrifice Route)

If choosing to accept Zhenbei True Lord's Robe-and-Bowl Trial ("Will you seal the Demon Abyss at the cost of your lifespan?" → §14.3), then:
- Player's lifespan −500 years (about 100-200 years remaining)
- Seal stabilized for 1000 years
- Gain the title "Continent Guardian," reputation permanently +50
- During this time may take disciples, found a sect, and seek the next guardian — the scenario turns into an endgame of "seeking a successor"

## Ending: The Gate of the Heavenly Dao

No matter which route is chosen, the destination of Act V is the same door — the Gate of the Heavenly Dao. Here the player looks back on a lifetime of choices:

> **GM Narration Example**:
> "Before the Gate of the Heavenly Dao, you see your whole life: the herb gatherer of Qingshi Town that year, the sword-song of the Sword-Washing Pool, the wind and snow of the Northern Frontier, Cang Wudao's Fallen Sky Strike, the choice before the seal. Every choice is engraved upon the Gate of the Heavenly Dao."
> "Then, the door opens."

**Ending Determination**:

| Ending | Condition |
|------|------|
| **Ascend to Immortality** | Route B complete, Tribulation Crossing success |
| **Guardian Sage** | Route C complete, becomes the sealer |
| **God-Slayer** | Route A complete, permanently destroy the Demon Ancestor |
| **Dao Ancestor's Inheritance** | Route A complete + Robe-and-Bowl affinity 100 + Dugu Wuji's recognition → obtain the Dao Ancestor's Inheritance |
