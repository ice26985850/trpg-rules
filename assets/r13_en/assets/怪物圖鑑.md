## 5.1 Challenge Rating (CR) System

| Challenge Rating (CR) | Mortal Squad (4) | Astartes Squad (4) |
|----|---------------|-------------------|
| 0 | Can handle 5-8 | Can handle 10+ |
| 1 | Can handle 2-3 | Can handle 5-6 |
| 2 | Can handle 1-2 | Can handle 2-3 |
| 3 | 1 = full-team challenge | 1 = normal combat |
| 4 | Requires tactics and luck | 1 = full-team challenge |
| 5 | Boss-tier | Difficult combat |
| 6+ | Party-wipe tier | Requires special conditions |

## 5.5 Non-combat Warp Phenomena Table (d20)

Use this table when a Psyker uses Psychic powers (Telepathy, Psyniscience, Divination, etc.) in a non-combat situation, or when characters remain in a corrupted environment for an extended period:

| d20 | Phenomenon | Effect |
|-----|------|------|
| 1-6 | **Calm** | Nothing happens. The Warp is momentarily at peace. |
| 7-8 | **Whisper** | Everyone hears faint murmurs — perhaps a name, a word, or a fragment of broken sentence. The GM may choose to give a vague hint, or merely set the mood. |
| 9-10 | **Chill** | Temperature drops sharply. Body Test (Standard) to avoid 1 point of Fatigue (Mortal). Astartes: armor's climate control system briefly fails — Tech-Use Test (Simple) to restart. |
| 11-12 | **Afterimage** | Each character sees a different image — mortals see deceased loved ones, Astartes see fallen battle-brothers. Mind Test (Standard) to avoid +1 Corruption. On success, the image may contain useful information. |
| 13-14 | **Time Ripple** | Perceived time and objective time fall out of sync. All characters are Confused for 1d3 rounds (Mortal) / 1 round (Astartes). |
| 15-16 | **Tempting Whisper** | A voice whispers to each character — offering seemingly reasonable advice, but carrying subtle Chaos taint. If the character accepts the advice, Corruption +1 but gains an Advantage. |
| 17 | **Minor Manifestation** | A small Warp entity coalesces nearby (non-hostile — at least not initially). It may answer a question (not necessarily truthfully) or propose a deal. |
| 18 | **Window of Truth** | A brief glimpse of the Warp's true face. Corruption +1d6 (Mortal) / +1d3 (Astartes). But the GM must grant a fragment of truth — possibly key to hidden plot. |
| 19 | **Time Rift** | The character briefly sees fragments of past or future (GM gives a prophetic scene). Corruption +2. Any Psyker present gains an extra consumable premonition (may reroll the next important Test once). |
| 20 | **Emperor's Eye** | Golden light flashes. All present: Corruption −2, Faith +3. Gain one free "Emperor's Hand" (valid for the rest of this game). The Psyker's Warp risk drops to a minimum for 24 hours. |
