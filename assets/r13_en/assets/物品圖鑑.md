### Full-Auto Modes

| Mode | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| Single Shot | 1 | Normal attack |
| Burst | 3 | Attack a single target twice (at Disadvantage), with each hit calculated separately |
| Suppression | 5 | Attack all targets within a 3×3m area (at Disadvantage) + **Suppression Effect** (see below) |
| Sweep | 10 | Attack all targets along a straight line (2m wide) |

**Suppression Effect (for the suppressed side):**
- Movement halved (next turn)
- All attack tests at Disadvantage (next turn)
- Attempting to leave the suppression zone triggers an opportunity attack from the suppressor (using remaining ammunition)
- If entering full cover → Suppression Effect ends immediately
- **Astartes vs Suppression:** Movement halved but do not suffer attack Disadvantage ("They know no fear")

> **Suppressor Limitation:** After performing suppression fire, cannot suppress again next turn (requires reloading or cooldown).

## 3.8B Equipment Maintenance & Durability

### Weapon Jamming (Critical Failure 96-00)

| Weapon Type | Jam Effect | Repair Method |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Solid-round weapons (Autogun/Shotgun) | Jam — cannot fire | 1 Minor Action + Tech-Use Test (Standard); success clears the jam. Failure requires 1 Major Action to disassemble |
| Energy weapons (Lasgun/Plasma Gun) | Overheat — power cell locked | 1 Major Action to cool down. If overheat weapons are used consecutively, the next Critical Failure range expands to 91-00 |
| Bolt weapons | Bolt jam — extremely dangerous | 1 Major Action + Tech-Use Test (Hard). Failure → bolt detonates in chamber, dealing the weapon's base damage to the wielder (ignores armor) |

### Armor Repair

| Damage Type | Repair Method | Cost |
|---------|---------|------|
| Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR) reduced by corrosion | Find an Adeptus Mechanicus facility or carry repair tools + Tech-Use Test (Hard) | Each repair restores 1 Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR), consuming 1 repair part (20 Throne Gelt (TG)) |
| Armor Sacrifice (on Critical Hit, −2 Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR) for −5 Damage) | Must return to base for repair | Restoring each point of Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR) costs 10 Throne Gelt (TG) + 1 day |
| Power Armor Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR) damaged | Tech Sergeant's litany + Tech-Use Test (Standard) restores 1 Armor Mitigation Armor Rating (AR); or rest at a holy site restores +3 Machine Spirit (APM) | Free (if facility available) |

### Permanent Weapon Destruction

When a second Critical Failure occurs in the same battle, or a single Critical Failure occurs in an extreme environment (underwater, mud, extreme heat), the GM may rule the weapon permanently destroyed — cannot be repaired on site.
