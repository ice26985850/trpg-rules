## Scenario A-3: "Whisper of the Bone Pile" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)
**Mode:** Mortal · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　**Map Reference:** maps/map13_bonestack.svg

**Difficulty:** Intermediate (Challenge Rating (CR) Total 4-5) | **Inquisitor:** Ilya Cole (Radical)  
**Target Region:** Tarr-Morag Hive City, Abandoned Hive "Bone Pile" Level 2000  
**Recommended Player Attributes:** Level 3-4 range

### Surface Goal

Infiltrate the Abandoned Hive "Bone Pile", find a Chaos cult outpost reportedly operating there, eliminate the cultists and destroy all Chaos items.

### True Goal

Ilya is eager to prove himself. He suspects the cult here is preparing a summoning ritual—but he did not tell the Penitents. If the ritual is completed, the Penitents become the sacrifice. If the Penitents stop the ritual—Ilya will claim the credit for himself.

### Known Threats

- Approximately 15-20 cultists
- At least 2 Acolytes
- Underhive environment is dangerous (darkness, structural instability)

### Hidden Threats

- The cult leader is an unregistered Psi 3 Psyker—he is performing a ritual to summon a Warp entity
- The ritual has reached day 7 (9 days total). If the players fail to stop it within two game sessions, the summoning completes
- Ilya deliberately under-provided Flare ration (only 3 given), citing "limited resources"

### Resource Ration

3 Flares (instead of 5). The rest standard.

### Temptation Note Distribution

- 1 player Note 1
- 1 player Note 3 (the true goal of the mission is to test who will betray)
- 2 players Note 4

### Key NPCs

| NPC | Role | Details |

| **"Lightless" Gang Contact** | Potential Ally | Underhive gang willing to provide intel and shortcuts—but demands a grenade in exchange |
| **Psyker Cult Leader "the Whisperer"** | Boss | Psi 3, grows stronger if the ritual completes. Flies into a rage when the ritual is interrupted |
