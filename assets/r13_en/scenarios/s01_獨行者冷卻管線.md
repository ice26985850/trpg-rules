## Scenario SA-1: "The Lone Walker's Cooling Tunnels" — Iron Ribs' Lone Walker

**Difficulty:** Introductory | **Inquisitor:** Astaroth (Puritan)  
**Target Region:** Tarr-Morag Hive, Mid Hive (Level 850), abandoned cooling tunnels  
**Recommended Attributes:** Levels 2-3 | **Challenge Rating (CR) Budget:** 1 (Simple)

### Background

You are one of the newest batch of Penitents. For this mission, the Inquisitor sent only you — and a guide you have never met. This is not trust. It is a test.

### Surface Goal

Infiltrate the small Chaos Cultist outpost operating within the abandoned cooling tunnels and recover an Imperial Relic they are said to possess.

### True Goal

Astaroth wants to see whether you can maintain discipline without the pressure of teammates. The guide is also an observer.

### NPC Companion

**Kaz Vell (Guide, former Hive Laborer / Penitent)**

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| One-line description | A silent middle-aged man, convicted of "Heresy" — his "heresy" was refusing to report his own neighbors |
| Motivation | Live to return and see his daughter (he does not know if she is still waiting) |
| Bottom Line | Never kill civilians |
| Initial Attitude | 0 (Neutral — no trust or hostility toward the player) |
| Attributes | Faith 3 / Body 3 / Mind 2 / Tech 3 / Fate 2 |
| Key Skills | Stealth (Trained), Awareness (Trained) |
| Equipment | Autopistol (1d6), Flak Vest (Armor Rating (AR) 1), fragment of a Hive City map |
| Special Ability | **Hive Rat:** Never gets lost in Hive City environments. Can provide the player with a one-time stealth route (player gains Advantage on Stealth tests) |

### Key Checks

| Scene | Check | Success | Failure |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| Build trust with Kaz | Oratory (Standard) | Kaz attitude +1, gain a Hive shortcut | Kaz remains silent |
| Find the outpost entrance | Awareness (Standard) or Kaz's assistance | Discover sentinel position, can sneak around | Alert the sentinel, enter combat |

### Combat Encounters (Solo Scaling)

| Encounter | Enemies | Tactics |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Pipeline patrol | Chaos Cultist ×2 | Basic gunfight |
| Inside the outpost | Chaos Cultist ×2 + small trap | Kaz can help disarm the trap (Tech-Use test) |

### Internal Seduction Trigger

When the player discovers the relic inside the outpost, the GM presents the Whisper of Chaos here (rules supplement §3.1):

> *"This relic is worth a fortune. The Inquisitor will never know its true value. You could say it was damaged in combat."*
