## Scenario SB-1: "Awakening of the Solitary Sleeper"

**Difficulty:** Introductory | **Planet:** Requiem  
**Recommended Attributes:** Initial character creation

### Background

You wake up in the wreckage of a Thunderhawk gunship. Your squad—your brothers—are dead. Their bodies still sit in their seats, seatbelts still fastened. Only you are alive. One mortal soldier is also alive—she is the gunship's enginseer.

### NPC Companion

**Tech-Sergeant Kaela (Imperial Navy Enginseer)**

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| One-line description | A young enginseer who lost an arm in the crash, but built a makeshift prosthetic from wreckage parts |
| Motive | To leave this planet alive. She has the log of a transport ship—the ship is in the northern mountains |
| Bottom Line | Never abandons a machine that can be repaired |
| Initial Attitude | +1 (Trust—you protected her during the crash) |
| Attributes | Faith 3 / Body 2 / Mind 3 / Tech 4 / Fate 3 |
| Key Skills | Tech-Use (Trained), Investigation (Trained) |
| Equipment | Laspistol (1d6+1), Flak Armor (Armor Rating (AR) 2), data-slate (with transport ship coordinates) |
| Special Ability | **Machine Cult Training:** Advantage on Tech-Use checks. Can repair damaged equipment (once per scenario, restores one piece of equipment by one condition level) |

### Key Checks

| Scene | Check | Success | Failure |
|------|------|------|------|
| Search the wreckage | Investigation (Standard) | Find extra resources | Only find basic supplies |
| Kaela decodes the transport ship data | Tech-Use (Standard) | Obtain precise coordinates | Only a rough direction |

### Combat Encounters

| Encounter | Enemies | Tactic |
|------|------|------|
| Mutants near the wreckage | Lesser Mutant ×2 | Kaela provides support with her laspistol |
