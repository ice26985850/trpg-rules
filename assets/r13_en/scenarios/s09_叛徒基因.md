## Scenario SB-4: "Traitor's Gene"

**Difficulty:** High | **Location:** Wreck of the fallen capital ship "Justicar"  
**Recommended Attributes:** 4-5 growths

### Background

The transport ship is repaired, but the orbital defense system beyond the atmosphere will shoot it down. You must go to the crashed cruiser "Justicar" to disable the defense system. Deep in the wreckage, you meet a man—a Chaos Space Marine. He was once Imperial Fists. 300 years ago, he too was a man stranded on this planet.

### NPC Companion

Continue with the previous companion. Inside the ship, the companion is trapped in a sealed compartment for 1 scene—the player faces the Chaos Space Marine Kastor alone.

### Oath Conflict Point (Ultimate, Internalized)

Kastor does not attack you. He sits down, removes his helmet, and speaks in a weary voice:

> *"300 years ago, I was like you. Loyal, praying, waiting. Do you know what 300 years of silence does to an Astartes? The Emperor did not answer. The Emperor never answers."*

- **The Chapter's Voice:** "He is a traitor—kill him. That is your duty."
- **The Voice of Fear:** "Is what he says true? 300 years from now, will I become him?"

**If you attack him:** A Challenge Rating (CR) 4 solo Boss battle (solo-mode scaling: Hit Points (HP) reduced to 45, only 1 attack per turn). The companion finds a way to escape and joins in the 3rd round of combat.

**If you hear him out:** He reveals the location of the black box data—Exterminatus may have been wrong. Then he stands, walks toward the ship's power core. "I will not fight you, brother. I have fought enough. Tell the Emperor… I did not forget Him." He destroys himself. You obtain the black box data.

**If you are persuaded by him (accept the gift of Chaos):** Permanent +8 Corruption. Kastor lets you leave—"Welcome to the ranks of the Forgotten."
