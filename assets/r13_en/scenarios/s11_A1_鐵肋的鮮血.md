## Scenario A-1: "Iron Ribs' Blood" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)
**Mode:** Mortal · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　**Map Reference:** maps/map11_cooling_tunnels.svg

**Difficulty:** Introductory (Challenge Rating (CR) Total 2-3) | **Inquisitor:** Astaroth (Puritan)  
**Target Region:** Tarr-Morag Hive City, Mid Hive (Levels 800-900), abandoned Cooling Tunnels  
**Recommended Player Attributes:** Level 2-3 range

### Surface Goal

Destroy a small Chaos cult outpost operating in the abandoned Cooling Tunnels, and bring back the relic used by the cult.

### True Goal

Astaroth wants to test whether this batch of new Penitents can maintain teamwork under pressure. The relic itself is of little value.

### Known Threats

- Approximately 6-8 low-ranking cultist followers
- Possibly led by 1 Acolyte

### Hidden Threats

- A section of the abandoned tunnels is structurally unstable—an explosion during combat could trigger a collapse
- The cult is actually awaiting "supplies" from the Underhive; they are merely an outpost

### Resource Ration

Standard ration. One extra Medikit (the Inquisitor is more lenient with newcomers).

### Temptation Note Distribution

- 2 players receive Note 4 ("Nothing")
- 1 player receives Note 1 (Betrayal for reduced sentence)—but the Inquisitor does not truly expect betrayal, only a test

### Key NPCs

| NPC | Role | Details |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Veteran Galen** | Neutral Guide | A former Astra Militarum Sergeant serving his punishment here. Knows the tunnel routes. Both sympathetic and contemptuous toward the Penitents |
| **Acolyte Valak** | Enemy Leader | A young, fanatical, unskilled Chaos Acolyte. You may choose to persuade him to surrender (Mind Test, Hard), but he will be executed before the Inquisitor |

### Evacuation Condition

Proceed to the designated evacuation point after mission completion. No time limit (Astaroth gives newcomers room).

### Key Check Nodes

| # | Scene | Check | Target Number (TN) | Success | Failure |
|----|------|------|----|------|------|
| 1 | Searching for the outpost in the tunnels | Investigation (Standard) | Mind×10+Investigation | Find a shortcut and avoid patrols | Encounter extra patrol (+2 cultists) |
| 2 | Discovering the relic is corrupted | Psyniscience (Standard) | Faith×10+Psyniscience | Detect Warp residue | Corruption+1 for whoever touches the relic |
| 3 | Persuading Valak to surrender | Oratory (Hard) | Faith×10+Oratory−20 | Avoid the final battle | Proceed to Boss fight |
