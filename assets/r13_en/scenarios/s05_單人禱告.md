## Scenario SA-5: "Solitary Prayer" — Solitary Prayer at Black Dock

**Difficulty:** Endgame | **Inquisitor:** Victor Black (Fallen)  
**Target Region:** Carcassonne, lower levels of the Tower of Silence → Black Dock  
**Recommended Attributes:** Level 5 (at the human limit) | **Challenge Rating (CR) Budget:** 3-4 (Difficult~Lethal)

### Background

The final mission. Victor Black personally chose this "ultimate trial" for you: travel to the abandoned prison district at the lower levels of the Tower of Silence and purge a long-forgotten Chaos contamination. He gave you an assistant — a prisoner trapped at the lower levels who has survived there for 47 years.

### NPC Companion

**Eileen (the Forgotten, a former Penitent trapped for 47 years)**  
> Data as in the original scenario A-5. See GM Guide §5 NPC: the Forgotten, Eileen. Attitude: +2 (she waited 47 years to meet another human).

### Internal Seduction Trigger (Ultimate)

At Black Dock, Victor Black speaks to you privately — while Eileen waits for you in the ruins nearby:

> *"You completed the mission. You proved your worth. Now — accept my gift. You no longer need to serve the Imperium. You are free. As for Eileen? She waited 47 years. Another 47 years makes no difference."*

**If you refuse:** Victor sends Inquisition assault troopers ×2 to "clean up the scene." Eileen fights alongside you (Faith 5; her Oratory test can inspire you). After victory, Eileen's testimony can let you and other Inquisitors expose Victor's betrayal.

**If you accept:** The character becomes a follower of Chaos. Eileen witnesses your choice — the last look she gives you. The GM takes over the character.
