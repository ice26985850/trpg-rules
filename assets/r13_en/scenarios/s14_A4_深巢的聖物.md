## Scenario A-4: "Relic of the Deep Hive" (Multiplayer Mode · 2–4 Players)
**Mode:** Mortal · Multiplayer (2–4 Players)　|　**Map Reference:** maps/map14_deep_temple.svg

**Difficulty:** High (Challenge Rating (CR) Total 5-6) | **Inquisitor:** Magnus Iron (Radical)  
**Target Region:** Tarr-Morag Hive City, Deep Hive (below Level 2300), a forgotten ancient underground temple  
**Recommended Player Attributes:** Level 4-5 range

### Surface Goal

Recover a "relic" forgotten in the underground temple—said to be a relic of the Great Crusade era.

### True Goal

Magnus knows that "relic" is actually a Warp corruption source—but he doesn't care. What he wants is the relic's technological value (possibly containing pre-Imperium era Standard Template Construct (STC) template fragments). Whether the Penitents get corrupted is not within his consideration.

### Known Threats

- Deep Hive environment is extremely hostile (total darkness, toxic gas pockets, structural collapse risk)
- Rumored to be a Mutant beast active in the area
- Possibly Warp entities

### Hidden Threats

- The "relic" is a Chaos-corrupted ancient Standard Template Construct (STC) terminal—those who approach gain +1 Corruption per minute
- There is a dormant Warp rift inside the underground temple—if triggered by psychic power or explosion, it activates immediately
- Magnus dispatched a surveillance squad trailing the Penitents—if the Penitents attempt to destroy the relic rather than bring it back, the surveillance squad will intervene

### Resource Ration

Reduced ration: only 2 Medikits, 2 Flares. Magnus believes "pressure makes you more efficient".

### Temptation Note Distribution

- 1 player Note 1
- 1 player Note 2
- 1 player Note 3
- 1 player Note 4

> This is the first mission where all four notes are distributed—maximizing internal team tension.

### Key NPCs

| NPC | Role | Details |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Blind Prophet of the "Chain-Breakers"** | Potential Ally/Enemy | An elder of a Mutant community "Rust Children" living in the Deep Hive. He knows the temple's location but warns that "what's inside is not a relic, it is sin". You may choose to believe him or ignore him |
| **Surveillance Squad** | Potential Threat | 3 Inquisition assault troopers trailing the Penitents. If the Penitents betray the Inquisitor (destroy the relic), they will attack |
