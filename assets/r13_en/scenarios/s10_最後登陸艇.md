## Scenario SB-5: "The Last Landing Craft"

**Difficulty:** Finale | **Location:** Mountain evacuation point  
**Recommended Attributes:** Levels 5-7

### Background

The orbital defenses are down. A distress signal has been sent. An Imperial frigate has answered. The Landing Craft is descending. It can only carry **2 people**.

You and your NPC companion (who survived the previous scenarios). And—the civilians you may have rescued in the ruins (if any). And the black box data—if brought back, it may overturn Exterminatus.

### Finale Calculation

| What you take aboard | Slots occupied | Consequence of leaving behind |
|--------------|---------|-----------|
| Yourself | 1/2 | Stay on the planet, gain a final mission |
| NPC Companion | 1/2 | Companion is trapped on the planet forever. You gain the "Death of a Comrade" rule effect |
| Black box data | 0 (can be carried) | Exterminatus will never be questioned |
| Civilians | Impossible—the Landing Craft has no space | Left to die or to wait for you (you won't return) |

### Inner Voice (given by GM)

> *"Two seats. You and whom?"*

If only one companion survived: the choice becomes "I board, or I let him board" (rule supplement §4.2).

If two companions survived: the choice becomes "Three people, two seats. Who stays?"

### Fate of Those Left Behind

| Who is left | Final Mission |
|---------|---------|
| **Player stays** | Choose one final mission (GM Guide §4.2). Gain Faith +5. Your name will not be remembered—but your choice will |
| **Companion stays** | The companion chooses their own final mission. The GM plays out their farewell. You gain their personal belongings as a relic |
| **Civilians stay** | You give them weapons and supplies. You do not know if they will survive. But you will remember them |

### Takeoff

The Landing Craft takes off. Requiem shrinks outside the window.

If you brought back the black box data—someone in the sector will know the truth.

If you left your companion behind—their face will appear in every dream of every Warp jump.

If you left yourself behind—from then on, a legend lived on this planet.

## GM Adjustment Suggestions

- **Shorten the campaign:** Run only SA-1, SA-3, SA-5 (Mortal) or SB-1, SB-3, SB-5 (Astartes)
- **Increase/decrease difficulty:** ±1 enemy in combat encounters
- **Companion death:** If a companion dies in an early scenario, later scenarios provide a replacement companion. But let the death matter—companions should not be consumables
- **Mixed mode:** Mixing Mortal and Astartes in solo mode is not recommended
