# Scenario 3: A Feast for One

## Basic Info

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★ Advanced (Solo) |
| **Suggested Reputation** | 20-40 |
| **Length** | 4-7 Turns |
| **Core Theme** | Large commission, extreme companion collaboration |

## Story Summary

A letter. The wax seal bears the mayor's stamp—but the contents come from farther away.

The commander at Border Town Watchkeep wants to host a graduation feast for new recruits. Sixty servings of food (or an equivalent order of equipment / potions). The reward is 800G. The deadline is four weeks.

You have only one person. You have one or two companions. Can you finish it?

> **Adaptive Note**: This scenario automatically adjusts the commission based on the player's Class. Cook → banquet; Smith → equipment order; Alchemist → medical supplies; Merchant → resource coordination; Gatherer → rare material supply; Researcher → recipe consultant.

## Key NPCs

| NPC | Role |
|------|------|
| **Commander Harold** | Stern but fair. He doesn't care how small your shop is—he cares whether you can deliver on time |
| **Your companion** | This commission will test their limits. Loyalty changes significantly |

## Turn Design

### Turn 1: Accept the Challenge

- Commander Harold comes to the shop in person. "I've heard of you. You kept this shop going alone. I need someone who can finish within the deadline—not a team. One person."
- The player must decide whether to accept. Accept → advance 200G in material costs. Decline → no penalty, but miss a major opportunity.

### Turns 2-3: Full Sprint

This is the ultimate test of the player's Action Points (AP) management and companion collaboration. The GM should give the player ample room for strategic decisions:

- "Should I do the core part myself and let the apprentice do the basics?"
- "Materials are running low—should I go Foraging or restock?"
- "Pull an all-nighter tonight?" (All-nighter: +1 crafting attempt, but Fatigue +2)

**Companion Spotlight**: In this scenario, each companion should have one "breakthrough contribution"—triggered by the GM based on narrative pacing:
- The apprentice independently makes a Fine-quality item for the first time (boosting morale)
- An old friend solves an unexpected problem with their special skill
- The animal companion finds urgently needed material at a critical moment

### Turn 4: Delivery and Judgment

The commander arrives with two lieutenants to inspect. Quality Check.

| Result | Condition | Outcome |
|------|------|------|
| **Beyond Expectations** | All Fine or above | Harold smiles, rare for him: "You didn't just finish an order. You helped me tell my recruits—what one person can do." Reputation +3d4, bonus 200G. |
| **On Time** | Standard or above | Reward 800G, Reputation +2d4. Harold nods: "As promised."—which is already the highest praise for him. |
| **Barely Passed** | Has Crude but no damage | Reward 600G. Harold doesn't complain, but won't come to you again. |
| **Failed** | Cannot meet deadline or quality too poor | Reputation −2d4, advance not returned. But Harold leaves a line: "There'll be new recruits graduating next year. I'll be back." |

## Rewards

| Achievement | Reward |
|------|------|
| Complete at or above On Time | Watchkeep military contract—a medium order once per season (passive income 30G/week) |
| Companion breakthrough | That companion's permanent Loyalty +2, and Skill +1 |
| Beyond Expectations | Commander introduces noble clients—noble customers unlocked |
