# Scenario 1: A Single Lamp

## Basic Info

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★ Introductory (Solo) |
| **Suggested Reputation** | 0-8 |
| **Length** | 1-3 Turns |
| **Core Theme** | First week of opening, finding your direction, meeting your first companion |

## Story Summary

You have the shop key. When you push the door open, dust dances in the afternoon sunlight. All the previous owner left behind is a cobwebbed counter, two askew chairs, and a yellowed note reading "For Transfer."

You have 500G. You have your tools. You still have your two hands.

No one out on the street knows you yet. Inside, this shop doesn't feel like home yet.

But you know it—every legend begins with the first lamp.

## Key NPCs

| NPC | Role | Key Line |
|-----|------|----------|
| **Baker Betty** | Friendly neighbor | "New here? Wonderful—this street finally has the smell of something baking again. I've got a spare oven; want it?" |
| **Little Stone** | Prospective apprentice | (Standing silently in the doorway, watching your tools. His eyes speak more than his mouth.) |
| **Old Grey** | Stray old hunting hound | (Lying by the back door. His left ear is missing a corner. He looks like he's been waiting a long time.) |

## Turn Design

### Turn 1: Opening

**Setup**: The GM does not roll the Market Fluctuation die—the first week is always steady. Let the player feel the rhythm first.

**GM Guidance**:
- Let the player decide the shop name and first menu / catalog of goods themselves. This isn't a game mechanic—it's the ritual of their first "home."
- Betty comes knocking with fresh bread. She isn't here to buy. She's here to welcome you.
- First customer: a retired miner who looks about your age. He orders the cheapest thing. After drinking it, he's silent a moment, then says, "Haven't tasted that in a long time." He pays. He'll be back tomorrow.

**Event**: Do not roll the Random Event die. The only "event" of the first week is discovering the stray dog at the back door (Old Grey). His leg is a bit lame. You can shoo him away, or leave him a bowl of water.

### Turn 2: The Choice

**Setup**: Roll Market Fluctuation normally.

The player has accumulated some small income. Now comes the first major decision—whom to recruit?

| Option | Content |
|------|------|
| **Take in Old Grey (Animal Companion)** | Spend 5G on dog food and ointment. After Old Grey recovers, Tracking Check +1. Loyalty automatically 10. Does not occupy a companion slot. |
| **Take Little Stone as Apprentice** | Introduced by Betty—this child lost both parents and does odd jobs on the street. He wants to learn a craft. Weekly wage 3G (discounted), starting Loyalty 7. |
| **Both** | If the player has ample funds—but this means less money for upgrades. |

### Turn 3: Standing Firm

- The first companion begins to work. Let the player feel the change of "no longer being alone"—Little Stone's first successful finished product, or Old Grey bringing back the first prey.
- The first tax bill arrives. 10G. Not much—but it reminds you: this isn't retirement. It's the first day of a new life.
- Roll a Random Event die (warm tones preferred).

## Rewards

| Achievement | Reward |
|------|------|
| Complete first transaction | Reputation +1d2 |
| Recruit a companion | Unlock companion system |
| Gain a repeat customer | Base Foot Traffic permanently +1 (in Solo Mode this effect ×2—each repeat customer +2) |
| Name the shop | GM records the shop name—it will appear in later scenarios |
