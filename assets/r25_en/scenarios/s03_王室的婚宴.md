# Scenario 3: The Royal Wedding Banquet

## Basic Info

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | ★★★ Advanced |
| **Suggested Reputation** | 15-40 (Neighborhood Favorite ~ City-Renowned) |
| **Suggested Party** | 3-5 players, at least 1 Cook, recommend a Merchant to assist |
| **Length** | 4-7 turns |

## Synopsis

Royal Capital Edwyna-Princess Selena, daughter of King Edwin III, will marry next month.

The Royal Protocol Office sent out a bid invitation for the wedding banquet to renowned shops across the kingdom. Your shop is on the list.

Not just honor-the winner will receive 2,000G in payment, a royal-certified lifetime advertisement, and a once-in-a-generation Reputation surge.

But you're not the only competitor. At least three shops are eyeing it covetously-one of them a chain restaurant secretly backed by the Myriad Guild.

## Competitors

| Competitor | Background | Strategy |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| **"Golden Platter" Restaurant** | Chain brand, Myriad Guild-funded | Price competition, flaunting rare material stock |
| **Old Chef Augustus** | Retired court chef | Lean on "authentic royal flavor" |
| **Exotic Cuisine "East Wind"** | Famous Seabreeze restaurant | Exotic ingredients and innovative techniques |

## Turn Design

### Turns 1-2: The Bid

**First Stage: Submit Tasting Dishes**

Each bidder must present three dishes before the royal panel. Each dish is a Crafting Check (DC 12/15/18 varying). The panel includes: the Princess herself, the Protocol Office chief, and the Royal Capital food critic.

- The three dishes' average quality. Only Fine or above is competitive
- Merchant may use "Sense the Opportunity" to probe the panel's preferences
- Social Check (DC 14) may be used to lobby before the tasting

**Second Stage: Pricing & Commitment**

If the tasting passes, enter price negotiation. Royal budget 2,000G, but you may propose added value (e.g.: use Legendary ingredients at no extra charge, offer lifetime after-sales service, etc.).

### Turns 3-5: Preparation

After the players win the bid (GM may adjust difficulty so players still have an alternate plot even if they don't win), enter the preparation phase.

**Ingredient Challenge**: Three "designated dishes" on the banquet menu require specific rare materials:

| Dish | Required Materials | How to Obtain |
|------|--------------------|---------------|
| Dragon's Breath Flame Steak | Volcanic sulfur marinade + Red-Horn beef | Purchase or hunt in Anvilhearth |
| Millennia Sap Dessert | Verdantvale millennia sap | Elf Social or self-forage |
| Deep-Sea Glow Platter | Seabreeze glowing cod | Port bid or sail out yourself |

**Time Pressure**: Each dish has a preparation deadline. Past the deadline → quality penalty.

**Events Interleaved**: During preparation, may trigger-competitor sabotage (ingredients stolen), supplier last-minute price hike, Princess changes the menu on short notice (more pressure but also more payment).

### Turn 6: Banquet Day

**High-Pressure Crafting Segment**:
- Must complete the full menu within a time limit (GM sets as N Checks)
- Each Check has time pressure (extra Action Points (AP) cost)
- Critical Failure (nat 3-5) is especially fatal here-cannot redo

**On-Site Adaptation**: GM may insert sudden situations:
- A noble guest suddenly requests a vegetarian version
- The Princess's wedding dress accidentally gets sauce on it (will the players step in to save the day?)
- A dish unexpectedly wins the favor of all the nobles (even if just a side dish)-room for improvisation

### Turn 7: Celebration & Aftermath

**Success**: The royal family issues the "Royal Warrant." Payment 2,000G + bonus (by quality). Reputation +3d6. At least 3 nobles privately hand over their cards.

**Flawed but Passed**: Receive payment but no warrant. The Princess writes a thank-you letter (Reputation +1d6).

**Failure**: If quality is below Standard or fails to finish on time → Reputation-damaged status. But the Protocol Office chief privately says: "There's a chance next year." (opens future plot line)

## Rewards

| Achievement | Reward |
|-------------|--------|
| Royal Warrant | Permanent effect: Royal Capital Foot Traffic +2, noble customers unlocked |
| Perfect Score (all Fine or above) | Princess personally inscribes the shop, Reputation +3d6 |
| Defeat Competitors | Competitors may close or ask for help-players may choose to acquire them |
| Legendary Dish | If any dish reaches Legendary quality-that dish is recorded into the royal recipe book, becoming part of Ynserghard's history |
