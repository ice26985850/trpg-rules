# Hearthside Tales — Material Codex v1.0

> A complete catalog of all known materials on the continent of Ynserghard. Each material is annotated with: Rarity, Origin, Harvest Season, Freshness Decay, Foraging Difficulty (DC), effect per class, and base price.

---

## How to Use

Each material is presented in a card format:

```
[Name] — Rarity | Type | Origin | Harvest Season
Freshness Decay | Foraging Difficulty (DC) | Base Price
Cooking Effect | Potion Effect | Smithing Effect | Research Use
Special: Origin Bonus / Seasonal Change / Storage Method / Note
```

- **Rarity**: Common / Uncommon / Rare / Legendary
- **Freshness**: 1–10, only Ingredients and Herbs
- **Harvest Season**: Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter; mark peak and off seasons
- **Foraging Difficulty (DC)**: 3d6 Check difficulty
- **Price**: base market price (affected by Market Fluctuation ±30%)

# Chapter 1: Ingredients

## Meats and Poultry Eggs

### Herbivorous Beasts

---

**[Plains Boar Meat]** — Common | Ingredient (Meat) | Plains/Grassland/Forest Edge | Abundant in Spring & Autumn, huntable year-round
Freshness: −2 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): Hunt 10 (Small) | Base Price: 5G/unit
- Cooking: Basic meat; Flavor Value +0. At Fine quality or above, gains the "Satiety" effect (Stamina check +1 for 1 scene after eating)
- Potion: No direct medicinal use
- Smithing: None
- Storage: 1 week at room temperature; pickling locks Freshness but Flavor Value −1; cellar extends to 2 weeks
- Note: The most common meat source. Boar meat is firm and suited to stewing.

---

**[Redhorn Beef]** — Uncommon | Ingredient (Meat) | Volcanic Region / Watchkeep Border | Fattest in Autumn, scarce in Winter
Freshness: −2 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): Hunt 14 (Medium) | Base Price: 20G/unit
- Cooking: Flavor Value +2. Dish gains "Minor Fire Resistance" effect (fire damage −1 for 1 scene). Redhorn cattle meat naturally carries a faint fire element
- Potion: Can extract fire-element essence (Alchemy Difficulty (DC) 12, yields "Fire Mote" material)
- Smithing: None
- Storage: Smoked locks Freshness, Flavor Value −1
- Note: Redhorn cattle have foul tempers; mind yourself when hunting—those horns can open a new hole in you.

---

**[Frostwool Mutton]** — Uncommon | Ingredient (Meat) | High Mountains / Snowy Mountains | Abundant in Winter, scarce in Summer
Freshness: −1 per week (naturally rot-resistant) | Foraging Difficulty (DC): Hunt 12 | Base Price: 15G/unit
- Cooking: Flavor Value +1. Dish gains "Warming" effect (Stamina check +2 in cold environments, lasts 2 scenes)
- Potion: Mutton fat can serve as a base for cold-resistance salve
- Smithing: Wool can be made into insulating cloth
- Storage: Naturally rot-resistant; keeps 2 weeks at room temperature
- Note: Frostwool sheep raised by highland herders. The meat is tender with a faint minty coolness—not seasoning, but the grass they eat.

---

**[Gale Antelope Meat]** — Rare | Ingredient (Meat) | Border / Wilderness | Abundant in Spring
Freshness: −2 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): Hunt 16 (Medium, extremely fast) | Base Price: 40G/unit
- Cooking: Flavor Value +3. After eating, agility-related Stamina check +2 for 2 scenes ("Gift of the Gale")
- Potion: Blood can make a speed potion (Potency +1)
- Smithing: None
- Note: The antelope outruns you. You must be smarter than it.

- Potion: Crab shell can be ground into a premium antidote base (Potency +1)
- Smithing: Crab shell fragments can serve as armor-enhancement material (Durability +3)
- Note: Catching Abyssal Crabs requires special trap cages. Every year fishers swear they'll never do it again—then every summer they set sail.

- Cooking: Flavor Value +7. Dish automatically gains "Legendary Potential" (critical success range expands to 15–18, that craft only). Can be named
- Potion: Legendary potion base; all effects ×2
- Smithing: Usable for legendary enchantment (enchantment effect +2 tiers)
- Note: "They take only a few drops each year. Not because there isn't enough—but because there is." — Verdantvale ranger

# Chapter 2: Medicinal Herbs

## Herb Type

---

**[Bloodstop Herb]** — Common | Herb | Plains/Forest | Spring & Summer
Freshness: −1 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): 6 | Base Price: 3G/bundle
- Potion: Base material of healing potion. Chewing alone restores 1d4 HP (crude but effective)
- Cooking: Can go into soup—dish gains faint recovery effect
- Note: The most common herb. The Snuffed Lantern (Adventurers' Guild) standard first-aid kit always has a handful of Bloodstop Herb.

---

**[Silverleaf Mint]** — Uncommon | Herb | Forest/High Mountains | Abundant in Spring
Freshness: −1 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): 10 | Base Price: 12G/bundle
- Potion: Core material of antidote. Potency +1. Can be brewed alone as tea to ease mild poisoning
- Cooking: Flavor Value +1. Cooling taste—used in seafood dishes to remove fishiness
- Note: The back of Silverleaf Mint leaves shows silver under moonlight—hence the name. It is the herb Verdantvale elves trade most.

---

**[Dragon's Tongue Herb]** — Rare | Herb | Volcanic Region / Dragon Activity Zones | Summer
Freshness: −1 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): 16 (often grows in dangerous areas) | Base Price: 45G/plant
- Potion: Core material of strength potion. Potency +2. Side-effect chance +5% (potent medicine)
- Cooking: Tiny amount can enter dishes—Flavor Value +1, dish gains "Faint Dragon's Breath" (small flames on exhale, decorative only)
- Note: Dragon's Tongue Herb only grows where dragons frequently pass. Hunters joke: "When you see Dragon's Tongue Herb, what you should worry about isn't how to gather it—it's whether the dragon is still nearby."

---

**[Dream Moss]** — Rare | Herb | Deep Forest / Ruins | Spring only
Freshness: −2 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): 14 | Base Price: 50G/portion
- Potion: Core material of insight draught. Potency +2. Can make sleep potion
- Cooking: Tiny use—Flavor Value +2, after eating dreams are unusually vivid (character may have prophetic dream, GM's discretion)
- Note: Grows only on certain tree species in Spring. Elves say Dream Moss absorbs the tree's dreams—so moss on old trees is strongest.

---

**[Ghost Orchid]** — Legendary | Herb | Ruins / Ancient Battlefield | Winter only
Freshness: −1 per week | Foraging Difficulty (DC): 20 | Base Price: 200G/each
- Potion: Core material of invisibility/illusion potion. Potency +4
- Cooking: Legendary ingredient—Flavor Value +5, dish gains "Interwoven Reality" effect (eater hard to track for 1 scene)
- Note: Ghost Orchid is only visible under moonlight—transparent by day. Said to grow where someone once died, absorbing unfinished wishes.

# Chapter 3: Ores and Metals

## Basic Ores

---

**[Iron Ore]** — Common | Ore | Mountains / Mines | Year-round
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 8 | Base Price: 5G/chunk
- Smithing: Basic metal material. Can forge iron sword (DC 9), iron shield (DC 9), various tools
- Note: The bedrock of civilization. Your first sword, first pot, first iron door—all begin with one chunk of iron ore.

---

**[Copper Ore]** — Common | Ore | Mountains | Year-round
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 8 | Base Price: 4G/chunk
- Smithing: Basic decoration/magic-conduction material. Copper pot (Cooking Difficulty (DC) −1), copper trinkets
- Potion: Copper ions have mild antibacterial effect
- Note: Copper is magic's good friend—magic-item base frames often use copper.

---

**[Coal]** — Common | Fuel | Mines / Mountains | Year-round
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 6 | Base Price: 2G/bag
- Smithing: Basic fuel. Consumes 1 bag per major craft. No coal → Crafting Difficulty (DC) +2 (insufficient heat)
- Note: Dwarves say coal is "the remains of ancient forests." Humans say dwarves overthinking.

---

## Rare Metals

---

**[Mithril]** — Rare | Ore | High Mountains / Deep Dwarf Mines | Year-round
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 16 | Base Price: 100G/chunk
- Smithing: Mithril weapon—half weight, Stamina requirement −1; Mithril armor—same defense but no stealth disadvantage
- Enchantment: Mithril weapon has +1 natural enchantment slot (does not occupy quality slot)
- Potion: Mithril powder can make anti-magic potion (Potency +1)
- Note: A dream material for dwarf smiths. Anvilhearth dwarves say: "To forge one Mithril work in your life is a life well lived."

---

**[Adamantine]** — Rare | Ore | Deep Volcanic Layers / Meteorite Crater | Year-round
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 18 | Base Price: 120G/chunk
- Smithing: Adamantine weapon—damage die +1 tier and ignores 1 point of defense; Adamantine armor—defense +1 (stacks with quality bonus)
- Note: Rarer than Mithril, and heavier. Not for your elegant rapier—for the great hammer that cracks dragon scales.

---

**[Deepsea Fine Iron]** — Rare | Ore | Deep Sea | Summer (brought by currents)
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 18 (deep-sea salvage) | Base Price: 80G/chunk
- Smithing: Deepsea Fine Iron weapon—Durability cap +5; armor—Durability cap +5
- Origin Bonus: From Seabreeze waters → Ice enchantment effect +1
- Note: Sunk at the sea bottom for a thousand years, tempered by pressure and salt countless times. When hauled up, usually covered in barnacles—but inside the shell, the iron still shines.

---

## Legendary Ores

---

**[Sky Meteoric Iron]** — Legendary | Ore | Meteorite Crater | Random (after meteorite fall events)
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 20 | Base Price: 350G/chunk
- Smithing: Meteoric iron weapon—weight −50%, damage die +1 tier, inherently carries "Star" enchantment (night attack +2)
- Enchantment: Naturally has 2 enchantment slots (does not occupy quality slot)
- Note: It wasn't dug from underground. It fell from the sky. Dwarves say meteoric iron holds the memory of stars. Elves say it's the fork the Wanderer God dropped from the heavenly table.

---

**[Dragonbone Ore]** — Legendary | Ore | Dragon Lair / Dragon Boneyard | No season
Foraging Difficulty (DC): 22 (or obtain the dragon's permission) | Base Price: 500G/chunk
- Smithing: Dragonbone weapon—base damage +2, Durability cap +10. Dragonbone armor—defense +2
- Enchantment: Dragonbone naturally carries fire affinity (fire enchantment effect +2 tiers)
- Ethical Note: Dragonbone Ore is the mineralized remains of ancient dragon corpses. Many dragons see it as desecration of ancestors. Consider before use—would you dig up a dragon's ancestral grave for power?
- Note: Ember (the old dragon in Scenario 4) knows the location of every dragon boneyard. He won't tell you—unless you're trustworthy.

# Chapter 5: Special Materials

---

Foraging Difficulty (DC): 14 | Base Price: 20G/each
- General: Ice-magic refrigeration (consumes 1/each use); enchantment consumption (1/slot); magic item recharging

Foraging Difficulty (DC): 20 (timing extremely rare) | Base Price: No market—cannot be bought or sold

- Note: Phoenix Ash left after rebirth exists only a few minutes before scattering on the wind. You must be on site—and phoenixes usually dislike an audience.
