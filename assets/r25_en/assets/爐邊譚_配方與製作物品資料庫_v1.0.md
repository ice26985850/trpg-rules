# Chapter 1: Recipe Database

## 1.1 Cook Recipes (10)

### Basic Recipes (DC 9)

---

**[Homestyle Stew]**
- Materials: Plains Boar Meat ×1 + any vegetable ×2 + Hundred-Flower Honey ×1
- Result: Flavor Value +1. After eating, gains "Satiety" (Stamina check +1, 1 scene)
- Fine: Flavor Value +2, "Satiety" effect extended to 2 scenes
- Superior: Flavor Value +3, additionally restores 1 Fatigue
- Legendary: Flavor Value +5, gains naming rights. "This isn't stew—this is the taste of home." — Ironfire

---

**[Adventurer's Bento]**
- Materials: any meat ×1 + Plains Wheat ×1 + Hundred-Flower Honey ×1
- Result: Flavor Value +0. Shelf life 3 days (field carry). After eating, restores 1 Fatigue
- Fine: Shelf life 5 days, restores 2 Fatigue
- Superior: Shelf life 7 days, gains "Full of Energy" (next any check +1)
- Legendary: Shelf life 14 days, gains "Pre-Battle Feast" (advantage on next combat)
- Note: Adventurers' favorite takeout option. After retirement you finally don't have to eat your own bento.

---

**[Grilled Meat Platter]**
- Materials: any meat ×2 + Volcano Salt ×1
- Result: Flavor Value +2. After eating, Stamina check +2 (1 scene)
- Fine: Flavor Value +3, +1 scene extra
- Superior: Flavor Value +4, gains fire resistance (fire damage −2)
- Legendary: Flavor Value +6, "Lingering Dragonbreath"—sparks on exhale (decorative + intimidate Social +1)

---

**[Seafood Soup]**
- Materials: River Fish ×1 + Silverleaf Mint ×1 + Hundred-Flower Honey ×1
- Result: Flavor Value +1. After eating, immune to mild poisoning (1 scene)
- Fine: Flavor Value +2, immune to moderate poisoning
- Superior: Flavor Value +3, restores all Fatigue
- Legendary: Flavor Value +5, "Tidal Memory"—Social with sea creatures +2 for 24 hours

## 1.4 Merchant Recipes (8)

Merchant recipes are mostly "bundles" or "value-added services"—repackaging, haggling, and reselling other classes' products.

---

**[Travel Supply Pack]** — Difficulty (DC) 9
- Materials: Adventurer's Bento ×1 + Healing Potion ×1 + Simple Antidote ×1
- Result: Bundle sells at 1.2× the total of individual items. Adventurer customers' purchase willingness +50%
- Note: "Buying separately is cheaper, but they're always too lazy to visit three shops." — Little Finger's notes

---

**[Noble Gift Box]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: any Fine-or-above dish ×2 + any Fine-or-above potion ×1
- Result: Exquisite packaging, sells at 1.5× total of individual items. Noble customers' Satisfaction automatically +1 tier
- Fine packaging: +2.0×
- Note: The box costs more than the contents. But nobles buy the box.

---

**[War-Prep Supply Crate]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: Strength Potion ×2 + Healing Potion ×3 + Travel Supply Pack ×2
- Result: Bundle sells at 1.3× total of individual items. Top choice for adventurer-team bulk purchase
- Note: Favorite of Snuffed Lantern (Adventurers' Guild) group orders. Low profit but steady volume.

---

**[Seasonal Limited Bundle]** — Difficulty (DC) 9 (adjusted by seasonal content)
- Materials: that season's specialty ×3 + any dish ×2
- Result: Limited feel + story packaging → sells at ×1.4. Foot Traffic +2
- Note: Spring "Hanami Bento", Summer "Adventurer Picnic Basket", Autumn "Harvest Gift Box", Winter "Warm Winter Hearth Set"

---

**[Rare Material Procurement Service]** — Difficulty (DC) 12 (Social)
- Not a good, a service. The Merchant uses connections to find specified rare materials for clients, charging a 20% procurement fee
- Fine (Social Difficulty (DC) 17+): finds a source below market price; both client and you are happy

---

**[Cross-City Futures Contract]** — Difficulty (DC) 15
- Locks a specific material's price for the next 3 turns. If Market Fluctuation turns against you—the contract protects you. If in your favor—you've locked the ceiling
- Merchant's "Sense the Opportunity" skill is extremely valuable here

---

**[Shop Collaboration Product]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Collaborate with another shop to launch a co-branded product (e.g., your potion + their packaging)
- Both shops get Foot Traffic +1 and Reputation +1d4

---

**[Royal Warrant Application]** — Difficulty (DC) 18 (Legendary Social challenge)
- Apply to the royal protocol office for "Warrant Certification". Requires submitting product + Reputation 30+ + Social Difficulty (DC) 18
- Success: permanent "Royal Warrant" tag—that product category sells at +30%, noble customers auto-unlocked
- Note: This isn't done in a day. It's trust accumulated from many commissions.

---

**[Field Foraging Kit]** — Difficulty (DC) 9

- Materials: Leather ×1 + Silverleaf Mint (dried) ×1 + Lichen Moss ×1
- Result: Herb Freshness decay changed to −1 per 3 weeks (normally −1 per week). Reusable 5 times
- Note: A traveler's essential for gatherers.

---

**[Hunter's Mark]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: Giant Spider Silk Sac ×1 + Wood ×1
- Result: Marks a specific prey. Next hunt of that prey gets Difficulty (DC) −3
- Note: The ancient craft of trackers. Not magic—patience.

---

**[Field First-Aid Kit]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: Bloodstop Herb ×2 + Silverleaf Mint ×1 + Lichen Moss ×1
- Result: Allows one emergency treatment in the wild (minor injury recovery time halved, medium injury downgraded to minor). One-time consumption
- Note: Deep in every gatherer's pack there's one. Hope it's never needed.

---

**[Portable Drying Rack]** — Difficulty (DC) 9

**[Prey Tracking Map]** — Difficulty (DC) 15
- Materials: paper & pen + at least 3 successful hunts in that region
- Result: Records specific region's prey migration routes. Hunting in that region gets permanent Difficulty (DC) −2

- Note: Steel Bristle said this was the most valuable thing his master left him—not his hunting knife, but a stack of worn maps.

---

**[Legendary Hunting Ground Notes]** — Difficulty (DC) 18
- Materials: successful legendary-prey hunt experience ×1 + paper & pen
- Result: Records a legendary prey's habits, weaknesses, and haunts. Next hunt of the same legendary prey gets Difficulty (DC) −5
- Note: You may be the only person in the world who knows a certain legendary creature's weakness. That knowledge is priceless—who will you pass it to?

## 1.6 Researcher Recipes (8)

---

**[Basic Recipe Analysis Notes]** — Difficulty (DC) 9
- Materials: any basic-recipe product ×1 (consumed)
- Result: Gains the recipe's basic principles. Future R&D of similar recipes gets Difficulty (DC) −1
- Note: Old Bookworm's basement is stacked with these notes. He no longer remembers how many breads he's analyzed.

---

**[Material Codex Page]** — Difficulty (DC) 9
- Materials: any material ×1 (not consumed) + paper & pen
- Result: Records all known attributes of that material in detail. Using that material gives related check +1
- Note: A complete Material Codex may take years—but every page has value.

---

**[Recipe Improvement Draft]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: any existing recipe + 1 Inspiration Point (IP) + paper & pen
- Result: An improved version of the recipe—material cost −1 (minimum 1) or Difficulty (DC) −1. Takes effect after experimental verification (1 successful craft)
- Failure: wrong improvement direction; recipe unusable this turn

---

**[Ancient Text Decryption]** — Difficulty (DC) 15
- Materials: ancient document/relic + at least 1 week of research
- Result: Decrypts ancient recipe or historical intel. GM decides content
- Critical Success: discovers a complete lost recipe

---

**[Patent Document]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: Custom Recipe ×1 + 50G filing fee + 1 week wait
- Result: Recipe gains legal protection. For 1 year, imitators must pay 50% of profit
- Note: A researcher's livelihood—and how researchers make enemies.

---

**[Cross-Discipline Recipe Index]** — Difficulty (DC) 12
- Materials: holds recipes from at least 3 different classes
- Result: Discovers cross-discipline common principles. All recipe R&D Difficulty (DC) −1 (permanent)
- Every 3 new recipes added, this bonus −1 again (max −5)
- Note: Old Bookworm's lifelong plan—compile all of Ynserghard's recipes into one book. He may never finish it. But he says: "Better that it's never finished."

---

**[Academic Paper]** — Difficulty (DC) 14
- Materials: original discovery + 1 Inspiration Point (IP) + 1 week writing
- Result: On publication gains 2d6 Reputation. Critical Success → additionally gains "Academic Authority" title (all checks in that field +1)
- Note: After publication there is peer review—scholars may challenge your conclusions. That's good—debate is scholarship.

---

**[Lost Technology Reconstruction]** — Difficulty (DC) 18
- Materials: ancient relic ×1 + 3 Inspiration Points (IP) + at least 2 weeks research
- Result: Unlocks an ancient lost technology (GM adjudicates specifics—could be aircraft blueprints, eternal-energy principles, or ancient weapon construction)
- Note: This isn't just a recipe—it may change Ynserghard's historical course. Part of the Wanderer God's cookbook isn't cooking—it's technology.
