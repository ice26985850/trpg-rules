# Hearthside Tales — Solo Mode Rules Supplement v1.0

> *Hearthside Tales: Solo Shopkeeping Rules*
>
> One player + one host. A small shop. A story.

---

## Why This Supplement?

Hearthside Tales' core rules assume 3–6 players running a shop cooperatively. When a single player opens a shop alone, the following mechanics break down:

| Problem | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| Economy too slow | Solo weekly income is only 5–15G; leveling feels out of reach |
| Insufficient class coverage | One person has only one class, unable to handle diverse orders |
| Action Points (AP) bottleneck | Stamina ×2 is not enough for foraging + crafting + socializing at once |
| Cooperative mechanics fail | No Support; all advanced crafting falls on you alone |
| Scenarios ill-suited | Original scenarios assume team division of labor (e.g., "The Royal Wedding Banquet" requires multi-class cooperation) |

This supplement provides a lightweight set of changes so the solo mode meshes with the original rules with minimal friction—without altering the core 3d6 framework or adding complex subsystems.

## Chapter 1: Core Adjustments

> **Cites core rules:** *Player Handbook* Chapter 1 "Basic Rules" in full.
> The following adjustments override or supplement specific entries of the above chapters.

### 1.1 Action Points (AP) Adjustment

The solo player gains extra Action Points (AP), representing the resilience of "one person shouldering the whole shop":

```
Solo Action Points (AP) = Stamina × 2 + 2
```

| Stamina | Original Action Points (AP) | Solo Action Points (AP) |
|---------|-----------------------------|-------------------------|
| 2 | 4 | 6 |
| 3 | 6 | 8 |
| 4 | 8 | 10 |
| 5 | 10 | 12 |

### 1.2 Foot Traffic Adjustment

The solo shop's Foot Traffic formula changes to the more stable `2d4` (replacing the original `1d6`), average 5 (original average 3.5):

```
Foot Traffic = 2d4 + Reputation mod + Location mod + Shop Level mod
```

### 1.3 Base Revenue Adjustment

The solo mode's base revenue increases to **10G/week** (originally 5G/week):

> **Cite:** *Player Handbook* §4.3 "Revenue Formula"—base revenue changed from 5G to 10G.

### 1.4 Upgrade Cost (Solo Only)

| Level | Funds (Gold) | Reputation | Maintenance |
|-------|--------------|------------|-------------|
| Lv.1 | — | 0 | 8G |
| Lv.2 | 300G | 8 | 15G |
| Lv.3 | 800G | 20 | 30G |
| Lv.4 | 2,000G | 40 | 60G |
| Lv.5 | 5,000G | 70 | 120G |

> **Cite:** *Player Handbook* §2.4 "Shop Upgrade"—this table replaces the original upgrade table.

### 1.5 Recipe Difficulty Flexibility

The solo player may use basic recipes of any type (not limited to their own class), but recipes outside their class get Difficulty (DC) +2:

- Cook making a basic potion: Difficulty (DC) 9+2 = 11
- Alchemist making a basic dish: Difficulty (DC) 9+2 = 11
- Advanced and above recipes are still restricted to your own class (or covered by an NPC companion)

> **Cite:** *Player Handbook* Chapter 3 "Crafting & Signature Skills" §3.1 Recipe Difficulty Table—non-class basic recipes get Difficulty (DC) +2.

## Chapter 2: NPC Companion System

NPC companions are the core of solo mode—they are not a "second player" but lightweight support characters that help fill class gaps.

### 2.1 Companion Slots

| Shop Level | Companion Cap |
|------------|---------------|
| Lv.1 | 1 |
| Lv.2 | 2 |
| Lv.3 | 2 |
| Lv.4 | 3 |
| Lv.5 | 3 |

### 2.2 Companion Types

| Type | Role | Specialty | Weekly Wage |
|------|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Apprentice** | Growth-type helper | 1 class (basic recipes, Quality Cap ★★) | 5G |
| **Old Friend** | Steady companion | 1 class (basic + advanced recipes, Quality Cap ★★★) | 15G |
| **Hireling** | Labor-type | No class; provides +2 Action Points (AP)/week (does not use companion actions) | 10G |
| **Animal Companion** | Support-type | Foraging/Hunting bonus; does not occupy a companion slot | 5G |
| **Partner** | Senior-type (Lv.3+) | 1 class (all recipes, Quality Cap ★★★★) | 30G + 10% profit |

### 2.3 Companion Action Rules

Each NPC companion has 2 Action Points (AP) per week (separate from the player's Action Points (AP)). They may perform the following actions:

| Action | Action Points (AP) | Description |
|--------|--------------------|-------------|
| Assist Crafting (Support) | 1 | Provides an assist check for the Lead (same Support rules as core rules) |
| Independent Crafting (Basic Recipe) | 1 | Uses the companion's own class for a Difficulty (DC) 9 basic craft |
| Independent Crafting (Advanced Recipe) | 2 | Only Old Friend/Partner may use; Difficulty (DC) 12 |
| Simple Foraging | 2 | Produces Common materials ×2 (no check required) |
| Mind the Shop | 1 | Keeps the shop open while the player is away (base foot traffic, Quality Cap ★★) |
| Social | 1 | Maintains supplier relations, gathers market intelligence |

> **Cites core rules:** *Player Handbook* §1.4 "Cooperative Crafting Check"—when a companion acts as Support, use the full Support rules (DC 10 assist check).

### 2.4 Companion Loyalty

| Loyalty | Status | Effect |
|---------|--------|--------|
| 8–10 | Loyal | +1 to quality check when spending Action Points (AP) |
| 5–7 | Stable | None |
| 3–4 | Unstable | 50% chance to refuse dangerous tasks |
| 1–2 | On the Verge of Leaving | May quit; requires a Social Difficulty (DC) 14 to retain |

Paying on time each week +1. Completing a shared goal (successful commission/defeating a competitor) +1. Putting a companion in danger −2. Withholding wages −3.

### 2.5 Companion Growth

Apprentices and Old Friends accumulate experience when successfully completing checks using their own class. Every 10 successes → raise one attribute +1 (up to the cap shown for that type). Partners do not grow (already at their peak).

## Chapter 3: Solo Risk Adjustments

> **Cites core rules:** *GM Guide* §3.2 "Risk & Conflict System". The following are solo-mode adjustments.

### 3.1 Lowered Encounter Difficulty

When solo and traveling, the encounter check's Difficulty (DC) drops by 2:

| Original Difficulty | Original Difficulty (DC) | Solo Difficulty (DC) |
|---------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Safe | 7 | 5 |
| Ordinary | 10 | 8 |
| Dangerous | 13 | 11 |
| Lethal | 16 | 14 |
| Cataclysm | 19 | 17 |

### 3.2 Hunting Adjustments

When solo hunting, Tracking Difficulty (DC) drops by 2 (smaller target, more cautious hunting strategy). In the outcome table, "Barely Got It" becomes "Ordinary Catch" (no quality penalty, no minor injury).

### 3.3 Companion Cover

When the player faces risk of injury, if a companion is present, they may spend 1 of the companion's Action Points (AP) to provide "Cover"—the companion takes the injury in the player's place (companion Loyalty −1).

## Chapter 4: Quick Start Guide

### Extra Solo Character Creation Steps

After the *Player Handbook* Chapter 2 eight-step character creation process, add:

**Step 9: Choose Starting Companion**

Choose one of the following (no Funds cost; this is a starting benefit):

| Companion | Type | Class | One-liner |
|-----------|------|-------|-----------|
| Little Stone | Apprentice | Smith | Dwarf orphan; deft hands but quiet |
| Loren | Old Friend | Alchemist | Human herbologist; retired and looking for something to do |
| Old Grey | Animal Companion | Hunting Hound | Retired hunting dog; nose keener than anyone you've met |

Or the GM may allow the player to customize a starting companion (limited to Apprentice or Old Friend types, using the companion card template).

### Starting Funds Adjustment

Solo starting Funds (Gold) raised to **500G** (originally 300–500G; the upper bound is taken to buffer solo risk).
