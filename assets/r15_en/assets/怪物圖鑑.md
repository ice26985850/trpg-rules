# The Backrooms Administration Bureau TRPG — Entity Bestiary / Monster Compendium

> Contains complete data for 10 major Backrooms Entities, for quick GM reference

| TL | Description | DC Bonus | Boss Rule |
|:--:|------|:------:|:------:|
| 1 | Minor — can be easily avoided or poses no threat | +0 | No |
| 2 | Common — cautious handling allows safe passage | +5 | No |

## Entity Attack Casualty Rate (Encounter Combat Reference)

This game does not use traditional hit points (HP). The consequences of an Entity's "attack" are represented by a Casualty Check. When an exploration team is attacked by an Entity, the base attack casualty rate is determined by the Entity's Threat Level (adjusted by equipment, medical, and doctrine; each attacked Explorer independently rolls d100):

| Entity Threat Level (TL) | Base Attack Casualty Rate |
|:--:|:--:|
| TL1 | 10% |
| TL2 | 20% |
| TL3 | 35% |
| TL4 | 40% |
| TL5 | 60% |

> Full rules (immediate injuries, team action options, Status Effects) are in the GM Rulebook, Chapter 2, sections 2.9–2.11.

## Entity #1: Smilers

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL2 (TL3 when unlit) |
| **Spawn Area** | Dark areas of Level 0–3 |
| **DC Bonus** | +5 (+10 when poorly lit) |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 25% |
| **Trait** | A glowing smiley face that emerges in darkness. No physical form — it is some presence between light and dark |

**Special Rules**:
- **Light and Dark**: Whenever any light source fails, TL immediately rises to 3, lasting until the light source is restored
- **Smile Spread**: When an Explorer in the team has the "Trauma" or "Breakdown" status, DC +5
- **Mostly Faced**: Smilers usually appear in groups of 1d3+1

**Countermeasures**: Adequate lighting allows safe passage. High-power flashlight (BE-02) provides a bonus to resist

**GM Narrative Advice**: Do not describe "the Smiler suddenly appearing" — describe the lights beginning to flicker unstably, and in the instant between light and dark, the players glimpse something smiling at the end of the corridor

## Entity #2: Partygoers (/ =)))

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL4 |
| **Spawn Area** | Level Fun only |
| **DC Bonus** | +20 |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 90% (almost certain to be encountered within Level Fun) |
| **Trait** | Humanoid creatures with yellow skin and cartoonish smiling faces. Convert humans through "invitation" |

**Special Rules**:
- **Friendly Face**: On first encounter, a Will Contest can be made (d100 ≥ 50); failure means being lured to follow
- **Party Rules**: Within Level Fun, retreat rules do not apply — one must complete the "party game" to leave
- **Irreversible Conversion**: Converted Explorers are permanently removed from the roster
- **Team Stress Effect**: For each "Nervous" or "Trauma" Explorer in the team, DC +3

**GM Warning**: Do not let Partygoers become a routine encounter. They should be synonymous with "you might permanently lose someone." Once an Explorer is converted — it's over. There is no rescue mission.

## Entity #3: Death Moths

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL5 (Boss) |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 5, large enclosed spaces |
| **DC Bonus** | +30 |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 15% |
| **Trait** | Giant moths with a 3-meter wingspan. Silent flight, highly acidic bodily fluids |

**Special Rules**:
- **Oviposition**: A heavily wounded Explorer has a 30% chance of being implanted with eggs. The implanted Explorer must pass a Medical Check at DC 80 within d6 days, or die
- **Nest**: Usually appears in groups of d3+1. The nest queen uses Boss Three-Phase rules
- **Silent Hunter**: Ignores doctrine when an encounter triggers (Cautious cannot reduce the encounter rate)

**Countermeasures**: High-frequency sonic emitter (EE-03) provides +3 casualty-rate resistance

**Boss Three-Phase**:
- Phase One (100%) — aerial attack, 3 DC 85 contests
- Phase Two (50%) — ground melee, acid spray, 2 DC 90 contests
- Phase Three (25%) — death-throes oviposition frenzy, 1 DC 95 contest. Failure → all present must make a forced oviposition check

## Entity #4: Facelings

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL1 (Aggressive type TL3) |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 1–4 |
| **DC Bonus** | +0 (Aggressive type +10) |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 40% |
| **Trait** | Faceless humanoid creatures. Mostly neutral or even friendly |

**Special Rules**:
- **Aggressive Check**: On encounter, roll d6; 5–6 indicates the aggressive type
- **Mirror Trick**: A team carrying small mirrors gets −5% casualty rate against Facelings (they are confused by their own reflection)
- **Child Facelings**: 20% chance to meet a child version (non-aggressive), which can be brought back to an Outpost — Intelligence permanently +1, but requires care

**GM Narrative Advice**: Facelings are the most "non-terrifying" Entity in the Backrooms — which is exactly what makes them frightening. They are too human. In a dimly lit corridor, you may not be able to tell a Faceling from a masked colleague from behind.

## Entity #5: Hounds

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL3 |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 0–3 |
| **DC Bonus** | +10 (pack +15) |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 30% |
| **Trait** | Large quadruped predators. Pack hunting, high-speed movement |

**Special Rules**:
- **Pack Hunting Instinct**: When the team has a wounded member, TL rises to 4
- **Howl Intimidation**: Non-elite Explorers who hear the howl must make a Will Contest (d100 ≥ 60); failure imposes −5 personal bonus
- **Tracking Scent**: Once locked on, 1 unit of Almond Water must be used to erase the scent to escape. Otherwise the subsequent encounter rate is doubled (×2)

**Countermeasures**: Erase scent with Almond Water, climb to high ground, or use loud noise to temporarily drive them off

## Entity #6: Skin-Stealers

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL4 |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 1–3 |
| **DC Bonus** | +20 |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 10% |
| **Trait** | Can perfectly mimic human appearance. Flays victims' skin for disguise |

**Special Rules**:
- **Infiltrator (GM use)**: Before departure, secretly roll d100; if ≥ 90, one Explorer in the team has already been replaced
- **Skin Memory**: Can pass basic identity checks. The replaced person acts like the original — but gives vague answers to "emotional" questions
- **Exposure Stun**: When exposed, the Skin-Stealer freezes for 1 turn — the team gains 1 free attack/escape opportunity

**GM Warning**: This is the strongest psychological horror element in the game. The replaced NPC should betray at a critical moment — not randomly, but at the moment most capable of causing harm. Give players clues, but not too obvious ones.

## Entity #7: Wretches

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL2 |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 1–2 |
| **DC Bonus** | +5 |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 35% |
| **Trait** | Were once human. Completely mentally broken in the Backrooms. Constantly wailing |

**Special Rules**:
- **Soul Echo**: Staying near Wretches for more than 30 minutes, the whole team automatically triggers a Psychological Check (d100 ≥ 50); failure → Stress +1
- **Someone You Know**: 10% chance the Wretch is a former Explorer known to someone in the team (GM selects from the deceased roster)
- **Salvage Possible**: Can attempt to bring back to an Outpost for treatment. After 3 months, 30% chance to recover as a Trainee Explorer

**GM Narrative Advice**: The horror of Wretches is not in the threat — it's that they remind you: this is the Backrooms' end for humans. Every Wretch once had a name, a contract, a Director's signature. Perhaps one day, one of your Explorers will become one of them.

|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL3 |
| **Spawn Area** | Level 2 pipe maze |
| **DC Bonus** | +10 (+20 in narrow pipes) |
| **Base Encounter Rate** | 20% |
| **Trait** | A giant mass of flesh formed by the fusion of multiple human limbs. Creeps through pipes, absorbing everything |

**Special Rules**:
- **Unstoppable**: Ignores ordinary knockback and physical attacks (must flee rather than fight)
- **Absorption Growth**: For each person (including Entities) absorbed, all subsequent encounters gain DC +2

**Countermeasures**: It cannot turn quickly — use the winding structure of the pipes to escape

## Entity #10: The Hollow

| Attribute | Details |
|------|------|
| **Threat Level** | TL5 (Unbeatable) |
| **Spawn Area** | Any Level (extremely rare, 2% encounter rate) |
| **DC Bonus** | +30 |
| **Trait** | An invisible, untouchable existential threat. Devours "Presence" |

**Special Rules**:
- **Existence Erosion**: Every 10 minutes, a random Explorer's "Presence" −1 (starts at 10). Those who reach zero vanish from everyone's memory — as if they never existed (detailed rules and GM handling of "Erasure" are in the GM Rulebook, section 2.11 Presence rules)
- **Emotional Anchor**: An Explorer carrying a Cherished Keepsake (SPC-05) gains a one-time +20 resist bonus. After use, the keepsake shatters
- **Power of Records**: Continuously writing or recording (describing once every 5 minutes) can lower the contest DC by −10
- **Unbeatable**: The Hollow cannot be "defeated." The mission objective is always survival and evacuation

**GM Ultimate Warning**: The Hollow is not a monster — it is a conceptual threat. Never describe the Hollow's "appearance." Describe the Explorers' perception: memories begin to blur, they can't recall a colleague's name, they are unsure whether that person was always in the team. If you let an Explorer be "erased" — make it the most unforgettable moment of the entire campaign. The forgotten do not merely disappear in the game — other Explorers should genuinely no longer remember them. In later play, players should stumble upon an empty bunk, unclaimed equipment — and wonder whose it was.
