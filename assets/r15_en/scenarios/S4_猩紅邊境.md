# S4: Crimson Frontier

> **Campaign**: Faction War (Campaign Three · Opening)
> **Recommended Level**: 8–9
> **Difficulty Class (DC)**: 85 (Hard)
> **Mission Type**: Diplomatic Mission (DPL) → Combat Encounter
> **Estimated Time**: 90–120 minutes

---

## Opening Description

Your Intel Officer walks into the office with a grave expression. His tablet displays a map of Level 4 — your jurisdiction. New markers on the map. Red markers.

> Su Ming: "The Crimson Order. They have established outposts in three areas of Level 4 within the past week. This is not recon — this is occupation."

Your comm terminal simultaneously receives a message from M.E.G.: **"Confirm identity. Assess intent. If hostile — authorized to use force."**

The war has not started. But the first shot will be fired by you.

---

## Scene Flow

### Scene One: Strategy Meeting — Three Paths

**Scene Description**: Your core team sits around the conference table. Level 4 maps are scattered across it. Everyone has their own view.

> Veteran Explorer "Chen Gang": "They planted a flag at our doorstep. You think we should sit down and have tea with them?"
> Elite Explorer "Hye-yeon" (Specialty: Negotiation): "Chen Gang, we don't know their strength. Negotiate first — if talks break down, then fight. At least by then we know their cards."

**Decision Points for the Three Routes**:

| Route | Mission Sequence | Advantage | Risk |
|------|----------|------|------|
| **Diplomatic Contact** | First send a negotiation team → negotiate → act on result | Lowest casualties, possible Intel gain | May be seen as weakness |
| **Forced Expulsion** | Full action → clear outposts | Swift resolution | High casualty risk, may escalate to full war |
| **Covert Observation** | Dispatch recon team → no contact → gather Intel → decide later | Maximum information gain | May lose initiative during observation period |

### Scene Two: Outpost Contact (using the "Diplomatic Contact" route as example)

**Scene Description**: An abandoned office building at Level 4. The fluorescent lights in the areas occupied by the Crimson Order have been replaced with dark red — their signature color.

Two guards stand at the door — soldiers in black tactical gear with red armbands.

> Guard Captain: "People of the Bureau. We've been expecting you. The Supreme Inquisitor said — you would come. Please enter."

**Diplomacy Check**: DC 75 (actual value). Negotiation specialty provides +5.

**Negotiation Content**:
- Crimson Order's demand: the Bureau withdraw from Level 4's East District (which they call the "Reclamation Zone")
- Their reason: the area has an "unstable exit" that needs to be managed by "those qualified"
- Your options:
  1. Accept withdrawal (avoid conflict but lose territory)
  2. Propose co-management (Diplomacy DC 85 — a higher-difficulty negotiation)
  3. Refuse and warn (may trigger conflict)

### Scene Three: Escalation of Conflict (optional, depends on diplomatic result)

If negotiations break down or the player chooses the force route:

**Combat Encounter — Outpost Assault**: DC 90 (equivalent difficulty to Entity hunting). At least 1 forced Casualty Check.

Tactics of Crimson Order soldiers:
- Use office cubicles for guerrilla warfare
- Use smoke grenades and flash-bang grenades (Tech Equipment effects)
- If on the brink of failure, activate "self-destruct protocol" — destroy data inside the outpost so the Bureau cannot obtain Intel

**Key Rewards** (depending on result):
- Capture Crimson Order comm equipment: decrypt their comm frequencies (Intelligence +3)
- Discover documents related to "The Unifier": a clue laying groundwork for Campaign Four
- Captives: can be interrogated (gain extra Intel, but may violate M.E.G.'s humanitarian rules)

### Scene Four: The Post-War Office — First Confrontation with the Choice of an "Enemy"

Whatever the result, this is the player's first time facing the armed forces of another organization as Director.

> If you chose Forced Expulsion and succeeded:
> "The office is very quiet. The numbers on the mission report are clear — enemy casualties: 12. Our casualties: 2. You won. But as you write the report, your pen stops at the 'Cause of Engagement' column — you find you need to justify why you fired the first shot."

> If you chose diplomacy:
> "As the Crimson Order's representative left, they left a sentence — 'The Supreme Inquisitor believes... you still have potential. But he will not wait forever.'"

## GM Information

| Item | Value |
|------|:--:|
| True DC | 75 (diplomacy) / 90 (force) |
| Intel Bias | ±15% (detailed Intel) |
| Minimum Personnel | 2 (diplomacy) / 4 (force) |
| Recommended Specialty | Negotiation (diplomacy route), Combat (force route) |
| Encounter Probability | 20% (diplomacy) / 60% (force) |
| Faction Influence | Diplomatic success: Crimson Order relation +5 (still negative, max −20); Force success: Crimson Order relation −15 |

**Campaign Progression**: This mission is the trigger point of the Faction War campaign. The player's route choice determines the overall direction of Campaign Three. The GM should prepare follow-up missions for the three routes.

**Moral Gray Area**: The Crimson Order is not purely an "evil faction" — they have a self-consistent ideology. Never let their actions be merely "because they are the bad guys." This gives the player's choices more weight.
