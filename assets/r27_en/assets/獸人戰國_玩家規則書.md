## Chapter One: Welcome to the Beastman Sengoku

### 1.1 What Is This Game?

**Beastman Warring States** is a tabletop role-playing game (TRPG) set in a Sengoku-era Japan — but this warring states period is not humanity's. It is the beastmen's.

You are a Beastman Samurai born into one of the Twelve Beast Clans. The blade in your hand is not merely a weapon — it is the vessel of your soul. You study under one of the Twelve Zodiac Schools, searching for your own "Giri" — why you draw your blade, and for whom you fight.

Across the Hundred Beasts Archipelago, the Daimyō wage war for dominion over the realm, the school masters seek worthy successors, and the legendary "Five Swords Under Heaven" await their fated bearers. And you shall inscribe your own legend amid the chaos of these troubled times.

### 1.2 Basic Game Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| **Player** | You. You portray a Beastman Samurai character, making choices and rolling dice to resolve outcomes |
| **Game Master (GM)** | The person who narrates the world, portrays Non-Player Characters (NPCs), adjudicates the rules, and drives the story forward |
| **Character Sheet** | A record of all your character's stats, abilities, and equipment |
| **Check** | When your character attempts an action with a risk of failure, you roll a Twenty-Sided Die (d20) to determine the result |
| **Scenario** | The narrative framework of a session, prepared and run by the GM |

### 1.3 The Rhythm of Play

A typical session of Beastman Warring States contains the following elements:

```
Cultivation (dōjō practice, meditation, test-cutting)
    ↓
Encounter (exploration, diplomacy, infiltration, combat)
    ↓
A Giri dilemma (loyalty vs. righteousness, school vs. province, companion vs. mission)
    ↓
Consequence (the world changes because of your choices)
    ↓
Cultivation (return to the dōjō with newfound insight)
```

### 1.4 Required Materials

- Twenty-Sided Die (d20): at least one, used primarily for Checks
- Six-Sided Dice (d6): several, used for damage calculation
- Four-Sided, Eight-Sided, Ten-Sided, and Twelve-Sided Dice: may be needed depending on weapon type
- Character sheet (a blank character sheet is included with this rulebook)
- Pen and paper

## Chapter Two: Core Rules

### 2.1 The Dice System

This game uses the **d20 (Twenty-Sided Die)** as its core resolution die. When you must make a Check, roll a d20, add the relevant Attribute value and skill bonus, and compare the result against the Difficulty Class set by the GM.

**Check Formula: d20 + Attribute Value + Skill Proficiency Bonus**

If the roll result is ≥ the Difficulty Class (DC), the Check succeeds.

### 2.2 Difficulty Class (DC)

The GM sets a Difficulty Class based on what you are attempting.

| Difficulty Class (DC) | Name | Description | Success Rate at Attribute 5 (average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Trivial | An everyday action, almost impossible to fail | 95% |
| 10 | Simple | Achievable with basic training | 80% |
| 13 | Ordinary | Requires some focus and competence | 65% |
| 18 | Difficult | Only reliably achieved by the skilled | 40% |
| 22 | Very Hard | A master-level challenge | 20% |
| 26 | Legendary | A feat worthy of the warring-states chronicles | 5% |
| 30 | Mythic | A miracle that transcends beastman limits | 5% (requires Attribute 12) |

### 2.3 Critical Success and Fumble

| Die Face | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Natural 20 | Critical (Crit) | Ignores the Difficulty Class (DC); the Check automatically succeeds. In combat it triggers a Critical Hit (double damage) |
| Natural 1 | Fumble | Ignores bonuses; the Check automatically fails. In combat it may trigger an adverse effect |

> A Natural Roll means the raw die face before any bonuses are added. It only triggers when the d20 shows 20 or 1.

### 2.4 Advantage and Disadvantage

When you are in a particularly favorable or unfavorable situation, the GM may grant you **Advantage (Adv)** or **Disadvantage (Disadv)**:

| State | Rule |
|---|---|
| Advantage (Adv) | Roll 2d20 and take the higher |
| Disadvantage (Disadv) | Roll 2d20 and take the lower |

> Advantage and Disadvantage cancel each other out. If you have both Advantage and Disadvantage, regardless of how many of each you have, simply roll 1d20 normally.

### 2.5 Margin of Success (MoS)

The amount by which a Check result exceeds the Difficulty Class (DC) is called the "Margin of Success."

| Margin of Success | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Ordinary Success | Achieves the basic goal |
| 5–9 | Excellent Success | Achieves the goal with an extra benefit (such as +1d4 bonus damage, or gaining extra information) |
| 10+ | Perfect Success | Achieves the goal at the highest quality (such as triggering a high-tier effect of a school technique) |

### 2.6 Contest Check

When you directly oppose another character (such as a blade-lock or an Intimidation contest), each side rolls d20 plus the relevant Attribute and compares results. On a tie, the defending side wins.

### 2.7 Group Check and Assist

- **Group Check**: Everyone makes their own Check; if more than half succeed, the whole group succeeds
- **Assist**: If you possess the relevant skill and your proficiency is at Chūden or higher, you may assist an ally. Make a Check at Difficulty Class (DC) 10; on success you grant your ally Advantage

## Chapter Three: Attributes and Skills

### 3.1 The Five Core Attributes

Every Beastman Samurai possesses five core Attributes, representing your talents and potential.

| Attribute | Abbr. | Meaning | Primary Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swordsmanship (SWM) | Sword | Blade technique and move precision | Attack accuracy, technique activation, unlocking School Secret Techniques |
| Physique (PHY) | Phys | Physical strength and vitality | Hit Points (HP) maximum, damage endurance, strength contests |
| Yokai Power (YP) | Yokai | Strength of bestial instinct | Yokai Pool maximum, innate skill effects, Beastification intensity |
| Agility (AGI) | Agi | Reaction speed and nimbleness | Initiative, Defense Value (DV), Movement |
| Spirit (SPT) | Soul | Willpower and mental strength | Blade Bond initial value, resisting Yokai loss of control, Giri-related Checks |

### 3.2 Attribute Value Ranges

| Stage | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Base Value | 1 | The minimum for all Attributes is 1 |
| Initial Allocation | 25 points freely allocated | Roughly 5 points per Attribute on average |
| Soft Cap | 10 | The maximum reachable through normal growth |
| Hard Cap | 12 | An absolute maximum, requiring Secret Technique points to break through |

### 3.3 The Skill System

Skills represent the professional abilities you gain through cultivation. Skills are divided into five proficiency tiers:

| Tier | Name | Bonus | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Untrained | −2 | Never learned the skill |
| 1 | Shoden (Initiate) | +0 | Beginner study; offsets the Untrained penalty |
| 2 | Chūden (Intermediate) | +2 | Has a solid foundation |
| 3 | Okuden (Hidden Transmission) | +4 | Approaching instructor level |
| 4 | Kaiden (Full Transmission) | +6 | Fully mastered; may create your own techniques |

### 3.4 Skill List

### 3.5 Saving Throws

When you are subjected to a passive effect (poison, Yokai Erosion, fear, etc.), you make a Saving Throw to resist.

**Formula: d20 + Attribute Value ≥ Difficulty Class (DC)**

| Saving Throw Type | Corresponding Attribute | Used to Resist |
|---|---|---|
| Physique Saving Throw | Physique | Toxins, disease, physical impact |
| Agility Saving Throw | Agility | Area attacks, traps |
| Spirit Saving Throw | Spirit | Yokai loss of control, mental attacks, Giri shaken |

## Chapter Four: Character Creation

### 4.1 Step Overview

Creating your Beastman Samurai character takes seven steps:

```
Step One: Choose your beast clan origin
Step Two: Choose your background
Step Three: Allocate attribute points
Step Four: Enroll in a school
Step Five: Your blade
Step Six: Your Giri
Step Seven: Your bond
```

### 4.2 Step One: Choose Your Beast Clan Origin

Choose one of the Twelve Beast Clans. Your clan determines your innate skills, Yokai traits, body size, and appearance. See [Chapter Five: The Twelve Beast Clans](#chapter-five-the-twelve-beast-clans).

### 4.3 Step Two: Choose Your Background

Your background determines your starting point amid the troubled times.

| Background | Attribute Bonus | Special Ability | Starting Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior Family | Swordsmanship +1 | "Family Tradition": Sword Arts skill begins at Shoden | Family uchigatana, dōmaru armor |
| Wild Samurai | Agility +1 | "Wilderness Survival": Survival skill begins at Shoden | Uchigatana, traveling clothes |
| Battle Orphan | Physique +1 | "Toughness": Hit Points (HP) +3 | Worn uchigatana |
| Merchant's Child | Spirit +1 | "Business Acumen": gain Advantage on negotiations (once per day) | Tantō, 50 ryō travel funds |
| Child of the Shrine | Spirit +1 | "Shinto Cultivation": Onmyōdō skill begins at Shoden | Wakizashi, protective charm |
| Smith's Descendant | Yokai Power +1 | "Blood of the Forge": Forging skill begins at Shoden | Wakizashi, forging tools |
| Ninja Village | Agility +1 | "Ninja Arts": Stealth skill begins at Shoden | Tantō, 3 shuriken |
| Rōnin's Child | Swordsmanship +1 | "Lone Wolf": +1 Defense Value (DV) when fighting alone | Uchigatana (slightly worn) |
| Hunter | Agility +1 | "Beast's Nose": Tracking skill begins at Shoden | Short bow, tantō |
| Scholar | Spirit +1 | "Erudition": History skill begins at Shoden | Wakizashi, ancient texts |
| Exile | Physique +1 | "Unyielding": when at death's door (HP 0), can endure 1 extra round | Broken wakizashi |
| Traveling Performer | Yokai Power +1 | "Metamorphosis": Deception skill begins at Shoden | Wakizashi, mask |

> The attribute bonus provided by a background may exceed the soft cap limit at creation (base 1 point from background bonus + free allocation).

When creating your character, in addition to the 1 skill provided by your background, you may **freely choose 3 skills** to begin at Shoden. If a chosen skill matches your background skill, that skill is raised to Chūden (+2).

Starting funds are **50 ryō** (plus any extra funds your background may provide).

### 4.4 Step Three: Allocate Attribute Points

Distribute **25 points** freely among the five core Attributes.

- Each Attribute's initial value is 1 (base value after adding clan bonuses)
- The Attribute soft cap is 10 (the normal maximum without background bonuses)
- The Attribute hard cap is 12 (cannot be exceeded by any means)
- Clan bonuses affect the final Attribute value (see each clan's Attribute bonuses in Chapter Five)

> Suggestion: Raise your primary ability (the Attribute related to your school) to 6–8, and keep secondary abilities at 3–5. You can still grow through leveling during play.

### 4.5 Step Four: Enroll in a School

Choose one of the Twelve Zodiac Schools. A school is your philosophy toward the "blade," not a class — it defines the techniques you learn and the path of your cultivation. See [Chapter Six: The Twelve Zodiac Schools](#chapter-six-the-twelve-zodiac-schools).

### 4.6 Step Five: Your Blade

How did you come by your first blade? This is not only backstory, but also affects the blade's initial state.

| Origin of the Blade | Blade Quality | Initial Blade Bond |
|---|---|---|
| Master's Inheritance | Fine Blade | 30 |
| Family Heirloom | Fine Blade (with family inscription) | 25 |
| Picked up on the battlefield | Nameless (common) | 5 |
| Self-forged | Nameless | 15 |
| Granted by the School | Fine Blade | 20 |
| Bought | Nameless | 10 |

> For the complete blade system, see [Chapter Seven: Swords and Equipment](#chapter-seven-swords-and-equipment).

### 4.7 Step Six: Your Giri

In these troubled times, for whom do you fight? Choose the object of your loyalty:

| Object of Loyalty | Initial Giri Value | Special Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Lord (Daimyō) | 3 | +1 Giri Value when completing the lord's missions |
| School (Master) | 3 | −10% Cultivation Points required to learn school techniques |
| Companion | 3 | Gain Advantage on defense Checks when protecting that companion |
| Self (conviction) | 3 | Gain Advantage on all Spirit-related Checks when acting alone |
| Family | 3 | Gain Advantage on attack Checks when fighting for the family's honor (once per day) |
| None (still searching) | 0 | On your first major Giri dilemma, gain a one-time +5 Giri Value |

### 4.8 Step Seven: Your Bond

Choose someone you trust (could be another Player Character or an NPC) and describe your relationship with them.

| Bond Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sworn Friend | When fighting beside your companion, the first time you are hit each battle may be negated (consumes the bond; once per scenario) |
| Master-Disciple | School Cultivation Points gain speed +10% |
| Blood Tie | Gain Advantage on Saving Throws against Yokai loss of control when a blood relative is present |
| Debt of Gratitude | Automatically succeed when using "Assist" on your benefactor (no roll needed) |
| Sworn Enemy | +1d4 damage on attacks against your sworn enemy |
| Protector | Gain +2 Damage Reduction (DR) when taking damage in place of your bond target |

## Chapter Five: The Twelve Beast Clans

### 5.0 General Rules for Innate Skills

Innate skills are abilities each clan is born with, usable without cultivation. Unless specifically noted otherwise, the following default rules apply:

| Rule | Description |
|---|---|
| **Action Type** | Using an innate skill is usually an **incidental action** (usable alongside your main action and move action within your own turn). Innate skills that consume a main action are specifically noted |
| **Usage Frequency** | **Usable at will** (no limit), but each use accumulates Yokai Power worth of Yokai Value. Skills marked "N times per day" or "N times per battle" follow their noted limits |
| **Permanent Passive** | Innate skills marked "permanent" or as passive effects require no activation and are always in effect |
| **Yokai Accumulation** | When using an innate skill, Yokai Value increases by **+ Yokai Power × 2**. Permanent passives do not accumulate Yokai |

> Example: The Tiger Clan's "Tiger Majesty" is a permanent passive — the Intimidation skill begins at Shoden, and gains Advantage against smaller-sized targets; no activation needed. The Tiger Clan's "Fierce Tiger Strike" automatically triggers on the first attack of each battle and consumes no action.

| Resonance | Attribute bonus matches | The clan's attribute bonus matches the school's core attribute → technique effect gains an extra bonus |
| Harmony | Attribute bonus partially matches | At least one attribute bonus relates to the school's core attribute → normal effect |
| Conflict | Attribute bonus completely opposite | Both attribute bonuses are unrelated to the school's core attribute → technique effect weakened, but you may choose an "alternative effect" |

**GM General Rule**: If a player proposes a creative clan × technique variant, the GM may freely adjudicate its effect. The core of chemical reactions is "reasonable and comes with a cost" — the more powerful the variant effect, the more significant the side effect or restriction should be.

## Chapter Eight: Combat Rules (Player's View)

### 8.1 Combat Flow

Combat is turn-based. Each round represents about 6 seconds of battle time.

```
【Complete Flow of One Round】

1. Round Start Phase
   ├── Ongoing effects trigger (burning damage, Yokai fade, etc.)
   └── First round: all participants make Initiative Checks

2. Action Phase (executed in order from highest to lowest Initiative)
   Each character performs the following actions:
   ├── Main action ×1
   ├── Move action ×1
   ├── Incidental action ×1
   ├── Reaction action ×1 (usable outside your own turn)
   └── Free action (unlimited, GM adjudicates)

3. Round End Phase
   ├── Ongoing effect durations decrement
   └── Natural Yokai fade
```

### 8.2 Initiative (determines action order)

At the start of combat, each participant makes an Initiative Check:

**Initiative Value = Agility value + 1d6**

The highest Initiative acts first. On a tie, the higher Agility goes first; if still tied, both roll d20 to compare.

### 8.3 Action Types

#### Main Action (once per round)

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| Attack | Use a weapon for melee or ranged attack |
| Use attack-type technique | Perform an attack-type school move |
| Use secret technique | Perform a school secret technique (subject to limits) |
| Use support-type technique | Perform a buff, debuff, or defense-type school technique |
| Defense stance | Defense Value (DV) +4 this round, but cannot attack |
| Charge | Move up to double Movement |
| Use item | Use pills, smoke bombs, charms, and other consumables |
| Suppress Yokai | Spend Giri Value to suppress loss of control |
| Heal | Administer first aid to self or a touched companion |

#### Move Action (once per round)

- Move up to "Movement" squares in distance
- Movement = 5 + Agility value
- May be split before and after the main action

#### Incidental Action (once per round)

- Draw or sheathe a weapon
- Use certain support-type techniques
- Open or close a door
- Pass an item to a companion

#### Reaction Action (once per round, usable outside your own turn)

- Opportunity attack: when an enemy moves away from you
- Use a defense-type school technique
- Loyalty Style's Shield-Blade Vow

### 8.4 Attack Resolution

**Attack Hit Formula: d20 + Swordsmanship value + weapon skill proficiency bonus ≥ target's Defense Value (DV)**

#### Defense Value (DV) Calculation

**DV = 10 + Agility value + armor DV modifier**

> Note: If armor has an Agility cap (such as Haramaki's cap at Agility 5), the Agility value in the DV calculation is capped at the armor's limit.

#### Damage Calculation

**Damage = weapon base die + Swordsmanship value + technique bonus + level damage bonus − target's Damage Reduction (DR)**

- Critical Hit (natural 20): damage die doubled (e.g., 1d8 becomes 2d8), other bonuses unchanged
- **Level Damage Bonus**: every 5 character levels, base attack damage +1d4 (Level 1-4: +0, Level 5-9: +1d4, Level 10-14: +2d4, Level 15-19: +3d4, Level 20: +4d4). This bonus applies only to normal attacks, not to school techniques (techniques already have their own bonuses)
- The interaction of various damage types with Damage Reduction (DR) is adjudicated by the GM (e.g., fire damage may ignore part of metal armor's DR)

### 8.5 Opportunity Attacks

When an enemy moves away from your melee attack range (usually adjacent, 1 square), you may spend a reaction action to make an opportunity attack. The opportunity attack is a normal melee attack.

> If you take a "disengage" action, you may retreat safely with a move action without triggering opportunity attacks.

### 8.6 Cover and Terrain

| Cover Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Half cover (low wall, bush) | Ranged attacks against you suffer Disadvantage; Defense Value (DV) +2 |
| Full cover (castle wall, building) | Cannot be targeted by ranged attacks |
| High ground (2+ squares above opponent) | Melee attacks gain Advantage |
| Difficult terrain (mud, rubble) | Movement cost doubled |

### 8.7 Status Effects

| Status | Effect | Typical Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Stunned | Cannot take any action | 1 round |
| Poisoned | Takes 1d4 poison damage at the start of each round | 1d4 rounds or until cured |
| Heavy Poison | Takes 1d8 poison damage at the start of each round | 1d6 rounds or until cured |
| Burning | Takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of each round | 1d4 rounds or until extinguished |
| Paralyzed | Cannot move, attacks suffer Disadvantage | 1d4 rounds |
| Bleeding | Takes 1d4 damage at the start of each round | Until bandaged (main action) |
| Prone | Melee attacks against the prone gain Advantage; the prone's attacks suffer Disadvantage | Stand using half Movement |
| Blinded | Attacks suffer Disadvantage; attackers against you gain Advantage | Depends on source |
| Feared | Cannot willingly approach the fear source, attacks suffer Disadvantage | 1d4 rounds + Spirit Saving Throw |
| Silenced | Cannot speak, cannot use techniques requiring chanting | Depends on source |
| Charmed | Cannot attack the charmer, suffers Disadvantage on social Checks against the charmer | Depends on source |

**Saving Throws**: Most statuses allow the target a Saving Throw at the end of each round to end them early.

**Formula: d20 + relevant Attribute value ≥ Difficulty Class (DC)**

DC is typically 10 + the inflictor's relevant Attribute value.

### 8.8 Death and Dying

When your Hit Points (HP) drop to 0:

| Stage | Rule |
|---|---|
| The instant HP drops to 0 | Character falls prone, loses consciousness. Yokai Value automatically resets to 0 |
| Start of each subsequent round | Make a death save: d20 (no Attribute added), ≥10 succeeds, <10 fails |
| 3 cumulative successes | Character stabilizes (HP stays 0, but no longer dying; regains consciousness after 1 hour, HP = 1) |
| 3 cumulative failures | Character dies |
| Natural 20 | Immediately regain 1 HP, wake, and act normally |
| Natural 1 | Counts as 2 failures |
| Taking damage | Automatically counts as 1 failure (Critical Hit counts as 2) |
| Healed by a companion | Regain HP, dying state ends, may act normally |

### 8.9 Rest and Recovery

| Rest Type | Time | HP Recovery | Yokai Pool Recovery | Yokai Value Fade | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short Rest | 1 hour | Physique × 3 | Full recovery | −20 | May bandage, meditate, etc. |
| Long Rest | 8 hours sleep | Full recovery | Full recovery | −50 | Reset all daily abilities, clear fatigue states |

> **Fatigue**: Going over 24 hours without a long rest causes Disadvantage on all physical Checks.

## Chapter Nine: The Giri System

### 9.1 What Is Giri?

"Giri" is the moral gravity of the Beastman Samurai. In these troubled times, there is no objective right or wrong — only the promises you make to yourself, to your lord, and to your school.

### 9.2 Giri Value

Giri Value is the "spiritual currency" you accumulate through moral choices.

#### Gaining Giri Value

| Action | Giri Value |
|---|---|
| Make a major choice aligned with your Giri (GM adjudicates) | +2 |
| Fulfill a vow to your object of loyalty | +3 |
| Refuse a great benefit that conflicts with your Giri | +5 |
| Sacrifice yourself to secure others' safety | +3 |
| Protect a companion in combat (first time per battle) | +1 |
| Complete an Atonement task | +5 (and clears one Giri-Breach) |

#### Spending Giri Value

| Cost | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 point | Reroll a combat Check |
| 3 points | Forcefully suppress Yokai — reduce Yokai Value by 20 |
| 5 points | Break through Secret Technique limits — use a school technique not yet learned (once only) |
| 10 points | "This cut, for XX" — declare a target; that attack deals maximum damage |

### 9.3 Vows

You make a Vow at character creation. A Vow is your promise to your object of loyalty.

| Vow Type | Example | Consequence of Breaking |
|---|---|---|
| Vow of Loyalty | "I will never betray my lord" | Giri Value −5, Blade Bond −15 |
| Vow of Protection | "I will never let a companion fall before me" | Giri Value −3, Loyalty Style techniques disabled until Atonement |
| Vow of Revenge | "I will slay the enemy who killed my master" | Giri Value −5, cannot learn new techniques until fulfilled |
| Vow of Cultivation | "I will not fight using means outside my school" | Giri Value −2, School Cultivation Points gain halved (until next long rest) |
| Vow of the Blade | "I will not let this blade taste the blood of the innocent" | Blade Bond −20, Blade Inscriptions nullified until Atonement |

### 9.4 Giri-Breach and Atonement

**Three Stages of Giri-Breach**:

| Stage | Number of Breaches | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Stain | 1 breach | All Spirit-related Checks suffer Disadvantage. May be cleared by a Giri dilemma granting +3 Giri Value |
| Fall | 2 breaches | School technique Yokai cost doubled. Blade Bond cannot increase. Requires completing an Atonement task (designed by GM) |
| Naraka | 3 breaches | Famous blades unusable, Sword Aura Value drops to 0, all social Checks suffer Disadvantage. Requires a full Atonement quest line |

**Atonement Task** (designed by the GM) typically includes:
- Making substantive amends to those you harmed or their faction
- Completing a highly challenging task to prove your remorse
- Making a Giri dilemma anew at a critical moment

## Chapter Ten: Yokai and Beastification

### 10.0 Key Concept: Yokai Pool vs. Yokai Value

A beastman's body holds **two different Yokai powers**, which must be managed separately:

| Concept | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Yokai Pool (YKP)** | Technique energy | The inner power consumed to use school techniques. Maximum = Yokai Power × 2 + 5. Fully restored on short rest |
| **Yokai Value (YK)** | Beastification progress | Bestial instinct accumulated when using innate skills, taking damage, or using powerful techniques. Range 0–100. The higher it accumulates, the closer to loss of control |

**Core Rule**: When using a school technique, you **simultaneously consume Yokai Pool and accumulate Yokai Value** — power always has its price.

> Example: The Tiger Clan samurai "Tora Gen" has Yokai Power 5, so Yokai Pool maximum = 5×2+5 = **15 points**. Using Tiger Might Suppression (Yokai Pool cost 3 points), the Yokai Pool drops to 12 points, and Yokai Value +3 at the same time.

### 10.1 Yokai Pool (Technique Energy)

**Yokai Pool Maximum = Yokai Power × 2 + 5**

| Yokai Power | Yokai Pool Maximum | Usable Shoden techniques (cost 2) | Usable secret techniques (cost 8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 11 | 5 times | 1 time |
| 5 | 15 | 7 times | 1 time |
| 7 | 19 | 9 times | 2 times |
| 10 | 25 | 12 times | 3 times |

**Yokai Pool Recovery**:
- **Short Rest (1 hour)**: Full recovery
- **Long Rest (8 hours)**: Full recovery
- Cannot recover Yokai Pool in combat (unless using special items like a Dragon-Vein Stone)

> When the Yokai Pool is insufficient, school techniques cannot be used. This forces you to manage carefully through consecutive battles — do you use Shoden techniques steadily for reliable output, or risk a secret technique for a one-shot win?

### 10.2 Yokai Value (Beastification Progress)

**Yokai Value Range: 0–100 (fixed maximum)**

Yokai Value is the accumulation of bestial instinct. It rises as you use power and fades as you rest. The higher the Yokai Value, the stronger you become — but the closer to loss of control.

### 10.3 The Four Yokai Stages

| Stage | Yokai Value | Name | Combat Effect | Roleplay Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First Stage | 0–30 | Calm | Normal | Normal conversation, full self-control |
| Second Stage | 31–60 | Restless | Innate skill effect +1; all non-combat intellect-related Checks suffer Disadvantage; Yokai-related Checks gain Advantage | Speech laced with bestial words, restless and anxious |
| Third Stage | 61–85 | Bestial | Innate skill effect +2; at the start of each round must make a Spirit Saving Throw (DC 15), on failure must attack the nearest target that round (friend or foe); attack Checks gain Advantage | Guttural speech, only short sentences possible |
| Fourth Stage | 86–100 | Loss of Control | Full Beastification: each round must make a Spirit Saving Throw (DC 20), on failure the GM takes over, attacking all targets wildly; on success may act autonomously but all Checks suffer Disadvantage | Cannot speak, body grows gigantic |

### 10.4 Yokai Gain Sources

| Source | Yokai Value Gained |
|---|---|
| Using an innate skill | +Yokai Power × 2 points (each time) |
| Using a school secret technique | +5 |
| Witnessing a companion knocked down (HP 0) | +3 |
| Hit by a specific clan's taunt skill | +5 |
| Suffering heavy wounds (HP < 30%) | +3 / round |
| Full-moon scene (bonus for Rabbit Clan) | +5 / round |
| Fighting at a Dragon-Vein node | +1 / round |
| Hit by an enemy's Yokai-related ability | +2 |

### 10.5 Yokai Fade

| Method | Fade Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Natural fade (per round) | −2 | Automatic in combat, no action needed |
| Rest (consume main action, 1 round) | −5 | Actively calm down in combat |
| Short Rest (1 hour) | −20 | Safe environment, restore Physique×3 Hit Points (HP), full Yokai Pool recovery |
| Long Rest (8 hours sleep) | −50 | Full Hit Points (HP) recovery, full Yokai Pool recovery, reset all daily abilities |
| Meditation (1 hour focus) | −15 | Requires Spirit Check DC 12, success grants extra −5 |
| Purification rite (requires Rabbit Clan or Pure Land Style companion) | −15 | Requires a 10-minute rite |
| Giri suppression | −10 | Spend 3 Giri Value, make Spirit Check DC 18 |
| Unconsciousness | Reset to zero | After Hit Points (HP) reach 0, Yokai Value automatically resets to 0 |

### 10.6 Full Beastification (Fourth Stage) Detailed Rules

When Yokai Value reaches 86–100, you enter Full Beastification:

- **Size increases by one step** (Medium becomes Large, Small becomes Medium): HP maximum temporarily +Physique×5
- **Attack power surges**: weapon damage die increases one step (d4→d6→d8→d10→d12→2d8)
- **Defense collapses**: Defense Value (DV) −4, Damage Reduction (DR) +4 (your body hardens but cannot dodge nimbly)
- **Double attack**: may attack twice per round (replacing one attack)
- **Yokai aura**: all creatures within 3 squares take 1d6 Yokai damage per round
- **Indiscriminate attack priority** (executed by the GM): 1. Counter the current attacker → 2. Nearest target → 3. Weakest target → 4. Random

> **⚠️ Important Reminder**: Beastification is not a punishment! The out-of-control you is extremely powerful — all stats surge. The problem is not "you are too weak," but "you are no longer you."

### 10.6 Yokai Critical Outburst (special rule)

The instant Yokai Value reaches 100, make a **Spirit Saving Throw DC 25**:

- **Success**: Yokai locks at 99, take 1d6 mental damage, gain the "Yokai Scorch" status (cannot naturally fade Yokai for 1 hour)
- **Failure**: Immediately enter Full Beastification, GM takes over for 1d4 rounds. Afterward Yokai resets to 50, character loses consciousness for 1 hour

## Appendix A: Character Creation Quick Reference

1. **Choose clan** → Record clan bonus, innate skills, Yokai trait
2. **Choose background** → Record background attributes and special ability
3. **Allocate attributes** → 25 points freely allocated; each Attribute base 1 + clan bonus. Suggest primary Attribute 6–8, secondary 3–5
4. **Choose skills** → Freely choose **3 skills** to begin at Shoden (the 1 skill from background not counted, may stack to Chūden)
5. **Choose school** → Gain Shoden technique. Starting School Cultivation Points is 0
6. **Decide blade origin** → Record blade quality and initial Blade Bond
7. **Choose object of loyalty** → Record initial Giri Value
8. **Choose bond** → Record bond target and type
9. **Starting funds** → 50 ryō (plus any extra from background, e.g., Merchant's Child +50 ryō = 100 ryō)
10. **Calculate derived Attributes**:
   - Hit Points (HP) = Physique value × 5 + Level × 3
   - Defense Value (DV) = 10 + Agility value + armor modifier
   - Movement = 5 + Agility value
   - Yokai Pool maximum = Yokai Power × 2 + 5 (energy for techniques, full recovery on short rest)
   - Yokai Value: starts at 0 (Beastification progress, cap 100, accumulates to loss of control)
   - Initiative = Agility value + 1d6
   - Blade Bond initial value = Spirit value × 5
   - Sword Aura Value = Swordsmanship value + Spirit value + School Cultivation Points / 10
11. **Rest and Recovery**:
   - Short Rest (1 hour): restore Physique×3 Hit Points (HP), full Yokai Pool recovery, Yokai Value −20
   - Long Rest (8 hours): full Hit Points (HP) recovery, full Yokai Pool recovery, Yokai Value −50, reset all daily abilities
