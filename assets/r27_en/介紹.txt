# Beastman Warring States TRPG

A tabletop role-playing game set in Sengoku-era Japan, yet ruled by the twelve zodiac beastmen. Here there are no "humans" — you are a beastman samurai who has pledged to a sword school, seeking your own "Giri" amid the turbulent age.

## What kind of world is this?

Across the Hundred Beasts Archipelago, the twelve beast clans each hold their provinces, ruled by beastman Daimyō. The twelve ancient Sword Saints left their legacies, becoming the twelve zodiac schools; the legendary "Five Swords Under Heaven" still slumber across the islands, awaiting the fated. This is a world where Yokai and blades intertwine: a sword is not merely a weapon but a vessel for the soul; and the Yokai within a beastman can become both sharp techniques and the abyss of uncontrolled Beastification.

## Who do you play? What do you do?

You are a beastman samurai. From choosing your beast-clan origin, pledging to a school, to gripping your first blade, you grow through training, encounters, and choices of Giri. The most moving moments are often choices with no standard answer: loyalty to your lord, or protecting your comrades? Following the school, or heeding the Giri in your heart?

## System Highlights

- **Yokai Dual-Track**: Techniques spend the "Yokai Pool (YKP)" as energy, yet simultaneously accumulate the "Yokai Value (YK)" to drive Beastification. The stronger the power, the nearer the risk of losing control — every draw of the blade is a gamble.
- **The Blade Is Bond**: A blade holds level, nature, and memory. Your "Blade Bond (BB)" with your sword deepens as you fight side by side, from strangers to soul-pact; the blade answers your growth.
- **Giri Choices**: Your conviction is quantified by the "Giri Value (GI)". You may give everything for those who matter, or fall through Giri-Breach — every choice is written into your legend.

## What kind of players is it for?

Players who love Sengoku warrior romance, savor a character's inner struggle, and enjoy digging into schools and resource synergy will feel right at home. The game blends blades-and-clashing combat with deep narrative immersion, suited to those with basic tabletop RPG experience, or willing to learn step-by-step from the rulebook.

## Game Style

- Combat weight: Significant
- Strategic depth: Moderate strategy
- Story-driven: Story-led
- Resource management: Moderate management
- Character customization: High customization
- Character creation complexity: Complex
- Rule density: Moderate rules
- GM needed: Requires GM

## Basic Information

- Player count: 1 or more (single-player compatible by default)
- Core dice: Twenty-Sided Die (d20) + Six-Sided Die (d6)
- Single scenario: approx. 3–5 hours
- Full campaign (levels 1–20, four connected acts): approx. 40–60 hours
