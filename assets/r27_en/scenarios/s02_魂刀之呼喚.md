# S2: Call of the Soul Blade

> Scenario No.: S2 | Corresponding Campaign: Act 2 (Levels 6–9) | Suggested Players: 1–4 (compatible with solo and group play) | Estimated Playtime: ~4–6 hours | Difficulty: Intermediate | Prerequisite Scenario: S1 (Shadow of the Wailing Plains)

---

## I. Background

During a meditation training, the player senses the presence of a Soul Blade—not one of the legendary "Five Swords Under Heaven," but a lesser Soul Blade left behind by an ancient Sword Saint.

The Soul Blade's resonance comes from the **Thousand-Blade Gorge (Senzangyō)**—a natural canyon on the eastern side of the Horse Clan's territory, and the traditional site where ancient Sword Saints underwent their trials.

The player must travel to the Thousand-Blade Gorge to seek this Soul Blade, but the Horse Clan Daimyō **Kiba no Jingorō** imposes strict restrictions on outsiders entering the canyon. How to obtain the Soul Blade while maneuvering among the various factions will be the challenge of this journey.

## III. Scene Flow

### Scene 1: Horse Clan Border Negotiation
- **Type**: Social
- **Description**:
  The player arrives at the border of the Horse Clan's Suruga Province. The guard captain demands to see the Daimyō's passage permit—but you don't have one.
  There are three ways to resolve this:
  - **Persuasion** (Difficulty Class (DC) 18): Request passage in the name of the Soul Blade's resonance
  - **Stealth** (Difficulty Class (DC) 16): Sneak in under cover of night
  - **Confrontation**: A small fight breaks out, but it offends the Horse Clan (affecting later plot)

- **Checks**:

  | Skill | Difficulty (DC) | Success Result |
  |------|-----------|----------|
  | Persuasion | 18 | The guard captain allows passage, but with a condition—help deal with the bandit problem at the Thousand-Blade Gorge |
  | Stealth | 16 | Successfully sneak into the Thousand-Blade Gorge, but lose the supplies the Horse Clan guards might have provided |

---

### Scene 2: Trial of the Thousand-Blade Gorge
- **Type**: Exploration / Combat
- **Description**:
  Within the Thousand-Blade Gorge, the rock walls are sharp as blades. The trial ground of the ancient Sword Saints lies deep in the canyon.
  The trial has three steps:
  1. **Climb the Blade-Sharp Cliff** (Climb Difficulty (DC) 16; failure takes 1d6 slashing damage)
  2. **Keep balance crossing a narrow bridge in strong winds** (Agility (AGI) saving throw Difficulty (DC) 15)
  3. **Face the Trial Guardian's test**

- **Boss Data**:

  | Item | Value |
  |------|------|
  | Name | Trial Guardian (Sprite · Form of Wind) |
  | Challenge Rating (CR) | 5 |
  | Type | Sprite (Friendly Neutral) |
  | Hit Points (HP) | 45 |
  | Defense Value (DV) | 17 |
  | Speed | 12 |

  - **Attacks**:
    - **Wind Blade**: Hit +7, Damage 1d8+4 slashing
    - **Storm Barrier**: Reaction action; fully negates one physical attack

  - **Trial Content**:
    The guardian does not truly wish to harm you—it wants to test your understanding of the "blade."
    It will ask three questions (chosen by the Game Master (GM)):
    - "What is a blade?"
    - "Why do you swing your blade?"
    - "What does your blade remember?"

    Each sincere answer (not a combat check, pure roleplay (RP)) weakens the guardian by 10 Hit Points (HP).
    After the three questions, the guardian will voluntarily cease combat.

- **Rewards**:
  - Experience (XP): 500
  - Gold: 300
  - Items:
    - Soul Blade "Wind-Cutter" (Yōmono-grade tachi)
    - Blade Inscription: Trial (Wind-Cutter exclusive; crit range 19–20 when using this blade in combat)

### Scene 3: Black Moon Society Ambush
- **Type**: Combat
- **Description**:
  Upon leaving the Thousand-Blade Gorge, the player is ambushed by the Black Moon Society—they too are searching for the Soul Blade.
  The Black Moon Society elite (Snake Clan) says to the player: "That Soul Blade does not belong to a weak samurai like you. Hand it over and you may leave alive."

- **Enemy Data**:

  | Item | Value |

  | Count | 1 |
  | Challenge Rating (CR) | 4 |

  | Defense Value (DV) | 16 |
  | Attack | +6, 1d6+4 + poison (Physique (PHY) saving throw Difficulty (DC) 16) |

  The Black Moon Society elite possesses a Soul-Blade-shard detection device—if during combat the player's Hit Points (HP) fall below 50%,
  the elite will retreat using a smoke bead and leave these words: "We will meet again at the place of the seal. Then—you will regret not handing over the Soul Blade today."

### Scene 4: Soul Blade Awakening—Dialogue with the Blade
- **Type**: Roleplay / Giri
- **Description**:
  At a safe location, perform the "Dialogue with the Blade" ritual.
  The Soul Blade "Wind-Cutter" begins to reveal its Blade Nature and memories.

- **Soul Blade Details**:
  - **Name**: Wind-Cutter
  - **Grade**: Yōmono
  - **Blade Nature (Personality)**: Swift Thunder (restless and active, craves speed)
  - **Memory**:
    Wind-Cutter remembers its former master—a Horse Clan Gale Style swordsman who completed an impossible trial in the storm of the Thousand-Blade Gorge.
    But the former master later died in battle protecting comrades.
    Wind-Cutter waited two hundred years in the gorge, awaiting the next "Wind-Chaser."

- **Moral Dilemma**:
  Wind-Cutter asks you a question: "My former master died protecting his comrades. If the same choice were laid before you—what would you choose?"
  Your answer will determine the initial Blade Bond (BB) value:

  | Answer Option | Blade Bond (BB) Effect | Blade's Reaction |
  |----------|---------------|----------|
  | "I would make the same choice" | Blade Bond (BB) +10 | The blade senses your righteousness (Giri) |
  | "I would find a way to protect both comrades and myself" | Blade Bond (BB) +5 | The blade admires your ambition |
  | "I would let my comrades protect me" | Blade Bond (BB) −5 | The blade despises your cowardice |

## IV. Soul Blade Awakening (Dialogue with the Blade)

> This section consolidates the core ritual content of Scene 4 to help the Game Master (GM) guide the players through roleplay.

At a safe location, the player initiates the "Dialogue with the Blade" ritual, and the Soul Blade "Wind-Cutter" awakens and reveals itself.

**Soul Blade "Wind-Cutter" Profile**
- Grade: Yōmono
- Blade Nature: Swift Thunder (restless and active, craves speed)
- Memory: Former master was a Horse Clan Gale Style swordsman who completed an impossible trial in the storm of the Thousand-Blade Gorge, then died protecting comrades; Wind-Cutter waited two hundred years in the gorge for the "Wind-Chaser."

**Dialogue with the Blade (Moral Dilemma)**
Wind-Cutter asks: "My former master died protecting his comrades. If the same choice were laid before you—what would you choose?"

| Player Answer | Initial Blade Bond (BB) | Blade's Assessment |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| "I would make the same choice" | +10 | Senses your righteousness (Giri) |
| "I would find a way to protect both comrades and myself" | +5 | Admires your ambition |
| "I would let my comrades protect me" | −5 | Despises your cowardice |

---

## V. Aftermath

- The player obtains their first Soul Blade, unlocking the **Blade Inscription system**.
- The Black Moon Society takes notice of the player—they may face pursuit in future scenarios.
- If the player helped the Horse Clan border guards deal with the bandits, they may obtain a **Horse Clan Passage Permit** (usable in Act 3).
- The School Grandmaster praises the player for finding the Soul Blade—**Cultivation Points +15**.
