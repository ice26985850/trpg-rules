# Chapter One: The Galactic World

## 4.1 Worldview Core

**Era Background**: Year 847 of the Stellar Union Era. The galaxy rests in a delicate balance—after long expansion, the great civilizations have formed an unstable power structure. The Hyperspace Lanes network connects thousands of star systems; the Galactic Community maintains a fragile peace, while ancient threats stir in the darkness.

### Cosmic Origin (Three versions selectable, chosen by GM)

| Version | Name | Core Narrative | Impact on the Game |
|:---:|------|------|------|
| A | **The Great Filter** | Countless civilizations destroyed themselves before reaching the interstellar stage. Survivors are rare; the galaxy is empty and dangerous. | Suitable for hard sci-fi, survival themes |
| B | **The Star-God Game** | Ancient "Star-Gods" regard the galaxy as a chessboard. The great civilizations are pieces; the grand "Destinies" are woven in the void. | Compatible with the Psionic/Ascension system; highly narrative |
| C | **Ancient Relics** | Multiple "Precursor civilizations" once ruled the galaxy then vanished, leaving ruins and technology scattered across the galaxy. | Suitable for exploration, archaeology-driven |

> **Recommended Version B (The Star-God Game)**: Most compatible with psionic ascension, civilization-ethics-driven, and crisis narratives.

## 4.2 Historical Timeline

| Stage | Period | Key Events |
|:---:|------|------|
| I | **Age of Silence** (Pre-FTL) | Each civilization evolved independently on its Homeworld; the last relics of the Precursor civilizations gradually faded away |
| II | **Age of Dawn** (FTL Invented) | The first civilizations invented the hyperspace engine; the first wave of First Contact; the beginning of interstellar colonization |
| III | **Age of Expansion** (Great Galactic Exploration) | Civilizations raced to expand; border friction and early wars; the Fallen Empires were first "awoken" |
| IV | **Age of the Community** (Since Year 1 of the Stellar Union Era) | The Galactic Community was founded; the stellar covenant system was established; undercurrents surged beneath relative peace |
| V | **Crisis Era** (Current) | Ancient threats awaken; the great civilizations choose between cooperation and confrontation; the endgame that decides the galaxy's fate |

### Galactic Community

**Organizational Structure**:
- **Galactic Council**: 5 permanent seats (elected by Diplomacy Power (DIP) weight) + 10 non-permanent seats
- **Covenant System**: Galactic Trade Covenant, Stellar Military Covenant, Environmental Protection Covenant, etc.
- **Sanction Mechanism**: Diplomatic isolation → Economic sanction → Military intervention
- **Voting Weight** = Diplomacy Power (DIP) attribute value + ascension/specialization modifier

**Proposal and Voting Procedure**:
1. Any member civilization may propose a **Galactic Community proposal** (1 Action Point (AP))
2. Proposing side rolls Diplomacy Power (DIP) check: Difficulty Class (DC) = 7 + ⌊number of opposing civilizations / 3⌋
3. The GM determines each NPC civilization's voting intention one by one (based on ethics and relations)
4. Each civilization's votes = its voting weight. Simple majority passes.
5. Emergency proposals (e.g., joint crisis defense) get Difficulty Class (DC) -1, but require a 2/3 majority.

**Example Proposal Types**:
- Economic sanction on a civilization (Difficulty Class (DC) 8) → Target civilization's galactic market trade tax doubled
- Joint defense (Difficulty Class (DC) 7) → All members automatically join the defensive war
- Environmental protection (Difficulty Class (DC) 6) → Bombardment of Gaia worlds / holy sites prohibited
- Censure resolution (Difficulty Class (DC) 6) → Target civilization's diplomatic relations across the galaxy -10

> Diplomat Ascension: Voting weight ×2. Some Fallen Empires may ignore Community resolutions.

## 4.5 Exploration Action Rules

> Exploration is the core driving force of *Galaxy Civilization*. In the early game, most of the players' turns are spent in the "survey → discover → investigate → surprise/crisis" loop. This chapter provides the complete exploration flow rules and GM tools.

### 4.5.1 Science Vessels and Survey Actions

#### Science Vessel Attributes

Each science vessel is an independent unit performing exploration:

| Attribute | Meaning | Base Value |
|------|------|:---:|
| Hull Points (HP) | Structural integrity (zero = science vessel destroyed) | 200 |
| Survey Range | How many jumps of systems can be surveyed around (number of Hyperspace Lane connections) | 1 |
| Survey Speed | Progress needed to complete one anomaly investigation | 2 progress/turn |
| Sensor Level | Affects anomaly discovery rate and survey Target Number (TN) | 1 (improves with tech) |

**Building a Science Vessel**: 50 Alloys, 1 turn, 1 Energy Credit (EC)/turn maintenance cost.

**Science Vessel Performance in Combat**: Non-combat unit, does not participate in fleet battles. If intercepted by a hostile fleet, automatically attempts emergency retreat (Speed check Difficulty Class (DC) 7); if retreat fails, it is captured or destroyed.

**Upgradeable Tech**:
- Hyperspace Survey T1 → Survey Range +1
- Gravity Sensor T2 → Survey Range +1, Sensor +1
- Hyperspace Engine T3 → Survey Range +2
- Omniscient Sensor T5 → Survey Range +3, all survey Target Number (TN) -1

#### Survey Action Flow

When a science vessel enters a **star system no civilization has surveyed before**, perform the following flow:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│     System Survey Flow (1 Action Point (AP))              │
│                                              │
│  ① Enter system                             │
│  ② Roll survey check: Science Power (SCI) dice pool vs Target Number (TN)        │
│     Target Number (TN) base = 5                              │
│     Inside nebula Target Number (TN) +1 | Black hole/Neutron star Target Number (TN) +2            │
│     Each sensor level Target Number (TN) -1                          │
│  ③ Determine survey result by Degree of Success:      │
│     Critical Success → Full data + auto-discover anomaly    │
│     Success → Full data (star + planets + resources)   │
│     Partial Success → Basic data (star + planet count, no detail) │
│     Failure → Star type only                     │
│     Critical Failure → Data error + science vessel damaged (Hull Points (HP) -20%)  │
│  ④ Record system data to star chart            │
│  ⑤ If anomaly discovered, trigger anomaly investigation phase │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Survey Target Number (TN) Modifier Table

| Modifier Source | Effect |
|----------|------|
| Nebula | Target Number (TN) +1 |
| Black Hole System | Target Number (TN) +2 |
| Neutron Star / Pulsar | Target Number (TN) +2 |
| Sensor Level (per level) | Target Number (TN) -1 |
| Scientist Lead (per skill level) | Target Number (TN) -0.5 (round down, i.e. -1 per 2 levels) |
| Outside Survey Range (forced survey) | Target Number (TN) +3 |
| Already-owned star chart fragment (purchased from Curator) | Target Number (TN) -1 |

### 4.5.3 Anomaly Investigation System

#### Anomaly Level and Difficulty Class (DC)

| Anomaly Level | Investigation Difficulty Class (DC) | Typical Type | Base Investigation Progress Required |
|:---:|:---:|------|:---:|
| Lv1 Common | 6 | Geological anomaly, biological signal | 4 progress |
| Lv2 Unusual | 7 | Gravitational wave, anomalous radiation | 6 progress |
| Lv3 Rare | 8 | Ancient tech signal, subspace echo | 9 progress |
| Lv4 Extremely Rare | 9 | Alien structure, Void rift | 12 progress |
| Lv5 Legendary | 10 | Precursor main ruins, galactic wonder | 16 progress |

#### Investigation Action Flow

```
1. The science vessel investigates a discovered anomaly (1 Action Point (AP))
2. Roll Science Power (SCI) dice pool check vs the anomaly's Difficulty Class (DC)
   Critical Success → 3 progress + extra discovery
   Success → 2 progress
   Partial Success → 1 progress
   Failure → 0 progress
   Critical Failure → -1 progress (data corruption) + may trigger a dangerous event
3. Accumulated progress ≥ requirement → anomaly complete, gain reward
4. If investigation is abandoned → anomaly becomes an "unsolved mystery" (other civilizations may investigate later)
```

#### Anomaly Reward Table (d20 after investigation completes)

| d20 | Reward Type | Example |
|:---:|------|------|
| 1-4 | Resource Discovery | Energy Credits +50-200 or Minerals +50-200 |
| 5-7 | Research Breakthrough | Random currently-researched tech +3 progress |
| 8-10 | Rare Resource | Discover a vein of a rare resource (in this system or adjacent system) |
| 11-13 | Tech Clue | Unlock a random selectable tech (skips Tier limit, but research Difficulty Class (DC) +2) |
| 14-15 | Precursor Clue | Gain Precursor event chain progress +1 |
| 16-17 | Ancient Relic | Gain an ancient relic (provides a permanent bonus) |
| 18 | Special Event | Trigger a narrative event (GM adjudication, usually a moral choice) |
| 19 | Lost Tech | Immediately unlock a random T1-T2 tech |
| 20 | **Major Breakthrough** | All Science Power (SCI) checks of this civilization this turn +1 die (one-time) + gain Precursor clue |

| d6 | Danger Event |
|:---:|------|
| 1 | Research equipment damaged: science vessel needs to return to port for repair (1 turn) |
| 2 | Radiation leak: scientist injured (Hull Points (HP) -30%, remaining checks this turn Target Number (TN) +1) |

#### Excavation Rules

Archaeological Sites advance by **chapters**. Each chapter requires accumulating progress points to complete:

1. The science vessel performs excavation action at the site system (1 Action Point (AP)/turn)
2. Roll `Science Power (SCI) dice pool + Scientist skill dice vs Site Difficulty Class (DC)`
3. Degree of Success determines this turn's progress (Critical Success 3 / Success 2 / Partial Success 1 / Failure 0)
4. When each chapter completes, trigger a **Chapter Event** (GM draws from the site event table or customizes)
5. All chapters complete → site fully excavated, gain final reward

#### Site Chapter Event Table (d10)

| d10 | Event Type |
|:---:|------|

| 4 | **Relic Found**: gain an ancient relic (GM selects from relic table) |

### 4.5.6 First Contact Rules

When a player civilization (or its science vessel) first enters a **star system where an NPC civilization exists**, the First Contact event triggers.

#### Contact Flow

```
1. Confirm the other civilization exists (automatic, unless they have cloaking tech)
2. Roll First Contact check:
   Initiator: Diplomacy Power (DIP) dice pool vs Target Number (TN) = 6 + target's Xenophobia modifier
   Xenophobia+2: Target Number (TN) +2 | Xenophobia+1: Target Number (TN) +1 | Xenophilia+1: Target Number (TN) -1 | Xenophilia+2: Target Number (TN) -2
3. Determine contact effect by Degree of Success
4. Establish initial diplomatic relation level
5. GM describes the other side's reaction and first impression
```

#### Contact Effect Table

| Check Result | Contact Effect | Initial Relation | Follow-up |
|----------|:---:|------|------|
| Critical Success | Deeply impressed, other side responds positively | +30 (Friendly) | May immediately propose a trade agreement |
| Success | Successfully established communication | +10 (Neutral-leaning-friendly) | Routine diplomacy |
| Partial Success | Communication difficult but progressing | 0 (Neutral) | First diplomatic action Target Number (TN) +1 |
| Failure | Communication failed, misunderstandings frequent | -10 (Cautious / Cold) | Other side closes border for 5 turns |
| Critical Failure | Severe diplomatic incident | -30 (Hostile) | Other side may immediately expel the science vessel |

#### First Contact Special Events (d8)

| d8 | Event | Impact |
|:---:|------|------|
| 1-2 | Standard Contact | Per the contact check result |
| 3 | Other side preemptively demands explanation | Roll Diplomacy Power (DIP)=7, failure means relation -20 |
| 4 | Discover a common enemy | Both sides' relation to the third party -10 simultaneously, +20 toward each other |
| 5 | Cultural misunderstanding leads to conflict | Small fleet skirmish (automatic 1-turn minor battle) |
| 6 | Other side makes an urgent request for help | Acceptable (relation +30) or refuse (relation -20) |
| 7 | Trade Opportunity | Immediately gain a favorable trade proposal |
| 8 | **Major Event** | Trigger a complete diplomatic event chain (GM adjudication) |

### Three Crises

|------|------|:---:|------|
| **Void Devourer** | Psionic civilization overuses the Void / GM-selected | 4 stages | Dimensional rifts expand, devouring systems |
| **Machine Awakening** | AI tech proliferation / synth ascension triggered | 4 stages | Infects machine civilizations, expands rebellion |
| **Predator Descends** | Intergalactic signal / random time trigger | 4 stages | Extragalactic invading fleet, swooping and devouring |

### Four-Stage Progression

| Stage | Name | Duration | Characteristics |
|:---:|------|:---:|------|
| 1 | **Omen** | 3-5 turns | Sporadic reports, anomalous signals, initial disturbances |
| 2 | **Outbreak** | 5-8 turns | Crisis formally appears, begins to expand, early civilizations may underestimate it |
| 3 | **Peak** | Variable | Galactic-level threat, may require pan-galactic joint response |
| 4 | **Endgame** | Variable | Defeat the crisis core or the galaxy falls |

### Galactic Joint Response

- **Galactic Community Emergency Session**: Diplomacy Power (DIP) check to initiate a joint defense proposal
- **Galactic Guardian**: civilizations with the "Eternal Guardian" ascension gain bonuses against endgame threats
- **Combined Operations**: multiple civilizations may form a joint fleet (lead side commands, assisting side +dice)
- **Sacrifice and Choice**: The GM should provide difficult moral choices (sacrifice a star region for time / activate ancient weapons with unknowable consequences)

## 4.7 GM Run Guide

### Five Eras Campaign Structure

| Era | Level | Turn Count | Theme | GM Focus |
|------|:---:|:---:|------|------|
| **Exploration** | 1-4 | ~20 Stellar Turns (ST) | Discover the galaxy, First Contact, colonial expansion | Introduce mechanics, build world sense |
| **Expansion** | 5-8 | ~20 Stellar Turns (ST) | Border delineation, resource competition, early wars | Introduce diplomatic maneuvering and conflict |
| **Competition** | 9-12 | ~20 Stellar Turns (ST) | Galactic politics, alliance formation, mid-game wars | Galactic Community operations, power reshuffle |
| **Crisis** | 13-16 | ~15 Stellar Turns (ST) | Crisis omen → outbreak | Raise the stakes, drive cooperation / confrontation |
| **Endgame** | 17-20 | ~15 Stellar Turns (ST) | The decisive battle that decides the galaxy's fate | Emotional climax, consequences of player choices |

### Standard GM Turn Flow

```
① Galactic Event Phase
   Roll event die → announce this turn's galactic-level event (anomaly / diplomatic shift / crisis advance)

② Player Action Phase
   Each civilization spends Action Points (AP) in order to perform actions → roll dice for resolution

③ NPC Action Phase
   NPC civilization actions auto-executed per NPC behavior AI decision tree

④ Diplomacy and Interaction Phase
   Player-player diplomacy / player-NPC diplomacy / Galactic Community vote

⑤ Settlement Phase
   Resource output settlement / tech progress settlement / war exhaustion update / leader level-up
```

### The "Magnifying Glass" Principle: Narrative in Civilization Management

> **Beneath the macro data, always attend to the micro-level people.**

- A fleet battle is not just a clash of numbers—describe the tense atmosphere on the flagship's bridge, a young captain commanding battle for the first time
- A planet being colonized is not just +1 population—describe the awe of the first colonists as they set foot on a new world
- A tech breakthrough is not just unlocking a new weapon—describe the ecstasy of scientists in the lab seeing breakthrough data
- Three parallel narrative threads: **Galactic Line** (macro changes) + **Civilization Line** (rise and fall of fortune) + **Personal Line** (representative character stories)

### NPC Civilization Behavior AI

```
① Assess current threat level (neighbor military / crisis presence / border pressure)
② Check diplomatic agreement constraints (alliance obligations / non-aggression / defense pacts)
③ Determine core objective (expansion / research / defense / revenge)
④ Allocate this turn's Action Points (AP) (based on personality weights)
   · Belligerent: Military Power (MIL) 60% / Economy Power (ECO) 20% / Science Power (SCI) 10% / Diplomacy Power (DIP) 10%
   · Tech-focused: Science Power (SCI) 50% / Economy Power (ECO) 25% / Diplomacy Power (DIP) 15% / Military Power (MIL) 10%
   · Diplomatic: Diplomacy Power (DIP) 40% / Economy Power (ECO) 30% / Science Power (SCI) 15% / Military Power (MIL) 15%
   · Isolationist: Economy Power (ECO) 40% / Science Power (SCI) 30% / Military Power (MIL) 20% / Diplomacy Power (DIP) 10%
⑤ Execute actions
⑥ Update attitude toward other civilizations (friendly ↔ hostile threshold trigger on accumulated change)
```

### Three-Layer Information Structure

| Layer | Content | Acquisition Method |
|:---:|------|------|
| **Public** | Known parts of the galactic map, Community resolutions, open wars | Automatically visible |
| **Diplomatic** | Civilization attitude, trade willingness, basic national power | Diplomacy Power (DIP) check or diplomatic agreement |
| **Secret** | Specific fleet positions, tech progress, espionage targets, crisis truth | Intelligence Power (INT) check, special events |

### Module Framework

**Standard Campaign Structure**: Hook (3 turns) → Development (5-8 turns) → Turning Point (2-3 turns) → Climax (3-5 turns) → Conclusion (2 turns)

**Preset Campaign Concept 1: "Great Galactic Exploration"**
Suitable for Eras I-II. The player civilization first steps into deep space, discovering anomalies, contacting new civilizations, encountering space creatures. Core conflict: First Contact choices, resource competition.

**Preset Campaign Concept 2: "Crisis Descends—The Hunger of the Void"**
Suitable for Eras IV-V. Dimensional rifts open at the galactic core, Void creatures pour in. The players must choose: unite against it? Expand in the meantime? Or seek help from a Fallen Empire?

## D. GM Preparation Checklist

### Before the Opening

```
□ Determine Cosmic Origin version (A/B/C)
□ Draw the initial star map (including player home system + 10-15 surrounding systems)
□ Create 2-4 NPC civilizations (using the quick generator)
□ Select 1 Fallen Empire (optional, no contact in early game)
□ Prepare 3-5 initial anomalies / events
□ Print a civilization character sheet for each player
```

### Per-Turn Operations

```
□ Roll event die, announce galactic event
□ Advance crisis timer (if any)
□ Process player actions (Action Points (AP) spent → roll dice → narrate)
□ Run NPC behavior AI
□ Settle diplomatic interactions
□ Settle resource output
□ Update war exhaustion
□ Advance tech / tradition progress
□ Note key events that happened this turn (for later narrative callbacks)
```
