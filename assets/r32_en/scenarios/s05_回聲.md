### Opening

A-Jie, a physics graduate student, has been "half-missing" for three weeks. He hasn't fully vanished — his roommate occasionally sees his shadow at the end of the hallway, food goes missing from the fridge, and footsteps are heard at 2 a.m. His roommate says: "He's not missing. He's trapped on the other side." A-Jie is in the Middle Stage of Gap Sickness — he no longer knows which layer he's on.

### NPCs

### Scenes

1. **Surface Investigation**: Awareness DC 10 finds the anomaly in A-Jie's room — a sticky note reading "Find the resonator." [[MAP-05 scene1]]
2. **Enter the Apartment Gap**: Natural Threshold DC 6. The apartment in the Gap is almost identical to the Surface — and that is the greatest fear: nothing changes. [[MAP-05 scene2]]
3. **Tracking A-Jie**: Awareness DC 8 finds signs of activity — a sticky note on the fridge is replaced every 24 hours. [[MAP-05 scene3]]
4. **Finding A-Jie**: He is in the Gap Layer of the apartment rooftop. Sitting on the edge, watching a false starry sky. Negotiation DC 14 (solo) to persuade him to leave. He argues: "Everything here means more — the laws of physics are derivable here." You must present an Anchoring Item or the Mother's Necklace (Memory Crystal DC 13, obtained from him). [[MAP-05 scene4]]
5. **Escape**: Bring A-Jie back to the Surface. The two of you exit together (each makes a separate Traverse DC 8 roll). [[MAP-05 scene5]]
6. **Aftermath**: A-Jie returns to the Surface, in the Early Stage of Gap Sickness — he needs 7 days of rest. Can become an apprentice Walker companion or an Informant (physics expert).

> **Map Reference:** Scenario 5 references `maps/MAP-05_apartment.html` throughout (A-Jie's apartment and rooftop map).

### Companion Recruitment

### Rewards

- Return Mark × 1
- Resonator (Gap Construct) retained — can be used in Scenario 8 or 10
