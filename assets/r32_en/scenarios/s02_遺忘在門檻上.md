> **Prerequisite Reading:** Original scenario (`間隙行者TRPG_系列劇本.md`, Scenario One). This version is adjusted for solo play; DCs have been calibrated.

### Opening

Mrs. Chen (a woman in her fifties) contacts you. Her father passed away last week in a nursing home. While sorting his belongings, she found a photo in his wallet she had never seen: a young woman holding an infant, with "Spring 1967, the station" written on the back. In his final months the old man kept walking to the doorway, murmuring "The photo... has she come..."

### NPCs

| NPC | Info |
|-----|------|
| **Mrs. Chen** | Client. Wants to know the truth, but also fears the truth will change her memory of her father |
| **Head Nurse** | Cared for Mr. Chen in his final three months. Can provide a key clue: the old man always walked to the doorway at dusk |
| **Old Station Master** | Over eighty, the only one who remembers the old station |

### Lost Items (DCs calibrated for solo)

| Lost Item | Quality | Solo DC | Location |
|--------|------|:---:|------|
| Photo Negative | Memory Crystal | 13 | Nursing home Gap Layer—at the door of Room 302 |
| Unsent Letter | Common Lost Item | 6 | Old station Gap Layer—under the platform bench |
| Baby Shoe | Benevolent Keepsake | 10 | Old station Gap Layer—beside the vending machine |

### Gap Creatures

| Creature | Type | Solo Negotiation DC | Location |
|------|------|:---:|------|
| Mr. Chen's Residual | Threshold Lingerer | 8 | Threshold of Room 302, nursing home |

### Scenes

1. **Investigate the Nursing Home**: Surface Awareness DC 8 (solo) → discover the golden Residual glow on the doorframe. Enter the Gap → the old man's Residual repeatedly walks toward the doorway.
2. **Interact with the Lingerer**: The old man wants "that photo." Show the photo → he smiles and crosses the Threshold, vanishing. Liberation reward: Return Mark +1.
3. **Head to the Old Station**: Move from the nursing home Gap to the station Gap (Traverse DC 8). The 1967 platform is well preserved.
4. **Reveal the Truth**: Retrieval DC 10 to find the baby shoe, DC 6 to find the letter. The letter reveals: Mr. Chen had a fiancée and child in his youth; in spring 1967 his fiancée came to the station with the child to find him—he never showed up.
5. **Return**: Hand the items to Mrs. Chen. After a silence she says, "Thank you. I'd rather know the truth." Return Mark +1.

### Rewards

- Return Mark × 2
- The Threshold of Room 302 in the nursing home becomes a Memory Threshold (DC -2)
