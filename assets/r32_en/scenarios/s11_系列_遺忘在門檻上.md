## Scenario 1: "Forgotten on the Threshold"

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Lost-Item Commission |
| **Recommended Marks** | 0-1 (beginner group) |
| **Players** | 2-4 |
| **Sessions** | 1-2 |
| **Core Attributes** | Awareness, Retrieval |
| **Difficulty** | ★☆☆☆☆ |

### Commission

A middle-aged woman—Mrs. Chen—contacted a Gap Walker. Her father passed away last week in a nursing home. While sorting his belongings, she found a photograph in his wallet she had never seen: a young woman holding an infant, with "Spring 1967, the station" written on the back. A nurse at the nursing home said the old man spent his last few months repeatedly walking to his door, touching the handle, murmuring "photo… photo…"

Mrs. Chen wants to know: who is the woman in the photo? Why had it stayed in her father's wallet for fifty years?

### Surface Map

- **Nursing home**: A three-story old building. Mr. Chen's room is at the end of the second-floor hallway (Room 302).
- **Old station**: Two blocks from the nursing home. Demolished in 1985, now a bus transfer station.
- **Mrs. Chen's home**: Holds her father's belongings box.

### Gap Layer Map

**The Gap Layer of the nursing home:** The hallway is twice as long as on the Surface. The Threshold at Room 302 is a Natural Threshold (DC 8)—the old man crossed here repeatedly, wearing a "membrane" into it. Faint golden Residual light on the doorframe—the "trace of his hesitation" left behind.

**The Gap Layer of the station:** Although the station was demolished, the 1967 platform remains in the Gap Layer. Benches, timetables, even an old vending machine—all translucent, floating about two meters below the ground of the bus transfer station.

### Key NPCs

| NPC | Info |
|---|---|
| **Mrs. Chen (Client)** | A woman in her 50s, pragmatic but emotional. She wants the truth, but also fears the truth will change her memory of her father. |
| **Head Nurse of the nursing home** | Cared for Mr. Chen in his last three months. Noticed the old man always walking to his door at dusk, "as if waiting for someone." |
| **Old Stationmaster** | A retired stationmaster in his 80s, living near the nursing home. The only one who remembers the old station. |

### Key Lost Items

| Lost Item | Quality | DC | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| The photo's negative | Memory Crystal | 15 | Nursing home Gap—at the door of Room 302 |
| A letter (unsent) | Common Lost Item | 8 | Old station Gap—under the platform bench |
| A baby shoe | Benevolent Keepsake | 12 | Old station Gap—beside the vending machine |

### Gap Creatures

| Creature | Type | Negotiation DC | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mr. Chen's Residual | Threshold Lingerer | 10 | Threshold of Room 302, nursing home |

### Key Scenes

1. **Investigate the nursing home**: Players investigate Room 302 on the Surface. Awareness DC 10 reveals faint golden Residual light on the doorframe. After entering the Gap, they see an old man's Residual repeatedly walking to the door, touching the handle, murmuring "photo… has she come…".

2. **Interact with the Threshold Lingerer**: The old man's Residual needs "that photo." If the players obtain the photo from Mrs. Chen (or the image from the Memory Crystal) and give it to him—he smiles, steps across the Threshold, and vanishes. Liberation reward: Return Mark +1.

3. **Head to the old station**: Move from the nursing home to the old station within the Gap. Although the Surface is demolished, the Gap Layer retains the 1967 scene. Retrieval DC 12 to find the baby shoe, DC 8 to find the unsent letter.

4. **Reveal the truth**: The letter's contents reveal—Mr. Chen had a fiancée and a child when young. In spring 1967, the fiancée came to the station with the child to find him. He did not show up. He hesitated. That photo was the last letter sent by the fiancée.

5. **Return**: Give the photo (a copy), the letter, and the baby shoe to Mrs. Chen. She is silent for a long time. Finally she says: "Thank you. I would rather know the truth." Return Mark +1.

### Ending Options

- Mrs. Chen keeps the photo, placing the letter and baby shoe before her father's gravestone.
- Players may choose to leave the items in the Gap—the Gap's benevolence reduces Endurance Cost in that area by -1.
- If the old man's Residual is liberated, the Threshold at Room 302 of the nursing home becomes safe from then on.
