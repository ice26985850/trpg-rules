# Scenario 6: "The Collector's Bargain"
| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Type** | Negotiation / Intelligence Gathering |
| **Recommended Marks** | 4-6 |
| **Sessions** | 1-2 sessions |
| **Core Attributes** | Negotiation, Retrieval |
| **Difficulty** | ★★★ |

### Opening

You notice an anomaly in the Gap — the Lost Items in a certain area are "moving." They aren't being picked up — they're changing position on their own. Tracking this phenomenon, you discover an extraordinarily ancient Lost Item Collector. It is no ordinary Collector — it has remained in the Gap for over a thousand years. Its "collection" contains lost Gap knowledge.

### NPCs

| NPC | Info |
|-----|------|
| **The Millennial Collector** | One of the oldest Gap Creatures. Not transformed from a human — a Han-dynasty bronze mirror that "awoke" amid layers of accumulated consciousness |
| **Three Lesser Collectors** | The Millennial Collector's "offspring." Each guards one key item |
| **Old Mo (optional)** | If already recruited as a companion, can provide historical background on the Millennial Collector |

### Lost Items

| Lost Item | Quality | Solo DC | Guardian |
|--------|------|:---:|------|
| Fragment of a Han-Dynasty Bronze Mirror | Gap Construct | 16 | Collector A (requires a poem) |
| A Lost Gap Map | Memory Crystal | 13 | Collector B (requires a period of silence) |
| Sealed Space Key | Gap Construct | 16 | Collector C (requires a name) |

### Scenes

1. **Tracking the Phenomenon**: Awareness DC 8 reveals the movement trajectory of the Lost Items. Traverse DC 8 to track it to a "Collector's Lair."
2. **First Contact**: The Millennial Collector is not hostile — it is curious. Negotiation DC 12 to establish initial dialogue. It speaks "like the overlapping sound of many letters of paper rubbing together."
3. **Three Trials**: The Millennial Collector is willing to share its knowledge — provided you pass the trials of its three "offspring":
   - **Collector A**: Wants a poem. The player must improvise or recite a poem related to "loss."
   - **Collector B**: Wants a period of silence. The player must remain silent for 3 turns — during which the GM describes anomalies in the Gap, testing whether the player speaks.
   - **Collector C**: Wants a name. You must obtain the name of the mirror's owner from Old Mo or another Walker.
4. **The Harvest**: After completing the trials, the Millennial Collector reveals a lost Gap map — showing the location of a Sealed Space never before discovered nearby (foreshadowing for Scenario 9/10).
5. **Extra Harvest**: Optionally trade a Lost Item with the Millennial Collector for a "Gap Knowledge Mark" — a permanent Negotiation Advantage (Negotiation DC -2 against all Collectors).

### Rewards

- Return Mark × 1 (not from a return — from "an exchange of knowledge")
- Bronze Mirror Fragment (Gap Construct — can serve as a light source in Sealed Spaces, illuminating hidden seal markings)
- Gap Knowledge Mark (permanent Advantage on Negotiation with Collectors)
- Unlock a new Sealed Space location (foreshadowing for Scenario 9/10)
