# Scenario 8: "The Mirror Maze"
| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Type** | Mirror Chase / Psychological Thriller |
| **Recommended Marks** | 7-8 |
| **Sessions** | 2-3 |
| **Core Attributes** | Anchor, Negotiation, Awareness |
| **Difficulty** | ★★★★ |

### Opening

This isn't a commission — it's **personal**.

Your Mirror has started making "movements it shouldn't." At first it was just small deviations — it lagged half a second when you stopped. But last time, in the Gap, it spoke to you. In your voice, saying things you didn't know: "Do you still remember that summer? Under the bridge — what was it you abandoned?"

You never abandoned anything under the bridge. But you do have a gap in your memory.

### GM Prep

This scenario is highly personal. The GM must communicate with the player:
1. A forgotten childhood memory of the character (may come from character creation step 5 "What You Lost" or step 7 "the Threshold You Won't Cross Again")
2. The Mirror's secret — related to that memory
3. The GM chooses an ending direction: Fusion, Separation, Sacrifice

> Reference: character creation steps are in the Player Rulebook §2.1-2.7.

### Three Mirror Stages

| Stage | Manifestation | Check |
|------|------|------|
| Hint | The Mirror lags, shows slight deviation | Awareness Difficulty Class (DC) 10 to confirm the anomaly |
| Dialogue | The Mirror speaks — in your voice, saying things you don't know | Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 12 to confirm "is this a hallucination or real" |
| Confrontation | The Mirror waits for you under the bridge (or at the core memory site) | See Scene 5 |

### Scenes

1. **First Anomaly**: During a routine traverse, you see the Mirror's anomalous behavior. It stops mimicking, stands still — then speaks a sentence. Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 10 (after solo -2) to confirm this isn't a hallucination.[[MAP-08 場景2]]

2. **Tracking**: The Mirror moves through the Gap, leaving a "resonant trail." Awareness Difficulty Class (DC) 10 to track. The trail leads you through your personal history — your childhood home, the old school, finally to the bridge.[[MAP-08 場景3]]

3. **Dig Up the Memory**: Retrieval Difficulty Class (DC) 13 to find a diary (Memory Crystal) in the Gap of your childhood home. You see the day your childhood friend drowned — you were there, and out of fear did not help. Afterward you "forgot" this memory.[[MAP-08 場景4]]

4. **Memory Guardian**: The path to the bridge is blocked by a Path Guardian (Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 12). You need "a name" or "a true memory" to pass. This proves you are ready to face it.[[MAP-08 場景5]]

5. **Confrontation Under the Bridge**: Three ending options —
   - **Accept the Memory**: The Mirror fuses with you. You lose the "Mirror's Sacrifice" ability. Gap Sickness reverses one stage. Permanent Advantage on all Anchor checks.
   - **Reject the Memory**: The Mirror stays in the Gap, becoming an independent existence. Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to resist Gap Sickness worsening. May be encountered again in future scenarios.
   - **The Mirror's Sacrifice**: If you are in any stage of Gap Sickness, the Mirror takes your place, bearing your Gap Sickness — you recover, and lose the Mirror permanently.

> **Map Reference:** Scenario 8 refers throughout to `maps/MAP-08_mirror_path.html` (Mirror Tracking Path map).

### Safety Net Trigger

Intuitive Dodge is unavailable in this scenario (the Mirror space interferes with the ejection path). But **Anchoring Item Resonance** is available (reroll once).

### Rewards

- Return Mark × 1 (returning the memory to yourself — a unique return)
- Fusion Ending: Permanent Advantage (all Anchor checks)
- Rejection Ending: Future Mirror-related plots may continue
- Sacrifice Ending: Character recovers + gains the first step of the Legendary Mark (the GM may choose to have the Mirror return in some form in Scenario 10)
