### Opening

The old Eastside neighborhood is "vanishing" — the alleys show wrong perspective, and when you open your front door it isn't your own living room; the basement goes three levels down and still isn't at the bottom. Three residents have disappeared. The city government has sealed off three blocks. You receive an urgent warning: a Crack in a Sealed Space is expanding. Someone put something inside it that shouldn't be there.

### Scenes

1. **Assess the Crack**: Awareness Difficulty Class (DC) 10 to see the spatial anomaly. Traverse Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to enter safely (solo already -2).[[MAP-07 場景1]]
2. **Path Guardian**: Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 14. Each 3 turns waited: Difficulty Class (DC) -2. Speaking your True Name auto-succeeds.[[MAP-07 場景3]]
3. **Find the Old Walker's Notes**: Three puzzle-like notes record the sealing method and the girl's name.[[MAP-07 場景4]]
4. **Liberate vs. Seal**: Find the little girl — she crouches in a corner humming the same song. Requires three items (photo + schoolbag + name) to liberate.
   - Liberate: Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 20 (with the three items, Difficulty Class (DC) 14). Success → she smiles and vanishes, the Crack begins to heal.[[MAP-07 場景5]]
   - Seal: Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 18. Temporarily safe; will expand again months later.
   - Failure: On the third day the Crack swallows the block. All residents begin to forget they ever lived there.
5. **Siblings' Reunion**: If the 80-year-old brother is willing to enter the Gap to see his sister — while present, the Liberate Difficulty Class (DC) drops to 8 (at the edge of automatic success).[[MAP-07 場景6]]

### Safety Net Trigger
> **Map Reference:** Scenario 7 refers throughout to `maps/MAP-07_community_crack.html` (Eastside Community Crack map).

If a player's Gap Endurance (GE) hits zero in this scenario — Intuitive Dodge ejects you to the nearest safe Threshold. The Crack expands half a day more — but no disaster. (Reference: Solo Expansion Rules §S2.3)
