### Opening

On the map of the Millennia Collector you found a mark: "Below this point — no one returns." That isn't a Sealed Space — it's **the Deep Gap**. The "bottom" of the Gap — a region built from millennia of all forgotten things stacked together. It keeps extending downward. No one knows what lies at the bottom. But your Anchoring Item resonated — it's pointing into the depths.

### Prerequisites

- Must possess an Anchoring Item
- Recommended: a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 3 accompanies you
- Recommended: obtained the Bronze Mirror Shard from Scenario 6 (the only stable light source in the Deep Gap)

### NPCs

| NPC | Info |
|-----|------|
| **Old Mo (Companion)** | If already recruited, may accompany you in. In his youth he once tried to explore the Deep Gap — retreated after reaching the second layer |
| **The Nonexistent One** | A "resident" of the Deep Gap — not a creature, but the remnant of a "possibility" utterly forgotten by everyone |
| **The Voice of the Gap** | Not a creature, not a consciousness — the Gap's reaction to "something moving" |

### Deep Gap Rules (exclusive to this scenario)

- Each layer's Gap Endurance (GE) cost +1 (first layer -2/turn, second layer -3/turn, third layer -4/turn)
- Time Flow uses d6+2 (minimum 3, maximum 8 days) — you don't know when you'll return to the Surface
- Gap Sickness worsening check frequency doubled (once per scene, not per chapter)
- No sense of direction — all Traverse Difficulty Class (DC) +2
- The Bronze Mirror Shard provides light and can briefly stabilize direction (1 use/layer, Traverse Advantage)

### Scenes

1. **First Layer: Memory Sediment Layer**: Building residuals from countless eras overlap — medieval stone walls pass through modern glass curtain walls. Fragments of text in unrecognizable languages are everywhere. Awareness Difficulty Class (DC) 10 to find the footprints of previous Walkers — they reached here, but did not continue downward.[[MAP-09 場景1]]

2. **Second Layer: Sea of Possibilities**: Physical laws begin to fail. The space is filled with "possibilities" — you see residuals of the paths in your life that were never chosen. Anchor Difficulty Class (DC) 14 (including -2) to resist being drawn into your own possibilities. Failure → Lost (condition).[[MAP-09 場景2]]

3. **Third Layer: City of the Nameless**: A city built from "forgotten existences" — streets, buildings, even bridges. But everything is slowly collapsing. The Nonexistent One wanders here — they have no faces, because no one remembers them. Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC) 16 to speak with one of them — it remembers something, but cannot say it.[[MAP-09 場景3]]

4. **The Bottom**: Not ground — it's a **mirror**. The entire bottom of the Deep Gap is an infinitely large mirror surface. The you in the mirror is the "closest to real" version among all your versions. The you in the mirror speaks to you — in your own voice, but not the Mirror's mimicry. It says: "You finally made it. Welcome to where all forgotten things pile up. Are you looking for this?" In its hand it holds the "counterpart" of your Anchoring Item — the Anchoring Item's essence in the Gap.[[MAP-09 場景4]]

5. **The Choice**:
   - **Touch the Mirror Surface**: Your Anchoring Item becomes a "Complete Anchoring Item" — Gap Endurance (GE) +10, permanent Advantage on all worsening contests. But a part of you stays in the mirror forever. Every time you look in a mirror, you see the you in the mirror standing somewhere you are not.
   - **Accept the Counterpart but Leave**: The Anchoring Item is upgraded to a "Deep Anchoring Item" — Gap Endurance (GE) +8. No side effects.
   - **Reject**: You prove you don't need a stronger Anchoring Item. Permanent Advantage on Anchor checks (your Anchor comes not from an item — from me).

> **Map Reference:** Scenario 9 refers throughout to `maps/MAP-09_deep_gap.html` (Deep Gap three-layer vertical cross-section map).

### Safety Net

If a Companion accompanies you — a Companion with Loyalty ≥ 4 may bear the Lost (condition) for you on the third layer. If a player's Gap Endurance (GE) hits zero — Intuitive Dodge bounces you back to the second layer's entrance (you won't return to the Surface — you must walk back yourself).

### Rewards

- Return Mark × 2
- Strengthen the Anchoring Item (by choice) + Gap Endurance (GE) Maximum increase
- Gap-Depth Knowledge Mark (Advantage on all future Deep Gap-related checks)
- Companion Loyalty +1 (if accompanied to the end)
