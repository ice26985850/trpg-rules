# Item and Key Object Codex

> The world of the Gap Walker has almost no "equipment" or "loot." Here, "items" refer to objects that hold real meaning while traversing the Gap — they are not for growing stronger, but for **remembering which side you came from.**

---

## I. Anchoring Item

An Anchoring Item is a physical object deeply connected to your personal history. It does not change in the Gap — while the Gap distorts everything, the Anchoring Item **stays as it is**. That is why it can anchor: it is the only thing unaffected by the Gap.

- **Cannot be**: ordinary items with no personal emotional connection (a common key won't do — unless it was the one your late grandmother gave you).
- **Can be**: items tied to major turning points in your life (a university acceptance letter, the ticket stub from your first trip abroad), or keepsakes left by someone important.

### Three Initial States

| State | Description | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Owned** | You have found your Anchoring Item and carry it | **Passive**: Gap Endurance (GE) maximum +5, Advantage on Gap Sickness worsening Checks, and relief from symptoms of Gap Sickness at Middle Stage or below. **Active**: each turn you may spend 1 Action Point (AP) to touch the Anchoring Item, gaining Advantage on Anchor Checks that turn (including marking paths, confirming layers, resisting being Lost). **Meditation**: once per day, spend 10 minutes in focused Meditation to recover Anchor Value × 3 points of Gap Endurance (GE). |
| **Lost (Known)** | You know what it might be, but haven't found it yet | No immediate mechanical effect. This is your personal Plot Goal; the GM should design "finding the Anchoring Item" as an important narrative milestone. |
| **Lost (Unknown)** | You don't know what your Anchoring Item is | Left blank at creation. The GM may arrange a moment of "discovering the Anchoring Item" within the story — usually when you need it most. |

### Wear and Loss

- **Worn**: After staying in the Gap too long, being coveted by Gap Creatures, or forcibly bringing it into emotionally intense Surface locations, the Anchoring Item develops cracks and feels cooler to the touch. While Worn, the Advantage from "touching the Anchoring Item," Meditation recovery, and the passive Gap Sickness Advantage are all temporarily disabled (the Gap Endurance bonus is retained) until repaired by living continuously on the Surface for 3 or more days.
- **Loss**: When stolen, lost, or taken in the Gap, Gap Endurance (GE) maximum –5, Gap Sickness risk rises, and recovering the Anchoring Item becomes the character's **highest-priority Plot Goal**.

---

## II. Lost Item

A Lost Item is something "stuck between two spaces for too long" — when someone forgot them, they fell into the Gap. The **Retrieval (RE)** attribute determines whether you can identify and recover them, and read the "forgotten story" within them.

- **Common Lost Item**: everyday forgotten objects, retrievable at Retrieval Difficulty Class (DC) 8–9.
- **Emotionally heavy Lost Item**: deeply bound to a certain memory or person, often a coveted bargaining chip for Gap Creatures (especially Lost Item Collectors).
- **Gravity-Zone Lost Item**: remains only in Zero-Gravity Zones, requiring special movement to obtain.

---

## III. Return Mark

A Mark earned after helping a Gap Creature complete its Transition. Collected, they unlock "Return" — sending the creature back to its rightful Surface location, or redeeming it for long-term abilities / plot progression. It is the core measure of progress and growth in Solo Mode.

---

## IV. Memory Crystal

Memory fragments condensed in the Gap, appearing as tangible crystalline objects. Gap Creatures feed on memory — exchanging with a Memory Crystal is the highest-value bargaining chip when negotiating with them (high Negotiation Difficulty Class (DC)). It can also be used to unlock clues to a Sealed Space, or restore someone's True Name.

---

## V. Special Objects in the Gap

- **Seal Key**: a medium for opening a Sealed Space — it may be a Lost Item, a Memory Crystal, or the Sealer themselves.
- **Gap Shortcut**: a "cut-through" permission that a Lost Item Collector or specific creature may grant, spanning the distance between Surface and Gap.
- **Memory Threshold**: after helping a Threshold Lingerer complete that one step, the Threshold becomes your Memory Threshold, and entering it thereafter automatically reduces Difficulty Class (DC) by 3.
