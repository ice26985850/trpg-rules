## Campaign Overview

### Main Plot Summary

An aberrant Stellaron fluctuation sweeps across the galaxy; all known Stellarons simultaneously enter a state of "Resonance"—they are calling to one another. The Astral Express receives a distress signal from the distant star system "Rustcrown," a world smothered by steam and gears that is collapsing. But Rustcrown is only the beginning: the truth of the Stellaron Resonance points to a forgotten Aeon—the "Voice of Annihilation"—whose final shard is awakening from the void.

The players begin as new passengers aboard the Express, traverse the steampunk world of Rustcrown, infiltrate the data necropolis of the quantum abyss, make choices in the cracks between factions, and finally face the advent of the Voice of Annihilation—and decide its fate.

## Act 1: First Cry of the Star Tracks (Lv 1–4)

### Module Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Theme** | Team assembly, first real combat, Belobog crisis |
| **Recommended Level** | 1–4 |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 4–5 sessions |
| **Suggested Core Paths** | At least 1 Preservation/Abundance, rest free |

### Story Background

The Astral Express receives an encrypted distress signal from Belobog—the Great Guardian requests that the Nameless assist in investigating anomalous Fragmentum activity in the Lower District. At the same time, Silver Wolf of the Stellaron Hunters appears on the Express's comm channel, proposing a deal: she holds intelligence on Rustcrown, but the players must first "prove their usefulness." The Express docks at Belobog, and an adventure of trials and assembly begins.

### Chapter Structure

#### Scene 1: Strangers Aboard the Express (Lv 1)

**Setting**: Observation Car of the Astral Express

**Plot**: Himeko gathers all new passengers to explain Belobog's distress call. Just then Silver Wolf's encrypted comm cuts in—she displays Stellaron Resonance fluctuation data from Rustcrown and proposes a trade: help her acquire a sample of the "Frostvein Core" beneath Belobog, and she will hand Rustcrown's intelligence to the Express. Himeko is reserved but agrees to let the players judge for themselves.

> **GM Note**: Scene 1 is the time for characters to get to know one another and build team relationships. Give each player a chance to introduce themselves and show off the traits of their Origin and Identity.

**Key NPCs**: Himeko (Friendly), Silver Wolf (guarded probing)

**Checks & Challenges**:
- DC 12 Perception: notice unusual quantum noise in the background of Silver Wolf's transmission (hinting she is being tracked)
- DC 13 Insight: judge how much of Silver Wolf's proposal is true (partly true—she does want the sample, but the "deal" itself is a test of the players)

**Reward**: Express supplies (basic survival kit resupply ×1)

---

#### Scene 2: Cracks in the Frozen City (Lv 1–2)

**Setting**: Belobog Lower District—entrance to the abandoned mine

**Plot**: The players arrive at Belobog; the Great Guardian's adjutant informs them the "Frostvein Core" lies deep within the abandoned mine, but Fragmentum monsters have multiplied recently and the mine is sealed. The players must traverse the mine to reach the Fragmentum rift where the core resides.

**Combat Encounters**:

| Encounter | Monsters | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|------------|
| Mine Entrance | Fragmentum Wraith ×3 | Challenge Rating (CR) 1/2 | Easy |
| Mid Mine Tunnel | Frozen Guard ×2 | Challenge Rating (CR) 1 | Common |
| Before the Fragmentum Rift | Frostvein Guardian (Elite) | Challenge Rating (CR) 2 (Toughness 39) | Hard |

**Frostvein Guardian** (Challenge Rating (CR) 2 Elite)
- Armor Class (AC) 14 | Hit Points (HP) 55 | Toughness 39 (30×1.3)
- Frost Strike: +5 to hit, 1d8+3 Ice damage, DC 11 Constitution (CON) save or be Slowed
- Fragmentum Summon: every 3 rounds, summons 1 Fragmentum Wraith
- Weakness: Fire, Physical

**Non-Combat Challenges**:
- DC 13 Engineering: repair the mine elevator (on failure, must climb a 30m ice wall, DC 14 Athletics)
- DC 12 Survival: track the movement paths of Fragmentum creatures to find a shortcut (saves 1 random encounter)

**Reward**: After defeating the guardian, each player gains 150 Experience Points (XP); Silver Wolf honors the deal—providing initial intelligence on Rustcrown

#### Scene 3: Silver Wolf's Trial (Lv 2–3)

**Setting**: Belobog Upper District—abandoned observatory

**Plot**: Silver Wolf appears, saying she is quite pleased with the players' performance. But she has one more "small favor"—the observatory has been occupied by an Antimatter Legion outpost that is scanning Stellaron fluctuation data. Silver Wolf needs the players to clear this outpost; in exchange she will reveal more intelligence about the "Stellaron Resonance."

**Combat Encounters**:

**Marauder Captain** (Challenge Rating (CR) 4 Elite)
- Armor Class (AC) 16 | Hit Points (HP) 80 | Toughness 58 (45×1.3)
- Quantum Cannon: +6 to hit, 2d8+4 Quantum damage, DC 12 Perception (PER) save or be Entangled
- Tactical Retreat: when Hit Points (HP) < 50%, move ×2 and call reinforcements (2 Marauders, Challenge Rating (CR) 3)
- Weakness: Wind, Physical

**Key Decision**: After clearing the observatory, the players find that the Antimatter Legion's communications log mentions the "Voice of Annihilation"—something the Legion itself fears. Tell Silver Wolf? Tell the Express? Different choices affect the flow of later intelligence.

**Reward**: 300 Experience Points (XP), Silver Wolf's Stellaron Resonance data chip (a key item for Act 2), Lv 3 level-up

#### Scene 4: Oath Beneath the Starlight (Lv 3–4)

**Setting**: Astral Express—Observation Car

**Plot**: The players return to the Express. Himeko analyzes Silver Wolf's data and the Legion records from the observatory, confirming the Stellaron Resonance is a real threat. She gives a formal mission: travel to Rustcrown to investigate the source of the resonance. At this moment Silver Wolf sends one last message—"Rustcrown's Stellaron was not discovered—it was 'manufactured.' Beware that prophet."

> **Character Development Moment**: Before departure, each player may engage in character interaction during a short rest—getting to know one another's backgrounds, discussing motivations, or holding private conversations with Himeko/Welt.

## Act 2: Heart of Rustcrown (Lv 5–8)

### Module Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Theme** | Steampunk apocalypse, class struggle, tech ethics |
| **Recommended Level** | 5–8 |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 5–6 sessions |
| **Core Setting** | Original planet "Rustcrown" (see Rulebook 4.4, Sample Planet A) |

### Story Background

Rustcrown—a planet whose industrialization was accelerated by a Stellaron. The city is built atop giant gears, and perpetual smoke obscures the sky. The Steam Duke rules the Upper District; his "Perpetual Furnace" powers the entire city, while workers in the Lower District struggle in toxic mist. The resistance "the Daughter of Gears" claims the furnace's core is the Stellaron itself—and that the Duke, to maintain his rule, feeds it with human lives.

But the truth is darker: it is not the Duke exploiting the Stellaron, but the Stellaron exploiting the Duke. Through industrial pollution it devours the planet's very "life force," using it as fuel to send resonance signals to other Stellarons. And the Corroded Prophet—a Resonator marked by the Stellaron—knows how to shut it down, but the cost is the collapse of Rustcrown's entire civilization.

### Chapter Structure

#### Scene 1: Descent Amid the Smoke (Lv 5)

**Setting**: Rustcrown—Upper District "Duke's Plaza"

**Plot**: The Express docks at Rustcrown's only Star Track node—Duke's Plaza. Greeting the players is the Duke's Steam Guard, polite but oppressive. The Duke holds a banquet in the palace, speaking earnestly—he needs the Nameless to help investigate the furnace's "instability," hinting that the resistance is sabotaging the city's heart. Meanwhile, a resistance member disguised as a waiter slips the players a note: "Do not trust the Duke. Midnight, Gear Alley, the truth."

**Check Challenges**:
- DC 14 Insight: the Duke's words are laced with fear—what he fears is not the resistance, but the furnace itself
- DC 13 Perception: in a corner of the banquet hall hangs a Stellaron fluctuation monitor, its needle slowly climbing
- DC 15 Technology: analyze the monitor's data—the fluctuation pattern matches exactly the resonance data Silver Wolf provided

**Social Challenge**: The Duke is a target with NPC Social Toughness 30 (Weakness: Intimidation; Resistance: Persuasion); attempt to extract more intelligence during the banquet.

**Reward**: 200 Experience Points (XP), Rustcrown map (industrial zone + Lower District)

---

#### Scene 2: The Truth of Gear Alley (Lv 5–6)

**Setting**: Lower District—Gear Alley resistance stronghold

**Plot**: The players infiltrate the Lower District and make contact with the resistance. The Daughter of Gears—a young woman who lost half an arm, replaced by a steam prosthetic—shows the evidence the resistance has gathered: the furnace's fuel is not merely steam, but also "Life Essence"—an energy extracted from the Fragmentum, and the Fragmentum's expansion is devouring the lifespans of Lower District residents. Most unsettling: the Corroded Prophet was once the Duke's chief engineer, and after discovering the truth chose to imprison himself deep in the furnace—he says only he knows how to stop it all, but "the stop button is also the city's death sentence."

**Combat Encounters**:

| Encounter | Monsters | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|------------|
| Gear Alley Patrol | Steam Guardsman ×3 | Challenge Rating (CR) 3 | Common |
| Duke's Spies | Mechanical Hound ×2 + Spy Captain | Challenge Rating (CR) 4 + 5 | Hard |

**Spy Captain** (Challenge Rating (CR) 5 Elite)
- Armor Class (AC) 17 | Hit Points (HP) 95 | Toughness 78 (60×1.3)
- Steam Jet: 15m cone, 3d6 Fire damage, DC 14 Agility (AGI) save for half
- Stealth Device: bonus action to become Invisible for 1 round
- Weakness: Lightning, Ice

**Key Decision**: Once the players have the full evidence, three routes are available:
1. **Confront the Duke**: face him directly with the evidence (social route, may trigger a coup)
2. **Seek the Prophet**: first infiltrate the furnace to ask the Corroded Prophet for a third path (exploration route)
3. **Aid the Resistance**: help the Daughter of Gears arm an uprising (combat route)

> **GM Note**: The three routes are not mutually exclusive; the players may mix them. But each route's difficulty and consequences differ—confronting the Duke is the riskiest yet may avoid bloodshed; seeking the prophet is the most time-consuming but may reveal a "perfect solution"; aiding the resistance is the most direct but with the greatest casualties.

**Reward**: 300 Experience Points (XP), an intelligence network provided by the resistance (advantage ×1 on information checks in Act 2)

---

#### Scene 3: Depths of the Furnace (Lv 6–7)

**Setting**: Steam Furnace—Core Abyss

**Plot**: Whichever route they take, the players ultimately must enter the furnace. Inside is a vertical abyss, steam pipelines crisscrossing like blood vessels, Fragmentum energy pulsing within. At the very bottom, the Corroded Prophet is trapped in a cage made of Stellaron shards—not imprisoned by others, but having walked in himself, because his very existence accelerates the Stellaron's resonance.

**Combat Encounters**:

| Encounter | Monsters | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|------------|
| Furnace Entrance | Steam Mecha ×2 | Challenge Rating (CR) 5 | Common |
| Pipeline Layer | Fragmentum Derivative ×4 | Challenge Rating (CR) 4 | Hard |
| Core Layer | Stellaron Guardian (Boss) | Challenge Rating (CR) 8 (Toughness 104) | Boss |

**Stellaron Guardian** (Challenge Rating (CR) 8 Boss, two-phase)
- **Phase 1**: Armor Class (AC) 18 | Hit Points (HP) 160 | Toughness 104 (80×1.3)
  - Quantum Lash: +8 to hit, 2d10+5 Quantum damage, DC 15 Constitution (CON) save or be Entangled
  - Fragmentum Shield: every 3 rounds, generates a 30 Hit Points (HP) Shield
  - Weakness: Wind, Fire
- **Phase 2** (after the shield is Broken): Armor Class (AC) 16 | remaining Hit Points (HP) unchanged | Toughness 0 (Broken)
  - Overload Burst: 30m burst, radius 2, 6d8 Quantum damage, DC 16 Agility (AGI) save for half
  - Each round summons 1 Fragmentum Derivative
  - Weakness: all elements (defense collapses under overload state)

**Environmental Effect**: Every 3 rounds, 2 random squares of pipeline rupture, spewing high-temperature steam (2d6 Fire damage; that square becomes hazardous terrain)

**Core Decision**: After defeating the guardian, the Corroded Prophet can finally speak. He offers three options:
1. **Shut Down the Furnace**: the Stellaron stops resonating, but Rustcrown's energy is cut off—the entire city will freeze within a week, and tens of thousands will die
2. **Transfer the Stellaron**: take the Stellaron away from Rustcrown, but someone must "share" the Stellaron's energy shock during the transfer—the sharer will be permanently marked as a Resonator and henceforth hunted by the Stellaron
3. **The Prophet's Sacrifice**: the Corroded Prophet seals himself together with the Stellaron in a dimensional rift—the Stellaron no longer threatens Rustcrown, but the prophet is lost forever between the rifts

**Reward**: 600 Experience Points (XP), different loot based on the choice (see below)

| Choice | Consequence | Loot |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Shut Down the Furnace | Rustcrown's civilization collapses, but the Stellaron stops resonating. The Daughter of Gears leads the refugees to evacuate | Duke's Vault: 1 × 4★ Light Cone + 1,000 Credit Points (CP) |
| Transfer the Stellaron | The sharer gains the extra ability "Stellaron Resonator (Origin)" | Resonance Crystal from the prophet: once per long rest, precise foreknowledge (+5 Initiative) |
| The Prophet's Sacrifice | Rustcrown is saved, but the prophet vanishes into the rift | The prophet's relic: 1 × 5★ Light Cone "In the Night" |

---

#### Scene 4: The Smoke Clears (Lv 7–8)

**Setting**: Rustcrown—Duke's Palace / Rebel HQ (depending on the choice)

**Plot**: With the Stellaron threat gone, Rustcrown's political landscape is reshaped. If the Duke survives, he may be overthrown or forced to reform; if the Daughter of Gears takes over, she faces the arduous task of rebuilding civilization. Either way, the players are the catalyst of this change—and Stellaron Hunter Silver Wolf appears again at this moment, her message brief and grave: "The Voice of Annihilation is no legend. Its first shard has been found on the Xianzhou. If you want to know why Rustcrown's Stellaron resonates, come find me."

**Character Development Moment**: After Rustcrown's major decision, the characters should have a sincere exchange—who is questioning their choice? Who regrets it? Who is more resolved?

**Reward**: Milestone level-up to Lv 8; the Express provides 200 Credit Point (CP) supplies. Silver Wolf provides passage clearance for the Xianzhou.

## Act 3: Echo of the Abyss (Lv 9–12)

### Module Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Theme** | Truth of the Voice of Annihilation, quantum abyss, the cost of memory |
| **Recommended Level** | 9–12 |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 5–6 sessions |
| **Core Setting** | Xianzhou Luofu + Abyssal Data Necropolis (original location) |

### Story Background

Silver Wolf's intelligence points to Xianzhou Luofu—the Cloud Knights discovered a data crystal "not from this universe" while purging an ancient Fragmentum. Analysis by the Erudition Society shows the crystal stores part of the "Voice of Annihilation's" memories—it was once among the oldest Aeons of the universe, defeated by a coalition of other Aeons and shattered into fragments scattered across the Stellarons of various planets. Each fragment retains an "echo" of the Voice of Annihilation—and these echoes are resonating anew.

But the crystal is sealed in the Xianzhou's "Abyssal Data Necropolis"—a quantum prison storing forbidden knowledge, jointly guarded by the Xianzhou's Six Offices and the Erudition Society. To enter the necropolis and retrieve the crystal, the players must obtain both parties' permission—or find a way in without it.

### Chapter Structure

#### Scene 1: The Xianzhou's Taboo (Lv 9)

**Setting**: Xianzhou Luofu—Erudition Society branch

**Plot**: The players arrive at the Xianzhou and meet the Erudition Society research team. The chief researcher "Mirror" is a scholar near-obsessed with studying the Voice of Annihilation. She shows a portion of publicly releasable data: the relationship between the Voice of Annihilation's fragments and the Stellarons resembles "parasitism"—the fragments attach to Stellarons and absorb planetary energy through them to repair themselves. Rustcrown's Stellaron accelerates its resonance because the fragment within it senses other fragments also awakening.

But Mirror conceals a fact: she once tried to link her own consciousness to the crystal, and as a result—a part of her memory was "devoured" by the crystal, and that part of her memory now wanders the necropolis in physical form.

**Social Challenge**: Persuade the Cloud Knights' Watch Commander to allow entry into the necropolis (NPC Social Toughness 40; Weakness: Lore argument; Resistance: Intimidation)

**Check Challenges**:
- DC 16 Lore: infer the number of Voice of Annihilation fragments from public data—at least 7, scattered across the Stellarons of 7 different planets
- DC 17 Insight: Mirror's expression is subtle when speaking of the crystal—she is afraid of something

**Reward**: 300 Experience Points (XP), a "Quantum Stabilizer" provided by the Erudition Society (prevents loss of consciousness in the abyss environment) ×1

---

#### Scene 2: The Data Necropolis (Lv 9–10)

**Setting**: Abyssal Data Necropolis—Quantum Prison, Layers 1 through 3

**Plot**: The necropolis entrance is a quantum encryption door requiring both the Cloud Knights' and the Erudition Society's keys. If the players successfully persuade both parties, they gain lawful entry; if persuasion fails, they may attempt to hack it (DC 20 Technology) or find the "back door" Silver Wolf provided (DC 14 Stealth to enter, but it triggers part of the defense system).

The necropolis has three layers in total:
- **Layer 1 "Gallery of Memory"**: stores ordinary forbidden knowledge; data pillars along the corridors seal away deleted histories
- **Layer 2 "Well of Oblivion"**: a vertical well structure holding "dangerous consciousness entities"—including the devoured memory fragment of Mirror
- **Layer 3 "Sanctum of the Core"**: the Voice of Annihilation's crystal is here, guarded by quantum entities

**Combat Encounters**:

| Encounter | Monsters | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|------------|
| Gallery of Memory | Data Wraith ×3 | Challenge Rating (CR) 6 | Common |
| Well of Oblivion | Mirror's Memory (Boss) | Challenge Rating (CR) 10 (Toughness 104) | Boss |
| Sanctum of the Core | Quantum Guardian ×2 (Elite) | Challenge Rating (CR) 9 (Toughness 116 ×2) | Hard |

**Mirror's Memory** (Challenge Rating (CR) 10 Boss; the devoured memory of Mirror materialized)
- Armor Class (AC) 19 | Hit Points (HP) 200 | Toughness 104 (80×1.3)—Special: when Toughness reaches 0 it does not become Broken; instead it triggers "Memory Release"
- Memory Plunder: +9 to hit, 2d8+6 mental damage, DC 16 Perception (PER) save or forget 1 skill proficiency (lasts until end of combat)
- Split Proliferation: when Hit Points (HP) < 50%, splits into 3 small memory fragments (Challenge Rating (CR) 5 each)
- Weakness: Quantum, Imaginary
- **Special Mechanic**: When a player speaks a correct memory related to Mirror's past (inferred from clues gathered earlier), the memory fragment loses 1 round of action

**Environmental Effect**: Each round, the Well of Oblivion randomly "erases" 1 square of space (that square becomes void; a character standing on it must make DC 15 Agility (AGI) save or fall, 2d10 fall damage)

**Key Decision**: After defeating Mirror's Memory, it briefly regains Mirror's consciousness. It begs the players—after obtaining the crystal, please help Mirror recover her devoured memory. But this means the players must take part of the memory fragment out of the necropolis, violating the Xianzhou's ban.

**Reward**: 600 Experience Points (XP), Voice of Annihilation crystal shard

#### Scene 3: The Crystal's Whisper (Lv 10–11)

**Setting**: Erudition Society branch—research lab / Xianzhou Cloud Knights HQ

**Plot**: The memories in the crystal begin affecting the bearer in the form of dreams. Every player who carries the crystal overnight has the same dream: they see the birth of the universe, the war of the Aeons, and the moment the Voice of Annihilation was shattered—not defeated by force, but voluntarily split. The Voice of Annihilation foresaw a future where the Aeons would eventually destroy one another, and so dispersed itself into the Stellarons, hoping that one day the fragments would reassemble and end this endless war of the Aeons with a "Final Note."

But over the long ages the fragments were distorted by the Stellarons' energy, shifting from a "will to end" into a "thirst for destruction." Now they no longer wish to end the war—they wish to end everything.

The crystal reveals the locations of the remaining 6 fragments, but at the same time draws a hunter—the Antimatter Legion's highest-ranking general, "Imaginary Echo," a Legion commander partially possessed by a Voice of Annihilation fragment.

**Combat Encounters**:

| Encounter | Monsters | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|-----------|----------|------------------------|------------|
| Xianzhou Port | Antimatter Legion Raiding Party ×4 | Challenge Rating (CR) 7 | Hard |
| Laboratory Defense | Imaginary Echo (First Form) | Challenge Rating (CR) 12 (Toughness 160) | Boss |

**Imaginary Echo (First Form)** (Challenge Rating (CR) 12 Boss)
- Armor Class (AC) 20 | Hit Points (HP) 260 | Toughness 160 (100×1.6)
- Annihilation Fluctuation: 30m burst, radius 3, 5d10 Imaginary damage, DC 17 Perception (PER) save for half
- Shard Resonance: each round drains 1d4 Energy Points (EP) from the crystal carried by the players
- Weakness: Physical, Quantum
- **Special Mechanic**: If a player uses the crystal to oppose Imaginary Echo (spends 1 action to resonate with the crystal), the Echo's Armor Class (AC) -2, and its weakness becomes all elements (lasts 1 round)

**Key Decision**: After repelling the First Form, Imaginary Echo retreats, leaving a threat—"The other fragments have already sensed your presence. On the journey ahead, you will not be alone." Mirror asks the players to return her devoured memory; the Cloud Knights demand the crystal be confiscated to ensure Xianzhou security. The players must make a choice.

**Reward**: 800 Experience Points (XP), the shard map left behind by Imaginary Echo (marking the approximate locations of the remaining 6 fragments)

#### Act IV: Revelation of the Star Map (Lv 11–12)

**Scene**: Astral Express — Meeting Car

**Plot**: Himeko and Welt analyzed the shard map and the memory data of the Voice of Annihilation. The conclusion is disturbing: among the remaining shards, 3 have already "activated" — they not only parasitize within Stellarons, but have begun parasitizing the creatures of planets as well. The most severe one is located in "Azure Abyss" (see rulebook 4.4, Sample Planet B), where the deep-sea civilization is collapsing due to shattered memories, and the source is precisely the Voice of Annihilation shard devouring the collective memory of the ocean.

Meanwhile, Silver Wolf sent an emergency communication — the Antimatter Legion has suddenly changed its strategy, shifting from "destroy everything" to "collect shards." The Imaginary Echo is using the Legion's power to collect other shards for itself. If he succeeds, the Voice of Annihilation will be resurrected in a distorted form.

**Decision Point**: The shards are scattered, and the players cannot address all threats simultaneously. They must decide on priorities:
1. Pursue the Imaginary Echo (stop him from collecting more shards, but Azure Abyss will continue to worsen)
2. Save Azure Abyss (resolve one planet's crisis, but the Imaginary Echo will grow stronger)
3. Seek a third path to "neutralize" the shards — Silver Wolf hints the World's End Tavern may hold the answer

> **GM Note**: This decision determines the direction of the content of Arc IV. Different choices lead to different faction alliances and final battle conditions. If the players choose the tavern route, it will introduce the deep involvement of the Masked Fools.

**Reward**: Milestone level-up to Lv 12, Lv 12 Ability Score Improvement (ASI). Himeko provides enhanced Light Cone upgrade materials — can upgrade one 3★ Light Cone to 4★.

## Arc IV: Path Crossing (Lv 13–16)

### Module Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Theme** | Faction maneuvering, moral dilemmas, foreshadowing of the final choice |
| **Recommended Level** | 13–16 |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 5–6 |
| **Core Scenes** | Multi-planet travel + World's End Tavern + faction negotiations |

### Story Background (continuing from the players' choice at the end of Arc III)

Regardless of which route the players chose at the end of Arc III, the core of Arc IV is: **all factions realize the threat of the Voice of Annihilation, but they have a fundamental divergence on "how to respond."**

- **IPC** wants to "commercialize" the shards — controlling the Voice of Annihilation's power as an energy source
- **Antimatter Legion**, manipulated by the Imaginary Echo, wants to collect the shards to resurrect the Voice of Annihilation
- **Erudition Society** wants to study the shards to understand the secrets of the Aeons (regardless of risk)
- **Cloud Knights (Xianzhou Cloud Knights)** advocate using force to thoroughly destroy all shards (which may destroy the host planets along with them)
- **Astral Express**'s stance is to find a way that does not sacrifice the innocent
- **Masked Fools** find all of this "very interesting" and are stirring things up from the shadows
- **Stellaron Hunters** want to use the shards to trade for some greater interest

The players must navigate this intricate web of relationships, building enough alliances to confront the Imaginary Echo — while ensuring the alliance itself does not collapse.

> **GM Note**: Arc IV is designed to be non-linear. The GM may freely combine the following modules based on the players' choices, and does not have to proceed in order.

### Module Menu

#### A. The Tavern's Gambit (Masked Fools route)

**Scene**: World's End Tavern (see rulebook 4.3, Key Faction Stronghold)

**Plot**: The players go to the tavern to find a way to "neutralize" the shards. The bartender proposes a gambit — the players must complete three "Challenges of the Fool," each revealing a weakness of the Voice of Annihilation shard. The challenge content is designed by the GM, and should be absurd yet secretly revealing.

**Challenge Examples**:
1. "Make the unhappiest guest in the tavern laugh" (reveals: shards have a repulsive reaction to positive emotional energy)
2. "Deliberately lose all your chips in a card game" (reveals: shards are weakened by "voluntary sacrifice")
3. "Dance with your own reflection" (reveals: the essence of the shard is "loneliness," and it is most vulnerable at the moment it realizes the existence of "connection")

**Reward**: Shard weakness intelligence — in the final battle of Arc V, the players can trigger the "Neutralization Ritual," and each completed challenge provides a +2 bonus to the ritual.

### Arc IV Key Decision

At the end of Arc IV, the players must make a final decision: **how to face the resurrection of the Voice of Annihilation?**

| Route | Consequence | Arc V Condition |

| **Neutralization Ritual** | Weaken the shard's power, attempt to restore the Voice of Annihilation's original "Termination" will rather than "Destruction" | Requires completing the tavern challenges + Core of Memory shard |
| **Forceful Termination** | Directly destroy all shards, may cause the shard energy to backlash | Requires military support from the IPC or Xianzhou |

## Arc V: The Descent of the End (Lv 17–20)

### Module Overview

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Theme** | Final showdown, ultimate sacrifice, the universe's choice |
| **Recommended Level** | 17–20 |
| **Estimated Sessions** | 5–6 |
| **Core Scenes** | Void Fragmentum (birthplace of the Voice of Annihilation) |

### Story Background

The Imaginary Echo — or the distorted shard of the Voice of Annihilation — has gathered enough power to begin rebuilding the "body" of the Voice of Annihilation in the Void Fragmentum. This is not resurrection, but birth: a brand-new existence pieced together from distorted shards is taking form. If it fully forms, the Stellarons of the entire galaxy will erupt simultaneously, and the Fragmentum will devour all known planets.

The players will enter the Void Fragmentum — a dimension composed of the shattered memories of the Voice of Annihilation. Here, the rules of time and space no longer exist; every step traverses the moment the Voice of Annihilation was shattered. The players must reach the core before the shards fully merge and make the final decision.

### Chapter Structure

#### Act I: The Fragmentum's Entrance (Lv 17)

**Scene**: Void Fragmentum — "The Beach of Memory Shards"

**Plot**: The entrance to the Void Fragmentum appears above multiple planets — meaning the Fragmentum has expanded uncontrollably. The alliance the players built provides support: the IPC's fleet establishes a blockade line on the periphery of the Fragmentum, the Xianzhou Cloud Knights handle the evacuation of residents of surrounding planets, and the Erudition Society's quantum stabilization devices slow the Fragmentum's expansion.

But the real battle is inside the Fragmentum. The first step into the Fragmentum, the players see the past shards of the Voice of Annihilation — the birth of the universe, the death of the first planet, the wars among the Aeons, and a lonely existence deciding to split itself to save the universe.

**Challenges**:
- Difficulty Class (DC) 18 Lore: understand the pattern of time flow within the Fragmentum — past, present, and future coexist here simultaneously
- Difficulty Class (DC) 16 Resonance: perceive the emotional residue of the Voice of Annihilation shards — it is not anger, but endless loneliness

**Reward**: passage rights to enter the Fragmentum, 500 Experience Points (XP)

---

#### Act II: The Aeons' Graveyard (Lv 17–18)

**Scene**: Void Fragmentum — "The Shattered Star Region"

**Plot**: The players traverse the "echo" of the ancient war in which the Voice of Annihilation was shattered. The battlefield is scattered with the energy residue of primordial Aeons, some of which have alienated into beasts. The Imaginary Echo's minions intercept the players here — they have already successfully fused 4 shards, with only the last one remaining.

**Combat Encounter**:

| Encounter | Monster | Challenge Rating (CR) | Difficulty |
|------|------|-----|------|
| Aeon Residue ×3 | Alienated Energy Entity | Challenge Rating (CR) 14 | Common |
| Shard Fusion Entity ×2 | Half-formed Annihilation Construct | Challenge Rating (CR) 16 (Toughness 208) | Hard |
| Hunter Unit | Imaginary Echo's Honor Guard | Challenge Rating (CR) 15–17 | Extreme |

**Shard Fusion Entity** (Challenge Rating (CR) 16 Elite)
- Armor Class (AC) 21 | Hit Points (HP) 350 | Toughness 208 (130×1.6)
- Four-Element Cycle: each round randomly uses one element attack (Fire/Ice/Lightning/Quantum)
- Shard Drain: when hitting a player, absorbs 1d4 Energy Point (EP) and converts it into its own Hit Points (HP)
- Weakness: Imaginary (Elemental Cycle downtime)

**Reward**: 800 Experience Points (XP), weakened shard energy (can be used to enhance the Ultimate — Energy Point (EP) max +20, final battle only)

---

#### Act III: The Antechamber of Fate (Lv 18–19)

**Scene**: Void Fragmentum — "The Sanctum of Memory"

**Plot**: The last area before reaching the core of the Voice of Annihilation. This is a sanctum composed of pure memory; every shard of the Voice of Annihilation is a stele, engraved with the end of every world it has witnessed — not caused by it, but "observed" by it. The Voice of Annihilation never actively destroys; its mission is "to play the requiem when the end arrives" — but after being distorted by the Stellaron, it began playing the requiem "prematurely."

At the center of the sanctum, the players meet the last "Pure Shard" of the Voice of Annihilation — it is the only shard not distorted by the Stellaron, because it was sealed within the sanctum at the very moment it was shattered. It has been waiting for someone who can understand it.

**Pure Shard** (no longer a monster, but an NPC):
- It shows the players the truth through its own memories: the Voice of Annihilation knows it will eventually be forgotten, and its only wish is "that at the moment of the end, someone will remember its melody"
- It makes a final request: let the distorted shards return to Nihility, let the pure shard return to peace. In return, it will grant the players the "Blessing of the End" — in the final battle, automatically recover 10 Energy Point (EP) each round

**Core Decision** (determines final battle conditions):
1. Accept the Pure Shard's request — the final battle objective is "Purification" rather than "Destruction"
2. Refuse and attempt to exploit the Pure Shard's power — gain +5 to all attributes in the final battle, but the Pure Shard will also be distorted
3. Hand the Pure Shard to a faction — gain that faction's ultimate support but lose autonomy

**Reward**: 1,200 Experience Points (XP), Blessing of the End (if Route 1 is chosen)

---

#### Act IV: Symphony of the End (Lv 19–20)

**Scene**: Void Fragmentum — "The Core of Birth"

**Plot**: The Imaginary Echo has completed the fusion of the last shard. The Voice of Annihilation "is born" in a distorted form — an aberrated existence composed of shattered memories, Stellaron energy, and the Antimatter Legion's hatred. The moment it speaks, the entire Void Fragmentum trembles:

*"I… remember. I remember every forgotten end. But no one remembers me. So now — I will make the entire universe remember my melody."*

**Final Battle: Voice of Annihilation · Distorted Form** (Challenge Rating (CR) 25 Ultimate Boss)

| Phase | Armor Class (AC) | Hit Points (HP) | Toughness | Ability |
|------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Phase I "Call of Memory" | 24 | 500 | 320 (200×1.6) | each round summon "memory projections" of extinct planets, random target 3d12 psychic damage |
| Phase II "Stellaron Liberation" | 22 | 400 | 250 (recovers after Broken) | field-wide Stellaron energy storm, each round all characters Difficulty Class (DC) 20 Constitution save or 4d10 Quantum damage |
| Phase III "Lonely Finale" | 20 | 300 | 200 | Armor Class (AC) lowered but each round field-wide 5d10 Imaginary damage, immune to single element (switches each round) |
| Phase IV "Final Requiem" | 25 | 200 | 0 (permanently Broken) | after charging 2 rounds, release a field-wide instant-death attack (Difficulty Class (DC) 25 any save) — must finish it before then |

**Special Combat Mechanics**:

**[Neutralization Ritual]** (if tavern challenges completed):
- Any player may spend 1 main action to make a "Neutralization Ritual" check (Difficulty Class (DC) 18 + current phase, may use Charisma "Resonance" skill)
- Success: BOSS's next attack deals half damage, loses 1 immunity
- Critical Success (Tier 2+): BOSS skips its next attack, randomly loses 1 shard ability
- Ritual bonus: +2 per tavern challenge completed (max +6)

**[Faction Support]** (based on Arc IV choice):
- IPC Support: 1 orbital strike every 4 rounds (8d10 Physical, blast radius 3)
- Xianzhou Support: each round automatically recover 2d6 Hit Points (HP) for the whole party
- Masked Fools Support: BOSS has 50% chance each 3 rounds to have its action interrupted by "Fool's Jest"

**[Exclusive to Fusion Succession Route]**:
If the players choose for one character to absorb the shard:
- That character may choose "Succession" in Phase III — expend all Energy Point (EP), Path resources, and 10 permanent Hit Points (HP) max
- BOSS is neutralized into "Pure Form," skipping Phase IV
- The succeeding character gains the title "Successor of the Voice of Annihilation" — the campaign ending will center on this character

**Final Battle Special Rules**:
- The Ultimate can "overload" in this battle: expend 150% Energy Point (EP) to double damage (once per battle)
- Character death in this battle is not permanent — the time flow of the Fragmentum turns "death" into "temporarily expelled from the Fragmentum" (reappears after 3 rounds, Hit Points (HP) at 25% of max, but loses all resources)

**Victory Conditions**:
- Reduce the BOSS's Hit Points (HP) to 0 before Phase IV's charge completes
- Or complete the "Neutralization Ritual" 4 times to achieve purification (no need to reduce Hit Points (HP) to 0)
- Or trigger "Fusion Succession" to directly skip Phase IV

**Reward**: 2,000 Experience Points (XP), campaign victory

---

#### Epilogue: The Continuation of the Star Track (Lv 20)

**Scene**: Astral Express — Observation Car

**Plot**: Regardless of which route the players chose to end the Voice of Annihilation, the galaxy's Stellarons cease resonating. The Fragmentum gradually contracts, and the eroded planets begin to slowly heal. The Astral Express travels steadily along the Star Track, with new planets waiting ahead.

Each character receives a **personal ending**, narrated by the GM based on the character's choices throughout the campaign. What matters is not what the character "obtained," but who the character "became."

> **GM Ending Guidance**:
> - Characters who chose the Neutralization Ritual: become mediators between the Stellaron and the Fragmentum, known as the "Requiems"
> - Characters who chose Fusion Succession: continue traveling with the memories of the Voice of Annihilation, becoming the guardian of the "Last Melody"
> - Characters who chose Forceful Termination: become defenders of galactic order, but forever bear the weight of "destroying the last Aeon"
> - Characters who remained neutral throughout: become the new generation of navigators for the Astral Express, continuing the mission of the Nameless

**Final Reward**: The campaign ends. Characters reach Lv 20. Each character gains 1 5★ Light Cone (of choice) as a mark of the legendary epilogue.
