# Honkai: Star Rail TRPG — Solo Mode Rules Supplement

> **Version**: v1.0 | **Companion**: HSR TRPG Rulebook v1.0  
> **Applicable Scenario**: 1 Player + 1 Game Master (GM)  
> **References**: This supplement is based on Rulebook §1.6 Weakness Break, §3.4 Combat, §3.6 Elemental Resonance; Economy System §11 Wealth Curve

---

## I. Core Changes Overview

| Item | Original Rules (4 Players) | Solo Mode |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Player Character | 4 PCs | 1 PC + 1–2 NPC Companions |
| Encounter Scale | 2–6 enemies per encounter | 1–3 enemies per encounter (half the count) |
| Weakness Element Coverage | 4–8 types across the party | PC 2 types + Companion 1–2 types |
| Elemental Resonance | Two-PC synergy | PC + Companion synergy (Companion participates via reaction) |
| Risk of Death | Teammates can rescue | Auto-stabilize at Dying (when Companion present) + escape mechanic |

## II. NPC Companion System

### 2.1 Companion Basic Rules

In solo mode, the player may bring **1 fixed NPC Companion** (replaceable per the story). The Companion is controlled by the player in combat, and played by the Game Master (GM) during social/exploration.

| Rule | Description |
|------|------|
| **Combat Control** | The player controls both the PC's and the Companion's actions. The Companion has independent Initiative, Hit Points (HP), Energy Point (EP), and actions |
| **Social Roleplay** | The GM plays the Companion's dialogue and personality. The player may make a "request," and the GM decides whether to comply based on the Companion's Loyalty |
| **Leveling Up** | The Companion levels up in sync with the PC (same level), but has no Talent nodes or Trace system |
| **Equipment** | The Companion may equip weapons/armor/Light Cones, but cannot equip Relics (to simplify tracking) |
| **Defeated** | When the Companion's Hit Points (HP) reach 0, it is immediately stabilized (no death saving throw), and must be treated after combat |

### 2.3 Companion Loyalty

| Event | Difficulty Class (DC) | Success | Failure |
|------|:--:|------|------|
| PC protects Companion in combat | 10 | +2 | — |
| PC shares important intel | 8 | +2 | — |
| PC gives loot to Companion | 8 | +3 | — |
| PC orders Companion to perform a dangerous task | 18 | −1 | −4 |
| Long rest conversation with Companion (RP) | 12 | +1 | — |

**Loyalty Effects**:
- ≥15: The Companion gains "Diehard" — automatically blocks one lethal attack per encounter in combat
- 10–14: Normal
- ≤4: The Companion leaves after the next long rest (may be retained with a Charisma Difficulty Class (DC) 18 check)

## III. Encounter Scale Adjustment

### 3.1 Enemy Count Conversion

| Original Scenario Enemy Count | Solo Mode | Note |
|-----------|:------:|------|
| ×6 → | ×2 | Originally a large number of mooks |
| ×4 → | ×2 | Originally a medium count |
| ×3 → | ×1 | Hit Points (HP) ×1.5 |
| ×2 → | ×1 | Hit Points (HP) ×1.2 |
| ×1 → | ×1 | Boss unchanged |

### 3.2 Enemy Stat Adjustment

| Adjustment | Formula |
|--------|------|
| Enemy Hit Points (HP) | Original Hit Points (HP) × 0.6 (since solo output is roughly 30–40% of a 4-person party, slightly lowered but not halved to preserve challenge) |
| Enemy attack frequency | Unchanged (the attack pressure a solo player endures is itself the challenge) |
| Toughness | Original value × 0.6 |

### 3.3 Boss Combat Special Rules

In solo mode, the Boss gains a "Suppression" mechanic (replacing the pressure of a multi-player surround):

**Suppression Value**: At the start of each of the Boss's turns, the Suppression value +1. The PC may spend a bonus action to consume 1 point of Suppression value.

| Suppression Value | Effect |
|:-----:|------|
| 3 | Boss's next attack +1d6 damage |
| 5 | Boss gains one extra attack |
| 7+ | Boss's attack becomes an area attack (both PC and Companion are affected) |

## IV. Weakness Break Adaptation

### 4.1 Solo Toughness Multiplier

| Enemy Type | Original Multiplier | Solo Multiplier |
|---------|:-----:|:-----:|
| Common | ×1.0 | ×0.8 |
| Elite | ×1.3 | ×1.0 |
| Boss | ×1.6 | ×1.3 |
| Summon | ×0.5 | ×0.4 |

### 4.2 Weakness Detection

The PC may spend a **bonus action** to perform a "Weakness Detection" check (Difficulty Class (DC) 12 Perception (Anticipation)), and upon success the GM reveals **all of the enemy's weakness elements**. This check is limited to 1 use per combat.

### 4.3 Physical Versatility Enhancement

In solo mode, the Toughness reduction value for Physical-attribute characters is adjusted:

| Attack Type | Physical Toughness Reduction (Original) | Physical Toughness Reduction (Solo) |
|---------|:----------:|:----------:|
| Basic Attack | −15 | **−18** |
| Technique | −20 | **−25** |
| Ultimate (Ult) | −35 | **−40** |

## V. Elemental Resonance Adaptation

### 5.1 Companion Resonance

The PC can trigger Elemental Resonance with an NPC Companion. The Companion spends a **reaction** to participate in the resonance (no roll needed, auto-succeeds).

| Combination | Effect (consistent with original rules) |
|------|------|
| Fire + Lightning | Overload (2d10 Fire, blast radius 1) |
| Ice + Lightning | Superconduct (Armor Class (AC) −2) |
| Wind + Fire | Swirl (Burn spreads, blast radius 2) |
| Physical + Ice | Shatter (Bleed doubled) |
| Quantum + Imaginary | Collapse (Armor Class (AC) −3, all saves −2) |
| Wind + Lightning | Storm (1d8 Lightning, all adjacent) |

### 5.2 Companion Resonance Limits

- Limited to 1 use per combat
- The Companion must be within 10 squares of the PC

## VI. Death and Escape

### 6.1 Dying Protection

| Condition | Effect |
|------|------|
| PC Dying + Companion present | The Companion automatically spends its turn to stabilize the PC (no check needed) |
| PC Dying + Companion absent | Normal death saving throw (§1.5a), but Difficulty Class (DC) reduced to 8 |
| Companion Dying | Immediately stabilized, no death saving throw |

### 6.2 Escape Mechanic

In solo mode, the PC may declare a "tactical retreat":

- Spend a **main action** to perform a Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Agility (Acrobatics) or Intellect (Technology) check
- **Success**: The PC and Companion disengage from combat; the GM determines the consequences (enemy occupies the area, loss of rewards, etc.)
- **Failure**: Unable to move this turn; the enemy gains one free attack
- After retreating, cannot return to the same combat (the enemy is now on alert)

## VII. Experience Points (XP) and Reward Adjustments

### 7.1 Experience Points (XP) Calculation

Solo mode Experience Points (XP) = sum of enemy Experience Points (XP) (**not divided**, granted in full directly).

### 7.2 Credit Point Rewards

Adopt the reward table from Economy System §11.2, **unchanged** — a solo character's spending needs are the same as a multiplayer one (still needs to buy equipment, upgrade Light Cones).

### 7.3 Companion Equipment

The Companion's weapons and armor are automatically issued by the GM at level-up nodes (no need to spend the PC's Credit Points). The Companion's Light Cone can be shared with the PC (Light Cones retired by the PC are given to the Companion).

## VIII. GM Solo Mode Guidelines

### 8.1 Pacing Suggestions

| Multiplayer Mode | Solo Mode |
|---------|---------|
| Combat 45 minutes | Combat 25–35 minutes |
| Party internal discussion 10 minutes | PC-Companion dialogue 5 minutes (mostly RP) |
| Team decision 10 minutes | PC's sole decision 3–5 minutes |

The pace of solo mode is far faster than multiplayer — the GM should prepare more backup content to prevent the game from ending early.

### 8.2 Social Challenge Adjustments

Without the buffer of party discussion — all social pressure falls directly on the PC. Suggestions:

- **NPCs more proactive**: The GM's NPCs should proactively provide information, ask questions, and challenge, rather than waiting for the player to initiate
- **Companion as buffer**: When the PC is at an impasse, the Companion may provide a hint (limited to 2 times per session)
- **Lower Difficulty Class (DC)**: Social checks' Difficulty Class (DC) is 2 lower than the original rules (reflecting NPCs' respect/pity for the "lone Trailblazer who came alone")

### 8.3 Story Focus

Solo mode's narrative should focus on the PC's **personal arc** — origin background, Path choice, and bonds with specific NPCs. With no team dynamics to rely on, the GM should deeply explore the PC's backstory.
