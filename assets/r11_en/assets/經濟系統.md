# Honkai: Star Rail TRPG — Complete Economy System

> **Version**: v1.0 | **Pairs with**: HSR TRPG Rulebook v1.0  
> **Design**: TRPG Rules Studio (Ke-Jiao-Ling × Zhan-Zhi-Ge × Jing-Shi-Wen, compiled by Cheng-Gui-Yao)  
> **Purpose**: A GM guide that replaces "reference the D&D 5e equipment list," providing a complete Credit Point economy loop

---

## Design Baseline

| System Parameter | Range | Pricing Impact |
|------------------|-------|----------------|
| Starting Credit Points | 300–800 (by Identity, rebalanced) | Lv1 purchasing power baseline |
| Hit Points (HP) | ~20–150 (Lv1–20) | Healing item pricing reference |
| Damage | ~1d6–2d12 | Weapon tier pricing |
| Armor Class (AC) / Evasion | ~10–20 | Armor tier pricing |
| Energy Point (EP) cap | 100–140 | Energy item pricing |
| Light Cone upgrade | 3→4★ = 500 Credit Points (CP) / 4→5★ = 2,000 Credit Points (CP) | Light Cone market pricing anchor |

> **Credit Point (CP)** is the interstellar common currency. 1 Credit Point (CP) ≈ one basic meal. All prices follow a rarity power law — each rarity tier up ×3–5.

# Chapter Three: Light Cone Market Pricing

## 3.1 Light Cone Prices

| Star Rating | Market Price (CP) | Upgrade Cost (CP) | Notes |
|:-----------:|-------------------|-------------------|-------|
| **3★** | 750 | 500 (→4★) | Available at ordinary shops |
| **4★** | 3,500 | 2,000 (→5★) | Mid-tier shops or black market |
| **5★** | 18,000 | — | Special shops / auctions / quest rewards |

> **Design Intent**: Market price > upgrade cost (750 vs 500, 3,500 vs 2,000+500), encouraging players to upgrade via materials rather than buying directly. 5★ Light Cones cannot be bought at ordinary shops — they must be obtained through auction houses, the interstellar black market, or Legendary quests.

## 8.2 Mercenary Contracts and Loyalty

### Contract Types

| Contract | Cost | Terms |
|---------|:----:|-------|
| **Single-Day Contract** | Daily wage ×1.0 | Settled that day, mercenary may terminate at any time |
| **Short-Term Contract (3–7 days)** | Daily wage ×0.9 | Half prepaid, remainder on completion |
| **Long-Term Contract (14–30 days)** | Daily wage ×0.8 | 30% prepaid, settled weekly |
| **Quest Contract** | Daily wage × days ×0.85 | Bound by completing a specific quest |

### Loyalty System

A mercenary's initial loyalty = 10 + employer's Charisma modifier. After each of the following events, the GM makes a loyalty check (d20):

| Event | Difficulty Class (DC) | Success Effect | Failure Effect |
|-------|:----------------:|----------------|----------------|
| Employer fails to protect mercenary in combat | 15 | No change | Loyalty −2 |
| Employer heals injured mercenary | 10 | Loyalty +2 | No change |
| Shares loot with mercenary | 8 | Loyalty +3 | No change |
| Demands mercenary perform suicide mission | 20 | Loyalty −1 | Loyalty −5, possible defection |
| Fails to pay wages for 3 consecutive days | Automatic | — | Loyalty −3/day |

**Loyalty Effects**:
- **≥15 (Diehard)**: Automatically blocks one lethal attack for the employer in combat
- **10–14 (Normal)**: Follows the contract
- **5–9 (Wavering)**: May delay 1 round before acting in combat (50% chance)
- **≤4 (Defecting)**: Leaves the battlefield at the start of the next battle, or turns to attack the employer (GM discretion)

# Chapter Thirteen: Stellar Jade Rules

**Stellar Jade** is not currency—it is a crystallized remnant of the Trailblaze Path left after Akivili's fall. These brilliant blue crystal fragments contain the lingering will and power of the Trailblaze Aeon. Extremely rare, cannot be artificially made, cannot be traded.

## 13.1 Stellar Jade Overview

| Use | Cost | Section |
|------|:--:|------|
| Reroll failed check | 1 jade | — |
| Activate special train function | 1–3 jade | 13.2 |
| Unlock hidden Path ability | 2–5 jade | 13.3 |
| Infuse Light Cone with Path Resonance | 1 jade | 13.4 |

- **Acquisition**: Complete a planet's main story line → 1–3 jade
- **Special Acquisition**: Resonate with Aeon fragments, repair broken Star Tracks, discover lost Trailblazer ruins
- **Non-Tradable**: Stellar Jade cannot be bought or sold with Credit Points. Any attempt to trade Stellar Jade provokes hostility from the Astral Express
- **Total Control**: Total acquisition across the entire campaign (Lv1–20) is recommended at 8–15 jade

## 13.2 Special Train Functions

Stellar Jade can activate the hidden Trailblazer heritage functions aboard the Astral Express. These functions must be activated by a player holding the Nameless Identity during the Conductor's rest (i.e., a long rest).

### Train Function Table

| Function | Cost | Effect | Duration |
|------|:--:|------|:------:|
| **Star Track Retrace** | 2 jade | The train can return to any visited planet. If that planet is in crisis, the retrace grants the whole party advantage on checks related to that planet (1 use) | One-way |
| **Trailblazer Training Pod** | 1 jade | Whole party gains temporary Experience Points (XP) equal to 1 level. Can be allocated to any skill for short-term intensive training. This Experience Points (XP) vanishes after the next long rest | Until next long rest |
| **Path Observatory** | 3 jade | Activates the star map projection at the top of the train. The whole party glimpses fragments of future adventures—the GM must reveal 3 true pieces of intel about the next chapter (cannot mislead) | 1 prophecy |
| **Stellaron Sealing Chamber** | 2 jade | The party can temporarily seal carried Stellaron shards in a specially-built train compartment. During sealing, characters are immune to Stellaron corruption (mental save advantage, no longer accumulate corruption value) | Until actively lifted |
| **Warp Accelerator** | 2 jade | Greatly shortens Star Track travel time—interstellar travel that would take weeks compressed to days. Also grants the whole party one extra long rest during the voyage | Single warp |
| **Resonance Amplifier** | 3 jade | Characters taking a long rest aboard the train gain ×2 Path resource acquisition rate and +10 Energy Point (EP) recovery/round in their next battle | 1 battle |

> **Note**: Train functions are limited to characters who hold an Astral Express pass (Nameless Identity or special permit). Non-Nameless characters using functions aboard the train must additionally spend 1 Stellar Jade as a "transit tax."

## 13.3 Hidden Path Abilities

Each Path possesses "hidden abilities" beyond the normal upgrade route—these abilities cannot be gained through normal leveling and must be unlocked via Path Resonance using Stellar Jade as a medium.

### Unlock Rules

- Requires character level ≥ the ability's "required level"
- Requires a ritual at a sacred site related to that Path (e.g., Erudition → Genius Society, Destruction → Antimatter Legion ruins, Abundance → Tree of Life, etc.)
- Each unlock consumes the corresponding Stellar Jade and requires one Difficulty Class (DC) 15 Charisma (Resonance) check
- Failure does not refund the Stellar Jade, but you may retry after the next long rest (Difficulty Class (DC) −2)

## 13.4 Light Cone Path Resonance

Infusing a Light Cone with Path Resonance via Stellar Jade awakens the sleeping memory fragments within the Light Cone, granting power beyond its original properties.

### Resonance Rules

- May only infuse a Light Cone that is **equipped** and **proficiently used**
- The Light Cone's Path must match the character's Path (no cross-Path resonance)
- Each Light Cone may be infused **at most once**
- Effect is permanent after infusion (unless the Light Cone is destroyed)

### Resonance Effects (random d6, rolled after using Stellar Jade)

| d6 | Resonance Effect | Description |
|:--:|---------|------|
| 1 | **Attribute Awakening** | Numeric values of the Light Cone's original effect +50% (rounded). E.g., original effect "Attack +2" → "Attack +3" |
| 2 | **Path Mark** | When using this Light Cone, corresponding Path resource gain +1/use |
| 3 | **Memory Echo** | 1/battle → immediately recover 25 Energy Point (EP) after releasing Ultimate (Ult) |
| 4 | **Trailblazer's Blessing** | The Light Cone's equipment requirement is lowered by 2 levels; allies affected by this Light Cone's effect also gain half the bonus |
| 5 | **Aeon Echo** | Critical hit damage +25% (i.e., total damage ×1.25 instead of ×1.0 on crit) |
| 6 | **Path Interweave** | Roll two d6 and take both effects (reroll duplicates). Extremely rare—about 3% chance |

### Reforge Option (optional rule)

If the player is unsatisfied with the random result, they may spend an additional **2 Stellar Jade** for a "Resonance Reforge":

- Keep the original resonance but reroll
- May choose to keep the old result or accept the new result
- Each Light Cone may be reforged at most once
