# Honkai: Star Rail TRPG — Spell Codex (Path Techniques / Ultimates)

> **Version**: v1.0 | **Pair With**: HSR TRPG Rulebook v1.0  
> **Design**: TRPG Rules Studio | Based on the *Honkai: Star Rail* IP (© HoYoverse)

This ruleset has no traditional "spell" system; character abilities are expressed as **Path Techniques and Ultimates**. This codex collects the core mechanics, level ability progressions, and Talent nodes of the seven Paths for quick reference by players and the GM. For detailed rules, see Chapter 2 of `崩壞星穹鐵道TRPG_玩家規則書.md`.

## Destruction — Ardor stacks

Core Mechanic: The lower your Hit Points (HP), the more Ardor stacks you gain; each stack adds +1~3 damage (scales with level).

**Level Progression**: 1 **Ardor Surge** / **Destruction Strike** → 2 **Combat Style** / **Unyielding Will** → 3 Talent I → 5 **Extra Attack** / **Ardor Empowerment** (15%/stack, 6-stack cap) → 7 Talent II → 9 **Aura of Destruction** (10m enemies Armor Class (AC) -1) → 10 **Ardor Empowerment II** (10-stack cap) → 11 Talent III → 13 **Phoenix Heart** (at 0 Hit Points (HP), restore half HP) → 14 **Extra Attack (2)** → 15 Talent IV → 17 **Ardor Maximization** (15 stacks, +3/stack) → 18 **Avatar of Destruction** → 19 Talent V → 20 **Descent of Terminus** (1 min full Ardor + control-immune + extra attack action)

**Representative Ultimates**: Destruction Strike (spends Ardor, +1d8/stack); Descent of Terminus (Lv 20 burst).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Bloodthirsty Frenzy** (Ardor ≥3, melee +2) / **Echo of Agony** (reflect 25% mental when damaged) / **Iron Body** (Hit Points (HP) <50%, Armor Class (AC) +2, damage reduction +1)
- Lv7: **Flesh Sacrifice** (spend 10 Hit Points (HP) for +2d6, critical 18–20) / **Ripple of Destruction** (kill overflow 50%) / **Unyielding Bastion** (once per short rest, reduce a lethal blow to 1 Hit Points (HP))
- Lv11: **Life Crucible** (Constitution (CON) modifier added to damage) / **Storm of Destruction** (Ultimate (Ult) half damage in 10m) / **Death Denial** (at 0 Hit Points (HP), free Destruction Strike)
- Lv15: **Herald of Collapse** (Break +2d10 typeless) / **Eternal Rage** (Ardor does not decay) / **Terminator** (advantage against Hit Points (HP) <25%, critical +2)
- Lv19: **Gaze of Nanook** (ignore resistances for 1 minute) / **Universal Collapse** (Ultimate (Ult) Toughness reduction -80) / **Undying War God** (death saving throw Difficulty Class (DC) 8)

## Hunt — Focus

Core Mechanic: Mark your prey; spend Focus to unleash Precision / Vital-Point / Soul-Chase / Limb-Bind attacks.

**Level Progression**: 1 **Mark Prey** / **Focused Shot** → 2 **Hunter's Step** / **Sniper's Intuition** → 5 **Extra Attack** / **Focus Expansion** 6 → 9 **Wind Walker** (teleport 15m) → 10 **Killing Blow** (+4d6, critical returns Focus) → 13 **Double Lock** → 14 **Extra Attack (2)** → 17 **Heart Seeker** (ignore cover, vital +6d6) → 18 **Eye of Death** (Hit Points (HP) <50%, take maximum) → 20 **Arrow of the Aeon** (auto-critical 10d10, ignore resistances)

**Representative Ultimates**: Killing Blow; Arrow of the Aeon (Lv 20).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Precision Shot** (aim, +3 to hit) / **Ranger's Footwork** (no ranged disadvantage within 5 squares) / **Wind's Favor** (Speed +2 squares, Initiative +3)
- Lv7: **Heart-Piercing Arrow** (ignore half/soft cover) / **Arrow Rain** (10m bursting ranged attack) / **Formless Hunter** (takes maximum against undiscovered targets)
- Lv11: **Aeon's Gaze** (mark ignores bonus action) / **Death Mark** (critical 17–20 against prey) / **Quantum Arrow** (2 Quantum damage instances per short rest)
- Lv15: **Arrow of the End** (Ultimate (Ult) auto-crits against prey) / **God of the Hunt** (Focus cap +3, +1 per round) / **Infinite Quiver** (common arrows cost nothing)

## Erudition — Inspiration

Core Mechanic: Used for Amplify / Spread / Penetrate / Instant-cast spells.

**Level Progression**: 1 **Inspiration Casting** / **Knowledge Surge** (2d6/3m, AGI save) → 5 **Elemental Specialization I** (Ice or Quantum) → 6 **Knowledge Surge Enhancement** (3d6/5m) → 10 **Elemental Specialization II** → 13 **Quantum Entanglement** (those hit are linked → disadvantage) → 14 **Group Amplification** (1 Inspiration, all targets +1d6) → 17 **Cataclysm** (5 Inspiration / 20m / 10d8) → 18 **Gaze of Nous** (Inspiration halved, range doubled) → 20 **Cosmic Truth** (immune to 1 element, ignore resistances)

**Representative Ultimates**: Cataclysm (Lv 17 AoE burst); Knowledge Surge (basic Technique).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Light of Nous** (Lore / Investigation / Technology +2) / **Energy Efficiency** (Energy Point (EP) +5/round) / **Area Specialization** (exclude 1 ally)
- Lv7: **Overload Calculation** (damage dice take maximum) / **Multiple Projectiles** (single-target to adjacent extra targets) / **Knowledge is Power** (Intellect (INT) modifier added to all damage)
- Lv11: **Predictive Computation** (declare a number to change the result) / **Energy Surge** (Inspiration cap +5) / **Elemental Master** (dual-element attack)
- Lv15: **Eye of Truth** (break illusion / Invisible) / **Final Computation** (Ultimate (Ult) to 50m single-target, 2x) / **Nous's Resonance** (GM answers yes/no question)

## Harmony — Resonance stacks

Core Mechanic: After linking allies, stack layers; used for Buff / Shield / Synergy.

**Level Progression**: 1 **Resonance Weave** / **Voice of Encouragement** (1d6 add to any d20) → 2 **Touch of Harmony** / **Teamwork** → 5 **Resonance Amplification I** (stacks doubled) → 6 **Voice of Encouragement Enhancement** (1d8) → 9 **Mental Link** → 10 **Resonance Amplification II** → 13 **Resonance Burst** (10 stacks / 6d6 sonic) → 14 **Eternal Harmony** → 17 **Grand Chorus** (all linked, advantage + Armor Class (AC) +2 + 5 Hit Points (HP) per round) → 20 **Harmony of All Things** (100m unlimited links)

**Representative Ultimates**: Resonance Burst; Grand Chorus (Lv 17).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Harmonic Resonance** (link +1) / **Encouragement Master** (advantage +1d4 temporary Hit Points (HP)) / **Rhythm Sense** (Initiative uses Charisma (CHA))
- Lv7: **Duet** (Encouragement affects 2 people) / **Defensive Chord** (linked ally Armor Class (AC) +1) / **Accelerating Beat** (linked ally gains 1 extra bonus action)
- Lv11: **Collective Will** (any successful save shared) / **Resonance Diffusion** (50% chance for +1 stack) / **Mental Chorus** (long rest grants whole party 1 skill proficiency)
- Lv15: **Symphonic Movement** (Ultimate (Ult) triggers party-wide Ultimates) / **Eternal Melody** (Resonance does not decay) / **Xipe's Blessing** (whole party saves + Charisma (CHA) modifier)

## Nihility — Nihility Mark

Core Mechanic: Stack to reduce Armor Class (AC) / saving throws; can detonate / spread / Withering Aura.

**Level Progression**: 1 **Erosion** / **Touch of Nihility** (1d8+INT, applies Mark) → 2 **Withering Aura** / **Shadow Step** → 5 **Erosion Enhancement I** (6-stack cap, reduction -4) → 9 **Spreading Despair** (death transfers all stacks) → 10 **Erosion Enhancement II** (detonate 3d6/stack) → 13 **Entropy Increase** (3 stacks, CON save → Exhaustion) → 14 **Eternal Erosion** → 17 **Realm of Nihility** (20m auto 1 stack) → 18 **Gaze of IX** (5 stacks + Fear) → 20 **Heat Death** (100m, stacks ×10 Hit Points (HP), no save)

**Representative Ultimates**: Erosion (basic); Heat Death (Lv 20 full-field burst).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Abyssal Gaze** (Marked target saving throws -1) / **Shadow Spread** (when applied, adjacent +1 stack) / **Nihility Resilience** (advantage on mental / charm-control saves)
- Lv7: **Echo of Pain** (Marked takes +1d4 mental when damaged) / **Withering Acceleration** (aura +1d4) / **Nihility Step** (teleport 10 squares)
- Lv11: **Entropy Acceleration** (triggers at 2 stacks) / **Chain of Despair** (chain 5 squares) / **Touch of IX** (all Marks trigger immediately)
- Lv15: **Realm of Nihility** (10m auto 1 stack each round) / **Erasure of Existence** (killing a Marked target restores 10 Energy Point (EP)) / **Avatar of Nihility** (immune to charm-control, 1 instant death)

## Preservation — Fortitude

Core Mechanic: Generate Shields to absorb damage (Fortitude ×2~3).

**Level Progression**: 1 **Builder's Wall** / **Guardian Strike** → 2 **Taunt Roar** / **Bedrock Body** → 5 **Extra Attack** / **Shield Empowerment** (Fortitude ×3) → 6 **Reflective Shield** → 9 **Guardian Aura** (5m damage reduction 2) → 10 **Unbreakable** (blocks any damage) → 13 **Reflective Barrier** → 14 **Extra Attack (2)** → 17 **Bastion Mode** (all resistances + 30m Taunt) → 18 **Qlipoth's Forge** (immune to critical, 50% Shield cost-free) → 20 **Unsinkable City** (0 Hit Points (HP) → 1, full Shield)

**Representative Ultimates**: Guardian Strike (generates Shield); Qlipoth's Forge (Lv 18).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Shield Reinforcement** (Shield +25%) / **Bedrock Reflection** (reflect damage equal to Constitution (CON) modifier) / **Guardian Aura** (allies within 3 squares, Armor Class (AC) +1)
- Lv7: **Taunt Roar Enhancement** (25m, Difficulty Class (DC) +2) / **Shield Burst** (detonate Shield, 10m) / **Unyielding** (Hit Points (HP) <25%, temporary Shield)
- Lv11: **Bastion** (self, Armor Class (AC) +1) / **Shared Guardian** (50% of Shield transferred to ally) / **Reflective Barrier Enhancement** (×3)
- Lv15: **Qlipoth's Wall** (15m whole party, Armor Class (AC) +3) / **Eternal Guardian** (Fortitude cap +5) / **Unbroken Shield** (Shield-shatter overflow halved)

## Abundance — Life Seed

Core Mechanic: After sowing, use Bloom for healing / purification / protection / vitality.

**Level Progression**: 1 **Life Seed** / **Touch of Abundance** (1d8+PER) → 2 **Natural Regeneration** / **Hand of Purification** → 5 **Life Seed Enhancement I** (+2 Seeds, 1d10) → 6 **Aura of Abundance** (15m heal each round) → 9 **Group Bloom** → 10 **Life Seed Enhancement II** (1d12, lasts until long rest) → 13 **Flower of Revival** (10 Seeds revive) → 14 **Endless Abundance** → 17 **Tree of Life** (15m 2d6 each round + death save advantage) → 18 **Yaoshi's Blessing** (healing maximized) → 20 **Song of Eternal Life** (30m immune to death + 10 Hit Points (HP) each round)

**Representative Ultimates**: Touch of Abundance (basic healing); Flower of Revival (Lv 13); Song of Eternal Life (Lv 20).

**Talent Nodes**:
- Lv3: **Touch of Life** (healing + Perception (PER) modifier) / **Purification Specialization** (remove 2 conditions) / **Natural Resilience** (Constitution (CON) modifier added to healing)
- Lv7: **Seed Diffusion** (affects 2 allies) / **Regeneration Enhancement** (Bloom grants 1d4/round Regeneration) / **Life Link** (bears 50% of linked ally's damage)
- Lv11: **Burgeoning Vitality** (Seed cap +3) / **Purification Aura** (auto-purify each round) / **Emergency Bloom** (ally at 0 Hit Points (HP) triggers healing as reaction)
- Lv15: **Yaoshi's Touch** (healing critical ×2) / **Life Cycle** (whole party heals 50% + revive) / **Eternal Bloom** (overflow converted to temporary Hit Points (HP))
