## Arc 5: Void Fragmentum

### Alienated Energy Entity (CR 14｜Common)
- **Armor Class (AC)** 19 | **Hit Points (HP)** 200 | **Toughness** 100 (Common) | **Speed** Flying 10 squares
- **Attack**: +10 (3d8+6 random element)
- **Special**: Element Shift (each round randomly changes weakness/resistance); Energy Drain (on hit drains 1d4 Energy Point (EP)); Void Form (at start of each round teleports to any location within 20m)
- **Weakness**: Imaginary (Elemental downtime) | **Experience Points (XP)** 1200

### Shard Fusion (CR 16｜Elite)
- **Armor Class (AC)** 21 | **Hit Points (HP)** 350 | **Toughness** 208 (130×1.6) | **Speed** 6 squares
- **Attack**: +12 (4d8+8 multi-element)
- **Special**: Four-Element Cycle (each round random Fire/Ice/Lightning/Quantum attack); Shard Drain (on hit drains 1d4 Energy Point (EP) converted to Hit Points (HP)); Fusion Shield (each round 30 Hit Points (HP) Shield, weakness element can Break it)
- **Weakness**: Imaginary (Cycle downtime) | **Experience Points (XP)** 3400

### Hunter Force (CR 16｜Elite)
- **Armor Class (AC)** 21 | **Hit Points (HP)** 280 | **Toughness** 208 (130×1.6) | **Speed** 8 squares
- **Attack**: +12 (3d8+7 Imaginary)
- **Special**: Hunter's Mark (once per combat, marks 1 character, attacks against the marked target have advantage + 2d6 damage); Imaginary Leap (once every 2 rounds, teleport to any location within 12 squares); Hunting Instinct (against targets with Hit Points (HP) < 50%, critical range 17–20); Guard Coordination (if another Hunter is within 3 squares, Armor Class (AC) +2)
- **Weakness**: Quantum | **Experience Points (XP)** 3400

### Annihilation's Voice · Distorted Form (CR 25｜Ultimate Boss · four-phase)
- **Armor Class (AC)** 24→22→20→25 | **Hit Points (HP)** 500→400→300→200 | **Toughness (TN)** 320→250→200→0 (permanently Broken) | **Speed** 8 squares
- **Attack**: +15 (4d12+10 Imaginary)
- **Phase 1 "Call of Memory"**: each round summons a memory projection of a doomed planet, random target 3d12 Psychic
- **Phase 2 "Stellaron Liberation"**: field-wide Stellaron energy storm, each round DC 20 Constitution (CON) save or 4d10 Quantum
- **Phase 3 "Lonely Finale"**: each round 5d10 Imaginary field-wide, immune to single element (switches each round)
- **Phase 4 "Requiem"**: charges 2 rounds, field-wide instant death, DC 25 any save
- **Neutralization Ritual** (requires Tavern Challenge, DC 18 + phase, success → Boss damage halved / loses 1 immunity); faction support: IPC orbital strike (8d10) / Xianzhou healing (2d6/round) / Fool's jest (50% interruption); Fusion Route: players may spend all resources + 10 permanent Hit Points (HP) to take over → skip Phase 4
- **Weakness**: Quantum, Physical → all elements (after ritual) | **Experience Points (XP)** 20000
