# II. Side Quests

Side quests can be interspersed between main quests, triggered by the GM based on player level and interest.

| ID | Quest | Suggested Level | Synopsis |
|:--:|------|:--:|------|
| GA1 | **Apprentice Evaluation** | 1–5 | First solo commission after joining the guild: clear 5 low-rank monsters at the Erosion Zone edge and report back. |
| GA2 | **Information War** | 10–15 | Assist Intelligence Officer Yamada in deciphering beastman communication runes confiscated from a werewolf stronghold. Requires Intelligence (INT) check at Difficulty Class (DC) 60. |
| GA3 | **Guild Infighting** | 20–30 | Two subordinate guilds fight over hunting rights to the same Erosion Zone. The player must mediate or choose a side. Different choices affect later NPC interactions. |
| GA4 | **Chairman's Trial** | 40–50 | After earning enough renown, Chairman Kurosaki personally gives a trial — a solo duel or tactical command of a simulated battle. |
| GA5 | **Five-Guild Joint Exercise** | 60–70 | An Awakener arena tournament jointly held by Tokyo / New York / London / Shanghai / São Paulo. Foreshadows S13. |

## GM Usage Notes

1. **Density Control**: 4 main quests + 2–4 side quests per season. Do not cram too many at once. One season is best run over 4–8 sessions.
2. **Side Quest Triggering**: Naturally introduced by the GM based on player actions and interest; no forcing. If players miss a side quest, it can become a later "compensation quest."
3. **Cross Influence**: Side quest choices may affect the main line — e.g. BD5 (accepting Malkis's deal) affects the ending options of S10 and S20.
4. **Solo Mode**: All side quests are designed to be completable solo. Demigod side quests (DM series) in solo mode suggest lowering the Difficulty Class (DC) or providing extra NPC assistance.
