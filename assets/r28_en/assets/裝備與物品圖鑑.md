# I. Rarity System (Core Rules)

## 1.1 One Equipment = One Base Type + One Rarity Tier

The same equipment has different stats and effects at different rarities. **Rarity does not equal a unique name** — a "Longsword" can be White · Common, or be reforged into Orange · Legendary.

```
Longsword (White · Common)  ──forging upgrade──→  Longsword (Green · Uncommon)
                                                   ──forging upgrade──→  Longsword (Blue · Rare)
                                                                           ──Otherworld tempering──→  Longsword (Purple · Unique)
```

| Concept | Description |
|------|------|
| Base Type | The "kind" of equipment — longsword, hand crossbow, protective vest, tactical boots, etc. |
| Rarity Tier | Determines stat range and number of special effects — six tiers: White / Green / Blue / Purple / Orange / Red |
| Final Equipment | Base Type + Rarity = one concrete piece of equipment |

## 1.3 Rarity Naming Rules

```
[Color] · [Rarity Name] [Base Type]

Examples:
  White · Common Longsword (+2 Physical Attack (PATK))
  Green · Uncommon Longsword (+5 Physical Attack (PATK))
  Blue · Rare Longsword (+10 Physical Attack (PATK), Hit Rate (HIT%) +5%)
  Purple · Unique Longsword — "Hound's Fang" (+10 Physical Attack (PATK), +20% damage vs. beast-type monsters)
  Orange · Legendary Longsword — "Sovereign's Blade" (+28 Physical Attack (PATK), Critical Rate (CRT%) +10%, recover 5% Hit Points (HP) on kill)
  Red · Mythic Longsword — "Osaikus's Noon" (+50 Physical Attack (PATK), Holy damage, ×2.0 vs. beastmen, once per battle Dawn Sanction)
```

> Purple-tier and above equipment usually gains a unique name — but the underlying base is still a "Longsword".

## 1.4 Equipment Slots

| Slot | Primary Influence | Secondary Influence |
|------|---------|--------|
| Weapon | Physical Attack (PATK) or Magic Attack (MATK) | Hit Rate (HIT%), Critical Rate (CRT%), special effects |
| Body | Physical Defense (PDEF), Magic Defense (MDEF) | Hit Points (HP) bonus, special effects |
| Feet | Movement speed | Dexterity (DEX), Dodge Rate (DGE%), special effects |
| Accessory | Minor attribute bonuses | Special effects |

# VII. Equipment Crates (Drop Format)

Equipment does not drop as finished products — it drops "Equipment Crates"; opening one randomly yields equipment of the corresponding rarity.

| Crate Name | Rarity | Contents |
|------|:--:|------|
| White · Common Equipment Crate | E | White · Common weapon/armor/accessory (random slot) |
| Green · Uncommon Equipment Crate | D | Green · Uncommon equipment (random slot) |
| Blue · Rare Equipment Crate | C | Blue · Rare equipment (random slot) |
| Purple · Unique Equipment Crate | B | Purple · Unique equipment (designated slot, GM may let players roll) |
| Orange · Legendary Equipment Crate | A | Orange · Legendary equipment (designated slot + one random special effect) |
| Red · Mythic Equipment Crate | S | Red · Mythic equipment (unique, usually tied to the defeated Boss) |

> GM suggestion: Have players roll 1d4 to determine slot (1=weapon, 2=body, 3=feet, 4=accessory), then roll 1d6 to pick one item from that slot's same-rarity pool.
