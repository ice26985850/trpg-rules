# II. General Rift Generation Rules

## 2.1 Rift Six-tier Specification Overview

| Rank | Zones | Encounter Types | Boss | Core | Special Rules |
|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|------|
| E-Rank | 1–3 | 1–2 | None | None | None special |
| D-Rank | 3–5 | 2–3 | 1 (D-Boss) | None | None special |
| C-Rank | 4–7 | 3–4 | 1–2 (C-Boss) | None | Optional: environment penalty (minor) |
| B-Rank | 5–8 | 4–5 | 1 (B-Boss) + mini-Boss | Yes (Hit Points (HP) 1,500–3,000) | Core destroyed → Boss weakened 30% |
| A-Rank | 7–10 | 5–6 | 2–3 (A-Boss) | Yes (Hit Points (HP) 5,000–10,000) | Multiple cores, environment penalty (severe) |
| S-Rank | 10+ | 6+ | Chief-God Projection + 3 Guardians | Yes (Hit Points (HP) 20,000+) | Law Chaos, god's domain |

## 2.2 Rift Generation Steps

### Step 1: Determine Rift Rank and Theme

Roll 1d6 to decide the interior environment theme:

| d6 | Environment Theme | Visual | Environment Effect |
|:--:|------|------|------|
| 1 | Crystal Cave | Glowing otherworld crystals cover the walls | Magic damage +10% |
| 2 | Fungus Maze | Glowing fungi, drifting spores | Each zone requires Difficulty Class (DC) 40 Constitution (CON) check, fail = mild Poison |
| 3 | Ruined City | Remains of an otherworld civilization | Abundant cover (DGE%+15% obtainable) |
| 4 | Lava Cavern | Magma rivers, high-temperature steam | 5 Fire damage to all every 3 turns |
| 5 | Shadow Abyss | Light sucked away, extremely poor visibility | Hit Rate (HIT%) −10%, stealth advantage |
| 6 | Flesh Nest | Living rift interior — biomass walls | 30% chance per zone to encounter a random patrol monster |

### Step 2: Generate Zone Structure

Roll d6 to decide the starting-point type:

| d6 | Starting Point | Description |
|:--:|------|------|
| 1–2 | Entrance Hall | Spacious initial zone, usually traces of previous expeditions (corpses, equipment fragments) |
| 3–4 | Narrow Passage | Single-file only, cannot retreat if enemies appear |
| 5 | Vertical Shaft | Requires climbing or flight, Difficulty Class (DC) 40 Strength (STR) climb |
| 6 | Fork in the Road | Two routes to choose — players must choose (cannot take both) |

Subsequent zones (roll d6 multiple times, each deciding the next zone type):

| d6 | Zone Type | Content |
|:--:|------|------|
| 1 | Combat Zone | Always an encounter (roll random encounter table by rift rank) |
| 2 | Resource Zone | Collect otherworld materials (1–3 crystals), 40% chance of trap or ambush |
| 3 | Trap Zone | Environmental trap (Difficulty Class (DC) by rift rank), with compensation reward after passing |
| 4 | Safe Zone | Short Rest (10 min) possible, no enemies |
| 5 | Puzzle / Mechanism Zone | Requires Difficulty Class (DC) 50–80 Intelligence (INT) to solve, with extra reward (or shortcut to next zone) after passing |
| 6 | Pre-Boss Chamber | Final zone of the rift, Boss or core zone ahead |

> The GM may manually arrange zone order to control pacing. Suggested structure: entrance → combat/resource → safe → combat → Boss.

### Step 3: Configure Encounters

Roll the encounter table by rift rank:

**E-Rank Rift Encounter Table (d6):**

| d6 | Encounter |
|:--:|------|
| 1–2 | 2–4 E-1 (Small Beast Pack) |
| 3 | 1–2 E-2 (Wanderer) |
| 4 | 1–3 E-3 (Flying Scout) |
| 5 | Mixed: 1 E-2 + 2–3 E-1 |
| 6 | No encounter (empty zone or traces of prior battle) |

**D-Rank Rift Encounter Table (d6):**

| d6 | Encounter |
|:--:|------|
| 1 | 2–3 D-1 (Hunter) |
| 2 | 1 D-2 (Heavy) + 1–2 E-1 |
| 3 | 1 D-3 (Spellcaster Prototype) + 1–2 D-1 |
| 4 | 3–5 E-Rank mixed group |
| 5 | Patrol: 1–2 D-1 (on alert, Perception Difficulty Class (DC) 50 to bypass) |
| 6 | No encounter (otherworld resource zone — collect D-Rank materials) |

> **Boss Zone**: The final zone of a D-Rank rift is always a D-Boss (use D-Rank Boss template + 1–2 unique abilities).

**C-Rank Rift Encounter Table (d6):**

| d6 | Encounter |
|:--:|------|
| 1 | 1–2 C-1 (Elite Hunter) |
| 2 | 1 C-2 (Heavy Fortress) |

| 4 | 2–3 D-Rank mixed group |
| 5 | Elite patrol: 1 C-1 (on alert, Difficulty Class (DC) 55 to bypass) |
| 6 | Environment encounter (trap + monster ambush combo) |

> **Boss Zone**: 1 C-Boss + optional 1 C-2 (vanguard).

**B-Rank Rift Encounter Table (d6):**
