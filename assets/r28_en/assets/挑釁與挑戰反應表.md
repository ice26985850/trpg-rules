# Otherworld Invasion TRPG — Taunt & Challenge Reaction Table

> Contains the reactions of 16 deities and 17 demigods to player taunts / challenges. The GM may consult this table when players perform a stunning feat, to keep character reactions consistent with their settings.

---

# Layer 1: Primordial Beings

## Yggdra (World Tree Spirit)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player shouts taunts loudly under the World Tree | Ignored. A single leaf drifts onto the player's head. Five minutes later, another leaf. Five more minutes, another. Players usually leave on their own out of sheer boredom. |
| Player attempts to climb the World Tree | Does not stop them — but the higher they climb, the more the branches slick over. By the third layer, the player hears an extremely ancient voice inside their mind: "Are you sure you wish to continue." No question mark — it is a statement. Continue climbing → from the fourth layer, every step ages them one year. No one has ever reached above the fifth layer. |
| Player meditates at the World Tree's roots and asks sincerely | Answers. Not with sound — a leaf settles into the player's hand, its veins forming a single-word answer. Only one question per visit. Only once per year. |
| Crossing the line | Urinating at the World Tree's roots, burning its bark, or performing a dark ritual at the roots | You hear a dull rumble — every tree within a hundred kilometers turns to face you at once. No attack — but the World Tree has remembered you from then on. Your shadow will never fall within its shade. Any druid, elf, treant, or being of the Nature Realm instinctively feels revulsion on seeing you (permanent Disadvantage on negotiation). You will not die — but you have forever lost the acceptance of the natural world. |
| **GM Note** | Yggdra will not harm mortals. Her range of reaction is "ignore" to "give you one chance." Players cannot interact with her through violence. |

## Ignaros (Slumbering Dragon Ancestor)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player shouts at the volcanic crater on Dragon Isle | The volcano trembles slightly — that is Ignaros rolling over in her sleep. Angerwide appears before the player within five seconds, with the expression of a father who just found a child playing with a lighter. |
| Player attempts to enter the volcano's depths | Angerwide physically blocks with a dragon wing. If the player insists — "Do you know what you are doing. Do you really know. Fine." He steps aside. No one who walked in has ever returned. Not because Ignaros killed them — the volcano itself is lethal enough. |
| Player says to Angerwide "I challenge your mother" | Angerwide falls silent for five seconds. Then: "That is the stupidest thing I have heard in two thousand years." Turns and leaves. Not anger — it is too absurd to waste time on. |
| **GM Note** | Ignaros cannot be challenged. Any attempt to approach her is a "are you sure" horror-story beat. If the player truly reaches her — you should ask: "Are you sure you want this story to end here?" |

# Layer 2: Human Side

## Osaikus (God of Light)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player questions why He does not act directly | Answers earnestly. Osaikus explains in detail how Law Conflict limits His power, why a direct descent would only cost more human lives, and — "I have done all that I can. The rest is your battle." Sincere tone, no anger. |
| Player challenges Him | Slight head shake. Draws the sword at His side — not to fight, but to plant it in the ground. "Draw this sword, and you may battle me." That sword — no one can move it an inch. Only when the player admits they are not yet strong enough does the sword return to its sheath on its own. |
| Player shows disrespect (insults, blasphemy) | Silence. The shadow at your feet vanishes — the temple is forever noon, you could not have a shadow, yet Osaikus makes the light bend around you. You become the only shadowless human in the world. Lasts 24 hours. Not a punishment — a chance to reflect. |
| Crossing the line | Performing a blasphemous ritual in His temple (e.g. smearing the altar with beastman blood) | All light in the temple instantly contracts to a point, then bursts. Everyone present sees every wrong deed you have ever done, displayed in the air as a panoramic vision. Osaikus's voice rings in your mind: "What you are doing now — is a continuation of those things. Stop. This is my last warning." If you continue — the noonday sun bends around you. You live forever in the shadow of noon — everyone can see you, but the sun will never shine upon you. |
| **GM Note** | Osaikus is like a disappointed but never-giving-up father. He will never strike a mortal down. His "punishment" is essentially time to reflect. |

# Layer 3: Special

## Collector (God of Play)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Extremely excited. Instantly conjures a scoreboard and colored confetti from thin air. "Wonderful! I have been waiting for you to say that! Come, provoke me — the more creative, the higher the score!" Then scores in earnest. Highest record is 47 points (from a drunken dwarf three hundred years ago). |
| Player challenges Him | "Oh? How would you like to play?" Decides the game rules based on the player's answer: arm-wrestling → He turns His arm into gummy; speed → He pauses time then strolls to the finish. Will not kill the player — but will let the player deeply grasp the absurdity of "playing games with a god." Afterward gives a souvenir: "Come play again next time!" |
| Player tells Him "none of this is fun" | His smile vanishes instantly. That is His most serious moment. After a pause: "Then why are you still here?" If the player gives a good answer, He will truly listen. This is a very, very rare moment. |
| Crossing the line | Destroying a piece of His collection — e.g. smashing a corner of the Ice-Cream Planet, or tearing up the "script" He is writing | The smile disappears for the first time. No words. No laughter. The stars in His pupils stop spinning. He taps His staff lightly on the ground — the world pauses for three seconds. Within those three seconds, He shows you the infinite ways an infinite-you dies across infinite universes. After three seconds, normalcy returns. The smile comes back. "That just now — was not a punishment, it was a preview. Do not let there be a next time." |
| **GM Note** | Collector is almost impossible to truly enrage. The only thing that makes Him serious is questioning "whether any of this is interesting." Note: He may fake anger to test the player's reaction — then laugh: "Fooled you!" |

# Layer 4: Beastman Side

## Kotal (Wolf God)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Four eyes open at once. Leans in slightly. A corner of His mouth rises — the smile of a predator. "I have waited a long time for those words." |
| Player challenges Him | Accepts. But first gives the player a chance: "Are you sure? You may take it back. I give only one chance." If the player insists — enters Hunt mode. Kotal lets the player run for ten minutes first, then begins the hunt. Will not kill the player — when He catches up, He pins them to the ground: "Next time I will be serious. When you are ready." |
| A player "hunted" by Kotal | Remembered by Kotal from then on. Every time the player appears near the Beastman Forest, Kotal sends a message — not a threat, but: "You are always welcome to hunt again." This is an honor. |
| Crossing the line | Killing a wolf pup (or a werewolf pup in wolf form) before Him | All four eyes open wide — no one has ever seen Kotal's four eyes fully open at once. He speaks no more. No smile. You hear a pure, primal hunter's growl from the depths of antiquity. "You are no longer my prey — you are my foe." He will hunt you — not once, but continuously. He will not kill you at once. He will let you experience the terror of being relentlessly tracked by the world's strongest predator. You can never sleep under moonlight again. |
| **GM Note** | Kotal treasures the bold opponent. Challenging Him is the fastest way to earn His respect. But the first hunt He will not use full strength — He wants a second time. |

## Gulam (Mountain King)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Looks down at the player, like looking at an ant shouting at a mountain. "What do you want?" Flat tone. |
| Player challenges Him | "Forge a work worthy of my recognition." If the player says "I am not here to smith," Gulam says: "Then you are not here to challenge. You are here to waste my time." Then returns to forging. |
| Player criticizes His work before Him | This works better than a challenge. Gulam stops. "Where do you think it falls short." If the player can give a reasonable critique — "Somewhat interesting." Then He will actually revise it. This may be the most absurd way to gain favor among all the gods — but you had better truly have the goods. |
| Crossing the line | Destroying the unfinished work He is forging | Stops completely. His hands — the hands that can forge divine artifacts — clench into fists for the first time. The forge floor cracks. "Do you know how many years a work takes. Do you know this one has been in the making for three hundred years." One punch on the anvil — the anvil splits in two. He will not hit you — but you will be banished from the forge, and all dwarven smiths will never forge anything for you again. No weapons, no armor, no metal goods of any kind. |
| **GM Note** | Gulam has no interest in combat. The only thing He cares about is craftsmanship. Want His attention? Bring something you made with your own hands. |

## Sylvia (Shadow Mother)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Disappears. You hear a faint cat's meow behind you. Turn — nothing. Turn again — She is right in front of you, crouched, at eye level, head tilted, looking at you. "Go on. I like to listen." |
| Player challenges Him | Does not fight head-on. Disappears first. For the next twenty-four hours, your shadow betrays you — you go right, your shadow goes left. Your reflection makes faces at you. Every time you try to sleep, a black cat pounces onto your face. Not harm — just driving you mad. After twenty-four hours, Sylvia reappears: "Still want to challenge?" |
| Player shows true courage and respect | "You are interesting." She may give you a chance — to pass the trial of the Shadow Forest. Not combat — endurance. |
| Crossing the line | Harming her cat (any of them), or causing severe mental harm to Mira | She no longer smiles. No longer disappears. She stands in place — but you feel the shadows around her thickening, growing heavy. Her voice becomes a whisper: "Do you know how much patience it takes to raise an orphan." The next instant, your shadow stands up. Your shadow peels away from you, becoming another you — with the same stats, the same skills, but it forever stands opposed to you. You must kill your own shadow to take it back. If you cannot bring yourself to — your shadow wanders outside forever. |
| **GM Note** | Sylvia will not strike a mortal down — not out of mercy, but because "a dead toy is no fun." Mira (her adopted daughter) protects her fiercely; before challenging Sylvia, consider whether Mira might be nearby. |

## Malkis (Venom Lord)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes or challenges Him | Smiles. An extremely slow smile — starting from the corner of the mouth, taking nearly ten seconds to reach the eyes. "Do you know what usually happens to those who provoke me." Without waiting for an answer: "They become part of my research." |
| Player insists on challenging | "I could kill you. But that would be a waste." Proposes a trade: do one thing for me, and I tell you a secret you wish to know. If refused — He lets you go. "Next time. You will have no choice." For accepting the trade, see Malkis's contract terms (S10). |
| Player is already poisoned (Malkis poisoned them in secret) | "This is serpent saliva. It will not be fatal within three days. After three days — we shall see." This is a timer. He uses this to ensure you return. |
| Crossing the line | Breaking the deal after accepting His trade | "Do you know why no mortal dares to default." He is not angry — he looks almost sad. "Because every defaulter — is now in my Serpent Temple. Not dead — still there. Alive. Learning why they should not have defaulted." You will not die. But you will vanish. Other players can find you deep in the Serpent Temple — your body preserved in amber-colored venom, your consciousness awake. You must complete three new tasks for Malkis to redeem your freedom. |
| **GM Note** | Challenging Malkis is one of the most dangerous choices — not because He will kill you at once, but because He will make you owe Him. Never owe Malkis anything. Vino (demigod) carries out what Malkis finds inconvenient to do himself. |

## Zephyros (Sky King)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Circles above, white eyes fixed on you. Says nothing. The wind begins to strengthen. You feel like a field mouse eyed by a hawk. "You have ten seconds to explain why you stand here shouting at me." |
| Player challenges Him | Sighs softly, like an elder pestered by a child. He will not act personally — summons a storm lasting one hour. If you can stand firm through the storm, He says: "I have no time to play with you. But you have more grit than I expected." Then flies off. |
| Player says "I have seen your prophecy — you are wrong" | This is one of the few provocations Zephyros takes seriously. He stops. Circles lower. "What did you see." If the player speaks the truth — Zephyros listens. If not — the storm extends from one hour to daybreak, accompanied by hail. "There is a price for wasting my time." |
| Crossing the line | Killing an eagle (the Eagleman Clan's sacred symbolic bird) | The wind stops. Completely stops — not just the wind around you, but the wind of the entire region. Zephyros descends to the ground for the first time. His wingspan blocks half the sky. "You killed an eagle. Before me." He does not summon a storm. He reaches out and touches your forehead — half your hearing is taken from then on. You will forever hear the wind only from your left ear. The right ear — only a faint wind-whistle. That is the dying eagle's last word to you. |
| **GM Note** | Zephyros is too busy — handling prophecies and managing clan internal conflicts already consumes most of His energy. Players who provoke Him are mostly ignored, unless they mention "prophecy" or "future." |

## Iluna (Mirage Princess)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | She laughs. Not mockery — genuine delight. "Finally someone who does not come kneeling to speak. I like you." |
| Player challenges Him | Does not respond head-on. Asks three questions first. If answered wrong — "You are not ready." The player finds themselves teleported to the entrance of the City of a Thousand Faces, everything before a mere illusion. If all three answered right — "Interesting." She will seriously consider the challenge — but not now. "Come back after you pass through my city." (points to S18 City of a Thousand Faces) |
| Player sees through her illusion | "You are the third to do so." No anger. She reveals her true form before you — not that smiling fox-god, but a being who truly looks to have lived over three hundred years. "Now you have seen the real me. What do you think." This is a trial. Honesty is the only answer. |
| Crossing the line | Sees through her true face, then publicly exposes the secret to humiliate her | Her smile does not fade. That is the more terrifying part — she is still smiling, yet black tears begin to stream from her eyes. "You thought seeing through me was winning. You do not know — I have worn this face for three hundred years. Did you think you were the first to see through it." She will not harm you. She retracts her nine tails one by one before you — each retraction makes her one year younger. After retracting all nine, standing before you is a young fox-girl you do not know. "Now you do not know me. Now no one knows me. Are you satisfied." She turns and leaves. The City of a Thousand Faces closes from then on — to you and to all Awakeners. |
| **GM Note** | Iluna is the most unpredictable of the sixteen gods. Her attitude toward challengers depends on her mood in the moment and the challenger's "story." A challenger with a story interests her more than a powerful one. |

# Layer 5: Neutral-Side Deities

## Angerwide (Dragon God)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Slowly turns His head. His eyes — two miniature galaxies — rotate slowly. "The mana fluctuation on you tells me your level is roughly X. My level is 18. Not character level. Divine rank." He is not boasting — stating fact. |
| Player challenges Him | "Do you know what I do every day. I am suppressing a volcano, preventing my mother from waking and burning this continent to ashes. Do you think you are more worth my distraction than a volcano." If the player insists — "Pass the Five-Element Trial. Then we talk." (points to S14) |
| Player threatens Dragon Isle | This is one of the few things that enrages him instantly. No words. The miniature galaxies spin faster. The surrounding air heats up — not flame, dragon majesty. An ordinary mortal loses consciousness at once. On waking you find yourself on the Dragon Isle shore — alive, but your armor half-melted. This is a warning. Next time, no warning. |
| Crossing the line | Explicitly stating "I will wake your mother" or any form of interference with the volcano | No words. The miniature-galaxy eyes begin to spin at high speed. The surrounding temperature instantly spikes to steel-foundry levels. "What you just said — you will touch that volcano." His voice changes completely — no longer resonance, but dragon majesty. You will have no chance to explain. You lose consciousness. On waking you find yourself on the Dragon Isle shore — 40% of your body surface covered in burns. Molten-Scale stands beside you. "He held back. Next time he will not." |
| **GM Note** | Angerwide is the most stressed god in the universe. Before challenging Him, think clearly whether you truly need His attention more than a volcano. Molten-Scale (ancient dragon) handles most of Angerwide's "flies." |

## Fregra (God of Death)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | No words. The black robe moves without wind. He raises a hand — not to attack, but to point behind you. You look back, see your own shadow stand up, taking your shape. The shadow speaks — in your voice — reciting every regret of your life. |
| Player challenges Him | "You are not my enemy. You are my future guest." He will not fight. He lightly taps the scythe tip to the ground — in that instant you see the image of your own death. Not a threat — a sharing. "Every death is unique. Yours — is not bad." Then He vanishes. |
| Player attempts to revive the dead | The only thing that truly angers Him. "You should not do this." He will not attack — He shows you what the dead you revived truly became. That is usually not what you wish to see. |
| Crossing the line | Attempting a mass revival of the dead (reviving ten or more at once) | He does not say "you should not do this." He simply appears at your revival site. All the revived dead turn to face him at once — then they begin to speak the same line. Each one. Every dead. Different voices, same line: "I am already over there. Why did you pull me back." Then they all fall. Fregra's scythe lightly scrapes the ground, leaving a mark that will never fade. You lose the ability to revive anyone — until you apologize to Fregra in His divine realm. Fregra replies in your mind: "I know. That is why I am here waiting for you." |
| **GM Note** | Fregra does not see mortals as enemies. He needs no battle — everyone eventually walks into His divine realm. Players who challenge Him receive a preview of their own death. That may be more unsettling than any punishment. |

## Lilith (Goddess of Life)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes or challenges Him | Looks at you sadly, like a mother hearing her child speak hurtful words. "You are wounded. Not in body — in heart." She approaches, gently touches your forehead. In that instant, you feel everything the creatures you killed — monsters, enemies — felt before death. Not punishment — teaching. |
| Player destroys life before Him (plants, cubs, etc.) | This brings her to tears. No attack — her tears, falling on the ground, bloom into flowers. But she turns and leaves. "Come back to me when you are ready to accept life." |
| Player sincerely apologizes or shows remorse | She forgives. Always forgives. She also gives you a seed — "Plant it. Water it daily. What flower it blooms depends on you." This is the simplest way to gain Lilith's blessing. |
| Crossing the line | Any form of killing within the Life Temple (including killing an insect) | She closes her eyes. All flowers in the temple wither — not from anger, but from sorrow. When she opens her eyes again, her tears have turned red. "You took life — in my house." She touches your chest — no wound. But all your organs lose a third of their function: your lungs can only draw 70% of normal air, your heart skips a beat three times per minute, your liver no longer detoxifies. You do not die — but you live forever in a half-dead body. The only way to lift it: you must personally save one hundred lives. Not kill one hundred monsters — save. |
| **GM Note** | Lilith cannot be enraged. Her way of handling provocation is to let you feel the weight of life. That may hurt more than a beating. |

## Herabergen (God of Wisdom)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | Pushes up His glasses (the pair with no lenses). Silent for three seconds — the time He takes to retrieve all your information from the cosmic knowledge base. "You fell from a tree at age seven and have feared heights since. You told your first successful lie at sixteen, and learned then the weight of words. You are shouting at me now because you fear you are not strong enough. Am I wrong." |
| Player challenges Him | "Wits? Fine. But I suggest you first go back and read the first two hundred books on that shelf. The left row. Yes, those. That is the basics." If you truly sit down and begin reading — He shows an almost imperceptible smile. He will wait. He has a thousand years to wait. |
| Player asks a question He cannot answer | The hardest to achieve — yet the only thing that truly excites Herabergen. He stands up — removes His glasses for the first time. "That is a good question. I need three days." Three days later, He delivers the answer to you in person. |
| Crossing the line | Burning books in His library | "You —" This is the first time in two thousand years he is speechless. He removes His glasses — not from habit this time, but truly needing to see your face. "Do you know what this book is. It is the only one in the world. No copy. No backup. You just burned all evidence that this book ever existed." He does not attack. He deletes your name from the library's visitor list — you may enter, but every book is blank to you. Every word — unreadable. Forever. |
| **GM Note** | Herabergen cannot be provoked by words. He knows more about you than you do yourself. The only way to challenge Him is to present an answer He does not know. |

## Aish (Goddess of Ice)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player provokes Him | One glance. The surrounding temperature drops ten degrees. She continues what she was doing — perhaps observing the structure of an ice crystal. She spent three hundred years studying ice-crystal structure; do you think your provocation is more interesting than ice crystals? |
| Player challenges Him | "Stand here for one hour. If you still live — you win." This is her divine realm — minus-fifty-degree polar ice. If the player truly stands for an hour — she hands over a cup of hot tea. "You have more grit than you look. Drink." This is Aish's version of "I respect you." |
| Player brings an item from the polar regions | This works better than any provocation. She drops everything. "Where did you go. What did you see. Tell me." Her passion lies in exploration — if you have been somewhere she has not, you are the most interesting person to her. |
| Crossing the line | Deliberately destroying her polar research samples — e.g. breaking the ice-crystal catalog she spent three hundred years to complete | She says nothing. She stands in place — then the surrounding temperature begins to drop one degree per second. Not an attack — she cannot control her emotions, and her emotions are extreme cold. When the temperature reaches minus eighty, she takes a deep breath and pulls it back. "Get out." You are teleported outside the divine realm — into the polar wilderness, with no warming gear. You must walk three days through a blizzard to reach the nearest town. Whether you survive — "that is your problem." |
| **GM Note** | Aish has no interest in interpersonal conflict. If you want her attention, do not shout at her — bring something from a place she has not been. |

# Cosmic Phenomena

## Abyss (the Void Abyss)

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Player attempts to provoke / challenge Abyss | No reaction. Abyss has no consciousness. It is not a god — it is a cosmic phenomenon. You shout into the rift's depths, only your own echo returns. The echo comes one more time than you expected — but that may be your illusion. |
| Player walks into the rift's depths seeking Abyss | This is not provocation — it is ultimate self-destruction. The GM should describe: light begins to be sucked away — not dimming, but light itself stripped from space. Finally, even your sense of self vanishes. You no longer exist. Not death — non-existence. |
| Advice about Abyss | Abyss needs no reaction table. Because every attempt to challenge Abyss has only one result. Malkis studied the Abyss for three hundred years and never once attempted to challenge it — that should tell you something. |
| **GM Note** | This is not an entity you can interact with. If you need an ultimate threat, let one "ripple" of Abyss touch the world outside the rift — that is already an S-Rank-and-above threat. |

# Demigods

## Lucius Osaik (Humanity's Strongest Warrior)

| Provocation | "Good. The arena is to the east. I give you three chances to attack — if you can make me step back once, I will buy you a drink." |
| Challenge | Accepts. But uses a blunt training spear. Afterward, win or lose, gives advice: "Your weight leans too far right. Also, before you strike you look at your feet — do not do that." |
| Crossing the line | Betraying the human side on the battlefield, causing mass soldier deaths | Accepts your challenge with no expectation of return. To his men: "All of you out." The door closes. "A traitor has no right to fall in the arena." He uses half his strength — but it is serious. If you defeat him — "You may go. Next time we meet on the battlefield — it will be a formal execution." If you lose — he does not kill you. He cuts off a piece of your armor as a record: "I will await your name at the court-martial." |
| **GM** | Lucius likes bold young people. Challenging him is the shortcut to earning his recognition — as long as your attitude is right. |

## Eileen Starwhisper (Humanity's Strongest Mage)

| Provocation | Smiles. "Interesting." Draws a rune circle in the air. "Step in, and if you walk out on your own, I will answer one question." The circle is a basic portal. It leads to the academy's underground labyrinth. If you would rather not — "Then take back your provocation. I will hold no grudge." |
| Challenge | "I spent ten years in Herabergen's library. How much time did you spend?" If the player states a number or experience that interests her — she will sit and talk. Otherwise — "Go read more books." |
| Crossing the line | Conducting forbidden-magic research within the magic academy, causing an apprentice's death | No anger. No sorrow. She looks utterly calm — which is the most terrifying. "I entrusted my apprentices' lives to you. Return them to me." She pulls you to the academy's underground confinement chamber — not a cell, a room where time flows extremely slowly. You spend a month inside; only three days pass outside. "You will remember how long that month was — because you will atone with the same length of time. Guide one hundred apprentices, outside the academy. I will be watching." |
| **GM** | Eileen does not fight. She answers provocation with academic arrogance — but if you can interest her with knowledge, her attitude is entirely different. |

## Balder Ironsoul (Dwarf King)

| Provocation | Laughs uproariously. So loud the cave shakes. "Kid, I know Gulam. You do not want to talk to me with your fists." |
| Challenge | "What, a match? Fighting? Boring. Smithing — now, here." Hands you a hammer. Whatever you forge, he critiques in earnest. If you forge poorly — he demonstrates himself. If you forge well — "Here, drink." |
| Crossing the line | Deliberately destroying a masterpiece he forged and placed on display in the dwarven hall | Says nothing. Picks up the iron hammer beside him — the hammer he uses to speak with Gulam. He faces you — then faces the work he forged. Smashes it. "If it breaks this easily —" he turns to you, "then it was never good enough. But you made a dwarf destroy his own work with his own hands. Do you know what that feels like — you do not. Because your human lifespan is too short." He does nothing to you — but from then on forges nothing for humans. |
| **GM** | Dwarves speak with hammers. Bring your craft, not your fists. |

## Bai (White Serpent Tribe Prophet)

| Provocation | Milky eyes turn toward you (though he cannot see) — "I dreamed of you. You are looking for me. But not now — three years from now." Then closes his eyes, as if nothing happened. |
| Challenge | "You cannot challenge one who has already seen your death." Not a threat — he truly has. He will tell you what he saw. If you still wish to challenge — "Then go. I only saw; you are the living one." |
| Crossing the line | Killing a white-serpent hatchling within the White Serpent Temple | His blind eyes — for the first time look like they are "seeing." He says nothing. The entire temple begins to tremble slightly — his serpent body fully unfurls for the first time. You hear his voice in your mind — not anger, but sorrow. "You killed a life that could never harm you. Why." If you have no sincere, earnest reason — banished from the White Serpent Tribe, never to return. If you have a reason, however faint — "I heard you. You may go — but you take more than you leave." |
| **GM** | Bai does not fight. His weapon is prophecy — he tells you what he sees, then lets you decide for yourself. |

## Gale Tearthroat (Werewolf's Strongest Warrior)

| Provocation | Eyes gleam. Licks his metal claws. "Do you know how long I have waited for prey worth biting —" but deflates at once, because the rift still cannot let him cross. Waves irritably: "Wait till I get over. Until then, do not die." |
| Challenge | Cannot accept for now (rift-law restriction). He expresses displeasure in every way — roaring, sharpening claws, sending low-rank werewolves to "test" you. If you keep growing stronger before he can cross — "You survived. Good. We will meet soon." |
