# Otherworld Invasion TRPG — Monster Compendium

## Low-Rank Monsters (Lv.1–15)

### Void Rat
- **Level:** Lv.1–5
- **Challenge Rating (CR):** 1–4
- **Classification:** Low-Rank Beast Type
- **Hit Points (HP):** 30–80
- **Mana Points (MP):** 0
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 8–20
- **Magic Attack (MATK):** 0
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 3–10
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 2–5
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 55–65
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 15–25
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 5
- **Damage Type:** Piercing (bite)
- **Resistance:** None
- **Weakness:** Fire ×1.5
- **Special Ability:** Pack Tactics (when allies of the same kind are nearby, Hit Rate (HIT%) +5%)
- **AI Template:** Beast Instinct (flees when Hit Points (HP) < 30%)
- **Drop:** 5–15 Experience Points (EXP), 1–3 Gold (G), Void Rat Hide (White · Common material)
- **Spawn:** E-Rank Rift, Erosion Zone edges

### Gargoyle
- **Level:** Lv.4–8
- **Challenge Rating (CR):** 3–7
- **Classification:** Low-Rank Flying Type
- **Hit Points (HP):** 60–140
- **Mana Points (MP):** 20
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 14–30
- **Magic Attack (MATK):** 5
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 12–25
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 8–15
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 60–72
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 18–30
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 8
- **Damage Type:** Slashing (claw strike)
- **Resistance:** Piercing ×0.5 (stone skin)
- **Weakness:** Bludgeoning ×1.5
- **Special Ability:** Flight (melee attacks require ranged weapons or skills); Petrifying Gaze (every 3 turns, Difficulty Class (DC) 40 Spirit (SPI) check; failure causes Paralysis for 1 turn)
- **AI Template:** Predator (prioritizes isolated targets)
- **Drop:** 15–30 Experience Points (EXP), 5–10 Gold (G), Gargoyle Wing (Green · Uncommon material)
- **Spawn:** E/D-Rank Rift, abandoned high-rise buildings

### Acid Slug
- **Level:** Lv.3–9
- **Challenge Rating (CR):** 2–7
- **Classification:** Low-Rank Elemental Type
- **Hit Points (HP):** 40–120
- **Mana Points (MP):** 10
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 6–18
- **Magic Attack (MATK):** 12–28
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 2–8
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 10–20
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 50–60
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 5–10 (slow)
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 0
- **Damage Type:** Shadow (acid)
- **Resistance:** Shadow ×0.5
- **Weakness:** Fire ×2.0, Frost ×1.5

## Mid-Rank Monsters (Lv.15–40)

### Shadow Wolf
- **Level:** Lv.15–25
- **Challenge Rating (CR):** 12–20
- **Classification:** Mid-Rank Beast Type
- **Hit Points (HP):** 250–550
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 35–65
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 20–40
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 15–30
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 70–82
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 25–40
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 12–18
- **Damage Type:** Slashing (fangs and claws)
- **Resistance:** Shadow ×0.5
- **Weakness:** Holy ×1.5
- **Special Ability:** Shadow Stealth (vanishes for 1 turn, next attack ignores 50% Dodge Rate); Wolf Pack Coordination (when in the same zone as other Shadow Wolves, damage +15%)
- **AI Template:** Predator (prioritizes low Hit Points (HP) targets, uses stealth)
- **Drop:** 80–200 Experience Points (EXP), 20–60 Gold (G), Shadow Wolf Pelt (Blue · Rare material)
- **Spawn:** C-Rank Rift, edges of Beastman Forest

- **Level:** Lv.20–35
- **Challenge Rating (CR):** 16–28
- **Classification:** Mid-Rank Heavy-Armor Type
- **Hit Points (HP):** 600–1,500
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 45–90
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 45–80
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 10–25
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 60–70
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 5–10 (cumbersome)
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 8–12
- **Damage Type:** Bludgeoning (charge)
- **Resistance:** Physical (Slashing/Piercing/Bludgeoning) ×0.5
- **Weakness:** Magic (Shadow/Holy/Elemental) ×1.5
- **Special Ability:** Charge (straight line, 2 zones, all enemies, damage ×1.5, Difficulty Class (DC) 60 Dexterity (DEX) for half damage); Iron Wall (every 3 turns, Physical Defense (PDEF) +20 for 1 turn)

- **Drop:** 150–350 Experience Points (EXP), 30–100 Gold (G), Steel Horn (Blue · Rare material)
- **Spawn:** C-Rank Rift, open terrain

## High-Rank Monsters (Lv.40–70)

- **Hit Points (HP):** 3,000–6,000
- **Physical Attack (PATK):** 160–340
- **Physical Defense (PDEF):** 120–220
- **Magic Defense (MDEF):** 60–120
- **Hit Rate (HIT%):** 82–90
- **Dodge Rate (DGE%):** 15–25
- **Critical Rate (CRT%):** 12–18
- **Damage Type:** Slashing + Shadow (Abyss Blade)
- **Resistance:** Shadow ×0.3, Physical ×0.7
- **Weakness:** Holy ×2.0
- **Special Ability:** Abyssal Slash (Attack (ATK) ×2.0, Cooldown (CD) 3 turns); Dark Aura (enemies within radius 1 zone suffer Hit Rate (HIT%) −10%); Unshakable (when Hit Points (HP) < 20%, all stats +30%)

- **Drop:** 1,500–3,000 Experience Points (EXP), 200–600 Gold (G), Abyss Shard (Purple · Unique material)
- **Spawn:** B-Rank and above Rifts
