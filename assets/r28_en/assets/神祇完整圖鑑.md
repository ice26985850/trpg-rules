# Layer One: Primordial Beings

## World Tree Spirit "Yggdra" (Yggdra)

> Divine Rank 20 | Absolute Neutral | not a god — the will of the World Tree itself

### Background

At the beginning of creation, the World Tree and the World Heart were born simultaneously as twin anchors. Yggdra is the incarnate will of the World Tree — or more precisely, the World Tree is Him, and He is the World Tree.

His stance is extremely simple: **What you do in other worlds is none of my concern. But on this continent — any god who descends in true body, I wake.** Beastmen invading Earth? That is their business with another world. But should a godwar be opened on this continent — whoever strikes first — Yggdra will forcibly expel all participating deities.

His attitude toward Collector is the most special relationship since creation: an old man and a mischievous grandson. Collector is the only being in the entire universe permitted to come and go freely in the World Tree. Other gods wish to see Yggdra? Wait beneath the roots, at His pleasure — maybe three days, maybe three years, maybe until you die of old age with no reply. But every time Collector comes, Yggdra "hmphs" once, then lets Him swing from the branches sharing "interesting things seen lately." This is the closest thing to "kinship" since creation.

### Appearance

No fixed form. The most common manifestation is a mass of gentle emerald light enfolding a towering ancient tree — canopy piercing the clouds, roots delving into the earth's heart. When He needs to "speak," an immensely ancient face appears on the bark — weathered as if carved over ten thousand years. The voice comes not from a mouth, but is assembled from the rustle of leaves, the vibration of roots, and the resonance you feel in your chest.

### Divine Domain

Life, nature, balance, world laws, forest, guardianship, witness of time

### Divine Power

Law Domain 5, All-Thing Communion 5, Eternal Body 5, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

Needs no separate "Divine Realm" — the entire World Tree is His domain. Within the Nature Realm of several hundred kilometers' radius around the World Tree, any deity's power is suppressed.

### Forbidden Law

Any deity stepping onto the continent's land in true body or as a Chief-God Projection → Yggdra wakes and forcibly expels all participating deities. "Tolerates" the Beastmen's invasion of Earth — that is the otherworld's affair, not His to govern.

### Divine Class

**Not provided.** Yggdra makes no contracts with mortals. He barely even cares about mortal existence — unless you climb to the World Tree's summit, which no one has ever done.

## Ancient Dragon Ancestor "Ignaros of Nirvana's Flame" (Ignaros)

> Divine Rank 20 | Chaotic Neutral | Sleeping | mother of Angerwide, ancestor of the Dragons

### Background

The first dragon. Born at creation simultaneously with Yggdra and the World Heart — if Yggdra is the beginning of earth and life, Ignaros is the beginning of flame and change.

In a devastating ancient war (the very cause of today's Ancient Battlefield Desert), Ignaros witnessed gods' war turn the entire continent to scorched earth. She chose neither side — she chose to **incinerate both at once.** After that, she spent nearly all her power and sank into sleep deep within the volcano beneath Dragon Isle. Not defeated — just tired.

Angerwide (the Dragon God) is her only son. He guards Dragon Isle, ostensibly fulfilling the Dragon God's duties — in truth guarding his mother's sleep. He has never seen his mother awake, but from the volcanic earth-vein tremors he can feel — his mother still lives.

**Consequence if awakened by chance:** Ignaros does not distinguish friend from foe. The instant she wakes she will indiscriminately burn away all nearby existence — the only exception being her son Angerwide, and whatever Angerwide at that moment is willing to shield with his own body. Normally, what Angerwide can protect is the other Dragons on the isle. Everything else — regardless of faction, regardless of race — turns to ash.

This is also why even a godwar at sea is impossible: Dragon Isle is right there. Ignaros is beneath Dragon Isle. A large enough divine-power fluctuation — and her eye opens.

### Appearance

None have seen her sleeping. Per Dragon-clan murals (painted by Angerwide himself): a dragon of scale beyond words — her wings spread can shadow the entire sky above Dragon Isle, scales formed of congealed magma and burning star-shards. The Dragon murals do not depict her eyes — because "if you paint them, she will look over."

### Divine Domain

Element, destruction, rebirth, primordial fire, Dragon ancestor, nirvana

### Divine Power

God of Elements 5, Bestow Life 5, Eternal Body 5, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

Deep within the Dragon Isle volcano — not a Divine Realm, just where she sleeps. Every eruption of that volcano is one of her breaths.

### Divine Class

**Not provided.** Ignaros is sleeping. Go make a contract with her? Find her first — you probably won't even reach Dragon Isle.

# Layer Two: Human Side

## God of Light "Osaikus" (Osaikus)

> Divine Rank 19 | Lawful Neutral | sole chief-god of the Human side | commissioner of the System

### Background

The otherworld's only Human chief-god. Through the long ages after creation, Osaikus built Human civilization in the north — teaching humans agriculture, navigation, law, and justice. His Divine Realm is a magnificent palace complex forever at noon, where no shadow falls.

When the Beastman clans captured the World Heart and opened dimensional Rifts to invade Earth, Osaikus faced a choice: stand by, or help the invaded side. Limited by Law Conflict and unable to descend on Earth directly, He found Collector — a god forever seeking "interesting things" — and commissioned Him to design a strengthening system for modern humans. This is why Awakeners see attribute panels, can take quests, and grow stronger by slaying monsters.

Osaikus is not all-knowing and all-powerful — He merely chose, among limited options, the one that best helps humanity.

### Appearance

Tall Human male figure, golden long hair, pure white armor. No helmet — His face is forever bathed in sunlight, so bright it sometimes blurs His features. At His waist hangs a sword never drawn (rumor says, once drawn, the sun flickers out for an instant). Behind Him floats a halo — not decoration, but a self-imposed limit: while the halo exists, He cannot wield full power.

### Divine Domain

Sun, justice, guardianship, cartography, navigation, trade, oath, loyalty

### Divine Power

Power of Truth 5, Guarding Power 2, Heavenly Decree 3, Keen Perception 1, Holy Radiance 1, Indirect Insight 3, Grant Divine Arts 3, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

A magnificent palace complex, forever at noon, with no shadow.

# Layer Three: Special

## God of Play "Collector"

> Divine Rank 19 | Chaotic Neutral | creator of the System | belongs to no faction

### Background

Collector is the strangest god in the universe. He cares nothing for justice, evil, order, or chaos — He cares for one thing only: **is this fun.**

When Osaikus asked Him to design a strengthening system for modern humans, Collector agreed almost instantly. Not out of sympathy — but because "a bunch of ordinary humans thrown into an otherworld-invasion disaster, then given a gamified growth system, watching them climb from zero to the top — that script is too interesting."

He is the creator of the System, but not the guardian of players. He will not save you. He only ensures the System runs normally — then sits in cosmic space, eating ice cream from the ice-cream planet, watching you struggle in the Rifts.

His relationship with Yggdra is the warmest thread in the entire divine system: Yggdra is the only being He quietly obeys. Every time He tires of playing, He flies back to the World Tree, lies on a branch, and tells the old man his recent sights. Yggdra finds Him noisy, but never drives Him away.

### Appearance

No fixed appearance. Common manifestation: a young man in an overly flashy tailcoat (roughly early twenties), hair a different color each meeting — fluorescent pink today, starry purple next. Always carries a cane (actually His divine artifact, but He only spins it for play). The strangest part is the eyes: within the pupils you can see stars flowing. When He takes interest in something, the stars in His pupils spin faster.

### Divine Domain

Time, space, game, illusion, adventure, mischief, lie, fraud, joy, inspiration

### Divine Power

Shapechange 5, Keen Perception 1, Indirect Insight 12, Divine Realm Descent 1

> Indirect Insight 12: Collector knows nearly everything happening in the universe — not because He is omnipotent, but because He placed "monitors" everywhere. These monitors are the Awakeners' System interfaces.

### Divine Realm

A vast cosmic space holding all manner of strange planets — ice-cream planet, cake planet, pudding planet. Each planet is a "fun thing" He collected.

### Divine Class: Game Master

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Acquisition Condition | S19 main quest — Collector appears in person and proposes the final game. Almost impossible to obtain by skipping the plot. |
| Divine-Granted Trait | **System Authority**: may view any enemy's complete data panel in combat (Hit Points (HP), Mana Points (MP), attributes, skills, weaknesses, resistances) |
| Divine-Granted Skill | **Law Modification** (once per battle): modify one current combat rule for 1 turn. Examples: "All attacks hit this turn" / "Dodge Rate cap removed this turn" / "Gravity reversed — no one can move." The GM has veto power over excessively absurd requests. |

# Layer Four: Beastman Side (Six Clan Chief-Gods)

## Wolf God "Kotal" (Kotal)

> Divine Rank 16 | Chaotic Neutral | chief-god of the Werewolf Clan | invasion vanguard

### Background

The moonlight of Beastman Forest forever illuminates Kotal's forest Divine Realm. He is the most aggressive chief-god among the six clans — not because he is the most warlike, but because of his near-obsessive pursuit of "the hunt." For the other clan chief-gods, invading Earth may be a conspiracy — for Kotal, it is an unprecedented great hunt. New prey, new environment, new challenge.

### Appearance

A tall werewolf, grey-white fur reflecting silver sheen under moonlight. Four eyes — two in normal position, two above the brow (the mark of his divinity: when all four eyes open at once, he sees the prey's future trajectory). Wears a translucent cloak woven from moonlight. When silent he looks beastly; when he speaks, his diction is unexpectedly refined — said to have read through the otherworld Humans' hunting poetry.

Forest, moon, beast, druid, ranger, tracking, speed, hunting, adventure, travel

## Mountain King "Gulam" (Gulam)

> Divine Rank 17 | Lawful Neutral | chief-god of the Bearman Clan | fights only by oath

### Background

Gulam is the most reluctant participant among the six clans. He joined the invasion not to conquer — but because of an ancient vow. In the distant past, the Bearman Clan owed the Werewolf Clan an irrefusable contract. When Kotal launched the invasion plan, he invoked that contract, and Gulam had no choice.

Gulam's Divine Realm is an underground forge — he is less a war god than a craftsman god. He has no interest in conquering the otherworld. All he cares about is that the oath be kept. If one day the contract's terms are met — or proven to have a loophole — he will be the first to exit this war.

### Appearance

A giant bear standing ten stories tall, body formed of granite and obsidian — not fur, but real mineral. His chest is inlaid with an ever-glowing rune (the physical manifestation of the contract). The ground trembles when he moves, yet he walks with extreme care — not from clumsiness, but because he does not want to step on any stone that could become a work. His voice when speaking is a low rumble from within the mountain.

### Divine Domain

Earth, mountains, forging, craft, oath, contract, endurance

### Divine Power

Guarding Power 3, Master of Craft 3, Earth Pact 2, Eternal Body 4, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

An underground forge, lit by eternal earth-core fire.

Sylvia is the youngest clan chief-god — she has reigned less than three hundred years (by godly standards, almost an infant). Her divine rank is the lowest, forcing her to play a delicate role among the six clans — showing the Catman Clan's value (thus sending the finest scouts and assassins to join the invasion) while not spending too much.

> Divine Rank 16 | Lawful Evil | chief-god of the Black Serpent Clan | Abyss researcher

Malkis is the most dangerous of the six clans — not for his combat power, but for his mind. He is the only clan chief-god researching the details of the Abyss contract. He knows what lies at the other end of the Rifts. He knows better than anyone — that the invasion will eventually spiral out of control.

But he told no other clan. Because he does not know what happens after the Abyss loses control, and he needs more data.

His Divine Realm lies deep underground, full of snakes and ancient texts. His mastery of poison is not only his divine domain — but the result of long self-experimentation.

Upper body human male (middle-aged, extremely gaunt, golden serpent eyes), lower body a giant black-scaled serpent tail. Wears a robe made of snake sheds, each shed inscribed with dense incantations. # Layer Five: Neutral Gods

## Dragon God "Angerwide" (Angerwide)

> Divine Rank 18 | Lawful Neutral | chief-god of the Dragons | son of Ignaros

### Background

Angerwide is Ignaros's only son. His divine rank ranks fourth in the entire divine system (below only Yggdra, Ignaros, Osaikus, and Collector), but most of his power is not spent on rule or expansion — it is spent suppressing the volcanic activity of Dragon Isle.

He has never seen his mother awake. Since birth, his only task has been to ensure his mother keeps sleeping. His stance that "the Dragons are neutral" is not merely political — the Dragons cannot afford battle losses. If the Dragons are massively destroyed in a godwar, his power weakens; once the power suppressing the volcano weakens — his mother wakes.

He joins no side's war. Who wins or loses matters not; what matters is not letting his mother wake.

### Appearance

A silver-blue giant dragon, scales formed of congealed starlight and meteoric iron. Much smaller than his mother — but still the largest dragon alive. His horns curve backward (unlike most dragons' straight ones — said because, suppressing his mother's heat deep in the volcano, the horns were bent by lava). Eyes are two miniature galaxies — slowly rotating. His voice comes not from a mouth — but from the resonance in your mind.

### Divine Domain

Element, storm, star, dawn, dusk, Dragon, flight, caster, wealth, fate

### Divine Power

Bestow Life 4, Master of Craft 5, Holy Radiance 1, Indirect Insight 1, God of Elements 3, Grant Divine Arts 1, Eternal Body 1, Shapechange 1, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

A mountain piercing the clouds — its peak breaking the atmosphere, directly seeing the stars.

## God of Death "Fregra" (Fregra)

> Divine Rank 18 | Neutral Evil | master of death and darkness

### Background

Fregra is not evil — He is neutral death. War, disease, murder, natural old age — all that takes life eventually passes through His Divine Realm. His attitude toward the invasion is indifference — more deaths just make Him busier.

But Fregra has one un-crossable bottom line: **the dead should not return.** If someone tries to mass-resurrect the dead (by any means), Fregra will act personally. This is also why even the maddest chief-god among the Beastmen dares not use undead legions — Fregra's scythe distinguishes no friend from foe.

### Appearance

No face. Fregra's form is a tall, thin black shadow in pitch-black robes, the head position bearing no face — only two hollow eye-positions emitting dark-red light. Hands are bony, gripping an impossibly oversized scythe — its blade inscribed with the names of all the dead. Each time someone dies, another notch appears on the blade. He speaks without a mouth — the voice comes from the darkness behind you.

### Divine Domain

Shadow, darkness, death, the dead, war, disease, destruction, doom

### Divine Power

War God's Power 2, Deific Curse 1, Hand of Death 1, Holy Radiance 1, Indirect Insight 3, War King 9, Divine Realm Descent 1

### Divine Realm

A world full of skeletons, mountains of corpses and seas of blood, and the breath of death — but not terrifying. It is quiet there. Very quiet.

> Divine Rank 17 | Absolute Neutral | guardian of knowledge, magic, and craft

Herabergen's Divine Realm is a gigantic library holding all books in existence. His attitude toward the invasion is like other neutral gods — non-participation, but conditional assistance. If someone comes to his library with the right question and proper price, he answers. The harder the question, the higher the price — but the answer is absolutely worth it.

He and Collector share a strange "book-friend" relationship: Collector occasionally runs to the library to share "interesting sights from the outside world," and Herabergen records these sights — Collector is the living encyclopedia, Herabergen the dead one.

Always holds a book, but its contents differ each time you look. ## Goddess of Ice "Aish" (Aish)

> Divine Rank 16 | Lawful Neutral | glacier, exploration, and endurance

### Background

Aish is a loner among the gods. Her Divine Realm is an extreme-cold ice land — she chose it not because exiled, but because she likes quiet. She is the least politically involved of all gods — she holds no stance on the invasion, because she barely pays attention to the outside world.

Her passion is exploration and recording. The northern polar glaciers, the peak of the world's highest mountain, the deepest ice layer of the seabed — she has been to all "places no one goes." If you can find her, she will share her discoveries — but only if you can stay conscious in minus-fifty-degree conditions.

### Appearance

A woman in ice-crystal armor, the armor not metal — but eternally frozen ice that never melts. Silver-white short hair, skin pale to near transparency. Eyes pure ice-blue, no pupils. A faint chill radiates around her — those near her shiver involuntarily.

### Divine Domain

# Cosmic Phenomenon

## Void Abyss "Abyss" (Abyss)

> Not a god | uncommunicable | the true cost of Rift contracts

### Background

In the void between the two worlds exists something that cannot be called "god." It is not evil — it has no consciousness. It is merely a phenomenon: all torn dimensional Rifts ultimately connect to it.

To open the Rifts, the Beastman clans performed a forbidden ritual at the World Heart — borrowing from the void the power to tear dimensions. But every Rift is a "straw" connecting to Abyss. The larger and longer-open the Rift, the more Abyss seeps in from both worlds at once.

Lawful Creep — monsters entering in order from low to high rank — is not the Beastmen's strategy, but Abyss's throughput limit. A Rift can only "exhale" a certain strength of thing each time, gradually increasing over time.

Malkis is the only god seriously studying this — he knows the Abyss will eventually spiral out of control. But he chooses silence, because he does not know what happens after losing control, and he needs more data.

### Appearance

No appearance. No one has seen Abyss — or more precisely, all who have seen it no longer exist. The "black light" occasionally appearing inside Rifts — the direction light is sucked into — is Abyss's tentacle peering through.

### In-Game Manifestation

- The environment inside Rifts: the deeper, the more bizarre; physical laws begin to fail
- Lawful Creep mechanic
- Potential ultimate threat: unknown regions beyond S-Rank Rifts
