# Otherworld Invasion TRPG — Demigod Compendium

> Contains 14 demigods of the otherworld — the strongest beings below the true gods. Each major race and faction has 1–2. A demigod is not necessarily the holder of a deity's divine class — some demigods are themselves the mouthpieces of gods, while others stepped into the demigod rank purely through their own strength.

---

# Otherworld Human Territory

## Holy Lance Knight "Lucius Ose" (Lucius Ose)

> Humanity's strongest warrior | Osaikus's Chosen | Marshal of the Human Kingdom

### Background

Lucius is the supreme military commander of the otherworld Human Kingdom. Forty years ago in the "Blood Moon War," he single-handedly slew three high-ranking priests of the Black Serpent Clan and an adult-dragon-class monster, earning humanity's title "Spear of Osaikus." Osaikus bestowed his blessing personally — not because he was the most pious, but because he was the strongest.

His existence is one of the key reasons the Human Kingdom has endured to this day under the pressure of the Beastman clans. Past sixty, his physical condition is no longer at its peak, but his experience and prestige remain irreplaceable in the army.

His attitude toward Awakeners (players): respectful but keeps his distance. He has seen too many "chosen ones" fall on the battlefield — he needs to see strength, not hear names.

### Appearance

A towering old veteran, short grey-white hair, and a claw scar running from forehead to chin on his left cheek (from the dragon-class monster forty years ago). Wears silver-white armor with no ornamentation — only countless dents left by combat. His weapon is a lance taller than himself, the "Holy Lance · Noon" — said to unleash its full power only when struck by direct noon sunlight.

## Grand Sage "Eileen Starwhisper" (Eileen Starwhisper)

> Humanity's strongest mage | Disciple of Herabergen, God of Wisdom | Head of the Kingdom's Academy of Magic

### Background

Eileen is the mortal closest to "god" among otherworld humans — not through power, but through knowledge. At age thirty she successfully entered Herabergen's library (itself considered a near-impossible feat) and studied there for ten years. Herabergen did not take her as a disciple — "The library is open to all; only those who stay are students."

After returning to the Human Kingdom she founded the Academy of Magic, training an entire generation of mages. Her attitude toward Awakeners is rational curiosity — "The System on you is Collector's handiwork; I've always wanted to study it." But she promises not to take it apart to look.

### Appearance

A woman in her fifties, silver hair tied in a simple braid. Wears the plainest grey robe — without knowing who she is, you would take her for a library administrator. Her eyes are pale purple (after ten years in Herabergen's library, her eye color changed permanently). She always wears three rings — one for defense, one for teleportation, and one whose purpose she herself no longer remembers.

# Dwarves and White Serpent

## Forge King "Balder Steelsoul" (Balder Steelsoul)

> King of the Dwarves | Mortal craftsman acknowledged by Gulam | Master of Runic Forging

### Background

Balder is the current king of the Dwarves, and the only mortal craftsman in ten thousand years personally acknowledged by Gulam, the Mountain King — Gulam once carved a rune into a warhammer Balder forged: "Not bad." — for Gulam, that is the highest praise.

On the invasion, Balder stays neutral — his stance is "whoever gives us ore, we give weapons to." But privately, he sympathizes with the humans' plight — the Dwarves' own history holds memories of being oppressed by stronger races.

### Appearance

A typical Dwarf — stocky, bearded, arms thick as others' thighs. His beard is iron-grey, tied in three braids with gold rings. His right hand's index and middle fingers were melted off in a high-heat forging — now replaced by two mithril prosthetics, which unexpectedly grant even greater precision. He never wears armor — "My skin is the best armor." (Not entirely true — beneath his skin run Gulam's protective runes.)

---

## White-Scale Prophet "Bai" (Bai)

> Eldest of the White Serpent Tribe | 460 years old | Master of prophecy and antidotes

### Background

Bai is the longest-lived member in the history of the White Serpent Tribe. When he was born, Kotal had not yet become the Wolf God. He has lived so long — so long that he is no longer sure which memories are real and which are prophecies he dreamed.

His prophecies are never a complete sentence — always fragments: an image, a sound, a scent. The reason the White Serpent Tribe has maintained neutrality under Beastman pressure is largely Bai's presence — the Beastmen dare not lightly move against a race that can see fragments of the future.

### Appearance

A snow-white giant python, over twenty meters long. Its scales are translucent white — light passing through refracts faint rainbows. Its eyes are milky white (blind for over two centuries — it now perceives the world through vibration and scent). It nearly always coils within the White Serpent Tribe's shrine — not unwilling to move, but too old to.

# Beastman Six Clans

## Bloodfang "Gore Throatcutter" (Gore Throatcutter)

> Strongest warrior of the Werewolf Clan | Kotal's right hand | Invasion vanguard commander

### Background

Gore is the only mortal in the Werewolf Clan to fight Kotal (the Wolf God) to a draw three times in hunting trials — of course, Kotal was not using his full power. But to make Kotal serious enough to wield part of his divine power is already enough to make Gore a clan legend.

Due to the Rift's law restrictions, demigod-level beings cannot yet cross over — Lawful Creep needs time before a Rift can admit individuals strong enough. Gore can only command the vanguard from the other side of the Rift, relaying orders through low-ranking officers. This feeling of "seeing the prey but unable to bite" keeps him at the edge of irritability every day. His only knowledge of humans comes from scout reports: "Too weak — but they adapt fast. Reminds me of when we were young." — he can't wait to confirm it with his own eyes.

### Appearance

Full werewolf form — over three meters standing straight. Fur is dark grey, streaked with silver-white (not aging white fur — Kotal's moonlight blessing). The three claws on his right hand are metallic — the originals were bitten off by prey in a hunt, and Gore forged replacements from meteoric iron. His left eye is blind, but he says "after losing an eye, the smell got better."

## Ironback "Ursa Greatbear" (Ursa Greatbear)

> High Priestess of the Bearman Clan | Gulam's mortal mouthpiece

### Background

Ursa is not a combat-type demigod — she is the high priestess of the Bearman Clan, Gulam's direct mortal mouthpiece. The reason she stepped into the demigod rank is not through killing — but because she successfully deciphered a hidden clause in Gulam's contract, a part even Gulam himself had nearly forgotten. This discovery could change the entire course of the invasion — if he has not already told the other clan chief-gods.

### Appearance

An aged she-bearman, not as huge as a warrior — more like a steady mountain. Fur is brown-grey. Around her neck hangs a necklace of rune stones — each blessed by Gulam personally. Her eyes are always half-closed — not from tiredness, but because she is simultaneously processing information from Gulam. She speaks very slowly, but every word carries weight.

## Thousand-Blade "Mira Shadowless" (Mira Shadowless)

> Legendary assassin of the Catman Clan | Sylvia's adopted daughter | undefeated to this day

### Background

Mira is an orphan Sylvia found in the Shadow Forest — an abandoned catman cub. Sylvia raised her personally and taught her the art of assassination completely. She is the youngest ever recipient of the Catman Clan's "Thousand-Blade" title — which requires completing a thousand assassination missions without a single failure.

She has never participated in a frontal battlefield — she always appears in the most unexpected place, at the most unexpected moment. Her role in the invasion is to handle targets "inconvenient to deal with head-on."

### Appearance

A slender black catman, entirely pitch black — not fur color, but true shadow clinging to her. Even in sunlight, a faint shadow always edges her body. Eyes are golden vertical slits. At her waist hang not one sword — but eleven daggers of different shapes, each for a different purpose. She never speaks — Sylvia is the only one who has heard her voice.

## Serpent-Pupil "Veno Poison-Tongue" (Veno Poison-Tongue)

> Chief toxin master of the Black Serpent Clan | Malkis's experimental assistant | second-in-command of Abyss research

### Background

Veno is Malkis's most trusted subordinate — which is itself an extremely dangerous thing. The reason Malkis trusts him is simple: he is the only one who, after working alongside Malkis for thirty years, emerged intact. His body has been remodeled by toxins to an absurd degree — his blood itself is a poison potent enough to kill a dragon, yet to him it is merely "a bit stimulating."

He is the number-two figure in Malkis's Abyss research. He likely knows more about the Abyss than most gods — except Malkis himself.

### Appearance

A Black Serpent tribesman, upper body human, lower body a dark-green scaled serpent tail. His body is so thin it is nearly all skeleton — the side effect of toxins keeps his weight permanently below forty kilograms. Eyes are dark green, pupils vertical slits. His fingers are black — not gloves, but permanent staining from long-term toxin contact. He always speaks with a faint smile — regardless of content.

## Wind-Speaker "Gale Wingsong" (Gale Wingsong)

> Strongest prophet of the Eagleman Clan | Zephyros's elder brother

### Background

Gale is Zephyros's blood brother. At their birth, the sky's prophetic power split in two — half to Zephyros (who sees vague futures), half to Gale (who hears words carried on the wind). Gale's prophecies are more specific than Zephyros's — he can hear complete sentences — but limited to what is happening now, not the future.

He is the only one in the Eagleman Clan who dares contradict Zephyros — not because he is stronger, but because they are brothers.

### Appearance

A silver-grey feathered eagleman, slightly smaller than Zephyros. His left wing has a distinct tear — not battle damage, but congenital. This tear makes him emit a unique wind-whistle in flight, and that sound is itself a language — Gale can "hear" what is happening from the changes in the wind-whistle.

## Phantom-Face "Kyūjū Thousand-Tails" (Kyūjū Thousand-Tails)

> Eldest nine-tailed fox of the Foxman Clan | Iluna's teacher | 375 years old

### Background

Kyūjū is Iluna's teacher — by the time Iluna became the Fox-God, Kyūjū had already lived three hundred years. She watched Iluna grow from a small fox into the clan's master. Now she almost never leaves the Foxman Clan's shrine — not unable to move, but because she feels "there is nothing new outside anymore."

Her illusion has reached a level needing no casting — she only needs to will it, and you cannot see the real her. Her attitude toward Awakeners is "Iluna finds you interesting — so let me see if that's true."

### Appearance

An aged nine-tailed fox, the tips of all nine tails pure white — a mark of extreme age. She wears a kimono woven from moonlight (a gift from Sylvia in her youth — the two goddesses shared a complicated friendship). Her eyes are forever curved in a smile — but you never know what that smile means.

# Nature Realm

## Moonchant "Seran Ancientleaf" (Seran Ancientleaf)

> Grand Druid of the Elves | Guardian of the World Tree | the only one permitted by Yggdra to meditate at the World Tree's roots

### Background

Seran is the Elves' Grand Druid. No one knows his age — the most conservative estimate is eight hundred years. In his youth he once climbed to the World Tree's fifth layer (already the highest record among mortals), and while meditating on a branch heard Yggdra's voice — not words, but a melody. He has been unable to reproduce that melody to this day, but every time he hears wind through the leaves, he stops — "The old man is speaking."

He is the main force keeping the Elves neutral — not out of fear of the Beastmen, but because Yggdra has not spoken to demand participation. As long as Yggdra does not speak, the Elves will not move.

### Appearance

A male elf of indeterminate age — looks like forty, looks like four hundred. Hair is silver interwoven with vivid green (a mark of resonance with the World Tree). Wears the simplest robe woven from leaves — the leaves' color shifts automatically each season. Ears are longer than ordinary elves' — said to have stretched from years of listening to the World Tree's "melody."

## Shadow Queen "Vysra Duskblade" (Vysra Duskblade)

> Ruler of the Dark Elves | strongest user of shadow magic

### Background

Vysra has ruled the Dark Elves for over three hundred years — for a race known for power struggles, maintaining three hundred years of rule is itself a miracle. Her method is simple: every fifty years she opens a "Throne Challenge" — any Dark Elf may challenge her in public. Each time she wins. Each time she lets the challenger go — "Killing the challenger only makes the next one more careful. Let them go, and they tell others: it cannot be won."

### Appearance

A dark-purple-skinned Dark Elf woman, silver-white long hair that glows in the dark. Eyes are pure red — the result of long immersion in shadow magic. Wears armor formed of shadow itself — not physical defense, but causing enemies' attacks to "veer" off course. She rarely leaves the Dark Elves' underground city — not photophobic, but because she feels "sunlight is too noisy."

## Ancient Tree "Oak Thousand-Rings" (Oak Thousand-Rings)

> Oldest Treant | among the World Tree's first seeds | at least 1,200 years old

### Background

Oak is one of the first seeds borne by the World Tree. When Yggdra let the World Tree's seeds scatter across the continent, Oak was the first to take root. He has witnessed everything that happened on this land — from the first human tribe's campfire, to the Beastman clans' first step out of the forest, to the World Heart's capture.

He never participates in war. His reason is simple: "I've seen wars bigger than this. That war turned a forest into desert. Now you're doing it again." (He refers to the Ancient Battlefield Desert — that war happened when Oak was still a young tree.)

### Appearance

A tree beyond description as "large" — his canopy covers nearly a square kilometer of land. His trunk is so thick it takes twenty people hand-in-hand to encircle. His bark bears over a thousand growth rings — look closely, and each ring records not a year, but a scene from his memory. He rarely appears in human form — he feels "human form is too uncomfortable; leaves can't photosynthesize."

## Galehoof "Ironhoof Thunderrun" (Ironhoof Thunderrun)

> Great Khan of the Centaurs | fastest being on the plains

### Background

Ironhoof is the great khan of the Centaur tribes. In Centaur culture, "khan" is not hereditary — it is won. In the decennial Ten-Thousand-Gallop Grand Race, the one who runs to the front becomes the next khan. Ironhoof has won four times. Not because he is fastest — but because he understands how to use the wind.

He can run ahead of the wind — when the wind catches up, it pushes him faster forward. This is not supernatural power — it is fifty years spent learning the wind's habits.

### Appearance

A chestnut-furred centaur, the muscles of his four legs carved like a knife. His upper body is a middle-aged male torso (about a forty-year-old human appearance), bronze skin, hair braided into dozens of small plaits — each plait's end tied with an ornament from a different tribe. His weapon is not bow or spear — but a flagpole, bearing the banners of all Centaur tribes.

# Sea Factions

## Coral Queen "Marina Deepsong" (Marina Deepsong)

> Queen of the Merfolk | Guardian of the Inner Sea | her song can calm storms

### Background

Marina was chosen as queen from the moment of her birth — because her song was different. Other Merfolk's songs bewitch sailors and navigators, but Marina's song can calm storms. In the "Wrath of the Sea" disaster a century ago — a sudden super-storm that nearly destroyed half the Merfolk coral city — Marina swam alone to the surface and sang through the entire night at the storm's fiercest center. The storm stopped at dawn. The next day, she became queen.

### Appearance

Upper body a beautiful woman, lower body a silver-blue fish tail — scales show a gradient from deep blue to pearl white under different light. Hair is seaweed-like dark green, drifting in water like a flowing forest. Around her neck hangs a coral necklace — each coral a relic of a past queen. Her voice is not drowned even underwater — because it does not travel through water, but sounds directly in your mind.

---

## Raging Wave "Deepfin Wavebreaker" (Deepfin Wavebreaker)

> Strongest warrior of the Fishmen | Guardian of the Outer Sea | said to have once fought the Dragons at sea

### Background

Deepfin is the largest individual in Fishman history — nearly four meters long, a giant among Fishmen. His legend comes from an unwitnessed battle: a hundred and twenty years ago, a young dragon hunting in the Outer Sea strayed into the Fishmen's spawning grounds. Deepfin met that dragon alone, struggling in the deep sea for a full day. The dragon finally fled back to Dragon Isle — bearing shards of Deepfin's trident in its body.

Angerwide personally apologized to the Fishmen — and confined that dragon to Dragon Isle for fifty years.

### Appearance

Typical fishman form — upper body fish, lower body two legs, but twice the size of a normal fishman. Skin is deep-sea blue, dorsal fin bright red (the highest honor mark of the clan, earned in challenge). Weapon is a trident — the shaft forged from Dragon Isle lava, the tip taken from his own fish bone. His left eye bears a scar running from the eye socket to the gill — left from that battle with the dragon.

# Dragons

## Ancient Dragon "Molten-Scale Earthfire" (Molten-Scale Earthfire)

> Oldest dragon beneath Angerwide | Dragon elder | at least 2,000 years old

### Background

Molten-Scale is the oldest individual among the Dragons besides Angerwide. When he was born, Ignaros had not yet sunk too deep into sleep — he vaguely remembers the warmth and searing pain of being swept by his mother's tail.

He is the Dragons' elder — not their ruler (Angerwide is), but all major Dragon decisions consult his opinion first. His age and wisdom place enormous pressure on Angerwide — not because Molten-Scale challenges him, but because Molten-Scale is too much like his mother. Every time Angerwide looks into Molten-Scale's eyes, he is reminded of the mother he never saw awake.

### Appearance

Molten-Scale is an extremely ancient red dragon, scales the dark red of cooled magma — edges black, as if burning from within. His size is two-thirds Angerwide's — but to mortals, this is already beyond words. His horns are straight forward (unlike Angerwide's curved ones — because he never suppressed his mother's heat deep in the volcano). His dragon wings bear dozens of holes — not battle damage, but time. Each tear records a century of flight.
