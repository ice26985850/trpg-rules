# Collector's Final Game (S19)
> Season: Chapter 5 "Godfall Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Recommended Level**: Lv.93-100
- **Difficulty**: Extreme
- **Expected Length**: 3-5 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end
## Core Conflict

Collector finally appears. He says: "I have been watching your journey, and you — are truly interesting. Now, the final game. Pass it, and I will tell you the way to end all this."

## Main Locations

- Collector's Game Space (an arena in cosmic space)

## Key Scenes

- Collector's Five Game Stages — mimicking the hardest battles you have faced before
- Special Rules in the Game — Collector freely alters the laws
- The Final Conversation — Collector tells you the truth of the World Heart

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 25000, Truth of the World Heart, Collector's Memento (special Accessory)

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Replica Bosses adjusted per solo rules
