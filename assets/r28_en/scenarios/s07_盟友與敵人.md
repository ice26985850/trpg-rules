# Allies and Enemies (S7)
> Season: Chapter 2 "Resistance Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.28-36
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.
## Core Conflict

Venture deep into the Otherworld Human Kingdom to secure their formal alliance. At the same time, discover that the clans are not united — the Bearman Clan has reservations about the invasion plan.
