# Clan Intrigue (S10)
> Season: Chapter 3 "Expedition Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.46-53
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.
## Core Conflict

The players learn of the internal conflict between Malkis (Black Serpent Chief-God) and Zephyros (Eagleman Chief-God). Malkis is willing to provide intelligence — but at a price.

- Discover Iluna (Fox-God) maneuvering between both sides — which side is she really on?
