# Total War (S13)
> Season: Chapter 4 — "Final Battle Chapter"
**Basic Info**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.61-68
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Expected Length**: 3-5 game sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

## Core Conflict

The fourth wave of invasion begins. A-Rank Rifts open simultaneously in five major cities across the globe. The players must choose a battlefield — or rush between multiple battlefields.

## Main Location

- Tokyo, New York, London, Shanghai, São Paulo (choose one of five, or rotate)

## Key Scenes

- Global Defense Coordination Conference — guild masters assemble / Defensive battle of the chosen city / Discovery that someone is coordinating behind the rifts — the Clan Chief-God commands in person

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 12000, Reputation with each guild +500, Orange · Legendary Equipment

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Focus on defending a single city
