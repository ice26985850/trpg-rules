# Expedition Prep (S5)
> Season: Season 2 "Resistance Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Recommended Level**: Lv.21-27
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end of the document.
## Core Conflict

The Guild decides to organize its first otherworld expedition. The players must collect three key materials: Stabilized Mana Crystal (E-Rank Rift), Otherworld Alloy (abandoned factory district), and Crossing Guidance Rune (to be negotiated from the Dwarves).

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 2000, Crossing Qualification Certificate, D-Rank Advanced Equipment

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Monsters on each route halved
