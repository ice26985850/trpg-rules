# Abyssal Expedition (S9)
> Season: Chapter 3 "Expedition Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.41-48
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.
## Core Conflict

Press deep into the Beastman Forest and explore the heart of clan territory. Discover that the Beastmen are performing some kind of ritual at the World Heart — accelerating the Creeping Law Erosion.
