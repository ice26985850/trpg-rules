# The Betraying Fox-God (S18)
> Season: Chapter 5 "Godfall Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Recommended Level**: Lv.87-94
- **Difficulty**: Extreme
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end
## Core Conflict

Iluna (Foxman Chief-God) finally reveals her true colors — she has been playing both sides from the very start. Now she has set up the ultimate mirage trial at the center of the God-Realm, claiming only those who pass are worthy to advance.

## Main Locations

- Iluna's Mirage Labyrinth (City of a Thousand Faces)

## Key Scenes

- City of a Thousand Faces — a perpetually shifting city where every district is a different illusion
- Iluna's Three Questions — not combat, but dialogue. Answer wrong and you return to the start.
- Truth Revealed — Iluna has actually been protecting humanity all along (in her own way)

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 18000, Iluna's Blessing (permanent immunity to illusions), Key of a Thousand Faces

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Illusion Difficulty Class (DC) −20
