# Guild Summons (S2)
> Season: Season 1 "Awakening Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Recommended Level**: Lv.3-8
- **Difficulty**: Simple
- **Expected Length**: 1-2 sessions (4-8 hours)
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end of the document.
## Core Conflict

Players are recruited by the Guild (Tower of Dawn) and accept their first formal commission—to investigate anomalous energy readings in Tokyo's Bayside district.

## Main Locations

- **Tower of Dawn**: The Guild's Tokyo headquarters, originally a 40-story office building, now converted into an Awakener base.
- **Bayside Industrial Zone**: An abandoned factory district where a D-Rank Rift is forming.

## Key NPCs

- **Chairman Kurosaki**: Chairman of the Tower of Dawn, a B-Rank Awakener. Assigns the players their commission.
- **Intelligence Officer Yamada**: Responsible for analyzing rift data and providing the mission briefing.

## Key Scenes

- First impressions of the Guild hall—Awakeners of all ranks coming and going
- Industrial zone infiltration—learning Stealth and recon checks
- Preventing the rift from fully opening—a timed mission
- Guild internal faction politics surface for the first time (setup for S3-S4)

## Enemy List

- **Gargoyle** (Quantity 2-3 | Lv.5, flying, high recon difficulty)
- **Rift Vanguard Beast** (Quantity 1 | Lv.8, precursor to a D-Rank Boss)

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 400, D-Rank Weapon Crate (Green · Uncommon) ×1, Guild Reputation +50

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: Gargoyle reduced to 1, Vanguard Beast Hit Points (HP) −20%
