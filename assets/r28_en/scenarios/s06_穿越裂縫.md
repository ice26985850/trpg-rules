# Through the Rift (S6)
> Season: Chapter 2 "Resistance Chapter"
**Basic Information**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.25-32
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 2-3 sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end.
## Core Conflict

Players cross the Rift for the first time into the otherworld. At the edge of the Beastman Forest, they encounter an Otherworld Human patrol — and an ambush by the Catman Clan.

## Rewards

Experience Points (EXP) 2500, Otherworld Map (partial), Otherworld Human Favor +20

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: ambush enemies reduced to 1-2.
