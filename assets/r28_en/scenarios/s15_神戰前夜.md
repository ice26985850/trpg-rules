# Eve of the Godwar (S15)
> Season: Chapter 4 — "Final Battle Chapter"
**Basic Info**
- **Suggested Level**: Lv.71-77
- **Difficulty**: Normal
- **Expected Length**: 1-2 game sessions
- **Play Mode**: Default multiplayer mode (1 GM + multiple players); also supports Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), see "Solo Mode Adjustments" at the end

## Core Conflict

The calm before the fifth wave. The players may choose to upgrade equipment, recruit allies, or delve deeper into unexplored Neutral Race territories. This is the final preparation before the storm.

## Main Location

- Player's choice (may return to any previously visited location)

## Key Scenes

- Equipment forging — using Dragon Scale and Otherworld materials to craft Red · Mythic equipment
- Diplomatic wrap-up — turning Neutral Races into allies
- Personal story — looking back on the journey from Lv.1 to now

## Rewards

Varies by activity (mainly equipment upgrades and diplomatic outcomes)

## Solo Mode Adjustments

When played in Solo Mode (1 GM + 1 player), apply the following adjustments: All diplomatic Difficulty Class (DC) −15
