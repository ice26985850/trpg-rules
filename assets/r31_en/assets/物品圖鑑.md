# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Complete Catalog of the 22 Pathways' Sequence Items v1.0

> **Contents:** Each pathway's exclusive sequence items — ritual items, pathway enhancement items, pathway relics, potion auxiliary materials, and pathway-associated Sealed Artifact prototypes
> **Purpose:** GM reward design, black market trade, character personalization equipment, and pathway side plots

---

## Item Type Descriptions

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Ritual Item** | Required or auxiliary item for a Promotion Ritual | Key to the Spirit World Gate (Apprentice Pathway) |
| **Pathway Enhancement** | Temporarily enhances pathway abilities after use | Seer Essential Oil (Divination check +10) |
| **Pathway Relic** | Remnant of a deceased Beyonder of that pathway, may contain residual will | Fallen Magician's Paper Figure |
| **Potion Auxiliary** | Auxiliary material for a specific pathway's potion, determines potion quality | Core of the Deep (Sailor Pathway) |
| **Sealed Artifact Prototype** | A pathway item that can be crafted into a Sealed Artifact | Marionettist's Last Marionette |
