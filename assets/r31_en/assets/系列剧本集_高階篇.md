# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Scenario Collection: Advanced v2.0 (Novel-Faithful Edition)

> **Scope:** Sequence 5 → the endgame gambit on the eve of the doomsday
> **Core premise:** The players need not become gods — they only need to hold that slate fragment. It is the survival manual the Ancient Sun God left for humanity, and the key every faction wants to seize or destroy.
> **Reference rules:** Player Rulebook, GM Rulebook, Complete Pathway Sequences, Solo Rules Supplement (including NPC Companion system, CR scaling)
> **Prologue:** At the end of Scenario 4, "The Mechanical Heart," the players obtained a decoded **Blasphemy Slate fragment** from Dr. Blackwood — it records the complete formulas of all 22 Pathways, the truth of the Ancient Sun God's fall, and a doomsday warning: **the Outer Deities will breach the barrier within decades.**

---

# Introduction: What Is "Qualification"?

In the first four scenarios, the players climbed from Sequence 9 to Sequence 5. In the world of the original *Lord of the Mysteries*, Sequence 5 is only a starting point — the real board lies at the level of Angels and True Gods.

But you need not become Sequence 0 to take part in this gambit. **The Blasphemy Slate fragment grants you a special kind of qualification: you know the doomsday is counting down, you know where the slate fragments are scattered, you know ancient secrets even some Angels do not.** This knowledge makes you a variable no faction can ignore.

**The arc of the four new scenarios:**

| Scenario | Sequence Range | Gambit Tier | Player Position |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Shadow of the Angel | 5→4 | Angel-level shadow war | Slate-Bearer — the Angel needs your fragment, and you need Its protection |
| Beneath the Rift | 4→3 | Barrier Defense | The only one who can read the sealing ritual |
| Eve of the Apocalypse | 3→1 | Multi-faction gambit | Hunter and prey of the slate fragment |
| The Final Slate | Endgame | Doomsday choice | The one holding humanity's last card |

# Scenario 5: Shadow of the Angel

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 5 → Sequence 4 | **Duration:** 4–6 sessions
**Recommended Party Size:** Team of 3–5, or Solo + 2 Sequence 4–5 NPC Companions
**Continuation:** About 2–3 weeks (in-game time) after the ending of "The Mechanical Heart"

## Background

In the third week after obtaining the slate fragment, you begin to notice something is wrong.

Your shadow occasionally moves on its own without wind. The reflection in the mirror lags half a beat before turning its head. When you wake at midnight, there is a faint ozone smell in the air by your bed — the residual trace of an Angel-level presence passing by. You know you are being watched, but the watcher never shows.

Until that night. Walking home from the Drowned Man Tavern, the street suddenly becomes three times longer than usual. The streetlamps on both sides go out one by one, leaving only the one above your head. You hear footsteps behind you — but when you turn, there is nothing.

Then the air before you begins to fold. A formless presence unfolds before her — not humanoid, but a constantly shifting geometric shape woven of dark-purple starlight. Her words are written directly into your mind, bypassing the ears:

**"You hold that slate fragment. In three days, someone will come to take it — not a human, but a Sequence 3 Saint. You will die. Unless — you accept my protection."**

She is a Sequence 2 Angel of the Church of the Evernight — a "Servant of Concealment." She is hunting a high-sequence traitor who escaped from a seal, and that traitor is collecting slate fragments. She needs your fragment as "bait" to locate the traitor. In exchange — she will protect you in combat, and provide "Concealment" protection when you advance to Sequence 4 (reduced Loss-of-Control risk).

**This is not friendship. This is a transaction.** An Angel does not befriend you — an Angel negotiates terms with you. She makes it clear: if you refuse, she will take the slate fragment; you lose it but will not die. If you accept — you step into the Angel-level shadow war.

## Hooks (optional, aside from the Angel's visit)

1. **Omen Dream**: For three consecutive nights you dream the same image — a stone stele slowly cracking in the dark, fragments flying in all directions. Spirituality + Divination (hard): reveals each fragment fell into different hands, and one is moving toward your Gray Mist City.
2. **Assassination**: One night, three Sequence 6 assassins break into your residence. After repelling them, you find they belong to the Twilight Hermit Order — Adam's organization. They leave a message: "The slate fragment does not belong to mortals."
3. **Messenger**: A Spirit World messenger (a glowing orb) appears in your room, leaving a verbal message: "In three days, when the bell tolls, come to the abandoned clock tower in the Iron Bridge District. Bring the slate. If you do not come — I will find you. —G"

## Act 1: The Board of the Shadow War

The Servant of Concealment gave you a list — three factions she is tracking, all searching for the same slate fragment:

**Clue Point 1 — Holy Wind Cathedral Underground Archive**

The Servant of Concealment sent authorization to the Nighthawks in your name. You gain access to the church's confidential archives.

- Mind + Mysticism (hard): Read the literature about the Blasphemy Slate. Success → learn: the Blasphemy Slate was created by the Ancient Sun God in the Third Epoch; after His fall the slate was shattered into "at least nine known fragments." Two are held by the Seven Churches (each church guards one), at least three are in the hands of hidden organizations, and the rest are unaccounted for. The complete contents of the slate are said to include "the exact time of the doomsday countdown" and "the method to oppose the Outer Deities."
- Risk: Reading highly confidential literature → Mind check (routine); failure causes LoC +1d3.

**Clue Point 2 — Abandoned Lighthouse in the Ash Harbor District**

The traitor Angel once briefly stayed here. You can find the traces It left:

- Spirituality + Spirit Sight (hard): Perceive residual spirituality at the top of the lighthouse — the traitor's spiritual scent is "burning stars," mixed with the "rotten sweetness" of Outer Deity power. This proves the traitor has been corrupted by an Outer Deity (suspected to be the Desire Mother Tree).
- Agility + Tracking (routine): Find a scorched piece of fabric on the floor — the traitor lost control here while being corrupted, burning off Its clothing. The fabric bears a spiritual coordinate — pointing to the ancient ruins beneath the Gear District.

**Clue Point 3 — Gear District Underground Ruins**

The traitor's hideout — a Third Epoch underground temple ruin. This was once a sanctuary of the Ancient Sun God; the slate fragment has a "resonance" reaction to the spiritual environment here.

- Spirituality + Ritual (hard): Use your slate fragment as a detector to track the traitor's spiritual trail. Success → discover the traitor hiding in the ruins' deep "Altar Chamber." It is performing some ritual with Its own slate fragment — trying to establish a deeper connection with the Outer Deity.
- Risk: Spiritual pollution in the ruins (the resentment left by the Ancient Sun God's fall) → LoC +1 for every 15 minutes spent here.

## Act 2: The Fragment Contest

The Servant of Concealment has placed a "Mark of Concealment" on your slate fragment — once the traitor touches the fragment, Its location is locked and the Angel arrives instantly. What you need to do now: **survive and bring the fragment before the traitor.**

But two other factions are closing in:

- **Twilight Hermit Order**: Sent three Sequence 5 executors. They do not rob the fragment — they want to "recover" all fragments and lock them in Adam's secret chamber. They consider mortals holding slate fragments an "unstable factor."
- **Desire Mother Tree's Believers**: A squad of mutated Beyonders corrupted by Outer Deity power. They want to destroy the slate fragment — because it records the method to oppose the Outer Deities.

**Scene 1 — Ambush at the Ruins Perimeter**

On the way to the Altar Chamber, you meet the Twilight Hermit Order's executors.

**Twilight Hermit Order Executor** | Spectator Pathway Sequence 5 "Dreamwalker" ×1 + Reader Pathway Sequence 6 "Polymath" ×2 | Team CR: 8 (Solo: 3)

- Dreamwalker attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 55 / Mind (MND) 60 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 40 / Charisma (CHA) 50
- HP: 85 / SP: 170 / LoCT: 130
- Powers: Dream Traction (2 AP / 10 SP / 20m / target enters half-dream state — next round's action must first pass a Mind check, failure means the action fails), Mental Deterrence (1 AP / 5 SP / Mind opposed, failure stuns 1 round)
- Tactics: They do not kill — they try to incapacitate you, then take the fragment. After you fall unconscious, they leave a Twilight Hermit Order mark and an "explanation letter" on the spot: "For the safety of humanity. Please understand."
- **Optional routes:**
  - Frontal combat (repel them)
  - Persuade them (Charisma + Negotiation, extreme): prove you are more fit than Adam to guard the fragment — because you are "using" it rather than "sealing" it.
  - Deceive them (Charisma + Deception, hard): lie that the fragment was already taken by the traitor, leading them to the traitor.

**Scene 2 — Ambush by the Outer Deity Believers**

At the Altar Chamber entrance, the Desire Mother Tree's believers have long lain in wait.

**Corrupted Beyonder** | Prisoner Pathway Sequence 5 "Puppet" ×2 (corrupted by the Desire Mother Tree) | Team CR: 7 (Solo: 2.5)

- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 45 / Mind (MND) 25 (severely corrupted) / Body (BOD) 50 / Agility (AGI) 45 / Charisma (CHA) 10
- HP: 115 / SP: 140 (auto-consumes 3 per round)
- Powers: Touch of Desire (1 AP / 5 SP / melee / 3d6 Dark + Outer Deity pollution — target LoC +1d3), Puppet Control (2 AP / 10 SP / control an ordinary NPC to attack the player)
- Appearance: Bark-like textures not belonging to humans grow on their skin. Their eyes have no pupils — only dark-red light.
- Weakness: Spiritual attacks have Advantage (Outer Deity pollution weakens their spiritual defense).
- Special drop: From them you can obtain a small vial of "Desire Mother Tree sap" (Tier 3 Sealed Artifact) — can be used to track the spiritual traces of other Outer Deity believers.

**Scene 3 — Altar Chamber: Confrontation with the Traitor**

The traitor Angel — once a Warrior Pathway Sequence 3 "Silver Knight," now corrupted by the Desire Mother Tree into a half-Outer-Deity construct.

**Fallen Silver Knight** | Warrior Pathway Sequence 3 (corrupted state) | Team CR: 12 (Solo: 4)

- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 40 / Mind (MND) 30 (severely corrupted) / Body (BOD) 75 / Agility (AGI) 60 / Charisma (CHA) 5
- HP: 165 / SP: 120 (but due to corruption, recovers 10 HP per round — only spiritual attacks can stop the recovery)
- LoCT: 70 (corruption greatly lowers the Loss-of-Control Threshold — every use of a power may trigger loss of control)
- Powers:
  - Corrupted Slash (1 AP / 5 SP / melee / 3d10+15 / Dark + physical): each hit → target LoC +1d3
  - Outer Deity Shield (reaction / 10 SP): blocks one attack, but own LoC +5
  - Desire Shriek (2 AP / 15 SP / whole area / Mind opposed): failure stuns 1 round + LoC +2
  - Dawn Blade (3 AP / all remaining SP / 50m line / 4d12+20 Light): His remaining Warrior Pathway power — the last echo of him as a "Silver Knight." Using it causes own LoC +15.
- Weakness:
  - The Third Epoch "Purification Rune" still remains on his Altar Chamber floor — can be activated with Spirituality + Ritual (hard), causing him 3d6 Light damage per round.
  - If you approach him holding the slate fragment, the fragment resonates — temporarily weakening his Outer Deity pollution (all checks −10, lasts 2 rounds).
- **Tactical turn**: When HP drops to 30%, he kneels — for an instant, the red light in his eyes vanishes, and you see a pair of weary gray eyes. In his original voice he says:
  > "Kill me. While I can still call myself 'me.' Kill me — then take my slate fragment. It records — where the next fragment lies. Go. Quick — He is watching me —"

This is what you wanted. The Angel will descend in this instant.

## Act 3: The Servant of Concealment's Judgment

The Servant of Concealment descends — the Altar Chamber walls are covered in dark-purple starlight. She does not manifest her full Mythical Form (for that would make all mortals present lose control), but the silhouette She forms from three meters of shadow is enough to let you feel the Angel's absolute status.

**Mind + Will check (hard):** Hold onto yourself before the Angel. Failure → LoC +2d6, and unable to act for 1 round.

She binds the Fallen Silver Knight with starlight. Then She turns to look at you — with that non-human gaze, like looking at an interesting species.

> "You did better than I expected. Mortal — what is your name?" (This is the first time She asks your name.)

She keeps her promise: the Fallen Silver Knight's slate fragment goes to you. She only takes the Fallen Silver Knight's Beyonder Characteristic — that was her original task in hunting the traitor. Before leaving, She leaves a sentence:

> "While you are within the fragment's resonance range — I can sense you. If you want to survive the next fragment resonance — call My name."

She gives you a portion of Her Honorific Name. This is extremely high recognition from an Angel to a mortal — but it also means you are formally drawn into the high-level vision of the Church of the Evernight. You are no longer a pawn — you are a card.

# Scenario 6: Beneath the Rift

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 4 → Sequence 3 | **Duration:** 5–7 sessions
**Recommended Party Size:** Team of 3–5, or Solo + 2 Sequence 4–5 NPC Companions

## Background

The second slate fragment finishes decoding. Its contents are unsettling:

> "Complete map of barrier weak points — seven in total. The nearest lies at the bottom of an unnamed island 40 nautical miles off Gray Mist City's waters. The next 'tidal weak period' is expected within the next two months. Then the barrier will briefly crack open — if not sealed, the Outer Deity's consciousness can pollute all Beyonders within a hundred miles through the crack."

You report this intel to the Servant of Concealment. Her reply is brief:

"All the other churches have been notified. In three days, the Seven Churches will each send a Sequence 2 Angel to the unnamed island — the largest heavenly alliance operation since the Fourth Epoch. You are coming too."

"—Why?"

"Because only the slate fragment in your hand records the complete sealing ritual. Without that ritual, no number of Angels can seal the crack."

"—Then why don't the Angels just take the fragment from me?"

"Because the slate fragment chooses its bearer. You were not chosen by it — you decoded it. The Ancient Sun God's slate is not readable by just anyone. You read it — now you and it are bound. An Angel could kill you and take the fragment, but the fragment will self-seal after your death. No one wants to risk that."

So you are "invited." Not to sit at the round table — but to stand behind the battlefield, performing the sealing ritual, while the Angels buy you time on the perimeter.

## Act 1: The Unnamed Island

**Scene: Arrival at the Unnamed Island**

The unnamed island is a volcanic island; at its center is a crater about 200 meters across. The crater has cooled — but at the bottom is not rock, but a translucent membrane. Below the membrane — you can see colors that do not belong to this world flowing. That is the barrier between the Spirit World and the material world. The membrane is thinning.

Seven Angels appear on the island in human form — not a meeting, but a military deployment. Their conversation is brief and efficient. No one introduces themselves. No one asks your name. They only confirm the ritual's timing, then take their positions.

But you can feel it — one of the Angels is wrong. Not from your intuition. From the slate fragment in your hand — it is slightly warm, as if warning you.

**Slate Fragment Warning Mechanism (GM mechanic):** In this scenario, when the player approaches an Outer-Deity-polluted existence, the slate fragment warms slightly (a physical sensation). The intensity of the warmth corresponds to the degree of pollution — faint warmth (low pollution), obvious warmth (moderate), burning hot (high, i.e., the traitor). This is the slate fragment's additional function as an "Outer Deity detector."

## Act 2: The Seven Angels

The GM must play seven Angels, each with a different personality and task. The players can interact with the Angels to gather intel — but the Angels will not treat you as an equal. At most they will "tolerate" your presence.

| Angel | Church / Faction | Pathway | Task | Attitude | Suspicion |
|------|----------|------|------|------|--------|
| **Servant of Concealment** (F) | Church of the Evernight | Sleepless Sequence 2 | Cover — deploys a "Concealment Domain" around the barrier crack to suppress the Outer Deity's consciousness | You know her. She maintains "transactional" respect for you | Lowest |
| **Calamity** (M) | Church of the Storm | Sailor Sequence 2 | Perimeter alert — seals the sea within 10 nautical miles of the island with a storm | Impatient, contemptuous of mortals | Low |
| **Lightseeker** (M) | Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun | Bard Sequence 2 | Main attacker — scorches the Outer Deity's consciousness with "Eternal Light" the instant the crack opens | Silent, focused, as if suppressing something | Medium (slate fragment warms slightly near him) |
| **Glory** (F) | Church of the God of War | Warrior Sequence 2 | Front-line defense — uses Mythical Form to stand before the crack, absorbing the Outer Deity's first wave of impact | Pragmatic, military bearing | Low |
| **Wisdom Angel** (M) | Church of Knowledge | Reader Sequence 2 | Record — records the barrier crack's structural changes throughout, providing data for future barrier repairs | Cold, academically interested in your slate fragment | Medium |
| **Ferryman** (M) | Spirit Sect (Death Pathway) | Corpse Collector Sequence 2 | Reserve — forcibly closes the crack with "Death Authority" if it runs out of control (at great cost) | Gloomy, speaks fewer than ten sentences | Low |
| **White Angel** (F) | Church of the Steam and Machinery God | Savant Sequence 2 | Technical support — builds the ritual platform with mechanical constructs | Friendly, interested in your difference-engine background | Low |

**Key interaction points:**

- The player can gather clues about "who is the traitor" by talking with the Angels. Each Angel has 2–3 unique lines of dialogue.
- The slate fragment's warmth is the core detection method — but every approach to an Angel draws the Angel's attention ("Your slate is glowing, did you know?").
- Mind + Observation (extreme): notice Lightseeker, when discussing the crack, unconsciously strokes his chest with his left hand — as if suppressing something.
- Mind + Mysticism (legendary): discern the "heterogeneity" from Lightseeker's spiritual aura — his Eternal Light is mixed with something not of the Sun Pathway.

**Truth:** Lightseeker is not the traitor — he is a victim. In the previous barrier-crack incident he was struck by a Desire Mother Tree fragment; the pollution within him is slowly spreading. He is hiding it — because once exposed, the other Angels would seal him immediately. His "suspicious" behavior is him using his own power to suppress the pollution within. The slate fragment warms near him not because he is the traitor — but because of the Outer Deity fragment inside him.

## Act 3: Beneath the Rift

**Scene 1 — The Sealing Ritual**

The crack opens on schedule. The barrier membrane tears — a gap about 10 meters wide appears. On the other side of the gap is not the Spirit World — it is a kind of "void" you have never seen. Not darkness — darkness is also a color. It is a negation of existence.

The slate fragment ritual you must perform has three stages:

1. **Stage 1: Locate** (Spirituality + Ritual, hard): Use the slate fragment to fix the crack's exact coordinates. Failure → ritual prep time extends; the Angels must endure one more round of the Outer Deity's will assault.
2. **Stage 2: Seal** (Mind + Will, extreme): Use the slate fragment to guide the seven Angels' power to converge at the crack. Failure → power scatters, crack expands by 5 meters.
3. **Stage 3: Lock** (Spirituality + Ritual, legendary −40): Use the slate fragment to "write" the seal into the barrier structure — permanently patching this weak point. Failure → the crack can be temporarily sealed, but will reopen within a year.

**Scene 1.5 — the "It" in the Crack**

During the ritual — the "It" on the other side of the crack notices you. Not an attack. A "gaze." The feeling of being gazed upon by an Outer Deity is described by the GM as: "Your consciousness suddenly becomes as smooth as ice — something is swimming beneath the ice; you cannot see Its shape, but you can feel Its presence slide across the surface of your thoughts, reading your memories word by word — from your earliest recollection to every second just past."

**Mind + Will (extreme, −20):** Resist the Outer Deity's conscious invasion. Failure → a memory is "stolen" — the GM chooses one of the player's memories (could be a skill, knowledge, or relationship), and checks related to that memory gain Disadvantage in the next scene. Critical Failure → the Outer Deity leaves a "door" in your consciousness — your Loss-of-Control Threshold permanently −15 (representing the Outer Deity can more easily influence you in the future through this door).

**Scene 2 — Lightseeker's Fall**

Halfway through the ritual, Lightseeker suddenly kneels. The Desire Mother Tree fragment inside him is "awakened" as the crack approaches. His Eternal Light begins to dim — turning a sickly purple.

The other Angels immediately notice. Calamity reacts fastest: "He is infected. Seal him — now!"

But sealing an infected Angel requires at least three Angels striking simultaneously — which weakens the crack's defense line.

**The moment of choice:**

| Choice | Execution | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| **Prioritize sealing Lightseeker** | Three Angels (Calamity + Glory + Ferryman) join to seal him | The crack's defense line is temporarily weakened — Stage 3 difficulty changes from −40 to −60. But Lightseeker is sealed into a sealing crystal, his life preserved. |
| **Let Lightseeker sacrifice himself** | Lightseeker proposes himself: "Let me plug the crack. The Outer Deity fragment inside me — it can momentarily neutralize the consciousness on the other side." | He walks into the crack — the Outer Deity's consciousness on the other side is confused for 10 minutes (because "its own fragment" suddenly appears before it). Ritual difficulty −20. But Lightseeker — stays on the other side of the crack forever. His last words: "Tell the church — I was not a traitor." |
| **Split up** | Persuade the Angels to let the player handle Lightseeker alone (Charisma + Negotiation, legendary) — use the slate fragment to suppress the pollution within him | The slate fragment can indeed temporarily suppress Outer Deity pollution (proven in Scenario 5). Lightseeker recovers his reason, but his power is greatly weakened — unable to continue participating in the defense. |

## Act 4: Aftershocks

The crack is sealed — no matter which path you chose. But after the ritual ends, a new line of text appears on the slate fragment — as if written in blood:

> "Fragment 3/9. Next fragment's location: Backlund — in the hands of one who should not have awoken."

The Servant of Concealment finds you before you leave:

> "You survived. Stronger than most Sequence 4 people — not in power. In luck." (She pauses) "In the heavens — we have a saying: a mortal who survives their first Angel-level operation is no longer mortal. Welcome to the board."

**Rewards:**

| Condition | Reward |
|------|------|
| Basic completion (all three ritual stages done) | Sequence 3 Promotion Ritual qualification + 60 Acting Progress |
| Stage 3 Critical Success (roll ≤ 5) | The crack is permanently repaired, the barrier structure stronger there than before. Talent "Barrier Mender": +10 to checks opposing Outer Deities |
| Successfully persuade the Angels to split up | Gain the collective respect of Angel-level NPCs — in future Angel-level events, your words will be truly heard |
| Lightseeker's ending (either) | Obtain Lightseeker's keepsake — can summon one "Echo of Eternal Light" (single attack, 8d10 Light, ignores Outer Deity defense) |
| No loss of control throughout the ritual | Talent "Anchor of Will": Advantage on Loss-of-Control checks (roll 2d100 take lower) |
| Slate fragment's new message | Location clue for the next fragment — clear direction for Scenario 7 |

**Loss-of-Control Risk:**

| Risk Point | Value |
|--------|-----|
| Close exposure to the barrier crack | +1d3 / 10 minutes (cumulative) |
| Gazed upon by an Outer Deity | +3d6 (first time) + permanent Threshold −15 (if Critical Failure) |
| Prolonged coexistence with Angels (Angel's Mythical Form pressure) | +1 / hour |
| Sealing ritual failure (any stage) | +1d5 / time |
| Spirit Exhaustion | +2d5 |
| **Total (whole scenario est.)** | **35–65** (higher if gazed upon by an Outer Deity) |

> **GM Note:** This scenario introduces the slate fragment's core new function: **Outer Deity Detector**. In later scenarios, this function can become a key tool for the player to detect hidden threats. Also — pay attention to controlling the Angels' sense of presence. Angels are not NPC companions; they are "roaming natural disasters." Every time the player interacts with an Angel, they should feel the enormous gap in status — their coldness or impatience is itself a kind of pressure.

# Scenario 7: Eve of the Apocalypse

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 3 → Sequence 1 (this scenario spans a large range) | **Duration:** 6–10 sessions
**Recommended Party Size:** Team of 3–5, or Solo + 2 Sequence 3–4 NPC Companions

## Background

The slate fragment's new message points to Backlund. There you will face not one enemy — but a forming configuration.

After the Angel-level operation of "Beneath the Rift," your name has spread among the Seven Churches and major hidden organizations. Not because of your power — but because of the living slate fragment in your hand, and the fact that you survived before the Angels. Some call you the "Slate-Bearer." Some call you the "Variable." Some have already decided — you must die.

When you arrive in Backlund, you find the news of the third slate fragment has leaked. Multiple factions are converging on Backlund:

| Faction | Goal | Method | Attitude toward you |
|------|------|------|-----------|
| **Twilight Hermit Order** | Recover the slate fragment — lock it in Adam's secret chamber | Tracking, pressure, negotiation | "You have proven you are not a threat. But you are not the master either." |
| **Church of the Storm** | Obtain the "method to oppose the Outer Deities" from the slate fragment | Semi-open bidding — "legally" acquire it | Suspicious — they do not trust "uncontrolled Beyonders" |
| **Rose School of Thought** | Destroy the slate fragment (its contents are unfavorable to the Chained) | Assassination, pollution, terror attacks | Hostile — you and your slate must disappear |
| **Desire Mother Tree's Believers** | Seize the slate fragment to stop humanity from finding the method to oppose the Outer Deities | Infiltration, corruption, replacing those around you | Hostile — but They would rather "convert" you than kill you |
| **Aurora Order** | Use the slate fragment to awaken the "True Creator" | Kidnapping, sacrifice | Neutral-leaning-hostile — they think you can become a "vessel" |
| **Your Angel Ally** | Protect you — but only from the shadows (cannot openly intervene in secular faction conflict) | Intel provision, emergency rescue | Cooperative — she needs you alive |

**This is not a structured competition.** No score, no rounds, no referee. All factions act simultaneously, obstructing each other, while you sit at the center — both hunter and prey.

## Core Mechanic: Faction Gambit Matrix

The GM uses a simple matrix to track each faction's dynamics:

| Faction | Intel Progress | Proximity to the Slate Fragment | Threat to You | Current Action |
|------|---------|-----------------|------------|---------|
| Twilight Hermit Order | 60% | Knows the fragment is in Backlund, not yet located | Medium | Sent observers to trail you |
| Church of the Storm | 40% | Only knows "someone entered Backlund holding a slate" | Low | Set up spiritual detectors at docks and stations |
| Rose School of Thought | 80% | Has locked the fragment bearer as "you" | High | First assassination attempt (see Scene A) |
| Desire Mother Tree Believers | 70% | Located your general position via Outer Deity sense | Extreme | Trying to replace your NPC companion |
| Aurora Order | 50% | Only knows the slate fragment is in Backlund | Medium | Preparing a sacrifice ritual — waiting for you to appear |

**Each time a scene passes, the GM rolls 1d10 for each faction:**
- 1–3: The faction makes progress (intel progress +10% or threat +1)
- 4–7: The faction stalls temporarily
- 8–10: The faction is obstructed (by other factions, intel progress −5% or threat −1)

**When a faction's "intel progress" reaches 100%:** they will find the fragment's whereabouts and take endgame action (seize, destroy, or negotiate) — the player must act first before then.

## Key Scene A: The Rose School of Thought's Assassination

On the second night after you arrive in Backlund — at midnight, the walls of your hotel room begin to bleed. Not human blood — a dark-red, corrosive liquid. A humanoid form gradually emerges on the wall. It is the Rose School of Thought's Sequence 3 "Disciple of Silence" — walking "into" this room from the other side of the wall.

**Disciple of Silence** | Prisoner Pathway Sequence 3 | Team CR: 12 (Solo: 4)

- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 60 / Mind (MND) 45 / Body (BOD) 55 / Agility (AGI) 60 / Charisma (CHA) 10
- HP: 130 / SP: 185 / LoCT: 100
- Powers: Curse Touch (1 AP / 5 SP / melee / 3d8 Dark + Curse — target all checks −15 next round), Living Wall (passive / can move through walls, cannot be physically blocked), Silence Spell (2 AP / 10 SP / whole area / all sound vanishes — cannot use verbal powers and social skills, lasts 2 rounds)

## Key Scene B: The Desire Mother Tree's Infiltration

When you meet with your allies — one of your NPC companions (chosen by the GM) behaves abnormally. There are brief pauses in his speech — as if listening to another voice. His shadow sometimes moves in the wrong direction. The slate fragment warms when near him.

**Truth:** The Desire Mother Tree's believers did not attack you directly — they contacted one of your companions on the first day you arrived in Backlund. Not replacement — "grafting." They planted an Outer Deity seed in your companion's subconscious. Your companion does not know he is polluted — but the seed will "bloom" at your most critical moment, transmitting your position and plan to the Desire Mother Tree's believers.

**Revelation method:**
- Slate fragment detection (warms when near)
- Mind + Observation (hard): notice the companion's abnormal behavior
- When alone with the companion — the companion suddenly speaks in a tone not his own, then completely forgets saying it.

**Clearing the pollution:** Spirituality + Ritual (extreme) — guide the ritual with the slate fragment. Success → the Outer Deity seed is cleared, the companion returns to normal. Failure → the seed "blooms," the companion is briefly controlled (1 round), attacks the player, then regains consciousness — terrified and guilt-ridden at his own actions.

## Key Scene C: The Church of the Storm's "Legal Hunt"

The Church of the Storm issued a notice to all Beyonders in Backlund:

> "Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate fragments are listed as a 'public safety threat.' Holders please hand the fragment to the nearest Church of the Storm outpost within 48 hours. Those who fail to comply will be regarded as illegally holding a Sealed Artifact and pursued by the Punishers."

This is a "legal hunting license." The Church of the Storm does not intend to come rob — they intend to make you "voluntarily surrender," while other factions will intercept you on your way to the church.

**Player options:**

| Option | Execution | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| **Ignore the notice** | Continue acting, but the Punishers will pursue you | 30% chance per public action to encounter a Punisher patrol (Sequence 6–5, 3–5 people) |
| **Surrender a replica** | Have your guardian Angel (Servant of Concealment) make a fake slate fragment — she can, but needs your slate fragment away from you for 24 hours | Temporarily lose the slate fragment's Outer Deity detection function. But the Church of the Storm temporarily drops its pursuit of you |
| **Negotiate with the Church of the Storm** | Directly contact Calamity (the Angel you fought alongside in Scenario 6) — persuade him to withdraw the notice. Charisma + Negotiation (legendary difficulty −60) | Success → Calamity agrees to withdraw the notice, but requires you share the "anti-Outer-Deity weapon" portion of the slate's contents with the Church of the Storm after you find the fragment |
| **Publicize the slate fragment's contents** | Make the decoded portion public — prove the fragment is a resource, not a threat | All factions' threat to you −1, but your location is found faster (because you exposed your position) |

## Key Scene D: The Fragment's Guardian

In the fog of pursuit and counter-pursuit, you finally find the third slate fragment's bearer — an unexpected person.

**Irene Morrison — Dr. Blackwood's ex-wife.**

Remember Scenario 4? In "The Mechanical Heart" ending, after the doctor lost his Beyonder powers, he was taken in by the Nighthawks. But his wife Irene — an ordinary person who never touched the Beyonder world — found another slate fragment while sorting the doctor's relics. The doctor never told anyone he had two — the second was his "control group" for difference-engine decoding research.

Irene does not know what it is. She only knows the slate glows at night, showing her images in her dreams she cannot understand. She hid the slate fragment in the family crypt in Backlund. She dared not tell anyone — because she knows what the doctor researched ultimately "made him disappear."

**Interacting with Irene:**

This is the only key scene in the whole scenario that needs no combat. Irene is an ordinary person — she does not know of the Beyonder world's existence (or only vaguely). She only knows her husband was taken by "those people" and never came back.

- Charisma + Negotiation (routine): Explain everything to her — the slate fragment, the doctor's fate, your connection to the doctor. Success → she will take you to the crypt and give you the fragment. She has only one request: "Tell me — what were his last words?"
- If the player obtained the doctor's last words in Scenario 4 — tell her. Her reaction: silence for a long time, then "He finally saw what he wanted to see."
- If the player lacks the doctor's last words — can choose to tell the truth or fabricate. Truth → she is sad but understanding. Fabricate → Charisma + Deception (hard), failure makes her demand you prove you knew the doctor.

**After obtaining the third slate fragment — its revealed contents:**

> "Slate 3/9. The first slate recorded the number of the doomsday countdown. The second slate recorded the map of barrier weak points. The third slate — records the Ancient Sun God's last command before He was betrayed."
>
> "He commanded His eight most trusted Angels — to shatter the slate, scattering the fragments across the world. Not to hide it — but to ensure 'someone could find all the fragments.' Because only by piecing all the fragments together — can one read the slate's last page: the complete method to oppose the Outer Deities."
>
> "Fragment 4/9 location: the Aurora Order's holy land — at the bottom of the True Creator's altar."

## Act 5: The Aurora Order's Gambit

The True Creator — not one of the Seven Orthodox Gods. He is an incomplete, polluted deity. The True Creator's altar is at the temple's center — a stone platform suspended in mid-air by iron chains. Embedded in the stone platform is the fourth slate fragment.

The Aurora Order's archbishop — Sequence 3 "Trinity Templar" — appears before you:

# Scenario 8: The Final Slate (Endgame)

**Recommended Level:** Player Sequence 2 or above, or equivalent influence level | **Duration:** 5–8 sessions (epic endgame)
**Recommended Party Size:** Team of 3–5, or Solo + all surviving NPC Companions

## Background

Four slate fragments in your hand. Decoding progress 4/9. You already know:

- Doomsday countdown: estimated from the first slate — 15–30 years remaining.
- Barrier weak points: one of seven already patched (Scenario 6), six still need repair.
- Ancient Sun God's last command: gather all nine fragments, read the slate's last page.
- Anti-Outer-Deity weapon: the slate 4/9 holds an incomplete "design blueprint" — needs the remaining fragments to complete.

But now a problem emerges — **you do not need to find the remaining fragments. The remaining fragments are finding you.**

A "resonance" exists between slate fragments — when enough fragments are held by the same person, the others automatically "converge" toward the bearer. This is the Ancient Sun God's design — He knew the human who gathered all the fragments would need help. So when creating the slate, He buried a "convergence" mechanism within it: when the bearer collects more than half the fragments, the others gradually draw near.

But this also means — **all factions holding other fragments now know your location.**

## Act 1: Convergence

Within the following week, three things happen simultaneously:

**Event 1: Fifth Slate Fragment (from the Church of Knowledge)**

The Wisdom Angel of the Church of Knowledge (whom you met in Scenario 6) visits in person. He brings a slate fragment — the Church of Knowledge has guarded it for two thousand years. Now he gives it to you:

> "The church voted. Rather than keep locking it in the basement hoping it never proves useful — better to give it to someone already proven able to use it. One condition: the final slate contents — all of them. We want a copy."

This is a vote of trust. The Church of Knowledge becomes your formal ally.

**Event 2: Sixth Slate Fragment (from the Twilight Hermit Order)**

The Twilight Hermit Order sends no one. What you receive is a message from Adam himself. Not a voice — text written directly into your consciousness:

> "The sixth fragment is on the Desire Mother Tree's altar. We cannot retrieve it — because none of us can approach an Outer Deity altar without being corrupted."
>
> "But you can — your slate fragment can suppress Outer Deity pollution (as you proved in Scenarios 5 and 6). We provide a five-person assault team — Sequence 4–3, absolutely loyal. You lead them in, retrieve the fragment. After — one-sixth of the slate's contents goes to us."
>
> (Then, a very long pause.)
>
> "Or — you can refuse. I will not force you. I am not like Him (you can vaguely guess who Adam means)."

**Event 3: The Desire Mother Tree's Full Invasion**

The Desire Mother Tree no longer sends believers to attack you. This time — She comes herself.

Backlund's sky turns a sickly purple in a single night. Trees begin to move without wind — branches twist into shapes not belonging to plants. A rotten sweetness can be smelled in every corner of the city. Beyonders begin to experience "mass hallucination" — they see dead relatives standing at street corners, beckoning.

The Desire Mother Tree does not want to destroy the city — She wants to "convert" everyone. Just as She converted Lightseeker (if you chose to let him sacrifice himself in Scenario 6). Only this time — the scale is all of Backlund.

**This is not combat. This is a race against time.**

## Act 2: The Desire Mother Tree's Altar

The altar lies at the bottom of an abandoned church on the outskirts of Backlund. The church's floor was dug open — beneath is not earth, but the roots of the Desire Mother Tree. The roots are alive — they entangle any Beyonders who approach, injecting Outer Deity pollution into them.

**Scene structure: Three-layer defense**

**Outer layer — the Desire Believers' defense line**

The Desire Mother Tree's Sequence 4–5 believers (about 15–20 people) form multiple defense layers around the church. A frontal breakthrough is possible — but every minute of delay spreads Backlund's pollution further.

**Twilight Hermit Order assault team (if Adam's deal accepted):**
- Five-person squad, Sequence 4–3.
- Tacit coordination: no one speaks — all communicate through the Spectator Pathway's mental transmission.
- Tactics: They automatically handle the outer defense, clearing the way for the player.
- Limitation: The assault team cannot approach within 50 meters of the altar (Outer Deity pollution would immediately corrupt them) — the final stretch must be walked by the player alone.

**Middle layer — the Maze of Desire**

The tunnel to the underground was transformed by the Desire Mother Tree's power into a "Maze of Desire." As the player passes through the tunnel, they see their deepest desires projected on the walls — not hallucinations, but "what would really happen if you chose a different life."

**Mind + Will (extreme, once per layer, three layers total):**
- Layer 1: Power — you see yourself seated on Backlund's throne, all bowing to you.
- Layer 2: Peace — you see yourself retired to the countryside, never again involved in the Beyonder world's strife. Your family's laughter. Afternoon sunlight.
- Layer 3: Answers — you see yourself meeting the Ancient Sun God in person. He tells you the ultimate truth of everything.

Each failure → the player is tempted by desire, wasting 1d3 hours (in-game time) in the hallucination. Each hour wasted increases Backlund's pollution spread by 10%. **Failing all three → all of Backlund is polluted, Beyonder conversion rate reaches 40% — but the player can still continue the mission.**

**Core — the Desire Mother Tree's Root-Heart**

The altar is not a stone platform. It is the Desire Mother Tree's "Root-Heart" on Earth — a roughly 3-meter-tall sphere woven of dark-purple roots. The sixth slate fragment is embedded at the sphere's exact center.

When you reach out to take the fragment — the Desire Mother Tree speaks to you herself.

Not with voice. Not with thought. With "longing." You suddenly feel an intense sense of belonging you have never experienced — as if you are not facing an Outer Deity, but a presence that "wants to protect you." She wants you. Not to kill you. Wants — to possess you.

> "You need not suffer. You need not choose. You need not be a hero. Put down the slate — put down this burden — come to me. I will make you never lonely again."

**Mind + Will (legendary −60):** Resist the Desire Mother Tree's direct recruitment. The slate fragment glows with all its power at this moment — every one of your fragments uses its Ancient Sun God power to oppose the Desire Mother Tree's consciousness. Each fragment held gives +10 to the check (4 fragments = +40). This means the actual difficulty is −20.

**The Desire Mother Tree's Recruitment vs. the Slate Fragment's Protection:**

| Fragments Held | Actual Will Check Difficulty | Extra Effect |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| 1–2 fragments | Legendary −40 | The slate fragment provides only minimal protection |
| 3–4 fragments | Legendary −20 | The slate fragments begin to resonate — ancient echoes of the Ancient Sun God sound in the player's ears (increasing resolve) |
| 5–6 fragments | Legendary −20 (gain extra +20 from the slate) | The resonance becomes a chorus — the whispers of all the fallen Ancient Sun God Angels converge in the player's consciousness |

## Act 3: The Slate's Last Page

You retrieve the sixth fragment. The six slates in your hand begin to arrange themselves — the resonance between them grows so strong it is visible to the naked eye — six white light-bands connect them, forming an incomplete ring.

You can now see the slate's last page — incomplete, but enough to understand the Ancient Sun God's ultimate plan:

> **"The method to oppose the Outer Deities — is not combat. It is sealing. You cannot only seal the barrier cracks — you must permanently close all weak points on the barrier. To do this — requires a ritual. A ritual requiring the simultaneous activation of all 22 Pathways' Uniqueness. No one but me can perform this ritual. And I — am already gone."**
>
> **"Unless — someone can aggregate enough Pathway power. Not become Sequence 0 — no time. But borrow temporarily — through the slate fragment as a medium, borrow the Uniqueness held by the Seven Churches and hidden organizations. Only a quarter-hour is needed. A quarter-hour is enough."**
>
> **"But the medium must be one who belongs to no single Pathway — one who absorbed the knowledge and power of multiple Pathways in the process of growing. In other words — one who collected the slate fragments."**

You are that person. You need not become a god. You only need to hold the slate, stand between the barrier and the Outer Deities, borrow all the Uniqueness — and patch the final crack.

**The cost:** Borrowing the power of 22 Uniquenesses will burn the medium — not physically. It is your "Pathway identity." After the ritual, you lose all Beyonder powers. You become an ordinary person again.

## Act 4: The Endgame Choice

From now on — there are no more fragments to collect. No more enemies to defeat. Only one choice: **Will you?**

| Choice | Requires | Consequence | Ending |
|------|---------|------|------|
| **Perform the Ritual** | The Seven Churches agree to lend their Uniqueness (if you built good relations with enough churches in the first seven scenarios — at least four churches — they will agree) | You stand on the barrier's seven weak points, slate in hand, borrowing the power of 22 Uniquenesses. The barrier is permanently reinforced. The Outer Deity invasion is delayed by at least a thousand years. You lose all Beyonder powers. But you saved a world. | Ending A: "The Nightwatcher" — you return to Gray Mist City, find a job at the Drowned Man Tavern. Every day you see Beyonders come and go, discussing their adventures. They do not know who you are. You pour their drinks. Smile. Say nothing. On the darkest nights — the slate fragment in your pocket glows faintly. Not power. It is — good night. |
| **Awaken the Ancient Sun God's Residual Will** | The slate fragment reaches 6/9 or above, and you obtained the Ancient Sun God's relic at the Aurora Order holy land (Scenario 7) | The Ancient Sun God's residual will is temporarily awakened — He takes over the ritual, you as the "body" rather than the "medium." The ritual succeeds — the barrier is repaired. But your personality will conflict with the Ancient Sun God's will. You will not vanish — but you are no longer entirely "you" | Ending B: "Dawn" — your body remains, your memories remain. But when you look in the mirror — the reflection sometimes looks at you with another gaze. Like a weary old man who has lived ten thousand years. You inherited the Sun God's legacy — but lost a part of yourself. All your friends say: "You look — a little different." |
| **Refuse the Ritual — seek an alternative** | Spend a great deal of time (months to years in-game) seeking a method that requires no sacrifice | During the search — the barrier continues to worsen. Before you find the alternative, one weak point is breached by the Outer Deities. A smaller-scale Outer Deity invasion occurs — recorded in history as the "First Crack War." You did not stop the doomsday — but you bought humanity time | Ending C: "Haven" — you organize a Beyonder alliance, building a defense line at the breach point. You did not save the world. You saved Gray Mist City and the surrounding region. Millions lived — because you were there. You do not regret the choice — but you can never be sure: if you had performed the ritual then, would more people have been saved? |
| **Scatter the slate fragments — let nine people guard them separately** | Scatter your slate fragments (if you have more than four) among your most trusted allies — one each | Nine slate bearers form a hidden network — the "Slate-Bearer Council." Each learns from the slate, each prepares for the doomsday. No one can read the complete last page — but humanity now has nine "sparks," not one. If one fails — the others remain | Ending D: "Spark" — you buy a small cabin in the suburbs of Gray Mist City. No electricity. No gaslight. Only a table, a chair, and your slate fragment. You do not know who will ultimately close the barrier — maybe you, maybe your companion, maybe the next person to pick up a slate. But you believe — humanity does not need a savior. Humanity needs many who refuse to give up. |

## Loss-of-Control Risk — Endgame

During the ritual or any scene involving the slate fragment running at full power:

| Risk Point | Value |
|--------|-----|
| Slate fragment full resonance | +3d10 (one-time) |
| Borrowing Uniqueness (per piece) | +1d6 |
| Desire Mother Tree's recruitment attempt | +2d10 (if resistance succeeds); if failure, directly triggers loss of control |

After the ritual succeeds — the player's loss-of-control state is adjudicated by the GM: they may be sealed by the Nighthawks, healed, or survive permanently in a half-loss-of-control state.

# Epilogue: Many Years Later

(GM reads aloud after the campaign ends — depending on the player's chosen ending)

Many years later. A young history-department student flips through documents in the archive of Mistgrave University. His thesis topic: "The Slate-Bearer Legend at the End of the Fifth Epoch — History or Myth?"

His advisor says it is myth. A mortal who collected slate fragments, borrowed the power of Angels and True Gods, opposed the Outer Deities, saved the world — too much like a story.

But in a yellowed Nighthawks internal report he reads one line. No signature. Only a single line of text:

**"The slate remains. Good night."**

He puts the report back. Before leaving the archive — he notices something glowing faintly at the bottom of a bookshelf.

He bends down. It is a slate fragment the size of a palm.

It is glowing. Beckoning to him.

(The End)
