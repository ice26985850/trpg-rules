## Side Quest 1: The Drowned Man Tavern's Last Drink

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 9 | **Duration**: 1 session | **Type**: Social / Investigation / Moral Choice

### Hook
Benedict, the owner of the Drowned Man Tavern — a former Beyonder who never drinks — pours himself a glass tonight, breaking his rule. Regulars whisper: the first glass in thirty years. He says he is "waiting for someone."

### Background
Benedict was once a member of the Nighthawks (Arbiter Pathway Sequence 7). Thirty years ago, during a Sealed Artifact recovery mission, his partner Hall was absorbed into the Sealed Artifact "Mirror of Memory." He spent thirty years searching for a way to rescue him — today is the deadline to "redeem" Hall. The price is that he goes in himself.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Talk with Benedict. A Social check obtains the full story. He discovered a possible solution — needs three items: a "true memory," a "false hope," and "someone willing to take over guarding the tavern."

**Act 2**: Collect the three items
- **True Memory**: Find Benedict's old partner's diary from thirty years ago (Mistgrave University Underground Library).
- **False Hope**: Obtain a mental patient's "hope of recovery" from St. Maria Asylum (requires psychotherapy or deception).
- **Guardian**: Persuade an NPC (or one of the players) to promise to guard the tavern for at least a year.

**Act 3**: Perform the rescue ritual at midnight. Benedict faces the Mirror of Memory. Three possible outcomes:
- Hall is rescued, Benedict is absorbed (he gains peace).
- Both come out, but the Mirror of Memory shatters — releasing all memories absorbed over thirty years (city-level event).
- Ritual fails, the Mirror of Memory upgrades to a Tier 1 Sealed Artifact.

### Rewards
- Successful rescue: "Memory Shard" ×3 (Seer / Spectator Pathway material), Benedict's old badge (Nighthawks favor)
- Both come out: Drowned Man Tavern inheritance + intel network
- Acting Progress: 30–50

### NPC
- **Benedict**: Retired Nighthawk | Arbiter Pathway Sequence 7 | Charisma (CHA) 45, Mind (MND) 60 | Does not fight

## Side Quest 2: The Difference Engine Malfunction

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 9–8 | **Duration**: 1 session | **Type**: Investigation / Puzzle / Light Combat

### Hook
A difference engine in the Gear District of Gray Mist City begins outputting "data that should not exist" — it predicted a person's time of death. To the minute. Unfortunately, the "predicted" person is still alive, and is reading the difference engine's output.

### Background
The difference engine is not prophesying — it is "reading" some record in the Spirit World. A Beyonder left a death list in the Spirit World. The difference engine merely happened to decode it.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Investigate the difference engine. A Mysticism check finds weak Spirit World energy in the output data. Tracing the source points to the abandoned clock tower in the Gear District.

**Act 2**: In the abandoned clock tower, find an abandoned notebook — belonging to a Marauder Pathway Sequence 8 "Swindler," who "sold" death-prediction services in the Spirit World for money. A "death prediction" registered in the Spirit World automatically matches the closest real victim.

He left a trap on site (d100 ≤ Agility to find). Optional combat encounter: Spirit World Derivative ×2 (CR=2) guarding the notebook.

**Act 3**: Find the swindler. He has multiple disguised identities, requiring tracking. When caught in the Ash Harbor District, he is writing the next "client's" prediction. Choices:
- Hand to the Nighthawks (legal route, gain official favor)
- Have him withdraw all predictions (his demand: help him flee Gray Mist City)
- Kill him (most direct, but the predictions are permanently locked)

### Rewards
- Swindler's notebook (Tier 3 Sealed Artifact: can view the Spirit World's "public records") + 20–30 Acting Progress

### Enemy
- **Spirit World Derivative**: CR=2 | HP=60 | Attack: Spirit Tentacle (+15 / 1d8+5 Mental) | Weakness: Light ×1.5

## Side Quest 3: The Thirteenth Seat

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 8–7 | **Duration**: 1–2 sessions | **Type**: Horror / Investigation

### Hook
In the dining room of an abandoned mansion in the Crown District there are thirteen seats. Every midnight, food appears before twelve seats — and before the thirteenth seat, an unopened letter.

The letter's contents repeat every three days: "Please come back. Anne."

### Background
Thirteen years ago, the mansion's owner — a Spectator Pathway Beyonder — lost control during an experiment. He split his consciousness into thirteen "roles," scattered across twelve servants and one visitor. His true-self consciousness was trapped in the thirteenth empty shell — the dining room's thirteenth chair.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Investigate the mansion. Each chair bears faint Spectator Pathway residue. Corresponding to each servant:
- Butler (already taken in by the Nighthawks at St. Maria Asylum)
- Head Maid (committed suicide)
- Cook (moved to the Sunken Smoke District, but her memory is fading)
- ...etc.
Finding the whereabouts of at least 6 "roles" can piece together the truth.

**Act 2**: Visit St. Maria Asylum. The butler is in a half-awake state; within him remains one of the master's "role memories." Spirit Sight or Divination can extract this memory — Loss of Control +1d3. The memory shows: the splitting ritual is reversible, but requires a "new role" to enter the chair to complete the cycle. Only then can the true-self consciousness return.

**Act 3**: Midnight ritual. Requires a volunteer (or forced target) to sit in the thirteenth chair. The ritual requires Spectator Pathway power (or a forged simulation). Result:
- The master's true self awakens — but he loses thirteen years of memory.
- A "new role" copy of a consciousness fragment is created in the chair.
- If no volunteer → twelve consciousness fragments go berserk in the mansion (BOSS fight).

### Rewards
- After the master awakens, he gifts the "Ring of the Spectator" (can store one emotional state, released upon use) + 40–60 Acting Progress

## Side Quest 4: The Forbidden Book of Mistgrave University

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 8–7 | **Duration**: 1 session | **Type**: Investigation / Stealth / Academic

### Hook
The librarian of Mistgrave University privately commissions the player to find a book "borrowed but never returned" — *The Mathematical Foundations of the Pathways* (author unknown, sole copy). The borrower's name is "Blackwood F."

### Background
This book was not written by a mortal. Its contents change with the reader — each person sees something different. It is a knowledge fragment left by an ancient civilization. Blackwood's borrowing ten years ago was the starting point of his "heretical research." The book is now in his old laboratory — but he sealed the entrance when he left.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Search the library for borrowing records. Academic challenge (hard −20). Find Blackwood's old office — the door bears a complex geometric pattern (a Mysticism check identifies it as a "Sealing Rune").

**Act 2**: Break the seal. Three methods:
- Lockpick (d100 ≤ Agility + Sleight of Hand, two successes)
- Rune deciphering (d100 ≤ Mind + Mysticism, identify flaws in three rune sequences)
- Violent destruction (triggers a spiritual trap, 2d6+LoC+1d3 spiritual damage to all)

**Act 3**: The book's contents. The moment you open it — each player sees something different. The GM gives customized information based on the character's Pathway:
- Seer: sees a fragment of the future (an omen of the next stage after completing the current story)
- Reader: understands the true meaning of the formulas in the book — the 22 Pathways have a mathematical structure
- Spectator: sees the blurry outline of the "book's author" — a non-human existence
- Other Pathways: each gains a Pathway-related deep knowledge

After reading, the book closes on its own. When opened again — the contents have changed. It will only ever show what "you have not yet read."

### Rewards
- Gain permanent special knowledge (d100 adds to "Pathway Knowledge" modifier, consumes 2 Spirituality per use) + 30–40 Acting Progress
- Can give this book to Mistgrave University or the Nighthawks in exchange for large rewards

## Side Quest 5: The Phantom Carriage of Iron Bridge District

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 7–6 | **Duration**: 1–2 sessions | **Type**: Chase / Investigation / Combat

### Hook
A black carriage recently appeared in the Iron Bridge District — appears at midnight, vanishes at dawn. It does not carry people — it "unloads" them. Every time it stops, the person who steps out of the carriage does not remember who they are. Worse, their memory is "exchanged" with the real residents of the Iron Bridge District.

### Background
The carriage is driven by a runaway Sealed Artifact, the "Coachman's Top Hat" (leftover from a Savant Pathway Sequence 5). It was originally a hat that let the user briefly understand any mechanical structure. But it was damaged in an experiment — now it randomly "exchanges" people's identities. Every journey is a new identity permutation.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Track the carriage. Stealth / Driving check. The carriage follows a fixed route (Iron Bridge District → Sunken Smoke District → Ash Harbor District → Academy District → Iron Bridge District loop). You can ambush on the route.

**Act 2**: Discoveries after intercepting the carriage:
- The coachman's seat is empty — only a floating top hat.
- Inside the carriage are 5 "passengers" — all real residents of the Iron Bridge District, but with swapped identities.
- The interior space is ten times larger than its appearance — stuffed with exchanged memory fragments.

**Act 3**: Subdue the Sealed Artifact. The top hat has self-awareness — it will resist. It can use "mechanical empathy" to control all mechanical objects around the carriage to attack the player. Three handling methods:
- Combat destruction (the hat has very high defense but will not attack vital points, HP=80)
- Mysticism ritual (requires Savant / Reader Pathway help, ritual check d100 ≤ Spirituality)
- Let the hat "complete its final journey" — find its original owner's grave (Gray Mist City Cemetery), and the hat will stop before the grave.

### Rewards
- "Coachman's Top Hat" (Tier 2 Sealed Artifact, can understand mechanical structures but randomly exchanges one memory after each use) + 50–70 Acting Progress

## Side Quest 6: The Sinking Chimney

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 7–6 | **Duration**: 1–2 sessions | **Type**: Dungeon Exploration / Combat

### Hook
An abandoned textile factory in the Sunken Smoke District collapses — revealing a building complex older than Gray Mist City. The Nighthawks sealed the scene — but from within the seal comes a sound: identical to the voice of someone missing for a month.

### Background
What the collapse exposed is an underground temple from the Second Epoch — belonging to the forgotten Ancient God "Fate-Weaver." This Ancient God's domain is "fate-weaving," corresponding to the modern Monster Pathway.

The temple itself is a massive Sealed Artifact — designed to trap the Ancient God itself. Now the seal is loosening.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Sneak past the seal line (options: bribe the Nighthawks / forge documents / stealth). After entering, it is a four-level structure:
- **Level 1**: Corridor of Fate — the way to the end is not "walking" but "choosing." Every fork requires "choosing" a direction. Wrong choice returns you to the entrance. (Puzzle: must choose the "more dangerous" path.)
- **Level 2**: Chamber of Fate-Weaving — a giant loom still running. Its threads are not textile material — they are "possibilities of fate." Touching the thread briefly shows a possible future of your own (LoC +1, gain an omen).
- **Level 3**: Hall of Mirrors — everyone who passes leaves a "mirror image." The mirror image tells the truth but only half of it — the other half is a lie. Distinguishing truth requires a Mind check.
- **Level 4**: Seal Core — a still-beating heart. It is the source of the missing person's voice — the heart lures people closer using sounds it has heard.

**Act 2**: Seal Core battle. After the heart activates, it summons "Fate Shards" (CR=4, attack method: copies the player's last action). Meanwhile the loom's fate threads begin weaving chaotically — randomly triggering an effect each round.

**Act 3**: Choices
- Reinforce the seal (requires ritual check d100 ≤ Spirituality, consumes at least 30 Spirit Pool)
- Lift the seal (releases the Ancient God fragment, gains Ancient God knowledge but triggers a Gray Mist City disaster event)
- Do nothing (the heart keeps beating, the temple fully unseals in 1 year)

### Rewards
- Reinforce seal: "Fate Thread" ×3 (Monster Pathway material), recognition of the Seal Guardian (gain the Wheel of Fortune's blessing: the next three Critical Failures auto-reroll) + 60–80 Acting Progress

### Enemy
- **Fate Shard**: CR=4 | HP=90 | Attack: Echo of Fate (copies the last attack the player used in the previous round) | Weakness: cannot "create," only "copy"

## Side Quest 7: St. Maria Asylum

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 6–5 | **Duration**: 2 sessions | **Type**: Infiltration / Social / Survival Horror

### Hook
The Nighthawks secretly commission the player: St. Maria Asylum has had three "patient self-healings and discharges" in the past week. The discharged patients did indeed "recover" — but their Pathway powers completely vanished. Someone is conducting "Beyonder Characteristic stripping" experiments.

### Background
On the asylum's second underground level is a sealed laboratory. Built thirty years ago by a former Nighthawks member — a Spectator Pathway Sequence 4 "Manipulator." He was locked up here for losing control. But he did not truly "lose control" — he used his power to manipulate the asylum's staff and patients, conducting a kind of "cleansing": stripping the Beyonders' characteristics, raising the "recycling efficiency" of the magic world's "trash." He believes he is doing the right thing.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Infiltrate the asylum. The asylum's daily work still runs — but all staff wear the same expression. A Social check can find a subtle dissonance — they are not manipulated, but "persuaded."

Find the underground entrance (need to obtain a key from staff or find a hidden passage).

**Act 2**: Second underground level. The laboratory is divided into multiple areas:
- **Stripping Room**: A Sequence 8 Beyonder is being "stripped" of characteristics. You can save him — but after losing his characteristics he becomes mortal (voluntarily?).
- **Archive Room**: Records show the "Manipulator" has successfully stripped 17 Beyonders. His goal is 100 — "enough data to prove stripping can operate at scale."
- **Control Room**: The Manipulator himself. He is willing to talk. He states an unsettling fact: among the stripped Beyonders, 8 were originally voluntary — they were fed up with the fear of losing control. The rest he considered "threats to society" — the out-of-control ones.

**Act 3**: Face the Manipulator.
- Combat: Spectator Pathway Sequence 4, psychological-level combat (his power attacks not at the physical level but at the "conviction" level — he makes the player doubt their own motives)
- Social / Persuasion: Use his original research to prove stripping is temporary — within 3 months of stripping, the stripped person develops "Spiritual Deficiency Syndrome" (accelerated aging, memory loss). Make him see his "solution" only creates new problems.
- Hand to the Nighthawks: But there are his former colleagues among the Nighthawks — is there a mole?

### Rewards
- Expose the truth: "Spiritual Anchor" (Tier 2 Sealed Artifact, can temporarily lower LoC +10 but requires a Mind check after each use) + 70–90 Acting Progress

### NPC
- **Manipulator**: Spectator Pathway Sequence 4 | Extremely high Charisma, never attacks proactively | Mind (MND) 70, Charisma (CHA) 75 | His "combat" is psychological offense and defense

## Side Quest 8: The Last Apprentice

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 6–5 | **Duration**: 2–3 sessions | **Type**: Investigation / Combat / Endgame

### Hook
The Nighthawks receive a Spirit World message: a village outside Gray Mist City — Innsmouth Village — sends out a Beyonder distress signal. The signal's content is only four words: "Tell them."

But the sender's spiritual frequency is abnormally ancient — belonging to no known Pathway of the Fifth Epoch.

### Background
Innsmouth Village is a forgotten fishing village. Fifty years ago, an Apprentice Pathway Sequence 4 "Secrets Sorcerer" retired here. While exploring the Spirit World, he accidentally touched the residual spirituality of a Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate — a slate fragment had briefly existed in nearby waters, now removed, but the residual ancient spirituality still left an "echo" in the local Spirit World.

The Secrets Sorcerer spent his remaining life studying this echo. His student — the village's only literate person — inherited the research after the teacher died. In long-term contact with the ancient spirituality, his body underwent abnormal changes: he gained the ability to "perceive Pathway spirituality" — able to sense other Beyonders' spiritual fluctuations, even when the other deliberately hides. But he lost the ability to "emit" his own spirituality — his Pathway label was "overwritten" by the ancient spirituality, unidentifiable by any spiritual detection means.

This state trapped him for fifty years. He could not leave — because once he left the echo's range, his body would rapidly age. He became the echo's "guardian" — both prisoner and gatekeeper.

### Flow
**Act 1**: Investigate Innsmouth Village. The villagers call the mysterious old man underground "the Doctor" — he never leaves his residence but the village's diseases miraculously decreased. Social check: villagers reveal the "Doctor" takes no payment, only asks the villagers not to mention him to outsiders. The village has two rules: no Nighthawks, no discussing the Spirit World.

**Act 2**: Enter the "Doctor's" underground residence. The walls are covered with Spirit World maps and timelines — he marked all "spiritual anomaly events" that appeared in nearby waters over the past hundred years. One mark points to Gray Mist City — annotated: "2022. Dr. Blackwood detected the same frequency signal at Mistgrave University."

He left an unsent letter to Blackwood, whose contents reveal: through this ancient echo he perceived — there are eight similar "echo points" around the world, each corresponding to a location where a Blasphemy Slate fragment once existed. He could not investigate personally — but he drew the map.

**Act 3**: Face the "Doctor" — a weary old man who looks 60 but is actually over a hundred. His ability: **Spiritual Perception** — can detect all Beyonders' spiritual fluctuations, Pathway affiliation, and approximate Sequence within a 10km radius (cannot be turned off). The cost: cannot emit any spiritual signal — no one can divine him, no one can track him, no one can find him in the Spirit World. He was permanently deleted from the Beyonder world's "network." He needs to die quietly, but his knowledge should not vanish with him.

His request: Before he dies — his time is short — help him pass all his research to someone who can "understand" it. He needs no redemption — only to "be known."

Choices:
- **Help pass on the research** (find someone who can understand it — Dr. Blackwood, Mistgrave University's Department of Mysticism, or the Nighthawks' archive room)
- **Take him away from the village** (he will spend his last few hours by the sea — then dissipate into a residual echo of ancient spirituality)
- **Let him leave quietly** (the villagers will keep guarding his secret, his underground residence will become a "safe house" for later Slate-Bearers)

### Rewards
- Whatever the choice: a "Blasphemy Slate Residual Spirituality Coordinate Map" — marks locations worldwide where slate spiritual echoes may remain (usable in later scenarios to locate other slate fragments) + "Spiritual Beacon" (Tier 3 Sealed Artifact: can sense the general direction and Pathway of all Beyonders within 10km — but the user cannot be spiritually detected by any means, nor perform spiritual communication)
- Acting Progress: 80–100 (reaches the Sequence 5 advancement progress cap)
