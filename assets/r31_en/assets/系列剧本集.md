# Scenario 1: The Crimson Ball of the White Room

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 9 | **Recommended Party Size**: 3–5 | **Duration**: 2–3 sessions

## Background
For three months, a rumor has spread through the artist circles of Gray Mist City: if you receive a white invitation embossed with a red rose, you will attend a "lifetime unforgettable" ball in the "White Room." Those who returned produced works that suddenly brimmed with astonishing vitality. After the most recent ball, a painter went missing — in his studio remained an unfinished painting: six people dancing, but the face of the sixth dancer is blurred.

## Hooks (choose 1 of 3)
1. **Commission**: The missing painter's family hires the players to investigate.
2. **Rumor**: The players hear the ball rumor at the Drowned Man Tavern; a Spiritual intuition triggers.
3. **Chance**: One player receives a white invitation.

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — The Missing Man's Home (edge of the Crown District)**
- Surface clue: The face of the sixth dancer on the painting keeps changing.
- Spiritual clue: Spirit Sight → residue of the Spectator Pathway; Divination → "dance intertwined with death."
- Risk: Spirit Sight too deep triggers Loss of Control +1d3.

**Clue Point 2 — 13 Moran Street (Iron Bridge District)**
- Surface clue: Neighbors say a black carriage comes to pick people up every Friday night.
- Spiritual clue: Divination shows the carriage comes from the direction of an abandoned theater in the Iron Bridge District.

**Clue Point 3 — Drowned Man Tavern (Ash Harbor District)**
- Social challenge: Must succeed at Negotiation / bribery of an informant.
- Information: Lady White was once a genius dancer; she "miraculously survived" the Royal Theater fire five years ago.
- Failure consequence: The informant tips off Lady White, increasing the final encounter's difficulty.

**Clue Point 4 — Mistgrave University (Academy District)**
- Academic challenge: Search old newspapers and find the fire report; all follow-up reports have vanished.
- Mysticism: A Spectator Pathway Sequence 6 can exert large-scale influence in highly emotional environments.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Ball Infiltration (basement of an abandoned theater in the Iron Bridge District)**
- Environment: A magnificent yet dilapidated ballroom; more than 20 guests are dancing.
- Key discovery: One of the six dancers is already dead, but Lady White refuses to admit it.
- Path A (Social): Talk with Lady White; discover she lives inside a hallucination of her own making.
- Path B (Stealth): Sneak backstage to search for a Sealed Artifact.
- Path C (Combat): Triggered once discovered.

**Scene 2 — Confrontation**
- Lady White unleashes her power: all guests become "dancers" under emotional control.
- Goal options: knock her out head-on, recall the controlled guests, or destroy the Sealed Artifact.

**Antagonist Stats**:
- Lady White | Spectator Pathway Sequence 6 "Hypnotist"
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 55 / Mind (MND) 50 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 45 / Charisma (CHA) 65
- HP: 115 / Spirit Pool (SP): 170 / Loss of Control (LoC): 45 / Threshold (LoCT): 110
- Powers: Dance Hypnosis (1 AP / 5 Spirituality / 20m / area Charm), Emotional Storm (2 AP / 10 Spirituality / whole area), Tempo Control (reaction / controls the actions of the hypnotized)
- Weakness: −20 to Mind when faced with the truth; actively showing her the memory of the fire can shake her power.
- Tactics: Uses the controlled guests as a front line, controls from behind. At HP < 50% triggers "Eternal Waltz" — a berserk state.

**Sealed Artifact Stats**:
- "Eternal Metronome" | Tier 2 | Spectator Pathway Sequence 6
- Positive: Controls the emotions of those around her.
- Side effect: Addicted to the perfect rhythm; thinks of everything in beats of eight.
- Hidden: At the stroke of midnight it sways on its own and summons dancers.

## Act 3: The Choice

**Revelation**: Five years ago, Lady White lost control in the fire and bound the spectators within a dancing hallucination. She has been "re-holding" the ball, convinced that if she dances well enough, everything can start over. That dead dancer — is herself, as she sees herself in the hallucination.

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| Eliminate | Defeat in combat | Tier 1 Sealed Artifact + 200 team Acting Progress |
| Hand to the Authorities | Persuade with evidence + Social check | Moderate reward + Nighthawks favor + 100 Progress |
| Redeem | Recall + help her face the truth | Lady White's keepsake + "Temperance" Tarot card + 300 Progress |

**Loss-of-Control Risk Points**: Spirit Sight +1d3 / affected by the dance +2 / contact with the metronome for >3 rounds +1d5 / witnessing the loss-of-control form +1d3 / forced Pathway opposed check +2 / Critical Failure +1d3 / Spirit Exhaustion +2d5 / witnessing the truth +1d5

# Scenario 2: Whispers Beneath the Gray Fog

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 8→7 (suggest 4–6 Sequence 8 characters) | **Duration**: 3–4 sessions

## Background
Within a month, seven worker disappearances occurred in the Gear District of Gray Mist City. The official investigation filed them as "labor mobility," but every missing person's last known location was the same textile factory — Morrison Textile Factory. Stranger still: no one remembers the missing people's names. Their coworkers know "someone is gone," but cannot recall who. The families' memories are also fading.

## Hooks (choose 1 of 3)
1. **Commission**: The Nighthawks outsource Beyonders to assist the investigation (official channels cannot enter the factory interior).
2. **Involvement**: Someone among the player's Surface Life contacts goes missing.
3. **Tip-off**: Drowned Man Tavern intel shows Morrison Factory is buying "forgotten materials."

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — Perimeter of Morrison Textile Factory**
- Surface clue: The factory still runs, but the workers look dazed. A 24-hour shift system — unusual.
- Spiritual clue: Spirit Sight → faint but persistent spiritual fluctuation above the factory, pulsing at the frequency of a heartbeat.
- Risk: Staying within 10 minutes of the factory perimeter triggers Loss of Control +1 (low-frequency spiritual pollution).

**Clue Point 2 — The Missing's Families (Sunken Smoke District)**
- Social challenge: The family cannot say the missing person's name, only describe "my son / my husband."
- Mind challenge: d100 ≤ Mind → realize they are not failing to remember the name — the name was "stolen."
- Clue: All the missing persons' addresses are within a 15-minute walk of the factory.

**Clue Point 3 — Gear District Bar**
- Information: Morrison Factory changed its plant manager three months ago. The old manager "retired" but no one saw him leave.
- Anomaly: The factory recently imported large amounts of paraffin and cremains — not textile materials.
- Risk: Digging too deep may attract secret attention.

**Clue Point 4 — Mistgrave University, Department of Mysticism**
- Academic challenge: Paraffin + cremains are common materials for the ritual of the Secrets Suppliant Pathway Sequence 7 "Shadow Ascetic."
- Deep information: A Shadow Ascetic can offer "memory" as a sacrifice to a hidden existence in exchange for power. The memory does not vanish — it is stored within an object.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Infiltrating Morrison Textile Factory**
- Environment: At the heart of the Gear District, a large textile factory threaded with steam pipes. 24-hour production means there is always someone present.
- Entrance options: Front gate (disguise as worker), side door (lockpick), skylight (climb), steam pipes (requires Body to endure high heat).
- Interior discovery: A ritual space in the factory basement. Seven wax figures — each made using a missing person's likeness.
- Key discovery: The missing persons' memories are sealed inside the wax figures. Touching a figure lets you read part of the memory — but Loss of Control +1d3.

**Scene 2 — Ritual in Progress**
- Antagonist: **Plant Manager Morrison** — Secrets Suppliant Pathway Sequence 7 "Shadow Ascetic," presiding over a sacrifice ritual.
- Environment: Bursting steam pipes fill the air with mist, limiting visibility. Mechanical noise imposes Disadvantage on auditory checks.
- Ritual purpose: Offer the seven missing persons' memories to a hidden existence in exchange for qualification to advance to Sequence 6.

**Enemy Stats**:
- Plant Manager Morrison | Secrets Suppliant Pathway Sequence 7 | CR: 4
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 60 / Mind (MND) 45 / Body (BOD) 40 / Agility (AGI) 30 / Charisma (CHA) 30
- HP: 130 / Spirit Pool (SP): 185 / Loss of Control (LoC): 35 / Threshold (LoCT): 100
- Attacks: Shadow Touch (+15 / 3d6 Dark / melee), Blade of Sacrifice (+15 / 2d8 Dark + Curse / 10m)
- Powers: Shadow Stealth (1 AP / 5 Spirituality / fully hidden), Hidden Ritual (full round / sustained), Summon Shadow Servant (2 AP / 10 Spirituality / summon Sequence 9 Secrets Suppliant ×2)
- Weakness: Interrupting the ritual causes backlash (LoC +10); Shadow Stealth fails in bright-light environments.
- Tactics: Uses steam to conceal his form, lets shadow servants take the hits. At HP < 50% attempts to sacrifice himself for power (LoC spikes → Edge of Loss).

**Scene 3 — Moral Dilemma**
- The wax figures can be destroyed — but this permanently erases the missing persons' memories; they become "people who never existed."
- The ritual can be interrupted — but the backlashed power will run out of control, possibly harming the workers still inside.
- The manager can be killed — but he displays a twisted belief: "They were only parts in the factory; I turned them into art."

## Act 3: Truth and Choice

**Revelation**: Plant Manager Morrison was once a worker himself. His wife died in a factory accident — officially ruled "operator error." He consumed the Secrets Suppliant potion to gain enough power to take revenge on the factory owner. But in the process of revenge, he heard the whispers of a hidden existence — the whisper told him that by offering "the forgotten" as sacrifices, the hidden existence would bring his wife back.

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| Combat Elimination | Direct combat. Caution: must avoid a steam explosion causing mass casualties | Sealed Artifact "Memory Wax Mold" (Tier 3) + Nighthawks reward + 200 Progress |
| Interrupt Ritual + Hand Over | Interrupt mid-combat then negotiate. Manager's LoC spikes → loss-of-control state, difficulty rises | Nighthawks high trust + official Beyonder status + 250 Progress |
| Attempt Redemption | Highest difficulty. Requires understanding his story + evoking his memory of his wife (rather than revenge) | "Hanged Man" mark + the hidden existence's "attention" + 350 Progress |

**Loss-of-Control Risk**: Touching wax figures +1d3 / witnessing the ritual +2 / steam leak (every 5 rounds +1) / manager's loss-of-control form +1d5 / Spirit Exhaustion +2d5 / hearing the whisper +1d3 per scene

# Scenario 3: Warehouse No. 10

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 7→6 (suggest 4–6 Sequence 7 characters) | **Duration**: 3–4 sessions

## Background
Warehouse No. 10 in the Ash Harbor District is a Sealed Artifact black market disguised as an ordinary warehouse. Over three months, multiple Tier 2+ Sealed Artifacts were stolen from various institutions / factions. Someone is systematically collecting high-tier Sealed Artifacts. The trail points to Warehouse No. 10 — but Warehouse No. 10 itself was also attacked. Its keeper, "Old John" (Spectator Pathway Sequence 5, a retired Beyonder), sends a distress call to the players.

## Hooks (choose 1 of 3)
1. **Commission**: The Nighthawks / Church commission the players as "unofficial" personnel to investigate — the authorities cannot directly intervene in the black market.
2. **Pursuit**: A common name is emerging behind the antagonists the players previously defeated.
3. **Old John's Distress Call**: One of the players knows Old John (can be set as an NPC relationship).

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — Warehouse No. 10 (Ash Harbor docks)**
- Status: The warehouse was ransacked; three Tier 2 Sealed Artifacts are missing. Old John is gravely wounded but alive.
- Clue: The attackers left a gear-shaped emblem — belonging to an organization called the "Gear Council."
- Old John's account: "They didn't come to buy. The leader was a woman wearing a bronze mask. She said this was 'restocking,' not 'robbery.'"

**Clue Point 2 — Tracking the Stolen Sealed Artifacts**
- List of missing Sealed Artifacts:
  1. "Blood Pact Goblet" (Tier 2 / Criminal Pathway) — can forcibly bind a contract.
  2. "Nightwatcher's Pocket Watch" (Tier 2 / Sleepless Pathway) — can pause time for 1 round.
  3. "Living Map" (Tier 2 / Seer Pathway) — can mark any person's real-time location.
- Mysticism check: What can these three Sealed Artifacts do in combination? → Track a specific person + forcibly bind a contract with them + create an inescapable timing window.

**Clue Point 3 — Gear District Difference Engine Center**
- Academic / Mysticism challenge: Someone recently queried the difference engine's encrypted records — keywords: "Blasphemy Slate," "Ancient Sun God," "Blackwood."
- The difference engine shows these queries came from different identities but the same handwriting — someone is using fake identities to study a dangerous subject involving ancient history.

**Clue Point 4 — Ash Harbor back alleys**
- Social challenge: Pump gang members for information about the Gear Council.
- Information: The Gear Council is a rising power that emerged in the last two years. Most members are artisans, inventors, and difference-engine programmers. But they don't sell things — they are "building things."
- Deep intel (high-difficulty Negotiation / bribe): The Gear Council's founder is a former Mistgrave University professor named "Blackwood." Five years ago he was expelled by the Church of the Steam and Machinery for "illegally researching the Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate" — he was originally a Savant Pathway Sequence 4 genius scholar.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Gear Council Stronghold (abandoned clock tower in the Gear District)**
- Environment: The clock tower has been converted into a mechanical workshop and difference-engine server room. Gears, conveyor belts, and steam pipes fill the space.
- Discovery: The workshop holds several half-finished Sealed Artifacts — the Gear Council's goal is not to collect Sealed Artifacts, but to "disassemble" them and reassemble new items.
- Document discovery: Experiment logs show they are trying to decode an "encrypted Third Epoch slate rubbing" — Dr. Blackwood believes the slate records the origin of the Pathway system. He uses the difference engine as a decoding tool, while Gear Council members collect Sealed Artifacts across the city — because the ancient spirituality remaining in certain Sealed Artifacts can aid decoding.

**Scene 2 — The Bronze-Masked Woman**
- Antagonist: **Engineer Valerie** — Savant Pathway Sequence 6 "Artisan," Dr. Blackwood's deputy.
- She is using the three stolen Sealed Artifacts to assemble a new item: the "Spiritual Decoder."
- Purpose: Use this device to crack a rubbing of a Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate — Dr. Blackwood needs the "decoder" to read the slate's secrets about the origin of the Pathways.

**Enemy Stats**:
- Valerie | Savant Pathway Sequence 6 | CR: 5
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 40 / Mind (MND) 60 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 45 / Charisma (CHA) 35
- HP: 115 / Spirit Pool (SP): 125 / Loss of Control (LoC): 20 / Threshold (LoCT): 130
- Attacks: Steam Jet (+20 / 2d8 Fire / 10m / knockback), Wrench Smash (+20 / 2d6+10 / melee)
- Powers: Machine Control (1 AP / 3 Spirituality / control difference engine and environmental machinery), Precision Traps (prep / set 3 mechanical traps), Sealed Artifact Disassembly (can dismantle a Sealed Artifact to obtain parts)
- Holds Sealed Artifact: Blood Pact Goblet (can forcibly bind contracts; used to muster Gear Council members)
- Weakness: Her difference engine is the source of her powers — destroying it halves all her abilities; she does not understand Mysticism, and has Disadvantage when pitting pure machinery against Beyonder powers.
- Tactics: Uses conveyor belts and mechanical traps to control the battlefield. Once the engine is damaged she joins the fight personally.

**Scene 3 — Choose 1 of 3**
- Pursue Valerie (gain Blackwood's trail, but the Sealed Artifacts remain in her hands).
- Recover the Sealed Artifacts (protect the three Sealed Artifacts, but Valerie escapes).
- Infiltrate the difference engine for data (gain all of Blackwood's research, but Valerie notices and detonates the engine).
- The players can only complete two of these; the third will fail due to lack of time.

## Act 3: The Choice

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| Prioritize Pursuit | Defeat Valerie, gain the doctor's whereabouts | Blackwood's research notes + Valerie's confession (she was forced to participate) + 300 Progress |
| Prioritize Recovery | The three Sealed Artifacts are safely recovered | Old John's permanent discount + Sealed Artifact borrowing rights + 250 Progress |
| Prioritize Data | Gain all of the doctor's experiment data | Understand the "Mechanical Heart" threat + gain key knowledge to oppose the doctor + 350 Progress |

**Introducing Blackwood**: No matter the choice, the players will discover one name threading through it all — "Dr. Blackwood." Valerie says: "He's not a bad man… he just found something he shouldn't have. That slate — what's written on it — he said it's humanity's only hope."

**Loss-of-Control Risk**: Contact with stolen Sealed Artifacts +1d5 / difference engine malfunction explosion +1d3 / Spirit Exhaustion +2d5

# Scenario 4: The Mechanical Heart

**Recommended Level**: Sequence 6→5 (suggest 4–6 Sequence 6 characters) | **Duration**: 4–5 sessions

## Background
Dr. Blackwood — Savant Pathway Sequence 4 "Mechanical Heart," former Mistgrave University professor, difference-engine genius. Five years ago he was expelled by the Church of the Steam and Machinery, because what he researched was not mechanical engineering — but a Third Epoch slate rubbing he found in the university's underground archive. The doctor spent five years using the difference engine to decode the slate's encrypted text. When he finally succeeded — what appeared on screen was not a formula, but a warning written in ancient script: **"The doomsday countdown has begun. Gather the scattered slate fragments, seek the cracks in the barrier — before They enter."**

Blackwood believes this slate is part of the Third Epoch "Blasphemy Slate" left by the Ancient Sun God — the complete slate is said to record the ultimate method to oppose the Outer Deities. His decoding work activated the ancient spirituality remaining in the slate fragments — and that spirituality is losing control.

## Hooks (choose 1 of 3)
1. **Pursuit**: The Blackwood clues seeded across the previous three scenarios converge into a clear threat.
2. **Omen**: All Seer Pathway players receive the same prophecy: "When the gears mesh, the world gains one more crack."
3. **Declaration of War**: Blackwood issues a public challenge to the Nighthawks / Church of the Steam and Machinery — "You hid the doomsday. I will make the slate's contents public. Everyone has the right to know — the Outer Deities are knocking."

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — Mistgrave University Old Laboratory**
- Blackwood's laboratory, sealed five years ago. On the dust-covered difference-engine screen remains a fragment of decoded text: "Fragment 1/9. Collect all nine. Before the barrier splits —"
- Academic challenge (very hard): Understand the meaning of this text → Blackwood believes he found a rubbing fragment of a Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate in the university archive. The complete slate has nine pieces — scattered across the world. He found the first.
- Mysticism: The Blasphemy Slate was created by the Ancient Sun God before his fall — it records Pathway formulas and knowledge to oppose the Outer Deities. The Seven Churches each guard part of the slate fragments, but have never made them public.

**Clue Point 2 — Gear District Anomaly Report**
- Anomaly statistics for the past week:
  - 17 difference engines simultaneously output the same encrypted code.
  - Factory owners report "machines working on their own without instructions."
  - 3 "machine out-of-control" incidents in the Gear District — machines suddenly attacked workers.
- Investigation finds: All anomalies occur within 3 km of the central difference-engine tower in the Gear District.
- Spiritual clue: A powerful ancient spirituality exists in the tower area — the residual energy released when the slate fragment was decoded is losing control.

**Clue Point 3 — Blackwood's Past**
- Social challenge: Find Blackwood's former colleague / wife.
- His wife: "He's not mad. He just saw what we cannot. He used to stay up all night, staring at that slate — then suddenly start crying. He said 'They are knocking. We must be ready.'"
- His former colleague (a retired deacon of the Church of the Steam and Machinery): "He said that slate was a Third Epoch thing — a relic of the Ancient Sun God. The church hierarchy knew of its existence, but chose to suppress the news. Blackwood said — 'If the church won't make it public, I will.'"

**Clue Point 4 — The Root of the Slate's Spiritual Loss of Control**
- Mysticism challenge (legendary difficulty): Why does decoding the slate fragment cause spiritual loss of control?
- Answer: When the Ancient Sun God created the slate, he buried a "protective seal" within the fragments. Forcibly decoding with a difference engine bypassed the seal — the ancient spirituality in the slate fragment was activated but is no longer controlled. Driven by instinct, this spirituality will "seek other fragments" — and the other fragments are scattered across the world. When the spirituality cannot find its target, it spreads explosively — destroying everything in range.
- The only solution: A Beyonder holds the slate fragment and uses their own spirituality to "tame" the fragment's runaway spirituality — that is, with their own will, performs the function the broken seal should have fulfilled.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Gear District Central Tower Siege**
- Blackwood's last line of defense: the Gear Council's elite members + 6 modified "Mechanical Sentinels."
- Environment: The central difference-engine tower in the Gear District — a 50-meter-tall steel monolith. Inside is a maze of gears, steam pipes, and difference-engine units. The fight is also a puzzle — you must find the right pipe route to reach the top floor.
- Goal: Break through the defense and reach the top-floor laboratory.

**Mechanical Sentinel** | Savant Pathway Sequence 7 | CR: 3
- HP: 90 / Evasion: −15
- Attacks: Steam Cannon (+20 / 3d8 Fire / 30m), Mechanical Arm (+20 / 2d8+10 / melee)
- Powers: Self-Destruct (triggers at HP < 10 / 3d6 area Fire damage)
- Weakness: Lightning attacks can short-circuit it for 2 rounds; oil / water can damage precision machinery.

**Scene 2 — Blackwood's Showdown**
- Dr. Blackwood | Savant Pathway Sequence 4 "Mechanical Heart" | CR: 9
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 70 / Mind (MND) 80 / Body (BOD) 40 / Agility (AGI) 35 / Charisma (CHA) 20
- HP: 130 / Spirit Pool (SP): 215 / Loss of Control (LoC): 65 / Threshold (LoCT): 170
- Environment: Rooftop laboratory — the difference-engine core array. Giant gears turn slowly on the ceiling. The doctor stands at the center of the array.
- Powers:
  - Mechanical Domination (passive): Controls all mechanical devices on the battlefield. Gears can become weapons, pipes can vent steam.
  - Difference-Engine Calculation (1 AP / 5 Spirituality): Analyzes the enemy's combat pattern; next round +30 attack against that target.
  - Rule Overwrite (2 AP / 15 Spirituality): Briefly rewrites one combat rule — e.g., "double gravity" / "time slowdown" / "invert fire damage."
  - Mechanical Construct (2 AP / 20 Spirituality): Summons a mechanical construct from the difference-engine array (CR = 6).
  - Mental Broadcast (1 AP / 3 Spirituality): Directly sends the slate contents he decoded into the players' minds — "You've been deceived — the doomsday is no legend. They are beyond the barrier. We must be ready."
- Weakness: The difference-engine array is the source of his life — destroying 30% of its components drops his powers to Sequence 5 level. Fully destroying the array strips him of Beyonder powers.

**Tactical Turn**: When HP drops to 50%, the doctor activates the difference-engine array's final form — channeling the slate fragment's ancient spirituality into the difference-engine network. Each round, the array releases a spiritual pulse to the surroundings — all present take 2d6 spiritual damage, LoC +1. The doctor does not intend the destruction — the slate fragment's spirituality has exceeded his control.

**Scene 3 — Awakening of the Slate**
- The ancient Sun God's spirituality remaining in the slate fragment begins to manifest — a Third Epoch historical projection appears in the lab: the Ancient Sun God standing on a pre-fall battlefield, shattering the slate into nine pieces.
- All players witness the Third Epoch historical projection → LoC +1d10+5.
- The doctor's body begins to be "overwritten" by the slate fragment's spirituality — the slate fragment is turning him into a "vessel" to store and control the runaway spirituality.
- The moment of choice: A decision must be made before the doctor is fully overwritten.

## Act 3: The Ultimate Choice

**Revelation**: Blackwood was never after power from the start. Five years ago he found the slate fragment's rubbing in the university archive and spent five years decoding it. When he finally succeeded — he saw not a formula, but a warning: "The doomsday countdown has begun." He could not accept the church hiding this fact — so he decided to make the slate's contents public with his difference engine. The slate's spiritual loss of control was an accident — he never meant to endanger Gray Mist City.

| Choice | Execution | Result | Reward |
|------|---------|------|------|

The doctor retains partial powers (drops to Sequence 5), the player gains full use rights of the slate fragment. **Common thread across all endings**: At the last moment the doctor looks at the slate fragment in your hand and smiles. "It told me — you would come." He closes his eyes. "Now — go find the remaining eight. Where I cannot keep up — walk to the end."
