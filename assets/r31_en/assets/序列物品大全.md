# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Complete Sequence Item Compendium for the 22 Pathways v1.0

> **Contents:** The pathway-exclusive sequence items of every pathway — ritual items, pathway enhancement items, pathway relics, potion auxiliary materials, and pathway-associated Sealed Artifact prototypes.
> **Purpose:** GM reward design, black-market trades, character customization, and pathway side-quests.

---

## Item Type Glossary

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Ritual Item** | Required or supplementary for a promotion ritual | Key to the Spirit World Gate (Apprentice pathway) |
| **Pathway Enhancement** | Temporarily strengthens a pathway ability after use | Seer's Essential Oil (Divination check +10) |
| **Pathway Relic** | A remnant left by a deceased Beyonder of that pathway, possibly carrying residual intent | Fallen Magician's Paper Figure |
| **Potion Auxiliary Material** | Auxiliary material for a specific pathway's potion, determines potion quality | Core of the Deep (Sailor pathway) |
| **Sealed Artifact Prototype** | A pathway item that can be crafted into a Sealed Artifact | A Marionettist's Last Marionette |
