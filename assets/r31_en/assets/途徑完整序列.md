# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Complete Pathway Sequences for the 22 Pathways v1.0

> **Scope:** All 22 pathways, Sequences 9→5 (player-usable) + Sequences 4→0 summary (GM reference).
> **Player Range:** Sequences 9–5 are the main range for street-to-city-level adventures.
> **NPC / Endgame:** Sequences 4–0 are for GMs designing high-authority entities, church higher-ups, and final villains.
