# Monster & NPC Compendium (Bestiary)

> This file collects common Beyonder enemies, Spirit World creatures, and summonable/assisting NPC templates for the Lord of the Mysteries TRPG.
> Stats use the d100 rules: five attributes (Spirituality/Mind/Body/Agility/Charisma), HP = Body×2+15, Loss of Control (LoC) applies to Beyonders only.
> Team-version CR and solo-version CR differ: solo-version enemy attributes and HP are scaled by roughly ×0.35; see each scenario for details.

## I. Wild Beyonders at the Edge of Loss

### 【Corrupted Diviner】 CR: 2 | Seer Pathway · Sequence 9
- Attributes: SPI 45 MND 30 BOD 35 AGI 30 CHA 10
- HP: 115 / Spirit Pool: 140
- Loss of Control: 58 / Threshold: 70 (Whisper stage)
- Attack: Dagger (+10/1d8+7), Mad Throw (+5/1d4+7/10m)
- Abilities: Dangerous Premonition (reaction/weakened), Pendulum Tracking (corrupted → points to the target with highest Spirituality)
- Weakness: Mental damage ×1.5
- Tactics: Paranoid; attacks the highest-Spirituality target; flees when HP < 30

## II. Sequence Antagonist Beyonders

### 【Witch Seraphina】 CR: 5 | Assassin Pathway · Sequence 7 Witch
- Attributes: SPI 55 MND 50 BOD 40 AGI 55 CHA 60
- HP: 130 / Spirit Pool: 170
- Attack: Poisoned Dagger (+25/1d8+11+Poison), Shadow Arrow (+20/2d8 Dark+Curse/15m), Spider Silk Bind (+15/Bind/15m)
- Abilities: Sneak Attack Specialization, Black Magic Curse (−15/3 rounds), Charm Eye (1 AP), Spider Walk (passive)
- Resistance: Dark halved | Weakness: Mental attack −2
- Tactics: Cunning; first-round Charm + control; retreats when HP < 50%

### 【Foreman Morrison】 (solo version) CR: 2 | Secrets Suppliant Pathway · Sequence 8 Listener
- Attributes: SPI 45 MND 40 BOD 30 AGI 30 CHA 35
- HP: 75 / Spirit Pool: 140
- Attack: Shadow Touch (+15/2d6 Dark/melee)
- Abilities: Shadow Stealth (1 AP/5 Spirituality), Listen to Whispers (gains intel but LoC +1d3)
- Weakness: −15 to all checks when confronted with evidence of his former identity (photographs)
- Note: Team version is CR 4 Sequence 7, HP 130, with Sacrificial Blade and Summon Shadow Servant abilities

### 【Lady White】 CR: 6 | Spectator Pathway · Sequence 6 Hypnotist
- Attributes: SPI 55 MND 50 BOD 35 AGI 45 CHA 65
- HP: 115 / Spirit Pool: 170 / Loss of Control: 45 / Threshold: 110
- Abilities: Dancing Hypnosis (1 AP/5 SP/20m/area Charm), Emotional Storm (2 AP/10 SP/all enemies), Beat Control (reaction/controls hypnotized targets' actions)
- Weakness: MND −20 when confronted with the truth; actively showing memories of the fire can shake her abilities
- Tactics: Uses controlled guests as a front line; triggers "Eternal Waltz" rampage when HP < 50%

### 【Fallen Silver Knight】 (corrupted by the Mother Tree of Desire) CR: 12 (solo 4) | Warrior Pathway · Sequence 3 (corrupted)
- Attributes: SPI 40 MND 30 (severely corrupted) BOD 75 AGI 60 CHA 5
- HP: 165 / Spirit Pool: 120 (recovers 10 HP per round; only Spiritual attacks can stop it)
- Loss of Control: 70 (corruption greatly lowers Threshold)
- Abilities: Corrupted Cleave (1 AP/5 SP/melee/3d10+15 Dark+Physical), Outer Deity Shield (reaction/10 SP), Desire Screech (2 AP/15 SP/all enemies/Mind opposed), Dawn Blade (3 AP/4d12+20 Light)
- Weakness: Third Epoch purification runes deal 3d6 Light damage per round; holding a slate fragment near temporarily reduces Outer Deity corruption (−10 to checks)

## III. Non-Human Monsters (Spirit World Creatures)

### 【Spirit World Dream Eater】 CR: 4 | Native Spirit World Predator
- Attributes: SPI 40 MND 20 BOD 50 AGI 45 CHA 5
- HP: 180 / Psychic Reserve: 60
- Attack: Spirit Tentacle (+20/2d8+10 Mental+Whisper/10m), Dream Devour (+15/3d6/only sleeping targets/drains 50% HP), Psychic Screech (auto-hit/1d8 Mental/15m radius)
- Abilities: Etherealize (1 AP/immune to physical/ hit by Light & Lightning), Dream Walk (vanishes for 1 round then reappears 30m away), Fear Aura (10m/every 3 rounds)
- Resistance: Physical halved (non-silver), Mental immune
- Weakness: Light ×1.5, Silver ×1.5 and ignores Etherealize, Lightning ×1.5 and suppresses Etherealize for 1 round
- Tactics: Prioritizes the highest-LoC target; Etherealizes to escape when Psychic Reserve is low
