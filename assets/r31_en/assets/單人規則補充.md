# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Solo Rules Supplement v1.0

> **Applicable Scenario:** 1 player + 1 GM (one-on-one group)
> **Reference Base:** Player Rulebook (core checks, character creation, combat), GM Rulebook (encounter design, reputation system)
> **Design Principle:** You are not alone — you have companions. But your choices weigh more than in a team game.

---

# I. Why Supplemental Rules Are Needed

The current rules assume a party of 4–5 players. The specific problems a one-on-one group faces:

| Problem | Impact |
|------|------|
| CR encounter budget calculated for 4–5 people | Facing a CR=5 enemy solo is suicide |
| Scenarios assume party Pathway diversity | Solo has only one Pathway, cannot cover all scene needs |
| No NPC companion combat rules | Lack of tank / healer / controller roles |
| Social encounters assume multi-person discussion | Cannot run "party debate" type scenes |
| Clue acquisition depends on multiple Pathways | Seer cannot see spirits? No more clues |

# II. NPC Companion System (Core Supplement)

## 2.1 Companion Rules

The player may recruit up to **2 NPC Companions** during adventures. Companions fall into two types:

| Type | Control Method | Roleplaying Method |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Combat Companion** | Player fully controls in combat (including actions, power use, reactions) | GM plays personality, gives suggestions, interacts in social scenes |
| **Non-Combat Companion** | Does not participate in combat | GM plays, provides plot help, intel, professional knowledge |

## 2.2 Companion Attributes

Companions use a simplified attribute card. Unlike player characters, companions **do not accumulate Acting Progress**, **do not perform Sequence promotions** (unless the plot requires). A companion's Sequence is fixed.

A companion's Sequence is usually 1–2 levels below the player (e.g., player Sequence 9, companion Sequence 9–8; player Sequence 7, companion Sequence 8–6).

## 2.3 Companion Combat Actions

In combat, the companion shares the round with the player:

- **Player round**: Player 3 AP + each combat companion 2 AP
- Companions use simplified Pathway powers (see templates below)
- Companions cannot use reaction actions (no evasion / block), but the player may spend their own reaction AP to block for a companion (must be adjacent)

## 2.4 Companion Casualties

- Companion HP ≤ 0 → companion falls, auto-recovers to 1 HP at combat end after stabilization
- Companions do not permanently die (unless the plot requires). A companion withdraws from the current adventure when gravely wounded, recovers next adventure
- After a companion is gravely wounded, the player gains a "Loneliness" state: all Social checks −10, until the companion recovers

## 2.5 Companion Loyalty

Each companion has Loyalty (1–10). Starting value determined by background.

| Loyalty Change | Trigger |
|-----------|------|
| +1 | Protect a companion from grave wounds during an adventure |
| +1 | Respect the companion's opinion (even if different from your own) |
| +2 | The companion's core motivation is satisfied |
| −1 | Ignore the companion's warning |
| −2 | Act against the companion's morals in front of them |
| −3 | Companion gravely wounded (single time) |

**Loyalty Effects:**

| Loyalty | Effect |
|--------|------|
| 8+ | Companion gains +10 to all checks in combat. Proactively provides hidden intel |
| 5–7 | Normal cooperation |
| 3–4 | −5 to checks in combat. Occasionally refuses high-risk actions |
| 1–2 | May leave the party (before next adventure). Does not proactively use powers in combat |

# III. Solo Encounter Adjustments

## 3.1 CR Scaling

Multiply the original CR encounter budget by **0.35** (1 player + 1–2 companions ≈ 2.5-person equivalent):

| Original Difficulty | Original CR | Solo CR | Note |
|--------|-------|---------|------|
| Easy | 3–4 | 1–2 | 1–2 CR 1/2 enemies |
| Normal | 5–7 | 2–3 | 1 CR 2–3 enemy |
| Hard | 8–10 | 3–4 | 1 CR 3 enemy + environment |
| Lethal | 11–14 | 4–5 | 1 CR 4–5 strong enemy |

> Solo groups **should not pile on mobs**. One strong enemy suits the solo experience better than four minions. Cap on minions = 3.

## 3.2 Solo Challenge Rating Reference

| Player Sequence | Companion Setup | Solvable CR |
|----------|---------|----------|
| 9 | 1 Sequence 9 companion | CR 2 (normal) / CR 3 (hard) |
| 8 | 1 Sequence 8 companion | CR 3 (normal) / CR 4 (hard) |
| 7 | 1–2 Sequence 7–8 companions | CR 4 (normal) / CR 5 (hard) |
| 6 | 2 Sequence 6–7 companions | CR 5 (normal) / CR 7 (hard) |
| 5 | 2 Sequence 5–6 companions | CR 7 (normal) / CR 9 (hard) |

# IV. Solo Investigation and Social Adjustments

## 4.1 Investigation

- **Remove the "3 investigations per scene per person" limit** — a solo player may make up to 6 Investigation checks
- **Simplify clue tiers**: Core clues and Key clues merge into "certainly discovered" (as long as the correct location is investigated)
- **Companion investigation assistance**: A non-combat companion may make one independent Investigation check (using own attributes + skills); the GM reports the result

## 4.2 Social

- **Remove the social-opposition "multi-faction" mode** — it is always the player vs. a single NPC, or GM-played interaction between companions
- **Companions can provide "social assistance"**: In a social opposition, the player may ask a companion to supplement one argument, gaining +10 to the check (once per social scene)
- **Conflict between companions is also plot**: If the player decides against a companion's morals, it may trigger a Loyalty change

## 4.3 Pathway Gap Filling

A solo group has only one Pathway. Handling for the following scenes:

| Scene | Alternative when no related Pathway |
|------|----------------------|
| Need Divination / Spirit Sight | Use the Sealed Artifact "Honest Pendulum" (GM may grant in advance) or a companion with that power |
| Need to treat Loss of Control | Use a sedative (£8/bottle) or companion is Spectator / Apothecary Pathway |
| Need Tracking | Use the standard Investigation skill (Mind + Investigation) instead of Hunter Pathway |
| Need to breathe underwater | Use potion / Sealed Artifact or companion is Sailor Pathway |
| Need mind reading | Use Social skill (Charisma + Negotiation) instead |

> **GM Core Principle:** In a solo group, the Pathway the player chooses is "the correct Pathway." Do not design checkpoints that must use a specific Pathway to pass. An alternative always exists.

The following 4 companion templates cover the main character positions. The GM may customize on this basis.

|------|-----|

| Pathway / Sequence | Warrior Pathway Sequence 9 (can rise to Sequence 7 as player advances) |
| Attributes | Spirituality (SPI) 25 / Mind (MND) 40 / Body (BOD) 55 / Agility (AGI) 45 / Charisma (CHA) 35 |
| HP | = Body×2+15 = 125 |
| Spirit Pool | = Spirituality×3+5 = 80 |

- Taunt (1 AP / 3 Spirituality / 20m): Mind opposed, success → target forced to attack this companion for 1 round

# VI. Companion Growth

A companion's Sequence can advance with the plot (GM decides timing, usually no more than 2 levels from the player's Sequence):

| Player Sequence | Suggested Companion Sequence | Growth Method |
|----------|------------|---------|
| 9 | 9 | Starting |
| 8 | 8–9 | Companion "digests" on its own — no Acting Progress needed |
| 7 | 7–8 | Triggered by plot event (e.g., companion's personal subplot) |
| 6 | 6–7 | Same as above |
| 5 | 5–6 | Companion's Sequence 5 promotion needs a full ritual (completed with player assistance) |

# VII. Solo Group GM Core Principles

1. **You are the player's teammate, not opponent.** In a one-on-one group, the GM's default stance is "make the player's story brilliant," not "defeat the player."
2. **Companions are tools, not crutches.** Companions should not solve all the player's problems — only fill Pathway gaps and provide emotional support.
3. **Failure is not the end.** A solo group has no "party wipe." Failure transforms into a new plot line — arrested, rescued, extorted.
4. **Slow the pace.** A solo group's plot density should be lower than a team group's. Give the player more RP space — because no one is "queuing to speak."
5. **Pathway gap = plot opportunity.** The player is a Seer but needs to fight? That is not a bug — that is the plot for finding a Guardian companion.
