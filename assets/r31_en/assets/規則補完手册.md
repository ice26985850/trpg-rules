# 🔴 P0-1: Investigation & Clue System

## Clue Tiers

| Tier | Description | Acquisition Difficulty | Can It Be Missed? | Example |
|------|-------------|------------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Core Clue** | A clue that must be discovered to advance the plot | Auto-discovered (so long as the correct location is investigated) | ❌ Cannot be missed | Beyonder Characteristic residue remains inside the victim |
| **Key Clue** | Provides important background but cannot solve the puzzle alone | Discovered on a successful check; partial info on failure | ⚠️ May be incomplete | The perpetrator's Pathway type |
| **Auxiliary Clue** | Provides extra advantage or background depth | Successful check + specific condition | ✅ Can be missed | The victim's secret relation to a certain faction |
| **Misleading Clue** | Leads in the wrong direction | Auto-discovered | ❌ Cannot be missed (but can be seen through) | An apparent suspect — actually innocent |

## Investigation Action Framework

For each investigation scene, the GM prepares 2–4 "clue packages" behind the screen. Each clue package contains:
- The clue's content
- How to acquire it (Observation / Listen / Investigation / Knowledge / Occultism / Spirit Sight / other)
- Its corresponding Core / Key / Auxiliary / Misleading tier
- The check DC (if required)

**Standard Investigation Procedure:**

1. **Scene Description**: The GM describes the scene (including only "conspicuous but uninteresting" surface information)
2. **Player Declares Investigation Method**: The player states which skill/ability they use to investigate what ("I use Observation to check the documents on the desk")
3. **Clue Return**:
   - Core / Misleading clues: auto-obtained
   - Key clue: success → full information; failure → partial information ("You see what's written on the document but the handwriting is blurred, possibly XXX")
   - Auxiliary clue: obtained on success + fulfilled condition
4. **The "20 Questions" Cap**: Each scene allows each player at most 3 independent Investigation checks. This prevents players from "searching every single corner of the entire room"

## Investigation Methods by Pathway

| Pathway | Investigation Advantage | Unique Acquisition Method |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Seer** | Pendulum divination points toward hidden clues | Spirit Sight to see a replay of local spirit activity (3 SPI / 2-round flashback) |
| **Reader** | Crime Scene Investigation +20 | Speed-read handwritten records (1 AP / reads 100 pages in 10 seconds) |
| **Spectator** | Emotion Reading reveals "who is lying" | Micro-expression analysis identifies a forged scene |
| **Secrets Suppliant** | Listening to Whispers gains forbidden knowledge | Sacrifice ritual asking a hidden entity to reveal the truth (Loss of Control +1d3) |
| **Sleepless** | Never sleeps → no penalty for night investigation | Vision 100% in dark environments (others only 50%) |
| **Corpse Collector** | Autopsy +20 | Last Words (ask someone who died within 72h "who killed you") |
| **Hunter** | Tracking +20 / Trap Detection +10 | Track footprints backward to reconstruct the full movement route |
| **Savant** | Encyclopedic memory. History / science / law recalled at will | Deduce complete background from seemingly unrelated details |
| Other Pathways | Generic skill checks | — |

## Connecting Clues: Reasoning Check

Once players have collected 3+ clues, they may attempt a **Reasoning Check**:

**d100 ≤ Mind (MND) + Investigation skill bonus − (number of clues × 5)**
(the more clues, the harder the reasoning — because there is more noise)

- **Critical Success**: The GM must clearly reveal the true connection between all clues
- **Success**: The GM points out the most likely correct direction + one still-missing piece of information
- **Failure**: The GM points out a "possible" direction — but with one misleading clue mixed in
- **Critical Failure**: The player becomes convinced of a wrong conclusion (the GM secretly records it)

# 🔴 P0-2: Surface Life Mechanics

## Surface Life Levels

After the player selects a Surface Life at character creation, the GM secretly tracks the Exposure Level:

| Level | Status | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **0 — Safe** | No one suspects you | Normal socializing, shopping, and intel-gathering unrestricted |
| **1 — Noticed** | Neighbors/colleagues think you are "a bit odd" | +20% price when buying Beyonder materials (the other party is wary) |
| **2 — Suspected** | Local rumors are spreading | Police may do spot-checks. Social checks −10. Some NPCs refuse contact |
| **3 — Watched** | Official/hostile forces are observing in secret | 50% chance any illegal action is witnessed. Cannot use a fixed safe house |
| **4 — Exposed** | Surface identity is burned | Nighthawks summon you for a talk / wanted. Must change identity or flee. All social ties based on that identity are severed |

## Exposure Value Accumulation

Each time a Beyonder action generates Exposure Value, the GM accumulates it behind the screen onto the Exposure Level. When the accumulated value exceeds the threshold, the Exposure Level rises.

| Action | Exposure Value |
|--------|----------------|
| Using conspicuous Beyonder abilities in public (thunder, flame-leap, werewolf transformation) | +3 |
| Mortals see traces of Beyonder combat (lightning scorch, shadow residue) | +1 |
| Frequently visiting Beyonder locations (Drowned Man Tavern, Warehouse No. 10) | +1 per 3 visits |
| Mortals notice your "anomaly" (not needing sleep, always going out at night) | +1 per week |
| A mortal sees you use a Beyonder item | +2 |
| Reported in the newspaper as connected to a Beyonder incident (even under an alias) | +5 |
| Nighthawks investigate you for another reason | +3 |

## Exposure Thresholds

| Residence District | Exposure Lvl 1 | Exposure Lvl 2 | Exposure Lvl 3 | Exposure Lvl 4 |
|--------------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Crown District (nobility) | 15 | 30 | 50 | 80 |
| Iron Bridge District (middle class) | 10 | 20 | 35 | 55 |
| Gear District / Ash Harbor District (workers) | 8 | 15 | 25 | 40 |
| Sunken Smoke District (slums) | 20 | 40 | 60 | 100 |

> Why are the thresholds higher in the slums? Because "no one cares." The noble district has the lowest thresholds — noble neighbors are the biggest gossips.

## Repairing Surface Life

| Action | Exposure Value Reduced | Cost | Limit |
|--------|------------------------|------|-------|
| Move (change district within the city) | −10 | £5–20 | At most 1 per quarter |
| Change identity | Exposure Level reset to 0 | £50–200 + Social check | All old social ties vanish |
| Bribe witness | −5 per person | Charisma (CHA) check | Once per person |
| "Return to normal life" for a month | −3 | — | May not use Beyonder abilities for that month |
| Use Spectator Pathway ability to alter memory | −10 | Sequence 6+ | Loss of Control +1d3 per use |

## Consequences of Surface Life Exposure

| Exposure Level | Triggered Consequence |
|----------------|-----------------------|
| 3 (Watched) | GM secretly rolls — each time the player thinks they are acting "safely," there is a 50% chance the Nighthawks are recording it in secret |
| 4 (Exposed) | Nighthawks come to talk (social scene). Choice: sign the Nighthawk Service Agreement (become an official Beyonder) or be taken into custody |

> **Design Intent:** This system is not meant to punish players — it provides mechanical tension for the "double life." Exposure is a story hook, not game over.

# 🔴 P0-3: Social Conflict System

## Social Conflict Framework

When a social scene reaches the level of a "key confrontation" (negotiation, interrogation, debate, winning an ally), use the following structured framework in place of a single check:

### Turn-based Social

**Social Initiative = Charisma (CHA) + d10 (same as combat Initiative)**

Each turn, each participant may take 1 **Social Action** (equivalent to 1 AP in combat):

| Action | Check | Success Effect | Failure Effect |
|--------|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **Present Argument** | Negotiation / Deception / Knowledge | Target's Conviction −1d3 | Own Conviction −1 |
| **Emotional Appeal** | Charisma (CHA) (Negotiation +10) | Target's Conviction −1d4+1 | Target's Conviction +1 (seen through) |
| **Intimidate** | Body (BOD) (Intimidation +10) | Target's Conviction −2 but relations worsen | Own Conviction −2 |
| **Expose Weakness** | Mind (MND) (Investigation) | Learn the target's current Conviction value | — |
| **Find Opening** | Observation / Mind Reading | Next turn's Social Action check +20 | No penalty |
| **Delay / Defend** | Charisma (CHA) | This turn, target's Social Action check −15 | None |
| **Bribe / Entice** | Charisma (CHA) + Funds | Target's Conviction −1d6 (only in scenes solvable by money) | Target's Conviction +1d3 (offended) |
| **Reveal Truth** | Spirituality (SPI) (Occultism) | Strike the target's Conviction core directly with Beyonder knowledge / Spirit Sight intel — Conviction −1d4+2 | Loss of Control +1 (what you saw should not be spoken) |
| **Call for Help / Retreat** | — | Exit the social conflict (target gains a "last impression") | — |

### Conviction (Social HP)

**Conviction = 10 + Mind modifier (Mind/5) + Social relation**

Social relation modifiers:
- The target's attitude toward the player: Trusting +5 / Friendly +3 / Neutral +0 / Wary −3 / Hostile −5
- How much the target cares about the matter: Core conviction +10 / Important +5 / Ordinary +0 / Trivial −5 (but still negotiable)

When Conviction drops to 0 → the target is persuaded / concedes / breaks down (depending on context). At half, the target may change strategy (from hostile → negotiation, or from negotiation → more hostile).

### Social Critical Success / Critical Failure

| Roll | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Critical Success (≤5) | Conviction damage doubled; and the target exposes a "true thought" (GM reveals one extra hidden piece of info) |
| Critical Failure (≥96) | Social conflict ends — you made an unforgivable mistake. Conviction backlash +3 to yourself |

### Multi-person Social

If 3+ people take part in the social conflict, use side formation:
- Participants of the same camp share a Conviction pool
- Different camps act in alternating turns
- When all members' Conviction pool of one camp hits 0 = that camp is persuaded / concedes

# 🔴 P0-4: Pathway Synergy Rules

## Synergy Types

### Type A: Ability Stacking

When two (+) abilities of the same type from the same Pathway act on the same target simultaneously:

| Pathway Combination | Synergy Effect | Example |
|---------------------|----------------|---------|
| Seer + Reader | Target's weakness perfectly analyzed — next hit is guaranteed | Reader analyzes weakness + Seer's pendulum locates weakness = attack auto Critical Success |
| Sailor + Bard | Holy Light + Lightning = Holy Thunderstorm | Sailor summons lightning (3d6) + Bard's Holy Light Bolt (2d8) = 5d6+2d8 holy lightning (×3 vs Dark) |
| Spectator + Lawyer | Mind Reading + loophole = unbeatable interrogation | Spectator reads thoughts + Lawyer finds logical loophole = target's Conviction directly −5 |
| Sleepless + Corpse Collector | Darkness + Undead = undead appear in daylight | Sleepless creates dark zone + Corpse Collector summons undead = undead all attributes +50% |
| Warrior + Hunter | Guardian tanks damage + Reaper executes = perfect combo | Warrior's Guardian Oath protects the Hunter; when the Hunter makes a precise shot, the Warrior provides an extra +20 to hit |
| Secrets Suppliant + Assassin | Shadow + sneak attack = an assassination that does not exist | Secrets Suppliant's Shadow Stealth covers the ally; the Assassin can sneak-attack in shadow without penalty |
| Spectator + Secrets Suppliant | ⚠️ Conflict: Hypnosis and Whispers interfere with each other | Both used simultaneously → target's Mind check +20 (the two mental influences cancel out) |
| Warrior + Prisoner | Guardian + Berserk = controllable berserk | Warrior's Guardian Oath negates the Prisoner's Berserk side effect of "can't tell friend from foe" |

### Type B: Sequential Synergy

Two characters use abilities in the same turn one after another; the second user's check gains Advantage:

**Condition:** Both characters act within the same turn + within 10m of each other + the second ability has a logical link to the first (GM adjudicates)

### Type C: Prepared Combo

A free action (0 AP) declares: "I prepare a combo [Ability 1] + [Ability 2] with [ally name]." The GM records it. When the trigger condition is met, it executes as a reaction.

**Cost:** Each participant spends 1 AP (prepaid from next turn)

**Effect:** The effects of both abilities stack with no decay. If either ability allows a save — the target resists against the higher of the two participants' attributes.

### Type D: Pathway Conflict

The following Pathway combinations produce unexpected side effects when attempting to "cooperate":

| Pathway A | Pathway B | Conflict Effect |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Bard | Secrets Suppliant | Light and Dark repel — when within 10m of each other, each one's ability check −10 |
| Spectator | Marauder | Mind Reading vs Deception — when targeting the same subject, both at disadvantage |
| Arbiter | Lawyer | Rules vs Loophole — two "rule manipulations" active within 3m simultaneously → randomly triggers a "Rule Paradox" (GM picks from the paradox table) |

# 🔴 P0-5: Large-scale Combat & Command System

## Abstraction Rules

When combat scale exceeds 6v6 (total participants > 12), it is no longer resolved person-by-person. Use **Unit** and **Wave** concepts instead:

### Unit Attributes

One "Unit" = an aggregate of 5–10 similar combatants.

| Unit Attribute | Calculation |
|----------------|-------------|
| Unit HP | Individual HP × headcount / 5 |
| Unit Attack | Individual attack + headcount modifier (+5 per 5 people) |
| Unit Damage | Individual damage × √headcount (rounded) |
| Morale | 10 (baseline) — see Morale System |

### Command Check

Beyonders with leadership talent (Hunter / Warrior / Arbiter pathways, or any player the GM deems to have leadership ability) may command:

**Command Check: d100 ≤ (Charisma (CHA) + Leadership skill bonus)**

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Critical Success | All friendly units' attack +20 this turn, Morale +3 |
| Success | All friendly units' attack +10 this turn |
| Failure | No effect |
| Critical Failure | Orders misunderstood — one friendly unit attacks a random target this turn (including allies) |

**Hunter pathway** commanders auto-Advantage on all Command Checks.
**Warrior pathway** commanders grant all friendly units +20% damage.

### Morale System

Each unit has a Morale value (baseline 10).

Morale changes:
- Commander check success: +1 / turn
- Friendly unit collapses: −3
- Enemy unit collapses: +2
- Unit HP < 50%: −2
- Unit HP < 25%: −4
- Facing the unspeakable (god descent / Old One fragment): −5

**Morale Effects:**

| Morale | Status | Effect |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 15+ | Fanatic | Attack +20, but 1% per turn chance to ignore orders (random charge) |
| 8–14 | Normal | Acts normally |
| 4–7 | Shaken | Attack −10, 25% per turn chance to retreat |
| 1–3 | Edge of Collapse | Attack −20, 50% chance to retreat |
| ≤0 | Total Collapse | The unit scatters — permanently leaves combat (unless a Command Critical Success pulls them back) |

### Quick Troop Types (GM picks directly)

| Troop | CR (per 10) | Attack | Damage | HP | Morale |
|-------|-------------|--------|--------|-----|--------|
| Mortal Mob | 1 | +5 | 1d6 | 30 | 6 |
| Armed Militia | 2 | +10 | 1d8+2 | 40 | 8 |
| Regular Soldier | 3 | +15 | 2d6 | 60 | 10 |
| Nighthawk Squad | 4 | +20 | 2d8 | 80 | 12 |
| Gear Council Mech | 5 | +20 | 3d6 | 100 | 14 (no Morale needed — they are machines) |
| Lost Mob | 6 | +25 | 4d6 | 120 | No Morale needed (already mad) |

## Special Rules for the Hunter's Promotion Ritual

The Sequence 5→4 Promotion Ritual requires "lead at least 30 people to victory," using the following simplified rules:

1. **Troops**: The player gains 30 temporary troops (the GM assigns them from the optional troop list based on the plot, usually 2–3 units)
2. **Battlefield**: The GM designs a 3-zone × 3-zone battlefield. Both sides' troops may move between zones
3. **Victory Condition (pick 1 of 2)**:
   - Enemy total casualties ≥ 50% (war of attrition)
   - Enemy commander defeated (decapitation tactic)
4. **Player Character**: May still use their own Beyonder abilities to directly join combat — commanding and fighting personally are not mutually exclusive
5. **Evaluation**: After victory the Nighthawks / Church evaluate — if it was a "real war" (not a small brawl), the ritual is complete

# 🟡 P1-6: Interlude Activities (Downtime)

After each adventure, the character gains **1d3 Interlude Activity points** (determined by adventure length — 1 game session = 1 point, 3+ sessions = 3 points).

Each Interlude Activity costs 1 point:

| Activity | Check | Success Effect | Special |
|----------|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Brew Potion / Craft Item** | Mind (MND) + relevant skill | Crafting complete. Check value determines quality | Apothecary pathway auto-succeeds |

| **Research Work** | Mind (MND) + Knowledge / Occultism | Gain a "Research Progress" mark. 3 marks = unlock one new knowledge / recipe | Savant / Reader speed ×2 |
| **Maintain Surface Life** | Charisma (CHA) | Reduce Exposure Value 1d5 | — |
| **Contact NPC** | Charisma (CHA) + Social | Raise reputation with one faction by 1 level (1→2 needs 3 successes) | — |
| **Gather Intelligence** | Mind (MND) + Investigation | Gain one piece of intel useful for future adventures | — |
| **Treat Long-term Trauma** | Body (BOD) + Medicine | Recover one permanent mental / physical trauma | Needs a doctor NPC or Apothecary pathway |
| **Meditate & Purify** | Spirituality (SPI) | Reduce Loss of Control 2d5 | — |
| **Part-time Work** | — | Gain monthly salary (based on Surface Profession) | Costs 1 Interlude point |

# 🟡 P1-8: Sealed Artifact Crafting Rules

## GM Crafting Procedure (5-step Method)

### Step 1: Determine Source

The power source of a Sealed Artifact:
- **① Relic of a Fallen Beyonder** (most direct): Characteristic naturally attaches
- **② Extracted Beyonder Characteristic** (lab-made): requires a workshop / laboratory
- **③ Natural Evolution of a Pathway Item**: an ordinary item long exposed to high-level entities / Spirit World contamination

### Step 2: Determine Tier & Positive Effect

| Sealed Artifact Tier | Equivalent Sequence | Positive Effect Baseline |
|----------------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Tier 3 | Sequence 9–7 | 1 low-tier ability or check +15/+20 |
| Tier 2 | Sequence 6–5 | 1 mid-tier ability or check +25 or multiple low-tier effects |
| Tier 1 | Sequence 4–2 | 1 high-tier ability or a battlefield-rule-altering effect |
| Tier 0 | Sequence 1–0 | Mythic level (not by the rules — GM designs freely) |

### Step 3: Balance Side Effects

**Side Effect Intensity = Positive Effect × Compensation Factor:**

| Sealed Artifact Tier | Compensation Factor | Side Effect Baseline |
|----------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Tier 3 | 0.5 | Mild: fatigue / headache / minor skill penalty |
| Tier 2 | 0.8 | Moderate: LoC accumulation / attribute penalty / psychological dependence |
| Tier 1 | 1.2 | Severe: permanent damage / will erosion / pathway conflict |
| Tier 0 | 2.0+ | Catastrophic: uncontrollable / GM reserves interpretation rights |

**Side Effect Type Table (d8):**
1. Loss of Control accumulation (each use +1dX)
2. Bodily mutation (attribute penalty, appearance change)
3. Psychological dependence (if not used within 24h after use → LoC +2)
4. Spirit backlash (after use, Spirit Pool extra consumption 50%)
5. Pathway conflict (extra penalty for non-same-Pathway users)
6. Hidden cost (random memory loss / accelerated aging / nightmares)
7. Autonomous consciousness (the Sealed Artifact actively influences the wielder's decisions)
8. Environmental contamination (use location left with Spirit contamination, lasts 1d6 days)

### Step 4: Define Usage Limits

- Uses per day
- Cumulative penalty for continuous use
- Specific conditions ("only effective on the full moon" / "requires faith in a dark entity")
- Cooldown time

### Step 5: Design Hidden Traits

At least 1 hidden trait — a trait that requires an Occultism check (d100 ≤ Mind + Occultism) or a specific condition to be triggered/discovered. May be positive, negative, or mixed.

## Crafting Recipe Baseline

| Sealed Artifact Tier | Required Material Value | Crafting Time | Required Check (d100≤) | Failure Consequence |
|----------------------|------------------------|---------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Tier 3 | £30–60 | 1 week | Mind + skill (60) | 50% material loss |
| Tier 2 | £80–150 | 2 weeks | Mind + skill (75) | Total material loss + LoC +1d5 |
| Tier 1 | £200–400 | 1 month | Mind + skill (90) | Total material loss + LoC +2d5 + explosion (2d10 area) |
| Tier 0 | Cannot be crafted | — | — | Only produced by high-level entities or special historical events |

# 🟡 P1-9: Long-term Loss of Control / Permanent Mental Trauma

## Trauma Acquisition

Whenever a character experiences one of the following, the GM may grant a **permanent Trauma** (max 3 per character):

| Trigger | Trauma Acquisition |
|---------|--------------------|
| Recalled after Full Loss of Control | Forced 1 (related to the LoC cause) |
| 3 consecutive Loss checks passed at the Edge | GM's choice — mental resilience strengthened (no trauma) or after-effect (1 trauma) |
| Witnessed the unspeakable (high-level entity / Old One fragment) | GM's choice |
| Spirit Pool exhausted 3+ consecutive times (same adventure) | GM's choice |

## Trauma Type Table (d20, or GM chooses by context)

| d20 | Trauma | Mechanical Effect | Roleplay Impact |
|-----|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Fear of Darkness** | All checks −10 in darkness | Always needs a light source — even the Sleepless |
| 2 | **Aversion to a Substance** | Charisma (CHA) −10 when seeing that substance | Feels nauseous at the smell of silver (werewolf after-effect) |
| 3 | **Whisper Companion** | Whisper-stage whispers no longer at disadvantage — instead +5 | But you can no longer tell whisper from your own thought |
| 4 | **Permanent Dream** | Repeats the same memory while sleeping — on waking, LoC +1 | You fear sleep |
| 5 | **Scar-type Mutation** | One body region permanently retains its mutated LoC form | Must always conceal it — Charisma (CHA) −5 |
| 6 | **Afterimage of Loss Reaction** | When extremely tense (HP < 25%), LoC +1d3 | You know it is still down there |
| 7 | **Sensitivity to a Pathway** | Approaching that Pathway's Beyonder (within 10m) → auto-success on spirit perception check | That Pathway's people make your skin crawl |
| 8 | **Lingering Sympathy** | You vaguely sense others' physical pain (unintentionally) | You flinch when you see someone hurt |
| 9 | **Amnesia Fragment** | A random stretch of past memory permanently erased | Sometimes others mention something you did — you have no recollection |
| 10 | **Obsession** | Irrational fixation on a target / person / place | You cannot say no to [target] |
| 11 | **Tactile Residue** | Body always feels "something is touching you" — though nothing is | You habitually swat the "hand" off your shoulder |
| 12 | **Mirror Fear** | Looking in a mirror, LoC +1 | You have covered every mirror in your home |
| 13 | **Immortality Delusion** | You truly believe you cannot die — at HP < 10, instead Attack +10 | You no longer value your own life |
| 14 | **Spirit Perception Overload** | When crowded (>20 people), headache +1d3 SPI loss | You hate markets and balls |
| 15 | **Double Thinking** | Each time you make a major decision, the GM offers a "madness version" option | There is another voice in your head always offering "advice" |
| 16 | **Number Compulsion** | A specific number unsettles you — rolling that number = auto disadvantage | You hate [number] — avoid it |
| 17 | **Survivor's Guilt** | When an ally is near death your Attack +10 — but when an ally truly dies, permanent Trauma +1 | You cannot lose anyone else |
| 18 | **Pathway Resonance** | You permanently resonate with a Pathway's "essence" — gain a weakened version of that Pathway's Sequence 9 ability | But sometimes it "uses itself" |
| 19 | **Deep Memory** | An extra "not yours" memory appears in your head — from whom? | This memory is clearer than any of yours |
| 20 | **Evolution** | Not a trauma — you experienced a kind of "purification." Loss of Control Threshold permanently +5 | You are tougher than after your last Loss |

## Removing Trauma

Trauma cannot be removed by ordinary means. The following may lessen or remove trauma:

- Sequence Promotion: each Promotion halves one trauma's mechanical effect
- Intervention by a high-level entity: Sequence 4+ Spectator pathway may perform "deep psychotherapy" (requires a plot-level event)
- Self-redemption: complete a "core Acting" scene directly tied to the trauma's source — GM adjudicates whether it can be removed

# 🟢 P2-11: 5-Minute Player Quick Start

## All You Need to Know to Start Playing

### Rolling Dice

**Roll two ten-sided dice (d100). You want to be below your "Target Number."** Lower is better. ≤5 = Critical Success. ≥96 = Critical Failure.

### Your Five Attributes

- **Spirituality (SPI)** = your "magic value" (what you spend to use abilities)
- **Mind (MND)** = your "sanity value" (the basis for resisting madness)
- **Body (BOD)** = your "health" (HP = Body × 2 + 15)
- **Agility (AGI)** = your "dexterity" (fighting, dodging, initiative)
- **Charisma (CHA)** = your "charm" (social, disguise)

### Making a Character (2-Minute Version)

1. Pick a template (Spirituality Caster / Physical Warrior / Agile Assassin / Social Manipulator / All-round Scholar)
2. Pick a Pathway (choose a cool one from the 8 key pathways)
3. Pick some numbers: d100 Background + d10 Potion + d20 Surface Profession + d8 Motivation = done
4. Calculate HP and Spirit Pool (auto-calculated)

### One Combat Turn

You have **3 Action Points (AP)**. Things you can do:
- Attack (1 AP) — roll d100 ≤ Agility (AGI) + weapon skill
- Use a Beyonder ability (1–2 AP) — spends Spirit Pool
- Move (1 AP) — run 10m
- Dodge (spends 1 AP next turn) — roll d100 ≤ Agility (AGI) to avoid an attack

### The Most Important Rule

**You are playing a game, not "winning" one.** Act as your character — he/she is a Beyonder struggling to survive in a dangerous world. Make decisions true to the character, even if they are not the "optimal" move.
