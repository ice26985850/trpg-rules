# Lord of the Mysteries TRPG — Solo Scenario Series v1.0

> **Target:** 1 player + 1 GM (one-on-one group)
> **Series scope:** 3 scenarios, covering Sequence 9→5
> **Reference rules:** Player Rulebook, Solo Rules Supplement (NPC Companion system, CR scaling)
> **Companion suggestion:** Each scenario recommends 1–2 NPC Companions (chosen from Templates A–D) to assist the player

---

# Scenario 1: The First Case Beneath the Gray Fog

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 9 → Sequence 8 | **Duration:** 2–3 sessions
**Recommended Companion:** Template B (Observer) or Template C (Stormbringer)

## Background

A retired lawyer in the Iron Bridge District — Harold Grant — was found dead in his study. Officially ruled "heart attack." But his daughter does not believe it — she says three days before his death he told her: "Someone put something in the book."

The player is commissioned by the daughter (or takes the commission through the Drowned Man Tavern). Reward: £15 + a family heirloom.

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — The Deceased's Study (Iron Bridge District)**

Core clue (auto-discovered):
- The deceased holds a note in his hand, with only two words: "Second to Last."
- The study has a whole wall of bookshelves — the arrangement looks chaotic, but one bookshelf is cleaner than the others (recently cleaned).

Investigation check (d100 ≤ Mind + Investigation):
- Success: Discover the books on the shelf are arranged by "publication date" — but one book's date was deliberately misplaced.
- Failure: GM says "the bookshelf was cleaned, but nothing special stands out."

**Clue Point 2 — Grant's Office (Iron Bridge District law firm)**

Core clue (auto-discovered):
- The file of Grant's last case before retirement has vanished.
- His secretary says: "Mr. Grant said before retiring that he had finally 'found' something — but didn't say what."

Social check (d100 ≤ Charisma + Negotiation):
- Success: The secretary reveals — Grant's last case was defending a "fired factory worker," against the opponent Morrison Textile Factory in the Gear District.
- Critical Success: The secretary gives a name — "James Morrison." That person is now the new plant manager of Morrison Textile Factory.

**Clue Point 3 — Drowned Man Tavern (Ash Harbor District)**

Intel (requires Negotiation / bribe):
- Grant came to the Drowned Man Tavern every Friday night for the past three months — not to drink, but to meet the same person.
- That person was a worker in the Gear District — no one knows the name. They always sat in the corner, speaking in low voices.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Morrison Textile Factory**

Grant's case file is hidden in the old plant manager's office at Morrison Textile Factory — now abandoned. After sneaking in:
- The file is in the office safe (lockpick d100 ≤ Agility + Sleight of Hand).
- File contents: Grant once defended a worker — that worker accused the new plant manager of Morrison Textile Factory of "not being the real James Morrison."
- The last page of the file reads: "Defendant withdraws the lawsuit. Reason: 'Cannot remember who he was supposed to sue.'"

Spiritual Perception (if the player or companion has related abilities):
- Success: Faint Secrets Suppliant Pathway residue in the plant manager's office — someone performed a memory-related ritual here.

**Scene 2 — The Truth of Grant's Study**

Return to the study and take out the "misplaced book." The book is titled *Workers' Rights in the Industrial Revolution*. Opening it reveals the book was hollowed out — inside hides:
- A photograph: the real James Morrison (the worker) standing with the current Morrison plant manager (a Secrets Suppliant Pathway Beyonder). They look identical.
- Grant's handwritten note: "He is not Morrison. The original Morrison is still alive — just doesn't remember who he is."
- Another note: "The last book. —H.G."

Clue reasoning (d100 ≤ Mind + Investigation − clue count ×5):
- Success: Grant discovered the truth — the current plant manager used Secrets Suppliant Pathway power to "exchange" the real Morrison's memory, replacing his identity. "The last book" does not refer to a book — it means "the last identity." Grant is the "Second to Last."
- Failure: GM gives a partial inference but keeps one misdirection — possibly "the plant manager and Morrison are twins."

**Scene 3 — The Study at Night**

That night, when the player returns to the study, they meet the "Morrison plant manager" — he came to "retrieve the last book." He thinks the book hides evidence proving his true identity (fragments of the original Morrison's memory).

**Enemy Stats (Solo CR adjustment):**

| Enemy | CR | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| Morrison Plant Manager (weakened) | 2 | Sequence 8 Secrets Suppliant rather than Sequence 7. Lowered attributes |

**Morrison Plant Manager (Solo version)** | Secrets Suppliant Pathway Sequence 8 "Listener" | CR: 2
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 45 / Mind (MND) 40 / Body (BOD) 30 / Agility (AGI) 30 / Charisma (CHA) 35
- HP: 75 / Spirit Pool (SP): 140
- Attacks: Shadow Touch (+15 / 2d6 Dark / melee)
- Powers: Shadow Stealth (1 AP / 5 Spirituality), Listen to Whispers (gain intel but LoC +1d3)
- Weakness: −15 to all checks when faced with evidence of his original identity (the photograph).
- Tactics: Sneak attack first, then attempt negotiation once exposed — "You don't understand. I just want to live."

> **GM Adjustment Note:** The team version of Morrison Plant Manager is CR 4 Sequence 7. The solo version is weakened to CR 2 Sequence 8, HP dropped from 130 to 75, removes the Blade of Sacrifice and Summon Shadow Servant powers.

## Act 3: The Choice

Truth revealed: The current Morrison plant manager was originally a poor factory worker — extremely similar in appearance to the real Morrison. Three years ago he obtained a Secrets Suppliant potion and used its power to exchange Morrison's memory and identity, replacing his life. The real Morrison lost his memory and drifted to the Sunken Smoke District.

After discovering the truth, Grant wanted to "restore the original Morrison's memory" — but the plant manager refused, because "if I give it back I'll have nothing."

He came to the study not to silence a witness — he never killed Grant (Grant really did die of a heart attack). He just wanted to retrieve the last piece of evidence.

**Ending branches:**

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Hand to the Nighthawks** | Contact the authorities. Requires a Social check proving the case involves the Beyonders. The plant manager is taken into custody. The real Morrison is rescued (but has lost three years of memory) | Nighthawks favor +15 / 60 Acting Progress / reward £15 |
| **Persuade the plant manager to return the identity** | Social opposed (plant manager Conviction 12). Success → plant manager returns the identity but asks for help to "start over" | Plant manager becomes an NPC relation (can assist in the future) / 80 Acting Progress |
| **Let the plant manager keep the identity but require compensation for Morrison** | Compromise — the plant manager stays plant manager, but must give the original Morrison a job and lodging. Both accept | 70 Acting Progress / gain a "gray-area" NPC ally |

## Promotion

By the end of the plot, the player should have accumulated enough Acting Progress (50–80 points). If they reach 100% and obtain potion materials, they can attempt the Sequence 9→8 Promotion.

# Scenario 2: The Blood Money Gang's Invitation

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 8→7 | **Duration:** 3–4 sessions
**Recommended Companion:** Already have 1–2 companions (continuing from the previous scenario or newly recruited)

## Background

The player's actions in the previous scenario (or a side quest) caught the Blood Money Gang's attention. The player received an unsigned letter — containing only an address and time: the "Seamstress's String" tavern in the Sunken Smoke District, Friday night at nine.

Upon arrival, it turns out to be an "interview" — the Blood Money Gang is recruiting Beyonders to handle an "internal problem": their district boss — "Crack" Charlie — has lost control. He is not just losing control when using powers — he is slowly transforming into a non-human form even in his "daily state." The Blood Money Gang wants to "deal with" him before he fully becomes a monster — but without the Nighthawks knowing.

## Act 1: Investigation

**Clue Point 1 — Seamstress's String Tavern**

The player meets the Blood Money Gang's number two — a female Beyonder named "Widow" Margaret (Hunter Pathway Sequence 7).

What she provides:
- Charlie began acting "off" three months ago — first irritable, then physical changes (skin hardened, eyes began to glow in the dark).
- Charlie was an old member who originally followed the Blood Money Gang's founder — Prisoner Pathway Sequence 7 (Werewolf).
- She suspects Charlie did not lose control naturally — "someone is feeding him things." But doesn't know who.

She offers a reward: £50 + a Tier 3 Sealed Artifact.

**Clue Point 2 — Charlie's Stronghold (Sunken Smoke District abandoned slaughterhouse)**

Cannot enter directly (Charlie and his men guard it). Requires Observation / Stealth.

Observation check:
- Success: Discover a deliveryman pushing a cart into the slaughterhouse every three days. The cart is covered with cloth — but the shape under the cloth is not food.
- Critical Success: Recognize the deliveryman as a maintenance worker at the Gear District Difference Engine Center.

**Clue Point 3 — Gear District Difference Engine Center**

Investigate the difference-engine personnel:
- Lore check: A night-shift maintenance worker at the difference-engine center named "Edwin Stone" has frequently worked unexplained overtime recently. His shift records show the nights he worked overtime — exactly two days before the nights the deliveryman appeared.
- Social check: Edwin's colleagues say he has recently "gotten rich" — started wearing new clothes, but his mental state is poor. "He says he's doing a 'side job' — helping a lab transport 'waste.'"

**Clue Point 4 — Edwin's Residence (Iron Bridge District)**

After sneaking in / persuading entry:
- Under Edwin's bed is a box of empty glass bottles — labeled "St. Maria Asylum · Medical Waste."
- The residue in the bottles has a very faint Beyonder Characteristic — a Mysticism check can identify it as "stripped Beyonder residue characteristics."

Connecting the clues:
- Someone at St. Maria Asylum is conducting Beyonder Characteristic stripping experiments (see team side quest 7, "St. Maria Asylum").
- The stripped characteristics need "disposal" — someone sold them to someone inside the Blood Money Gang.
- Charlie was not "fed" into losing control — someone deliberately used the stripped characteristics to accelerate his bestial transformation.

## Act 2: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Abandoned Slaughterhouse (mostly infiltration)**

Environment: A three-story abandoned slaughterhouse. Charlie's men (3 mortal gang members, CR 1/2 each) patrol.

Stealth / combat optional. When you find Charlie — he is no longer human. His Werewolf form lost control — larger and more twisted than an ordinary Werewolf.

**Charlie (Loss-of-Control Werewolf Form)** | Prisoner Pathway Sequence 7 (Edge of Loss) | CR: 3
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 30 / Mind (MND) 15 / Body (BOD) 55 / Agility (AGI) 45 / Charisma (CHA) 5
- HP: 125 / Loss of Control (LoC): 85 / Threshold (LoCT): 40 (already far exceeded)
- Attacks: Tearing Claw (+18 / 3d6+11 Bleed), Frenzied Bite (+15 / 2d8+11)
- Powers: Bestial Frenzy (passive / +2 LoC per round), Scent Tracking (passive), Loss-of-Control Berserk (50% chance of indiscriminate attack, roll 1d100 ≤ 50 each round)
- Weakness: Silver damage ×2; −15 attack in bright light.
- Tactics: Indiscriminately attacks the nearest target. No distinction between friend and foe. Will not retreat.

**Scene 2 — The Mastermind Appears**

After defeating Charlie, searching the slaughterhouse reveals:
- A crate of glass bottles — from St. Maria Asylum.
- A note: "Dear Charlie, do you like these 'gifts'? — Your old friend M"
- M is "Widow" Margaret — the Blood Money Gang's number two.

**Scene 3 — Facing Margaret**

Return to the Seamstress's String Tavern. Margaret is waiting for your report. She does not know you have discovered the truth.

- Social path: Directly confront — she admits it. Her reason: "Charlie knew too many gang secrets. Rather than kill him, better to let him 'naturally eliminate.' No one would suspect."
- Combat path: Her men (3 mortals + Margaret herself CR=3) swarm the player.
- Compromise path: She gives you a choice — "Take the money, forget about the bottles. Or I tell the Nighthawks you 'broke into private property' last night."

**Margaret "the Widow"** | Hunter Pathway Sequence 7 | CR: 3 (solo version)
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 40 / Mind (MND) 50 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 50 / Charisma (CHA) 45
- HP: 85 / Spirit Pool (SP): 125
- Attacks: Military Revolver (+20 / 2d6 / 20m), Hunting Knife (+18 / 1d8+10)
- Powers: Fire Throw (1 AP / 5 Spirituality / 3d6 Fire damage), Sow Discord (make companion and player distrust each other — Mind opposed)

## Act 3: The Choice

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Report to the Nighthawks** | Margaret is arrested. Blood Money Gang in internal turmoil — but the player gains high Nighthawks trust | Nighthawks reputation +20 / free access to Nighthawks HQ / 80 Acting Progress |
| **Accept Margaret's terms** | Take the money and leave. Margaret owes you a "favor" — but she will not forget you know her secret | £50 + Tier 3 Sealed Artifact / Blood Money Gang reputation +10 / 60 Acting Progress |
| **Kill Margaret** | After combat victory the Blood Money Gang is leaderless. Sunken Smoke District gang order collapses — random street conflict events appear in the next few sessions | Margaret's loot / 70 Acting Progress / Blood Money Gang reputation −30 |

## Promotion

By the end of the plot, Acting Progress should reach 60–90. Sequence 7 Promotion requires: 100% Acting Progress + potion materials + Mind check.

# Scenario 3: Echoes of the Mechanical Heart

**Recommended Level:** Sequence 7→5 | **Duration:** 4–6 sessions (can be split into two sub-chapters)
**Recommended Companion:** 2 Sequence 6–7 companions

## Background

After Scenario 1 "The First Case Beneath the Gray Fog" and Scenario 2 "The Blood Money Gang's Invitation," the player begins to notice a name recurring behind the scenes — "Mechanical Heart." Whether it is Morrison Textile Factory's memory-exchange experiment or St. Maria Asylum's leaked stripped characteristics — all point to the same source: an organization experimenting on Beyonders.

Its name is the "Gear Council." Its founder — Dr. Blackwood (Savant Pathway Sequence 4 "Mechanical Heart") — whose ultimate goal is to pry at the Beyonder rules themselves.

This scenario is the main storyline's final chapter — integrating all clues from the first two scenarios, facing Dr. Blackwood directly.

> **Relationship to the team version "The Mechanical Heart" scenario:** This scenario shares the worldview and antagonist with the team version "The Mechanical Heart," but the plot route, encounter design, and CR adjustments are redesigned for solo. The team version's "30-man war" is changed to an "infiltrate and sabotage" route. Blackwood's moral dilemma is preserved — he is a tragic figure, not an evil antagonist.

## Act 1: The Puzzle

**Clue Point 1 — Grant's Relics (corresponds to Scenario 1)**

Review Scenario 1's files. Morrison Plant Manager's "identity exchange" — where did the exchanged memories go? Answer: "recycled" by the Gear Council. Memory is "data" — the Gear Council's difference engine is collecting data from all Beyonder experiments.

**Clue Point 2 — St. Maria Asylum Leak (corresponds to Scenario 2)**

Investigate where the asylum's stripped characteristics ultimately went:
- Mysticism / Social check: The stripped characteristics are periodically collected by a doctor — a woman named "Valerie" (Savant Pathway Sequence 6 "Artisan") — and shipped to an abandoned clock tower in the Gear District.
- That is the Gear Council's final stronghold.

**Clue Point 3 — Blackwood's Old Laboratory (ref. Scenario 1: Mistgrave University clue)**

Head to the abandoned clock tower in the Gear District. Discover:
- The difference-engine array is still running — it has self-replicated.
- Experiment logs show Blackwood's formula is close to complete — only the last variable is missing.
- That variable is "a complete Beyonder Pathway cycle" — the complete advancement data from Sequence 9 to Sequence 5.

**Key turn:** Blackwood does not need the player dead — he needs to witness a Beyonder's complete advancement process with his own eyes. Every one of the player's advancements — his difference engine secretly records. The player themselves are part of his experiment.

## Act 2: Clock Tower Assault

**Chapter 1: Infiltrate the Clock Tower**

The abandoned clock tower was converted into a multi-level laboratory:

- **First level**: Mechanical Sentinels (CR 2) ×2 — patrol. Can bypass with Stealth.
- **Second level**: Valerie's workshop — she is assembling a new Sealed Artifact. Optional: combat (CR 3) or persuasion (she has doubts about the doctor's research — can become an inside informant).
- **Third level**: Difference-Engine Core Array — Blackwood is here.

**Valerie (optional combat / persuasion)** | Savant Pathway Sequence 6 | CR: 3 (solo version)
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 40 / Mind (MND) 60 / Body (BOD) 30 / Agility (AGI) 40 / Charisma (CHA) 35
- HP: 75 / Spirit Pool (SP): 125
- Attacks: Steam Jet (+15 / 2d8 Fire / 10m), Wrench (+15 / 2d6+8)
- Powers: Precision Traps (2 already set, damage 2d8+Burning)
- Weakness: Persuasion path available — she privately suspects the doctor's formula is wrong. Requires Charisma + Negotiation (DC 65) or presenting research evidence.

**Chapter 2: Face Blackwood**

Dr. Blackwood waits in the difference-engine core. He **does not attack** — he wants to talk.

**Dr. Blackwood (solo version)** | Savant Pathway Sequence 4 | CR: 6 (non-hostile)
- Attributes: Spirituality (SPI) 70 / Mind (MND) 80 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 30 / Charisma (CHA) 20
- HP: 85 / Spirit Pool (SP): 215 / Loss of Control (LoC): 65

He shows the player a set of data — the difference engine's record of the player's every advancement. From Sequence 9 to now.

"You see," he says, "every one of your Sequences, every time at the Edge of Loss, every time pulled back — they are all data points. You proved a Beyonder can hold onto humanity and power at the same time. You are the final proof of my formula."

His request: Not to help him complete the formula — but to help him prove the formula **does not need to be completed**. He needs a "living proof" — proof that beyond the rules, a Beyonder can still hold onto humanity. That person is the player.

## Act 3: The Ultimate Choice

**Ending A: Stop the Formula from Completing**

Destroy the difference-engine array (destroy 30% of components). The doctor loses Beyonder powers. He does not resist — just stands there watching the difference engine collapse.

"Perhaps this is also a kind of result," he whispers — the words like talking to himself, yet also answering some unheard question.

**Reward:** 100 Acting Progress + the doctor's complete research notes + highest Nighthawks honor.

**Ending B: Persuade the Doctor That He Himself Is the Proof**

Social opposed (doctor Conviction 16). If success — the doctor shuts down the difference engine himself. This is the first time he "chooses" to stop calculating.

"You are right," he says to the player. "I spent ten years searching for the formula's last variable — now I find that variable never needed to exist."

He asks the Nighthawks to take him in: "I still have so many books I want to read — not about the Beyond, about… other things."

**Reward:** 100 Acting Progress + "First Blasphemy Slate fragment (1/9)" — records the complete formulas of all 22 Pathways and the doomsday warning + the doctor becomes a long-term NPC contact (can use the difference engine to assist in decoding subsequent slate fragments)

**Ending C: Assist in Decoding the Slate (hardest path)**

Help the doctor safely decode the slate fragment's ancient seal — not triggering spiritual loss of control, but using ritual means to slowly extract the slate's contents.

This requires:
1. Complete ritual materials (three Tier 3+ Sealed Artifacts as spiritual buffers — need the player to collect them in previous games).
2. The doctor's difference engine as the decoding tool.
3. The player maintains spiritual stability throughout (three-stage Spirituality + Ritual check, hard to legendary difficulty).

After success, the slate fragment is fully decoded and spiritually stable — will not lose control. This is the only route of the three where the doctor does not lose Beyonder powers. The slate's contents are fully preserved — including the doomsday warning and the complete formulas of 22 Pathways.

**Reward:** 100 Acting Progress + "First Blasphemy Slate fragment (fully tamed version)" + the doctor retains Sequence 4 powers and becomes a summonable NPC helper (once per scenario, can provide difference-engine decoding assistance in non-combat scenes)

## Promotion Ritual

By the end of the plot, the player's Acting Progress has reached 100%. The Sequence 5→4 Promotion Ritual should take place in the next game (as the climax of a large plot event).

The GM arranges the corresponding Promotion Ritual plot based on the player's Pathway (refer to Player Rulebook §2.3's 22-ritual table).
