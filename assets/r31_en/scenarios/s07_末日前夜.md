# Scenario Seven: Eve of the Apocalypse

**Applicable Level:** Seq 3 → Seq 1 (this scenario spans a wide range) | **Duration:** 6–10 game sessions
**Recommended Players:** Team of 3–5, or Solo + 2 Seq 3–4 NPC companions

## Background

The tablet fragment's new message points to Backlund. There, you will face not a single enemy — but a forming configuration of power.

After the angel-level operation of *Beneath the Rift*, your name has spread among the Seven Churches and all the major secret organizations. Not because of your power — but because of the living tablet fragment in your hand, and the fact that you survived before the angels. Some call you the "Stone-Bearer." Some call you the "Variable." Some have already decided — you must die.

When you arrive in Backlund, you find that news of the third tablet fragment has already leaked. Multiple factions are converging on Backlund:

| Faction | Goal | Means | Attitude toward you |
|------|------|------|-----------|
| **Twilight Hermit Order** | Recover the tablet fragment — lock it in Adam's secret chamber | Tracking, pressure, negotiation | "You have proven you are not a threat. But you are not the master either." |
| **Church of the Storm** | Obtain the "method to fight the Outer Deities" from the tablet fragment | Semi-public bidding — "legally" acquired | Suspicious — they do not trust "uncontrolled Beyonders" |
| **Rose School** | Destroy the tablet fragment (its contents are unfavorable to the Chained) | Assassination, corruption, terror attacks | Hostile — you and the tablet in your hand must both disappear |
| **Followers of the Tree of the Desire Mother** | Seize the tablet fragment to stop humanity from finding the method against the Outer Deities | Infiltration, corruption, replacing those around you | Hostile — but They would rather "convert" you than kill you |
| **Aurora Order** | Use the tablet fragment to awaken the "True Creator" | Kidnapping, sacrifice | Neutral-leaning-hostile — they believe you could become a "vessel" |
| **Your angel ally** | Protect you — but only from the shadows (cannot openly intervene in secular faction conflicts) | Intelligence, emergency rescue | Cooperative — she needs you alive |

**This is not a structured competition.** No score, no rounds, no referee. The factions act simultaneously, obstructing one another, while you sit at the center — both hunter and prey.

## Core Mechanic: Faction Power Matrix

The GM uses a simple matrix to track the dynamics of each faction:

| Faction | Intelligence Progress | Proximity to the tablet fragment | Threat Level to you | Current Action |
|------|---------|-----------------|------------|---------|
| Twilight Hermit Order | 60% | Knows the fragment is in Backlund, not yet located | Medium | Sends observers to track you |
| Church of the Storm | 40% | Only knows "someone bearing the stone entered Backlund" | Low | Sets up Spirit Detectors at docks and stations |
| Rose School | 80% | Has locked onto "you" as the fragment's bearer | High | First assassination attempt (see Scene A) |
| Followers of the Tree of the Desire Mother | 70% | Has locked onto your approximate position via Outer Deity resonance | Extreme | Attempting to replace your NPC companion |
| Aurora Order | 50% | Only knows the tablet fragment is in Backlund | Medium | Preparing a sacrifice ritual — awaiting your appearance |

**Each time a scene passes, the GM rolls 1d10 for each faction:**
- 1–3: The faction makes progress (Intelligence Progress +10% or Threat Level +1)
- 4–7: The faction is temporarily stalled
- 8–10: The faction is set back (obstructed by another faction; Intelligence Progress −5% or Threat Level −1)

**When a faction's "Intelligence Progress" reaches 100%:** They will find the fragment's whereabouts and take endgame action (seize, destroy, or negotiate) — the player must move first before then.

## Key Scene A: The Rose School's Assassination

On the second night after your arrival in Backlund — at midnight — the walls of the hotel room you checked into begin to bleed. Not human blood — a dark red, corrosive liquid. Gradually, a humanoid shape emerges on the wall. It is the Rose School's Seq 3 "Disciple of Silence" — stepping "into" the room from the other side of the wall.

**Disciple of Silence** | Prisoner Pathway, Seq 3 | Team CR: 12 (Solo: 4)

- Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 60, Mind (MND) 45, Body (BOD) 55, Agility (AGI) 60, Charisma (CHA) 10
- HP: 130 / SP: 185 / LoCT: 100
- Abilities: Curse Touch (1 AP / 5 SP / Melee / 3d8 Dark+Curse — target's all checks −15 next turn), Living Wall (Passive / can move through walls, cannot be physically blocked), Silence (2 AP / 10 SP / Whole field / all sound vanishes — cannot use verbal abilities or social skills, lasts 2 turns)
- Weakness: While in Living Wall state, Spirit attacks ignore its physical defense
- Tactics: Does not linger in battle — its goal is to snatch the tablet fragment, not to kill you. Once it grabs the fragment, it immediately dives into the wall and flees
- Extra Mechanic: The tablet fragment grows **intensely warm and glows** when he approaches — this partially disables his "Living Wall" ability (he cannot fully sink into the wall)

**Discovery after repelling:** The Disciple of Silence dropped an item while fleeing — a Rose School communication Sealed Artifact, the "Blood Rose Pin." Through it, you can monitor the Rose School's internal communications (every other scene, the GM may reveal one piece of the Rose School's latest movement).

## Key Scene B: The Tree of the Desire Mother's Infiltration

When you meet with your ally — one of your NPC companions (chosen by the GM) behaves abnormally. There are brief pauses in his speech — as if listening to another voice. His shadow sometimes moves in the wrong direction. The tablet fragment grows warm near him.

**Truth:** The followers of the Tree of the Desire Mother did not attack you directly — they made contact with one of your companions on the very first day you arrived in Backlund. Not a replacement — a "graft." They implanted an Outer Deity seed in your companion's subconscious. Your companion does not know he is corrupted — but the seed will "bloom" at your most critical moment, transmitting your position and plans to the followers of the Tree of the Desire Mother.

**Means of exposure:**
- Tablet fragment detection (grows warm when approached)
- Mind+Observation (Difficult): Notice the companion's abnormal behavior
- When alone with the companion — the companion suddenly speaks in a tone that is not his own, then completely forgets having said it

**Cleansing the corruption:** Spirit+Ritual (Extreme) — guide the ritual with the tablet fragment. Success → the Outer Deity seed is cleansed, the companion returns to normal. Failure → the seed "blooms," the companion is briefly controlled (1 turn), attacks the player, then regains awareness — terrified and remorseful of his own actions.

## Key Scene C: The Church of the Storm's "Legal Hunt"

The Church of the Storm issued a notice to all Beyonders in Backlund:

> "The Third Epoch Blasphemy Slate fragment has been classified as a 'public safety threat.' Holders are to deliver the fragment to the nearest Church of the Storm outpost within 48 hours. Those who fail to do so will be regarded as unlawfully possessing a Sealed Artifact and pursued by the Punishers."

This is a "legal hunting license." The Church of the Storm does not intend to come and seize it — they intend to make you "voluntarily hand it over," while other factions will intercept you on your way to the church.

**Player options:**

| Option | Execution | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| **Ignore the notice** | Continue acting, but Punishers will hunt you | Each time you act in a public place, there is a 30% chance of encountering a Punisher patrol (Seq 6–5, 3–5 members) |
| **Hand over a replica** | Have your guardian angel (Servant of Concealment) craft a fake tablet fragment — she can do it but requires your tablet fragment to be away from you for 24 hours | Temporarily lose the tablet fragment's Outer Deity detection function. But the Church of the Storm temporarily lifts its pursuit of you |
| **Negotiate with the Church of the Storm** | Directly contact Calamity (the angel you fought alongside in Scenario Six) — persuade him to revoke the notice. Charisma+Negotiation (Legendary difficulty −60) | Success → Calamity agrees to withdraw the notice, but requires that after you find the tablet fragment, you share the "anti-Outer-Deity weapon" portion of its contents with the Church of the Storm |
| **Publicly reveal the tablet fragment's contents** | Make public the portion you have decoded — proving the fragment is not a threat but a resource | All factions' Threat Level to you −1, but your location is found faster (because you exposed your position) |

## Key Scene D: The Fragment's Guardian

In the fog of pursuit and counter-pursuit, you finally find the bearer of the third tablet fragment — an unexpected person.

**Eileen Morrison — Dr. Blackwood's ex-wife.**

Do you remember Scenario Four? After the Doctor lost his Beyonder powers at the ending of *The Mechanical Heart*, he was taken in by the Nighthawks. But his wife Eileen — an ordinary person who had never touched the Beyonder world — discovered another tablet fragment while sorting through the Doctor's relics. The Doctor never told anyone he possessed two — the second was his "control group" for studying the difference engine's decoding.

Eileen does not know what it is. She only knows the tablet glows at night, showing her images she cannot understand in her dreams. She hid the tablet fragment in the family crypt in Backlund. She dared not tell anyone — because she knows what the Doctor was researching ultimately "made him disappear."

**Interaction with Eileen:**

This is the only key scene in the entire scenario that requires no combat. Eileen is an ordinary person — she does not know of the Beyonder world's existence (or only knows fragments of it). She only knows her husband was taken away by "those people" and never returned.

- Charisma+Negotiation (Routine): Explain everything to her — the tablet fragment, the Doctor's fate, your connection to the Doctor. Success → she will take you to the crypt and hand you the fragment. She has only one request: "Tell me — what were his last words?"
- If the player obtained the Doctor's last words in Scenario Four — tell her. Her reaction will be: silent for a long time, then "He finally saw what he wanted to see."
- If the player does not have the Doctor's last words — you may choose to tell the truth or fabricate. Tell the truth → she will be sad but understanding. Fabricate → Charisma+Deception (Difficult); if you fail, she will demand you prove you knew the Doctor

**After obtaining the third tablet fragment — what it reveals:**

> "Tablet 3/9. The first tablet recorded the numbers of the doomsday countdown. The second tablet recorded the map of the barrier's weak points. The third tablet — recorded the Ancient Sun God's final command before his betrayal."
>
> "He commanded his eight most trusted angels — to shatter the tablet and scatter the fragments across the world. Not to hide it — but to ensure 'that someone could find all the fragments.' Because only by piecing all the fragments together — can one read the tablet's final page: the complete method against the Outer Deities."
>
> "Location of Fragment 4/9: the Aurora Order's holy site — at the base of the True Creator's altar."

## Act Five: The Aurora Order's Gamble

The True Creator — not one of the Seven Gods. He is an incomplete, corrupted deity. The Aurora Order's followers believe: so long as all the tablet fragments are sacrificed to the True Creator, He will complete his godhood ritual — becoming humanity's front line against the Outer Deities. They are fanatical followers — not enemies, but their "solution" is diametrically opposed to the Seven Churches'.

**Scene: The Aurora Order's Holy Site**

The Aurora Order's holy site lies deep beneath Backlund — an inverted temple left over from the Third Epoch. The ceiling is an abyss-like floor. The floor is a starry sky. The True Creator's altar stands at the center of the temple — a stone platform suspended in mid-air by iron chains. The fourth tablet fragment is embedded in the stone platform.

The Aurora Order's High Bishop — the Seq 3 "Trinity Templar" — appears before you:

> "You have come. The one chosen by the tablet fragment. I know you did not come to fight — if you had, you would already be dead. Speak — what do you want?"

**Trinity Templar** | Secrets Suppliant Pathway, Seq 3 | Team CR: 14 (but combat may not trigger)

- Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 70, Mind (MND) 65, Body (BOD) 60, Agility (AGI) 50, Charisma (CHA) 55
- HP: 135 / SP: 215 / LoCT: 140
- Abilities: Three Heads (Passive / can simultaneously use three different pathway abilities — because three different pathways' Beyonder Characteristics are fused within him), Oracle (1 AP / 5 SP / declare a prophecy — must occur next turn), Mark of Sacrifice (Reaction / 5 SP / transfer one instance of damage taken to a willing follower)
- Personality: Fanatical but not mad. He is a true believer — he believes what he does is right. He can be persuaded — but only in "the language of faith"

**Three outcomes:**

| Outcome | Execution | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| **Negotiate** | Offer terms: you share the decoded contents of the tablet fragment with the Aurora Order in exchange for the fourth fragment | The Aurora Order becomes your intelligence ally. The High Bishop gives you a communication Sealed Artifact — can summon Aurora Order support at a critical moment (one-time use) |
| **Deceive** | Falsely claim you are the "Tablet Guardian" chosen by the True Creator (Charisma+Deception, Legendary) | If success → the Aurora Order hands over the fragment and regards you as a "Prophet." If failure → combat. The High Bishop feels blasphemed and will kill you at any cost |
| **Reveal the truth** | Use the tablet fragment to show evidence of the True Creator's corruption — proving He cannot become humanity's savior | The High Bishop collapses — his faith dealt a fatal blow. He will hand over the fragment, then kneel before the altar for a whole night. The Aurora Order splits into two factions — the "Truth Faction" that follows you and the "Orthodox Faction" that holds to the old faith |
