# Scenario Three: Echoes of the Mechanical Heart

**Applicable Level:** Seq 7 → 5 | **Duration:** 4–6 game sessions (can be split into two sub-chapters)
**Recommended Companions:** 2 companions of Seq 6–7

## Background

Since Scenario One (*The First Case Beneath the Gray Fog*) and Scenario Two (*The Blood Money Gang's Invitation*), the player has begun to notice a name recurring behind the scenes — "Mechanical Heart." Whether it is the memory-exchange experiment at Morrison Textile Factory or the leaked stripped Characteristics from St. Maria Asylum — they all point to the same source: an organization experimenting on Beyonders.

Its name is the "Gear Council." Its founder — Dr. Blackwood (Savant Pathway, Seq 4 "Mechanical Heart") — has the ultimate goal of prying loose the Beyonder rules themselves.

This scenario is the finale of the main line — integrating all the clues of the previous two scenarios, facing Dr. Blackwood directly.

> **Relationship to the team-version *Mechanical Heart* scenario:** This scenario shares the worldview and antagonist with the team-version *Mechanical Heart*, but the plot route, encounter design, and CR adjustments are redesigned for solo play. The team version's "30-person war" is changed to an "infiltrate and sabotage" route. Dr. Blackwood's moral dilemma is preserved — he is a tragic figure, not an evil villain.

## Act One: The Puzzle

**Clue Site 1 — Grant's Relics (corresponds to Scenario One)**

Review the files of Scenario One. Morrison factory manager's "identity exchange" — where did the exchanged memories go? Answer: "recycled" by the Gear Council. Memory is "data" — the Gear Council's difference engine is collecting data from all Beyonder experiments.

**Clue Site 2 — The Leak from St. Maria Asylum (corresponds to Scenario Two)**

Investigate the final destination of the stripped Characteristics from the asylum:
- Mysticism/Social check: The stripped Characteristics are periodically collected by a doctor — a woman named "Valerie" (Savant Pathway, Seq 6 · Artisan) — and shipped to an abandoned clock tower in the Gear District
- That is the Gear Council's final stronghold

**Clue Site 3 — Blackwood's Old Laboratory (ref. Scenario One: Mistgrave University clue)**

Head to the abandoned clock tower in the Gear District. Discover:
- The difference-engine array is running — it has already self-replicated
- The experiment records show Blackwood's formula is close to complete — only the final variable is missing
- That variable is "a complete Beyonder pathway cycle" — the data of the complete Promotion process from Seq 9 to Seq 5

**Key Turning Point:** Blackwood does not need the player dead — he needs to personally witness a Beyonder's complete Promotion process. Every Promotion of the player — his difference engine secretly records it. The player itself is part of his experiment.

## Act Two: The Clock Tower Assault

**Chapter One: Infiltrate the Clock Tower**

The abandoned clock tower was converted into a multi-level laboratory:

- **First Level**: Mechanical Sentry (CR 2) ×2 — patrolling. Can be bypassed with stealth
- **Second Level**: Valerie's workshop — she is assembling a new Sealed Artifact. Optional: combat (CR 3) or persuasion (she has doubts about the Doctor's research — can become an inside informant)
- **Third Level**: Difference Engine core array — Blackwood is here

**Valerie (optional combat / persuasion)** | Savant Pathway, Seq 6 | CR: 3 (Solo version)
- Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 40 / Mind (MND) 60 / Body (BOD) 30 / Agility (AGI) 40 / Charisma (CHA) 35
- HP: 75 / Spirit Pool: 125
- Attack: Steam Jet (+15 / 2d8 Fire / 10m), Wrench (+15 / 2d6+8)
- Abilities: Precision Trap (2 already set, damage 2d8+Burning)
- Weakness: Persuasion path available — she privately suspects the Doctor's formula is wrong. Requires Charisma+Negotiation (DC 65) or presentation of research evidence

**Chapter Two: Face Blackwood**

Dr. Blackwood waits in the difference-engine core. He **does not attack** — he wants to talk.

**Dr. Blackwood (Solo version)** | Savant Pathway, Seq 4 | CR: 6 (non-hostile)
- Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 70 / Mind (MND) 80 / Body (BOD) 35 / Agility (AGI) 30 / Charisma (CHA) 20
- HP: 85 / Spirit Pool: 215 / Loss of Control: 65

He shows the player a set of data — every Promotion of the player recorded by the difference engine. From Seq 9 to now.

"You see," he says, "every one of your Sequences, every time at the Edge of Loss, every time pulled back — they are all data points. You proved that a Beyonder can simultaneously preserve humanity and power. You are the final proof of my formula."

His request: not to help him complete the formula — but to help him prove the formula **does not need to be completed**. He needs a "living proof" — proof that beyond the rules, a Beyonder can still hold onto humanity. That person is the player.

## Act Three: The Ultimate Choice

**Ending A: Stop the Formula's Completion**

Sabotage the difference-engine array (destroy 30% of the components). The Doctor loses his Beyonder abilities. He does not resist — he just stands there watching the difference engine collapse.

"Perhaps this is also a kind of outcome." he whispers — the words like talking to himself, yet also like answering some unheard question.

**Reward:** Acting Progress 100 + the Doctor's research notes (complete edition) + the Nighthawks' highest honor

**Ending B: Persuade the Doctor that He Himself Is the Proof**

Social Opposed Check (Doctor's Conviction 16). If success — the Doctor shuts down the difference engine himself. This is the first time he "chooses" to stop calculating.

"You are right." he says to the player. "I spent ten years searching for the formula's final variable — and now I find that variable simply need not exist."

He asks the Nighthawks to take him in: "I still have so many books I want to read — not about the Beyonders, about... other things."

**Reward:** Acting Progress 100 + the "First Blasphemy Slate Fragment (1/9)" — recording the complete recipes of the 22 pathways and the doomsday warning + the Doctor becomes a long-term NPC contact (can use the difference engine to assist in decoding subsequent tablet fragments)

**Ending C: Assist in Decoding the Slate (the most difficult path)**

Help the Doctor safely decode the ancient seal within the tablet fragment — without triggering spiritual loss of control, but using ritual means to slowly extract the tablet's contents.

This requires:
1. Complete ritual materials (three Tier 3+ Sealed Artifacts as spiritual buffers — must be collected by the player in previous games)
2. The Doctor's difference engine as the decoding tool
3. The player maintains spiritual stability throughout (three-stage Spirit+Ritual checks, from Difficult to Legendary difficulty)

After success, the tablet fragment is fully decoded and spiritually stable — no loss of control. This is the only route of the three that does not make the Doctor lose his Beyonder abilities. The tablet's contents are fully preserved — including the doomsday warning and the complete recipes of the 22 pathways.

**Reward:** Acting Progress 100 + the "First Blasphemy Slate Fragment (fully tamed version)" + the Doctor retains Seq 4 abilities and becomes a summonable NPC helper (once per scenario, can provide difference-engine decoding assistance in non-combat scenes)

## Promotion Ritual

By the end of the scenario, the player's Acting Progress has reached 100%. The Seq 5→4 Promotion ritual should be conducted in the next game session (as the endpoint of a major plot event).

The GM arranges the corresponding Promotion ritual plot according to the player's pathway (refer to §2.3, the 22-ritual table in the Player Rulebook).
