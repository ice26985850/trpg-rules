# Scenario One: The First Case Beneath the Gray Fog

**Applicable Level:** Seq 9 → Seq 8 | **Duration:** 2–3 game sessions
**Recommended Companions:** Template B (Observer) or Template C (Storm User)

## Background

A retired lawyer in the Iron Bridge District — Harold Grant — was found dead in his own study. Officially classified as "heart attack." But his daughter does not believe it — she says that three days before his death, her father told her: "Someone put something in the book."

The player is commissioned by the daughter (or receives the commission through the Drowned Man Tavern). Reward: £15 + a family heirloom.

## Act One: Investigation

**Clue Site 1 — The Victim's Study (Iron Bridge District)**

Core Clues (auto-discovered):
- The victim held a note in his hand, with only two words on it: "Second to Last"
- The study has an entire wall of bookshelves — the arrangement looks completely random, but one bookshelf is cleaner than the others (recently cleaned)

Investigation check (d100 ≤ Mind+Investigation):
- Success: Discover that the books on the shelf are arranged by "publication date" — but one book's date was deliberately misplaced
- Failure: The GM informs you "the shelf was cleaned, but nothing unusual stands out"

**Clue Site 2 — Grant's Office (Iron Bridge District law firm)**

Core Clues (auto-discovered):
- The file of Grant's last case before retirement has vanished
- His secretary says: "Before he retired, Mr. Grant said he had finally 'found it' — but didn't say what he found"

Social check (d100 ≤ Charisma+Negotiation):
- Success: The secretary reveals — Grant's last case was defending a "fired factory worker," against the Morrison Textile Factory in the Gear District
- Critical Success: The secretary gives a name — "James Morrison." That man is now the new factory manager of Morrison Textile Factory

**Clue Site 3 — Drowned Man Tavern (Ash Harbor District)**

Intelligence (requires negotiation / bribe):
- For the past three months, Grant came to the Drowned Man Tavern every Friday night — not to drink, but to meet the same person
- That person was a worker from the Gear District — no one knows the name. They always sat in the corner, speaking in low voices

## Act Two: Conflict

**Scene 1 — Morrison Textile Factory**

Grant's case file was hidden in the old factory manager's office of Morrison Textile Factory — now abandoned. After sneaking in, you find:

- The file is in the office safe (lockpick d100 ≤ Agility+Sleight of Hand)
- File contents: Grant once defended a worker — that worker accused the new manager of Morrison Textile Factory of "not being the real James Morrison"
- The last page of the file reads: "The defendant withdrew the lawsuit. Reason: 'Cannot remember who he was supposed to sue.'"

Spiritual Perception (if the player or companion has a related ability):
- Success: The factory manager's office has faint Secrets Suppliant pathway residue — someone performed a memory-related ritual here

**Scene 2 — The Truth of Grant's Study**

Back at the study, take out the "misplaced book." The book is titled *Workers' Rights in the Industrial Revolution*. After opening it, you find the book was hollowed out — hidden inside:

- A photograph: the real James Morrison (worker) standing with the current Morrison factory manager (a Secrets Suppliant pathway Beyonder). The two look identical.
- Grant's handwritten note: "He is not Morrison. The original Morrison is still alive — he just doesn't remember who he is."
- Another note: "The last one. — H.G."

Clue Deduction (d100 ≤ Mind+Investigation − Clue Count×5):
- Success: Grant discovered the truth — the current manager used a Secrets Suppliant pathway ability to "exchange" the real Morrison's memory, replacing his identity. "The last one" does not refer to a book — it refers to "the last identity." Grant is the "second to last."
- Failure: The GM gives a partial deduction but keeps one misdirection — possibly "the manager and Morrison are twins"

**Scene 3 — The Study at Night**

That night, when the player returns to the study, they meet the "Morrison factory manager" — he came to "retrieve the last book." He believes the book hides evidence that could prove his true identity (fragments of the original Morrison's memory).

**Enemy Data (Solo CR Adjustment):**

| Enemy | CR | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| Morrison Factory Manager (weakened) | 2 | Secrets Suppliant pathway Seq 8 rather than Seq 7. Attributes lowered |

**Morrison Factory Manager (Solo version)** | Secrets Suppliant Pathway, Seq 8 · Listener | CR: 2
- Attributes: Spirit (SPI) 45 / Mind (MND) 40 / Body (BOD) 30 / Agility (AGI) 30 / Charisma (CHA) 35
- HP: 75 / Spirit Pool: 140
- Attack: Shadow Touch (+15 / 2d6 Dark / Melee)
- Abilities: Shadow Stealth (1 AP / 5 Spirit), Listening to Whispers (gain intelligence but Loss of Control +1d3)
- Weakness: All checks −15 when facing evidence of his original identity (the photo)
- Tactics: Sneak attack first; after being exposed, attempts negotiation — "You don't understand. I just want to live."

> **GM Adjustment Note:** The team-version Morrison Factory Manager is CR 4 Seq 7. The solo version is weakened to CR 2 Seq 8, HP dropped from 130 to 75, with Sacrificial Blade and Summon Shadow Servant abilities removed.

## Act Three: The Choice

Truth revealed: The current Morrison factory manager was originally an impoverished factory worker — extremely similar in appearance to the real Morrison. Three years ago he obtained a Secrets Suppliant potion, and used the ability to exchange Morrison's memory and identity, replacing his life. The real Morrison lost his memory and drifted into the Sunken Smoke District.

After Grant discovered the truth, he wanted to "restore the original Morrison's memory" — but the manager refused, because "if I give it back, I'll have nothing left."

He came to the study not to silence a witness — he never killed Grant (Grant genuinely died of a heart attack). He just wanted to retrieve the last piece of evidence.

**Ending Branches:**

| Choice | Result | Reward |
|------|------|------|
| **Hand over to the Nighthawks** | Contact the authorities. Requires a Social check to prove the case involves the Beyonders. The manager is taken into custody. The real Morrison is rescued (but has already lost three years of memory) | Nighthawks Favor +15 / Acting Progress 60 / Reward £15 |
| **Persuade the manager to return the identity** | Social Opposed Check (manager's Conviction 12). Success → the manager returns the identity but asks for help to "start over" | The manager becomes an NPC contact (can assist in the future) / Acting Progress 80 |
| **Let the manager keep the identity but demand compensation for Morrison** | Compromise — the manager continues as factory manager, but must give the original Morrison a job and a place to live. Both sides accept | Acting Progress 70 / gain a "gray-area" NPC ally |

## Promotion

By the end of the scenario, the player should have accumulated enough Acting Progress (50–80 points). If they reach 100% and obtain potion materials, they may attempt the Seq 9→8 Promotion.
