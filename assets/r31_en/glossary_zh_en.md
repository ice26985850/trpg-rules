> **Display name (英文顯示名):** `Lord of the Mysteries TRPG`  (中文: 《詭祕之主》TRPG)
> Use **`Lord of the Mysteries TRPG`** everywhere in the English release.
> Original novel: 《诡秘之主》by 爱潜水的乌贼 (Cuttlefish That Loves Diving). Official English novel title: *Lord of the Mysteries*. This is a non-official fan TRPG adaptation.

> **Reading note:** The left column is intentionally **Chinese** (reference column). That is NOT a translation miss — the glossary is a reference lock so translators can always trace an English term back to the exact Chinese source string. English is the target translation.

## 2. 核心屬性 (Core Attributes — "English Full Name (ABBR)" format)

| 中文 | English (ABBR) | 說明 / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 靈性 | Spirituality (SPI) | Beyonder power fuel; drives Spirit Pool, divination/spirit-sight/ritual checks |
| 心智 | Mind (MND) | Reason, logic, memory; sets Loss-of-Control Threshold, resists mental effects |
| 體魄 | Body (BOD) | Physique, endurance; sets HP, resists toxin/disease |
| 身手 | Agility (AGI) | Dexterity, coordination, combat; melee/ranged, stealth, initiative, dodge |
| 風度 | Charisma (CHA) | Charm, social, performance; negotiation/deception, disguise, "surface life" |
| 生命值 | Health Points (HP) | = Body×2 + 15 |
| 靈性池 | Spirit Pool (SP) | = Spirituality×3 + 5; resource to use Beyonder powers |
| 失控值 | Loss of Control (LoC) | Accumulated Beyonder corruption |
| 失控閾值 | Loss of Control Threshold (LoCT) | = Mind×2 + 10 |
| 先攻 | Initiative | = Agility + d10 (rolled once at combat start) |
| 行動點 | Action Point (AP) | 3 AP per turn |

## 9. 注意事項 / 待確認 (Notes & Items to Verify)

1. **Savant Seq4 localization (途徑15).** The source names 通識者 Seq4 **機械之心** ("Mechanical Heart") — a TRPG-specific name (also Dr. Blackwood's title and the s04/s11 scenario titles). The novel canon for Savant/Paragon Seq4 is **Alchemist (鍊金術士)**. The glossary locks **Alchemist** as the canonical sequence name and keeps **Mechanical Heart** as the TRPG-localized label. Decide per project style: use "Alchemist (Mechanical Heart)" or keep "Mechanical Heart" only.

2. **Prisoner / Criminal god swap (途徑16 & 17).** The source's pathway **headers** label 囚犯 as `(Abyss / Prisoner)` and 罪犯 as `(Chained / Criminal)`, but the source's **appendix high-sequence table** (and novel canon) assign the gods the other way: 囚犯 → **Chained (被縛者)**, 罪犯 → **Abyss (深淵)**. The glossary follows novel canon + the appendix. If the TRPG deliberately re-themed these two pathways, revert to the header labels — but note the inconsistency either way.

3. **Tyrant Seq4 rendering.** Wiki returned "Cataclysmic Interrer" (likely a typo/rendering artifact for **Cataclysmic Hierarch**). Locked as **Cataclysmic Hierarch** (災難主祭); confirm if a preferred spelling exists.

4. **God-name parentheses.** Several Seq0 god names carry alternate renderings in the wiki (e.g., Sun = "Eternal Blazing Sun", Darkness = "Evernight Goddess", Death = "Eternal Sleeper", Demoness = "Primordial Demoness"). Both forms are listed; pick one display form for the release.

5. **All 220 sequence names** were verified against the *Lord of the Mysteries* official wiki standard-pathway chart and match the source's Chinese sequence names exactly (except the two localized items above). No sequence name is left unverified beyond the three flagged notes.
