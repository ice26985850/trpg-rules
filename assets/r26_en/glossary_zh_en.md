## (g) Character Name Romanization Rule

**Mandatory rule:** All *Hunter × Hunter* proper nouns (characters, places, factions) already have official Western/anime romanizations. **Use the official English romanization and DO NOT convert to pinyin** (e.g., `Netero`, `Zoldyck`, `Yorknew`, `Heavens Arena`, not pinyin forms). For original/creator-designed NPCs introduced by the rulebook (e.g., 東巴, 雙尾蛇·黑爾貝爾), keep the CN name transliterated consistently per the CN source; do not invent new English names unless the CN source itself gives one.

---

## (h) Filename / Path Rule

**Filename/path references (strings ending in `.md` / `.yaml` / `.svg` / `.xlsx` / `.pdf`) are kept in their original Chinese — do NOT translate them.** Examples that must remain verbatim: `介紹.txt`, `獵人X獵人TRPG_完整規則書.md`, `怪物圖鑑.yaml`, `s01_獵人考試.md`, `chimera_ant.svg`, `空白玩家角色卡.xlsx`. When an English sentence references a CN source file, keep the filename in Chinese and wrap it in backticks.
