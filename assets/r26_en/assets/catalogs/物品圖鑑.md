# Item Codex (Legendary & Special Items)

> Collects data on Legendary-tier items and special items in *Hunter × Hunter TRPG*, for reference by the Game Master (GM) and Player Characters (PC).

## VII. Legendary Item Pool

Used as loot for random rewards or special encounters. These items are all new content; the GM selects from the table below or rolls d12 to decide randomly. Each item comes with complete usage rules and stats.

### Balance Design Principles
- Legendary-tier items do not make the holder "invincible"—each has clear limitations or a usage count
- Items with permanent effects are extremely rare (only #10 Ai Crystal), and all have specific acquisition conditions
- Item Challenge Rating (CR) recommendation: only distribute to Star-tier or higher Player Characters (PC). Professional-tier may issue at most 1 per session

## VIII. Special Item Detailed Data

The following items come from rewards of Legendary commissions, with complete rule data.

### 8-1 Mythic-tier Items

#### Gift of the Ancient God (Commission #8 Friendly Ending)

When the Ancient God is satisfied with the PC's answer, it raises an item from the abyss (GM chooses or rolls d3):

| d3 | Item | Detailed Rules | Balance Limit |
|:---:|------|------|------|
| 1 | **Ancient God's Scale** | Can be inlaid on the body (replaces 1 accessory slot). Effect: automatically triggers when HP reaches zero—the scale shatters, and the PC's HP recovers to 50% of maximum (not revival—must trigger within 1 round after HP reaches zero). **One-time.** | A one-time get-out-of-jail-free card. Does not exceed the Legendary range—because once used it's gone. |
| 2 | **Core Fragment of the Dark Continent** | Fist-sized black crystal, always slightly warm. Effect: the holder has permanent advantage on survival checks across the entire Dark Continent; can perceive the direction and distance of the nearest "Gate" at any location; once per month consumes 20 SP to create a 10m-radius safe zone (no random encounters triggered within 8 hours). | Pure exploration QoL, no combat stat impact. Gives Legendary PCs more control over movement in the Dark Continent. |
| 3 | **Whisper of the Sleeper** | Not an item—the Ancient God directly engraves a piece of "knowledge" into the PC's memory. Effect: the PC permanently gains one of the following abilities (self-chosen): ① Understand the meaning of all languages (not speak, but comprehend) ② Can perceive the approximate location of Nen ability users within 1km (not precise, direction+distance) ③ Gain one final memory from before the First Civilization's destruction—containing a GM-customized major secret. | Information-type ability. Does not affect combat stats but profoundly changes the narrative. The GM must prepare a worldview-affecting secret for option ③. |

#### Ultimate Legacy (Commission #2)

The complete memory crystal of the First Civilization's founder—not knowledge nor a weapon, but a person's entire life.

**Usage:** After the PC touches the crystal, they experience the complete life of the First Civilization's founder, the "First Judge," from birth to the establishment of the Tribunal (about 10 minutes of vision experience, taking 8 hours in-game).

**Effect (permanently gained after experience):**
- Intelligence +1 (cap 22), from experiencing the life of a highly intelligent being
- Gain "Blessing of the First Civilization": when facing the Five Calamities, advantage on Persuasion checks (the calamities' collective consciousness recognizes the First Civilization's aura)
- Unlock hidden info: the PC now knows the "True Name of the Ancient God"—gains +5 bonus on any check related to the Ancient God
- Mental cost: after the experience ends, requires Willpower DC 22 or gains 48 hours of "sense of existential void" (cannot gain rest rewards)

### 8-2 V5 Secret: Anti-Nen Resonance Device (Commission #7)

**Nature:** The ultimate anti-Nen weapon secretly developed by V5 over 50 years. Using the "specific frequency released when a Nen user employs Nen" for reverse resonance, it can remotely paralyze or eliminate Nen users.

**Device Data:**
- **Activation Time:** 72 hours charging
- **Effective Range:** Global (resonance waves propagated via satellite network)
- **Effect (after activation):** All Nen users within range must make a Willpower saving throw DC 30. Success → Nen ability sealed for 24 hours (equivalent to Nen Seal state); Failure → permanently lose Nen ability (SP reduced to zero, cannot learn or use any Nen ability)
- **Weakness:** ① The device must be placed at a specific location on the equator (V5 chose an uninhabited island in the Barusa Archipelago) ② During activation the device releases a massive Nen field (Nen users within 100km can all perceive it—"someone is attacking Nen itself") ③ The device can be physically destroyed (HP 500, AC 18, no Nen Defense—because it is itself aimed at Nen) ④ If the resonance frequency is reverse-engineered—it can be changed into a selective weapon "targeting only specific Nen types"

**Choices the PC Can Make and Consequences:**

| Choice | Mechanism | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| Destroy Device | Infiltrate the island (CR 16 security), destroy the core | V5's 50 years of research go up in smoke. The PC becomes V5's #1 wanted criminal—but gains the secret gratitude of all Nen users |
| Control Device | Intelligence DC 26 + tech Nen ability assistance, rewrite target parameters | The device becomes a "selective anti-Nen weapon"—usable against criminals who abuse Nen. The Association gains a controversial ultimate weapon |
| Persuade V5 | Persuasion DC 28, collect testimonies from 12 nations proving "the value of Nen users to humanity exceeds the threat" | The device is permanently sealed but not destroyed—may be restarted in the future. This is the most peaceful but most fragile solution |
| Reveal Truth | Make the device's existence public | Global panic. The relationship between Nen users and ordinary people changes from then on. May trigger a global "Hunter vs Human" conflict |

### 8-3 Spacetime Items (Commission #9)

**First Civilization Time Calibration Device** (repaired, takeable version):

| Attribute | Value |
|------|------|
| **Size** | Handheld (disc about 10cm in diameter) |
| **Energy** | Built-in Eternal Crystal as power source (no external charging needed) |
| **Effect 1: Time Buffer** | Once per day, activated as a reaction—the PC's next round gains a "time buffer": within the round, can split 1 standard action into 2 move actions, or double move speed. Lasts 1 round. |
| **Effect 2: Time Mark** | Once per week, the PC leaves a "Time Mark" at the current position. Within the next 1 hour, the PC can consume 1 standard action to instantly return to the mark position (regardless of distance, but only within the same plane). After returning, the mark disappears. |
| **Effect 3: Time Echo (one-time)** | The device's strongest feature—after use the device permanently fails (Eternal Crystal depleted). The PC can return to the state of **1 round ago**: HP, SP, and position all restored to 1 round ago's values. But memory is retained—the PC knows everything that happened in that 1 round. Can reverse the most lethal 1 round of a battle. |

> **Balance Note:** Effect 3 is the strongest one-time ability in the entire system, but the device is scrapped after use. The PC must use it at the most critical moment—perhaps the final boss's last blow, or saving an unrevivable NPC. A Legendary character can use Time Echo only once in a lifetime.
