# Monster Codex (Enemy Character Data)

> Collects the enemy characters (Non-Player Characters (NPC) and Bosses (BOSS)) in *Hunter × Hunter TRPG*, sorted by Challenge Rating (CR). For complete stats, refer to the accompanying `敵人角色卡合集.xlsx`.

## V5 Special Forces
- Challenge Rating (CR): 3
- Type: Mook-tier (squad unit)
- Hit Points (HP): 50
- Armor Class (AC): 14
- Drop: Anti-Nen Rifle (usable by players: 2d6 damage, ignores Nen Defense, range 50m), Anti-Nen Armor Fragment

## Brion Combat Mech
- Challenge Rating (CR): 8
- Type: Mid-tier (autonomous weapon)
- Hit Points (HP): 100
- Armor Class (AC): 20
- Weakness: Electric attacks deal double damage. Hacking: Intelligence DC 22 can paralyze it for 1d4 rounds.
- Drop: Brion Alloy Fragment (Legendary armor material), Experience Points (XP) 200

## Brion Command Mech
- Challenge Rating (CR): 12
- Type: Boss-tier (command core)
- Hit Points (HP): 200
- Armor Class (AC): 22
- Weakness: The console is beside the command core—entering the shutdown code (Intelligence + Nen Knowledge DC 25, with 3 people pressing switches simultaneously) can shut down all mechs. Alloy Armor: physical attack damage reduction -5.
- Drop: Brion Control Core (summons a CR 4 scout mech, maintains SP 3/round), Experience Points (XP) 350

## Ging's Core
- Challenge Rating (CR): 14
- Type: Boss-tier (part system)
- Weakness: Cannot learn attacks it has never seen; after clearing spores it needs 1 round to re-analyze.
- Drop: Ging's Seed (when planted, grows a defensive barrier after 1 hour; +3 AC to all allies within 24hr), Experience Points (XP) 30-40

## V5 Secret Fleet Flagship
- Challenge Rating (CR): 15
- Type: Vehicle-type Boss
- Hit Points (HP): 500
- Armor Class (AC): 22
- Weakness: Engine room (must infiltrate the ship, Perception DC 20 to find entrance). After destroying the engine, AC -6, cannot move.
- Drop: V5 Anti-Nen Tech Blueprint, V5 Top Secret File

## Twin-Tail Serpent · Helbel
- Challenge Rating (CR): 18
- Type: Legendary-tier (part-system subjugation)
- Weakness: During slumber AC -4, Initiative -5. Twin-Tail Linked Nerves (Perception DC 20): when attacking both tails simultaneously, damage ×1.5.
- Drop: Helbel Scale ×10 (AC +3, Nen Defense +2 armor material), Twin-Tail Crystal (absorption-type Nen equipment material), Experience Points (XP) 60-80

## Judge (Echo of the First Civilization)
- Challenge Rating (CR): 20
- Type: Ultimate Boss (optional combat)
- Hit Points (HP): 400
- Armor Class (AC): 24
- Special Mechanic: The Judge is not a mandatory combat enemy. The dialogue trial is the main gameplay content.
- Drop: First Civilization Omniscience Crystal (one ultimate question), Experience Points (XP) 100 (passed dialogue) / 500 (defeated)

## V5 Destroyer

- Type: Vehicle-type Mid-tier
- Hit Points (HP): 250
- Armor Class (AC): 18
- Weakness: After destroying the engine room, AC -4.
