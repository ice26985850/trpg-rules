# Hunter × Hunter TRPG — Side Quest Collection & Random Encounters

> **Version:** v1.0
> **Purpose:** To fill the gap between Star and Legendary tiers, and to provide randomized content so experiences do not repeat.
> **Usage:** After the Player Characters (PCs) complete the main scenarios, the Game Master (GM) draws quests from the corresponding table according to the growth stage.

---

## I. Usage Guide

After clearing the five main scenarios, the Player Characters (PCs) are roughly between Licensed Hunter and Professional Hunter. Star Hunter (151 Experience Points (XP)) and Legendary (250 Experience Points (XP)) require additional Experience Points (XP) sources. This document provides:

- **Side Commission Pool** (randomly drawn to ensure each playthrough differs)
- **Random Encounter Tables** (travel / urban / wilderness)
- **Association Special Missions** (high difficulty, designed for Star tier and above)
- **World Events** (global random events that affect multiple scenarios)

---

## II. Side Commission Pool

### Usage

Before each session, the Game Master (GM) draws **2–3 commissions** from the commission pool of the corresponding stage for the Player Characters (PCs) to choose from (or decides randomly by rolling dice).

**Effect (permanently gained after experience):**
- Intelligence +1 (cap 22), from experiencing the life of a highly intelligent being
- Gain "Blessing of the First Civilization": when facing the Five Calamities, advantage on Persuasion checks (the calamities' collective consciousness recognizes the First Civilization's aura)
- Unlock hidden information: the Player Characters (PCs) now know "the Ancient God's True Name"—gain +5 bonus on any check related to the Ancient God
- Mental cost: after the experience ends, requires Willpower Difficulty Class (DC) 22 or gain 48 hours of "existential void" (cannot gain rest benefits)

### 8-2 V5 Secret: Anti-Nen Resonance Device (Commission #7)

**Nature:** V5's ultimate anti-Nen weapon, secretly developed over 50 years. Using the "specific frequency released when Nen users use Nen" for reverse resonance, it can remotely paralyze or eliminate Nen users.

**Device Data:**
- **Activation time:** 72 hours charging
- **Effective range:** Global (resonance waves propagated via satellite network)
- **Effect (after activation):** All Nen users in range must make a Willpower saving throw Difficulty Class (DC) 30. Success → Nen ability sealed for 24 hours (equivalent to Nen Seal state); Failure → permanently lose Nen ability (SP to zero, cannot learn or use any Nen ability)
- **Weaknesses:** ① The device must be placed at a specific location on the equator (V5 chose an uninhabited island in the Barusa Archipelago) ② During activation the device emits a huge Nen field (Nen users within 100km can all sense it—"someone is attacking Nen itself") ③ The device can be physically destroyed (HP 500, Armor Class (AC) 18, no Nen Defense—because it targets Nen itself) ④ If the resonance frequency is reverse-engineered—it can be rewritten as a selective weapon "targeting only specific Nen types"

**Choices the Player Characters (PCs) can make and consequences:**

| Choice | Mechanism | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| Destroy the device | Infiltrate the island (CR 16 security), destroy the core | V5's 50 years of research go up in smoke. The Player Characters (PCs) become V5's most-wanted criminal—but gain the secret gratitude of all Nen users |
| Control the device | Intelligence DC 26 + tech-type Nen ability assistance, rewrite target parameters | The device becomes a "selective anti-Nen weapon"—usable against Nen abusers. The Association gains a controversial ultimate weapon |
| Persuade V5 | Persuasion DC 28, collect testimony from 12 countries proving "Nen users' value to humanity exceeds their threat" | The device is permanently sealed but not destroyed—may restart in the future. The most peaceful but most fragile solution |
| Expose the truth | Make the device's existence public | Global panic. The relationship between Nen users and ordinary people changes from then on. May trigger a global "Hunters vs. Humanity" conflict |

## IX. Game Master (GM) Daily Preparation Quick Table

Five minutes before the session starts, the Game Master (GM) rolls the following tables to prepare the session's random elements:

```
1. Roll d20 to pick 2 side commissions (matching PC stage)
2. Roll d20 to pick 1 travel encounter (if PCs travel)
3. Roll d8 to decide whether a modifier triggers (DC 15 → if triggered, roll d8 to pick modifier)
4. If a temporary NPC is needed, roll d12+d8+d10 to generate quickly
5. If this session has a Legendary reward, roll d12 to pick an item
```
