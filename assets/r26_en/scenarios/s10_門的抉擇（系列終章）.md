# Scenario 10: The Choice at the Gate (Series Finale)

> **Player Character (PC) stage:** Legendary (400+ Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 6-8 hours
> **Prerequisite:** Complete at least three of Scenarios 5–9

---

## Background

The Dark Continent is changing. The Five Calamities' activity is increasing, the Gate's energy is waning, and the First Civilization's Tribunal has foreseen an event —

**The Gate will be forced open. Not from the inside — but from the outside.**

V5's secret fleet is assembling at Mobius Lake. After three hundred years of the Silence Treaty, they have decided not to wait — to open the Gate by force and conquer the Dark Continent's resources.

But they do not know what they are doing. The Gate is not a door that can be "forced open by force." Forcing it open will cause the Gate's collapse — the boundary between the two worlds vanishes, and the Dark Continent's ecology (including the Five Calamities) floods wholly into the human world.

---

## Scene 1: Final Preparations

**Duration:** 90 minutes

The Player Characters (PCs) have 72 hours to make choices and prepare among the following options:

| Preparation Action | Effect |
|------|------|
| Persuade V5 to retreat (Persuasion skill chain) | Difficulty Class (DC) starts at 18, +2 each failure. On success, V5 retreats |
| Summon Dark Continent allies (Gatekeeper, Kalvi, Eh) | Gain Non-Player Character (NPC) reinforcements |
| Reinforce the Gate (using First Civilization knowledge) | Gate's durability increases |
| Evacuate human coastal cities | If the final battle spills into the human world, reduce casualties |

---

## Scene 2: The Fleet Arrives

**Duration:** 120 minutes

> Seventy-two hours later, the V5 fleet appears on Mobius Lake — fifty warships, equipped with "anti-Nen weapons" secretly developed by V5 over three hundred years.

### V5 Secret Fleet Configuration

| Unit | Count | Challenge Rating (CR) | Capability |
|------|:---:|:---:|------|
| Flagship | 1 | 15 | Anti-Nen field (within range, Spirit Point (SP) cost ×3) |
| Destroyer | 5 | 10 | Anti-Nen cannon (3d10 energy damage, ignores Nen Defense) |
| Landing Craft | 20 | 6 | Each carries 10× V5 special forces (CR 3) |

### Key NPC: V5 Commander — Edmund Ray

> A sixty-year-old man stands on the flagship's command deck. He is no soldier — he is a politician, a diplomat, the one who has maintained the Silence Treaty for three hundred years.
>
> "I know you are heroes." He says into the communicator, his voice weary. "But heroes cannot change one thing: this world is running out of resources. The Dark Continent is the only hope."
>
> "I beg you — step aside."

**Edmund is not a villain.** He is a man of despair. The PCs may choose:
- Combat: Sink the fleet
- Persuade: Prove the Dark Continent is not "resources" but "alive" (needs to reveal the true nature of the Five Calamities)
- A third path: Propose a new accord — V5 may obtain specific Dark Continent resources, but must obey the Gate's rules

---

## Scene 3: The Choice at the Gate

**Duration:** 120 minutes

Whatever the PCs do with V5, the final choice will come: the Gate's energy is running dry. If nothing is done, the Gate will close within 24 hours — permanently.

### Three Choices

| Choice | Result |
|------|------|
| **Close the Gate — Forever** | The Dark Continent and the human world are permanently separated. The PCs must choose before the Gate closes: withdraw to the human world, or stay in the Dark Continent (see "Staying Beyond the Gate" below). After the Gate vanishes, Mobius Lake returns to an ordinary ocean. |

### If the PCs Choose to Stay Beyond the Gate (after the Gate closes)

This is the PCs' voluntary ultimate sacrifice. The Game Master (GM) has the following handling routes:

| Route | PC Fate | Future Availability |
|------|------|------|
| **Last Gatekeeper** | The PC continues to live in the Dark Continent, becoming its guardian on that side. Though the Gate is closed, the PC can send information back to the human world through First Civilization ruin equipment (once per year, 50 words max per time). | That PC becomes a Legendary Non-Player Character (NPC), appearing as a mysterious messenger in future Dark Continent foreshadowing |
| **Beginning of a New Civilization** | The PC discovers other trapped humans in the Dark Continent (descendants of survivors from earlier expeditions) and establishes a small settlement. A thousand years later, this settlement grows into a new civilization. | The GM may arrange in future scenarios the plot "when the Gate reopens, a new human civilization already exists on the other side" |
| **Return Foreshadowing** | The PC is "frozen" by First Civilization ruin power in the last second before the Gate closes, set to awaken under specific conditions (e.g., "when humanity opens the Gate again"). | The PC temporarily retires but retains the possibility of return — suitable for players who do not want to permanently abandon the character |
| **Legendary Finale** | The PC spends the rest of their life in the Dark Continent, leaving behind a large amount of notes and research. These notes are preserved by the First Civilization ruins and discovered at some point in the future. | The PC formally retires. The player may create a new character, who might discover the former PC's notes in the future |
| **Open the Gate — To All** | Cancel all restrictions; any human may enter the Dark Continent. This means colonization, plunder, war — but also exploration, discovery, new possibilities. The boundary of human civilization disappears from then on |
| **Guard the Gate — Maintain the Status Quo** | Keep the Gate operating, with the Gatekeeper (PC or NPC) continuing to control entry and exit. This is the hardest path, but perhaps the only balanced one |

## Finale: The World After

### If the Gate Is Closed

> The Gate turns into a beam of light, then vanishes from the lake's surface. The Dark Continent, the Five Calamities, the First Civilization, the Crystallized Wasteland, the Tear Sea — everything becomes memory.
>
> You stand on the deck, watching the empty sea. In your hand you clutch a memory crystal — the images inside still clear.
>
> There was a world behind that Gate. You saw it. Now you must live — with that loneliness no one can understand — through the rest of your days.
>
> But you know: the one who made this choice will not regret it.

**Reward:** Title "Sealer of the Gate." The PCs gain lifetime honor from the Association. But the memory of the Dark Continent exists only in the PCs' hearts.

### If the Gate Is Opened

> The Gate expands. No longer a hole in a water wall — the entire Mobius Lake becomes a waterfall. The Dark Continent's sky and the human world's sky join into one.
>
> On the first day, a Kakin merchant ship crosses the waterfall.
> In the first month, twelve nations establish footholds in the Dark Continent.
> In the first year, a new era begins.
>
> You watch all this, unsure whether you are a liberator — or a destroyer.
>
> But a voice in your heart says: the Gate was never meant to block. It was meant to test. You passed. Now — it is humanity's own journey.

**Reward:** Title "Pioneer." The PCs become symbols of the new era. Opens unlimited expansion possibilities afterward — a "Dark Continent Colonial Era" scenario series may be released.

### If the Gate Is Guarded

> The Gate stabilizes. You or your companions stand before the Gate — the new Gatekeepers. The V5 fleet retreats, carrying a new accord: each year, one vetted expedition team may enter the Dark Continent. No more, no less.
>
> The world has not changed. But the world has a new guardian.
>
> Ou's scale glows faintly on your chest — you can feel it; somewhere, he is smiling.

**Reward:** Title "Guardian of the Gate." The PCs become Legendary Non-Player Characters (NPCs). The Gate becomes a permanently usable facility — any future scenario may depart from the Gate to enter the Dark Continent.

# Afterword: Words to the Game Master (GM)

These ten scenarios form a legendary journey from "a candidate who does not even know what Nen is" to "a Gatekeeper who holds humanity's fate."

The five Dark Continent expansion scenarios (Six–Ten) are not independent units — they are interwoven. The PCs might meet Kalvi in Scenario 6 and meet him again in Scenario 8; might help a certain prince in Scenario 7, and that prince becomes a key negotiation chip in Scenario 10.

**The best scenario is one that lets the PCs' choices leave a mark.**

Remember one thing: the Dark Continent is not the final Boss — it is a mirror. It reflects not monsters, but humanity itself. The five calamities are the dying words of five civilizations, the First Civilization's Trial is a question to all, and the Choice at the Gate is a question each PC must answer for themselves.

**May your session be brilliant.**
