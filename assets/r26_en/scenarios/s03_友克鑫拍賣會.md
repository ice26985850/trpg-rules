# Scenario 3: Yorknew Auction — Bidding in the Dark Night

> **Growth Stage:** Licensed Hunter (41-80 XP)
> **Suggested Players:** 3-5
> **Estimated Duration:** 6-8 hours
> **Prerequisites:** Hold a Hunter License

---

## Background

Every September, Yorknew City — the world's most prosperous capital of sin — hosts the world's largest underground auction. Rich people, Mafia, collectors, and Hunters from all over the world gather to bid on rare treasures from beast specimens, ancient ruin artifacts, to legendary Nen equipment.

But this year's auction has a special item: **"Heart of the Dark Night" — a black crystal said to come from the Dark Continent.** Its starting bid is 5 billion Jenny, and around it, five factions are moving in the shadows.

Your client — a special liaison from the Hunter Association's Intelligence Division — has given you a mission.

## Scene 2: The Auction — Night Falls

**Duration:** 120 min
**Scene Type:** Infiltration / Social

### Auction Hall

> The hall is on the underground third floor of Yorknew's central tower. Behind heavy blast doors is a giant auditorium seating five hundred. Crystal chandeliers, velvet seats, well-dressed rich and bodyguards — if you didn't know half the goods traded here were stolen, you'd think you were at an elegant charity gala.

### Bidding Order

| Order | Auction Item | Starting Bid | Bidder |
|:---:|------|:---:|------|
| 1 | Rare beast horn | 50 million J | Collector |
| 2 | Ancient civilization stone slab | 80 million J | Ruins Hunter |
| 3 | Nen Equipment · Shadow Blade | 200 million J | Mafia, Hunters |
| 4 | Phantom Beast eggs ×3 | 500 million J | Beast Hunter |
| 5 | **Heart of the Dark Night** | **5 billion J** | **Everyone** |
| 6 | (Undisclosed) | — | — |

### Sixth Item (Plot Twist)

When the Heart of the Dark Night bidding is halfway:

> The lights go out. Not figuratively — every light source in the hall, including emergency lights, goes out at once.
>
> In the dark, you hear glass shattering, human screams, the scent of Nen bursting in the air.
>
> A low voice comes from all directions:
> "Esteemed guests, sorry to interrupt your shopping. We've come to take something."

**The Phantom Troupe raid begins.**

## Scene 3: Melee — Five Factions Clash

**Duration:** 150 min
**Scene Type:** Combat / Infiltration / Chase

### Melee Map

The auction hall is divided into five zones:

| Zone | Environment | Current State |
|------|------|------|
| Main Hall | Spacious, lots of cover (overturned tables/chairs) | Troupe main attack zone, three here |
| VIP booth | Narrow, single exit | Mafia protecting VIP evacuation |
| Backstage corridor | Long and narrow, many corners | Guards fighting the Troupe |
| Vault | Thick iron door (DC 25 to break) | Heart of the Dark Night storage (already breached) |
| Underground parking | Open, vehicles as cover | Convergence point of all factions' evacuation routes |

### Troupe Member Deployment (adjust by PC strength)

| Troupe Member | CR | Position | Behavior |
|------|:---:|------|------|
| Fighter A | 6 | Main Hall | Suppress guards, won't attack Hunters proactively |
| Fighter B | 5 | Vault | Seize the Heart of the Dark Night |
| Support | 4 | Parking | Secure evacuation route |

> **GM Note:** The Troupe's real members should be CR 8+, but here we use the Troupe's "outer members" or "new recruits." This lets the PC experience the Troupe's threat without making the scenario too hard. If the PC is already Professional Hunter tier, raise the Troupe members' CR to 8-10.

### Key Choice Point: Fate of the Heart of the Dark Night

When the PC catches up to the Troupe member seizing the Heart of the Dark Night, they face a choice:

| Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| **Recover the Heart of the Dark Night** | Gain Association favor, but the Troupe marks the PC as a target (foreshadows future scenarios) |
| **Let the Troupe go** | A Troupe member leaves a word: "Owe you one," may appear as unexpected reinforcements sometime later |
| **Negotiate with the Troupe** | If the PC can provide intel or items the Troupe is interested in, a deal may be struck |
