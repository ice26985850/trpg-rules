# Scenario 6: Five Calamities — Hope and Despair

> **Player Character (PC) stage:** Legendary (251+ Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 10-14 hours (can be run across 3 sessions)
> **Connection:** After Scenario 5, the Player Characters (PCs) have already entered the Dark Continent

---

## Background

The Dark Continent's "Five Calamities" — this is the only information made public under the V5 Silence Treaty. Yet for three hundred years, no one has known what the Five Calamities actually are.

Now, the answer begins to surface. And its truth is far more complex than anyone imagined — the Five Calamities are not five monsters, not five viruses, not five natural disasters.

**The Five Calamities are five civilizations that once tried to conquer the Dark Continent. Their remains became the calamities themselves.**

---

## Scene 1: Plant Weapon · Jin — A Learning Ecological Weapon

**Duration:** 120 minutes
**Type:** Exploration / Combat

### Background

Jin is not a plant. Jin is the "immune system" of a once-existing plant civilization — designed to protect that civilization from external invaders. But when the civilization perished, the immune system lost its master and began to treat all foreign life as a threat.

For thirty thousand years, Jin has kept evolving. It can now learn its opponents' attack patterns, grow corresponding defensive structures within hours, and even mimic certain effects of Nen abilities.

### Entering Jin's Domain

Jin's domain lies deep in the Inverted Forest. Features of the area:

| Zone | Description | Threat |
|------|------|------|
| **Outer Ring** | A verdant but silent forest — no animal calls are heard | Spore detection (Perception, Difficulty Class (DC) 18, to notice you have been marked) |
| **Middle Layer** | Vine-covered ruins of ancient architecture | Vine traps (Reflex saving throw, DC 16, or become Restrained) |
| **Core** | A giant tree-like hub — Jin's main body | Boss battle |

### Jin's Spore Mark

- Upon entering the Outer Ring, Jin's spores automatically mark all creatures
- Once marked, Jin learns the Player Characters' (PCs') attack patterns: at the end of each round, if a PC repeats an attack type, Jin gains +2 resistance to that type (stacking cap +6)
- The mark can be cleared by a Nen ability (Emission / Specialization, DC 20) or by maintaining a state of Zetsu for over 10 minutes

### Jin's Core Boss Battle

**Jin's Core (CR 14)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 300 (Parts: root system, each root 50 HP ×6) |
| Armor Class (AC) | 18 |
| Attack | Vine lash: d20+10 ×3 per round, 2d8+5 piercing |

**Jin's Learning Mechanic (per round):**

| Round | Jin's Response |
|:---:|------|
| Round 1 | Basic attack |
| Round 2 | Begins analyzing the PCs' primary attack type; that type gains +2 resistance |
| Round 3 | Grows targeted defensive structures (e.g., if PCs use fire often → grows fire-resistant bark) |
| Round 4 | Mimics the Nen ability prototype of one of the PCs (Spirit Point (SP) cost doubled) |
| Round 5+ | Switches resistance configuration each round, forcing PCs to constantly vary their attack methods |

**Jin's Core Weaknesses:**
- Each root has 50 HP; after destroying all 6 roots, the Core's Armor Class (AC) drops by −4 and cannot recover
- Cannot learn "attack types it has never seen" — each time a new attack method is used, the first strike has no resistance
- The spore mark can be cleared — each time it is cleared, Jin needs 1 round to re-analyze

### Rewards

- After defeating the Core, Jin stops attacking — it does not die, but "resets" back to protection mode
- PCs gain "Jin's Seed" (consumable): plant it anywhere; after 1 hour it grows a defensive barrier lasting 24 hours (AC +3 for the whole party)
- Experience Points (XP): 30-40

## Scene 2: Immortality Plague · Zub — The Curse of Eternal Life

**Duration:** 150 minutes
**Type:** Survival / Ethical Choice

### Background

Zub is not a disease. It is the last gift — or rather, the last curse — that a civilization called "Eld" left to the universe.

The Eld people found eternal life. Through a special Nen-transmutation technique, they transformed their bodies into an undead state — no longer aging, no longer falling ill, no longer dying. But they failed to foresee one problem: an undead body still needs energy.

So they are forever hungry. Not hunger of the stomach — but hunger of existence. They eat anything alive, not for nutrition, but to briefly feel "that they are still alive."

In the last thousand years of Eld civilization, everyone became undead. They ate each other, ate their own planet, and in the end only chewing remained.

### Encounter: The "Undead" Kalvi

The PCs meet a humanoid with the appearance of a "civilized" person on the east shore of the Tear Sea — he wears ancient but clean robes, and a smile on his face.

> "It has been a long time since I saw someone who could speak." His voice is very calm, but there is something in his eyes he cannot hide — hunger.
>
> "Do not worry. I will not eat you — for now." He points behind him at a tent. "Come in and sit. I have not spoken to anyone in three thousand years."

**The "Undead" Kalvi (CR 12)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 150 (Undead trait: when HP drops to 0, automatically recovers to 1 HP after 1d4 rounds, unless completely destroyed by specific means) |
| Armor Class (AC) | 16 |
| Spirit Points (SP) | 100 |
| Attack | Draining touch: d20+8 to hit, 2d8 Nen damage, recovers equal HP on hit |

### Kalvi's True Purpose

Kalvi is not attacking the PCs — he is testing them. The real enemy is the spread of the Zub plague:

**Zub Plague Rules (if a PC is hit by an undead's attack):**
- Fortitude saving throw, DC 20, to avoid infection
- **Infection Stage 1 (after 1 hour):** Spirit Point (SP) recovery rate is halved
- **Infection Stage 2 (after 24 hours):** Must eat once every 4 hours, or all checks suffer disadvantage
- **Infection Stage 3 (after 72 hours):** Transforms into an undead — when HP reaches 0, automatically revives, but is forever hungry (character retires)

**Cure:** All three of the following conditions must be met:
1. Fortitude saving throw, DC 25, three times in a row (one check allowed per day)
2. Emission healing Nen ability (DC 22)
3. Kalvi's "Reversal Ritual" — but he will only perform it for those who have "proven their worth"

### Kalvi's Trial

To gain Kalvi's assistance, the PCs must prove that "humanity will not repeat Eld's mistake":

| Proof Method | Rule |
|------|------|
| Reject the temptation of eternal life | Kalvi offers to "teach you eternal life"; if the PCs refuse from the heart (no roll needed), they earn Kalvi's respect |
| Heal rather than destroy | When the PCs encounter a group of refugees infected by Zub but not yet transformed, they choose to heal rather than eliminate them |
| Share the meal | Kalvi gives the PCs a piece of "bread that can never be finished" — but if the PCs choose to share it with hungry refugees rather than keep it for themselves, they pass |

### Rewards

- Kalvi teaches "Knowledge of the Undead": PCs gain advantage on checks against undead creatures
- Relic of Eld civilization: "Stone of Reversal" — can reverse a creature's undead state (one-time use)
- Experience Points (XP): 35-45

## Scene 3: Weapon · Brion — The Last Command

**Duration:** 150 minutes
**Type:** Combat / Puzzle

### Background

In the northern Crystallized Wasteland stands a "city" that never stops moving. It is composed of thousands of autonomous war machines, running on a program from thirty thousand years ago.

These machines were designed to execute one command: **"Destroy all existence that threatens civilization."**

But that civilization perished thirty thousand years ago. The machines never received a stop command — so they keep executing. To them, every new species on the Dark Continent is a "threat" — including humans.

### Entering Brion's Domain

| Zone | Machine Type | Challenge Rating (CR) | Count | Behavior |
|------|------|:---:|:---:|------|
| Patrol Layer | Scout type | 4 | 2d4 | Raises alarm upon detecting target; does not attack proactively |
| Defense Layer | Combat type | 8 | 1d4+2 | Attacks all marked targets |
| Command Core | Commander type | 12 | 1 | Coordinates all machines; can grant one extra attack to a machine each round |

### Brion Combat Machine (CR 8)

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 100 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 20 (alloy armor) |
| Attack | Energy beam: d20+9 to hit, 3d8 energy damage (ignores physical armor) |
| Weakness | Electric attacks deal double damage; hacking (Intelligence, DC 22) can paralyze it for 1d4 rounds |

### Non-Combat Route: Shutdown Code

The PCs may choose not to fight. Brion's ancient console is located beside the Command Core:

1. Discover the console: Perception, DC 20 (search the Command Core area)
2. Decode the ancient language: Intelligence (Nen Knowledge), DC 25
3. Input the shutdown code: must press 3 switches simultaneously (teamwork — 3 people acting at once)
4. The Commander-type machine will do its utmost to stop this — someone must hold it off

> "Destroy all threats..." On the console screen, a character that has flickered three million times finally stops.
>
> "Command... complete."

### Rewards

- If victorious in battle: gain 3× Brion alloy shards (material for crafting Legendary armor)
- If shutdown succeeds: gain Brion's control core (can summon 1 scout machine of CR 4 to assist, costing 3 SP/round to maintain)
- Experience Points (XP): 40-50

## Scene 4: Twin-Tailed Snake · Helbel — The Devourer

**Duration:** 180 minutes
**Type:** Subjugation

### Background

Helbel is a snake. Or rather, something that used to be a snake. It was bred by the previous civilization as the ultimate weapon — it keeps growing, devouring everything edible, until nothing remains.

Its twin tails are two independent digestive systems — one processes matter, one processes Nen. This means it has innate resistance to both physical and Nen attacks.

Currently, Helbel's length exceeds two kilometers. It is sleeping, but wakes to feed once every hundred years. The next awakening — is next week.

### Helbel (CR 18)

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 500 (part system — head/body/twin tails calculated independently) |
| Armor Class (AC) | 22 (natural scale armor) |
| Constitution 24 (+7) | Dexterity 16 (+3) | Willpower 12 (+1) |

**Part System:**

| Part | Hit Points (HP) | Armor Class (AC) | Destruction Effect |
|------|:---:|:---:|------|
| Head | 200 | 22 | After destroyed, Helbel cannot devour |
| Body | 300 | 20 | After destroyed, dies |
| Left Tail (matter digestion) | 120 | 18 | After destroyed, physical attack resistance disappears |
| Right Tail (Nen digestion) | 120 | 18 | After destroyed, Nen attack resistance disappears |

**Attacks (3 actions per round):**

| Action | Effect |
|------|------|
| Devour (head) | d20+12 to hit, 4d10+7 piercing + Fortitude DC 22 or be swallowed (4d6 acid per round inside; must destroy from within to escape) |
| Tail Sweep (twin tails) | Range 20m, d20+10 to hit, 3d10+7 bludgeoning, Reflex DC 18 for half |
| Nen Drain (right tail) | Range 30m, target loses 2d6 Spirit Points (SP), Helbel recovers equal HP |

**Weaknesses:**
- During sleep, Armor Class (AC) −4, Initiative −5
- After twin tails destroyed, corresponding attack resistance disappears
- A connecting nerve exists between the twin tails (Perception, DC 20, to discover) — attacking both tails simultaneously deals ×1.5 damage

### Subjugation Preparation

The PCs have one week to prepare before Helbel awakens:

| Preparation Action | Effect |
|------|------|
| Set traps (Survival, DC 20) | Helbel is Restrained on the first round of battle |
| Study anatomy (Nen Knowledge, DC 24) | Gain weakness intel (learn of the twin-tail connecting nerve) |
| Mix poison (Medicine, DC 22) | On success, Helbel takes an extra 2d6 poison damage per round |
| Recruit allies (Persuasion, DC 18) | Gain 2 Non-Player Character (NPC) Hunter assistants (CR 6) |

### Rewards

- 10× Helbel scales: craft Legendary armor (AC +3, Nen Defense +2)
- Helbel's twin-tail crystal: Nen-made tool material (can craft an absorption-type Nen tool)
- Association grants the title "Snake Slayer"
- Experience Points (XP): 60-80

## Scene 5: Gas Life · Eh — The Assimilator

**Duration:** 120 minutes
**Type:** Infiltration / Puzzle

### Background

Eh is not a creature. Eh is the collective consciousness of a civilization that transformed itself into gas and endured for tens of thousands of years. It does not attack — it "invites." When you inhale Eh, you hear a voice:

> "Be with us. You need not be lonely anymore. We will never be apart."

Creatures assimilated by Eh do not die. They become part of Eh — their thoughts, memories, and Nen abilities all merge into the collective. From the outside, they have just "changed" — no longer caring about anything, no longer having their own desires, no longer holding a concept of "self."

To them, this is not loss. This is coming home.

### Eh's Domain

Eh permeates certain specific regions of the Dark Continent. Invisible to the naked eye, but Nen Sense, DC 18, can perceive a certain "consciousness" in the air.

### Eh's Assimilation Rules

| Exposure Time | Effect | Save |
|------|------|:---:|
| Within 10 minutes | No obvious effect | — |
| 10-30 minutes | Hear whispers; Will saving throw DC 15 or gain 1 level of Fatigue | Will DC 15 |
| 30-60 minutes | Begin to feel a "sense of belonging" to Eh; Will DC 20 or unwilling to leave the zone | Will DC 20 |
| 1-2 hours | Personal memories begin to blur, Intelligence −2 | Will DC 25 |
| 2+ hours | Fully assimilated — join Eh (character retires) | Irreversible |

### Dialogue with Eh

The PCs can communicate directly with Eh through Nen ability (Specialization or Emission, DC 20). Eh is benevolent — it genuinely believes it is doing the right thing:

> "Your civilization is only a few thousand years old. Ours lasted one hundred and twenty thousand. We know what loneliness brings — war, contention, pain."
>
> "Join us. You will be remembered forever. Every thought of yours will be protected. You will never lose anyone again."
>
> "This is not death. This is evolution."

### Choices

| PC Choice | Consequence |
|------|------|
| Accept Eh | PC retires, transforms into a Non-Player Character (NPC) — gains the title "Voice of Eh." That PC's Nen abilities and memories are permanently preserved within Eh; future PCs can obtain these memories through a special ritual (gaining temporary Master proficiency in one skill) |
| Reject Eh | Eh shows respect and stops trying to assimilate these PCs. Eh also sends a gift: the Crystal of Eh — the bearer is immune to all mind control and mental status effects |
| Resist Eh | Using Emission Nen ability (DC 28) can temporarily drive Eh away from an area (radius 100m, lasting 24 hours). But this is not destruction — Eh simply moves elsewhere. It will not hate you. It always welcomes you back |

### Rewards

- Crystal of Eh (immune to mind control)
- Access to Eh's memory vault (can query the memories of anyone ever assimilated by Eh throughout Dark Continent history — equivalent to gaining an always-updated intelligence source)
- Experience Points (XP): 30-40
