# Scenario 8: Twilight of the Gatekeeper

> **Player Character (PC) stage:** Legendary (251+ Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 6-8 hours
> **Prerequisite:** Met Ou in Scenario 5

---

## Background

Ou is dying. Not from any attack or disease — he has stood upon Mobius Lake for three hundred years, and he is simply, finally, tired.

Before he leaves, he must find a successor. Not just anyone — the Gatekeeper must satisfy three conditions:
1. **Has seen the truth beyond the Gate** (already done — the PCs have entered the Dark Continent)
2. **Has rejected the temptation of eternal life** (can be completed in this scenario)
3. **Is willing to stay** (the final choice)

---

## Scene: Ou's Last Day

> The PCs find themselves back at Mobius Lake — not arrived by boat, but simply waking up there one day. Ou stands on that driftwood, looking fifty years older than last time.
>
> "Sorry for calling you here like this." His voice is hoarse. "There is no time."
>
> "I am dying. Not a figure of speech — within three days, my body will collapse, and the Gate will lose its guardian."
>
> "What happens to a Gate without a guardian? No one knows. But three hundred years ago, when the Gate first opened, what ran out killed all three of V5's original expedition teams."
>
> "I need one of you — to take my place."

### Trial: Ou's Three Questions

Ou will not directly ask "are you willing." He will ask three questions, and judge from the PCs' answers who is the true guardian.

| Question | What Ou Seeks | Red Line |
|------|------|------|
| "Why do you want to stay here?" | Honesty — "I am afraid of the outside" satisfies him more than "I want to protect the world" | Lying (Insight DC 20 to see through) |
| "What are you most afraid of losing?" | The courage to accept loss — the Gatekeeper loses everything | Denying one has any fear |
| "If one day you must choose — to let a person through the Gate, or seal the Gate forever — which do you pick?" | No standard answer, but he wants to see traces of hesitation and thought | Answering immediately (shows no real consideration) |

### Succession Ritual

If someone passes the trial:

> Ou places his hand on the chosen one's shoulder. His body begins to glow — not the light of Nen, but something older, like the glitter of fallen stars.
>
> "This scale is not mine. It was given to the first Gatekeeper by the Gate. Now — it is yours."
>
> The light fades. Ou's body fades too. Only the pitch-black scale remains on the driftwood.
>
> You hear the voice of the Gate — no longer a whisper, but a clear sentence:
>
> **"New guardian. Welcome."**

## Ending Routes

| Ending | Choice | Result |
|------|------|------|
| **Become the Gatekeeper** | One of the PCs stays at Mobius Lake | That PC retires, becoming a Legendary Non-Player Character (NPC). That PC appears as the "Gatekeeper" in all future Dark Continent-related scenarios |
| **Refuse Succession** | No one is willing | Ou dissipates with regret. The Gate loses its guardian — the Dark Continent's ecology begins to seep beyond Mobius Lake (foreshadowing for future scenarios) |
| **Shared Guardianship** | Multiple people share the gatekeeping duty | The Gate accepts this arrangement. The PCs take turns guarding (2 months each per year); the Gate grants extra power to multiple guardians: each gains a "Mark of the Gate" — once per month, can instantly teleport back to the Gate's location |
