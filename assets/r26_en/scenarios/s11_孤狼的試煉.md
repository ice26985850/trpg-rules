# Scenario S1: Trial of the Lone Wolf

> **Growth Stage:** Rookie (0-15 Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 3-4 hours
> **Premise:** The Player Character (PC) has obtained a Hunter License but has not yet completed an independent commission.

---

## Background

The excitement of getting your Hunter License lasted only three days. Now you sit on a bench in the Association's commission hall, watching the scrolling task list on the screen—most require "a squad of 3 or more."

Then you notice a commission posted in the corner, signed by a single letter: **"K"**.

> "Need a Hunter. No squad needed. One person is enough. Reward: 5,000J. Details to be discussed in person. Location: Warehouse No. 7, Port District, Talaba City."

Below it, a smaller line: **"Please come before nightfall. After dark, the warehouse is not safe."**

---

## Scene One: Warehouse Meeting — Commissioner K

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Type:** Social

> The iron door of Warehouse No. 7 is half open. You push it open and see a woman around fifty sitting on a wooden crate, with a birdcage covered by canvas beside her.
>
> "Hunter. Please sit." She points to the crate opposite her.
> "My name is Kira. I was once a logistics officer for a ruins excavation team."
>
> She lifts the cloth from the birdcage. Inside is a bird with dull feathers—but its third eye is glowing.
>
> "I found this bird deep within a ruins. The ancient civilization called it the 'Spy Plume'. It can see things ordinary people cannot."
> "But ever since I brought it out—someone has been following me."
>
> "I need you to escort me to Association Headquarters. We depart tonight. Take the underground passage—the surface is too dangerous."

**Kira (NPC)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Challenge Rating (CR) | 1/2 (non-combatant, but has a Spy Plume) |
| Personality | Calm, pragmatic, secretive |
| Provides to PC | Underground passage route map, 1 first-aid kit |

**Optional: Kira as a Temporary Companion**

If the PC suggests "You'd be safer coming with me," Kira agrees. She uses the **Support-type NPC Companion** rules (Solo Play Rules Supplement §3): Hit Points (HP) 30 / Spirit Points (SP) 15 / Skills: Perception +2, Nen Knowledge +2 / Nen ability: Emergency Treatment.

---

## Scene Two: Underground Passage — Not Empty

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Type:** Exploration / Combat

> The underground passage is the drainage system of the old district—only wide enough for two people side by side. The Spy Plume in Kira's hand occasionally gives a low chirp—it has sensed something.

### Passage Structure (Four Segments)

| Segment | Environment | Event |
|:---:|------|------|
| A | Dry brick tunnel | Fresh footprints found (Tracking DC 12 → someone has set an ambush in advance) |
| B | Flooded area | Manipulated rat swarm hidden in the water (Manipulation Nen user's trap—CR 1/4 ×3, Perception DC 14 to avoid) |
| C | Old sluice gate | Gate locked with chains (Sleight of Hand DC 14 to pick, or Constitution DC 15 to break) |
| D | Shadow before the exit | Boss—the stalker appears |

### Stalker: "Rat Tamer" Grey (CR 2)

A thin man steps out of the shadows. He wears a tattered long coat; rat tails poke out of his pockets.

> "Hand over the bird. It's not yours—it belongs to 'us'."

**"Rat Tamer" Grey (Manipulation, CR 2)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 45 |
| Spirit Points (SP) | 25 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 12 |
| Attack | Rat Swarm Attack: d20+3, 1d6+1 piercing + Poison DC 12 |
| Nen Ability | Rat Dominion (SP 2): Summon 1d4 CR 1/4 rats to join the fight (once per combat) |
| Weakness | Grey's body is extremely weak (HP only 45, AC 12). Defeating the body causes the rat swarm to scatter automatically. |

**Combat Hints for the GM:**
- This is a solo tutorial fight. Let the PC feel the tactical choice of "attack the body vs. clear the minions"
- If the PC is at a disadvantage, Kira uses the Spy Plume's ability: spend 1 turn to point out Grey's weakness (next turn, PC's attacks against Grey gain advantage)

---

## Scene Three: On the Way to the Association — Kira's Secret

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Type:** Social / Roleplay

After defeating Grey, the PC and Kira continue on their way. While resting at a safe spot—

> Kira is silent for a long time. Then she says:
> "I'm not an ordinary logistics officer."
>
> "Five years ago, I was a Ruins Hunter. I discovered the Spy Plume's nest at the edge of the Dark Continent."
> "That ruins held more than birds—there were other things. The Association... sealed those things away. But someone sold them."
>
> "Grey was just a middleman. The real mastermind is inside the Association."
>
> She looks at the PC: "Take me to see the Association's higher-ups. I need to tell them in person—that someone is experimenting with ruins creatures."

**PC's Choice:**

| Choice | Result |
|------|------|
| Agree to help (Persuasion DC 10 or direct agreement) | Kira becomes the PC's long-term contact. In future Scenario S3 (Echoes in the Depths of the Ruins), Kira reappears to provide intel. |
| Only complete the escort, do not get involved further | Kira says she understands and goes to the Association alone. The PC's reputation is unaffected, but loses a potential ally. |

---

## Scene Four: Association Headquarters — An Unexpected Ending

**Duration:** 20 minutes

> The receptionist at Association Headquarters takes Kira's report. She looks at the PC:
> "You're a rookie, right? Completed a commission alone?"
>
> She flips through Kira's commission record—then looks up, her expression changing:
> "A K-class commission—no backup, no teammates, designated solo. And you did it."
>
> She stamps the PC's Hunter file:
> "Independent Operation Permit—issued."

**Scenario S1 Rewards:**

| Reward | Details |
|------|------|
| Experience Points (XP) | 10-12 XP |
| Items | 5,000J, Independent Operation Permit (allows accepting solo-only commissions) |
| Relationship | Kira (contact—used in Scenario S3) |
| Companion | Optional: If the PC builds a good relationship with Kira, she lends the Spy Plume to the PC (small scouting companion, CR 1/4) |
