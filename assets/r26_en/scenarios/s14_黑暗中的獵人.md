# Scenario S4: Hunter in the Dark

> **Growth Stage:** Professional Hunter (81-150 Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 5-6 hours
> **Premise:** Completed Scenario S3

---

## Background

Four months later. The Association's Intelligence Division tracked down the "Collector"—he is hiding in a secret base in the Kakin Kingdom. The Intelligence Division does not plan to send a large force—because the Collector is Kakin nobility, open action would trigger a diplomatic crisis.

They need a **plausibly deniable person**. A Hunter. One person.

> "This is a solo mission. No backup. No official record. If you're caught—we'll say you acted on your own."
> "Are you sure you want to take it?"

---

## Scene One: Kakin Kingdom — Infiltration

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Type:** Infiltration / Social

> The capital of the Kakin Kingdom—you've never seen such a lavish city. Noble carriages travel streets paved with golden brick, secret police at every corner.
>
> The Collector's base is a three-story mansion in the noble district. Six guards at the front gate. But the side servant passage is watched by only one old butler.

### Infiltration Routes (PC chooses one)

| Route | Check | Risk |
|------|:---:|------|
| Disguise as a delivery worker | Deception DC 18 | If failed, interrogated by guards (Persuasion DC 16 to slip away) |
| Jump from the neighboring building's roof | Acrobatics DC 16 | If failed, fall 6m (2d6 damage + draw attention) |
| Bribe the butler | Persuasion DC 16 or 3,000J | Always succeeds, but costs money |
| Sneak in through the garden after nightfall | Stealth DC 14 | Requires waiting 4 hours |

> **If the PC holds the "Shadow"-engraved coin from Scenario S2:** Present this coin in Kakin's underground society—a black-market merchant recognizes it and is willing to provide the mansion's interior map (free), because he owes the twins a favor.

---

## Scene Two: Inside the Mansion — The Collector's "Collection"

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Type:** Exploration / Moral Choice

> The mansion's interior is starkly different from its exterior luxury—the corridors are dim, the walls hung not with oil paintings but with specimens. Beast skulls, rare magical beast horns, even a glass cabinet displaying a "human-sized cocoon"—labeled: "Specimen #017—Nen Beast embryo".

- **Study:** Find the Collector's transaction records—with names of Association internal personnel on them (Insight DC 18 to recognize three names, one of whom served as an examiner in the PC's Hunter Exam)
- **Basement:** Live specimens in cages—a juvenile Beast, its Nen continuously drained (to sustain the Collector's Nen ability)

### Moral Choice

The PC may choose:
- **Complete only the mission** (steal the evidence and leave)—the juvenile Beast is left behind. The Collector is not alerted.
- **Release the Beast** (Sleight of Hand DC 16 to pick the lock)—when the Beast escapes it triggers an alarm; the Collector will know someone came. But the Beast will draw away the guards, giving the PC more time to search for evidence.
- **Escape with the Beast**—most difficult, but the Beast becomes the PC's permanent companion (CR 3 juvenile Beast, grows slowly but can evolve to CR 6 in Scenario S5). Requires Stealth DC 20 to lead it through the mansion.

---

## Scene Three: The Collector's Entertainment — Underground Beast Arena

**Duration:** 90 minutes
**Type:** Combat

> Just as you're about to leave, you hear cheers from deep in the basement. You follow the sound—and discover a small beast arena hidden beneath the mansion.
>
> The Collector sits in the VIP box, watching two Beasts tear each other apart. In a cage on the other side of the box—a person is locked up. A Hunter. You recognize the Association badge on that jacket.
>
> The Collector sees you. He smiles.
>
> "Ah—a guest of the Association. I've been waiting for you."
> "How about—care to make a wager? You enter the cage. Beat my pet. I'll let that Hunter go."

### Arena Fight: The Collector's Pet (CR 9)

**"Iron-Scale Lizard" (CR 9, Boss)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 150 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 18 (iron scales) |
| Attack | Bite: d20+9, 2d10+4 piercing + Grapple (Constitution DC 16) |
| Special | Iron Scale Retaliation: When hit by a physical melee attack, the attacker takes 1d6 reflected damage |
| Weakness | No scales below the neck (AC 12). The PC can jump onto its back to attack the weak point via Acrobatics DC 18, or use an Emission Nen ability for a ranged attack on the neck |

**The Collector himself does not fight**—he sits in the box watching. If the PC attempts to attack the Collector during the fight, he activates "Nen Beast Barrier" (SP reduced by 20 directly, creating an impenetrable Nen wall for 3 turns) then flees.

### After the Fight

After defeating the Iron-Scale Lizard—

> The Collector stands from his seat and claps three times. "Splendid. A Hunter trained by the Association is truly extraordinary." He presses a button—the cage door opens. The Hunter inside stumbles out.
>
> "Remember—this is not a defeat. It's an investment. You owe me a favor." The Collector says with a smile.
>
> Only after you leave Kakin do you discover an extra business card in your pocket. It reads only one line:
> **"Next time we meet—I'll bring more interesting pets."**
