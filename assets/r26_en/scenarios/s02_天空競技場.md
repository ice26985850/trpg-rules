# Scenario 2: Heavens Arena — Path to the Summit

> **Growth Stage:** Apprentice Hunter (16-40 XP)
> **Suggested Players:** 2-4 (can be solo)
> **Estimated Duration:** Replayable; 2-3 hours each time
> **Prerequisites:** Hunter License obtained

---

## Background

Heavens Arena — a giant tower on the Uroubian Continent, over 250 floors tall. It is the holy land of fighters, the trial ground of Nen users, the paradise of gamblers, and the dreamland where slum kids make a name in a single battle.

The rules are simple: win, climb up. Lose, drop down. But you'll soon discover that the real game inside this tower — is people.

---

## Arena Tier System

### Promotion Rules

- Win 10+ matches (not necessarily consecutive) → may apply to promote to the next tier
- Lose a cumulative 4 matches → forcibly demoted to the previous tier
- Above 200th floor: must be able to use Nen, and tier changes require review

### Tier Details

| Tier | Opponent CR | Per-match Prize | XP | Nen Ability Rules | Special Rules |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|------|------|
| 10-50F | 1/4 ~ 1/2 | 500-2,000J | 1 | Forbidden | — |
| 50-100F | 1 ~ 2 | 3,000-10,000J | 2 | Forbidden | "Floor Master Challenge" begins to appear |
| 100-150F | 3 ~ 5 | 15,000-50,000J | 2-3 | Allowed | Weapon restrictions lifted |
| 150-200F | 6 ~ 8 | 60,000-200,000J | 3-4 | Required | Nen-user exclusive zone |
| 200+F | 9+ | 250,000J+ | 4-5 | Nen decides | Association review required before each match |

## Floor Master Challenge: 200th Floor "Wolf Smoke" Wald

> "You have talent. But talent is the easiest thing to waste."
> "If you really want to stand here — prove to me you didn't rely on talent."

**"Wolf Smoke" Wald (CR 8, Boss tier)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| HP | 160 |
| SP | 100 |
| AC | 16 |
| Initiative | +5 |
| Constitution 16 (+3) | Dexterity 16 (+3) | Willpower 18 (+4) |
| Four Major Techniques | Ten Lv.3, Zetsu Lv.2, Ren Lv.3, Hatsu Lv.3 |

**Nen ability: Wolf Smoke (Emission, Binding Pledge points (BP) balance 6/6)**
- Restriction: usable only inside the arena (+2 BP), cannot use any Emission ability again within 1 hour after use (+2 BP), if the opponent voluntarily surrenders during the match this ability immediately ends and Wald must concede (+2 BP)
- Enhancement: range 10m circle (2 BP), adds Paralyzed effect (2 BP), lasts 3 rounds (1 BP), damage +1d6 (1 BP)
- Effect (SP 8): releases dense Nen smoke; all targets in range take 3d6 Nen damage per round + Fortitude DC 16 or Paralyzed

**Three-phase behavior:**
| Phase | HP | Behavior |
|:---:|:---:|------|
| Probe | 100% | Uses only martial arts; observes the PC's Nen ability type |
| Serious | 60% | Uses Wolf Smoke; begins tactical movement |
| Limit | 25% | Two Wolf Smokes released simultaneously; SP cost doubled; attack frequency doubled |

**Loot (after defeat):** 500,000 J, 200th-floor Floor Master qualification, learn "Wolf Smoke's Tactical Notes" (advantage on Tactics checks against Emission-type opponents next time)
