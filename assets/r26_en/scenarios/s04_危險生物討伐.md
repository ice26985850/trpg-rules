# Scenario 4: Dangerous Beast Subjugation — Fang of the Abyss

> **Growth Stage:** Professional Hunter (81-150 XP)
> **Suggested Players:** 4-5
> **Estimated Duration:** 8-12 hours (can split into 2-3 sessions)
> **Prerequisites:** Has completed at least one Hunter commission

---

## Background

At the southern tip of the Batokia Continent, the no-man's-land known as the "Rift Zone" has recently shown anomalies — an entire village vanished in a single night. No blood, no bodies, no signs of struggle. The villagers seemed to melt into the air.

The Association sent three Hunter teams to investigate; the first two lost contact. The third returned alive but mentally broken, repeating only one phrase:

> **"The abyss… is breathing."**

Now, the Association has formed a subjugation squad — and you have been chosen.

---

## Target Creature: "Fang of the Abyss" Abyssal

A creature presumed to have leaked from the edge of the Dark Continent. It does not hunt — it "absorbs," converting creatures into its own nourishment, then spitting out lifeless husks.

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| **Challenge Rating (CR)** | 10 (adjustable to 8-12) |
| **HP** | 220 (including part HP) |
| **SP** | 120 |
| **AC** | 17 |
| **Constitution 20 (+5)** | **Dexterity 10 (+0)** | **Willpower 18 (+4)** |
| **Nen Defense** | 10 (innate, non-Nen) |
| **Innate Abilities** | Abyssal Absorption, Husk Generation, Ley-line Sense (non-Nen, biological trait) |

### Abyssal's Composition

| Part | HP | AC | Destruction Effect |
|------|:---:|:---:|------|
| **Core (main body)** | 100 | 17 | Can only be attacked after all parts destroyed; destruction = death |
| **Tentacles ×4** | 30 each | 15 | After destruction, that tentacle's attack halved, regenerates in 1d4 rounds |
| **Absorption Maw (abdomen)** | 50 | 13 | After destruction, loses absorption (cannot recover HP) |
| **Sense Antenna** | 20 | 18 | After destruction, loses Ley-line Sense (cannot predict PC position) |

### Abyssal's Three Phases

| Phase | Trigger | HP State | Behavior |
|:---:|------|:---:|------|
| **Lurk** | Default | Core 100% | Hides underground; only tentacles exposed to attack. Can attack 2×/round (tentacle 1d10+3 bludgeoning). PC must find and destroy the Absorption Maw to force out the core. |
| **Awaken** | Absorption Maw destroyed | Core 70-100% | Core surfaces; tentacle attacks become 4×/round. Begins using "Husk Generation" — spits out husks of absorbed creatures from a round prior (CR 1/2 × d4) as fodder. |
| **Frenzy** | Core HP ≤ 30% | Core ≤ 30% | All tentacle attacks gain absorption effect (on hit, target must Fortitude DC 18 or lose 1d4 SP and Abyssal recovers equal HP). Move speed becomes 20m/round (was 5m). |

## Phase 1: Preparation — Intel and Equipment

**Duration:** 120 min
**Type:** Exploration / Social

### Intel Gathering Goals

| Intel | Source | Check | Effect |
|------|------|:---:|------|
| Abyssal's weakness | Sole survivor Hunter's diary (must calm him first) | Willpower DC 18 calm + Investigation DC 15 | Learn "the Absorption Maw is the most fragile part" |
| Underground structure | Geological survey report | Investigation DC 14 | Gain Rift Zone map marking possible nest locations |
| Past subjugation records | Association archives | Persuasion DC 15 (requires clearance) | Learn tentacle regeneration speed |
| Anomalies before village vanished | Survivor testimony from neighboring village | Insight DC 16 | Learn "sulfur smell always precedes earthquakes" (ecological weakness hint) |

### Key NPC: "Breaker" Karen

The sole surviving Hunter. She sits in an isolation room in the Association Medical Division, her eyes vacant.

> "Don't go in. No matter how strong you are, don't go in."
> "It won't kill you. It'll make you look alive, but inside — inside is already empty."
> She suddenly grabs your wrist. The grip is astonishingly strong.
> "If you really must go — bring sulfur. It hates sulfur."

**Ways to help Karen recover:**
- Use Medicine DC 20 or Nen ability (Emission specialized healing)
- Accompany her three days straight (Charisma DC 15 to gradually build trust)
- After completion she gives more tactical advice and assists as a "remote consultant" in the final subjugation (radio provides tactical advantage)

## Phase 3: Subjugation — Fang of the Abyss

**Duration:** 240 min (including retreat and re-prep)
**Type:** Combat

### Subjugation Loop Rules

The PC does not need to defeat Abyssal in one go. They can:
1. Enter nest → fight → judge cannot win → **retreat** (all must escape nest successfully)
2. Return to camp → heal, replenish consumables, analyze tactics → **march again**
3. Each re-march, Abyssal recovers 1d4 × 5 HP (but not all parts regenerate)

> **Retreat rule:** Success when all reach the nest exit. During retreat Abyssal does not pursue out of the nest (this is its territory limit, and the key reason the PC can return alive).

### Nest Tactical Environment

The nest is an underground cavern about 80m in diameter, with environmental objects:

| Object | Position | Interaction Effect |
|------|------|------|
| Sulfur crystal pillars ×4 | Around the cavern | On destruction release sulfur smoke (radius 10m); within range all attacks vs Abyssal +2d6 fire damage; PC must Fortitude DC 12 or cough (attack disadvantage 1 round) |
| Stalagmite columns ×6 | Random | Provide half cover (AC +2); can climb (Athletics DC 14) to top for attack advantage vs large tentacles |
| Underground pools ×2 | East and west sides | Attacks vs tentacles can deal conductive damage (if Lightning-type Nen): +1d6 damage within water range |
| Ancient ruin fragment | Cavern center | Can be thrown as weapon (1d8 bludgeoning, range 10m, one-time) |

### Recommended Subjugation Tactics (hint clues for the PC)

The GM should guide the PC to discover these tactics via NPC Karen or environmental description:

| Tactic | Effect | Clue Source |
|------|------|------|
| Focus fire the Absorption Maw | Prevent Abyssal HP recovery | Karen's intel |
| Destroy the Sense Antenna | Abyssal's attacks randomized (disadvantage) | Perception DC 16 in combat |
| Use sulfur | Extra damage | Sulfur smell in environment, Karen's intel |
| Spread out | Reduce tentacle AoE efficiency | Tactics DC 14 |
| Clear husks first | Prevent fodder interference | Naturally derived after first encounter |
