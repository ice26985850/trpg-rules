# Scenario S5: Call of the Gate

> **Growth Stage:** Star Hunter ~ Legendary (151-350+ Experience Points (XP))
> **Duration:** 6-8 hours
> **Premise:** Completed Scenario S4

---

## Background

You wake in your own apartment. On the nightstand lies that business card—the Collector's card. But a line has been added to its back. Not written by you.

> **"Mobius Lake. In three days. Come see the real collection."**

At the same time, an urgent message arrives from the Association: the Gate's energy readings are abnormal. The Gatekeeper needs all available Hunters.

These two things—point to the same place.

---

## Scene One: Mobius Lake — The Collector's Collection

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Type:** Dialogue / Preparation

> On the shore of Mobius Lake, the Collector stands on a small expedition boat. He wears expedition gear—not the luxurious attire you saw him in last time.
>
> "You came. Good." He points to the center of the lake—the Gate's location. "Three days ago, the Gate's energy readings began to fluctuate. I sent people to investigate—those who went in came back saying the same thing."
>
> "The Gate is 'choosing'. It is looking for someone."
>
> He looks at the PC: "What do you think—who is it looking for?"
>
> "I propose cooperation. I have a boat, supplies, and firsthand research on the Gate—ten years' worth." He hands over a stack of documents.
> "You go in. When you come out, tell me what you saw. That is my only condition."

**PC's Choice:**
- Accept cooperation—use the Collector's supplies (gain SP recovery potion ×5 + Gate energy reading data; all checks inside the Gate at DC −2)
- Refuse cooperation—go alone, but the Collector will not obstruct ("I respect the choice")
- Demand more—Persuasion DC 22; on success the Collector adds: his private bodyguard (CR 7 NPC companion) accompanies the PC into the Gate's outskirts (first scene only)

---

## Scene Two: The Gate — A Solo Trial

**Duration:** 90 minutes
**Type:** Ritual / Dialogue

> You stand before the Gate—the hollow in the waterfall. The difference is, today the Gate does not whisper.
>
> It spoke one word.
>
> **"You."**
>
> No five proofs. No protecting teammates. No deciphering ancient text.
>
> The Gate requires only one thing: **acknowledgment.**

> Quoted from the original Scenario Five "The Gate's Trial"—this solo version's Gate trial differs from the original five-person five-proof version. The original is in 《獵人X獵人TRPG_劇本集_v1.md》 Scene Two. The solo version is as follows.

### The Gate's Questions

The Gate speaks directly to the PC. Not with sound—the PC hears the following three questions in their mind (GM asks in turn):

**Question One:** "What decision have you made entirely alone—that no one knew, no one approved, yet you never regretted?"

**Question Two:** "If you could save one person—yourself, or someone you haven't met yet, who will die in the future because of your choice?"

**Question Three:** "You hold a Gate in your hands. Behind the Gate are all answers. Before the Gate are all questions. Do you open it, or close it?"

**Judgment Criteria (GM reference):**

| Answer Trait | Gate's Reaction |
|------|------|
| Answers immediately (without thought) | The hollow widens a bit—the Gate is dissatisfied |
| Silence over 30 seconds before answering | The hollow stabilizes—the Gate is listening |
| Answer mentions "others" | The water wall shimmers—the Gate remembers |
| Answer contains only "self" | The Gate does not react—it is still waiting |
| To Question Two says "I refuse to choose—I will find a third path" | The Gate emits a low resonance—an answer it has never heard |

> Passing standard: At least two of the three questions elicit a reaction from the Gate (widen/stabilize/shimmer/resonate). No "correct answer" is forced—the Gate seeks "true self-awareness," not a perfect answer.

## Scene Three: Behind the Gate — The Collector's Collection

**Duration:** 120 minutes
**Type:** Exploration / Combat

The Gate opens. You step inside.

> This is not your first time entering the Dark Continent. But the place the Gate takes you this time—is not on the map.
>
> You stand in a circular stone chamber. The walls have hundreds of niches, each holding a glowing crystal—each one a memory crystal.
>
> This is the First Civilization's "museum." What it collects is not objects—but memories.
>
> The Collector's voice comes through your communicator (if you brought it): "Tell me what you see."

### Museum Areas

| Area | Content | Check |
|:---:|------|:---:|
| A | Memories of the first seven species—from rise to fall, compressed into a ten-minute illusion | Willpower DC 22 to view, otherwise 1d4 mental damage |
| B | Human memories—from the day the first human used Nen to now | Intelligence DC 18 to find "the memory the Collector is interested in" |
| C | The future—the First Civilization's prediction model (not a real future, a simulation) | Nen Knowledge DC 24 to understand the model's nature |

### Boss: Museum Guard

> The stone slab in the chamber's center suddenly rises. A form composed of pure memory steps out of it. It has no fixed shape—each step shifts its form: sometimes a warrior, sometimes a child, sometimes a beast.
>
> Its voice is the voice of all memories: "You are not the first to want to steal memories. Nor will you be the last."

**Memory Guard (CR 13)**

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:---:|
| Hit Points (HP) | 200 |
| Armor Class (AC) | 17 (pure Nen, physical attack damage halved) |
| Attack | Memory Strike: d20+10, 3d8 Nen damage + Willpower DC 20 or Confused 1 turn |
| Special | Form Shift: At the start of each turn, randomly becomes one form (d4: 1=Warrior AC+2 / 2=Assassin attack count ×2 damage halved / 3=Guardian self-heal 2d10 / 4=Observer no action that turn, but PC all checks at disadvantage) |
| Weakness | If the PC "speaks a true memory" to the guard—the PC voluntarily shares a memory of their own (no roll needed, roleplay only), the guard pauses one turn (that form's effect canceled, and AC −4). The PC may do this at most 2 times per combat. |

## Scene Four: The True Collection

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Type:** Ending

After defeating the guard—

> The stone chamber begins to collapse—memory crystals lose their light one by one. The museum is destroying itself.
>
> But at the last moment, a crystal floats to your hand. Not from the past—it is empty.
>
> First Civilization text appears:
> **"This is for the next guardian. Fill in your own memory. Let the next person know—that you were once here."**

The PC may choose:
- Leave their own memory (any one—the GM records it, may appear as an easter egg in future scenarios)
- Leave with the empty crystal (the crystal can store a memory—similar to a legendary item vault's memory storage)

Back at Mobius Lake—

> The Collector waits on the shore. He looks at the crystal in your hand, silent for a long time.
>
> "This is not what I wanted." He finally says.
> "But it is better than what I wanted."
>
> He slips a note into your hand. "Next time we meet—" he pauses. "Call me Sebastian. I'm done being a Collector. I'll be your first investor."
>
> The note bears an address and one line: **"Come anytime. The Gate is always open for you."**

## Postscript: Words for the GM

These five solo scenarios are designed as a complete arc: from a lonely rookie taking their first commission, to becoming the Gate-acknowledged solo walker.

The biggest difference from multiplayer scenarios is—in solo scenarios, the PC's choices weigh heavier. No teammates to share the burden, no one else to rely on. Every decision is the PC's own.

But precisely because of this—the sense of accomplishment in solo scenarios is purer.

The three questions the Gate poses to the solo PC are a review of the entire journey. The GM can use this to guide the PC to review key choices from Scenario S1 to S5—making the finale's dialogue a character summation.

**May your solo session be brilliant.**
