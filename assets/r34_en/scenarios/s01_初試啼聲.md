# First Cry (S1)

A chance encounter at an abandoned station. The players must find an exit before the awakened horde closes in.

## Scene Objectives
- Find the station control-room key within 3 turns
- Avoid alerting "The Manager" at the end of the corridor

## Key NPC
- Old Zhang: a survivor who knows where the key is, but is injured and cannot travel with the group

## Difficulty Hints
- A spare key can be found in the ticket booth drawer (DC 12)
- The Manager's perception range is 2 tiles; exceeding it triggers a chase
