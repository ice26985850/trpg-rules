# Technique Compendium (Magic / Ability System)

> This compendium explains the Technique classification and known Technique examples in the Z Corporation TRPG, for players to understand character abilities and for the GM's reference when designing encounters.
> Source: "Core Concepts" and "Sublimation" from *Z Corporation Player Rulebook*.

---

## Technique Classification

### Innate Technique
The inborn talent ability of most Sorcerers, fixed at birth. The higher the Complexity (1–5), the more it demands Intelligence; one must possess Intelligence equal to the Complexity to master it.

### Heavenly Restriction
Sacrificing certain costs (such as Cursed Energy, human form, or language) in exchange for extreme enhancement of some part of the body. An extreme cost-for-power exchange.

### Reverse Technique
A rare ability that converts negative Cursed Energy into positive energy. Its uses range from healing oneself to reshaping limbs, and it is often tied to a turning point in a character's fate.

### Binding Vow
A Sorcerer voluntarily accepts restrictions in exchange for stronger Technique effects. Every Binding is a declaration of the character's convictions.

### Sublimation / Upgrade
The development and qualitative transformation of a Core Ability. Tiers: No Sublimation < Grade 4 Sublimation < Grade 3 Sublimation < Grade 2 Sublimation < Grade 1 Sublimation < Special-Grade Sublimation.
Each Sublimation brings a breakthrough in the Core Ability; the specific effect is customized by the GM based on the character.

## Known Technique Examples

| Technique Name | Holder | Type | Brief Description |
|----------------|--------|------|-------------------|
| Moon Game | Little Red Riding Hood | Innate Technique | A highly random power tied to lunar phases; after Grade 4 Sublimation can remove randomness or fix a class ability |
| Lake's Eye | Miss Axe | Innate Technique | Marking and perception; Grade 4 Sublimation enhances marking and can disrupt the target's Cursed Energy output |
| Duck Call | Duke Duck | Innate Technique | Forcefully draws attention; Grade 4 Sublimation increases range and turns passive into active |
| Rabbit Guard | Bunny Doll | Innate Technique | Defense and leaping; Grade 4 Sublimation enhances guarding power |
| Ten Shadows Technique | Black Shadow | Innate Technique | Manipulates shadow shikigami; Grade 2 Sublimation unlocks more shikigami |
| Shepherd's Tent | the Shepherd | Innate Technique | Barrier protection; Grade 2 Sublimation expands barrier range |
| Dessert Technique | Ice Cream Witch | Innate Technique | Three modes — flight / materialization / delicious ice — and can feed others |

> GM Tip: Technique design follows the logic of "cost exchange" — the stronger the ability, the more it is accompanied by flaws (e.g., Black Slip loses human form and language, Catgirl's Intelligence declines year by year). When designing new Techniques, preserve this tragic / trade-off undertone.
