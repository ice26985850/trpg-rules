# Cursed Tool Compendium (Items)

> This compendium explains the role and usage principles of "Cursed Tools" in the Z Corporation TRPG. Cursed Tools are manufactured, maintained, and managed by the Cursed Tool Department, and generally remain neutral.
> Source: Department structure and mission system from *Z Corporation Player Rulebook*.

---

## Definition of Cursed Tools
Cursed Tools are implements that carry or amplify Cursed Energy, and can be used for:
- **Amplifying Techniques**: Enhancing the power or stability of specific Techniques.
- **Sealing / Containment**: Sealing Cursed Spirits and dangerous Cursed Tools (see Containment Department duties).
- **Mission Support**: Specialized equipment for communication, reconnaissance, protection, medical care, and more.

## Acquisition and Management
- Uniformly manufactured and maintained by the **Cursed Tool Department**, generally neutral and not involved in factional disputes.
- The GM may distribute corresponding Cursed Tools as mission rewards or Early-Warning Department support based on mission keywords (such as "Seize," "Medical," "Vigilance").
- Employees may indirectly obtain specialized Cursed Tools through mechanics such as "Interlude Investment" or "Scout Talent."

## Design Principles (GM Reference)
This rulebook does not preset a fixed list of Cursed Tools; the GM may design them following the directions below, keeping them consistent with the worldview:
- **Functional Correspondence**: Each Cursed Tool corresponds to one mission keyword (medical Cursed Tool, reconnaissance Cursed Tool, guard Cursed Tool...).
- **Cost Mechanism**: Powerful Cursed Tools may carry restrictions (such as Cursed Energy consumption or required Binding), echoing the trade-off spirit of the Technique system.
- **Rarity**: Everyday Cursed Tools are common; Cursed Tools related to "Privileged Members" or the "Singularity" are extremely rare and may serve as Crisis Contract rewards.

---

> Tip: If a specific list of Cursed Tools is needed, the GM may design corresponding implements in reverse from the Technique types in the *Technique Compendium*, or expand by referencing the mission keyword table.
