# Threat Compendium (Monsters / Risk Sources)

> This compendium catalogs the six major risk sources that missions may encounter in the Z Corporation TRPG, for the GM's reference when designing encounters and for players assessing threats.
> Source: "Mission Risk Sources" from *Z Corporation Player Rulebook*.

---

## 1. Cursed Spirit
- **Nature**: A pure aggregate of emotion, born from and materialized by human negative emotions.
- **Traits**: Grade and strength depend on the intensity of accumulated emotion; can possess and devour.
- **Weakness**: Reverse Technique (converting negative to positive), positive-emotion Techniques, high-Cursed-Energy suppression.
- **Narrative Potential**: Behind every Cursed Spirit there is often a story of human nature.

## 2. Curse User
- **Nature**: An illegal group that uses Cursed Energy, or an individual suffering a mental break.
- **Traits**: Possesses Techniques and Cursed Tools; varied combat styles.
- **Weakness**: Varies by individual Technique and requires on-site analysis (Intelligence check to investigate).
- **Note**: Unlike "captured Sorcerers," Curse Users are generally not recruitable.

## 3. Mutant Beast
- **Nature**: Dangerous creatures from the depths of the outskirts or wilderness, with abnormal physiology or ecology.
- **Traits**: High Stamina, strong environmental adaptability.
- **Weakness**: Varies by species; can be discovered through ecological knowledge (Intelligence/Culture check).

## 4. Troublemakers
- **Nature**: Non-Sorcerers who become dangerous through brainwashing, coercion, or voluntarily.
- **Traits**: Mostly appear in towns and outskirts; may be tied to gangs or cults.
- **Weakness**: Negotiation (Charisma/Diplomacy), subduing (Stamina); not necessarily requiring elimination.

## 5. Environment
- **Nature**: Area-wide crises caused by environmental pollution, natural disasters, or man-made calamities.
- **Traits**: No specific enemy; emphasizes protection and evacuation.
- **Weakness**: Early warning (Vigilance), evacuation (Rescue/Guard), engineering response.

## 6. Unknown
- **Nature**: Threats not yet clearly identified by the Intelligence or Early-Warning Departments.
- **Traits**: Insufficient intelligence; uncertain risk.
- **Weakness**: Reconnaissance first (Investigate keyword), then dispatch after rating by the Early-Warning/Intelligence Departments.

---

> GM Tip: The six sources can be combined freely (e.g., a "Cursed Spirit + Environment" pollution mutation), and paired with "Stage Keywords" (Urban / Outskirts / Town / Wilderness) to adjust check modifiers.
