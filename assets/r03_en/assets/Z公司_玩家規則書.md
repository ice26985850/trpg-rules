## I. Worldview and Organizational Structure

### 1. Z Corporation

Z Corporation is a giant enterprise that controls "Cursed Energy" and the "Singularity," responsible for guiding and managing Sorcerers to serve the public (especially Privileged Members).

#### Internal Rank System

？？？ > Board of Directors > Director (Full) > Temporary Minister / Vice-Minister / Acting Minister > Squad Captain > Regular Employee

#### Main Departments

* **Main Department (where the Supervisor is stationed)**: Handles core dispatch, management, and command of Sorcerers.
* **Intelligence Department**: Analyzes intelligence and runs extreme-case simulations; holds partial say in grading Crisis Contracts. Rivals the Early-Warning Department.
* **Early-Warning Department**: Handles urban early warnings and alerts, holds first-hand intelligence, and assigns mission grades. Holds major say in grading Crisis Contracts. Rivals the Intelligence Department.
* **Training Department**: Organizes training, exams, Sublimation, and the development and recruitment of new Sorcerers. Favors a numbers-based approach.
* **Advanced Training Department**: Handles cross-company cooperation (module retrofitting) and concentrated, resource-intensive training of elite Sorcerers. Emphasizes an elite approach.
* **PR & Commerce Department**: Handles commerce, communications control, security, diplomacy, and translation coordination. Values revenue. At odds with the Welfare Department.
* **Welfare Department**: Handles employee benefits, compensation, and health management. Values employee physical and mental well-being. At odds with the PR & Commerce Department.
* **Containment Department**: Handles long-term containment/sealing of Curse Users, specific Cursed Spirits, and dangerous Cursed Tools. Remains neutral.
* **Cursed Tool Department**: Manufactures/maintains/manages Cursed Tools. Usually neutral.

### 2. Core Concepts

#### Cursed Energy

A special energy used to realize supernatural phenomena. Like electricity, where the Technique is the appliance that determines its use.

#### Technique

* **Innate Technique**: The inborn ability of most Sorcerers.
* **Heavenly Restriction**: Sacrificing certain costs (such as Cursed Energy) in exchange for extreme enhancement of some part of the body.
* **Reverse Technique**: A rare ability that converts negative Cursed Energy into positive energy. Uses range from healing oneself to regenerating limbs.
* **Sublimation (Upgrade)**: The development and advanced application of a Core Ability. Sublimation tiers: No Sublimation < Grade 4 Sublimation < Grade 3 Sublimation < Grade 2 Sublimation < Grade 1 Sublimation < Special-Grade Sublimation. Sublimation can bring about a qualitative transformation.
* **Complexity**: The difficulty of understanding a Technique, ranked high to low (1–5). High Complexity demands Intelligence; one needs at least the Intelligence value corresponding to the Complexity to master it.
* **Binding**: A Sorcerer may voluntarily accept restrictions in exchange for stronger Technique effects.

Wounded > Seriously Wounded > Gravely Wounded > Critically Wounded > Dead

**Dead** cannot be recovered through automatic treatment; special means are required (such as Reverse Technique or plot-based revival).

## IV. Supervisor Resource System

### 1. Computing Power

* **Initial Cap**: 100
* **Capacity Increase**: Interlude Investment "Expand Computing Power" gives +5 each time, at 100 Capital each.
* **Zero Effect**: When Computing Power is 0, abilities can still be used normally; it only means Computing-Power Intervention skills (Delay Task, Loser's Hour, Intervene Causality) can no longer be used.
* **Cycle Conversion**: After each Cycle's work ends, unused remaining Computing Power converts to Capital at a fixed ratio:

| Remaining Computing Power | Capital Converted |
|---------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 – 20 | 10 Capital |
| 21 – 50 | 30 Capital |
| 51 – 80 | 60 Capital |
| 81 – 100 | 100 Capital |

> *Note: When the Computing Power cap exceeds 100, the excess is converted to Capital at the same ratio.*

### 2. Satisfaction

* **Initial Cap**: 100
* **Use**: Represents the Board of Directors' trust. Deducted on mission failure/abandonment; reaching zero means loss of Supervisor qualification (game over).
* **Cap Increase**: Interlude Investment "Invest in Company" gives +5 each time.
* **Capital Income**: After each Cycle's work ends, gain Capital income equal to "Satisfaction × 2".

### 3. Capital

* **Initial Capital**: Set by the GM (suggested starting range 200–300).
* **Expenses**: Employee salaries, Interlude Investments (Promote Employee / Expand Computing Power / Invest in Company / Scout Talent), grade promotion costs, emergency recruitment, etc.
* **Income**: Satisfaction × 2 + remaining Computing Power converted at Cycle end (see table above).
* **Zero Consequence**: Capital reaching zero does not end the game, but employees will automatically resign when salaries cannot be paid.

## V. Game Flow

### 1. Single Cycle Structure

Each Cycle is divided into three phases:

1. **Recruitment Phase**: Sign in new employees, Special Recruitment of Captives, arrange training content (from Chapter 2), assign Advanced Training (from Chapter 3).
2. **Dispatch Phase**: Assign employees to missions (missions, training, exams, Revenue Side-Jobs, crisis handling).
3. **Interlude Phase**: Invest Capital, Promote Employees, make Crisis Contract decisions.

### 2. Mission Flow

* The Early-Warning/Intelligence Department issues missions and assigns a risk level.
* The Supervisor assembles a Sorcerer team based on mission keywords and stage, then dispatches them.
* A d100 Check determines success or failure; injury changes are recorded.

### 3. Communication Protocol

* **Routine Communication**: Each person may communicate only once per day (per Cycle).
* **Special Communication**: Promotion, discipline, and onboarding communications are exempt from the frequency limit.
* **Extra Contact**: Each Cycle has 3 opportunities to proactively contact others outside dispatch periods. The other party may refuse; refusal does not consume an opportunity and in principle has no consequences.
* **Effect of Communication**: Mainly narrative/intelligence-oriented, but may also affect concrete values such as Affinity / Loyalty / Battle Rapport.

## VI. Training System

### 1. Training Rules

* Training is conducted by the Training Department and increases the corresponding Attribute EXP.
* **Cancellation and Abandonment**:
  * Before assignment in the Dispatch Phase, an unstarted training Time Slot can be cancelled directly, with no penalty.
  * **Once training has begun, it cannot be cancelled.**
* Training has no success or failure — EXP is always gained.
* Training description is at most 10 characters.
* The number of Sorcerers dispatched can be freely adjusted (no requirement to fill all slots).
* Training and mission growth are tallied together at Cycle end.

### 2. Training Bonuses

After cooperating with the Training Department:
* Attribute training gains are doubled.
* During recruitment, Capital rewards can be given that exceed those at recruitment (capped at no more than the initial recruitment EXP).
* Training Department assistants are only effective during this chapter's recruitment period; employees transferred out cannot be transferred back.

### 3. Attribute EXP Rules

* Attribute EXP **cannot directly raise attributes**.
* EXP is mainly used for:
  1. **Attribute Exam**: When accumulated EXP reaches the threshold, an exam can determine whether to raise the attribute grade.
  2. **Training Gain Influence**: Personality affects growth magnitude (e.g., "meticulous" greatly increases it, "arrogant" reduces it).

---

## VII. Advanced Training System

* Can be assisted by the Advanced Training Department for cross-company cooperation (module retrofitting).
* Concentrates resources to train elite Sorcerers.
* Can raise attributes, upgrade Sorcerer grades, and develop special tags (Reverse Technique, barriers, etc.).
* Sorcerers sent for Advanced Training are temporarily unable to participate in missions.

## VIII. Secret Missions

Can involve gray-market industries, covert alliances with opposition factions, escorting supplies, etc. Consumes Computing Power or doubles the Time Slot occupancy.

## IX. Supplementary Mechanics

### 1. Mission Keywords (unlocked as chapters progress)

**Core Keywords**:
Annihilate (completely eliminate the target) / Seize (obtain items of significant value from the target) / Apprehend (goal is capture alive) / Stall (core is delaying the target and draining its time and energy) / Support (mainly assist and back up) / Guard (protect important targets) / Rescue (provide safety or life aid) / Investigate (track targets, gather intelligence, investigate and analyze leads) / Diplomacy (win over and improve relations, negotiate) / Publicize (broadcast widely to attract attention) / Dirty Work (thankless, involves gray or illegal business) / Justice (related to justice, virtue, honor) / Culture (related to literature, art, philosophy, document handling) / Covert (related to hiding, covering up, keeping a low profile) / Laborer (selling labor, heavy and harsh physical work) / Instruct (through direction, teaching, and guidance) / Commerce (providing goods and services for profit) / Vigilance (guarding against potential danger, area patrol missions) / Medical (treatment, diagnosis, healing) / Oppress (using power or force to compel obedience) / Command (leadership and command-related missions) / Mislead (providing false information or guidance)

**Stage Keywords**:
Urban (bustling, lively area, divided into commercial, industrial, residential, inner-ring, and core zones) / Outskirts (suburban, sparsely populated, remote settlements) / Town (backward suburban area with poor security, possibly with gangs or cults) / Wilderness (many dangerous uninhabited zones, divided into outer rim, shallow zone, and depths)

**Risk Sources**:
Cursed Spirit (pure collective of emotion) / Curse User (illegal Sorcerer group or individually mentally afflicted person) / Troublemakers (bad people from barren lands — possibly brainwashed, coerced, or voluntary non-Sorcerers) / Environment (environmental pollution, natural disasters, acts of nature or man) / Mutant Beast (dangerous creatures from suburbs or wilderness) / Unknown (handled by the Intelligence or Early-Warning Department for detection)

### 2. Mission Result Levels

Missions may yield **Critical Success**, **Success**, **Partial Success**, **Failure**, or **Abandoned**.

* **Critical Success**: Depends on whether extra notes / character abilities / personality / dice value / team rapport fit the situation. Grants extra rewards.
* **Partial Success**: An awkward state between success and failure. Counts as Failure for resolution, but Satisfaction penalty is halved.
* **Failure**: Bear the corresponding penalty based on mission severity.

| Mission Type | Failure Penalty |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Bronze / City Contract | Satisfaction -10 |
| Silver / City Contract | Satisfaction -20 |
| Gold / City Contract | Satisfaction -40 |
| Platinum / City Contract | Satisfaction -80 |
| Diamond / City Contract | Satisfaction -160 |

* **Abandoned**: Penalty is double that of mission failure.
* Abandoning training does not cause loss of Satisfaction.

### 3. Mission Mileage (Tag Accumulation)

Counted from recruitment onward; each mission executed adds +1.

| Cumulative Count | Tag Effect |
|------------------|------------|
| 10 | Removes the (Rookie) tag |
| 30 | Gains the (Professional) tag |
| 60 | Gains the (Veteran) tag (replaces Professional) |
| 100 | Gains the (Battle-Hardened) tag (replaces Veteran) |
| 200 | And possessing (Battle-Hardened) plus a Special-Grade Sorcerer → gains the (Legendary Sorcerer) tag |

### 4. Low-Grade Employees Taking High-Grade Missions

* In principle there is **no direct penalty** (no Satisfaction deduction).
* But if the employee is underpowered, it may lead to **Serious Wounds or even Death**. The actual result is judged by the GM based on the employee's attributes and the mission's requirements.

## X. Dice and Check Mechanics

### 1. d100 Basic Rules

* Checks use **d100**.
* Roll result **≤ attribute converted value** → Success.
* Roll result **≥ attribute converted value × 10** → Critical Success.
* The GM may choose to roll hidden or open.

### 2. Auto-Managed Mode

Once confirmed active:
* Time Slot occupancy, attributes, exams, and other tedious dice for missions are all rolled automatically.
* Only key mission d100 rolls are made by the Supervisor.
* The Supervisor may customize whether certain ability parts are auto-resolved.

### 3. Check Bonuses/Penalties

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Mission keyword match | Related attribute check +10 |
| Stage keyword match | Related attribute check +10 |
| Injured (Wounded / Seriously Wounded / Gravely Wounded) | Check -10 |
| Heavily injured (Critically Wounded) | Check -30 |
| High team rapport | Support-type checks +10 |
| Core Ability Complexity exceeds Intelligence tolerance | Check -10 |

### 4. Computing-Power Intervention (cost-incurring adjudication options)

During dispatch, Computing Power may be used to modify checks or mission results, at the following costs:

| Intervention | Description | Computing Power Cost |
|--------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Delay Task** | Makes the mission occupy 1 extra Time Slot, lowering risk. Per 1 slot delayed | 5 Computing Power/slot |
| **Loser's Hour** | Return to the first slot and reset the entire Cycle (equivalent to rewinding). | Total slots × 5 |
| **Intervene Causality** | Re-roll the d100 check, take the best result. **Cannot be used once the mission result is confirmed.** | 10 Computing Power |

> **Timing Reminder**:
> * Delay Task and Intervene Causality may be used at any time **during a mission**.
> * Loser's Hour may only be used **after the dispatch phase ends but before the mission starts**.
> * Intervene Causality is permanently consumed once the mission result is confirmed (including Success/Failure/Critical Success) and cannot be withdrawn.

## XIV. Detailed Cycle Mechanics

### 1. Time Slot System

- Chapter 1 generally uses 6 slots; from Chapter 2 onward this increases to 8 or more.
- After an employee is assigned to a mission, they must wait until the mission completes (X + slots occupied) before returning to base and entering the Idle state.
- A mission may extend beyond the current time slot (starting only in the final slot), functioning as overtime that ends when the work is done.

### 2. Mission Description Word Count

| Chapter | Mission Extra Description | Training/Exam Description |
|---------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Chapter 1 – Chapter 3 | 20 characters | 10 characters |
| From Chapter 4 onward | 30 characters | 20 characters |

### 3. Special Note (Unlocked from Chapter 4)

- Each use costs **5 Computing Power**.
- At most **3 uses** per mission.
- Each Special Note is **20 characters** and is visible only to the designated employee.
- May additionally request one Cursed Tool of the same grade as that employee.
- A Special Note cannot be applied consecutively to the same employee within one mission.

### 4. Training and Exams

**From Chapter 2 onward, may be scheduled after the Recruitment Phase:**

- **Training**: Increases the corresponding Attribute EXP. Description limited to 10 characters.
- **Exam**: Scheduled in the final slot. Designates one attribute to test; if the attribute meets the threshold, its grade rises, otherwise substantial EXP is accumulated (for special employees, the test instead accumulates Core Ability growth).

## XVII. Crisis Contract System

### 1. Trigger Conditions

Crisis Contracts appear from Chapter 2 onward. Generally referred to as "Disasters," they present a chain of events and choices.

### 2. Crisis Phases

**Phase 1: Trigger and Assessment**

- Fangfang (assistant AI) assesses the Supervisor's team's current strength against the threat.
- Branches are divided based on the strength gap:
  - **Branch 1**: Team strength > Threat → Normal difficulty route
  - **Branch 2**: Team strength < Threat → Threat increases further, making the confrontation harder

**Phase 2: Crisis Confrontation**

- Choose a course of action (cannot be changed midway once decided).
- Shows a situation progress bar: **Positive progress bar** vs **Negative progress bar**
- At each emergency notification node during the mission, you may choose to dispatch additional personnel for support.

**Threshold Judgment**:

- **Positive situation reaches threshold**: The Supervisor wins and proceeds to the next phase.
- **Negative situation reaches threshold**: The crisis intensifies and proceeds to the next phase (negative consequences).
- **Chapter 4 deployment ends without entering next phase**: Directly compare the positive and negative situations, with the larger one prevailing.

### 3. Crisis Example: Dessert Syndicate Crisis

| Situation | Status | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Positive | 0/10 | Weaken Mr. Tian's influence |
| Negative | 2/10 | Tensions escalate |

**Branch 1 Route (Strength > Threat) Choices**:

1. Cooperate with the Intelligence Department to prop up Mr. Tian (cannot recruit the Ice Cream Witch).
2. Bring down Mr. Tian (can recruit the Ice Cream Witch).

**Branch 2 Route (Strength < Threat) Choices**:

1. Negotiate with the Advanced Training Department, then join forces to eliminate the Dessert Syndicate.
2. Maintain the status quo, unlocking a route that attempts to reconcile with the Dessert Syndicate.

### 4. Crisis-Exclusive Bonuses

When a Sorcerer related to desserts performs a secret Crisis-handling mission:

- Slots occupied **-1**
- For checks, treated as **all attributes raised one grade**

## XVIII. Sublimation Check Mechanics

### 1. Sublimation Path

**Lowest No Sublimation → Grade 4 Sublimation → Grade 3 Sublimation → Grade 2 Sublimation → Grade 1 Sublimation → Special-Grade Sublimation**

Each grade represents a qualitative change in ability. The specific enhancement effects are custom-designed by the GM based on each character's Core Ability, and are **not fixed**.

### 2. Sublimation Trigger

- Triggered through **Interlude Investment "Promote Employee."**
- Condition: The Sorcerer's grade and Core Ability must meet the Sublimation-eligible standard.

### 3. Sublimation Check Procedure

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| ① Attribute Threshold Check | Prerequisite attributes for the corresponding grade are met (e.g., Grade 2 requires 2 attributes at C, Grade 1 requires 2 attributes at A, Special Grade requires 3 attributes at SS). |
| ② Core Ability d100 Test | Roll d100; if the result ≤ the value tied to the Core Ability's Complexity → check passes. The specific threshold is set by the GM based on the ability. |
| ③ Sublimation Effect | Success → Core Ability rises one grade, GM announces the new effect. Failure → this promotion yields no Sublimation. |

### 4. Promotion Failure Effects

Even if Sublimation fails, the promotion is still beneficial:

- A random attribute related to the ability gains **+20 EXP**.
- Or three random attributes each gain **+10 EXP**.
- Or a random attribute-related test is performed once.
- Or Core Ability growth (making the next-stage Sublimation trial easier).

### 5. Sublimation Case References

| Employee | Before Sublimation | After Sublimation |
|----------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Little Red Riding Hood | Moon Game (No Sublimation) | Moon Game (Grade 4 Sublimation) — can remove randomness, or fix a certain class ability |
| Miss Axe | Lake's Eye (No Sublimation) | Lake's Eye (Grade 4 Sublimation) — strengthens marking ability, can interfere with the target's Cursed Energy output |
| Duke Duck | Duck Call (No Sublimation) | Duck Call (Grade 4 Sublimation) — forcibly draws attention, range increased |
| Bunny Doll | Rabbit Guard (No Sublimation) | Rabbit Guard (Grade 4 Sublimation) — strengthens defense/jumping power |
| Black Shadow | Ten Shadows Technique (Grade 3 Sublimation) | Ten Shadows Technique (Grade 2 Sublimation) — unlocks more shikigami |
| the Shepherd | Shepherd's Tent (Grade 3 Sublimation) | Shepherd's Tent (Grade 2 Sublimation) — barrier range expanded |

## XIX. Injury Treatment Mechanics

### 1. Auto-Treatment

- A Sorcerer with medical abilities (such as Medical Manual, Old-Healer Secret Art, etc.) in the **Idle state** will automatically attempt to treat another Sorcerer who is also in the Idle state and has injuries.
- Can only treat once per Cycle.

### 2. Treatment Check

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| ① GM sets target value based on injury level | Wounded → d100 ≤ 30, Seriously Wounded → ≤ 40, Gravely Wounded → ≤ 50, Critically Wounded → ≤ 60 |
| ② Medical Sorcerer attribute check | Use a relevant attribute (Intelligence or Cursed Energy) for a d100 check. If the check result ≥ target value → success |
| ③ Result | Success: injury drops one level. Failure: no effect, retry next Cycle. |

### 3. Treatment Limits

- The **Dead state** cannot be recovered through auto-treatment.
- Special medical Sorcerers (e.g., those with Reverse Technique) can raise the target value by **+10**.
- The GM may adjust the target value based on the situation.

## XX. Employee Growth and EXP System

### 1. Mission EXP Gain

- Whether successful or not, Sorcerers who perform missions gain EXP growth.
- **Success**: normal EXP, fewer or no injuries compared to failure.
- **Failure**: generally yields **more EXP** (except for certain personalities such as arrogant or indifferent types).
- Failing dangerous missions risks greater injury or even death.

### 2. Personality Effects on EXP

| Personality Type | EXP Effect |
|------------------|------------|
| Meticulous | Related attribute grows enormously |
| Normal | Normal growth |
| Proud | Related attribute gains reduced (falls to large growth rather than enormous) |

### 3. Training Reward Doubling

After partnering with the Training Department, training rewards double, and recruitment Capital bonuses are capped at no more than the initial recruitment EXP.

### 2. Computing Power Consumption Details (lines 29–31) — Confirmed

| Intervention Method | Description | Computing Power Cost |
|---------------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Delay Task** | Makes the mission occupy 1 extra time slot, reducing risk. Per 1 slot delayed | 5 Computing Power/slot |
| **Loser's Hour** | Returns to the first slot, resetting the entire Cycle (equivalent to rewinding). | Total slots × 5 (Chapter 1 6 slots = 30, Chapter 2 8 slots = 40) |

> **Important reminder**: The above Computing Power costs are **mutually non-conflicting**. A single mission may use Delay + Intervene Causality simultaneously (before result confirmation), but Special Note is limited to use from Chapter 4 onward.

### 3. Satisfaction and Capital Dual-Track System — Confirmed

- Satisfaction ≠ Capital. Satisfaction only affects Supervisor qualification (zero means elimination), while Capital is used for daily operations.
- When Satisfaction is 0 the game ends immediately; when Capital is 0 employees quit over unpaid wages (does not affect Satisfaction).
- The Satisfaction cap can be raised through the Interlude Investment "Invest in Company"; Capital has no cap.

### 4. Injury and Death Handling — Patched

| Injury State | Treatable? | Dispatchable? | Notes |
|-------------|------------|---------------|-------|
| Uninjured | - | Yes | Normal dispatch |
| Wounded | Auto-treatment | Yes, check -10 | Auto-recovers to Uninjured at Cycle end |
| Seriously Wounded | Auto-treatment | Yes, check -20 | Auto-recovers to Wounded at Cycle end |
| Gravely Wounded | Auto-treatment | No, only Idle or being treated | Auto-recovers to Seriously Wounded at Cycle end |
| Critically Wounded | Auto-treatment | No, only Idle or being treated | Check -30; auto-recovers to Gravely Wounded at Cycle end |
| Dead | No auto-treatment | No | Requires Reverse Technique/special means to revive; 2 deaths trigger Emergency Recruitment |

### 5. Training Rule Clarification — Patched

- **Before training slot assignment**: Canceling during the Recruitment Phase, before dispatch assignment → no penalty.
- **After training slot assignment**: Once dispatched into training → **cannot cancel**; EXP is gained after training completes.
- **Abandon training vs. cancel training**:
  - "Cancel" = backing out before dispatch, no penalty.
  - "Abandon" = unable to complete after dispatch (employee too injured/fled, etc.), no Satisfaction deducted, but the employee may be injured.
  - Training itself has no success/failure distinction; EXP is always gained.

### 6. Definition of Mission Dice Critical Success — Patched

The original rule "dice result ≥ attribute value conversion × 10" would exceed 100 under most attribute conversions (e.g., Grade F 10 × 10 = 100, Grade EE 40 × 10 = 400), so it is adjusted to:

- **Success**: roll result **≤ attribute value conversion**
- **Critical Success**: roll result is a **natural 1**, or **≤ 50% of the attribute value conversion** (rounded down)
- **Fumble**: roll result **> attribute value conversion × 2** (but not exceeding 100)

> Example: An employee with Intelligence D (50), key check value = 50.
> * Critical Success: 1-25 (including natural 1)
> * Success: 26-50
> * Failure: 51-100
> * Fumble: ≥ 100 (triggers special adverse consequences, judged by the GM)

### 7. Letter of Recommendation and Talent Pool Vacancy Handling — Patched

- Vacant IDs (09, 15-18, etc.) if randomly drawn when Scouting Talent:
  1. The GM must create a new employee of the corresponding grade (Grade 4 / Grade 3) and place them into the Letter of Recommendation.
  2. The new employee's attribute range is generated per that grade's general standard (Grade 4 all attributes F~DD, Grade 3 F~D).
  3. The new employee's Core Ability is customized by the GM referencing existing employee templates.
- If the Talent Pool is full (letters = employees), excess Letters of Recommendation go directly into the Talent Pool as reserves.

### 8. Revenue Side-Job Rewards — Patched

Revenue Side-Job rewards are received immediately as Capital after the mission ends:

| ID | Mission | Reward Calculation | Description |
|----|---------|--------------------|-------------|
| a-01 | Wilderness Hunt | 5d10+10 | 1d10 = an integer between 1~10, 5d10 is the sum of five ten-sided dice |
| a-02 | Escort Supplies | Fixed 30 | Simple mission, fixed 30 Capital |
| a-03 | Civilian Tutor | D20+10 | D20 = random integer 1-20 |

> *Note: The Revenue Side-Job d100 check still follows the dice mechanic (Chapter 10), and mission rewards are adjusted by result.*
> * Critical Success → reward doubled
> * Success → normal reward
> * Partial Success → reward × 0.5 (rounded down)
> * Failure → no reward, injury -1 level

### 9. End-of-Cycle Order — Patched (New)

The calculation order for each Cycle is as follows:

1. **Calculate employee salary**: Deducted from Capital. If insufficient, employees quit.
2. **Settle missions/side-jobs**: Calculate rewards, Satisfaction gains/losses, injury changes, and EXP accumulation.
3. **Auto-recover injuries**: Employees at base with no mission have injuries drop one level.
4. **Auto-treatment**: Medical Sorcerers attempt to treat injured colleagues.
5. **Satisfaction → Capital conversion**: Satisfaction × 2.
6. **Remaining Computing Power → Capital conversion**: Per the table.
7. **Talent Pool replenishment**: From Chapter 2, if employees < 4 → replenish to 4; from Chapter 4, if employees < 5 → replenish to 5.
8. **Enter next chapter**: Unlock new keywords, new mechanics, new mission options.

### 10. Blank ID Recruitment Limit Clarification — Patched

The Basic/Mid talent pool for Scouting Talent is 09~40 (32 IDs total), of which:

- **Grade 4 pool (09~29)**: 16 IDs (including known employees 10-14, 16, 19, 25 + blanks 09, 15, 17, 18, 20-24, 26-29)
- **Grade 3 pool (30~40)**: 11 IDs (including known employees 31, 33-36, 38, 40 + blanks 30, 32, 37, 39, 41-48)

> **Special note**: "Blank ID" employees drawn from Scouting Talent have their Core Ability, flaws, tags, etc. all temporarily created by the GM at draw time, and do not affect existing employee data.

### 11. Special Recruitment (Captive) Salary Rules — Patched

- Captive salary = normal salary × 50%.
- Captives whose crimes are not pardoned additionally **need not be paid base salary** (i.e., only the extra 50% salary, not 50% + base salary).
- Example: A Grade 3 Sorcerer's normal salary is 40, captive salary = 20. If crimes are not pardoned, only 0 base salary + extra 50% special salary need be paid.

### 12. Affinity / Loyalty / Battle Rapport — Patched (New)
