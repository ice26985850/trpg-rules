# Scenario S5: The Final Price

**Applicable Mode:** Solo (Lone Wolf mode, 4 Action Points (AP) per round) / Multiplayer (1 Game Master (GM) + 1–5 players, 3 AP each)
**Difficulty:** Legendary **Recommended Level:** 5–8 (suggested starting level 7) **Estimated Duration:** 5–8 hours
**Mission Type:** Total Cripple **Player Count:** 1–6

## Mission Briefing

This is the turning point of the Rift War. The Concord United Fleet (CUF) decided to launch a full assault on the Alliance flagship "Ruthless" — it is the Alliance's command hub in the star sector, and its destruction will completely collapse the Alliance defense line.

But intelligence missed a critical detail: the Ruthless is docked and connected to a Remnant (Remnant) AI drone vessel, the "Void." In desperation the Alliance struck a deal with the AI Remnant — the Void provides technical support in exchange for the Alliance not attacking the Remnant fleet.

The Mothership Arbitrator's boarding assault team is given an impossible mission: simultaneously cripple the Ruthless's command system (the bridge) and the Void's AI core (the central processing node), and both must be completed within 15 rounds — because the Alliance commander launched the self-destruct.

Worse still, halfway through the mission, the Arbitrator sent its final communication: "The Arbitrator is heavily damaged; we can't hold. You have 15 minutes."

## Enemy Vessel Data

- **Vessel Name:** Twin Doom (S5) — Flagship "Ruthless" + AI escort vessel "Void"
- **Vessel Type:** Alliance Battleship + AI Drone Vessel
- **Affiliation:** Free Star Alliance (FSA) + Remnant
- **Scale:** Super-Giant (Flagship 1,500 m + escort 600 m, two vessels docked) / 8 decks (flagship 6 + escort 2)
- **Gravity:** Flagship Standard Gravity (1G) + partially Variable-G; AI vessel Zero-G (0G)
- **Self-Destruct System:** Flagship has one (15-round countdown); AI vessel none, but it self-replicates its defenses

## Advance Flow

### Turning Point 1: Irreversible Choice
There is only one passage at the docking connection between the two vessels. The players must decide: attack the Ruthless first (human defenses) or the Void first (AI defenses)? Is there enough time left to complete both objectives?
- Attack the Ruthless first: Face stubborn resistance from human defenders, but crippling the command system will cause the AI vessel's enemies to lose coordination.
- Attack the Void first: Face dynamically reorganizing corridors and self-replicating defenses, but crippling the AI core will disable all of the Ruthless's electronic systems.
- Split forces: Most efficient but either side is extremely dangerous, requiring perfect team coordination.

### Turning Point 2: The Arbitrator's Farewell
Round 8 of the countdown — the Arbitrator sends its final communication and withdraws, and the players' link to the mothership is cut. Only one path remains: after completing the mission, seize an enemy escape pod. Effect: all mothership support fails, Extraction DC +3.

### Turning Point 3: Final Choice — Mission vs Survival
Round 3 of the countdown — only one objective remains unfinished. Continue? Or withdraw now?
- Continue: Requires passing 3 consecutive DC 18+ checks; success means the mission is complete but the countdown hits zero, triggering a desperate extraction (Resilience (RES) DC 20) — success means a miraculous survival, failure means death but becoming a legend.
- Withdraw: Full squad survives, but the mission fails, the Alliance flagship endures, the war continues.

## Key Encounters

### E1 Before the Ruthless Bridge — Command Deck
- **Enemies:** Marine × 4 (CR 3) + Commander (Boss, Flagship Captain) × 1 (CR 6, HP 40, DEF 18)
- **EL:** 18
- **Notes:** The captain has tactical command ability and can order marines to coordinate attacks; upon defeating the captain, the Ruthless's command system is crippled.

### E2 Void AI Core
- **Enemies:** Defense Robot × 3 (CR 4) + Blockade Turret Matrix (Boss) × 1 (CR 6)
- **EL:** 18
- **Notes:** The AI core chamber is Zero-G; walls reorganize every 2 rounds, changing cover positions; breach the central AI (Engineering (ENG) check DC 22) to cripple it.

### E3 Withdrawal — Seize Escape Pods
- **Challenge:** Find a usable escape pod (Perception (PER) check DC 15); each escape pod holds 2 people, and more than that requires a second pod.
- **Environment:** The escape pod area is partially destroyed, with vacuum exposure; requires maintaining Armor Integrity (ARMR) to survive.

## Endings

- **Legendary Fall:** Complete the mission but sacrifice yourself in the explosion. Your name is engraved on the Arbitrator's monument; three months later the Alliance defense line collapses. Legacy: new characters gain the free feat "Inheritor" — Initiative (INIT) +2 on all human vessels.
- **Miraculous Survival:** Complete the mission and succeed at the desperate extraction. The whole squad gains legendary Reputation; permanent morale advantage for the whole squad on missions against Alliance vessels.
- **Survivor:** Withdraw but the mission fails. The Alliance flagship endures, the war continues; in later campaigns the Alliance defense is stronger, and players gain the new objective "Next time, we will not withdraw."

## Rewards

- **Experience Points (XP):** Primary Objective 16 / Secondary Objective 14 / Full Survival 3 / GM Generic 8
- **Reputation:** 5 (completing the mission after the Arbitrator withdraws, additional +3)
- **Special Loot:** Alliance Flagship Captain's Command Blade (enemy morale −2, mooks may surrender outright); Remnant AI Core Fragment (permanently −3 to breach DC against Remnant vessels, does not stack with System Mastery; the Engineer's System Mastery is doubled against Remnant vessels).

## Special Rules

- **Two Vessels Coexist:** The Ruthless and the Void are two independent maps; the docking connection is the only passage; crossing it may encounter a patrol.
- **Mothership Withdrawal:** At round 8 communications are cut; thereafter mothership support is unavailable, Extraction DC +3, and enemy escape pods must be seized. The GM should create the strongest sense of despair.
- **Level 10 Exclusive Challenge:** If the squad includes a Void Breacher (level 10 character), their "Refuse Death" ability is critical in this mission; it is recommended that the GM make this ability the focal point of the story at a critical moment.
