# Starship Boarding Assault TRPG — Monster Codex (Monster Codex)

> This codex catalogs all enemy units, alien creatures, and automated defense systems in the game.

> **ARMR (Armor Pool) Model:** The ARMR is a depletable "armor pool." When damage is taken, it is first subtracted from the ARMR pool; only the overflow counts toward HP. Once ARMR reaches zero, remaining damage is deducted directly from HP.

> **Damage Type Note:** The damage types marked in this codex's attack entries comprise seven kinds: "kinetic / energy / explosive / bio / fire / vacuum burn / psychic." Among them, **psychic damage (psychic damage)** comes from psionic attacks (e.g., the Brood Brain Worm's "Psionic Blast"), ignores the armor pool (deducted directly from HP), and is ineffective against mechanical and energy units (no mind). On a hit, there is a 50% chance to inflict Confused (Confused) for 1 round. Full interaction rules are in the Player Rulebook 4.1.
