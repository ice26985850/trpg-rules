# Starship Boarding Assault TRPG — Item Codex (Item Codex)

> This codex catalogs all weapons, armor, tools, and consumables in *Starship Boarding Assault TRPG*.

---

## I. Light Weapons (Light Weapons)

### Standard Pistol (Standard Pistol)
- **Damage:** 1d6 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 10/30 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 15
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Special Trait:** Can be used in a grapple
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** Standard-issue sidearm from Concord armories. Reliable, lightweight, and easy to maintain.

### Heavy Pistol (Heavy Pistol)
- **Damage:** 1d8 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 10/30 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 12
- **Weight:** 1.5 kg
- **Special Trait:** At close range (within 5 m), damage +1d4
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** A large-caliber sidearm with formidable close-range stopping power.

### Submachine Gun (Submachine Gun)
- **Damage:** 1d8 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 15/40 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 30
- **Weight:** 2.5 kg
- **Special Trait:** Fully automatic — can fire three bursts at the same target, each subsequent check at −2
- **Reputation Tier:** Recognized
- **FQ Cost:** 3 (Reputation: Recognized)
- **Description:** The ideal choice for close-range fire suppression.

### Medical Pistol (Medical Pistol)
- **Damage:** 1d6 (energy damage)
- **Range:** 10/30 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 20
- **Weight:** 1 kg
- **Special Trait:** Non-lethal mode — target must pass Resilience (RES) DC 12 or be Stunned
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** Standard issue for Medics. Equipped with a medical scanner for rapid wound assessment.

### Combat Knife (Combat Knife)
- **Damage:** 1d6+1 (melee damage)
- **Range:** Melee
- **Weight:** 0.5 kg
- **Special Trait:** Can be used in a grapple
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** Standard-issue melee weapon. More dependable than a firearm in confined spaces.

### Shock Baton (Shock Baton)
- **Damage:** 1d4 (energy damage) + Stunned
- **Range:** Melee
- **Charges:** 10 (battery)
- **Weight:** 0.8 kg
- **Special Trait:** On hit → target must pass Resilience (RES) DC 12, or be Stunned for 1 round
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** A non-lethal riot-control weapon. Ineffective against mechanical units.

## II. Medium Weapons (Medium Weapons)

### Assault Rifle (Assault Rifle)
- **Damage:** 1d10 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 15/60 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 30
- **Weight:** 3.5 kg
- **Special Trait:** Reliable — extremely low malfunction rate
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** The boarding assault team's standard primary weapon. Trustworthy in any environment.

### "Breacher" Assault Shotgun ("Breacher")
- **Damage:** 2d8 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 5/15 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 8
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Special Trait:** At close range (within 5 m), damage +1d8. Fitted with an under-barrel breaching ram
- **Class Restriction:** Assault
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** The Assault's signature weapon. Its heavy frame and under-muzzle breaching ram can smash open a standard hatch directly.

### Marksman Rifle (Marksman Rifle)
- **Damage:** 1d10 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 30/100 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 10
- **Weight:** 4 kg
- **Special Trait:** Aimed shot +1d6. Ignores half cover
- **Class Restriction:** Scout
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** The Scout's precision tool. Aboard an enemy vessel's long corridors, a single hit can be the distance between life and death.

### Plasma Rifle (Plasma Rifle)
- **Damage:** 2d6 (energy damage)
- **Range:** 15/50 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 20 (battery)
- **Weight:** 3 kg
- **Special Trait:** +1d6 against mechanical units
- **Reputation Tier:** Recognized
- **FQ Cost:** 3 (Reputation: Recognized)
- **Description:** An energy weapon, especially effective against the mechanical units of AI drone vessels.

### Electromagnetic Rifle (Electromagnetic Rifle)
- **Damage:** 1d12 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 25/80 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 8
- **Weight:** 5 kg
- **Special Trait:** Penetration — ignores 2 points of Armor pool (deducts from the ARM pool first, then penetrates an additional 2 points)
- **Reputation Tier:** Trusted
- **FQ Cost:** 6 (Reputation: Trusted)
- **Description:** Fires electromagnetic-accelerated projectiles. Heavily armored foes are like paper before it.

## III. Heavy Weapons (Heavy Weapons)

### "Thunder" Squad Machine Gun ("Thunder")
- **Damage:** 2d10 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 20/80 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 100
- **Weight:** 10 kg
- **Special Trait:** Fitted with a bipod (when deployed, range becomes 30/100). Exclusive to Heavy Gunners
- **Reputation Tier:** Unknown
- **FQ Cost:** 0 (within free Quota)
- **Description:** The Heavy Gunner's soulmate. The sound of its belt feeding is the last thing enemy corridors ever hear.

### Anti-Materiel Rifle (Anti-Materiel Rifle)
- **Damage:** 3d10 (kinetic damage)
- **Range:** 50/200 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 5
- **Weight:** 12 kg
- **Special Trait:** Double damage against large targets. Requires mounting
- **Reputation Tier:** Trusted
- **FQ Cost:** 6 (Reputation: Trusted)
- **Description:** In an interior vessel environment, this rifle can punch through two layers of bulkhead.

### Plasma Cannon (Plasma Cannon)
- **Damage:** 3d8 (energy damage)
- **Range:** 15/40 m
- **Magazine Capacity:** 10 (large battery)
- **Weight:** 13 kg
- **Special Trait:** Splash — on hitting a target, adjacent targets take half damage
- **Reputation Tier:** Respected
- **FQ Cost:** 10 (Reputation: Respected)
- **Description:** Fires an entire ball of plasma. The white-hot glow on impact lights up the whole corridor.

### Flamethrower (Flamethrower)
- **Damage:** 2d8 (fire damage)
- **Range:** 5/15 m
- **Charges:** 8 (fuel tank)
- **Weight:** 9 kg
- **Special Trait:** Persistent burn — on hit, target takes 1d6 each round for 3 rounds or until extinguished. ×1.5 against mechanical units. ×2 against insectoid structures
- **Reputation Tier:** Trusted
- **FQ Cost:** 6 (Reputation: Trusted)
- **Description:** Aboard Insectoid Bio-Ships, the flamethrower is not a weapon — it is a tool for survival.
