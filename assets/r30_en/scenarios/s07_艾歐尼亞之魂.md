# s07_Soul of Ionia (Excerpt from *League of Legends TRPG Campaign Script Collection* · Multiplayer Scenario · Side Story S3)

## S3: Soul of Ionia (Lv.5-8 · Ionia)

**Background**: The scars of the Noxian War still linger. The players are invited to Ionia to help with reconstruction, only to discover that the "wraiths" left behind by the war have begun to corrode the Spirit Tree. They must find a path of balance amid the opposition between Zed and Shen.

| Chapter | Level | Type |
|:--:|:--:|------|
| Tears of the Spirit Tree | Lv.5-6 | Exploration + Spiritual Cultivation |
| Shadow Stream vs. Equilibrium | Lv.7 | Faction Choice |
| the Wraith Lord | Lv.8 | Boss + Dual Endings (choose Zed's or Shen's path) |

**Reward**: "Yin-Yang Resonance" (can simultaneously possess Zed's passive and Shen's passive, but each switch costs 1 turn)
