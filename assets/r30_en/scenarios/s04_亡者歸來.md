# Finale: Return of the Dead (Lv.17-20)

> **Main Campaign Finale · Shadow Isles**

## Background

The failure of the Ascension ritual forced the Obsidian Circle to launch its final desperate plan — go to the Shadow Isles, where death is not the end and the power of the dead is inexhaustible. Their leader, Malzahar, the Void Prophet (the true mastermind), has already prepared to open a **Void Rift** deep within the Shadow Isles, connecting the world of the living to the Void. The players must find allies in the unlikeliest of places — even including Thresh and Hecarim — and stop the Void's descent on the night of the Harrowing.

## Ultimate Boss Battle: Malzahar, the Void Prophet (Lv.20)

| Attribute | Value |
|------|:--:|
| HP | 8,000 |
| AD/AP | 150/180 |
| AR/MR | 160/160 |
| Special | Void Shield: gains Ability Power (AP)×0.5 shield at the start of each turn |

**Four-Phase Mechanics** (special treatment for the finale Boss):
- **P1 (100-75%)**: Void Summon (summons 3 Void Wraiths each turn: Health Points (HP) 300, Attack Damage (AD) 50) + Void Grasp (single target Ability Power (AP)×1.5 + Suppress 1 turn)
- **P2 (75-50%)**: Opens the first Void Rift — all combatants take Health Points (HP)×2% True Damage each turn; Void Wraiths upgrade to Void Beasts (Health Points (HP) 600, Attack Damage (AD) 80)
- **P3 (50-25%)**: Void Descent — all players gain the "Void Corruption" debuff (Health Points (HP)×3% True Damage each turn, can be resisted with a Difficulty Class (DC) 22 Constitution (CON) save); summons a Void Eye (Health Points (HP) 1,200, deals Ability Power (AP)×0.5 gaze damage to all each turn)
- **P4 (25-0%)**: Final Prophecy — Malzahar channels for 3 turns to open the ultimate rift. If not killed within 3 turns: instant death for all (scripted death). During this, the Undead Coalition (Thresh/Kalista/Hecarim) grants the players 1 extra action each turn.

## Multiple Endings

| Ending | Condition | Consequence |
|------|------|------|
| **Perfect Victory** | Kill Malzahar within 3 turns of P4 + at least 1 player survives | The Void Rift is sealed, Runeterra gains peace. The players become legendary heroes. |
| **Pyrrhic Victory** | Kill Malzahar but the whole party dies | The rift is sealed, but the players become the new guardians of the Shadow Isles (undying form). |
| **Void Descent** | P4 channel completes unstopped | The world enters the Void Era. Opens a new campaign line — "Void Counterattack". |

- Finale-Exclusive Augment: "Child of the Void" — may choose any 1 Legendary-tier Augment Device
- Campaign Completion Achievement: "Runeterra's Guardian" title (all attributes +5%)
