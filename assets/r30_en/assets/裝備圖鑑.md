## I. Equipment Rules Overview

### Slots & Limits
- 6 equipment slots (any type; one of each unique name)
- 1 boot slot (separate; boots only)
- 1 trinket slot (separate; wards/scanners only)
- 1 elixir slot (separate; combat elixirs only)

### Equipment Quality
| Tier | Price Range | Notes |
|:--:|------|------|
| Basic Component | 300–500 gold | Crafting material |
| Starter Item | 700–1,200 gold | Early-game transition |
| Core Item | 1,800–2,800 gold | Mid-game power |
| Legendary Item | 3,000–3,800 gold | Endgame core |
| Mythic Item | 3,200–3,800 gold | 1 per game; grants Mythic Passive |

### Recall & Trading
- Recall: 2-round channel (must be out of combat); restores to full status
- Sell: 70% of purchase price refunded
- Undo: purchases made within 10 seconds before recalling can be refunded at full price
- Crafting: Legendary Item = Basic Component + Starter Item + crafting fee

### Gold Income Quick Reference
| Source | Gold | Source | Gold |
|------|:--:|------|:--:|
| Passive income | 20/round | Assist | 150 |

| Ranged minion last hit | 14 | Shutdown (3 streak) | 450 |
