## I. Arcane World Setting

### The Twin Cities

Arcane focuses on two symbiotic cities of Runeterra — the upper city of **Piltover** (the City of Progress) and the lower city of **Zaun** (the Undercity). The tension between them is the core engine of the entire Arcane story.

| Dimension | Piltover (Upper City) | Zaun (Undercity) |
|------|------|------|
| Environment | Sunlight, marble, Hextech | Toxic haze, rust, chem-waste |
| Society | Ruled by elite council | Gang-territory division |
| Technology | Hextech — clean magical energy | Shimmer — chem-alchemical mutagen |
| Atmosphere | Bright, progressive, hypocritical | Dark, survivalist, real |
| TRPG Fit | Political intrigue, academy exploration, social maneuvering | Street survival, gang warfare, dungeon delving |

### Arcane Timeline

This DLC is set in the **gray zone** between Arcane Season 1 and Season 2 — Jinx has fired her super-rocket, the Piltover Council has been attacked, and war between Zaun and the Upper City is imminent. The players' intervention can **rewrite the timeline**.

## II. Arcane-Specific Rules

### 2.1 Shimmer System

Shimmer is an alchemical serum developed by Singed — it briefly grants superhuman strength, but carries risks of addiction and mutation.

#### Using Shimmer

Using Shimmer is a **Bonus Action**. Immediately gain the following effects (last 3 rounds):

| Effect | Value |
|------|------|
| AD +20% | — |
| AS +30% | — |
| MS +2 cells | — |
| Damage Reduction 15% | — |
| Vision turns red (Shimmer Sight) | Can see Stealth units within 8 cells |

#### Shimmer Side Effects

After 3 rounds end, must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**:

| Save Result | Effect |
|:--:|------|
| Success | No side effect |
| Failure | Gain 1 stack of "Shimmer Dependency" |
| nat 1 | Gain 2 stacks of "Shimmer Dependency", and cannot act next round (collapse) |

#### Shimmer Dependency (debuff)

| Stacks | Effect | Removal |
|:--:|------|------|
| 1–2 stacks | No noticeable effect | −1 stack after Long Rest |
| 3–4 stacks | Disadvantage on Constitution checks | −1 stack after Long Rest; requires DC 18 Constitution save or it does not recede |
| 5–6 stacks | Max HP −10%; Disadvantage on Constitution checks | Requires withdrawal treatment (story quest) |
| 7+ stacks | Max HP −20%; purple veins appear across body (Social disadvantage); Constitution disadvantage | Requires epic-tier treatment |

#### Shimmer Overdose (Risk Play)

Players may choose to take a **double dose** — effects doubled (AD+40%, AS+60%, MS+4, Damage Reduction 30%), lasting 2 rounds. The side-effect DC rises to **DC 20**, and failure directly adds +3 Dependency stacks.

### 2.2 Hextech Forging (Expanded)

This is an **Arcane-themed deepening** of the Piltover Regional Enhancement in Player Rulebook §4.5.1. Usable only by characters of Piltover/Zaun origin.

#### Hextech Gem Tiers

The quality of Hextech forging depends on the tier of **Hextech Gem** used:

| Gem Tier | Source | Forge Effect |
|:--:|------|------|
| Unstable Shard | Zaun scrap yard (free) | +1 stat, but 20% chance to explode (forger takes AD×0.5 damage) |
| Standard Crystal | Shop (500 gold) | +3 stats |
| Refined Gem | Jayce's Lab (1,000 gold or quest reward) | +6 stats + random Common augment |
| Perfect Gem | Boss drop / Council reward | +10 stats + random Rare augment |
| Legendary Core | Arcane finale Boss drop | Legendary: item gains an Active skill (see table below) |

#### Legendary Hextech Forge Effects (choose one)

| Active Skill | Cooldown | Effect |
|------|:--:|------|
| **Hextech Overload** | 8 | For 3 rounds AS+100%, MS+3; afterwards Stun 1 round |
| **Gravity Oscillation** | 6 | All enemies within 3 cells: MS−2, Airborne 1 round (lose move action) |
| **Energy Shield** | 8 | Gain shield AD×1.0 + AP×0.5 for 2 rounds; Crowd Control immunity while shield lasts |
| **Spacetime Distortion** | 10 | Self gains 1 extra Standard Action for the next 2 rounds |

### 2.3 Zaun Undercity Survival Rules

The Zaun Undercity is an extreme environment. Apply the following rules when adventuring in the Undercity:

| Environmental Factor | Effect |
|------|------|
| **Toxic Haze** | At the start of each round, non-Zaun-origin characters must make DC 12 Constitution save or take HP×1% True Damage |
| **Darkness** | With no light source, vision is only 1 cell |
| **Scrap Zone** | Movement cost +1 cell; each round 10% chance to step on a trap (AD×0.5) |
| **Ventilation Ducts** | Small characters may enter (size ≤1 cell); medium characters need DC 15 Athletics check |
| **Rooftop Route** | Zaun-origin characters' rooftop movement costs no extra movement |

#### Advantages for Zaun-Origin Characters

Characters from Piltover/Zaun (Regional Origin §4.2) gain extra advantages in the Undercity:
- Auto-succeed on Toxic Haze saves
- Never trigger traps in Scrap Zones
- No check needed for Ventilation Ducts

### 2.4 Arcane Story-Specific Rules

#### Sister Bond

If two players choose to play characters with a blood/adoptive relationship (such as a Vi-and-Jinx-style sister pair), they gain a special bond:

| Effect | Trigger |
|------|------|
| +2 to hit when within 3 cells of each other | Passive aura |
| When one is below 25% HP, the other deals +50% damage next round | Once per combat |
| On one's death, the other gains "Vengeance" buff (+30% all damage, Crowd Control immunity, until end of combat) | Irreversible once triggered |

#### Council Political Maneuvering

Use the following expanded skills in social scenes within the Piltover Council:

| Action | Skill | DC | Effect |
|------|:--:|:--:|------|
| Proposal lobbying | Persuasion (CHA) | 18 | 1 councilor turns to your side |
| Expose scandal | Perception (PER) → Persuasion | 20 | 1 councilor is coerced into supporting you |
| Hextech demo | Lore (INT) | 15 | Persuade tech-faction councilors |
| Bribe | — | Spend 1,000 gold | 1 neutral councilor votes in favor (no check, but moral consequence) |

The Council has 8 councilors total. Passing a proposal requires ≥5 votes.
