# S1 — Who Killed the Mayor

> **Difficulty**: Beginner | **Suggested Memory Value**: Memory 3-5, 1-3 cycles | **Time Range**: Three days (Monday to Wednesday)
> **Core Skills**: Insight (INS), Social (SOC) primary; Execution (EXE) secondary
> **Players**: 1 player + 1 GM

---

## Opening Description

*Eyes open. Blue light rises slowly from the Eternal Well, illuminating the stone walls of the Holy Relic Cathedral. Bells toll in the distance — the morning bell of Bell Tower Square, seven strokes. Monday morning.*

*A young nun stands by the well in prayer. She notices you have woken, and turns to smile at you.*

*"You look like you've just had a very long dream," she says. Her name is Liz — not that you remember how many times you've heard those words before.*

*Today, Aldric Fane, the mayor of Ashfall City, is to deliver a speech at Bell Tower Square. Three days from now, late at night, he will die in the chair of his study. Poison.*

*You already know who the killer is. But you must prove it. Or — you can stop it before any of this happens.*

---

## Scenario Summary

Aldric Fane, the mayor of Ashfall City — a reformist politician known for his integrity — is found dead in his study late on Wednesday night. The cause of death is poison. There are no signs of forced entry. The killer is clearly someone the mayor knew and trusted.

The mayor had recently been pushing a secret investigation — into the illegal activities of a certain faction within Ashfall City. And this investigation ultimately pointed toward a disturbing truth.

## Timeline

### Day 1 — Monday

| Time | Scene | Type | Key Elements |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Morning** | **Eternal Well** — the player wakes. Sister Liz prays by the well. | Fixed | The starting point of every cycle. Liz always smiles at the player. If the player initiates conversation, she mentions "Today's mayoral speech might be worth a listen." |
| **Forenoon** | **Bell Tower Square** — Mayor Aldric Fane gives a public speech. This may be the player's first time (this cycle) seeing the living mayor. | Fixed | The mayor hints at "a truth soon to be revealed" in his speech. A perceptive player (Insight DC 12) will notice the unnatural reactions of a few people below the stage: a middle-aged man in gray (Edwin the doctor) is trembling; a nobleman (Lord Morton) wears a gloomy expression. |
| **Afternoon** | **Hearth District Market** — gather information, build preliminary relationships. | Variable | The player is free to explore. Each NPC and shop has different gossip. The baker says "the mayor has gotten much thinner lately"; the apothecary complains "someone's been buying strange herbs lately." |
| **Evening** | **Mayor's Mansion** — the mayor's routine meeting with the Merchant Guild. | Semi-fixed | If the player manages to attend (requires a Social check or disguise), they may observe a certain merchant being unusually nervous around the mayor. Guard Captain Callen is present — a potential ally worth befriending. |
| **Night** | **Free Action** — the player's first major window of action. | Variable | Options: sneak into the Mayor's Mansion to investigate, tail a suspect, visit an NPC to build a relationship, or explore the Old City Ruins for clues. |

### Day 2 — Tuesday

| Time | Scene | Type | Key Elements |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Morning** | **Mayor Absent from Routine Breakfast** — the news spreads through the market. | Fixed | The mayor has received a threatening letter. The player may learn of it through different channels: the chatter of the mansion servants, the guards' conversation, the Mayor's Wife's complaints. The content of the threatening letter is a key clue. |
| **Daytime** | **Investigate the Source of the Threatening Letter** — track the mayor's political enemies and suspicious figures. | Variable | Unfolds based on the route the player chooses. Political route: visit the opposition leader; underground route: trace the letter's source through the Shadow Guild; church route: investigate whether the Church knew. |
| **Evening** | **Mayor's Private Meeting** — the mayor meets someone privately. | Fixed (meeting happens) / Variable (identity of the other party) | In the first cycle the meeting party may be Lord Morton (Red Herring). In later cycles it may become Guard Captain Callen, or Edwin the doctor. The player can attempt to tail or eavesdrop (Stealth DC 16 or Social DC 14). |
| **Night** | **Mayor's Study** — the mayor works late into the night. | Semi-fixed | This is the last time the living mayor is seen (if the player does not act). Edwin the doctor, during his routine evening examination, prepares a sleeping draught laced with chronic poison for the mayor. If the player visits the mayor at this time, they may notice something amiss. |

### Day 3 — Wednesday

| Time | Scene | Type | Key Elements |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **Daytime** | **Final Investigation Window** — tension rises. | Variable | If the player has gathered enough clues, they may attempt to prevent the murder during the day. The mayor refused all meeting requests today — his secretary says "the mayor is unwell." |
| **Afternoon** | **Mayor's Last Public Sighting** — the mayor briefly appears on the mansion balcony, looking utterly exhausted. | Fixed | If the player sees the mayor at this time, an Insight DC 14 check reveals his pale complexion and trembling hands — the poison has already begun to take effect. |
| **Evening to Midnight** | **Murder Occurs** — the mayor dies in his study. | Fixed (unless prevented) | The exact time and details may shift slightly each cycle. If the player is present — it may be a confrontation scene with the killer. If not — the next morning, the Mayor's Wife's scream will echo through the mansion. |
| **After the Murder** | **Body Discovered** — the city enters a state of alert. | Fixed (if the murder occurs) | The time of discovery depends on the player's actions. If the player is the first discoverer, they may perform Cause-of-Death Analysis (Will + Cause-of-Death Analysis DC 14). On-site investigation (Insight DC 12-18) may reveal the medicine vial, fragments of a letter, and the mayor's final notes. |

## Three Investigation Routes

### Route A — Political Investigation (Social primary)

**Suited Attributes**: High Social (SOC), Moderate Insight (INS)

- Attend a noble banquet, searching for clues in conversation
- Visit the mayor's political enemy, Lord Morton (Red Herring — has motive but is not the killer)
- Win the trust of the Mayor's Wife, Alicia
- Key NPC: Chief Secretary Renard (nervous, but actually protecting the mayor's investigation files)
- Advantage: Safe, information-rich
- Challenge: Nobles do not easily trust strangers; requires high Social attribute

### Route B — Underground Investigation (Execution + Insight primary)

**Suited Attributes**: High Execution (EXE), Moderate Insight (INS)

- Obtain intelligence through the Shadow Guild (information broker Rena)
- Track the suspicious people the mayor contacted before his death
- Sneak into the crime scene to gather evidence
- Visit a back-alley doctor to analyze the poison's composition
- Advantage: Can obtain intelligence the official investigation cannot reach
- Challenge: Dangerous, may encounter combat; failed stealth leads to arrest

### Route C — Church Route (Insight + Will primary)

**Suited Attributes**: High Insight (INS), Moderate Will (WIL)

- Use the Church of Death's "goodwill" toward Returners to gain support
- Build a relationship with Sister Liz — she will become your most important ally
- Investigate the Church itself — how much do they know about the mayor's investigation?
- Consult the Church archives (requires permission or infiltration)
- Advantage: Can obtain Church resources and access
- Challenge: If the Church itself is involved in the murder, you are walking into the tiger's den

## Key Clue Distribution

### Fragment-level Clues (5-7 obtainable per cycle)

- At the speech, the doctor standing in the back row is trembling
- The baker says the mayor has gotten much thinner lately
- The apothecary complains someone has been buying strange herbs
- The mayor's secretary has been working late every night lately
- The Mayor's Wife's maid says her mistress has been suffering insomnia often
- The flower girl at Bell Tower Square saw a gray-clad figure loitering near the mansion for three consecutive days
- The guard captain's shift change is half an hour earlier than last week

### Clue-level Intelligence (2-3 per cycle)

- The content of the threatening letter — mentions something the mayor was investigating
- The type of poison — a rare chronic plant toxin
- The source of the medicine vial — the "Old Tree Pharmacy" in the Hearth District
- The hidden location of the mayor's investigation files
- A certain noble's unnatural hostility toward the mayor (Red Herring)

### Breakthrough-level Intelligence (1 per cycle)

- The pharmacy owner is a cousin of the mayor's personal doctor, Edwin
- Edwin the doctor sent his daughter to the Church three years ago — but the Church records show no record of receiving her
- The mayor's investigation files mention a "Missing Returners List"
- The Mayor's Wife privately met Edwin the doctor the day before the mayor's death

### Truth-level Intelligence (at the climax)

- Edwin the doctor was coerced by the Church — his daughter is alive, held in the Church's underground Returner laboratory
- The Church's promise to Edwin: stay silent, and your daughter will be released (this is a lie)
- The truth the mayor was about to reveal: the Church is systematically "consuming" Returners — forcing them to die repeatedly until their souls break, extracting a certain "essence of time"

## Key NPCs

### Victim

**Mayor Aldric Fane**
Mayor of Ashfall City, fifties, silver-gray hair, blue eyes. A reformist known for his integrity. Privately investigating the disappearances of Returners — he discovered that Returners "guided" by the Church never appeared again.

### Killer (GM Only — Do Not Reveal to Players)

**Personal Doctor Edwin Mor**
Fifties, wears gold-rimmed glasses, always in a gray coat. Gentle, speaks slowly and deliberately. His daughter Amelia awakened as a Returner three years ago, was "guided" by the Church, and vanished. Edwin spent two years investigating and uncovered the Church's truth. The Church told him: stay silent, and your daughter will be released. He added a chronic poison to the sleeping draught he prepared for the mayor — the poison acts hours after ingestion, so he poisoned him on Tuesday evening, and the mayor died late Wednesday night.

**Weakness**: Mentions of his daughter cause his composure to collapse; his hatred of the Church can be redirected; deep down he knows what he is doing is wrong.

### Other Important NPCs

| NPC | Role | Relation to the Case |
|-----|------|-----------|
| **Alicia Fane** | Mayor's Wife | Knows her husband's secret investigation but stays silent. Grieving yet suspiciously calm — she privately met Edwin the doctor and knows the truth but dares not speak. |
| **Chief Secretary Renard** | Mayor's Chief Assistant | Red Herring. Nervous because he is protecting the mayor's investigation files. If the player wins his trust, he will hand over the key documents. |
| **Guard Captain Callen** | Mayor's Security Chief | Former Imperial officer, blindly loyal to the mayor. A powerful potential combat ally. |
| **Lord Morton** | Opposition Leader in the Council | Has motive but is not the killer. He is also investigating something — related to what the mayor was investigating, but from a different angle. |

## Possible Endings

### Perfect Ending
Prevent the murder within 3 cycles, expose the truth of the Church coercing Edwin, and find the whereabouts of Edwin's daughter. Edwin is forgiven (or at least understood), the Church's conspiracy is partly exposed. The mayor survives.

### Justice Ending
Prevent the murder and expose the truth, but Edwin is arrested. The Church's conspiracy is made public, but there is no hard evidence — the mayor and the player know the truth, yet cannot bring down the Church completely.

### Tragedy Ending
The murder is prevented, but Edwin dies in the confrontation (suicide or resisting arrest). The whereabouts of his daughter become a forever mystery.

### Unfinished Ending
Failed to prevent the murder, but successfully identified the killer and provided evidence. The mayor is dead, but justice is served.

### Dead End (Not Recommended, but possible)
Failed to find the killer. Time runs out. The player returns to the Save Point with new information — at least, you know more than the last cycle.

## GM Notes

### First-Cycle Guidance

In the first cycle, ensure the player can:
1. See the mayor (attend the speech) — build emotional connection
2. Build a preliminary relationship with at least 2 NPCs
3. Obtain at least 1 fragment-level clue pointing toward Edwin (though the player will not yet realize its importance)
4. Experience a death (if the player does not die naturally, arrange a controllable dangerous scene on the third day)

### Changes in Subsequent Cycles

- **Cycle 2**: The threatening letter's content provides more detail. The mayor's private meeting party becomes Edwin the doctor (hinted).
- **Cycle 3**: The poison type is confirmed. The vial's source can be traced. If the player already suspects the doctor, they can confront him this cycle.
- **Each cycle micro-adjusts**: The poison type, the time of poisoning, and the time of body discovery may vary slightly, keeping things fresh.

### Shortcut Suggestions

When the player repeats a scene for the 3rd time or more:
- Mayor's speech: "You've heard this three times now — this time you notice a new detail: when Edwin mentions the word 'family,' his hand visibly trembles."
- Market investigation: "You skillfully weave past the crowded stalls and go straight to the apothecary. He still wears that impatient expression."
- Mansion infiltration: "You know the guards' patrol routes like the back of your hand — you even know exactly when that dozing guard closes his eyes."

## Multiplayer GM Tips

This scenario is designed for solo play (1 player + 1 GM). If you start with 2-3 players per GM Rulebook 14-3, note:

- Each player manages their own Rift Mark (RM) and Memory Fragments (MF); players may share a MF with 1 minor action. Encounter Budget (EB) = sum of all players' Memory + highest Execution value.
- S1 is investigation-heavy with scarce combat. With multiple players, "splitting up to chase leads" works best: assign different players to different routes (political / underground / church), gathering fragment-level clues in parallel within the same cycle — far more efficient than solo. Key checks (on-site investigation, confrontation) may use a Team Check (all roll; pass if more than half succeed).
- The only scene requiring enemy scaling is the conflict after a failed infiltration: for each additional combat unit (player or combat companion), enemy HP ×1.5 or count +1 (whichever is the greater threat).
