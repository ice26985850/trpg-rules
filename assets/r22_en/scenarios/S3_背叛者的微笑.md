# S3 — The Traitor's Smile

> **Difficulty**: Intermediate | **Suggested Memory Value**: Memory 5-8, 5-9 cycles | **Time Range**: Five days
> **Core Skills**: Social (SOC), Memory (MEM), Insight (INS)
> **Players**: 1 player + 1 GM

---

## Opening Description

*You look at their faces — Liz, Kai, Rena, Grigo, Ordin, Callen. Your most trusted companions. You have gone through countless cycles with them. You remember their secrets, their fears, their favorite colors.*

*But you also remember the last cycle — when you turned around, one of them smiled at you. That smile was wrong. Then the knife. Then the darkness.*

*This time, you must find that person. Before they smile again.*

---

## Core Mechanic — Variable Traitor System

In each cycle, **the traitor's identity may differ**. The traitor is not a fixed person, but a fixed "role" — an external faction (the Church, the Tower of Time, the Guild) turns a different person each cycle. But all traitors leave the same "signature": a smile mark.

### GM Setup (decided secretly at the start of each cycle)

1. Choose the traitor's identity (from the companion pool)
2. Choose the subverting faction (Church / Tower of Time / Guild)
3. Choose the reason for turning (family threatened / false promise / deceived)
4. Decide where the smile mark appears

## Companion NPC Pool (4-6 participate each cycle)

| NPC | Background | Possible Reason for Being Turned | Value When Not the Traitor |
|-----|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Sister Liz** | Low-ranking nun of the Church of Death | The Church threatens her family | Medical ability, Church internal intelligence |
| **Apprentice Kai** | Apprentice of the Tower of Time | Conditioned on "healing a Returner's awakening" | Tower of Time intelligence, magical knowledge |
| **Information Broker Rena** | Shadow Guild intermediary | The Guild threatens to expose her brother's whereabouts | Underground network, lockpicking skill |
| **Scavenger Grigo** | Former Imperial officer | Promised the restoration of the Empire | Combat ability, tactical knowledge |
| **Ordin the Silent** | Returner Mutual Aid Society | Notebook stolen, coerced | Cycle experience, written intelligence |
| **Guard Captain Callen** | Former mayor's guard captain | Framed as an S1 accomplice | Combat ability, noble-circle intelligence |

## Five-Day Timeline

| Day | Event | Type |
|------|------|------|
| **Day 1** | Companions assemble. Shared goal established (escort an important item / investigate an incident) | Fixed |
| **Day 2-3** | Mission in progress. First anomaly — a small mistake causes a plan deviation. The player first realizes there may be a mole | Semi-fixed |
| **Day 4** | Crisis moment — the traitor's action causes severe consequences (companion death / severe wound / mission failure) | Fixed (betrayal occurs) |
| **Day 5** | Confrontation — find the traitor and decide how to handle them | Variable |

---

## Three Investigation Routes

### Route A — Trust and Observation (Social + Insight)
Build deep relationships with each companion, observe behavioral anomalies. **Advantage**: The relationship itself has value. **Risk**: The more emotion invested, the more painful the betrayal.

### Route B — Cross-Verification of Information (Memory + Insight)
Give different companions different pieces of information, track which leak. **Advantage**: Calm and precise. **Risk**: Companions' normal exchanges complicate verification.

### Route C — Pre-set Trap (Memory + Execution)
Use multi-cycle memory to set traps, creating situations "only the traitor would react to." **Advantage**: Catch in the act. **Risk**: Wrong judgment hurts innocent companions.

---

## Core Experience

The purpose of this scenario is not "find the bad guy and beat him," but to let the player experience **"understanding matters more than punishment."** Every traitor has their reasons — usually coercion or deception. The ultimate "victory" may be to save the traitor rather than destroy them.

---

## Clear Conditions

- **Main Objective**: Find the traitor before Day 5
- **Perfect Clear**: Understand the traitor's motive, save rather than punish
- **Minimum Clear**: Successfully identify the traitor and stop the key betrayal action

---

> *"Trust is your scarcest resource in the cycles — because every restart, you must persuade them anew from zero. But sometimes, trust is also the strongest weapon."*

## Multiplayer GM Tips

This scenario is designed for solo play (1 player + 1 GM). If you start with 2-3 players per GM Rulebook 14-3, note:

- Multiple players form a "Returner Squad"; the companion NPC pool (Liz / Kai / Rena / Grigo / Ordin / Callen) remains NPCs; the traitor is still turned by the GM from the pool each cycle, one person at a time. Each player may build trust with a particular companion, making the betrayal of the "smile mark" more dispersed and more personal.
- As governed by 14-3: when S3 recruits combat-type companions, enemy strength rises one tier in sync. Cross-verification of information (Route B) is stronger with multiple players — give different players different intel fragments, and who leaks is plain to see.
- Team Check (pass if more than half succeed) applies to confrontation and final choice; the ending (understand-and-save vs. identify-and-punish) is decided by squad consensus.
