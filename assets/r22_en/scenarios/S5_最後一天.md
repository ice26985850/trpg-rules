# S5 — The Last Day

> **Difficulty**: Epic | **Suggested Memory Value**: Memory 8-12, 10-15 cycles | **Time Range**: Fourteen days
> **Core Skills**: All five attributes
> **Players**: 1 player + 1 GM
> **Prerequisites**: Recommended to have completed at least three of S1-S4

---

## Opening Description

*The fourteenth day. You stand before the Eternal Well, looking up at the sky. The sky is cracking.*

*Not a metaphor — literally cracking. Through the fissures flicker images of past and future: the armies of the Radiant Gold Empire on the march, a child you have never seen smiling, your own funeral — several times over.*

*Time itself is collapsing. Different districts of the city run at different speeds: a day in the Bell Tower District equals an hour in the Hearth District. Some streets repeat the same minute — already repeated seven times. The Returners' power is running out of control. You see a Returner forced to Return without dying, screaming as they vanish into the air.*

*You have only fourteen days. No Save Point can skip this day. You must find a way to stop the time collapse before everything freezes into eternal stillness.*

---

## Core Concept

On the fourteenth day, time begins to "fracture." If not stopped: time will fully collapse — not the destruction of the world, but **eternal stagnation**. Everything freezes at the final instant, forever.

---

## Three Routes to Stop It

### Route One — "Repair the Serpent Scales"

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Goal** | Find and repair the wound of Ouroboros (the Time Serpent) |
| **Requires** | Tower of Time research data (S2) + ancient ritual knowledge of the Time Watcher Ruins (S4 clue) |
| **Key Scene** | Travel to the Serpent's Blood Ruins in the Wailing Forest and perform the repair ritual |
| **Cost** | A willing Returner dedicates all their cycles — returning to the moment the Serpent's Blood first dripped |
| **Result** | The Return phenomenon vanishes from the world. All existing Returners lose their power and become ordinary people |

### Route Two — "Break the Cycle"

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Goal** | Completely destroy the mechanism of the time cycle |
| **Requires** | Shadow Guild underground intelligence (S3) + the Imperial device deep in the Old City Ruins |
| **Key Scene** | Activate the "Time Fracture Device" — built by the Emperor to break his own cycle |
| **Cost** | The memories of all Returners are erased — forgetting everything built across the cycles |
| **Result** | The Return phenomenon vanishes. No one remembers what happened. The world continues — from zero |

### Route Three — "Coexist with the Serpent"

| Item | Content |
|------|------|
| **Goal** | Learn to coexist with the cycle — not repair or break it |
| **Requires** | Ancient Church scriptures (S1) + the collective wisdom of the Returner Mutual Aid Society |
| **Key Scene** | Persuade enough Returners to perform a collective ritual together — synchronize Save Points to form a Time Anchor |
| **Cost** | Never truly able to "leave" the cycle — Returners live within it forever |
| **Result** | The Return phenomenon stabilizes. Returners form a community spanning the cycles. A new era begins |

## Fourteen-Day Timeline

| Day | Phase | Event |
|------|------|------|
| Day 1-3 | Identify | Minor temporal fluctuations. Citizens uneasy. The player must recognize this as a prelude to the end |
| Day 4-6 | Mobilize | The Church declares the "End-Cycle." The Tower of Time holds an emergency meeting. The Guild profits in the chaos. Choose cooperation partners |
| Day 7-9 | Deepen | Temporal anomalies worsen. Some districts unstable. Deeply investigate at least one route |
| Day 10-12 | Turning Point | Key NPCs may sacrifice themselves. The player obtains the key item/knowledge the route requires |
| Day 13 | Farewell | Final interaction with each important NPC — possibly the last cycle |
| Day 14 | **The End** | Execute the final plan. Multiple endings |

---

## Multiple-Ending Matrix

| | Route One: Repair the Serpent Scales | Route Two: Break the Cycle | Route Three: Coexist with the Serpent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Success** | "Silent Ending" — the world returns to normal, you keep your memory but lose your power | "Blank Ending" — no one remembers, starting from zero | "Resonance Ending" — a new community is built, the future full of hope |
| **Partial Success** | Ritual succeeds but permanently lose part of memory | Trapped in the final cycle | Community built but conflict with the Church / Tower of Time continues |
| **Failure** | "Eternal Stagnation" — everything freezes | Same as left | Same as left (but a fourth route may be discovered...) |

---

## Clear Conditions

- **Main Objective**: Stop time from fully collapsing on the fourteenth day
- **Perfect Clear**: The chosen route fully succeeds, its cost bearable
- **Minimum Clear**: Partial success — the world is saved but at a heavy cost

---

## Key NPC Convergence

All important NPCs from the first four scenarios may appear in S5. Based on the player's choices and relationship values in previous scenarios:
- NPCs with established Bonds will proactively offer help
- NPCs once betrayed may become obstacles now
- Core NPCs (Archbishop Ethlyn, Chief Scholar Victor, Information Broker Rena) reveal their final stance in this scenario

## Multiplayer GM Tips

This scenario is designed for solo play (1 player + 1 GM). If you start with 2-3 players per GM Rulebook 14-3, note:

- Each player manages their own Rift Mark (RM) and Memory Fragments (MF); Encounter Budget (EB) = sum of all players' Memory + highest Execution value. S5 combat scenes (out-of-control Returners, threats in temporal anomaly zones) follow 14-3 enemy scaling.
- S5 is the finale most suited to multiple players: the three routes (Repair the Serpent Scales / Break the Cycle / Coexist with the Serpent) may be advanced in parallel by different players, each negotiating the corresponding faction (Church / Tower of Time / Guild), converging on Day 13-14 to execute the final plan. Route Three's "synchronized Save Point collective ritual" is naturally a Team Check.
- The multiple-ending matrix is decided by squad consensus; the "prerequisite: complete at least three of S1-S4" applies to the squad as a whole (individual experiences may be combined).
