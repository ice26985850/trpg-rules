### Lady Viper (CR 9) — Boss

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Insight (INS) | 7 |
| Memory (MEM) | 5 |
| Execution (EXE) | 6 |
| Social (SOC) | 8 |
| Will (WIL) | 6 |
| Hit Points (HP) | 20 (includes Boss bonus +5) |
| Damage Tolerance (TOL) | 5 (includes Silken Armor +2) |
| Armor Value (AV) | 2 |
| Initiative | INS 7 + 1d10 |
| Attack | Poison Needle (Piercing + Poison) 1d6 / Poisoned Dagger (Sharp + Poison) 1d8 |

**Three-Phase Combat**:

**Phase One (HP 20→14): "Charming Gaze"**
- Each turn, a minor action: Social 8 vs player Will contest; on failure the player loses 2 TU
- Primarily ranged Poison Needle attacks, keeping distance

**Phase Two (HP 13→7): "Serpent Summoning"**
- Spend 3 TU to summon 1d3 venomous snakes (Minion tier, HP 4, attack 1d4+Poison)
- Hides within the serpent swarm (Dodge Advantage)
- Switches to melee Poisoned Dagger

**Phase Three (HP 6→0): "Poison Mist Outbreak"**
- One-time ability: all zones become Poison Mist (player takes 1 Light Wound of Poison per turn)
- Immune to poison itself

**Weakness**:
- **Fire Fear** (Psychological Trauma): Fire Damage +1 tier, Disadvantage on Will checks
- **Narcissism**: Social distractions (SOC 8 contest; on success loses 1 TU)

**AI**: Cunning Tier — keeps distance, uses Social abilities to divide the party, prioritizes eliminating wounded targets, attempts negotiation when HP < 3

### Time Devourer Prototype (CR 12) — Boss

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Insight (INS) | 8 |
| Memory (MEM) | 10 |
| Execution (EXE) | 5 |
| Social (SOC) | 1 |
| Will (WIL) | 4 |
| Hit Points (HP) | 18 |
| Damage Tolerance (TOL) | 2 |
| Armor Value (AV) | 0 (Ethereal) |
| Initiative | INS 8 + 1d10 + 5 (Time Sense) |
| Attack | Time Erosion (Mental) 2d6 / Memory Drain (Special) |

**Special Abilities**:
- **Time Devour**: The struck target loses 1d4 Memory Fragments (effective that round)
- **Time Corridor**: Creates a time-loop zone — characters inside repeat the same action each turn (resist with Will DC 18)
- **Precognitive Dodge**: Automatically dodges the first attack each turn (knows when the attack will come)
- **Memory Echo**: Uses memories of the player's death scenes from past loops to launch Mental attacks (Rift Mark +1d3)

**Weakness**:
- **Time Anchor**: Loses Precognitive Dodge near the Eternal Well or a Save Point
- **Memory Overload**: Taking damage from 3+ different sources in the same round causes time-sense confusion
- **Not Yet Fully Formed**: Essentially a collapsed Returner, still retaining fragmented humanity — can be moved by emotion

**Background**: The Time Devourer Prototype is a Returner whose Rift Mark exceeded 40 and became completely Uncontrolled. Their mind has collapsed, and their body is warped by temporal energy. They cannot distinguish past, present, and future — to them, everything happens at once.

### Immortal Inquisitor (CR 14) — Legendary

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Insight (INS) | 9 |
| Memory (MEM) | 8 |
| Execution (EXE) | 8 |
| Social (SOC) | 4 |
| Will (WIL) | 10 |
| Hit Points (HP) | 26 (includes Legendary bonus +5) |
| Damage Tolerance (TOL) | 9 (includes Ritual Plate +6, at soft cap) |
| Armor Value (AV) | 6 |
| Initiative | INS 9 + 1d10 + 2 |
| Attack | Greatsword (Sharp) 2d6+3 / plus Fire 1d4 |

**Legendary Traits**:
- 2 reactions per turn (not 1)
- Immune to fear effects
- Revives once 1d6 turns after death (HP restored to 10)
- Not affected by Memory-Fragment weakness discovery (must be observed independently)

**Four-Phase Combat**:

**Phase One (HP 26→20): "Judgment Declaration"**
- Declares a "crime" upon the player → player Will DC 16; on failure, all attacks against the Inquisitor suffer Disadvantage

**Phase Two (HP 19→13): "Sin Settlement"**
- Attacks twice per turn (6 TU)
- Each hit restores 1 HP

**Phase Three (HP 12→6): "Eye of Truth"**
- Each turn predicts one player action; if executed, auto-counters
- May use "Shield of Faith" to negate one attack

**Phase Four (HP 5→0): "Final Judgment"**
- All attacks become area attacks (within 2 zones)
- Self loses 2 HP per turn (self-combustion)

**Cross-Loop Memory** (exclusive to Genius-tier AI):
- "Remembers" player tactics from earlier loop combats
- Repeating the same tactic → auto-countered
- Player reveals too much future knowledge → Inquisitor begins to ask: "What exactly are you?"
- After multiple encounters, may become a key narrative figure

**Weakness** (must be discovered independently; Memory Fragments ineffective):
- **Holy Emblem**: The emblem on the chest is the source of power → upon destruction, loses Legendary traits
- **Doctrinal Contradiction**: Citing a doctrinal contradiction (Social DC 18) → loses 1 turn
- **Embers of Flame**: The phase-four self-combustion can be interrupted with water/ice
