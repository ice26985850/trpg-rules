## Currency & Economy

- Base currency: **Gold Coin (G)**
- 1 Gold ≈ one day's wage for an ordinary worker
- Returner starting funds: by background (**20-60 Gold**, see table above)
- After each Death Return, funds reset to the starting value (but **Save-Point-bound equipment** is not lost, see Player Rulebook 3.1)
- **In-loop income (to fix economic failure)**: completing a commission or stage bonus 10-30G; looting a defeated enemy yields 2-8G; at the Ash Alley Black Market, you can sell intelligence fragments to the Shadow Guild for 5-15G. This enables a healthy trade-off between "buy medicine vs. save up for gear."
