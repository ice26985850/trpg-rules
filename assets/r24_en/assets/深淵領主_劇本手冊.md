# Abyss Lord — Modules & Campaign Scenarios

> **Version**: v2.1  
> **Purpose**: Standalone GM scenario manual — contains the Starter Module "First Incursion" and the full Lv1–15 campaign framework "From Cavern to Abyss Empire"  
> **Prerequisite**: Requires the *Abyss Lord* Player Rulebook and GM Guide

---

## Part One: Starter Module "First Incursion"

> **Suitable for**: New GMs and players, onboarding guide  
> **Dungeon Level**: Lv1  
> **Estimated Duration**: 1–2 sessions

### Scene 1: Establishing the Initial Dungeon

GM narration:
> "You are a newly ascended Dungeon Lord. The mana torches of your Throne Hall are already lit — this is your first dungeon. For now there are only three chambers: your Throne Hall, and the ends of two undeveloped, pitch-black tunnels. A few freshly recruited monsters prowl restlessly at your feet. From afar comes the faint sound of surface humans — are they coming?"

**Players need**: Familiarize themselves with attributes, assign starting monsters, plan which side to dig first.

**GM Preparation**:
- Have players fill in the core sections of their character sheet (archetype, attributes, starting monsters)
- Introduce the resource system — players' starting resources are listed in each archetype's configuration
- Introduce the turn structure — this module has 5 scenes, each scene = 1 in-game turn

---

### Scene 2: First Dig & Expansion

The player spends 1 action + Building Materials ×2, and rolls IND + Engineering check to perform the first dig.

**Check**: IND + Engineering skill vs Difficulty 1 Success

| Result | Narration |
|------|------|
| Failure | The pickaxe hit a hard rock layer — you only chipped off a few pieces of rubble. Building Materials are consumed, space not unlocked, but the GM may give a clue: "Something behind the rock seems to be glowing…" |
| 1 Success | You dug out a small cave. You raise the mana torch, illuminating the rough stone walls |
| 2+ Success | You not only dug out the space, but also discovered shimmering crystals on the rock wall — a **Vein**! That space's mine output permanently +50% |

**GM Guidance**: Narrate the discoveries from exploration — you may stumble onto a vein, or find a broken diary inside an ancient ruin. Let the player choose what to build in this space (recommend: Trap Workshop or Mine).

---

### Scene 3: First Wave of Invasion

**Warning**: If a Scout Outpost was built — automatically gain intel. Otherwise roll CUN + Espionage check.

> Recon check reveals: "2 T1 Adventurers approaching. A burly man carrying a shield, and a lithe, slender figure leaping across the rock walls."

**Preset Adventurers**:

| Name | Class | CP Dice Pool | HP | Trait | Background |
|------|------|--------|----|------|------|
| **Allen ** | Warrior T1 | 1d6+2 | 5 | Shield: Defense +1 die | "Loyal and simple, saving up for his sister's medicine" |
| **Mira ** | Thief T1 | 2d6+1 | 3 | Can scout traps: Trap DC -2 | "Impatient and reckless, wants to prove himself" |

**Adventurer AI Behavior** (roll 2d6):

| 2d6 | First-floor Behavior |
|:---:|------|
| 2-4 | Mira scouts traps first, Allen covers the rear |
| 5-7 | The two advance cautiously, Allen front, Mira to the side |
| 8-10 | Mira attempts to sneak past the monsters, Allen draws fire head-on |
| 11-12 | Allen charges recklessly: "Mira, keep up!" |

**Invasion Route**: Outer Layer → Middle Layer. GM guides the player to deploy monsters and traps.

**Combat Tips**:
- This is a teaching battle — let the players make mistakes. If the player forgets to deploy traps, let Allen step on a rockslide that "fortunately didn't trigger"
- If the player is at a disadvantage — the dungeon structure itself triggers a "rockslide", dealing 1d3 damage to the adventurers (the GM may use the environment to help beginners at any time)
- If Mira's HP drops below 2 she will attempt to retreat — this is a good moment to introduce the "capture vs. escape" mechanic

---

### Scene 4: First Captive & Interrogation

If the adventurers are defeated (HP to 0 = unconscious): they may be captured.

**GM introduces the interrogation mechanic**:
- Player chooses a method: **Interrogation** (DOM) / **Torture** (WAR) / **Charm** (DOM + spend ◆)
- Allen's weakness: "Eyes flicker when his sister is mentioned" — if the player uses Charm and brings up family, DOM +1 die
- Mira's weakness: "Impatient" — if the player makes her wait more than 2 turns before interrogation, WIL -1

**Interrogation Sample Dialogue**:

> **GM (Allen)**: "I won't talk… you monsters…"
> 
> If using Charm:
> "Your sister… she's still waiting for you out there, right? If the Adventurers' Guild found out you were captured, would they send someone to rescue you — or abandon you? Which do you think is more likely?"
> 
> Allen's WIL contest: WIL=3, roll 3d6. Lord's DOM + Interrogation vs 3d6.

**First Captive Reward**: Regardless of interrogation result, the player gains the achievement "First Prisoner" — an extra +1 Experience Point (EXP).

---

### Scene 5: Post-Battle Settlement & Second Expansion

**Loot Settlement** (by defeated adventurer):
- Each defeated T1 adventurer: ◆1d3 + ◇1 + ●1
- Each captive: no loot (the captive itself is the loot)

**Experience**: Repelling a T1 invasion +3 EXP, first captive +1 Experience Point (EXP).

**Second Expansion**: Guide the player to use the gained Building Materials for a second dig. By now the player should have experienced the full "**Dig → Build → Assign → Defend**" loop.

**Transition to next turn**:
> Second week after the battle. Allen is locked in the prison cell you newly dug. Mira escaped — she will bring people back. But you also have your first real stash of resources. The dungeon's mana torches seem brighter than before.

## Part Two: Campaign Framework — From Cavern to Abyss Empire

> **Suitable for**: Lv1–15 full long-term campaign  
> **Estimated total duration**: 15–30 sessions

---

### Act One: Establishment (Lv1–3) — 2~4 sessions

**Theme**: Survive. A newly enthroned Dungeon Lord starts from the Throne Hall + 2 pitch-black tunnels. The outside is unknown — every sound of digging may draw the first adventurers.

| Dungeon Lv | Key Event | GM Focus |
|:---:|------|------|
| 1 | **First Incursion** (see Part One). Repel 2 T1 adventurers, first taste of defense combat | Teaching battle. Let players familiarize with combat phases, trap triggers, monster deployment |
| 2 | First dig expansion succeeds. Discover the first natural resource (Vein / Mana Node). Neighboring NPC dungeon appears for the first time — a letter arrives at your Throne Hall | Introduce expansion system. Introduce diplomacy concept (reply to the letter or not?) |
| 3 | **"The Adventurers' Guild has noticed you"**. Two consecutive turns of invasion — the second wave is a revenge squad (led by the adventurer who escaped last time). Must decide: strengthen defense or accelerate expansion? | Introduce continuous invasion pressure. Player must make strategic trade-offs |

**Revenge Squad Data** (Lv3 second wave):
- 2–3 T2 adventurers (dice pool 4–5d6), including the escaped adventurer + 1 reinforcement
- Escaped adventurer gains the "Revenge" trait — first-round attack +1 die

**Act One Climax**: "Adventurers' Guild Wanted" — after INF reaches 3, the Guild officially places your dungeon on the "Wanted List". GM introduces a physical wanted poster (printable prop).

### Act Two: Growth (Lv4–7) — 3~6 sessions

**Theme**: Expansion and choice. The dungeon takes shape. The Abyss Embassy is built — you must choose one of the Four Alliances to join. The choice will affect all later diplomacy.

| Dungeon Lv | Key Event | GM Focus |
|:---:|------|------|
| 4 | Can build the Abyss Embassy. Two known NPC dungeons each invite you to join their alliance. **Balzec, "Earth-Render"**'s flame missive vs **Marquis Malthus**'s bone dove. Must choose — or stay neutral | Alliance choice. This affects all subsequent diplomacy |
| 5 | Unlock elite monster recruitment. First elite invasion (T3, includes Boss captain). If allied before — allied dungeon may proactively send reinforcements | Boss battle mechanic introduced (Phase 2 trigger). First experience of allied coordination |
| 6 | Archetype Lv6 advancement unlocked. After the dungeon reaches a certain size — first discovery that conflicts exist between other dungeons. **Moris, "Thousand-Faces"** asks you to mediate his territorial dispute with Balzec | Inter-dungeon politics. Player's choice affects the tripartite relationship |

**Two Invitation Letters**:

*Balzec's flame missive* (a parchment scorched at the edges, the writing seeming to burn):
> "New kid — heard your dungeon is starting to look the part. The gates of the Abyssal Legion are always open to the strong. Come to Burning-Sky Fortress, accept the trial. Pass — you're one of us. Fail — you won't even leave bones. Awaiting your choice. — Earth-Render"

*Malthus's bone dove* (a bird formed of white bone, blue flames burning in its eye sockets, lands on the armrest of your Throne Hall, clutching a pristine white letter in its claws):
> "To our respected new neighbor: the Bone Mound Council has taken note of your activities in the Sea of Shadows. We admire lords who value order. On the day your embassy is completed, allow me to visit in person — to discuss maintaining Abyssal order in the face of the Order of Light's threat. — Malthus, Marquis of the Eternal Slumber Corridor"

**Act Two Climax**: "Abyss Black Market" — **Grix, "Goldtooth"** (Ancient Wyrm) hosts the annual black market auction in the Scrap Canyon. All known NPC dungeon lords attend.

**Black Market Scene Framework**:
1. **Entry**: Describe the lively scene of the Scrap Canyon. Depictions of each NPC lord's appearance/demeanor (vividly, per players' known NPCs)
2. **Socializing**: The player may converse with any NPC. Each conversation is a small diplomacy check opportunity
3. **Auction**: 3–5 items auctioned in sequence (at least 1 legendary item, starting bid ◆50)
4. **Assassination**: At the auction's climax — intel from the Shadow Network reveals an assassin has infiltrated. CG: player may choose to expose / connive / exploit the chaos to grab bargains

---

### Act Three: Threat (Lv8–11) — 4~8 sessions

**Theme**: War and betrayal. The dungeon becomes a regional power. The Order of Light begins organizing large-scale expeditions — no longer adventurer squads, but company-level offensives.

| Dungeon Lv | Key Event | GM Focus |
|:---:|------|------|
| 8 | **"The Order's Expedition"** — the Order of Light dispatches 3–4 T3 squads invading simultaneously from different entrances! Must call on allied reinforcements. Dungeon diplomacy check determines how many NPC dungeons are willing to send troops | First large-scale multi-front defense. Tests the player's resource allocation and diplomatic results |
| 9 | Archetype Lv9 ultimate advancement unlocked. Around this time — cracks appear within your alliance. Bone Mound Council: is Malthus being challenged? Abyssal Legion: does Balzec demand you join the flame trial? Free Artificers: tech-piracy dispute? Shadow Network: traitor leaking intel? | Intra-alliance politics. Player's reaction determines the alliance's direction |
| 10 | Unlock the first legendary monster. **Moris, "Thousand-Faces"**'s forbidden experiment suffers a serious accident — his dungeon is spiraling out of control. If you were friendly with him — he sends you a desperate distress signal | Time-sensitive side quest. Rescue / exploit / ignore — three choices affecting the whole picture |

**The Order's Expedition Scene**:

Three preset invasion entrances:
- **Main Entrance** (front): T3 elite squad ×2, includes Boss captain (Paladin)
- **Side Rift** (right): T3 standard squad ×1
- **Abandoned Passage** (left): T2 patrol ×1 (probing invasion)

**Allied Troop Deployment Check** (per allied dungeon):
> Roll DOM + Diplomacy check vs Difficulty 3. Success = that dungeon sends reinforcements (1d3 monsters, total CP ≤6) to assist defense of 1 entrance.

**Act Three Climax**: "Breath of Nexis" — abnormal fluctuations in the Abyssal mana tide. **Ethlin** (Void-Weaver) warns: the mana tide may be a premonition of "something" awakening.

> Ethlin's whisper spreads through your Throne Hall: "Do you feel it? The Mana Crystals are trembling. Not just yours — the mana of the entire Abyss is trembling. I went to the Primordial Rift… it is breathing."

GM introduces the first anomaly of the **Primordial Rift (D-16)** after Lv10 — it made a sound. The ancient dungeon begins to awaken.

---

### Act Four: Overlord (Lv12–15) — 6+ sessions

**Theme**: Fateful choice. Your dungeon has become a top-tier Abyssal power. The Order of Light prepares a "Crusade" — a full-scale assault on the Abyss. And the sleeping entity in the **Primordial Rift** is about to wake.

| Dungeon Lv | Key Event | GM Focus |
|:---:|------|------|
| 12 | **"The Dungeon Covenant"** — you convene (or are invited to) the Abyssal Council. All Lv8+ dungeon lords gather. Topic: unite against the Order of Light, or each fight alone? Your diplomacy check determines the strength of the united front | Multi-NPC diplomatic negotiation. This is a complete social encounter scene |
| 13 | **"The Light Crusade"** begins. T4–T5 adventurer legions attack all allied dungeons simultaneously. The forces assigned to your dungeon are determined by INF — high Infamy attracts the strongest enemies | Ultimate defense battle. Use all accumulated resources, monsters, allies, captive cannon fodder |
| 14 | Crusade continues. If defense succeeded last turn — adventurers suffer heavy losses but the Order's reinforcements keep coming. Now a turning point appears: **Corwin, "Rusted Duke"** (former Paladin) reveals the Order has a fatal weakness inside — he needs your help to obtain the final piece of intel | Surface infiltration mission. This may be the lord's last expedition |

**The Dungeon Covenant Scene**:

Assembly point: the Scrap Canyon, in Grix's mint hall.
Attending NPCs (at least the 8 below, possibly more depending on player's diplomatic results):
- Malthus (Bone Mound Council), Balzec (Abyssal Legion)
- Vera (Shadow Network), Wrench (Free Artificers)
- Moris, Ethlin, Selathil (neutral attendance), Corwin

**Debate Structure**:
1. Player's opening statement (DOM + Diplomacy, determines overall atmosphere)
2. Three rounds of debate — one topic per round (unite / independent action / resource allocation)
3. Final vote — each NPC dungeon's voting tendency depends on its relationship value with the player

---

### 🏁 Ultimate Event: "Awakening of Nexis"

> *On the 14th full-moon night of the Abyssal month in Year 700 of the Rift Era, the Primordial Rift split open. What emerged from it was not a monster — but a consciousness. Nexis, the Mother of the Abyss, opened her eyes after ten million years of slumber. Her first words spread through every dungeon in the Abyss:*
>
> *"My children — what have you made of my Underworld?"*

**GM Scene Framework**:

| Phase | Content | Check |
|------|------|------|
| **Descent** | Nexis's voice resounds through the Abyss. All dungeons' Mana Crystals glow in resonance simultaneously. Dungeon mana temporarily ×2 — but may be reclaimed afterward | No check. Narrative scene |
| **Judgment** | Nexis examines each great alliance in turn. What she sees is not a united Abyss — but division, infighting, and compromise with the surface. Her question: **"With my blood, what have you built?"** The player responds on behalf of their dungeon | DOM check (respond to the questioning). ARC check (understand her true intent) |
| **Choice** | Nexis gives two choices: **A.** Become her right hand — gain ultimate power, but the dungeon becomes her extension (losing some autonomy). **B.** Refuse — be exiled from the Abyss, becoming a Wandering Lord. **C.** Persuade her — the Abyss has already evolved, no single mother-goddess needed (extremely hard challenge: DOM + Diplomacy ×3 multiple checks, all must succeed) | This is the campaign's ultimate check. The player may use all accumulated intel, ally support, captive testimony to aid the debate |
| **Consequence** | A = gain the "Mark of Nexis" (dungeon MAG locked at 15, all monsters CP +2), but you can never again defy her will. B = dungeon reclaimed — but at the last moment you receive a joint letter from all allies: you are not alone. Become a "Wandering Lord" — dungeon jointly held by allies. C = Nexis smiles. "Perhaps this era truly no longer needs a mother-goddess." She dissipates into mana filling the sky — the entire Abyss gains permanent mana +3. Your dungeon becomes the new center of the Abyss | |

**Multiple Check Details (Route C)**:

| Check | Goal | Usable Supporting Evidence |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1st Dominion (DOM) + Diplomacy | Prove the Abyss Lords deserve autonomy | Ally relationships with at least 3 NPC dungeons (each ally +1 die) |
| 2nd Dominion (DOM) + Diplomacy | Prove the Order of Light can be contained | Corwin's inside intel on the Order (+2 dice), record of defeating the Order's expedition (+1 die) |
| 3rd Arcana (ARC) + Arcane Knowledge | Prove Abyssal mana has evolved a new ecology | Player's magic research results (+1 die), Ethlin's dimensional research (+1 die), dungeon MAG ≥12 (+1 die) |

> All three checks succeed = Route C achieved. Any single failure = Nexis unmoved — return to A/B choice.

---

### Act Four Aftermath — Finale Events

Regardless of choice, the campaign ends with a launch ceremony for a **New Abyssal Era**:

- **If A chosen**: Your dungeon becomes Nexis's shrine. All NPCs come to pay homage. Your character becomes the Abyss's "Right Hand of God" — unmatched in power, but can never again say "no" to Nexis.

- **If B chosen**: You lose your dungeon, but gain the "Wandering Legend" identity — you may freely roam between any allied dungeon. A new adventure begins — you may assist allies against the Crusade, seek a way to build a new dungeon, or — turn to infiltrate the surface. A 24-hour surface counterattack side-event is playable.

- **If C chosen**: Your dungeon is publicly recognized as the "new center of the Abyss". The Order of Light is forced to sign a ceasefire agreement. From then on — dungeons are no longer monster lairs. **They are kingdoms.**

**Finale Scene**:
> Three Abyssal months later. The day the ceasefire agreement is signed. You stand at the highest point of the Throne Hall, looking down over your dungeon — now it is no longer a string of caves, but a prosperous underground kingdom. Monsters and converted former adventurers coexist in peace. In the distance, the newly built Abyss Embassy is brightly lit — that is the congratulatory envoys from sixteen dungeons. The Abyss will never be the same again.
