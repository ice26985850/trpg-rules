## Forging & Equipment System (orig. §2.4.1)

## 2.4.1 Forging & Equipment System ⭐

The Forge Room can smelt metal ingots and rare ores produced by the Mine into equipment for monsters to wear or to trade.

### Ores & Materials

While producing Building Materials during excavation, the Mine additionally yields the following random materials each round (roll d6):

| d6 | Material | Quality | Use |
|:---:|----------|:---:|------|
| 1-3 | **Iron Ingot** | Common | Forge basic weapons/armor (consume 2 Iron Ingots → 1 equipment) |
| 4-5 | **Obsidian Steel** | Fine | Forge fine equipment (consume 2 Obsidian Steel +1◇ → 1 equipment) |
| 6 | **Abyss Alloy** | Epic | Forge epic equipment (consume 1 Abyss Alloy +2◇ +1● → 1 equipment; requires Forge Room Lv3) |

**Mine Lv2**: material output doubles (roll d6 twice); **Mine Lv3**: roll d6 three times, rare-ore chance +1 (Abyss Alloy produced on 5–6).

### Forgeable Equipment

| Equipment | Material Req. | Forge Room | Effect | Trade Price (◆) |
|-----------|---------------|:---:|--------|:---:|
| **Iron Claw** | Iron Ingot×2 | Lv1 | Equipped monster CP+1 | 8 |
| **Iron Armor** | Iron Ingot×3 | Lv1 | Equipped monster Defense dice +1 die | 10 |

| **Abyss Bastion** | Abyss Alloy×2+◇×3+●×1 | Lv3 | Equipped monster Defense dice +3 dice; Max HP +8 | 80 |
| **Lord Armament** | Abyss Alloy×2+◇×5+●×3 | Lv3 | Lord physical attack dice +2 dice; can enchant 1 extra effect | 150 |

### Equipment Rules

- Each monster may equip at most **1 weapon + 1 armor**
- The Lord may equip at most **1 Lord Armament**
- Equipment can be swapped at any time (free action, performed during the Management Phase)
- When a monster dies → its equipment drops back into the dungeon (recoverable), unless "captured" by an adventurer in combat (Boss adventurers have a 1/6 chance to capture equipment)
- Equipment may be sold as trade goods to other dungeons (at Trade Price × relation coefficient)

### Special Equipment (requires enchanting to unlock)

See §3.8.5 "Enchantment Spells" — enchanted equipment layers additional magical effects on top of base forged equipment.
