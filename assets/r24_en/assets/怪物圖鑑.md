## 2.7 Monster Random Management Abilities ⭐

When each monster is acquired, roll **d66** (two d6, one as tens, one as ones) to gain 1 random management ability. Ability strength has three tiers: Normal (ML1-2), Enhanced (ML3-4), Expert (ML5+). Legendary / Elite monsters may hold 2 management abilities.

### Ability Upgrade Mechanism

A monster's management ability does not stay at its initial tier forever. Through the following means it can be raised from **Normal Tier** to **Enhanced Tier**, then to **Expert Tier**:

#### Upgrade Paths (Four Routes)

| Route | Condition | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|------|------|
| **🟢 Training Ground Polish** | Monster completes training at the training ground (base = 4 rounds / enhanced = 8 rounds / expert = 16 rounds) | Training ground Lv2+ (enhanced), Lv3 (expert); each training costs ◆×(3/6/12) | Ability upgraded 1 tier |
| **🟡 Combat Evolution** | Monster participates in ≥3 battles total (normal→enhanced) or ≥10 battles + kills a Boss (enhanced→expert) | Auto-triggered. Monster must survive | Ability upgraded 1 tier + gains "Veteran" trait (Loyalty (LOY)+1) |
| **🟠 Archetype-Specific Catalysis** | Specific archetypes can catalyze corresponding monster upgrades: Necromancer Lord→undead monsters / Demon→demon monsters / Wizard→mutant creatures / Dragon→dragon-kind / Shadow→shadow monsters / Goblin→constructs | Cost ◇×3 + ●×1 (normal→enhanced) or ◇×5 + ●×3 (enhanced→expert) | Ability upgraded 1 tier, and reroll once on d66 (may keep original ability or pick new) |
| **🔴 Abyssal Blessing** | Triggered by special GM event. E.g. Abyssal mana tide / Nexis whisper / ancient artifact activation | GM adjudication | Ability directly upgraded to Expert Tier (skips Enhanced), may carry extra positive mutation |

#### Upgrade Example

> Your **Gargoyle** initially rolled d66=21 and gained **Swift Builder (Normal Tier)**: Build check +1 die. You put it in a Lv2 training ground, costing ◆×6; after 8 rounds of training — the ability upgrades to **Swift Builder (Enhanced Tier)**: Build check +2 dice. Later, in the holy war against the Order of Light, this Gargoyle joined 12 battles and personally shattered the church Boss's shield — after the battle, Combat Evolution auto-triggers: **Swift Builder (Expert Tier)**: Build check +3 dice, build time halved.

#### Legendary / Elite Monster Exclusive

| Monster Tier | Ability Slots | Upgrade Method |
|---------|:---:|------|
| Normal (ML1-2) | 1 slot, Normal Tier | Can be tiered up via the four routes |
| Elite (ML3-4) | 2 slots (2nd slot needs extra unlock) | Starts with 1 Enhanced-Tier slot. 2nd slot unlockable via training (cost ◆×15) |
| Legendary (ML5+) | 2 slots, Expert Tier | Starts with 1 Expert-Tier slot. 3rd slot unlockable via Abyssal Blessing |

> **Design Intent**: Ability upgrades give low-tier monsters long-term cultivation value — a Skeleton Soldier that accompanies you from Lv1 to Lv15 may gain an Expert-Tier ability and become a dungeon legend.
