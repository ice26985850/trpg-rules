### 5.6.3 Eight Preset NPC Dungeons (Core Diplomacy Targets)

#### 1. Malthus, "Iron-Bone Marquis" — Necromancer Lord Lv8
- **Dungeon**: Eternal Slumber Gallery (an elegant labyrinth built of pure-white bone)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 8 / Infamy (INF) 7 / Defense (DEF) 5 / Wealth (WLH) 6 / Magic (MAG) 9
- **Specialty**: High-grade skeleton soldiers (Combat Value (CV) 1 / Management Value (MV) 1, free upkeep), bone pigeons (long-range instant communication), Soul Essence
- **Personality**: Taciturn and efficiency-worshipping. Intensely dislikes the Order of Light.
- **Attitude**: Neutral (0). Fellow undead lord → Friendly +3. Shared enemy in the Order of Light → Favor +2.
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Joint church defense, undead-labor tech exchange, clues to the lost Bone Mound ruins, request to mediate Bone Mound Council disputes.

#### 2. Balzec, "Earth-Render" — Abyssal Overlord Lv10
- **Dungeon**: Sky-Burning Fortress (a giant obsidian castle ringed by magma rivers)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 9 / Infamy (INF) 9 / Defense (DEF) 7 / Wealth (WLH) 5 / Magic (MAG) 6
- **Specialty**: Hellhounds (CV 3 / MV 1), flame rune stones (trap enchantment), demon minions
- **Personality**: Hot-tempered and arrogant, despises the weak. "Might is right." Naturally scorns Necromancer Lords.
- **Attitude**: Cold (-3). Must show strength (Dominion (DOM) check or defeat specific adventurers) to improve.
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Trial by flame (prove strength), joint surface expedition, designated adventurer bounty hunt, Inferno Lake Abyss monster infestation.

#### 3. Vera, "Web-Weaver" — Shadow Council Lv6
- **Dungeon**: Shadow Hall Labyrinth (a shadow labyrinth whose corridors move on their own)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 7 / Infamy (INF) 10 / Defense (DEF) 5 / Wealth (WLH) 8 / Magic (MAG) 7
- **Specialty**: All kinds of intel (highest quality), shadow carrier pigeons, intel-shielding service
- **Personality**: Mysterious, forever smiling. "Information is worth more than armies."
- **Attitude**: Neutral-leaning-friendly (+2). Extremely eager on intel trades. Cold toward demons (-2: "too loud").
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Intel exchange, joint assassination operations, request to investigate some adventurer's background, Shadow Network membership invitation.

#### 4. Wrench, "Grand Artificer" — Goblin Tinkerer Lv5
- **Dungeon**: Gear Fortress (a mechanical labyrinth of interwoven steam pipes, forever ringing with riveting)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 5 / Infamy (INF) 5 / Defense (DEF) 6 / Wealth (WLH) 9 / Magic (MAG) 3
- **Specialty**: Mechanical sentries (CV 2 / MV 1, constructs), rare Building Materials (output ×2), auto-trap kit
- **Personality**: Enthusiastic chatterbox, tech-obsessed. "Look at this auto-riveting device!"
- **Attitude**: Very friendly (+5). Open trade with all dungeons. Worships lords with Industry (IND) ≥ 4.
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Tech workshop, show him your new invention, joint R&D, bulk Building Materials order discount.

#### 5. Moris, "Thousand-Faces" — Mad Sorcerer Lv7
- **Dungeon**: Twisted Laboratory (cluttered jars, walls seeping colored fluid, gravity on and off)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 6 / Infamy (INF) 7 / Defense (DEF) 4 / Wealth (WLH) 7 / Magic (MAG) 11
- **Specialty**: Mutant monsters (CV random 2-5 + random management ability), forbidden knowledge, mutagen potions
- **Personality**: Mad yet friendly. Research-fanatic, indifferent to politics. Random attitude (roll d6 at the start of each round).
- **Attitude**: Random (d6): 1-2 = Friendly, 3-4 = Neutral, 5 = Cold, 6 = Extremely eager (free mutagen potion included).
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Chaotic mutation experiments (could be very good or very bad), share "interesting phenomena", forbidden knowledge exchange.

#### 6. Selathil, "Ancient Scale" — Ancient Wyrm Lv12
- **Dungeon**: Dragon's Rest Abyss (a giant cavern piled with gold; the clink of sliding coins is the only ambient sound)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 10 / Infamy (INF) 8 / Defense (DEF) 8 / Wealth (WLH) 14 / Magic (MAG) 5
- **Specialty**: Dark Coin loans, ancient magic items, Dragonborn Guard (CV 5 / MV 3)
- **Personality**: Proud and cold. "You may gaze upon me, but do not disturb my counting of coins."
- **Attitude**: Cold (-2). Cares nothing for any dungeon's politics. Only interested in high-value trade (single transaction ≥ ◆50 to even talk).
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Enough coins, clues to ancient artifacts (pique her curiosity), loan-interest negotiation.

#### 7. Ethlin, "Void-Weaver" — Mad Sorcerer Lv5
- **Dungeon**: Void Weaving Workshop (a space-warped violet magic workshop, portals everywhere)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 5 / Infamy (INF) 6 / Defense (DEF) 3 / Wealth (WLH) 6 / Magic (MAG) 12
- **Specialty**: Space-fold bag (carry capacity doubled), dimension-jump rune (single teleport), mini-dimension prison (high-grade cell)
- **Personality**: Quiet and focused, occasionally distracted. "Oh, sorry — I was in another dimension just now."
- **Attitude**: Friendly (+3) toward lords with Arcana (ARC) ≥ 3. Especially welcomes mana exchange.
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Space-magic cooperative research, portal-network construction (reduces expedition time), dimensional-anomaly warning.

#### 8. Corwin, "Rusted Duke" — Necromancer Lord Lv6
- **Dungeon**: Rusting Sanctum (the final resting place of fallen paladins, holy emblems hung inverted from the ceiling)
- **Attributes**: Depth (DEP) 6 / Infamy (INF) 5 / Defense (DEF) 7 / Wealth (WLH) 5 / Magic (MAG) 6
- **Specialty**: Fallen paladins (CV 4 / MV 2, can resist holy light), holy-light resistance training, intra-church intel
- **Personality**: Silent and sorrowful. "I was once their hero… now I am their nightmare." — longs to be understood.
- **Attitude**: Neutral-leaning-friendly (+2). +2 extra toward lords willing to hear his story.
- **Diplomacy Hooks**: Church intel (no one knows the Order of Light better than he), holy-light resistance training, help him recover lost holy-emblem fragments.
