# Scenario Six: The Brotherhood's Armory [Level (Lv.) 6]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 6 × 3-4 players | **Duration:** 5-6 hours | **Map:** Rusted Factory

## Commission
The Smuggler's Ring offers 8,000 ₽ to "borrow" a batch of prototype weapon attachments from the Brotherhood's armory in the Administration Building. Includes a copy of the Administration Building's internal route map.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — West Side Pipeline Entrance
Infiltration route. Inside the pipeline requires Tech Target Number (TN) 11 to navigate (otherwise get lost, +15 minutes). Inside the pipeline are Mutant Rats ×4.

### Scene 2: Administration Building Perimeter
Brotherhood Patrol ×4 + Sentinels ×2 around the building. Approach with Stealth TN 14, or look for a side door (Perception Target Number (TN) 14 to find a vent).

### Scene 3: Administration Building Interior — Armory Floor
Third floor. Brotherhood Soldiers ×3 guard the armory. Electronic lock (Tech Target Number (TN) 17).
- Inside the armory: prototype weapon attachments (Gold ×2) + special operations rifle (Gold) + large amount of military ammunition (Purple ×3)

### Scene 4: Extraction
- After obtaining the items, the alarm triggers (unless the alarm system was disabled beforehand — Tech Target Number (TN) 20)
- Brotherhood reinforcements: +2 soldiers/turn on turn 1 (up to 8)

### Scene 5: Extraction Point
- **Route A:** Underground pipeline (safe but may encounter enemies along the way)
- **Route B:** Random beacon at the railway freight yard (high risk)
- **Route C:** Administration Building rooftop (requires radio; helicopter arrives in 3 turns, defend in the meantime)
