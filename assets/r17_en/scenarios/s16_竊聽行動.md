# Scenario S6: Eavesdropping Operation [Level (Lv.) 6]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 6 × 1 player + 1 companion | **Duration:** 3-4 hours | **Map:** Rusted Factory

## Commission
Owl needs you to install a listening device in the Iron Brotherhood's Administration Building. He emphasizes: "Whatever you do, don't fire a shot. One person's footsteps are lighter than a whole squad's." Reward: 8,000 ₽ + Intelligence Center Level (Lv.) 4 Upgrade Materials. Time limit: 120 minutes.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — East Scrap Yard
Companion Scout: "There's a ventilation duct on the third floor of the Administration Building — you can reach it by climbing the forklift from the scrap yard." (Tech (TEC)/Physique (PHY) Target Number (TN) 11)
- The scrap yard has surveillance equipment abandoned by QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) (Electronics Target Number (TN) 14 to repair → gain real-time interior surveillance feed of the building)

### Scene 2: Ventilation Duct Infiltration
Narrow space. Requires Stealth Target Number (TN) 14 (+2 Advantage). Failure → 1 Iron Brotherhood soldier hears the noise and comes to investigate (can hide in a duct branch to avoid them).
- **Solo-Specific:** The duct can only fit one person; the companion stays at the duct entrance to keep watch (warns the player via radio when enemies are encountered)

### Scene 3: Administration Building 3rd Floor — Install Listening Device
Target location: communications room. 1 Iron Brotherhood sentry patrols the corridor (fixed route, passes the communications room door every 5 minutes).
- Install listening device: Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 11
- Optional: install a second listening device in the Armory (Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 14 + Stealth Target Number (TN) 17)
- **Consequences of Being Discovered:** Alarm triggered → 1 elite guard arrives within 2 rounds. Must extract quickly

### Scene 4: Extraction
- Return via the same ventilation duct route (fastest)
- If alarm is raised: must extract from the rooftop (flare gun + wait 3 rounds for helicopter)
- Successful eavesdropping: before each subsequent deployment, the Intelligence Center automatically provides 1 piece of internal Iron Brotherhood intel

## Solo Adjustment
- **Too Hard:** Sentry is sleeping (Perception (PER) Target Number (TN) 8 to detect snoring); remove the second listening device option
- **Too Easy:** After obtaining intel, discover the Iron Brotherhood is hunting for "a lone infiltrator" — Industrial Zone encounter chance +10% thereafter
