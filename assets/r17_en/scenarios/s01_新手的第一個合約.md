# Scenario One: A Rookie's First Contract [Level (Lv.) 1]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 1 × 2-4 players | **Duration:** 3-4 hours | **Map:** Ash Mall (Edge Zone)

## Commission
Commissioner "Owl" requires obtaining encrypted data from the management office on the third floor of Ash Mall. Reward: 1,200 ₽ + Intelligence Center Level (Lv.) 1 upgrade materials. Time limit: 75 minutes.

## Entry Point
- Default: East Service Entrance (low risk, direct access to the underground parking garage)

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — Parking Garage Edge
Perception check (Target Number (TN) 11) can detect the slow footsteps of Residuals deep in the parking garage.
- Optional exploration: 1 Residual inside the parking garage (see stats below); defeating it allows scavenging vehicle parts (White ×1)

### Scene 2: Food Court (First Floor)
Basic supply point. Two overturned first-aid kits (White ×2). Perception Target Number (TN) 8 can find bandages in the corner.
- Optional encounter: A Newbie Team (2 players) is arguing about whether to go deeper. You can exchange intel for directions to the management office.

### Scene 3: Electronics Store
Most goods have been looted; the back warehouse door is closed. Tech Target Number (TN) 14 (Lockpicking) or Physique Target Number (TN) 14 (Force the Door).
- Inside the warehouse: Electronic Parts (Blue ×1) + Tools (White ×2)

### Scene 4: Management Office (Third Floor)
Locked door. Tech Target Number (TN) 14 (Lockpicking). Inside the office:
- Primary Objective: Encrypted Data (Purple, 2 Slots)
- Random loot (d6): 1-2=Pistol Ammo (White), 3-4=First-Aid Kit (White), 5=Radio Module (Blue), 6=Rooftop Key (triggers Hidden Extraction Point)

After obtaining the data: Perception Target Number (TN) 14 reveals approaching footsteps — a Lone Wolf Veteran also has eyes on the office.

### Scene 5: Extraction
- **Route A:** East Parking Garage (fixed/flare gun/attracts 1d3 Residuals)
- **Route B:** Behind the Gas Station (fixed/wait d6 rounds/Perception TN 11 during wait)
- **Route C:** Rooftop Helipad (hidden/requires office key + flare gun/safest)
- **Route D:** Return the way you came (easy/longest walking distance)

## Adjustment Guide
- **Too Hard:** Reduce the number of Residuals; place an extra first-aid kit in the food court
- **Too Easy:** Extraction Point occupied by the Newbie Team; Pulse Surge arrives early at 50 minutes
- **Not Enough Time:** Go directly Scene 1→4→5 (skip Scenes 2, 3)
