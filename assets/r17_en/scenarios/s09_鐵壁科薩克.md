# Scenario Nine: Iron Wall Cossack [Level (Lv.) 9]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 9 × 4 players | **Duration:** 5-7 hours | **Map:** Rusted Factory — Iron Brotherhood Headquarters

## Commission
All-faction joint bounty 25,000 ₽ + Gold-tier weapons ×2: eliminate the Iron Brotherhood leader "Iron Wall" Cossack. Includes complete blueprint of the Brotherhood Headquarters.

**Prerequisite:** Must complete Scenario Seven or an equivalent pre-quest designed by the GM. The Brotherhood has reached "Hostile" Reputation with the players.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — Deepest Part of the Underground Pipeline
Beneath the Brotherhood Headquarters there is an unmarked maintenance passage. Tech Target Number (TN) 17 to navigate + Stealth TN 14 to avoid the vibration detectors inside the pipeline.

### Scene 2: Brotherhood Headquarters — Barracks Floor
Cossack's Elite Guards ×6 + Officers ×2. This is the Brotherhood's most elite unit. Can be avoided by stealth (TN 17 ×3 times = pass three zones separately).
- Loot: the barracks contains a weapons cache (military assault rifles ×2 + ceramic plate IV ×1 + large amount of ammunition)

### Scene 3: Command Center
Cossack + Elite Guards ×4 + heavy machine gun turrets ×2 (fixed weapons, operator = soldier).
- Turret: HP 20, DR 5, damage 3d6 full-auto, can be disabled by Tech TN 17

### Scene 4: Extraction
After Cossack's death, a self-destruct device activates (see Boss data). Zone-wide alarm — remaining Brotherhood forces regroup from all locations.
- **Only safe route:** Headquarters rooftop — QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) will provide helicopter extraction upon learning of Cossack's death (radio call, arrives in 5 turns)
