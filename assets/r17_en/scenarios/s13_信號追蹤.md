# Scenario S3: Signal Tracking [Level (Lv.) 3]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 3 × 1 Player + 1 Companion | **Duration:** 3-4 hours | **Map:** Silent Community (Old Town)

## Commission
"The Doctor" received an intermittent distress signal from the Silent Community bell tower—the signal is too weak to confirm how many people. He needs one person to go check. "Sending a team is too conspicuous. One person—in and out fast." Reward: 2,500 ₽ + Military First Aid Kits ×2. Time limit: 120 minutes.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry—Elementary School Wall
Stealthy entry. Companion Scout: "There's a Scav Gang active on the north-side street—three people, sounds like they're arguing. We'll cut through the school."

### Scene 2: Elementary School—Brief Safe Zone
Infirmary: First Aid Kits ×2 (White) + possibly adrenaline (Perception Target Number (TN) 14 → Blue ×1, hidden behind the medicine cabinet).
- Classroom Residuals ×2 (former teachers). **Solo Option:** The classroom door can be locked from the outside (Tech Target Number (TN) 8), completely avoiding combat.

### Scene 3: Convenience Store Street → Bell Tower
Optional Encounter: Scav Gang ×3 searching the convenience store. **Solo Option:**
- Have the Companion create a noise at the street corner to lure them away (Companion Stealth Target Number (TN) 14)
- Or the player sneaks alone (+2 advantage, Target Number (TN) 11)

### Scene 4: Bell Tower
Researchers ×2 trapped on the top floor (one severely wounded, one guarding). Surrounding Residuals ×3.
- **Solo-exclusive rescue strategy:** Use the Companion to draw Residuals from the other side (spend Companion 2 Action Points (AP) to make noise); the player takes the chance to lower the researcher down from the window (Physique Target Number (TN) 11) + First Aid Target Number (TN) 14 to stabilize the severely wounded.

### Scene 5: Extraction
- The researcher has a flare gun → can call for extraction at the top of the bell tower (hidden Extraction Point)
- Or return via the north gate of the community

## Solo Adjustment
- **Too Hard:** Residuals ×2 (not 3); Scav Gang does not appear
- **Too Easy:** During extraction, the signal attracts Cult Members ×2 (drawn by the sound of the flare gun)
