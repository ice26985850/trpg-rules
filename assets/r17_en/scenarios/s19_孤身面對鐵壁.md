# Scenario S9: Facing Iron Wall Alone [Level (Lv.) 9]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 9 × 1 player + 2 companions | **Duration:** 5-6 hours | **Map:** Brotherhood Headquarters

## Commission
All factions bounty 25,000 ₽: eliminate "Iron Wall" Cossack. Challenging the Iron Brotherhood leader alone sounds like suicide — but Old Ke hands you a key: "Someone inside the Brotherhood wants to overthrow him. Use this — you'll have help inside headquarters."

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — Route Provided by the Traitor
Old Ke's informant (Iron Brotherhood traitor) provided a backup passage into headquarters. The companion Scout confirms it's safe, then guides entry.
- The traitor meets you on the barracks level — provides 1 extra sneak-attack opportunity (2 Iron Brotherhood soldiers are locked in their bunks from behind by the traitor)

### Scene 2: Barracks Level
Originally 6 elite guards + 2 officers → reduced to 3 guards thanks to the traitor's help.
- Can be fully bypassed via stealth (traitor informs of patrol gaps, Stealth Target Number (TN) 11)

### Scene 3: Command Center — Cossack Boss Fight
Cossack (solo weakening: Chest Hit Points (HP) 24→20, full-body Damage Reduction (DR) 4→3, first-stage reinforcements canceled) + 1 elite guard.
- **Traitor Assist:** On the 3rd combat round, the traitor throws a flashbang from the vent (Courage (CRG) Target Number (TN) 17 within Cossack's area) — giving him a 1-round opening
- **Boss Two Phases (simplified from three):**
  - Phase One (Hit Points (HP) >40%): heavy machine gun suppression
  - Phase Two (Hit Points (HP) <40%): switch to axe charge + self-destruct countdown (4 rounds)
- **Loot:** Heavy Assault Rifle (Gold) + Full-body Heavy Armor (Gold) + Brotherhood Sigil

### Scene 4: Extraction — Final Escape
After Cossack dies, a self-destruct device activates (4 rounds). Base-wide alarm.
- The traitor directs a backup extraction route (headquarters rooftop → QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) helicopter)
- During the 3-round helicopter wait, must hold off 2 elite guards

## Solo Adjustment
- **Too Hard:** Cossack Hit Points (HP) ×0.5, self-destruct canceled, elite guards do not join combat
- **Too Easy:** The traitor's betrayal is discovered → lose support during combat. Cossack regains his full first stage (calls reinforcements ×2)
