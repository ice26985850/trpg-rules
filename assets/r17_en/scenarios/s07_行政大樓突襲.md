# Scenario Seven: Administration Building Raid [Level (Lv.) 7]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 7 × 3-4 players | **Duration:** 5-6 hours | **Map:** Rusted Factory

## Commission
The Scavengers' Compact puts a 12,000 ₽ bounty: eliminate the Iron Brotherhood's regional commander in the Administration Building, "Iron Fist" Viktor. Intel included: Viktor appears during the supply meeting on the third day of every month.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — East Side Scrap Yard
Ambush preparation area. Perception, Target Number (TN) 14 reveals an abandoned anti-aircraft autocannon in the scrap yard (non-functional, but usable as a sniping position).

### Scene 2: Administration Building Lobby
Brotherhood Soldiers ×4 + Elite Guards ×2. You may choose a frontal assault or loop around from the underground pipeline.

### Scene 3: Command Center (Fifth Floor)
"Iron Fist" Viktor + bodyguards ×4. Viktor uses a heavy machine gun for suppressive fire.

### Scene 4: Extraction
After killing Viktor, the Brotherhood triggers a zone-wide alarm — all extraction points are tightly sealed. The players must use a hidden plan.
