# Scenario Three: Distress Signal from Silent Community [Level (Lv.) 3]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 3 × 3-4 players | **Duration:** 4-5 hours | **Map:** Silent Community (Old Town)

## Commission
"The Doctor" received a radio distress call from Silent Community — a research team got trapped while investigating a Time Bubble. Reward: 2,500 ₽ + Advanced Medical Kit. Time limit: 120 minutes.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — Community North Gate
Perception Target Number (TN) 14: glints of reflection from a distant bell tower — possibly someone there. The radio intermittently transmits a distress signal: "We're at... the bell tower..."

### Scene 2: Convenience Store Street
Optional encounter: Scav Gang ×4 is looting a convenience store. You may negotiate (Persuasion Target Number (TN) 14, gain community intel) or slip past (Stealth TN 11).
- Loot: Food (White ×2) + Misc. Items (White ×1) + possible Blue (Perception TN 14 search → d6≥5 = Rare Electronic Parts)

### Scene 3: Elementary School
Medical supplies remain in the infirmary: First-Aid Kit ×2 (White) + Military First-Aid Kit ×1 (Blue) + possible Adrenaline (d6≥5 = Blue).
Inside: Residuals ×2 (former teachers, retaining the "teaching class" behavior pattern — an excellent opportunity for the GM to build an unsettling atmosphere).

### Scene 4: Bell Tower Apartment
The research team is trapped on the top floor, with at least 3 Residuals prowling nearby. The stairwell below is warped — abnormal time flow.
- Rescue: must kill or lure away the Residuals. One research team member is critically wounded (requires First Aid TN 14).
- Loot: Research Data (Blue ×1) + Anomalous Sample (Purple ×1, 3 Slots) + a crate of equipment (containing a GPS locator, Blue)

### Scene 5: Extraction
- **Route A:** Community North Gate (fixed, may be occupied by the Scav Gang)
- **Route B:** Bell Tower Top Floor (hidden, requires flare gun + clearing Residuals) — the research team has a flare gun
- **Route C:** Sewer Escape Hatch (conditional, requires Community Center key)
