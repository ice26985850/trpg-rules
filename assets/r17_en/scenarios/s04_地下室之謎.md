# Scenario Four: The Basement Mystery [Level (Lv.) 4]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 4 × 3-4 players | **Duration:** 4-5 hours | **Map:** Silent Community

## Commission
"Owl" wants to investigate why every detached house in Silent Community has a locked basement. Reward: 3,500 ₽ + Intelligence Center Level (Lv.) 3 unlock.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — Elementary School Wall
Stealthy entry. Perception Target Number (TN) 11 can spot a faint light toward the detached-house district (some basement lights are still on).

### Scene 2: Detached House District
Five houses can be explored. Each locked basement: Tech Target Number (TN) 14-17 (Lockpicking). The GM may place content in two of them:
- **House A:** Former residents' underground shelter — Personal Items (White ×2), Shotgun (old) + 12-gauge ×12
- **House B:** Order of the Watchers' secret altar — Cult Relic (Blue ×1) + Diary (Purple, narrative key)
- **Houses C-E:** Random loot or empty (GM's decision)

Perception Target Number (TN) 17 at House B: airflow from deep in the basement — there is a hidden passage behind the wall.

### Scene 3: Hidden Passage — Underground Tunnel
Leads to a secret space beneath the community center. Encounter on the way: Mutant Hound ×3 patrolling the tunnel. Inside the tunnel, a supply crate left by the cult (First-Aid Kit ×2 + Rare Censer (Blue)).

### Scene 4: Underground Sanctum
The Order of the Watchers' secret stronghold. Cult Member ×3 + Cult Guardian ×1 (elite Mutant).
- Loot: "Curtain Fragment" (Gold, 3 Slots, extremely valuable) + Cult Scriptures ×3 (Purple, 2 Slots each) + abundant Blue offerings
- The cult's "Oracle Stone Tablet" reveals fragments of Core Zone secrets (the GM may use this to set up later plot)

### Scene 5: Extraction
- **Route A:** Community North Gate (fixed, but the cult may give chase)
- **Route B:** Bell Tower (hidden/flare gun)
- **Route C:** Sewer (conditional/key is at the Community Center)
