# Scenario S4: Basement Echoes [Level (Lv.) 4]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 4 × 1 Player + 1 Companion | **Duration:** 3-4 hours | **Map:** Silent Community

## Commission
Owl learned that there is Cult activity in the basement of the Silent Community. He only needs one person to confirm it—"If things go wrong, one person runs faster than a team." Reward: 3,500 ₽. Time limit: 120 minutes.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry—Sewer Exit
The Companion notices Cult symbols on the wall: "This is the mark of the Order of the Watchers—they've made this a holy site."

### Scene 2: Detached Housing Area—Explore the Basement
Three residences can be explored. Each basement bears traces left by the Cult.
- Building B basement: There is a hidden passage behind the wall. **Solo-exclusive:** The Companion can sense a faint airflow in the passage (Awareness Target Number (TN) 11) and find the secret door.

### Scene 3: Underground Tunnel—Cult Stronghold
Cult Members ×2 + Cult Guardian ×1 (solo-weakened version—Chest Hit Points (HP) reduced to 17, Damage Reduction (DR) reduced to 2).
- **Solo Tactic:** Use the tunnel's narrow terrain—face only one enemy at a time. You can close the passage gate (Tech Target Number (TN) 14) to isolate the Guardian.
- Loot: Cult Relic (Blue) + diary fragment (Purple, narrative key) + Curtain Shard (Gold, 3 slots)

### Scene 4: Extraction
- The Cult stronghold has a backup exit (leading behind the community center)—discovery requires Perception Target Number (TN) 14
- Or return the way you came + extraction via the bell tower signal

## Solo Adjustment
- **Too Hard:** The Cult Guardian does not join the fight (performing a ritual, unaware); Cult Members ×1
- **Too Easy:** The diary fragment mentions "the door to the Core Zone requires three keys" → triggers a follow-up side quest
