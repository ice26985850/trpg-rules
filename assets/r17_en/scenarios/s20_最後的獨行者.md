# Scenario S10: The Last Solo Runner [Level (Lv.) 10]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 10 × 1 player + 2 companions | **Duration:** 5-7 hours | **Map:** Core Zone — Ashila Facility Deep Laboratory

## Commission
Finale. QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) + Scavengers' Compact + Smuggler's Ring tripartite joint commission: enter the Deep Laboratory and close the Dimensional Rift. Reward 100,000 ₽ + Legend status. Time limit: 240 minutes.

> **Prerequisite:** Level (Lv.) 10, possess the "Curtain Resonator" (Scenario S8), complete at least 1 Reputation Worship tier line

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) Special Permit Passage
QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) opens the Core Zone entrance exclusively for you. Someone among the companions says: "Last time four went in, only two came out." Another: "This time, we only need one person to make it out alive."

### Scene 2: Facility Perimeter — Time-Distorted Ruins
1 Teleport-type Mutant (weakened: Threat Value 11→8) + 1 Core Zone Mutant (weakened: Threat Value 12→9).
- The player holding the "Curtain Resonator" is unaffected by time distortion (normal movement, no Disadvantage on checks)
- The abandoned laboratory can be scavenged: Energy Cell (Gold ×1) + Prototype Weapon (Gold, random)

### Scene 3: Deep Entry
Gravity Collapse Zone. The companion Technician can activate a backup elevator (Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 17, success → direct to bottom; failure → must climb the elevator shaft, Physique (PHY) Target Number (TN) 14).
- 2 Teleport-type Mutants (weakened: each Threat Value 8)

### Scene 4: Deep Laboratory — Rift Core
The Curtain Resonator plays a key role here: all players' Disadvantage penalties are canceled (originally all Core Zone checks had Disadvantage).
- 2 Core Zone Mutants (weakened) + Dimensional Guardian (ultimate weakening: Chest Hit Points (HP) 30→21, full-body Damage Reduction (DR) 4, time stasis stage canceled, self-destruct mode canceled)
- **The Companions' Last Stand:** During combat, the GM may choose to have one companion sacrifice themselves to buy the player a crucial 1 round (draw the guardian's attack, block a fatal blow, or activate the console)

### Scene 5: Control Room — Close the Rift
Ultimate Trial:
- Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 23 × 3 times (companion Technician may assist, Target Number (TN) reduced to 20)
- Each attempt: the guardian releases an energy wave (all 2d6, Courage (CRG) Target Number (TN) 17)
- Three successes → the rift begins to close
- **Crucial Choice:** Closing the rift requires someone to manually lock it in the control room — one person must stay behind. The player may choose: stay personally (character retirement ending) or order a companion to stay (companion permanently lost)

### Scene 6: Final Escape
Closing the rift triggers a chain collapse. Extract within 10 rounds.
- Residuals encountered along the extraction route (each round d6: 5-6 = 1 Residual)
- QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) helicopter waits outside the facility (arrives in the last 3 rounds)

### Endgame Choice
| Choice | Ending |
|---|---|
| Stay to close the rift personally | **Sacrifice Ending** — the character becomes a legend of the Quarantine Zone. The companion inherits their will, and the Hideout becomes a museum. |
| Companion stays | **Survival Ending** — the player survives but loses their most trusted companion. Gains the title "Solo Runner". |
| Activate timer (Tech (TEC) Target Number (TN) 23) | **Perfect Ending** — after setting a timed lock, everyone extracts. But Target Number (TN) 23 is Very Hard (success rate only 5%). |

---

> If you completed these ten scenarios alone — the history of the Quarantine Zone has already been rewritten. The Solo Runner is not one who lacks teammates, but one who chooses to bear everything alone.
