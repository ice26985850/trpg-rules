# Scenario Two: Secrets of the Underground Parking Garage [Level (Lv.) 2]

**Suitable:** Level (Lv.) 2 × 3-4 players | **Duration:** 3-4 hours | **Map:** Ash Mall

## Commission
"The Blacksmith" received intel: deep in the parking garage sits a military supply vehicle abandoned before the lockdown, possibly still carrying unopened weapon crates. Reward: 1,800 ₽ + keep any weapons found. Time limit: 90 minutes.

## Scene Sequence

### Scene 1: Entry — East Service Entrance
Perception Target Number (TN) 11 can find tire tracks (military vehicle) leading deeper into the parking garage. Also discover signs of a Mutant Rat swarm (Survival TN 11).

### Scene 2: Underground Parking Garage — Shallow Level
Mutant Rat swarm ×4 (see below). After defeating them, scavenge: Vehicle Parts (White ×1) + Fuel Can (White ×1).

### Scene 3: Parking Garage Mid-Level — Blockade Line
An abandoned QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) temporary blockade facility. Tech Target Number (TN) 14 can bypass the electronic lock to enter.
- Inside: QZAC (Quarantine Zone Administration Commission) records (Blue ×1) + Body Armor IIA (DR 2, White ×1) + Submachine Gun ammo

Perception Target Number (TN) 14: hear mechanical sounds from deep within — Residuals are gathered near the supply vehicle.

### Scene 4: Parking Garage Deep Level — Military Supply Vehicle
Residuals ×3 + 1 Enhanced Residual (former soldier, wearing partial armor).
- Inside the supply vehicle: 2 surplus military pistols (1d8) + 9mm ×90 + possible: military SMG (d6≥5 = Blue SMG, d6≤4 = ammo crate (White))

### Scene 5: Extraction
- **Route A:** East Parking Garage (fixed, but may be occupied by a Lone Wolf Veteran)
- **Route B:** Behind the Gas Station (fixed/wait d6 rounds)
- **Route C:** Emergency Stairs → Mall Interior → Rooftop (must pass possibly respawning Residuals)
