# Search, Attack, Retreat TRPG — Hideout Complete System v1.0

> **Six Facilities × Five Levels, with per-level costs, effects, materials, and prerequisites fully detailed.**

# I. Overview

The Hideout is the players' safe base outside the Quarantine Zone (QZ)—an abandoned apartment, an underground warehouse, or any place you can hold. Starting from an empty room, gradually expand it into your operations command center.

| Facility | Core Function | Lv.0 Status | Lv.5 Ultimate Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armory | Weapon storage & modification | On-person equipment | Unlimited storage, Legendary modification, repair cost -50% |
| Infirmary | Treatment & recovery | Consumables only | 6 HP/day, biochemical treatment, permanent injury removal |
| Workbench | Crafting & repair | Unavailable | Repair cost -80%, replicate Gold attachments |
| Intelligence Center | Commissions & intel | Basic commissions (1-2) | Gold commissions, full-zone live intel |
| Trading Post | Buy/sell/trade | Basic trader (list price) | Buy price -25%, sell price +20%, Gold random |
| Vault | Equipment insurance | Cannot insure | Premium 10%, 6 items 100% payout, instant |
