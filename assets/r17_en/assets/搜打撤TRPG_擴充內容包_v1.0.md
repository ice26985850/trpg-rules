# Chapter Two: Side Story Lines (12)

## 2.1 Old Ke's Past
**Trigger:** Complete 3 Scavengers' Compact commissions
**Progress:**
1. Old Ke begins to reveal that he was once the head of security at the Ashila Facility
2. Commission the player to find his personal belongings left in the Silent Community bell tower
3. Find a photo of Old Ke's daughter — she was working inside the facility at the time of the Collapse
4. Old Ke gives you the backup security code to the Deep Laboratory (key item of Scenario Ten)
5. If you return alive and tell him the truth, Old Ke leaves you all his savings before departing the Quarantine Zone

## 2.2 The Blacksmith's Secret Recipe
**Trigger:** Blacksmith Affinity Level (Lv.) 3+
**Progress:**
1. The Blacksmith reveals he was once an engineer in the military's weapon R&D department
2. Commission to retrieve the design blueprints confiscated from him back then from the Rusted Factory
3. The blueprints reveal the complete manufacturing process of the prototype concussion hammer
4. The Blacksmith can forge Gold weapons for you (materials provided by yourself)
5. If he learns the blueprints were copied by the Iron Brotherhood, he commissions you to destroy the Brotherhood's version

## 2.3 The Doctor's True Identity
**Trigger:** Doctor Affinity Level (Lv.) 3 + complete 2 medical commissions
**Progress:**
1. While treating you, the Doctor casually mentions "the side effects of the Curtain experiment"
2. You may press further — the Doctor was once the medical director of the Ashila Facility
3. He carries one thing with him: an unaltered Collapse-day medical record
4. The record mentions "Test Subject Zero" — a person infected by rift energy before the Collapse
5. The Doctor is searching for this person. The clues point to the Core Zone.

## 2.4 Owl's Chess Game
**Trigger:** Owl Affinity Level (Lv.) 4 + complete 5 intelligence commissions
**Progress:**
1. Owl reveals he is not acting alone — he is an agent of an outside-world organization
2. That organization's goal is to ensure "Curtain technology" is not monopolized by any faction
3. He needs you to steal one piece of key intel from each of the Five Factions
4. Upon completion it is revealed: an outside-world secret military operation against the Quarantine Zone is brewing
5. Branching ending: assist Owl to stop the operation / sell the intel to QZAC / wait to reap the spoils

## 2.5 The Debtor's Redemption
**Trigger:** Character Origin is "Debtor"
**Progress:**
1. The creditor sends someone to collect the debt — a Bounty Hunter appears at the Hideout door
2. Options: pay off the debt / take the creditor's dangerous commission to offset the debt / try to deal with the creditor
3. If you choose the debt-offset commission: go deep into the Industrial Zone to retrieve a Gold item
4. The faction behind the creditor is the upper echelon of the smuggler network
5. Upon completion, gain a permanent passive: Negotiation Instinct upgraded (buy price -10%, sell price +10%)

## 2.6 The Border Dweller's Hidden Route
**Trigger:** Origin is Border Dweller + Level (Lv.) 3+
**Progress:**
1. A mark on the hand-drawn map suddenly makes sense — it is a sign your grandfather left behind
2. On-site inspection of the marked location: an abandoned weather station at the edge of the Core Zone
3. Beneath the weather station is a secret shelter built before the Collapse
4. Inside: your grandfather's mementos + a route in and out of the Core Zone never discovered by anyone
5. This route can be used permanently, and completely avoids all known factions

## 2.7 The Mutant Adapted's Mutation
**Trigger:** Origin is Mutant Adapted + Level (Lv.) 5+
**Progress:**
1. Your mutation begins to evolve — gain a new sensory ability (GM's choice)
2. The Doctor is very interested in this and offers a free examination
3. Test result: your DNA is actively adapting to rift energy — you are a living "Curtain" Resonator
4. Once the Order learns of this, they try to recruit you (or kidnap you as the ritual core)
5. Endgame: you may choose to suppress the mutation (return to normal) or fully accept it (permanently gain teleportation ability, but Charisma permanently -2)

## 2.8 The Veteran's Brotherhood Ties
**Trigger:** Origin is Veteran + first encounter with Iron Brotherhood soldiers
**Progress:**
1. The Iron Brotherhood soldier hesitates after seeing your dog tags — does not open fire immediately
2. One of them recognizes your unit insignia: he served in the same unit as you
3. He gives you a choice: join the Iron Brotherhood, or at least remain neutral
4. If you choose to cooperate: you can obtain an Iron Brotherhood pass (free movement in the Industrial Zone)
5. If you refuse: the Iron Brotherhood treats you as a "traitor," Threat Value +2 (against all Iron Brotherhood soldiers)

## 2.9 The Quarantine Zone Radio
**Trigger:** Radio equipment + frequency-search during any sortie
**Progress:**
1. You accidentally receive a mysterious broadcast: a woman reading poetry
2. The frequency is different each time. The content received each time is different — as if conveying some kind of message
3. Owl tells you this radio is called "Voice of the Curtain" within the Quarantine Zone
4. Trace the signal source: the main broadcast tower of the Ashila Facility deep in the Core Zone
5. If you enter the broadcast tower: you find a female researcher trapped in a time bubble by rift energy — she has been broadcasting there for three years

## 2.10 The Brotherhood's Traitor
**Trigger:** First defeat of an Iron Brotherhood patrol captain (after Scenario Five)
**Progress:**
1. On the patrol captain's body you find an undelivered letter — written to his daughter outside the Quarantine Zone
2. The letter reveals: he no longer wants to serve Cossack, but defecting would mean execution
3. You may choose: ignore / deliver the letter (requires going through a smuggler) / find more Iron Brotherhood members who want to defect
4. If you dig deeper: you discover a small group within the Iron Brotherhood is planning to overthrow Cossack
5. Endgame: assist the coup — your Scenario Nine difficulty is lowered (someone inside the Iron Brotherhood opens the door)

## 2.11 The Smuggler's Treasures
**Trigger:** Snake Eye Affinity Level (Lv.) 5
**Progress:**
1. Snake Eye invites you to his private storage room — filled with contraband shipped in from the outside world
2. He takes out one thing: the "Prototype Blueprint" that leaked from the Ashila Facility before the Collapse
3. The blueprint depicts the true purpose of the "Curtain" experiment: it was never an energy experiment, but **weaponization**
4. QZAC knows the truth but has suppressed the information. Some people in the outside world want to restart this experiment.
5. Snake Eye asks you: sell the blueprint to the highest bidder, or destroy it?

## 2.12 The Children of the Quarantine Zone
**Trigger:** Discover children's belongings in the Silent Community during any sortie
**Progress:**
1. You discover traces of children living inside the Quarantine Zone — small footprints, leftover food
2. Old Ke tells you: a group of children separated from their parents during the evacuation are being protected by the Scavengers' Compact
3. One of the children is sick and needs a specific medicine (only obtainable at the Industrial Zone chemical factory)
4. If you obtain the medicine: all Scavengers' Compact Reputation is reset to "Worship"
5. The children will give you a "lucky charm" they found in the ruins — a random Gold item

3. Encounter intervals per sortie must be no less than 5 minutes of game time (corner encounters excluded)
