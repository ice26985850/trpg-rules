# Search, Attack, Retreat TRPG — Solo Rules Supplement v1.0

> **For:** 1 GM + 1 player
> **Core Changes:** Introduces NPC Companion System, Action Point (AP) adjustments, Threat Value Recalibration, Death Protection, Stealth Enhancement

---

# Chapter One: Why It Doesn't Suit Solo Play — and How to Fix It

## 1.1 The Solo Bottleneck of the Original System

| Problem | Original Design | Solo Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Action Economy** | 2-4 players = 4-8 Action Points (AP)/round | Solo only 2 Action Points (AP) → surrounded = instant death |
| **Role Division** | Team can divide roles (Tank/Shooter/Technician/Medic) | Solo cannot fill multiple roles → encounter design limited |
| **Death Consequence** | Teammates can carry out your gear | Solo death = total loss, no buffer |
| **Suppressed/Prone** | Teammates can cover or drag you out | Solo prone = nearly certain death |
| **Part Injury** | Teammates can provide first aid | Solo with injured right arm, even self-first-aid is hard |
| **Encounter Budget** | Threat Value calculated for 4 people | Level (Lv.) 1 minimum encounter = Residuals×3 (Threat Value 9), excessive for solo |

## 1.2 Core Fixes

| Fix | Method |
|---|---|
| **NPC Companion System** | Player may bring 1-2 NPC Companions on missions (§Chapter Two) |
| **Action Point (AP) Adjustment** | Solo player + companion total ≥ 4 Action Points (AP)/round (§1.3) |
| **Threat Value Recalibration** | All encounter Threat Values ×0.5 (§Chapter Three) |
| **Death Protection** | After going prone, companion can drag to extract; death can trigger companion inheritance (§1.4) |
| **Stealth Enhancement** | Solo easier to avoid combat, Stealth bonus +2 (§Chapter Four) |

## 1.3 Action Point (AP) Adjustment

In solo mode, the player and companions share the round:

| Configuration | Action Points (AP)/round |
|---|---|
| Player + 1 Companion | Player 2 Action Points (AP) + Companion 2 Action Points (AP) = **4 Action Points (AP)** |
| Player + 2 Companions | Player 2 Action Points (AP) + Companion A 2 Action Points (AP) + Companion B 2 Action Points (AP) = **6 Action Points (AP)** (but Threat Value unchanged, GM may raise it) |

The player controls all companion actions. Companions use simplified data cards (see §2.4).

## 1.4 Death Protection

| Situation | Original Rule | Solo Modification |
|---|---|---|
| Player Hit Points (HP) = 0 (Chest) | Mortal (Near Death), Courage Target Number (TN) 14 each round | **Companion may spend 2 Action Points (AP) to drag for extraction** (movement halved, no opportunity attack) |
| Player Death | All gear lost, new character inherits | **Companion may inherit player's gear** (companion becomes new protagonist); or companion returns gear to Hideout and recruits a new character |
| Companion Death | — | Companion permanently lost. May recruit a new companion at the Hideout (§2.5) |

> **Reference:** Player Rulebook §2.12 (Death and Inheritance) — the inheritance rules apply equally in solo mode. A companion returning to the Hideout is treated as "successful extraction but loss of player character," with a 100% gear retention rate (replacing the original 80% fund inheritance).

# Chapter Two: NPC Companion System

## 2.1 Acquiring Companions

| Method | Condition | Gain |
|---|---|---|
| **Starting Companion** | At character creation | Choose 1 companion (from 5 presets) |
| **Recruit** | Hideout Rest Phase, spend 1 action | After successful socializing (TN 14), may recruit from tavern/safe house |
| **Story Acquisition** | Complete specific side quest | Side-quest NPC joins (e.g., rescued Newbie Team member) |
| **Hire** | Trading Post Level (Lv.) 2+ | Pay 2,000-5,000 ₽ to hire a professional mercenary (1 mission) |

## 2.2 Companion Growth

Companions do not gain XP independently; instead they grow with the player's level:

| Player Level | Companion Attribute Bonus | Companion Feats Gained |
|---|---|---|
| Level (Lv.) 1-2 | Baseline | 0 |
| Level (Lv.) 3-4 | Baseline +1 | 1 |
| Level (Lv.) 5-6 | Baseline +2 | 1 |
| Level (Lv.) 7-8 | Baseline +3 | 2 |
| Level (Lv.) 9-10 | Baseline +4 | 2 |

## 2.4 Companion Control Rules

- Companions use a **simplified NPC data card** (see the "Companion Card" sheet in Excel)
- Companion Hit Points (HP) use the Seven-Part system (same as the player), but simplified to: **Chest Hit Points (HP) reaching zero = unconscious, Head Hit Points (HP) reaching zero = death**
- Companions do not make Fear/Greed checks (the player makes them on their behalf)
- Companions will not proactively make major decisions — the GM may hint "Wraith looks worried about this path," but the player makes the final call
- Companions have 2 Action Points (AP)/round, commanded by the player

## 2.5 Losing and Replacing Companions

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Companion dies during a mission | Permanently lost. Gear can be recovered (unless there's no time). A new companion may be recruited at the Hideout |
| Companion severely wounded (Chest Hit Points (HP) = 0) | Requires 1d3 missions to recover after returning to the Hideout |
| Player dies but companion survives | Companion may become the new protagonist (use companion data + inheritance rules), or recruit an entirely new character |

# Chapter Three: Threat Value Recalibration

## 3.1 Solo Threat Budget

| Player Level (+1 Companion) | Suggested Total Threat Value | Typical Encounter |
|---|---|---|
| Level (Lv.) 1 | 3-6 | Residuals×1-2, or Mutant Rat×2-4 |
| Level (Lv.) 2 | 4-8 | Residuals×2-3, or Lone Wolf Veteran×1 |
| Level (Lv.) 3 | 6-10 | Residuals×3-4, or Cultist Member×2 |
| Level (Lv.) 4 | 8-14 | Warlord Soldier×1-2, or Mutant×1 |
| Level (Lv.) 5 | 10-16 | Warlord Soldier×2-3, or Patrol Captain×1 |
| Level (Lv.) 6-7 | 12-20 | Elite Guard×2, or Bounty Hunter×1 + Soldier×2 |
| Level (Lv.) 8-9 | 16-28 | Iron Fist Victor (HP halved), or Cult Guardian×1 + Member×2 |
| Level (Lv.) 10 | 20-35 | "Iron Wall" Cossack (Hit Points (HP) -30%), or Dimensional Guardian (weakened version) |

> **Reference:** GM Rulebook §3.8 (Encounter Difficulty Budget) — the original table is designed for 4 people; this section is the solo-recalibrated version.

## 3.2 Boss Weakening Rules

In solo mode, all Bosses automatically apply one of the following weakenings (choose one):

| Weakening Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Health Reduction** | All Boss Part Hit Points (HP) ×0.7 (rounded) |
| **Armor Weakening** | All Boss Part Damage Reduction (DR) −1 |
| **Phase Simplification** | Three-phase Boss reduced to two phases (skip the middle phase) |
| **Reinforcement Cancel** | Boss will not call for reinforcements |

# Chapter Four: Solo Tactical Enhancement

## 4.1 Stealth Advantage

In solo mode, Stealth checks gain a **permanent +2 bonus** (easier to avoid combat).

> **Reference:** Player Rulebook §3.7 and GM Rulebook §2.10 (Stealth/Vision rules). The original Target Number (TN) values are unchanged; this section provides an additional bonus.

## 4.2 Preemptive Strike

In solo mode, if the player launches an attack while the enemy is unaware:

- First round, player + companion gain **+3 Initiative**
- Enemy first round is **caught off guard** (cannot use Reactions)
- First shot automatically counts as an **Aimed Shot** (can specify target part, no extra Action Point (AP) required)

> **Reference:** Player Rulebook §3.2 Shooting Modifiers (Aimed Shot rules).

## 4.3 First Aid Enhancement

In solo mode, the Target Number (TN) for the player performing first aid on themselves drops from 14 to **11**. The companion's first-aid Target Number (TN) for the player remains 11.

> **Reference:** GM Rulebook §2.5 Special Actions (First Aid DC).

## 4.4 Retreat Options

In solo mode, the following retreat options are added:

| Action | Action Point (AP) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Smoke Retreat** | 2 | Throw smoke grenade + move toward safety, no opportunity attack triggered |
| **Emergency Extraction Call** | 1 | If within 50m of an Extraction Point and holding a flare gun, may call the extraction vehicle 1d3 rounds early |
| **Sacrifice Cover** | 1 | Order companion to suppressing fire (companion stays in place), player moves in the meantime. Companion may be focused-fired next round |

# Chapter Five: Character Creation Suggestions

## 5.1 Recommended Origins (Solo)

| Priority | Origin | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| ★★★ | Veteran | High Physique + Precision, combined with a Medic companion enables independent combat |
| ★★★ | Scavenger | High Perception → detects threats early, paired with an Assault companion to supplement firepower |
| ★★☆ | Former Corporate Employee | High Tech → handles Lockpicking/Electronics alone, paired with an All-Rounder |
| ★★☆ | Medic | Brings own first-aid ability, letting the companion focus on combat |
| ★☆☆ | Debtor | Solo debt = more pressure, but negotiation advantage yields better Commissions |
| ★☆☆ | Border Dweller | High Perception + Courage, hidden entrances safer for solo |
| ★☆☆ | Mutant Adapted | Strong resistance but low Tech → needs a Technician companion |

## 5.2 Recommended Skills

| Essential | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Firearms Level (Lv.) 3+ | Solo firepower is the foundation of survival |
| First Aid Level (Lv.) 2+ | Backup self-treatment (even with a Medic companion) |
| Stealth Level (Lv.) 2+ | Solo combat avoidance priority |
| Awareness Level (Lv.) 2+ | Detect threats first = choose fight or retreat first |

> **Reference:** Player Rulebook §2.2 (Origin List), §1.5 (Twenty Skills System), §2.1 Quick Character Creation (solo players are advised to use the "Balanced" or "Scout" configuration of quick creation).
