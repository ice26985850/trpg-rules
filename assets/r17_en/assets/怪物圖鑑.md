| Armor | Full-Body Heavy Armor (Damage Reduction (DR) 4), Heavy Riot Helmet (Damage Reduction (DR) 2) |

**Three-Phase Mechanic:**
1. **Phase One (Hit Points (HP) > 70%):** Fire Suppression — every round uses full-auto sweep, calls in soldier reinforcements (1d4 troops, arrive after d6 rounds)
2. **Phase Two (Hit Points (HP) 30-70%):** Axe Charge — melee primary, Morale Roar (within 3m radius, Courage Target Number (TN) 14)
3. **Phase Three (Hit Points (HP) < 30%):** Self-Destruct Activated — 3-round countdown, detonates 3d6 radius 10m

**Threat Value:** 16
